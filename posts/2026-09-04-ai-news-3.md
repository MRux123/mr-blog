## WORLD

*   **Volkswagen Announces Massive Workforce Reduction**: In a significant blow to the European automotive sector, the Volkswagen Group has confirmed plans to cut an additional 50,000 jobs. This brings the company's total projected layoffs to 100,000 by 2030, reflecting the severe structural challenges facing legacy automakers in the transition to new technologies.
*   **UN Proposes Revolutionary World Map**: The UN General Assembly is set to vote on a Togo-sponsored resolution aimed at phasing out the traditional Mercator map. The initiative seeks to adopt a projection that accurately reflects the true scale of the African continent, correcting historical geographical biases that have long misrepresented its size relative to northern territories.
*   **Escalating Tensions in the Middle East**: The U.S. government is investigating reports that a missile strike hit a wedding ceremony in Iran, allegedly resulting in four fatalities. The incident, reported by the Iranian Red Crescent Society, threatens to further destabilize an already volatile region.

## POLAND

*   **Political Maneuvering Over Maciej Berek's Candidacy**: Parliamentary proceedings continue to focus on the potential appointment of Maciej Berek. As highlighted by Bartosz Romowicz in recent media appearances, the final vote regarding this candidacy remains a pivotal point of discussion in the current legislative agenda, signaling shifts in political alignments within the Sejm.
