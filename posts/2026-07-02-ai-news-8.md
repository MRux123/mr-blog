## WORLD

*   **Escalation in Kyiv:** Russian forces have launched their most significant missile and drone strikes on the Ukrainian capital in recent weeks. The attack has resulted in at least 13 fatalities, including children, marking a tragic intensification of the ongoing conflict.
*   **Humanitarian Crisis in Gaza:** International concern grows as patients in Gaza face life-threatening delays in medical evacuation. Reports indicate that approximately 300 Palestinians have died while awaiting transfer for critical treatment abroad since the implementation of the ceasefire.

## POLAND

*   **Intelligence Operation:** The Internal Security Agency (ABW) has apprehended two individuals—a Belarusian national and a Polish citizen—on suspicion of espionage. The pair is accused of conducting reconnaissance and propaganda activities directed by Belarusian intelligence services.
*   **Legal and Political Developments:** The legal proceedings surrounding former Minister of Justice Zbigniew Ziobro have taken a new turn, with discussions shifting toward potential extradition as the primary mechanism for prosecution. In a separate political incident, a local councilor has faced disciplinary action following a public confrontation with a taxi driver, an event that has sparked a broader debate on public conduct.
*   **Accountability in Healthcare:** Authorities have taken decisive action at the Szpital Południowy in Warsaw, where the coordinator of the hospital's morgue has been summarily dismissed. This follows mounting public pressure regarding mismanagement within the facility.
