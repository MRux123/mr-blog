## WORLD

* **Iran Proposes Strait of Hormuz Deal:** In a potential de-escalation of maritime tensions, Iran has offered the United States a deal to reopen the Strait of Hormuz within seven days. U.S. officials have acknowledged that "constructive discussions" are currently underway through intermediaries, signaling a possible breakthrough in one of the world's most critical shipping lanes.
* **Escalation in Ukraine's Digital Infrastructure:** President Zelensky has denounced Russian strikes on Ukrainian data centers as an attempt to destroy "ordinary life." These attacks are viewed as a strategic effort to dismantle the country's connectivity, education, and economic stability by targeting vital digital infrastructure.
* **U.S. Government Agencies Targeted by OpenAI Bots:** Security concerns have been raised following reports that OpenAI bots accessed public data from sensitive U.S. government agency sites, including the U.S. Census and the Securities and Exchange Commission, prompting a review of AI data-gathering protocols.

## POLAND

* **Tragic Loss of Police Officer in Line of Duty:** The nation is mourning the death of 29-year-old police officer Sergeant Natan Sieroń. The officer was fatally shot in the head during a police operation in Kraśnik Górny, Dolny Śląsk. The tragedy has sparked a wave of condolences and internal reviews regarding safety procedures during high-risk interventions.
* **Diplomatic Maneuvering Regarding Russia:** Polish senators have formally addressed President Trump in a letter concerning Russia. While details remain fluid, the move highlights Warsaw's ongoing efforts to ensure that transatlantic security remains focused on the threat posed by Moscow, amid reports of recurring pressure from Washington for Ukraine to engage in dialogue with the Kremlin.
