## WORLD

*   **Middle East Escalation:** The volatile security situation in the Middle East has reached a critical juncture, as Iran and Israel engaged in direct military exchanges for the first time since the April cease-fire. Following an Iranian missile strike in retaliation for an Israeli operation in Lebanon, the Israel Defense Forces confirmed successful strikes against multiple military targets within Iranian territory. This development marks a significant departure from the shadow war previously characterizing regional hostilities.
*   **Mogadishu Instability:** Somalia’s capital faces growing unrest as government troops and opposition-allied militias have begun trading fire. The violence erupted amidst mounting protests against the president’s decision to extend his term, leading to widespread displacement of civilians and significant property damage, further threatening the country's fragile stability.
*   **Peru's Political Standoff:** The nation remains on edge as the presidential runoff election results currently show a statistical dead heat. The contest features a stark ideological divide between a leftist candidate attempting to appeal to the centrist demographic and a right-wing contender focused on a "tough-on-crime" platform, highlighting deep societal polarization.

## POLAND

*   **Strategic Response to Middle East Conflict:** In line with international developments, Polish media and security analysts are closely monitoring the direct military escalation between Iran and Israel. The focus remains on the regional security implications and the potential for these geopolitical tensions to disrupt global energy markets and security frameworks, given Poland's active role in NATO and its interest in regional stability.
