## WORLD

* **Escalating Conflict in the Middle East:** The security situation remains dire as Israeli military operations in Lebanon and the West Bank intensify. Reports confirm hundreds of casualties in Lebanon following strikes aimed at Hezbollah infrastructure, while a tragic incident in the West Bank resulted in the deaths of a Palestinian family. Meanwhile, global markets are bracing for prolonged energy instability as the Strait of Hormuz remains largely closed to U.S. and allied vessels.
* **Geopolitical Tensions Over Russian Oil:** Ukrainian President Volodymyr Zelensky has publicly accused European Union allies of "blackmail" regarding the resumption of Russian oil flows via Ukraine, arguing that such actions effectively undermine the international sanctions regime. 
* **Humanitarian and Environmental Challenges:** Africa faces a dual crisis as severe flooding in Kenya has claimed dozens of lives, while experts raise alarms over the proliferation of Chinese-built AI-powered mass surveillance systems across the continent, which are reportedly eroding privacy and fundamental human rights.

## POLAND

* **Police Search for Former Ekstraklasa Player:** Law enforcement agencies have issued an official search warrant for Kamil Sylwestrzak, a 37-year-old former professional footballer. He is suspected of leading an organized crime group and involvement in large-scale drug trafficking.
* **Tragic Railway Accident in Płock:** A fatal collision occurred at an unguarded railroad crossing in Ogorzelice, where a BMW vehicle was struck by a railbus. The passenger of the vehicle was killed, and the driver remains in the hospital.
* **Political Polarization:** Significant public protests have taken place in front of the Presidential Palace, reflecting heightened domestic tensions regarding current legislative debates and government policies, illustrating a deepening divide in the Polish political landscape.
