## WORLD

* **Iran in Transition:** Following the death of Ayatollah Ali Khamenei, Tehran is undergoing a significant transformation. The government is utilizing the funeral proceedings to project an image of internal strength and continuity, though public sentiment remains divided. Critics argue that the unfinished state of the monumental site hosting the funeral serves as a poignant metaphor for the unrealized socio-political promises of Khamenei’s long-standing leadership.
* **Diplomatic High-Stakes Meeting:** In a major development for the ongoing conflict in Ukraine, the United States has confirmed that President-elect Donald Trump will hold a bilateral meeting with President Volodymyr Zelensky in Ankara. This summit is expected to be a pivotal moment in shaping the future trajectory of U.S. foreign policy toward the war and the potential for a ceasefire.

## POLAND

* **Public Transport Tragedy in Warsaw:** A major incident occurred at the Rondo Zesłańców Syberyjskich in Warsaw, where a city bus collided with a tram before careening through traffic and crashing into an underground pedestrian crossing. Local authorities report at least six individuals have been injured, with rescue operations currently underway.
* **Political Controversy Over Defense Policy:** National security has become a flashpoint for internal political debate, as Konfederacja MEP Anna Bryłka leveled serious accusations against former and current defense ministers regarding the transfer of Patriot missile systems to Ukraine. The discourse highlights deep systemic tensions concerning transparency and the management of Poland’s defense capabilities during regional instability.
