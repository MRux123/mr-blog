## WORLD

* **Escalation in Kyiv:** Russian forces have executed one of the most massive missile and drone barrages against the Ukrainian capital to date. Local authorities report a death toll of at least 21 people. This strike follows recent Ukrainian military operations deep within Russian territory, marking a significant intensification in the ongoing conflict.
* **Vatican Excommunications:** In a rare and sweeping ecclesiastical move, the Vatican has announced the excommunication of approximately 600,000 followers of the Society of Saint Pius X, a traditionalist Catholic sect. This action represents a major rupture within the Church hierarchy and its splinter factions.

## POLAND

* **Loss of Refugee Status for Ziobro and Romanowski:** The Hungarian government has officially revoked the refugee status previously granted to former Polish Justice Minister Zbigniew Ziobro and associate Marcin Romanowski. Additionally, their travel documents, including the Geneva Refugee Travel Document, have been invalidated. This development complicates the legal situation of these figures, who have been central to ongoing political investigations in Poland.
* **Donald Trump's NATO Rhetoric:** Former U.S. President Donald Trump has publicly criticized Poland's financial relationship with the United States and the broader NATO alliance. In a post on Truth Social, Trump described the current state of NATO defense spending as "ridiculous," specifically highlighting Poland as a nation in which the U.S. invests significantly without receiving commensurate benefits.
