## WORLD

* **Colombia's Presidential Runoff:** The Colombian presidential election has officially moved to a second round of voting, scheduled for June 21. The ballot will feature a stark ideological contrast between a leftist senator and a pro-Trump conservative challenger. Analysts view the outcome as a pivotal moment for the broader political trajectory of Latin America, reflecting a deepening divide between the left and an emerging right-wing wave in the region.
* **Diplomatic Tensions regarding US-Iran Relations:** Reports from major news outlets indicate that the United States is seeking specific amendments to the existing deal with Iran. The proposed changes focus on critical security concerns, specifically regarding the freedom of navigation in the Strait of Hormuz and the mandatory removal of highly enriched uranium stockpiles.

## POLAND

* **Public Assessment of President Nawrocki:** A recent poll conducted for "Rzeczpospolita" reveals a nuanced public perception of President Karol Nawrocki’s tenure. In a ranking of Third Republic presidents, Nawrocki secured the third position. Respondents placed his performance behind that of Aleksander Kwaśniewski and Lech Kaczyński, yet ahead of Lech Wałęsa, highlighting the electorate's shifting priorities and complex historical assessment of state leadership.
