## WORLD

*   **Escalating Conflict in Ukraine:** Rescue operations continue following a devastating Russian strike that claimed 22 lives, including an eight-year-old child. The tragedy highlights the intensifying civilian toll of the ongoing war.
*   **Mideast Diplomatic Balancing Act:** While Israel continues its military campaign in southern Lebanon, a de facto pause in strikes on Beirut suggests the effectiveness of recent U.S. diplomatic pressure. Tensions remain high as potential peace talks with Iran face mounting uncertainty.
*   **U.S.-Kenya Tensions over Health Policy:** The Trump administration’s plan to establish an Ebola quarantine unit in Kenya has hit a legal impasse. The proposal has faced significant backlash from local residents in Nanyuki, who fear potential health risks and have labeled the initiative an example of geopolitical double standards.

## POLAND

*   **Political Controversy in the Capital:** The Law and Justice (PiS) party has officially nominated Michał Drewnicki as its candidate for the upcoming mayoral election in Kraków. The decision reflects the party’s strategy to consolidate its position during the snap election period.
*   **High-Level Diplomatic Question Marks:** Former German Chancellor Gerhard Schröder’s recent appearance in Moscow has drawn sharp criticism from both Kyiv and the EU. The timing of his visit—shortly after Vladimir Putin proposed him as a peace mediator—has fueled concerns regarding his ongoing ties to the Kremlin.
*   **Economic Outlook and Government Policy:** Minister of Energy Miłosz Motyka is set to address the public on the future of fuel price subsidies ahead of the long weekend. The discussion is critical, as it may signal a shift in the government’s approach to temporary anti-inflationary measures and the potential phasing out of energy support programs.
