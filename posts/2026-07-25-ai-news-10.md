## WORLD

* **Political Victory for Indian Student Protesters:** The Indian education sector faces a major transition as Education Minister Dharmendra Pradhan steps down. This resignation marks a significant triumph for the student-led movement against Prime Minister Narendra Modi’s government, highlighting deep-seated societal frustration and the growing influence of youth activism in Indian politics.
* **Escalating Security Concerns in NATO Airspace:** Tensions in Eastern Europe have spiked after the Romanian Ministry of Defence reported the intrusion and subsequent shootdown of an unauthorized drone in its airspace. This incident, following a similar breach only days prior, underscores the fragile security environment along NATO’s eastern flank.
* **Environmental Crisis in Western Europe:** Spain and France are currently grappling with severe wildfires. The catastrophic smoke levels have forced widespread evacuations and indoor mandates, highlighting the region's increasing vulnerability to extreme weather events exacerbated by summer heatwaves.

## POLAND

* **Political Realignment within Law and Justice (PiS):** The conservative PiS party is experiencing significant internal upheaval. In response to recent fractures within the party structure—described by Mateusz Morawiecki as a painful, forced removal—President Andrzej Duda has initiated a high-level meeting in Jurata to address the party's future direction.
* **Legendary Goalkeeper Retires:** Łukasz Fabiański, a cornerstone of the Polish national football team with 57 appearances, has officially announced his retirement from professional football. Fabiański stated that while he is closing this chapter of his life, he intends to remain involved in the sport in a different professional capacity.
