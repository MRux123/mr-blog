## WORLD

* **Populist Retreat in Serbia and Brazil:** A wave of electoral challenges to nationalist incumbents has gained momentum. In Serbia, a long-standing populist administration has faced a decisive defeat following a student-led protest movement, signaling a shift in the regional political landscape. Simultaneously, in Brazil, Luiz Inácio Lula da Silva has secured a fourth presidential term, defeating Flávio Bolsonaro in a dramatic political comeback that challenges the recent right-wing dominance in Latin America.
* **Escalating Conflict and Human Rights Concerns in Sudan:** International pressure is mounting on Sudanese military factions as the UK imposes sanctions on leaders implicated in a campaign of sexual violence. The conflict, now marking a grim anniversary since the siege of El Fasher, has led to further civilian suffering, including large-scale drone strikes on critical power infrastructure that have crippled eastern states.
* **Technological and Geopolitical Risks:** Anthropic has issued a stark warning regarding "catastrophic risks" to humanity as it prepares for its initial public offering. This highlights the growing tension between rapid AI commercialization and global safety governance, mirroring the broader geopolitical anxieties currently affecting national security landscapes in the Middle East and beyond.

## POLAND

* **Russian Airstrikes Impact Kyiv:** The ongoing conflict continues to cause civilian tragedy, with recent Russian attacks on Kyiv resulting in significant damage to critical infrastructure, including hospitals and student housing, leading to reported casualties.
* **Political Polarization Intensifies:** The domestic political scene remains volatile, characterized by internal party accusations of election fraud within the Poland 2050 movement and emerging debates regarding potential political alignments for future legislative cycles. 
* **Liberalism Under Scrutiny:** Prominent voices within the intellectual sphere, including Prof. Marcin Matczak, are urging the current administration to pivot from procedural maneuvers to a more robust, emotional defense of liberal values, suggesting that the failure to foster a sense of community is enabling the political opposition to gain ground.
