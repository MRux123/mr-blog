## WORLD

* **Escalating Wildfires in Europe:** The continent faces a dire environmental and human crisis as wildfires continue to ravage areas in France. The situation has intensified, resulting in the death of a third firefighter, highlighting the extreme risks faced by emergency crews battling the blazes amid worsening climate conditions.
* **Health Emergency in Congo:** A lethal Ebola outbreak is threatening displaced populations in the Democratic Republic of Congo. Amidst violence and a lack of basic infrastructure, including clean water, the virus is rapidly spreading through crowded refugee camps, raising fears of an uncontrollable humanitarian catastrophe.
* **Strategic Shift in Ukraine's Air War:** Ukraine has adopted a new, aggressive approach to its long-range offensive, targeting commercial logistics hubs within Russia. By attacking infrastructure similar to Amazon's distribution centers, Kyiv aims to disrupt the Russian economy and bring the psychological reality of the conflict directly to the Russian populace.

## POLAND

* **Defense Spending and Military Expansion:** The Polish government has reaffirmed its commitment to military modernization, with officials declaring an ambitious goal to establish the strongest land army in Europe. This acceleration in defense procurement reflects heightened security concerns in the region.
* **Corporate Accountability and Environmental Impact:** Public sentiment is turning against the state-run energy giant, Tauron, following its refusal to provide compensation to local businesses affected by the ecological disaster on the Bóbr River. The case has sparked a wider debate about corporate responsibility and environmental governance in Poland.
* **Regulatory Action Against Big Tech:** In a significant move targeting global market dominance, the European Commission has imposed a nine-figure fine on Google. The penalty underscores the EU's increasingly strict regulatory environment regarding anti-competitive practices and digital market transparency.
