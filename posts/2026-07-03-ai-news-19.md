## WORLD

*   **Rising Geopolitical Tensions in the Middle East:** Tehran has transformed into a high-security fortress as the region braces for significant developments. Meanwhile, reports suggest the United States issued secret warnings to Iran, fearing that Israel might attempt to sabotage critical diplomatic negotiations by targeting high-ranking Iranian officials, including the foreign minister and the parliamentary speaker.
*   **Human Rights Crisis in Niger:** A severe crackdown on the LGBTQ+ population is underway, with at least 40 individuals arrested following the introduction of a new penal code. International observers express deep concern over the "witch-hunt" atmosphere and the resulting loss of access to essential HIV/AIDS medication, further destabilizing the public health landscape in the region.

## POLAND

*   **Russian Threat and NATO Unity:** Polish Prime Minister Donald Tusk has warned of critical months ahead, citing intelligence regarding potential Russian provocations. In alignment with this, Deputy Foreign Minister Marcin Bosacki emphasized that NATO members are demonstrating unprecedented unity as they prepare for various security scenarios, with upcoming discussions in Ankara expected to solidify the alliance's response.
*   **Controversy in West Pomerania:** Local authorities are facing a major scandal as officials linked to the regional marshal have been named among the defendants in an investigation into project mismanagement. The case highlights ongoing concerns regarding administrative integrity in the region, particularly involving the funding and implementation of infrastructure projects like the "Drawa and Korytnica Papal Kayaking Trail."
