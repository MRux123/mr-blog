## WORLD

* **US escalates economic pressure on Iran:** The United States has launched "Operation Economic Fury," successfully seizing approximately $1 billion in Iranian-held cryptocurrency. Washington officials state these assets were being used to bypass international sanctions and fund the Islamic Revolutionary Guard Corps (IRGC). This move coincides with high-level meetings between President Trump and his advisers, who are finalizing a definitive determination regarding the future of the Iran nuclear deal.
* **Dramatic rescue operation in Laos:** Emergency responders have successfully extracted the first of five individuals trapped for nine days in a flooded cavern. The rescue mission, which has captured international attention, remains ongoing as teams work to reach the remaining survivors still stranded underground.

## POLAND

* **Security breach at Polish Consulate in New York:** Polish diplomatic officials and local authorities are investigating a significant act of vandalism at the Polish Consulate General in New York. The building’s facade was damaged, and surveillance equipment along with outdoor exhibition displays were defaced. The incident is being treated with the utmost gravity by Polish diplomatic services as NYPD investigations proceed.
