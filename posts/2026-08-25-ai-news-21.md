## WORLD

*   **Deadly Heatwave Impacts Europe:** A staggering 35,000 excess deaths have been attributed to this summer's extreme temperatures across Europe. Health experts and data suggest this figure may climb further, highlighting a deepening climate crisis that continues to strain public health infrastructure across the continent.
*   **Surge in US Measles Cases:** Public health authorities in the United States have reported the highest number of measles infections since 1991. The situation has intensified following the confirmation of two fatalities in Pennsylvania, marking the first deaths from the virus in the country this year.
*   **Ukrainian Defense Outlook:** Former Ukrainian Minister of Defense, Mykhailo Fedorov, has issued a stark warning regarding the ongoing conflict. Describing the current situation as a pivotal turning point, Fedorov stated that Ukraine is "slowly losing" ground and requires significant strategic shifts to avoid eventual defeat.
