## WORLD

*   **Conflict Erupts in the Strait of Hormuz:** The geopolitical landscape has shifted dramatically as the United States and Iran move toward open warfare. Following Washington's announcement of a reinstated blockade on Iranian ports, Tehran has reportedly attacked two oil tankers and fired upon U.S. military installations. This escalation threatens global energy supplies and signals a significant breakdown in regional stability.
*   **High-Level Political Purge in China:** Beijing has executed a major reshuffle within its top leadership, purging Politburo member Ma Xingrui over corruption and moral misconduct charges. As a former leader of the Xinjiang region and the nation’s space program, Ma’s fall from grace highlights ongoing volatility and internal friction within the Chinese Communist Party.
*   **Humanitarian Crisis at India-Bangladesh Border:** Tensions are soaring as India initiates the mass expulsion of undocumented Bangladeshi immigrants. With the border regions becoming a bottleneck, thousands of individuals remain stranded, forcing both nations into a precarious diplomatic standoff.

## POLAND

*   **Judicial and Parliamentary Crisis:** The Polish political scene faces a significant test as formal motions have been filed to strip three members of Parliament of their immunity. These requests, linked to serious criminal allegations, suggest an impending legal battle that could disrupt the current legislative session and heighten political polarization.
*   **Invasive Species Threat:** Public health authorities have issued an alert regarding the rapid spread of an invasive "hell-spawn" larva across the country. The biological threat has prompted calls for increased vigilance and environmental monitoring to curb its impact on local ecosystems.
