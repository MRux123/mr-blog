## WORLD

* **Escalation in Lebanon:** Israeli Prime Minister Benjamin Netanyahu has declared the capture of Beaufort Castle a "turning point" in the ongoing offensive against Hezbollah. With military operations expanding north of the Litani River, the geopolitical situation has deteriorated significantly, prompting France to demand an emergency UN Security Council meeting.
* **Colombia Election Uncertainty:** Polls have closed in the Colombian presidential election, an event with significant implications for regional stability and future relations with the United States. The election follows a period of heightened diplomatic friction between President Gustavo Petro and his American counterpart, Donald Trump.

## POLAND

* **NATO Eastern Flank Vulnerability:** New wargame simulations have highlighted critical vulnerabilities in the defense of the Baltic states, specifically regarding the potential for a multi-front Russian incursion and delays in NATO's Article 5 response. Experts are now debating revolutionary strategic shifts required to ensure regional security and maintain the credibility of the alliance.
* **Wieczysta Kraków’s Historic Promotion:** In a landmark achievement for Polish football, Wieczysta Kraków has secured a historic promotion to the PKO BP Ekstraklasa. The club’s rapid ascent from the first league to the top tier of Polish football marks a significant shift in the domestic sports landscape.
* **Passing of a Hero:** Poland mourns the loss of Jerzy Substyk, known as "Jurny," a 98-year-old Warsaw Uprising veteran and vice-president of the Association of Warsaw Insurgents. His death marks the end of a heroic generation that fought for the capital’s freedom during World War II.
