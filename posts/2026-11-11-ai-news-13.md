## WORLD

* **Diplomatic Efforts to De-escalate Conflict:** Following critical negotiations in Abu Dhabi, U.S. envoys have launched an urgent mission to Moscow, with subsequent plans to meet with President Zelenskyy. This move signals a high-stakes attempt to address the ongoing geopolitical instability affecting Eastern Europe.
* **Economic Crisis Grips Lebanon:** The intersection of Hezbollah’s conflict with Israel and the broader U.S.-Israel-Iran regional tensions has plunged Lebanon into a severe economic catastrophe. Surging living costs and systemic mismanagement continue to drive the population toward widespread despair.
* **Security Hoax Across Italy:** Italian authorities are investigating a massive bomb hoax that triggered the evacuation of hundreds of schools across major cities, including Rome, Naples, and Florence. Officials confirmed the threats were delivered via coordinated emails.

## POLAND

* **Independence Day Amidst Political Discourse:** Poland marked its Independence Day with significant state ceremonies. Notably, President Karol Nawrocki utilized the occasion to advocate for a "free Constitutional Tribunal," calling for a collaborative effort among political leaders to restore the institution’s independence from partisan influence.
* **Public Sentiment and Political Friction:** Commemorative events in Warsaw saw a mix of patriotic celebration and political tension. Deputy Prime Minister Władysław Kosiniak-Kamysz faced audible protests during his address, responding with a message of national unity and historical reflection, while thousands gathered for the annual Independence March.
* **Maritime Development:** In a symbolic move for the Polish economy, Prime Minister Donald Tusk participated in the christening of the new ferry *Bursztyn Unity* in Szczecin, emphasizing the importance of sovereignty and national infrastructure on this historic holiday.
