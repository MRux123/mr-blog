## WORLD

* **Escalating Russian Missile Attacks on Kyiv:** Russian forces have launched a massive ballistic missile strike on the Ukrainian capital and surrounding regions. Local officials report at least 14 fatalities and significant damage to both residential and industrial infrastructure, as rescue operations continue to address widespread fires.
* **Tensions in the Strait of Hormuz:** U.S. President Donald Trump has issued a stern warning that the Hormuz Strait must remain open, threatening severe repercussions if shipping lanes are obstructed. High-level talks involving Secretary of State Marco Rubio and Treasury Secretary Scott Bessent are reportedly underway to stabilize the region and ensure the flow of oil shipments.
* **Humanitarian Crisis at the Spanish Border:** The situation in Ceuta remains critical as thousands of migrants continue to attempt the crossing. Local authorities and humanitarian groups in Morocco are struggling to account for those missing, with the death toll rising as many individuals have perished at sea during the crossing.

## POLAND

* **Severe Weather Warnings:** The Institute of Meteorology has issued red alerts across most of the country. Poland is bracing for extreme weather conditions, including temperatures reaching 40°C, dangerous storms, and persistent tropical nights, posing significant risks to public health and safety.
* **Economic Challenges and Cost of Living:** New reports highlight a troubling trend in the Polish economy, with analysts suggesting that the domestic cost of living now exceeds that of Scandinavian nations. This "staggering bill" for the Polish public has sparked a broader debate regarding economic policy, inflation, and fiscal burden on the citizenry.
