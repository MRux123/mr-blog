## WORLD

* **US-Iran Diplomatic Breakthrough:** A new agreement between the United States and Iran has been signed, potentially stabilizing energy transit through the Strait of Hormuz. While the deal addresses immediate economic and military concerns, officials remain cautious regarding the long-term viability of nuclear non-proliferation efforts.
* **NATO’s Strategic Reassessment:** Defense Secretary Hegseth has signaled a reduction in US commitments to European readiness forces, coinciding with broader internal reviews of NATO obligations. Simultaneously, the alliance has announced a renewed focus on modernizing nuclear deterrence capabilities in response to the deteriorating global security climate.
* **Taliban Imposes Digital Restrictions:** The Taliban administration in Afghanistan has implemented a strict ban on smartphone usage for civil servants and military personnel, threatening the destruction of devices and physical punishment for non-compliance as part of a campaign to enforce internal order.

## POLAND

* **Enhanced NATO Nuclear Posture:** Polish officials participated in the latest Nuclear Planning Group session, where member states reached a consensus on accelerating investments in nuclear deterrence to bolster regional security against growing external threats.
* **US Military Presence Negotiations:** Defense Minister Kosiniak-Kamysz has confirmed that the United States has officially responded to Poland's proposal regarding the establishment of a permanent US military base on Polish soil, marking a pivotal moment in bilateral defense relations.
* **Crisis at Szpital Południowy:** The President of Warsaw has issued a critical decision regarding the ongoing administrative and operational crisis at Szpital Południowy, addressing long-standing management concerns at the facility.
