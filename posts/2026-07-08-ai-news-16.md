## WORLD

*   **NATO Summit Tensions:** During the summit in Ankara, President Trump criticized NATO allies for their lack of participation in the conflict in Iran, while simultaneously signaling a shift in policy by granting Ukraine authority to deploy the Patriot defense system. The geopolitical landscape remains volatile as regional powers recalibrate their defense strategies.
*   **Political Shifts in France:** Marine Le Pen has officially launched her presidential campaign following a court ruling that cleared her to seek office. Her kickoff in a rural stronghold underscores a significant moment in French political discourse, drawing polarized reactions from the electorate.
*   **Medical Malpractice Scandal:** A German palliative care physician has been sentenced to life imprisonment for the murder of 15 patients, with investigations ongoing into further suspected cases, highlighting a grave breach of professional ethics within the healthcare sector.

## POLAND

*   **High-Level Diplomatic Dialogue:** Polish President Karol Nawrocki held discussions with Ukrainian President Volodymyr Zelenskyy on the sidelines of the Ankara summit. The talks touched upon sensitive historical issues, including the UPA, with both leaders acknowledging that deep-seated historical grievances remain unresolved.
*   **Banking Sector Regulatory Action:** The Office of Competition and Consumer Protection (UOKiK) has launched major inspections involving mBank, ING, and BIK. The probe focuses on creditworthiness assessment practices, signaling an increased regulatory push for transparency in the Polish financial market.
*   **Accountability in Local Government:** A controversy has emerged in Lubartów following reports that a local PiS councilman underwent surgery at a hospital department that was officially suspended. While the facility claims the procedure was an emergency intervention, investigations continue into whether the access was appropriate given the department's operational status.
