## WORLD

* **Iran-Israel Conflict Remains Fluid:** Diplomatic efforts are intensifying as Pakistani mediators arrive in Iran to stabilize the ongoing crisis. Despite rumors of a potential ceasefire extension, the White House has maintained a cautious stance while continuing to engage in talks to reach a sustainable agreement.
* **Sudan Humanitarian Crisis Response:** Following a critical international conference in Berlin, donors have pledged over £1bn to address the world’s largest humanitarian crisis in Sudan. The funding aims to support two-thirds of the population currently in need of urgent assistance, though prospects for a formal ceasefire remain slim.
* **Regional Escalation in Lebanon:** Israeli strikes in Lebanon have resulted in the deaths of three paramedics, an incident described by the Lebanese government as a "flagrant crime." The strikes highlight the volatile nature of the ongoing cross-border hostilities.

## POLAND

* **Heightened Security Alerts:** The Polish Operational Command has scrambled fighter jets and suspended operations at two regional airports in response to intensive long-range Russian aviation activity targeting Ukraine. The move reflects the increasing strain on regional security caused by the ongoing war across the eastern border.
* **Internal Political Turbulence:** Tensions are mounting within the Law and Justice (PiS) party following reports regarding MP Łukasz Mejza’s latest traffic offense and the announcement by former PM Mateusz Morawiecki to form an independent political association, which party leadership fears may lead to internal fragmentation.
