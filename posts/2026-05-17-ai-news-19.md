## WORLD

* **Escalating Drone Warfare in the Middle East and Russia:** The geopolitical landscape remains volatile following a series of high-stakes drone strikes. In the UAE, an unidentified drone attack triggered a fire near the Barakah nuclear power plant; authorities confirmed that critical infrastructure remains intact, though the incident is being treated as a major security escalation. Simultaneously, Ukraine has launched a "long-range sanction" campaign, executing a massive, coordinated drone assault on key strategic targets within Russia, including an oil refinery in Moscow, semiconductor manufacturing plants, and military assets in Crimea.
* **Heightened Tensions in the Indo-Pacific and Cuba:** Concerns regarding global stability are mounting as intelligence reports surface about Cuba’s acquisition of over 300 military-grade drones. With reports of potential plans targeting U.S. installations, including Guantanamo Bay and Key West, CIA leadership has intervened directly with warning missions to Havana. Meanwhile, the prospect of a Chinese invasion of Taiwan looms larger, as U.S. analysts fear that recent diplomatic shifts may have emboldened Beijing’s strategic planning for the next five years.

## POLAND

* **Shifting Dynamics in U.S.-Poland Relations:** Former Foreign Minister Jacek Czaputowicz has offered a sobering assessment of the Polish-American alliance. During a recent public appearance, Czaputowicz argued that the current U.S. administration, under Donald Trump, no longer views the defense of Poland as a core national interest, citing the recent cancellation of a U.S. brigade deployment as a significant signal of this strategic pivot.
* **Political Sentiment and Social Priorities:** Public discourse in Poland remains fragmented, as reflected in recent polling data. When asked to identify which political figures would be invited to a social gathering, 34% of respondents opted for none, reflecting a profound sense of political disillusionment. Among those who did select a candidate, Karol Nawrocki held a lead over Prime Minister Donald Tusk, underscoring the ongoing volatility in domestic political preference.
