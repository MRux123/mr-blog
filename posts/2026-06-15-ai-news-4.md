## WORLD

*   **US-Iran Peace Framework:** In a significant diplomatic shift, the United States and Iran have reached a framework for peace, confirmed by both Washington and international mediators. The agreement, aimed at stabilizing the Middle East, includes a permanent cessation of hostilities across multiple fronts, including Lebanon. A key component of the deal involves the reopening of the Strait of Hormuz and the lifting of the U.S. naval blockade on Iranian ports. While market reactions were immediate—evidenced by a slide in global oil prices—analysts remain cautious, noting that complex issues, such as Iran’s nuclear program, remain unresolved.
*   **Surge in Attacks on Education:** Global research indicates a 40% rise in attacks on educational institutions. Between 2024 and 2025, over 10,600 students and staff were killed, injured, abducted, or arrested across 83 countries. The report highlights critical security failures, particularly in conflict zones including Ukraine, Ethiopia, and the Democratic Republic of the Congo.

## POLAND

*   **Expanded Defense Cooperation:** Polish presidential minister Marcin Przydacz has outlined the country's strategic goal to increase the number of U.S. troops stationed in Poland. Warsaw is currently negotiating an expansion of the American military presence, setting a target of 15,000 personnel, with a fallback agreement to maintain at least 11,000 troops.
*   **Rising Diplomatic Tensions with Moscow:** Russia has issued formal warnings regarding the seizure of the Russian consulate building in Gdańsk. The Russian Foreign Ministry threatened "severe steps" in retaliation for what it characterizes as attacks on its diplomatic infrastructure within Poland, further straining bilateral relations amidst the ongoing regional security crisis.
