## WORLD

* **Xi Jinping Engages Taiwan’s Opposition:** In a significant diplomatic development, Chinese leader Xi Jinping held rare talks with Taiwanese opposition figure Cheng Li-wun. The dialogue, the first of its kind in a decade, included discussions on a potential future visit by Mr. Xi to Taiwan, marking a shift in cross-strait communication strategies.
* **Geopolitical Blame and Economic Strain:** British Prime Minister Keir Starmer has publicly equated the global economic volatility affecting UK families and businesses to the policies and actions of both Donald Trump and Vladimir Putin. The Prime Minister's remarks signal a hardening stance against external political influences.
* **Ukrainian Security Initiatives in the Middle East:** President Volodymyr Zelensky has confirmed the successful deployment of Ukrainian experts to the Persian Gulf. The mission, focused on countering Iranian military hardware, successfully intercepted Shahed drones, representing a new operational theater for Ukrainian defense forces abroad.

## POLAND

* **Escalation in Constitutional Tribunal Dispute:** A deepening institutional crisis has emerged following the swearing-in of four new Constitutional Tribunal judges by the Sejm. While the new judges are actively seeking to commence their duties, the Presidential Palace and the Tribunal’s leadership have rejected the move, declaring the appointments devoid of legal standing.
* **Mourning the Death of Jacek Magiera:** The Polish Football Association (PZPN) has confirmed the passing of national team assistant coach Jacek Magiera at the age of 49. The loss is being widely mourned within the Polish sports community and by the national federation.
* **16th Anniversary of the Smoleńsk Tragedy:** Political leaders, including President Karol Nawrocki and Law and Justice chairman Jarosław Kaczyński, gathered at the Presidential Palace in Warsaw for a memorial service commemorating the 16th anniversary of the 2010 Smoleńsk air disaster.
