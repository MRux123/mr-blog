## WORLD

* **US-Iran Ceasefire Agreement:** A pivotal two-week truce has been established between the United States and Iran, narrowly avoiding a major escalation following the recent coordinated strikes by the US and Israel. The deal, mediated by Pakistan, specifically addresses the security of the Strait of Hormuz, which is set to reopen. President Donald Trump has framed the development as a significant American victory, even signaling potential support for Iranian reconstruction efforts.
* **Israel’s Strategic Autonomy:** Despite the broader regional ceasefire, the Israeli government has issued a critical clarification. Prime Minister Benjamin Netanyahu’s office emphasized that the agreement does not extend to military operations in Lebanon, signaling that Israel intends to pursue its own security objectives in the region independently of the US-Iran truce.
* **Economic Impact:** Global markets have responded sharply to the de-escalation, with oil prices experiencing a significant plunge following the announcement that the critical trade route through the Strait of Hormuz will resume operations.

## POLAND

* **Executive Turnover in State-Owned Entities:** Poland’s state-controlled sector is facing significant leadership instability. Reports indicate that in the first few months of 2026, approximately 20% of major state-owned companies have seen a change in their CEO, with a notable portion of these departures attributed to sudden, unplanned resignations or dismissals. 
* **Economic Policy Dialogue:** As the government navigates these structural changes, the focus remains on macroeconomic stability. Finance Minister Andrzej Domański is set to address the current economic landscape and fiscal challenges in upcoming media appearances, highlighting the administration's efforts to maintain market confidence amidst both domestic organizational shifts and volatile international geopolitical conditions.
