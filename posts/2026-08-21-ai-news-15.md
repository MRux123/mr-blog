## WORLD

*   **US-Iran Economic Standoff Escalates:** President Trump’s declaration of an "economic D-Day" against Iran has triggered a significant response from Tehran. Officials are currently crafting survival strategies to mitigate the impact of impending US sanctions. Meanwhile, international analysts highlight China's calculated stance, suggesting that Beijing remains resilient against these economic threats due to its strategic control over critical mineral supply chains and diverse energy imports.
*   **Violent Incident in Sweden:** A serious security incident occurred at a school in Sweden, where an individual launched an attack using a sword. Police have confirmed multiple injuries, and while the perpetrator has been apprehended, the event has prompted widespread lockdowns in the local region, underscoring ongoing concerns regarding school safety.
*   **Public Health Crisis in DR Congo:** The World Health Organization has raised an urgent alarm regarding the acceleration of Ebola infections in the Democratic Republic of Congo. With approximately 50% of the total 2,500 recorded deaths occurring within the last 20 days, international medical efforts are shifting focus toward a new vaccine trial to curb the rapid transmission of the virus.

## POLAND

*   **Diplomatic Friction with Moscow:** Polish Prime Minister Donald Tusk has become the target of aggressive rhetoric from the Russian Senate. Following the Prime Minister’s criticism of Germany regarding the detention of a suspect linked to the Nord Stream gas pipeline sabotage, Russian officials—including senator Vladimir Dzhabarov—responded with personal attacks, signaling a deepening of the diplomatic impasse between Warsaw and the Kremlin.
*   **Legislative Consensus on Rural Disputes:** In a rare display of political unity, Polish lawmakers are moving toward a non-partisan agreement aimed at resolving long-standing conflicts regarding noise and odor nuisances in rural areas. The initiative seeks to streamline regulations and minimize the friction between agricultural operations and local residents.
*   **Escalating Political Controversy in Slovenia:** The investigation into an accident involving the Slovenian Head of State has unexpectedly uncovered potential links to Russian interests. The revelation has ignited a political firestorm, sparking intense public debate and parliamentary scrutiny regarding the country’s internal security and foreign influence.
