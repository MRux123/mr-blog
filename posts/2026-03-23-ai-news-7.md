## WORLD

* **Unprecedented Escalation in Middle East:** A massive joint military operation by the U.S. and Israel has targeted facilities in Tehran, causing significant unrest and uncertainty. The geopolitical fallout has triggered a sharp decline in Asian stock markets, as energy experts warn that the ongoing hostilities could lead to the most severe energy crisis in decades. Tehran has responded defiantly, threatening to target critical electrical and water infrastructure in retaliation.
* **Fatal Incident at LaGuardia Airport:** A collision between a landing Air Canada Express aircraft and a ground support vehicle at New York's LaGuardia Airport has resulted in at least two fatalities. The airport has been closed, causing major disruptions to regional air travel, with flights being diverted or canceled indefinitely.

## POLAND

* **Antisemitic Arson Attack in London:** A disturbing hate crime targeting the Jewish community has occurred in London’s Golders Green district. Four ambulances belonging to the Hatzola organization were deliberately set on fire near a local synagogue. Authorities are currently treating the incident as a hate crime.
* **National Health Fund (NFZ) Austerity Measures:** The NFZ is preparing to implement significant funding cuts to Ambulatory Specialist Care (AOS). New regulations, expected to be published this week, will restrict reimbursements for medical procedures performed beyond established plans to approximately 40%, a move intended to save the state budget roughly 1.5 billion PLN annually.
* **Easter Consumption Trends:** Amidst ongoing economic shifts, recent data indicates that while half of the Polish population plans to maintain their previous year's holiday spending levels, one in five Poles intends to reduce their expenditure for the upcoming Easter period, with the majority of shoppers budgeting between 201 and 450 PLN.
