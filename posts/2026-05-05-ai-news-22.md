## WORLD

*   **Diverging Truce Proposals in Ukraine:** As the death toll from Russian strikes surpassed 20, both Kyiv and Moscow have announced separate plans for a ceasefire. Kyiv intends to initiate a truce on May 6, with plans to act "symmetrically" in response to Moscow's scheduled pause for the upcoming Victory Day parade.
*   **Strategic Shift in Iran:** U.S. Secretary of State Marco Rubio has announced the end of the combat-focused operation "Epic Fury" in Iran. The mission is being replaced by "Project Freedom," a strictly defensive operation centered on protecting maritime traffic in the Strait of Hormuz. Washington is now signaling a diplomatic push for regional stability, calling on Russia and China to support the continued openness of the strategic waterway.

## POLAND

*   **Tragic Firefighting Aircraft Crash:** A fatal accident involving a firefighting aircraft has occurred in the Lublin region. Authorities have confirmed the death of the pilot involved in the mission.
*   **Defense Funding Milestone:** The Polish Ministry of National Defense announced that its SAFE loan agreement has become the first to be officially approved by the European Commission, marking a significant step in the nation's current defense financing strategy.
*   **Constitutional Debate:** Prof. Ryszard Piotrowski, a constitutional scholar recently appointed to President Karol Nawrocki’s Council for a New Constitution, has publicly challenged the necessity of drafting a new constitution. Despite the council's name, Piotrowski argues that the current legal framework does not require a "reset" or replacement.
