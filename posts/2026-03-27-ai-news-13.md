## WORLD

* **Iran-Israel Conflict Intensifies:** The military situation remains critical one month after the initiation of strikes. Tehran residents report devastating civilian consequences, while international focus shifts to the diplomatic front with Secretary of State Marco Rubio joining G7 talks. President Trump has extended deadlines regarding the Strait of Hormuz, as the UN human rights chief calls for an urgent investigation into a school strike that resulted in mass casualties.
* **Gulf State Security Crackdowns:** Following the escalation of hostilities, authorities in the UAE and Qatar have initiated a wave of arrests targeting individuals circulating online footage of Iranian attacks, citing both national security risks and concerns regarding their reputation as stable havens.

## POLAND

* **Legislative Impasse over Veto:** The Polish Sejm has failed to override President Nawrocki’s veto regarding the amendment to the Code of Criminal Procedure. The vote highlighted internal tensions within the opposition, as two PiS members broke ranks during the proceedings, leading to sharp criticism from Prime Minister Donald Tusk.
* **Economic and Security Forecasts:** As political debates continue, analysts warn of upcoming shifts in fuel prices, estimating significant increases if government packages are not implemented effectively. Meanwhile, international observers and military experts continue to monitor the impact of Ukrainian technological innovations, specifically in drone warfare, noting their significant role in shifting the momentum of the regional frontlines.
