## WORLD

*   **Berlin Pride Attack:** Authorities in Germany have confirmed that the suspect responsible for the violent attack during the Berlin Pride event has been shot and killed by police. The operation took place in the Spandau district following an intensive manhunt. Officials are investigating the incident as a suspected act of Islamist terrorism. The tragedy resulted in the death of one woman, with 29 others sustaining injuries.

## POLAND

*   **Tadej Pogačar’s Historic Victory:** Slovenian cyclist Tadej Pogačar has cemented his place in sporting history by securing his fifth Tour de France title. This monumental achievement marks a new milestone in professional cycling, drawing global attention to the endurance and tactical brilliance required for such a feat.
*   **Death of a Warsaw Uprising Heroine:** Poland mourns the passing of Józefa Marciniak, known by her nom de guerre "Kropka." The 104-year-old was a distinguished sanitariuszka (medic) and liaison officer who served with bravery during the Warsaw Uprising. Beyond her wartime contributions, she dedicated her post-war life to historical preservation and documenting the experiences of her generation.
*   **Political Stance on Retirement Age:** In a definitive statement, Family and Social Policy Minister Agnieszka Dziemianowicz-Bąk has ruled out any changes to the current retirement age. The minister explicitly rejected proposals to link the retirement age of women to the number of children raised, describing such concepts as unacceptable and reaffirming the government's commitment to maintaining current labor policies.
