## WORLD

* **Macron and Trump Clash Over Iran Strategy:** The diplomatic rift between Paris and Washington deepened as President Macron openly criticized Donald Trump’s inconsistent approach to the Iran war. Macron specifically faulted the U.S. President for shifting military goals and his antagonistic rhetoric toward NATO, suggesting these maneuvers undermine the stability of the conflict.
* **Moldova Formally Severs Ties with CIS:** In a historic geopolitical shift, the Moldovan Parliament has officially voted to exit the Commonwealth of Independent States (CIS). This move marks a definitive end to the country’s post-Soviet era, signaling a decisive pivot toward European integration and deepening tensions with Moscow.

## POLAND

* **Sabotage Trials Commencing for Marywilska Arson:** Judicial proceedings are set to begin against a group of four foreign nationals—three Ukrainians and one Belarusian—accused of orchestrating a series of high-profile arson attacks, including the destruction of the Marywilska commercial center in Warsaw. Prosecutors allege the acts were carried out as sabotage on behalf of Russian intelligence.
* **Diplomatic Spat Between Tusk and Orbán:** Tensions between Warsaw and Budapest have flared after Viktor Orbán publicly retorted to Prime Minister Donald Tusk. Orbán called for the cessation of European energy sanctions against Russia, claiming Europe must prioritize national survival over "warmongering," a direct rebuke to Tusk’s recent assertions regarding Russian influence on global instability.
* **Attack on Polish Soldiers in Lebanon:** The Polish Prosecutor’s Office has launched a formal investigation into a targeted attack on Polish UNIFIL forces in Lebanon. The incident, involving an improvised explosive device, resulted in injury to a Polish soldier, marking a significant escalation in the security risks facing Polish troops deployed in the region.
