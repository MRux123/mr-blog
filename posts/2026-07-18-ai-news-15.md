## WORLD

* **Political Scandal in Germany:** Jens Spahn, a prominent leader within Chancellor Friedrich Merz’s administration, has resigned following public controversy regarding his use of surrogacy. The incident has sparked a significant debate, as the practice remains illegal under current German law, placing additional pressure on the governing coalition.
* **Conservative Realignment in London:** The UK recently hosted a notably understated version of the Conservative Political Action Conference (CPAC). Unlike its American counterpart, known for exuberant MAGA-centric rhetoric, the London event signals a pivot toward a more subdued, policy-focused approach to modern conservatism.
* **Escalation in the Russia-Ukraine Conflict:** Military tensions have spiked following reports of a major fire at a fuel depot near Moscow and successful Ukrainian strikes against naval targets in the vicinity of Crimea, marking a new phase in the ongoing regional conflict.

## POLAND

* **Regional Administrative Shifts:** The Podkarpacie region is preparing for significant administrative restructuring, with plans currently underway to designate new municipalities, reflecting broader demographic and urban planning changes in the area.
* **Geographical Curiosities:** Scientific and travel interest is focusing on the unique hydrological phenomenon in Wągrowiec, where the rivers Wełna and Nielba intersect without blending, serving as a rare natural landmark that continues to draw both tourists and researchers.
