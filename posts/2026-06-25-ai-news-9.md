## WORLD

*   **Devastating Earthquake Strikes Venezuela:** A powerful seismic event has hit Venezuela, resulting in at least 32 fatalities and over 700 injuries. Caracas has been severely impacted, with widespread building collapses and reports of citizens trapped under debris. Acting President Delcy Rodríguez has declared a state of emergency, noting that the casualty toll is expected to rise as rescue operations continue in the hardest-hit regions.
*   **Geopolitical Instability and Economic Fallout:** Global markets remain in flux following the escalating conflict involving Iran. The United States is currently seeking substantial congressional funding to address the military situation, while energy markets face volatility as oil prices drop following the closure of the Strait of Hormuz.

## POLAND

*   **Tusk Outlines Path for Ukraine’s EU Integration:** The Ukraine Recovery Conference in Gdańsk has commenced amidst diplomatic friction, highlighted by the notable absence of President Volodymyr Zelensky. Prime Minister Donald Tusk emphasized that while Poland supports Ukraine’s European aspirations, the path to integration remains strictly conditional upon historical reconciliation and a mutual understanding of shared challenges.
*   **Expansion of Legal Investigations in Warsaw:** A new criminal investigation has been launched concerning the falsification of death certificates at the Szpital Południowy in Warsaw. The prosecutor's office is currently investigating unauthorized signatures on records, marking a concerning development in the ongoing scrutiny of administrative practices within the medical facility.
*   **Justice Served in Zgorzelec Munitions Theft:** The district court in Zgorzelec has received an indictment regarding the high-profile theft of U.S. artillery ammunition from a freight train in 2023. The three suspects involved in the heist face up to 10 years of imprisonment for their role in the security breach.
