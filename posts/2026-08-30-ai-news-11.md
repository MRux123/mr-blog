## WORLD

* **Missing Foreigners in Tibet Flood:** Chinese authorities have confirmed that 261 foreign nationals from 23 countries remain missing following catastrophic flooding in Tibet. This disclosure marks the first significant insight into the scale of the disaster near the border, highlighting the severity of the humanitarian situation as information finally begins to emerge from the isolated region.
* **Political Volatility in Germany:** With state elections approaching, the Alternative for Germany (AfD) party is positioned to potentially secure a power base. The prospect of an far-right victory has triggered a crisis among state and federal leaders, who are actively exploring legal and bureaucratic strategies to restrict the party's access to sensitive national security intelligence should they assume office.
* **Humanitarian Crisis in Sudan:** The situation in El Obeid continues to reach a breaking point. As civilians flee the ongoing civil war between the Sudanese Armed Forces and the RSF militia, thousands of families are overwhelming local camps. Aid organizations report that conditions are rapidly deteriorating, leaving over 200,000 displaced people in a state of extreme vulnerability.

## POLAND

* **Strategic Airstrike on Russian Airbase:** Ukrainian drone forces have successfully targeted a critical Russian airfield used as a launchpad for long-range bomber sorties. The operation signifies a tactical shift in the ongoing conflict, aiming to disrupt the infrastructure utilized for sustained aerial bombardment of Ukrainian territory.
* **Commemoration of Volhynian Massacre Victims:** A somber ceremony took place in Ostrówki, where 55 victims of the World War II-era Volhynian massacre were laid to rest. The event has been recognized as a significant moment for Polish national memory and diplomacy, underscoring the ongoing process of historical reconciliation and the honoring of historical grievances.
* **Political Intrigue Surrounding Zondacrypto:** Questions are mounting regarding the potential involvement or knowledge of former Prime Minister Mateusz Morawiecki concerning the emerging Zondacrypto controversy. As the investigation progresses, the case has become a focal point of domestic political scrutiny, raising concerns about transparency and financial governance.
