## WORLD

* **Tragedy in Gaza:** At least 21 people, including eight children, have been killed following the collapse of a war-damaged building in Gaza. The incident, which occurred at a site previously struck by Israeli forces, highlights the catastrophic living conditions for civilians amidst the ongoing regional instability.
* **Escalating Geopolitical Strains:** Tensions between Western powers and Eastern adversaries continue to mount, as the United States accuses Russia of orchestrating a global campaign to intimidate or eliminate Ukrainian allies. Meanwhile, China has underscored its growing influence in the Middle East by hosting Iranian officials ahead of a critical U.S.-China summit, signaling a strategic shift in diplomatic alliances.
* **EU-Canada Deepening Ties:** In a potential realignment of North American trade and political strategy, Canada is actively seeking "associate member" status within the European Union, a move spurred by ongoing diplomatic friction with the United States administration.

## POLAND

* **Accountability at the Academy of Justice:** The Ministry of Justice has confirmed the disciplinary dismissal of Rector Michał Sopiński from the Academy of Justice, marking a significant development in the government's efforts to reform judicial and academic institutions.
* **National Security Funding:** The Polish government has announced a new initiative to bolster national defense at the local level. Municipalities are set to receive increased funding specifically earmarked for the construction of shelters, the procurement of power generators, and civil defense training.
* **Presidential Stance on Constitutional Tribunal:** President Karol Nawrocki has set a firm condition regarding the appointment of Maciej Berek to the Constitutional Tribunal. The President insists that Berek must provide comprehensive documentation proving he meets all formal requirements, citing the necessity for "crystal clear" credentials for those assuming judicial roles at the highest level.
