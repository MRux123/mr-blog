## WORLD

* **Middle East Conflict Intensifies:** The military confrontation in Iran has entered a volatile stage as Tehran faces heavy airstrikes, including hits on residential areas and universities. Despite international scrutiny and criticisms of U.S. strategy, Iran continues to demonstrate its retaliatory capacity through ongoing missile and drone strikes. The regional security architecture is further strained by Houthi involvement and the arrival of U.S. Marine reinforcements in the region.
* **North Korean Missile Advancements:** Global security concerns are mounting following North Korea's successful testing of new solid-fuel engines for intercontinental ballistic missiles. These advancements signify a continued push by Pyongyang to enhance its strike capabilities, with current technology potentially placing the United States within range of their arsenal.

## POLAND

* **Kamil Stoch Bids Farewell:** The Polish sporting landscape marks the end of an era as ski jumping legend Kamil Stoch officially announces his retirement. His final competitive jump in Planica serves as a symbolic conclusion to a storied career that defined a generation of Polish winter sports.
* **Economic Pressures and Holiday Preparations:** As Poles prepare for the upcoming Easter holidays, the retail sector faces scrutiny regarding market accessibility and rising costs. Consumers are grappling with significantly higher prices for produce, particularly raspberries, while public discourse remains focused on the government's economic policies, including heated political debates over fuel pricing and the legislative status of Sunday trade.
