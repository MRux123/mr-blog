## WORLD

* **Spanish Policy Shift on Migration:** The Spanish government has announced a significant policy reversal regarding unaccompanied migrant children currently in Ceuta. Following previous statements indicating that individuals entering the territory illegally would be returned, Madrid will now allow 500 migrant minors to relocate to the Spanish mainland. This decision highlights the state's legal duty of care toward unaccompanied young migrants, distinguishing them from the broader group of individuals who arrived in the territory earlier this summer.

## POLAND

* **Severe Storms Impact National Infrastructure:** Heavy storms passing through Poland have resulted in widespread emergency response operations. Firefighters have been deployed over 300 times to mitigate damage caused by the intense weather patterns, emphasizing the ongoing challenges of maintaining public safety during volatile meteorological conditions across the country.
