## WORLD

* **Israel-Iran Standoff Paused:** Tensions in the Middle East have entered a delicate phase of de-escalation as both Israel and Iran have announced a pause in active military strikes. Prime Minister Benjamin Netanyahu confirmed that Israel’s fire is currently "on hold," a move that follows intense behind-the-scenes diplomatic pressure from U.S. President Donald Trump, who is reportedly pushing for progress toward broader nuclear negotiations. Both nations maintain that they are prepared to resume hostilities should the ceasefire conditions be breached.

## POLAND

* **Diplomatic Pressure on Ukraine:** President Volodymyr Zelensky has expressed optimism following a high-level discussion with U.S. representatives Steve Witkoff and Jared Kushner. The conversation focused on accelerating diplomatic efforts to conclude the ongoing conflict with Russia. Meanwhile, the domestic political scene in Poland remains divided over the country's relationship with Kyiv, as heated debates continue regarding the potential stripping of the Order of the White Eagle from the Ukrainian leader, sparking criticism from former diplomats and opposition figures.

* **Tragic Aviation Accident:** A fatal incident occurred in Bielsko-Biała, where a glider crashed onto the airport tarmac from an altitude of 50 meters. Local authorities confirmed the death of a 35-year-old student pilot, while an investigation into the cause of the crash is currently underway.
