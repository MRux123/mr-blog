## WORLD

*   **Escalating Wildfires in Canada:** A severe wildfire in British Columbia, known as the Bald Range fire, has forced the immediate evacuation of 18,000 residents. The blaze has consumed over 25,000 acres, with officials noting the fire's unprecedented intensity, spreading across 2,000 acres in just one hour.
*   **Ukraine Warns of Russian Mobilization:** President Volodymyr Zelensky has issued a formal warning regarding Russia’s military strategy. In a recent interview, he alleged that the Kremlin is preparing for a new, large-scale clandestine mobilization and may attempt to undermine geopolitical stability through the manipulation of international agreements with the United States.

## POLAND

*   **Government Reconfiguration Speculation:** Political veteran Leszek Miller, alongside other senior figures like Bronisław Komorowski, has criticized potential plans to integrate Szymon Hołownia into Donald Tusk’s government. Analysts describe the move as a superficial attempt to manage parliamentary arithmetic rather than a long-term strategic shift, with critics comparing the scenario to an irreparable "fish soup."
