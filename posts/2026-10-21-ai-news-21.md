## WORLD

* **Escalation of Russian Sabotage and Aggression:** Intelligence reports suggest a coordinated intensification of Russian sabotage operations across NATO territory, following a failed attack on Leipzig Airport. Concurrently, Kijiv faces a barrage of ballistic missile strikes, heightening fears of a widening conflict.
* **Sudan Civil War Atrocity:** A devastating drone strike in Abyei has resulted in over 150 civilian deaths. Both factions in the Sudanese civil war are increasingly utilizing long-range drone technology, leading to catastrophic humanitarian consequences.
* **Diplomatic Tensions in the Middle East:** The United States Department of State has issued an emergency "ordered departure" notice for government personnel and their families in Saudi Arabia, citing imminent threats and regional instability.

## POLAND

* **Enhanced Air Defense Posture:** Following Russian ballistic missile attacks on Ukraine, the Polish government issued a vital RCB alert for the Lublin region. The Polish military has scrambled fighter jets to patrol the national airspace as a precautionary measure against potential border violations.
* **Nuclear Energy Infrastructure:** The Polish government has confirmed the definitive location for the country’s first nuclear power plant in Choczewo, with preparatory site work officially underway, signaling a major shift in national energy policy.
* **Local Governance Tensions:** The Krakow City Council has sparked political debate by setting the lowest possible salary for the newly inaugurated mayor, Łukasz Gibała, reflecting ongoing tensions within the local administration.
