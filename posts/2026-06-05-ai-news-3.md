## WORLD

*   **Escalation in Lebanon:** Hezbollah has formally rejected the proposed cease-fire agreement between the Lebanese government and Israel. The group’s leadership denounced the U.S.-brokered talks, characterizing the truce terms as a capitulation that fails to account for their strategic interests, further complicating efforts to de-escalate the regional conflict.
*   **Environmental Recovery:** Mangrove forests are showing significant signs of ecological regeneration following decades of degradation. This resurgence offers a vital boost to climate resilience and coastal biodiversity, providing a rare positive development in global environmental efforts.

## POLAND

*   **New U.S. Sanctions Against Russia:** The U.S. House of Representatives has passed a significant legislative package aimed at intensifying economic pressure on Russia. The bill, which passed with a vote of 227 to 191, also earmarks $8 billion for military assistance to Ukraine and regional allies. While the legislation reflects strong bipartisan support from Democrats and select Republicans, it now faces the final hurdle of Senate approval before becoming law.
