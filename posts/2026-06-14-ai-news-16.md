## WORLD

* **Escalation in Lebanon:** Israeli strikes on the suburbs of Beirut have resulted in three fatalities, sparking immediate international concern. The operation, which targeted Hezbollah positions, has drawn sharp criticism from U.S. President Donald Trump, who urged restraint and warned that such military actions could jeopardize delicate, ongoing cease-fire negotiations between the U.S. and Iran.
* **Diplomatic Deadlock:** Iranian negotiators have expressed skepticism regarding the current peace process, citing a perceived lack of political will or capability from the United States to uphold its side of the agreement. This impasse leaves the regional security architecture increasingly fragile.
* **Swiss Referendum Outcome:** In a significant domestic vote, Switzerland has officially rejected a legislative proposal intended to cap the national population at 10 million. The measure, framed by its proponents as a necessity for sustainability and affordability, failed to secure the required support from the electorate.

## POLAND

* **"Czarnek Plan" Unveiled:** The Law and Justice (PiS) party has presented the first segments of its new economic manifesto, dubbed the "Czarnek Plan." The proposal includes controversial structural shifts, such as withdrawing from the EU's Emissions Trading System (ETS), raising the tax threshold, and introducing tax exemptions for pensioners earning up to 2,500 PLN.
* **Sporting Achievement:** Polish athlete Kamil Majchrzak has achieved a major career milestone by winning an ATP tournament, marking a significant success for Polish tennis on the international stage.
* **Wildlife Alert:** Local authorities in the vicinity of Tarnów have issued urgent safety warnings following multiple sightings of a brown bear in the region, specifically near the municipalities of Szerzyny and Ryglice.
