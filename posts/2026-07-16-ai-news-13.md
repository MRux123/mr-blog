## WORLD

*   **Political Upheaval in Kyiv:** Ukraine has formed a new government, led by Prime Minister Serhiy Koretsky, amid significant public and military unrest. The dismissal of former Defense Minister Mykhailo Fedorov, a key proponent of drone warfare and military modernization, has sparked widespread protests and raised concerns over the future direction of the country's defense strategy during the ongoing conflict.
*   **Geopolitical Realignment:** A new Pew Research Center poll indicates a significant shift in global sentiment, with more countries now viewing China more favorably than the United States. This trend presents a complex challenge for U.S. foreign policy as it navigates potential escalations in the Middle East and concerns over regional stability.

## POLAND

*   **New Government Formation:** Poland is witnessing a major administrative transition following the formal appointment of a new government. This change comes at a critical time for national security, as the state remains on high alert regarding regional provocations.
*   **Heightened Military Activity:** Security remains a top priority following repeated incidents of Russian fighter jets being intercepted by Polish forces over the Baltic Sea. Authorities are closely monitoring the situation as defense officials work to strengthen airspace surveillance.
*   **Internal Political Friction:** The Polish political landscape is further complicated by internal developments within the Law and Justice (PiS) party, where leadership disputes have led to an ultimatum being issued to former Prime Minister Mateusz Morawiecki, highlighting ongoing instability within the country's opposition ranks.
