## WORLD

*   **US-Iran Diplomatic Overtures:** President Donald Trump has confirmed that Iran has initiated a request for high-level diplomatic talks. While the US administration has expressed openness to the dialogue, the situation remains fragile, as Trump emphasized that the previous ceasefire has effectively ended and characterized current relations with Tehran's leadership as deeply strained.
*   **Declassification of UFO Records:** The US government has released a fourth installment of previously classified files regarding Unidentified Anomalous Phenomena (UAP). This ongoing transparency effort aims to provide data on unresolved cases, as authorities commit to a rolling release schedule to address persistent public and scientific interest in the subject.

## POLAND

*   **Legal Challenges in the Justice Fund Investigation:** The District Court in Warsaw has upheld the European Arrest Warrant (EAW) for former Deputy Minister of Justice Marcin Romanowski. Despite ongoing legal efforts by the defense to lift the warrant, the court’s decision reinforces the pressure on the suspect, whose current whereabouts remain officially unknown following his reported departure from Hungary.
*   **Asylum Policy Reform:** The Polish government has formally moved to extend the temporary suspension of the right to asylum along the border with Belarus. This decision reflects the ongoing state strategy to manage security challenges and migration pressures in the border region.
*   **Political Shifts in Domestic Polling:** A new CBOS survey indicates that the Civic Coalition (KO) maintains its lead in voter support. Notably, the gap between the second-place Law and Justice (PiS) party and the rising Confederation has narrowed to less than 4 percentage points, signaling a potential shift in the political landscape as five parties continue to compete for significant legislative representation.
