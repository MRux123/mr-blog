## WORLD

* **China's Internal Dilemma Over AI:** The Chinese government is grappling with a dual-sided narrative regarding artificial intelligence. While Beijing publicly rebukes Western accusations of "malicious competition," its top spy chief has internally identified AI as a significant threat to Communist Party control, signaling a shift in how the state manages technological autonomy versus ideological security.
* **Geopolitical Volatility in the Middle East:** Saudi Arabia is reportedly in a state of strategic shock following a deterioration in relations with the U.S. and an increase in regional hostility from Iran. Analysts warn that the Kingdom is facing a "worst-case scenario" as its traditional security guarantees appear increasingly fragile.
* **Surveillance and Control in Moscow:** New reports highlight the evolution of the Russian capital into a "digital utopia" that functions simultaneously as an advanced surveillance trap. The integration of cutting-edge technology into urban life has optimized public order while tightening the state's grip on the populace.

## POLAND

* **Military Tension and Border Security:** Tensions near the Polish border have escalated following the discovery of a suspected Russian military drone, identified by the Polish Ministry of National Defense as a "Gerbera" type, recovered from the Baltic Sea near Jarosławiec. This incident coincides with the commencement of "Czarny Obrońca-26," large-scale military maneuvers involving troop movements across northern and western Poland.
* **Judicial and Political Developments:** The Polish legal and political landscape remains turbulent, highlighted by the District Court in Katowice maintaining the temporary arrest of Radosław Piesiewicz, head of the Polish Olympic Committee. Simultaneously, political discourse remains polarized, with new legal inquiries launched regarding the conduct of political figures such as Antoni Macierewicz.
