## WORLD

*   **High-Level Iranian Official Killed in Israeli Strike:** The regional conflict has intensified following the reported death of Ali Larijani, head of Iran's Supreme National Security Council, in an Israeli airstrike. This development marks a significant escalation, as Larijani was the second-highest-ranking official in the country following the death of Ayatollah Ali Khamenei in late February.
*   **Growing Confrontation Between the US and Iran:** Tensions have reached a new peak, characterized by direct rhetorical escalations. Iranian officials have dismissed Donald Trump's social media commentary as ineffective, asserting that the conflict will be resolved on the battlefield rather than through digital diplomacy.
*   **Tragic Casualties in Kabul:** A violent air strike has decimated a rehabilitation center in Kabul, resulting in dozens of deaths. The attack, which has drawn international concern, has been blamed on Pakistani forces, further complicating the volatile security landscape in the region.

## POLAND

*   **Diplomatic Friction Over Energy Policy:** President Karol Nawrocki has formally intervened in the government’s energy strategy, sending a decisive letter to Prime Minister Donald Tusk. The President is advocating for a complete departure from the Emissions Trading System (ETS) ahead of the upcoming European Council meeting, signaling a potential clash over climate policy and economic regulation.
*   **Military Security Breach Involving US Official:** An embarrassing incident has emerged involving retired US General Antonio Aguto, who lost classified military maps while traveling on a train in Poland. Investigations revealed the general was in a state of intoxication at the time of the incident, leading to significant scrutiny regarding the security protocols of personnel returning from the Ukrainian front.
