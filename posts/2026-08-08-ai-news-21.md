## WORLD

* **Strait of Hormuz Diplomacy:** Talks regarding the crucial shipping routes in the Strait of Hormuz show signs of progress, according to Omani officials. However, Iran maintains a firm stance, warning that any potential agreement is unlikely to lead to an official opening of the strait, keeping global energy and trade markets in a state of uncertainty.
* **Geopolitical Pressure on Ukraine:** President Volodymyr Zelensky has characterized Russia's current military strategy as a "mobilization without an official declaration," highlighting the ongoing attrition in the conflict. Simultaneously, there is a focus on the urgent need for replenishment of Patriot missile batteries, which remain vital for Ukraine’s air defense architecture.

## POLAND

* **Military Command Reshuffle:** The Pentagon has officially confirmed the removal of a high-ranking general previously responsible for operations in Europe. This sudden shift in command structure is currently a subject of intense analysis regarding its potential impact on NATO’s eastern flank strategy.
* **Energy Infrastructure Incident:** Authorities are investigating an incident involving a drone explosion in close proximity to a gas pipeline in Bulgaria. The Ukrainian government has issued an official statement regarding the event, as regional security concerns regarding critical energy infrastructure remain heightened.
