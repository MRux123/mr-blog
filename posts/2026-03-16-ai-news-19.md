## WORLD

* **Escalation in the Middle East:** The security situation in the Strait of Hormuz remains volatile as President Donald Trump asserts that multiple countries have pledged military support for the U.S.-led initiative to secure the vital maritime route. Despite this, the administration remains publicly critical of allies deemed hesitant to join the mission. Simultaneously, the conflict has widened, with the Israeli military significantly intensifying ground operations against Hezbollah in Lebanon.
* **Humanitarian Crisis in the West Bank:** Concerns are mounting following reports of a tragic incident in the occupied West Bank, where a Palestinian family was killed by Israeli forces. The death of the parents and their two children during a transit incident has drawn international scrutiny and highlights the deteriorating security climate on the ground.
* **Iran-U.S. Diplomatic Uncertainty:** President Trump has suggested that Tehran may be signaling a desire for negotiations, though he remains skeptical of Iran's current readiness, framing their potential overtures as a reaction to their recent military setbacks.

## POLAND

* **Public Backlash Against Presidential Veto:** A recent IBRIS survey for Polsat News reveals that 56.9% of Poles oppose President Karol Nawrocki’s decision to veto the EU’s "SAFE" security program. Only 33.8% of respondents supported the veto, underscoring a significant divide between the electorate and the presidential administration regarding national security funding and cooperation with European partners.
* **Legal Jeopardy for Political Aides:** The political landscape in Poland faces fresh instability as prosecutors have brought charges against three associates of MP Adam Gomoła. The investigation centers on alleged irregularities concerning campaign financing and organization, further complicating the internal situation of the Poland 2050 party.
