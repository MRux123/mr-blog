## WORLD

* **Escalation in Ukraine:** Russian forces have intensified their campaign against critical Ukrainian infrastructure, targeting a second major bridge in Kyiv. This strategic shift poses a significant threat to civilian logistics and military supply lines in the capital.
* **Aviation Security Incident:** Authorities are investigating a security breach on a Flydubai flight to Israel, where a co-pilot allegedly attacked the captain with an axe. Reports indicate the suspect may have held extremist views, raising serious questions regarding international pre-flight security protocols.
* **Economic Instability:** The G7 has announced a coordinated release of oil and diesel reserves to mitigate price volatility triggered by recent geopolitical threats, particularly following warnings concerning US diesel exports.

## POLAND

* **Major Cybersecurity Breach:** The travel platform Wakacje.pl has suffered a significant data leak. Unauthorized access to the company's servers resulted in the exposure of sensitive client information, including full names and residential addresses, marking a concerning escalation in cyberattacks against Polish commercial entities.
* **Economic Impact of Ukrainian Refugees:** A new report highlights the vital role of the Ukrainian community in the Polish economy, revealing that they contribute approximately 100 billion PLN to the national GDP. Despite this, the presence of these migrants remains a point of heated political debate, as evidenced by controversial remarks from former Prime Minister Leszek Miller regarding the allocation of state funds and regional security.
