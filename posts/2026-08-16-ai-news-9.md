## WORLD

* **Tragedy in Indonesia:** A devastating earthquake has struck Indonesia, resulting in at least 51 fatalities. The disaster has displaced thousands, with rescue operations currently hampered by widespread destruction of public infrastructure and residential housing.
* **Escalating Attacks in Ukraine:** Russian forces have launched a series of major aerial strikes across Ukraine, targeting civilian infrastructure. The attacks have resulted in at least five deaths, further escalating tensions in the ongoing conflict.
* **Public Health Alert:** The Ebola outbreak in the Democratic Republic of the Congo continues to accelerate, spreading to a sixth province. The World Health Organization warns that this outbreak is on track to become the deadliest in history, already surpassing 2,100 fatalities.

## POLAND

* **Bus Tragedy in Hungary:** A catastrophic accident involving a Polish tourist bus on a Hungarian motorway has claimed 12 lives. Polish authorities have launched an emergency helpline as the President and Prime Minister expressed their condolences following the loss of the pilgrims. 
* **Security Concerns:** Domestic police forces are currently engaged in high-stakes manhunts following two separate violent incidents: a stabbing attack that left four people wounded, and a shooting involving a woman on a residential staircase.
