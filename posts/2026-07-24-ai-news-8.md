## WORLD

* **US Implements New Trade Tariffs:** The United States has initiated a fresh wave of tariffs targeting dozens of trading partners. The administration cited forced labor concerns as the primary justification for the levies, which replace temporary global duties established earlier this year following a Supreme Court ruling.
* **Wildfires Force Mass Evacuations in France:** Emergency services have ordered the total evacuation of the Cap Ferret peninsula as wildfires continue to spread rapidly. Hundreds of residents and tourists are being transported to safety by boat as local authorities struggle to contain the blaze.

## POLAND

* **Strategic Defense Initiative with the US:** Poland has formally proposed a joint venture with the United States for the production of Patriot missile system components, with the potential inclusion of Ukraine. Government officials emphasize that the initiative aims to secure defense supply chains within stable, protected regions, with trilateral discussions expected to commence shortly.
* **Legislative Crackdown on 'Patostreaming':** New legislation aimed at prohibiting profit-driven "patostreaming" has been officially published in the Journal of Laws. The act, which will come into force on August 22, follows a sustained public and media campaign highlighting the social harms associated with the exploitation of controversial internet content.
* **Policy Debates on Short-Term Rentals:** The Polish government is currently navigating internal tensions regarding regulations for short-term rental properties. The Ministry of Funds and Regional Policy is advocating for municipal authority to designate "prohibition zones" for such rentals, citing the need to protect the residential stability and quality of life for permanent inhabitants in densely populated areas.
