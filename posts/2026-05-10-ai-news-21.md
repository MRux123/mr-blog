## WORLD

*   **US-Iran Tensions Escalate:** President Donald Trump has formally rejected Iran's latest response to proposed terms for ending the ongoing conflict, labeling it "totally unacceptable." The negotiations, which reportedly included a potential 30-day ceasefire and the reopening of the Strait of Hormuz, remain deadlocked as both nations exchange harsh rhetoric.
*   **US-China Diplomatic Thaw:** In a significant shift in geopolitical strategy, the White House announced that the US and China will establish joint councils for trade and investment. President Trump is scheduled to visit Beijing to address critical friction points, including China’s support for Iran and Russia, as well as concerns regarding the expansion of the Chinese nuclear arsenal.

## POLAND

*   **Release of Andrzej Poczobut:** In a major diplomatic breakthrough, journalist and activist Andrzej Poczobut has been released from detention. President Trump publicly credited the collaborative efforts of Polish officials, specifically citing the role of Karol Nawrocki, and acknowledged the cooperation of Belarusian President Alexander Lukashenko in the matter.
*   **Political Developments:** Zbigniew Ziobro has confirmed his presence in the United States, stating his intention to "exercise American freedom," a move that continues to spark debate within Polish political circles.
*   **Sports Tragedy:** The nation is mourning following a catastrophic accident during a speedway match in Gniezno, where 19-year-old Patryk Budniak sustained life-threatening injuries after a high-impact crash, highlighting the extreme risks associated with the sport.
