## WORLD

* **Escalating Conflict in the Middle East:** Military tensions between the United States and Iran have intensified significantly. Despite extensive U.S. airstrikes targeting Iranian military infrastructure, Tehran remains defiant. Recent reports indicate that Iran attempted, albeit unsuccessfully, to target a joint allied base in the Indian Ocean with ballistic missiles.
* **Mass Protests in Prague:** Approximately 250,000 citizens have gathered in the Czech capital to voice strong opposition against the current government. Demonstrators expressed deep concerns over the country’s political direction, citing fears that current leadership is steering the Czech Republic toward a path similar to that of Hungary.

## POLAND

* **Tragic Fire in Warsaw:** A devastating blaze in the Ursynów district has claimed the lives of four men. Emergency services and firefighting experts have categorized the fire as exceptionally intense, noting that the victims reportedly attempted to combat the flames themselves before being overwhelmed by the lack of oxygen.
* **Grim Discovery in Oświęcim:** Police have launched an investigation following the discovery of two bodies in a residential apartment in Oświęcim. Neighbors alerted authorities after noticing an unpleasant odor emanating from the premises. Preliminary findings suggest the victims were a 77-year-old mother and her 49-year-old daughter.
* **Violent Attack in Capital City:** A 20-year-old man remains in critical condition following a brutal assault in the center of Warsaw. A 48-year-old suspect has been apprehended and charged with causing severe bodily harm, with potential sentences reaching up to 20 years of imprisonment.
