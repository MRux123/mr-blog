## WORLD

* **Economic Warfare in Iran:** Iran’s two largest steel plants have ceased operations following a series of strikes. Tehran officials have attributed the disruption to coordinated efforts by Israel and the United States, raising concerns about the potential for significant long-term damage to the Iranian economy.
* **Escalation in the Middle East:** A coalition of 18 European nations, including Poland, has issued a formal appeal demanding an immediate cessation of hostilities between Israel and Hezbollah. The statement stresses that military operations in Lebanon and rocket attacks against Israel must end to prevent further regional destabilization.
* **Migrant Deportation Policy:** In a controversial move under the Trump administration’s new immigration strategy, the first group of deportees from the U.S. has arrived in Uganda. The deal, which aims to transition migrants to third countries, is facing intense legal scrutiny and criticism from human rights groups.

## POLAND

* **Internal Discord in PiS:** Jarosław Kaczyński has held a high-level meeting with former Prime Minister Mateusz Morawiecki in Warsaw. Reports suggest the meeting was an attempt by the party leader to impose discipline on Morawiecki following a series of unauthorized political initiatives and controversial public statements.
* **Legal Battles with Pfizer:** The European Commission has declined to intervene in the ongoing legal dispute between Poland and pharmaceutical giant Pfizer, leaving the Polish government to navigate the judgment independently.
