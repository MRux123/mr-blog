## WORLD

* **Assassination Attempt on Donald Trump:** President Donald Trump was safely evacuated by the Secret Service during a dinner with White House correspondents following reports of gunfire. A suspect, identified by the President as a man from California, opened fire in the hotel lobby before being apprehended. One Secret Service agent was wounded in the incident but is reported to be in stable condition.
* **Geopolitical Tensions and Diplomacy:** President Trump has officially called off a high-level diplomatic mission to Pakistan aimed at peace talks with Iran. The administration claims that the cancellation followed the reception of a "better document" from Tehran, though Iranian officials have expressed skepticism regarding the long-term sincerity of U.S. diplomatic overtures.
* **German Political Shift:** The populist, right-wing party Alternative for Germany (AfD) has reached a record-breaking 28 percent in recent polling conducted by the Insa institute. This surge signals a significant potential shift in the German political landscape, reflecting growing voter frustration and a trend toward eurosceptic sentiment.

## POLAND

* **40th Anniversary of the Chernobyl Disaster:** As the world marks four decades since the 1986 nuclear catastrophe, current geopolitical instability has brought new attention to the site. The ongoing war resulting from the Russian invasion of Ukraine has added a dangerous dimension to the management and security of the radioactive zone, highlighting that the consequences of the tragedy remain a contemporary concern.
* **Tragedy Involving a Polish Influencer:** The Polish media is mourning the death of influencer Klaudia Zakrzewska, who succumbed to her injuries in a London hospital. She was struck by a vehicle driven by British celebrity Gabrielle Carrington following a reported personal conflict. Local authorities have arrested Carrington, and prosecutors are currently reviewing charges, with the initial accusation of attempted murder expected to be upgraded to a more severe offense following the victim's death.
