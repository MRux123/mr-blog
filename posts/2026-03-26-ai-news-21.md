## WORLD

*   **Escalating Iran-U.S. Standoff:** President Trump has extended the deadline for Iran to reopen the critical Strait of Hormuz for oil transit to April 6. The threat of potential military strikes on Iran's power grid has triggered global market instability, leading to a sharp decline in stock indices and a surge in oil prices, signaling deep economic concern over energy security.
*   **Legal Precedent in Right-to-Die Case:** The death of Noelia Castillo Ramos following a lengthy legal battle marks a significant milestone in European jurisprudence. After an ECHR ruling in her favor, the 25-year-old’s case has reignited intense debates across the continent regarding the ethical and legal frameworks surrounding euthanasia and the right to a dignified death.
*   **Political Turmoil in Slovakia:** Prime Minister Robert Fico is facing a formal police investigation following allegations of high treason. The case, initiated by opposition leader Branislav Gröhling, centers on Fico’s controversial foreign policy and rhetoric, which critics claim serves Russian interests at the expense of national sovereignty.

## POLAND

*   **EU Conflict over VAT Policy:** The European Commission has officially stated that Poland’s recent fuel tax reduction measures are in violation of EU law. The Commission maintains that the existing VAT directive does not permit reduced rates on motor fuels, setting the stage for a potential fiscal clash between Warsaw and Brussels.
*   **Public Health Alert in Krakow:** Local health authorities have initiated emergency protocols at a Krakow high school after a student was diagnosed with tuberculosis. Over 100 individuals identified as having had contact with the student are currently undergoing medical screening to prevent a potential outbreak.
