## WORLD

* **Russia-Iran Strategic Alignment:** President Vladimir Putin has publicly pledged Moscow's support for Iran amidst escalating regional tensions in the Middle East. During a high-level meeting in St. Petersburg, Putin praised Tehran's stance, signaling a deepening of the strategic partnership between the two nations as they seek to navigate the current geopolitical instability.
* **Canada Shifts Economic Strategy:** Prime Minister Mark Carney has announced the creation of a sovereign wealth fund aimed at reducing Canada’s economic reliance on the United States. While the fund is expected to be modest in scale compared to major oil-producing nations, the policy marks a significant strategic pivot in Canadian economic sovereignty.

## POLAND

* **VAT Relief Extended for Fuel:** The Ministry of Finance and Economy has officially extended the temporary reduction of VAT and excise duty on gasoline and diesel. The relief measures, originally slated to expire at the end of April, will remain in effect until May 15, providing a brief reprieve for consumers and the transportation sector amidst ongoing inflationary pressures.
* **Legal Clearing for Peter Magyar:** Hungarian opposition leader and TISZA party head, Peter Magyar, has been cleared of criminal charges. An investigation into allegations made by his former spouse, ex-Justice Minister Judit Varga, was closed on Monday due to a lack of evidence, removing a significant legal hurdle for the emerging political leader.
