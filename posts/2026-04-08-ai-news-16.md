## WORLD

* **Fragile Cease-Fire Between U.S. and Iran:** While a two-week truce has been officially announced following tensions exacerbated by threats of regional destruction, the situation remains highly volatile. Despite the diplomatic breakthrough, Israel continues targeted strikes against Iranian-backed Hezbollah in Lebanon, and incidents such as a drone attack on a key Saudi Arabian oil pipeline underscore the fragility of the peace process.
* **Greece to Enforce Social Media Ban for Minors:** Moving to address concerns regarding the impact of digital platforms on adolescent mental health, the Greek government has announced a ban on social media access for children under the age of 15. This legislative move aligns Greece with a growing international trend of nations—including France, Spain, and Australia—seeking to curb youth exposure to online environments.

## POLAND

* **Healthcare System Under Scrutiny:** Amid mounting public concern regarding the stability of the Polish healthcare system, the National Health Fund (NFZ) faces intense questioning over its budgetary deficit. With record-high healthcare spending failing to address systemic inefficiencies, officials are being forced to defend the state of medical infrastructure and administrative oversight.
* **Mandatory Health Education Reform:** In a significant shift for the national curriculum, reports indicate that health education is set to become a mandatory subject in Polish schools, signaling a policy pivot toward preventative care and public health awareness among the younger generation.
