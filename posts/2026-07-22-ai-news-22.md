## WORLD

*   **Escalating Financial Tensions in the Middle East:** Israeli financial institutions are threatening to terminate their long-standing partnership with Palestinian banks. This move poses a severe risk to the West Bank’s economy, as these firms are essential conduits for regional imports and exports.
*   **Strategic Shift in Maritime Logistics:** In a move to circumvent the volatile Strait of Hormuz, a Dubai-based logistics giant has finalized a deal to develop new shipping terminals on the UAE’s east coast, signaling a significant shift in regional energy and trade infrastructure.
*   **US-Saudi Nuclear Cooperation:** The United States has signed a landmark peaceful nuclear energy agreement with Saudi Arabia, granting American firms expanded access to the Saudi nuclear program, marking a new chapter in energy diplomacy between the two nations.

## POLAND

*   **Passing of Professor Stanisław Gomułka:** The academic and economic community mourns the death of Prof. Stanisław Gomułka at age 85. A pivotal figure in Poland's post-communist economic transformation, he served as a former deputy finance minister and advisor to the National Bank of Poland.
*   **International Manhunt:** Argentinian authorities, supported by Interpol, have launched a search for a 45-year-old Polish national suspected of murdering a 38-year-old woman in Argentina.
*   **Railway Incident in Zagnańsk:** Emergency services responded to a serious freight train derailment in the Świętokrzyskie region. While no casualties were reported, the accident highlights ongoing concerns regarding regional rail infrastructure safety.
