## WORLD

*   **Escalation in Yemen:** The regional security landscape remains precarious as the conflict in Yemen intensifies. Overnight clashes between the Yemeni government and Iranian-backed Houthi rebels have raised concerns over stability, particularly as the international community monitors potential disruptions to key maritime chokepoints like the Strait of Hormuz.
*   **Trump Administration Appointments:** The US political landscape continues to evolve following the confirmation of Todd Blanche, a former lawyer for Donald Trump, as the new US Attorney General. The appointment, finalized after a narrow Senate vote, marks a significant consolidation of the administration's legal and executive influence.
*   **Energy Infrastructure Threat:** Security fears have been amplified following a drone explosion near the Trans-Balkan Pipeline in Bulgaria. The incident, confirmed by the Bulgarian Prime Minister, highlights the vulnerability of critical energy infrastructure in Eastern Europe amid ongoing geopolitical tensions.

## POLAND

*   **Military Readiness and Parade Preparations:** Warsaw is bracing for intensified military activity as the army conducts full-scale rehearsals for the upcoming Armed Forces Day parade. Residents should expect significant travel disruptions in the capital as air and land units finalize preparations for the national celebration.
*   **Political Shifts and Presidential Rankings:** Recent polling data has provided a fresh perspective on the national political landscape. A study by United Surveys for Wirtualna Polska reveals that nearly 48% of Poles consider Aleksander Kwaśniewski the most effective president since 1989, followed by Lech Kaczyński and Karol Nawrocki, the latter gaining significant momentum in public perception.
*   **Regional Command Changes:** A notable change in military leadership is underway, as Gen. Lt. Charles D. Costanza, the commander of the US V Corps responsible for operations in Europe, has been unexpectedly relieved of his duties. The move is viewed as part of a broader structural reorganization within the US Department of Defense.
