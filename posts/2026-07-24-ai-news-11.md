## WORLD

*   **Russian Missile Barrage Hits Kyiv:** Russian forces launched a major ballistic missile attack on the Ukrainian capital, deploying advanced weaponry, including Zircon missiles. The assault, which occurred Friday morning, marks a significant intensification of the ongoing conflict, forcing air defense systems into emergency operations across the city.
*   **NATO Territory Compromised by Aerial Incursion:** A Romanian F-16 fighter jet intercepted and destroyed an unidentified drone that violated Romanian airspace. President Nicusor Dan confirmed this as the first successful engagement of its kind by Romanian forces, highlighting growing security risks on the alliance's eastern flank.
*   **US-Iran Military Friction Intensifies:** Fears of regional escalation in the Gulf have reached a new high as the US and Iran engage in tit-for-tat strikes. The situation remains volatile, further complicated by the US plan to utilize frozen Iranian assets for war reparations, a move heavily condemned by Tehran.

## POLAND

*   **Political Purge Within PiS:** Law and Justice (PiS) is facing a significant internal crisis. Party leader Jarosław Kaczyński announced that over 30 prominent members, including reportedly Mateusz Morawiecki, will be removed from the party register. The split stems from a refusal to sign a new loyalty declaration regarding political activities outside the party structure.
*   **Security Alert from Belarus:** The Polish Ministry of Foreign Affairs, led by Radosław Sikorski, is currently investigating an official warning received from Belarus concerning a potential terrorist attack. Authorities are treating the intelligence with high priority as they verify the veracity of the threat.
