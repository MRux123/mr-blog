## WORLD

*   **Deadly Russian Missile Barrage on Kyiv:** Ukraine’s capital has suffered a devastating missile strike, resulting in at least nine fatalities and numerous injuries. The assault, characterized by Moscow’s intensification of ballistic missile use, highlights the ongoing volatility and escalation in the conflict, with emergency services reporting that the death toll may continue to rise.
*   **Rising Geopolitical Tensions between the US and Iran:** A significant standoff is developing as the United States reportedly prepares for a major military operation against targets within Iran. In response, Iranian state-affiliated media have threatened retaliatory strikes against critical infrastructure in Israel and across the Persian Gulf, signaling a precarious shift toward regional instability.

## POLAND

*   **Latvia Closes Border with Belarus:** In a move with direct implications for regional security and Polish eastern border stability, Latvia has shuttered its last operational crossing with Belarus. While authorities cite technical failures in information systems, the decision is widely viewed as a strategic reaction to the surging migratory pressure orchestrated by the Belarusian regime.
