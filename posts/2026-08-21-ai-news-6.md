## WORLD

* **Yung Filly Retrial:** British rapper and content creator Yung Filly is set to face a retrial in Australia. While a jury cleared him of several sexual assault charges last month, they failed to reach a verdict on three remaining counts, necessitating further legal proceedings.

* **Pacific Survival Miracle:** In a remarkable story of survival, two fishermen were rescued by the Mexican Navy after drifting for five days across the Pacific Ocean. The men, aged 32 and 53, were found alive on a portable cooling box after their colleagues reported them missing.

## POLAND

* **Terror Plot Against Politicians:** Law enforcement has arrested a 23-year-old man accused of planning a terrorist attack targeting leading Polish politicians. The suspect faces up to 20 years in prison. The incident has triggered a wave of concern, with politician Michał Dworczyk calling for an immediate de-escalation of political rhetoric, citing a dangerous rise in hate speech.

* **Security Concerns on Rails:** Authorities, including the Internal Security Agency (ABW) and the Central Bureau of Investigation (CBŚP), are investigating suspicious findings on railway tracks in the Lublin region. While a train driver reported a potential explosive sound, subsequent inspections found no damage or physical evidence of explosive materials, though the investigation remains ongoing.

* **Musk-Ukraine Communication Gap:** Ukraine is currently navigating a significant logistical challenge as critical communication lines with Elon Musk have been disrupted. The situation is critical, as Kyiv requires prompt authorization from Musk to maintain technical operational capabilities.
