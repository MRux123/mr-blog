## WORLD

*   **Middle East Diplomacy:** High-level Iranian negotiators have arrived in Qatar for urgent peace talks. Despite the presence of senior delegations, both the United States and Iran have tempered expectations, noting that a breakthrough remains elusive amidst conflicting reports on the progress of negotiations.
*   **Papal Stance on Technology:** Pope Leo has issued a comprehensive 42,300-word encyclical addressing the ethical implications of artificial intelligence. The document highlights the Vatican’s growing concern regarding the potential for systemic misuse and the rapid, unchecked integration of AI into global society.
*   **Humanitarian Crisis in DRC:** The Ebola outbreak in the Democratic Republic of the Congo has escalated, with suspected cases surpassing 900. Health workers continue to face severe operational challenges, including physical attacks and critical resource shortages, as international agencies warn of a high regional risk.

## POLAND

*   **Judicial Leadership Change:** President Karol Nawrocki has officially appointed Judge Zbigniew Kapiński as the new First President of the Supreme Court, marking a significant development in the ongoing restructuring of Poland’s judicial administration.
*   **Political Instability in Municipalities:** The political climate in major Polish cities is increasingly volatile. Following the recall efforts in Kraków, Rzeszów is now facing a potential referendum regarding the removal of its mayor, Konrad Fijołek, as local opposition groups gain momentum. 
*   **Tragic Infrastructure Accident:** A fatal collision involving a train and a truck in the Greater Poland (Wielkopolska) region has resulted in one confirmed death and multiple injuries, prompting an immediate investigation into local safety protocols.
