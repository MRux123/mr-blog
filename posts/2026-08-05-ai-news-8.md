## WORLD

*   **Escalation in Ukraine:** At least 17 people have been killed following a significant Russian strike on logistics hubs near Kyiv. President Zelensky has emphasized that the tragic loss of life could have been prevented with adequate interceptor capabilities, fueling the ongoing debate regarding international military support.
*   **Climate-Driven Food Insecurity:** The UN has issued a dire warning that the El Niño weather phenomenon is projected to push an additional 50 million people into acute hunger by the end of next year. This crisis compounds existing food insecurity in 45 countries already suffering from the aftermath of regional conflicts and prolonged drought.
*   **Mediterranean Border Tensions:** The Spanish enclave of Ceuta has become the latest European flashpoint following a massive influx of migrants. The situation, characterized by misinformation and political instability, has prompted an emergency response from EU ministers, who are struggling to reconcile humanitarian concerns with border security requirements.

## POLAND

*   **Security Gaps Questioned:** Following the recent impact of a Russian missile on Polish territory, internal investigations have exposed serious systemic failures. Reports indicate that warning sirens failed to activate due to lapses in personnel attendance, sparking a national debate on the state of readiness of Polish air defense protocols.
*   **Presidential Office Controversies:** The President’s office is currently facing scrutiny over two major fronts: the unusual, high-profile preparations for an upcoming anniversary event, and the controversial pardon of Piotr "Staruch" Staruchowicz, a prominent football fan leader. The administration defends the latter as an act of "correcting an error" and a gesture toward conservative outreach.
*   **Extreme Weather Alerts:** Poland is currently grappling with record-breaking heatwaves, with temperatures expected to hit 40°C in the southeast. The extreme conditions are not only challenging public safety but also revealing historical artifacts, such as the emergence of WWII-era German shipwrecks in the Danube, exposed by the receding water levels.
