## WORLD

*   **EU Pushes for Stricter Border Security:** European Commission President Ursula von der Leyen has called for a major reinforcement of EU borders following the recent migration crisis in Ceuta. Highlighting the need for vigilant monitoring and physical barriers, von der Leyen emphasized the necessity of a more robust strategy for returning individuals who do not meet asylum criteria.
*   **Strategic Talks on Strait of Hormuz:** Iran has announced that negotiations with Oman concerning the security and management of the Strait of Hormuz have reached their final stages. While the vital waterway remains a critical chokepoint for global oil transit, neither the United States nor Oman has yet confirmed the details of the proposed agreement.
*   **Afghan Women’s Football Team Finds Sanctuary:** A symbol of resilience has emerged as the Afghan women’s national football team, recognized by FIFA, has reunited after being forced to flee their home country following a ban on women's sports. The team continues to operate in exile, framing their athletic pursuit as a platform for independence and freedom.
