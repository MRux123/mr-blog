## WORLD

* **Escalation in US-Iran Maritime Conflict**: Tensions in the Middle East have reached a critical juncture as the United States and Iran engaged in retaliatory strikes against naval vessels. The US reported hitting three Iranian oil tankers, while Iranian forces claimed to have successfully targeted three US-linked ships, signaling a dangerous shift in the maritime confrontation.
* **Diplomatic Mourning in Norway**: The international community prepares for the funeral of King Harald V of Norway in Oslo. Representing the British Monarchy, the Prince of Wales is scheduled to attend the ceremony next week, underscoring the deep diplomatic ties between the two nations during this period of national mourning.

## POLAND

* **Political Decorum Under Fire**: A controversial incident in the Sejm has sparked a wave of criticism against the Law and Justice (PiS) party. During an address by Deputy Prime Minister Władysław Kosiniak-Kamysz, MP Dominika Chorosińska was observed making barking noises, a gesture joined by other party members. PiS Vice President Tobiasz Bocheński has since publicly addressed the incident, suggesting he is open to issuing a formal apology for the conduct.
* **Success on the International Stage**: Polish athlete Iga Świątek has successfully advanced to the 1/8 finals of the US Open after defeating Marie Bouzková. In other sports news, Klaudia Kazimierska has set a new Polish record, achieving a world-leading result in her discipline, marking a significant moment for Polish athletics.
* **Violent Incident in Kutno**: Local authorities in Kutno are investigating a violent altercation that left a Polish citizen severely injured. A Georgian national has been detained in connection with the incident, and the case remains under active police investigation.
