## WORLD

* **Escalating Unrest in France:** Authorities have imposed curfews in parts of central Paris and other major cities following a wave of violent rioting. The move comes as the French interior minister acknowledged a loss of control in several urban centers, signaling a significant domestic security crisis that is currently testing the resilience of the nation’s law enforcement.
* **Trump’s Shift on Ukraine and Global Diplomacy:** Former U.S. President Donald Trump has expressed willingness to engage in a summit with Vladimir Putin and Xi Jinping to discuss nuclear disarmament. In a controversial pivot, Trump suggested that Ukrainian President Volodymyr Zelenskyy should no longer lead negotiations on behalf of Kyiv, further complicating the geopolitical landscape surrounding the ongoing war.

## POLAND

* **NATO Consultations on Air Defense:** Following Poland’s lead, Lithuania has initiated consultations with NATO regarding the potential for shooting down aerial objects that violate national airspace without prior visual confirmation. This policy shift follows recent security incidents involving unauthorized drones, highlighting growing regional concerns over border integrity.
* **Unauthorized Military-Style Presence in Warsaw:** The Warsaw Garrison Command has issued a formal statement following an incident at the Tomb of the Unknown Soldier. A group identifying as "Rodacy Kamraci," dressed in uniforms resembling law enforcement, marched in formation to lay a wreath without official authorization, raising concerns about the misuse of national symbols by nationalist movements.
