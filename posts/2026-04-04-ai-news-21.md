## WORLD

*   **Heightened Conflict in the Middle East:** The situation surrounding the Strait of Hormuz has reached a critical juncture. Following an Israeli strike on a major petrochemical complex in Iran, President Trump has issued an ultimatum demanding the reopening of the strategic waterway. Meanwhile, U.S. forces are conducting an urgent search for a missing crew member from a downed F-15 Eagle, with concerns mounting over the potential for capture by Iranian forces. Iran has begun selectively relaxing transit restrictions for specific nations, including Iraq, Japan, and Turkey, while continuing to target assets linked to Israel.
*   **Humanitarian Crisis in Afghanistan:** The nation is grappling with a dual catastrophe. Widespread flooding has resulted in at least 77 fatalities and severe infrastructure damage, compounded by a recent earthquake that claimed a dozen additional lives, further straining the country’s limited resources.

## POLAND

*   **Growing Anxiety Ahead of Hungarian Elections:** Recent polling from the Publicus Institute reveals significant instability in the Hungarian political climate. More than half of the electorate fears that the upcoming parliamentary elections on April 12 will be fraudulent, with 79% of respondents expressing concerns over potential foreign interference in the democratic process.
*   **Regional Military Mobilization:** In response to the deteriorating security situation in Europe, neighboring states—most notably Germany and France—are taking drastic measures. France has officially moved toward a "war economy," allocating 8.5 billion euros to ramp up the production of drones and interceptor missiles. Simultaneously, Germany has introduced stringent travel regulations requiring men aged 17 to 45 to obtain military approval for long-term stays abroad, a move that has caught many citizens by surprise.
