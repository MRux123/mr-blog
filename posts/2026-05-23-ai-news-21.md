## WORLD

*   **Escalating Tensions in Ukraine:** The U.S. Embassy in Kyiv has issued a high-level warning regarding a potential "significant air attack" expected within the next 24 hours. President Volodymyr Zelensky, citing Western intelligence, indicated that Russia may be preparing a strike involving medium-range ballistic missiles, specifically referencing the "Oreshnik" system.
*   **Progress in Iran Negotiations:** U.S. President Donald Trump has suggested that a deal with Iran is "largely negotiated," which reportedly includes provisions for reopening the Strait of Hormuz. While Iranian officials have acknowledged progress in talks, they maintain that the issue of nuclear weapons remains outside the scope of the current framework.

## POLAND

*   **Security Incident at President’s Residence:** Security services forcibly entered the home of President Karol Nawrocki in Gdańsk following false reports of a fire and a medical emergency. High-ranking officials, including Prime Minister Donald Tusk and the Minister of the Interior, confirmed the incident was triggered by hoax emergency calls, leading to a coordinated forced entry by emergency services.
*   **Polish Cinema Success at Cannes:** Polish director Paweł Pawlikowski received a prestigious award for Best Director for his film *Ojczyzna* at the closing gala of the 79th Cannes Film Festival. The festival concluded with the Palme d'Or awarded to Cristian Mungiu for the film *Fjord*.
