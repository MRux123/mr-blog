## WORLD

*   **Fatal Cargo Plane Crash in Miami:** Investigations are underway following a devastating Boeing 767-300 crash at Miami International Airport. The incident, which resulted in five fatalities and several serious injuries, occurred when the aircraft overshot the runway. Flight recorders have been successfully recovered, which are expected to provide critical data regarding the cause of the disaster.
*   **Corruption Scandal Impacts Ukrainian Judiciary:** The Chief Prosecutor of Ukraine, Ruslan Kravchenko, has resigned amid an escalating corruption scandal involving call centers. While Kravchenko denies all allegations, stating he refuses to allow his office to become an instrument of political maneuvering, his departure marks a significant moment for the country’s legal and political stability.
*   **Civil Unrest in Paris:** The iconic Eiffel Tower has been shuttered due to staff protests. The industrial action was triggered by reports that female employees were sidelined or "humiliated" during a religious visit at the landmark. The Mayor of Paris has ordered an immediate investigation into the discriminatory allegations.

## POLAND

*   **Political Fallout from AfD Electoral Success:** The recent victory of the AfD party in Saxony-Anhalt has triggered widespread protests in Berlin. Demonstrators gathered to express their opposition to the political shift, highlighting the deep social and political polarization currently affecting regional German politics and its implications for the Polish-German border region.
*   **Escalating Trade Tensions with the US:** Former President Donald Trump has initiated a move to boycott Canadian manufacturer Bombardier, marking a new phase in cross-border economic friction. This aggressive protectionist stance is expected to cause ripple effects across the regional supply chain and impact trade relations that are closely monitored by Polish economic observers.
