## WORLD

* **Escalation in Kyiv:** A Russian drone attack struck the Pivnichyi Bridge in Kyiv during a surprise visit by German Chancellor Friedrich Merz. The incident, marked by a significant explosion caught on footage, underscores the volatile security situation in the Ukrainian capital as the conflict persists.
* **Flydubai Security Breach:** Authorities are investigating a serious security incident involving a Flydubai co-pilot who attempted to seize control of a flight bound for Israel. Intelligence agencies are currently reviewing the suspect's background, with Israeli officials facing internal criticism over their failure to identify the pilot's extremist ties.
* **New Space Race:** Major global powers are accelerating efforts to establish a permanent presence on the Moon, with the U.S. aiming for a nuclear reactor installation by 2030, followed by a joint Russian-Chinese initiative by 2036. The move has drawn concern from scientists regarding the potential environmental and safety risks of lunar nuclear deployment.

## POLAND

* **Passing of Sister Anastazja:** Poland mourns the death of Sister Anastazja Pustelnik, aged 76, a renowned author whose culinary books achieved massive commercial success with over four million copies sold. Her work leaves a significant imprint on Polish culinary culture.
* **Road Safety Tragedy in Nowy Sącz:** A fatal collision at a pedestrian crossing in Nowy Sącz, where a 51-year-old man was killed by a driver overtaking illegally, has sparked national outrage. The incident has been widely discussed following the release of disturbing footage documenting the crash.
