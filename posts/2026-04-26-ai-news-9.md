## WORLD

*   **Israeli Political Mediation:** President Isaac Herzog has officially opted against granting a pardon to Prime Minister Benjamin Netanyahu regarding his ongoing corruption case. Instead, the President is pursuing a formal mediation process to facilitate a potential plea deal, marking a pivotal moment in the nation's legal and political stability.
*   **Escalation in Mali:** Regional security has deteriorated sharply as armed groups launched a series of highly coordinated attacks across northern and central Mali. Analysts describe the offensive as the most significant jihadist challenge the country has faced in years, raising concerns about the government's ability to maintain territorial integrity.
*   **Cartel Gold Scandal:** A troubling investigation by the New York Times has exposed deep-seated corruption, revealing that the U.S. Mint has inadvertently acquired gold linked to drug cartels, which was subsequently branded as "American." The findings highlight a systemic breakdown in precious metal supply chain oversight.

## POLAND

*   **Ukrainian Strike on Russian Chemical Infrastructure:** In a significant tactical move, Ukrainian forces conducted a large-scale drone operation against Russian territory. The strike successfully damaged a sulfuric acid pipeline at the JSC Apatit chemical plant in the Vologda region. Despite Russian claims of intercepting over 250 drones, the successful hit on critical industrial infrastructure signals a new phase in the ongoing conflict.
*   **Security Concerns in the U.S. and Polish Reactions:** Following a high-profile attack in Washington D.C., Polish political commentators and security experts have begun scrutinizing the resilience of Western security protocols. Critics argue that the breach underscores dangerous negligence in protective services, prompting internal debates in Poland regarding the effectiveness of intelligence cooperation and national security readiness in the face of global instability.
*   **Severe Weather Disruptions:** The Polish government has issued urgent weather alerts as high-velocity winds impact approximately half of the country. Meteorological services report over 450 emergency interventions by the State Fire Service, with at least one casualty reported, underscoring the ongoing danger posed by extreme weather patterns in the region.
