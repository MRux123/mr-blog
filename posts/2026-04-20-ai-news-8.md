## WORLD

* **Escalating Conflict with Iran:** Global markets are reeling as oil prices climbed to $96 a barrel following the U.S. military attack and seizure of an Iranian cargo ship. Prime Minister Benjamin Netanyahu has signaled further operations, fueling uncertainty as the conflict continues to send economic shockwaves throughout the Asia-Pacific region.
* **Geopolitical Realignment:** Middle powers, including Turkey, are recalibrating their diplomatic stances in response to an increasingly unpredictable U.S. foreign policy, as the war in Iran forces nations to reconsider their reliance on American strategic stability.
* **Political Shifts in Madagascar:** Concerns regarding the stability of Madagascar's military regime have intensified following the arrest of Gen Z activists, casting doubt on the transition to a more democratic government.

## POLAND

* **Educational Reform and Political Controversy:** Minister of Education Barbara Nowacka has announced significant reforms to the "Education for Security" curriculum in Polish schools. The move follows a heated public exchange with Archbishop Marek Jędraszewski, who drew sharp criticism from the government for his inflammatory remarks comparing current state educational policies to historical authoritarian regimes.
* **Election Results in Bulgaria:** In a notable regional development, the "Progressive Bulgaria" coalition led by Rumen Radev has secured a parliamentary majority, signaling a potential end to the country's prolonged period of political instability.
* **Digital Infrastructure Disruptions:** A major technical failure of the Polcard payment system has disrupted retail operations across Poland, highlighting the country's ongoing vulnerability to systemic financial infrastructure issues.
