## WORLD

*   **Extreme Heatwave Grips Western Europe:** France and neighboring nations are grappling with historic temperatures, triggering power outages and infrastructure strain. Meteorologists warn that the heatwave is intensifying and expected to migrate eastwards over the coming weekend, testing the continent's climate resilience.
*   **Strategic Escalation in Crimea:** Ukrainian forces have launched a significant strike on the Crimean Peninsula, leading to major power outages in Sevastopol and surrounding areas. The intensity of these strikes, heard as far as Bakhchysarai and Kerch, marks a notable escalation in the ongoing conflict.
*   **Geopolitical Concerns Regarding Belarus:** Analysts from the Institute for the Study of War (ISW) have issued warnings regarding potential Russian maneuvers to integrate Belarus more deeply into the war effort. There is growing concern that Moscow may attempt to open a second front by leveraging regional security treaties to conscript Belarusian forces.

## POLAND

*   **Healthcare Ethics and Oversight Crisis:** The Polish medical community is currently navigating a period of severe reputational turbulence. Following revelations concerning high-earning physicians allegedly engaged in political activity during their shifts at the Szpital Południowy, the Supreme Medical Council is moving to suspend the professional licenses of the individuals involved.
*   **Controversial Diplomatic Engagement:** The Polish government has confirmed its participation in discussions with Taliban representatives in Brussels. The talks, aimed at addressing migration and repatriation protocols, have drawn sharp domestic criticism, with political figures comparing the engagement to "sitting at the table with the devil."
