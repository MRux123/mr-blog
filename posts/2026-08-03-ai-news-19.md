## WORLD

* **Tragedy on the Black Sea:** Russian authorities have reported a deadly incident in which a Ukrainian drone strike hit a crowded beach at a popular resort. The attack resulted in at least seven fatalities, including three children, and left 40 others injured. This escalation marks a significant and tragic development in the ongoing regional conflict.
* **Geopolitical Resource Pressures:** Romania has been forced to import electricity from Ukraine due to critically low water levels in the Danube, which have severely hampered local hydroelectric power generation. This move underscores the precarious nature of energy security in the region amidst changing environmental conditions.

## POLAND

* **Morawiecki’s Political Realignment:** Former Prime Minister Mateusz Morawiecki has officially announced plans to establish a new political party by October 15, following his exit from the PiS structure. The move has triggered immediate reactions, with Civic Coalition (KO) leadership categorically rejecting any potential future cooperation with the new formation, labeling it a continuation of previous policies.
* **High-Profile Presidential Pardons:** Recent judicial news includes the pardon of Adam Borowski, as well as the controversial decision by Karol Nawrocki to grant clemency to Piotr "Staruch" Staruchowicz, a prominent leader of Legia Warsaw football fans. The pardon effectively overturns a two-year stadium ban, sparking public debate regarding the criteria for executive leniency in Poland.
