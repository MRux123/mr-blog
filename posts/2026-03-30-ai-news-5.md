## WORLD

*   **Trump-Iran Negotiations:** U.S. President Donald Trump has reported significant progress in diplomatic talks with Iran, noting that Tehran has accepted a majority of a 15-point proposal. Despite the optimism, the President cautioned that talks could still fail and hinted at the possibility of seizing the island of Kharg or confiscating Iranian oil reserves.
*   **Russian Tanker Reaches Cuba:** In a direct challenge to U.S. blockade efforts, a Russian crude oil tanker is expected to reach Cuba by Monday. The delivery serves as a vital lifeline for the island, highlighting the ongoing geopolitical friction between Washington and its adversaries.
*   **Digital Violence in Africa:** Experts and activists are sounding the alarm over a surging crisis of digital violence across Africa. With a massive influx of internet users under the age of 30, reports of gender-based online harassment and the use of digital tools to control women and girls have reached critical levels, prompting calls for immediate legislative intervention.

## POLAND

*   **Border Security Investment:** The Polish government has initiated a 450 million PLN project to construct a state-of-the-art electronic security barrier along the eastern border. The project, aimed at bolstering national security and improving border control efficiency, is currently in the contractor selection phase.
*   **New Fuel Regulations:** The Polish government has implemented a significant reduction in excise taxes for gasoline and diesel, effective today. Authorities are also expected to publish official price caps for fuel stations, which will go into effect this Tuesday, providing immediate relief at the pumps.
*   **New Road Safety Penalties:** Stricter traffic laws have taken effect as of March 30, 2026. The most notable change is a formal ban on "drifting," with authorities promising severe legal consequences for drivers who fail to comply with the new mandate.
