## WORLD

* **Escalation in Ukraine:** Kyiv faces a critical humanitarian and security crisis following a massive Russian air strike that resulted in at least 13 fatalities. The targeting of essential infrastructure, including supermarket warehouses, has led to severe supply chain disruptions, leaving grocery shelves bare across the country as the government struggles with critical air defense shortages.
* **Geopolitical Standoff with Iran:** Tensions between the United States and Iran continue to intensify following the expiration of a 60-day ceasefire. The Trump administration has signaled a shift toward more aggressive economic measures targeting Iran and its supporting nations, while both sides currently remain in a precarious, high-stakes diplomatic stalemate.
* **Evergrande Collapse Conclusion:** The Chinese real estate crisis has reached a definitive legal milestone as the founder of Evergrande, Hui Ka Yan, was sentenced to life in prison. The verdict marks the official end of a corporate empire whose collapse has served as a primary catalyst for long-term instability in the Chinese economy.

## POLAND

* **Air Defense Strategy:** Defense Minister Władysław Kosiniak-Kamysz has addressed public concerns regarding the deployment of air defense systems, confirming that all assets are being strategically positioned according to the specific tactical requirements and recommendations of the military command.
* **Legislative and Economic Adjustments:** Prime Minister Donald Tusk has announced a necessary correction to the proposed tourist tax, indicating an intent to refine the policy to balance fiscal goals with tourism sector requirements. Furthermore, the Ministry of Climate has signaled upcoming modifications to the national deposit-return system, reflecting ongoing efforts to optimize environmental regulations.
