## WORLD

*   **Progress in Iran Crisis:** Diplomatic efforts have reached a critical juncture as U.S. President Donald Trump announced that a peace agreement with Iran is "largely negotiated." While technical details remain under discussion, the deal aims to stabilize the Strait of Hormuz. Meanwhile, British forces are preparing for potential de-mining missions to ensure the security of this vital maritime corridor.
*   **Ongoing Instability in Eastern Europe:** Tensions remain high in the aftermath of a deadly strike on Russian-occupied Luhansk, which claimed 18 lives and injured over 40. Russia has formally accused Ukrainian forces and promised retaliatory measures, further complicating the regional security landscape.
*   **Security Incident at the White House:** A violent confrontation occurred near the White House, resulting in the death of a suspect who opened fire near a security checkpoint. A bystander was also reported wounded, prompting heightened security alerts in the U.S. capital.

## POLAND

*   **Security Breach at President’s Family Home:** A serious security incident occurred in Gdańsk, where emergency services forcefully entered the family home of President Karol Nawrocki following a series of false alarms concerning a fire and a medical emergency. Prime Minister Donald Tusk and the Minister of Interior have intervened, with a high-level government briefing scheduled to address the implications of this incident.
*   **Cinematic Success in Cannes:** Polish filmmaker Paweł Pawlikowski has been honored at the 79th Cannes Film Festival, receiving the award for Best Director for his latest feature, *Ojczyzna* (Homeland). The accolade, shared with Spanish directors Javier Calvo and Javier Ambrossi, marks a significant moment for Polish cinema on the international stage.
