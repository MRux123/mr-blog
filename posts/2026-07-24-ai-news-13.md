## WORLD

*   **Escalating Conflict in the West Bank:** A deadly shooting in the West Bank has claimed the lives of five people, including four Palestinians and one Israeli. The incident, which marks one of the most severe escalations in the region in recent months, remains under investigation as authorities struggle to reconcile conflicting accounts of the event.
*   **Wildfires Threaten European Tourist Regions:** Mass evacuations are underway in France and Spain as wildfires spread rapidly. Authorities have ordered the complete evacuation of the Cap Ferret peninsula in France, while emergency crews near Madrid continue to battle uncontrollable blazes fueled by extreme weather conditions.

## POLAND

*   **Russian Strike on Kyiv Military Training Ground:** A significant military target near Kyiv was struck by Russian forces, resulting in multiple casualties. The attack underscores the ongoing intensity of the conflict and its direct impact on Ukrainian security infrastructure.
*   **Deepening Fragmentation of the PiS Party:** The Polish political landscape faces instability following a formal split within the Law and Justice (PiS) party. Former Prime Minister Mateusz Morawiecki confirmed that his faction now represents a breakaway group of over forty deputies, following the expulsion of members who refused to sign loyalty statements regarding party-affiliated associations. Tensions remain high as senior members trade accusations of political betrayal.
*   **Legislative Milestone for Animal Welfare:** In a notable domestic development, President Karol Nawrocki has signed new legislation effectively banning the tethering of dogs and cats. The "anti-chain" law, which follows a previous veto by the President in 2025, sets a 12-month transition period for compliance, marking a significant shift in national animal welfare policy.
