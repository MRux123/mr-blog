## WORLD

*   **Coordinated Militant Offensive in Mali:** The West African nation of Mali is facing a severe security crisis following a massive, coordinated attack by the al-Qaida-linked group JNIM and the Azawad Liberation Front. Targets included the international airport in Bamako and four other strategic cities, marking one of the largest insurgent operations in the region in recent years.
*   **Trump Aborts Diplomatic Mission to Pakistan:** Plans for a high-level U.S. delegation, led by Steve Witkoff and Jared Kushner, to travel to Pakistan for discussions regarding the conflict in Iran have been canceled. The move follows Iran's previous denial of any intentions to engage in direct talks with U.S. representatives, signaling a continued diplomatic impasse in the region.

## POLAND

*   **Deadly Russian Assault on Dnipro:** A brutal 20-hour barrage of missiles and drones struck the Ukrainian city of Dnipro, resulting in at least eight fatalities and nearly 50 injuries. The scale of the destruction has prompted urgent calls from local authorities for an intensified international response to mitigate further civilian casualties.
*   **Ebbing Momentum for Karol Nawrocki:** A fresh poll conducted by United Surveys for Wirtualna Polska indicates a stagnation in public support for Karol Nawrocki. The data reveals a deeply polarized electorate, where the candidate's approval ratings are inextricably linked to the political affiliations of the respondents, suggesting an uphill battle in maintaining broader national momentum.
