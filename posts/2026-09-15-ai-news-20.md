## WORLD

* **Escalating US-Iran Conflict Costs and Military Strain:** A new Pentagon report reveals the staggering financial and strategic toll of the conflict with Iran. The first four months of hostilities have cost billions of dollars, leading to critical ammunition shortages and significant equipment losses, raising concerns about the long-term sustainability of current US military operations in the region.
* **Geopolitical Instability in the Strait of Hormuz:** The security of global energy routes remains precarious following an attack on an oil tanker in the Strait of Hormuz. While Iran claims the vessel struck sea mines, the US maintains that the tanker was targeted by a missile followed by a drone strike, an incident that has resulted in missing crew members and heightened tensions.
* **Houthi Weaponization and Strategic Autonomy:** Intelligence suggests that Yemen’s Houthi rebels are rapidly advancing their military capabilities through the integration of artificial intelligence. This shift towards self-reliance in advanced weapon manufacturing marks a significant evolution in regional proxy warfare, diminishing their traditional dependence on Iranian supply chains.

## POLAND

* **Financial Irregularities in Warsaw-Kyiv Metro Cooperation:** Polish and Ukrainian authorities have uncovered a major financial fraud involving subway carriages gifted by Warsaw to Kyiv. Investigations by the National Anti-Corruption Bureau of Ukraine (NABU) indicate that a criminal network embezzled over 11.5 million PLN, casting a shadow over the bilateral aid process.
* **Drone Incident at German Military Base:** Security concerns have rippled through the region following the discovery of drone fragments near a Luftwaffe base in Wunstorf, Germany. While the origin of the device remains under investigation, the incident has prompted heightened scrutiny of airspace security near critical European military infrastructure.
* **Diplomatic Deadlock and Funding Challenges:** Poland and Ukraine face potential fiscal hurdles as political maneuvering within the European Union complicates funding mechanisms. Recent reports suggest that a blockade, involving French interests, is threatening the continuity of financial support, further complicating the geopolitical landscape for both nations.
