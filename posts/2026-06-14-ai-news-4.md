## WORLD

*   **Climate-Driven Migration Restrictions:** A recent analysis by *The Guardian* indicates that the current U.S. administration’s immigration policies appear disproportionately targeted at nations most susceptible to environmental instability. Data suggests that of the 39 countries currently facing entry restrictions, a significant majority are on the front lines of climate-driven disasters, raising ethical questions regarding the intersection of fossil fuel advocacy and border policy.
*   **Medical Transparency Advocacy:** Renowned author Chimamanda Ngozi Adichie has publicly challenged a Lagos-based hospital, alleging that the institution is actively obstructing an inquest into the circumstances surrounding the death of her 21-month-old son. The case has ignited a broader debate regarding medical accountability and the rights of families in pursuing justice in Nigeria’s healthcare system.

## POLAND

*   **Historical World Cup Milestone:** Qatar achieved a significant result in the group stage of the World Cup in San Francisco, securing a 1-1 draw against Switzerland. The late-game equalizer, netted by Boualem Khoukhi in the fourth minute of stoppage time, marks the first point ever earned by the Qatari national team in a World Cup tournament following their qualification through the standard elimination process.
