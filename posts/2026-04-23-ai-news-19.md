## WORLD

*   **Renewed Conflict Fears in Tehran:** Despite a fragile ceasefire, tensions in the Middle East have flared again. Reports indicate air defenses were activated in Tehran following reports of explosions, prompting the White House to convene emergency talks with Israeli and Lebanese officials to prevent the collapse of wider peace negotiations.
*   **Massive EU Financial Aid for Ukraine:** In a signal that the European Union anticipates a prolonged conflict, a new $106 billion loan package has been announced. Unlike previous initiatives, the focus has shifted heavily toward sustained military funding, preparing Ukraine for a long-term strategic defense.
*   **Election Violence Crisis in Tanzania:** An official inquiry has confirmed the deaths of over 500 people during the recent election period. The findings have ignited a political firestorm, as opposition groups point to systemic failure and state violence, despite the inquiry's failure to explicitly assign responsibility.

## POLAND

*   **Tragic Death of MP Łukasz Litewka:** The Polish political scene is in mourning following the death of Nowa Lewica MP Łukasz Litewka, who was killed in a road accident. Authorities have confirmed the arrest of a 57-year-old driver involved in the collision, and an investigation is currently underway.
*   **Rising Security Concerns:** Tensions on the international stage are resonating in Warsaw, with recent Russian warnings directed at Poland, characterizing the country as a "strategic threat." The geopolitical atmosphere is further strained by economic pressures, as domestic airlines like PLL LOT are forced to adjust flight schedules due to soaring fuel costs linked to the instability in the Middle East.
