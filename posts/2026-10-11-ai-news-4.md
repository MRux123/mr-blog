## WORLD

* **Deadly Attack on Riyadh Airport:** A major security incident in Saudi Arabia has left twelve people dead and over 300 injured. The Saudi-led military coalition has formally characterized the assault on the capital's airport as a "war crime," heightening regional tensions and raising significant concerns regarding infrastructure security in the Middle East.
* **Human Rights Crisis in Myanmar:** Reports from the BBC indicate an escalating humanitarian crisis in Myanmar, where the ruling junta is resorting to the forced conscription of young men to bolster its ranks against ethnic militias and civilian resistance forces. The struggle, described as a brutal war of attrition, continues to threaten the stability of the region.

## POLAND

* **RCB Security Alerts Following Regional Escalation:** The Polish Government Security Center (RCB) issued emergency alerts to residents in the Podkarpackie and Lubelskie voivodeships following intense Russian military strikes against Ukraine. While authorities have confirmed that the immediate threat has subsided, the situation underscores the heightened state of alertness in Poland’s border regions.
* **Public Debate on Internet Oversight:** Former Prime Minister Leszek Miller has voiced significant concerns regarding a new government proposal aimed at blocking specific online content in response to rising violence in schools. The proposal has sparked a wider national debate regarding the balance between public safety initiatives and the risks of potential government censorship without judicial oversight.
