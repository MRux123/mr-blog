## WORLD

* **Iran-US Tensions in the Strait of Hormuz:** Despite Tehran’s declaration that the Strait of Hormuz is "open," the U.S. blockade remains in effect, signaling a continued escalation in the region. In response to the instability following the conclusion of the American-Iranian conflict, France and the United Kingdom have announced plans to lead an international coalition to secure commercial shipping lanes in the area.
* **Proposals for a New European Defense Alliance:** EU Defense Commissioner Andrius Kubilius has unveiled plans for a "European Defense Union." This new structure is designed to operate beyond current EU legal frameworks, focusing on enhanced military cooperation with key partners, specifically Ukraine, the United Kingdom, and Norway, to bolster regional security against Russian threats.

## POLAND

* **Growing Political Polarization and Governance Strains:** Recent polling from CBOS indicates a widening gap between the ruling Civic Coalition (KO) and the opposition Law and Justice (PiS) party. Meanwhile, internal government tensions have surfaced, with PSL MP Marek Sawicki publicly warning Prime Minister Donald Tusk that the current treatment of smaller coalition partners threatens the government's future electoral viability.
* **Military Concerns on the Eastern Border:** President Volodymyr Zelensky has alerted international observers to increased military activity in Belarus near the Ukrainian border. Intelligence reports suggest the construction of new roads toward the border and the establishment of artillery positions, raising concerns about potential Russian troop regrouping and the expansion of the conflict theater.
