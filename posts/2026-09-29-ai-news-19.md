## WORLD

*   **Renewed Fears of Civil War in Ethiopia:** The security situation in Ethiopia is deteriorating rapidly as Tigrayan rebels form a new coalition aimed at toppling the administration of Prime Minister Abiy Ahmed. Reports of offensive movements into the Afar and Amhara regions have revived memories of the brutal 2020–2022 conflict, raising alarms among international observers regarding the stability of the Horn of Africa.
*   **Humanitarian Crisis Deepens in Myanmar:** At least 50 civilians were killed following a military airstrike on a crowded market within rebel-held territory. This incident marks one of the most lethal assaults on non-combatants in recent months, further intensifying the protracted violence between the military junta and various opposition forces.
*   **West Bank Tensions:** Escalating violence in the West Bank continues to draw global scrutiny, as Israeli settlers reportedly blocked a Palestinian family from returning to their home despite a Supreme Court ruling. This confrontation underscores the ongoing breakdown in the rule of law and the increasing influence of extremist groups on regional security dynamics.

## POLAND

*   **US Military Posture in Poland:** The Ministry of National Defense is currently evaluating the strategic possibility of a permanent American military base in Poland. High-ranking Pentagon officials have addressed the matter, signaling that discussions regarding the long-term US presence on NATO’s eastern flank remain a core priority for bilateral security cooperation.
*   **Defense Reform:** The Polish military is undergoing a period of structural adjustment, with reports indicating that sweeping changes at the command level are imminent. The restructuring, initiated by the Pentagon’s latest policy directives, is expected to focus on streamlining administrative functions and reducing the size of the general officer corps to increase operational efficiency.
