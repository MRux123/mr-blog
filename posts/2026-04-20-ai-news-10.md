## WORLD

*   **Heightened Tensions in the Middle East:** The geopolitical landscape remains volatile as Iran officially withdraws from peace negotiations with the United States. Following a U.S. strike on an Iranian cargo vessel and subsequent regional unrest, the conflict is increasingly destabilizing energy security, with Germany calling a National Security Council meeting to address the threat of aviation fuel shortages across Europe.
*   **Seismic Crisis in Japan:** A powerful 7.5 magnitude earthquake struck off the northeastern coast of Japan. Authorities have issued immediate tsunami warnings, prompting widespread evacuation orders as the region braces for potential infrastructure damage and humanitarian impact.

## POLAND

*   **Political and Criminal Investigation into Zondacrypto:** The Regional Prosecutor's Office in Katowice has launched a high-profile investigation into the Zondacrypto exchange. The probe, involving allegations of fraud and money laundering, is now expanding to examine potential links between the platform’s operations and the political sphere.
*   **National Infrastructure Vulnerabilities:** Authorities are addressing a significant, ongoing failure of the Polcard payment system, which has disrupted retail operations nationwide. Simultaneously, the country is monitoring reports of Russian "electronic fog" affecting neighboring Norway, raising concerns regarding electronic warfare threats in the Baltic region.
