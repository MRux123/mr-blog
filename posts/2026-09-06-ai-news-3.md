## WORLD

* **Diplomatic Thaw Efforts:** US envoys Steve Witkoff and Jared Kushner have concluded high-level talks in Moscow and are now headed to Ukraine. The mission aims to de-escalate tensions and explore potential pathways for negotiation, with both sides signaling an interest in productive dialogue following the Russian leg of the visit.
* **Miracle Survival in Nepal:** In a remarkable turn of events, a 64-year-old Nepali woman, Chandika Shrestha, was rescued alive after being trapped for over ten days in her home, which had been buried by a mudslide. She has been airlifted to a hospital, marking a rare success in ongoing recovery efforts following the natural disaster.
* **Capital Punishment Ruling in Egypt:** An Egyptian court has sentenced 11 individuals, including television presenter Sarah Khalifa, to death in connection with a major drug trafficking case. The verdict has sparked international discourse regarding judicial transparency and the severity of sentencing in high-profile criminal cases within the country.
