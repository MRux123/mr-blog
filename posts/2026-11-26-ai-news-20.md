## WORLD

* **Rising Geopolitical Instability and Survivalism:** Across Europe, governments are increasingly urging citizens to prepare for emergencies, ranging from cyberattacks to potential kinetic conflicts. This shift reflects a growing concern over the fragility of infrastructure and national security in an era of heightened geopolitical tensions.

## POLAND

* **Judicial and Political Protest Maneuvers:** A court has intervened in the ongoing dispute regarding protests before the Constitutional Tribunal, overriding the Warsaw city administration’s decision to limit the duration of the demonstration. This highlights the deepening legal and political tensions surrounding the tribunal.
* **Refining National Crisis Preparedness:** Amidst growing concern over regional security, the III Mazovian Local Government Forum has prioritized the development of civil defense and crisis management plans. Officials are focusing on decentralizing security strategies to ensure that municipalities are equipped to handle threats ranging from natural disasters to military contingencies.
* **Ongoing Investigations into Campaign Finance:** Political discourse remains dominated by questions regarding the transparency of campaign financing within the ruling coalition, with senior figures like Grzegorz Schetyna publicly calling for clarification from implicated parties.
