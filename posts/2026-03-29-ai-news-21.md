## WORLD

* **Middle East Conflict Escalates:** The situation in the Middle East has reached a critical juncture as Israel launches a wave of strikes against infrastructure in Tehran and other Iranian territories. In response, Iran has retaliated, leading to a significant expansion of the conflict. Reports from Bloomberg confirm that the U.S. has suffered a major strategic blow, with the loss of a Boeing E-3 Sentry AWACS aircraft during an Iranian strike on a base in Saudi Arabia, amid allegations of Russian involvement in facilitating the attack.
* **Geopolitical Maritime Standoff:** A Russian oil tanker currently under U.S. sanctions is defying an American blockade, heading toward Cuba to alleviate the island's severe energy crisis. This move represents a direct challenge to U.S. regional influence and further strains international relations.

## POLAND

* **Security Risks Regarding AfD:** Diplomatic sources and German parliamentarians have raised alarms regarding the potential for sensitive EU documentation to be leaked to Russia or China via the German right-wing party, AfD. There are mounting fears that the party’s ties could undermine the security of confidential European Union deliberations.
* **Labor Exploitation Investigations:** Recent reports from *Polsat News Ujawnia* have uncovered a disturbing trend of systemic abuse and modern slavery practices involving foreign workers in Poland. Investigations revealed accounts of physical violence, extreme working hours, and inhumane living conditions, highlighting the dark side of the current labor market boom.
* **Political Alliances and Hungary:** Jarosław Kaczyński, leader of the Law and Justice (PiS) party, has publicly backed Viktor Orbán's reelection bid in Hungary. Kaczyński emphasized that a victory for the Hungarian Prime Minister would be a crucial element in a broader, projected revival of right-wing influence across Europe, framing it as a necessary shift in the continent's political direction.
