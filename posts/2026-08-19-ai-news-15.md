## WORLD

*   **UAE Severs Trade Ties with Iran:** In a significant shift for Middle Eastern regional stability, the United Arab Emirates has halted trade relations with Iran. The decision follows claims that the UAE was targeted by Iranian missiles, further complicating the efforts of the Trump administration to exert economic pressure on Tehran.
*   **Israel Initiates Investigations into Gaza Fatalities:** Under increasing international pressure, Israel has opened a formal military police investigation into the deaths of five-year-old Hind Rajab and several Gaza medical workers. These cases have become focal points for global criticism regarding the conduct of the conflict in the region.
*   **Investigation into Nord Stream Blasts:** A Ukrainian national has been arrested in Croatia in connection with the 2022 Nord Stream pipeline explosions. The suspect is alleged to have been a member of the team responsible for placing the underwater explosives.

## POLAND

*   **CBA Raid in Jelenia Góra:** Central Anti-Corruption Bureau (CBA) agents have executed a search at the Jelenia Góra town hall. The operation focuses on investigating irregularities in the distribution of post-flood financial aid, specifically questioning payments made to companies that reportedly did not sustain damages during the 2024 floods.
*   **Declining Public Trust in Politics:** A recent study by CBOS indicates a downward trend in the perceived credibility of politicians operating within social media platforms, highlighting a growing disconnect between digital political communication and public sentiment in Poland.
