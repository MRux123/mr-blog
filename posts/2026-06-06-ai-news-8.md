## WORLD

*   **Heightened Military Tensions in the Persian Gulf:** The geopolitical situation has deteriorated significantly as the United States and Iran exchange direct strikes. Following U.S. military operations targeting Iranian drone and radar installations, Tehran retaliated by hitting U.S. bases in Kuwait and Bahrain, marking a critical escalation in the region.
*   **Rising Threat of Ebola Outbreak:** Public health officials have issued an urgent warning regarding the spread of Ebola in Central Africa. CDC models suggest the current outbreak is on a trajectory that could rival the 2014-2016 West African crisis, with projections indicating a potential scale of 10,000 to 20,000 cases.
*   **Climate Crisis and Labor Stability:** In India, record-breaking heat waves are forcing essential workers into a precarious position, creating an acute conflict between personal health and economic survival as temperatures continue to soar.

## POLAND

*   **Diplomatic Repairs Following UPA Controversy:** Defense Minister Władysław Kosiniak-Kamysz is set to meet with Kyrylo Budanov, Head of the Office of the President of Ukraine, in an effort to de-escalate bilateral tensions. The friction stems from the recent decision by Ukrainian authorities to name a military unit after the "Heroes of the UPA," a move that has caused significant political distress in Warsaw.
*   **Defense Cooperation Uncertainty:** The security landscape remains volatile following a policy shift regarding the deployment of 5,000 U.S. troops to Poland. Confusion persists following recent statements attributed to Donald Trump, raising questions about the future of established defense agreements.
*   **Public Perception of the First Lady:** A recent SW Research poll for "Wprost" indicates that nearly 50% of Poles hold a favorable view of First Lady Marta Nawrocka one year into her tenure, with particularly high support among younger demographics and rural populations, although a notable portion of the electorate remains undecided.
