## WORLD

* **UK Reaffirms Ukraine Support Amid Russian Accusations:** Prime Minister Burnham has pledged continued support for Kyiv, dismissing Russian claims that supplying British-made drones constitutes an escalation. The statement comes as tensions rise regarding Western involvement in the ongoing conflict.
* **Economic Fragility in the West Bank:** Intensifying financial restrictions imposed by Israel have severely crippled the West Bank economy. International observers are increasingly concerned that these policies may render the Palestinian Authority insolvent, exacerbating the humanitarian and administrative crisis in the territory.

## POLAND

* **Premier Tusk Condemns Attack on Ukrainian Children:** Prime Minister Donald Tusk has issued a stern warning to the perpetrators of an assault on two Ukrainian children in Bydgoszcz. The government has labeled the act as "anti-Polish," urging suspects to surrender to police immediately as investigations intensify.
* **Military Aviation Activity Increases:** There has been a marked rise in Polish military aircraft operations across national airspace. While official communications remain limited, the activity follows a period of heightened regional volatility and weather-related disruptions impacting infrastructure.
* **Passing of Warsaw Uprising Heroine:** The nation mourns the death of Krystyna "Stokrotka" Kodymowska, a 98-year-old veteran of the Warsaw Uprising. Kodymowska, who served as a courier and medic for the Home Army, was a prominent symbol of the fight for Polish freedom and autonomy.
