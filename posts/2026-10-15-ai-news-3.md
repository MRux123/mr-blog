## WORLD

* **Zelenskyy Denounces Trump-Putin Deal:** President Volodymyr Zelenskyy has expressed a sense of betrayal following reports of an agreement between US President Donald Trump and Russian leader Vladimir Putin. During a visit to the UK, Zelenskyy stated that the US administration has "chosen the wrong side," signaling a major shift in the geopolitical landscape regarding the ongoing conflict in Ukraine.
* **Musk's Starlink Under Scrutiny:** Concerns regarding US government influence on communication infrastructure have surfaced as Elon Musk suggested that officials may be intentionally slowing down Starlink speeds for Ukrainian military drones. While access has improved, persistent speed caps continue to hinder critical battlefield operations.
* **Controversial Vaccine Trial in Africa:** A research trial involving the withholding of hepatitis B vaccines from newborns in Guinea-Bissau has been greenlit by an ethics committee. The project, which has been associated with US Health Secretary Robert F. Kennedy Jr., has sparked an international debate regarding medical ethics and the oversight of humanitarian health initiatives.

## POLAND

* **Political Unrest at the Constitutional Tribunal:** Tensions surrounding the Constitutional Tribunal have escalated into physical confrontations. Three individuals were detained during protests in front of the Tribunal building, prompting Interior Minister Marcin Kierwiński to announce that state protection (SOP) has been granted to two judges amid rising security concerns.
* **Shift in Defense Procurement:** In a notable pivot in foreign policy and military logistics, Canada has moved to distance itself from US defense suppliers, signaling a deepening friction in Ottawa-Washington relations that could have long-term implications for regional defense cooperation.
