## WORLD

* **Catastrophic Wildfires in Western Europe:** France and Spain are currently battling unprecedented wildfires, resulting in the evacuation of over 340,000 residents. The crisis is compounded by extreme heat waves, forcing authorities to halt critical infrastructure, including rail transport south of Bordeaux. International aid is mobilizing, with Poland among those deploying specialized firefighting units to assist Spain.
* **Terrorist Attack at Berlin Pride:** German authorities have launched a massive manhunt for a 21-year-old suspect following a fatal vehicle-ramming attack at a Pride event in Berlin. The incident is being treated by law enforcement as a potential act of terrorism linked to radicalized groups.
* **Geopolitical Tensions and Global Hunger:** The U.S.-Iran conflict has entered a fragile, second-day pause due to reported logistics constraints regarding missile stockpiles. Meanwhile, a sobering UN report highlights a systemic global crisis, revealing that over 2.69 billion people—one in three individuals worldwide—cannot afford a healthy, nutritious diet.

## POLAND

* **Legal Controversy Over Border Security:** The Warsaw District Prosecutor's Office has filed an appeal regarding the acquittal of a 25-year-old soldier who discharged 12 shots from his service weapon at the Belarusian border. The case, which drew national attention, centers on the balance between border security protocols and the rules of engagement in the presence of migrants.
* **Internal Political Turbulence:** Tensions within the Law and Justice (PiS) party have surfaced following speculation regarding a potential splinter movement led by Mateusz Morawiecki. The situation reflects growing instability within the opposition, as internal party figures exchange accusations regarding the future direction of the political group.
* **Shifting Political Landscape:** New polling data suggests a complex outlook for the 2027 electoral cycle. While questions persist regarding the durability of the current ruling coalition, public opinion on potential leadership candidates—such as Radosław Sikorski versus incumbent Donald Tusk—signals an evolving debate within the Civic Coalition (KO) regarding their future governance strategy.
