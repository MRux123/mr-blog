## WORLD

*   **NATO and the Trump Factor:** As NATO Secretary General Mark Rutte prepares for a critical visit to Washington, the alliance faces mounting pressure to maintain unity under the unpredictable administration of Donald Trump. Rutte's diplomatic approach is currently being tested as he attempts to balance European security concerns with the evolving geopolitical expectations of the U.S. presidency.
*   **Escalation in the Strait of Hormuz:** Regional stability is further strained by Iran’s recent maneuvers to assert control over the Strait of Hormuz. Reports indicate that Tehran is moving beyond merely weaponizing the waterway through disruption, now seeking to impose transit fees on commercial vessels, a move that experts warn could significantly impact global trade and maritime security.
*   **Extreme Heatwave Grips Europe:** A severe, persistent heatwave continues to impact much of Europe. Authorities in France, the UK, Italy, and Spain have issued emergency alerts, with schools closing and local governments struggling to manage the public health risks associated with the record-breaking temperatures.

## POLAND

*   **Diplomatic Uncertainty Regarding Zelensky's Visit:** Conflicting reports have emerged regarding President Volodymyr Zelensky's attendance at the upcoming Reconstruction of Ukraine Conference in Gdańsk. While some government sources initially suggested his attendance was unchanged, recent reports from Polsat News indicate that Deputy Prime Minister Yulia Svyrydenko will likely represent Ukraine instead, signaling a potential shift in high-level diplomatic planning.
*   **Mediation Proposed in Presidential Dispute:** Lithuania has offered to mediate in the escalating political friction between Polish President Karol Nawrocki and Ukrainian President Volodymyr Zelensky. Lithuanian diplomatic officials characterized the current standoff as an "emotional" impasse, suggesting that third-party intervention may be necessary to restore stable bilateral communications.
*   **Stricter Penalties for Traffic Offenders:** Poland is implementing significant changes to its road safety regulations. Starting Wednesday, June 24, individuals participating in mandatory re-education courses—specifically those penalized for driving under the influence of alcohol or drugs—will see their course fees increase fivefold, from 500 PLN to 2,500 PLN, as part of a broader effort to deter dangerous driving.
