## WORLD

*   **Escalating Hostilities in Ukraine:** Russia has launched a significant wave of overnight air strikes targeting the Kyiv and Kharkiv regions, resulting in multiple civilian casualties and extensive damage to infrastructure. The attacks have triggered high-level security responses in neighboring countries.
*   **Political Violence in Australia:** Concerns over political stability have intensified following a deliberate vehicle-ramming attack on independent lawmaker Helen Haines. The incident has prompted urgent national calls to de-escalate political rhetoric.
*   **Space Exploration Milestone:** A groundbreaking mission has launched with the ambitious goal of retrieving lunar dust samples from one of Mars' moons, marking a significant step forward in interplanetary research.

## POLAND

*   **National Airspace Protection:** In response to the Russian aerial offensive against Ukraine, the Polish Armed Forces have scrambled fighter jets to patrol the skies. The Government Security Centre (RCB) has issued alerts to residents in the Lublin and Podkarpackie regions regarding increased military activity near the border.
*   **Major Security Operation in Wrocław:** Authorities have initiated the evacuation of approximately 3,000 residents in Wrocław to facilitate the safe removal of a 250kg unexploded World War II aerial bomb discovered at a local construction site.
*   **Diplomatic and Political Shifts:** Amidst the regional crisis, domestic political discourse has intensified, with government officials addressing concerns regarding the efficacy of the ruling coalition’s promises in light of recent polling trends.
