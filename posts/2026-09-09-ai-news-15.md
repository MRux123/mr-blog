## WORLD

* **Escalating Tensions in Germany:** Chancellor Merz has launched a sharp critique of the AfD, accusing the party’s migration policy of targeting skilled labor in a manner he equated to "ethnic cleansing." This verbal escalation follows significant electoral gains by the far-right party, signaling a deepening political divide in Europe's largest economy.
* **Nuclear Proliferation Concerns:** The International Atomic Energy Agency (IAEA) has raised alarms regarding a newly identified two-storey uranium enrichment facility in North Korea. International monitors describe the development as a matter of "serious concern" regarding global non-proliferation efforts.
* **Escalation in Russia-Ukraine Conflict:** The conflict has intensified with reciprocal strikes, including a fatal incident at the Moldova border and a targeted strike in Novorossiysk, which reportedly resulted in civilian casualties.

## POLAND

* **Nationwide Police Raids:** Law enforcement authorities have launched a series of coordinated searches across Poland targeting the "Gromda Fight Club" federation. The operation, focused on content disseminated online, has drawn public comment from high-level officials, including Minister of Justice Waldemar Żurek.
* **Military Integration with NATO:** Polish F-16s participated in joint reconnaissance flights over the Black Sea alongside French and Belgian air forces. Minister of National Defence Władysław Kosiniak-Kamysz confirmed the mission, emphasizing the focus on enhancing procedural cooperation and multi-national operational capabilities.
* **Security Incident at Poznań Railway Station:** A 23-year-old individual was apprehended after a security scare at the Poznań main railway station. The suspect made bomb threats demanding transport to Georgia, leading to a temporary evacuation of the site; no explosives were found, and the suspect now faces up to eight years in prison.
