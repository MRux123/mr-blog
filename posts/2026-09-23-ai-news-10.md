## WORLD

* **US-Iran Diplomatic Re-engagement:** Following recent threats of "annihilation" from the Trump administration, US and Iranian officials have held their first formal talks since June. This diplomatic contact marks a significant development in a volatile region, aiming to address the collapse of the ceasefire and the intermittent exchange of fire between the two nations.
* **US Reconstruction Plan for Gaza:** The Trump-appointed Peace Council has unveiled a $2.45 billion proposal aimed at the comprehensive reconstruction of the Gaza Strip. The ambitious plan focuses on clearing debris, building essential shelters, and restoring healthcare and administrative infrastructure, though its execution remains heavily dependent on the cooperation of regional stakeholders.
* **Surge in Gender-Based Violence in Gaza:** Reports from the UN Population Fund highlight a distressing escalation in violence against women in Gaza, alongside a significant rise in early and forced marriages, underscoring the severe humanitarian consequences of the ongoing conflict.

## POLAND

* **Political Debate Over 'Fort Trump':** Prime Minister Donald Tusk has distanced himself from claims regarding the establishment of a "Fort Trump" US military base in Poland. Tusk described the ongoing public debate as an "unserious" bidding war, following assertions from presidential candidate Karol Nawrocki that the base's creation is already a settled matter.
* **Judicial Ruling in Kłobuck:** In a significant legal development, a court has increased the sentence for the perpetrator involved in the murder of a parish priest on a rectory in Kłobuck, signaling a firm judicial stance on the crime.
* **Kraków Mayoral Race Realignment:** The upcoming mayoral election in Kraków has seen a notable shift as candidate Jan Hoffman officially withdrew from the race. Hoffman, a key figure in the local referendum movement, has pledged his support to Łukasz Gibała, who has subsequently adopted several of Hoffman’s campaign objectives.
