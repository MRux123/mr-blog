## WORLD

*   **Taliban’s Governance in Afghanistan:** Five years after their return to power, the Taliban continues to fundamentally reshape Afghan society. Recent reporting highlights how the group enforces strict control over personal lives, including marital laws and the integration of smartphone surveillance, marking a deepening of their authoritarian grip on the nation.
*   **Safety Concerns in Food Production:** China, the world's leading producer of cabbages, has launched an official investigation into the use of formaldehyde to preserve fresh produce. The use of this carcinogenic substance in the food supply chain poses a significant public health risk and highlights critical gaps in food safety enforcement.

## POLAND

*   **Tragedy at a Waste Disposal Site:** At least 30 people have lost their lives following a catastrophic waste avalanche. The scale of the disaster, which has left families devastated and communities in mourning, has prompted an urgent examination of safety protocols and landfill management in the region.
*   **Public Health Alert:** Pediatricians have issued a formal warning to parents regarding the dangers of circulating health advice. The medical community is urging caregivers to rely on verified professional guidance, as unverified tips currently spreading may pose a direct threat to children's health.
