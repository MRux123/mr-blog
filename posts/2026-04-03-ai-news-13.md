## WORLD

* **Conflict Escalation in the Middle East:** Following the onset of military actions by the United States and Israel against Iran, the regional geopolitical landscape has shifted dramatically. Reports indicate that the conflict is already driving secondary economic interests, with private firms linked to high-profile figures promoting military hardware to Gulf states amidst a volatile security environment. 
* **Democratic Backsliding in Burkina Faso:** Military ruler Ibrahim Traoré has formally declared that democracy is "not for" Burkina Faso. Since his 2022 coup, Traoré has systematically consolidated power, stifling political opposition and banning political parties, signaling a definitive turn away from democratic governance in the region.

## POLAND

* **Infrastructure Strain in Świnoujście:** The city of Świnoujście is facing significant traffic paralysis due to a surge in cross-border "fuel tourism." Drivers from the German region of Mecklenburg-Western Pomerania are flocking to Polish stations in anticipation of potential fuel supply shortages, leading to major congestion and necessitating emergency logistics solutions.
* **Public Health Alert:** The Chief Sanitary Inspectorate (GIS) has issued a critical warning regarding the contamination of popular food products. Laboratory tests confirmed the presence of *Listeria monocytogenes* in Ustrzycka Roulade and *Salmonella* in frozen raspberries, prompting an urgent recall to protect vulnerable populations, including pregnant women and individuals with compromised immune systems.
* **Mountain Rescue Operation:** A high-risk evacuation was successfully concluded in the Tatra Mountains, where five tourists had been stranded at the Five Polish Ponds valley shelter. The operation was conducted via helicopter under extreme conditions, as the region remains under a fourth-degree avalanche threat.
