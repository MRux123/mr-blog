## WORLD

* **European Wildfire Emergency:** A catastrophic wildfire season continues to grip France and Spain, with over 330,000 evacuations reported. Emergency services are struggling to contain rapidly spreading blazes fueled by extreme heat waves and strong winds, prompting warnings of worsening conditions as new heat fronts approach.
* **U.S.-Iran Standoff:** Military operations between the United States and Iran have entered a temporary lull for the second consecutive day. Reports suggest that the U.S. administration has paused escalation efforts, partly due to concerns over depleted interceptor missile stockpiles.
* **Middle East Unrest:** Tensions in the West Bank have surged following reports of Israeli settlers attacking Palestinian villages, resulting in widespread property damage including the destruction of mosques and farmland. The violence follows a significant recent clash that resulted in multiple fatalities.

## POLAND

* **Severe Weather Alerts:** The Institute of Meteorology and Water Management (IMGW) has issued urgent warnings for Poland as a dangerous transition from extreme heat to violent storms approaches. The incoming front is expected to bring heavy rainfall, strong winds, and hail across eight voivodeships.
* **Military Oversight Audit:** The Commander-in-Chief of the Armed Forces of Ukraine, Mykhailo Drapaty, has initiated a comprehensive audit of military units. The move is designed to assess combat readiness and root out corruption, with officials emphasizing that no personnel will be exempt from potential accountability.
* **Tragedy in the Caucasus:** A high-altitude mountaineering accident in Russia has resulted in the deaths of five climbers attempting to scale the country’s highest peak. Rescue operations are currently focusing on the aftermath of the incident.
