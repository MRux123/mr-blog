## WORLD

*   **Zelensky Cautious on Trump’s "Energy Ceasefire":** Ukrainian President Volodymyr Zelensky has expressed conditional support for Donald Trump’s proposal for an "energy ceasefire." Despite this diplomatic opening, the Ukrainian leadership remains skeptical, citing persistent Russian strikes on critical power infrastructure that continue unabated.
*   **Taliban Leveraging Migration Concerns:** The Taliban government is reportedly exploiting European anxieties regarding migration. Several European nations, despite official stances condemning the Taliban's human rights record, are increasingly engaging with the regime to facilitate the deportation of Afghan refugees back to Afghanistan.
*   **Expansion of Extremist Influence:** Emirati investigators have linked the recent attack on a FlyDubai flight to radicalization occurring within specific Australian religious circles. The incident has intensified scrutiny over the influence of certain extremist groups on foreign students studying in Australia.

## POLAND

*   **Plot Against Prime Minister Thwarted:** Authorities have detained a 66-year-old suspect accused of planning an assassination attempt against Polish Prime Minister Donald Tusk. The suspect is currently being transferred to a detention center in Warsaw, with further details regarding the investigation expected to be released by the prosecutor's office shortly.
*   **Pegasus Surveillance Report Published:** The parliamentary investigative committee on the Pegasus spyware has released its findings, painting a damning picture of state power overreach. The report alleges that the surveillance tool was systematically abused by state agencies to target political opponents, judges, and prosecutors rather than focusing on legitimate criminal investigations.
*   **Economic Nobel Laureates Announced:** The Royal Swedish Academy of Sciences has awarded the Nobel Prize in Economics to Robert E. Hall and Michael Woodford. The duo was honored for their pioneering work on the microeconomic foundations of macroeconomic stabilization, contributing significantly to modern economic theory.
