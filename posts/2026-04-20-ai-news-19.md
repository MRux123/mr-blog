## WORLD

*   **Fico’s Controversial Moscow Trip:** Tensions between Slovakia and Poland have intensified after Prime Minister Robert Fico requested permission to fly through Polish airspace en route to Moscow for the May 9th Victory Day celebrations. Polish officials have signaled strong reluctance to facilitate this journey, framing it as an incompatible diplomatic move given the current geopolitical climate.
*   **The Rise of Autonomous Warfare:** Defense analysts are warning of a "terrifying" new phase in the war in Ukraine. Experts suggest that the mass deployment of combat robots and drones has fundamentally altered the nature of the conflict, potentially creating a precedent where autonomous systems replace human soldiers, thus shifting the strategic balance of power in ways that may have unpredictable long-term consequences for global security.

## POLAND

*   **Diplomatic Standoff with France:** A notable breach in protocol has surfaced regarding French President Emmanuel Macron’s recent visit to Poland. It has been confirmed that no bilateral meeting took place between Macron and his Polish counterpart, Karol Nawrocki, a decision attributed to the Élysée Palace that reflects current friction or shifting priorities in Franco-Polish relations.
*   **Humanitarian Resilience:** Despite economic and political pressures, a new "Humanitarian Barometer" report indicates that 85% of Poles engaged in various forms of social or charitable aid over the past year. This data suggests a high level of societal cohesion and a sustained commitment to humanitarian efforts, reinforcing Poland's role as an active participant in regional and global civil society initiatives.
