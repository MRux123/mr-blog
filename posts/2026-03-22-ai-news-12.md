## WORLD

* **Iran-Israel Conflict Intensifies:** President Trump has issued a severe ultimatum to Iran, threatening to target its power infrastructure unless the Strait of Hormuz is reopened. The escalation follows Iranian missile strikes on Israeli cities, including an attack near a critical nuclear research facility, marking a dangerous new phase in Middle Eastern hostilities.
* **Humanitarian Crisis in Sudan:** The Sudanese army has faced international condemnation following a deadly attack on a hospital that resulted in 64 deaths, including 13 children and medical staff. The incident, which occurred during the Eid holiday, has drawn sharp criticism from the WHO.
* **Tragic Helicopter Crash in Qatar:** A military helicopter crash in Qatari waters has left seven people dead, including three Turkish nationals. Authorities report that the incident was caused by a technical failure.

## POLAND

* **Tusk Alleges Leaks to Moscow:** Prime Minister Donald Tusk has publicly addressed concerns regarding potential security breaches within the EU Council. Responding to reports from *The Washington Post* about secret communications between Hungarian and Russian officials, Tusk suggested that long-standing suspicions regarding information sharing between Budapest and Moscow are being validated.
* **Speculation on Political Alignments:** Grzegorz Schetyna (KO) has sparked debate regarding future coalition possibilities, stating that he does not rule out potential cooperation with the right-wing Konfederacja party in future parliamentary terms, emphasizing that "everything is possible" in the current political landscape.
* **Aviation Incident on A1 Motorway:** A light aircraft made an emergency landing on the A1 motorway on Saturday. While the landing caused significant traffic disruptions for motorists traveling toward Łódź, authorities confirmed that both pilots survived the event without injuries.
