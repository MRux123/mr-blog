## WORLD

* **Deep-Strike Operations in Russia:** In a significant escalation of drone warfare, Ukrainian forces successfully targeted a Lukoil fuel facility in Perm, located approximately 1,550 km from the front lines. This operation challenges previous Russian assumptions regarding the security of the Ural region and suggests a growing capability of Ukrainian unmanned aerial systems to project power deep into Russian territory.
* **Health Tech Controversy in Kenya:** Investigations into Kenya's newly implemented AI-driven healthcare reform have revealed systemic flaws. Intended to provide equitable access as promised by President William Ruto, the algorithm has paradoxically increased costs for the poorest citizens, favoring wealthier demographics and sparking public concern over the rollout of state-sponsored automated systems.
* **International Hantavirus Alert:** Authorities are currently racing to trace passengers who disembarked from the *MV Hondius* cruise ship at St. Helena. The operation follows the death of a 69-year-old passenger in South Africa, prompting fears of a broader health crisis linked to the vessel, which is reportedly captained by a Polish national.

## POLAND

* **Shifting Political Landscape:** A recent CBOS poll indicates that the Konfederacja party is rapidly closing the gap with the ruling Law and Justice (PiS) party. The survey suggests that while the Civic Coalition (KO) maintains a lead, the potential loss of a key coalition partner could significantly alter the governing majority if an election were held today.
* **Legal Scrutiny of University Incident:** The case of Mieszko R., accused of the murder of a Warsaw University employee, continues to generate intense public and legal debate. The victim's family has formally challenged psychiatric findings that deemed the perpetrator legally unaccountable, setting the stage for a critical court review in late August.
* **Liability Concerns for Former MP:** The legal troubles of former PiS MP Łukasz Mejza have intensified following a formal request to revoke his parliamentary immunity. Local authorities in Turek are pursuing charges related to a traffic violation involving the use of a mobile phone while driving, potentially creating further political liability for the former official.
