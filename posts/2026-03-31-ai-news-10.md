## WORLD

*   **Russian Digital Isolation:** President Vladimir Putin has intensified efforts to decouple Russia from the global internet. New outages and state-mandated blockages mark a significant escalation in the Kremlin's strategy to exert total control over communication channels, effectively isolating the Russian populace from the global information space.
*   **European Security Dilemma:** European leaders are struggling with a "guns vs. butter" crisis. Growing instability in Iran and rising geopolitical tensions are forcing a pivot toward military spending, threatening to strain national budgets and potentially provoking voter discontent as domestic programs face funding cuts.
*   **Rising Tensions in the Taiwan Strait:** U.S. lawmakers are applying pressure on Taiwan to expedite a $40 billion military budget. The initiative, urged by a visiting delegation of senators, underscores Washington's deepening anxiety regarding the threat posed by China in the Indo-Pacific region.

## POLAND

*   **Nuclear Energy Advancement:** Polish authorities have officially filed an application to the National Atomic Energy Agency for the construction of the country's first nuclear power plant in Choczewo. This marks a critical milestone in Poland's long-term energy transition strategy.
*   **Geopolitical Defense Pressures:** Reports indicate that the United States has informally suggested that Poland contribute a portion of its Patriot missile defense assets—including batteries and PAC-3 MSE interceptors—to support operations in the Middle East, sparking domestic debate over security readiness.
*   **Border Security and Visa Fraud:** Polish border services have dismantled a major human smuggling operation involving a foreign-led entity that facilitated the illegal entry of hundreds of Belarusian nationals through fraudulent employment documentation, highlighting ongoing challenges in managing regional migration flows.
