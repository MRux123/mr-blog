## WORLD

* **High-Stakes Negotiations in Budapest:** An EU delegation has arrived in Hungary to hold critical talks with the government of Péter Magyar. The discussions center on the release of €17 billion in frozen funds. The capital infusion is contingent upon Hungary meeting 27 specific "super milestones" outlined in their Recovery and Resilience Plan, marking a pivotal moment for EU-Hungarian relations.
* **Escalating US-Iran Diplomatic Friction:** A growing diplomatic rift between Washington and Tehran has intensified following conflicting reports regarding upcoming negotiations in Islamabad. While President Donald Trump has publicly discussed the scope of nuclear talks and the role of his administration, Iranian President Masud Pezeszkian has openly challenged the legitimacy of the US demands, questioning the basis of Trump's pressure regarding Iran’s nuclear program.

## POLAND

* **Public Health Crisis Alert:** A major scandal has emerged in Austria involving the discovery of rat poison in baby food products. The incident has triggered international concern, leading to the urgent withdrawal of affected batches from retail shelves across several countries, including reports currently circulating in Polish media.
* **Media Oversight Controversy:** The newly established "Council of New Media" is facing immediate internal pressure. Sources indicate that the position of popular YouTuber Paweł Swinarski is under review following allegations of his involvement in spreading Russian disinformation. The council, appointed by President Karol Nawrocki, now faces its first significant test regarding its integrity and selection criteria.
