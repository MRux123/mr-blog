## WORLD

*   **US Military Stance on Iran:** President Donald Trump has signaled a significant escalation in tensions with Iran, announcing in a primetime address that the United States intends to launch major military strikes against the country within the next few weeks. Trump stated his objective is to "set Iran back to the Stone Age," emphasizing that the U.S. no longer views the Strait of Hormuz as a strategic necessity.
*   **Artemis II Launch:** A historic milestone in space exploration has been achieved as the Artemis II mission successfully launched from Florida. This marks the first crewed lunar mission in over 50 years, successfully placing the spacecraft into Earth's orbit after years of technical delays.
*   **Regional Instability:** Tensions continue to rise in Europe as Ukraine significantly ramps up its drone campaign against Russian infrastructure. Reports from a NATO member state indicate that Ukrainian drone strikes on ports near the Finnish border have led to temporary closures of Russian airspace, with stray munitions landing on Finnish territory, raising security concerns in the Baltic region.

## POLAND

*   **Security and Foreign Policy Debate:** Former Polish President Bronisław Komorowski has raised critical questions regarding the future of the transatlantic alliance and the U.S. commitment to NATO, sparking a domestic debate on Poland's strategic security architecture amidst shifting U.S. geopolitical priorities.
*   **Domestic Political Scrutiny:** As political discourse intensifies, key figures in the opposition, including MEP Krzysztof Brejza, are engaging in public debates regarding the current administration’s policy direction and its impact on the country's international standing.
