## WORLD

* **Israel initiates peace talks with Lebanon:** Prime Minister Benjamin Netanyahu has ordered the commencement of peace negotiations with Lebanon, aiming for the disarmament of Hezbollah and long-term regional stability. Despite this diplomatic push, the situation remains volatile, as Israel maintains it will continue military operations against Hezbollah, while Iran disputes the current cease-fire terms regarding Lebanese territory.
* **Economic downturn in Germany:** Germany is facing a severe wave of bankruptcies, signaling a potential "black quarter" for Europe’s largest economy. The scale of insolvencies has reached levels not seen in years, raising concerns about broader economic stability across the Eurozone.

## POLAND

* **Constitutional crisis deepens:** A significant legal and political conflict has emerged surrounding the Constitutional Tribunal (TK). President Karol Nawrocki has refused to recognize the swearing-in of four newly elected judges, leading those judges to announce plans to pursue their claims through labor courts. Prominent legal experts have labeled the President's actions as a potential violation of the constitution.
* **Security and Healthcare challenges:** The Polish Ministry of National Defense confirmed the interception of a Russian IL-20 reconnaissance aircraft over the Baltic Sea by Polish F-16s, highlighting persistent regional security tensions. Meanwhile, the Ministry of Health has disclosed a significant funding shortfall of 18 billion PLN, underscoring the ongoing pressure on the national healthcare system.
