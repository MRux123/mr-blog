## WORLD

*   **Escalation in Iran:** The geopolitical landscape remains volatile as the Pentagon increases troop deployments to the region. Despite potential interest in negotiations regarding Pakistan's brokerage offer, Tehran has formally dismissed recent overtures from the Trump administration, signaling continued diplomatic friction.
*   **Disruption in Russian Energy:** Ukrainian drone strikes have targeted critical Russian oil infrastructure, specifically in the ports of Primorsk and Ust-Luga. These operations have led to a suspension of oil exports, marking a significant escalation in the economic impact of the ongoing conflict on Russia’s energy sector.

## POLAND

*   **Diplomatic Fallout over Hungary Visit:** Foreign Minister Radosław Sikorski has sharply criticized President Karol Nawrocki’s recent visit to Budapest. Sikorski argued that the meeting with Viktor Orbán undermined Polish foreign policy interests and specifically lamented the lack of progress on the potential return of figures involved in past public fund controversies.
*   **Institutional Setbacks:** Poland has officially lost its bid to host the European Union’s Customs Agency (EUCA), with the headquarters awarded to the French city of Lille. Despite a strong performance in the selection process, the result highlights the challenges currently faced by Polish diplomatic efforts within EU institutional competition.
*   **Internal Political Friction:** The opposition party 'Nowa Nadzieja' is facing internal discord regarding the status of MEP Ewa Zajączkowska-Hernik. While Zajączkowska-Hernik denies resigning, party leadership suggests that her recent shift in European Parliament faction alignment constitutes an automatic exclusion, creating further volatility within the political landscape.
