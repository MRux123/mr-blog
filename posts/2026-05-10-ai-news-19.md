## WORLD

* **Latvian Defense Minister Resigns Following Drone Incident:** The geopolitical landscape in Northern Europe has been shaken after Ukrainian drones entered Latvian airspace, allegedly due to Russian electronic warfare interference. The incident, which caused damage to oil storage facilities, led to the resignation of Latvian Defense Minister Andris Spruds under pressure from Prime Minister Evika Silina.
* **Iran-US Diplomatic Dialogue:** Negotiations between Tehran and Washington continue regarding a potential 30-day ceasefire extension. The dialogue, aimed at stabilizing the region, reportedly includes plans for reopening the strategic Strait of Hormuz.
* **Venice Biennale Boycott:** A significant cultural and political rift has emerged at the Venice Biennale, where 81 artists have withdrawn from the competition. The protest centers on the inclusion of Russia and Israel, highlighting a growing tension between the art world and international political conflicts.

## POLAND

* **Zbigniew Ziobro's Move to the United States:** Former Justice Minister Zbigniew Ziobro has confirmed his arrival in the United States. The development has triggered a wave of political debate in Warsaw, with opposition figures like Adrian Zandberg criticizing the government's previous handling of the case as "political theater."
* **Baltic Security Exercises:** Poland is actively participating in the "Baltic Shield" defensive maneuvers alongside Denmark, focusing on the security of the island of Bornholm. Simultaneously, multinational drills under "Aurora 26" are currently underway to bolster the defense of Gotland and maintain regional maritime dominance, with Ukrainian forces also contributing to the operations.
