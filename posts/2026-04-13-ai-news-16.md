## WORLD

*   **US Enforces Strait of Hormuz Blockade:** The United States military has officially initiated a blockade of the Strait of Hormuz, effectively restricting maritime traffic to and from Iranian ports. President Donald Trump has issued a stark warning, threatening the "swift elimination" of any Iranian vessels that attempt to challenge the operation. The move aims to dismantle Iran's oil revenue streams, though it has created significant friction with European allies.
*   **European Allies Split on Middle East Strategy:** Both the United Kingdom and France have distanced themselves from the American-led blockade, opting against participation. While EU High Representative Kaja Kallas supports a coalition to ensure maritime security, she has explicitly criticized the blockade strategy, signaling a growing diplomatic rift between Washington and key European capitals regarding containment tactics in the Persian Gulf.

## POLAND

*   **Political Shift in Hungary and Regional Impact:** Following the defeat of Viktor Orbán in the Hungarian parliamentary elections, Péter Magyar’s party (TISZA) is poised to reshape the country's political landscape. Reports from Budapest indicate that outgoing officials, including Peter Szijjarto, are allegedly destroying sensitive diplomatic documents. Moscow has reacted by declaring Hungary an "unfriendly country," refusing to congratulate the new leadership.
*   **Presidential Pardon in Poland:** President Karol Nawrocki has exercised his executive prerogative to grant a pardon to Weronika Krawczyk. The case, which involved a conviction for the defamation of a physician, has drawn attention to the boundaries of legal recourse and presidential intervention in the Polish judicial system.
