## WORLD

*   **Escalation in the Strait of Hormuz:** The region faces renewed instability as Iranian officials threaten to re-close the critical maritime chokepoint. This follows a period of volatile negotiations with the United States, where President Trump has oscillated between pursuing a diplomatic breakthrough and threatening renewed military action. The presence of Iran's "mosquito fleet"—a highly disruptive force of high-speed naval vessels—remains a primary tactical concern for international security.
*   **Shifting US Policy on Russian Energy:** In a move that has drawn significant scrutiny, the Trump administration has extended the suspension of sanctions on Russian oil transported via sea by 30 days. This decision, lacking a formal justification, stands in contrast to previous statements from the Treasury and suggests a strategic realignment, potentially linked to the broader containment efforts regarding Iran.
*   **Diplomatic Developments in Lebanon:** A 10-day ceasefire between Israel and Lebanon appears to be holding, with thousands of displaced civilians attempting to return home. Success here is viewed as a vital step in easing broader regional tensions, potentially removing a major barrier to ongoing U.S.-Iran peace talks.

## POLAND

*   **Political Fragmentation Concerns:** Mateusz Morawiecki has publicly defended his new association amid growing speculation regarding a potential split within the Law and Justice (PiS) party. Morawiecki maintains that his political future remains tied to the party, despite internal frictions.
*   **Presidential Stance on Judicial Appointments:** President Karol Nawrocki has addressed the controversy surrounding the incomplete swearing-in of Constitutional Tribunal judges. Labeling the current situation a "circus in the Sejm," the President signaled that the process could have been concluded earlier if not for legislative obstruction, maintaining that he has not refused to swear in the remaining candidates.
