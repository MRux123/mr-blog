## WORLD

*   **Escalation in Iran:** Tensions in the Middle East have reached a critical juncture as Iran reportedly rejected a U.S.-backed cease-fire proposal mediated by the Iraqi Prime Minister. Simultaneously, the U.S. military has intensified its regional posture, launching a 13th consecutive night of airstrikes against targets in Iran, signaling a significant deterioration in diplomatic efforts to contain the conflict.
*   **Broad U.S. Tariff Implementation:** The United States has initiated a sweeping trade policy shift, imposing new tariffs on approximately 60 trading partners. The administration justifies the move by citing systemic failures in these nations to enforce prohibitions against forced labor. These measures replace previous global tariff structures, marking a more aggressive protectionist stance on human rights-related trade compliance.
*   **Human Trafficking Crisis:** International authorities are grappling with a disturbing human trafficking case involving infants smuggled from Indonesia to Singapore. The incident has raised urgent questions regarding regional border security and the plight of the victims, as the legal and humanitarian status of the trafficked children remains uncertain.

## POLAND

*   **Impact of New U.S. Trade Policy:** Polish economic analysts are assessing the implications of the new U.S. trade tariffs, which affect 60 countries, including key international partners. With the implementation of a 10–12.5% tariff rate on goods associated with forced labor concerns, domestic industries are bracing for potential disruptions in global supply chains. The move by the Trump administration effectively replaces the expiring 10% global rate, pressuring Polish exporters to ensure full transparency and compliance with stringent new U.S. labor standards.
