## WORLD

* **Putin and Musk in Direct Dialogue:** President Vladimir Putin has publicly commended Elon Musk as a "real genius," confirming that the two have been in close contact. The announcement follows satellite-related discussions, signaling a potential shift in the influence of private sector tech giants on international geopolitical relations.
* **Himalayan Construction Crisis:** A surge in tourism-driven infrastructure development across Nepal and India's Uttarakhand region has put an estimated 300,000 people at significant risk of environmental catastrophes. Experts are raising alarms over the lack of regulation in high-altitude construction projects.

## POLAND

* **Political Realignment in Poland:** Tensions and strategic planning dominate the internal political scene as Prime Minister Donald Tusk prepares to meet with Szymon Hołownia. The talks are seen as crucial for the future of the Polska 2050 party, which is currently navigating a search for new leadership.
* **Stricter Passport Regulations:** The government has unveiled a proposal to tighten the criteria for obtaining a Polish passport. While details are still under review, the move represents a shift in national administrative policy regarding citizenship documentation.
* **Urban Infrastructure Expansion:** Local authorities have taken the preliminary steps to explore ambitious development projects, including a potential tunnel under the Oder River and the creation of a metro system in Wrocław, aiming to modernize the region's transport network.
