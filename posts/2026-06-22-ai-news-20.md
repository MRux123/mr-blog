## WORLD

*   **Keir Starmer Resigns as UK Prime Minister:** In a significant political development, UK Prime Minister Keir Starmer has announced his resignation. The move paves the way for Andy Burnham, the prominent Labour Party mayor, to potentially become the seventh Prime Minister of the United Kingdom in just one decade, signaling a period of continued political volatility in Westminster.
*   **Mideast Diplomatic Developments:** The U.S. government has eased certain sanctions on Iranian oil exports. Vice President JD Vance reported that Iran has signaled an openness to permit U.N. nuclear inspectors back into the country, though Iranian officials maintain that no formal new commitments have been solidified, leaving the status of the regional nuclear impasse precarious.

## POLAND

*   **Tragedy at Murcki-Staszic Mine:** A fatal accident occurred at the Murcki-Staszic coal mine in Katowice, resulting in the death of a 45-year-old miner. According to reports from the Polska Grupa Górnicza, the worker was crushed by a falling rock, highlighting ongoing safety concerns in the Polish mining sector.
*   **Diplomatic Controversy Regarding President Nawrocki:** Former Polish Foreign Minister Jacek Czaputowicz has publicly criticized President Karol Nawrocki for his decision to revoke the Order of the White Eagle previously awarded to Ukrainian President Volodymyr Zelenskyy. Czaputowicz warned that the move risks further damaging Poland's international standing and diplomatic leverage within the region.
*   **Imminent Extreme Weather:** The Institute of Meteorology and Water Management (IMGW) has issued warnings for extreme temperatures, predicting that parts of Poland will experience a tropical heatwave with temperatures reaching up to 40 degrees Celsius, prompting concerns regarding public health and infrastructure stability.
