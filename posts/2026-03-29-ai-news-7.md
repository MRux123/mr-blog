## WORLD

*   **Escalating Violence in Sudan:** The brutal civil war in Sudan continues to claim civilian lives as it approaches its fourth year. Recent drone strikes targeted a market in North Darfur and a civilian vehicle in North Kordofan, resulting in at least 28 deaths, including an infant. The ongoing conflict between the Sudanese army and the Rapid Support Forces (RSF) remains a severe humanitarian crisis.
*   **Strategic Objectives in Ukraine:** Military experts, including Professor Andrij Charuk, emphasize that the primary goal of Russian forces in Ukraine remains the systematic destruction of the country. Territorial gains, such as those sought in the Donbas, are characterized as intermediate objectives within a broader campaign of attrition and destabilization.

## POLAND

*   **Fatal Road Accidents:** The weekend has been marked by a series of tragic accidents on Polish roads. A catastrophic incident involving a car crashing into logs resulted in the death of an 11-year-old child, while a separate collision between a truck and a passenger vehicle in Wilków Wielki claimed the lives of three people.
*   **Political Friction Over Military Command:** Tensions persist regarding the division of authority between the President and the Ministry of National Defense. Presidential minister Marcin Przydacz defended the President’s right to consult with military commanders, amid ongoing disagreements with Władysław Kosiniak-Kamysz, the Minister of National Defense, concerning legislative and oversight issues within the armed forces.
