## WORLD

*   **US Compensation for "Havana Syndrome" Victims:** The United States government has initiated a $3 million payout program to provide relief for intelligence officers and diplomats affected by the mysterious "Havana Syndrome." The condition, which emerged a decade ago, continues to be a subject of intense scrutiny regarding its origins and long-term health impact on personnel stationed abroad.
*   **Security Concerns Over Missile Production:** Reports indicate that plans for producing Patriot missile system components within Ukraine may be abandoned in favor of German-based production. Security considerations are cited as the primary driver for this shift, as tensions with Russia remain high and the Kremlin continues to frame Western military support as a catalyst for prolonging the ongoing conflict.

## POLAND

*   **Renewed Dialogue on MiG-29 Transfers:** Polish Minister of National Defence, Władysław Kosiniak-Kamysz, has confirmed that talks with Ukraine regarding the transfer of Polish MiG-29 fighter jets have resumed. The potential agreement involves a swap for drone technology, with Poland offering support for the modernization of the aircraft, provided the financial burden is shared by Ukraine or third-party international coalitions.
*   **Volhynia Massacre Commemorations:** Karol Nawrocki, head of the Institute of National Remembrance, used recent commemorations of the Volhynia massacre to address the sensitive nature of Polish-Ukrainian historical relations. While condemning the atrocities, he emphasized that true reconciliation and historical memory require acknowledging the "Righteous Ukrainians" who protected Poles during the conflict, suggesting they hold a more rightful place in the national pantheon than controversial historical figures.
*   **Mining Incident in Lubin:** A high-magnitude seismic event occurred at the Lubin mining facility in Lower Silesia. While the incident resulted in the entrapment of a machine operator, rescue efforts were successfully concluded by early afternoon. A dedicated commission has been established by KGHM Polska Miedź S.A. to investigate the structural causes of the tremor.
