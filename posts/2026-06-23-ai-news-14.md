## WORLD

*   **Escalating Mideast Nuclear Impasse:** Tensions between the United States and Iran have reached a critical juncture following conflicting accounts regarding nuclear inspections. While President Trump claims Iran has committed to high-level oversight, Iranian officials have categorically denied any such agreements, specifically rejecting inspections of previously damaged nuclear facilities.
*   **Genocide Allegations Against Israel:** A UN commission of inquiry has formally accused Israel of committing genocide in Gaza, citing the deliberate targeting of children as a primary factor. The Israeli government has vehemently denied the allegations, characterizing the report as a "libellous sham."
*   **Human Rights Concerns in Sudan:** Evidence presented to a UK parliamentary committee suggests that the British government prioritized diplomatic and economic ties with the United Arab Emirates over addressing reports of mass atrocities in Sudan. Investigators claim that intelligence linking the UAE to the support of the Rapid Support Forces was suppressed to preserve bilateral relations.

## POLAND

*   **Deepening Diplomatic Rift with Ukraine:** A growing political dispute between Polish officials and President Volodymyr Zelenskyy has drawn sharp criticism from the European Commission. Brussels has warned that the public friction undermines EU unity and serves the strategic interests of Russia, labeling the situation as detrimental to regional stability.
*   **Security Alarms in Kherson:** Polish and Ukrainian intelligence services have issued warnings regarding the situation in occupied territories of the Kherson region. Reports indicate that Russian forces are mismanaging the disposal of animal remains, creating conditions that could lead to an outbreak of anthrax, posing a significant threat to both civilian populations and agricultural safety.
