## WORLD

* **Humanitarian Disaster in Nigeria:** A Nigerian Air Force airstrike, intended to target jihadist militants, has resulted in a significant civilian tragedy. Reports from Amnesty International and local sources confirm that at least 100 people were killed after the munitions struck a market in Yobe state. Authorities have acknowledged a "misfire" occurred during the operation, sparking urgent questions regarding military accountability.
* **Geopolitical Standoff over Chagos Islands:** The process to "decolonize" the Chagos Islands has stalled after UK Prime Minister Keir Starmer was forced to suspend handover legislation. The shift in US policy under Donald Trump has undermined support for the deal, leaving the Mauritian government committed to pursuing its claim despite the sudden political roadblock.
* **Passing of a Legend:** Asha Bhosle, the iconic voice of Indian cinema and a celebrated figure in global music, has died at the age of 92. Her passing marks the end of an era for the Bollywood industry, where her versatile and adventurous vocals defined the soundtrack of several generations.

## POLAND

* **High-Stakes Election on the Danube:** Hungary is currently at a critical political juncture as the nation concludes its parliamentary election. The contest, framed as a pivotal test for the future of the European Union, centers on Viktor Orbán’s long-standing administration and his primary challenger, Peter Magyar. With no traditional exit polls available, observers remain in suspense as the vote count proceeds.
* **Tragic Aviation Incident:** A fatal air accident has occurred involving the collision of two gliders in mid-air. Authorities have confirmed that a Polish pilot lost their life in the incident. Investigations are currently underway to determine the exact circumstances that led to the collision.
