## WORLD

* **Escalating Middle East Hostilities:** Tensions between the United States and Iran have reached a critical juncture, with both nations trading direct strikes. The Revolutionary Guards report targeting an American military base in retaliation for US operations in southern Iran, jeopardizing months of delicate ceasefire negotiations. Simultaneously, Israeli military forces have initiated heavy strikes across southern Lebanon, following large-scale evacuation orders affecting nearly 17% of Lebanese territory.
* **Tragedy in Kenya:** A devastating fire at the Utumishi Girls Academy in Gilgil, Nakuru County, has claimed the lives of at least 15 students. Local authorities are currently investigating the cause of the blaze, which also left dozens of students injured and has triggered an anxious search for information among affected families.

## POLAND

* **Judicial Developments in Ziobro Case:** The District Court in Warsaw has expedited the hearing regarding the arrest warrant for former Justice Minister Zbigniew Ziobro. Due to changes in the judicial panel's vacation schedule, the hearing originally slated for September has been moved up to early July, marking a significant turn in the ongoing legal scrutiny.
* **Political Immunity Dispute:** The Sejm is currently evaluating a request to strip Sławomir Mentzen, leader of the Konfederacja party, of his parliamentary immunity. The request, filed by police, relates to charges regarding the possession and use of a flare during the 2025 Independence March. Mentzen was notably absent from the committee session tasked with issuing an opinion on the matter.
