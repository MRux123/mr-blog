## WORLD

*   **Ebola Outbreak Declared International Emergency:** The World Health Organization has officially designated the recent Ebola outbreak in the Democratic Republic of the Congo (DRC) as an international health emergency. With at least 100 fatalities reported and infections confirmed in neighboring regions, the situation has sparked widespread panic. The identification of a case in a US doctor working in the region has further elevated concerns regarding the potential for international transmission.
*   **Escalating Conflict in the Middle East:** Despite a nominal ceasefire, the death toll from Israeli strikes in Lebanon has surpassed 3,000. This milestone marks a significant intensification of hostilities between Israel and Hezbollah, fueling concerns about regional stability and the failure of existing diplomatic containment efforts.

## POLAND

*   **Political Controversy Surrounding MP's Husband:** Public attention is currently focused on MP Izabela Bodnar from the Centrum party, following serious allegations involving her husband, Maciej B. The investigation into the surveillance of local government officials in Lower Silesia has prompted calls for clarification from within her own parliamentary club, raising questions about political integrity.
*   **Imminent Legalization of Same-Sex Marriage Recognition:** The Polish government is set to sign a regulation this week allowing for the transcription of same-sex marriage certificates obtained abroad. Minister of Interior and Administration Marcin Kierwiński confirmed that the new legislation will pave the way for formal recognition of these unions in Polish civil registries, marking a significant shift in national social policy.
*   **Legal Challenges for Sławomir Mentzen:** A formal request to lift the parliamentary immunity of Konfederation leader Sławomir Mentzen has reached the Sejm. The motion follows a six-month investigation into his actions during the Independence March, where he allegedly set off a flare in violation of safety regulations.
