## WORLD

* **Catastrophic Earthquake in Colombia:** A major earthquake has struck Colombia, resulting in at least 111 confirmed deaths and significant infrastructure damage. With over 1,500 homes destroyed, rescue efforts are currently focused on locating survivors amidst the rubble. Authorities warn that the death toll is expected to rise as emergency teams reach remote areas.
* **Geopolitical Escalation in the Middle East and Ukraine:** The security landscape remains volatile as reports emerge of Russia utilizing North Korean ballistic missiles in strikes on Zaporizhzhia, resulting in multiple casualties. Simultaneously, in the Middle East, the post-conflict sentencing of former Syrian dictator Bashar al-Assad for war crimes marks a significant, albeit symbolic, turning point in regional accountability.
* **Ebola Outbreak Crisis:** Global health officials are monitoring a rapid surge in Ebola cases, with reports indicating a record-breaking death toll of 1,000 victims within a single month, signaling a severe public health emergency that demands immediate international intervention.

## POLAND

* **Political Landscape in Flux:** A new poll conducted for Wirtualna Polska suggests a potential shift in the Sejm’s power structure. The findings indicate that the political formation led by Mateusz Morawiecki, "Rozwój Plus," is exerting pressure on the current ruling coalition. Projections show that parties like PSL and Polska 2050 could struggle to reach the threshold for parliamentary representation if current trends persist.
* **National Mourning:** The country is mourning the loss of Andrzej Beya-Zaborski, a highly respected stage and film actor best known for his iconic role as the police commander in the "U Pana Boga..." film series. His passing has triggered an outpouring of tributes from the Polish artistic community and the public.
* **Safety Concerns in Local Institutions:** Investigations are currently underway following a tragic fire at a children’s home near Piaseczno and a separate mass food poisoning incident at a rehabilitation center in Międzywodzie. Health authorities (Sanepid) are under pressure to provide conclusive findings on the causes behind these incidents as public concern regarding institutional safety grows.
