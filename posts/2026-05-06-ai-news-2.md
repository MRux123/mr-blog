## WORLD

* **U.S. Abandons "Project Freedom" in Strait of Hormuz:** President Trump has announced a sudden halt to the U.S.-led escort operation through the Strait of Hormuz, just one day after its launch. The decision follows claims of progress in diplomatic negotiations with Iran, signaling a potential shift in strategy from military posturing to de-escalation. The move has reportedly caused friction within the administration, as Secretary of State Marco Rubio had publicly affirmed the mission only hours before the President's reversal.

* **Vivek Ramaswamy Secures Ohio Gubernatorial Nomination:** In a significant development for the Republican party, Vivek Ramaswamy has won the nomination for the Ohio governor race. His campaign, defined by sharp criticism of the incumbent Democratic administration's handling of the Covid-19 pandemic, reflects an ongoing effort by the GOP to capitalize on post-pandemic policy grievances at the state level.
