## WORLD

* **Typhoon Dolphin Hits China:** China is grappling with the aftermath of Typhoon Dolphin, the strongest storm to strike the country this year. Following severe flooding in the Philippines, the typhoon made landfall on Sunday evening, triggering widespread mass evacuations as authorities struggle to mitigate the impact of the extreme weather event.
* **Climate Change Threatens Pakistani Agriculture:** The "Red Chile Capital" of Pakistan is facing an existential threat. Soaring temperatures and the escalating costs associated with climate change are pushing traditional farming methods to the brink, forcing local producers to urgently seek new, adaptive strategies to ensure their crops' survival.

## POLAND

* **Political Disciplinary Actions in PiS:** The Law and Justice (PiS) party has initiated disciplinary proceedings against Ryszard Czarnecki. Party leadership, led by Jarosław Kaczyński, moved to suspend the politician and push for his exclusion from the party following his controversial participation in a conference alongside Grzegorz Braun.
* **Tensions Over Presidential-Government Relations:** A recent poll by Opinia24 highlights deep public concern regarding the state of governance in Poland. Over 50% of respondents believe that the strained relationship between the government and the President is negatively impacting national security, with only 13% viewing the current cooperation as constructive.
* **Modernization of the Polish Navy:** In a significant milestone for national defense, a historic moment took place in Gdynia as the hull of the ORP Wicher was moved out of the PGZ Naval Shipyard. This event marks a symbolic and operational step forward in the modernization program for the Polish Navy.
