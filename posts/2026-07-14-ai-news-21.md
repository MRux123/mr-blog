## WORLD

* **Rising Tensions in the Strait of Hormuz:** The geopolitical situation remains highly volatile as the United States continues military operations against Iranian interests. While President Trump has retracted his threat to impose a 20% fee on cargo ships transiting the Strait, the U.S. remains committed to a naval blockade, effectively challenging Iran's influence over the critical waterway. Analysts warn that the collapse of the latest ceasefire agreement has exposed severe structural weaknesses in U.S.-Iran diplomatic relations.
* **Tragedy in Brussels:** Emergency services are responding to a disaster in central Brussels following a fire at the Oxy building. The incident, which occurred Tuesday morning, resulted in the recovery of six bodies from an elevator, marking a significant local tragedy that has drawn international attention.

## POLAND

* **Constitutional Tribunal Standoff:** Political friction in Warsaw has escalated, with reports of police intervention at the Constitutional Tribunal. Bogdan Święczkowski has characterized the internal events as an "attempted coup," signaling a deepening crisis within the state's judicial and administrative institutions.
* **Technological Defense Shift:** In a notable development for European security, a former Russian national has been appointed to lead the development of a new European ballistic missile defense system. This strategic move highlights the continent's intensifying focus on hardening its defensive capabilities amidst evolving security threats.
* **Public Safety Incident in Łódź:** Authorities are investigating a serious traffic incident in Łódź, where a driver struck a group of pedestrians, resulting in multiple casualties. The event has prompted immediate concerns regarding urban safety and traffic enforcement.
