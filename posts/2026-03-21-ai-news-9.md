## WORLD

* **Middle East Conflict Intensifies:** Tensions have reached a critical threshold as Iran launched ballistic missiles targeting the joint U.S.-U.K. base on Diego Garcia. While the projectiles reportedly missed their intended targets, the incident marks a significant escalation in regional instability, further compounded by ongoing Israeli military operations in Lebanon.
* **Ukrainian Diplomatic Push:** Amidst a deteriorating security climate, President Zelensky has dispatched a high-level negotiation team to the United States. The Ukrainian leadership has expressed profound concern that the escalating conflict between Iran and Western allies could further divert international focus and resources away from the war in Ukraine.
* **Ongoing Casualties in Zaporizhzhia:** The civilian cost of the war continues to mount, with recent Russian drone strikes in Zaporizhzhia resulting in two deaths, including the injury of two children, just as diplomatic efforts for peace are being attempted in Washington.

## POLAND

* **Presidential Visit to the U.S.:** President Karol Nawrocki is scheduled to travel to the United States next week. The visit will feature his participation in the Conservative Political Action Conference (CPAC) and is expected to include a high-profile meeting with Donald Trump, signaling a strategic focus on transatlantic conservative cooperation.
* **Tensions in Security Appointments:** Coordinator of Special Services Tomasz Siemoniak has publicly urged President Nawrocki to expedite appointments within the Internal Security Agency (ABW). Siemoniak warned that the delay in staffing critical national security roles undermines Poland's defensive capabilities, despite recent progress in general officer nominations.
* **Political Rebranding:** The political landscape is set for a shift as the "Polska 2050" party prepares for an official name change today. Observers anticipate that the new branding may be linked to ongoing discussions regarding government reconstruction and coalition dynamics.
