## WORLD

* **Strait of Hormuz Blockade:** The geopolitical situation has deteriorated rapidly as Iranian military command announced the closure of the Strait of Hormuz. Tehran claims the move is a direct response to the United States' failure to ensure Israel’s compliance with the Lebanon ceasefire. This development risks massive disruption to global energy supplies and signals a significant escalation in regional hostilities.
* **Fragility of Lebanon Ceasefire:** Despite a formal agreement, active combat continues in southern Lebanon. The IDF reports strikes against Hezbollah targets following a barrage of over 50 projectiles launched into Israel. The breakdown of this truce has complicated diplomatic efforts, currently centered in Switzerland.
* **UK Political Uncertainty:** The British Labour Party faces internal turmoil as momentum builds to replace Prime Minister Keir Starmer with Andy Burnham. This potential leadership shift highlights the ongoing struggle within the UK government to navigate complex electoral and national challenges.

## POLAND

* **"Bronimy Polskiej Granicy" Investigation:** Polish authorities have launched a nationwide search operation targeting members of the "Bronimy Polskiej Granicy" (We Defend the Polish Border) group. The Ministry of the Interior and Administration stated that the operation aims to curb groups attempting to impersonate state officials, emphasizing that such unauthorized actions will be met with a firm state response.
* **Rail Disaster Near Bedford:** Emergency services are managing a critical situation following a collision between two trains near Bedford, UK, which resulted in the death of one train driver. With nine individuals currently in critical condition, the incident has drawn significant attention to railway safety protocols.
