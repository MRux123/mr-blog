## WORLD

* **Iran Widens Regional Conflict with Strikes on Qatar:** In a significant escalation of its ongoing campaign, Iran has launched a targeted strike against Qatar’s Ras Laffan gas facility. The facility, which provides roughly 20% of global LNG supplies, suffered significant damage. In response to the breach of sovereignty and security, Qatar has ordered the immediate expulsion of Iranian military attachés.
* **Geopolitical Spillover in the Middle East:** The conflict continues to intensify, with Iranian missile strikes reaching the Israeli-occupied West Bank, marking the first time the current war has claimed Palestinian lives in that territory. Simultaneously, reports indicate a rare Israeli naval engagement against Iranian vessels in the Caspian Sea, signaling an expansion of the operational theater closer to European borders.
* **Ecuadorian Authorities Capture High-Profile Fugitive:** In a major development for regional stability, Ecuadorian security forces have arrested a key gang leader, "Lobo Menor," who was a primary suspect in the 2023 assassination of presidential candidate Fernando Villavicencio.

## POLAND

* **Sikorski Warns of Anti-EU Disinformation Wave:** Polish Foreign Minister Radosław Sikorski has issued a strong condemnation of the rising tide of anti-European Union propaganda. During an academic address in Florence, he characterized the current information environment as a "tsunami of lies and disinformation" aimed at destabilizing Poland's relationship with the European bloc.
