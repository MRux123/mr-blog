## WORLD

*   **Geopolitical Realignment as Trump-Xi Summit Approaches:** Asian nations are expressing growing concern ahead of the upcoming summit between President Donald Trump and Xi Jinping. The primary fear among middle powers is that Washington may leverage security commitments in the region to secure more favorable economic terms from Beijing, potentially altering the regional balance of power.
*   **Standoff with Tehran Deepens:** The diplomatic situation concerning Iran has reached a critical juncture. Tehran has issued a list of demands—including U.S. war reparations, recognition of sovereignty over the Strait of Hormuz, and the lifting of American sanctions—which the Trump administration has formally deemed "unacceptable," further escalating regional uncertainty.
*   **Ukraine's Strategic Pivot:** President Volodymyr Zelensky appears to be distancing Ukraine from U.S. reliance. With peace negotiations currently stalled, Kyiv is increasingly shifting toward a self-reliant strategy, signaling a potential long-term change in the geopolitical trajectory of the conflict.

## POLAND

*   **Constitutional Clash Over CBA Liquidation:** President Karol Nawrocki has formally vetoed the legislation aimed at liquidating the Central Anti-Corruption Bureau (CBA). This decision highlights the ongoing political friction between the executive branch and the legislative majority, reinforcing the current state of institutional gridlock.
*   **Legislative Controversy Over Digital Assets:** A new proposal introduced by Law and Justice (PiS) parliamentarians has sparked significant debate, as it advocates for the complete delegalization of cryptocurrency trading in Poland. The move is being scrutinized not only for its economic implications but also as a tactical political maneuver in the lead-up to future electoral cycles.
*   **Legal Scrutiny of Zbigniew Ziobro:** The National Prosecutor’s Office has clarified the legal status of Zbigniew Ziobro following his departure for the United States. Officials stated that he is currently not a wanted individual under international arrest warrants, though they maintain that they are preparing for the possibility of his return to European jurisdiction.
