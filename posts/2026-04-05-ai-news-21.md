## WORLD

* **Escalating US-Iran Conflict:** The geopolitical standoff has reached a critical phase following the rescue of a US airman in Iranian territory. President Donald Trump has issued a series of shifting ultimatums regarding the Strait of Hormuz, threatening severe strikes on Iranian infrastructure if the maritime route remains closed. Iran has officially condemned these threats, characterizing them as "war crimes" and warning of retaliatory strikes against US interests.
* **Energy Market Volatility:** In response to the growing conflict, the OPEC+ alliance has moved to increase oil production quotas. This measure is a direct attempt to stabilize global energy markets, which have been severely rattled by the potential for a large-scale confrontation in the Persian Gulf.
* **Sabotage Investigation in Serbia:** Tensions have reached the Balkans as investigators explore explosive devices found near the Balkan Stream pipeline. While evidence suggests American-made components were used, Serbian officials caution that this does not necessarily implicate the US. The incident has triggered a diplomatic spat, with Ukraine categorically denying any involvement following accusations from regional neighbors.

## POLAND

* **Threats on the Eastern Flank:** Intelligence reports highlight a rising security threat to Poland as Russia accelerates the construction of long-range drone bases in Belarus. Analysts warn that these installations not only shorten the reach of potential Russian strikes on Ukraine but also increase the risk of unauthorized incursions into NATO airspace, specifically over Poland and Romania.
* **Political Controversy over Geopolitics:** A heated debate has emerged in the Polish political arena regarding the country's potential role in the ongoing international crisis. Criticism has been directed at presidential minister Marcin Przydacz for his comments on the Strait of Hormuz, which sparked a sharp rebuke from opposition senators calling for an immediate apology.
* **Mountain Safety Alerts:** Amidst national holiday travel, the Tatra National Park (TPN) has reopened access to the popular Morskie Oko route. However, authorities emphasize that the region remains highly dangerous due to an active third-degree avalanche warning, urging tourists to prioritize safety over accessibility.
