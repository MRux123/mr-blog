## WORLD

* **Iran-US Diplomacy on the Brink:** International attention is centered on a potential 14-point memorandum of understanding between Washington and Tehran. While the White House reports positive momentum in negotiations to end the war, Iranian officials confirm the proposal is currently under formal review. President Trump has signaled cautious optimism despite the complexities remaining in the agreement.
* **Escalation in Lebanon:** Tensions in the Middle East have been further exacerbated as Israel conducted a targeted strike in Beirut, the first such military action since the mid-April ceasefire with Hezbollah. This move underscores the fragility of the regional security situation even as wider peace talks progress.
* **Missing US Personnel in Morocco:** A multi-national search and rescue mission is underway following the disappearance of two US service members during the African Lion military exercises in south-western Morocco. Africom is coordinating efforts with local authorities to locate the missing personnel.

## POLAND

* **Diplomatic Alert in Kyiv:** The Russian Foreign Ministry has issued a formal appeal for foreign diplomats to evacuate Kyiv. Moscow has threatened "inevitable retaliation" against the Ukrainian capital and its strategic command centers, specifically citing the upcoming May 9th Victory Day celebrations as a potential trigger for escalated military action.
