## WORLD

* **Middle East Conflict Intensifies:** Tensions have reached a critical threshold as the U.S. and Iran engage in direct military exchanges. Following the downing of a U.S. military helicopter, American forces launched strikes in southern Iran. Tehran responded with drone and missile attacks targeting U.S. military positions in Bahrain and Jordan, putting the regional cease-fire at severe risk.
* **Ukraine’s Tactical Shift:** Ukrainian forces have successfully intensified their drone campaign against Russian infrastructure. Recent strikes have targeted strategic oil facilities and logistics hubs, including power disruptions in Mariupol, significantly complicating Russian supply lines and troop rotations.
* **Corporate Accountability and Human Rights:** A new investigation by Global Witness has linked major global technology brands, including Amazon and Sony, to mineral supply chains controlled by M23 rebels in the Democratic Republic of the Congo. The report alleges that coltan used in consumer electronics may be funding militias involved in systemic violence and human rights atrocities.

## POLAND

* **Hybrid Warfare Threats:** Deputy Prime Minister and Minister of National Defence Władysław Kosiniak-Kamysz has warned of a sharp increase in hybrid warfare tactics originating from the East. The government reports a coordinated disinformation campaign aimed at destabilizing national security and eroding public trust, specifically targeting programs like "SAFE" and circulating false narratives regarding military support for Ukraine.
* **Legal Developments:** High-profile legal proceedings continue to dominate the domestic agenda. Former Minister of Justice Zbigniew Ziobro faces potential loss of asylum status in the U.S. following a missed summons deadline, while Jarosław Kaczyński has appeared before the District Prosecutor's Office in Warsaw to testify in the long-standing "two towers" investigation.
