## WORLD

*   **Middle East Conflict Continues:** Despite ongoing peace talks in Washington, the conflict between Israel and Hezbollah remains volatile. Fresh exchanges of fire have been reported, with Israeli airstrikes targeting Nabatieh and Hezbollah responding with attacks on Kiryat Shmona, underscoring the fragility of current diplomatic efforts.
*   **Ukraine Under Renewed Pressure:** Kyiv faces a critical moment as intelligence reports warn of imminent, large-scale Russian missile and drone attacks. Residents remain under severe psychological strain, with the infrastructure damage from recent strikes highlighting the escalating humanitarian crisis.
*   **US Intelligence Shake-up:** President Trump has nominated Bill Pulte, the head of the Federal Housing Finance Agency, to serve as acting Director of National Intelligence, a move signaling a potential shift in the administration's approach to national security operations.

## POLAND

*   **Diplomatic Rift with Ukraine:** Tensions between Warsaw and Kyiv have intensified following President Zelenskyy’s decision to name a Ukrainian military unit after the "Heroes of the UPA." Polish government officials, including Foreign Minister Radosław Sikorski, have publicly labeled the move a mistake and are engaged in back-channel diplomacy to address the diplomatic fallout and the status of state honors previously awarded to the Ukrainian president.
*   **Safety and Regulatory Updates:** In domestic policy, the Polish health authorities (GIF) have issued an urgent alert regarding the quality of specific ophthalmic products, ordering an immediate halt to the distribution of "Travoprost + Timolol Medical Valley" eye drops.
