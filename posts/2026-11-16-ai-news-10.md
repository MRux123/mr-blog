## WORLD

* **Humanitarian Pressure on Israel:** The United Kingdom, in coordination with Australia, Canada, and Italy, has issued a formal call for Israel to grant immediate and rapid humanitarian access to Gaza. International allies report that critical supplies, including 150,000 tents, remain blocked at border crossings, raising alarms about the deteriorating living conditions for civilians in the region.
* **Geopolitical Realignment in Asia:** Canadian leader Mark Carney has returned to the international stage in Asia, positioning himself as a key diplomatic player. His upcoming summit attendance, which includes meetings alongside Xi Jinping, Vladimir Putin, and Donald Trump, signals a pivotal moment for Western diplomacy as nations maneuver to balance economic interests with shifting global power dynamics.

## POLAND

* **Counter-Terrorism Operations:** The Internal Security Agency (ABW) has successfully apprehended three teenagers—a 16-year-old and two 17-year-olds—linked to international neo-Nazi networks. According to Minister Tomasz Siemoniak, the suspects were actively planning acts of a terrorist nature, marking a significant intervention by state security services against domestic radicalization.
* **Institutional Gridlock at the Central Bank and Constitutional Tribunal:** A deepening crisis of governance has emerged as the National Bank of Poland (NBP) signaled it might withhold payment orders for the Constitutional Tribunal (TK). The NBP, led by Adam Glapiński, cited the ongoing dispute over the legitimacy of the Tribunal’s leadership as the primary reason for demanding a comprehensive legal assessment before proceeding with administrative operations.
* **Diplomatic Standoff:** Foreign Minister Radosław Sikorski has characterized the current situation regarding foreign representation as the "demolition of the state," noting that 46 ambassadorial nominations remain pending due to a lack of presidential signature. This impasse continues to hamper Poland's diplomatic efficacy on the international stage.
