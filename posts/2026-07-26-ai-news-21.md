## WORLD

* **Escalating Wildfires Across Europe:** Extreme heat waves and powerful winds are currently fueling catastrophic wildfires in France and Spain. The scale of the destruction has forced the mass evacuation of hundreds of thousands of residents, with authorities warning that incoming weather patterns may further exacerbate the already critical situation.
* **Violence in the West Bank:** The security situation in the Middle East has deteriorated significantly following reports that settlers have set fire to two mosques. This act has triggered fears of a renewed, large-scale escalation of violence in the region.

## POLAND

* **Hazardous Weather Alert:** The Institute of Meteorology and Water Management (IMGW) has issued nationwide weather warnings for Poland. An abrupt end to the summer heat is expected on Monday, with authorities forecasting violent storms, heavy rainfall, and strong gales affecting nearly the entire country.
* **Industrial Emergency in Reda:** A major safety incident occurred at the Reda aquapark, where a leak of approximately 600 liters of sodium hypochlorite necessitated the emergency evacuation of over 400 people. Emergency services were deployed to contain the toxic spill, which is commonly used in pool water purification.
* **Passing of a National Heroine:** Poland mourns the loss of Józefa Marciniak, a veteran of the Warsaw Uprising, who has passed away at the age of 104. Her death marks the loss of one of the few remaining living witnesses to one of the most pivotal moments in modern Polish history.
