## WORLD

* **Serbia’s Political Earthquake:** In a historic shift, veteran leader Aleksandar Vučić has conceded defeat in the parliamentary elections to a student-led protest movement. This outcome marks a significant political transformation for the country, signaling a clear rejection of long-standing populist rule in favor of a new, grassroots-driven mandate.
* **Geopolitical Negotiations:** Ukrainian President Volodymyr Zelenskyy has announced plans for trilateral talks involving Ukraine, the United States, and Russia scheduled for November. This development is being closely watched, as it marks a potential, albeit delicate, effort to address territorial tensions at the leadership level.
* **Civil Unrest in the Czech Republic:** Tens of thousands of citizens have taken to the streets in Prague and Olomouc to protest in defense of public media. The scale of the mobilization led to significant logistical disruptions, with protesters drawing parallels between recent attacks on the democratic process and the polarization observed globally.

## POLAND

* **Local Governance Referendum:** Citizens in Częstochowa have concluded a significant referendum aimed at deciding the political future of Mayor Matyjaszczyk. The vote reflects ongoing local debates regarding leadership accountability and municipal administration efficiency.
* **Sporting and Community Engagement:** The "PKO Biegowe 360 stopni" event has concluded, successfully drawing large crowds and fostering a spirit of athletic community, further highlighting the ongoing focus on health and local sports initiatives across the country.
