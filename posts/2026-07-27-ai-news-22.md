## WORLD

* **Escalating Geopolitical Strains:** US President Donald Trump has indicated that dialogue regarding potential conflict with Iran is ongoing, despite Tehran's public denial of direct negotiations. The situation remains volatile as the administration monitors the current lull in hostilities.
* **Environmental Crisis in France:** The city of Bordeaux is currently under threat from rapidly approaching wildfires, which are now within nine miles of the city limits. Local officials have described the situation as catastrophic, with emergency evacuation protocols currently on standby.
* **Corruption Scandal in Ukrainian Diplomacy:** Ukrainian anti-corruption authorities have dismantled a criminal network operating within the Ministry of Foreign Affairs. The group, led by a high-ranking official and involving several diplomats, is accused of embezzling over $1.2 million in foreign aid intended for national defense.

## POLAND

* **Warsaw Rejects Russian Narrative:** The Polish Ministry of Foreign Affairs has issued a sharp rebuttal to recent claims made by the Russian president regarding alleged plans for the partition of Ukraine. Warsaw has officially dismissed these accusations as a deliberate disinformation campaign.
* **Political Realignment:** Former Prime Minister Mateusz Morawiecki has signaled significant upcoming political shifts. Amid speculation regarding his future within his party, Morawiecki announced plans for a potential new political move, with further decisions expected to be finalized this week.
* **International Firefighting Support:** Demonstrating solidarity during the Mediterranean climate crisis, Poland is deploying firefighting units and specialized equipment to assist Spain in combating the severe wildfires that have devastated large swaths of the country's landscape.
