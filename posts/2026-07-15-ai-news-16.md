## WORLD

* **US-Iran Conflict Intensifies:** The military standoff between the United States and Iran has entered a volatile new phase, with both nations exchanging direct strikes for the fifth consecutive day. The ongoing hostilities show no signs of de-escalation, creating a regional security crisis that has left allies, including Israel, in a precarious geopolitical limbo.
* **Ukraine Shifts Maritime Strategy:** As part of its broader campaign to undermine Russian logistics and isolate occupied Crimea, Ukrainian forces have intensified strikes against Russian naval assets. By targeting critical sea routes, Kyiv aims to disrupt fuel supplies and exert increased pressure on the Kremlin to reconsider its military objectives.

## POLAND

* **Diplomatic Tensions with Russia:** The Russian Ministry of Foreign Affairs, represented by Maria Zakharova, has publicly criticized recent Polish military exercises near the border with Russia and Belarus. Moscow labeled the maneuvers an "irresponsible" provocation, highlighting the deteriorating security climate in the Baltic region.
* **Polish-Ukrainian Exhumation Dispute:** Tensions regarding historical memory persist as the Ukrainian government officially requested access to conduct search and exhumation works in several Polish locations. While Ukrainian officials express optimism about starting in August, the move remains a sensitive subject in bilateral relations, complicated by differing expectations regarding reciprocal research access on Ukrainian territory.
* **Internal Politics and Oversight:** The Law and Justice (PiS) party has implemented a strict ban on members participating in independent foundations and political associations. This move, which impacts organizations like Mateusz Morawiecki’s "Rozwój Plus," signals a tightening of party discipline amid ongoing shifts in the Polish political landscape.
