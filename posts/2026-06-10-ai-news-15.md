## WORLD

* **Middle East Tensions Surge:** The geopolitical climate remains volatile following a series of mutual strikes between the United States and Iran. President Trump has issued a stern warning, stating that Tehran will "pay the price" for delays in reaching a peace agreement, marking a significant escalation in the ongoing conflict.
* **Ukrainian Deep-Strike Operations:** Kyiv has reported successful military operations targeting high-value assets deep within Russian territory. The coordinated strikes reportedly hit a military manufacturing plant, an oil refinery, and a "shadow fleet" oil tanker, underscoring Ukraine's evolving long-range tactical capabilities.
* **Diplomatic Fallout in Kenya:** A growing political crisis in Kenya has erupted over the establishment of a U.S.-managed Ebola quarantine facility. The exclusive nature of the unit—reserved for American personnel—has sparked widespread public protests and intensified anti-Western sentiment in the region.

## POLAND

* **EU Funding Dispute:** A major diplomatic friction has emerged between Warsaw and Berlin regarding the allocation of 6.6 billion euros from the EU Peace Facility. While Germany advocates for directing the full sum to Ukraine, Poland is aggressively negotiating for the reimbursement of approximately 450 million euros related to its own military support for Kyiv.
* **Economic Milestone:** Despite regional instability, Poland is experiencing a rapid surge in its billionaire population, positioning the nation as one of Europe's fastest-growing luxury and investment hubs, according to the latest market reports.
* **Shift in Kiev-Warsaw Rhetoric:** In a move to mitigate recent historical tensions, the Ukrainian Foreign Ministry has issued an official appeal for unity. The statement emphasizes historical cooperation and shared security goals, signaling a potential diplomatic pivot to improve bilateral relations between Warsaw and Kyiv.
