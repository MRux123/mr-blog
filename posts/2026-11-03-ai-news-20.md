## WORLD

*   **Humanitarian Crisis Deepens in Ukraine:** The death toll continues to climb following a series of intensive Russian strikes targeting civilian infrastructure, including an aid center, a hospice, and a hospital. With at least 38 fatalities reported in the last 24 hours, President Zelensky has warned that rescue efforts are ongoing, with many more citizens feared trapped under debris.
*   **Aviation Security Fears Across Europe:** Authorities are investigating a string of suspected bomb threats targeting seven commercial flights across various European nations, including Poland, Spain, Bulgaria, and Italy. While all aircraft landed safely and no explosives were detected, the coordinated nature of the threats has triggered heightened security protocols across the continent.
*   **Political Shifts and Deportations:** The U.S. administration has initiated the deportation of dozens of Afghan immigrants back to Afghanistan, drawing sharp criticism from human rights organizations regarding the potential for persecution under Taliban rule.

## POLAND

*   **Security Reinforcement at the Eastern Border:** Prime Minister Donald Tusk has announced a strategic bolstering of security along the Polish-Russian border. The decision follows reports of “disturbing military movements” within the Kaliningrad exclave, raising concerns about regional stability.
*   **Constitutional Court Instability:** Domestic political tensions remain high as the government and the presidency engage in a public dispute over the functioning of the Constitutional Tribunal. Amidst calls for de-escalation, Prime Minister Tusk has proposed a direct meeting with the President to address the crisis and restore institutional order.
*   **Heightened Vigilance:** The regional instability has had direct domestic implications, with the Government Security Centre (RCB) issuing emergency alerts for eastern provinces due to aerial activity associated with the ongoing conflict in neighboring Ukraine, signaling a high state of readiness for the Polish defense apparatus.
