## WORLD

*   **Escalation in the Middle East:** U.S. military forces have launched a significant strike against Iranian military infrastructure, targeting critical assets including bridges and a primary control tower in the country's south. The Central Command maintains that the operation was focused exclusively on military targets, raising concerns about the potential for further regional destabilization.
*   **Political Shift in the UK:** Andy Burnham has officially been named the new Labour Party leader and is poised to assume the office of the Prime Minister. Burnham has signaled a major policy pivot, promising to prioritize the expansion of public control over essential services and a significant decentralization of authority toward local governments to catalyze economic growth.
*   **Ebola Crisis and Diplomatic Friction:** A brewing legal and diplomatic conflict has emerged in Kenya, where seven American aid workers have been placed in quarantine at a controversial facility following new U.S. travel restrictions. The incident has drawn sharp local opposition and sparked a legal battle regarding the state's authority to enforce such isolation measures.

## POLAND

*   **Political Pressure on PiS Leadership:** The Law and Justice (PiS) party is facing an internal ultimatum, with 41 MPs and four MEPs signing a formal letter addressed to party chairman Jarosław Kaczyński. The document underscores a collective demand for greater unity and strategic reform, following reports of high-level meetings between Kaczyński and former PM Mateusz Morawiecki.
*   **Infrastructure Crisis in Małopolska:** A major railway incident has occurred between Wierchomla and Piwnicza-Zdrój, where a freight train derailed, sending several wagons into the Poprad river. While local services are working to restore order, the accident has caused significant disruption to regional transit, necessitating the deployment of replacement bus communication.
*   **Corruption Allegations in Medical Sector:** Prosecutors have launched an investigation into the University Clinical Center of the Medical University of Warsaw following allegations of illicit financial gains linked to the operation of a private funeral home on hospital premises. The case highlights broader concerns regarding the intersection of public healthcare and private commercial interests.
