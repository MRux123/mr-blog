## WORLD

* **Rising Tensions with Russia:** Renowned chess grandmaster and opposition figure Garry Kasparov has issued a stern warning regarding Vladimir Putin’s long-term strategy. Kasparov suggests that Russia is preparing provocations at NATO’s land borders, not necessarily to seize territory, but to undermine the alliance's credibility. This sentiment is echoed by General Stanisław Koziej, who argues that NATO's current reaction to Russian aggression is too passive, proposing a more proactive stance, including a forward-deployed air defense zone.
* **Geological Crisis in Italy:** A significant earthquake has struck the Naples region, resulting in injuries and structural damage. Emergency services are currently assessing the impact on local infrastructure as the region deals with the aftermath of the seismic activity.
* **Military Aviation Incident:** A US Marine Corps F-35 fighter jet has crashed at a military base in San Diego, California. While the pilot successfully ejected, the state of their health remains under investigation.

## POLAND

* **82nd Anniversary of the Warsaw Uprising:** The nation is observing the 82nd anniversary of the Warsaw Uprising. President Karol Nawrocki led ceremonies in the capital, honoring the insurgents' commitment to independence and characterizing the uprising as a deliberate act of seeking freedom rather than a purely impulsive romantic gesture.
* **Political Protest in Kyiv:** Tensions persist in the Ukrainian capital as citizens continue to march against the recent dismissal of a government minister. The demonstrations underscore a growing divide between civil society and the current administration regarding political stability and governance.
