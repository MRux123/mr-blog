## WORLD

* **Solar Eclipse Across Europe:** As of today, regions spanning from Iceland to Spain are preparing for a total solar eclipse. The celestial event has drawn significant international attention, with sky-watchers gathering in key observation zones to witness the moon obscuring the sun.
* **Escalating Trade Friction:** The prospect of 50 percent tariffs on Canadian exports, proposed by the Trump administration, is creating a crisis for businesses. Some Canadian firms are reportedly considering relocating operations south of the border to the United States to ensure economic survival against potential trade barriers.
* **Security Concerns in Ecuadorian Waters:** Maritime security in the Pacific is under scrutiny following reports of mysterious, drone-equipped attackers targeting Ecuadorian fishing fleets, resulting in fatalities and raising questions about stability in the region.

## POLAND

* **Strengthening Naval Capabilities:** Prime Minister Donald Tusk has declared a "historic moment" for national defense, highlighting the commissioning of the new frigate ORP *Wicher*. The government aims to position Poland as a formidable maritime power in the Baltic Sea, emphasizing the strategic importance of domestic shipbuilding industries in deterring external threats.
* **Controversy Surrounding Marcin Romanowski:** The whereabouts of politician Marcin Romanowski remain a focal point of public discourse. Reports suggest he is currently in Tiraspol, Transnistria, from where he allegedly sent correspondence to a Warsaw court, further complicating ongoing legal proceedings.
* **Public Opinion on Migration Policy:** A recent IBRiS poll reveals that a significant majority of the Polish public supports the deportation of military-age Ukrainian men who are not legally employed within the country. This finding reflects shifting societal attitudes toward migration management amidst ongoing regional geopolitical instability.
