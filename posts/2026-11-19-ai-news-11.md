## WORLD

* **Historic Trilateral Summit in Shenzhen:** Xi Jinping, Donald Trump, and Vladimir Putin have concluded a high-stakes meeting focused on global security, energy stability, and international peace. Analysts suggest that while the meeting provides a diplomatic platform for the leaders, it reveals shifting power dynamics, with Putin appearing increasingly isolated compared to his counterparts.
* **Anthropic’s Record-Breaking IPO:** In a landmark moment for the artificial intelligence industry, Anthropic has raised $81 billion in its stock market debut. This move solidifies the company behind the Claude chatbot as one of the most valuable corporations globally, signaling massive market confidence in the future of AI development.
* **Violence Against Rohingya and UN Human Rights Report:** Legal debates over potential genocide in Myanmar continue to intensify as international courts weigh the evidence of systematic violence against the Rohingya. Simultaneously, a disturbing UN report highlights a global crisis in gender-based autonomy, revealing that one in four women worldwide remains unable to refuse unwanted sexual activity within their marriages, a figure that is significantly higher in parts of Africa and the Arab world.

## POLAND

* **Infrastructure Failure on the Central Rail Line (CMK):** A critical failure of the traffic control system has paralyzed one of Poland’s most important railway arteries. The disruption has led to significant delays exceeding 60 minutes and forced trains onto single-track operations, highlighting vulnerabilities in the country's transportation infrastructure.
* **Legal Breakthrough in Missing Person Case:** A long-standing mystery has reached a somber conclusion as authorities discovered the remains of Mateusz Bąkowski, missing since 2016, inside a submerged vehicle in a pond near Śrem. DNA analysis confirmed the identity, providing closure to the family while local authorities continue to investigate the circumstances surrounding the death.
* **Judicial Decision Concerning the Academy of Justice:** A court has issued a ruling regarding the status and operations of the Academy of Justice (*Akademia Wymiaru Sprawiedliwości*), a matter that has remained a point of political and legal contention in the Polish public sphere.
