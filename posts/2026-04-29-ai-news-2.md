## WORLD

*   **Former FBI Director James Comey Faces Criminal Charges:** US federal prosecutors have indicted James Comey over an Instagram post that authorities claim constitutes a threat against President Donald Trump. A grand jury has issued an arrest warrant for the former FBI director. This legal action marks the second set of charges brought against the high-profile critic of the administration, escalating tensions between the former official and the current executive branch.

## POLAND

*   **Tensions Within Poland 2050 Over Ministerial Accountability:** Minister of Climate Paulina Hennig-Kloska is set to appear before the Poland 2050 parliamentary club to address growing concerns regarding her performance. Katarzyna Pełczyńska-Nałęcz, head of the party, confirmed the meeting, noting that the agenda will focus on the urgent need to rectify flaws in the national deposit-refund system and the management of the "Czyste Powietrze" (Clean Air) program, signaling a critical period for the ministry.
