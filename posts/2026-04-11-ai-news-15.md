## WORLD

* **Breakthrough in US-Iran Relations:** In a historic move, high-level peace talks have commenced in Islamabad between the United States and Iran. Led by Vice President JD Vance, this diplomatic initiative marks the most significant face-to-face engagement since the 1979 Islamic Revolution, aiming to resolve the conflict that escalated on February 28th.
* **Tensions in the Strait of Hormuz:** While diplomatic channels remain open, military activity persists in the Gulf. President Donald Trump announced a "clearing operation" in the Strait of Hormuz, claiming that US naval forces have neutralized Iranian minelaying capabilities. Tehran has officially denied the presence of US vessels in the region, reflecting the fragile nature of the ongoing de-escalation efforts.

## POLAND

* **Political Accountability in Hungary:** Peter Magyar, leader of the opposition Tisza party, has clarified his stance on the future of Viktor Orbán. In a recent interview, Magyar emphasized that under his potential administration, justice would be handled exclusively by independent courts rather than by political decree, addressing public calls for the former leader's imprisonment.
* **Child Rescue in France:** Authorities in France have rescued a nine-year-old boy who was held captive in his father's van for approximately 18 months. The child is currently receiving medical care in an eastern French hospital.
* **Public Safety Incident:** A tragic accident occurred in Chrzanów (Lower Silesia) where an intoxicated driver collided with a train at an unguarded crossing. Both the driver and his 12-year-old son were hospitalized, prompting a renewed discussion regarding railway safety and impaired driving enforcement in the region.
