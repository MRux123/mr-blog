## WORLD

* **Trump halts planned Iran military strike:** Following requests from Gulf state leaders, including Saudi Arabia, Qatar, and the UAE, President Donald Trump has suspended a military operation against Iran originally scheduled for Tuesday. While diplomatic negotiations are currently prioritized, the White House maintains that the military option remains on the table should the talks with Tehran fail to yield a resolution.
* **Ebola resurgence in DRC:** The World Health Organization has officially declared an international emergency as a new Ebola outbreak in the Democratic Republic of the Congo has claimed at least 100 lives. Regional panic is rising, particularly in the Ituri province, where residents report a lack of vaccines for the specific Bundibudyo strain, complicating containment efforts six years after the previous regional outbreak.
* **Humanitarian crisis in Afghanistan:** Reports indicate a dire situation in Afghanistan, where systemic instability has left three out of four people unable to secure basic necessities. The economic collapse has forced many families into extreme measures, including the desperate sale of children to ensure survival.

## POLAND

* **Diplomatic shake-up with Budapest:** The Hungarian government has announced a significant shift in its diplomatic representation in Warsaw. Foreign Minister Anita Orban confirmed the recall of Ambassador Istvan Ijgyarto, who had been serving in the position since October 2024, signaling a potential "new opening" in Polish-Hungarian bilateral relations.
