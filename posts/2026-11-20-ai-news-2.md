## WORLD

* **Anthropic’s Market Debut:** Anthropic has officially become the first pure-play artificial intelligence company to list on the public stock market. This move represents a significant test for the AI industry, as investors weigh the long-term scalability of generative AI business models against intense global competition.
* **ICJ Ruling Impacts International Law:** A fresh ruling from the International Court of Justice regarding genocide allegations is set to influence ongoing legal debates. The decision is being closely monitored for its potential implications on the high-profile case brought against Israel concerning the conflict in Gaza.

## POLAND

* **NATO Strategy and Security Cooperation:** Polish Defense Minister Władysław Kosiniak-Kamysz and his German counterpart, Boris Pistorius, have emphasized a pivotal role for their nations in the development of the "NATO 3.0" security strategy. Both countries have pledged to strengthen transatlantic ties and share increased responsibility for regional stability.
* **Potential US Military Presence:** Negotiations regarding a permanent US military base in Poland have reached a critical stage. Deputy Defense Minister Cezary Tomczyk indicated that a formal decision or significant announcement from the Trump administration could be expected in early December, marking a potential shift in Central European defense posture.
