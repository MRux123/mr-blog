## WORLD

* **Historic U.S.-Iran Agreement Finalized:** In a surprise development following the G7 summit in Versailles, U.S. President Donald Trump and Iranian President Masoud Pezeshkian have signed a preliminary agreement aimed at de-escalating regional tensions. The deal includes a commitment to end active hostilities and establishes a framework for 60 days of intensive negotiations regarding Iran’s nuclear program. Central to the agreement is a $300 billion redevelopment package intended to stabilize the Iranian economy.
* **Japan Shifts Defense Policy:** Amid growing global instability, Japanese Defense Minister Shinjiro Koizumi has signaled a critical departure from the nation's post-World War II pacifist posture. Koizumi emphasized that Japan must significantly ramp up its defense capabilities to effectively deter the threat of war, marking a major shift in Asian security architecture.

## POLAND

* **Domestic Reaction to Washington-Tehran Deal:** Polish officials are closely monitoring the developments regarding the U.S.-Iran agreement. Following the announcement by President Trump, national media outlets, including RMF24, have highlighted the "digital" nature of the signatures exchanged between the American and Iranian leaderships. The Polish government is expected to issue a formal statement assessing the impact of this rapprochement on Middle Eastern stability and global energy security.
