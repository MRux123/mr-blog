## WORLD

* **US Military Actions in Iran Under UN Scrutiny:** UN experts have formally raised concerns regarding potential war crimes following US strikes on a school in Minab and a sports complex in Lamerd. The investigation highlights instances involving civilian casualties, labeling the failures in target verification as exceeding mere negligence.
* **Geopolitical Standoff at the UN:** Tensions between global powers have peaked as Russia and China exercised their veto power to block a US-led initiative aimed at renewing UN monitoring of Iran’s nuclear program. This diplomatic deadlock reflects the deepening divide regarding US pressure tactics on Tehran.
* **Macron Convenes Emergency Security Summit:** In response to the deteriorating international security landscape—characterized by instability in the Middle East and the ongoing war in Ukraine—French President Emmanuel Macron has summoned political party leaders to address the urgent implications for European energy security and defense.

## POLAND

* **Economic Confrontation with Moscow:** The Russian government has issued a decree effectively seizing control of the local assets of major Western corporations, including Auchan and Nestle. This move signals a significant escalation in the economic conflict between the Kremlin and international business entities operating in the region.
* **EU Financial Support for Kyiv:** Following a direct communication between President Volodymyr Zelensky and European Commission President Ursula von der Leyen, a major new financial aid package for Ukraine has been officially announced, underscoring the European Union’s continued commitment to supporting the war-torn country.
* **Internal Political Realignment:** The "Poland 2050" party has confirmed a transition in its leadership, appointing a new head for its parliamentary club as the party seeks to refine its political strategy amidst evolving domestic challenges.
