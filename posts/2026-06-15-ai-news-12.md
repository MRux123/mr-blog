## WORLD

*   **US-Iran Diplomatic Breakthrough:** The United States and Iran have reached a preliminary agreement aimed at reopening the strategically vital Strait of Hormuz. While the deal has prompted a positive reaction in global markets, with shares surging and oil prices stabilizing, analysts remain cautious as the framework defers resolution of the most contentious geopolitical issues to future negotiations.
*   **Escalation in Ukraine:** A significant Russian missile and drone strike has caused extensive damage to a historic Orthodox cathedral in Kyiv. This attack marks a grim intensification of the conflict, occurring as Ukraine prepares to initiate formal negotiations for European Union membership, highlighting the immense challenges on its path toward Western integration.

## POLAND

*   **Security Incident in Biała Podlaska:** A massive police manhunt is currently underway following a fatal shooting in Biała Podlaska. The incident, in which a pedestrian was killed, has triggered an emergency security operation across the Lublin region as authorities search for the perpetrator.
*   **Resolution of Olympic Medalist Funding:** The Polish Olympic Committee (PKOl) has officially resolved the controversy surrounding financial premiums for athletes who competed in Milan. Following the collapse in value of promised cryptocurrency tokens, the PKOl has successfully replaced them with cash payments, secured through contributions from private sponsors, effectively closing a major PR crisis for the organization.
