## WORLD

*   **Conflict Escalation in the Middle East:** Tensions have reached a critical juncture as Iran and the United States trade strikes. A significant drone attack has struck civilian infrastructure in Kuwait, including its international airport, resulting in at least one confirmed fatality. The situation remains volatile, with diplomatic channels appearing largely ineffective at containing the growing regional hostilities.
*   **Expansion of the Russia-Ukraine War:** The conflict has further intensified, with Ukraine launching strikes on a Russian naval base and an oil terminal in St. Petersburg. Simultaneously, tragedy struck in Russian-occupied Crimea, where seven people were killed after a drone attack targeted a civilian bus traveling between Moscow and Simferopol.

## POLAND

*   **Diplomatic Pressure on Russia:** Poland, alongside a coalition of ten other nations, has formally requested that the European Commission implement a total blockade on visas for Russian citizens. This move represents a significant hardening of stance toward Moscow, reflecting a coordinated regional push for increased isolation of the Russian state.
*   **Search for Diplomatic Mediation:** Reports suggest an emerging sentiment within European capitals, specifically Berlin and Paris, that a "window of dialogue" regarding the war in Ukraine may be slowly opening. An anonymous German official indicated that Europe is positioning itself to play a more proactive role in future peace negotiations.
*   **Legal Controversies in the Judiciary:** A high-profile legal scandal continues to unfold, as former lawyer Paweł K. faces new narcotics charges while his wife, also a practicing lawyer, has been detained by the Regional Prosecutor's Office in Krakow. The case is linked to a prior, infamous fatal traffic accident involving the couple.
