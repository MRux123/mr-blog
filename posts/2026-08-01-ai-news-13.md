## WORLD

*   **Escalating Conflict in Kyiv:** Russian forces have launched a devastating series of ballistic missile strikes on the Ukrainian capital, resulting in nine confirmed fatalities. This surge in violence coincides with reports of a shifting geopolitical landscape, as U.S. President Trump appears to have withdrawn support for previous commitments regarding Ukraine’s air-defense capabilities, leaving the country’s defensive infrastructure increasingly vulnerable.
*   **Mountaineering Tragedy in Pakistan:** The global climbing community is in mourning following a catastrophic avalanche on Broad Peak. Among the ten casualties is the renowned British-Nepali mountaineer Nirmal Purja, famous for his record-breaking ascent of the world's 14 highest peaks. 
*   **Geopolitical Tensions in the Middle East:** Kuwaiti authorities report successfully intercepting Iranian drones amidst a period of heightened instability. The situation remains volatile as regional embassies prepare for potential further escalation, following threats from the U.S. administration to intensify campaigns against Tehran.

## POLAND

*   **Commemorating the Wola Massacre:** On the eve of the 82nd anniversary of the Warsaw Uprising, Karol Nawrocki delivered a poignant address at the monument commemorating the Wola Massacre. He criticized the international community's lack of accountability regarding Nazi war crimes and emphasized the necessity of confronting historical atrocities to ensure future justice.
*   **Regional Security and Migration Crisis:** In response to the massive influx of approximately 60,000 migrants in the Spanish enclave of Ceuta, a coalition of 22 EU leaders—including Polish Prime Minister Donald Tusk—has issued a formal warning. The group is demanding an urgent European council meeting to address hybrid threats and develop a more robust, collective approach to border security.
