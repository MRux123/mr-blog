## WORLD

*   **Political Scandal in Ukraine:** Andriy Yermak, the former chief of staff to President Volodymyr Zelenskyy, has been released from detention after posting a record-breaking bail of over 140 million hryvnias (approx. 13 million PLN). The high-profile corruption case continues to send shockwaves through the Ukrainian political establishment.
*   **Seismic Disaster in China:** A powerful earthquake with a magnitude exceeding 5.0 has struck the city of Liuzhou. While rescue efforts are ongoing, authorities confirmed multiple fatalities and widespread infrastructure collapse, including the miraculous recovery of a 91-year-old survivor from the debris.
*   **Geopolitical Shifts in the Middle East:** Ongoing instability and blocked shipping routes are forcing nations to seek alternative trade corridors. Syria is emerging as a critical, albeit controversial, economic nexus in the region due to its strategic geography, as global powers scramble to navigate the current maritime crisis.

## POLAND

*   **Judicial Leadership Crisis:** Dagmara Pawełczyk-Woicka has resigned from her position as the head of the National Council of the Judiciary (KRS). Her departure follows mounting friction between the judiciary, the Constitutional Tribunal, and the current parliamentary administration.
*   **Industrial Unrest:** Major labor strikes have paralyzed production at three separate facilities of a key international mining equipment supplier. Workers in Polkowice, Truskolasy, and Siemianowice Śląskie have halted operations, demanding significant wage increases amidst rising economic pressures.
*   **Political Controversy Surrounding Investigations:** The domestic political scene remains tense following the detention of the husband of MP Izabela Bodnar. Maciej B. faces serious charges related to the surveillance of local government officials, sparking calls for transparency and formal explanations from the Centrum parliamentary club.
