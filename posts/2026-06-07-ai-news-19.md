## WORLD

* **Escalation in the Middle East:** Despite recent efforts for a US-brokered truce, the security situation in Lebanon has deteriorated significantly. Israel conducted targeted strikes on the southern suburbs of Beirut, describing them as retaliatory measures against Hezbollah’s aggression. With Iran threatening a "painful response," the region faces a renewed cycle of violence.
* **Geopolitical Realignment:** President Volodymyr Zelensky has arrived in London for high-stakes security talks with leaders from the United Kingdom, France, and Germany. These discussions are critical as European allies seek to coordinate defense strategies amidst shifting priorities in the United States under President Donald Trump, who is increasingly focused on the ongoing diplomatic standoff with Iran.

## POLAND

* **Controversy Over Presidential Honors:** A significant domestic debate has emerged following calls by the head of the Institute of National Remembrance, Karol Nawrocki, to strip Ukrainian President Volodymyr Zelensky of the Order of the White Eagle. Public figures, including Professor Grzegorz Motyka, have cautioned against such a move, labeling it an overreaction that could damage Polish-Ukrainian relations.
* **Tragic Loss in the Tatra Mountains:** A search and rescue operation in the High Tatras concluded in tragedy as mountain rescuers recovered the body of a 25-year-old Polish climber. The victim, who had successfully reached the summit of Staroleśny Szczyt shortly before the accident, fell into the Kwietnikowy Żleb, marking a somber end to the weekend for the climbing community.
