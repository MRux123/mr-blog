## WORLD

*   **Third Anniversary of October 7 Attacks:** Israel marks the third anniversary of the Hamas-led incursions. Memorial events are being held across the nation, reflecting on the tragedy that triggered the ongoing conflict in Gaza and continues to reshape regional geopolitics.
*   **Spanish Housing Crisis Symbol Passes Away:** Maricarmen Abascal, the 87-year-old pensioner whose forced eviction from her home of 70 years ignited nationwide protests and influenced Spain's political landscape, has died. Her case remains a central point of contention in the country's housing policy debate.
*   **Canadian Policy Shift on MAID:** Canada has officially halted plans to expand Medical Assistance in Dying (MAID) to individuals whose sole underlying condition is mental illness, highlighting the ongoing ethical complexities surrounding end-of-life care legislation.

## POLAND

*   **Security Alert: FBI Foils Mall of America Plot:** International security agencies, including the FBI, have thwarted a potential terror attack targeting the Mall of America. An 18-year-old resident of Minnesota was apprehended in connection with the plot, drawing global attention to domestic security threats.
*   **Trump Addresses Polish Governance:** In a notable international exchange, former U.S. President Donald Trump responded to inquiries from Polish media (RMF FM) regarding the potential for government turnover in Poland, sparking discussions on the influence of U.S. political discourse on Polish internal affairs.
