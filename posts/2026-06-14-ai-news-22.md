## WORLD

*   **US-Iran Cease-Fire Agreement:** President Donald Trump has announced a preliminary peace agreement with Iran. The deal includes the immediate lifting of the blockade on Iranian ports and the reopening of the Strait of Hormuz. While the administration frames this as a major diplomatic breakthrough, official confirmation from Tehran remains pending.
*   **G7 Summit Tensions:** Ahead of the G7 summit in Geneva, security forces were forced to intervene against anti-summit protesters. Swiss police utilized water cannons and tear gas to disperse crowds, highlighting growing civil unrest surrounding global economic governance.

## POLAND

*   **Expansion of US Military Presence:** President Karol Nawrocki, currently on a diplomatic visit to the United States, has indicated that the strengthening of American troop presence in Poland remains a top strategic priority. Following discussions surrounding the administration of President Trump, Nawrocki expressed confidence that the US is receptive to expanding military cooperation in the region to bolster national security.
