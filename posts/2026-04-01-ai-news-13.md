## WORLD

* **Middle East Crisis Evolution:** The conflict involving Iran has reached a critical juncture as President Trump signaled a potential wind-down of operations within weeks, while simultaneously confirming that the Iranian regime has requested a ceasefire. The geopolitical landscape is shifting further with the UAE reportedly preparing for a military intervention to secure the Strait of Hormuz, marking a departure from its traditional neutrality.
* **NATO and US Strategic Realignment:** The stability of the Western alliance is under scrutiny following reports of President Trump’s frustration with allies regarding the Iran offensive. The President has suggested a potential US withdrawal from NATO, prompting concerns from global security experts about a wider American strategy aimed at reducing its military footprint in Europe and the Arctic.
* **Escalation in Lebanon:** Israel continues to intensify military operations in Lebanon, moving beyond traditional Hezbollah-controlled zones. Reports indicate a push for large-scale evacuations in southern regions, raising international concerns about the displacement of civilian populations.

## POLAND

* **Political Stalemate:** A new United Surveys poll for Wirtualna Polska reveals a complete deadlock in the Sejm. With six parties currently holding enough support to enter parliament, neither of the two major political blocks would be able to form a stable majority, signaling a period of significant legislative instability.
* **The End of an Era in Mining:** The symbolic closure and liquidation of the "Wujek" coal mine in Katowice marks a historic moment for the Polish industrial sector. Having operated for 127 years, the site served as a landmark for both the country's economic history and its labor movements.
* **Sovereignty and Defense Debate:** In response to shifting American foreign policy, prominent Polish political figures are calling for a "Polonization" of defense capabilities. Former Prime Minister Mateusz Morawiecki emphasized the necessity for Poland to prioritize national production and self-reliance over complete dependency on international security guarantees, reflecting growing unease regarding the future of the Article 5 commitment.
