## WORLD

* **Sudan Conflict Escalates:** A recent BBC investigation has revealed that Sudan’s Rapid Support Forces (RSF) are deliberately targeting hospitals. The United Nations human rights chief, Volker Türk, has condemned these actions, warning that they may constitute war crimes under international law.
* **Starlink and Geopolitics:** Donald Trump has publicly claimed that Elon Musk has restored full access to Ukraine’s Starlink network. This development is significant as Ukraine remains heavily reliant on the satellite system for military operations, despite previous denials from Musk regarding the restriction of services.
* **Economic Impact of GLP-1 Drugs:** Beauty conglomerate L’Oreal has officially identified the rise of weight-loss drugs (GLP-1s) as a "very important phenomenon" affecting global sales in the beauty and skincare sector, signaling a shift in consumer behavior linked to medical advancements.

## POLAND

* **Airspace Security Measures:** Following a heavy aerial assault on Western Ukraine, the Polish Government Security Center (RCB) issued an urgent alert informing the public that Polish and allied aviation are currently operating within Polish airspace to ensure the security of the country's borders.
* **Political Realignment:** In a surprising political move, Law and Justice (PiS) MP Marcin Warchoł has offered public praise for the outgoing minister Katarzyna Pełczyńska-Nałęcz. Warchoł, appearing on *Debata Gozdyry*, suggested that the minister was pushed out for being "inconvenient" and did not rule out the possibility of her joining the PiS parliamentary club, highlighting potential shifts in political alliances.
