## WORLD

* **State Funeral and Regional Instability:** World leaders have gathered in Iran for the funeral ceremonies of Supreme Leader Ali Khamenei. The transition of power comes at a time of heightened regional volatility, exacerbated by revelations that the U.S. previously intervened to prevent Israeli operations against high-ranking Iranian officials, which could have derailed fragile peace negotiations.
* **Escalating Pressure on Russia:** The conflict in Ukraine has intensified, with Crimea emerging as a critical focal point. Increased drone strikes, coupled with severe fuel shortages, are signaling an erosion of stability within the region, further straining the Russian government's internal narrative of normalcy.
* **Geopolitical Assertions:** China has officially defended its "Ethnic Unity" law, despite mounting international criticism from rights groups and Western officials who argue that the legislation poses a significant threat to the cultural autonomy of Tibetans and Uyghurs.

## POLAND

* **Diplomatic Strategy:** Polish Foreign Minister Radosław Sikorski has held high-level talks with his Ukrainian counterpart, Andrii Sybiha, focusing on defining the fundamental distinctions in the geopolitical trajectories of Russia and Ukraine. 
* **Institutional Reform:** In a significant move regarding domestic accountability, Cardinal Grzegorz Ryś has established a new commission tasked with investigating the sexual abuse of adults, marking a shift in how the Church addresses institutional misconduct.
* **Environmental Alerts:** Meteorological experts have issued urgent warnings regarding the impact of El Niño, which is expected to strike with unprecedented intensity, bringing extreme weather phenomena that threaten to disrupt agricultural and civil infrastructure.
