## WORLD

* **Middle East Cease-fire Extended:** President Donald Trump has announced a three-week extension of the cease-fire between Israel and Lebanon. The agreement, finalized during Oval Office talks, is viewed by the administration as a pivotal step toward broader regional stability and a potential pathway to de-escalating the ongoing conflict with Iran.
* **Aggressive Maritime Posture:** The U.S. has tightened its blockade against Iranian oil shipments, with Central Command reporting the interception of 33 vessels. This move coincides with rising tensions, as the U.S. military monitors Iranian forces amidst allegations of staged propaganda regarding ship seizures.
* **Diplomatic Shifts and Scandal:** In a surprising diplomatic maneuver, reports indicate that the Trump administration plans to invite Vladimir Putin to the upcoming G20 summit in Florida. Meanwhile, the Department of Justice has charged a U.S. soldier for allegedly using classified information to secure a $400,000 profit via betting on the political future of Venezuela's Nicolás Maduro.

## POLAND

* **Tragic Accident in Łódź Voivodeship:** A severe automotive accident occurred in Bolimów, near Skierniewice, where a vehicle plunged into a reservoir. While one passenger managed to escape and alert authorities, emergency services were required to extract three other individuals from the submerged car, all of whom required resuscitation.
