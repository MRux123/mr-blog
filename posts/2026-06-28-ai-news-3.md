## WORLD

*   **Conflict Escalation in the Middle East:** The security situation in the region has reached a critical juncture as the U.S. and Iran have engaged in a series of direct retaliatory strikes. The exchange, which the U.S. frames as a response to oil tanker attacks, has expanded to involve Iranian strikes on American infrastructure in Kuwait and Bahrain, severely testing the stability of existing ceasefires.
*   **Humanitarian Crisis in Venezuela:** Following twin earthquakes earlier this week, the death toll has climbed past 1,400. Emergency response efforts are currently being hampered by an influx of uncoordinated civilian volunteers, which has created logistical bottlenecks in the disaster zone.

## POLAND

*   **Diplomatic Summit in Jurata:** President Nawrocki is currently hosting a regional summit in Jurata, bringing together leaders from the Baltic states and Romania. While the official agenda focuses on NATO cooperation and support for Ukraine, observers note underlying diplomatic friction between Warsaw and Kyiv regarding historical policy and national honors.
*   **Political Shifts in Serbia:** In a surprise move that could impact Balkan regional stability, Serbian President Aleksandar Vučić announced his intention to resign within the coming weeks. This decision follows over a year of sustained mass protests against his administration.
