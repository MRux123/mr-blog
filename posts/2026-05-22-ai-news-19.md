## WORLD

*   **Trump Appoints New Federal Reserve Chair:** Donald Trump has officially sworn in Kevin Warsh as the new head of the US Federal Reserve. While Trump has publicly emphasized the need for Warsh’s independence, the appointment carries significant political weight due to Warsh's personal connections to billionaire Ronald Lauder, a close Trump ally. This move marks a pivotal shift in US economic policy management.
*   **Escalating Hostilities in Russia-Ukraine Conflict:** Vladimir Putin has vowed retaliation following allegations that Ukraine struck a student dormitory. This follows reports of a targeted Ukrainian strike on the Rubicon drone military unit located in Russian-occupied eastern Ukraine, signaling a dangerous intensification of cross-border military engagement.
*   **EU Rejects UK Post-Brexit Trade Proposal:** The European Union has formally dismissed a British proposal to establish a joint goods market. The plan, reportedly presented by high-ranking UK officials to rebuild trade relations, failed to gain traction, highlighting the persistent friction in post-Brexit diplomatic and economic negotiations.

## POLAND

*   **Massive Search Operation in Krakow:** A large-scale search effort is currently underway in Krakow for 12-year-old Elżbieta Wojtacka, who went missing on Thursday afternoon. Approximately 200 personnel, including police, firefighters, soldiers, and drone operators, are scouring the Wolski Forest area to locate the girl.
*   **Political Standoff Over WSI Archive:** A constitutional impasse has emerged between the Sejm, the Senate, and the President. Speakers Włodzimierz Czarzasty and Małgorzata Kidawa-Błońska have refused to issue opinions on the annex to the report regarding the liquidation of the Military Information Services (WSI), opting to return all relevant documentation to President Karol Nawrocki.
