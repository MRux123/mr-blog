## WORLD

* **Geopolitical Shifts in the Middle East**: The U.S. administration has signaled a pivot in its military posture regarding Iran. President Trump announced plans to wind down the current military campaign, with a formal address to the nation scheduled for Wednesday. This development comes amid escalating instability in the region, punctuated by a tragic security incident in Lebanon, where multiple Indonesian UN peacekeepers were killed by a roadside explosion.

* **India’s Historic Demographic Milestone**: India has officially launched an unprecedented, two-phase census operation aimed at documenting its population of over one billion people. The project, described as the most ambitious of its kind globally, will utilize three million officials to conduct a comprehensive count, providing critical data for the nation's future economic and social policy planning.
