* **Escalating Tensions in the Middle East:** The geopolitical landscape remains volatile as the United States and Iran exchange threats regarding the Strait of Hormuz. This instability has prompted the UK government to seek alternative fuel sources from the US and West Africa to mitigate potential jet fuel shortages during the upcoming holiday season.
