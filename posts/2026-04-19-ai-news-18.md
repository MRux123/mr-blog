## WORLD

* **Tragedy in Louisiana:** A horrific domestic disturbance in Shreveport, Louisiana, has resulted in the deaths of eight children, aged one to 14. Local law enforcement is currently investigating the incident, which has sent shockwaves through the community.
* **Geopolitical Standoff with Iran:** Tensions remain high as Tehran pushes back against announced U.S. negotiations mediated by Pakistan. With a two-week cease-fire set to expire, the international community remains concerned about the stability of the region amidst conflicting reports regarding the potential for diplomatic progress.
* **Political Shift in Bulgaria:** Preliminary exit poll results from Bulgaria indicate a victory for the Progressive Coalition, led by former President Rumen Radew. The nation, which has struggled with extreme political volatility, faces the prospect of its eighth parliamentary assembly in just five years.

## POLAND

* **Public Demand for Constitutional Clarity:** A recent United Surveys poll suggests that 56.6% of Poles believe President Karol Nawrocki should formally swear in the four disputed Constitutional Tribunal judges, highlighting growing public pressure for institutional resolution in the judiciary.
* **Fractures within Law and Justice (PiS):** Political analysts warn of a potential disintegration of the PiS party. Internal friction, characterized by public infighting between factions—most notably involving Mateusz Morawiecki’s new association, "Rozwój Plus"—has led experts to speculate that the party is facing its most significant existential crisis since 1993.
* **Diplomatic Tensions regarding Macron's Visit:** A diplomatic rift has emerged ahead of French President Emmanuel Macron’s visit to Gdańsk. Reports indicate that despite efforts by the Presidential Palace, a meeting between Macron and President Nawrocki will not take place, allegedly due to opposition from Prime Minister Donald Tusk’s administration.
