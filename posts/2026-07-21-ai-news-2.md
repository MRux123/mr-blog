## WORLD

* **Escalating Trade War with Canada:** The U.S. administration has imposed a 50% tariff on Canadian goods, prompting Mark Carney to vow an intensification of trade negotiations. This move represents a significant escalation in North American trade tensions, threatening cross-border supply chains.
* **Andy Burnham’s Leadership Debut:** Andy Burnham has officially assumed office as the seventh UK Prime Minister in a decade. His first day signals a departure from previous governance styles, as he seeks to stabilize a turbulent political landscape.
* **Diplomatic Stand-off Over Netanyahu:** A potential diplomatic crisis has emerged as U.S. political figures debate the arrest of Israeli Prime Minister Benjamin Netanyahu during his upcoming visit to the UN General Assembly in New York, with the U.S. executive branch taking a firm stance against such actions.

## POLAND

* **Constitutional Crisis at the Tribunal:** The President of the Constitutional Tribunal, Bogdan Święczkowski, has formally demanded explanations from the National Police Commander regarding an intervention by police and prosecutors at the Tribunal’s headquarters during an ongoing session of the General Assembly.
* **Political Shifts in Hungary Impacting Region:** In a surprising development for regional politics, legendary chess grandmaster Judit Polgar has declined an offer from Hungarian Prime Minister Peter Magyar to become the country's president, despite broad support for her candidacy.
* **Systemic Property Rights Issues:** A troubling case from Gdynia has highlighted systemic failures in the Polish real estate market, where homeowners remain burdened by a developer's debt 16 years after purchase, despite having paid in full, drawing renewed attention from housing rights advocates.
