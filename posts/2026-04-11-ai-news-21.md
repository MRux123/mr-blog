## WORLD

*   **US-Iran Peace Negotiations:** High-stakes diplomatic talks between the United States and Iran are underway in Islamabad. Vice President JD Vance and senior Iranian officials are engaged in marathon sessions aimed at securing a permanent truce, with the contentious status of the Strait of Hormuz serving as a primary point of friction.
*   **UK National Mobilization Planning:** In response to an increasingly unstable geopolitical climate, the British government is revising its comprehensive mobilization plans. Sir Richard Knighton, head of the armed forces, has signaled that the era of sustained peace has concluded, prompting the state to prepare its military, industrial, and medical sectors for potential wartime operations.
*   **NYC Security Incident:** A violent confrontation occurred at Grand Central Terminal in New York City, where an individual wielding a machete and claiming to be "Lucifer" attacked three civilians. NYPD officers neutralized the assailant after he failed to comply with repeated commands to drop the weapon.

## POLAND

*   **Zelenskyy Proposes Extended Truce:** Ukrainian President Volodymyr Zelenskyy has officially proposed that the temporary ceasefire observed during the Easter holiday be extended, urging Russia to maintain the cessation of hostilities beyond the festive period.
*   **Constitutional Court Crisis Debate:** Former President Bronisław Komorowski has weighed in on the ongoing dispute regarding the Polish Constitutional Court. He suggested that the deadlock is likely to persist until changes in presidential appointments alter the internal composition of the court, potentially enabling a change in leadership.
*   **Political Tensions Ahead of Hungarian Elections:** Zbigniew Ziobro, former Polish Minister of Justice, has voiced his support for Viktor Orbán ahead of the Hungarian elections. Ziobro criticized opposition figures, framing the upcoming vote as a pivotal moment for national sovereignty against perceived European Union overreach.
