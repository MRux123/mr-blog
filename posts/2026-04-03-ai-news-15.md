## WORLD

*   **U.S. Military Jet Downed Over Iran:** International tensions have reached a critical juncture as U.S. officials confirm an American fighter jet was downed over Iranian territory. While the status of the crew remains uncertain, the incident has triggered urgent search and rescue operations, further destabilizing the already volatile Middle East security architecture.
*   **Military Rule in Burkina Faso:** Capt. Ibrahim Traoré has signaled a definitive pivot away from democratic governance in Burkina Faso. Declaring that democratic structures are fundamentally incompatible with the nation's current needs, the leadership's stance marks a significant shift in the Sahel’s political landscape, signaling long-term entrenchment of military authority.

## POLAND

*   **Constitutional Impasse at the Sejm:** A deepening conflict regarding the appointment of Constitutional Tribunal judges has pitted the Chancellery of the Sejm against President Karol Nawrocki. The dispute centers on the President's refusal to swear in all selected candidates, with the Sejm formally accusing the head of state of abusing his constitutional powers.
*   **Cultural Tragedy in Warsaw:** A historic landmark in Warsaw, the papal cross—a centerpiece associated with Pope John Paul II’s inaugural visit to Poland—has been destroyed by fire on Good Friday. Authorities are currently investigating the circumstances surrounding the blaze to determine if it was accidental or a deliberate act of destruction.
