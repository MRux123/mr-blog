## WORLD

*   **US Conducts Targeted Strike on Iranian Launchers:** In a significant escalation, US forces have launched a military strike against Iranian missile launchers located on Larak Island. This marks the first direct US attack on Iranian soil since late July, resulting in reported civilian casualties. The move underscores rising regional instability in the Middle East.
*   **Humanitarian Crisis in Nepal Following Devastating Floods:** Authorities in Nepal have begun preparations for mass burials as the death toll from catastrophic flooding continues to climb. With over 2,500 people still missing and only a small fraction recovered, officials warn that the final toll is expected to be substantially higher as rescue operations struggle to navigate the aftermath.
*   **Grand Canyon Flash Flooding:** Emergency rescue teams were deployed to the Grand Canyon following "terrifying" flash floods that left hikers stranded and missing. The event, described by survivors as an abrupt shift from a clear day to a life-threatening torrent, highlights the increasing unpredictability of extreme weather events in national parks.

## POLAND

*   **Airspace Violation Near Transnistria:** The Polish and international security community is monitoring a security breach in Moldova, where an unidentified aircraft entered national airspace from the direction of Ukraine. The object, which appeared near the breakaway region of Transnistria, remained in Moldovan territory for approximately ten minutes before returning along the same flight path, triggering a high-alert status among regional defense forces.
