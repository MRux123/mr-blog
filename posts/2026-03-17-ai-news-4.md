## WORLD

* **Escalation in the Persian Gulf:** The regional security crisis has intensified following a drone and missile attack on the US Embassy in Baghdad, described as the most significant strike since the outbreak of hostilities. This follows reports of a missile strike on a tanker near Fujairah, further destabilizing the critical maritime routes in the Strait of Hormuz.
* **Diplomatic Fissures:** European allies, led by the UK, Germany, and France, continue to distance themselves from US military initiatives in the region. Finnish President Alexander Stubb highlighted that the lack of prior consultation regarding US actions against Iran remains the primary barrier to unified Western support.
* **Humanitarian Crisis in South Sudan:** Violence has reached a critical tipping point in Jonglei state, with approximately 50,000 civilians forced to flee their homes as the military prepares for a major offensive. Reports from the ground describe widespread devastation and fear of an impending civil war.

## POLAND

* **Strategic Standoff:** Polish media and political analysis underscore the growing rift between the US and its European partners regarding the confrontation with Iran. The reluctance of key European capitals to engage in the Strait of Hormuz crisis is being framed as a consequence of President Trump’s unilateral decision-making style, which leaves Poland and its neighbors in a precarious geopolitical position.
* **Security Implications:** The attacks on both the US diplomatic mission in Baghdad and commercial shipping vessels are being treated by regional observers as signs of a rapidly deteriorating security environment that threatens both global energy supplies and the stability of the entire Middle Eastern theater.
