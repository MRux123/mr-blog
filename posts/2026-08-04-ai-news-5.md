## WORLD

* **Escalating Tensions in the Black Sea:** A merchant vessel bound for Turkey has been struck by drones, marking a significant escalation in regional instability. The attack underscores the persistent volatility of maritime shipping routes in the conflict zone, raising concerns about supply chain security and international maritime law.
* **EU Border Policy Crisis:** Following intensified migrant crossings in Ceuta, European Commission President Ursula von der Leyen has called for an urgent meeting of EU interior ministers. The bloc is under increasing pressure to bolster external border security and develop a more cohesive response to migratory flows.

## POLAND

* **Drone Warfare Intensifies on the Eastern Front:** The conflict between Ukraine and Russia has seen a dramatic flare-up, with Ukrainian drones targeting the Moscow region. In a retaliatory move, Russia launched a significant attack on Sumy, signaling that the intensity of the war remains at a critical peak with no signs of de-escalation.
* **Large-Scale Smuggling Interdiction:** Polish authorities have successfully thwarted a massive smuggling operation, apprehending the perpetrators in the act. The operation highlights the ongoing efforts of law enforcement agencies to secure domestic borders against organized crime syndicates.
