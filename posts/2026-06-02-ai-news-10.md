## WORLD

* **Escalation in Ukraine:** Russia has launched a series of massive, coordinated strikes against cities across Ukraine. The attacks have resulted in at least 18 confirmed fatalities, including children, as residential areas were struck by Russian forces. The timing of the assault follows a week of direct threats from Moscow, suggesting a strategic effort to maximize the psychological impact on the Ukrainian capital.
* **Middle East Tensions:** Despite signals that both Israel and Hezbollah have accepted a partial U.S.-brokered ceasefire plan, clashes persist on the ground. The conflict remains fluid, with U.S. intervention aimed at preventing a wider regional escalation—specifically targeting the potential for an Israeli strike on Beirut—as diplomatic channels struggle to maintain momentum in peace negotiations.

## POLAND

* **Educational Reform:** The Polish government is set to move forward with a controversial and significant amendment to education law, which would mandate a total ban on the use of smartphones within school facilities. Prime Minister Donald Tusk has acknowledged the difficulty of implementing such a measure, framing it as a necessary step to address common behavioral challenges faced by schools throughout the country.
* **New Military Leadership:** Gen. div. pil. Ireneusz Nowak has been appointed as the new Operational Commander of the Polish Armed Forces. The nomination, finalized on Tuesday by the President, marks a transition in military command, highlighting the strategic importance of modernizing Poland’s aerial capabilities, notably underscored by the recent integration of F-35 fighters into the Polish defense system.
