## WORLD

* **U.S.-Saudi Nuclear Agreement Risks Regional Stability:** The United States is reportedly finalizing a deal to provide Saudi Arabia with a civilian nuclear program. While the agreement marks a significant diplomatic milestone, it has sparked immediate alarm in Israel and among regional observers, who fear it could trigger a nuclear arms race across the Middle East.
* **Escalation in the Strait of Hormuz:** President Trump has issued a stern warning to Iran, threatening retaliatory strikes against civilian targets, including infrastructure in Tehran, should Iranian forces attempt to disrupt navigation in the Strait of Hormuz. The rhetoric marks a dangerous peak in current U.S.-Iran hostilities.
* **Diplomatic Standoff Over Ebola Quarantines:** A legal and humanitarian crisis is unfolding in Kenya, where seven American aid workers are being held in an isolation facility following new U.S. travel restrictions. The facility, which faces intense local opposition and ongoing legal challenges, has become a focal point of international friction regarding pandemic management protocols.

## POLAND

* **"Szpiegowo" Redevelopment Project:** The Warsaw municipal government has announced a major urban renewal initiative for the notorious "Szpiegowo" complex (Sobieskiego 100). The site is set to be transformed into 180 municipal residential units, signaling a long-awaited resolution for the iconic post-Soviet property.
* **Political Maneuvering Within PiS:** Law and Justice (PiS) leadership has officially clarified its position regarding the upcoming elections, naming Przemysław Czarnek as their candidate for Prime Minister. Spokesman Rafał Bochenek formally dismissed speculative reports suggesting that Zbigniew Bogucki was being considered for the role, aiming to unify the party line.
* **Deteriorating Public Sentiment:** Recent data from the CBOS polling institute highlights a deepening pessimism among the Polish public. Currently, 50 percent of respondents believe the country is heading in the wrong direction, reflecting a clear trend of declining social optimism regarding the current political and economic climate.
