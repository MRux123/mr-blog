## WORLD

*   **Red Sea Instability:** Houthi militants have claimed responsibility for fresh attacks on oil tankers, prompting an aggressive series of retaliatory strikes by the United States. This escalation marks the most significant regional disruption since the group declared a maritime embargo.
*   **Strategic Drone Strikes in Russia:** Ukrainian forces have successfully targeted major retail logistics hubs belonging to Wildberries, Russia’s leading online retailer. These persistent drone strikes are placing unprecedented strain on Russian internal supply chains and commercial infrastructure.
*   **Diplomatic Shadowboxing:** US Secretary of State Marco Rubio and Russian Foreign Minister Sergey Lavrov held an unannounced, closed-door meeting in Manila. While the specific agenda remains undisclosed, the high-level contact underscores a critical moment in ongoing international tensions.

## POLAND

*   **Political Turbulence within PiS:** Speculation regarding a potential fracture within the Law and Justice (PiS) party has intensified. Party figurehead Jacek Sasin is scheduled for a high-profile interview to address growing rumors of internal divisions that threaten the party's cohesion.
*   **Juvenile Cybercrime Network:** Polish law enforcement has dismantled a criminal operation involving the sale of DDoS attack services. Authorities have expressed alarm as investigations revealed that the youngest participant in the cyber-attack ring was only 12 years old.
