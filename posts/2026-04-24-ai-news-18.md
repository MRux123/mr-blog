## WORLD

* **High-Stakes Iran Negotiations:** As regional tensions persist, diplomatic efforts intensify with U.S. envoys Jared Kushner and Steve Witkoff set to meet with Iranian officials in Pakistan. This follows reports that Iran’s foreign minister, Abbas Araghchi, is also coordinating a series of diplomatic visits in the region. The complexity of Iran’s internal power structure remains a significant variable in these international dialogues.
* **Justice in Kosovo:** A court in Kosovo has delivered significant sentences to three Serbian nationals following a deadly 2023 gun battle and monastery siege. The verdict—including two life sentences—marks a pivotal moment in the legal accountability process regarding recent regional security volatility.

## POLAND

* **Crisis of Civil Protection Infrastructure:** A nationwide audit of emergency shelters has been ordered following investigative reporting by RMF FM. The probe revealed that a significant portion of locations listed in the official "gdziesięukryć.pl" database are currently inaccessible to the public, raising serious concerns regarding the efficacy of national civil defense readiness.
* **Geopark Preservation Dispute:** Local communities and environmental advocates are mounting strong opposition against plans to establish a quarry within the Kraina Wygasłych Wulkanów, a UNESCO Global Geopark. Protestors argue the development poses an existential threat to the region's unique geological heritage, pitting economic development against ecological preservation.
