## WORLD

* **Death of Senator Lindsey Graham:** The political landscape in the United States is reeling following the sudden death of 71-year-old Senator Lindsey Graham. A prominent figure and close ally of Donald Trump, Graham’s passing has drawn tributes from high-ranking officials. The event has also triggered hostile reactions from Russian state media, which noted his status on Moscow's list of "terrorists and extremists."
* **Government Shake-up in Ukraine:** President Volodymyr Zelensky has announced significant changes within the Ukrainian government, signaling a potential broader restructuring of his cabinet. Among the notable developments is the anticipated dismissal of Prime Minister Yulia Svyrydenko, raising questions about the stability and future direction of the administration amid ongoing reconstruction efforts.
* **Escalation of Fraud Scandal in Nigeria:** A major political crisis is unfolding in Nigeria after it was discovered that a fictitious federal agency, created by a private individual, was allocated over £700,000 in the 2026 national budget. The revelation has sparked public outcry and an official presidential investigation into how such a body was granted state funding.

## POLAND

* **Formation of 'Unia Centrum':** The Polish political scene has seen the formal registration of a new party, *Unia Centrum*. According to MEP Michał Kobosko, the entity is the result of weeks of negotiations between two distinct political circles, marking a significant consolidation in the centrist wing of the government coalition.
* **Governmental Tensions and Security:** The Polish government is currently navigating complex diplomatic waters, with defense officials like Cezary Tomczyk addressing the friction in military cooperation with Ukraine caused by lingering historical disputes. Simultaneously, the Ministry of Foreign Affairs has issued a high-level travel warning for Qatar, urging citizens to exercise extreme caution due to heightened regional tensions in the Middle East.
