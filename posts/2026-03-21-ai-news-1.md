## WORLD

* **Middle East Conflict Intensifies:** Recent analysis reveals that Iranian retaliatory strikes against U.S.-linked bases have resulted in approximately $800 million in damages. While President Trump has publicly claimed the U.S. is "obliterating the other side," he has simultaneously signaled a potential interest in "winding down" the conflict, marking a shift in the administration's public rhetoric regarding a cease-fire.
* **Energy Markets and Sanctions:** In a strategic move to stabilize soaring global gas prices, the U.S. Treasury Department has officially lifted sanctions on specific tranches of Iranian oil. This policy reversal is expected to inject an additional 140 million barrels of oil into the market, aimed at curbing inflation and easing global energy volatility.
* **Legal Setback for Musk:** A San Francisco jury has delivered a verdict against Elon Musk, finding that the billionaire misled Twitter investors in 2022. The ruling highlights significant legal and financial accountability for the tech mogul's past public statements.

## POLAND

* **Diplomatic Tensions with Washington:** President Donald Trump has expressed overt dissatisfaction with Ukraine's stance in the ongoing U.S.-Iran conflict. During an interview with MS NOW, Trump criticized President Volodymyr Zelenskyy, accusing Ukraine of failing to support American strategic interests and dismissing recent Ukrainian official statements as merely performative. This signals a potential cooling of relations between Warsaw's key Western allies, which remains a focal point for Polish foreign policy analysts.
