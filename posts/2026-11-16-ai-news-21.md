## WORLD

*   **Major Escalation in Ukraine:** Russia has reported the largest Ukrainian drone offensive of the conflict, involving over 2,000 UAVs launched in a single day. The strikes have caused significant damage, including to the landmark Crimea bridge, marking a critical intensification of hostilities.
*   **EU Outsourcing Asylum Processing:** Five European nations—Germany, Austria, the Netherlands, Greece, and Denmark—have formally announced plans to establish a "return hub" in Rwanda. This pilot scheme, designed to house failed asylum seekers, represents a controversial shift in EU migration policy, drawing immediate criticism regarding its moral implications and potential legal challenges.
*   **Security Concerns in the Middle East:** Yemeni forces have begun a tactical withdrawal from the Bab al-Mandab Strait to prioritize the defense of Aden against encroaching Houthi insurgents, highlighting the shifting balance of power in the region's ongoing instability.

## POLAND

*   **Poland Joins New Diplomatic Coalition on Ukraine:** President Volodymyr Zelensky has confirmed that Poland will be among the select group of nations—alongside France, Germany, the UK, Italy, and EU/NATO representatives—tasked with leading diplomatic negotiations regarding Russia. The coalition is scheduled to convene in Berlin to coordinate future strategies.
*   **Political Tensions Over Institutions:** Domestic political friction remains high as the government continues to spar over the status of the Constitutional Tribunal. Recent criticisms from cabinet members regarding presidential initiatives suggest a deepening rift between the executive branch and the presidential palace, further complicating the internal political landscape.
