## WORLD

* **Wave of Security Alerts Targets European Aviation:** A series of bomb threats has forced multiple emergency landings across Europe, including flights traveling to Warsaw, Rome, and various destinations in Bulgaria. Security agencies are currently investigating the coordinated nature of these incidents.
* **Sentencing of Hong Kong Activist:** Joshua Wong, a prominent figure in Hong Kong's pro-democracy movement, has been sentenced to 10 years in prison. The verdict follows charges related to conspiracy to collude with foreign forces under the region's national security law.
* **Bavarian Deputy Premier Found Dead:** Hubert Aiwanger, the 53-year-old Deputy Premier of Bavaria and leader of the Free Voters party, has been found dead in a hotel room. Local police have launched an investigation into the circumstances surrounding his passing.

## POLAND

* **Political Turmoil at the Constitutional Tribunal:** Tensions continue to escalate as Bogdan Święczkowski, head of the Constitutional Tribunal, confronted police officers at the building's entrance, labeling their presence illegal. Meanwhile, a new resolution from the Tribunal concerning the upcoming state budget has been officially submitted to the Sejm.
* **Administrative Changes in Częstochowa:** Following the recall of Mayor Krzysztof Matyjaszczyk in a recent referendum, Prime Minister Donald Tusk has appointed Łukasz Pabiś, the former deputy mayor, to serve as the city's commissioner. The change in leadership comes amid ongoing legal proceedings against the previous administration.
