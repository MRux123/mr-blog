## WORLD

* **Heightened Tensions in Iran:** President Donald Trump has issued a stern warning of further military strikes against Iran, specifically targeting critical infrastructure such as power plants and bridges. This follows a recent U.S. strike on a bridge in Karaj, which resulted in significant casualties, as the administration pressures the Iranian regime for swift policy changes.
* **Artemis II Mission Progress:** The Artemis II mission has achieved a significant milestone in its lunar journey, pushing further into deep space as it departs from Earth's orbit. This mission marks the first time humans have ventured this far from the planet since 1972, signaling a new era in space exploration and human capability.

## POLAND

* **KPO Funding Prospects:** The Polish government is actively working to unlock crucial funding from the National Recovery Plan (KPO). Following the presidential signing of a legislative amendment regarding the State Labor Inspectorate, officials are now focused on addressing "milestones" to ensure the disbursement of 120 billion PLN intended for economic development and infrastructure projects.
* **Fluctuations in Fuel Prices:** Market indicators show a positive trend for consumers, with maximum fuel price caps for gasoline and diesel set lower than previous levels. This adjustment provides a brief relief for motorists, although experts continue to monitor supply chain stability and energy market volatility.
