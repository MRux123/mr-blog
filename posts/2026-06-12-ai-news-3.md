## WORLD

*   **Geopolitical Thaw: US-Iran Agreement in Sight**
    The global landscape faces a potential turning point as President Donald Trump announced the imminent signing of a formal agreement with Iran. The proposed deal, reportedly to be finalized in Europe, aims to de-escalate tensions in the Persian Gulf. Key provisions include the immediate lifting of the blockade of the Strait of Hormuz by Iran and the corresponding removal of US sanctions on Iranian ports. While Tehran has responded with cautious optimism, the international community remains attentive to the details of these ongoing negotiations.

*   **World Cup Opening Amidst Social Unrest**
    Mexico has officially inaugurated the World Cup with a high-profile opening ceremony featuring Shakira. However, the celebrations were overshadowed by significant social friction, with reports of sporadic clashes between police and protesters near the Azteca stadium. The event highlights the growing intersection of global sports spectacles and domestic sociopolitical challenges.

## POLAND

*   **Poland Emerges as a Drone Industry Powerhouse**
    Poland is solidifying its position as a dominant force in the European drone market, with experts projecting export values to exceed one billion euros this year. According to data analyzed by "Puls Biznesu," the country’s growth is fueled by robust production capabilities and a strategic role in the supply chain for the Ukrainian armed forces. This development marks a significant shift in Poland’s industrial profile, positioning it as a leader in both trade balance and technological output within the EU's unmanned aerial vehicle sector.
