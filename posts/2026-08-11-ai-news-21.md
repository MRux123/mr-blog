## WORLD

* **Tragedy in Colombia:** A devastating earthquake has claimed at least 181 lives, with the toll expected to rise as search and rescue operations continue in Cali. Emergency teams are working under intense pressure, navigating aftershocks to recover survivors trapped beneath the debris.
* **Escalation in the Gulf of Oman:** Tensions in the Middle East have spiked following a U.S. military operation in the Gulf of Oman. A U.S. helicopter disabled a Panama-flagged cargo vessel, which authorities claim was in breach of the blockade against Iran.
* **Ebola Health Emergency:** Public health officials in the Democratic Republic of the Congo are reporting a massive surge in the ongoing Ebola outbreak, with confirmed cases now exceeding 4,000. Experts have expressed grave concerns regarding potential viral mutations, prompting an urgent shift to more aggressive, house-to-house containment strategies.

## POLAND

* **Security Concerns:** The Korean Peninsula remains a point of international instability, as North Korea has reportedly test-fired an unidentified projectile. The move has drawn immediate attention from global observers and Polish defense monitoring.
* **Athletic Performance at European Championships:** Polish athletes have shown significant results on the continental stage. Pia Skrzyszowska secured a silver medal in the 100-meter hurdles, narrowly missing the top spot, while Paweł Fajdek demonstrated strong form in the opening rounds before ultimately finishing outside the podium positions.
