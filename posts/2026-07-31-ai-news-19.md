## WORLD

* **Migration Crisis at the Spanish Border:** Ceuta remains at the center of an international crisis following a massive influx of roughly 60,000 migrants from Morocco. While the majority have returned to Moroccan territory, the incident has triggered significant diplomatic fallout, including Italy’s decision to temporarily suspend the Schengen agreement with Spain. The event underscores deep systemic vulnerabilities in European border management and the political pressure mounting on the Spanish administration.
* **F-35 Military Incident:** Reports have emerged regarding a serious accident involving an American F-35 fighter jet. While details remain limited, the incident highlights ongoing concerns regarding the operational safety and technical maintenance of advanced fifth-generation stealth platforms.

## POLAND

* **82nd Anniversary of the Warsaw Uprising:** Poland honors the heroes of the 1944 Warsaw Uprising, marking 82 years since the historic insurrection. Commemorations across the capital focus on the legacy of the insurgents, emphasizing their role as vital historical mentors for the younger generation.
* **Berlin Terror Investigation Update:** New developments have surfaced regarding the suspect behind the recent Berlin attack, with investigators probing potential ties to the Islamic State. Authorities are working to determine the extent of the suspect's radicalization and possible international connections.
* **Landmark Verdict in Funeral Home Scandal:** A judicial decision has been reached in a harrowing case involving a funeral home where families were provided with the wrong cremated remains, and human bodies were left in storage for years. The verdict brings a measure of closure to a scandal that deeply impacted the local community and raised significant ethical questions regarding industry regulations.
