## WORLD

*   **Escalating Geopolitical Tensions:** The security landscape remains volatile as Denmark reports a Russian warship firing flares at a military helicopter, an incident labeled as "reckless" by the Danish government. Simultaneously, European infrastructure is on high alert following suspected sabotage on the Dutch rail network, where foreign objects were intentionally placed on tracks to disrupt transport.
*   **AI Governance Deadlock:** Despite urgent warnings from tech industry leaders regarding the need for a mandatory AI "kill switch," U.S. President Donald Trump has dismissed these safety concerns as a "hoax." This stance has effectively halted legislative momentum in Congress, creating a significant regulatory vacuum at a time of rapid technological advancement.

## POLAND

*   **Heightened Rhetoric from Moscow:** Tensions between Poland and the Kremlin have surged following inflammatory remarks from a Russian presidential advisor. The official threatened that Poland could "cease to exist within two to three days," a bellicose escalation following recent comments made by Polish Foreign Minister Radosław Sikorski.
*   **Orlen Financial Scandal:** Prime Minister Donald Tusk has labeled reports of a 1.6 billion PLN loss at the state-owned energy giant Orlen as a "global embarrassment." The government is facing intense scrutiny regarding the management of the company, with the Prime Minister describing the situation as one of the largest scandals in recent national history.
*   **Legislative and Judicial Conflict:** Grzegorz Braun has lost his immunity as a Member of the European Parliament following a vote in Strasbourg, specifically concerning his controversial statements denying the Holocaust. Meanwhile, internal political friction continues as Włodzimierz Czarzasty publicly rebuked the presidential camp regarding the constitutional appointment of judges, asserting that the government will continue to exercise its mandate without interference.
