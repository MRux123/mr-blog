## WORLD

*   **Japan Earthquake Crisis:** Rescue efforts are intensifying following a powerful 6.8-magnitude earthquake in Kumamoto. Authorities report 13 fatalities and are racing against time to reach survivors trapped under collapsed buildings, while over 100 aftershocks—some potentially reaching magnitude 7—keep the region on high alert.
*   **Geopolitical Escalation in the Middle East:** U.S. President Donald Trump has issued a stern warning to Iran following a failed missile attack on American bases in Jordan. Tensions continue to rise as the U.S. prepares for a significant retaliatory response to the aggression.
*   **Legal Action Against Telegram:** Russian authorities have formally charged Pavel Durov, the founder of the messaging platform Telegram, with facilitating terrorism. This development signals a major escalation in the global struggle for control over private digital communication channels.

## POLAND

*   **Diplomatic and Political Clash over Wrocław Assault:** A brutal physical attack on a Ukrainian couple in Wrocław has triggered a heated political row. Two suspects have been placed in temporary detention, while the President’s office and the Prime Minister’s administration have traded accusations regarding responsibility for public safety and the appropriate response to the incident.
*   **Shifting Defense Priorities:** Minister of National Defense Władysław Kosiniak-Kamysz confirmed that negotiations regarding the transfer of MiG-29 fighter jets to Ukraine remain active despite initial hurdles. The situation has gained complexity as reports indicate rising competition for these assets, with Bulgaria also expressing interest in acquiring Polish military hardware.
