## WORLD

* **US-Iran Diplomatic Shift:** Vice President JD Vance has signaled that the Trump administration may release a framework for a new US-Iran deal as early as this Friday. Described as a brief, one-and-a-half-page document, the agreement remains highly generalized, with technical details expected to be negotiated in the coming months.
* **Hungarian Constitutional Reform:** In a historic move, the Hungarian parliament has passed a constitutional amendment imposing an eight-year term limit on the Prime Minister’s office. The legislation effectively bars Viktor Orban from future premierships, fulfilling a key campaign promise made by current Prime Minister Peter Magyar.
* **Aviation Tragedy in California:** An American B-52 bomber has crashed shortly after takeoff at Edwards Air Force Base in California. Official reports confirm that all eight crew members on board perished in the incident.

## POLAND

* **Diplomatic Slights:** Ukrainian President Volodymyr Zelenskyy has once again bypassed Poland during an international transit, opting to fly to the G7 summit from Moldova. This marks the second recent instance where the Ukrainian leader has chosen the Chisinau airport over Polish infrastructure, signaling potential shifts in regional logistics and diplomatic priorities.
* **Accountability in Opolskie:** Authorities have initiated criminal proceedings against a childcare worker in an Opole nursery. The suspect faces up to five years in prison following allegations of causing bodily harm to a two-year-old child, a case brought to light by disturbing footage from the facility’s internal monitoring system.
* **Medical Ethics Debate:** The case of doctor Dawid Kacprzyk continues to ignite public discourse in Poland. Politicians and activists remain divided on the issue of professional accountability versus systemic state failure, with debates centering on whether economic conditions can ever mitigate the legal and moral responsibility of medical professionals.
