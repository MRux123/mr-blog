## WORLD

*   **Escalation in Ukraine:** A critical infrastructure strike in Kyiv has paralyzed transportation, with Russian missiles hitting the Southern Bridge and damaging subway lines. At least one fatality and 14 injuries have been reported, marking a significant intensification of the conflict's impact on civilian mobility.
*   **Regional Instability:** Tensions between Jordan and Syria have flared following a deadly strike by Jordanian forces on a Syrian village. The operation, conducted in retaliation for an attack on a church, resulted in multiple civilian casualties, including women and children, raising concerns over cross-border stability.
*   **Democratic Shifts:** Israel heads to a pivotal national election today amid deep uncertainty, with polls suggesting a fractured political landscape that may lead to prolonged legislative deadlock.

## POLAND

*   **Cybersecurity Breach at Recruitment Centers:** A mysterious systemic failure within the Military Recruitment Center (WCR) has triggered investigations into the disappearance of sensitive data, coupled with reports of unauthorized anonymous actions and sudden personnel departures. 
*   **Digital Identity Fraud:** Minister of Interior Marcin Kierwiński announced a major law enforcement success against a criminal group that forged the "mObywatel" digital identity application. The operation led to the arrest of 21 individuals, 13 of whom are minors, highlighting the rising threat of digital document fabrication among youth.
*   **Regulatory Standoff with Big Tech:** Digital Affairs Minister Krzysztof Gawkowski has issued a stark warning to the platform X (formerly Twitter), citing systemic failures to curb illegal content, hate speech, and disinformation. The government has not ruled out restrictive measures against the platform, citing the need for information integrity ahead of upcoming campaigns.
