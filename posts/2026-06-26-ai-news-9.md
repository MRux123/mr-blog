## WORLD

*   **Catastrophic Earthquake in Venezuela:** The humanitarian crisis in Venezuela has deepened as the death toll from two powerful earthquakes has reached 235. International rescue efforts, led by the U.S. and Mexico, are currently underway to locate hundreds of missing persons trapped beneath the rubble.
*   **Heightened Tensions on the Belarus-Ukraine Border:** Ukrainian officials have issued stern warnings to Belarus, alleging that the country is facilitating Russian drone strikes by allowing its radio relay stations to assist in guiding attacks. This development marks a significant escalation in the regional conflict.
*   **Geopolitical Realignment in the Gulf:** Following three months of intense regional warfare, Gulf Arab nations are undergoing a strategic pivot. Faced with a potential U.S.-Iran diplomatic deal that leaves them exposed, these countries are rapidly reshaping their defense policies and economic trade routes to ensure long-term stability.

## POLAND

*   **Volkswagen Faces Massive Restructuring:** The Polish industrial landscape is bracing for significant impact as reports emerge of a potential crisis within Volkswagen, with up to 100,000 jobs currently at risk due to proposed company-wide cuts.
*   **Increased Forest Fire Alerts:** The Government Security Centre (RCB) has issued urgent warnings regarding the high risk of forest fires across the country, urging citizens to exercise extreme caution as environmental conditions worsen.
