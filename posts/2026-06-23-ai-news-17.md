## WORLD

*   **Intelligence Alliance Issues AI Warning:** The "Five Eyes" intelligence community—comprising the US, UK, Canada, Australia, and New Zealand—has issued a rare, urgent advisory regarding the advancement of Frontier AI. The alliance warns that sophisticated AI models are already fundamentally reshaping cyber-warfare capabilities, significantly altering the global balance of power and necessitating immediate shifts in private and public sector defense strategies.
*   **Strait of Hormuz Reopens:** Following a landmark deal between the United States and Iran aimed at de-escalating tensions, maritime traffic in the Strait of Hormuz has rebounded sharply. Records indicate 42 commercial vessels successfully transited the waterway on Saturday, marking a major milestone in restoring regional trade stability.
*   **Volatile Ceasefire in Lebanon:** Despite a recent lull in hostilities, violence has erupted in southern Lebanon, where Israeli military forces engaged and killed two Hezbollah operatives. While Israel identifies the deceased as combatants, Hezbollah has publicly condemned the action as a direct violation of the standing ceasefire agreement.

## POLAND

*   **ZUS Leadership Shake-up:** Prime Minister Donald Tusk has officially signed the document to remove Zbigniew Derdziuk from his position as the president of the Social Insurance Institution (ZUS). The transition is set to take effect on June 24, signaling a significant shift in the management of the state's primary social security body.
*   **Diplomatic Controversy in Brussels:** The Polish government is facing scrutiny following its participation in a diplomatic meeting in Brussels involving Taliban representatives. The event has sparked significant controversy regarding the nature and intent of the engagement.
