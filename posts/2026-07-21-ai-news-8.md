## WORLD

* **Potential Leadership Change in Ukraine:** Reports from Bloomberg suggest that Oleksandr Syrsky, the Commander-in-Chief of the Armed Forces of Ukraine, may be facing removal. The move comes amid increasing public pressure and protests across Ukrainian cities. General Mykhailo Drapaty has been identified as a potential successor as the country navigates ongoing military and social volatility.

* **Security Alert in Athens:** Two tourists were injured in a knife attack near the Acropolis, sparking concerns over safety in one of Europe’s most prominent tourist hubs. Authorities are investigating the motive behind the assault as investigations continue.

* **Violent Incident at New York Federal Building:** A 43-year-old military veteran, identified as Andrew Arrabaca, was apprehended after setting a fire outside a federal building in Manhattan. The suspect was reportedly armed with multiple weapons, including axes and a machete, prompting a swift response from the FBI.

## POLAND

* **Political Internal Tensions within PiS:** Former Prime Minister Mateusz Morawiecki has issued a public statement reaffirming his commitment to the Law and Justice (PiS) party. His comments act as an appeal for unity following reports of growing internal conflicts and fractured communication within the party's leadership ranks.

* **National Warning Systems Test:** The Polish government, in coordination with the RCB, MSWiA, and MON, is scheduled to conduct a country-wide test of the "Alarm-26" siren system. Officials have issued an appeal for public calm, stressing that the activation of sirens between 7:00 AM and 7:00 PM is strictly for testing purposes.
