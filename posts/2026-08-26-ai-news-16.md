## WORLD

*   **Catastrophic Floods in Nepal:** A devastating flash flood in the Himalayan river valley has claimed at least 95 lives, with hundreds—including foreign tourists and border officials—reported missing. Authorities warn that the death toll is likely to rise significantly as rescue operations continue following the wall of water that swept away entire villages.
*   **Escalation in Gaza Cease-Fire Tensions:** Tensions regarding the regional conflict have reached a boiling point. Nickolay Mladenov, the Gaza representative for the Board of Peace, has formally criticized Israeli military actions, accusing the state of jeopardizing the fragile cease-fire agreement by continuing strikes after rejecting a disarmament proposal for Hamas.
*   **CIA-Russia Diplomatic Outreach:** President Trump has characterized a recent visit by CIA Director John Ratcliffe to Russia as "routine," though he hinted at potential future developments stemming from the high-level meeting. The visit comes amidst complex U.S. efforts to broaden sanctions against Iran beyond oil, targeting their digital assets, aviation, and tech sectors.

## POLAND

*   **Zondacrypto Controversy:** A significant political clash has emerged regarding the Zondacrypto exchange. Zbigniew Bogucki, Chief of the Presidential Cabinet, publicly challenged Prime Minister Donald Tusk over the handling of cryptocurrency legislation. Bogucki criticized the current government for its stance, citing previous failures of the prosecutor's office to resolve legal issues surrounding the exchange during earlier investigations.
*   **The Search for Marcin Romanowski:** High-profile detective Krzysztof Rutkowski has launched an independent search operation for politician Marcin Romanowski, reportedly heading to Moldova. Rutkowski has publicly offered a financial reward for credible information regarding Romanowski’s whereabouts, marking a dramatic escalation in the search for the wanted figure.
