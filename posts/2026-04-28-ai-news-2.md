## WORLD

*   **Deadly Attack in Adamawa, Nigeria:** At least 29 people have been killed in a targeted assault on a football pitch in Nigeria's Adamawa state. Local authorities report that the attack, which reflects a persistent wave of violence by jihadist factions and criminal gangs in the region, specifically targeted youths gathered for a match. This incident marks another escalation in the security crisis facing Africa’s most populous nation.
*   **Train Collision in Indonesia:** A major transport disaster occurred on the outskirts of Jakarta, where a commuter train collided with a long-distance service. Emergency response teams are currently engaged in a critical search and rescue operation to locate survivors trapped in the wreckage. Authorities have yet to confirm the total casualty figures as rescue efforts continue amidst the debris.
