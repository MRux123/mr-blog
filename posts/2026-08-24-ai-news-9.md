## WORLD

* **Escalating AI Warfare in Ukraine:** A significant escalation in military technology has been reported, as Ukrainian officials confirmed a lethal attack carried out by a Russian drone guided entirely by Artificial Intelligence. This development marks a concerning shift toward autonomous weaponry, raising ethical and strategic questions about the future of modern conflict.
* **Geopolitical Solidarity in Kyiv:** Amidst the 35th anniversary of Ukraine's independence, British Prime Minister Andy Burnham and other European leaders visited Kyiv. The visit serves as a defiant signal of Western support for Ukraine, despite renewed and aggressive warnings from Moscow regarding the consequences of such diplomatic backing.

## POLAND

* **Passing of General Mirosław Różański:** The Polish political and military landscape is in mourning following the death of General Mirosław Różański at age 63. A former General Commander of the Branches of the Armed Forces and a sitting senator, Różański was a prominent voice on national security matters. He had been battling cancer in recent months.
* **High-Level Corruption Probe:** A significant political and business scandal is unfolding as details emerge regarding the relationship between the head of the Polish Olympic Committee (PKOl), Radosław Piesiewicz, and Przemysław Kral, the former head of the Zondacrypto exchange. Kral is currently cooperating with the prosecutor's office, providing testimony that potentially links the exchange's illicit funding to influential figures within the Polish political right.
