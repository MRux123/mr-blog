## WORLD

* **Escalating Crisis in Kuwait:** Iranian drone strikes on Kuwait International Airport have resulted in at least one fatality and dozens of injuries. The attack, which Tehran describes as retaliation for previous U.S. strikes on Iranian assets, represents a significant escalation in regional hostilities, marking one of the largest assaults on a Gulf nation since the April cease-fire.
* **Ukrainian Strikes on Russia:** Long-range Ukrainian drone strikes have hit oil storage facilities near St. Petersburg. The timing is particularly sensitive, as it coincides with the opening of Russia's flagship economic forum, casting a shadow over President Vladimir Putin’s upcoming address.
* **Tragedy in New Delhi:** A catastrophic fire at a hotel in New Delhi has claimed the lives of at least 21 people, including foreign nationals. Authorities are currently investigating the cause of the blaze, which is being described as the deadliest incident of its kind in the Indian capital in recent years.

## POLAND

* **Geopolitical Realignment – "NATO 3.0":** High-ranking NATO officials, including Marshal Johnny Stringer, have signaled a strategic shift toward a "NATO 3.0" framework. This adjustment comes as European member states begin to adapt to a reduced U.S. military contribution, emphasizing a greater reliance on internal European defense capabilities.
* **Record Gold Reserves:** The National Bank of Poland has announced a significant milestone in national fiscal security, reporting holdings of over 613 tons of gold. This accumulation places Poland among the top ten countries globally in terms of gold reserves, bolstering the nation’s economic stability.
* **Skepticism Toward Rule of Law:** A recent CBOS poll highlights a critical public sentiment in Poland, with 79% of respondents expressing the belief that politicians operate with impunity regarding legal transgressions, and 69% questioning the independence of the judiciary from political influence.
