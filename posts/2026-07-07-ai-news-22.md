## WORLD

* **US Military Strikes Iran Following Strait of Hormuz Incident:** Tensions in the Middle East have reached a critical juncture after the United States launched retaliatory strikes against Iranian targets. The military action follows attacks on commercial tankers in the Strait of Hormuz, prompting the US to signal a policy of imposing "heavy costs" on Tehran. Consequently, the US has revoked sanctions waivers regarding the sale of Iranian oil, marking a significant escalation in diplomatic and economic pressure.
* **Heightened Security Concerns Over Russian Intentions:** Intelligence reports suggest that the United States has issued warnings to its allies regarding potential Russian aggression. There is increasing concern that Moscow is actively planning to target NATO infrastructure, a development that is forcing a major strategic reassessment within the Western military alliance.

## POLAND

* **Strengthening Polish-US Defense Ties:** Defense Minister Władysław Kosiniak-Kamysz held high-level discussions with US Secretary of Defense Pete Hegseth. The meeting underscored Poland's position as a key strategic partner for the United States, further solidifying the security cooperation between the two nations in the face of regional volatility.
* **Domestic Political Friction Over Defense Support:** A heated debate has erupted regarding Poland’s security contributions, specifically concerning the transfer of Patriot missile components to Ukraine. Prominent academics and public figures have criticized the Presidential Palace's handling of the matter, describing the current political atmosphere as detrimental to national consensus and effective foreign policy.
* **Natural Disaster Preparedness on the Baltic Coast:** Local authorities in Krynica Morska have initiated emergency procedures, including the evacuation of 300 scouts, as the region braces for severe weather conditions. Coastal infrastructure is being reinforced to withstand anticipated high-force winds and storm surges, as meteorological warnings remain in effect for the northern provinces.
