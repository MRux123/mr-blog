## WORLD

*   **Japan Responds to Major Earthquake in Kumamoto:** Emergency services and rescue teams are currently engaged in a critical operation in the Kumamoto prefecture following a powerful 6.8 magnitude earthquake. The disaster has caused structural collapses and widespread fires, prompting a race against time to locate survivors amidst the debris.
*   **Global Food Security Crisis:** A sobering report from five UN agencies highlights that a healthy, nutritious diet remains financially inaccessible for 2.69 billion people—roughly one in three individuals globally. Despite a slight decline in hunger rates in 2025, experts warn that achieving zero hunger by 2030 remains an immense challenge unless the costs of essential food groups, such as fruits and dairy, are aggressively addressed.

## POLAND

*   **Internal Political Realignment within the Right Wing:** The Polish political scene is witnessing intensified maneuvering as speculation grows regarding potential shifts from the PiS party to Mateusz Morawiecki’s new association. Observers note that political tensions are rising as party leadership attempts to consolidate internal discipline, particularly concerning figures like Jacek Kurski.
*   **Safety Investigation Following Pool Tragedy:** Legal proceedings have been launched by the prosecutor’s office in Łódź following a life-threatening incident at an aquapark, where a seven-year-old child was rescued after spending six minutes underwater. The incident has triggered an urgent review of safety protocols during swimming instruction.
*   **Military Discovery in Warsaw:** Authorities in Warsaw recently conducted a bomb squad operation at St. Anne's Church in Wilanów. Construction workers uncovered an unexploded ordnance dating back to the Second World War on the church's attic, highlighting the ongoing risk of historical munitions in urban development projects.
