## WORLD

*   **Massive Drone Offensive Targets Moscow:** Russian authorities have reported the largest drone strike on the capital to date, with hundreds of unmanned aerial vehicles intercepted over Moscow and surrounding regions. The attack, attributed to Ukrainian forces, has caused widespread disruption to civil aviation, with over 400 flights delayed and at least 12 cancellations, signaling a significant escalation in the ongoing conflict.
*   **Controversial Infrastructure Plans by Trump:** Former U.S. President Donald Trump has proposed that his planned triumphal arch project be dual-purposed as a strategic military complex. Citing national security and requests from the U.S. military, Trump aims to utilize the structure for ammunition storage, sparking debate over the intersection of commemorative architecture and military logistics.

## POLAND

*   **Security and Civil Liability Concerns:** Amid rising regional tensions, the Polish public is increasingly focused on defense readiness. Military experts have begun clarifying procedures regarding the potential requisition of private vehicles for state use in the event of an official mobilization, highlighting the government’s efforts to streamline national logistics.
*   **Legal Action Following Threats Against the Prime Minister:** Polish law enforcement has apprehended a 56-year-old resident of Przemyśl in connection with threats made against Prime Minister Donald Tusk. The suspect, who disseminated threatening content via social media, is expected to face formal charges, underscoring the authorities' zero-tolerance policy toward political violence.
*   **Cultural Diplomacy: Maria Skłodowska-Curie on the Euro:** Prime Minister Donald Tusk is spearheading a public campaign to honor Polish-born scientist Maria Skłodowska-Curie by featuring her portrait on the new 20-euro banknote. The initiative is part of an ongoing European Central Bank consultation process, with the final decision expected later this year.
