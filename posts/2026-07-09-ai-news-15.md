## POLAND

* **Security Concerns on the Horizon:** Foreign Minister Radosław Sikorski has issued a warning regarding intelligence reports suggesting that Russia is preparing new provocations, underscoring the deteriorating security climate in the region.
* **Legal Developments for Former NIK Chief:** The Prosecutor's Office in Białystok has filed an indictment against the former head of the Supreme Audit Office (NIK), Marian B. The charges relate to the unauthorized disclosure of information protected by tax secrecy, marking a significant development in Polish domestic politics.
* **Defense Modernization:** In a move to bolster its strategic capabilities, Germany has received U.S. approval to acquire Tomahawk cruise missiles. This purchase is seen as a key step in strengthening European military deterrence in the face of evolving threats.
