## WORLD

*   **Humanitarian Catastrophe in Venezuela:** The death toll following the catastrophic earthquakes has risen to at least 589, with nearly 3,000 injured. Search and rescue operations are ongoing under increasingly difficult conditions as the government moves to militarize the affected zones. International aid from the U.S. and Mexico is arriving as hundreds remain trapped.
*   **Geopolitical Standoff in the Strait of Hormuz:** Iran has asserted control over the critical maritime chokepoint following an unprovoked attack on a commercial container ship. The situation remains volatile as world leaders weigh their response to what has been described as a "foolish" act of aggression.
*   **Legal Developments in the U.S.:** Former Trump adviser John Bolton has pleaded guilty to charges regarding the mishandling of classified documents. He faces up to five years in prison and a substantial financial penalty.

## POLAND

*   **Defense Modernization:** The Polish Ministry of National Defense has finalized negotiations with Spain to acquire additional Airbus A330 MRTT flying tankers, significantly bolstering the operational reach of the Polish Air Force.
*   **Economic Forecasts:** Experts warn of potential sharp increases in fuel prices following the scheduled expiration of government support packages. This development is expected to impact household budgets and inflationary pressures in the coming months.
*   **Security Challenges:** Recent analytical reports suggest that the Kremlin continues to pose a significant long-term security threat to Europe, necessitating a strategic reassessment of military readiness in the region.
