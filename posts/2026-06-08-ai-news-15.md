## WORLD

*   **Mideast De-escalation:** Following an exchange of fire that saw Iran launch approximately 30 missiles at Israel in retaliation for strikes in Lebanon, both nations have signaled a pause in hostilities. Reports suggest that Prime Minister Benjamin Netanyahu, facing significant international pressure, has refrained from immediate further military action, marking a temporary stabilization in the region.
*   **Philippines Earthquake Tragedy:** A powerful 7.8-magnitude earthquake has struck off the coast of Mindanao in the Philippines, resulting in at least 32 confirmed deaths. The seismic event, which triggered tsunami warnings in neighboring Japan and Indonesia, has caused extensive destruction, widespread landslides, and displaced tens of thousands of residents.

## POLAND

*   **Tragedy in Tczew:** Local authorities have launched a murder investigation following the discovery of a 42-year-old woman's body in a Tczew apartment. A 19-year-old suspect, identified as the victim's son, has been detained by police as the investigation continues.
*   **Fatal Accident in Ząbki:** The Prosecutor’s Office in Wołomin is seeking the arrest of a 37-year-old Turkish national involved in a fatal vehicle collision on the border of Zielonka and Ząbki. The incident claimed the lives of two young men and left a third in critical condition; the suspect faces up to eight years in prison.
*   **New Life Saved in Częstochowa:** An infant left in the "Window of Life" (Okno Życia) managed by the Sisters Servants in Częstochowa is in stable condition. This marks the ninth life saved at this specific location since 2008, drawing public praise for the mother's decision to ensure the child's safety.
