## WORLD

* **Passing of Robert Mueller:** The global legal and political landscape mourns the loss of Robert Mueller at the age of 81. As the former FBI director, Mueller became a central figure in American institutional history, most notably for leading the high-profile investigation into Russian interference in the 2016 US presidential election. His death marks the end of an era for the U.S. justice system.
* **UK Aid Budget Overhaul:** The United Kingdom has announced a significant restructuring of its foreign aid policy, with a 56% reduction in bilateral funding for African nations by 2028-29. The cut, totaling nearly £900 million, is part of a broader government strategy to reallocate £6 billion toward domestic defense spending, sparking concerns regarding the stability of essential infrastructure such as clinics and schools in the world’s most vulnerable regions.

## POLAND

* **Escalating Middle East Conflict:** Poland's diplomatic and security concerns have intensified following a stark ultimatum issued by Donald Trump to Iran. The U.S. has demanded the unconditional opening of the Strait of Hormuz within 48 hours, threatening the destruction of Iranian power infrastructure if the demand is not met. Tehran has responded with threats of retaliation against U.S. and Israeli energy and IT assets, creating a volatile situation that forces Warsaw and its allies to reassess regional security stability.
