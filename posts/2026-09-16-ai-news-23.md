## WORLD

* **US Interest Rates Hike:** In a significant shift in monetary policy, the United States has increased interest rates for the first time in three years. The decision, reached unanimously, stands in direct defiance of President Donald Trump’s public opposition and calls for rate cuts, signaling the central bank's focus on long-term economic stability over political pressure.

## POLAND

* **Legislative Action Against Deepfake Abuse:** The Polish government is moving to address the growing threat of non-consensual sexual content generated via artificial intelligence. A new legislative project aimed at criminalizing "sexual deepfakes" is expected to be submitted to the Sejm, reflecting an urgent push to close legal loopholes regarding digital harassment.
* **Ongoing Political Protest:** Maria Kurowska, a Law and Justice (PiS) Member of Parliament, has been forced to vacate the Academy of Justice building due to a power cut. Despite the displacement, Kurowska confirmed she is continuing her hunger strike, framing the event as a forced removal by authorities while maintaining her protest outside the facility.
