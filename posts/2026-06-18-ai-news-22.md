## WORLD

* **French Presidential Frontrunner Praises Poland:** Jordan Bardella, leading the polls in the upcoming French presidential election, has expressed strong support for Poland. During an interview with Polsat News, Bardella described Poland as a "brother nation" to France and lauded the country's recent economic growth, suggesting that Warsaw’s model could serve as an effective example for other European nations.
* **Tusk and Zelensky Address Bilateral Agenda:** On the sidelines of the EU summit in Brussels, Polish Prime Minister Donald Trump and Ukrainian President Volodymyr Zelensky held high-level talks. The meeting focused on navigating the current bilateral agenda and addressing the path toward Ukraine's European integration, amidst ongoing diplomatic tensions regarding historical memory issues.

## POLAND

* **Political Controversy Erupts Over Medical Data:** A heated parliamentary confrontation occurred after PiS deputies circulated a graphic targeting Coalition Platform (KO) politicians. The controversy centered on allegations regarding priority medical access, drawing sharp condemnation after the materials featured MP Katarzyna Piekarska, who collapsed earlier in the day during a legislative session. 
* **Public Safety Incidents in Urban Areas:** Authorities in both Przemyśl and Kraków have been forced to respond to hazardous encounters between residents and wild animals. In Przemyśl, local services issued alerts regarding a bear roaming city streets, while in Kraków, a severe incident involving a wild boar overturning a stroller on the Kliny estate has prompted the city to convene an emergency crisis meeting.
