## WORLD

* **Renewed Hostilities in Gaza:** Despite a recent cease-fire framework proposed by President Trump, which includes requirements for Hamas to disarm and Israel to halt offensive operations, the region has seen a resumption of violence. Fresh bombardment in Gaza has resulted in reported fatalities, casting doubt on the stability of the nascent peace agreement.

## POLAND

* **82nd Anniversary of the Warsaw Uprising:** Poland commemorated the 82nd anniversary of the Warsaw Uprising today. The "Hour W" remembrance saw cities across the country come to a standstill, with siren alarms sounding at 17:00 to honor the heroes of the 1944 insurrection. The tribute received international recognition, including a formal statement from European Parliament President Roberta Metsola.
* **Shifts in Political Leadership:** A new survey by United Surveys highlights a period of flux within the Polish right-wing political spectrum. One-third of respondents suggest that the right is currently without a clear leader. Among defined figures, President Karol Nawrocki leads the support, followed by Jarosław Kaczyński and Mateusz Morawiecki, reflecting ongoing challenges for opposition consolidation.
