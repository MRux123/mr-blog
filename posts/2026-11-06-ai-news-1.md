## WORLD

*   **Trump’s Diplomatic Shift on Ukraine:** US President Donald Trump has signaled that a potential peace agreement concerning the conflict in Ukraine may have a broader scope than previously anticipated. Following meetings with Kremlin envoy Kirill Dmitriev, Trump suggested that the deal could affect the security architecture of the entire European continent. The administration also confirmed personal exchanges between the White House and the Kremlin, including private letters from Melania Trump.
*   **Widespread Security Alerts:** Air travel across Europe, including Poland, Italy, and Spain, faced significant disruptions following a series of bomb threats. Passengers were subjected to emergency landings and rigorous security screenings; authorities are currently investigating the incidents as coordinated hoaxes designed to rattle international transportation.
*   **Political Realignment in the US:** The outcome of the Los Angeles mayoral race has marked a notable shift in American urban politics. The defeat of the incumbent by a democratic socialist candidate means that the two largest US cities—New York and Los Angeles—are now under the leadership of progressive, democratic socialist platforms.

## POLAND

*   **Strategic Political Consultations:** Polish Prime Minister Donald Tusk has publicly endorsed recent high-level consultations between Deputy Prime Minister Władysław Kosiniak-Kamysz and Sławomir Mentzen. Tusk emphasized that he was informed of the meeting beforehand, signaling a potential shift in the domestic political landscape and governing strategy.
*   **EU Migration Stance:** The Polish government has successfully negotiated an exemption from the current mandatory migrant relocation mechanism, with Brussels expected to formalize the decision shortly. This outcome represents a significant policy victory for the current administration in the context of broader EU integration debates.
