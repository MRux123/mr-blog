## WORLD

* **Far-Right Surge in Germany:** Exit polls in the state of Saxony-Anhalt indicate a historic and dominant performance by the Alternative for Germany (AfD) party. Securing approximately 44% of the vote, the party has significantly outperformed the conservative CDU. This result represents a major shift in the German political landscape, raising significant questions about potential governance structures and the stability of traditional political coalitions.
* **Diplomatic Efforts in Ukraine:** US envoys have concluded high-level talks with President Volodymyr Zelensky in Kyiv. Following discussions with representatives of the Trump administration, Zelensky emphasized the urgency of potential trilateral negotiations involving Russia. The focus remains on establishing a substantive path toward conflict resolution, marking a critical moment in the ongoing geopolitical crisis.
* **Logistical Crisis in Indonesia:** A major volcanic eruption has paralyzed travel infrastructure in the region, leaving over 170,000 airline passengers stranded. The presence of volcanic ash in the airspace near Jakarta has forced authorities to suspend flights, creating a significant humanitarian and logistical challenge for both domestic and international travelers.

## POLAND

* **Police Pursuit Ends in Tragedy:** A high-speed chase in Uniemyśl has resulted in a serious traffic incident involving a 17-year-old driver who failed to stop for inspection. The subsequent collisions have left five people hospitalized, highlighting ongoing concerns regarding road safety and juvenile traffic offenses.
* **Global Legal Controversies:** In a high-profile international case, an Egyptian television presenter, Sara Khaliifa, has been sentenced to death by a Cairo court. The ruling, which also affects 11 others, is linked to narcotics trafficking charges, though the defendant maintains her innocence, drawing international attention to the severity of regional judicial sentencing.
