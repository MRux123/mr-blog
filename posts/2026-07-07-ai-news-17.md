## WORLD

*   **Funeral Proceedings for Ayatollah Khamenei:** Iran continues a weeklong national mourning period following the death of Supreme Leader Ayatollah Ali Khamenei. The transition of power and the symbolic significance of these ceremonies in Qom mark a critical juncture for the nation’s political and religious stability.
*   **Marine Le Pen’s Legal Challenges:** A French court has ruled against Marine Le Pen, finding her guilty in a high-profile case. Despite the verdict, the court has allowed her to potentially continue her presidential campaign, provided she adheres to strict judicial conditions, including the use of an electronic monitoring tag.
*   **Political Turbulence in the UK:** Reform U.K. leader Nigel Farage has announced his resignation amidst growing scrutiny over undisclosed financial gifts and donations. Farage intends to run again in an upcoming special election, framing the move as a challenge to his critics.

## POLAND

*   **NATO Defense Spending Disparities:** Recent data highlights Poland's commitment to high defense expenditures, placing the country among the top contributors in NATO. Conversely, the report indicates that the United States has fallen slightly below the established threshold, sparking discussions regarding alliance-wide burden sharing.
*   **Public Opinion on Premiership:** A recent IBRiS poll has provided an interesting look at national sentiment regarding leadership since 1989. Respondents identified Tadeusz Mazowiecki as the most effective prime minister, followed by current leader Donald Tusk and his predecessor, Mateusz Morawiecki.
*   **Crisis in Healthcare Governance:** A growing political controversy has emerged surrounding excessive salary payments in the Mogilno hospital. The affair has triggered a heated debate between the government and opposition figures, with accusations of financial mismanagement being traded across party lines.
