## WORLD

* **Stalemate in Middle East Negotiations:** Tensions remain high as Iran sets strict preconditions for peace talks, demanding both a ceasefire in Lebanon and the release of frozen assets. Despite a shaky truce brokered by the US and Iran, critical infrastructure like the Strait of Hormuz remains closed, and military operations persist, casting doubt on the upcoming diplomatic summit in Islamabad.
* **Western Diplomatic Friction:** British Prime Minister Keir Starmer has issued a stern condemnation of the geopolitical strategies pursued by Donald Trump and Vladimir Putin. Starmer argues that their combined policies are directly destabilizing energy markets and placing an unsustainable financial burden on British households and industries.

## POLAND

* **Security Alert in Lublin Region:** Polish authorities have launched an investigation following the discovery of a rocket fragment in a private orchard in Jarosławiec. Initial findings suggest the debris is linked to regional air defense activities against Russian drones conducted in September 2025.
* **National Security and Mental Health Concerns:** A significant security incident involving a foreign national at Warsaw's Chopin Airport—where a 23-year-old was apprehended using radio-frequency jamming equipment—has reached a new legal stage. Prosecutors have ordered psychiatric evaluations for the suspect, emphasizing the seriousness of the potential threat to aviation communication systems.
