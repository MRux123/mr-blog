## WORLD

* **Standoff Over Iran-US Ceasefire:** Tensions remain high as Washington and Tehran struggle to align on the timeline for the end of their current ceasefire. Conflicting statements regarding the expiration hour have caused uncertainty in peace negotiations, while the U.S. continues to pressure Baghdad to dismantle Iran-backed militias to stabilize the region.
* **Geopolitical Instability in Moldova:** Following recent regional developments, Russia’s Security Council has signaled a potential escalation regarding the protection of citizens in the breakaway region of Transnistria, raising international concerns about Russian military intentions in the area.
* **Taiwanese Diplomatic Setback:** President Lai Ching-te has been forced to cancel a critical diplomatic mission to Eswatini. The move follows the revocation of overflight permits by several nations, a decision Taipei attributes to intense diplomatic and economic pressure exerted by Beijing.

## POLAND

* **Pipeline Repair and Potential EU Breakthrough:** A critical repair of the "Druzhba" pipeline has been completed, signaling a potential resumption of oil transport. This development is being linked to potential political shifts in Hungary, where incoming leadership has indicated a willingness to unblock a €90 billion EU aid package for Ukraine in exchange for energy security normalization.
* **Internal Political Maneuvering:** Expert analysis continues to surround the future of Mateusz Morawiecki within the Law and Justice (PiS) party, as internal dynamics and the party's strategic direction face heightened scrutiny by political observers.
* **Crackdown on Online Harassment:** The Polish government has announced plans to implement strict regulations targeting "patostreaming" (abusive livestreaming) before the summer parliamentary break, following growing public demand for stronger legal action against harmful content creators.
