## WORLD

The situation underscores the increasing volatility of climate-related risks in the Mediterranean, forcing authorities to prioritize coastal beach evacuations as containment efforts remain challenging.

## POLAND

* **Public consensus on legislative reforms:** A recent survey indicates that nearly 90 percent of Poles support a specific, undisclosed legislative change. This level of broad national agreement is rare, suggesting that the proposed reform addresses a matter of high social importance that transcends traditional political divides.
* **Shifting political landscape:** The "Rozwój Plus" movement, led by Mateusz Morawiecki, continues to expand its reach through targeted political transfers. These strategic acquisitions signal an attempt to consolidate opposition influence and reshape internal party dynamics ahead of future electoral cycles.
* **Changing attitudes toward Ukraine:** A new public opinion poll reveals evolving societal sentiments regarding Poland’s relationship with Ukraine and Ukrainian citizens residing in the country. This data serves as a critical barometer for the current state of bilateral social integration and domestic perceptions of ongoing geopolitical alliances.
