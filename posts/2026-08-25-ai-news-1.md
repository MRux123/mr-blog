## WORLD

* **Shifting Dynamics in Syria:** The Trump administration has taken a controversial step by removing Syria from the official list of state sponsors of terrorism. This move follows the international recognition of the new Syrian president, Ahmed al-Sharaa, formerly linked to militant groups, signaling a profound realignment of U.S. foreign policy in the Middle East.
* **Escalation in Russian Military Recruitment:** Human rights monitors report a disturbing surge in forced conscription across at least 17 Russian regions. Reports indicate that authorities are increasingly using intimidation and abduction tactics to bolster army ranks, highlighting growing domestic instability as the conflict continues.
* **Political Stagnation in Washington:** President Donald Trump’s approval ratings remain locked at a historic low of 33 percent. Recent polling confirms that the administration has failed to secure a political breakthrough, leaving the President facing significant challenges as his term progresses.

## POLAND

* **Episcopate Challenges Education Reform:** The Polish Bishops' Conference (KEP) has issued an urgent appeal to the government regarding the status of religion classes in public schools. Church leaders expressed deep concern over recent directives from the Ministry of National Education, while also criticizing the newly mandated health education curriculum.
* **Environmental Warnings:** Experts warn that the record-breaking temperatures in the world's oceans, exacerbated by the El Niño phenomenon, present an immediate and severe threat. These shifts are expected to have cascading effects on regional weather patterns and ecological stability, serving as a critical alert for climate policy.
