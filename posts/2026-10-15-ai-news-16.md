## WORLD

* **Escalation in Ukraine:** As the winter season approaches, Russia is intensifying its efforts to cripple Ukrainian energy infrastructure. Analysts suggest Moscow is shifting its strategy toward inflicting systemic human suffering to force a submission, despite a lack of significant battlefield breakthroughs.
* **Black Sea Tensions:** A drone strike on an oil tanker off the coast of Romania has left seven crew members missing, marking another dangerous flashpoint in the regional conflict. Meanwhile, tensions have flared further as Russia accused Ukraine of targeting an oil refinery, signaling a breakdown in the temporary pause of energy-site strikes.
* **U.S. Execution Controversy:** President Trump has unexpectedly canceled the live broadcast of the execution of Nidal Hasan, the Fort Hood attacker. The decision, following concerns over the optics of the first military execution in six decades, highlights the contentious nature of capital punishment in the American political climate.

## POLAND

* **Executive-Judiciary Conflict:** President Karol Nawrocki continues to assert his authority, actively challenging the current government's appointments. Most notably, he has labeled the swearing-in process of Maciej Berek to the Constitutional Tribunal as a "circus," refusing to recognize the legitimacy of the appointment.
* **Presidential Vetoes:** Continuing his trend of legislative confrontation, President Nawrocki has exercised his veto power against three key bills, including controversial amendments to the Electoral Code and regulations regarding political advertising. These actions underscore the deepening institutional gridlock between the President and the parliamentary majority.
* **Defense Vulnerabilities:** Reports citing CNN indicate that Ukraine faces a critical shortage of Patriot missiles, leaving the country increasingly vulnerable to Russian aerial bombardment. This situation places additional pressure on Poland and other NATO neighbors to reassess their own defense contributions and regional security posture.
