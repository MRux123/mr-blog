## WORLD

* **U.S.-Iran Negotiations Face Immediate Obstacles:** The inaugural day of high-stakes talks between Washington and Tehran has been marked by significant internal discord and external pressure. Iranian negotiators have set the cessation of the conflict in Lebanon as a mandatory prerequisite for further dialogue. The situation is further complicated by a discordant messaging strategy within the U.S. administration; while Vice President JD Vance has publicly signaled optimism regarding progress, President Trump has simultaneously reiterated aggressive threats against Tehran, casting doubt on the long-term viability of the current diplomatic track.

## POLAND

* **Ukrainian Leadership Criticizes Polish Political Trajectory:** President Volodymyr Zelensky has issued a pointed critique of Karol Nawrocki, drawing a controversial parallel between the Polish politician and Hungarian Prime Minister Viktor Orbán. Zelensky warned that the current path taken by figures aligning with such political frameworks could lead to unfavorable consequences for bilateral relations and regional stability. This diplomatic friction underscores rising concerns in Kyiv regarding the potential shift in Polish political alliances.
