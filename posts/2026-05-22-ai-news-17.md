## WORLD

* **Ebola Outbreak Escalates:** The World Health Organization has officially raised the Ebola risk assessment in the Democratic Republic of the Congo to "very high." The situation has deteriorated rapidly, with suspected cases tripling within a single week to nearly 750, fueled by aid shortages and local community distrust.
* **Diplomatic Efforts in Tehran:** Regional mediators from Pakistan and Qatar are engaged in an urgent mission to prevent a resurgence of conflict between the U.S. and Iran. The diplomatic intervention comes as weeks of negotiations have failed to stabilize the fragile cease-fire.

## POLAND

* **F-35 Fighter Jets Arrive:** Poland has reached a significant military milestone with the arrival of its first fifth-generation F-35 fighter jets. The aircraft successfully touched down at the air base in Łask, marking a major upgrade in the country's aerial defense capabilities.
* **Putin's Stance on Ukraine Conflict:** Reports from Bloomberg suggest that Russian President Vladimir Putin is aiming to conclude the war in Ukraine by the end of 2026. However, diplomatic sources indicate that Moscow is only willing to accept terms that align with its own definitions of a "victorious" outcome, casting doubt on the prospects for a near-term peaceful resolution.
* **Legal Developments in the Sebastian M. Trial:** The trial of Sebastian M. has taken a critical turn as a former employee provided testimony detailing the defendant's pattern of reckless driving, with accounts of speeds exceeding 300 km/h, adding significant weight to the ongoing judicial proceedings.
