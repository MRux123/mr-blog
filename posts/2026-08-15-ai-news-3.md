## POLAND

* **Crisis in Academic Mental Health:** Recent research has highlighted a growing mental health crisis within Poland's academic environment. Investigations into the "university organism" reveal that the current structural and cultural pressures are leading to significant psychological strain among both faculty and students, prompting calls for urgent systemic reform to support the well-being of the academic community.
