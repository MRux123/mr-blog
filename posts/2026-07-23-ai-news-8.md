## WORLD

* **European Union Escalates Sanctions:** EU ambassadors have reached a unanimous agreement on the 21st sanctions package against Russia. The latest measures include a long-term extension of the crude oil price cap, maintaining economic pressure as the conflict in Ukraine continues.
* **Middle East Security Crisis:** Global energy markets are bracing for volatility following intensified attacks on tankers in the Middle East. The escalation, coupled with increased regional instability, has triggered an immediate rise in oil prices.
* **Geopolitical Realignment in Asia:** Japanese Prime Minister Sanae Takaichi is actively utilizing the legacy of the late Shinzo Abe to advance a nationalist agenda. This strategic move aims to reshape Japan's defense posture and economic policy, marking a potential shift in the regional balance of power.

## POLAND

* **Political Turmoil within PiS:** The Law and Justice (PiS) party faces an internal crisis as uncertainty looms over a potential split between Jarosław Kaczyński and Mateusz Morawiecki. Analysts and coalition members suggest that such a division could lead to the ultimate collapse of the party’s current political structure.
* **Ziobro Case Ruling:** The District Court in Krakow has issued a verdict in the long-running trial involving doctors accused in the death of Jerzy Ziobro. The court upheld the acquittal of most defendants, citing a lack of evidence of medical malpractice, while dismissing one case due to the statute of limitations.
