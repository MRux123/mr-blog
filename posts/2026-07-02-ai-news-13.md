## WORLD

* **Massive Russian Offensive Strikes Kyiv:** The Ukrainian capital is observing a day of mourning following an intense drone and missile assault. At least 20 casualties have been confirmed, marking one of the most significant escalations in the ongoing conflict.
* **Geopolitical Shifts in Iran:** As the nation prepares for the long-delayed funeral of Ayatollah Ali Khamenei—killed during the early stages of the current conflict—regime stability remains a primary focus. Officials aim to use the ceremony to project unity and resilience to both domestic and international audiences.
* **Religious Fracture in the Vatican:** Pope Leo XIV faces his first major institutional crisis as the Vatican moves to excommunicate the Society of St. Pius X. The action follows the group's defiance of papal authority regarding the illicit consecration of new bishops.

## POLAND

* **Leadership Succession Speculation:** Public attention is focused on Nikolai Lukashenko, who recently received a diploma in China, fueling speculation regarding his potential role in the future succession of power in Belarus.
* **Agricultural Crisis in Opole:** A devastating fire has destroyed 70 hectares of barley in the Opole region, highlighting growing concerns over rural security and agricultural losses.
* **Success on the Courts:** Iga Świątek continues her strong performance at Wimbledon, securing a convincing victory and reinforcing her status as a frontrunner in the tournament.
