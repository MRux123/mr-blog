## WORLD

* **Military Escalation in the Strait of Hormuz:** Tensions in the Middle East have reached a critical point following an Iranian attack on a Cyprus-flagged vessel. In a decisive response, the United States has launched a series of strikes against Iranian targets. The incident, which occurred shortly after diplomatic discussions between Iran and Oman regarding maritime access, threatens the stability of one of the world's most vital shipping routes.
* **Security Tragedy at Toronto Festival:** A violent incident occurred at a street festival in Toronto, resulting in at least two fatalities. Local law enforcement has secured the area and continues to investigate the circumstances of the shooting, urging the public to remain vigilant.

## POLAND

* **Premeditated Attack on High-Ranking Police Officials:** A man suspected of planning the assassination of the head of the Central Bureau for Combating Cybercrime (CBZC), Chief Inspector Wojciech Olszowy, and other officers has been remanded in custody. The suspect, involved in a Friday assault on police officers in Warsaw, now faces serious charges, and a court has ordered a three-month pre-trial detention as the investigation continues.
