## WORLD

*   **Amnesty International Reports Crimes Against Humanity in Sudan:** The Rapid Support Forces (RSF) are facing severe accusations from Amnesty International regarding their offensive in El Fasher. The human rights organization documents a systematic campaign involving murder, torture, rape, and enslavement, which experts characterize as ethnic cleansing and crimes against humanity. The report underscores the worsening humanitarian crisis in the region as civilian populations remain the primary targets of the paramilitary group's military operations.

## POLAND

*   **The Paradox of Vacation: Why Time Off Isn't Always Beneficial:** Recent reports in Polish media are highlighting the psychological challenges associated with summer holidays. While vacations are intended to serve as a period of recuperation, not all individuals experience improved well-being. Experts are analyzing factors such as the "anticipatory stress" of travel, difficulties in detaching from professional responsibilities, and the psychological impact of unrealistic holiday expectations, which can lead to significant mental strain rather than the intended rest.
