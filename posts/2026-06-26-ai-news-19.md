## WORLD

*   **Tensions escalate in the Strait of Hormuz:** President Trump has officially accused Iran of breaching the ceasefire following an attack on a Singapore-flagged cargo ship in the Strait of Hormuz. The incident, which the U.S. President labeled as "foolish," has halted large-scale evacuations in the region, raising fears of a renewed military escalation in one of the world's most critical maritime corridors.
*   **DR Congo challenges Rwanda in International Court:** The Democratic Republic of Congo has initiated legal proceedings against Rwanda at the international level. Kinshasa formally accuses its neighbor of orchestrating a series of systematic violations and conflicts dating back to the aftermath of the 1994 Rwandan genocide, marking a significant escalation in regional diplomatic hostilities.

## POLAND

*   **Diplomatic anxiety ahead of Ankara NATO Summit:** European capitals are reportedly bracing for the upcoming NATO summit in Ankara. Sources indicate significant concern among member states regarding the security agenda and the potential for friction during the high-stakes meeting, signaling a period of strategic uncertainty for the Alliance.
*   **President clarifies renovation timeline for national monument:** In a move to address public concern regarding the maintenance of a prominent historical site, the President has officially specified the timeline for repairs on the national monument reservoir, aiming to restore the landmark by early July.
