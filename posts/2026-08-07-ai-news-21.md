## WORLD

*   **US Senate Approves Sweeping Sanctions Against Russia and Iran:** The US Senate has passed a significant piece of legislation introduced by Senator Graham. The bill, described as "hellish" in scope, signals a major hardening of American foreign policy, targeting both Russia and Iran with comprehensive economic and strategic sanctions.
*   **Diplomatic Rift Over Migration Intensifies in Europe:** Tensions between Spain and Italy have reached a critical point following a massive influx of approximately 78,000 migrants into the Spanish exclave of Ceuta. In response to the humanitarian and security pressure, border controls are being implemented, further straining relations within the European Union.
*   **Tragedy in Thailand Sparks Gun Control Debate:** A 14-year-old perpetrator killed eight people, including five teachers, in a shooting incident at his school and home. The incident has prompted the Thai Prime Minister to vow immediate and strict reforms to the country’s gun laws to prevent further domestic violence.

## POLAND

*   **Security Breach Concerns at German Defense Facility:** Polish news sources report alarm in Germany following unauthorized drone flights over a high-priority "Patriot" shipyard. The incident has raised questions regarding critical infrastructure security in the region and potential foreign surveillance.
*   **Violent Incident in Kamienna Góra:** Local authorities are investigating a severe assault on a teenager in Kamienna Góra. The case has garnered significant public attention as investigations into the motives and circumstances of the attack continue to unfold.
