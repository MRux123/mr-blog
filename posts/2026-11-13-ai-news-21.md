## WORLD

*   **Microsoft Global Outage:** The tech giant remains under heavy scrutiny after a massive service disruption impacting Outlook, Teams, and other critical services persisted for nearly 24 hours. Experts warn that this event underscores the precarious global reliance on a concentrated group of technology providers, sparking renewed debates on infrastructure resilience.
*   **European Representation in Ukraine Negotiations:** In a significant diplomatic shift, Europe has successfully negotiated a seat at the table regarding Ukraine war discussions. The continent plans to field a six-member delegation to represent its interests in future peace negotiations involving Russia.
*   **Rising Tensions in the Middle East:** Political instability continues to mount as Gadi Eisenkot publicly accused Prime Minister Benjamin Netanyahu of fostering "racist" hostility. Simultaneously, a new military pact between Turkey and Qatar signals a significant realignment of regional security architectures.

## POLAND

*   **Public Support for Nuclear Capability:** A recent GLOBSEC study indicates that Poland now shows the highest level of support in Europe for the development of nuclear weapon capabilities, reflecting heightened security concerns within the region.
*   **Energy Price Freeze Legislation:** Government officials, including Minister Miłosz Motyka, have confirmed that work is underway on a multi-billion PLN project aimed at freezing gas prices for individual consumers, with hopes to implement the legislation before the end of the year.
*   **Failed Local Referendum in Gliwice:** The recall referendum in Gliwice has been declared a failure, largely due to procedural irregularities, including the discovery of deceased individuals listed on the petition documents, marking a chaotic end to the effort.
