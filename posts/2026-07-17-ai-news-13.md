## WORLD

* **Escalation in US-Iran Relations**: Tensions have reached a new peak as Iran accuses the United States of targeting critical civilian infrastructure. The Pentagon maintains that the recent military operations were strictly aimed at undermining Iranian military capabilities, raising concerns about the potential for further regional destabilization.
* **Humanitarian Tragedy at Sea**: A humanitarian crisis is unfolding following reports that hundreds of Rohingya refugees are feared drowned after their vessels sank while attempting to flee Myanmar. The incident highlights the ongoing plight of the persecuted minority group in their search for asylum.
* **Political Fallout from World Cup**: A sporting event has transformed into a diplomatic flashpoint after the Argentina-England World Cup semi-final. Argentina’s victory was overshadowed by nationalist rhetoric regarding the Falkland Islands (Malvinas), prompting FIFA to open an investigation into the matter.

## POLAND

* **Legislative Breakthrough on Patostreaming**: In a rare show of cross-party cooperation, the Polish parliament has successfully passed legislation aimed at curbing harmful "patostreaming" content online. The bill, which received presidential support, represents a compromise between Law and Justice (PiS) and the Civic Coalition (KO), signaling a unified stance on digital safety.
* **Internal Instability in Opposition**: Speculation regarding the future of the Law and Justice (PiS) party is mounting, with reports suggesting that approximately 40 deputies may be considering departure. This potential exodus poses a significant challenge to the party's cohesion and parliamentary influence.
* **Governmental Coalitional Friction**: The governing coalition faces increasing internal pressure and public disagreements, as evidenced by recent statements suggesting "no one should be surprised" by the rising temperature of political debates within the cabinet.
