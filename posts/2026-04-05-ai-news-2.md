## WORLD

* **Escalating Iran-U.S. Standoff:** The geopolitical situation in the Middle East has reached a critical juncture following Iran’s rejection of the U.S. ultimatum regarding the closure of the Strait of Hormuz. As global oil prices surge, the situation has been further inflamed by an Israeli military strike on a major petrochemical facility in southwestern Iran, while U.S. forces continue search and rescue operations for a missing airman amidst ongoing threats from President Trump.
* **New York Tragedy:** Law enforcement authorities in New York have charged two individuals in connection with a fatal shooting in Williamsburg, where a seven-month-old infant was killed in a stroller. The investigation remains ongoing as the city grapples with the violence.

## POLAND

* **Political Discourse on Foreign Policy:** A sharp exchange between former President Bronisław Komorowski and former Prime Minister Leszek Miller took place on Polsat News, centered on the strategic positioning of Poland. The debate highlighted conflicting visions regarding Poland's security identity, with Komorowski distancing himself from the narrative of Poland acting as the "Israel of Europe" in relation to the United States, while Miller emphasized the deterrent power of NATO and the U.S. security umbrella.
