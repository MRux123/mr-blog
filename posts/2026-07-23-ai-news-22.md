## WORLD

* **Political and Social Unrest in India**: Prime Minister Narendra Modi is facing mounting pressure as a student-led protest movement escalates in response to economic stagnation and systemic failures. The situation remains volatile, further underscored by activist Sonam Wangchuk ending a 26-day hunger strike, signaling a strategic pause intended to prevent further violence amid the ongoing nationwide calls for educational and economic reform.
* **Environmental Crisis in Spain**: The region surrounding Madrid is currently grappling with a severe wildfire crisis. The emergency has forced the evacuation of over 3,500 residents, with transport links suspended and local authorities issuing high-level warnings. The disaster is part of a broader, alarming trend, with over 100,000 hectares of land scorched across the country this season.

## POLAND

* **Diplomatic Tensions with Belarus**: The Belarusian Foreign Ministry has issued a provocative communication, claiming to have "warned" Polish authorities regarding an alleged planned terrorist attack on Polish soil. Analysts suggest this may be a strategic maneuver by Minsk to exert political pressure on Polish security services rather than a genuine security alert.
* **New Ombudsman Takes Office**: Sylwia Gregorczyk-Abram, recently confirmed by the Senate as the new Human Rights Ombudsman (RPO), has officially addressed concerns regarding her impartiality. Addressing accusations of political bias, Gregorczyk-Abram emphasized her commitment to neutrality, stating that her personal views will be set aside to ensure the office serves all citizens equally.
* **Judicial Milestone for Ziobro**: Former Justice Minister Zbigniew Ziobro has publicly responded to a significant court ruling concerning the death of his father. Ziobro described the verdict as a crucial moment for his family, marking the culmination of a twenty-year legal battle to establish the truth behind the incident.
