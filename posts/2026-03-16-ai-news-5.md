## WORLD

* **Geopolitical Standoff in the Middle East:** A drone-induced attack near Dubai International Airport has triggered a significant fire in a fuel storage tank, forcing authorities to suspend all air traffic. The incident marks a serious escalation in regional security concerns.
* **Allied Pushback Against U.S. Policy:** Japan and Australia have officially declined President Donald Trump's request to deploy naval forces to the Strait of Hormuz. The rejection serves as a notable setback for U.S. maritime strategy in the region amidst ongoing tensions with Iran.
* **Constitutional Crisis in Washington:** President Trump has launched a scathing critique of the U.S. Supreme Court, labeling it a "political weapon" following the court's decision to invalidate specific tariffs and obstruct an investigation into Federal Reserve Chair Jerome Powell.

## POLAND

* **Cinematic Success at the 98th Academy Awards:** Polish animator Maciek Szczerbowski has achieved international recognition, winning the Oscar for Best Animated Short Film for his work, *The Girl Who Cried Pearls*, directed alongside Chris Lavis.
* **Oscars Overview:** While the night was dominated by the triumph of Paul Thomas Anderson’s *One Battle After Another*, which secured top honors for Best Film, Best Director, and Best Screenplay, Polish costume designer Małgorzata Turzańska fell short of a win in her category.
