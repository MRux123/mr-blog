## WORLD

*   **Escalating Tensions in the Middle East:** Donald Trump has issued a severe ultimatum to Oman, threatening military action should the nation impede the US position regarding ongoing Iran nuclear deal negotiations. The threat follows the expiration of a 60-day window for peace talks and highlights the growing volatility surrounding the Strait of Hormuz.
*   **Continued Escalation in Russia-Ukraine Conflict:** The conflict remains in a high-intensity phase, with both sides reporting lethal strikes over the past 24 hours. Moscow has reported nine casualties, while Kyiv confirms four fatalities, marking another grim day in the prolonged regional war.
*   **Political Crackdown in Russia:** In a significant move against domestic opposition, prominent anti-war politician Lev Schlosberg has been sentenced to 11 years in prison. The verdict, based on charges of "discrediting" the Russian army, is widely viewed by international observers as a targeted effort to silence political dissent.

## POLAND

*   **Repatriation of Hungarian Crash Victims:** The first transport of Polish citizens injured in the recent bus accident in Hungary has landed at Rzeszów-Jasionka Airport. The tragic incident claimed 12 lives, and the return of the survivors marks the beginning of complex medical support efforts, with some victims being transferred to specialized facilities in Warsaw.
*   **Political Tensions within the Coalition:** The appointment of Maciej Berek as a Constitutional Tribunal judge has sparked internal friction within the government. Anna Maria Żukowska of the Left criticized the decision, labeling it a power play by Prime Minister Tusk that undermines the collaborative spirit of the governing coalition.
*   **Extreme Weather Disruptions:** A massive supercell storm has moved across southern Poland, causing significant destruction in the Silesia region. Residents reported hail stones reaching the size of golf balls, highlighting the increasing frequency of extreme weather phenomena in the country.
