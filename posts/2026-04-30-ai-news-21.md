## WORLD

* **Iran Signals New Policy on Strait of Hormuz:** Ayatollah Mojtaba Khamenei has announced plans to establish "new legal frameworks" regarding the Strait of Hormuz. This strategic move, coupled with his declaration that Iran will retain its nuclear capabilities, marks a significant shift in regional geopolitical stability.
* **Escalating Tensions in Lebanon:** Despite a standing ceasefire, deadly Israeli strikes on southern Lebanon have resulted in at least nine fatalities, including children. The incident highlights the fragility of the current peace agreements in the Levant.
* **Trump Targets European Alliances:** Former U.S. President Donald Trump has suggested he would consider withdrawing American forces from Italy and Spain, labeling the former unhelpful and the latter's performance "terrible." This rhetoric further complicates the future of transatlantic security cooperation.

## POLAND

* **Presidential Criticism of Prosecution Tactics:** President Karol Nawrocki has sharply rebuked the current investigation into administrative decisions regarding the Constitutional Tribunal. Nawrocki characterized the prosecution's actions as a distraction from their primary duty—fighting crime—rather than investigating presidential advisory circles.
* **Political Fallout for Janusz Kowalski:** The resignation of Janusz Kowalski from the PiS parliamentary club is reportedly linked to the "Zondacrypto" scandal. Leaked allegations suggest that anonymous reports regarding the politician's ties to the cryptocurrency exchange's leadership led to his departure from the party's inner circle.
* **Internal Discord within Poland 2050:** The governing coalition faces internal friction after MP Bartosz Romowicz cast a dissenting vote in favor of a motion of no confidence against Minister Paulina Hennig-Kloska. The move has prompted sharp criticism from party colleagues, leading to questions regarding party discipline and the future of the coalition.
