## WORLD

* **Kyiv Hostage Crisis:** At least two people have been confirmed dead following a violent armed assault and hostage situation in a Kyiv supermarket. Security services conducted a major operation, resulting in the gunman being killed during a shootout with police.
* **Casualties in Lebanon:** A French peacekeeper serving with the UNIFIL mission in southern Lebanon has been killed in an attack. French President Emmanuel Macron has directly blamed Hezbollah for the incident, although the Iran-backed group has denied any involvement.
* **Diplomatic Clarification:** Pope Leo has issued a formal response clarifying his recent remarks, stating that his comments during his Africa trip were misconstrued by international media as direct criticisms of Donald Trump.

## POLAND

* **Aerial Search in Lower Silesia:** Emergency services, including multiple fire brigades and a police helicopter, have launched an intensive search operation in the Lower Silesia region following reports of a balloon falling in the vicinity of Leśno and Bożkowice.
* **Severe Weather Warnings:** The Institute of Meteorology and Water Management (IMGW) has issued urgent alerts for the weekend, warning of heavy rainfall, storms, and ground frosts across the majority of the country, advising caution to all citizens.
