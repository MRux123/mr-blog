* **Sharpeville Massacre Victims Seek Restitution:** Decades after the 1960 apartheid-era tragedy in Sharpeville, South Africa, survivors and families of victims are launching a new wave of litigation. The claimants are demanding compensation for the state-sanctioned violence that claimed hundreds of lives during protests against restrictive pass laws, highlighting an ongoing quest for justice and historical closure.
