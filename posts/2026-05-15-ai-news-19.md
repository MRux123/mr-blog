## WORLD

* **Middle East Truce Extended:** The U.S. State Department has confirmed a 45-day extension of the ceasefire between Israel and Lebanon. Despite the agreement, the region remains volatile, with reports of continued sporadic fire between the parties involved.
* **Trump’s Stance on Taiwan:** Following a summit with Chinese President Xi Jinping, President Trump has issued a formal warning to Taiwan regarding any potential declaration of independence, urging both Beijing and Taipei to de-escalate tensions over the self-governing island.
* **Strategic Strike in Russia:** Ukrainian forces have successfully targeted a strategic oil refinery in Ryazan. The strike, reportedly involving nearly 100 drones, has led local authorities to declare a state of emergency in the region.

## POLAND

* **Uncertainty Over U.S. Military Presence:** The Polish government has demanded urgent clarifications from Washington following reports that the deployment of a planned U.S. army brigade to Poland has been cancelled. Deputy Foreign Minister Marcin Bosacki emphasized that the lack of clear communication from the American side has caused significant diplomatic friction.
* **Legal Crisis in Ukraine Affects Polish Media Discourse:** The detention of Andrij Jermak, a key figure in the Ukrainian administration, continues to draw attention. Following his inability to post bail despite assistance from high-profile figures, the case remains a focal point for regional political stability discussions.
* **Tragedy in Lubin:** Local authorities continue to investigate the shooting in Lubin that resulted in the deaths of two individuals. The perpetrator, a 21-year-old, died by suicide, leaving law enforcement to piece together the motives behind the incident.
