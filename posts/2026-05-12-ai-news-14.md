## WORLD

* **Global Economic Shock:** US inflation has reached its highest level since May 2023, hitting 3.8%. The surge is directly attributed to escalating energy costs stemming from the ongoing war in Iran, signaling a period of potential instability for global markets.
* **Geopolitical Standoffs:** Tensions remain high as President Xi Jinping prepares to pressure President Trump regarding US arms sales to Taiwan. Meanwhile, concerns are growing over the Trump administration's muted response to deliberate attacks on American corporate facilities located in Ukraine, including subsidiaries of major firms like Coca-Cola and Cargill.
* **Political Volatility:** In the United Kingdom, Prime Minister Keir Starmer is facing significant pressure to resign following poor performance in local elections, though he has vowed to maintain his position for the time being.

## POLAND

* **Legal and Security Implications:** Former justice official and PiS politician Zbigniew Ziobro has reportedly fled Europe via unofficial routes, effectively evading local law enforcement. This incident has triggered intense debate regarding the integrity of the border and judicial oversight.
* **Shift in Social Policy:** Prime Minister Donald Tusk has signaled an acceleration in work regarding the transcription of same-sex marriages performed abroad. Crucially, the Premier emphasized that the upcoming regulation will be limited to administrative recognition and will not extend to adoption rights.
* **Public Safety and Environment:** Following a fatal bear attack in the Płonna region, regional authorities have announced a major operation to capture and relocate 18 brown bears. The animals will be tagged with GPS monitors as the government responds to the rising frequency of human-wildlife encounters.
