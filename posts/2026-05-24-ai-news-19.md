## WORLD

*   **Growing Instability Within the Kremlin:** As the protracted war in Ukraine and deepening economic crises persist, reports indicate an increasing sense of impending catastrophe among the Russian political and business elite. The Kremlin is struggling to maintain a façade of control as domestic support reaches historic lows, marking what analysts describe as the most precarious period of Vladimir Putin's 25-year tenure.
*   **Ukraine Calls for Emergency UN Action:** Following a massive Russian missile strike on Kyiv and its surrounding region, the Ukrainian Ministry of Foreign Affairs has formally demanded an emergency session of the UN Security Council and the OSCE, citing a severe escalation in threats to regional security.
*   **Diplomatic Stance on Iran:** US President Donald Trump has advised American negotiators to avoid rushing into a final deal with Iran. Despite claims made on Saturday that an agreement was largely negotiated, the administration now insists that both parties must take their time to ensure the terms are handled with due diligence.

## POLAND

*   **Political Earthquake in Krakow:** Exit poll data from the recent local referendum indicates that residents of Krakow have voted in favor of removing Mayor Aleksander Miszalski and the City Council from office. With the required threshold for a valid referendum met, the city now faces the prospect of snap elections.
*   **Cracking Down on False Alerts:** The Ministry of Interior and Administration (MSWiA) has announced a firm response to a wave of fraudulent emergency reports impacting public figures and institutions. Deputy Minister Czesław Mroczek confirmed that law enforcement is currently preparing a significant statement regarding the prosecution of individuals responsible for orchestrating these false security threats.
