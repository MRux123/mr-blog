## WORLD

*   **Geopolitical Tensions in the Middle East:** Iran has issued an ultimatum, threatening to maintain the closure of the Strait of Hormuz until the United States changes its current diplomatic and military posture. This development poses a significant risk to global energy supplies and maritime security.
*   **Defense Strategy Shift in Taiwan:** In a response to the ongoing conflict in Ukraine, Taiwan is increasingly prioritizing drone technology to build a "hellscape" defense. This strategy aims to deter a potential amphibious invasion by the People's Republic of China, highlighting a major evolution in asymmetric warfare tactics.

## POLAND

*   **Intensification of Hostilities:** Poland faces heightened security concerns following reports of Russian missile strikes on the Ukrainian cities of Kharkiv and Odesa, resulting in civilian casualties. Simultaneously, the border town of Belgorod has experienced a massive drone assault, bringing the kinetic reality of the war closer to Polish territory.
*   **Infrastructure Milestones:** Despite regional instability, Poland is nearing a major domestic achievement, with only 150 kilometers of construction remaining to complete its national strategic highway network, signaling continued internal development amidst external geopolitical pressures.
