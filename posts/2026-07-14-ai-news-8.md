## WORLD

* **Escalating Conflict in the Middle East:** U.S. forces have launched strikes against Iranian targets for the third consecutive night. The situation has been further inflamed by President Trump’s announcement of a 20 percent tariff on cargo transiting the Strait of Hormuz, a move that prompted Iranian retaliatory attacks on two tankers.
* **NATO Borders Brace for Security Risks:** Amidst ongoing regional tensions, the Estonian city of Tartu has begun reinforcing school infrastructure with protective film. Local authorities cite the potential threat of drone attacks and point to Ukrainian defensive strategies as a model for civil protection.
* **Ukraine's Path to the European Union:** Ukraine and Moldova have reached a significant milestone in their integration process, with the European Union officially opening the second cluster of accession negotiations.

## POLAND

* **Enhanced Military Cooperation:** Prime Minister Donald Tusk announced following summit talks in Paris that Poland will host joint military exercises with French and British forces this autumn. These maneuvers are intended to provide concrete security guarantees for Ukraine and the broader Eastern Flank.
* **Developments in Public Services:** The government has unveiled new admissions limits for medical universities to address staffing shortages in the healthcare sector. Simultaneously, the Ministry has introduced updated letter configurations for vehicle registration plates, marking a change in domestic administrative standards.
