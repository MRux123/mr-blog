## WORLD

* **Escalation in US-Iran Conflict:** The geopolitical situation in the Middle East has reached a critical juncture as the United States continues its military campaign against Iran. Reports indicate an 11th consecutive night of strikes, with President Trump specifically threatening to target key underground nuclear infrastructure. In response, Iranian authorities have activated air defense systems in Tehran, signaling a potential widening of the confrontation.
* **Ukrainian Military Command Overhaul:** President Volodymyr Zelensky has officially appointed Mychajło Drapaty as the new Commander-in-Chief of the Armed Forces of Ukraine, replacing Ołeksandr Syrski. This significant change in leadership is being framed by the Ukrainian administration as a strategic move to bolster the nation's defense capabilities against ongoing Russian aggression.
* **EU Social Media Regulation:** France is spearheading a legislative shift within the European Union by implementing a ban on social media access for individuals under the age of 15. The new law mandates that platforms must strictly verify the age of all users, marking a significant step in the EU’s approach to regulating digital safety and child protection online.

## POLAND

* **Growing Public Dissatisfaction with Healthcare:** A new poll conducted by Dziennik Gazeta Prawna reveals that nearly 50% of the Polish public supports the dismissal of Minister of Health Jolanta Sobierańska-Grenda. This sentiment follows recent government announcements regarding proposed reforms to the healthcare system, suggesting that the public remains skeptical about the current administration's ability to address systemic challenges.
* **Regional Security Initiatives:** In a move to strengthen public order and international cooperation, joint Polish-Italian police patrols have resumed operations on the streets of Kraków. This initiative underscores the ongoing commitment to maintain security in major urban centers through enhanced European collaboration.
