## WORLD

The visit, confirmed by flight tracking data and reported by major networks, appears to coincide with US requests for a temporary de-escalation of hostilities regarding the conflict in Ukraine.
*   **US-Canada Trade War Escalation:** Economic tensions between Washington and Ottawa have intensified, with Canada announcing retaliatory tariffs reaching as high as 50%. The measures target a wide range of American goods, from industrial steel to consumer products, marking a significant escalation in the ongoing trade dispute.

Minister Katarzyna Pełczyńska-Nałęcz has publicly questioned the appropriateness of the move, given Giertych’s dual role as a prominent politician and legal counsel.

Recent polling indicates a near-split in voter sentiment, highlighting the ongoing political polarization concerning legislative changes in the country.
