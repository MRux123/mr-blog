## WORLD

* **China and Russia Strengthen Strategic Alignment:** Following a high-profile visit from Donald Trump, Chinese leader Xi Jinping has moved to deepen ties with Vladimir Putin. Xi is positioning China as a central power broker, advocating for a Middle East ceasefire while pointedly ignoring Russia’s actions in Ukraine and criticizing U.S. foreign policy. 
* **Escalating Tensions in the Strait of Hormuz:** Iran’s use of "triangular coercion"—attacking Gulf states and effectively manipulating maritime traffic—has exposed significant vulnerabilities in U.S. strategic influence. The situation remains volatile as the region navigates these asymmetric threats.
* **Health Crisis in Central Africa:** The World Health Organization (WHO) is closely monitoring an Ebola outbreak in the Democratic Republic of the Congo. While health officials currently advise against labeling it a "pandemic emergency," the medical evacuation of an American doctor to Germany highlights the international concern surrounding the scale and speed of the virus's spread.

## POLAND

* **Demographic Crisis Threatens Local Government Stability:** A new report by Bank Gospodarstwa Krajowego (BGK) warns of a severe fiscal crisis for Polish municipalities. Projections indicate a 30% decline in the national workforce by 2060, which will significantly erode the tax base and threaten the financial viability of local governments across the country.
* **Military Preparedness Amid Global Instability:** In response to heightened global tensions and concurrent Russian nuclear exercises, the U.S. Air Force successfully test-fired a Minuteman III intercontinental ballistic missile. This demonstration underscores Poland’s reliance on NATO’s collective nuclear deterrence strategy during a period of increasing geopolitical friction in Eastern Europe.
