## WORLD

* **Critical Situation at Bushehr Nuclear Plant:** International concerns are mounting as the IAEA reports direct attacks near Iran’s Bushehr nuclear facility. The situation has reached a critical threshold, with Russia's Rosatom confirming the evacuation of its personnel following an attack that resulted in a fatality and structural damage. The agency describes the events as the "worst-case scenario" for regional nuclear security.
* **Tensions in the Strait of Hormuz:** The geopolitical standoff continues to intensify, with U.S. forces searching for a missing airman following the downing of a fighter jet. While Iran has allowed limited transit for humanitarian cargo and specific nations, the overall situation remains precarious as President Trump maintains an ultimatum regarding the reopening of the vital maritime chokepoint.

## POLAND

* **Impending High-Level U.S. Delegation to Kyiv:** A significant diplomatic development is on the horizon, as Ukrainian officials report an expected visit by a high-profile U.S. delegation, including figures such as Jared Kushner and Senator Lindsey Graham. The talks are reportedly aimed at discussing potential frameworks for ending the conflict in Ukraine, with the meeting tentatively scheduled shortly after the Orthodox Easter.
* **Defense Maneuvers on NATO’s Flank:** In response to the heightened security environment, the Finnish Gulf Coast Guard is set to launch specialized military exercises on Easter Sunday. The drills, focusing on countering combat drones and artillery response, underscore the growing vigilance among NATO member states in Northern Europe amidst the current global instability.
