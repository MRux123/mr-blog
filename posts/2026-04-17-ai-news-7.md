## WORLD

* **Lebanon-Israel Cease-Fire Implemented:** A ceasefire between Israel and Hezbollah has officially come into effect. While the agreement, facilitated amidst regional tensions, represents a significant diplomatic shift, its long-term viability remains uncertain as stakeholders question the level of commitment from both the Israeli government and the Iranian-backed militia.
* **Concerns Over 'Mythos' AI Model:** Top global finance ministers and banking leaders have issued a formal warning regarding the 'Mythos' artificial intelligence model. Security experts highlight that the model possesses an unprecedented, potentially dangerous capacity to identify and exploit critical cybersecurity vulnerabilities in global financial infrastructure.

## POLAND

* **Parliamentary Unrest and Expulsions:** The Sejm witnessed significant disruption as the Speaker, Włodzimierz Czarzasty, excluded opposition MPs Dariusz Matecki and Michał Woś from proceedings. The tensions flared following political protests on the chamber floor, highlighting the deepening polarization within the Polish legislative body.
* **Internal Dynamics within PiS:** The Law and Justice (PiS) party is navigating internal restructuring regarding the political future of Mateusz Morawiecki’s circle. While party leadership has proposed an "Expert Council" to integrate the former Prime Minister's associates, public statements from party officials continue to reflect a complex, often contradictory narrative regarding the unity and strategic direction of the party.
* **Russian Drone Incursion into NATO Airspace:** The Romanian Ministry of Defense confirmed that a Russian drone violated NATO airspace during a large-scale strike on Ukraine. The incident has prompted an immediate investigation and underscores the heightened security risks posed to the Alliance's eastern flank.
