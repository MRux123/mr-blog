## WORLD

* **U.S.-Canada Trade War Escalates:** Tensions have reached a critical point following the collapse of trade negotiations. President Trump has escalated rhetoric, suggesting that Canada seeks the benefits of U.S. statehood, while Canadian Prime Minister Justin Trudeau has announced retaliatory tariffs, marking a significant deterioration in bilateral relations.
* **Political Turmoil in Ukraine:** The Ukrainian government faces internal pressure following accusations of corruption. A sacked former defense minister has publicly challenged President Zelensky, calling for investigations into his administration and advocating for national elections.
* **Tragedy in Sweden:** Authorities are investigating a fatal sword attack that has left a 17-year-old girl dead and two minors injured. An 18-year-old suspect has been detained, sparking a national debate on public security.

## POLAND

* **Major Tax Reform Announced for 2027:** The government has unveiled a comprehensive tax overhaul for 2027, featuring adjustments to tax brackets, higher CIT rates, and changes to the lump-sum tax system. Financial analysts are currently assessing the potential winners and losers of these structural economic shifts.
* **Severe Weather Disruptions:** A powerful windstorm has swept across Poland, resulting in widespread power outages for thousands of households and significant infrastructural damage. Emergency services remain on high alert as they conduct repair operations.
* **Tragic Incident in Krakow:** Following a fatal car accident in which a driver struck a family on a sidewalk in Krakow, the prosecutor's office has released new updates on the investigation, highlighting the ongoing legal proceedings against the suspect.
