## WORLD

*   **Heightened Escalation Between Iran and the United States:** The geopolitical situation has reached a critical juncture as the U.S. intensifies military pressure on Iran ahead of a looming diplomatic deadline. Following reports of strikes on the oil-exporting island of Kharg and threats regarding the Strait of Hormuz, global markets and diplomats remain on high alert. While President Trump has signaled a potential willingness to reconsider his ultimatum if concrete negotiations emerge, Iran has reportedly suspended direct communications, signaling a severe breakdown in de-escalation efforts.
*   **Humanitarian Crisis Deepens in Gaza:** The World Health Organization (WHO) has suspended medical evacuations from Gaza following the death of a contractor by Israeli fire. The Israeli military maintains the action was taken due to an "immediate threat," but the halt in operations significantly exacerbates the humanitarian emergency in the region. 
*   **Security Incidents in Turkey:** An armed attack at the Israeli consulate in Istanbul has resulted in one fatality and two injuries. While the diplomatic post has been inactive for over two years, the incident highlights the volatile security environment currently affecting Israeli interests abroad.

## POLAND

*   **Judicial Council Reform:** The process for selecting members of the National Council of the Judiciary (KRS) is reaching a critical stage. The names of all candidates have been finalized, with the Sejm expected to hold a decisive vote on these appointments by mid-May, marking a significant step in the ongoing restructuring of the Polish judicial system.
*   **Domestic Political Conflict:** Tensions within the Polish government and opposition have intensified, highlighted by a formal dispute between Marcin Przydacz and Michał Szczerba. The clash, stemming from public comments made on political media programs, underscores the current polarization in the Polish parliamentary landscape.
*   **Investigation into the Death of Damian Sobol:** The District Prosecutor’s Office in Przemyśl has extended its investigation into the death of Polish volunteer Damian Sobol, who was killed in an Israeli airstrike in the Gaza Strip in the spring of 2024. The extension until October indicates the ongoing legal efforts to clarify the circumstances surrounding the death of the World Central Kitchen worker.
