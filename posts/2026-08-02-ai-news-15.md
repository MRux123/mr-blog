## WORLD

*   **Escalating Conflict in Gaza:** Following a reported disarmament agreement with Hamas, Israeli strikes on Gaza City, Khan Younis, and Deir el-Balah have resulted in at least 13 fatalities. The Israeli military maintains that the operations were targeted at specific military operatives.
*   **Aerial Tragedy in Greece:** Mid-air collision involving two firefighting helicopters has occurred while battling severe wildfires west of Athens. The incident has intensified concerns regarding the containment of the ongoing natural disaster, which has already claimed the lives of three firefighters.
*   **Continued Russia-Ukraine Hostilities:** The conflict remains volatile as Ukraine reports successful strikes on a major Russian oil refinery and an airfield. In response, Moscow claims to have intercepted a record number of Ukrainian drones, while simultaneously conducting military operations against Ukrainian maritime assets in the Black Sea.

## POLAND

*   **Historic Volleyball Victory:** The Polish national men's volleyball team secured their third-ever Nations League title. In a gripping five-set final, the Polish squad defeated the United States with a score of 3:2, cementing their position as a global powerhouse in the sport.
*   **Political Debate Proposals:** Former Prime Minister Mateusz Morawiecki has publicly declared his readiness to engage in a televised debate with current Prime Minister Donald Tusk. The proposal, which has generated significant media interest, aims to address key issues currently concerning the Polish public.
*   **Technical Glitches in Retail:** A widespread IT failure affecting discount calculations at Żabka convenience stores caused nationwide disruption on Sunday. The company confirmed that while the issue impacted a large portion of its retail network, recovery operations were largely successful by late afternoon.
