## WORLD

*   **Political Gridlock in Israel:** Following the latest elections, both Prime Minister Benjamin Netanyahu and opposition leader Gadi Eisenkot have claimed success, leaving the country's political future in a state of flux. With final vote counting expected to take days, President Isaac Herzog faces the challenging task of navigating the formation of a stable government amidst deeply divided national sentiment.
*   **Spanish Rail Disaster:** A major rail collision near Toledo has resulted in at least 30 fatalities. The accident, involving high-speed passenger trains, has drawn immediate parallels to previous infrastructure failures, raising urgent concerns regarding systemic safety protocols and human error in European high-speed rail operations.
*   **Ebola Outbreak Risks:** Health officials, including Dr. Jean Kaseya of the Africa CDC, have warned that the current "unprecedented" Ebola outbreak—which has already claimed nearly 5,000 lives—could spread internationally if global funding for containment and preparedness is not significantly scaled up immediately.

## POLAND

*   **Political Fallout from Senator Lenz Case:** A growing controversy has emerged surrounding the prosecution's investigation into Senator Tomasz Lenz. Allegations that authorities seized a hospital director's phone to identify the source of reports regarding facility irregularities have triggered demands for transparency from political leaders and an inquiry by the Ministry of Justice.
*   **Call for Rail Safety Reform:** Domestic discourse in Poland has shifted toward the safety of local transport networks following the tragic train crash in Spain. Experts, including the head of the Polish Train Drivers' Union, are urging local authorities to reassess domestic rail safety systems to ensure similar human or technical failures do not occur on Polish tracks.
*   **Mourning the Loss of Radosław Ratajszczak:** The passing of Radosław Ratajszczak, the influential former director of the Wrocław Zoo (2007–2022), has been met with national tributes. Ratajszczak was widely recognized for his pioneering work in conservation and the establishment of the DODO Foundation, which focuses on protecting endangered species globally.
