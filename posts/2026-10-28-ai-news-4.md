## WORLD

* **Uncertainty Looms Over Israeli Elections:** Following a tightly contested election, both Prime Minister Benjamin Netanyahu and opposition leader Gadi Eisenkot have claimed confidence in their ability to form the next government. With official vote counts potentially spanning several days, the political future of the nation remains in a state of flux as both sides signal a desire for a decisive mandate.
* **Ebola Outbreak Concerns Intensify:** Health authorities are investigating the origin of an Ebola case in Nigeria, the first in over a decade. The patient, a Ugandan-born wife of a Swiss diplomat, is believed to have been infected during travel in Uganda. This development has heightened global concerns regarding the potential for the virus to spread beyond its current reach in Central and East Africa.

## POLAND

* **Diplomatic Fallout Following Bolsonaro’s Departure:** Reports indicate that former Brazilian President Jair Bolsonaro has left his home country, with authorities in the United States currently preparing legal documentation. This move follows ongoing investigations in Brazil and signals potential escalation in international legal proceedings.
* **Trump’s Vision for Venezuela:** In a striking geopolitical statement, U.S. President Donald Trump has suggested the possibility of Venezuela becoming the 51st U.S. state. The announcement coincided with the arrival of a tanker carrying one million barrels of Venezuelan oil, which is slated for refining in Texas, marking a significant shift in diplomatic and economic rhetoric regarding the South American nation.
