## WORLD

* **Middle East Crisis Intensifies:** The geopolitical situation in the Persian Gulf has reached a critical boiling point. Following a series of failed missile and drone attacks directed by Iran toward Bahrain and Kuwait, the United States military has engaged in direct "self-defense" strikes against Iranian targets, including infrastructure on Qeshm Island. Ceasefire negotiations have effectively stalled as both nations trade military blows, raising concerns over a broader regional conflict.
* **Ukraine War Stalemate:** Despite Russia's continued intensification of attacks along the Ukrainian front, there are emerging signs of fatigue within the Russian domestic sphere. Analysts suggest that the prolonged nature of the four-year-old conflict is beginning to erode the traditional consensus even among segments of the population historically loyal to the Kremlin, though Vladimir Putin maintains an uncompromising stance regarding the war's objectives.

## POLAND

* **Public Health Alert:** The Main Pharmaceutical Inspectorate (GIF) has issued an urgent nationwide recall for "Travoprost + Timolol Medical Valley," a medication used to treat high intraocular pressure and glaucoma. The decision follows quality control concerns, forcing the immediate suspension of sales across all Polish pharmacies to ensure patient safety.
* **Political Polarization on Cultural Funding:** A heated debate surrounding the government's proposed legislation for ZUS (Social Insurance Institution) contribution subsidies for artists has sparked controversy. During a recent televised discussion, sharp exchanges between coalition members and the Left highlighted deep-seated ideological divides regarding the state’s role in financing the creative sector, leaving observers questioning the bill's future in parliament.
