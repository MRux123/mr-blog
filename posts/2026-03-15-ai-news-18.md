## WORLD

* **Escalation in the Middle East:** The conflict involving Israel, Lebanon, and Iran continues to intensify. Lebanon’s health ministry reports a death toll of 850, while concerns over global energy stability persist as U.S. officials warn that oil prices are unlikely to drop soon due to the closure of the Strait of Hormuz. Additionally, the U.S. has confirmed the loss of six crew members in an aircraft crash in Iraq linked to ongoing operations against Iranian influence.
* **Geopolitical Tensions in Eastern Europe:** President Volodymyr Zelensky has accused EU allies of "blackmail" regarding the resumption of Russian oil flows via Ukrainian pipelines, arguing that such actions effectively undermine existing sanctions. Simultaneously, Swedish authorities have arrested a Russian ship captain for operating under a fraudulent flag, highlighting ongoing maritime security concerns in the Baltic region.

## POLAND

* **Major Traffic Tragedies:** Two severe transport accidents have shocked the country. In Poznań, a car struck a tram stop, leaving a 25-year-old woman in critical condition. Meanwhile, near Płock, a collision between a BMW and a railbus at an unguarded crossing resulted in the death of a passenger.
* **High-Profile Criminal Investigation:** Police have issued a public appeal for information regarding Kamil Sylwestrzak, a former Ekstraklasa football player. Authorities are seeking the 37-year-old in connection with a major investigation into narcotics trafficking and the leadership of an organized crime group.
