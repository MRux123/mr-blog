## WORLD

*   **Climate Crisis Escalates in Europe:** Massive wildfires in France and Spain have forced over 250,000 people to flee their homes. Authorities in Bordeaux describe the situation as critical, with containment efforts struggling against the scale of the blazes. Poland has announced it will send specialized firefighting teams to assist in the relief efforts.
*   **Political Shift in India:** The "Cockroach Janta Party" has declared an end to weeks of intense protests following the resignation of the Indian Education Minister. The movement, which became a significant symbol of youth dissatisfaction with Prime Minister Narendra Modi’s administration, claims a major victory, though underlying socio-economic tensions remain a point of concern.
*   **Diplomatic Pressure on Moscow:** In a surprising move, Kazakh President Kassym-Jomart Tokayev, typically considered an ally of Moscow, has openly urged Vladimir Putin to freeze the conflict in Ukraine. Tokayev warned that the ongoing war serves only the opponents of Russia and Ukraine and must reach a conclusion.

## POLAND

*   **Tragedy in Łódź Voivodeship:** A devastating road accident on the national road DK74 near Wójcin has resulted in the death of two children, aged nine and 11. The incident, which left five others injured, has caused major traffic disruptions on the route between Piotrków and Kielce, prompting an ongoing police investigation.
*   **Frontline Technology:** Recent reports indicate that Ukrainian forces, specifically the 26th Artillery Brigade, successfully utilized a Polish-made FlyEye reconnaissance drone to guide HIMARS strikes against a Russian ammunition depot and artillery position near Bakhmut. The operation highlights the continued importance of Polish military support in the region.
