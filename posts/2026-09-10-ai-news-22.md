## WORLD

* **Tragedy in Bukavu:** A devastating fire has ripped through two schools in the eastern DR Congo city of Bukavu, resulting in the deaths of more than 20 children. Local authorities report that a stampede occurred as students attempted to flee the engulfed buildings. The situation remains critical as rescue operations conclude and officials investigate the origins of the blaze in the rebel-held territory.

## POLAND

* **Political Exchange:** President Andrzej Duda has publicly addressed recent comments made by Robert Korzeniowski, accusing the former athlete of resorting to slander. The exchange marks a significant escalation in tensions, drawing attention to the broader political discourse and the nature of public criticism against the head of state.
* **Public Health Alert:** The Chief Pharmaceutical Inspector (GIF) has issued an urgent directive to withdraw specific pharmaceutical products from the market. While the specific list is being finalized, the regulator has confirmed that the recall is a precautionary measure to ensure patient safety, urging consumers to verify their medications against official announcements.
