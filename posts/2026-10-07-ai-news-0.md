## WORLD

* **Environmental Catastrophe Looms off Russian Coast:** A massive fire has engulfed an oil tanker, part of a "shadow fleet," near the Russian city of Sochi. Reports indicate the vessel was carrying approximately 105,000 tons of crude oil destined for India. Local authorities have issued public health warnings, urging residents to minimize outdoor activity due to potential environmental hazards.
* **Unprecedented Survival in US Death Row Case:** Legal experts are grappling with an unprecedented medical and ethical situation involving Christa Pike. Following a failed lethal injection attempt, Pike remains awake and responsive. Lawyers and medical professionals emphasize that her survival is an anomaly, raising significant questions regarding the administration of capital punishment.

## POLAND

* **Diplomatic Fallout Over Historical Revisionism:** Agnieszka Gozdyra, a prominent Polish journalist, has publicly denounced American commentator Tucker Carlson following his controversial remarks regarding the 1939 German invasion of Poland. The incident underscores growing tensions over the international interpretation of World War II history and the role of foreign media in Polish public discourse.
* **Border Security Escalation:** Tensions on the border with Belarus have intensified following reports of armed, masked individuals threatening Latvian border guards. This incident adds to the growing climate of uncertainty and security concerns regarding state-level provocations in the region, which continues to be a focal point for Polish and Baltic national security strategy.
