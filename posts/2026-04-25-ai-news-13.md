## WORLD

* **Escalation in Ukraine:** A major Russian missile strike has targeted the city of Dnipro, resulting in at least seven fatalities. The attack, which struck residential infrastructure, highlights the intensifying nature of the conflict as experts warn that Europe currently lacks a concrete strategy to facilitate a path toward a negotiated peace.
* **Coordinated Violence in Mali:** The central and northern regions of Mali are witnessing a significant surge in instability following a series of coordinated attacks by armed groups. Described as the largest jihadist offensive in years, the assault has sparked widespread concern regarding security in the Sahel region.
* **Breakthrough in Medical Research:** A major scientific development has emerged regarding the treatment of *noma*, a neglected tropical disease. Researchers have identified a previously unknown bacterial species associated with the condition, a discovery that could prove vital in developing life-saving treatments for a disease that is fatal in 90% of untreated cases.

## POLAND

* **Death of Andrzej Olechowski:** The Polish political landscape is in mourning following the passing of Andrzej Olechowski at the age of 78. A key figure in the country’s democratic transformation, he served as Minister of Finance and Minister of Foreign Affairs, and was a co-founder of the Civic Platform (PO), famously known as one of the party's "three tenors."
* **Long-Range Drone Strikes:** The conflict in Ukraine has reached a new technological milestone, with Ukrainian drones successfully striking targets in the Ural region of Russia. Covering a distance of approximately 1,800 kilometers, the operation resulted in structural damage to a residential high-rise in Yekaterinburg and left six people injured, signaling a significant expansion in the operational reach of the war.
* **Debate on State Reform:** Jarosław Kaczyński has publicly called for a fundamental restructuring of the Polish political system. During a recent press conference, the leader argued that national economic development is currently hindered by the existing institutional framework, stressing the need for a reform that would effectively exclude individuals who do not adhere to established political and ethical standards.
