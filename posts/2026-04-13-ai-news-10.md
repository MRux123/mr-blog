## WORLD

*   **Political Earthquake in Budapest:** The victory of Peter Magyar’s TISZA party in the Hungarian parliamentary elections marks a definitive shift in European politics. By ousting the long-standing ally of Vladimir Putin, Hungary has signaled a desire to pivot away from Moscow’s influence. While the Kremlin has publicly claimed to respect the results, the political implications for European unity and regional alliances are profound.
*   **Papal Tensions with Washington:** A high-profile diplomatic friction has emerged between Pope Leo XIV and U.S. President Donald Trump. Following sharp criticisms from the White House regarding the Pontiff’s foreign policy and stance on crime, the Pope has maintained a firm, non-confrontational posture, emphasizing that his peace-oriented mission transcends individual political administrations.

## POLAND

*   **The Fate of Zbigniew Ziobro:** Following the power shift in Hungary, the legal situation of former Polish Minister of Justice Zbigniew Ziobro has become a focal point of intense legal proceedings. Warsaw’s Regional Court has initiated formal inquiries regarding the European Arrest Warrant (EAW) issued against the former official, who had sought refuge in Hungary. Observers are now looking to see if the new Hungarian government will honor the extradition requests.
*   **Diplomatic and Economic Challenges:** The political landscape in Warsaw is further complicated by rising tensions between the U.S. Ambassador and the Speaker of the Sejm, alongside domestic adjustments in energy policy. Minister Miłosz Motyka has announced a national reduction in fuel price caps, set to take effect this Tuesday, in a bid to stabilize consumer costs amidst broader economic uncertainties.
