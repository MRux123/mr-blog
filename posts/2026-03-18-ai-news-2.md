## WORLD

* **Death of Ali Larijani Triggers Regional Upheaval:** The confirmation of the death of Ali Larijani, one of Iran's most influential policy makers and a projected successor to the supreme leadership, has sent shockwaves through the Middle East. Following his death in an Israeli strike, Iran has launched a direct missile attack on Tel Aviv, marking a significant escalation in the conflict. The situation in Tehran remains dire, with residents caught in a cycle of intensified regional airstrikes and a regime struggling to maintain domestic control.

* **Spain Reopens Colonial History Debate:** King Felipe of Spain has initiated a formal acknowledgement of "abuse" regarding the conquest of Mexico. This diplomatic gesture aims to thaw previously frosty bilateral relations while simultaneously triggering a renewed and fierce historical debate about events dating back 500 years.

## POLAND

* **Polish Tennis Showdown in Miami:** The tennis world is turning its eyes to the WTA tournament in Miami, where a "Polish-Polish" duel is set to take place. Following her successful opening round, Magda Linette will face second-seeded Iga Świątek in what is considered a marquee matchup for the tournament.

* **IPN Intervenes in Virtuti Militari Auction:** The Institute of National Remembrance (IPN) has launched an urgent intervention regarding a listing on a Ukrainian auction site. The item in question is the Virtuti Militari medal belonging to Captain Juliusz Roman Heinzel, who was murdered by the NKVD in 1940. The Institute has publicly denounced the auction as scandalous and has appealed for the historic artifact to be returned to the Polish state.
