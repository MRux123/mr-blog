## WORLD

*   **Assassination attempt on Donald Trump:** An attack on U.S. President Donald Trump took place during the White House Correspondents' Association gala in Washington, sparking global condemnation. The situation has prompted an immediate international response, with leaders emphasizing the gravity of the assault on the U.S. administration.
*   **Stagnation in U.S.-Iran relations:** Tensions between Washington and Tehran remain high as diplomatic talks regarding their ongoing conflict have stalled. Analysts warn that the current "no war, no peace" limbo creates a dangerous stalemate, while military exchanges between Israel and Hezbollah in Lebanon further destabilize the region.
*   **Geopolitical instability in Scandinavia:** Norway is facing a dual crisis, involving both a rare 3.6-4.4 magnitude earthquake near Oslo and warnings from intelligence experts regarding unprecedented espionage activities by foreign intelligence services, specifically citing Russian and Chinese operations.

## POLAND

*   **Polish government condemns Washington attack:** Polish Prime Minister Donald Tusk expressed full solidarity with the United States following the attempted attack on President Trump. The Polish government’s official stance underscores the importance of maintaining international stability in the face of political violence.
*   **Public health concerns in sports:** The WTA 1000 tournament in Madrid is facing significant operational challenges as a viral outbreak forces top-tier athletes, including Iga Świątek, to withdraw from the competition, raising concerns about the feasibility of the event's continuation.
