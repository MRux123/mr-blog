## WORLD

*   **U.S. Accelerates Multi-Billion Dollar Arms Deals to Middle East Partners:** The U.S. State Department has bypassed congressional review to fast-track $8.6 billion in arms transfers to Israel and Persian Gulf allies. This strategic move comes in the wake of recurring Iranian attacks amidst the ongoing U.S.-Israeli conflict with Iran.
*   **Standoff Intensifies Over Iran Peace Proposal:** The United States has formally rejected a diplomatic overture from Iran that included a proposal to open the strategic Strait of Hormuz. In a concurrent pressure tactic, Washington has threatened international shipping firms with sanctions should they continue to pay transit tolls to Iranian authorities, as the White House signals skepticism regarding Tehran's current peace commitments.

## POLAND

*   **Tragic Accidents Overshadow Local May Initiatives:** A series of fatal incidents has shocked the country. A 22-year-old was killed in a collision involving a fire truck in the Pomeranian province, while a 17-year-old died in a farming accident in the Podkarpackie region. Additionally, local authorities are investigating a separate traffic incident in which a police officer struck a civilian during a pursuit.
*   **Debate Over Health Education and Constitutional Reform:** Tensions between the state and the Catholic Church have resurfaced, as the Primate of the KEP (Konferencja Episkopatu Polski) publicly challenged the government’s implementation of health education in schools, citing a lack of parental consent. Meanwhile, political focus remains on the formation of the Council for a New Constitution under the President's patronage, a key governance development to watch in the coming weeks.
