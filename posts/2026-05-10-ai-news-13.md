## WORLD

* **Diplomatic Maneuvers and Rejections:** Germany has officially rejected Vladimir Putin’s suggestion to appoint former Chancellor Gerhard Schroeder as a mediator in peace talks with Ukraine. European officials interpret the proposal as a strategic attempt by Moscow to sow discord among Western allies.
* **Slovak Prime Minister’s Moscow Visit:** In a move drawing significant international attention, Slovak Prime Minister Robert Fico visited Moscow to attend the Victory Day parade. During the visit, Fico engaged in talks with President Putin and publicly urged Ukrainian President Volodymyr Zelensky to pursue direct contact with the Russian leadership to address the ongoing conflict.

## POLAND

* **Legal Pursuits Against High-Profile Figures:** Polish authorities are intensifying efforts regarding the whereabouts of key political figures. Following reports of Zbigniew Ziobro’s potential move to the United States, judicial officials have confirmed that extradition procedures are being evaluated. Simultaneously, investigations into Marcin Romanowski have moved to the next phase as his residence in Budapest was found to be abandoned, with no indication of his current location.
* **Tragic Accident at National Championships:** A fatal incident occurred during the jet ski national championships in Płock, where an 18-year-old participant lost his life. Local authorities have initiated a formal investigation to determine the circumstances surrounding the accident on the Vistula River.
