## WORLD

* **Escalation in Iran Conflict:** President Trump has signaled a intensified military campaign against Iran, pledging to exert extreme pressure over the coming two to three weeks. Despite a lengthy address from the White House, the administration has refrained from outlining a specific exit strategy or a clear timeline for the cessation of hostilities, leaving international markets and observers in a state of high alert.
* **Integrity of Information Under Fire:** A concerning trend of misinformation has surfaced, with major media outlets—including Fox News and the New York Post—falsely reporting that the government of Somaliland had requested the extradition of U.S. Representative Ilhan Omar. The reports, which appear to have originated from an unverified social media account, highlight the growing vulnerability of mainstream newsrooms to fabricated digital narratives and the intersection of political polarization with journalistic verification standards.
* **High-Level Corruption in African Football:** Congolese authorities have initiated an international manhunt for Jean-Guy Blaise Mayolas, the president of the football federation (Fecofoot), following his conviction for embezzling $1.1 million in FIFA Covid-19 relief funds. The case underscores persistent challenges regarding the oversight and integrity of international sports funding in regions where institutional accountability remains fragile.
