## WORLD

*   **Escalation in Lebanon:** Israeli forces have secured a strategic fortress in Lebanon, marking a significant intensification of their ground offensive against Hezbollah. Prime Minister Benjamin Netanyahu has characterized this tactical gain as a "decisive shift" in the ongoing military campaign.
*   **Humanitarian Crisis in DRC:** The World Health Organization (WHO) has issued an urgent appeal for community cooperation in the Democratic Republic of Congo to combat a growing Ebola outbreak. Resistance to standardized medical protocols for handling the deceased remains a critical hurdle for health authorities.
*   **Tragedy in Myanmar:** A large-scale explosion in a rebel-held village near the Chinese border has resulted in dozens of fatalities. While initial reports suggest the blast was linked to mining explosives, the incident highlights the ongoing instability in the region.

## POLAND

*   **Security Concerns regarding Russian Intelligence:** British officials have expressed mounting concerns that Ireland is being utilized as a staging ground for Russian intelligence operations aimed at the UK. Dublin’s liberal visa policies and the Common Travel Area are being cited as significant vulnerabilities in regional security.
*   **Fatal Traffic Accident:** A tragic accident in Brzuzem, Kujawsko-Pomorskie, has claimed two lives. A 32-year-old woman, operating a vehicle without a driver's license, lost control and drove into a swampy area, resulting in her death and that of a passenger.
*   **Infrastructure Disruptions:** Polish drivers face significant travel difficulties as major maintenance work begins on the S8 expressway. Starting June 1st, a two-month construction project at the Łask junction will necessitate diversions, complicating regional transit in the Łódź province.
