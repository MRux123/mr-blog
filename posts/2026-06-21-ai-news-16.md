## WORLD

*   **US-Iran Peace Negotiations:** Direct diplomatic talks between the United States and Iran have commenced in Switzerland. Led by Vice President JD Vance, the U.S. delegation is seeking an initial peace deal amid escalating tensions, including Iran’s threats to close the Strait of Hormuz and ongoing instability in Lebanon.
*   **Heatwave Crisis in France:** France is facing a severe environmental challenge, with half of the country under a red heat alert. Authorities have implemented strict measures, including a ban on alcohol at public street music festivals, as temperatures are expected to reach historic highs.
*   **Ethiopian Political Instability:** Following a landslide election victory, Ethiopian Prime Minister Abiy Ahmed faces increasing pressure. Despite his party's parliamentary majority, growing internal unrest and strained relations with neighboring countries raise concerns about a new wave of regional conflict.

## POLAND

*   **Severe Weather Damage:** A violent storm system swept across Poland over the weekend, resulting in over 1,900 emergency interventions by fire services. Significant infrastructure damage was reported, including a tree collapse on a school building in the West Pomeranian Voivodeship.
*   **Digital Well-being Concerns:** New European Commission research highlights a growing anxiety among Polish parents regarding their children's screen time. Despite high levels of concern over the negative influence of social media on youth health, the report notes that Polish parents are statistically less likely to support the implementation of strict age restrictions compared to their European counterparts.
