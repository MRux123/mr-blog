## WORLD

* **U.S. Military Casualties in Jordan:** Tensions in the Middle East have spiked following an Iranian attack on a U.S. base in Jordan. The incident resulted in the deaths of two American soldiers, with one service member reported as missing. This marks a significant escalation, as these are the first U.S. combat fatalities in the region since the collapse of the April truce.
* **Political Instability in Ukraine:** The Ukrainian leadership is facing intense domestic pressure. President Zelensky is reportedly considering the dismissal of General Oleksandr Syrskyi as mass protests grow against the commander. Concurrently, Kiev reports that Moscow is preparing a major mobilization effort, aiming to enlist 500,000 additional troops by autumn.
* **Hungarian Presidential Resignation:** In a surprise move, Hungarian President Tamas Sulyok has signed a constitutional amendment to vacate his office. This decision comes following mounting political pressure and demands from Prime Minister Peter Magyar.

## POLAND

* **Triumphant Youth Volleyball Team:** Poland celebrates a major sporting achievement as the national youth volleyball team has secured the gold medal at the European Championships, confirming the country's rising status in international youth sports.
* **Tragedy in the Slovak Paradise:** Emergency services in the Slovak Paradise national park reported a serious accident involving a 17-year-old Polish national who fell from a height of 20 meters, highlighting the risks associated with mountain tourism.
