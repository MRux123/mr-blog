## WORLD

* **Escalation in Russia-Ukraine Conflict:** Ukrainian drone operations have significantly intensified, targeting deep-territory infrastructure, including an oil refinery in Yaroslavl—located over 700 kilometers from the border. These raids, which have extended to Moscow and Rostov-on-Don, are causing substantial disruption to Russian aviation and regional logistics.
* **UK Election Turmoil:** Preliminary results from the United Kingdom suggest a major electoral setback for Prime Minister Keir Starmer’s Labour Party. While ballot counting continues, early data indicates widespread losses for incumbent council candidates, signaling potential political instability for the current administration.
* **Global Security Concerns:** Analysts are closely monitoring the impact of ongoing U.S.-Iran tensions on the global stage. Experts suggest that America's strategic focus on the Middle East may be inadvertently creating a power vacuum, potentially emboldening Beijing’s stance regarding Taiwan.

## POLAND

* **Political Friction Over Referendum:** Minister of Energy Miłosz Motyka (PSL) has sharply criticized President Karol Nawrocki’s proposal for a referendum on EU climate policy. Motyka characterized the move as a desperate attempt by the President to distance himself from the legacy of the Law and Justice (PiS) party, describing the initiative as a "vote of no confidence" in his own political base.
* **Financial Strategy and EU Cooperation:** Finance Minister Andrzej Domański is set to sign the SAFE agreement today, a move designed to stabilize Poland’s fiscal landscape. During a morning briefing, the Minister defended the loan terms, asserting that the agreement is highly advantageous for Poland, despite remaining questions regarding specific interest rate projections from the European Commission.
