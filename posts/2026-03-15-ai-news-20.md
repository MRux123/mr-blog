## WORLD

* **Iran’s Political Instability and Foreign Ties:** Following the passing of Ayatollah Ali Khamenei, his son Mojtaba Khamenei has assumed the role of Supreme Leader despite his late father’s documented reservations regarding his capability. Reports suggest Mojtaba is currently in Moscow, signaling a potential shift toward closer alignment with Russia. Concurrently, the U.S. has warned that global energy prices face significant volatility as Iran continues to restrict transit through the Strait of Hormuz.
* **Escalating Conflict in the Middle East:** Violence remains at a peak as Israeli forces target Hezbollah infrastructure in Lebanon and clash with militants in the West Bank, resulting in civilian casualties. Meanwhile, the conflict has deepened the humanitarian crisis for displaced residents in northern Israel, who remain under constant rocket fire despite previous government assurances of safety.
* **Geopolitical Rifts in Eastern Europe:** President Volodymyr Zelensky has intensified pressure on EU allies, accusing them of "blackmail" regarding the potential restoration of Russian oil transit through Ukraine, arguing that such a move would undermine existing sanctions.

## POLAND

* **Political Polarization Over European Integration:** The debate over Poland’s relationship with the European Union has sharpened, with reactions to President Karol Nawrocki’s rejection of the "SAFE" program dominating headlines. German media outlets have characterized the move as an anti-European sentiment, while domestic opposition figures like Anna Bryłka of Konfederacja dismiss government accusations of "Polexit" as political demagoguery, defending the right to challenge EU mandates.
* **Ukraine Support Initiatives:** In a significant display of bilateral cooperation, Polish authorities—led by the Mazovian regional government and the Polish Center for International Aid (PCPM)—have deployed over 560 power generators to Ukraine to bolster energy security for communities in the Kyiv and Vinnytsia regions during the ongoing crisis.
* **Legislative Pressure in Kyiv:** President Zelensky’s ultimatum to Ukrainian parliamentarians—demanding that they either serve their country within the Rada or transition to active military duty—has sent shockwaves through the political establishment, highlighting the growing domestic strain of the prolonged war effort.
