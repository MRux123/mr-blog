## WORLD

* **U.S. Secures Permanent Security Rights in Greenland:** Following heightened diplomatic pressure and annexation threats, the United States has reached a deal with Denmark, granting the U.S. permanent control over security and strategic operations in Greenland.
* **Escalating Media Crackdown:** In an unprecedented move, President Trump has announced a ban on CNN, MSNBC, and Politico from White House access, signaling a deepening conflict between the administration and major news outlets.
* **Human Rights Concerns in U.S. Deportation Program:** Reports have emerged regarding the brutal treatment of individuals deported by the U.S. to Equatorial Guinea, where detainees are allegedly being held in poor conditions, bound, and beaten in a facility acting as a "nightmare prison" for the administration's program.

## POLAND

* **Economic and Judicial Strain:** Moody's has downgraded Poland’s credit rating, prompting an official response from the Ministry of Finance. Meanwhile, the legal system remains in the spotlight following the court's decision to deny the arrest of MP Wojciech Król, despite pressure from the European Public Prosecutor's Office.
* **Political Polarization Intensifies:** Tensions have reached a boiling point outside the Constitutional Tribunal in Warsaw, where protestors engaged in heated confrontations, reflecting the deepening divide in the Polish political landscape.
