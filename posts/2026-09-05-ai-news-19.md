## WORLD

* **High-Level Diplomatic Engagement in Moscow:** US presidential envoys Jared Kushner and Steve Witkoff have arrived in Moscow for direct talks with Vladimir Putin regarding the ongoing conflict in Ukraine. The meeting comes amid reports that Russia has paused military strikes on Kyiv. President Putin described the current geopolitical situation as "not so easy," marking a significant moment in potential back-channel diplomacy between Washington and the Kremlin.
* **Capital Punishment Verdict in Egypt:** A prominent Egyptian television presenter, Sarah Khalifa, has been sentenced to death alongside 11 others for their roles in a large-scale narcotics manufacturing and trafficking operation. The case has drawn international attention due to the high profile of the defendant, known for her crime-focused television programming.

## POLAND

* **Legal and Border Controversy Involving Gen. Pytel:** Minister of Justice Waldemar Żurek has intervened following an incident where the Border Guard prevented former Military Counterintelligence Service (SKW) chief, Gen. Piotr Pytel, from leaving the country at the Pyrzowice airport. Following the Minister's intervention, it was announced that the General is now free to travel, signaling a tense clash within state security and judicial institutions.
* **Expansion of the Zondacrypto Investigation:** The investigation into the Zondacrypto platform continues to intensify, with Minister of Justice Waldemar Żurek confirming further arrests. Among those detained is a long-term associate of Sylvestr Suszek, Roman Ż., as well as regional officials including the Marshal of the Lublin Voivodeship, Jarosław S. Authorities have cited a flight risk as the primary justification for the ongoing series of detentions.
