## WORLD

*   **Escalation in Space Security:** NATO leadership has formally pledged to bolster space defenses following intelligence reports suggesting Russia is planning to deploy a satellite equipped with a nuclear warhead. This move signals a significant escalation in orbital militarization, raising concerns about the potential vulnerability of global satellite infrastructure.
*   **Expansion of the Ukraine Conflict:** The theater of war between Russia and Ukraine has widened unexpectedly after a Ukrainian strike successfully sank a Russian battleship in a Libyan port. This incident highlights the increasingly globalized nature of the conflict and suggests that the war is spilling over into North African territories, complicating geopolitical relations in the region.
*   **Trump’s Intervention in Nvidia’s Leadership:** The tech sector faces renewed scrutiny as Donald Trump claims credit for persuading Jensen Huang to return to the helm of Nvidia. The development follows a massive $1 trillion fluctuation in the company's market value, prompting questions about the influence of political power on corporate governance and Beijing's strategic response to Nvidia's future.

## POLAND

*   **Diplomatic Standoff with the ICC:** In a major shift in foreign policy, Poland has moved to withdraw from the International Criminal Court (ICC). The decision follows controversy surrounding the court’s failure to act on the arrest warrant for Vladimir Putin, signaling a significant rift between Warsaw and international judicial bodies.
*   **Appeals in the Kamil of Częstochowa Case:** The legal proceedings regarding the tragic death of Kamil from Częstochowa have entered a new phase. Legal representatives have filed an appeal, arguing that the sentences issued in the case were "grossly disproportionate," renewing public discourse on child protection and judicial standards in Poland.
*   **Grave Negligence at a Funeral Home:** A shocking error in a Lublin funeral home, involving the accidental swapping of newborn bodies, has sparked a police investigation and widespread public outcry. The incident has drawn attention to the need for stricter regulatory oversight within the funeral industry.
