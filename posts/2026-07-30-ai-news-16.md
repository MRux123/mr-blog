## WORLD

* **Escalating Mideast Conflict:** The geopolitical situation in the Middle East has reached a critical juncture following a drone attack in Egypt, which threatens to broaden the ongoing war. The United States and Iran have engaged in direct strikes, compounded by Saudi Arabia’s formal acknowledgment of its direct military involvement in the theater of operations.
* **European Wildfire Crisis:** Extreme weather conditions continue to ravage Southern Europe. Greece is facing a devastating wildfire season, with mass evacuations in Crete and the tragic loss of three firefighters. The situation remains volatile as blazes also threaten ecosystems and communities across France and Spain.

## POLAND

* **NATO Borders Under Scrutiny:** Tensions have spiked following a missile strike in Tarnawa-Kolonia. Prime Minister Donald Tusk has indicated a high probability that the projectile was of Russian origin and was armed upon impact. The incident has drawn urgent attention from NATO and EU leadership, raising critical concerns regarding the security of the Alliance's eastern flank and the risk of direct confrontation with Russia.
* **National Mourning:** Poland mourns the loss of Iga Cembrzyńska, the renowned actress, composer, and director, who passed away at the age of 87. A prominent figure in Polish cinema, she leaves behind an extensive artistic legacy, including iconic roles in films such as *Hydrozagadka*.
