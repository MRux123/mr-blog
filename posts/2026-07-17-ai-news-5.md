## WORLD

* **Escalation in US-Iran Conflict:** Tensions in the Middle East have reached a critical point as US forces conducted their sixth consecutive night of airstrikes against Iranian military targets. While the US claims the operation is necessary to degrade regional threats, Iranian authorities report significant damage to civilian infrastructure, including bridges. The situation is further complicated by the boarding of a vessel in the Strait of Hormuz, signaling a potentially wider regional maritime conflict.
* **Political Volatility in the United States:** President Donald Trump has leveled serious allegations regarding the security of the upcoming midterm elections, accusing China of direct interference in the 2020 election process. These claims, which contradict previous intelligence findings, have sparked a domestic debate on electoral integrity and the administration's foreign policy stance toward Beijing.
* **Humanitarian Crisis at Sea:** International concern is growing as over 500 Rohingya individuals remain missing following the disappearance of two boats that departed Myanmar on June 29. Search efforts are ongoing, but the fate of the passengers remains unknown, highlighting the persistent dangers faced by refugees in the region.

## POLAND

* **Criminal Syndicate Dismantled:** The Central Bureau of Investigation (CBŚP) has successfully neutralized a criminal organization involved in the production of illegal pharmaceuticals. Authorities confiscated five pallets of counterfeit medical products, preventing a significant volume of potentially hazardous substances from reaching the Polish market.
* **Economic Warning Signs:** Economists have raised alarms over a "black scenario" for the Polish economy, predicting a sharp increase in food prices. The forecast suggests that current inflationary pressures and supply chain challenges could significantly impact household budgets in the coming months.
* **Unexplained Disappearance:** Local authorities are currently investigating the mysterious disappearance of a bishop, who was reported missing immediately following a church service. The investigation is ongoing, and police have yet to uncover definitive leads as to the cleric's whereabouts.
