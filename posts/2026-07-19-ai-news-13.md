## WORLD

* **Escalation Between US and Iran:** A significant military standoff is unfolding following the deaths of two American service members in Jordan. The U.S. has launched retaliatory strikes against Iranian-backed forces, while Iran claims these attacks have targeted critical infrastructure, including an under-construction nuclear facility. The international community fears a shift toward a wider regional conflict.
* **Intensified Russian Assault on Ukraine:** Russia has executed a massive ballistic missile campaign targeting major Ukrainian urban centers. The attacks have resulted in multiple fatalities and dozens of injuries, marking a severe escalation in the ongoing conflict as Ukraine simultaneously continues its own operations against Russian strategic infrastructure.

## POLAND

* **Political Realignment on the Left:** Włodzimierz Czarzasty, Speaker of the Sejm and leader of the New Left party, has publicly called for a unified front of the Left ahead of the upcoming elections. Czarzasty expressed a strong desire to bring the Razem party into a common coalition, while concurrently criticizing some of their recent parliamentary voting patterns.
* **Public Safety and Crime Concerns:** The domestic landscape is marked by serious security incidents, including a violent assault at the Łemkowska Watra festival where a local official was stabbed, and a concerning rise in road accidents involving intoxicated drivers, highlighted by a recent high-profile case on the A1 motorway. Additionally, health authorities have issued a stark warning regarding the safety of nearly 100 swimming areas in northern Poland, marked by red flags due to local hazards.
