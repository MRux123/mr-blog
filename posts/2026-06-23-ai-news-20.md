## WORLD

*   **Evacuation of sailors in the Strait of Hormuz:** International authorities, including the IMO, are preparing a massive humanitarian operation to evacuate approximately 11,000 sailors stranded on vessels in the Strait of Hormuz. The initiative follows a tentative agreement between the US and Iran aimed at de-escalating tensions. US Secretary of State Marco Rubio has issued a strong warning against any attempts by Iran to impose illegal transit tolls, emphasizing the importance of freedom of navigation.
*   **EU accession process gridlock:** Hungary has effectively stalled the procedural advancement of EU membership negotiations for Ukraine and Moldova. This move signals a significant diplomatic hurdle for both nations, as Budapest’s decision halts the progress required for further integration steps, potentially impacting the geopolitical stability of the region.

## POLAND

*   **Crisis in healthcare management:** Former department head Dr. Emil Jędrzejewski has leveled serious accusations regarding the quality of care at the SOR (emergency department) of the Szpital Południowy in Warsaw. Citing systemic failures and insufficient oversight, the whistleblower's claims have sparked a public debate on the standards of medical education and patient safety within the Polish public healthcare system.
*   **Diplomatic outreach in Ankara:** President Karol Nawrocki held high-level talks with Turkish President Recep Tayyip Erdoğan. The meeting underscored the strategic importance of the Polish-Turkish partnership within NATO. Notably, Turkey expressed its continued support for Poland’s geopolitical objectives, while strengthening bilateral ties between the two nations.
