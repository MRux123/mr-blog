## WORLD

* **Humanitarian Crisis in Ceuta:** Spain is grappling with a severe migration emergency as approximately 49,000 people crossed into the North African enclave of Ceuta within 24 hours. Prime Minister Pedro Sánchez has arrived on-site as reports confirm at least 24 fatalities, prompting significant tension within the European Union regarding border security and humanitarian management.
* **Deadly Blast in Pakistan:** A coal mine explosion has claimed at least 34 lives in one of Pakistan's primary mining hubs. Rescue efforts are ongoing, though the incident has once again highlighted the systemic dangers of methane gas and structural instability prevalent in the region’s mining sector.
* **Ebola Outbreak Status:** The Democratic Republic of the Congo is currently experiencing the fastest-growing Ebola outbreak in history. With nearly 1,500 deaths recorded and 3,360 confirmed cases, the situation is further complicated by regional conflicts that continue to hamper international health responses.

## POLAND

* **Russian Missile Incident Confirmed:** Polish prosecutors have officially identified the projectile that crashed in the Lublin region as a Russian Kh-101 cruise missile. Authorities have traced the origin and production timeline of the weapon, signaling a significant escalation in the regional security situation.
* **Legal and Political Developments:** The Polish Sejm has voted on the status of KO MP Wojciech Król, opting to strip his immunity while denying the prosecution's request for detention. Simultaneously, political tensions remain high as an indictment has been issued against Robert Bąkiewicz regarding inflammatory rhetoric.
* **Extreme Weather Alerts:** Meteorologists have issued the highest level of weather warnings for large parts of Poland. A severe heatwave, with temperatures reaching up to 36°C, is expected to be accompanied by violent storms, posing a direct threat to public health and infrastructure.
