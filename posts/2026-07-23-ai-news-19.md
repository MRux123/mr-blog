## WORLD

* **Middle East Crisis and Energy Markets:** Global oil prices have surged past $100 a barrel following Houthi-led attacks on Saudi oil tankers in the Red Sea. The escalating volatility has intensified geopolitical concerns, as the United States Congress moves to restrict presidential war powers amidst the deepening regional conflict.
* **Political Instability in Ukraine:** The post-war political landscape remains fragile as the ousted defense minister, Mykhailo Fedorov, publicly challenges President Zelensky’s administrative decisions. Fedorov’s refusal to accept an alternative post signals a significant internal struggle within the Ukrainian government.

## POLAND

* **Institutional Shifts and Legal Appointments:** Sylwia Gregorczyk-Abram has been confirmed by the Senate as the new Ombudsman (RPO). Her appointment comes amid promises to maintain political neutrality and uphold the independence of the office, despite public debate regarding her previous political alignment.
* **Security Concerns and Regional Relations:** Belarusian authorities have issued an alert to Poland regarding a potential terrorist threat, adding to the climate of uncertainty. Simultaneously, public discourse in Poland remains focused on the possibility of a full-scale mobilization in Russia, as observers track signs of a potential transition to a state of war within the neighboring country.
* **International Criminal Investigation:** Polish authorities are cooperating with Argentine investigators in the search for a Polish national suspected of the murder of Luciana Ojeda. The case has gained international attention following the discovery of evidence linking the suspect to the crime.
