Global markets have already reacted to the instability, with analysts warning of the most severe energy market disruption in decades.

As the El Niño phenomenon looms, the UN weather agency reports that the global climate is currently more unbalanced than at any point in recorded history, raising concerns over long-term environmental and humanitarian impacts.
*   **Political Turmoil in Madagascar:** Amidst a coup-led leadership change, the nation’s military president has mandated lie detector tests for all new government ministers. The controversial measure aims to address deep-seated public dissatisfaction regarding corruption within the political elite.

## POLAND
