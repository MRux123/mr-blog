## WORLD

* **Trump-Putin Energy Deal Sparks Global Outcry:** US President Donald Trump has finalized a deal to purchase Russian diesel, a move hailed by Vladimir Putin as a boost to the global economy but sharply criticized by Ukrainian President Volodymyr Zelensky as a "gift to Putin" and an "investment in the war." The development marks a significant shift in US foreign policy, drawing condemnation for its potential to prolong the conflict in Ukraine.
* **FlyDubai Flight Incident Linked to Extremist Plot:** Authorities in the United Arab Emirates have revealed that an attacker involved in a recent incident on a FlyDubai flight intended to crash the aircraft into an Israeli airport. The investigation points to a co-pilot radicalized by extreme Islamist views, with the UAE attorney general citing inspiration from the September 11 attacks.
* **Magnitude 7.7 Earthquake Strikes Panama:** A powerful earthquake has hit Panama, causing significant structural damage. Regional authorities have issued tsunami warnings as footage of the destruction continues to emerge, raising concerns about a potential humanitarian crisis in the affected coastal areas.

## POLAND

* **Political Scandal Involving Michał Kołodziejczak:** Former Deputy Minister of Agriculture Michał Kołodziejczak is facing serious allegations from former female associates. The accusations, surfaced via the Kanał Zero platform, include reports of physical violence, death threats, and alleged illicit drug use, sparking a significant political controversy.
* **New Legislation on Labor Relations:** President Karol Nawrocki has signed a deregulation amendment designed to streamline communication between trade unions, employee councils, and employers. The new law aims to increase the flexibility of administrative processes regarding formal requests and responses within the workplace.
