## WORLD

*   **Heightened Tensions in the Middle East:** Following the U.S. seizure of an Iranian-flagged vessel near the Strait of Hormuz, the geopolitical situation remains volatile. While Iranian officials have issued threats of retaliation, the nation's leadership has signaled a desire to avoid an all-out war, with diplomatic channels for peace talks reportedly remaining open.
*   **Tragedy in Kyiv:** The security situation in Ukraine’s capital has deteriorated following a fatal shooting incident in a local supermarket. The standoff, which resulted in a hostage situation, claimed seven lives before special forces neutralized the assailant.
*   **Economic Fallout in the Tech Sector:** Over 1,000 workers in Kenya have been laid off by Sama, an outsourcing firm, following Meta’s decision to terminate its contract. The move has sparked a global debate regarding the stability and ethics of AI-training and content moderation labor practices in the Global South.

## POLAND

*   **Legal Action Against Grzegorz Braun:** The Wrocław prosecutor’s office has filed a formal request with the European Parliament to strip MEP Grzegorz Braun of his immunity. The move is linked to allegations of unlawful imprisonment of a physician at a hospital in Oleśnica.
*   **Diplomatic Friction with Israel:** A heated exchange occurred between Polish Foreign Minister Radosław Sikorski and his Israeli counterpart. The conflict stems from Sikorski's criticism of the Israel Defense Forces (IDF) after footage emerged of an Israeli soldier destroying a crucifix in southern Lebanon, an act the Israeli minister labeled as a misunderstanding of the context.
*   **Cross-Border Smuggling Intercepted:** The Polish Border Guard has dismantled an illegal operation involving the use of drones to smuggle contraband from Belarus into Poland. Three individuals have been arrested and are currently facing formal charges, signaling a tightening of security measures along the eastern border.
