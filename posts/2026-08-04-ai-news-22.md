## WORLD

* **Escalating Tensions in Ukraine:** The Ukrainian State Border Guard Service reports that Russia is potentially preparing an invasion of the Chernihiv region. Analysts suggest this strategic move is designed to force Kyiv to divert critical military resources to the northern front. Meanwhile, President Volodymyr Zelenskyy has officially confirmed the approval of new, unspecified operational actions aimed at countering Russian aggression, marking a shift in the current phase of the conflict.
* **Legislative Shifts in Germany:** As debates regarding the potential restoration of mandatory military service intensify, the German government is reportedly drafting a framework for a new substitute service. This initiative is intended for conscientious objectors, ensuring a structured alternative should compulsory conscription be reinstated in the country.
* **Human Rights Concerns in West Africa:** Campaigners warn of a concerning trend across West Africa, where governments are increasingly leveraging anti-LGBTQ+ legislation to consolidate power. Recent legislative crackdowns in Senegal and Niger highlight a growing regional shift that activists describe as the use of marginalized communities as political scapegoats.

## POLAND

* **Extreme Weather Alert:** Polish authorities have issued urgent warnings as the country prepares for a significant heatwave. Temperatures are expected to soar to 38°C, necessitating extreme caution for residents and vulnerable populations.
* **Public Safety Incidents:** Reports indicate a surge in critical incidents, including a mass poisoning event that has hospitalized over a dozen people, and a serious medical emergency involving a three-year-old child attacked by a dog, requiring the intervention of the Air Ambulance Service (LPR).
