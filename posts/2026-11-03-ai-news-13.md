## WORLD

* **US-Iran Tensions Escalate:** The geopolitical landscape remains volatile as US forces conducted limited strikes on Iran’s Qeshm Island. The operation, described by US officials as defensive, comes amidst significant anxiety in the Gulf region as countries scramble to mediate and prevent a broader conflict ahead of the US midterm elections.
* **Widespread Cyber-Attacks on European Infrastructure:** A series of sophisticated cyber-attacks has crippled supermarket refrigeration systems across nine European nations, from Estonia to Italy. The incident has forced major retailers to discard significant quantities of food, raising concerns about the vulnerability of critical supply chain infrastructure to coordinated digital threats.
* **Escalation of Drone Warfare in Sudan:** The United Nations has identified drones as the leading cause of civilian deaths in Sudan this year. These attacks are exacerbating an already dire humanitarian crisis, marked by widespread famine and drought, further destabilizing the region.

## POLAND

* **Judiciary Standoff Deepens:** A significant institutional crisis is unfolding within the Supreme Court, where 28 judges have formally boycotted an extraordinary General Assembly convened by the First President. The dissenters challenge the legality of the assembly and allege that court leadership is attempting to improperly align the judiciary with the executive branch.
* **Constitutional Tribunal Accessibility Dispute:** Tensions surrounding the Constitutional Tribunal have reached a breaking point, with Judge Anna Korwin-Piotrowska formally requesting police intervention to clear demonstrators blocking access to the facility. The situation highlights the ongoing friction between judicial operations and political protests.
* **Cultural Heritage Recovery:** In a rare piece of positive news, a 19th-century masterpiece, "The Lady with a Fan," has been successfully recovered and returned to the Wilanów Palace collection after being classified as missing since World War II.
