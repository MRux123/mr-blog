## WORLD

* **Escalating Tensions in the Middle East:** A critical situation is unfolding following the reported seizure of two ships near the Strait of Hormuz by Iran’s Revolutionary Guards. While a spokesperson for President Trump stated that these actions do not violate the current cease-fire, the development has introduced significant instability and uncertainty regarding ongoing peace negotiations.
* **Allegations of War Crimes in Lebanon:** Lebanon’s Prime Minister has formally accused Israel of committing war crimes following an air strike that killed a journalist. The situation is further aggravated by reports that IDF strikes on Red Cross vehicles prevented rescue teams from accessing the site of the incident.

## POLAND

* **PKOl Financial and Governance Crisis:** The Polish Olympic Committee (PKOl) is facing intense scrutiny following remarks from the Minister of Sport, which have sparked a political firestorm. While the PKOl president maintains that athletes have received their financial dues, the administration, including the President's office, is demanding formal explanations, fueling speculation regarding a potential resignation.
* **The Mystery of the "Bitcoin King":** New investigative reports have shed light on the disappearance of Sylwester Suszek, the founder of one of Poland's largest cryptocurrency exchanges. Findings suggest that company shares were transferred to entities in the UAE, revealing complex links to Luxembourg and potential Russian connections, raising serious concerns regarding money laundering and the fate of the missing entrepreneur.
