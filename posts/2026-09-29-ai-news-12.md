## WORLD

* **Pentagon Data Security Breach:** Significant concerns have emerged regarding the integrity of US defense systems following a major cyberattack targeting the Pentagon’s database. The incident, which reportedly compromises the personal data of millions, signals a severe escalation in state-sponsored or organized cyber-warfare.
* **Humanitarian Crisis in Myanmar:** A devastating military airstrike on a civilian market has resulted in at least 50 fatalities. International observers characterize the attack, which left victims’ bodies unrecognizable, as one of the most violent escalations of force against civilians in the region in recent months.
* **Argentina's Controversial AI Strategy:** As global powers move toward regulating artificial intelligence, President Javier Milei is positioning Argentina as an "AI haven." By rejecting oversight, the administration risks international friction, underscored by Milei's concurrent threats of legal action against the UK regarding oil exploration rights in the Falkland Islands.

## POLAND

* **Public Funds Misuse Investigation:** The National Institute of Freedom has formally notified the prosecutor’s office regarding the "Osuchowa" Foundation. Allegations suggest that public funds, derived from tax designations, were diverted to finance political activities linked to Grzegorz Braun’s party, raising critical questions about the transparency of civil society financing.
* **Environmental Hazard in the Baltic:** Coastal authorities have implemented beach closures along the Polish coastline—including areas near Ustka and Jarosławiec—following the discovery of oily, black substances. Preliminary assessments indicate a spill of petroleum-based products, currently under laboratory analysis to determine the ecological impact.
