## WORLD

*   **Political Divergence in Ukraine:** A significant rift has emerged in Ukrainian leadership as former Defense Minister Mykhailo Fedorov has publicly called for national elections. This move challenges President Zelensky's current stance, highlighting growing internal tensions regarding the political future of the country amidst the ongoing conflict.
*   **Escalation in US-Iran Relations:** The United States has pivoted back to a strategy of economic pressure, imposing new sanctions on Iran. Analysts warn that given Tehran's history of navigating long-term economic isolation, this policy may lead to regional escalation rather than diplomatic concessions.
*   **Justice in Gaza:** The Israeli military has officially ordered a criminal investigation into the death of six-year-old Hind Rajab, whose case drew international outcry after she was discovered dead in Gaza City following a prolonged plea for help from paramedics.

## POLAND

*   **Fiscal Reform:** Prime Minister Donald Tusk has announced a significant adjustment to the national tax structure. The upper limit of the first tax bracket is set to rise from 120,000 PLN to 130,000 PLN, a move aimed at easing the tax burden on middle-income earners.
*   **Bus Accident Investigation:** Polish authorities are seeking to take control of the investigation into a recent bus crash involving Polish citizens in Hungary. Diplomatic efforts are currently underway to ensure the well-being of the survivors and facilitate their return to Poland, with reports confirming they are receiving adequate medical care.
*   **Countering Disinformation:** The Latvian military has officially debunked a viral, fabricated video alleging that a Ukrainian soldier killed NATO personnel during joint training. The institution labeled the content as a coordinated effort to undermine the alliance's reputation.
