## WORLD

*   **Deadly Russian Strike on Zaporizhzhia:** A Russian glide bomb attack on the city of Zaporizhzhia has resulted in at least 15 fatalities, including children. President Zelenskyy has condemned the assault, characterizing the timing of the attack as a response to recent international trade agreements involving the United States.
*   **Airport Attack in Riyadh:** King Khalid International Airport in the Saudi capital has been targeted in an attack, leading to multiple injuries and a disruption of flight operations. The incident follows claims from Yemen’s Houthi rebels regarding their intent to strike the facility, prompting increased regional scrutiny.
*   **Navi Pillay Honored with Nobel Peace Prize:** Renowned judge and human rights advocate Navi Pillay has been awarded the Nobel Peace Prize. The recognition honors her lifelong commitment to fighting institutional racism and her work in fostering justice on the international stage.

## POLAND

*   **Russian Air Strikes Trigger Polish Military Response:** In response to renewed Russian aerial attacks on Ukrainian territory, the Polish Operational Command has deployed military aircraft to secure the national airspace. Residents in the Lublin and Subcarpathian regions were issued RCB (Government Security Centre) alerts as a precautionary measure.
*   **Wave of Security Incidents in Educational Institutions:** Polish schools are facing a heightened security crisis following a knife attack by a 21-year-old woman at a school in Warsaw, which was only contained due to the quick intervention of school staff. Concurrently, police in Garwolin have detained a 14-year-old in possession of an unauthorized arsenal of weapons, following suspicious online activity. These incidents have prompted regional neighbors, including Slovakia, to increase police presence around educational facilities.
