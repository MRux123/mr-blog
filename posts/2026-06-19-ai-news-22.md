## WORLD

*   **Zelensky stripped of Poland’s highest honor:** In an unprecedented diplomatic move, Ukrainian President Volodymyr Zelensky has been stripped of the Order of the White Eagle. The decision, announced by Polish President Karol Nawrocki, follows Ukraine’s recent designation of a military unit using the name "Heroes of the UPA." The move has drawn sharp international criticism, with Kyiv labeling it a "strategic mistake" that undermines solidarity during the ongoing conflict.

## POLAND

*   **Tusk warns of division as political friction escalates:** The decision to revoke the presidential honor has triggered a fierce domestic debate. Prime Minister Donald Tusk issued a firm rebuke, suggesting that the conflict is playing into the hands of the Kremlin. Key political figures, including Szymon Hołownia and Robert Biedroń, have voiced concerns that the move alienates allies and creates dangerous internal divisions at a time when national unity is paramount.
*   **Targeted assassination in Biała Podlaska:** Polish authorities have confirmed the arrest of a suspect, Elnur A., in connection with the murder of a Russian artist and vocal Vladimir Putin critic, known by the pseudonym "Siemion Skriepiecki." The investigation by the National Prosecutor's Office is ongoing, marking a rare and concerning instance of political violence on Polish soil.
*   **Severe weather devastates Wielkopolska:** A series of violent storms swept through western Poland, causing widespread destruction. Firefighters reported nearly 400 interventions related to flash flooding, downed trees, and structural damage, with the human toll highlighted by the injury of a teenager crushed by a falling tree.
