## WORLD

*   **Escalating Maritime Tensions in the Strait of Hormuz:** Iran has significantly increased regional friction by publishing a map asserting "armed forces oversight" over 22,000 square kilometers of the strategic waterway. This move signals a more assertive stance on regional control, challenging international navigation norms in one of the world's most critical oil transit chokepoints.
*   **Air France and Airbus Convicted in 2009 Crash:** A French court has delivered a verdict of manslaughter against both Air France and Airbus regarding the 2009 air disaster that claimed 228 lives. The ruling concludes a long-standing legal battle concerning corporate accountability in aviation safety.
*   **Rising Public Health Crisis in Europe:** Health agencies have reported record-breaking levels of gonorrhea and syphilis across Europe. Experts attribute the surge to a combination of post-pandemic behavioral shifts and systemic gaps in regional testing and preventative healthcare infrastructure.

## POLAND

*   **Public Dissatisfaction with Government Performance:** According to recent CBOS data, 51% of Poles now view the government's performance negatively. Public support for the current ruling coalition has declined, with growing criticism regarding economic policy and the overall leadership of Prime Minister Donald Tusk.
*   **Legal Scrutiny on Former Officials:** In a significant anti-corruption development, the Central Anti-Corruption Bureau (CBA) has arrested former MP Jacek Żalek. The arrest is part of a broader investigation conducted by the European Public Prosecutor's Office, casting a spotlight on the accountability of previous political leadership.
*   **Cinkciarz.pl CEO Detained in the United States:** Polish authorities have confirmed the arrest of the CEO of Cinkciarz.pl on a U.S. military range. Marcin P. is currently facing serious fraud charges involving over 5,000 clients, marking a major development in an ongoing high-profile financial investigation.
