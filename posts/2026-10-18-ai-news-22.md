## WORLD

* **France Faces Widespread Rioting:** President Emmanuel Macron has issued an urgent national appeal for "calm and order" as violent unrest and looting continue to spread across France. The situation remains volatile, with authorities struggling to contain the disorder.
* **Geopolitical Maneuvering in US-Russia Relations:** In a surprise move, President Trump has suspended a new round of sanctions against Russia, citing the necessity to maintain the momentum of ongoing peace negotiations. The decision has drawn significant international scrutiny regarding the administration's strategic long-term goals.
* **Controversy Over Vaccine Trials in Guinea-Bissau:** The government of Guinea-Bissau has formally denied authorizing a controversial, Danish-led hepatitis B trial in newborns. The proposed study, supported by RFK Jr., has faced intense ethical debate due to its focus on non-specific health outcomes rather than traditional safety metrics.

## POLAND

* **Airspace Security and RCB Alerts:** Rządowe Centrum Bezpieczeństwa (RCB) issued emergency alerts for the Lubelskie Voivodeship following intensive Russian aerial attacks on neighboring Ukraine. Polish fighter jets were scrambled to monitor the airspace as a precautionary measure, though authorities confirmed later that the immediate threat to Polish territory had subsided.
* **Zelensky's Stance on Presidential Elections:** Ukrainian President Volodymyr Zelensky has stated he is prepared to run for re-election, even if the electoral process were to take place before the formal conclusion of the war with Russia. This declaration signals a significant shift in political discourse amidst the ongoing conflict.
