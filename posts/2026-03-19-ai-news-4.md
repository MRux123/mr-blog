## WORLD

* **Middle East Conflict Escalates:** A deadly missile strike in the West Bank has claimed the lives of at least three people. This marks a significant development in the ongoing regional conflict, as it is the first time an Iranian-attributed attack has resulted in Palestinian casualties within the Israeli-occupied territory since the war's inception.
* **Trump Issues Severe Warning to Iran:** Following an Israeli strike on the South Pars gas field, U.S. President Donald Trump has issued a stern ultimatum to Tehran. He warned that any retaliatory Iranian strike on Qatari LNG facilities would trigger a massive U.S. response, threatening the total destruction of the South Pars energy infrastructure.
* **Security Alert in the U.S. Capital:** Washington D.C. is on high alert after unidentified drones were detected hovering over a restricted military installation housing top-tier government officials, including Secretary of State Marco Rubio and Secretary of Defense Pete Hegseth. The incident has prompted an intensification of security protocols both domestically and at U.S. facilities abroad.

## POLAND

* **Historical Retrospective on the 'Meat Affair':** Public discourse has revisited the 1965 execution of Stanisław Wawrzecki, the central figure in the infamous "meat affair." As a symbol of the communist regime's weaponization of the judicial system, the execution remains the only instance in post-1956 Poland where an individual was put to death for an economic crime, highlighting the brutal nature of the PRL era's legal practices.
