## WORLD

* **Political Fallout in Spain:** Prime Minister Pedro Sánchez faces a critical threat to his re-election campaign following a second major train disaster this year. The incident has intensified public scrutiny over his government's competence, overshadowing his intended platform of housing and equality.
* **Escalating Security Concerns in France:** French authorities are bracing for widespread civil unrest as protesters plan demonstrations coinciding with Halloween. This follows recent violent episodes in Paris, where residents and shopkeepers struggled to protect their neighborhoods from looting and arson.
* **Geopolitical Stasis in Iran:** Despite high-stakes political developments in the United States and Israel, Tehran’s leadership anticipates no significant shift in foreign policy, as the region remains caught in a cycle of persistent war-readiness regardless of electoral outcomes abroad.

## POLAND

* **Constitutional Tribunal Standoff:** Tensions surrounding the Constitutional Tribunal have escalated, leading to public protests and police intervention. The situation remains volatile, with high-profile figures, including Bogdan Święczkowski, insisting on a forceful resolution to the dispute over access to the institution's building.
* **Evacuation of Oleshky:** The humanitarian crisis in occupied Ukraine continues as a group of residents from the devastated city of Oleshky successfully crossed into Belarus under a local ceasefire. The arrival raises urgent questions regarding the long-term support and status of displaced civilians caught in the crossfire.
