## WORLD

*   **Zelensky Seeks Direct Dialogue with Putin:** Ukrainian President Volodymyr Zelensky has issued an open letter to Russian leader Vladimir Putin, proposing a face-to-face meeting to end the ongoing war. Zelensky emphasized that direct engagement is the only viable path to peace as the international focus shifts toward regional instability in Iran.
*   **Trump and Putin Signal Potential for Compromise:** U.S. President Donald Trump has publicly endorsed the call for direct talks, stating that both nations must move toward a compromise. In a significant development, President Putin confirmed that the U.S. has reached out to Moscow regarding a potential peace deal, signaling a willingness to negotiate provided Kyiv reciprocates.
*   **Hezbollah Rejects Cease-Fire:** Tensions in the Middle East remain high as Hezbollah has officially rejected a U.S.-brokered cease-fire deal between Lebanon and Israel. The group characterizes the truce as an act of surrender, highlighting a widening gap between Lebanese state diplomacy and the group's paramilitary agenda.

## POLAND

*   **Escalating Political Conflict over IPN Leadership:** The friction between the governing coalition (KO) and the opposition (PiS) has intensified, with KO MP Witold Zembaczyński publicly calling for the "neutralization" of the role played by Karol Nawrocki, the head of the Institute of National Remembrance (IPN). The rhetoric underscores the deepening polarization within the Polish political landscape as the ruling party seeks to consolidate its influence over state institutions.
