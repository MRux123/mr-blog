## WORLD

* **Escalating Conflict in Ukraine:** Russian strikes near Kryvyi Rih have resulted in the tragic deaths of eight people, including three children. The incident underscores the continued intensity of the ongoing conflict and the severe human cost facing the civilian population.
* **Geopolitical Trade Friction:** Tensions between the United States and China have intensified following Washington's ban on robotics imports. Beijing has officially warned of retaliatory measures, signaling a potential escalation in the ongoing global trade dispute between the two economic superpowers.
* **Environmental Crisis in Greece:** Massive wildfires, exacerbated by strong winds, have forced the mass evacuation of thousands in the Rethymno region of Crete, highlighting the increasing frequency and severity of extreme weather events across Europe.

## POLAND

* **Investigation into Foreign Object Landing:** The District Prosecutor's Office in Lublin has confirmed that the object which fell in Tarnawa-Kolonia was a missile, likely a CH-101, containing a significant payload of explosives. Authorities are currently conducting specialized examinations of the site with the assistance of the Territorial Defense Forces (WOT).
* **New Educational Regulations:** President Andrzej Duda has signed a package of legislation, most notably introducing a ban on the use of smartphones in schools. The move is aimed at reforming the digital habits of students and minimizing distractions within the classroom environment.
