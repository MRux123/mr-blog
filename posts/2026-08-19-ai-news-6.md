## WORLD

*   **US-South Korea Military Strategy Adjustment:** In a move signaling shifting regional dynamics, South Korea has moved to shorten joint military exercises with the United States. The decision follows a request from Washington, framed within the context of the current administration's diplomatic outreach and perceived improved relationship with North Korean leadership.
*   **Debate on Wartime Elections in Ukraine:** Former Ukrainian Defense Minister Mykhailo Fedorov has publicly advocated for holding presidential elections despite the ongoing conflict. Fedorov argued that democratic processes must be maintained to demonstrate Ukraine’s commitment to European values, asserting that the nation’s democracy should not become a hostage to Russian aggression.

## POLAND

*   **Poland Mourns Wanda Traczyk-Stawska:** The nation is mourning the loss of Wanda Traczyk-Stawska, a legendary hero of the Warsaw Uprising, who passed away at age 99. Her death has prompted an outpouring of tributes from the highest levels of Polish government, recognizing her extraordinary contribution to the country’s history.
*   **Political Pressure on Tusk’s Cabinet:** A recent UCE Research poll indicates growing public sentiment in favor of a government reshuffle. Nearly half of the respondents support changes to the ministerial lineup, with Education Minister Barbara Nowacka specifically highlighted by the public as a figure whose position is currently under scrutiny.
*   **Controversy Over Social Assistants:** Interior Minister Tomasz Siemoniak has addressed the controversy surrounding funds allocated to social assistants, specifically following reports of a local council chair receiving substantial government funding for personal business repairs. The Minister has distanced his office from the actions of social assistants and confirmed that he has opted to eliminate the role within his jurisdiction entirely.
