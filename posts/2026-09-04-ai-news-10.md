## WORLD

*   **Rising Sabotage Concerns in Europe:** Security analysts and regional governments are expressing growing alarm over a sophisticated campaign of sabotage across Europe, with Russia identified as the primary suspect. The attack on Leipzig airport has intensified discussions regarding the continent's vulnerability to hybrid warfare and the potential for these provocations to spiral into an uncontrollable security crisis.
*   **Nepal Flood Rescue Efforts:** Following the catastrophic floods in Nepal, rescue operations at the Upper Trishuli 3A hydropower plant have become a focal point of international reporting. While rescuers have successfully extracted survivors from buried tunnels, the scale of the disaster has forced local families to turn to symbolic funeral rites for those still missing.
*   **Argentine Territorial Claims:** President Javier Milei has reignited long-standing tensions with the United Kingdom by reiterating Argentina's claim over the Falkland Islands. Milei’s rhetoric, which includes threats to sanction oil firms operating in the region, has been met with a firm commitment to the status quo from British officials.

## POLAND

*   **Parliamentary Standoff Over Crypto Regulation:** The Polish Sejm has upheld the presidential veto regarding the cryptocurrency market act. The legislative process remains deadlocked, with Prime Minister Donald Tusk citing investigative protocols alleging potential corruption and improper influence, suggesting that the resistance to the bill may be linked to external interests.
*   **Severe Weather Warnings:** Meteorological authorities have issued urgent warnings for Poland, forecasting a significant weather breakdown. The country is bracing for intense rainfall and wind gusts exceeding 80 km/h, with first-degree alerts currently in place across all provinces.
*   **Crackdown on Patostreaming:** In a significant legal development, the prosecutor's office in Białystok has received a formal notification regarding the exploitation of vulnerable individuals in online broadcasts. This action, stimulated by a media campaign against the "patostreaming" phenomenon, marks a major step in local efforts to regulate offensive and harmful digital content.
