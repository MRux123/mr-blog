## WORLD

*   **Heightened US-Iran Tensions:** President Trump has issued a stern warning to Iran, stating that the United States is prepared to take significant military action over the next few weeks. While the President’s address lacked specific operational details, it signals a potential escalation in the region. Iran has responded swiftly to the rhetoric, further increasing uncertainty regarding a clear path toward de-escalation.
*   **Artemis II Moon Mission Launch:** In a historic advancement for space exploration, the Artemis II mission successfully launched from the Kennedy Space Center. The crew of four is embarking on a 10-day journey to orbit the Moon and observe its far side, marking a critical milestone in NASA's efforts to return humans to the lunar surface and test long-term life-support systems in deep space.
*   **Belarusian Military Mobilization:** President Alexander Lukashenko has publicly declared that Belarus is "preparing for war" following a comprehensive inspection of the nation's armed forces. Citing deficiencies identified by intelligence services, the announcement reflects the deepening regional instability and the Belarusian leadership’s shift toward a militarized posture.

## POLAND

*   **Political Instability for Hennig-Kloska:** Climate and Environment Minister Paulina Hennig-Kloska faces an uncertain future as reports suggest she has lost the support of her coalition partners. Discussions regarding her potential dismissal are reportedly underway, signaling internal friction within the ruling coalition.
*   **Anniversary of John Paul II:** As Poland marks the 21st anniversary of the death of Pope John Paul II, the national discourse has turned toward his final messages regarding the role of the clergy. The anniversary, which coincides with Maundy Thursday this year, has prompted reflection on the late pontiff’s warnings against the politicization of the Church.
