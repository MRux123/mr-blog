## WORLD

*   **Europe Faces Escalating Wildfire Threat:** A massive fire crisis is unfolding across France and Spain, forcing hundreds of thousands of residents and tourists to evacuate. The situation remains critical as fire crews struggle to contain blazes moving perilously close to major urban centers, including Madrid and Bordeaux.
*   **Typhoon Crisis in China:** Natural disaster relief operations are underway as a powerful typhoon approaches the Chinese coast. Authorities have already evacuated over 20,000 people from high-risk areas in anticipation of the storm’s landfall.

## POLAND

*   **Political Fallout within Law and Justice (PiS):** Former Prime Minister Mateusz Morawiecki has publicly addressed internal party tensions, dismissing claims of being an advisor to Donald Tusk. His rhetoric regarding the party's failed "two-lungs" strategy highlights growing friction within the opposition party's leadership following comments from Jarosław Kaczyński.
*   **Record-Breaking Sporting Achievement:** Polish fencer Barbara Brych has secured a place in history, winning the bronze medal at the World Championships, marking a significant milestone for Polish fencing.
*   **Safety Alerts Across the Country:** Meteorological services have issued high-temperature warnings for 12 provinces. Citizens are advised to exercise caution as extreme heat is expected to dominate the weekend, with further risks of thunderstorms moving in later in the day.
