## WORLD

* **ExxonMobil Nears Strategic Venezuela Deal:** In a significant geopolitical shift, U.S. oil giant ExxonMobil is nearing an agreement to resume oil extraction in Venezuela. This development, seen as a victory for the Trump administration, marks a potential thawing of relations with the country’s socialist regime and carries major implications for global energy markets.
* **Ebola Outbreak Escalates International Tensions:** The ongoing Ebola crisis in the Democratic Republic of Congo (DRC) continues to strain international diplomacy. While Uganda has suspended flights to and from the DRC, critics and the Africa CDC argue that travel bans are ineffective and exacerbate structural injustices. The situation remains volatile, further evidenced by the recent arson attack on an Ebola treatment facility in the DRC.
* **US Indictment of Raúl Castro:** Russia and China have publicly condemned the United States following the indictment of former Cuban leader Raúl Castro on murder charges, signaling deepening polarization between Western powers and their adversaries.

## POLAND

* **Ukraine Issues Warning to NATO:** Ukrainian Foreign Minister Andrij Sybiha has held critical discussions with NATO Secretary General Mark Rutte regarding the escalating security threat posed by Russia, particularly in light of developments in Belarus. Kyiv is calling for a unified, robust deterrence strategy to prevent further aggression in the region.
* **Security Commitments Reaffirmed:** Following diplomatic talks in Washington, Deputy Minister of National Defense Paweł Zalewski has confirmed that there are no plans to reduce U.S. military forces in Poland. Discussions are reportedly shifting toward establishing a permanent American troop presence in the country to bolster regional security.
* **Political Deadlock in Krakow:** Municipal operations in Krakow have been disrupted as an extraordinary session of the City Council was canceled due to a lack of quorum. The absence of councilors from the Civic Coalition and the New Left underscores the intense local political climate ahead of the upcoming city referendum.
