## WORLD

*   **Vatican Warns of Schism Risks:** Pope Francis has raised alarms regarding a potential schism within the Catholic Church following the unauthorized ordination of controversial bishops. The ceremony, held by the breakaway Society of Saint Pius X in the Swiss Alps, underscores deep-seated theological and administrative fractures within the institution.
*   **Trump Faces Pressure Over Damages:** Legal representatives for E. Jean Carroll are intensifying efforts to collect $5 million in damages from Donald Trump. This follows the failure of the former president’s appeal against a jury verdict finding him liable for sexual abuse and defamation, marking a significant legal and financial hurdle for his ongoing political activities.

## POLAND

*   **Grave Allegations at Southern Hospital:** Investigation reports from Onet have uncovered disturbing systemic irregularities at Warsaw's Southern Hospital. Allegations suggest that the facility’s morgue was involved in the illicit "trading" of human remains, with coordinators reportedly soliciting unauthorized fees, prompting a serious review of the institution's management.
*   **Tragic Rise in 'Train Surfing':** Public concern is mounting following a series of fatal accidents involving the dangerous social media trend of "train surfing." Recent tragedies, including a double incident resulting in one death and one critical injury, have prompted calls for stricter preventative measures and public awareness campaigns to curb this lethal pursuit of adrenaline among adolescents.
*   **Political Shifts in Defense:** High-level discussions between Karol Nawrocki and Defense Minister Władysław Kosiniak-Kamysz have concluded, focusing on establishing a unified Polish stance ahead of the upcoming NATO summit. The meeting reflects a crucial effort to synchronize defense policy amidst shifting internal political dynamics.
