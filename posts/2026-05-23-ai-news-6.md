## WORLD

* **Tragedy in China:** A devastating explosion at a coal mine in northern China has resulted in at least 82 fatalities. The incident, occurring at 19:29 local time on Friday, has prompted a massive emergency response as authorities investigate the cause of the disaster.
* **Escalation in Russia-Ukraine Conflict:** Following a strike on a student dormitory, which Russia attributed to Ukraine, President Vladimir Putin has vowed swift retaliation. Ukraine maintains that the operation targeted a military unit belonging to the elite "Rubicon" drone division in occupied territory.
* **SpaceX Achievement:** SpaceX successfully launched the Starship V3 rocket, marking a milestone in aerospace history. This test flight of the most powerful rocket ever built follows a previously postponed attempt, signaling a significant leap in deep-space exploration capabilities.

## POLAND

* **LOT Polish Airlines Legal Defeat:** A federal jury in Seattle has ruled in favor of Boeing in a high-profile legal dispute involving LOT Polish Airlines. The court found that Boeing did not withhold critical safety information regarding the 737 MAX aircraft leased by the Polish carrier, dismissing claims of corporate negligence.
* **Lewandowski’s Barcelona Farewell:** Robert Lewandowski is set to play his final match for FC Barcelona this Saturday against Valencia. The departure marks the end of a high-profile three-year tenure, during which the Polish striker cemented his legacy in Spanish football through numerous records and accolades.
