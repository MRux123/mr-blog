## WORLD

*   **Escalating Iran-Israel Conflict:** The situation in the Strait of Hormuz remains critical as the waterway stays largely closed. Amidst ongoing regional instability, President Trump has announced that U.S. officials are set to travel to Pakistan for mediation talks, as international efforts to reach a deal to end the conflict continue to face significant hurdles.
*   **Controversial U.S. Sanctions Waiver:** President Zelensky has formally condemned the U.S. decision to extend a sanctions waiver for Russia. While Washington frames the move as a necessary measure to stabilize global energy supplies currently strained by the ongoing conflict in the Middle East, the decision has drawn sharp criticism regarding its geopolitical implications.
*   **Public Health Crisis in Austria:** Authorities are investigating a major safety breach after rat poison was discovered in jars of HiPP baby food. Police have issued a warning to the public, labeling the consumption of the contaminated products as potentially life-threatening.

## POLAND

*   **Commemoration of the Warsaw Ghetto Uprising:** Poland marked the 83rd anniversary of the Warsaw Ghetto Uprising this Sunday. The solemn ceremony, attended by state officials and the President of Warsaw, included the sounding of sirens across the capital to honor the victims and survivors of the tragic historical event.
*   **Diplomatic Tensions over Airspace:** The Slovak government has signaled its intent to fly over Poland as part of an alternative route to Moscow for the upcoming Victory Day celebrations, following travel restrictions imposed by Lithuania and Latvia. This proposed flight path highlights the complex regional diplomatic landscape surrounding Russia.
*   **Public Safety and Preparedness:** Following a series of local incidents—including a fatal road accident on the S19 highway and a small aircraft crash near Poznań—the Government Security Centre (RCB) has issued an urgent warning to citizens to remain vigilant and properly prepare for potential flooding, as weather risks remain a priority for regional authorities.
