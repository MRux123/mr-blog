## WORLD

* **María Corina Machado Returns to Venezuela:** After ten months of exile, opposition leader María Corina Machado has returned to Venezuela. Her arrival marks a significant escalation in the pressure against President Nicolás Maduro, signaling a renewed push for democratic reform and upcoming electoral challenges.
* **Record Voter Turnout in Serbia:** Serbia reports historic levels of voter participation as the nation faces a pivotal election. A grassroots student movement is actively challenging the long-standing populist administration of President Aleksandar Vučić, suggesting a potential shift in the country's political landscape.
* **Ebola Outbreak Fears in Nigeria:** Health authorities in Nigeria have confirmed a case of Ebola in Abuja. The patient, who recently arrived from Uganda, has sparked concerns regarding the potential spread of the virus across western Africa’s most populous nation.

## POLAND

* **High Voter Engagement in Serbian Elections Echoes in Polish Media:** The record turnout in Serbia has captured significant attention in Poland, highlighting the regional interest in the stability and democratic processes of the Balkans.
* **Inclement Weather Impacts Infrastructure:** Heavy winds across northern Poland have caused significant disruption, leading to accidents such as falling trees damaging vehicles and hindering local traffic.
* **Sports Rivalry Continues:** The Polish Ekstraklasa remains a focal point for national discourse, as Lech Poznań secures a victory in the league classic against Legia Warsaw, marking the first defeat of the season for the latter.
