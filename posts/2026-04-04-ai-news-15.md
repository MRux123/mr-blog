## WORLD

* **Trump Issues Ultimatum to Iran:** US President Donald Trump has issued a severe 48-hour warning to Iran via his social media platform, Truth Social, threatening unspecified "hell" if an agreement is not reached regarding the Strait of Hormuz. This follows intelligence reports indicating that Iran is rapidly rebuilding its missile infrastructure despite targeted airstrikes by the US and Israel.
* **Russian Strike on Ukraine:** A Russian drone attack targeted a crowded market in a southern Ukrainian town on Saturday. The strike resulted in five fatalities and left at least 21 people injured, marking another escalation in the ongoing conflict.
* **Political Turmoil in Hungary:** Peter Magyar, leader of the opposition TISZA party, has proposed a constitutional amendment to limit the tenure of the Hungarian Prime Minister to eight years. The proposal, which would apply retroactively to Viktor Orbán, signals a significant challenge to the long-standing leadership in Budapest.

## POLAND

* **Significant Drop in Border Migration:** The Polish Ministry of the Interior and Administration (MSWiA) reports a 96% reduction in illegal migration attempts at the Belarusian border. Authorities attribute this decline to the policy of temporarily suspending the right to asylum for those attempting to cross the border illegally.
* **Cross-Border Fuel Tourism:** Polish gas stations near the German border are experiencing high demand as German motorists cross the border to benefit from lower fuel prices. Drivers report savings of approximately €30 per refueling, with price differences reaching over 50 eurocents per liter, leading to queues in border towns like Lubieszyn.
* **Russian Propaganda Targets Poland:** Recent reports highlight that Russian military experts and propagandists are actively portraying Poland as a primary regional threat. These narratives falsely accuse Polish special services of destabilizing Belarus and harboring territorial ambitions, fueling regional information warfare.
