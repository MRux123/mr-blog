## WORLD

* **Coordinated Iranian Strikes Ignite Tensions in the Persian Gulf:** The geopolitical landscape has shifted dramatically following overnight strikes by Iran across several nations, including Israel, Bahrain, Jordan, and Saudi Arabia. Reports indicate critical infrastructure damage, such as a fuel depot fire at an international airport in Kuwait, sparking fears of a wider regional conflict.
* **Pentagon Mobilizes Airborne Troops:** In a significant military escalation, the Pentagon has ordered 2,000 airborne troops to be deployed to the Middle East. This strategic move follows a failed diplomatic proposal delivered via Pakistan, suggesting that the United States is preparing for a range of intensified military options in response to the Iranian aggression.
* **Global Economic Warning:** Larry Fink, the CEO of BlackRock, has issued a stark warning regarding the global economy. He cautioned that if oil prices remain sustained at the $150 per barrel mark, the world should brace for a significant global recession.

## POLAND

* **Presidential Intervention in Judicial Case:** President Karol Nawrocki has initiated a formal request for documentation concerning the case of Adam Borowski, an opposition activist from the PRL era. By demanding the files from Prosecutor General Waldemar Żurek and invoking constitutional pardoning powers, the President has signaled a potentially controversial stance on historical judicial review.
* **Major Lottery Jackpot Claimed:** A record-breaking Lotto draw has concluded with a single winner securing over 32 million PLN. The prize, which had been accumulating for nearly two weeks, represents one of the highest individual payouts in the history of the Polish national lottery.
* **Economic Policy Debates:** Ryszard Petru has opened a public discourse regarding the economic proposals of the ruling coalition, specifically questioning the viability and implications of implementing a new form of cadastre tax and adjustments to the second tax threshold, fueling speculation about potential shifts in fiscal policy.
