## WORLD

*   **Trump Envoys Launch New Diplomatic Push in Russia-Ukraine War:** President Donald Trump’s special peace envoys, Steve Witkoff and Jared Kushner, are scheduled to visit Moscow and Kyiv this weekend. The mission aims to break the current diplomatic deadlock, though the complexity of the conflict continues to challenge the administration's efforts to reach a sustainable ceasefire.
*   **UN Adopts Revised World Map Reflecting Accurate African Geography:** In a significant move for global cartography, the United Nations has voted to adopt a new, more accurate world map that corrects the visual representation of Africa’s size. The resolution, sponsored by Togo and backed by 164 nations, marks a shift in how the continent is projected globally, with the United States standing as the sole dissenting vote.
*   **Tragedy at Bolivian Military Barracks:** At least two people have been confirmed dead following a major blast at a military facility in Viacha, Bolivia. Local authorities are urging civilians to maintain distance from the site, citing the risk of secondary explosions and ongoing rescue operations that may lead to an increased casualty count.
