## WORLD

*   **Trump’s Diplomatic Outreach:** Donald Trump has engaged in high-stakes conversations with both Ukrainian President Volodymyr Zelensky and Russian President Vladimir Putin. While details remain limited, the diplomatic activity coincides with heightened tensions in the Middle East, where the Israeli military continues to strike Hezbollah targets in Beirut amid warnings that the conflict could threaten broader US-Iran stability.
*   **Aviation Tragedy in the US:** A private aircraft carrying 11 skydivers and a pilot crashed near an airport in Butler, Missouri. Local authorities have confirmed that there were no survivors in the incident, which occurred shortly after the plane's departure.
*   **Swiss Population Policy:** In a significant domestic vote, nearly 55% of Swiss voters rejected a controversial proposal to impose a hard cap on the national population through restricted migration.

## POLAND

*   **Political Tensions at Auschwitz Commemoration:** The Auschwitz Museum denied access to representatives of the Konfederacja Korony Polskiej during the 86th anniversary of the first mass deportation of Poles to the camp. The museum cited the party's history of controversial statements and public denial of gas chambers as the basis for the exclusion.
*   **Nawrocki Criticizes Government Rhetoric:** Karol Nawrocki has voiced concerns regarding the current administration's "anti-American narrative," warning that such posturing hinders effective diplomatic cooperation with the United States and complicates the execution of strategic agreements made at the highest political levels.
*   **Czarnek Involved in Road Incident:** Prof. Przemysław Czarnek, a prominent political figure, was involved in a road collision. Party officials confirmed that while the vehicle was damaged, the incident resulted from a right-of-way violation by another driver, and the situation is being handled by local police.
