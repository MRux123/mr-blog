## WORLD

* **Tragic Escalation in Zaporizhzhia:** A Russian glide bomb attack on the city of Zaporizhzhia has resulted in the deaths of at least 20 people, including children. President Zelensky has framed this strike as a retaliatory gesture linked to a controversial diesel agreement between Russia and the United States.
* **Political Fallout Over Energy Deals:** Tensions between Washington and Kyiv have deepened significantly. Donald Trump has publicly called for a change in Ukrainian leadership following President Zelensky’s vocal condemnation of a U.S.-Russia diesel deal, which critics argue provides Moscow with crucial wartime funding.
* **Hurricane Isaias Devastation:** The United States is grappling with the aftermath of Hurricane Isaias, which has left four people dead and thousands without electricity. The storm caused significant infrastructure damage, including the loss of roofs across affected regions, before weakening into a post-tropical cyclone.

## POLAND

* **Addressing School Violence and Misinformation:** The Polish Prime Minister has convened an emergency briefing to address rising concerns regarding violence within educational institutions and the increasing threat posed by organized disinformation campaigns.
* **Diplomatic Stance on Ukraine:** Official discourse surrounding Polish-Ukrainian relations remains complex. High-level discussions, including commemorations of historical tragedies like the Volhynia massacre, underscore the delicate balance between current strategic military cooperation and the unresolved historical grievances that continue to shape the bilateral dialogue.
