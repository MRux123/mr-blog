## WORLD

* **Jay Clayton Appointed to Lead US Intelligence Community**: In a significant shift for American national security, Jay Clayton has been tapped to oversee the nation's intelligence apparatus. The move marks a high-level restructuring within the U.S. intelligence community, likely signaling a shift in policy focus and internal management.
* **Health Alert Following Outbreak**: US health officials have reported the first two fatalities linked to a parasitic outbreak causing severe gastrointestinal distress. While the illness, often spread through contaminated food or water, is generally not fatal, authorities are monitoring the situation closely to contain its further spread.

## POLAND

* **Russian Disinformation Campaign Following Missile Impact**: Polish military officials have issued a formal warning regarding a surge in hostile disinformation campaigns originating from Russia, coinciding with the recent impact of a cruise missile in the vicinity of Tarnawa Kolonia. The military is calling for public vigilance as state actors attempt to manipulate the narrative surrounding the incident.
* **Prosecutor Reopens Investigation into Bartłomiej Misiewicz**: The District Prosecutor's Office in Warsaw has resumed an inquiry into the controversial hiring of Bartłomiej Misiewicz at the Ministry of National Defense. The investigation focuses on the circumstances surrounding his rapid appointment as the ministry's spokesperson at the age of 25, a case that has remained a subject of public and political scrutiny.
* **Diplomatic Tensions Over Soviet Monument Removal**: The Russian Embassy in Poland has lodged a formal protest following the removal of a monument in Pyrzyce. The structure, dismantled on July 31st, had been identified by the Institute of National Remembrance (IPN) as a Soviet propaganda object rather than a memorial, further straining bilateral diplomatic relations.
