## WORLD

* **Middle East Conflict Intensifies:** The regional conflict has reached a critical turning point following an Israeli airstrike that reportedly killed Iranian security chief Ali Larijani. The move, described by Israel as a major blow to the Iranian regime, has triggered a series of diplomatic and military shocks. In response, U.S. President Donald Trump has signaled a readiness to deploy ground troops, while simultaneously rebuking NATO for its refusal to assist in securing the Strait of Hormuz.
* **Internal Dissent in Washington:** The aggressive military stance against Iran has caused high-level friction within the U.S. administration. Joe Kent, head of the National Counterterrorism Center, resigned in protest, publicly criticizing the White House and Israeli influence, asserting that Iran did not pose an immediate threat to U.S. national security.
* **Kabul Rehabilitation Center Tragedy:** A separate humanitarian catastrophe has emerged in Afghanistan, where a Pakistani airstrike hit a drug rehabilitation facility, resulting in high civilian casualties. The Afghan government has vowed retaliation, threatening to open a new front of instability in the region.

## POLAND

* **Vatican Legal Precedent:** In a historic development, the Vatican’s appellate court has overturned the so-called "trial of the century" involving Cardinal Angelo Becciu. The court cited significant procedural errors committed by both the prosecution and Pope Francis, a decision that challenges the judicial authority of the Holy See.
* **Domestic Political Maneuvering:** High-level political speculation continues in Warsaw as Interior Minister Marcin Kierwiński dismissed the possibility of Przemysław Czarnek becoming Prime Minister, labeling him merely a "political bumper" for Jarosław Kaczyński’s strategy.
* **Public Health Alarm:** Data from the "WF z AWF" project has revealed a worrying trend in the physical development of Polish youth. Over 90% of students exhibit insufficient motor skills, with nearly nine out of ten children in grades I-III unable to perform basic physical tasks such as rope jumping or ball handling, signaling a long-term public health crisis.
