## WORLD

* **Ukraine shifts diplomatic focus to Europe:** With the U.S. distancing itself from mediation roles, President Volodymyr Zelensky has initiated high-level talks in London, Berlin, and Paris. This strategic pivot aims to secure European leadership in potential future negotiations with Russia.
* **Escalation in the Middle East:** Conflict in the region has intensified as Israel strikes the outskirts of Beirut. Despite active diplomatic pressure from the Trump administration to facilitate a cease-fire in Washington, Hezbollah has officially rejected the truce, casting a shadow over regional stability.
* **Humanitarian crisis in Iran:** Decades of economic mismanagement and international pressure have culminated in a profound sense of hopelessness among the Iranian public. Skyrocketing inflation and the human cost of ongoing war have deepened the divide between the state and its citizens, regardless of their political alignment.

## POLAND

* **Political upheaval over historical rhetoric:** Przemysław Czarnek, the Law and Justice (PiS) candidate for Prime Minister, has formally demanded the resignation of the current government. This radical move follows a controversial statement by Deputy Minister of Science Karol Szeptycki, who characterized members of the UPA as "Ukrainian soldiers of independence," sparking a firestorm regarding national memory and historical policy.
* **Political maneuvering in Kraków:** Major political factions, including the Civic Coalition (KO) and the Polish People's Party (PSL), are currently engaged in intense negotiations to finalize a joint candidate for the mayoral race in Kraków, signaling an attempt to consolidate center-liberal influence in the city.
