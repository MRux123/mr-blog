## WORLD

* **Escalation in Iran:** The conflict between the U.S. and Iran has entered a critical phase, with Pentagon officials confirming that B-52 bombers are conducting missions over Iranian territory for the first time. Defense Secretary Pete Hegseth warned of "decisive days" ahead, emphasizing that while Iran maintains some missile capabilities, U.S. military strategy remains focused on eliminating threats regardless of where they are positioned.
* **Geopolitical Strain:** Global security concerns are mounting as the U.S. urges international allies to increase their naval presence in the Strait of Hormuz. Meanwhile, diplomatic friction is evident as the U.K. navigates a strained relationship with the Trump administration regarding the handling of the Middle East crisis, a backdrop that complicates the upcoming visit of King Charles to the United States.

## POLAND

* **Constitutional Court Appointments:** A significant political shift is underway as President Andrzej Duda has officially invited newly elected Constitutional Tribunal judges, Dariusz Szostek and Magdalena Bentkowska, to take their oaths of office at the Presidential Palace. The move has drawn immediate reaction from Prime Minister Donald Tusk, signaling potential future clashes regarding the composition and legitimacy of the judiciary.
* **Russian Ultimatum to Ukraine:** President Volodymyr Zelensky has publicly revealed that Russia has issued a two-month ultimatum demanding the withdrawal of Ukrainian forces from the Donetsk region. The development adds severe pressure to ongoing trilateral negotiations, as Moscow continues to utilize threats of military escalation to dictate terms.
