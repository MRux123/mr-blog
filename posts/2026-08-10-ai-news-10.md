## WORLD

* **Escalation in Ukraine-Russia Conflict:** At least 13 casualties have been reported following a significant Ukrainian drone strike in Russia's Tatarstan region. This incident marks one of the most lethal aerial incursions into Russian territory since the commencement of the war, signaling a potential shift in the intensity and geographical scope of drone operations.
* **Mass Evacuations in China:** Typhoon Dolphin has made landfall in eastern China, prompting the emergency relocation of one million people. The storm, which is impacting major hubs including Shanghai, has brought severe weather conditions, leading to widespread infrastructure concerns.
* **Legal and Political Shifts in the US:** A federal judge has ruled in favor of the Trump administration’s move to terminate Temporary Protected Status (TPS) for South Sudanese nationals. This decision follows a broader legal trend concerning the limitation of protections for immigrant populations within the United States.

## POLAND

* **Judicial Decision on Controversial Figure:** The Supreme Administrative Court (NSA) has upheld a previous decision barring the Ukrainian streamer known as "Crawly" from entering Poland. The ruling concludes a high-profile legal dispute regarding the presence and influence of the creator within the country.
* **Deepening Political Polarization:** The opposition party, Law and Justice (PiS), has formally announced a legislative push to withdraw Poland from the EU Emissions Trading System (ETS). The initiative follows the Senate’s rejection of a motion to hold a referendum on the European Union's climate policies, highlighting a growing divide over environmental legislation.
* **Tragic Case of Child Abuse:** Authorities are investigating a disturbing case in Warsaw, where an infant has been hospitalized with severe injuries. The situation has drawn national attention due to the revelation that the victim's sibling suffered similar abuse six years prior, sparking a critical debate on the effectiveness of child protection services.
