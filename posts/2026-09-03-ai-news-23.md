## WORLD

* **Norway Seizes Russian Vessel:** In a move testing diplomatic relations in the High Arctic, Norwegian authorities have impounded a Russian ship under an international court order. The seizure is part of a broader global initiative to target Russian assets. The Kremlin has characterized the action as "piracy," signaling a potential escalation in regional maritime tensions.
* **Trump’s Policy Shift on Ukraine:** U.S. President Donald Trump has issued a stern warning regarding the financial scope of American aid to Ukraine. On Truth Social, Trump criticized the volume of ammunition provided by the Biden administration, demanding reimbursement for military assistance and suggesting a fundamental shift in U.S. involvement in the ongoing conflict.

## POLAND

* **Funeral Arrangements for Ratko Mladić:** The remains of convicted war criminal Ratko Mladić have been transported to Belgrade. The formal scheduling of his funeral follows his death while serving a life sentence at the detention center in The Hague, marking the end of a long and controversial legal chapter.
* **Polish Scientific Achievement:** Polish researchers have been formally recognized at the 2026 Ig Nobel ceremony in Zurich. The awards, which celebrate scientific research that "makes people laugh, and then think," highlight the country’s ongoing contributions to both experimental and unconventional science on the global stage.
