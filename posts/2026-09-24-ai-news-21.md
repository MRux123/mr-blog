## WORLD

*   **UN Diplomatic Uproar:** Prime Minister Benjamin Netanyahu’s address to the UN General Assembly faced significant backlash, with numerous delegates walking out in protest. The Israeli leader adopted a combative stance, defending military operations in the Middle East and labeling his critics as "moral cowards," further deepening the divide in international diplomatic discourse.
*   **Ethiopia on the Brink:** Fears of a renewed civil war in Ethiopia are intensifying as Tigrayan rebels launch a major offensive into neighboring states. The formation of a new rebel coalition aimed at toppling Prime Minister Abiy Ahmed’s government signals a collapse of stability, threatening a repeat of the devastating 2020-2022 conflict.
*   **Regional Instability:** Pakistan has launched cross-border strikes into Afghanistan, claiming to target specific military objectives. Meanwhile, Saudi Arabia successfully intercepted six ballistic missiles fired by Houthi forces in Yemen, underscoring the ongoing volatile security environment across the Middle East.

## POLAND

*   **Security Alert Following Russian Strikes:** The Government Security Centre (RCB) has issued alerts for the Podkarpackie and Lubelskie provinces as Russia launched a fresh wave of attacks against Ukraine. Polish military aircraft have been scrambled to monitor the airspace and ensure territorial security.
*   **Tragedy at the Abbey:** A fatal knife attack at an abbey in Jarosław has resulted in the death of a priest and injuries to four others. Polish authorities have arrested a Ukrainian national in connection with the incident, an event that occurs against a backdrop of sensitive diplomatic efforts between the two nations to navigate historical tensions.
*   **Political Discourse on Economic Pressures:** Prime Minister Donald Tusk addressed rising fuel prices during a campaign rally in Krakow. He attributed the ongoing economic strain to global factors, specifically citing the influence of the conflict in the Middle East and the geopolitical actions of Vladimir Putin, firmly rejecting domestic criticism regarding governmental responsibility for global energy market fluctuations.
