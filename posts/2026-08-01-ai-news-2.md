## WORLD

* **Migration Crisis at the Spanish Border:** A significant influx of approximately 60,000 migrants into the Spanish enclave of Ceuta from Morocco has triggered a humanitarian and political crisis. While the majority of those who entered have returned to Morocco within 24 hours, the event resulted in nearly three dozen fatalities, placing severe pressure on Spain’s current pro-immigration administration.
* **Legal U-turn in Peru:** Former President Ollanta Humala has been released from prison following the overturning of his 15-year corruption sentence. Humala, who was convicted alongside his wife on money laundering charges in 2025, now faces a complex legal aftermath as the judicial system reassesses the previous verdict.

## POLAND

* **FIFA Abandons Controversial Reform:** Polish media reports confirm that FIFA has officially retracted plans for a radical structural change involving the sale of portions of the World Cup’s equity. The decision halts a proposal that had sparked widespread controversy within the international sporting community, signaling a return to the status quo for the football governing body.
