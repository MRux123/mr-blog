## WORLD

* **Trump's Ultimatum to NATO:** U.S. President Donald Trump has issued a stern warning regarding the future of the NATO alliance. In an interview with the *Financial Times*, Trump linked the alliance's long-term viability to the active participation of member states in securing the Strait of Hormuz. Furthermore, the President hinted at potential diplomatic cooling with China, threatening to postpone an upcoming state visit if Beijing fails to provide necessary support for U.S. strategic objectives.
* **Rising Security Measures in Tehran:** Reports from BBC News indicate a heightening of internal security in Iran's capital. Residents have observed a surge in new checkpoints and stop-and-search operations, suggesting a pre-emptive effort by the establishment to suppress potential anti-government protests amid ongoing regional instability.

## POLAND

* **The SAFE Program Debate:** A new CBOS poll reveals a deeply divided public opinion regarding the European Union's "SAFE" initiative for defense-related loans. While 43% of respondents view the program as beneficial, 39% remain skeptical. Notably, the data challenges the assumption that support for European integration is solely a youth-driven movement, as the initiative finds its strongest backing among older demographics.
* **Political Clash Over European Future:** The debate over Poland's standing in the EU has intensified, with PiS MEP Adam Bielan publicly refuting accusations that his party seeks to exit the Union. Bielan countered the opposition's claims by branding them "nonsense" and accusing Prime Minister Donald Tusk of being the only significant political figure who has historically used the threat of "Polexit" as a narrative tool.
