## WORLD

* **Rising Escalation in Iran:** Tensions in the Middle East have reached a critical point following the downing of a U.S. aircraft by Iranian forces and a subsequent, high-stakes American rescue operation. Both nations are showing increased resolve, raising concerns among international observers about a potential rapid expansion of the conflict. 
* **Strategic Strikes on Russian Infrastructure:** In a major tactical development, Ukrainian forces conducted a series of precision strikes on critical energy infrastructure within Russia overnight. The operation effectively paralyzed key fuel supply chains for the Moscow region and disrupted logistics for Russian military units, marking one of the most significant deep-strike operations of the current conflict.
* **Papal Call for Peace:** On Easter Sunday, Pope Leo XIV delivered his Urbi et Orbi address, issuing a stern plea for global peace. Amidst ongoing international volatility, the pontiff condemned the indifferent stance of the world toward violence, urging leaders to cease hostilities and prioritize human life.

## POLAND

* **Security Alert Over Balkan Stream Sabotage:** Security concerns have rippled into Central Europe after the discovery of explosives near the Balkan Stream gas pipeline in Serbia. As the pipeline is a vital artery for Hungarian energy security, Prime Minister Viktor Orbán has taken the situation seriously, immediately convening an emergency defense council.
* **Widespread Weather Warnings:** The Institute of Meteorology and Water Management (IMGW) has issued Level 1 warnings for 13 provinces across Poland. A powerful wind front moving across the country has already triggered dozens of emergency interventions, particularly in the Pomeranian region, with authorities urging residents to exercise extreme caution.
