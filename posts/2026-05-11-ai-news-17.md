## WORLD

*   **Trump Threatens Iran Truce:** President Donald Trump has cast doubt on the future of the ceasefire with Iran, dismissing Tehran’s peace proposals as "garbage." The administration is now warning of a potential long-term energy crisis, with Trump floating the idea of suspending the federal gasoline tax to mitigate costs, a move that would require congressional support.
*   **Starmer Struggles for Stability:** British Prime Minister Keir Starmer is facing significant pressure following a disappointing election result. While he has managed to avert an immediate leadership mutiny within the Labour Party, his long-term grip on power remains under scrutiny.
*   **Ecological Impact of Maritime Rerouting:** Scientists have issued a warning regarding the environmental consequences of the ongoing shipping crisis in the Middle East. With vessels rerouting around the Cape of Good Hope to avoid conflict zones, migratory whale populations face an increased risk of collisions and disruption.

## POLAND

*   **Political Gridlock Over CBA Liquidation:** President Karol Nawrocki has vetoed legislation aimed at dissolving the Central Anti-Corruption Bureau (CBA). The President condemned the move as a return to the unchecked political influence of the 1990s, while government spokespeople have accused the veto of actively undermining state security infrastructure.
*   **Energy Diplomacy with Germany:** Poland is currently in high-level talks with Berlin regarding potential oil supplies via the port of Gdańsk. The negotiations follow critical supply shortages at the Schwedt refinery, which is vital for providing fuel to Berlin and Brandenburg, highlighting Poland’s growing strategic importance in the regional energy landscape.
