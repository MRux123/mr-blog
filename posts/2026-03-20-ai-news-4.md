## WORLD

* **Escalation in the Middle East:** During the celebration of Eid at the Al-Aqsa Mosque in Jerusalem, sirens and explosions were reported, marking a volatile shift in regional stability. Prime Minister Benjamin Netanyahu has officially confirmed that Israel independently targeted Iranian gas infrastructure at the South Pars field. Netanyahu stated that after nearly 20 days of ongoing conflict, Iran's defensive capabilities have been significantly compromised, vowing to persist with military operations until strategic objectives are met.

## POLAND

* **Major Upset in Miami:** Polish tennis witnessed a historic and unexpected outcome at the WTA 1000 tournament in Miami. Magda Linette achieved a significant career milestone by defeating Iga Świątek in the second round with a score of 6:1, 5:7, 3:6. This loss marks a rare and notable setback for Świątek, as it is the first time since August 2021 that she has been eliminated in her opening match of a tournament.
