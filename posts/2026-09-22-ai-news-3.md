## WORLD

* **Pentagon Clarifies Schedule Conflict Regarding Kosiniak-Kamysz:** The U.S. Department of Defense has officially addressed the situation concerning the cancelled or adjusted meeting with Poland’s Deputy Prime Minister and Minister of National Defence, Władysław Kosiniak-Kamysz. Pentagon officials cited a "conflict of schedules" as the primary reason for the diplomatic adjustment, downplaying speculation regarding any underlying political friction.

## POLAND

* **Karol Nawrocki Appoints "Special Ambassador":** Karol Nawrocki has announced the appointment of a new "special ambassador," framing the move as a strategic necessity to ensure the President maintains an effective "listening channel" (odsłuch) to the needs and sentiments of the public. The appointment is being viewed by analysts as a key maneuver in positioning the president's office for upcoming political challenges and enhancing direct communication lines.
