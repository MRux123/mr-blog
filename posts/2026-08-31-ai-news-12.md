## WORLD

*   **US-Iran Military Escalation:** Regional stability in the Middle East has reached a critical point as the United States conducted its first strike against Iranian targets in weeks. The operation, centered on Larak Island in the Strait of Hormuz, resulted in two fatalities. This development marks a significant escalation in the ongoing shadow conflict between the two nations, raising concerns over potential retaliatory actions and broader maritime security.
*   **Shifting Security Perspectives in Israel:** A notable ideological pivot is emerging among Israel’s former top military and intelligence officials. Several high-ranking retired generals have publicly categorized settler activity in the West Bank as a form of state-supported ethnic cleansing. This internal dissent highlights a growing divide between veteran security experts and current government policy, with some warning that these actions pose an existential threat to Israeli societal norms.
*   **Global Call for Slavery Reparations:** The United Nations Committee on the Elimination of Racial Discrimination (CERD) has issued formal guidance stating that member states have a legal obligation to consider reparations for the transatlantic slave trade. This landmark interpretation suggests that international legal frameworks, specifically the 1965 convention on racial discrimination, mandate comprehensive measures to address the enduring legacies of historical racial injustices.

## POLAND

*   **Public Safety and Professional Hazards:** National discourse is currently dominated by concerns over public safety and labor stability. Recent reports of a repeat offender driving under the influence with children on board have sparked public outrage, while the prospect of a widespread teachers' strike looms as educators demand wage increases amidst rising living costs.
*   **Lech Wałęsa on Democracy:** During the 46th anniversary of the August Agreements, former President Lech Wałęsa offered a somber assessment of the current democratic landscape. Wałęsa expressed grave concern regarding the erosion of democratic principles both in Poland and globally, urging for immediate civic action to preserve the values established during the Solidarity movement.
*   **Tragedy in Greece:** Polish citizens are reeling from the death of an 11-year-old girl in a hotel swimming pool on Crete. Eyewitness accounts, including those from Polish tourists who attempted emergency resuscitation, allege systemic failures and a lack of proper medical equipment at the facility, prompting renewed discussions about safety standards for Polish tourists abroad.
