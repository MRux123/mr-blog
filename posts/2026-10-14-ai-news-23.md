## WORLD

* **Critical Vulnerabilities in Ukraine:** Concerns over battlefield communications have intensified as satellite internet access for Ukrainian forces faces unexplained restrictions. While Elon Musk maintains that limit decisions rest with the US government, the disruption coincides with an escalating Russian aerial campaign that has left hundreds of thousands in Kyiv without power, significantly straining Ukraine’s defensive capabilities.
* **Nobel Peace Prize Awarded to Navi Pillay:** The Norwegian Nobel Committee has honored ICJ judge Navi Pillay for her instrumental work in international law. The recognition highlights her leadership in UN commissions, most notably her investigation into the conflict in Gaza, underscoring the role of legal frameworks in mitigating wartime violence.

## POLAND

* **Diplomatic Tensions over Greenland:** Tensions have surfaced following recent remarks from Donald Trump regarding Greenland, which have drawn a firm and defensive response from Copenhagen. The incident highlights ongoing concerns regarding Arctic sovereignty and US-European diplomatic relations.
* **Weather-Related Infrastructure Failure in Istanbul:** While reports focus on international developments, the flooding in Istanbul has served as a critical reminder of urban vulnerability to extreme climate events. The paralysis of the city's transport infrastructure underscores the growing challenge of climate adaptation for major global hubs, a topic of increasing relevance to policymakers in Warsaw and beyond.
