## WORLD

*   **Massive Russian Strike on Kyiv:** The Ukrainian capital faces a state of mourning following one of the most intense drone and missile attacks of the ongoing conflict. Authorities report at least 18 fatalities, marking a significant escalation as Russia attempts to counter recent Ukrainian strikes deep within its own territory.
*   **Geopolitical Instability in Iran:** As the nation prepares for the funeral of Ayatollah Ali Khamenei—who was killed during U.S.-Israeli strikes—the regime faces a critical test of survival. The international community is closely monitoring whether this transition will lead to a shift in Iran’s ideological stance or exacerbate regional tensions.

## POLAND

*   **Public Sentiment and Policy Shifts:** A recent IBRiS poll reveals a notable shift in Polish social attitudes, with over 33% of respondents reporting a decline in positive sentiment toward Ukrainians. This trend follows escalating historical debates regarding the UPA, which have begun to impact bilateral political relations.
*   **Weather Extremes:** Large swaths of Poland have been struck by violent storms, resulting in widespread infrastructure damage and hundreds of emergency interventions, particularly in the Świętokrzyskie region. The intensity of these weather events has reignited domestic discussions on emergency preparedness and climate resilience.
