## WORLD

* **Sudden Death of US Senator Lindsey Graham:** The political landscape in Washington is reeling following the death of Senator Lindsey Graham, a prominent voice in foreign policy and a key ally of Donald Trump, after a sudden illness. His passing comes shortly after a high-profile diplomatic visit to Kyiv, where he met with President Volodymyr Zelensky. The loss of such an influential figure, particularly given his involvement in international relations, is expected to have significant implications for US legislative priorities.
* **Escalating Hard-Line Stance in Iran:** Reports indicate that conservative factions in Iran, emboldened by a shifting leadership vacuum, are intensifying their rhetoric and military posturing against the United States. This surge in hard-line influence threatens to further destabilize the region as internal groups seek to assert control over the country's geopolitical trajectory.
* **Cruise Ship Refused Entry in Middle East:** The *Scarlet Lady*, carrying 2,000 passengers, has been denied entry into Egyptian ports following a similar rejection by Turkey. The incident underscores growing regional tensions regarding the cruise, which is centered on LGBTQ+ travel, highlighting the widening gap between international tourism norms and local social policies.

## POLAND

* **Political Reaction to Senator Graham’s Passing:** Polish authorities, led by President Zelensky’s recognition of the event, have expressed condolences following the death of Senator Lindsey Graham. Given his active role in supporting Ukraine, his passing is being closely monitored in Warsaw as a development affecting the security architecture of the Eastern Flank.
* **Focus on National Defense:** Polish Deputy Prime Minister Władysław Kosiniak-Kamysz has reinforced the government’s commitment to military modernization, stating that deterrence remains the primary path to preventing conflict. This reaffirms Poland's ongoing strategic shift toward rapid rearmament amidst the current geopolitical climate.
* **Security Advisory Update:** The Polish Ministry of Foreign Affairs has issued the highest level of travel warnings for specific regions, urging citizens to avoid travel due to deteriorating security conditions and escalating risks in the affected areas.
