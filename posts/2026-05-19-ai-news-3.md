## WORLD

* **San Diego Mosque Shooting:** A tragic hate-crime incident at the Islamic Center of San Diego has resulted in five fatalities, including two teenage suspects. Authorities are investigating the attack, which involved suspects aged 17 and 18, after a note containing "generalized hate rhetoric" was discovered at the scene.
* **Trump’s Iran Policy Shift:** U.S. President Donald Trump has officially called off a planned military strike on Iran. According to the President, the decision followed assurances from leaders of three Middle Eastern nations regarding a new agreement that aims to prevent Iran from developing nuclear capabilities.

## POLAND

* **Diplomatic Controversy Over Ziobro's U.S. Entry:** Reports from Reuters indicate that former Polish Minister of Justice, Zbigniew Ziobro, received significant assistance from high-ranking U.S. officials, including former Ambassador Christopher Landau, to secure a journalistic visa for entry into the United States. The disclosure has sparked questions regarding the nature of the diplomatic intervention involved in facilitating the high-profile politician's travel.
