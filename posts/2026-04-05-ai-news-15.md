## WORLD

* **US-Iran Tensions Reach Breaking Point:** Following a high-risk rescue operation of a downed American airman in Iran, President Donald Trump has issued severe ultimatums to Tehran. The U.S. President threatened to destroy critical Iranian infrastructure—specifically power plants and bridges—if the Strait of Hormuz is not reopened to international traffic. Both nations have claimed strategic victories, significantly increasing the risk of further military escalation in the region.
* **Papal Easter Appeal:** In his first Easter address as pontiff, Pope Leo XIV used the global platform to urge world leaders to prioritize peace. His message comes at a time of heightened geopolitical instability, calling for diplomatic restraint amidst rising international conflicts.

## POLAND

* **Police Shooting in Ostrów Mazowiecka:** A tragic incident occurred at a hospital in Ostrów Mazowiecka, where a 51-year-old detainee was fatally shot by police. The individual was attempting to escape from medical supervision when the officer opened fire after the suspect failed to comply with orders to halt.
* **Massive Industrial Fire in Oława:** Emergency services, involving 20 fire units, were deployed to Oława following simultaneous fires at a scrap yard and a nearby furniture factory. The incident resulted in significant air pollution, with the town engulfed in thick smoke, though authorities have reported no casualties.
* **Political Allegations Regarding Energy Infrastructure:** Tensions have surfaced in Central Europe as Hungarian opposition leader Peter Magyar suggested that the discovery of explosives near the Hungary-Serbia gas pipeline could be a "false flag" operation, warning against government-led panic strategies.
