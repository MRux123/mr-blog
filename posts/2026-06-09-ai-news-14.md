## WORLD

* **Escalation in the Middle East:** Israel has initiated a new wave of strikes in Southern Lebanon, further complicating the precarious regional security landscape. These actions occur amidst ongoing U.S.-Iran diplomatic efforts, with the Israeli campaign against Hezbollah remaining a significant barrier to de-escalation.
* **Drone Warfare Overspill:** The ongoing conflict between Russia and Ukraine has reached a new level of complexity as stray drones frequently drift into the airspace of neighboring neutral countries. This phenomenon is heightening regional anxiety and forcing local populations to seek emergency shelter as the kinetic theater of war expands geographically.
* **Protest Violence in Kenya:** A protest in Nanyuki, central Kenya, against the proposed establishment of a U.S. Ebola quarantine facility, has turned violent. Reports indicate that a demonstrator was shot during clashes with police, underscoring deep-seated local opposition to the facility's location.

## POLAND

* **Zełenski Signals Potential Turning Point:** During his visit to Tallinn, Ukrainian President Volodymyr Zełenski indicated that the months of June and July could be decisive for the course of the war. He highlighted the strategic success of Ukrainian drone strikes on Russian logistics and energy infrastructure as a catalyst for potential diplomatic negotiations.
* **Tusk Addresses Historical Sensitivities:** Prime Minister Donald Tusk has publicly criticized the Ukrainian decision to name a military unit after UPA figures. In a transparent move to balance strategic cooperation with domestic sensibilities, Tusk emphasized the need for "historical common sense," stating that he has engaged in long discussions with President Zełenski regarding the varying national perceptions of sensitive historical issues.
* **Coalition Dynamics in Kraków:** The governing coalition (KO and PSL) has officially nominated Senator Monika Piątkowska as their joint candidate for the mayoral election in Kraków. This move signals a consolidated effort by the ruling parties to secure control over the major urban center, underscoring their commitment to electoral unity.
