## WORLD

*   **US Defense Strategy in Asia:** US Secretary of Defense Hegseth has reaffirmed the United States' long-term commitment to its Asian allies during a security summit in Singapore. While emphasizing that the US is "not turning back" from the region, Hegseth clearly signaled that Washington expects regional partners to significantly ramp up their own defense expenditures and capabilities in response to evolving regional threats.
*   **German Military Presence in Lithuania:** In a notable historical shift, German troops have been deployed to Lithuania to bolster NATO’s eastern flank. The mission, aimed at deterring potential Russian aggression, marks a significant change in regional security dynamics; unlike historical occupations, this presence is welcomed by the Lithuanian government as a vital security guarantee.

## POLAND

*   **Diplomatic Tensions Over Order of the White Eagle:** A diplomatic rift between Poland and Ukraine has deepened following the decision by the Head of the National Security Bureau (BBN) to stand by the move to strip President Volodymyr Zelensky of the Order of the White Eagle. The tension stems from the naming of a Ukrainian military formation after UPA figures, a move perceived by Polish officials as a grave provocation. BBN representatives have suggested that a formal apology or admission of error from the Ukrainian leadership is required to de-escalate the situation.
*   **Escalation in Ukraine and Regional Impact:** Following intense Russian aerial assaults—involving nearly 300 drones and multiple missiles—Poland remains on high alert. While the majority of the projectiles were intercepted, the continued scale of these attacks remains a primary security concern for the region, directly impacting Polish internal policy and defense considerations.
