## WORLD

* **Heightened US-Iran Conflict:** The geopolitical situation in the Middle East has reached a critical boiling point. President Trump has confirmed further military strikes against Iranian infrastructure, including critical bridges and power plants, vowing to push the regime toward collapse. This follows the destruction of a major highway bridge near Tehran and a significant public health facility.
* **Military Confrontation:** Tensions are further exacerbated by reports from Iran claiming the downing of a second American F-35 fighter jet over the Markazi province, signaling an intensifying direct military confrontation between the two nations.
* **Moldova’s Geopolitical Pivot:** In a significant regional shift, the Moldovan Parliament has formally voted to withdraw from the Commonwealth of Independent States (CIS). This move marks a decisive step in the country's strategic alignment with the European Union and a complete departure from the post-Soviet sphere of influence led by Russia.

## POLAND

* **Political Uncertainty Regarding NATO:** The Polish Ministry of Foreign Affairs, represented by Marcin Bosacki, has clarified that there are no official government assessments predicting a potential US withdrawal from NATO. However, the ministry acknowledged that President Trump's unpredictable rhetoric necessitates a robust, independent strategic evaluation by European allies.
* **Constitutional Crisis Concerns:** Human Rights Ombudsman (RPO) Prof. Marcin Wiącek has intervened in the ongoing debate regarding the swearing-in of newly appointed Constitutional Tribunal judges, emphasizing that the process must strictly adhere to the constitutional authority granted solely to the Sejm.
* **Domestic Safety Incidents:** Public order remains a concern following a series of violent incidents, including a brutal assault in Warsaw and a serious tram derailment in Łódź, both of which have drawn significant attention to urban security and infrastructure safety protocols.
