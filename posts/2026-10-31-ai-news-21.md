## WORLD

*   **Humanitarian Toll in Ukraine:** Civilians fleeing the long-occupied front-line city of Oleshky describe harrowing survival stories, recounting desperate journeys across minefields after years of living in a conflict zone. The accounts shed light on the ongoing humanitarian crisis in occupied territories.
*   **Civil Unrest in France:** Large-scale protests continue across France for the third consecutive weekend. Despite a reported dip in attendance, 320,000 citizens mobilized nationwide to voice opposition against the rising cost of living and declining quality of public services, with fresh reports of clashes between demonstrators and police in Paris.

## POLAND

*   **Constitutional Court and Judicial Crisis:** The domestic political landscape remains tense as a standoff develops around the Constitutional Tribunal (TK). Prime Minister Donald Tusk, in a televised address, maintained that the government would not compromise on its efforts to restore the rule of law, while opposition figures accused the administration of utilizing illegal methods to seize control of the judicial body.
*   **Supreme Court's Emergency Response:** In reaction to the escalating constitutional crisis, the First President of the Supreme Court, Zbigniew Kapiński, has convened an extraordinary General Assembly of Judges. The session is set to address perceived threats to the constitutional order and the independence of the judiciary, marking a significant escalation in the institutional friction between state branches.
