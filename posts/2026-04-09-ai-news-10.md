## WORLD

* **Middle East Stability in Peril:** The fragile U.S.-brokered cease-fire between Iran and Israel faces a potential collapse following major Israeli strikes in Lebanon. With a death toll exceeding 182, the international community is debating the scope of existing de-escalation agreements, casting doubt on American diplomatic efficacy in the region.
* **Ukrainian Defense Evolution:** Ukraine’s private sector is rapidly pivoting toward high-tech warfare, with former pet toy manufacturers now producing sophisticated "killer drones." This shift underscores the country’s industrial transformation into a defense powerhouse capable of indigenous, final-strike precision.

## POLAND

* **Coalition Tensions Rise:** Internal government friction has intensified following disagreements between the Ministry of National Education and Poland 2050. Minister Barbara Nowacka faces harsh public criticism from Katarzyna Pełczyńska-Nałęcz regarding health education reforms, highlighting a lack of communication within the governing coalition.
* **Border Security Alert:** The Polish Air Force scrambled F-16 fighter jets to intercept a Russian aircraft detected maneuvering over the Baltic Sea. The incident underscores the continued readiness of Polish forces amid elevated tensions along NATO's eastern flank.
* **Criminal Investigation:** Authorities have detained five individuals in connection with the disappearance of 23-year-old Adrian P. from Ciechanów. Investigations are currently underway to confirm if human remains discovered in the vicinity are linked to the ongoing kidnapping case.
