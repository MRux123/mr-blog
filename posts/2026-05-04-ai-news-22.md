## WORLD

* **Trump’s Firm Stance on Iran:** Former President Donald Trump has issued a stark warning to Tehran, stating that Iran would be "wiped off the face of the Earth" should they strike U.S. naval vessels involved in the "Freedom Project" operations. Simultaneously, the U.S. is bolstering its military presence in the Middle East, while Trump claims that diplomatic pressure is forcing Iran toward more flexible negotiations.
* **Czech Political Corruption Scandals:** The Czech Republic is facing significant internal turmoil as high-ranking officials face legal scrutiny. Former Justice Minister Pavel Blažek is under investigation regarding a suspicious cryptocurrency donation case involving potential illicit funds. Furthermore, the conviction of Jana Nagyova—a former associate of ex-Prime Minister Andrej Babiš—for EU subsidy fraud highlights ongoing efforts to root out systemic corruption within the country’s elite.

## POLAND

* **Ukraine Conflict and Security Concerns:** President Volodymyr Zelenskyy has signaled a shift in focus toward the northern front, explicitly rejecting Russian claims regarding a potential ceasefire. This development underscores the persistent instability in the region and suggests that a diplomatic resolution remains elusive.
* **Geopolitical Vulnerability:** Prof. Andrzej Nowak, a social advisor to the Polish President, has issued a sobering warning regarding Poland’s foreign policy, specifically concerning Germany’s historical tendency to negotiate with Russia by bypassing Polish interests. Nowak emphasized that the continued presence of U.S. troops remains an essential, non-negotiable security guarantee for the nation's sovereignty.
