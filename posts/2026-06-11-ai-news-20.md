## WORLD

*   **US-Iran Diplomatic Ambiguity:** President Trump has claimed a breakthrough in peace negotiations with Iran, stating that a deal is imminent and citing the cancellation of a planned retaliatory strike. However, the situation remains volatile as Tehran has officially denied that any formal agreement has been reached, casting doubt on the stability of the de-escalation.
*   **Violence Overshadows World Cup Kick-off:** While the 2026 World Cup has officially commenced in Mexico with a vibrant opening ceremony, the event is being marred by local instability. Reports confirm a deadly attack on police in the state of Michoacán by the Jalisco New Generation Cartel just a day before the inaugural match, highlighting the security challenges facing the tournament hosts.
*   **Escalating Sudan Crisis:** Rights groups have reported a devastating drone strike targeting a funeral procession in the city of el-Obeid. The Rapid Support Forces (RSF) paramilitary group has been accused of civilian killings as the civil war continues to intensify on the front lines.

## POLAND

*   **Political Gridlock Over Cryptocurrency Regulation:** The Polish government, led by Minister of Finance and Economy Andrzej Domański and Deputy Prime Minister Krzysztof Gawkowski, is signaling a continued push for cryptocurrency oversight despite a series of presidential vetoes. The administration has explicitly criticized President Karol Nawrocki’s latest intervention, leading to sharp exchanges between the executive branch and the President's office.
*   **Expansion of National Minority Recognition:** In a significant domestic shift, President Karol Nawrocki has signed legislation formally recognizing the Greek community as the tenth protected national minority in Poland. This move follows years of advocacy by the local Greek diaspora.
*   **Public Health Alert:** Polish authorities have issued a warning regarding the presence of high concentrations of acrylamide—a potentially carcinogenic chemical compound—in specific batches of "Petit Beurre" biscuits currently on the market, prompting a swift reaction from sanitary inspectors.
