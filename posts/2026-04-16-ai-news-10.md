## WORLD

*   **Major Russian Offensive in Ukraine:** Moscow has launched one of its most significant aerial assaults in recent months, with strikes resulting in at least 15 fatalities. The attack effectively signals the end of any potential for a lasting ceasefire around the Orthodox Easter period, as Russia intensifies its targeting of civilian infrastructure.
*   **Iran-Pakistan Diplomacy Amid Conflict:** Amid the ongoing regional conflict involving Iran, Pakistan has engaged in shuttle diplomacy. A delegation is currently in Tehran to facilitate communication, as the international community watches closely for the potential for a second round of U.S.-Iran talks.
*   **Political Shift in Hungary:** The defeat of Prime Minister Viktor Orbán marks a pivotal moment for European politics. While his exit punctures the momentum of the far-right movement in Europe, political analysts are closely observing whether his successors can learn from the corruption and economic mismanagement that ultimately led to his downfall.

## POLAND

*   **Russian Threats Against Polish Assets:** The Russian Ministry of Defense has explicitly identified specific Polish firms as potential targets, citing their support for the Ukrainian defense industry. This escalation, highlighted by officials such as Dmitry Medvedev, raises serious security concerns regarding the safety of Polish industrial infrastructure.
*   **Counter-Espionage Investigation:** The Internal Security Agency (ABW) has apprehended a soldier from the Territorial Defense Forces (WOT) on charges of espionage. While the court has not yet granted a motion for pre-trial detention, the military has dismissed the individual, and the prosecutor’s office continues its investigation into the breach.
*   **Internal Political Stability:** Despite internal dissent, Law and Justice (PiS) leadership has decided against penalizing members who joined Mateusz Morawiecki’s new association. Jaroslav Kaczyński’s decision to avoid mass suspensions points to a strategic effort to maintain party cohesion amidst growing pressure and shifting political alliances.
