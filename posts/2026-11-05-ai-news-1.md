## WORLD

* **Trump Signals Strategic Pivot:** Following significant losses in the Congressional elections, Donald Trump has signaled a major shift in his political rhetoric. The former president expressed a newfound willingness to cooperate with Democrats on key legislative issues, particularly regarding fiscal policy, distancing himself from his previous vow to act as a "veto-wielding" obstacle.
* **Political Transition in Israel:** President Isaac Herzog has initiated formal consultations with party leaders to determine the future of the Israeli government. The deliberations will decide whether Prime Minister Benjamin Netanyahu or his political rivals will be granted the initial mandate to attempt the formation of a stable coalition.
* **Health Emergency in DRC:** A British healthcare worker diagnosed with Ebola in the Democratic Republic of the Congo is currently being evacuated to the UK. The patient is reported to be in stable condition and will be transferred to a high-consequence infectious disease unit under established government protocols.

## POLAND

* **Economic and Security Updates:** International financial observers are noting a significant trend as Russia accelerates the sell-off of its gold reserves on an unprecedented scale. Meanwhile, in Poland, government officials are addressing public concerns regarding the retail prices of heating pellets, following a noted downward trend in wholesale costs.
* **Political Clashes and Legislative Tensions:** Parliamentary discourse has turned confrontational, with MP Łukasz Kmita using a symbolic prop—an abacus—during a televised debate to criticize the current government's legislative processes. This follows recent controversy surrounding the Constitutional Tribunal, where disputes over the legitimacy of quorum requirements continue to fuel domestic political polarization.
