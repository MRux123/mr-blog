## WORLD

*   **Diplomatic Fallout over Ukraine's Libya Operation:** Tensions have flared between Kyiv and Rome following reports of Ukrainian naval operations against Russian vessels in Libyan waters. Italian Prime Minister Giorgia Meloni expressed frustration, alleging that the action was carried out without prior coordination or notification to Italian partners, leading to a urgent diplomatic dialogue between the two leaders.
*   **Escalating Rhetoric between Russia and Latvia:** The Russian Foreign Ministry, represented by Maria Zakharova, has issued a sharp warning to Latvia, accusing the Baltic nation of provoking an "open conflict." Moscow has indicated that it reserves the right to take decisive political and diplomatic countermeasures, heightening security concerns in the region.
*   **Political Climax in Spain:** Prime Minister Pedro Sánchez is making a final, high-stakes push to secure support from voters. As the campaign concludes, Sánchez faces intense scrutiny and accusations of "betrayal" from his primary political rivals, reflecting a deeply polarized electoral climate.

## POLAND

*   **Bundestag Rejects Compensation for Polish Victims:** In a move that has sparked widespread disappointment in Poland, the German Bundestag has opted not to include funds for reparations in the 2027 budget. The decision affects thousands of elderly survivors of the German occupation, with the Foundation for Polish-German Reconciliation emphasizing the urgent need for humanitarian aid for the remaining 30,000 to 50,000 victims.
*   **Legislative Gridlock Over Tax Reforms:** President Karol Nawrocki’s recent veto of proposed amendments to PIT and CIT taxes has triggered political debate. Ryszard Petru, representing the Centrum parliamentary club, publicly supported the veto, criticizing the government's proposed mix of tax threshold changes and corporate tax hikes as fundamentally flawed.
*   **Sports Achievement Overshadows Loss:** Despite a defeat in their match against Croatia, the Polish national basketball team is celebrating a historic achievement, having secured their qualification for the World Cup in Qatar.
