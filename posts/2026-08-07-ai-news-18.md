## WORLD

*   **Russian Expansionist Signals:** NATO member states are expressing growing concern over potential Russian moves toward further territorial annexation, closely monitoring military signals in the region.
*   **Smuggling Network Dismantled in Spain:** Spanish authorities have successfully dismantled one of the largest human and drug trafficking networks in the region. The group operated a sophisticated logistics chain moving synthetic drugs to Algeria, while exploiting the return routes to smuggle migrants and weapons into Europe.
*   **Trump Administration Limits TPS:** A US federal judge has ruled in favor of the Trump administration’s efforts to terminate Temporary Protected Status (TPS) for hundreds of South Sudanese nationals, marking another shift in U.S. immigration policy following similar rulings regarding Haitian and Syrian citizens.

## POLAND

*   **Crisis in Public Healthcare:** Reports highlighting extreme irregularities in Hospital Emergency Departments (SOR) have sparked public outrage. Investigations reveal systemic issues including astronomical salary payouts for management and medical staff working illegal, multi-day shifts that jeopardize patient safety.
*   **Orlen Corruption Investigation:** The ongoing investigation into massive financial losses at the state-controlled energy giant, Orlen, has intensified. Former executives now face potential prison sentences of up to 25 years as prosecutors document the scale of the alleged mismanagement.
*   **Tuberculosis Outbreak in Warsaw:** Health authorities have confirmed a tuberculosis outbreak at a kindergarten in Warsaw’s Białołęka district. Both a staff member and a child have been diagnosed with the disease, triggering mandatory sanitary procedures and health monitoring for the rest of the facility.
