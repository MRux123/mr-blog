## WORLD

*   **Geopolitical Uncertainty in Iran:** The conspicuous absence of Mojtaba Khamenei from his father’s funeral has intensified international speculation regarding the state of Iran's leadership succession and the potential internal power struggle within the country’s top hierarchy.
*   **Escalation on the Ukrainian Front:** Heavy combat continues in the eastern Donetsk region. Despite Russian claims of territorial gains, Ukrainian military forces maintain that they are successfully holding defensive positions against ongoing infiltration attempts, underscoring the relentless attrition on the front lines.

## POLAND

*   **Healthcare Reform Initiatives:** The Polish Presidential office has announced the preparation of legislative projects aimed at structural changes within the national healthcare system, signaling a potential shift in domestic health policy.
*   **Environmental Crisis on the Bóbr River:** Following the discovery of mass fish die-offs, the government and energy producer Tauron have initiated an "environmental roundtable" to address the ecological catastrophe and implement mitigation strategies for the river's recovery.
*   **Political Debate on Aid to Ukraine:** A shift in the national discourse has emerged, with high-profile political figures, including Mariusz Błaszczak, openly questioning the current scope and sustainability of Poland’s support for Ukraine, highlighting a growing domestic debate on foreign policy priorities.
