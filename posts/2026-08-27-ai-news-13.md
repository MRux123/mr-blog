## WORLD

*   **Himalayan Flood Disaster:** A catastrophic flood, likely triggered by a collapsed glacier, has devastated the border region between Nepal and Tibet. Rescue operations are currently underway as authorities confirm hundreds of fatalities, with over 1,000 people still reported missing. Experts point to rapidly melting Himalayan ice as the primary environmental stressor behind this tragedy.
*   **Escalating Tension in the Red Sea:** Analysts warn that intensifying Houthi attacks on Saudi targets have pushed the kingdom toward the brink of a full-scale military conflict. This development risks opening a volatile new front in the broader regional war involving Iran-backed militias.
*   **Civilian Infiltration in Ukraine:** Despite the ongoing use of advanced drone surveillance, the war in Ukraine has devolved into a high-stakes "cat and mouse" game. Russian forces are reportedly attempting to infiltrate the reservoir basin of a destroyed dam, leading to intense close-quarters combat with Ukrainian defenders.

## POLAND

*   **Romanowski Legal Saga Continues:** The District Court in Warsaw has upheld the European Arrest Warrant (EAW) concerning Marcin Romanowski, rejecting a motion to repeal it. The court also declined to proceed with a request to summon Prime Minister Donald Tusk for testimony, marking a significant moment in the ongoing political and judicial friction.
*   **Prosecution of Extremist Rhetoric:** The Institute of National Remembrance (IPN) has initiated a new investigation into statements made by European Parliament member Grzegorz Braun. The inquiry centers on remarks made in Krakow that questioned the historical existence of gas chambers at Auschwitz, adding to existing charges of denying Nazi crimes.
*   **Controversial Prosecution of Nationalist Leader:** The Court of Appeal in Gdansk has resumed proceedings against Wojciech Olszański, known as "Jaszczur." The case concerns charges of inciting ethnic and national hatred, as well as accusations of physical assault motivated by racial prejudice, highlighting the state's firm stance against extremist provocations.
