## WORLD

* **Brazil Moves to Reduce Bolsonaro’s Sentence:** The Brazilian Congress has approved a legislative plan that would significantly shorten the jail term of former President Jair Bolsonaro. Bolsonaro was previously sentenced to 27 years for his role in orchestrating a coup following his 2022 election defeat. This development signals a potential shift in the country's political landscape and the legal consequences faced by the former leader.
* **Geopolitical Realignment in Lebanon:** As the cease-fire remains fragile and Israeli military operations in southern Lebanon continue, public sentiment is shifting. Many residents who previously expressed frustration with Hezbollah are increasingly turning to the group for security and protection, illustrating the complex and volatile nature of the ongoing conflict.
* **China Targets Western Influence on Youth Culture:** The Chinese Ministry of State Security has accused "hostile foreign forces" of orchestrating the "lying flat" (*tang ping*) movement. Beijing views the youth-led trend—which rejects the intense pressure of the country's rigorous "996" work culture—as a deliberate subversion of national productivity, marking another front in the state’s effort to manage social behavior.

## POLAND

* **Polish Apples Eye Brazilian Market:** Through breakthroughs in trade negotiations with Mercosur countries, Polish producers of "Grójec apples" are poised to enter the Brazilian market. Supported by the European Commission, this expansion marks the culmination of a decade-long effort to secure access for Polish agricultural exports to South America.
* **Political Fallout in the Right-Wing Bloc:** Janusz Kowalski has officially departed from the Law and Justice (PiS) party, citing deep-seated dissatisfaction with local party structures. Kowalski, a vocal critic of the party's central leadership, claimed that the tolerance of local "power cliques" made his continued membership untenable, signaling potential instability within the conservative opposition.
* **Judicial Appeal in Tragic Tram Accident:** The prosecutor's office has filed an appeal following the sentencing of a tram driver involved in a fatal accident in Warsaw. The initial ruling of 18 months of imprisonment for the incident, which resulted in the death of a four-year-old child, is being contested, highlighting ongoing public debate regarding professional liability and safety standards in public transport.
