## WORLD

*   **Historic US-Iran Diplomatic Engagement:** A potential face-to-face summit between US officials and Mohammad Bagher Ghalibaf is being viewed as the most significant diplomatic development since the 1979 revolution. Analysts suggest that these talks are a high-stakes attempt to bridge decades of deep-seated distrust, though outcomes remain uncertain.
*   **Hungary on the Brink of Political Change:** The opposition movement, led by Péter Magyar, is challenging the long-standing dominance of Viktor Orbán’s Fidesz party. With tens of thousands of citizens rallying in Budapest and polls showing a shift in momentum, the upcoming parliamentary election appears to be the most competitive in recent history.
*   **Artemis II Mission Conclusion:** International space exploration marks a milestone as the Artemis II crew, consisting of three American and one Canadian astronaut, prepares for re-entry and splashdown in the Pacific, effectively concluding a historic lunar-vicinity mission.

## POLAND

*   **Political Polarization Over Election Integrity:** Tensions in Hungary have reverberated into political discourse as the ruling Fidesz party establishes a "Democracy Center." The body, tasked with monitoring the election, has drawn sharp criticism and allegations of intimidation from the opposition, further escalating the atmosphere of distrust ahead of the vote.
*   **16th Anniversary of the Smoleńsk Tragedy:** Marking the 16th anniversary of the 2010 disaster, Jarosław Kaczyński used the annual Marsz Pamięci (March of Remembrance) to reiterate his call for the investigation to be treated as a targeted attack. His rhetoric continues to be a focal point of intense debate regarding the historical and political narrative surrounding the crash.
