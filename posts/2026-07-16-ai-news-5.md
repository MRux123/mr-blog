## WORLD

*   **Heightened Tensions in the Middle East:** Military hostilities have intensified in the Persian Gulf, with the United States launching a fresh wave of strikes against Iranian targets. The operation specifically targeted infrastructure capable of disrupting maritime traffic in the Strait of Hormuz. In a separate engagement, US forces opened fire on a tanker attempting to breach a blockade of Iranian ports, marking a significant escalation in regional stability.
*   **Moroccan Intelligence Surveillance Scandal:** A major security breach has been exposed regarding the use of Pegasus spyware. An insider from the Moroccan domestic intelligence agency confirmed that the state has been deploying the intrusive hacking software since 2017 to monitor journalists, human rights activists, and high-ranking foreign officials, including cabinet ministers and police officers from France and Spain.

## POLAND

*   **Governmental Shake-up in Ukraine Affects Polish Regional Stability:** The Ukrainian government has initiated a significant cabinet reshuffle, with President Zelensky dismissing a key minister due to deepening conflicts with the military leadership. Given the ongoing war, this development is being closely monitored by Polish officials as it signals potential instability within the Ukrainian command structure.
*   **Public Sentiment on Ukraine Assistance:** Despite geopolitical tensions, a new IBRiS poll reveals that a majority of Poles continue to support ongoing military aid for Ukraine. The data highlights a distinct ideological divide: support is strongest among voters of the current governing coalition, while opposition toward the assistance is primarily concentrated among supporters of the PiS and Confederation parties.
