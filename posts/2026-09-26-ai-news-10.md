## WORLD

* **US-China Geopolitical Maneuvering:** Recent high-level discussions between President Trump and Xi Jinping underscore an intensifying struggle for influence in Asia. The Chinese leadership is actively challenging the established US security framework concerning Taiwan and Japan, employing a combination of diplomatic pressure and historical narratives to reshape regional alliances.
* **Escalating Conflict in Eastern Europe:** Military volatility persists as cross-border drone strikes between Russia and Ukraine continue to target critical energy infrastructure, including the Ilsk refinery. Simultaneously, the humanitarian and financial strain on Ukraine grows, with President Zelenskyy urging the European Commission to expedite a 90-billion-euro loan package to address critical defense budget deficits.

## POLAND

* **Defense Strategy Shift:** In a move signaling a major recalibration of national security, the PiS party has formally outlined a new strategic direction for Poland’s defense, emphasizing the mobilization and integration of hundreds of thousands of reservists into the national defense apparatus.
* **Diplomatic Dilemma:** The upcoming summit attendance has stirred domestic political debate, as reports emerge that both President Andrzej Duda and Russian leader Vladimir Putin have received invitations to the same international event, prompting questions about Poland's diplomatic stance on the global stage.
