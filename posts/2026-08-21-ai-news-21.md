## WORLD

* **Escalating Conflict in Ukraine:** A devastating "double-tap" drone strike on a shopping centre in Ukraine has resulted in at least 15 fatalities and over 130 injuries, including 23 children. This tactical strike on civilian infrastructure marks a grim intensification of the ongoing war.
* **Geopolitical Tensions Over Netanjahu:** Turkey has officially initiated legal proceedings against Israeli Prime Minister Benjamin Netanjahu, filing a formal request with Interpol for a "red notice." This move signifies a major diplomatic escalation regarding the conflict in Gaza and maritime aid missions.
* **Security Concerns in Europe:** A violent sword attack at a Swedish school has left one person dead and three others injured. An 18-year-old suspect is currently in custody following a police intervention.

## POLAND

* **Passing of a National Hero:** The nation mourns the death of Danuta Szlajmer, known by her nom de guerre "Siódemka." A legendary figure of the Warsaw Uprising, Szlajmer served as a combatant and nurse, notably fighting for the PAST-a building. Her death marks the loss of a significant witness to Polish history.
* **Political Fallout Over Government Resignation:** The sudden resignation of Barbara Mróz-Gorgoń, the government plenipotentiary for the promotion of the Polish brand, has triggered a wave of sharp criticism. Political opponents have labeled the administration’s handling of the subsequent public discourse as a display of arrogance.
* **Public Health Alert:** The Chief Sanitary Inspectorate (GIS) has issued an urgent warning regarding contaminated products sold in Lidl stores. A protein pudding was found to contain *Bacillus cereus*, a bacteria capable of causing severe food poisoning, prompting an immediate recall.
