## WORLD

*   **Global Microsoft Outage:** A massive, ongoing technical failure has severely impacted Microsoft services, including Teams, Outlook, and Copilot. While the company has ruled out a cyber-security incident, restoration efforts remain largely unsuccessful, causing widespread operational disruption for businesses and organizations globally.
*   **Escalation in Russia-Ukraine Diplomacy:** Tensions surrounding potential peace negotiations continue to evolve. Reports suggest that Russia has set stringent conditions for peace, including a demand for Finland to withdraw from NATO. Meanwhile, Europe is currently engaged in internal negotiations to determine who will represent the continent in upcoming discussions with Moscow, reflecting a fragmented diplomatic front.
*   **China’s Military Purge:** The appointment of Zhang Shengmin as a senior "military inquisitor" signals that the leadership purge within China’s People's Liberation Army is far from over. Analysts suggest this internal instability could complicate Beijing’s strategic planning and military posture regarding Taiwan.

## POLAND

*   **Increased Political Tensions:** The political climate has sharpened following the vandalism of the office of Deputy Minister of Development and Technology, Michał Jaros. The incident, involving slogans targeting TV Republika and the Prime Minister, has drawn condemnation and sparked a wider debate regarding the intensity of public discourse.
*   **Strategic Shifts in the Opposition:** Former Prime Minister Mateusz Morawiecki has issued a firm declaration regarding the future path of his political camp, emphasizing a move toward independent strategies ("Idziemy sami"). This signals a potential shift in the opposition's approach to coalition-building and electoral tactics ahead of future political cycles.
