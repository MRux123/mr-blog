## WORLD

*   **U.S. Escalation in the Middle East:** Following President Trump's order to reinstate a blockade on Iranian ports, U.S. forces have launched a new series of military strikes against Iran. The administration has further announced a 20% tariff on all cargo navigating the Strait of Hormuz, signaling a significant intensification of regional geopolitical instability.
*   **Political Upheaval in Hungary:** The Hungarian Parliament has voted to remove President Tamás Sulyok from office. The ousting of Sulyok, a key ally of former Prime Minister Viktor Orbán, marks a profound shift in the political landscape following the collapse of the Orbán administration after its 16-year tenure.
*   **NATO Reinforcement:** In a strategic move to bolster the Eastern Flank, Estonia has announced plans to construct a new military base in Narva. The facility, designed to house approximately 1,000 soldiers including NATO units, serves as a direct deterrent to Russian influence in the region.

## POLAND

*   **Diplomatic Stance on Ukraine:** A new "coalition of the willing" has issued a formal declaration emphasizing that any future peace process regarding the conflict in Ukraine must be conducted exclusively with the full consent and participation of Kyiv, rejecting any unilateral external settlements.
*   **Tragedy in the Tatra Mountains:** Rescue services have confirmed the death of a Polish tourist who tragically fell 200 meters while traversing the Slovak side of the Tatra Mountains. The incident has once again highlighted the dangers of high-altitude mountaineering in the region.
