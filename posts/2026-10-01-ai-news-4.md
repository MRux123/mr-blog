## WORLD

* **Mid-Air Incident Over Israel:** Security concerns are mounting following a violent confrontation aboard a Flydubai flight en route to Tel Aviv. Israeli Prime Minister Benjamin Netanyahu confirmed that one of the pilots is currently under investigation for stabbing a fellow crew member, an act that nearly caused a crash. The motive remains unknown, and the airline has temporarily suspended all flights to and from Israel as authorities probe the security breach.
* **US Strategic Realignment:** The Trump administration has officially commenced the withdrawal of US troops from Iraq, marking the end of a long-standing military presence. President Trump characterized the move as a long-overdue exit from a failed engagement, signaling a shift in American foreign policy as the administration prioritizes domestic interests and regional stability.
* **Russia’s Continued Aggression:** International observers report that Vladimir Putin shows no signs of de-escalating the war in Ukraine. Russia continues to intensify its military efforts, with analysts suggesting that the invasion will remain a full-scale operation for the foreseeable future, despite growing concerns regarding regional security in Europe.

## POLAND

* **Uncertainty Over US Military Presence:** Amidst the global restructuring of American forces, questions have arisen regarding the status of US troops currently stationed in Poland. With Washington calling forces back home, Polish officials and defense experts are closely monitoring the potential impact on regional deterrence strategies and the broader NATO eastern flank security.
* **Security Scare in Nowa Dęba:** A security alert was triggered at a military unit in Nowa Dęba following the receipt of a suspicious package addressed to the Military Gendarmerie. While the facility was briefly placed on high alert, official reports later confirmed that laboratory tests excluded the presence of any biological, chemical, or radiological threats, allowing operations to return to normal.
