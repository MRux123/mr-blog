## WORLD

* **Rising Civilian Casualties in Ukraine-Russia Conflict:** As the intensity of Ukrainian air strikes increases, the humanitarian toll inside Russia is mounting. While these attacks have sparked localized discontent, analysts remain skeptical regarding whether the heightened domestic pressure will effectively influence the Kremlin’s strategic approach to the ongoing war.
* **Devastating Earthquake Strikes Colombia:** A powerful 7.4-magnitude earthquake has struck Colombia, resulting in at least 100 fatalities and widespread structural damage. It stands as the strongest seismic event in the region in over a decade, with emergency services currently engaged in major recovery efforts.

## POLAND

* **Legislative Action on 'Clean Air' Program Fraud:** The Polish government has formally adopted a draft bill aimed at providing support to citizens who fell victim to fraudulent practices within the "Clean Air" (Czyste Powietrze) subsidy program. The legislation seeks to address financial losses and restore public trust in environmental support schemes.
* **Debate Over Potential Healthcare Privatization:** Growing public discourse regarding the future of the Polish healthcare system has prompted a formal inquiry into the potential privatization of services. The head of the National Health Fund (NFZ) is expected to address these concerns amid broader systemic challenges.
* **EU Funding Dispute Over Agricultural Drought Losses:** The European Commission has signaled that no specific funding is currently available for drought-related agricultural losses. The Polish government is actively lobbying for structural changes to EU policy to better support farmers affected by increasing climate-related disasters.
