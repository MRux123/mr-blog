## WORLD

*   **Aleksandar Vučić Resigns Presidency:** In a major political maneuver, Serbian President Aleksandar Vučić has announced his resignation. The move is widely interpreted as a strategic transition intended to position him for the office of Prime Minister following next month’s elections, signaling a potential shift in the country's governance structure.
*   **Canadian Leadership Insights:** Prime Minister Mark Carney has participated in an in-depth interview with The New York Times, addressing critical policy issues and the current direction of his administration. The transcript provides a rare, candid look into the priorities of the Canadian government amid ongoing international challenges.

## POLAND

*   **Krakow Mayoral Election Results:** Citizens of Krakow are awaiting final confirmation as late poll data and initial PKW results begin to surface. The election marks a pivotal moment for the city’s administration, with high public interest in the transition of local leadership.
*   **Shifting Voter Dynamics:** Data from the mayoral race highlights a significant trend among Confederation party supporters. Exit polls indicate that a majority of those who traditionally back the right-wing party chose to support Łukasz Gibała, demonstrating a pragmatic shift in voting behavior despite the absence of an official party candidate.
