## WORLD

* **Middle East Diplomacy in Flux:** Jared Kushner is set to visit Israel for high-stakes talks regarding Gaza. The mission comes as the U.S.-backed peace deal faces significant hurdles, with Israeli Prime Minister Benjamin Netanyahu openly rejecting proposals to disarm Hamas, further complicating regional stability.
* **Europe’s Environmental and Social Crises:** Southern Europe is once again battling extreme heatwaves and devastating wildfires, testing the limits of regional infrastructure. Simultaneously, the border crisis in Ceuta is being exacerbated by social media networks, where Instagram accounts are actively promoting and monetizing dangerous migration attempts, drawing increased scrutiny from international observers.

## POLAND

* **Political Landscape Shift:** A new survey by the Ogólnopolska Grupa Badawcza reveals a potential major shift in the Polish parliament. While the Civic Coalition maintains a lead at 33.53%, the combined support for right-wing factions—including PiS, Konfederacja, and others—suggests they could secure a governing majority, creating a significant challenge for the current coalition government.
* **Security and Infrastructure Developments:** Poland continues to face regional security concerns, evidenced by unauthorized drone activity and heightened defense awareness. Domestically, the capital is bracing for major disruptions as preparations for a significant military parade on Warsaw's Wisłostrada arterial road have forced large-scale traffic restrictions, affecting key transit hubs across the city.
