## WORLD

* **Escalation in Ukraine:** President Zelensky has denounced Russia’s "Easter escalation," confirming that a massive wave of drone and missile strikes has resulted in the deaths of six civilians and left 40 others injured. This latest surge in violence marks a significant intensification of hostilities during a period that typically prompts calls for a ceasefire.
* **Geopolitical Foreign Policy Shifts:** The New York Times reports that the U.S. administration is increasingly integrating deportation strategies into its core foreign policy, seeking agreements with international autocrats. This approach highlights a broader trend of leveraging domestic political agendas on the global diplomatic stage.
* **Shipping Risks in the Middle East:** European powers are currently evaluating high-risk options for securing the Strait of Hormuz. Despite post-war planning efforts, analysts remain skeptical about the long-term feasibility and safety of these initiatives in such a volatile maritime corridor.

## POLAND

* **Severe Weather Warnings:** The Institute of Meteorology and Water Management (IMGW) has issued level one alerts for 10 provinces. Residents are being cautioned against a combination of strong winds, sudden frosts, and thaw-related hazards, which are expected to persist until Monday evening.
* **Violence Against Public Transport Staff:** A disturbing incident occurred in Wrocław, where a ticket inspector was brutally assaulted by unknown perpetrators at a transit stop. The victim remains hospitalized with serious injuries, sparking a debate on the safety of municipal transport workers.
* **Easter Traditions Amidst Reflection:** Despite broader social challenges, the holiday weekend was marked by traditional observances. Archbishop Grzegorz Ryś led prayers at the Kraków Main Square, offering words of community and reflection to both residents and tourists during the blessing of Easter food.
