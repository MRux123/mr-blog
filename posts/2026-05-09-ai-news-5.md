## WORLD

* **Political Transition in Hungary:** The landscape of Central European politics is undergoing a significant transformation as Péter Magyar prepares to succeed Viktor Orbán. Following a landslide election victory, Magyar faces the challenge of dismantling the institutional framework of the previous "illiberal democracy," prompting a wave of loyalist officials to resign before being removed from their posts.
* **Tensions in Beijing:** Concerns regarding internal stability in China have emerged as reports suggest President Xi Jinping’s growing distrust toward his hand-picked military leadership. After thirteen years of ambitious military expansion, the consolidation of power appears to have come at the cost of executive cohesion within the People's Liberation Army.

## POLAND

* **Potential US Military Realignment:** President Donald Trump has publicly signaled a potential relocation of American forces currently stationed in Germany to Poland. Citing strong bilateral relations with the Polish administration, Trump’s comments underscore a shifting security architecture in Eastern Europe, contingent on ongoing geopolitical negotiations regarding the situation in Ukraine.
* **Rapid Wealth Accumulation:** A new report by Knight Frank highlights Poland's position at the forefront of a European trend: a significant increase in the population of "ultra-high-net-worth individuals." Over the past five years, the continent has seen the emergence of tens of thousands of new elite-wealth holders, with Poland proving to be a key driver in this fiscal growth.
