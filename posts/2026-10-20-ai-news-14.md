## WORLD

* **France Extends Curfews Amid Persistent Unrest:** Following days of intense rioting that have gripped the nation, French authorities have decided to extend curfews to stabilize the country. Analysts suggest these disruptions underscore a broader European political trend, where centrist parties are losing influence among youth and the working class, who are increasingly drawn to populist narratives.
* **Geopolitical Realignment in the Middle East:** Britain has announced the deployment of R.A.F. Typhoon fighter jets to Saudi Arabia. This move comes in response to an escalation in attacks by the Iran-backed Houthi militia, which has also recently laid siege to the Yemeni city of Taiz, threatening a major humanitarian catastrophe.
* **Diplomatic High-Stakes:** U.S. Secretary of State Marco Rubio is navigating complex international relations, currently holding trade talks in India, while concurrently addressing concerns regarding U.S. intelligence sharing and diplomatic strategy in the ongoing conflict between Ukraine and Russia.

## POLAND

* **Security Alert on the Eastern Flank:** The Government Security Centre (RCB) issued a temporary alert for the Subcarpathian (Podkarpacie) region, warning residents of a potential airborne threat. Although the alert was cancelled after 20 minutes, the incident highlights the heightened security sensitivity in regions bordering the ongoing conflict.
* **Presidential Stance on Energy Strategy:** President Karol Nawrocki, during a meeting with his Lithuanian counterpart, emphasized the need for a "shared reflection" regarding the U.S. agreement with Russia concerning Russian diesel exports. The President signaled that, while stability is important, it should not come at the cost of easing economic pressure on Moscow.
* **Legislative Shifts for Working Parents:** The government is advancing new social policies, including a proposed two days of paid leave for parents of teenagers, aimed at supporting work-life balance for families.
