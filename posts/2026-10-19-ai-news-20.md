## WORLD

* **Kenyan Cult Leader Convicted of Mass Murder**: A court in Kenya has found cult leader Paul Mackenzie guilty of the murder of 191 individuals, primarily children. The verdict follows a horrific discovery of bodies in the Shakahola forest, marking the culmination of one of the most devastating cult-related tragedies in recent history.
* **Escalating Violence and Riots in Europe**: France is implementing curfews in Paris and other cities following widespread national unrest, while Italy faces simultaneous protests in its northern and southern regions, fueled by youth-led riots and farmer-led demonstrations against rising energy costs.
* **Deadly Drone Strike in Sudan**: A devastating drone attack at a transport hub in al-Dibab, South Kordofan, has claimed the lives of approximately 80 people. The incident represents one of the most lethal single attacks in the ongoing conflict in Sudan, targeting civilians returning home from a market.

## POLAND

* **Military Alert in Polish Airspace**: The Polish military has launched emergency operations involving combat aircraft following a heightened security alert, with the Government Security Centre (RCB) issuing public warnings regarding the situation in Polish airspace.
* **Political Maneuvers for Government Formation**: Following recent political developments, a figure has been tasked with forming a new government, despite explicitly stating they do not intend to assume the role of Prime Minister, highlighting a significant period of institutional uncertainty.
* **Natural Disaster in Spain Affects Public Discourse**: While localized, the fatal floods caused by the DANA weather phenomenon in Spain are being closely monitored in Poland, underscoring broader European concerns regarding extreme weather events and emergency preparedness.
