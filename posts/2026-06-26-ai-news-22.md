## WORLD

*   **Humanitarian Catastrophe in Venezuela:** A devastating earthquake has claimed at least 920 lives, with hundreds still trapped under rubble. The international community is mobilizing, with the United States dispatching rescue teams, aircraft, and naval assets to support the overwhelmed Venezuelan medical infrastructure.
*   **Military Escalation in the Middle East:** U.S. forces have conducted precision strikes against Iranian missile and drone storage facilities, as well as coastal radar installations. The military action comes as a direct retaliation for an attack on a cargo vessel in the Strait of Hormuz, further heightening geopolitical volatility in the region.
*   **Climate Records Shattered in Europe:** A severe heatwave has gripped the continent, pushing temperatures to historic highs. Germany provisionally recorded its hottest temperature ever at 41.3°C, leading to widespread disruptions of public events and infrastructure challenges.

## POLAND

*   **Law Enforcement Action:** Polish police have successfully apprehended a local "ultras" football fan following a criminal investigation involving threats and insults directed at a store cashier. The incident has drawn attention to public safety and the ongoing efforts to curb hooliganism.
*   **Sports Highlights:** The international sports scene saw a dramatic display of skill as France and Norway engaged in a high-scoring football encounter, highlighted by a standout hat-trick performance that captivated viewers.
