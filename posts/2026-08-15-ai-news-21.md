## WORLD

* **Tragedy on Lake Kariba:** The death toll following the capsizing of a ferry on Lake Kariba, on the border between Zimbabwe and Zambia, has risen to 68. Rescue operations are ongoing, with officials confirming that 18 children are among the victims. Preliminary investigations suggest that the vessel was severely overloaded and struggled under heavy wind conditions.
* **Significant Discovery in Belgium:** A major archaeological and financial curiosity has emerged in East Flanders, where construction workers unearthed an estimated $10 million in gold buried beneath a former brewery. Authorities are now launching a formal investigation to determine the origin and legal ownership of the long-forgotten fortune.

## POLAND

* **Political Tensions over Military Commemorations:** The Polish political scene saw a sharp exchange between former President Bronisław Komorowski and Karol Nawrocki. Komorowski criticized Nawrocki for invoking divine themes during the Armed Forces Day celebrations, arguing that such rhetoric is inappropriate for high-ranking officials, while former Prime Minister Leszek Miller challenged the historical precision of the language used during the event.
* **International Support and Recognition:** President Volodymyr Zelensky extended formal congratulations to Poland on the anniversary of the Battle of Warsaw and Armed Forces Day. His statement emphasized the historical alliance between the two nations, noting the joint efforts against Bolshevik forces in the past and underscoring the necessity of continued cooperation in the face of contemporary security threats from Moscow.
