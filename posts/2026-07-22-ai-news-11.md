## WORLD

* **Leadership Shake-up in Ukraine's Military:** Following the departure of General Oleksandr Syrskyi, Major General Mykhailo Drapatyi has assumed command of the Ukrainian military. His appointment marks a significant transition in strategy, as he faces the complex task of balancing operational independence with evolving political demands amid an intensified drone-led campaign against Russian logistics, including recent strikes on Wildberries hubs.
* **Escalating Mideast Tensions:** The ongoing conflict with Iran remains a primary focus of U.S. foreign policy. Secretary of State Marco Rubio has signaled a firm commitment to continued strikes to maintain control over the Strait of Hormuz, while diplomatic friction between Washington and Tehran persists. Notably, in a separate diplomatic development, Sergey Lavrov and Marco Rubio are scheduled to meet on the sidelines of the ASEAN conference in the Philippines.
* **Political Turmoil in Hungary:** Viktor Orbán has accused the Hungarian judiciary of political bias after the national prosecutor's office raided the offices of the Fidesz party and seized server infrastructure. The government claims the investigation concerns the National Cultural Fund, though the Prime Minister maintains the move is a politically motivated attempt to undermine the party.

## POLAND

* **Internal Ultimatum within PiS:** The Law and Justice (PiS) party has issued a definitive ultimatum to former Prime Minister Mateusz Morawiecki and other members. Spokesman Rafał Bochenek stated that failure to resign from specific associations by Thursday will result in an immediate and irrevocable exclusion from the party and its parliamentary club, marking a pivotal moment in the party's internal consolidation.
