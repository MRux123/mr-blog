Analysts point to the role of social media in amplifying the crisis, which has further exposed deep-seated ideological divisions among EU member states regarding migration policy.

President Trump has indicated that planned strikes on Iran could be averted, contingent upon a rapid movement toward a diplomatic deal, highlighting the administration's precarious "maximum pressure" strategy.
