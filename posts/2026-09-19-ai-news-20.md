## WORLD

*   **Escalating Tensions in the Middle East:** Houthi rebels have launched a significant coordinated attack involving missiles and drones against Riyadh, Saudi Arabia. The strike targeted the vicinity of the King Khalid International Airport and local oil facilities, leading to major flight disruptions and raising fears of a broader regional conflict.
*   **Media Access Crackdown in Washington:** In a move sparking international concern over press freedom, the Trump administration has revoked White House press credentials for several major media outlets, including CNN, MS NOW, and Politico. The incident has drawn sharp criticism from media advocacy groups.
*   **Surveillance Incidents in NATO Territory:** Security concerns are mounting across Europe following reports of unidentified drones operating in sensitive airspace. Most recently, authorities in Luxembourg were forced to shut down the capital's international airport to conduct an investigation into unauthorized drone activity.

## POLAND

*   **Geopolitical Security Debates:** The prospect of permanent U.S. military bases in Poland remains a subject of intense domestic debate. While President Trump has signaled support for Karol Nawrocki’s initiatives, former President Bronisław Komorowski has expressed skepticism, arguing that shifting the nature of U.S. troop presence may not inherently enhance national security.
*   **NATO Leadership Changes:** General Carsten Breuer, a German military official, has assumed the leadership of the NATO Military Committee, marking a significant transition in the alliance's command structure as it navigates complex security challenges on its eastern flank.
