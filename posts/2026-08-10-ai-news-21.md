## WORLD

*   **Catastrophic Earthquake in Colombia:** A powerful 7.4 magnitude earthquake has struck Colombia, impacting major urban centers including Bogotá, Cali, and Medellín. Official reports confirm at least 111 fatalities, with the death toll expected to climb as rescue operations navigate the debris of collapsed infrastructure.
*   **Expansion of Ukrainian Long-Range Strikes:** President Volodymyr Zelensky has confirmed a successful operation targeting a Russian oil refinery deep within Siberia. This strike signals a strategic shift in the conflict, demonstrating Ukraine's increasing capability to reach critical industrial targets well inside Russian territory while citing internal data regarding growing war-weariness among the Russian populace.
*   **Climate and Geological Alerts in Spain:** Spanish authorities are mobilizing security forces to the Balearic Islands ahead of the total solar eclipse on August 12. Concerns regarding massive tourist influxes and heightened wildfire risks during this astronomical event have prompted the government to declare a state of heightened preparedness.

## POLAND

*   **Severe Weather Warnings:** The Government Security Centre (RCB) has issued urgent alerts for six provinces due to incoming intense storms. Northern and northeastern regions are at the highest risk, with authorities warning of severe wind gusts and flash flooding.
*   **Sports Achievement:** Polish sprinter Ewa Swoboda has achieved significant international recognition, securing a silver medal in the 100-meter dash, marking a notable highlight in the nation's recent athletic performance.
