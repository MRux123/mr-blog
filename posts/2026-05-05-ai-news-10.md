## WORLD

* **Escalating Conflict in the Gulf:** Military tensions are on the rise in the Strait of Hormuz. Following drone and missile interceptions by U.S. warships, the United Arab Emirates has formally blamed Iran for orchestrating a coordinated drone attack, signaling a potential for renewed regional instability.
* **Deadly Explosion at Chinese Fireworks Facility:** A catastrophic blast at a fireworks factory in Hunan Province has claimed the lives of 26 people, with at least 61 others injured. The incident stands as one of the most lethal industrial accidents in the region in recent years.
* **Strategic Security Concerns in Europe:** The European Political Community summit in Armenia has been overshadowed by concerns regarding a "strategic vacuum" within the continent, with media reports suggesting an implicit, anti-Trump consensus dominating the diplomatic agenda.

## POLAND

* **Legal and Parliamentary Controversies:** The European Public Prosecutor’s Office has formally requested the lifting of immunity for a Polish member of parliament, marking a significant legal development. Meanwhile, a probe has been launched into the actions of two opposition MPs who removed Ukrainian flags from the Cieszyn town hall.
* **Political Pressure on Crypto Regulation:** Prime Minister Donald Tusk has issued a stern ultimatum to opposition parties, including PiS and Konfederacja, regarding an upcoming legislative project on cryptocurrency regulation, citing the "ZondaCrypto" affair as a central point of contention.
* **Academic Integrity Crackdown:** The Central Examination Commission (CKE) has nullified the matura examinations of several students nationwide for violating strict protocols by bringing mobile phones into the exam hall, highlighting a rigid enforcement of national testing standards.
