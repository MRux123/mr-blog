## WORLD

*   **Direct Military Confrontation Between Iran and Israel:** The Middle East is facing its most dangerous escalation in years. Following a barrage of Iranian missiles that struck the southern Israeli cities of Dimona and Arad, causing dozens of injuries, the Israeli Defense Forces (IDF) have launched a retaliatory wave of airstrikes targeting key infrastructure within Tehran. While U.N. officials confirmed that Iran's stated target—nuclear facilities near Dimona—remains intact, the situation remains highly volatile.
*   **The Hormuz Crisis and U.S. Ultimatum:** President Trump has issued a 48-hour ultimatum to Iran, demanding the full reopening of the Strait of Hormuz. Failure to comply has been met with explicit threats of military action against Iranian power plants. Tensions in the region were further punctuated by reports from the UKMTO regarding an explosion near a merchant vessel off the coast of Sharjah, signaling a growing threat to global maritime trade routes.
*   **Total Energy Collapse in Cuba:** The nation is grappling with its second major nationwide power failure in a week. The Cuban Ministry of Energy has declared a total breakdown of the grid, citing the debilitating impact of U.S. sanctions on infrastructure maintenance alongside chronic underinvestment in the sector.

## POLAND

*   **Political Realignment Ahead of 2027:** The domestic political landscape is bracing for significant shifts as the ruling coalition and the opposition prepare for the next election cycle. Senator Grzegorz Schetyna is expected to provide critical insight into the potential for new coalition configurations, the emergence of "Polexit" rhetoric as a mainstream political talking point, and the personal rivalry developing between Karol Nawrocki and Jarosław Kaczyński within the right-wing opposition.
*   **Heightened Media Scrutiny:** As the country processes both the international security crisis and domestic political maneuverings, top media outlets are pivoting to intense analysis. Programming shifts this Sunday highlight the urgent need for comprehensive debate regarding Poland's positioning within the shifting global security architecture.
