## WORLD

* **Massive Russian Air Strike on Ukraine:** Russian forces launched a coordinated, large-scale assault against central and southern Ukrainian cities, deploying 21 cruise missiles and over 360 drones. The strikes targeted critical infrastructure and residential areas, including Kyiv, Dnipro, Odessa, and Kharkiv. Casualties remain high, with tragic reports confirming the death of a 12-year-old child among the victims.
* **Middle East Diplomatic Breakthrough:** U.S. President Donald Trump has announced a significant diplomatic development, stating that Israel and Lebanon are scheduled to hold direct talks—the first such engagement in 34 years. This move, intended to de-escalate long-standing regional tensions, follows intensified international efforts to prevent further conflict in the Gulf.

## POLAND

* **Political Tensions within the Coalition:** The stability of the governing coalition faces renewed pressure as Poland 2050 and the PSL demand compliance with three specific conditions. The focus of the immediate political agenda is the status of Paulina Hennig-Kloska, with urgent inter-party meetings scheduled for today to navigate these legislative and personnel disagreements.
* **Public Opinion on Constitutional Crisis:** A recent IBRIS poll conducted for *Rzeczpospolita* reveals that over one-third of Poles hold Karol Nawrocki responsible for exacerbating the ongoing crisis surrounding the Constitutional Tribunal. Public sentiment remains divided regarding the failure to accept the oaths of four appointed judges, highlighting deep-seated friction in the Polish judicial reform process.
