## WORLD

*   **Houthi Strike on Saudi Capital:** The Houthi movement has escalated its military campaign by launching a lethal strike on Riyadh's airport, resulting in 13 deaths. This second attack in 48 hours highlights the persistent and asymmetrical threat posed by the Iran-backed group against Saudi infrastructure and regional stability.
*   **Stalemate in Ukraine’s Energy War:** Despite attempts by the Trump administration to mediate an "energy truce" between Kyiv and Moscow, the conflict remains intensified. Reports indicate that Russia launched a fresh strike on a Kharkiv power plant shortly after diplomatic overtures, signaling that both sides remain committed to targeting critical infrastructure as a primary lever of pressure.

## POLAND

*   **Security Alert Over PM Tusk:** Polish authorities have detained an individual suspected of plotting an assassination attempt against Prime Minister Donald Tusk and his defense minister. The arrest follows intelligence reports regarding credible threats to the country's leadership, raising alarms about the current security climate.
*   **Political Shift in Kraków:** Exit polls from the Kraków mayoral election suggest a significant shift in local governance, with Łukasz Gibała emerging as the projected victor. This outcome marks a major moment in Polish municipal politics, reflecting changing public sentiment in one of the country's largest urban centers.
*   **Public Safety Concerns:** Prime Minister Tusk has publicly linked a series of violent incidents in Polish schools to the online glorification of extremism. The government is now under pressure to address the underlying causes of rising societal aggression, which has recently culminated in tragic stabbings.
