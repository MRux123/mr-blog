## WORLD

* **Trump Questions NATO's Future:** Former U.S. President Donald Trump has sparked significant international alarm by suggesting he is seriously considering withdrawing the United States from NATO. Labeling the alliance a "paper tiger," his rhetoric has prompted an immediate response from British Prime Minister Keir Starmer, highlighting growing instability in transatlantic security commitments.
* **China's Diplomatic Pivot in Iran:** Beijing has launched an unexpected effort to act as a mediator in the ongoing Iran conflict. This shift marks a notable departure from China's previously muted stance, raising questions about the strategic objectives and potential efficacy of China's direct involvement in Middle Eastern power dynamics.

## POLAND

* **Constitutional Tribunal Controversy:** The appointment of new judges to the Constitutional Tribunal has intensified political friction. With judicial oaths being administered under disputed circumstances, legal experts and officials—including Minister of Justice Waldemar Żurek—have warned that the procedures may be in gross violation of the Polish Constitution.
* **Orban's Political Challenges:** Recent polling data suggests a shifting political tide in Hungary, as the opposition party TISZA has significantly narrowed the gap against Viktor Orban’s Fidesz. With the support gap widening to 16 percentage points among decided voters, questions regarding the sustainability of Orban’s long-standing grip on power and media control have intensified, drawing close observation from regional observers.
