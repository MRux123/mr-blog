## WORLD

* **Trump Halts Planned Strikes on Iran:** President Trump has officially called off military strikes against Iran, citing progress in the preliminary negotiations for a diplomatic framework. The move follows urgent requests from regional partners to de-escalate tensions, signaling a shift toward a potential de-escalation of the volatile standoff in the Middle East.
* **Deadly Explosion Hits Moscow Restaurant:** A bombing at the Balzi Rossi restaurant in Moscow has resulted in multiple casualties, including three fatalities. The incident occurred during a private banquet for a high-ranking Russian Air Force commander, suggesting a targeted attack rather than a random act of violence.
* **Migration Crisis Strains Spanish Border:** Tensions in the Spanish enclave of Ceuta have reached a breaking point, with far-right factions intensifying calls for the mass expulsion of migrants. The situation remains a critical point of diplomatic contention and domestic political instability in Spain.

## POLAND

* **Critical Attack in Wrocław:** A violent stabbing incident in Wrocław has left a 30-year-old woman in critical condition. Authorities have apprehended an 18-year-old Ukrainian national in connection with the attack, sparking public debate over security and social integration.
* **Regulation of AI and Deepfakes:** In response to the growing threat of digital disinformation, the European Union is introducing stringent new regulations targeting AI-generated content. These measures aim to curb the proliferation of malicious deepfakes, ensuring greater accountability in the digital information ecosystem.
