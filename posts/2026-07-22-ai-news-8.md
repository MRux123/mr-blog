## WORLD

* **Shadow Diplomacy in Baku:** A clandestine meeting has surfaced between German political figures and representatives of Vladimir Putin in Baku, Azerbaijan. The revelation has caused a significant stir, with German Chancellor Friedrich Merz distancing his administration from the talks, and Azerbaijani authorities denying prior knowledge of the event.
* **Ukrainian Military Command Transition:** Amidst ongoing regional tensions, Major General Mykhailo Drapatyi has emerged as a central figure following his appointment as Ukraine's new top general. His tenure comes at a critical juncture as logistics hubs, including those of the Wildberries retailer, continue to be targeted in the conflict.
* **Rising Tensions at the Strait of Hormuz:** U.S. Secretary of State Marco Rubio has issued a stark warning regarding Iran's potential control over the Strait of Hormuz, labeling it a dangerous precedent for global stability and a potential catalyst for wider territorial disputes.

## POLAND

* **Political Realignment within the Governing Coalition:** Minister of Infrastructure Dariusz Klimczak has publicly signaled that the Polish People's Party (PSL) is exploring a new political project, effectively aiming to forge a "new Third Way." Klimczak definitively ruled out any cooperation with Mateusz Morawiecki, stating that the former Prime Minister’s political faction does not align with the PSL’s future vision.
* **Dismissal of the Defense Minister:** Political instability has intensified following the resignation of Ukraine’s defense minister, Mykhailo Fedorov. The situation has triggered public protests in Ukraine, further complicated by reports that Fedorov declined alternative government positions offered by President Zelensky.
* **Economic Indicators:** The Social Insurance Institution (ZUS) has released updated data for 2026, revealing that the average gross pension in Poland has climbed to 4,480.74 PLN, marking a significant increase following the March indexation period.
