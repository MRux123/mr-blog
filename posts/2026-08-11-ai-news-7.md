## WORLD

* **U.S. Consular Retrenchment:** The U.S. State Department has notified Congress of plans to close five consulates globally, including facilities in Canada, Japan, and Cameroon. Officials cite fiscal constraints and a broader reassessment of bilateral relations under the current Trump administration. Critics warn that these closures may create a strategic vacuum, potentially allowing China to expand its diplomatic influence in these regions.
* **Trump’s Tactical Maneuvers:** Reports indicate a high-level operational shift involving President Trump, marked by a sudden, unscheduled departure from Air Force One. The event has sparked speculation regarding security protocols and the administration's focus on clandestine crisis management.

## POLAND

* **Political Debate on Ukrainian Migrants:** The discourse surrounding Ukrainian presence in Poland has intensified. Law and Justice (PiS) MP Andrzej Śliwka has publicly advocated for the deportation of Ukrainian men of military age who lack legal employment, suggesting that such individuals could form significant military units—up to the size of a division—for their home country.
* **Economic Sentiment and Energy Policy:** Public dissatisfaction regarding government intervention in fuel prices remains high, with 59% of respondents in a recent Opinia24 poll describing the state's efforts as ineffective. Energy Minister Miłosz Motyka has signaled that a decision on the proposed "Ceny Paliw Niżej" (Lower Fuel Prices) program is expected this week.
* **Severe Weather Impacts:** A violent storm swept through Warsaw, causing extensive damage to infrastructure, including fallen trees and widespread power outages across the Mazovia region. Local authorities are continuing efforts to restore electricity to affected households.
