## WORLD

* **Hubert Hurkacz Advances at US Open:** Polish tennis star Hubert Hurkacz has successfully secured his place in the second round of the US Open. The victory in New York marks a strong start for the athlete in one of the most prestigious Grand Slam tournaments of the season, maintaining high momentum in his professional campaign.

## POLAND

* **Security Incident at Military Facility:** Polish authorities have detained two individuals following a false security alarm at a military installation. The Żandarmeria Wojskowa (Military Gendarmerie) intervened swiftly to neutralize the threat, confirming that the situation was a false alarm. An investigation is currently underway to determine the motives and circumstances behind the breach.
