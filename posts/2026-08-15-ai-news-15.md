## WORLD

*   **Deadly Earthquake Strikes Indonesia:** A powerful magnitude 7.7 earthquake has hit the island of Flores, resulting in at least 47 confirmed fatalities. Search and rescue operations are currently intensive as authorities scramble to locate survivors amidst the wreckage.
*   **Escalation in Lebanon:** Israeli military strikes in southern Lebanon have claimed the lives of eleven people. The IDF stated the operation targeted Hezbollah infrastructure in direct response to regional hostile activities directed at its soldiers.
*   **Geopolitical Tensions Over Detained Pilots:** Iran has publicly alleged that Qatar captured three of its pilots following the downing of fighter jets on March 2. The incident, occurring early in the conflict with the U.S., remains a sensitive diplomatic issue as Doha has yet to issue an official response.

## POLAND

*   **Security Doctrine Debate:** Following remarks by the President regarding national defense, Deputy Prime Minister Władysław Kosiniak-Kamysz has clarified the "SAFE" security doctrine. The strategy focuses on four core pillars: strengthening alliances, modernizing the army, expanding local defense manufacturing, and deepening European security integration.
*   **Major Industrial Fire in Pomerania:** A large-scale fire has broken out at a waste management facility in Chlewnica. Local authorities have issued an urgent health warning, advising residents to keep windows closed and limit outdoor activity due to heavy smoke and potential toxic emissions.
*   **Controversial Landmark Inaugurated:** A 55-meter statue of the Virgin Mary, funded by business magnate Roman Karkosik, has been unveiled in Konotop, Warmian-Masurian Voivodeship. The monument, currently the tallest of its kind in Europe, has sparked a national debate regarding its aesthetic impact and the role of religious iconography in the public sphere.
