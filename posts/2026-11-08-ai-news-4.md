## WORLD

*   **Israeli Political Deadlock:** Following the recent inconclusive election, President of Israel has tasked opposition leader Eisenkot with the formation of a new government. The move comes as both the incumbent Prime Minister Benjamin Netanyahu and his opposition bloc failed to secure a clear parliamentary majority, signaling a period of intense coalition negotiations ahead.
*   **Escalating Conflict in Yemen:** A Saudi-led coalition air strike has reportedly resulted in at least 33 fatalities among Houthi forces. While the Houthis have publicized the incident, the Saudi coalition has yet to provide an official statement regarding the strike, further complicating the long-standing regional instability.

## POLAND

*   **National Mourning Following Krakow Shooting:** Poland has declared a period of national mourning following a horrific shooting in Krakow's main square, which left six victims dead. President Karol Nawrocki has officially signed the decree, as the country grapples with the aftermath of the violence.
*   **International Security Response:** The impact of the Krakow tragedy has transcended borders, with Czech authorities increasing police presence in public spaces as a precautionary measure against copycat incidents. 
*   **Public and Official Support:** As investigation into the attack continues, the nation has turned its focus to the medical recovery of an injured police officer. A nationwide appeal for blood donations has been launched, while local officials, including Jakub Kulik and Artur Morański, are being hailed as heroes for their response during the crisis.
