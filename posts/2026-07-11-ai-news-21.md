## WORLD

*   **Escalating Conflict in the Middle East:** Following the death of his father during the initial phase of the US-Israeli operations against Iran, Mojtaba Khamenei has declared that vengeance is "inevitable." This rhetoric marks a significant escalation in regional instability, further complicating the already volatile military situation in the area.
*   **Trump Seeks Cognitive Assessment:** In a move that has sparked global media interest, Donald Trump has requested a new round of cognitive function testing, raising questions regarding his health and fitness for office as the political landscape remains in a state of high tension.

## POLAND

*   **Volhynia Massacre Commemoration:** President Volodymyr Zelensky has issued a statement calling for unity, truth, and Christian commemoration regarding the massacres of the 1940s. While the Polish government has pledged to erect a memorial for the victims of the genocide, diplomatic friction remains, highlighted by the public criticism directed at a Polish diplomat for his remarks during commemorations in Ukraine regarding pre-war state violence.
*   **Political Polarization in the Sejm:** Domestic political discourse continues to be fraught with conflict, as exemplified by Fr. Tadeusz Rydzyk’s sharp critique of parliamentarians, labeling some as "thugs in elegant ties," and MEP Patryk Jaki’s ultimatum, in which he threatened to resign from politics unless a "hard-line" approach toward the European Union is adopted by his party.
