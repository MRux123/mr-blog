## WORLD

* **Escalating US-Iran Conflict:** The geopolitical landscape is increasingly volatile as the United States has launched strikes against Iran, citing retaliation for the death of American soldiers. With reported explosions of ships in the Strait of Hormuz and reciprocal attacks in Syria and Jordan, analysts warn that both nations are edging toward an uncontainable wider conflict, necessitating an urgent return to diplomatic negotiations.
* **Political Transition in the United Kingdom:** Andy Burnham is set to officially assume the role of Prime Minister on Monday, succeeding Keir Starmer. Burnham inherits a complex domestic and international agenda, with experts identifying critical infrastructure and economic reforms as his most immediate challenges upon entering Downing Street.

## POLAND

* **Major European Union Fine for AliExpress:** The European Commission has taken decisive action against the Chinese e-commerce giant AliExpress, imposing a record-breaking fine of 550 million euros. This move highlights the EU's ongoing commitment to enforcing strict regulatory standards on global digital platforms operating within the European market.
* **Strategic Military Exercises:** Amidst heightened regional security concerns, the Polish government has confirmed that the United States military will conduct a series of tactical exercises over Polish territory. These operations serve as a key component of NATO’s collective defense posture in Eastern Europe.
