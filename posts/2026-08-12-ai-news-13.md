## WORLD

*   **Total Solar Eclipse Approaches:** Europe is on the brink of a major astronomical event as the moon prepares to fully block the sun. Thousands of observers have converged across a path stretching from Iceland to Spain, marking a rare spectacle of significant scientific and cultural interest.
*   **Tragedy on Lake Kariba:** At least 44 people have lost their lives following the capsizing of an overcrowded ferry in Zimbabwe. With the vessel carrying well beyond its capacity, rescue operations are currently underway, though the incident has ignited serious concerns regarding maritime safety and regulatory oversight on one of the world's largest man-made lakes.
*   **West Bank Tensions:** The situation in the West Bank remains critical as Israeli settlers continue to besiege Palestinian families. Reports indicate that local security forces have withdrawn, leaving vulnerable civilians isolated and under threat, drawing further attention to the escalating volatility in the region.
