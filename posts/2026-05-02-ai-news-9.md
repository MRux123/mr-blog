## WORLD

* **US-German Defense Ties Strain Following Troop Withdrawal:** The decision by the Pentagon to withdraw 5,000 U.S. troops from Germany has sparked a significant diplomatic rift. German Defense Minister Boris Pistorius stated that Europe must now assume greater responsibility for its own security, while critics note that the German government failed to anticipate the severity of President Trump’s rhetoric regarding military presence.
* **Escalation in Lebanon Despite Ceasefire:** Violent clashes continue in southern Lebanon, with Israeli strikes resulting in at least thirteen fatalities, including four women and a child. This escalation highlights the fragility of existing ceasefire agreements in the region.
* **Humanitarian Crisis in Gaza:** Reports from displaced person camps indicate a severe deterioration of living conditions, with infestations of rats and weasels posing immediate health risks to the civilian population amidst the ongoing conflict.

## POLAND

* **Pistorius Emphasizes Polish Role in European Security:** Following the reduction of American forces in Germany, the German Defense Minister has signaled a pivot toward closer military coordination with key European allies, explicitly identifying Poland as a central partner for future regional security missions.
* **Ukraine Outraged by Trump Administration Stance:** Tensions have surfaced between Kyiv and Washington, with Ukrainian officials expressing public disappointment and a perceived "lack of respect" from members of Donald Trump’s administration, signaling potential friction in the ongoing conflict with Russia.
* **Escalating Russian Strikes on Ukraine:** The conflict continues to take a heavy toll on civilians, with recent Russian drone attacks on Kherson and Kharkiv resulting in fatalities and injuries, prompting condemnation from President Zelenskyy.
