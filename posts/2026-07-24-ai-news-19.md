## WORLD

* **Tragic Strike on Kyiv Defense Expo:** A Russian ballistic missile attack on a drone exhibition near Kyiv has resulted in at least ten fatalities. The event, which drew key figures from Ukraine's defense sector, marks a significant and lethal breach of security near the capital, signaling a shift in the intensity of regional hostilities.
* **Trump Targets EU Tech Fines:** President Trump has pledged a rigorous investigation into European Union regulatory bodies following recent record-breaking fines imposed on U.S. tech giants including Google, Apple, Meta, and Amazon. The administration signals a major confrontational shift in transatlantic economic policy, demanding a full reversal of these financial penalties.
* **Escalating Iran-U.S. Standoff:** White House officials are currently engaged in high-level cabinet meetings to determine the next steps regarding military operations against Iran. With direct clashes ongoing and a pivotal meeting with Prime Minister Benjamin Netanyahu approaching, the geopolitical situation in the Middle East remains increasingly volatile.

## POLAND

* **Domestic Political Friction:** Internal political discourse remains heated, with high-profile figures such as MP Paweł Jabłoński offering critical insights into ongoing negotiations. Meanwhile, Ryszard Terlecki has publicly defended the leadership of Jarosław Kaczyński, further highlighting the ongoing polarization within the Polish political landscape.
