## WORLD

* **Iran-U.S. Conflict at a Turning Point:** The geopolitical situation in the Middle East has reached a critical juncture. Tehran is currently reviewing a U.S. proposal aimed at de-escalating the conflict following direct threats from President Trump regarding renewed military strikes. The situation remains highly volatile after U.S. forces disabled an Iranian-flagged tanker and Israeli strikes were reported near Beirut.
* **Iran's Economic Strain:** The sustained U.S. blockade is taking a significant toll on Iran's economy. With the oil sector struggling to find export markets, the regime is facing internal pressure to reach a diplomatic resolution or risk total economic isolation.

## POLAND

* **Political Polarization Intensifies:** The political landscape in Poland remains deeply fractured. Sławomir Mentzen (Konfederacja) has doubled down on his aggressive critique of Chief of the General Staff Gen. Wiesław Kukuła, labeling him "abnormal" and expressing regret for not being harsher in his initial remarks.
* **Presidential Stance on U.S. Presence:** President Karol Nawrocki has signaled a proactive foreign policy approach, appealing to Donald Trump to maintain or relocate U.S. troops currently stationed in Germany to Poland, should the U.S. decide on a withdrawal from German territory.
* **Constitutional and Institutional Friction:** Legal experts and state officials are at odds over the state of the rule of law. Prof. Ryszard Piotrowski argued that the nation's political dysfunction stems from human behavior rather than constitutional flaws, while Ombudsman Marcin Wiącek reported an ongoing standoff with the Presidential Palace, emphasizing the primacy of ECHR rulings regarding the independence of the Constitutional Tribunal.
