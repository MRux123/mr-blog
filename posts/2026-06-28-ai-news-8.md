## WORLD

*   **Venezuela Earthquake Crisis:** The humanitarian situation in Venezuela has reached a critical stage following twin earthquakes that have claimed over 1,400 lives. While recovery efforts are underway, the influx of spontaneous volunteers into the disaster zones has inadvertently hindered the mobility of specialized search and rescue teams, complicating the ongoing mission to locate survivors trapped under rubble.
*   **Security Incident in Beijing:** Chinese authorities have confirmed a fatal incident involving a small aircraft crashing into the city's tallest tower. The event, which occurred within five miles of the Communist Party headquarters, left at least 13 individuals injured and has sparked intense speculation regarding aviation security and potential internal threats.
*   **Dengue Outbreak in Sri Lanka:** A severe surge in dengue fever is straining Sri Lanka’s healthcare infrastructure. In a bid to control the mosquito population, the military has been deployed to assist in large-scale eradication efforts, highlighting the dual pressure of public health crises and ongoing energy instability in the region.

## POLAND

*   **Heightened Security Concerns:** Polish intelligence agencies have issued warnings regarding new, concerning Russian strategic plans directed toward Poland. Officials describe the current climate as a "period of danger," emphasizing the need for increased vigilance amid ongoing regional geopolitical volatility.
*   **Escalation in Ukraine-Russia Conflict:** President Zelensky has confirmed successful, large-scale Ukrainian drone strikes targeting oil refineries deep within Russian territory. This development marks a significant uptick in the technological and tactical reach of Ukrainian offensive capabilities, further escalating the ongoing conflict.
