## WORLD

*   **Massive Crypto Theft Linked to North Korea:** Binance is currently working to recover $6.3 billion following a sophisticated cyber-attack. Security experts suspect North Korean actors are behind what is being described as the largest hack in the history of the cryptocurrency sector.
*   **High-Speed Rail Disaster in Spain:** Following a deadly collision that claimed at least 69 lives, the Spanish government is under intense pressure. Official investigations have pointed to critical traffic control errors, with allegations that controllers authorized a high-speed train to proceed despite another vehicle being stalled on the same track.
*   **Russian Media Censorship:** *Novaya Gazeta*, one of the most prominent independent news outlets in Russia, has officially ceased operations. The closure marks a significant blow to press freedom, occurring under intense pressure from the authorities.

## POLAND

*   **Constitutional Tribunal Turmoil:** A dramatic escalation has unfolded at the Constitutional Tribunal in Warsaw. Police intervened at the premises following reports of a security threat submitted by Judge Anna Korwin-Piotrowska, who has reportedly assumed control over the institution. The move has sparked a fierce political debate regarding the state of the judiciary and institutional stability.
*   **Political Emergency:** The gravity of the situation at the Constitutional Tribunal has triggered an immediate response at the highest levels of government. Karol Nawrocki has cut short an official visit to Senegal to return to Poland, citing the urgent necessity of his presence in the country amidst the escalating institutional crisis.
