## WORLD

*   **Heightened Tensions in the Middle East:** The geopolitical landscape remains volatile as the United States and Iran appear to be edging closer to direct conflict. President Trump has announced the renewal of a shipping blockade on Iranian ports, coupled with a 20 percent levy on goods traversing the Strait of Hormuz, signaling a sharp escalation in regional hostility.
*   **Japan’s Intelligence Pivot:** In a historic shift from its post-WWII security doctrine, Japan is establishing a centralized intelligence agency. Prime Minister Sanae Takaichi is spearheading this initiative, citing the need to counter growing threats from Russia and China through enhanced cooperation with Western allies.
*   **FIFA World Cup Expansion:** FIFA President Gianni Infantino has officially signaled support for a 64-team Men’s World Cup format, with detailed assessments planned to begin following the 2026 tournament, aiming to broaden the sport's global reach.

## POLAND

*   **Stalemate on Russian Sanctions:** European unity continues to face significant strain, as member states fail to reach an agreement on a new package of sanctions against Russia. Disagreements persist, with France and Italy reportedly opposing travel bans for Russian nationals.
*   **Governmental Transition in Ukraine:** The political situation in neighboring Ukraine sees a potential leadership shift, as Naftohaz head Serhiy Korecki has reportedly accepted President Zelensky’s proposal to become the new Prime Minister, following the dismissal of Julia Swyrydenko.
*   **Healthcare Infrastructure Status:** Despite domestic economic challenges, recent Eurostat data reveals that Poland maintains a significantly higher number of hospital beds per capita compared to the European average, highlighting a unique aspect of the country’s public health capacity.
