## WORLD

* **Geopolitical Tensions in the Strait of Hormuz:** The region faces renewed instability following a direct fire exchange in the Strait of Hormuz. Despite the gravity of the situation, Donald Trump has dismissed the incident as a minor diplomatic friction, downplaying international security concerns surrounding the vital maritime corridor.
* **Latvia’s Security Breach:** The Latvian armed forces have confirmed a significant failure in their aerial defense systems after unidentified drones breached the country's airspace and crashed on its territory. The incident has prompted an official protest and a formal inquiry, highlighting growing anxieties regarding the vulnerability of Eastern Europe’s borders.
* **War Profiteering Amid Conflict:** Global markets are seeing a clear divide as major oil and banking corporations report record profits linked to the ongoing war in Iran, sparking a global debate on the ethics of corporate gain during geopolitical crises.

## POLAND

* **EU Funding for Defense Modernization:** Poland has secured a landmark agreement to receive a multi-billion euro loan package under the EU’s SAFE initiative. As the first of 19 participating nations, Poland will leverage these funds to rapidly accelerate the modernization of its armed forces and bolster the domestic defense industry.
* **National Security Debate:** Amidst calls from Minister of National Defence Władysław Kosiniak-Kamysz for 24/7 defense production, the country is grappling with questions regarding equipment readiness. General Mirosław Różański is set to address critical concerns about potential gaps in military capabilities and the feasibility of an immediate industrial ramp-up.
* **Public Sentiment on EU Membership:** A recent survey by Opinia24 for "Gazeta Wyborcza" indicates strong public support for European integration, with only 15% of respondents in favor of a "Polexit," suggesting that European unity remains a cornerstone of the Polish political landscape.
