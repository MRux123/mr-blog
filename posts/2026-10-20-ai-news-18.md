## WORLD

* **Tragedy in Sudan:** A devastating drone strike by the Sudanese army has claimed the lives of over 300 people. The attack targeted a truck transporting funeral mourners and a local market suspected of supplying fuel to the Rapid Support Forces (RSF), marking one of the deadliest single incidents in the ongoing conflict.
* **Ukraine-US Diplomatic Friction:** President Zelensky has notably shifted his tone toward the Trump administration. Ukrainian officials indicate that the decision to abandon previous diplomatic restraint follows the U.S. president's increasingly overt prioritization of Russian interests, a move Kyiv views as an existential risk.
* **French Historical Reckoning:** In a symbolic shift, the French Senate has moved to repeal the "Code Noir" regulations. Although slavery was abolished long ago, these colonial-era statutes remained on the books, serving as a reminder of France’s complex imperial legacy.

## POLAND

* **Executive Shake-up:** Prime Minister Donald Tusk has officially announced his intention to nominate Minister of Finance Andrzej Domański for the role of Deputy Prime Minister. The proposal, framed as a move to stabilize the economy, has met with fierce resistance from the opposition, which labeled the decision a confrontation with the executive branch.
* **Constitutional Court Standoff:** The institutional gridlock surrounding the Constitutional Tribunal continues as Maciej Berek reports successful entry into the building, challenging the blockades previously enforced by Tribunal President Bogdan Święczkowski.
* **Diplomatic Dispute with Lviv:** A significant diplomatic rift has emerged following the Lviv Regional Council's decision to designate 2027 as the year of Roman Shukhevych. The Polish Institute of National Remembrance (IPN) has condemned the move as an affront to the victims of the crimes committed by the UPA under his command.
