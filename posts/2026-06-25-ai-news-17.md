## WORLD

*   **Devastating Earthquakes in Venezuela:** A series of powerful earthquakes, registering magnitudes of 7.2 and 7.5, has struck Venezuela. Authorities report at least 164 fatalities, with rescue operations currently underway to locate hundreds of citizens feared trapped under the debris in cities including Caracas and La Guaira.
*   **European Heatwave Crisis:** A historic heatwave is gripping Europe, leading to record-breaking temperatures in Britain and a surge in heat-related fatalities across Spain and Italy. Health agencies warn that the situation remains critical as the extreme weather persists.
*   **Constitutional Dispute in Zimbabwe:** The Zimbabwean Senate has moved to extend presidential term limits from five to seven years, allowing President Emmerson Mnangagwa to remain in power until 2030. Opposition leaders have condemned the legislative change as a "constitutional coup," heightening internal political tensions.

## POLAND

*   **Economic Outlook for Ukraine:** During an address in Gdańsk, World Bank President Ajay Banga announced that the reconstruction of Ukraine will require over $580 billion over the next decade. The initiative emphasizes the necessity of private sector involvement and structural reforms to facilitate the post-war recovery.
*   **Defense Technology Advancement:** The Ukrainian firm Fire Point is accelerating the development of a new European anti-missile defense system. Reports indicate that the company, known for its "Flamingo" cruise missile technology, expects to complete its initial interceptor prototypes by the end of the year.
*   **Infrastructure Incident:** Emergency services are currently responding to a train collision in the Greater Poland (Wielkopolska) region. Details regarding the cause and casualties are still being gathered as authorities coordinate the rescue effort.
