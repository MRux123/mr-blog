## WORLD

*   **Terror Attack Near Berlin Pride:** German authorities have launched an urgent investigation following a violent incident near a Pride celebration in Berlin. Officials have officially classified the act as a likely manifestation of Islamist terrorism, initiating a manhunt for a 21-year-old suspect.
*   **Tragedy on Mount Elbrus:** A climbing expedition on the Caucasus range ended in disaster, as five Bosnian mountaineers lost their lives while attempting to summit Mount Elbrus. Local reports confirmed the fatalities, marking a somber conclusion to the high-altitude expedition.
*   **Russia-Ukraine Conflict Stance:** Kremlin spokesperson Dmitry Peskov has ruled out a "freezing" of the conflict in Ukraine, citing the current position of the Kyiv government. This follows calls from international leaders, including Kazakhstan’s President Tokayev, for an immediate cessation of hostilities to prevent further regional instability.

## POLAND

*   **NATO Air Defense Response:** Romanian airspace security remains in the spotlight after a series of drone incursions. The Romanian military successfully intercepted a hostile drone using F-16 fighter jets, demonstrating rapid reaction capabilities within five minutes of the object entering their sovereign territory.
*   **Industrial Safety Hazards:** Serious environmental and safety concerns have emerged regarding a local industrial waste dump. Reports indicate that spontaneous heating and gas emissions from the site pose a direct threat to nearby public areas, including children’s playgrounds, prompting urgent calls for oversight.
*   **Journalists Under Threat:** A growing climate of fear has permeated the media landscape, as journalists report increasing concerns over their personal safety while covering sensitive and politically charged topics, highlighting a deepening crisis for press freedom and journalistic security.
