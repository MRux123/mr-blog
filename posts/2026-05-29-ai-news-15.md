## WORLD

* **Escalation in Iran-West Relations:** President Trump has announced he is nearing a "final determination" regarding the ongoing Iran proposal. While threats to lift the naval blockade of the Strait of Hormuz loom, the situation remains volatile as Iran has yet to confirm any concessions, keeping global markets and geopolitical observers on high alert.
* **NATO Condemns Drone Incursion:** The alliance has issued a sharp rebuke to Russia following a drone strike on a residential area in Romania. With two individuals injured and an emergency meeting scheduled in Bucharest, the incident underscores the heightened risk of spillover effects from the ongoing regional conflict into NATO territory.
* **Global Health Crisis:** The World Health Organization (WHO) has confirmed that the current Ebola outbreak in the Democratic Republic of the Congo has reached a critical mortality rate of 30-50%. WHO Chief Tedros Adhanom Ghebreyesus has called for an immediate ceasefire among armed factions to facilitate humanitarian intervention and containment efforts.

## POLAND

* **Espionage Probe within Defense Sector:** Polish authorities have detained an employee of the PGZ Group (Polska Grupa Zbrojeniowa) on charges of espionage. The arrest is a significant development for national security, as the state-owned defense conglomerate plays a central role in the modernization and logistics of the Polish Armed Forces.
* **Diplomatic Friction Over Honors:** Minister of Foreign Affairs Radosław Sikorski has openly criticized a proposal by President Karol Nawrocki to strip Ukrainian President Volodymyr Zelenskyy of the Order of the White Eagle. The tension stems from unresolved historical sensitivities between the two nations, highlighting a deepening rift within the Polish political establishment regarding foreign policy strategy.
* **Heightened Rhetoric from Moscow:** Former Russian President Dmitry Medvedev has issued fresh, aggressive warnings directed at the European Union, stating that the "peaceful sleep" of European citizens has come to an end. These statements, framed as a response to perceived Western involvement in the Ukraine conflict, reflect the Kremlin’s continued strategy of psychological warfare against European states.
