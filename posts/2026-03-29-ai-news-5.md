## WORLD

* **U.S. Military Engagement Deepens in the Middle East**: The regional conflict has intensified significantly as U.S. Marines have arrived in the Middle East following a series of escalations. A major security breach occurred at a U.S. base in Saudi Arabia, where an Iranian strike resulted in injuries to nearly two dozen American service members.
* **Pentagon Evaluating Tactical Options**: Reports from the *Washington Post* indicate that the Pentagon is drafting plans for targeted ground operations within Iran. While the White House, through spokesperson Karoline Leavitt, stated that President Donald Trump has not yet authorized a full-scale invasion, the situation marks a critical juncture in U.S.-Iranian relations.

## POLAND

* **Observance of Palm Sunday**: As the Catholic Church enters Holy Week, communities across Poland are marking Palm Sunday, a day of traditional reflection and prayer commemorating the Passion of Christ, which sets the liturgical tone for the upcoming Easter period.
* **Final Retail Push Before Easter**: In an effort to facilitate last-minute holiday preparations, today, March 29, has been designated as a trading Sunday. Major retail chains remain operational until 10:00 PM, allowing consumers to finalize their holiday shopping ahead of the Easter break.
