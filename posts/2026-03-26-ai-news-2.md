## WORLD

* **Middle East Conflict Intensifies:** Tensions between Israel and Iran have reached a critical juncture. Tehran has officially dismissed U.S. cease-fire conditions, countering with demands for reparations and insisting on its own terms. As a result, Israel has signaled plans to intensify military operations, driven by concerns that current diplomatic efforts might prematurely halt the dismantling of Iran’s weapons programs. Despite ongoing indirect communication channels, a viable peace deal remains elusive.

* **Strategic Expansion in the Arctic:** The United States is actively pursuing increased military presence on Greenland. Reports from Danish media indicate that discreet negotiations between Washington and Copenhagen are nearing a resolution, potentially granting the U.S. access to three additional defense zones. This move underscores the growing geopolitical importance of the Arctic in the context of shifting global security alliances.

## POLAND

* **Leadership Change in GROM:** The Polish Ministry of National Defense has announced a significant shift in the command of Jednostka Wojskowa GROM, the nation's premier special operations force. Colonel Krzysztof Mila, an experienced officer with a long tenure in special forces, has been appointed as the new commander. The transition is scheduled to take effect at the beginning of April, marking a strategic update in Poland's military leadership structure.
