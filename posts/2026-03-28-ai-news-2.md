## WORLD

* **Escalation in Middle East Conflict:** A major security breach occurred at the Prince Sultan Air Base in Saudi Arabia, where 12 U.S. troops were injured following an attack. This strike represents one of the most significant challenges to American defenses since the beginning of regional hostilities on February 28, heightening tensions in the ongoing conflict involving Iran.
* **Legal Trouble for Tiger Woods:** Renowned golfer Tiger Woods has been arrested in Florida following a vehicle collision. Authorities suspect he was operating a vehicle under the influence of narcotics, as he reportedly refused a required toxicology test. This marks the latest in a series of legal and traffic-related incidents in the athlete's career.

## POLAND

* **Bomb Alert Disrupts International Rail Transit:** A major security incident unfolded on the Frankfurt-Warsaw train line, causing an emergency stop in Łowicz. A 22-year-old passenger claimed there was an explosive device in his luggage, triggering an evacuation. Pirotechnicians later confirmed the threat was a hoax containing only personal belongings; the suspect has been detained by local authorities.
* **Botched Escape Results in Immediate Arrest:** In an unusual turn of events near Naples, Italy, two suspects fleeing from the Carabinieri accidentally leaped over a fence directly into the courtyard of a police station. They were taken into custody immediately upon landing, ending the pursuit in an ironic fashion.
