## WORLD

*   **Humanitarian Crisis in Venezuela:** Following devastating twin earthquakes that flattened entire neighborhoods, the official death toll of 1,719 is feared to be a significant undercount. Rescue efforts are ongoing, but the true scale of the catastrophe remains buried under the rubble, raising concerns about the adequacy of the international response.
*   **Diplomatic Efforts in the Middle East:** U.S. and Iranian officials have converged in Qatar for high-level talks mediated by local authorities. These discussions follow a new wave of regional attacks that have jeopardized recent efforts to secure a lasting peace agreement, marking a critical juncture for regional stability.
*   **Safety Failures in Pakistan:** A tragic building collapse at a tuition centre in the Kahna suburb of Lahore has claimed the lives of 14 children. Authorities have detained two individuals as the investigation into building code violations and safety standards continues.

## POLAND

*   **Russian Border Security Policy:** In a significant shift in regional border management, Russia has officially closed its border crossings with Finland, Latvia, and Estonia. The move signals a further hardening of diplomatic and logistical relations between Russia and its Baltic neighbors.
*   **Legislative Developments on End-of-Life Care:** France is conducting a pivotal vote on the issue of assisted dying. While the matter is French-centric, the legislative progress is being closely monitored by Polish observers and policymakers as it touches upon sensitive moral and legal debates currently echoing across Europe.
*   **Political Discourse on Ukraine Relations:** Krzysztof Bosak, leader of the Konfederacja party, has publicly voiced concerns regarding Poland’s long-term strategic relationship with Ukraine. He argued that Warsaw risks being marginalized in the European Union as Kiev potentially shifts its focus toward a partnership with Berlin, underscoring the complexities of regional geopolitical alignment.
