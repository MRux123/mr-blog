## WORLD

* **Iran-U.S. Cease-fire Extended Amid Tense Diplomacy:** President Trump has extended the cease-fire with Iran, citing a request from Pakistan, which is currently mediating between Washington and Tehran. Despite the extension, the atmosphere remains hostile; Iranian officials have dismissed the move as a tactical ruse, while the U.S. has confirmed that the blockade of Iranian ports will remain strictly enforced.
* **Pentagon Reverses Vaccine Mandates:** In a move described by Secretary of Defense Pete Hegseth as a step toward restoring combat readiness, the Pentagon has officially scrapped the long-standing requirement for U.S. troops to receive influenza vaccinations. The administration has labeled the previous policy as irrational and detrimental to the military's operational capacity.
* **Controversial Relocation Plan for Afghan Allies:** Reports suggest the U.S. is currently in negotiations to relocate Afghan citizens who previously aided American forces to the Democratic Republic of Congo. Those affected reportedly face a stark choice: resettlement in Central Africa or returning to life under Taliban rule in Afghanistan.

## POLAND

* **Lufthansa Groundings Impact Polish Routes:** The German airline group Lufthansa has announced the cancellation of 20,000 flights, a disruption that includes significant impacts on air travel connections to and from Poland.
* **Suspicious Russian Maritime Activity Near NATO Infrastructure:** Norwegian security services have identified a potential intelligence operation involving the Russian tanker *Apple*. The vessel remained stationary for eight hours over a critical subsea fiber-optic cable connected to the strategic Globus radar station in northern Norway. While the crew cited mechanical failure, authorities suspect the maneuver was a deliberate reconnaissance mission by the Russian "shadow fleet."
