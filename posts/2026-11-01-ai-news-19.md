## WORLD

*   **Violent Cost-of-Living Protests in Germany:** Widespread demonstrations against the rising cost of living have escalated into violence across major German cities, including Berlin, Hamburg, and Frankfurt. The unrest coincides with mass protests in France, highlighting a growing pan-European frustration with economic conditions.
*   **Continued Escalation in Gaza:** Israeli strikes in Khan Younis and Gaza City have resulted in multiple casualties, including members of the local police force and civilians. The humanitarian situation remains dire as regional tensions show no signs of de-escalation.

## POLAND

*   **Constitutional Tribunal Standoff:** A physical confrontation at the Constitutional Tribunal has triggered a significant political crisis. The President’s office has announced formal legal complaints against three judges, citing the violation of the head of state's bodily integrity during a scuffle, while opposition figures and ruling party members trade accusations regarding the legitimacy of the proceedings.
*   **Russian Rhetoric on Polish Internal Affairs:** Former Russian President Dmitry Medvedev has publicly mocked the Polish political situation, specifically targeting Prime Minister Donald Tusk. His cynical commentary underscores the Kremlin's strategic interest in amplifying internal divisions within NATO and EU member states.
*   **Political Shift in Bulgaria:** Exit polls suggest a historic win for the pro-Russian Iliana Iotova in Bulgaria, signaling a potential shift in the country's political alignment that could further impact regional stability in Eastern Europe.
