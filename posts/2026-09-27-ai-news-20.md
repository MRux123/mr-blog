## WORLD

* **Serbian Presidency in Turmoil:** Serbian President Aleksandar Vučić has announced his resignation following prolonged nationwide protests against alleged systemic corruption. This move signals a significant political vacuum in Belgrade and paves the way for early elections, likely increasing regional uncertainty.
* **Security Alert at RAF Fairford:** British counter-terrorism authorities have arrested five individuals near the RAF Fairford air base. The operation followed reports of suspicious vehicles approaching the facility, raising concerns over heightened security threats in the UK.
* **Iran-US Diplomatic Standoff:** Despite recent developments, the Iranian government continues to await an official response from the United States regarding a proposed cease-fire. Foreign Minister Abbas Araghchi suggested that despite President Trump's recent public rejection, the diplomatic window may not yet be closed due to conflicting messaging from the US administration.

## POLAND

* **Krakow Election Shake-up:** The first round of the mayoral election in Krakow has concluded with Łukasz Gibała securing a leading position with approximately 36% of the vote. Gibała, running without major party backing, will face Monika Piątkowska, representing the ruling coalition, in the second round.
* **Public Sentiment on the Government:** A new poll conducted by OGB for Polsat News and Newsmax indicates growing public dissatisfaction with the administration of Prime Minister Donald Tusk. The survey shows that 47% of respondents evaluate the government negatively, with "disappointment" cited as the primary emotional response among the electorate.
* **Far-Right Gains in France:** While occurring internationally, the rise of the far-right in the French Senate elections is being closely monitored by Polish political analysts as a potential indicator of shifting European political tides, which could influence future regional diplomatic and economic cooperation.
