## WORLD

* **Escalation in the Middle East:** Following a significant missile strike by Iranian forces that injured 160 people near an Israeli nuclear site, tensions have reached a critical inflection point. U.S. President Donald Trump has issued a direct ultimatum to Tehran, threatening the targeting of Iran’s power infrastructure should the Strait of Hormuz remain blocked.
* **Ukraine’s Tactical Role in the Middle East:** In an unprecedented development, reports indicate that Ukrainian specialized units are actively engaging Iranian-made "Shahed" drones in the Middle East. President Zelensky has framed this as a strategic deployment of Ukraine’s unique, combat-proven expertise in countering mass drone swarms.
* **France at the Ballot Box:** France faces a pivotal moment as the country holds the second round of municipal elections. The results, influenced by intense far-right political financing and the rise of local political movements, are expected to signal the future ideological trajectory of the French Republic.

## POLAND

* **Tragic Railway Accident in Podlasie:** A passenger train traveling from Hajnówka to Warsaw collided with a herd of bison. While no human passengers were injured, the incident resulted in the loss of three animals, highlighting the ongoing challenges of wildlife protection in the region's transport infrastructure.
* **Political Impasse over Mercosur:** The Polish government is currently experiencing an internal deadlock regarding a formal complaint to the European Court of Justice (ECJ) concerning the Mercosur trade agreement. Sources suggest the ruling coalition is hesitant to challenge the European Commission directly, preferring to wait for potential intervention from the European Parliament.
* **Security Concerns in Krakow:** Local authorities in Krakow have reported an increase in violent incidents, including a disturbing assault on the city's Main Market Square. The surge in public safety interventions has prompted discussions regarding the effectiveness of city security measures during weekend nightlife.
