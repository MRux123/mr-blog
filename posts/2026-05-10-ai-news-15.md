## WORLD

* **Diplomatic Breakthroughs in Tehran-Washington Relations:** Iran has officially submitted a 14-point response to US proposals aimed at ending the ongoing conflict. While specifics remain classified, the move—channeled through Pakistani mediators—marks a critical juncture in regional stability as the international community awaits a reaction from the Trump administration.
* **Escalating Tensions in Lebanon:** The conflict between Israel and Hezbollah has intensified, resulting in significant civilian casualties, including the deaths of eight members of a single family. This surge in violence threatens to derail recent ceasefire efforts, heightening concerns over the humanitarian impact on the region.
* **Political Stability in the United Kingdom:** Prime Minister Keir Starmer has publicly dismissed calls for his resignation, reinforcing his leadership position. Despite political pressure and speculation regarding potential leadership challenges, Starmer maintains that his mandate remains secure.

## POLAND

* **Historic Volleyball Championship:** Aluron CMC Warta Zawiercie has captured the Polish volleyball championship title for the first time in the club's history. The team secured victory after a decisive 3:1 win against the defending champions, Bogdanka LUK Lublin, in a highly competitive final series.
* **Environmental Warning for the Amazon:** New research published in the journal *Nature* highlights an alarming trajectory for the Amazon rainforest. Scientists warn that deforestation and climate change are pushing the region toward an ecological tipping point, which could see the ecosystem transform into a savanna-like landscape as early as the 2040s, posing a severe threat to global climate stability.
