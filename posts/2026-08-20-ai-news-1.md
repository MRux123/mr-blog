## WORLD

*   **Tragedy in the Central African Republic:** A devastating landslide at an artisanal goldmine in the village of Zamboye has resulted in the deaths of over 100 people. Recovery efforts are currently underway, though local authorities warn that the casualty count may rise further. The incident, triggered by unstable ground, highlights the severe risks associated with informal mining operations in the region.
*   **Diplomatic Tensions over Gaza Aid Worker:** The Australian government has expressed outrage following Israel's decision not to prosecute individuals involved in the killing of aid worker Zomi Frankcom. Canberra has announced plans to summon the Israeli ambassador to express formal discontent, describing the decision as "insulting" to the victim's family and the international community.
*   **Trump Issues Economic Warning to Iran Allies:** Former U.S. President Donald Trump has threatened "tremendous economic consequences" for any nation providing support to Iran. This statement coincides with the expiration of a 60-day ceasefire, heightening fears of further regional instability as diplomatic efforts struggle to find a viable path forward.

## POLAND

*   **Environmental Crisis in the Gliwice Canal:** Authorities have reported the discovery of three tons of dead fish in the Gliwice Canal. Initial investigations confirm that the mass mortality event was caused by a bloom of "golden algae" (*Prymnesium parvum*), a toxic species that has repeatedly threatened the ecological stability of Polish waterways. Local services are working to mitigate the environmental impact as monitoring continues.
