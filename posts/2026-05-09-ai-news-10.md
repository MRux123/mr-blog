## WORLD

* **Putin’s Victory Day Parade Signals Internal Vulnerability:** Russia’s annual Victory Day celebrations in Moscow were notably scaled back this year, eschewing the traditional displays of heavy military hardware. Analysts interpret the reduced scope—officially attributed to security concerns—as a reflection of the Kremlin’s growing sensitivity to the ongoing war in Ukraine and internal instability. Vladimir Putin used the platform to reiterate anti-NATO rhetoric and justify the "special military operation."
* **Hungary’s Political Transition:** A significant shift in Central European politics occurred as Peter Magyar, leader of the Tisza party, assumed power following a victory over Viktor Orbán’s Fidesz. The inauguration of the new government marks a potential turning point in Hungarian foreign and domestic policy.
* **Strategic Realignments in the Caspian:** Iran is increasingly utilizing the Caspian Sea as a vital trade and logistical corridor, strengthening its ties with Russia. This route serves as a strategic lifeline for Tehran, facilitating the transport of military and commercial goods to bypass international pressures.

## POLAND

* **Unidentified Drone Discovery Near Bartoszyce:** Security authorities are investigating the discovery of a drone bearing Cyrillic inscriptions near the town of Bartoszyce. Given the proximity to the Russian border, the incident has sparked concerns regarding unauthorized airspace incursions and regional security.
* **Economic Trends Among the Wealthy:** A recent report by Knight Frank highlights a significant increase in the number of ultra-high-net-worth individuals across Europe. The data reflects broader trends in wealth accumulation, with Poland seeing a growing population of multimillionaires over the past five years, signaling shift in the country's economic landscape.
