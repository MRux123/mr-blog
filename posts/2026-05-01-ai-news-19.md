## WORLD

* **Escalation in US-Iran Relations:** President Trump has expressed continued dissatisfaction with Iran’s proposals regarding the ongoing conflict. As the deadline for congressional approval for potential military action approaches, the administration remains skeptical, citing confusion within Iranian leadership as a primary obstacle to a diplomatic resolution.
* **Corruption Crisis in Mexico:** A significant indictment issued by the United States against the Governor of Sinaloa has sent shockwaves through Mexico. The legal move corroborates long-standing allegations that the boundaries between organized crime and the upper echelons of the Mexican government have become dangerously porous.
* **Historic Sporting Feat in Kenya:** Sabastian Sawe, the first athlete to complete an official marathon in under two hours, has returned to his native Kenya. His achievement has been met with national celebrations, highlighting a rare moment of unity and inspiration in the region.

## POLAND

* **Ukrainian Long-Range Strike:** Ukraine has reportedly conducted a sophisticated military operation targeting Russian fighter jets at a distance of 1,700 kilometers from the border. This strike marks a significant escalation in the reach and capability of Ukrainian offensive assets.
* **Environmental Emergency in the Beskids:** Firefighters have successfully contained a major forest fire on the slopes of Barania Góra in the Beskids. The blaze, which scorched approximately three hectares of forest and grassland, posed a difficult logistical challenge due to the rugged terrain of the region.
