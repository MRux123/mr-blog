## WORLD

* **Human Cost of the US-Israel-Iran Conflict:** The toll on civilians in the ongoing military confrontation between the US-Israeli alliance and Iran continues to rise. Recent reports highlight the tragic loss of non-combatant lives, including professionals and local activists, underscoring the severe humanitarian impact of the current geopolitical standoff.
* **Russian Influence Operations in Angola:** Legal proceedings have commenced in Angola against a group of Russian and Angolan nationals accused of orchestrating anti-government protests. The trial brings to light concerns regarding alleged foreign interference in local political stability, a claim which the defendants continue to refute.

## POLAND

* **Netanyahu Shifts Focus Toward Trump:** Israeli Prime Minister Benjamin Netanyahu has publicly signaled his confidence in Donald Trump's ability to protect Israeli interests, particularly concerning ongoing, albeit contested, diplomatic overtures toward Iran. This development highlights the shifting landscape of Middle Eastern diplomacy as Netanyahu relies on US mediation.
* **Easter Celebrations Scaled Back in Jerusalem:** Due to the volatile security environment in the Middle East, church authorities have made the difficult decision to cancel key Holy Week events in Jerusalem. The move reflects the broader impact of regional instability on religious observances and signifies the gravity of the current situation.
