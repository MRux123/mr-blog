## WORLD

*   **Lebanon-Israel Truce and Regional De-escalation:** A 10-day cease-fire between Israel and Hezbollah has triggered a mass return of displaced civilians to southern Lebanon. While Hezbollah remains cautious, keeping its forces on high alert against potential breaches, the diplomatic ripple effects are already visible. Iran has announced that the Strait of Hormuz is fully open to commercial shipping, signaling a broader attempt to ease regional tensions amidst ongoing peace talks with the U.S.
*   **Diplomatic Breakthrough on the Ukraine Conflict:** Kyiv has formally proposed a summit between President Volodymyr Zelensky and Vladimir Putin, suggesting Turkey as the mediator. Ukrainian officials, including Foreign Minister Andrij Sybiha, have expressed readiness for high-level talks, potentially involving Recep Tayyip Erdoğan and Donald Trump, as international efforts to find a peaceful resolution for the conflict intensify.

## POLAND

*   **Tusk’s Ultimatum to the Ruling Coalition:** Prime Minister Donald Tusk has issued a stern warning to his coalition partners, specifically the Polska 2050 party. The upcoming vote on the motion of no confidence against Climate Minister Paulina Hennig-Kloska is being framed as a definitive test of coalition loyalty. Tusk stated that failure to support the minister would lead to the dissolution of the current political alignment.
*   **Major Financial Investigation into Zondacrypto:** Polish authorities have launched a formal criminal investigation into the crypto-exchange platform Zondacrypto. The probe centers on allegations of fraud and money laundering. As part of this sweeping enforcement action, high-profile individual Marcin D. has been detained and hit with four formal charges, marking a significant step in the government's crackdown on illicit financial activities in the digital asset sector.
