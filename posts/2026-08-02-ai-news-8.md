## WORLD

*   **US-Iran Escalation Paused:** President Trump has announced the cancellation of planned strikes against Iran, citing progress in negotiations toward a "rapid deal." The decision follows requests from regional partners to allow diplomatic channels to resolve the current perimeter of the conflict, marking a potential cooling of tensions in the Middle East.
*   **European Far Right Reshaping Migration Policy:** The ongoing migration crisis in Ceuta has underscored a significant shift in European politics. The far right, previously relegated to the fringes, is now effectively setting the agenda and tone for the continent's response to asylum seekers, reflecting a dramatic change in European public sentiment since the 2015 refugee crisis.
*   **China’s Digital Surveillance Exposed:** An unsecured police dashboard has provided an unprecedented look into the state's surveillance apparatus. The leak reveals the systematic collection and aggregation of vast amounts of private data used by Chinese authorities to monitor foreign nationals within their borders.

## POLAND

*   **Tragic Fire at Allotment Gardens:** Emergency services discovered two charred bodies following a fire that broke out in a recreational garden shed. An investigation is currently underway to determine the cause of the fatal incident.
*   **Fatal Hit-and-Run Suspect Apprehended:** Police have successfully detained a 31-year-old suspect at the border. The individual was attempting to flee the country after being involved in a fatal vehicle collision.
*   **Public Health Alert Regarding Wildlife:** Authorities in Finland have implemented measures to restrict the movement of wildlife across borders to contain a dangerous virus, a move being closely monitored by Polish veterinary and environmental agencies due to the potential cross-border impact.
