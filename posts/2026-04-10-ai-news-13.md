## WORLD

* **Middle East Crisis Escalates:** The situation in Lebanon remains critical, with over a million people displaced due to renewed clashes between Israel and Hezbollah. Meanwhile, U.S. Vice President JD Vance has departed for high-stakes talks regarding Iran, as international pressure mounts to prevent the conflict from expanding despite Iranian demands for a broader regional truce.
* **Geopolitical Realignment in Asia:** In a significant diplomatic shift, a Taiwanese opposition leader has arrived in Beijing for a meeting with Xi Jinping. This marks the first visit by a sitting Kuomintang leader to China in a decade, signaling a potential change in cross-strait relations.
* **Political Shifts in Hungary:** Donald Trump has publicly endorsed Viktor Orbán just days before Hungary's parliamentary elections, praising his leadership. However, recent independent polls suggest that the opposition party TISZA remains a strong contender, potentially signaling a major shift in Hungarian governance.

## POLAND

* **Constitutional Crisis Deepens:** The standoff at the Constitutional Tribunal (TK) continues, as four newly sworn-in judges were met with a police cordon while attempting to enter the building to begin their duties. Despite their appointment in the Sejm, the President has questioned the validity of their oath, and the Tribunal’s president, Bogdan Święczkowski, has refused to grant them access to their official responsibilities.
* **Sudden Loss of Coaching Legend:** The Polish football community is in mourning following the unexpected death of Jacek Magiera. Known for his tactical knowledge and professionalism, Magiera served as the coach of Śląsk Wrocław between 2021 and 2024. Clubs across the country are set to hold a minute of silence in his honor.
* **Controversy Over School Reform:** The Ministry of National Education faces ongoing scrutiny regarding the grading of homework. Despite pressure from teachers and experts, the Ministry has confirmed that there are no immediate plans to reinstate formal grading for homework assignments, leaving the education community in a state of uncertainty.
