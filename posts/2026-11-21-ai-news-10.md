Representatives from nearly 200 nations remain unable to bridge deep-seated disagreements regarding the future of fossil fuel policies, casting doubt on the efficacy of current diplomatic efforts.

The Military Gendarmerie is currently securing evidence in Rudnik nad Sanem and Nowy Lipowiec, as U.S. senators warn that these hybrid attacks represent a "dangerous pattern" targeting NATO members.
*   **Political Realignment on the Right:** A new poll indicates a shifting landscape within the Polish conservative movement. Karol Nawrocki has surpassed Jarosław Kaczyński in public support as the preferred leader of the right, signaling a potential generational or structural change in the leadership of the conservative bloc.
