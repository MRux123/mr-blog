## WORLD

* **Escalation in Russia-Ukraine Conflict:** A massive drone strike involving over 600 unmanned aerial vehicles has targeted the Moscow region and surrounding areas, resulting in multiple casualties and widespread fires. This surge in kinetic activity follows increasing international tensions, underscored by Russia's explicit warnings toward the United Kingdom regarding its support for Ukraine through drone supplies.
* **Political Fragility in Washington:** U.S. President Donald Trump is facing significant domestic pressure as his approval ratings have hit a record low of 33% in the latest Ipsos polling. This decline coincides with high-stakes trade negotiations, as Canada faces a Tuesday midnight deadline to secure a deal and avoid the imposition of new U.S. tariffs.
* **Economic and Political Shadows in China:** Following the funeral of former Premier Zhu Rongji, the Chinese government has intensified public censorship to prevent nostalgia for the past from evolving into criticism of the current administration. Beijing remains concerned that comparisons between current economic stagnation and the optimism of the Zhu era could undermine political stability.

## POLAND

* **Systemic Failures in Waste Management:** The national deposit-return system for beverage containers is under intense scrutiny. Members of the ruling KO coalition have publicly criticized the program, citing malfunctioning infrastructure, long queues, and consumer frustration, labeling the current implementation as poorly managed and burdensome for the public.
* **Security Concerns Regarding Russian Conscription:** Polish intelligence and media reports highlight a concerning trend of Russian authorities quietly detaining military-age men. This "covert" mobilization strategy is viewed as a calculated effort to bypass public scrutiny while maintaining a steady flow of personnel for the ongoing conflict.
