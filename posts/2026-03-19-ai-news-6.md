## WORLD

*   **Escalating Tensions in the Middle East:** President Donald Trump has issued a stern ultimatum to Iran, warning that any further Iranian strikes on Qatari LNG infrastructure will result in the total destruction of the South Pars gas field. This development follows a period of heightened regional instability, which has prompted widespread concern over the potential for a severe energy crisis across Europe.
*   **Surge in Global Terrorism Trends:** Despite a decade-long downward trend in global terrorism-related deaths, a new report highlights a alarming spike in jihadist violence in Nigeria and the Democratic Republic of Congo. Nigeria recorded a 46% increase in fatalities over the last year, cementing its position among the world's most affected nations.

## POLAND

*   **Political Debate over ETS and Energy Prices:** A major domestic policy debate has emerged regarding the European Union's Emissions Trading System (ETS). Presidential advisors suggest that exiting the system could reduce energy costs by 30%, though such a move would likely trigger significant legal conflict with Brussels and potential infringement proceedings.
*   **EU Summit and Energy Security:** As Polish Prime Minister Donald Tusk attends an EU summit in Brussels, reports indicate that Poland has successfully secured a "safety mechanism" (bezpiecznik) designed to stabilize electricity prices, marking a critical point of negotiation for the government.
*   **International Friction over Media Regulation:** Tensions between Budapest and Warsaw have surfaced following accusations from a Hungarian government spokesperson, who publicly named a Polish employee of Meta, alleging that Facebook is actively interfering in Hungarian elections by suppressing pro-Orban content.
