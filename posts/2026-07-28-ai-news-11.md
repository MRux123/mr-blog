## WORLD

* **6.8-Magnitude Earthquake Strikes Japan:** A powerful earthquake has hit the Kumamoto prefecture in southwestern Japan. While local authorities have lifted the tsunami warning, the region is grappling with reports of fires and injuries. Emergency services remain on high alert as they assess the structural damage and provide aid to the affected communities.
* **Diplomatic Tensions at the UN:** The United States has staged a walkout during a UN Security Council meeting, signaling a deepening rift among major powers. The friction follows a contentious vote regarding the UN high commissioner for human rights, where the US aligned with North Korea and Russia in opposing the term extension, highlighting a complex and fragile global diplomatic landscape.
* **Zelensky to Address US Security Commitments:** Ukrainian President Volodymyr Zelensky is heading to Washington to press for bolstered US support. As Russia intensifies its campaign of missile attacks, Kyiv is seeking urgent commitments to strengthen its anti-ballistic missile infrastructure to protect critical urban centers.

## POLAND

* **Tusk Decries Political Patronage of Extremism:** Prime Minister Donald Tusk has issued a stern warning regarding the rise of public violence, explicitly linking the emboldened behavior of hooligans and criminal elements to what he describes as "political patronage" from opposition figures. The statement reflects growing polarization in the national discourse following a series of recent violent incidents.
* **Escalating Violence and Public Security:** A brutal assault on a Ukrainian couple in Wrocław has prompted an immediate investigation by the public prosecutor's office. The incident has sent shockwaves through the country, further fueling the ongoing debate regarding public safety and the treatment of foreigners in Polish cities.
* **Shifting Dynamics on the Right:** Internal political maneuvering on the Polish right-wing stage has intensified, as Sławomir Mentzen openly challenged Jarosław Kaczyński’s political relevance. This ongoing power struggle suggests a potential realignment in conservative politics as various factions compete for dominance ahead of future electoral cycles.
