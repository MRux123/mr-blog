## WORLD

*   **Massive Israeli Strikes on Iran:** The Middle East has entered a phase of heightened volatility following a series of coordinated Israeli military strikes against Iranian targets. The escalation coincides with reports of Hezbollah operations targeting Tel Aviv and drone strikes across the Persian Gulf, further destabilizing the region and creating a significant distraction for Western powers.
*   **Ukraine Security Guarantees and US Policy:** President Volodymyr Zelenskyy has revealed that the United States is conditioning long-term security guarantees for Ukraine on the cession of the Donbas region to Russia. Zelenskyy expressed concern over this strategy, suggesting that the US administration—preoccupied with the conflict in Iran—is prioritizing a swift conclusion to the war over Ukrainian territorial integrity. Additionally, Zelenskyy accused Russia of attempting to leverage intelligence sharing with Iran as blackmail against Washington.
*   **Diplomatic Shifts and Shadow Fleets:** Amidst the ongoing four-year conflict, international responses are intensifying. British Prime Minister Keir Starmer has authorized the military to intercept and detain "shadow fleet" Russian oil tankers circumventing sanctions. Meanwhile, Belarusian President Alexander Lukashenko has made a high-profile visit to North Korea, signaling deepening ties between Moscow’s primary allies.

## POLAND

*   **Orban’s Rhetorical Escalation:** Hungarian Prime Minister Viktor Orban has significantly intensified his anti-Ukraine rhetoric, labeling the country a "terrorist state" during a public rally in Ostrzyhom. This characterization marks a deepening diplomatic rift between Budapest and Kyiv, suggesting that Hungary’s government intends to maintain a confrontational stance regarding the ongoing war, refusing to align with the security consensus of its neighbors.
