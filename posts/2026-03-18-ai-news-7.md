## WORLD

* **Escalation in the Middle East:** Military tensions have reached a critical point as Israeli airstrikes hit Beirut and Tehran following a significant rocket offensive. The conflict has caused multiple casualties and widespread damage in Lebanon, marking a dangerous expansion of the regional power struggle.
* **Geopolitical Realignment:** The intelligence and military partnership between Russia and Iran is deepening. According to reports from the *Wall Street Journal*, this cooperation is strategically designed to bolster Tehran’s capabilities against the military influence of the United States and Israel, signaling a shift in the global security architecture.
* **Naval Setbacks for the US:** The USS Gerald R. Ford, the United States' largest aircraft carrier, is being withdrawn from the Middle East. Beyond persistent mechanical failures, including sewer system issues, reports suggest possible internal sabotage by crew members exhausted by an extended nine-month deployment, raising questions about military readiness and morale.

## POLAND

* **Controversy Over SAFE Program Veto:** Political friction surrounds President Karol Nawrocki’s decision to veto the EU-backed SAFE program. Recent IBRiS polling indicates that nearly 57% of the public disapproves of this move. Analysts are now focusing on how the opposition party, PiS, plans to address the shortfall in defense funding without the disputed European financial support.
* **Church-State Collaboration on Civil Protection:** The Ministry of Interior and Administration (MSWiA) is formalizing a new partnership with the Catholic Church to bolster national disaster resilience. Parish infrastructure is slated to serve as auxiliary hubs for emergency shelter and the distribution of humanitarian aid during crises, reflecting a shift toward utilizing non-governmental institutional networks for public safety.
