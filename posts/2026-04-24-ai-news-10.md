## WORLD

* **Escalation in US-Iran Standoff:** Tensions continue to rise as analysts suggest both nations are locked in a high-stakes diplomatic stalemate. Recent reports indicate that the Pentagon has internally debated potential measures against NATO allies perceived as failing to support US actions in the region, signaling a potential fracture within the alliance.
* **First Local Elections in Gaza:** Deir al-Balah is preparing for its first municipal elections in two decades. While Hamas has opted out of participation, residents view the vote as a crucial, long-awaited opportunity to engage in local governance and address pressing city infrastructure and social issues.
* **Syrian War Crimes Investigation:** A key suspect involved in the notorious Tadamon massacre during the Syrian civil war has been apprehended. The arrest marks a significant step for international justice, focusing on the brutal summary executions that occurred during the protracted conflict.

## POLAND

* **Pro-Animal Rights Prosecution Update:** The Regional Prosecutor's Office in Gdańsk has formally challenged a court decision regarding former senator Waldemar Bonkowski. The court had previously moved to dismiss enforcement proceedings based on the politician's health, following his conviction for the extreme mistreatment of a dog.
* **Fuel Price Regulations:** The Ministry of Energy has announced updated maximum fuel prices effective for the upcoming weekend. The move comes as authorities attempt to stabilize costs for consumers amidst fluctuating market conditions, with specific caps set for gasoline and diesel variants.
* **Legislative Succession:** Following the tragic death of MP Łukasz Litewka, legal procedures regarding his mandate have been initiated. According to the Polish Election Code, no by-elections will be held; instead, the seat will be offered to the next candidate on the electoral list who secured the highest number of votes, ensuring institutional continuity.
