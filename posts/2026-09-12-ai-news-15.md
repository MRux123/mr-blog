## WORLD

*   **Geopolitical Friction Over Irish Unification:** Donald Trump’s recent remarks in Dublin, labeling Irish unification as "fantastic," have sparked a swift diplomatic response. Downing Street and Northern Irish political figures have dismissed the comments as detached from the Good Friday Agreement, underscoring the delicate balance of post-conflict diplomacy.
*   **Escalating Winter Risks for Ukraine:** A senior UN official has warned that Ukraine is facing its most difficult winter since the start of the full-scale Russian invasion. With critical infrastructure under constant threat, the international community faces a race against time to secure essential power and water supplies for the population.
*   **Security Concerns in Europe:** Former Ukrainian Foreign Minister Dmytro Kuleba has issued a stark warning regarding European defense capabilities, suggesting that current missile stockpiles would be exhausted within a week in a large-scale conflict, highlighting a systemic crisis in European air defense readiness.

## POLAND

*   **Security Alert in Border Regions:** The Government Security Centre (RCB) has issued an urgent alert for the Lublin and Podkarpackie provinces following the increased activity of Russian drones in western Ukraine. The Operational Command of the Polish Armed Forces has raised the state of readiness for selected units in response to the proximity of the threat.
*   **Public Safety Incident in Gdynia:** An investigation is underway at a Gdynia high school following a dangerous incident during a school event where students were allegedly forced to consume snus (nicotine pouches). The situation resulted in multiple students requiring medical attention and a subsequent police intervention.
*   **Criminal Gang Dismantled in Warsaw:** Metropolitan police have successfully neutralized an organized crime group composed of Lithuanian nationals responsible for a violent robbery at a Warsaw shopping center. Authorities confirmed that the group was planning further criminal activities before their apprehension.
