## WORLD

* **Diplomatic Pressure on China:** Families of US scholars imprisoned in China are intensifying efforts to bring their cases to the forefront of international diplomacy. Sylvia Zin and other relatives are calling on President Trump to address these detentions directly during his upcoming high-stakes summit with Chinese leader Xi Jinping, highlighting the human rights tensions underlying bilateral trade and security discussions.

## POLAND

* **Strengthening NATO's Eastern Flank:** The Slovak Parliament has officially approved participation in the NATO operation "Eastern Sentry." This move signifies a tightening of regional military cooperation, reinforcing the defensive posture of the Alliance along its eastern borders in response to the current geopolitical climate in Central and Eastern Europe.

* **Lech Poznań's European Campaign Stumbles:** Lech Poznań has suffered a significant defeat in their opening match of the UEFA Europa League. The result serves as a wake-up call for the Polish side, which faces a challenging path ahead in the group stage as they look to recover from this disappointing inaugural performance.
