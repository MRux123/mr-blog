## WORLD

* **Record-Breaking Heatwave Paralyzes France:** France has officially recorded its hottest day since records began in 1947. The extreme temperatures have led to widespread power outages and sparked a heated national debate regarding the country's reliance on air conditioning, traditionally viewed with skepticism but now seen as an increasingly necessary adaptation to climate change.
* **Ebola Case Identified in France:** Health authorities have confirmed the first case of Ebola in France. The patient, a physician who recently returned from the Democratic Republic of Congo, is currently under medical supervision. The French Ministry of Health has moved to reassure the public, stating that the risk of transmission to the broader population remains low.

## POLAND

* **Legislative Move Toward Romania-Moldova Unification:** The Romanian Chamber of Deputies has passed a bill proposing the initiation of formal negotiations regarding the unification of Romania and Moldova. The move, championed by the SOS Romania party, marks a significant development in Eastern European geopolitics, potentially altering the regional landscape.
* **Legal Action Against Online Threats:** Polish prosecutors in Koszalin have charged a 61-year-old individual for inciting the assassination of high-profile political figures. The suspect allegedly posted multiple threats against the Polish President, the Prime Minister, and the President of Ukraine online over the past three months, prompting a firm response from the judiciary.
* **Iga Świątek Suffers Early Exit in Germany:** In a surprising setback ahead of Wimbledon, tennis world number one Iga Świątek has been eliminated in the second round of the WTA 500 tournament in Bad Homburg. The defeat by Emma Navarro marks a difficult transition back to grass courts for the defending Wimbledon champion.
