## WORLD

*   **Extreme Weather Patterns:** A dangerous heatwave is gripping parts of the United States and Mexico, with temperatures expected to soar 10-15°C above seasonal norms. Authorities have issued extreme heat warnings as the region braces for intense conditions. Simultaneously, South Africa faces catastrophic flooding, highlighting a period of global climate volatility.
*   **Impeachment Crisis in the Philippines:** Vice President Sara Duterte has been impeached for the second time. The potential conviction in a Senate trial poses a significant threat to her political future, as it would effectively disqualify her from any future presidential run.
*   **Health Alert on Cruise Vessels:** International health officials are monitoring a hantavirus outbreak linked to a cruise ship, with confirmed cases among American and French nationals. Patients are currently under medical isolation, prompting concerns over cross-border health safety protocols.

## POLAND

*   **Judicial Appointments Controversy:** President Andrzej Duda has proceeded with judicial nominations, sparking public discourse regarding the composition and political neutrality of the bench. The event has reignited debates over the current state of the Polish judiciary.
*   **Legal Uncertainty in High-Profile Cases:** Questions surrounding the potential extradition of former Justice Minister Zbigniew Ziobro remain unresolved, with legal experts suggesting that a formal application may not even be pursued by the authorities.
*   **Security Incident in Wrocław:** A violent incident occurred on a tourist vessel in Wrocław, where an armed individual disrupted a private event, causing damage and physical harm. The suspect has been remanded to three months in custody as police continue their investigation.
