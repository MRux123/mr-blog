## WORLD

*   **Devastating Earthquake in Indonesia:** A powerful 7.7-magnitude earthquake has struck the island of Flores, resulting in at least 38 confirmed deaths. Emergency search and rescue operations are currently underway as authorities attempt to reach survivors in what is described as one of the strongest seismic events to hit the region in decades.
*   **Diplomatic Efforts on Ukraine-Russia Conflict:** Amid growing uncertainty regarding U.S. policy under President Trump, European powers—specifically France, Germany, and the U.K.—are intensifying efforts to establish a framework for potential future peace negotiations. Diplomats are seeking to secure a role for Europe in the process, despite Russia showing minimal interest in engaging in formal talks.
*   **Tragic Passing of Academic Jason Arday:** The academic community is mourning the death of Jason Arday, the youngest Black professor at the University of Cambridge. His death follows a period of intense public scrutiny regarding accusations of plagiarism, with family members citing the pressure of a coordinated disinformation campaign as a significant factor in his final days.

## POLAND

*   **Armed Forces Day Celebrations:** Poland is observing its Armed Forces Day with state ceremonies in Warsaw, including a traditional parade and a solemn changing of the guard at the Tomb of the Unknown Soldier. The events, attended by top state officials including President Karol Nawrocki, commemorate the historical significance of the Polish military and the legacy of figures such as Józef Piłsudski.
*   **Strikes on Russian Strategic Facilities:** Ukrainian forces have reportedly executed a strike on the Progress Rocket and Space Centre in Samara, Russia. The facility, critical to the Russian satellite and aerospace industry, sustained fire damage. Officials in Kyiv have highlighted the strike as a targeted effort to degrade Russian space-based communication infrastructure.
