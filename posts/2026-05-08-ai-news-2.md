## WORLD

*   **Heightened Tensions in the Strait of Hormuz:** The fragile ceasefire between the United States and Iran has been severely compromised following an exchange of fire in the Strait of Hormuz. While President Trump maintains that the truce remains in place, the U.S. military confirmed strikes against Iranian targets in retaliation for attacks on American warships. Iran has countered these claims, accusing the U.S. of violating the agreement by targeting an oil tanker and coastal installations.
*   **Trade Ultimatum to the EU:** President Trump has issued a formal ultimatum to the European Union, demanding the swift approval of a new trade deal. This diplomatic pressure arrives at a critical juncture, as a specialized trade court has simultaneously ruled that the current U.S. global tariff policy violates domestic law, further complicating the administration's economic agenda.
*   **Hantavirus Outbreak on Cruise Ship:** A severe public health emergency is unfolding aboard the cruise ship *MV Hondius* in the Atlantic. The World Health Organization is investigating a suspected hantavirus outbreak that has already resulted in three fatalities. Emergency medical teams are currently struggling to evacuate critically ill passengers and crew members, including several British nationals, as the ship remains isolated.
