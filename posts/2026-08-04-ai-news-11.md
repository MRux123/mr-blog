## WORLD

* **Russia-Ukraine Conflict Escalation:** The intensity of the conflict continues to spiral, with Ukraine conducting strategic strikes on facilities operated by Wildberries in Russia. Simultaneously, Russian retaliatory drone attacks are increasingly targeting Ukraine’s civil infrastructure, specifically repurposing fuel stations as strategic military targets, further straining the humanitarian and economic situation in the region.
* **Biometric Border Security Disruptions:** The European Union's rollout of its new biometric border system has resulted in significant operational friction. Travelers are facing prolonged delays and systemic bottlenecks at major transit hubs, highlighting the logistical challenges of integrating high-tech security measures into existing border infrastructure.
* **Political Shifts in the UK:** Prime Minister Andy Burnham is facing an early litmus test in his tenure. While his modern communication strategy, utilizing social media and podcasts, has gained traction among voters, he now faces mounting pressure to address the fiscal sustainability of his electoral promises.

## POLAND

* **Cybersecurity Vulnerability:** Retail giant Żabka has fallen victim to a significant cyberattack, forcing the company to engage state security services. This incident highlights the growing threat profile of critical retail infrastructure within the Polish digital landscape.
* **Strategic Military Supply Concerns:** Reports indicate that U.S. military stocks of critical munitions are reaching depletion levels following operations related to Iran. This development raises questions regarding the logistical capacity of the West to sustain prolonged military engagement in multiple theaters of operation simultaneously.
* **Infrastructure and Public Safety:** The transport sector saw mixed developments, as a new section of the S19 expressway was opened to improve regional connectivity, even as authorities continue to combat reckless behavior on national roads, highlighted by recent high-speed driving enforcement actions on the S17 route.
