## WORLD

* **Escalation of Military Conflicts in the Middle East:** A series of coordinated nighttime attacks by Iran has significantly heightened regional volatility. Iranian forces launched drone and missile strikes against the United Arab Emirates, resulting in civilian casualties in Abu Dhabi and triggering emergency alerts across Bahrain and Kuwait.
* **Attack on U.S. Military Personnel:** In a major escalation, 12 American troops were injured—two critically—following an Iranian strike on the Prince Sultan Air Base in Saudi Arabia. This represents a severe breach of U.S. defensive perimeters and marks a critical turning point in the ongoing regional instability.
* **U.S. Legislative Standoff:** The U.S. House of Representatives is set to vote on Department of Homeland Security (DHS) funding. Despite the legislative push, experts anticipate the impasse will persist, further exacerbating operational chaos and security delays at major American airports.

## POLAND

* **Bank of America Reaches Settlement in Epstein Case:** A significant legal development has concluded, with Bank of America agreeing to pay $72.5 million to survivors of the late financier Jeffrey Epstein. The settlement addresses allegations that the institution facilitated the financial operations underpinning Epstein’s criminal activities.
* **Public Figure Legal Incident:** Tiger Woods has been taken into custody in Florida following a vehicular accident. The renowned golfer is facing charges related to operating a motor vehicle under the influence of controlled substances.
