## WORLD

*   **Counter-Terrorism Operation:** US President Donald Trump announced that American forces, in collaboration with the Nigerian military, successfully eliminated Abu-Bilal al-Minuki, identified as the Islamic State's second-in-command. The operation was characterized by the administration as a "meticulously planned" and "very complex" mission against a high-value target.
*   **UK Political Instability:** The British political landscape remains in flux following a turbulent week. While Prime Minister Keir Starmer retains his leadership, the emergence of rival Andy Burnham as a potential successor suggests a growing challenge to the current administration's grip on Downing Street.

## POLAND

*   **Pentagon Restructuring:** Reports indicate that the Pentagon is considering a significant reorganization of its military presence in Europe and Africa, specifically involving the downgrade of the US Army's command status in these theaters. While official sources remain cautious, the potential shift by mid-summer suggests a broader reassessment of US strategic priorities on the continent.
*   **Border Security:** Estonia has extended its nighttime closure of border crossings with Russia for an additional three months, through the end of August. Tallinn officials cited a persistent lack of good faith from Moscow and ongoing incidents involving Russian services as the primary drivers for these security measures.
