## WORLD

*   **US Diplomatic Push for Ukraine Peace:** President Trump’s envoys, Steve Witkoff and Jared Kushner, have arrived in Moscow to present a new proposal aimed at ending the war in Ukraine. In a significant gesture of goodwill, Vladimir Putin has ordered a temporary pause in strikes on Kyiv ahead of the high-stakes meetings. The delegation is expected to travel to Ukraine next to continue discussions.
*   **Military Escalation in the Middle East:** Tensions between the United States and Iran have spiked following retaliatory U.S. strikes on three Iranian oil tankers. The military action, described by the U.S. as a response to Iranian missile attacks on two American warships, marks a sharp deterioration in the relative calm previously observed in the region.
*   **Diplomatic Engagement in the West Bank:** U.S. Ambassador to Israel, Mike Huckabee, has taken the unusual step of traveling to the West Bank to meet with Palestinian representatives. The move comes amid international criticism regarding recent attacks carried out by Israeli settlers, signaling a potential shift in U.S. outreach efforts in the conflict zone.

## POLAND

*   **Tragedy at Greek Airshow:** A major incident occurred during an aviation display in Greece, leading to significant casualties and public shock. Local and international reports confirm that authorities are investigating the circumstances surrounding the disaster.
*   **Diplomatic Correspondence:** Polish internal affairs continue to intersect with European relations, as the Italian Prime Minister has sent a formal message of gratitude to President Karol Nawrocki, highlighting ongoing bilateral cooperation.
*   **Severe Weather in Recz:** A meteorological event caused chaos at a local harvest festival (*dożynki*) in Recz, where strong winds caused structural damage, tearing down tents and causing injuries that necessitated a response from the Air Ambulance Service (LPR).
