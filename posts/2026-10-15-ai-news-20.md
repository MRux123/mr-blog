## WORLD

* **Maduro Faces International Scrutiny:** Venezuelan leader Nicolás Maduro has officially pleaded not guilty to new charges related to torture in a New York court. Maduro has publicly rejected the legal document, marking a significant escalation in the international legal pressure surrounding his regime.
* **Houthi Strike on Yemeni Presidential Palace:** A ballistic missile attack by Iran-backed Houthi forces on the Maashiq Presidential Palace in Aden has left two people dead. While government officials survived the strike, the incident underscores the deepening volatility in the region and the escalating conflict involving Saudi Arabia and Iranian influence.
* **Public Unrest in Albania:** Violent protests have erupted in Albania, fueled by growing dissatisfaction with government corruption, cost-of-living increases, and local opposition to a controversial luxury resort project linked to Jared Kushner.

## POLAND

* **Strategic Naval Expansion:** The Polish military has officially commenced the construction of its first modern submarine, signaling a major breakthrough for the national fleet’s modernization program and defense capabilities.
* **Political Tensions Over Judiciary Appointments:** A significant internal conflict has emerged regarding the appointment of Maciej Berek to the Constitutional Tribunal (TK). While the ruling coalition defends the selection process, members of the administration, including Katarzyna Pełczyńska-Nałęcz, have expressed internal concerns about adherence to democratic standards, while opposition figures criticize the current government's handling of the institution.
* **Diplomatic Secrecy:** President Volodymyr Zelensky and Prime Minister Donald Tusk held a high-level meeting in Brussels, reinforcing a strong bilateral alliance. The two leaders discussed sensitive, confidential matters regarding Ukraine's defense, emphasizing mutual trust in the face of shifting global political landscapes.
