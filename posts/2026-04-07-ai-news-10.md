## WORLD

*   **Escalation in the Middle East:** Tensions between Israel and Iran have reached a critical juncture. Following the rejection of a cease-fire proposal by President Trump, the administration has issued an ultimatum demanding the reopening of the Strait of Hormuz. With reports of potential Israeli strikes on Iranian railway infrastructure and ongoing violence near the Israeli consulate in Istanbul, the region faces an increased risk of broader conflict. Adding to the geopolitical instability, reports suggest that Iran's leadership is in a state of flux due to the health crisis of Modżtaba Chamenei, prompting concerns over decision-making within the regime.
*   **Diplomatic Manoeuvring:** US Vice President JD Vance has arrived in Hungary to bolster support for Viktor Orbán ahead of the upcoming election, signaling a deepening alignment between the current US administration and the Hungarian government. Meanwhile, in East Asia, Taiwan’s opposition leader has traveled to China for a high-level meeting with Xi Jinping, framing the visit as a pursuit of a "bridge for peace" amidst heightened cross-strait sensitivities.

## POLAND

*   **Referendum on Krakow’s Leadership:** The electoral commissioner has officially scheduled a referendum for May 24th regarding the recall of Krakow’s president, Aleksander Miszalski, and the City Council. The vote will require a significant turnout—at least 3/5 of the participation level from the last mayoral runoff—to be legally binding, marking a pivotal moment for the city's political future.
*   **NATO Defense Spending Debate:** Polish Foreign Minister Radosław Sikorski has publicly engaged with Euro-MP Adam Bielan regarding a proposal to penalize NATO member states that fail to meet defense spending targets. Sikorski noted that the idea of stripping voting rights from countries failing to hit specific GDP percentages for defense aligns with proposals he previously advocated, highlighting the current administration's focus on collective security responsibilities within the alliance.
