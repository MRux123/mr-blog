## WORLD
* **Russian Aerial Assault on Ukraine:** A massive overnight missile and drone attack by Russian forces has resulted in significant casualties, with at least eight people killed, including an entire family in their home. The strikes mark another escalation in the ongoing large-scale aerial campaign against Ukrainian infrastructure.
* **Natural Disasters Intensify:** Extreme weather continues to ravage regions across the globe. Thousands have been evacuated on the Greek island of Crete as out-of-control wildfires, driven by powerful winds, threaten local communities. Meanwhile, in Japan, the death toll from a devastating earthquake has risen to 34, with rescue operations hampered by extreme heat.

## POLAND
* **Security Breach in Tarnawa Kolonia:** A high-level investigation is underway after an unidentified object—believed by officials to be a Russian cruise missile—impacted territory in Tarnawa Kolonia, approximately 100km from the Ukrainian border. Prime Minister Donald Tusk has visited the site, while the Lublin prosecutor's office confirmed that Ukrainian authorities have offered assistance in the forensic examination of the impact crater.
* **Emergency Procedures Questioned:** The incident has sparked internal debate regarding national security protocols. Reports from journalists who reached the site hours before military police have raised serious concerns about the responsiveness and operational efficacy of current defense procedures during border incidents.
* **International Response:** The European Union has issued a swift reaction to the violation of Polish airspace. Antonio Costa, President of the European Council, condemned the incident, stating that Russian aggression continues to pose a direct threat to the stability and security of the entire European continent.
