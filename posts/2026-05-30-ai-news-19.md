## WORLD

* **PSG Claims Back-to-Back Champions League Glory:** Paris Saint-Germain has successfully defended their European title, defeating Arsenal in a dramatic penalty shootout (4-3) in Budapest. The match, which ended 1-1 after extra time, confirms PSG’s dominance in European football for the second consecutive season.
* **Escalating Security Concerns in Romania:** Residents in a Romanian city are grappling with fear following a targeted drone strike on a residential block. The incident has intensified anxieties across the region regarding the spillover effects of ongoing conflicts.
* **Marcia Lucas Passes Away at 80:** The film industry is mourning the loss of Oscar-winning editor Marcia Lucas. Widely recognized as a creative architect of the original *Star Wars* trilogy, her contributions remain a cornerstone of modern cinema history.

## POLAND

* **Vandalism Targets Polish Consulate in New York:** In an act of diplomatic provocation, the Polish Consulate and the adjacent statue of Jan Karski were defaced with red paint. Local officials have condemned the attack, marking the second such incident in less than 72 hours, as an assault on international diplomatic decorum.
* **Diplomatic Friction Over Order of the White Eagle:** Political discourse in Poland has sharpened following President Karol Nawrocki's suggestion that the Order of the White Eagle could be rescinded from Ukrainian President Volodymyr Zelensky. Former Prime Minister Leszek Miller has expressed support for the initiative, citing ongoing grievances in bilateral relations, while other political voices have urged for de-escalation and pragmatic diplomacy.
* **Political Maneuvering Ahead of Krakow Elections:** Tensions between the Law and Justice (PiS) party and Confederation have reached a boiling point. Attempts by PiS to form a unified front for the upcoming Krakow mayoral elections have been met with harsh criticism from Confederation leader Krzysztof Bosak, who accused PiS of employing hypocritical rhetoric to mask internal candidate selection difficulties.
