## WORLD

*   **Escalation of Drone Warfare in Russia:** A significant escalation in the conflict has been observed as Ukraine intensifies drone strikes deep within Russian territory. Attacks on logistics infrastructure, including major warehouses near Moscow and St. Petersburg, have resulted in at least five fatalities. International observers suggest this marks a new phase in Ukraine’s strategic campaign to disrupt Russian military and supply operations.
*   **Maritime Security Concerns:** Tensions in the Black Sea have heightened following a drone strike on the Turkish cargo ship *Nadeżda* near the port of Novorossiysk. The incident, which left three crew members severely injured and necessitated a full evacuation, underscores the increasing risks to commercial shipping in the region amidst the ongoing hostilities.
*   **Political Challenges for the US Administration:** President Trump is currently navigating a period of internal and external friction, facing significant opposition from top allies regarding key policy directions. Analysts suggest the administration is struggling to maintain political cohesion in the face of recent legislative and diplomatic setbacks.

## POLAND

*   **Heatwave Mortality Surge:** The Ministry of Health has reported a alarming spike in mortality rates linked to the recent heatwave in Poland. Official data indicates nearly 500 deaths during the late June and early July period, a trend that peaked on June 29, when the country recorded a historic temperature of 40.5°C.
*   **Controversy Over Education Reforms:** Former Education Minister Przemysław Czarnek has challenged the government’s new policy on health education. By filing a motion with the Constitutional Tribunal, the opposition seeks to block the implementation of mandatory health and sexual education curricula, arguing that the government's approach is demoralizing and constitutionally questionable.
*   **Institutional Accountability and Healthcare Oversight:** The Polish healthcare sector is under intense scrutiny, with the National Health Fund (NFZ) finalizing its audit of the South Hospital in Warsaw. Simultaneously, the Ministry of Health has demanded an immediate explanation regarding irregularities reported at the prosectorium of the University Clinical Center at Banacha Street, highlighting ongoing concerns about administrative standards within public medical facilities.
