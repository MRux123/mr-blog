## WORLD

* **Tragic Escalation in Kyiv:** A massive Russian missile assault on the Ukrainian capital has resulted in at least 15 fatalities. The attack, which targeted residential and warehouse infrastructure, underscores a critical vulnerability in Ukraine's defense systems, as the nation faces a severe depletion of air defense interceptors, allowing Russian forces to intensify their aerial campaigns with greater impact on the civilian population.
* **Royal Status for Belgian Salesman:** In a rare case of historical clarification, Clément Vandenkerckhove has been officially recognized as a prince. The formal induction into Prince Laurent’s family follows confirmation of his royal parentage, resolving a long-standing question regarding the family’s lineage.

## POLAND

* **Presidential Intervention on Legislative Reform:** A significant political standoff has emerged following President Karol Nawrocki’s decision to refer the "Lex szarlatan" (Anti-Charlatan) bill to the Constitutional Tribunal. The bill, intended to regulate dangerous, unverified medical therapies, faced criticism from the government, with Prime Minister Tusk accusing the President of protecting fraudulent interests. Additionally, the President issued a veto regarding the reform of the "PKS plus" bus fund, further highlighting tensions between the executive and legislative branches.
* **Legal Action Against Former Influencer:** The judicial process involving Stuart K.-B., widely known as "Stuu," has reached a new milestone as the District Court for Warsaw-Mokotów mandated a one-month remand in custody. The influencer faces serious charges, including the sexual exploitation of minors under the age of 15 and the illegal distribution of alcohol to minors, with potential prison sentences reaching up to 12 years.
