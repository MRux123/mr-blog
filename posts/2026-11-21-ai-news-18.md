## WORLD

*   **Zelensky Dismisses "Yalta 2.0" Aspirations:** Following a high-stakes summit in Shenzhen between President Zelensky, Xi Jinping, and Donald Trump, the Ukrainian leader declared that Putin’s attempt to orchestrate a "Yalta 2.0"—a new division of global influence and borders—has failed. Zelensky emphasized that any future negotiations must involve European stakeholders, explicitly stating that no deal can be reached without the inclusion of Poland.
*   **Mexican Authorities Capture High-Profile Drug Lord:** In a major victory for the Mexican government, the military has successfully apprehended Fausto Isidro Meza Flores, known as "El Chapo Isidro." The operation, which involved significant gunfire, marks the removal of one of the FBI's top 10 most wanted fugitives, potentially destabilizing the regional criminal landscape.
*   **Escalating Tensions in the Baltic:** A Russia-bound vessel was targeted in a drone attack within the Gulf of Finland. Finnish defense authorities have pointed to Ukrainian involvement, highlighting the broadening theater of the ongoing conflict as Ukraine continues to target Russian logistical assets.

## POLAND

*   **Tragic Discovery in Zakopane:** Local services are investigating a grim incident near the popular Krupówki street. Following a report of a man falling from a window, police discovered the body of an elderly woman inside the residence, prompting a full-scale criminal investigation.
*   **Diplomatic and Political Friction:** Tensions have risen between Confederation leader Grzegorz Braun and journalist Agnieszka Gozdyra. Braun has initiated legal threats against the Polsat News host, who publicly labeled him a "Kremlin politician" during a broadcast—a move Gozdyra has refused to retract, setting the stage for a heated legal confrontation regarding media accountability.
*   **Cultural Recognition for Moszna Castle:** Despite broader political and economic challenges, the tourism sector received a boost as the historic Moszna Castle was awarded the prestigious Golden Certificate from the Polish Tourist Organization. Recognized as the "Oscars of tourism," the award highlights the site's success in attracting over one million visitors in recent years.
