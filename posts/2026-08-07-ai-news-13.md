## WORLD

*   **NATO on High Alert:** New intelligence reports from the United States, as cited by The Wall Street Journal, suggest that Vladimir Putin may be planning a limited offensive against a NATO member state this autumn. Analysts believe such a move would be intended as a stress test for the Alliance's cohesion and resolve.
*   **New Tripartite Defense Pact:** Saudi Arabia, Turkey, and Pakistan have formalized a landmark defense agreement. The pact includes a collective security clause, stating that an act of aggression against one signatory will be viewed as an attack on all, significantly shifting the balance of power in the Middle East.
*   **Escalating Nuclear Concerns:** Recent analysis of Iran’s nuclear program indicates that despite international conflict, core enrichment activities continue to advance. Experts warn that the program remains active, posing a persistent challenge to regional stability.

## POLAND

*   **Political Response to Deportation Proposals:** The government has addressed calls to deport Ukrainian men of conscription age who lack legal employment in Poland. Deputy Minister of the Interior Maciej Duszczyk dismissed these proposals as non-credible, citing data that challenges the feasibility and necessity of such mass deportations.
*   **Legislative Developments:** President of Poland has signed four new legislative acts, one of which—concerning the registry of liens—has been referred to the Constitutional Tribunal for follow-up review. The package also includes new regulations regarding real estate pricing data and maritime standards.
