## WORLD

*   **Heightened Tensions in Iran:** The conflict between the US, Israel, and Iran continues to escalate, with international concerns mounting over the civilian toll. While President Trump has signaled a temporary postponement of strikes on Iranian power plants to facilitate negotiations, Iranian officials have dismissed these claims as a tactical maneuver to mitigate market volatility and secure military advantages.
*   **Military Disaster in Colombia:** A Colombian Air Force C-130 Hercules has crashed in Puerto Leguizamo, carrying 110 soldiers. Early reports indicate that while the incident is being treated as a tragic accident, rescue operations are ongoing, with at least 69 military personnel confirmed as survivors.

## POLAND

*   **Security Concerns on the Eastern Flank:** A drone has been detected entering Lithuanian airspace and potentially crashing near Lake Lavysas. In response, Prime Minister Inga Ruginiene has convened an emergency session of the National Security Commission, highlighting the growing unease regarding regional security in the Baltic states.
*   **Political Confrontation:** A public rift has intensified between President Karol Nawrocki and Prime Minister Donald Tusk. Tusk has publicly rebuked the President, urging him to maintain decorum following an aggressive exchange between Nawrocki and the media, which touched upon sensitive issues related to foreign influence in Poland.
*   **Social Polarization regarding EU Membership:** A recent IBRiS poll reveals that while a solid majority of Poles support continued membership in the European Union, approximately 20% of the population favors leaving the bloc. Analysts suggest that the growing divergence in views regarding Poland’s specific role within the Union is a more pressing concern than the exit figures themselves.
