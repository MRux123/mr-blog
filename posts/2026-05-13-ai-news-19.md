## WORLD

* **Trump and Xi in High-Stakes Beijing Talks**: U.S. President Donald Trump has arrived in Beijing for a critical diplomatic mission. The agenda, dominated by trade, the status of Taiwan, and concerns regarding Iran, marks the first such high-level visit since 2017. Analysts are closely watching for signs of a thaw or further hardening of positions in the U.S.-China relationship.
* **Escalating Regional Conflicts and Shifting Alliances**: As tensions in the Middle East intensify, the conflict with Iran is accelerating authoritarian trends in the Gulf, with reports of mass arrests involving suspected Iran-linked cells. Simultaneously, Israeli Prime Minister Benjamin Netanyahu has made a clandestine visit to the UAE, marking what his office describes as a "historic breakthrough" in diplomatic ties.
* **The Erosion of Cease-Fire Utility**: Observers warn that the traditional concept of a cease-fire has lost its strategic meaning in the current global climate, particularly under the current U.S. administration. Analysts suggest that temporary truces have devolved into "performative diplomacy," serving as tools for political optics rather than genuine pathways to conflict resolution in theaters like the Russia-Ukraine war.

## POLAND

* **Tensions Rise Over Religious Education in Schools**: The Sejm has become a focal point of intense political friction following a committee meeting on the "Yes for Religion and Ethics in Schools" project. The debate, which saw opposition parties voice significant frustration, has highlighted deep ideological divides within the Polish parliament regarding the role of religion in the public education system.
* **Diplomatic Friction with the UK**: Dominik Tarczyński, a Member of the European Parliament, was denied entry to the United Kingdom, where he intended to participate in a patriotic march. British authorities cited the public interest in their decision. The incident has triggered a sharp domestic debate, with Foreign Minister Radosław Sikorski urging a more tempered approach, while opposition figures have publicly criticized the government's handling of the situation.
* **Increased Military Vigilance on the Baltic**: The Polish Ministry of National Defence confirmed that Polish fighter jets successfully intercepted a Russian Ił-20 reconnaissance aircraft operating over international waters. While the Ministry emphasized that no violation of Polish airspace occurred, the event underscores the heightened state of military alertness in the Baltic region.
