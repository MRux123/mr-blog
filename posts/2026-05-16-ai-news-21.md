## WORLD

* **Escalating Tensions in Transnistria:** The geopolitical situation in Eastern Europe has sharpened following a decree by Vladimir Putin regarding the accelerated issuance of Russian passports to residents of the separatist Transnistria region. Ukrainian President Volodymyr Zelensky has issued a stern warning, characterizing the move as a de facto recognition of the territory by Russia and a potential precursor to formal annexation.
* **Straits of Hormuz Crisis:** Amidst the ongoing instability in the Middle East, European nations have initiated direct diplomatic talks with Tehran. The objective is to secure the resumption of shipping through the Strait of Hormuz, a vital maritime artery that has remained effectively blocked for over two months, threatening global trade routes.
* **Tragic Incident in Italy:** Authorities in Modena are investigating a car-ramming incident that left eight pedestrians injured, four of whom remain in serious condition. Local residents are being credited for their swift intervention in preventing the suspect from fleeing the scene.

## POLAND

* **Tragic Death of a Minor:** Local authorities in the Barciany municipality (Warmian-Masurian Voivodeship) are investigating the death of a 13-year-old boy. Initial reports suggest a fatal physical altercation occurred, leading to the detention of three adult male suspects. 
* **Political Controversy Over Media Freedom:** President Karol Nawrocki has voiced strong criticism regarding a recent police intervention at the residence of editor Tomasz Sakiewicz. Highlighting the fundamental role of the press, Nawrocki emphasized that a functioning democracy requires journalists to operate free from pressure by state services.
* **Lech Poznań Secures Championship:** In a significant sporting achievement, Lech Poznań has successfully defended its title as the Polish football champion. The "Kolejorz" sealed their victory with a 3:1 win against Radomiak Radom, securing the top spot with one match remaining in the season.
