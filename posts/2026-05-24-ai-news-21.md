## WORLD

* **U.S.-Iran Diplomatic Breakthrough:** Negotiations between the United States and Iran have reached a critical juncture, with officials signaling that a peace deal is nearing completion. The agreement in principle focuses on reopening the strategic Strait of Hormuz and mandates that Iran dismantle its highly enriched uranium reserves. While the deal remains unsigned, the progress represents a significant cooling of regional tensions.
* **Escalating Health Crisis in DRC:** The Ebola outbreak in the Democratic Republic of Congo has surpassed 900 suspected cases, with 119 fatalities reported. Health organizations are struggling to contain the disease amidst ongoing security challenges, including attacks on medical personnel and critical resource shortages. The WHO maintains that while the local risk is "very high," the global threat level remains contained for now.

## POLAND

* **Krakow Political Shake-up:** Recent exit polls indicate that residents of Krakow have voted to dismiss both the mayor, Aleksander Miszalski, and the city council. Voter dissatisfaction appears to be primarily driven by the implementation of the Clean Transport Zone. In the wake of this referendum, former NIK president Marian Banaś has officially declared his intent to run for the city's highest office, signaling a major shift in local governance.
* **Charitable Milestone:** The "Łatwogang" initiative has successfully concluded a major fundraising cycling campaign, raising over 20 million PLN for children's medical treatments. Despite hitting their target, the campaign continues to focus on raising the remaining 15.4 million PLN required for the specialized treatment of a two-year-old child, highlighting the power of social-media-driven philanthropy in Poland.
