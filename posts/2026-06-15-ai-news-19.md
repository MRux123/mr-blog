## WORLD

* **U.S.-Iran Preliminary Deal:** A significant de-escalation in the Strait of Hormuz has been reached following a preliminary agreement between the U.S. and Iran. While the deal aims to reopen the critical shipping lane, the absence of publicly disclosed terms suggests that core disagreements remain unresolved, casting doubt on the long-term stability of the region.
* **Global Surge in Attacks on Education:** New data from the GCPEA reveals a alarming 40% increase in violent attacks on schools, students, and staff. With over 10,600 casualties recorded across 83 countries, particularly in conflict zones like Ukraine, Colombia, and the DRC, the global protection of educational infrastructure is facing a critical failure.
* **UK Implements Social Media Ban:** In a bold move toward child protection, the British government under Prime Minister Keir Starmer has announced a formal ban on social media access for individuals under the age of 16, mirroring similar legislative trends seen in Australia.

## POLAND

* **"Tramway Scandal" Rocks Silesia:** The political landscape in the Silesian region has been shaken by a corruption scandal involving infrastructure modernization. Wojciech Król, a high-ranking official of the Civic Coalition (KO), has been dismissed following requests by the European Public Prosecutor's Office to lift his immunity over allegations of paid protection.
* **Diplomatic Friction with Ukraine:** President Volodymyr Zelensky’s decision to bypass Poland during his transit to the G7 summit in France has sparked intense speculation regarding the state of bilateral relations. Despite official denials from Kyiv, the repeated logistics changes suggest persistent underlying tensions between the two governments.
* **KO Party Internal Purge:** The Civic Coalition is facing a crisis of integrity following reports that Warsaw councilor and physician Dawid Kacprzyk amassed 1.6 million PLN during his medical specialization. The controversy has resulted in his removal from the party and triggered extensive audits across municipal healthcare facilities.
