## WORLD

*   **Escalating Conflict in Lebanon:** Israeli military strikes in southern Lebanon have resulted in at least 11 to 12 fatalities, further destabilizing the region. This surge in violence threatens the viability of the US-brokered ceasefire aimed at de-escalating the long-standing conflict between Israel and Hezbollah.
*   **Political Polarization in Germany:** The "firewall" against the far-right in Germany is under unprecedented pressure following a decisive electoral victory by the Alternative for Germany (AfD) party. The result has triggered a national debate on political strategy and the future of Germany’s consensus-based democratic approach to extremism.
*   **Tensions Over Ratko Mladić Funeral:** The funeral of convicted war criminal Ratko Mladić has drawn thousands of supporters, sparking international criticism. Despite the Serbian government's attempt to frame the event as a private matter, the attendance of state officials and the praise from state-aligned media have drawn warnings from the EU regarding the glorification of war criminals.

## POLAND

*   **Legal Proceedings Against Former Police Chief:** The trial of former Police Commander-in-Chief Jarosław Szymczyk has officially commenced. Szymczyk faces charges related to the accidental discharge of a grenade launcher within the National Police Headquarters, including the illegal possession and transportation of military weaponry from Ukraine.
*   **Regional Security Concerns:** The Ukrainian military intelligence (HUR) has issued a warning to Poland and other Eastern flank NATO members. Officials suggest that Russia is set to aggressively test NATO’s "red lines," particularly through incursions into sovereign airspace, as a tactic to undermine support for Kyiv.
*   **PKOl Leadership Crisis:** Radosław Piesiewicz, head of the Polish Olympic Committee (PKOl), has been placed under arrest. The development raises significant questions regarding the future governance and stability of the organization.
