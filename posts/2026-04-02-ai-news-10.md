## WORLD

* **Iran-US Conflict Intensifies:** President Donald Trump has issued a severe ultimatum to Iran, warning of intensified military operations over the coming weeks. The rhetoric has escalated significantly, with the Iranian military leadership vowing to continue the conflict until the US faces "humiliation and capitulation." The situation remains volatile as Gulf nations and Israel brace for potential new attacks.
* **Counter-Terrorism Operations in Syria:** International authorities have successfully dismantled a network linked to the former Assad regime. Antoine Kassis, a cousin of Bashar al-Assad, has been convicted in a sting operation for conspiring to sell weapons to a Colombian militia, marking a significant blow to illicit arms trafficking networks.
* **Russian Dissidence Under Pressure:** Amidst a severely restricted media landscape in Russia, Nobel Peace Prize laureate Dmitri A. Muratov continues to challenge state narratives. His refusal to go silent remains a rare, defiant voice within a country that has seen most independent journalists flee into exile since the 2022 invasion of Ukraine.

## POLAND

* **Arrests Linked to Czech Terror Plot:** The Polish Prosecutor's Office has charged two young Polish citizens, aged 22 and 23, with facilitating a terrorist act. The duo is accused of aiding in the arson of an Elbit Systems production facility in Pardubice, Czech Republic, and faces up to 15 years in prison.
* **Public Safety Alert in Jelenia Góra:** Elevated levels of radon, a radioactive gas, have been detected at a primary school in Jelenia Góra. Authorities have recorded concentrations exceeding 2000 Bq/m3 in several rooms, prompting immediate remediation efforts, including basement renovations and strict ventilation protocols, following recommendations from the Institute of Occupational Medicine.
* **Criminal Probe into Counterfeit Supplement Ring:** A major police operation in Kraków has uncovered an illicit network manufacturing and distributing fraudulent medical supplements. The investigation revealed dangerous production practices, including the use of animal fur as an ingredient in the counterfeit capsules, which had been reaching markets across the country.
