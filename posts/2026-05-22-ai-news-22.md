## WORLD

* **Gaza Flotilla Detainees Allege Abuse:** Activists involved in the recent Gaza flotilla have leveled serious accusations against Israeli forces, reporting physical assault, humiliation, and instances of sexual violence during their detention. The Israel Prison Service has categorically denied these claims, labeling them as false, as international scrutiny regarding the treatment of detainees continues to intensify.

## POLAND

* **US Democrats Seek Clarification on Zbigniew Ziobro’s Visa:** A diplomatic inquiry has been launched by two Democratic members of the U.S. House of Representatives directed at Secretary of State Marco Rubio. Lawmakers are demanding transparency regarding the involvement of Deputy Secretary Christopher Landau in allegedly expediting a U.S. visa for Poland's former Justice Minister, Zbigniew Ziobro. The request highlights growing concerns among American legislators regarding the political circumstances surrounding the former minister's travel.
