## WORLD

* **Escalation in Ukraine-Russia Conflict:** Ukraine has conducted a significant, record-breaking strike deep into Russian territory, targeting critical gas infrastructure 3,000 kilometers from the border. This move signals an intensification of long-range operations aimed at disrupting Russia's energy-export capabilities.
* **Middle East Nuclear and Military Risks:** Tensions in the Middle East are reaching a critical threshold. Iran has declared a new maritime restricted zone amid growing international concern over accelerated construction at suspected nuclear sites deep underground. Simultaneously, Saudi-Houthi clashes in Yemen are escalating, pushing the region toward full-scale war and threatening global oil-shipping routes.
* **Security Threat to Leadership:** A significant security incident occurred involving the Ukrainian President; the Norwegian Prime Minister confirmed that Volodymyr Zelenskyy’s aircraft was narrowly missed by a drone during his departure to attend the royal funeral of King Harald V.

## POLAND

* **Major Transport Safety Overhaul:** Following a tragic rail accident involving an Intercity train in Sokolniki Suche, the Polish government has announced a swift and radical tightening of traffic regulations. New measures, aimed at curbing illegal crossings at rail intersections, are set to be introduced at the next Council of Ministers meeting, including a three-month suspension of driving licenses for violators.
* **Political Fractures and Public Discourse:** The ongoing internal divisions within the Law and Justice (PiS) party have drawn criticism from former President Andrzej Duda. Labeling the public disputes as "degrading," Duda expressed concern over the political instability within the conservative camp, emphasizing a need for unity while distancing himself from current party maneuvers.
