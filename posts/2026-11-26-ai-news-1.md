## WORLD

* **Global Trade Tensions Spike:** The United States has initiated a significant shift in trade policy, announcing a new wave of tariffs targeting major economic partners, including China and the European Union. The move has triggered immediate concerns regarding global supply chain stability and potential retaliatory measures.
* **Financial Market Turbulence:** US stock markets are facing substantial downward pressure as investor anxiety surrounding the rapid development and integration of Artificial Intelligence reaches a boiling point. The tech-heavy Nasdaq index recorded a sharp decline of nearly 4% in early trading, reflecting broader systemic fears.
* **Developments in Middle Eastern Geopolitics:** Israeli judicial authorities have recommended the dismissal of the most serious corruption charges against Prime Minister Benjamin Netanyahu. This legal shift may significantly impact the political landscape in Israel, potentially altering the trajectory of the long-standing trial that has divided the nation.

## POLAND

* **Russian Oligarchs File Lawsuit Against Poland:** A high-stakes legal battle has emerged as two Russian businessmen, Sergey Kolesnikov and Igor Rybakov, have filed a lawsuit against Poland seeking over 462 million PLN in damages. The claim pertains to the state’s takeover of a mineral wool factory in Wykraty, highlighting the complexities of seizing sanctioned assets.
* **Diplomatic Strategy Regarding US Presence:** The Polish government has provided an update on the long-term goal of establishing a permanent US military base in Poland. Deputy Defense Minister Paweł Zalewski stated that an official decision is expected by the turn of the year, dismissing recent media reports suggesting significant delays in the negotiation process with the White House.
* **Security and Weather Alerts:** Authorities have issued urgent warnings regarding icing conditions across six Polish provinces following sudden weather shifts. Concurrently, regional security remains a primary concern following reports of a Russian ballistic missile attack targeting Kyiv, which continues to heighten alertness levels among Polish border security services.
