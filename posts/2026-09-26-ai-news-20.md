## WORLD

* **Escalation in the Strait of Hormuz**: President Trump has formally rejected the Iranian proposal concerning the Strait of Hormuz, setting a seven-day ultimatum. Washington claims the diplomatic outreach from Tehran is a tactical maneuver born out of military desperation.
* **Healthcare Crisis in the Global South**: A new report from Accra Reset highlights a worsening healthcare crisis in Africa, directly attributed to the recent withdrawal of key U.S. aid programs and the ongoing impact of global economic instability.
* **Public Mobilization in Paris**: Pope Francis addressed a crowd of approximately 700,000 in Paris, focusing his message on the potential of youth activism and their role in addressing contemporary societal challenges.

## POLAND

* **Diplomacy and the G20 Dilemma**: The potential attendance of Polish representative Nawrocki at the G20 summit alongside Vladimir Putin has sparked a national debate. Former President Aleksander Kwaśniewski suggested that while attendance is necessary, diplomatic protocol regarding the Russian leadership must remain firm.
* **Defense Strategy and U.S. Presence**: The Ministry of National Defense has signaled an upcoming timeline for critical decisions regarding the establishment of a permanent U.S. military base in Poland, a move seen as vital to the country's long-term security architecture.
* **Solidarity in Jarosław**: In the wake of a tragic attack at a local abbey, the community of Jarosław held a march of silence. The event served as both a memorial for the deceased priest and a collective plea against collective culpability and social division.
