## WORLD

* **US Retaliatory Strikes on Iran:** Tensions in the Middle East have reached a critical inflection point following the downing of a U.S. Apache helicopter over the Strait of Hormuz. President Donald Trump has officially confirmed the incident, labeling it an act of aggression that necessitates a military response. U.S. Central Command has initiated strikes against Iranian targets, marking a significant escalation in regional hostilities.
* **Myanmar Conflict Intensifies:** Frontline reports from Myanmar indicate a deteriorating situation for rebel forces. Military operations continue to suppress opposition groups, with reports of forced conscription into the national army as the junta attempts to regain territorial control.

## POLAND

* **Diplomatic Friction with London:** The British government has issued a formal statement expressing a desire for continued cooperation with European partners, following critical remarks from Polish Prime Minister Donald Tusk. The Prime Minister had publicly questioned Poland’s exclusion from a recent high-level meeting in London involving Ukrainian President Volodymyr Zelensky.
* **Tensions Over Krakow Mayoral Bid:** The upcoming municipal elections in Krakow have sparked internal political debate. Senator Monika Piątkowska, the candidate for the presidential office representing the Civic Coalition (KO) and the Polish People’s Party (PSL), is facing scrutiny regarding her ties to the city. Critics, including MP Rafał Komarewicz, have questioned her local roots, fueling a broader discussion about political candidate selection processes in Poland.
