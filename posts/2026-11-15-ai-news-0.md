## WORLD

*   **SpaceX Achieves Historic Rocket Catch:** SpaceX has reached a significant milestone in aerospace engineering by successfully capturing a portion of its Starship rocket upon its return to Earth. The operation, executed using mechanical arms affectionately dubbed "chopsticks," marks a new era in the reusability of heavy-lift launch vehicles.
*   **Macron Backs Ukrainian NATO Integration:** During recent remarks, French President Emmanuel Macron affirmed that there is a clear place for Ukraine within the NATO alliance. Macron emphasized that Ukraine is poised to play a pivotal role alongside the Alliance, highlighting the need for a coherent European strategy regarding both Russia and the United States.
*   **Civil Unrest in France:** Significant protests across France have escalated into violent clashes with local law enforcement, resulting in a large number of arrests. The situation remains volatile as authorities struggle to maintain public order amid growing social tensions.

## POLAND

*   **Diplomatic Tensions Following Monument Vandalism:** The Ukrainian Embassy has officially blamed Russian provocateurs for the recent wave of vandalism targeting monuments across Poland, including the Memorial to the Heroes of the Ghetto. The incidents, which involve pro-UPA graffiti, are being treated by the embassy as deliberate attempts to destabilize Polish-Ukrainian relations.
*   **Public Safety Concerns in Krakow:** Following a high-profile shooting on the Main Market Square, updates from the regional governor's office indicate that while some victims have been discharged, nine individuals remain hospitalized. The incident has prompted a heightened state of alert and scrutiny regarding security in urban centers.
*   **National Football Team Faces Defeat:** The Polish national football team suffered a 1-2 loss against Romania in the UEFA Nations League. Despite a late rally in the 82nd minute, the team could not overcome the deficit created by two quick goals conceded in the second half, sparking internal discussions regarding the team's tactical performance.
