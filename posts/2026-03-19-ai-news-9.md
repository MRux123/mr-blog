## WORLD

* **Escalating Conflict in the Middle East Disrupts Global Energy Markets**: Fresh strikes on Qatar’s critical energy infrastructure—specifically the Ras Laffan facility—have triggered a sharp surge in global energy prices. With oil prices climbing toward $114 a barrel and gas benchmarks rising by over 30%, global markets are bracing for a prolonged energy crisis reminiscent of 2022. The situation remains volatile as the U.S. warns of potential retaliatory measures against Iranian energy assets.
* **Diplomatic Tensions and Regional Stability**: While DR Congo and Rwanda have engaged in talks in the U.S. to de-escalate regional hostilities, the broader geopolitical landscape remains fragile. Meanwhile, the E.U. is facing a significant political hurdle as Prime Minister Viktor Orban utilizes the pending €90 billion loan to Ukraine as leverage ahead of the Hungarian elections, complicating European unity in the face of external threats.

## POLAND

* **NBP Faces Unprecedented NIK Audit**: The Supreme Audit Office (NIK) is launching an unprecedented investigation into the National Bank of Poland (NBP). The move is widely seen as a direct regulatory response to the controversial "Safe 0%" mortgage program, signaling heightened scrutiny of central bank policy.
* **Proximity of Conflict and Diplomatic Friction**: Regional security concerns are mounting in Poland following reports of Russian military strikes in Novovolynsk, Ukraine, occurring in dangerous proximity to the Polish border. Simultaneously, diplomatic tensions between Warsaw and Moscow have intensified after a Polish court approved the extradition of archaeologist Aleksandr Butiagin to Ukraine, a decision the Kremlin has publicly condemned as a "political trial."
