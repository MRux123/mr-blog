## WORLD

* **Escalation in Russia:** Russian authorities have described a Ukrainian drone strike on St. Petersburg as "unprecedented," with local officials taking the rare step of advising residents to shelter indoors. This move marks a significant shift in the operational reach of the conflict.
* **Geopolitical Tensions in the Middle East:** Lebanon has reported the deaths of three soldiers following an Israeli strike on a vehicle in the southern region. The Israeli military has initiated an investigation into the incident amidst ongoing confrontations with Hezbollah.
* **Strategic Energy Infrastructure:** Algeria has officially commenced construction on a 4,000 km trans-Saharan pipeline designed to transport natural gas from Nigeria to Europe. This project is a critical attempt to bolster European energy security and reduce reliance on Russian supplies.

## POLAND

* **Diplomatic Relations with Kyiv:** Deputy Prime Minister Władysław Kosiniak-Kamysz met with Kyrylo Budanov in Warsaw to address diplomatic friction between Poland and Ukraine, specifically concerning the Ukrainian government’s recent decision to name a military unit after the "Heroes of the UPA."
* **Tragic Domestic Incident:** Investigations are ongoing following a harrowing incident in Włocławek, where a five-year-old child fell from an 11th-floor window. Authorities confirmed that the child was under the care of a mother who was reportedly unable to provide adequate supervision at the time.
* **Infrastructure Disruption:** Rail traffic in the Pomeranian Voivodeship has been suspended following the derailment of six freight train wagons at the Jezierzyce Słupskie station. Services are working to clear the line and restore transit.
