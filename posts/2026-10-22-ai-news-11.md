## WORLD

* **Russia-U.S. Diplomatic Thaw:** Moscow is successfully navigating efforts to separate the war in Ukraine from diplomatic and business negotiations with the United States. Reports suggest that Russia is nearing a significant diplomatic prize by restoring ties while refusing to concede on its ongoing military objectives.
* **Escalating Afghanistan-Pakistan Conflict:** A volatile "open war" has broken out between Afghanistan and Pakistan. Following eight months of low-level hostilities, both nations have increasingly turned to proxy groups, significantly destabilizing the region and placing a severe burden on local civilian populations.
* **First Human Fatality from Shamonda Virus:** Health authorities in France have confirmed the first-ever human death caused by the Shamonda virus. Previously known to only affect livestock, this unprecedented case has prompted urgent scrutiny from global epidemiological organizations.

## POLAND

* **Political Realignment in the Sejm:** The Confederation party has secured a significant political gain by welcoming Tomasz Rzymkowski, a former deputy minister from the PiS government, into its parliamentary club. This move strengthens the party's position as it integrates the former ruling coalition member.
* **Security Intervention in Bukowina Tatrzańska:** Law enforcement agencies have conducted a major operation in Bukowina Tatrzańska, resulting in the arrest of a 44-year-old individual who was found in possession of an illegal arsenal. The case remains under investigation as authorities assess the potential threat posed by the discovery.
