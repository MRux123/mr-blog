## WORLD

*   **Escalating Heatwaves in Europe:** Spain and France are preparing for extreme heatwaves, with temperatures expected to exceed 40°C. The conditions are severely complicating efforts to contain wildfires, particularly near Bordeaux, as emergency services struggle with the compounded threat of high temperatures and fire spread.
*   **Johnson & Johnson Settlement Offer:** The pharmaceutical giant has proposed a settlement of up to $5.5 billion to resolve long-standing litigation regarding its talc-based products. The legal battle centers on allegations that the products contain carcinogens, a claim the company seeks to resolve through this massive financial commitment.

## POLAND

*   **Legal Developments in the Żak Case:** Judicial proceedings in the case involving the defendant known as Żak have hit a snag as several co-defendants failed to report to authorities for detention. Police have officially initiated a search for at least one individual involved in the case.
*   **Controversial Migration Agreement:** Prime Minister Viktor Orbán has approved a deal allowing China to facilitate the movement of migrants into Hungary. The decision has sparked significant political discourse regarding national sovereignty and migration policy within the region.
