## WORLD

* **Trump Announces Iran Peace Deal:** Donald Trump has declared that an agreement to end the war with Iran has been signed. While details remain sparse, the former president indicated that the Strait of Hormuz is expected to be opened by Friday. The international community awaits the official release of the agreement's contents amid ongoing ambiguity.
* **US Air Force Asset Lost:** A B-52 bomber crashed in California shortly after takeoff. Military authorities are currently investigating the circumstances surrounding the incident, as the loss of this strategic aircraft raises concerns regarding operational readiness.

## POLAND

* **Diplomatic Standoff with Kyiv:** Presidential spokesperson Rafał Leśkiewicz has issued a firm ultimatum to the Ukrainian government regarding the naming of a military unit after UPA figures. President Zelensky has been given a short window to address Poland’s concerns, as Warsaw signals that the current lack of response is reaching a diplomatic breaking point.
* **Economic Policy Pivot:** The Polish government has announced the gradual phase-out of the "CPN" fuel subsidy program, which provided lower VAT and excise rates. Officials cited the intensifying instability in the Middle East as the primary driver for this fiscal recalibration, signaling potential inflationary pressures ahead.
* **High-Profile Assassination Investigation:** Authorities are investigating a targeted killing in Biała Podlaska involving a prominent critic of the Kremlin. The nature of the attack, which officials described as an "execution," has prompted concerns about security and foreign influence within Polish borders.
