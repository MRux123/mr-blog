## WORLD

*   **US-Iran Tensions Peak in Strait of Hormuz:** President Donald Trump has confirmed that the U.S. Navy destroyer USS Spruance intercepted and damaged an Iranian-flagged cargo ship, the *TOUSKA*, in the Strait of Hormuz. The incident marks a significant escalation in the ongoing blockade, as both nations contest maritime control.
*   **High-Stakes Negotiations Loom:** Despite the military confrontation, the White House is preparing for urgent diplomatic talks in Islamabad, Pakistan. A high-level delegation, led by Vice President JD Vance, is expected to participate, though reports from Tehran remain contradictory regarding their willingness to negotiate under the threat of U.S. airstrikes on critical infrastructure.
*   **Political Shift in Bulgaria:** Bulgaria has moved toward a potential shift in geopolitical alignment following parliamentary elections. Projections indicate a victory for the "Progressive Bulgaria" party, led by former President Rumen Radev, a figure frequently compared to Viktor Orbán due to his pro-Russian stance.

## POLAND

*   **Public Outrage Over Archbishop’s Remarks:** Former Kraków Metropolitan Archbishop Marek Jędraszewski is facing severe criticism after comparing modern youth to the era of Hitler, suggesting a state of "helpless masses" for labor. The rhetoric has sparked widespread condemnation from political figures, including Krzysztof Brejza and Bartosz Arłukowicz, with calls for legal accountability.
*   **Security Crisis in Kyiv Affects Polish Discourse:** Domestic attention remains fixed on the aftermath of a tragic terror attack in Kyiv, which resulted in the death of six civilians. The subsequent resignation of the Ukrainian patrol police chief, Jewhen Żukow, following reports of law enforcement fleeing the scene, has resonated deeply within Polish media and diplomatic circles.
