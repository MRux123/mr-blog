## WORLD

*   **UN General Assembly Marked by Geopolitical Friction:** The 81st session of the UN General Assembly has become a focal point for escalating global tensions. Ukrainian President Zelensky warned of the deteriorating global impact of Russia’s war, highlighting rising fuel costs and the proliferation of drone technology. Simultaneously, Iran’s President Masoud Pezeshkian issued a defiant message to the U.S., rejecting the threat of "annihilation" in exchange for peace, further complicating the administration's Middle East policy.
*   **Shifting Strategic Alliances:** The U.S. administration is currently recalibrating its global posture. While President Trump prepares to host Chinese President Xi Jinping for high-stakes talks on trade and artificial intelligence, his administration is also actively pivoting toward a deeper, potentially historic partnership with Mexico. Meanwhile, the U.S.-led "Board of Peace" has announced a $2.45bn reconstruction plan for Gaza, aiming to stabilize the region amidst intense diplomatic pressure.

## POLAND

*   **Heightened Polish Rhetoric at the UN:** Poland has taken an increasingly assertive stance on the international stage. Foreign Minister Radosław Sikorski delivered a pointed rebuke to Russian representative Sergey Lavrov during a UN Security Council session, declaring that Russia will not win the war in Ukraine despite continued hostilities. 
*   **Presidential Diplomacy in New York:** Polish President Karol Nawrocki addressed the General Assembly, framing the current global situation as one of the most dangerous periods since the UN's inception. Reports confirm that President Nawrocki held a direct meeting with Donald Trump during an official dinner, where they discussed the critical issue of establishing a permanent American military presence in Poland, a move seen as vital to the nation's long-term security strategy.
