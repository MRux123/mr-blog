## WORLD

* **Tragic Military Air Disaster in Colombia:** A C-130 Hercules transport aircraft has crashed in Puerto Leguizamo, Colombia. Latest reports confirm the death toll has risen to 66 victims. With 125 people on board at the time of the incident, rescue operations remain critical as authorities continue to assess the scale of the tragedy.
* **Diplomatic Uncertainty Over US-Iran Relations:** Tensions and confusion characterize the current diplomatic climate between the United States and Iran. While President Trump has alluded to ongoing talks, top Iranian officials have officially denied these claims, despite reports from other sources suggesting that back-channel communication through intermediaries is active.

## POLAND

* **Growing Controversy Over Diplomatic Visits to Budapest:** Significant political debate has erupted regarding recent visits by Polish officials, including Karol Nawrocki, to Budapest to meet with Viktor Orbán. Critics and political analysts are questioning the strategic rationale behind these engagements, describing them as potentially misaligned with Poland's broader national interests.
* **Tax Authority Surveillance Concerns:** Polish entrepreneurs are being advised to exercise extreme caution following reports that the tax office is conducting proactive, informal phone calls to businesses regarding KSeF (National e-Invoicing System) compliance. While the Ministry of Finance characterizes these interactions as "supportive," financial experts warn against disclosing sensitive or confidential information during these unverified contacts.
