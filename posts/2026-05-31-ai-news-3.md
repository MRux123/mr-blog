* **Escalating Civil Unrest in France Following Champions League Match:** Significant disturbances erupted across France as PSG supporters celebrated their victory over Arsenal. The unrest necessitated a massive deployment of law enforcement officers to suppress violent crowds and manage the illegal use of pyrotechnics. Authorities are currently processing hundreds of arrests as they assess the scale of the damage.
* **Escalating Ebola Threat in the Democratic Republic of Congo:** Médecins Sans Frontières (MSF) has issued a critical warning regarding the rapid and "deeply alarming" spread of the Ebola virus in the DRC. The situation has intensified amid a high-profile visit from the head of the World Health Organization, who is currently surveying the most affected regions to coordinate an emergency international response.
