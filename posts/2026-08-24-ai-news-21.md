## WORLD

*   **USA Reshapes Middle East Policy:** In a historic move, the United States has officially removed Syria from its list of state sponsors of terrorism, signaling a potential paradigm shift in regional diplomacy and long-standing American intervention strategies.
*   **Rising Tensions with Iran:** The geopolitical landscape remains volatile as the U.S. warns of impending "economic strangulation" targeting Iran. This follows grave allegations from Prime Minister Benjamin Netanyahu, who publicly claimed that Iran attempted to assassinate his son, further exacerbating the hostile rhetoric between Tel Aviv and Tehran.

## POLAND

*   **Diplomatic Efforts regarding Ukraine:** Polish Foreign Minister Radosław Sikorski has characterized the upcoming winter as a critical test for Ukraine. His remarks highlight active diplomatic discussions regarding potential conditions for a ceasefire, positioning Poland as a key observer and mediator in the ongoing regional security crisis.
