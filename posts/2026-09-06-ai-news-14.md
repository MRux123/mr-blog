## WORLD

* **Diplomatic High-Stakes Mission in Kyiv:** U.S. envoys Steve Witkoff and Jared Kushner have arrived in Kyiv to meet with President Volodymyr Zelensky. This high-profile visit follows their recent talks with Vladimir Putin in Moscow, marking a critical moment in international efforts to explore avenues for ending the war in Ukraine.
* **Rising Tensions Following Leipzig Incident:** Russian Foreign Minister Sergey Lavrov has issued a stern warning to Germany, characterizing a recent drone incident at Leipzig airport as the "beginning of a real war." The German government has openly attributed the provocation to Moscow, signaling a further deterioration in bilateral relations.
* **Democratic Concerns in Guinea-Bissau:** Voters in Guinea-Bissau have approved a new constitution via referendum, which significantly consolidates presidential power. International observers and political opponents warn that the changes, which reduce parliamentary oversight, threaten the stability of democratic institutions ahead of the December elections.

## POLAND

* **Strategic Strike on Russian Energy Infrastructure:** Ukrainian forces have successfully targeted a Russian oil refinery in Ryazan, part of a broader, coordinated overnight assault that also hit a military airfield and air defense installations deep within Russian territory. 
* **Nawrocki Addresses Jasna Góra:** During the Harvest Festival (Dożynki) at Jasna Góra, President Karol Nawrocki publicly criticized the European Green Deal and the Mercosur trade agreement. His remarks, specifically his comment on having assumed the presidency "too late" to stop these policies, have sparked significant debate regarding his political agenda.
