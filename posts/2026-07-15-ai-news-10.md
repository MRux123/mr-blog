## WORLD

*   **Conflict Escalation in Ukraine:** The ongoing war has reached new levels of intensity, with Russian bombing of Odesa resulting in three fatalities. Simultaneously, Ukrainian forces reported a tactical success in the Belgorod region, where a drone strike successfully downed a Russian Mi-28 attack helicopter.
*   **Political Instability in Italy:** Prime Minister Giorgia Meloni is facing significant political pressure following accusations of a "treacherous attack" on the public. Opposition factions are calling for early elections, signaling a potential shift in the Italian political landscape.

## POLAND

*   **CBA Investigation into RARS:** The Central Anti-Corruption Bureau (CBA) has detained three individuals, including a former RARS employee and two former heads of the Bank Gospodarstwa Krajowego Foundation, as part of an investigation into irregularities regarding government donations and strategic reserves.
*   **Security Incident at Presidential Palace:** Security services apprehended an intoxicated individual carrying an object resembling a firearm outside the Presidential Palace in Warsaw. No injuries were reported, and the incident remains under investigation.
*   **Tragedy in Northern Ireland:** A Polish family of three—a 41-year-old man, a 39-year-old woman, and an 8-year-old girl—was found deceased in a home in Ballymena, Northern Ireland. Authorities are treating the discovery with the utmost seriousness as they work to determine the circumstances of the deaths.
