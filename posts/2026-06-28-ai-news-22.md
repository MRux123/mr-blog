## WORLD

* **Mideast Escalation:** Hostilities in the Middle East have intensified, with Iran launching drone and missile strikes against U.S. and Kuwaiti military assets. The conflict centers on a deepening dispute regarding strategic waterways, marking the fourth consecutive day of military engagement. 
* **Venezuelan Rescue Crisis:** The humanitarian situation in Venezuela has reached a critical stage. Over 72 hours after devastating twin earthquakes, rescue operations are struggling with severe logistical delays, significantly lowering the probability of finding survivors.
* **Constitutional Controversy in Zimbabwe:** Zimbabwe’s senate has moved to extend presidential term limits from five to seven years, allowing President Emmerson Mnangagwa to remain in power until 2030. Opposition leaders have condemned the legislative change as a "constitutional coup."

## POLAND

* **First Heatwave Fatality:** Polish authorities have confirmed the first death attributed to the ongoing record-breaking heatwave. Emergency services remain on high alert as temperatures continue to pose a significant risk to public health.
* **Political Scandal:** Allegations have emerged regarding a potential assassination attempt targeting Karol Nawrocki during the recent election campaign. The situation is currently under scrutiny, raising concerns regarding political security.
* **Industrial Fire in Podkarpacie:** Emergency crews are currently engaged in a large-scale operation to contain a fire at an industrial facility in the Podkarpacie region. Local authorities are managing the response to prevent further escalation.
