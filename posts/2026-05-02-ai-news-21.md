## WORLD

* **Escalating U.S.-Iran Tensions:** The geopolitical landscape in the Middle East faces further instability following a stark warning from Iranian military officials. General Mohammad Jafar Asadi, deputy chief of staff of the Iranian armed forces, indicated that a renewal of direct conflict with the United States is increasingly probable. This assessment follows President Donald Trump’s formal rejection of Tehran’s latest peace proposal, signaling a hardening of stances on both sides.
* **Environmental Crisis in Czech Republic:** Authorities have declared a state of emergency in the Bohemian Switzerland region as a massive wildfire consumes approximately 100 hectares of forest. Emergency services are currently engaged in a critical effort to contain the blaze, which poses a significant threat to the national park's ecosystem.

## POLAND

* **Expanding Military Space Assets:** Poland is set to significantly enhance its reconnaissance capabilities with the launch of its fourth radar satellite. Defense Minister Władysław Kosiniak-Kamysz confirmed that the EYCORE-1 satellite, part of the MikroSAR program, is scheduled to launch from California. Equipped with advanced synthetic-aperture radar (SAR) technology, the asset will provide the Polish Armed Forces with critical orbital monitoring capabilities.
* **National Honors for Andrzej Poczobut:** In a symbolic gesture of state recognition, Andrzej Poczobut, a prominent journalist and activist recognized for his opposition to the Lukashenko regime, is set to be honored with the Order of the White Eagle. The ceremony, scheduled at the Royal Castle, serves as a reaffirmation of the Polish state's support for democratic values and human rights defenders currently held under foreign authoritarian rule.
