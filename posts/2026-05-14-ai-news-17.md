## WORLD

*   **US-China Diplomatic Standoff:** President Trump’s visit to Beijing has been marked by high-stakes rhetoric regarding Taiwan. While President Xi Jinping explicitly warned that poor handling of the Taiwan issue could lead to a direct conflict, both leaders utilized the state dinner to project a narrative of necessary cooperation and an uneasy trade truce.
*   **UK Political Instability:** British Health Secretary Wes Streeting has resigned, delivering a scathing indictment of Prime Minister Keir Starmer’s leadership. His departure has triggered formal calls for a leadership contest, signaling a significant period of internal volatility for the British government.
*   **Global Climate Emergency:** Scientists have issued a dire warning as the developing El Niño climate pattern shows signs of becoming one of the strongest on record. The phenomenon is expected to drive global temperatures to new heights, raising concerns about widespread environmental and societal consequences.

## POLAND

*   **Passing of Krzysztof Piesiewicz:** The death of renowned screenwriter, lawyer, and former senator Krzysztof Piesiewicz at age 80 has been confirmed. A central figure in Polish cinema and a significant political figure, his passing marks the end of an era for both the arts and public service in Poland.
*   **Escalation of Humanitarian Crisis in Gaza:** Reports from the Polish Medical Mission, citing findings from the UN and World Bank, categorize the Gaza Strip as currently the most difficult place on Earth to live. The shortage of medical supplies, clean water, and the destruction of hospital infrastructure have created a catastrophic humanitarian vacuum.
