## WORLD

*   **Humanitarian Crisis in Sudan:** The UK has imposed sanctions on military leaders from both sides of the Sudanese conflict, citing a systematic "campaign of rape" and sexual violence during the siege of El Fasher. These measures, including travel bans and asset freezes, mark a significant international response to the atrocities committed during the country's ongoing civil war.
*   **Ebola Outbreak in DR Congo:** The Democratic Republic of Congo is facing its deadliest Ebola outbreak to date, with official government data confirming over 5,000 fatalities. The crisis has reached a critical scale, with more than 10,000 recorded infections, highlighting the urgent need for international health intervention.
*   **Spanish Rail Disaster:** Prime Minister Pedro Sánchez faces mounting political pressure following a major rail accident in Spain. Already considered an election underdog, the incident has turned public discourse toward questions of government competence and infrastructure management, further complicating his campaign platform centered on inequality and housing.

## POLAND

*   **Constitutional Crisis and Political Standoff:** Poland is gripped by a deepening institutional crisis involving the Constitutional Tribunal. The situation has escalated to the deployment of riot police, as government-backed judges moved to suspend the chief justice. Opposition leaders and various political factions are now engaged in urgent talks with President Karol Nawrocki to address the deadlock, with some calls for a complete reset of the judicial body.
*   **Heightened Security Concerns:** Security analysts are closely monitoring potential threats, with reports citing NATO estimates that suggest a high casualty rate in the event of a full-scale conflict with Russia. Meanwhile, the domestic political instability has drawn unwanted attention from Moscow, with Russian officials attempting to exploit the current governance crisis to sow further discord.
