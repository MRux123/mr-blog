## WORLD

* **Iran-US Standoff at Tipping Point:** The international community is bracing for potential conflict as the deadline set by President Donald Trump for Iran to reopen the Strait of Hormuz approaches. With reports of a "wipe out" threat, diplomatic efforts are intensifying, notably involving Pakistan as an intermediary for a potential two-week cease-fire. Tensions were further exacerbated after Russia and China vetoed a UN Security Council resolution aimed at securing commercial shipping in the region.
* **Global Calls for Restraint:** Pope Leo XIV has issued a sharp rebuke against the rhetoric surrounding the crisis, labeling threats against the Iranian people "unacceptable" and emphasizing that attacks on civilian infrastructure violate international law. 
* **Migrant Deportation Policy:** The United States has initiated a controversial new deportation strategy, with the first flight of individuals landing in Uganda. This move, part of a "third-country agreement," has drawn significant criticism from human rights groups describing the process as "dehumanizing."

## POLAND

* **Political Accountability:** Senator Tomasz Lenz (KO) has issued an official statement following media reports regarding a medical procedure performed on his son. The Senator vehemently denied allegations of procedural irregularities, maintaining that all medical standards and documentation requirements were strictly observed.
* **Passing of a Football Icon:** Polish and international sports are in mourning following the death of Mircea Lucescu at the age of 80. The legendary coach, renowned as one of the most successful and long-serving figures in football history, passed away after suffering a heart attack.
