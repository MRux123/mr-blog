## WORLD

*   **US Strikes Iranian Military Infrastructure:** The Middle East faces a significant escalation after the US military conducted precision strikes on an ammunition depot in Isfahan, Iran. The operation, utilizing bunker-busting munitions, follows heightened rhetoric and ongoing threats to regional infrastructure.
*   **Maritime Security Concerns:** The situation is further complicated by the reported attack on a Kuwaiti oil tanker near Dubai. The incident, occurring shortly after threats from the US administration regarding potential strikes on Iranian targets, raises concerns over the safety of critical energy shipping lanes.
*   **European Strategic Posture:** British Prime Minister Keir Starmer is navigating a complex geopolitical landscape, balancing support for US military efforts on British soil with the strategic necessity of preventing a broader regional war.

## POLAND

*   **Heightened Security Near the Baltic:** The Baltic states are on high alert as Ukrainian long-range drone strikes against Russian port infrastructure, specifically in Ust-Luga, continue to intensify. The frequency of these strikes has increased significantly over the past week, drawing regional concern.
*   **Regional Patriot Missile Speculation:** Despite international reports suggesting potential discussions about relocating a Polish Patriot missile battery to the Middle East, the Polish President’s office (KPRP) has officially denied knowledge of any such procedural steps or formal requests.
*   **Economic Strategy and Fuel Prices:** The Polish government, represented by the Ministry of Energy, is closely monitoring the domestic fuel market. Amidst concerns regarding "fuel tourism" from neighboring countries with higher prices, authorities have signaled that they are prepared to intervene to maintain market stability and competitive pricing.
