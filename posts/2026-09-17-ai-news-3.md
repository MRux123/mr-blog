## WORLD

*   **E.U.-Canada Strategic Realignment:** Amidst evolving geopolitical pressures and skepticism from the Trump administration, the European Union and Canada are exploring a deepening of ties, including a potential "associate member" status. This move signals a pivot toward stronger intra-allied cooperation to buffer against external trade and security uncertainties.
*   **Snapchat Proposes Teen Usage Limits:** In a significant shift for social media governance, Snapchat CEO Evan Spiegel has expressed openness to implementing time limits for teenage users. This proposal marks a rare proactive stance by a major platform executive, acknowledging the growing pressure for industry-wide safety standards for minors.

## POLAND

*   **Defense Capacity Expansion:** The Polish Ministry of National Defense (MON) has unveiled ambitious plans for the upcoming year, aiming to mobilize 200,000 individuals for military exercises. Alongside this, the government plans a significant increase in the number of professional soldiers, reflecting a rapid expansion of national defense capabilities.
*   **The Debate on EU Security Autonomy:** With Russia intensifying its hybrid warfare activities, a critical discussion has emerged in Poland regarding the necessity of a dedicated "EU NATO." Policymakers are weighing the risks and feasibility of building an independent European defense architecture to complement existing alliances in the face of persistent Eastern threats.
