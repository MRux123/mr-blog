## WORLD

* **Escalation in the Strait of Hormuz:** The geopolitical landscape has entered a precarious phase as the U.S. initiates a blockade of Iranian ports and coastal areas. This move has triggered an immediate market reaction, with global oil prices surging back above $100 per barrel. The conflict now represents a high-stakes standoff between Iranian resilience to military strikes and the Trump administration's willingness to absorb the economic costs of a wider regional confrontation.
* **Political Earthquake in Hungary:** Prime Minister Viktor Orban’s tenure faces an abrupt end following the election victory of Peter Magyar. This transition is expected to fundamentally alter Hungary’s strained relationship with the European Union. While regional allies like Slovakia’s Robert Fico are already positioning themselves to engage with the new administration, Brussels anticipates a potential thaw in long-standing diplomatic tensions.

## POLAND

* **Legal and Political Fallout of the Hungarian Election:** The shift in power in Budapest is reverberating through Polish domestic politics. Włodzimierz Czarzasty, Marshal of the Sejm, has signaled that the Hungarian results provide the necessary political momentum to move forward with the State Tribunal process against former Justice Minister Zbigniew Ziobro.
* **Diplomatic Missteps Regarding Budapest:** Polish political discourse remains dominated by the fallout from recent diplomatic efforts. Criticism has intensified against President Karol Nawrocki following his meeting with the outgoing Viktor Orban, with government figures accusing the presidency of damaging Poland's international reputation during a pivotal moment for Central European alliances.
