## WORLD

* **Surge in Settler Violence:** Tensions in the West Bank have reached a critical point after Israeli settlers attacked Palestinian residents and a European diplomatic convoy. The incident, occurring during the volatile olive harvest season, has prompted international concern regarding the breakdown of security and the rule of law in the territory.
* **Widespread Social Unrest:** Europe is facing a wave of civil disobedience as citizens take to the streets to protest the escalating cost of living and deteriorating public infrastructure. Major demonstrations, characterized by violent clashes, were reported in France, Italy, Albania, and Portugal, signaling deep-seated economic frustration across the continent.
* **Geopolitical Instability:** The conflict in the Middle East continues to widen, with intensified fighting reported in Yemen, threatening the stability of the Red Sea region. Simultaneously, security concerns were heightened near the Black Sea after Romania was forced to scramble fighter jets following the incursion of drones into its airspace.

## POLAND

* **Diplomatic Incident on the West Bank:** The Ministry of Foreign Affairs (MSZ) has officially confirmed that Polish diplomats were caught in an attack by Israeli settlers on the West Bank. Warsaw has demanded immediate clarifications from the Israeli government regarding the safety of its diplomatic staff.
* **Regional Security and Transit Demands:** Hungary has signaled a shift in diplomatic outreach, reporting that Ukrainian President Volodymyr Zelensky has requested transit support from Poland and other neighbors to facilitate grain exports to the West. The situation underscores the ongoing logistical and geopolitical challenges surrounding Ukrainian agricultural corridors.
* **Tragic Aviation Accident:** A fatal accident involving a glider occurred near the Pruszcz Gdański aeroclub. Despite immediate efforts by emergency responders to revive the pilot, the individual succumbed to their injuries, leading to an investigation into the circumstances of the crash.
