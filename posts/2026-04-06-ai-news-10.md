## WORLD

* **Iran-US Standoff at the Strait of Hormuz:** Global markets and security are under threat as President Trump’s 48-hour ultimatum to Tehran regarding the reopening of the Strait of Hormuz nears its deadline. Iran has officially refused to comply, warning of a "crushing" response should the US strike civilian infrastructure, such as power plants or bridges. This standoff marks a significant escalation in the Persian Gulf.
* **Assassination of IRGC Intelligence Chief:** Regional instability has been further compounded by the confirmed death of Majid Khademi, the intelligence chief of Iran's Islamic Revolutionary Guard Corps. Reports indicate he was killed in a coordinated operation involving US and Israeli forces, a move that is expected to trigger retaliatory measures from Tehran.
* **Sabotage at Balkan Stream Pipeline:** A major geopolitical incident has emerged following the discovery of explosives near the strategic Balkan Stream gas pipeline. Serbian authorities have identified the components as US-manufactured, sparking a heated diplomatic dispute involving accusations directed at Ukraine, Russia, and the Hungarian government.

## POLAND

* **Political Shifts in Latest IBRiS Polling:** A new survey conducted by IBRiS for PAP reveals a shifting landscape in Polish public sentiment. President Karol Nawrocki remains the most trusted political figure, while Prime Minister Donald Tusk faces a decline in support. Conversely, Speaker of the Sejm Włodzimierz Czarzasty has seen a notable improvement in his approval ratings.
* **Severe Weather Alerts:** The Institute of Meteorology and Water Management (IMGW) has issued urgent weather warnings across twelve Polish provinces. Residents in the path of the storm, particularly along the Baltic coast, are bracing for extreme wind gusts reaching up to 95 km/h, with the Government Security Centre (RCB) issuing corresponding safety alerts.
