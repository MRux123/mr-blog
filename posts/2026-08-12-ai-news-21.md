## WORLD

*   **Total Solar Eclipse Sweeps Across Europe:** In a rare celestial event, a total solar eclipse plunged parts of Europe into darkness today. Observers from Iceland to Spain witnessed the moon completely obscure the sun, drawing crowds to prime viewing locations across the continent.
*   **ICE Controversial Gear Upgrade:** The US Immigration and Customs Enforcement (ICE) agency has initiated a $20 million procurement of "shock gloves." Designed to deliver electric shocks to ensure subject compliance, the move has sparked immediate debate regarding the ethical implications of non-lethal enforcement technology in immigration operations.
*   **White House Personnel Shake-up:** Press Secretary Karoline Leavitt is set to depart her high-profile role. President Trump announced that Leavitt is leaving the administration to focus on her family and young children.

## POLAND

*   **Romanowski Seeks Safe Conduct:** In a significant legal development, Marcin Romanowski has filed a formal request for a "safe conduct" (list żelazny). His defense attorney confirmed the filing, marking a pivotal moment in the ongoing investigation.
*   **Athletic Success in Birmingham:** Jakub Szymański has achieved a notable success on the international stage, securing a bronze medal at the European Championships held in Birmingham. 
*   **Eurovision Rules Overhaul:** The Eurovision Song Contest is set to implement significant rule changes. Organizers are signaling a shift in policy as geopolitical dynamics increasingly influence the selection process and the nature of the competition itself.
