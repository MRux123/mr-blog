## WORLD

*   **Shift in Russian Military Posture:** In a notable departure from tradition, Russia’s upcoming Victory Day parade in Moscow will feature no military hardware, with organizers focusing exclusively on infantry. Analysts interpret this as a clear signal that the ongoing conflict in Ukraine is putting significant strain on Russian military resources. Concurrently, the Kremlin has issued heightened warnings regarding potential strikes on Kyiv should Ukraine attempt to disrupt the May 9 commemorations.
*   **Geopolitical Standoffs in the Middle East:** Efforts to navigate ongoing conflicts remain precarious. While the U.S. awaits Tehran’s formal response to proposed peace initiatives, tensions remain high as Iranian officials remain skeptical. Meanwhile, concerns over a resurgence of the Gaza war persist, as negotiations regarding the disarmament of Hamas have reached a critical impasse, prompting speculation that Israel may resume active combat operations.

## POLAND

*   **NBP Reserves and Monetary Policy:** During a press conference, NBP Governor Adam Glapiński confirmed that Poland's gold reserves have reached 595 tonnes as of the end of April. Total reserve assets rose to 254.8 billion euros. Addressing the domestic economic outlook, the Governor indicated that an interest rate cut remains unlikely in the near term, signaling a continued focus on inflation management.
*   **Constitutional Reform Initiatives:** Significant political developments have emerged as Paweł Kukiz accepted an invitation to join the Council for a New Constitution, an advisory body established by President Karol Nawrocki. The move marks a formal step toward potential constitutional reform, a process described by supporters as a long-awaited initiative for the country’s legal framework.
*   **High-Profile Legal Developments:** The Polish judicial and law enforcement landscape has seen a major breakthrough with the arrest of the attorney known in media reports as the advocate of the "coffin on wheels" case. The individual, who had been subject to an arrest warrant following a fatal accident involving two women, is now in custody.
