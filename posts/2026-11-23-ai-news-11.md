## WORLD

*   **Climate Diplomacy Stalls:** The COP31 summit in Antalya has concluded without a final agreement, marking a historic failure in UN climate negotiations. Host nations pointed to a volatile geopolitical environment as the primary reason for the impasse, leaving major global emission targets in limbo.
*   **Escalating Conflict in Libya:** Tensions have intensified as Ukrainian naval drones reportedly struck Russian military assets off the coast of Libya. President Zelensky confirmed the operation, claiming the sinking of a corvette and damage to a supply vessel, a development that highlights the expanding maritime theater of the Russia-Ukraine war.
*   **Israel and Iran:** Israeli Prime Minister Benjamin Netanyahu faces growing domestic pressure amid calls for renewed military action against Iran. While framed as a national security imperative, critics and opposition leaders argue that the push for further escalation serves the Prime Minister's personal political interests rather than the country's strategic needs.

## POLAND

*   **Diplomatic Confrontation with Russia:** The Polish Ministry of Foreign Affairs is set to summon the Russian ambassador following a violation of Polish airspace. The government has stated that formal procedures, involving military and security services, are currently underway to verify the breach before official diplomatic action is finalized.
*   **Collegium Humanum Scandal:** The investigation into the Collegium Humanum university continues to gain momentum, with the prosecutor’s office filing motions to strip three members of Parliament—Michał Kołodziejczak, Ewa Leniart, and Daniel Milewski—of their parliamentary immunity. The move is a significant step in the ongoing probe into irregularities at the institution.
*   **Environmental Concerns:** The Polish Alarm for Clean Air (PAS) has issued a stark warning that rural areas are struggling with severe smog, effectively debunking the myth of "clean air" in the countryside. The findings suggest that smaller municipalities lack the infrastructure and resources to monitor or mitigate pollution effectively compared to major urban centers.
