## WORLD

*   **Escalating Middle East Conflict:** Tensions between Israel and Iran have reached a critical juncture following new Israeli strikes in Lebanon. The escalation threatens the stability of a fragile U.S.-brokered ceasefire, with Iranian officials warning that the United States must choose between war and a diplomatic resolution.
*   **Russian Submarine Activity:** The United Kingdom has reported the detection of three Russian submarines operating near British waters. Defense Secretary John Healey stated the vessels were likely engaged in intelligence gathering on undersea infrastructure, suggesting a deliberate attempt to distract Western powers from the ongoing crisis in the Middle East.

## POLAND

*   **Constitutional Tribunal Standoff:** Poland is gripped by a deepening political crisis following the swearing-in of six new Constitutional Tribunal judges in the Sejm. The ceremony, conducted by Speaker of the Sejm Włodzimierz Czarzasty in the absence of President Karol Nawrocki, has sparked polarized reactions, with the opposition calling it an "assault on the rule of law" and threatening legal action.
*   **Institutional Deadlock:** The procedure has triggered protests outside the Constitutional Tribunal, highlighting a fundamental disagreement over legal legitimacy. While the ruling coalition insists the process is complete, legal challenges and intense rhetoric from figures like Jarosław Kaczyński underscore a profound fracture in the country's governance and judicial independence.
