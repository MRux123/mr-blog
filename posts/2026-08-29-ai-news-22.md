## WORLD

* **Emergency Rescue Operation in Nepal:** Search and rescue teams, led by the Nepal Army, are currently engaged in a high-stakes operation to reach 900 workers trapped in tunnels following catastrophic flooding. Experts are working under extreme pressure to extract the workers as hopes for their survival depend on the success of these critical drilling efforts.
* **Escalation of Attacks on Kyiv:** Russian forces have launched a fresh wave of aerial assaults on the Ukrainian capital. A drone strike in the Obolon district has resulted in a significant fire within a 12-story residential apartment block, marking a further escalation in the ongoing regional conflict.
* **Vienna Museum Heist:** Authorities in Austria are investigating the theft of a historically significant diamond necklace from a Vienna museum. The piece, once belonging to an Egyptian queen, was taken in a smash-and-grab operation, highlighting ongoing security concerns for high-value museum collections globally.

## POLAND

* **Public Solidarity in the Wake of Tragedy:** A massive crowdfunding effort has been launched for Oliwier, a young boy orphaned following a fatal traffic accident in Krakow. The public response has been overwhelming, with over one million PLN raised in a short period to support the child’s future and medical recovery.
* **Armed Incident in Gdańsk:** A police intervention in Gdańsk resulted in an officer-involved shooting scenario where a 39-year-old woman was struck by a pellet gun. While the victim is currently hospitalized and out of danger, the event has triggered an investigation into the circumstances surrounding the use of force and public safety.
* **Political Stance on Zondacrypto and Administrative Reform:** Political tensions remain high as declarations regarding the Zondacrypto affair and the veto concerning the electronic journal (eDziennik) take center stage. Key figures have issued firm statements signaling a shift in policy and accountability measures.
