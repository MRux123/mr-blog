## WORLD

*   **Ukraine-US Relations Strained Over Starlink:** The operational effectiveness of Ukrainian forces in the Donbas is currently being tested by access restrictions to the Starlink network. While the U.S. administration denies direct involvement in throttling these services, the deteriorating relationship between President Trump and President Zelensky threatens the vital operational support necessary for Ukraine's continued defense.
*   **Pope's Diplomatic Appeal:** Following his recent surgery and lung cancer diagnosis, Pope Leo XIV has taken a significant diplomatic step by appealing to Vladimir Putin for a ceasefire in the embattled Ukrainian city of Oleshky. The move aims to provide relief to approximately 2,000 civilians trapped by mines and drone warfare.
*   **Escalating Regional Risks:** The geopolitical landscape remains volatile as Houthi-led attacks disrupt Saudi Arabia's international business and sports initiatives, while a deadly school roof collapse in Pakistan has sparked national outrage regarding building safety and regulatory oversight.

## POLAND

*   **Tusk Warns of Imminent Escalation:** Prime Minister Donald Tusk has issued a stern warning following the recent EU summit, suggesting that Russia may intensify its aggression toward Poland and the region before the upcoming Christmas holidays. Tusk highlighted concerns regarding border security and critical infrastructure, including rail networks.
*   **Constitutional Court Standoff:** Domestic political friction remains high as the government prepares to restore the functionality of the Constitutional Tribunal. The initiative has been met with intense resistance and accusations of politicization from the opposition, culminating in heated parliamentary debates and public confrontations involving law enforcement.
