## WORLD

*   **Tensions over Iran Deal:** US President Donald Trump has publicly defended his stance on the Iran nuclear agreement, dismissing claims of a massive rebuilding fund and asserting that his policy framework is superior to the 2015 Obama-era deal.
*   **Diplomatic Standoff in Brazil:** Brazilian President Luiz Inácio Lula da Silva has issued a sharp rebuke to Donald Trump, warning the US leader against interfering in Brazil’s internal affairs following Trump’s endorsement of political challenger Flavio Bolsonaro.
*   **European Security Divide:** The Italian government has reaffirmed its refusal to participate in the PURL initiative regarding prioritized arms supplies to Ukraine, signaling a growing divergence in European strategic cooperation on the conflict.

## POLAND

*   **Tragedy at Eko-Plus Mine:** A fatal accident has occurred at the Eko-Plus mine in Bytom following a seismic tremor. Emergency services confirmed that one trapped miner has lost his life after an arduous rescue operation.
*   **Rule of Law Controversy:** The Venice Commission has issued a critical opinion regarding the President's proposed judiciary legislation, warning that the bill fails to resolve the current institutional deadlock and poses a threat to judicial independence.
*   **Political Accountability Debate:** Tensions within the Polish parliament have escalated as opposition figures spar over alleged favoritism in the Warsaw healthcare system, while voices from the Left call for an end to shifting all systemic blame onto previous administrations.
