## WORLD

*   **DRC Ebola Outbreak Reaches Historic Death Toll:** The ongoing Ebola epidemic in the Democratic Republic of Congo has become the deadliest in the nation's history. Government data confirms at least 2,325 fatalities, surpassing the tragic figures of the 2018–2020 outbreak. With 4,945 confirmed cases and a rapid surge in new infections, public health authorities are struggling to contain the virus.
*   **Trump Announces Military Drill Reduction with South Korea:** Citing his personal diplomatic relationship with North Korean leadership, President Trump has declared a decision to scale back military exercises with South Korea. The move marks a significant shift in regional defense posture, following the U.S. decision to remain out of the recent Iranian conflict.
*   **Legislative Milestone in Liechtenstein:** Liechtenstein has officially amended its laws to grant women the right to ascend the throne. This modernizing step aligns the principality with the majority of European nations maintaining royal families, reflecting a significant shift in regional succession policies.

## POLAND
