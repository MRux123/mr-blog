## WORLD

* **Diplomatic Tensions and Nuclear Stance:** King Charles III’s historic address to the US Congress and subsequent dinner with President Donald Trump underscored significant diplomatic shifts. The discussions highlighted a unified stance on the Iranian nuclear threat, while simultaneously reflecting underlying tensions regarding the future of NATO and the ongoing conflict in Ukraine.
* **Shifting Energy Landscapes:** The United Arab Emirates’ departure from OPEC marks a pivotal moment for global energy markets. While immediate impacts on existing oil blockades are expected to be minimal, the move signals a broader long-term restructuring of global oil supply dynamics.

## POLAND

* **High-Stakes Prisoner Exchange:** In a major diplomatic development, Polish journalist and political prisoner Andrzej Poczobut has been released from Belarus. The operation, facilitated by a prisoner swap involving Russian national Aleksander Butiagin, is being hailed as a milestone in Polish-American cooperation, with leadership from both the Tusk administration and the Trump White House playing key roles.
* **Political Volatility:** Recent polling data from IBRiS indicates a tightening domestic political landscape. While the Civic Coalition maintains its lead, the data suggests potential losses for the ruling coalition’s reach, fueling ongoing debates regarding ministerial stability, specifically concerning the future of Paulina Hennig-Kloska.
