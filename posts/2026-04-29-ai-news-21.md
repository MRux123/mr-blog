## WORLD

* **Oil Price Surge Following Iran Blockade Reports:** Crude oil prices briefly spiked to $120 per barrel following reports of an "extended" blockade involving Iran. The volatility underscores the deepening uncertainty surrounding the ongoing conflict in the Middle East, which continues to rattle global energy markets.
* **Diplomatic Channels Open Between Putin and Trump:** In a notable development, Russian President Vladimir Putin and U.S. President Donald Trump held a phone conversation to discuss global security, including the situation in Iran and Ukraine. President Trump indicated he has suggested a potential truce in the Ukrainian conflict, while Putin officially condemned the recent assassination attempt on the American leader.
* **Mexican Governor Linked to Sinaloa Cartel:** U.S. federal prosecutors have issued a significant indictment against Rubén Rocha Moya, the governor of the Mexican state of Sinaloa. The charges detail a years-long conspiracy, alleging that the governor and other high-ranking officials provided protection to the powerful Sinaloa Cartel.

## POLAND

* **Historic Record for Cancer Fighters Charity:** The "Łatwoganga" charity stream has concluded, raising an unprecedented 282 million PLN for the Cancer Fighters foundation. This figure marks not only a national record for Poland but represents one of the largest single-event charitable collections globally in recent memory.
* **Political Turmoil Over Potential No-Confidence Vote:** Tensions within the ruling coalition have escalated following comments by MP Witold Zembaczyński during a televised debate. Zembaczyński sharply criticized potential members of the coalition who might support a vote of no-confidence against Minister Paulina Hennig-Kloska, labeling such dissent as disloyal to the government mandate established in 2023.
