## WORLD

* **Tragic Explosion in Athens:** A devastating gas explosion in the historic Plaka district near the Acropolis has resulted in at least six fatalities. Among the victims are four Americans, one British national, and one Greek citizen. Authorities are currently investigating the circumstances surrounding the blast, which caused a three-story building to collapse in the popular tourist area.
* **Military Disaster in Kazakhstan:** A maritime training exercise on the Caspian Sea has ended in tragedy, with 14 soldiers confirmed dead. Initial reports indicate that 17 service members were swept away by high waves, with only three survivors rescued. High-ranking military officials are currently in custody as investigations into potential safety protocol violations proceed.
* **Bangkok Faces State of Emergency:** The Thai capital is reeling from severe flooding following 48 hours of relentless rainfall, with precipitation levels reaching 300 mm. All 50 districts have been declared disaster zones as infrastructure, including the city's main airport and critical transport arteries, faces significant disruptions.

## POLAND

* **Humanitarian Crisis in Oleshky:** The Ukrainian Foreign Minister, Andrij Sybiha, has issued a desperate plea regarding the dire situation in the occupied town of Oleshky. Residents are facing starvation, and officials have emphasized that diplomatic statements alone are insufficient to ensure the survival of those trapped in the Russian-occupied region of the Kherson oblast.
* **European Defense Readiness Concerns:** The Council of Europe has issued a stark warning regarding the continent's military preparedness, suggesting that Europe remains critically undersized and under-equipped to face the challenges of modern, full-scale warfare.
* **Personnel Shifts in Polish Football:** National team preparations are undergoing adjustments as coach Urban reacts to the injury of Jakub Kamiński. 18-year-old Oskar Pietuszewski has been called up to the squad ahead of critical Nations League fixtures, signaling a necessary generational pivot in the national team lineup.
