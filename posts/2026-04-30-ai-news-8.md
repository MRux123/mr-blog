## WORLD

* **Oil Prices Surge on Iran Strike Reports:** Global energy markets have reacted sharply following reports that U.S. Central Command is finalizing plans for potential strikes against Iran. The news triggered a spike in crude oil prices, reaching their highest levels since 2022, as investors weigh the implications of a significant escalation in Middle Eastern hostilities.
* **Christchurch Shooter Appeal Denied:** A New Zealand court has formally rejected the appeal of the Christchurch mosque shooter, Brenton Tarrant, to overturn his conviction. The judiciary characterized the attempt as "utterly devoid of merit," effectively closing a painful chapter for the victims and the national legal system.

## POLAND

* **Trump's Misstatements Spark Diplomatic Confusion:** Following a telephone conversation with Vladimir Putin, U.S. President Donald Trump made inaccurate claims regarding the status of the Ukrainian military. His assertion that the Ukrainian fleet had been destroyed, despite the reality of the ongoing conflict, has raised concerns in Warsaw and other NATO capitals regarding the administration's grasp of the regional security situation.
* **CBA Raids RARS Over Coal Procurement:** Polish authorities have initiated a major corruption investigation following the detention of three individuals by the Central Anti-Corruption Bureau (CBA). The probe focuses on the Rządowa Agencja Rezerw Strategicznych (RARS) and alleged irregularities involving the purchase of low-quality coal, valued at approximately half a billion PLN.
* **Government Faces Confidence Vote:** The Polish Sejm is set to debate and vote on motions of no confidence against the Minister of Climate and Environment, Paulina Hennig-Kloska, and the Minister of Health, Jolanta Sobierańska-Grenda. While the opposition has criticized the ministers over policy implementation, the ruling coalition, Poland 2050, has indicated it will maintain its support for the officials.
