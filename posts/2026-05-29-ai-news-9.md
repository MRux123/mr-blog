## WORLD

*   **Russian Drone Strike in Romania:** NATO has condemned a "reckless" incident in which a Russian drone crashed into an apartment building in Romania. While the scale of the damage is still being assessed, the event marks a significant escalation, drawing the alliance directly into the geopolitical fallout of the ongoing conflict in Ukraine.
*   **Expansion of Conflict in Gaza:** Prime Minister Netanyahu has ordered the IDF to extend control over 70% of Gaza. This strategic shift directly threatens the stability of the ceasefire agreement reached in October 2025, raising concerns about a potential return to full-scale regional hostilities.
*   **Ebola Crisis in the DRC:** A deadly outbreak of Ebola in the Ituri province has claimed at least 240 lives. Public health officials warn that reduced international aid and the region's status as a conflict zone are creating a perfect storm, allowing the virus to spread rapidly across borders, including into Uganda.

## POLAND

*   **Presidential Veto on Civil Partnership Law:** President Karol Nawrocki has declared he will veto the newly passed legislation regarding the status of "persons in a close relationship" (umowa o wspólnym pożyciu). Nawrocki framed his decision as a constitutional duty, stating he will not support any legal framework that acts as an alternative to marriage.
*   **EU SAFE Program Funding:** Prime Minister Donald Tusk announced the receipt of the first 6 billion euro tranche from the EU's SAFE program. Poland, set to be the largest beneficiary with a total of 43.7 billion euros allocated, is already moving to leverage these funds to bolster national defense capabilities.
*   **Political Tensions Over Foreign Policy:** President Nawrocki has publicly criticized Defense Minister Władysław Kosiniak-Kamysz regarding the handling of a recent Polish-British defense treaty. The President’s camp claims the administration failed to properly inform the head of state, while the government has dismissed the criticism, highlighting a deepening rift between the executive branches of power.
