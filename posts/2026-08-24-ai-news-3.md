## WORLD

*   **UK reaffirms commitment to Ukraine:** British official Burnham is set to visit Kyiv for his first foreign trip, signaling a reinforcement of UK support amid escalating tensions with Moscow. The diplomatic move follows Russian warnings regarding the reported deployment of British-supplied drones in the conflict zone.
*   **Mass Tesla recall in China:** Nearly 3 million Tesla vehicles have been recalled in China due to safety concerns regarding concealed door handles. The recall, which also affects domestic manufacturers such as Xiaomi and XPeng, highlights ongoing regulatory and technical scrutiny within the world's largest electric vehicle market.
*   **Tragedy in Guinea:** At least 30 people have lost their lives following a catastrophic landslide at a waste dump in Conakry. The disaster, triggered by heavy seasonal rains, occurred despite recent government pledges to relocate the facility due to safety risks.
