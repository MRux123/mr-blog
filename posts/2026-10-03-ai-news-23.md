## WORLD

*   **Security Alert in the UK:** British authorities have confirmed that a suspect accused of murdering politician Ann Widdecombe was also plotting an assassination attempt against Nigel Farage, leader of the Reform UK party. Home Secretary Shabana Mahmood has officially categorized the incident as a grave concern regarding the safety of public figures in the UK.
*   **Political Shifts in Latvia:** Exit poll data from the parliamentary elections in Latvia suggests a victory for the "United List" (Zjednoczona Lista). According to reports from the LETA agency, the ruling party led by Prime Minister Andris Kulbergs secured 35.6% of the vote, signaling potential changes in the nation's political landscape.
*   **Rising Rhetoric from Moscow:** Dmitry Medvedev, Deputy Chairman of the Security Council of Russia, emphasized that Russia's primary objective remains the defeat of Western powers, specifically citing NATO. Medvedev accused the Alliance of using Ukraine as a "battering ram" against Russian interests, further highlighting the escalating geopolitical tensions.

## POLAND

*   **Hazardous Incident in Częstochowa:** A Biedronka supermarket near Częstochowa was the scene of an emergency evacuation today, involving 57 individuals. Local emergency services identified a potential malfunction of a refrigeration unit, which risked carbon dioxide poisoning for those inside the facility. Authorities are currently investigating the cause of the equipment failure.
