## WORLD

*   **Heightened Tensions in Iran:** The conflict between the United States and Iran has entered a critical stage, with U.S. strikes continuing for the seventh consecutive day. Military actions have targeted vital infrastructure, including bridges and water treatment facilities. Tehran has signaled that a full-scale offensive operation may be imminent if the current pace of strikes is maintained.
*   **Political Transition in the UK:** Andy Burnham is set to assume the office of Prime Minister this coming Monday, marking a significant leadership change amidst a period of economic stagnation and political volatility in Britain. 
*   **Instability in Ukraine:** The internal political situation in Kyiv remains volatile following the removal of the defense minister. The move has sparked public protests and triggered vocal criticism from frontline soldiers, raising concerns about military morale during the ongoing conflict.

## POLAND

*   **Shifting Public Sentiment on Refugees:** For the first time, a CBOS poll indicates that the number of Poles opposing the reception of Ukrainian refugees has surpassed those in favor. The data also reveals a growing belief that the current level of aid provided by the state is excessive, marking a pivotal shift in domestic public opinion.
*   **Political Realignments:** Tensions within the opposition circles continue, highlighted by Mateusz Morawiecki’s public defense of his relationship with Jarosław Kaczyński. This follows recent directives within the Law and Justice (PiS) party aimed at curbing members' participation in independent associations, underscoring efforts to maintain internal party discipline.
*   **Weather Extremes:** Intense storms caused significant disruptions in the Greater Poland region, forcing the emergency evacuation of an entire scout camp in Grójec Wielki. Emergency services reported over 120 weather-related incidents in the area as the country faces severe meteorological threats.
