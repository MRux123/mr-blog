## WORLD

* **Tragedy in Greece:** Two people have died and hundreds were forced to evacuate as twin wildfires erupted on the Greek island of Salamina. Local authorities have deployed nearly 200 firefighters to contain the blazes and prevent them from merging, which would escalate the threat to residential areas.
* **Seismic activity in Indonesia:** The death toll continues to rise following a significant earthquake that struck Indonesia. Rescue operations are currently underway as officials assess the full extent of the structural damage and casualties.

## POLAND

* **Emergency response to Hungary bus accident:** A Polish government aircraft, carrying a specialized medical team including an anesthesiologist, a surgeon, and an orthopedist, has been dispatched to Hungary. The mission aims to conduct a triage of Polish citizens involved in a recent road tragedy and prepare them for medical evacuation.
* **Fatal incident in Lithuania:** A 17-year-old Polish tourist drowned in the Baltic resort of Połąga. The teenager was swept away by a strong current while swimming with his siblings; despite a rescue effort that saved his brother and sister, the boy could not be resuscitated after being located by divers.
* **Violence at a wedding in Wadowice:** A 37-year-old man was killed following an altercation at a hotel wedding reception. The individual had reportedly gatecrashed the event and behaved aggressively, leading to a brawl. Polish authorities have detained seven participants of the incident as the investigation continues.
