## WORLD

* **Transatlantic Tensions Rise as U.S. Withdraws Troops from Germany:** The Pentagon's decision to pull approximately 5,000 troops from Germany signals a significant cooling of relations between Washington and Berlin. Analysts suggest this move confirms the breakdown of the personal and diplomatic rapport between President Donald Trump and Chancellor Friedrich Merz, marking a volatile turning point for the NATO alliance.
* **RightsCon Summit Abruptly Cancelled in Zambia:** The Zambian government has blocked the world's largest human rights and technology summit, RightsCon 2026, just days before its scheduled opening. Officials cited a lack of alignment with "national values," raising international concerns regarding censorship and the suppression of digital freedom in the region.
* **Diplomatic Balancing Act by Robert Fico:** Slovak Prime Minister Robert Fico continues to navigate a complex foreign policy path, attempting to balance pro-European aspirations for Kyiv with the maintenance of traditional diplomatic ties to Moscow, including planned attendance at Russia’s Victory Day parade.

## POLAND

* **Leadership Concerns Over NATO Stability:** Prime Minister Donald Tusk has issued a stern warning regarding the future of the North Atlantic Alliance, asserting that the primary threat to NATO today stems from internal decay and eroding solidarity rather than external adversaries. This follows the high-profile withdrawal of U.S. forces from Germany.
* **Tragic Gas Explosion in Anielin:** Local authorities are investigating a fatal gas explosion in Anielin, Łódź Voivodeship, which claimed the life of a 15-year-old girl and left a 50-year-old woman in critical condition. Emergency services, including LPR air ambulances, were deployed to the scene immediately following the blast.
* **Debate Over Health Education Curriculum:** The Polish Episcopate, led by Archbishop Tadeusz Wojda, has formally criticized government plans to make health education mandatory in schools. The Church argues that the curriculum contains controversial content and lacks sufficient parental consensus, calling for a more inclusive national dialogue on the matter.
