## WORLD

*   **Political Fallout from Iran Ceasefire:** Prime Minister Benjamin Netanyahu faces a severe political crisis following Donald Trump’s brokered ceasefire with Iran. The agreement has created a significant security dilemma for Israel, as the regional power shift leaves the current administration struggling to reconcile the deal with national security interests.
*   **Military Tragedy in California:** An investigation is underway following the fatal crash of a US Air Force B-52 bomber during a routine test mission in California. The incident resulted in the loss of eight lives, marking a somber moment for the US military aviation sector.
*   **Economic Shift in Japan:** The Bank of Japan has officially raised interest rates to their highest level in 31 years, signaling a definitive departure from the near-zero rate policy maintained since 2024. This move reflects a broader effort to stabilize the economy amid changing global fiscal conditions.

## POLAND

*   **Cybersecurity Threats at the Border:** A critical security breach has been reported in regions bordering Belarus, where residents attempting to reach emergency services via the 112 number are being intercepted by Belarusian dispatchers. Authorities have labeled this a sophisticated element of hybrid warfare aimed at destabilizing local safety.
*   **Investigation into Political Assassination:** New details have emerged regarding the murder of a Russian national in Biała Podlaska. The victim, known for his vocal opposition to the Russian regime, had reportedly received threats prior to the shooting. Two Belarusian citizens have been detained in connection with the case.
*   **Legislative Review of Public Official Earnings:** Prime Minister Donald Tusk has announced a new legislative project aimed at increasing transparency regarding the earnings of public officials and government employees. The move follows public scrutiny surrounding the compensation of high-profile medical professionals who hold political roles.
