## WORLD

*   **Heightened Military Conflict in Iran:** Tensions in the Middle East have reached a critical point as U.S. military assets face direct engagement. Officials confirmed that an American airman has been rescued following the downing of an F-15E fighter jet over Iran, with the status of a second crew member remaining uncertain. Reports also indicate a separate crash involving a U.S. combat plane in the Gulf region, further destabilizing the area.
*   **Trump Administration Proposes Record Defense Budget:** In a significant shift in U.S. fiscal priorities, the White House has announced plans to request a $1.5 trillion defense budget for the 2027 fiscal year. If approved, this would mark the largest military expenditure in modern history, accompanied by austerity measures in non-defense sectors.
*   **Geopolitical Shadow War Extends to Africa:** Investigative reports have unveiled the presence of Ukrainian special forces in Libya, marking a strategic shift in the ongoing conflict between Kyiv and Moscow. These operations are reportedly targeting Russian intelligence figures, remnants of the Wagner Group, and critical Russian commodity trade routes in Africa.

## POLAND

*   **Political Standoff Over Constitutional Tribunal:** Former Justice Minister Adam Bodnar has publicly criticized President Andrzej Duda’s recent actions regarding the Constitutional Tribunal. Bodnar accuses the administration of attempting to usurp power, specifically regarding the contentious appointment of judges and the legal validity of current procedural shifts.
*   **Public Safety Incident in Mazovia:** A serious collision involving a municipal bus and a private vehicle in Marki, near Warsaw, has left eight people injured. Local authorities, including Mayor Jacek Orych, have reported significant traffic disruptions as emergency services work to clear the site and investigate the cause of the accident.
