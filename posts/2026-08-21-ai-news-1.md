## WORLD

*   **Trump’s "Economic D-Day" and Iran:** The global energy and security landscape remains volatile as analysts assess the potential impact of Donald Trump’s intensified economic strategy against Iran. Despite years of stringent sanctions, Tehran has demonstrated significant resilience in navigating trade restrictions, though the new proposed measures represent a significant escalation that could further destabilize regional markets.
*   **Security Shifts in the Korean Peninsula:** Heightened concerns have emerged regarding the future of US-South Korea military relations. The early conclusion of joint exercises occurs amid a period of growing military confidence in the North, raising critical questions about the reliability of security guarantees should the US administration pursue a policy of personal rapprochement with Pyongyang at the expense of regional allies.

## POLAND

*   **Diplomatic Standoff with Israel:** The Polish Ministry of Foreign Affairs has summoned the Israeli Ambassador following a controversial decision by Israeli authorities to close the investigation into the death of Polish aid worker Damian Soból, who was killed in a strike in the Gaza Strip. Warsaw has formally demanded comprehensive explanations, signaling a sharp rise in diplomatic friction as Poland insists on receiving critical evidence to support its own ongoing investigation into the incident.
