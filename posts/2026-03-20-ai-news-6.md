## WORLD

* **Escalation in Iran Impacts Global Economy:** The deepening conflict in Iran is sending shockwaves through international markets. President Trump has sought to reassure the American public, hinting at potential adjustments to sanctions on Iranian oil to curb rising energy prices, while simultaneously drawing controversial comparisons between the current crisis and Pearl Harbor.
* **Security Concerns for Ukraine:** President Volodymyr Zelenskyy has warned that a protracted war in the Middle East plays directly into Russia's strategy. Zelenskyy expressed deep concern that sustained regional conflict will deplete Western air defense reserves, specifically mentioning the critical need for Patriot systems, which are essential for intercepting Russian Iskander missiles.
* **EU Standoff with Hungary:** Despite a deadlock at the Brussels summit where Viktor Orban blocked a 90-billion-euro loan for Kyiv, European Commission President Ursula von der Leyen has remained firm, declaring that the EU will find alternative ways to provide the necessary support to Ukraine.

## POLAND

* **Economic Anxiety and Energy Prices:** Amidst global instability, the Polish government is facing mounting pressure regarding fuel costs. Energy officials have acknowledged that options to prevent prices from reaching 8 PLN per liter are limited, with the administration focusing on tax adjustments and margin monitoring at state-controlled energy firms like Orlen.
* **Shifts in EU Climate Policy:** In a notable departure from long-standing policies, the European Union has begun to align with Warsaw’s critical stance on the Emissions Trading System (ETS). There is growing consensus within the EU that the current carbon pricing mechanism is a primary driver of high energy costs and requires urgent reform.
* **Public Consensus Against Military Involvement:** A recent IBRiS poll reveals a decisive public stance on the Middle East crisis, with 84.7% of Poles opposing the deployment of Polish troops to the region, even under a hypothetical request from the United States.
