## WORLD

*   **Tragedy in Indonesia:** A powerful 7.7-magnitude earthquake has struck near Flores island, resulting in at least 14 confirmed fatalities. Emergency response teams are currently assessing the scale of destruction, with fears that the casualty count may rise as search and rescue operations continue in the affected regions.
*   **Legal Challenges for the Trump Family:** The BBC is pursuing a subpoena against members of Donald Trump's family in relation to the ongoing Panorama lawsuit. Legal representatives contend that the family possesses critical, firsthand knowledge regarding the former President's intent during the events of January 6, 2021.

## POLAND

*   **Russian Strike on Dnipropetrovsk:** The security situation in Ukraine remains dire, as a fresh Russian attack on the Dnipropetrovsk region has resulted in civilian casualties, including the death of an infant and several injured children, further intensifying the humanitarian crisis near the Polish border.
*   **Aviation Safety Near-Miss:** Polish aviation authorities are investigating a potentially catastrophic incident where two aircraft were cleared for operation with identical flight numbers. The incident has raised serious questions regarding air traffic control protocols and safety management systems.
