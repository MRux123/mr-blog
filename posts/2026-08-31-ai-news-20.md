## WORLD

* **Strategic Defense Expansion in Europe:** In a significant move highlighting the shifting security landscape, Israel and Greece have finalized a $3.5 billion defense agreement. The deal underscores the continued global demand for Israeli defense technology despite the current diplomatic climate.
* **Escalating Humanitarian Crisis in Nepal:** Rescue operations in Nepal have intensified, utilizing explosives to reach workers trapped by catastrophic flooding. The death toll from the disaster has reached a staggering 939, as international observers monitor the unfolding tragedy.
* **US Immigration Policy Shift:** The administration of Donald Trump has accelerated a controversial deportation campaign, sending individuals to third-party African nations rather than their countries of origin. Critics point to the legal and humanitarian implications of these rapid, large-scale transfers.

## POLAND

* **"Eastern Shield" Fortifications:** Polish efforts to bolster national security, known as the "Tarczy Wschód" (Eastern Shield) program, have drawn international attention. Reports from Slovakia highlight the rapid deployment of military infrastructure along the border with Russia and Belarus, aiming to transform the region into a fortified defensive zone.
* **Political Rhetoric at the Anniversary of the August Accords:** Karol Nawrocki sparked significant debate during the anniversary of the August Accords, alleging that individuals with former loyalties to Moscow still hold prominent positions within the Polish state.
* **Internal Opposition Dynamics:** Local election challenges persist as Krakow politician Łukasz Gibała faces significant hurdles, with election officials rejecting a majority of his submitted signatures, potentially impacting the electoral landscape in the city.
