## WORLD

* **Escalation of Conflict in Iran**: The geopolitical landscape in the Middle East has reached a critical juncture. U.S. military operations against Iran have intensified, with reports indicating that over 8,000 targets have been struck in an effort to degrade Tehran's offensive capabilities. Despite the scale of the campaign, the strategic objectives of the U.S.-led intervention remain ambiguous, while Iranian forces continue to respond with persistent missile and drone strikes.
* **Rising Jihadist Violence in Africa**: A new report reveals a concerning trend in global security: while aggregate terrorism-related deaths have declined globally, localized violence has surged. Nigeria and the Democratic Republic of Congo have experienced a sharp increase in jihadist activity, with Nigeria seeing a 46% rise in fatalities, underscoring a growing security vacuum in parts of the African continent.

## POLAND

* **Tragic Fire on Ursynów**: Emergency services in Warsaw have confirmed a catastrophic incident in the Ursynów district. A vehicle fire, which spread to an adjacent building undergoing renovation as a shooting range, has resulted in four confirmed fatalities. Local authorities are currently investigating the circumstances surrounding the blaze.
* **Geopolitical Interference Allegations**: Reports have surfaced regarding a suspected Russian plot involving Hungarian Prime Minister Viktor Orbán. According to intelligence information cited by *The Washington Post*, Russian agencies allegedly proposed a staged assassination attempt to bolster Orbán's political standing ahead of upcoming elections, marking a potential escalation in foreign interference tactics within the region.
