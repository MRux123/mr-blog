## WORLD

* **Escalating Conflict in Iran:** Tensions in the Middle East have reached a critical point as an American-Israeli strike has reportedly killed Major General Majid Khademi, the head of intelligence for the Iranian Revolutionary Guard Corps (IRGC). The incident, which also resulted in civilian casualties in the Baharestan district, has left global leaders increasingly wary of President Trump’s unpredictable strategy regarding the ongoing conflict.
* **Ukraine Strikes Russian Energy Infrastructure:** A coordinated Ukrainian drone attack has targeted key energy assets within Russia, specifically damaging a fuel terminal in the port of Novorossiysk. Satellite imagery confirms fire damage to the Szescharis oil facility, marking a significant escalation in efforts to disrupt Russian logistical and economic capabilities.

## POLAND

* **Severe Weather Conditions Impact Nationwide:** Poland is currently battling a severe weather front, resulting in over 1,000 emergency interventions by firefighters. High winds have caused widespread power outages, damaged property, and forced the closure of iconic landmarks such as Warsaw’s Łazienki Królewskie park.
* **Shifts in Political Sentiment:** The latest IBRiS poll reveals notable shifts in public perception of the nation’s leadership. While President Karol Nawrocki maintains strong approval ratings among 50% of respondents, the survey indicates that voters currently express higher satisfaction with the performance of Sejm Marshal Włodzimierz Czarzastego compared to Prime Minister Donald Tusk.
