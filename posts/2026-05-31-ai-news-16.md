## WORLD

* **Escalation in Southern Lebanon:** Israeli forces have seized the historic Beaufort Castle, a site that served as a powerful symbol during Israel's long-term occupation of Lebanon. The capture comes as the conflict with Hezbollah continues to widen, raising concerns regarding the longevity and intensity of the military operations.
* **Deadly Mining Explosion in Myanmar:** A catastrophic explosion in the Kaungtup region has resulted in at least 46 fatalities and over 70 injuries. The incident, which occurred in a facility housing industrial mining explosives, highlights the ongoing safety and security challenges in the country.
* **Ebola Crisis in the DRC:** The World Health Organization (WHO) has issued an urgent appeal for community cooperation in Ituri province. Despite resistance to medical protocols, WHO Director-General Tedros Adhanom Ghebreyesus emphasized that containing the Ebola outbreak is a collective necessity for the region.

## POLAND

* **Political Landscape Shifts:** A new poll from the Pollster Institute indicates a tightening political race. While the Civic Coalition (KO) maintains a lead over Law and Justice (PiS), both major parties have experienced a dip in support. Notably, the Confederation party and Grzegorz Braun’s faction have seen gains, suggesting a shift in voter sentiment.
* **Police Intervention Under Investigation in Płock:** Authorities have launched a probe into the death of a 46-year-old man during a police intervention in Płock. The man lost consciousness while being handcuffed following a report of a disturbance, sparking public scrutiny regarding police protocols and incident management.
* **Strategic Pressure on Russian Logistics:** Military experts point to increasing operational difficulties for Russian forces, specifically regarding supply lines in southern Ukraine. Ukrainian strikes on these critical arteries have been described by analysts as a "heart attack" for the Russian logistical network, significantly hampering their military capabilities in the theater of war.
