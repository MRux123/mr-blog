## WORLD

* **U.S. Pilot Rescued in Iran:** American forces have successfully executed a high-stakes extraction mission deep within Iranian territory. The operation followed the downing of a U.S. F-15 fighter jet on Friday. President Trump confirmed the rescue, praising the military’s effectiveness in bringing the crew member back to safety despite the extreme tactical risks.
* **Drone Industry Restrictions in China:** The Chinese government has significantly tightened regulations on the national drone sector. While officials state the measures are intended to curb illegal flight activity, the move has sparked criticism from users and industry stakeholders, who argue the new policies are stifling legitimate operations and restricting airspace access too aggressively.
* **Festival Tragedy in Louisiana:** A major incident occurred in New Iberia, Louisiana, during a festival celebrating the Laotian New Year. A vehicle drove into a crowd of participants on Saturday, resulting in at least 18 injuries. Several victims are reported to be in serious condition as local law enforcement investigates the circumstances of the crash.

## POLAND

* **Liturgy and Easter Observances:** As the nation marks Resurrection Sunday, the Catholic Church in Poland celebrates the most significant day in the religious calendar. Broadcasters, including Polsat News, are providing nationwide coverage of the holy mass from the Temple of Divine Providence in Warsaw, emphasizing the spiritual and cultural importance of the holiday.
* **The Musical Depth of Easter:** Experts and liturgical musicians are reflecting on the diverse landscape of Easter music, which spans from solemn, reflective pieces expressing suffering to triumphant compositions celebrating the resurrection. The discussion highlights the bridge between theological tradition and the human experience of hope and life.
