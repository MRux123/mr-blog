## WORLD

* **Hamas Appoints New Leader:** Khalil al-Hayya has been named as the new overall leader of Hamas. Based in Qatar, the veteran negotiator steps into the role following the death of Yahya Sinwar, who was killed by Israeli forces in 2024. This leadership transition marks a significant shift for the organization amidst the ongoing regional conflict.
* **Escalating Black Sea Conflict:** Tensions in the Black Sea have reached a critical point as Russian strikes on merchant vessels intensify. Local authorities in the Odesa region report that 28 people have been killed in July alone, highlighting the increasing danger to maritime logistics and regional stability.
* **South China Sea Confrontation:** A volatile incident was reported in the South China Sea, involving what authorities described as a "malicious attack" using makeshift weapons, including oars and sticks, further inflaming maritime territorial disputes in the region.

## POLAND

* **Discovery of Mass Graves in Volhynia:** Ongoing archaeological efforts in Volhynia have led to a significant discovery by the Institute of National Remembrance (IPN). Over the second week of work, researchers uncovered the remains of at least 38 individuals, marking another chapter in the investigation of historical atrocities.
* **Healthcare Salary Disputes:** The Polish government has signaled intentions to curb medical professionals' salaries, sparking a heated national debate. Critics and unions warn that this policy shift could serve as a precursor to the privatization of the public healthcare sector, raising concerns about the future accessibility of medical services.
* **Public Unrest in Kyiv:** Protests in Kyiv have intensified, with demonstrators issuing an ultimatum to President Zelensky. The protesters are demanding the reinstatement of Mychajło Fedorow as Minister of Defense and the removal of Oleksandr Syrsky from his role as commander-in-chief, citing these as essential conditions for national stability.
