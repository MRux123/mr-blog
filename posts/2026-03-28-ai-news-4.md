## WORLD

* **Nepal’s Former Prime Minister Arrested:** Authorities have detained the former Prime Minister of Nepal in connection to a violent crackdown on protests that occurred last year. The uprising, which ultimately led to the ousting of his government, resulted in more than 70 casualties, marking a significant moment of political reckoning in the region.
* **UN Debate on Reparations for Slavery:** The United Nations is currently navigating a complex discourse regarding the legacy of slavery. African and Caribbean nations are pressing for compensation from countries that historically benefited from the institution. While the international community labels it a "grave crime against humanity," the path toward tangible restitution remains legally and diplomatically fraught.
* **Resilience of BBC Russian Service:** Celebrating 80 years of operation, the BBC Russian service continues to navigate significant state-imposed obstacles. Having transitioned from decades of signal jamming during the Cold War to the current era of total website blockades, the outlet remains a critical source of information, maintaining a massive audience despite attempts to silence its digital presence.
