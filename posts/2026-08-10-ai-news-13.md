## WORLD

* **Deadly Drone Strike in Tatarstan:** A significant Ukrainian drone attack on an industrial center in Russia's Tatarstan region has resulted in at least 13 deaths and 75 injuries. This incident marks one of the most lethal incursions into Russian territory since the full-scale invasion began, signaling a critical escalation in the conflict's reach.
* **Escalation in the Strait of Hormuz:** Geopolitical stability in the Middle East remains fragile as Iran confirms the continued closure of the Strait of Hormuz. Tehran has stated that the waterway will remain inaccessible to shipping until specific demands are met by the Trump administration, complicating regional trade and security dynamics.
* **Military Pressure in Southern Iran:** U.S. forces have intensified their aerial campaign in southern Iran, specifically targeting Qeshm island. While ground fighting has largely subsided elsewhere, these sustained airstrikes are causing significant disruption to local infrastructure and civilian life.

## POLAND

* **Flood Relief Scandal:** A major controversy has emerged regarding the mismanagement of 4.5 million PLN in flood relief funds. Investigations suggest that grants from the Karkonosze Regional Development Agency were funneled to firms associated with Anna Konieczyńska, a local KO politician, leading to her immediate suspension and sparking a wider political debate.
* **National Power Grid Alert:** The Government Security Centre (RCB) has issued an urgent alert across five Polish provinces. Authorities have warned residents of potential power grid failures, signaling a period of infrastructure vulnerability that requires immediate public vigilance.
* **Judicial Misconduct:** A significant legal issue has surfaced following the detention of a judge found driving under the influence. The case has ignited a public outcry, as the official remains shielded from standard police prosecution due to judicial immunity, renewing discussions about the limits of professional privileges.
