## WORLD

* **Iran signals shift in diplomatic stance:** In a significant development, Iran's Supreme Leader, Mojtaba Khamenei, has signaled a willingness to pursue diplomatic avenues to conclude the ongoing conflict. This shift has prompted immediate action from the United States, with Vice President J.D. Vance expected to travel to Pakistan alongside special envoys Steve Witkoff and Jared Kushner to facilitate further talks.
* **FBI investigates wave of scientist disappearances:** The Federal Bureau of Investigation has confirmed it is actively probing the mysterious deaths and disappearances of 11 scientists over the past four years. The scale and nature of these incidents have triggered an inter-agency investigation to determine if a broader, coordinated threat exists.

## POLAND

* **Internal stability restored within Law and Justice (PiS):** Following intense speculation, former Prime Minister Mateusz Morawiecki and party leader Jarosław Kaczyński have reached a formal reconciliation. The agreement confirms that Morawiecki’s newly formed "Development Plus" association will operate within the existing structure of the Law and Justice party, effectively silencing rumors of a fractured opposition wing.
* **Industrial fire containment in Mazovia:** Local authorities have successfully brought a major fire at a food processing plant in Bramki under control. The incident, which resulted in the total destruction of two industrial halls, led to the injury of one firefighter during the emergency response. An investigation into the cause of the blaze is currently underway.
