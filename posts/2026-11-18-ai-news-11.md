## WORLD

* **High-Stakes Diplomacy in Asia:** President Trump has arrived in the region for a critical summit, meeting with Philippine leadership before heading to Shenzhen for high-level discussions with China’s Xi Jinping and Russia’s Vladimir Putin. These meetings occur against the backdrop of heightened geopolitical tension, specifically concerning the "Nvidia spy scandal," which has intensified scrutiny over advanced chip exports to China.
* **Legal and Humanitarian Concerns at the ICJ:** The International Court of Justice is under intense scrutiny as it prepares to rule on potential genocide charges regarding Myanmar’s actions against the Rohingya. Fears of political friction are mounting, with reports suggesting that the United States may consider sanctions against ICJ judges if the court delivers a ruling that might set a sensitive legal precedent for future international conflicts.

## POLAND

* **Economic Solidarity with Ukraine:** The European Union has moved forward with a significant financial package, pledging a €45 billion loan for Ukraine. This initiative, designed to ensure broad participation from all EU member states, reinforces the bloc’s ongoing commitment to supporting Kyiv’s economic and defensive resilience.
* **Strategic Energy Policy Alignment:** In a move mirroring recent Polish domestic policies, the German government has finalized a late-night agreement to lower electricity taxes and implement fuel price caps. This decision marks a significant shift in regional energy policy as nations across Europe scramble to mitigate the impact of rising costs for consumers entering the new year.
