## WORLD

* **U.S. Military Strategic Realignment in Europe:** President Trump has signaled a significant escalation in the withdrawal of American forces from Europe, suggesting the reduction in Germany will exceed the previously stated 5,000 troops. Plans to potentially withdraw personnel from bases in Italy and Spain are also under consideration, marking a major shift in transatlantic security dynamics.
* **Escalation in Lebanon:** Following alleged breaches of the ceasefire agreement, the Israeli military has issued urgent evacuation orders for 11 towns and villages in southern Lebanon. The move indicates a renewed intensity in operations targeting Hezbollah, raising concerns about the stability of the fragile truce.
* **Geopolitical Volatility in Pakistan:** U.S. mining interests in Pakistan are facing severe instability due to an insurgency led by the Baloch Liberation Army. The group’s activities threaten a significant billion-dollar investment deal, further complicated by the hazardous security environment in the Balochistan region.

## POLAND

* **Recognition of Andrzej Poczobut:** In a high-profile ceremony at the Royal Castle, Andrzej Poczobut, a prominent activist and prisoner of the Belarusian regime, is set to be honored with the Order of the White Eagle. The award serves as a testament to his resistance and a symbolic gesture of national support.
* **Large-Scale Industrial Fire in Wielkopolska:** A massive fire broke out at a sawmill in Racot, requiring a significant emergency response. At its peak, 23 fire units were deployed to contain the blaze, which destroyed the production hall. Fortunately, no casualties were reported.
* **End of Railway Disruptions:** After persistent technical issues that caused significant delays, authorities have confirmed that normal rail operations have been restored on the strategic Poznań-Berlin line, providing relief to thousands of passengers traveling over the long weekend.
