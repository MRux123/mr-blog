## WORLD

*   **Massive Drone Offensive Targets Moscow:** Russian airspace defenses reported the interception of 108 Ukrainian unmanned aerial vehicles (UAVs) in a coordinated strike directed at the capital. The intensity of the attack, which stands as the largest of its kind this year, necessitated the partial suspension of flight operations at all airports serving the Moscow metropolitan area, marking a significant escalation in the ongoing conflict.

## POLAND

*   **Systemic Inaction Toward Pathostreaming:** A new investigative report by Polsat News has brought the controversial phenomenon of "pathostreaming" back into the public spotlight. Despite heightened public discourse and declarations of legislative reform, high-profile content creators continue to monetize abuse, substance misuse, and the exploitation of vulnerable individuals. The report underscores a profound disconnect between the escalating harms of these broadcasts and the sluggish response of the Polish legal and regulatory systems.
