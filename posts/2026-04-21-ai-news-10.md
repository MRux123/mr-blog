## WORLD

*   **Geopolitical Tension in the Middle East:** Uncertainty persists regarding U.S.-Iran diplomatic talks as a potential cease-fire nears its expiration. The situation remains volatile, with reports suggesting that U.S. Navy destroyers in the region are operating with upgraded defensive capabilities, a direct response to lessons learned from historical naval engagements in the area.
*   **Japan’s Strategic Pivot:** In a significant departure from its post-WWII pacifist doctrine, Japan has announced the reversal of strict limits on arms exports. Prime Minister Sanae Takaichi’s decision reflects growing regional security concerns, specifically regarding China’s military posture and shifting alliances in the Indo-Pacific.
*   **Political Controversy in the UK:** The office of British Prime Minister Keir Starmer faces scrutiny following claims from a fired official regarding the appointment of Peter Mandelson as ambassador to Washington. Concerns have been raised regarding the administration's dismissal of security vetting reports associated with Mandelson’s past ties.

## POLAND

*   **CBA Raid at the Ministry of Climate:** Central Anticorruption Bureau (CBA) agents have executed a search at the Ministry of Climate and Environment. The investigation focuses on irregularities within the "Czyste Powietrze" (Clean Air) government program, marking a high-profile escalation in administrative oversight.
*   **Zondacrypto Investigation:** The Regional Prosecutor's Office in Katowice, supported by the Central Bureau for Combating Cybercrime, is investigating a major fraud case involving Zondacrypto. With potentially thousands of victims affected, the case highlights growing risks in the digital asset sector.
*   **40th Anniversary of Chernobyl:** Poland joins the international community in commemorating four decades since the Chernobyl disaster. The event serves as a poignant reminder of the enduring health and environmental consequences of the tragedy, as shared by surviving liquidators who continue to deal with the long-term impacts of radiation exposure.
