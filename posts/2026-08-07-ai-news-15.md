## WORLD

* **Strategic Defense Pact Signed:** Saudi Arabia, Turkey, and Pakistan have formalized a joint defense agreement. The treaty mandates a mutual response to any aggression against the signatory nations, marking a significant and potentially volatile shift in Middle Eastern and South Asian geopolitical power dynamics.
* **Thai School Shooting Tragedy:** A 14-year-old student has killed his grandparents and five teachers before taking his own life. The incident has sent shockwaves through the international community and reignited a contentious debate regarding firearm legislation and youth mental health access in Thailand.
* **Diplomatic Thaw:** Mexico and Peru have officially restored diplomatic relations, ending a months-long standoff that began following an asylum row involving former Peruvian Prime Minister Betssy Chávez.

## POLAND

* **Wołyń Exhumations:** The Institute of National Remembrance (IPN) has released a sobering report on the latest exhumation efforts in the former Polish villages of Ostrówki and Wola Ostrowiecka. Forensic teams recovered the remains of 55 victims of the World War II-era massacres, alongside over 300 personal items, providing closure for many families.
* **Tribute to Fallen Volunteer:** Ukraine and Poland have joined in mourning the loss of Marek Rusiek-Wolski, a Polish volunteer killed in a Russian drone strike near Kharkiv. His death has been highlighted by officials as a testament to the tangible solidarity between the two nations in the ongoing conflict.
* **Diplomatic Friction with Moscow:** Tensions between Warsaw and the Kremlin have intensified following aggressive remarks by Russian Foreign Ministry spokesperson Maria Zakharova, who launched a personal attack on Polish President Karol Nawrocki, accusing him of "clinical Russophobia" in response to recent official statements regarding historical and contemporary Polish-Russian relations.
