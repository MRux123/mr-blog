## WORLD

*   **Political Turmoil in Georgia:** The Georgian parliament has descended into chaos following a violent altercation between ruling party members and the opposition. The incident, which necessitated medical intervention, was sparked by intense scrutiny regarding a video featuring Prime Minister Irakli Kobachidze alongside high-ranking officials, highlighting deep-seated instability within the nation's political landscape.
*   **Ukrainian Air Force Losses:** Ukraine’s air defense capabilities have suffered a setback following the crash of a MiG-29 fighter jet in the Poltava region. This incident marks the second loss of a military aircraft in less than two weeks, raising questions regarding operational maintenance and the pressures of sustained combat missions.

## POLAND

*   **Military Vehicle Accident:** A serious road accident involving a military truck occurred in the Warmian-Masurian Voivodeship, resulting in injuries to two soldiers. The vehicle veered off the road and struck a tree, necessitating an emergency response from the Polish Medical Air Rescue (LPR). The Military Gendarmerie has launched an investigation to determine the cause of the crash.
*   **Industrial Fire in Małopolska:** A major fire has broken out at a production facility in Mszana Dolna, located in the Limanowa district. Emergency services are currently on-site managing the blaze. Officials have confirmed that at least one person was injured during the evacuation process as firefighters work to contain the spread of the flames.
