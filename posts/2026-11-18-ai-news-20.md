## WORLD

* **Trilateral Diplomacy in China:** President Trump has arrived in Shenzhen for a high-stakes regional summit, marked by a significant diplomatic meeting with Chinese leader Xi Jinping and Russian President Vladimir Putin. Observers are closely watching the trilateral interactions, which may signal a shift in the current geopolitical landscape.
* **Escalating Gulf Tensions:** The Middle East faces renewed instability as Kuwait and Bahrain report Iranian missile and drone attacks. The strikes follow recent U.S. military action against the Iranian island of Qeshm, further complicating efforts to maintain regional peace and leading to fears of a wider conflict.
* **Legal Precedent and Myanmar:** The International Court of Justice is expected to deliver a pivotal ruling on whether the Myanmar government committed genocide against the Rohingya. The case carries significant international implications, as concerns mount regarding potential U.S. sanctions against ICJ judges involved in sensitive rulings.

## POLAND

* **Constitutional Tribunal Reform Deadlock:** Negotiations concerning the status and future of the Constitutional Tribunal continue between the Presidential Chancellery and opposition-led political factions. While officials claim consensus on roughly 70 percent of proposed solutions, significant friction remains regarding the legitimacy of current judicial appointments and the scope of necessary reforms.
* **Supreme Court Issues Key Ruling:** The Polish Supreme Court has issued a significant resolution regarding the Constitutional Tribunal, clarifying that a valid ruling requires a panel composed strictly of judges who have been properly sworn in. The decision adds a new legal layer to the ongoing political dispute over the functioning of the country’s highest constitutional body.
* **Medical Transparency and Healthcare Concerns:** The healthcare system remains under public scrutiny following reports of bureaucratic failures in the management of post-miscarriage medical care in Oleśnica. The case has sparked national debate over patient rights and the quality of procedures in state hospitals.
