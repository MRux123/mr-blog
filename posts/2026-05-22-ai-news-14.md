## WORLD

*   **NATO and US Deployment Strategy:** President Donald Trump has signaled a push for increased US troop deployments in Poland, seemingly reversing a recent administrative decision to cancel such movements. Senator Marco Rubio is currently engaged in diplomatic efforts to reassure NATO allies regarding the consistency of US military commitments amidst these policy shifts.
*   **Geopolitical Shifts in Hungary and Ukraine:** The Russian military has launched strikes on the Transcarpathia region of Ukraine, home to a significant ethnic Hungarian population. Analysts interpret this as a strategic message to the new Hungarian government under Peter Magyar, signaling the end of the informal non-aggression status that the region enjoyed under the previous administration of Viktor Orbán.
*   **Afghanistan: Human Rights Concerns:** The United Nations has issued a strong warning regarding a new Taliban decree concerning divorce in Afghanistan. The law formalizes child marriage practices by requiring girls to reach puberty before they can seek to dissolve a marriage, while also mandating mediation processes for women attempting to escape abusive domestic situations.

## POLAND

*   **Political Corruption Investigation:** The investigation into the "Fast Track" (Szybka ścieżka) digital innovation grants at the National Centre for Research and Development (NCBiR) has reached a critical stage. The European Public Prosecutor's Office has ordered the arrest of eight individuals, including a former deputy minister, Jacek Ż., marking a significant escalation in the probe into misappropriated public funds.
*   **Legal Challenges for Former Officials:** Law enforcement and judicial authorities are moving forward with legal proceedings against Michał Woś. The development is part of a broader trend of increased legal scrutiny toward members of the former government.
*   **Maritime Security Alarms:** The Swedish press is raising alarms over the passage of the "Russian shadow fleet" through the Sund Strait. The activity, occurring simultaneously with a high-level NATO diplomatic meeting in Helsingborg, has prompted security concerns regarding Russia's ability to operate largely unchecked in proximity to critical Baltic shipping lanes.
