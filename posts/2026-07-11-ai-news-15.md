## WORLD

*   **Tragic Wildfire in Southern Spain:** A devastating wildfire in southern Spain has resulted in 12 fatalities, with over 20 people reported missing. The disaster, described as one of the deadliest in the region's history, has claimed victims primarily from Belgium and Britain, triggering an urgent international response.
*   **Escalation Concerns Regarding Russia:** Intelligence assessments from Kyiv suggest that the Kremlin is preparing for a major mobilization phase this autumn. Analysts point to a shift in the rhetoric employed by Kremlin spokesperson Dmitry Peskov as a clear indicator that the Russian government is conditioning its population for restrictive measures, including potential travel bans.

## POLAND

*   **Political Clash over Volhynia Exhumations:** Tensions between the Presidential Palace and the current government have reached a new peak. Presidential spokesperson Rafał Leśkiewicz accused Prime Minister Donald Tusk of manipulation regarding the authorization of exhumations in Volhynia, Ukraine. Defense Minister Władysław Kosiniak-Kamysz refuted these claims, advocating for a more balanced historical narrative between the two nations.
*   **Tragic Killing in Jędrzejów:** A 19-year-old suspect has been charged with the murder of a 29-year-old woman in Jędrzejów. The prosecutor's office has filed a motion for temporary arrest, though the suspect has chosen to remain silent and has not entered a plea, marking a somber turn for the local community.
