## WORLD

* **Strategic Escalation in Crimea:** Ukrainian forces have successfully executed an "unprecedented" and multi-stage attack on the Crimean Bridge, a key logistical artery for Russian forces. The operation caused partial structural collapse, marking a significant tactical blow to Moscow's supply lines in the occupied territory.
* **Geopolitical Realignment:** U.S. President Donald Trump is scheduled for a high-stakes trilateral meeting with Xi Jinping and Vladimir Putin in Shenzhen, signaling a major shift in international diplomatic relations amid ongoing global conflicts.
* **Corruption Crackdown in Hungary:** Authorities in Hungary have uncovered a massive cache of $20 million in illicit cash hidden in forests and rural properties, sparking a national investigation into the legacy of former Prime Minister Viktor Orban’s administration.

## POLAND

* **Strategic Infrastructure Risks:** The Polish government is facing scrutiny over national security after reports revealed that a critical strategic mine in Norway has fallen under the control of a businessman with close ties to the Kremlin.
* **Regional Security Threats:** Security concerns in Eastern Europe have intensified as the Moldovan government was forced to close its airspace following reports of Russian drone incursions over the capital, Chisinau.
* **Diplomatic Legacy:** Former U.S. President Joe Biden has addressed Poland's geopolitical standing in his latest publication, offering an analytical perspective on the nation's role within the current transatlantic security framework.
