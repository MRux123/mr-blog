* **Legal Crisis for Julius Malema:** South African political landscape faces uncertainty following a five-year prison sentence handed down to Julius Malema, leader of the Economic Freedom Fighters (EFF). The conviction stems from an incident in 2018 involving the discharge of a firearm at a public rally. While Malema remains free pending an appeal, the verdict marks a pivotal moment for one of the country's most prominent and polarizing opposition figures.
