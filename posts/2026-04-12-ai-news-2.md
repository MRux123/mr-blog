## WORLD

* **Iran-US Peace Talks Stagnate:** Diplomatic efforts to de-escalate the conflict between the United States and Iran have hit a critical impasse. Following a 21-hour marathon session in Islamabad, Vice President JD Vance announced that the Iranian delegation rejected American terms for a ceasefire. The failure of these talks leaves the region in a state of continued volatility.
* **Artemis Mission Success:** The Artemis crew has returned safely to Houston following a historic nine-day lunar orbit. The mission represents a significant milestone in space exploration, as the crew traveled further from Earth than any humans in history, successfully proving long-range deep space operational capabilities.
* **Chimpanzee 'Civil War' Observed:** In an unprecedented discovery in Uganda's Kibale National Park, researchers have documented what appears to be the first instance of a unified chimpanzee community collapsing into a coordinated, violent internal conflict. The study provides new insights into primate social structures and the roots of collective aggression.

## POLAND

* **Referendum on Orbán’s Tenure:** Hungarian voters are heading to the polls in a pivotal election that could end Viktor Orbán’s 16-year governance. The atmosphere is tense as challenger Péter Magyar, leading a grassroots movement, gains momentum in the polls against the incumbent Prime Minister. The outcome is expected to have profound implications for the political landscape of Central and Eastern Europe, with significant reverberations likely felt across the Polish political spectrum.
