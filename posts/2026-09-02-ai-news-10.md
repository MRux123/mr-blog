## WORLD

*   **Crisis in Nepal and Tibet:** The humanitarian situation in the Himalayan region has reached a critical stage, with over 1,000 confirmed dead and thousands still missing following severe flooding. Relief operations are struggling to reach isolated communities, relying heavily on emergency airlift supplies.
*   **Escalation in Lebanon:** Tensions in occupied Lebanon have intensified as United Nations peacekeeping missions report that Israeli forces are actively fortifying positions and conducting ongoing demolitions, further complicating the precarious security landscape in the region.
*   **Global Health Risk:** A comprehensive study covering 2006–2023 reveals that nearly half of the world's farmers suffer from acute pesticide poisoning annually. With 11,000 fatalities reported each year—a majority of which occur in India—the findings call for an urgent re-evaluation of global agricultural safety standards.

## POLAND

*   **Russian Hybrid Warfare:** Polish Foreign Minister Radosław Sikorski has characterized recent hostile activities as part of an ongoing hybrid war waged by Russia. The statement reflects growing alarm within the Polish government regarding foreign-state-sponsored destabilization attempts.
*   **Sabotage Concerns:** Security services are investigating potential sabotage attempts targeting critical energy infrastructure in Germany, which has led to increased monitoring of power grids and related facilities across the region.
*   **Domestic Political Friction:** Tensions between the legislative leadership and opposition figures have peaked, with Włodzimierz Czarzasty publicly questioning the integrity of presidential project management in the Sejm, further highlighting the polarized political climate in the country.
