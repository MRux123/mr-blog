## WORLD

*   **Trump and the G7 Iran Deal Paradox:** Tensions remain high as President Trump praises a preliminary agreement with Iran at the G7 summit, while simultaneously threatening to resume attacks if the deal is not strictly implemented. The President has drawn a sharp contrast between his current approach and the 2015 Obama-era framework, which he continues to belittle.
*   **FOI Plot Against White House Event:** The FBI has revealed details of a dismantled plot by an extremist group that planned to use snipers and drones to attack a UFC event hosted at the White House. The group’s grievances reportedly centered on corruption allegations and specific government data practices.
*   **Mass Child Abuse Charges in Portugal:** In a landmark legal development, the former Deputy Minister of Justice of Portugal, Paulo Abreu dos Santos, is facing nearly 8,000 charges related to child sexual abuse and the distribution of illicit materials. The suspect has reportedly confessed to the acts, shaking the nation's political landscape.

## POLAND

*   **Strengthening Polish-German Defense Cooperation:** The Polish and German Ministers of Defense, Władysław Kosiniak-Kamysz and Boris Pistorius, have signed a significant bilateral agreement in Warsaw. The pact focuses on enhancing regional security through joint military exercises, infrastructure development, and integrated cyber-defense strategies, emphasizing the importance of NATO and EU unity.
*   **Political Accountability and Local Turmoil:** Dawid Kacprzyk, involved in a high-profile controversy, has been dismissed from the Southern Hospital in Warsaw, with the institution formally terminating his contracts. Simultaneously, administrative instability continues at the municipal level, as a movement to trigger a referendum to recall the President of Częstochowa, Krzysztof Matyjaszczyk, officially begins its signature collection phase.
