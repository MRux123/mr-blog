## WORLD

* **High-Stakes Diplomacy in Washington:** President Trump is hosting Ukrainian President Volodymyr Zelensky at the White House this week. The meeting is being closely watched to see if there is any thawing in the relationship between the two leaders, as the international community assesses the shifting dynamics of the U.S.-Ukraine alliance.
* **Catastrophic Earthquake in Japan:** Emergency services in the Kumamoto prefecture are engaged in a desperate search-and-rescue operation following a 6.8 magnitude earthquake. Initial reports indicate a mall collapse with many people feared trapped, complicating relief efforts as responders battle secondary fires.
* **Escalation in Geopolitical Stability:** Concerns are mounting over global security, with reports suggesting a potential escalation involving Iran and its stance toward the ongoing conflict in Ukraine, signaling a worrying complexity in current international security architectures.

## POLAND

* **Government Challenges Constitutional Court Ruling:** The Polish government has issued a sharp rebuke regarding a recent Constitutional Tribunal (TK) ruling. Officials have openly questioned the legitimacy of the decision, stating that it is "difficult to treat as a formal ruling," which underscores the deepening legal and political impasse within the country.
* **International Vandalism Incident:** A Polish national has been arrested in Medjugorje, Bosnia and Herzegovina, following the alleged desecration of a monument dedicated to the Virgin Mary. Local authorities are investigating the motive behind the act, which has sparked significant public reaction.
