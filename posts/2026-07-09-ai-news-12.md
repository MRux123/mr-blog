## WORLD

*   **US-Iran Conflict Intensifies:** The cycle of violence in the Persian Gulf has escalated significantly as both the U.S. and Iran trade strikes for the second consecutive night. Following President Trump's declaration that the ceasefire is effectively "over," maritime traffic through the Strait of Hormuz has plummeted. Analysts warn that Iran’s aggressive posturing, including attacks on tankers, risks severe economic and military consequences.
*   **NATO Dynamics:** While the NATO summit has become a platform for public friction between President Trump and European allies, behind-the-scenes negotiations suggest a pragmatic shift. The alliance is steadily moving toward a consensus that demands greater defense responsibility from European member states, signaling a long-term strategic adjustment regardless of political rhetoric.

## POLAND

*   **Healthcare System Strains:** Tensions between the government and the medical community have reached a critical point. Recent negotiations between the Ministry of Health and medical representatives ended in disappointment, with doctors expressing frustration over the lack of concrete proposals. Simultaneously, investigations into "scandalous" contractual practices at regional facilities, such as the hospital in Braniewo, have exposed deep systemic inefficiencies and concerns over patient safety.
*   **Legal and Security Developments:** A Polish court has handed down sentences to two Russian nationals for espionage-related activities, including intelligence gathering on Russian dissidents and involvement in the delivery of explosive materials. The verdict underscores the ongoing security challenges posed by foreign intelligence operations within Polish borders.
*   **Local Governance Changes:** The city of Krakow is set to tighten its alcohol policy by extending nighttime prohibition hours from 22:00 to 6:00. While local authorities cite a 70% decrease in police interventions since the initial policy rollout as justification, the move faces stiff opposition from small business owners concerned about economic impact.
