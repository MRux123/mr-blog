## WORLD

*   **Top Iranian Security Official Killed in Airstrike:** Ali Larijani, a high-ranking Iranian security figure often described as the country's de facto leader in recent weeks, has been killed in an Israeli strike. State media also confirmed the death of the commander of the Basij militia, marking a significant escalation in regional hostilities.
*   **Mass Detentions Linked to Starlink in Iran:** Amid heightened tensions, Iranian authorities have arrested dozens of individuals, including ten foreign nationals, accusing them of espionage for the US and Israel. Reports indicate the detainees were utilizing the Starlink satellite network to bypass strict government internet censorship, which the regime is now characterizing as an act of treason.

## POLAND

*   **Court Verdict in Holland-Ziobro Defamation Case:** The District Court in Warsaw has ruled against former Justice Minister Zbigniew Ziobro, finding that he violated the personal rights of film director Agnieszka Holland. Ziobro criticized the verdict, claiming it undermines the integrity of Polish uniformed services during current border hybrid threats.
*   **IPN Intervenes in "Scandalous" Auction:** The Institute of National Remembrance (IPN) is taking action regarding the attempted sale of a Virtuti Militari order—the highest Polish military decoration—belonging to Captain Juliusz Roman Heinzl, who was murdered by the NKVD in 1940. The medal appeared on a Ukrainian auction platform, prompting an official call for its repatriation.
*   **Procedural Setback in "Trial of the Century":** The Vatican’s appellate court has ordered a retrial for Cardinal Angelo Becciu, citing significant procedural irregularities in the initial proceedings regarding financial misconduct and real estate investments.
