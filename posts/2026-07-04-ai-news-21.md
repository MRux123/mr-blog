## WORLD

* **State Funeral for Ayatollah Khamenei:** Tehran has become the epicenter of global attention as massive crowds gather for the state funeral of Ayatollah Ali Khamenei. His death, following the recent U.S.-Israeli military engagement, has plunged the region into a state of deep uncertainty and mourning.
* **USA Commemorates 250th Anniversary:** The United States is marking its Semiquincentennial, though festivities across the nation have been severely disrupted by extreme weather. Temperatures reaching 44°C and impending severe storms forced officials, including Vice President J.D. Vance, to scale back major events, including the naval review in New York.

## POLAND

* **Controversy Over Patriot Missiles for Ukraine:** Tensions between the Polish government and the President’s office have surfaced regarding the potential transfer of PAC-3MSE missiles to Ukraine. Zbigniew Bogucki, head of the Chancellery of the President, drew parallels to previous controversies over fighter jet transfers, suggesting a lack of transparency in defense decision-making.
* **Constitutional Tribunal Standoff:** A political deadlock persists concerning the appointment of Sławomir Patyra to the Constitutional Tribunal. Despite being selected for the position, Patyra remains unable to take his oath of office, with presidential officials citing that a formal invitation from the President remains contingent on further political and legal developments.
