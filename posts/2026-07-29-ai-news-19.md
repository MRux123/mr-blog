## WORLD

*   **Middle East Conflict Intensifies:** The regional conflict has reached a critical juncture as the U.S. and Saudi Arabia conducted joint airstrikes against Iran-backed militias in Iraq, following retaliatory strikes by Iran on U.S. military sites in Jordan. The situation presents a difficult dilemma for Saudi Arabia, which is balancing the necessity of deterrence with the urgent need to prevent a full-scale regional war.
*   **Devastating Wildfires in Southern Europe:** Extreme weather conditions and gale-force winds are fueling severe wildfires across parts of Southern Europe, specifically impacting Crete and the Gironde region of France. The tragedy has claimed the lives of two firefighters in Greece, while emergency services continue to scramble to evacuate villages and contain the spread of the flames.

## POLAND

*   **Political Scheduling in Krakow:** Following the successful referendum in May to remove the previous leadership, the Prime Minister has set the date for the special mayoral election in Krakow for September 27. This decision marks a significant step in re-establishing administrative stability in the city.
*   **Tragedy in the Tatra Mountains:** Mountain rescue operations have been active following the death of a 59-year-old Polish tourist on the Rysy trail. This incident marks the second fatal tragedy in the same area within a 48-hour period, highlighting the inherent risks for hikers under current conditions.
*   **Domestic Political Rhetoric:** The political landscape remains polarized as figures such as Adrian Zandberg (Razem) publicly challenged the legislative record of the former administration, specifically questioning the outcomes of Mateusz Morawiecki’s governance policies during a recent media appearance.
