## WORLD

* **Donald Trump Nominated for Nobel Peace Prize:** Amidst escalating global tensions, the Norwegian Nobel Committee has confirmed that U.S. President Donald Trump is among the 287 candidates for this year's Nobel Peace Prize. The nomination highlights the ongoing international debate regarding the administration's foreign policy approach, which remains a focal point for global stability.
* **London Stabbing Attack Treated as Terrorism:** British authorities have officially classified the recent stabbing of two Jewish men in London as a terrorist incident. The attack has triggered urgent warnings from police regarding the surge in antisemitic hate crimes, prompting a broader security review across the United Kingdom.

## POLAND

* **Generals Nominations Under President Nawrocki:** President Karol Nawrocki has formally approved new general and admiral nominations for the Polish Armed Forces. The ceremony, scheduled for May 2, marks a significant moment for the nation's defense leadership, underscoring the ongoing restructuring and professionalization of the military establishment.
* **Mercosur Beef Imports Face Tariff Changes:** The European Commission has officially confirmed that a substantial volume of high-quality beef from Mercosur countries will enter the European market duty-free starting tomorrow. This policy shift regarding the "Hilton quota" affects trade dynamics and domestic agricultural markets, ending the previous 20 percent tariff regime.
* **Political Realignment in the Opposition:** Janusz Kowalski has officially departed from the Law and Justice (PiS) party, with his resignation being formally accepted. This departure represents a noteworthy change in the landscape of the Polish parliamentary opposition as political actors recalibrate ahead of upcoming legislative sessions.
