## WORLD

* **Escalation of Hybrid Threats in Germany:** German authorities are investigating a serious security incident at Leipzig airport, where a drone equipped with an explosive device was discovered. Interior Minister Alexander Dobrindt has characterized this event as a "new quality of threat," explicitly suggesting the involvement of foreign state actors in what appears to be an act of hybrid warfare.
* **Tensions in the US Administration:** Reports from *The Washington Post* indicate rising friction within the Trump administration regarding military logistics. President Trump has reportedly demanded urgent clarifications from Pete Hegseth concerning depleted ammunition stockpiles, expressing frustration over allegedly misleading information provided to him regarding the status of these supplies.
* **Large-scale Rescue Operation in Nigeria:** In a significant security development, Nigerian government forces successfully conducted a major operation to rescue over 300 abductees. This marks one of the largest single-day recovery missions in the country's recent history, highlighting ongoing efforts to combat kidnapping rings.

## POLAND

* **Potential Revision of National Holidays:** The Polish government is considering legislative changes to the national calendar. Proposals include renaming Independence Day celebrations and expanding the significance of Flag Day to include "The White Eagle," aiming to strengthen national identity through state symbolism.
* **Heightened Security Alert:** Following regional concerns, Polish authorities are intensifying vigilance regarding potential security breaches. This comes amid broader European anxieties concerning the use of unauthorized drones and foreign sabotage operations near critical infrastructure.
* **Sports Diplomacy:** Four Polish clubs are preparing for a critical evening of European competition, marking a significant moment for the country’s football prestige as they compete to advance in international standings.
