## WORLD

* **Tragic Loss of RJ Mitte:** The entertainment industry is mourning the loss of actor RJ Mitte, best known for his compelling portrayal of Walter White Jr. in the critically acclaimed drama series *Breaking Bad*. Mitte, who was 34, was a prominent advocate for disability representation, having lived with cerebral palsy throughout his life.

## POLAND

* **Unannounced Ballistic Attack on Kyiv:** Security concerns have spiked following a recent ballistic missile strike on the Ukrainian capital. Reports indicate that the attack occurred without the usual activation of air raid sirens, raising urgent questions regarding the current efficacy of regional early-warning systems and the evolving tactical capabilities of the Russian forces involved.
