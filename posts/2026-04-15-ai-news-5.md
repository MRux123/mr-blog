## WORLD

*   **Historic Diplomatic Breakthrough:** Israel and Lebanon have agreed to initiate their first direct peace negotiations since 1993. This development, facilitated by U.S. diplomatic efforts, marks a significant shift in regional relations aimed at de-escalating long-standing hostilities.
*   **De-escalation in the Persian Gulf:** Amidst reports of a potential U.S.-Iran agreement, shipping lanes in the Strait of Hormuz are showing signs of stabilization. Dozens of commercial vessels have successfully traversed the route, signaling a cooling of the recent maritime crisis that threatened global energy supplies.
*   **Humanitarian Crisis in Sudan:** As the conflict enters its fourth year, international attention turns to Berlin, where global leaders are convening to address the devastating humanitarian fallout in Sudan. Despite international pressure, analysts remain skeptical that current diplomatic efforts will lead to an immediate cessation of the ongoing violence.

## POLAND

*   **Public Sentiment Against Government:** A recent IBRiS survey conducted for Polsat News reveals that approximately 53% of Polish citizens view the current government led by Donald Tusk unfavorably. The polling suggests a challenging political climate for the ruling coalition as they look toward the 2027 parliamentary elections.
*   **Controversial Stobnica Castle Ruling:** The Supreme Administrative Court has issued a final ruling regarding the "Stobnica Castle" development, declaring the construction permit legal. The decision effectively concludes the long-standing administrative legal battle surrounding the contentious project.
*   **Public Reaction to U.S. Foreign Policy:** Polish citizens remain deeply skeptical of aggressive U.S. rhetoric, with 62.6% of respondents characterizing Donald Trump’s recent warnings regarding the "destruction of civilization" in Iran as unacceptable, highlighting a significant divide between Polish public opinion and the current U.S. administration's stance.
