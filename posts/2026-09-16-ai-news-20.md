## WORLD

*   **Heightened Tensions and Iranian Strikes:** New photographic evidence obtained by CBS News confirms widespread damage at U.S. military sites following Iranian attacks. This visual confirmation marks a significant escalation in regional instability, forcing a reassessment of defense vulnerabilities.
*   **Russian Intelligence Networks Targeted:** In a major counter-intelligence success, the FBI has thwarted a plot by Russian services to assassinate a prominent dissident on U.S. soil. The incident underscores a broader, concerning trend of Kremlin-backed operations aimed at critics across the United States and Europe.
*   **Belarus-U.S. Prisoner Deal:** The Trump administration has secured the release of 25 political prisoners in Belarus. The move is viewed as a highly transactional shift in diplomatic relations, reflecting a new pragmatic approach toward a key Russian ally that has historically faced isolation from the West.

## POLAND

*   **Constitutional Impasse:** Maciej Berek, newly elected to the Constitutional Tribunal, has announced his intention to challenge President Karol Nawrocki regarding his swearing-in ceremony. The legal standoff, dubbed by Berek as a "strange discussion," highlights the deepening friction between the judiciary and the executive branch.
*   **Nobel Laureate Becomes Polish Citizen:** In a rare and symbolic moment of national pride, American molecular biologist and Nobel laureate Victor Ambros has been granted Polish citizenship. Prime Minister Donald Tusk personally conveyed the news, emphasizing the government's efforts to strengthen ties with the global scientific diaspora.
*   **Parliamentary Accountability:** The Sejm’s regulatory committee has recommended lifting the parliamentary immunity of MP Łukasz Mejza. The move comes amidst a criminal investigation into alleged irregularities regarding e-learning training programs, sparking a fierce political debate over accountability and judicial impartiality.
