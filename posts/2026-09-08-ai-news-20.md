## WORLD

*   **Sanctions on West Bank Settlements:** The United Kingdom, Canada, and France have announced significant sanctions against Israeli settlements in the West Bank. The UK foreign secretary explicitly accused settlers of "ethnic cleansing," leading to a severe diplomatic fallout, including Israel's decision to close the British consulate in East Jerusalem. This marks a critical shift in Western policy regarding the Israeli-Palestinian conflict.
*   **Escalation in Ukraine:** Russian drone attacks have struck the capital, Kyiv, targeting a major TV station building and resulting in multiple fatalities. President Zelensky has condemned the assault as a new low in the ongoing conflict, highlighting the persistent danger to Ukrainian civilian infrastructure.
*   **Global Cartographic Shift:** In a symbolic and practical move, the United Nations General Assembly has officially voted to move away from the traditional Mercator projection in favor of the "Equal Earth" design. This decision aims to correct historical geographical distortions, particularly regarding the perceived size of the African continent compared to northern landmasses.

## POLAND

*   **Strategic Security Cooperation:** Defense Minister Tomasz Siemoniak held high-level discussions with the director of the CIA in Poland. The meeting focused on reinforcing national security architecture in the context of the ongoing regional instability.
*   **Diplomatic Warnings:** Foreign Minister Radosław Sikorski has issued sharp commentary on Russia’s strategy in Ukraine, comparing Vladimir Putin’s tactics to those of the Third Reich during the bombardment of London. Simultaneously, he expressed deep concern regarding journalist Andrzej Poczobut’s return to Belarus, characterizing the act as "playing with fire."
*   **Constitutional Tensions:** A legal and political debate is intensifying regarding the Constitutional Tribunal (TK). Former TK president Jerzy Stępień has publicly challenged the authority of Karol Nawrocki, accusing him of overstepping legal boundaries concerning the appointment of judges, further deepening the divide within Poland's judicial governance.
