## WORLD

* **Mass Protests in France:** Widespread demonstrations across France have led to nearly 1,000 arrests. While tensions remain high, authorities have successfully prevented a recurrence of the extensive rioting and looting that characterized previous weekend protests, despite the participation of hundreds of thousands of citizens.
* **Escalation in Hebron:** The security situation in the region has deteriorated as gates in Hebron have been shuttered. Reports indicate that Israeli authorities are proceeding with the displacement of Palestinian residents, marking a significant escalation in local tensions.
* **Emergence of Shamonda Virus:** Health authorities are monitoring a rare case of the Shamonda virus in France, which has resulted in a fatality. While the Chief Sanitary Inspectorate (GIS) characterizes the risk of a broader epidemic as low—noting no evidence of human-to-human transmission—the situation remains under strict medical observation.

## POLAND

* **Sports Update:** In a setback for Polish basketball fans, Jeremy Sochan will not be joining the NBA for the current term and will instead continue his development in the league's developmental circuit.
