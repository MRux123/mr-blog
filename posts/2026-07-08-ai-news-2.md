## WORLD

* **Byzantine City Discovered in Egypt’s Western Desert:** Archaeologists have unearthed a remarkably well-preserved fourth-century Byzantine city within the Dakhla oasis. The site features residential and religious infrastructure, including a basilica-style church, providing significant new insights into the daily life, economic systems, and urban planning of the era. Recovered artifacts, such as coinage, tools, and pottery, are expected to further clarify the historical significance of this remote settlement.

## POLAND

* **Red Weather Alerts Issued for Northern Poland:** Authorities have issued the highest level of weather warnings for the northern regions of the country due to severe gale-force winds. Residents are urged to exercise extreme caution as the weather conditions pose a significant threat to infrastructure and public safety. Emergency services remain on high alert as the storm front traverses the region.
