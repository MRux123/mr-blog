## WORLD

* **Diplomatic Front in Ukraine:** European Foreign Ministers, led by Radosław Sikorski, convened in Kyiv and Bucha, reinforcing a unified front of support for Ukraine. The visit serves as a definitive signal of European commitment to holding Russia accountable for war crimes committed during the ongoing conflict.
* **Geopolitical Volatility:** Donald Trump has issued a blunt appeal to international allies, urging them to secure energy supplies from the U.S. and take assertive measures regarding the Hormuz Strait. This rhetoric highlights an escalating strain in trans-Atlantic and global trade relations amidst broader Middle Eastern instability.
* **Russian Economic Maneuvers:** A Russian oil tanker has docked in Cuba, marking a strategic breach in the near-total blockade of the island. This move provides critical temporary relief to the Cuban economy, signaling Russia's intent to maintain influence in the region despite international sanctions.

## POLAND

* **Structural Shifts in Foreign Policy:** Recent expert analysis from "Internationale Politik" characterizes Germany’s historical reliance on Moscow as a major strategic failure. This shift in discourse reflects a broader re-evaluation of European security dependencies and the necessity for a more robust, independent geopolitical stance in the region.
* **Domestic Crisis in the Tatra Mountains:** The closure of the Tatra National Park until further notice remains the most pressing domestic concern, signaling a potential environmental or safety emergency that has necessitated immediate, sweeping measures by authorities.
* **Social and Legal Intervention:** A high-profile case in Chorzów involving a woman who has remained isolated for 27 years highlights the complex intersection of mental health, social welfare, and individual rights. The ongoing court proceedings regarding her placement in a care facility underscore the challenges of human rights advocacy in extreme domestic circumstances.
