## WORLD

* **Escalation in the Strait of Hormuz:** Tensions have reached a critical peak as the United States and Iran exchange direct strikes. Following claims from Iranian forces that the vital Strait of Hormuz has been closed amidst attacks on regional US bases and allies, the US insists that the maritime passage remains open, signaling a volatile standoff with global economic implications for oil transit.
* **Deadly Wildfires in Spain:** A catastrophic wildfire season has claimed 12 lives in Spain. Residents recount harrowing escape stories, noting that chaotic communication infrastructure hindered evacuation efforts as flames rapidly swept through the region, marking one of the deadliest fire events in the country's recent history.

## POLAND

* **Warnings of Russian Aggression:** Security experts and analysts are flagging growing concerns that Vladimir Putin may be preparing to expand military operations beyond Ukraine. Reports suggest a high risk that the Kremlin is seeking to leverage the coming months to escalate military activity, with analysts identifying three specific signals indicating a potential push toward wider European instability.
* **Operational Readiness Exercises:** The Operational Command of the Polish Armed Forces has issued an urgent notice regarding military exercises scheduled for Monday and Tuesday in the country's northeast. Residents have been alerted to expect significant movements of troops and heavy military equipment on public roads as part of regional defensive preparations.
