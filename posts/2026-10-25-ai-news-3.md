## WORLD

*   **Escalation Risks in the Middle East:** Iran remains on high alert as both the United States and Israel finalize contingency plans for potential strikes. The atmosphere among the Iranian populace is characterized by profound anxiety and war-weariness, as the prospect of renewed military conflict looms over the region.
*   **Starlink Tensions in Ukraine:** Elon Musk has publicly accused Ukraine of violating the operational protocols of the Starlink satellite service. This development follows recent disruptions in connectivity, highlighting the fragile reliance of military operations on private-sector satellite infrastructure, particularly concerning the use of Pentagon-funded services like Starshield.
*   **Civil Unrest Across Europe:** France and Italy are witnessing significant public demonstrations. While French authorities managed to contain property damage through extensive policing and strategic route management, Italy is facing renewed youth-led protests driven by frustrations with the political establishment and the ongoing cost-of-living crisis.
