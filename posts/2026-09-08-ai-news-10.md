## WORLD

* **Resurgence of Somali Piracy:** The shipping routes off the Horn of Africa are facing a major security crisis. Regional instability, compounded by the US-Israel conflict with Iran, has led to a decade-high surge in hijackings, directly threatening global maritime trade and military supply chains.
* **Escalating Saudi-Houthi Conflict:** A cycle of violence has reignited as Saudi Arabia vows retaliation for Houthi attacks that left dozens injured. This escalation risks dragging the region back into a full-scale war, further destabilizing an already volatile geopolitical landscape.
* **Far-Right Gains in Germany:** The German political scene faces significant tremors following a disastrous electoral loss for Chancellor Friedrich Merz’s conservative party to the far right. This shift signals a growing crisis of confidence in current leadership and poses severe challenges for future policy stability in Europe's largest economy.

## POLAND

* **Andrzej Poczobut Crosses Belarus Border:** The situation involving Andrzej Poczobut remains the focal point of current Polish geopolitical concerns, following reports that he has crossed the border with Belarus under unclear circumstances. This development is being monitored closely by diplomatic and national security officials.
* **Zelensky's Strategic Diplomacy:** President Volodymyr Zelensky is reportedly orchestrating a pivotal high-level meeting involving three key nations, slated for late September. The diplomatic maneuvering suggests an attempt to secure a breakthrough in international support and military cooperation for Ukraine.
