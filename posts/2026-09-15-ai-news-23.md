## WORLD

*   **Security Threats to President Zelensky:** Ukrainian President Volodymyr Zelensky has revealed that his aircraft was targeted by drones on two separate occasions while traveling between Chisinau and Oslo. The incidents, which nearly resulted in a strike, highlight the persistent and escalating aerial threats faced by Ukrainian leadership in the ongoing conflict.
*   **Ed Sheeran Tour Controversy:** The logistics of Ed Sheeran's US tour are in disarray following the departure of all support acts, including Macklemore. The mass exit followed the removal of Macklemore from the lineup, an action triggered by his controversial pro-Palestinian statements during performances.

## POLAND

*   **Geopolitical Capability Analysis:** Renowned political analyst George Friedman, speaking on the "Dzień na świecie" program, addressed the current state of the Russian military. Friedman argues that Western European perceptions significantly overestimate Russia's offensive capabilities, asserting that the Kremlin currently lacks the necessary military capacity to launch an invasion of Poland.
*   **Diplomatic Military Negotiations:** High-level discussions continue regarding the future presence of US troops in Germany. Following a summit meeting between international leaders, the status of American military forces remains a focal point for European security discourse, with observers awaiting further clarity on the long-term strategic adjustments between the US and the German government.
