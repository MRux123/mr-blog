## WORLD

* **US-Russia Diplomatic Pivot:** President Trump has moved to waive several sanctions against Russia, citing national security concerns. The decision, which has been labeled a "historic moment" by Kremlin advisors, follows a controversial agreement to supply Russian diesel to the US market. The move has further intensified tensions with the Ukrainian leadership.
* **Vatican Health Crisis:** Pope Leo XIV is set to be discharged from Gemelli Hospital this Saturday following surgery for lung cancer. The transparency of the Vatican regarding the Pontiff's condition has reignited a long-standing debate concerning the institution's communication practices and the scrutiny of papal statements.

## POLAND

* **Tragedy in Nisko:** A devastating fire at a warehouse and store in Nisko has claimed the lives of two experienced firefighters. The rescue operation turned fatal when the pair became trapped and issued a distress signal, leaving the local community in mourning for the fallen officers.
* **Educational Controversy:** The Polish Ministry of National Education is facing pressure from civil society organizations to withdraw a religious studies textbook for seventh-graders. The publication has drawn criticism for requiring students to provide arguments justifying the condemnation of the in vitro fertilization (IVF) method, sparking a debate over the role of ideology in the national curriculum.
