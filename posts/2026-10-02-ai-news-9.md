## WORLD

* **Imminent Winter Energy Crisis in Ukraine:** Intelligence reports indicate that Russia is orchestrating a campaign to cripple Ukraine’s infrastructure, with plans to systematically disconnect major cities from heating, power, and water supplies. This strategy represents a significant escalation in the ongoing conflict, aiming to undermine civilian resilience during the winter months.
* **Geopolitical Weaponization of Migration:** Russia’s strategy to destabilize the West has evolved, with recent reports highlighting the deliberate use of migrants as a tool to sow discord and tension along European borders. This tactic adds a new layer of complexity to the security landscape of the European Union.
* **Instability in the Strait of Hormuz:** Despite fluctuating oil flows, the Strait of Hormuz remains a high-risk zone as Iranian forces continue aggressive maneuvers. Weekly drone and missile attacks on commercial vessels are testing international security protocols and regional stability.

## POLAND

* **Enhanced Drone Detection Capabilities:** In a move to strengthen regional security, Lithuania has begun implementing an advanced drone detection system developed by Ukrainian engineers. This cooperation underscores the deepening defense technological partnership between the two nations in response to modern battlefield threats.
* **Urban Infrastructure Accountability:** Polish authorities have taken decisive action following a building collapse in Częstochowa. The local property manager has been held accountable, sparking a broader national conversation regarding the safety standards of aging residential architecture.
