## WORLD

* **Iran-US Diplomatic Thaw:** Efforts to de-escalate regional tensions have reached a critical juncture, with both Iran and Pakistan signaling that a formal agreement to end hostilities with the United States is "closer than ever." The development aligns with recent optimistic statements from US President Donald Trump, suggesting a potential shift in Middle Eastern geopolitical dynamics.
* **Security Crisis in Nigeria:** The security situation in the Zamfara state has deteriorated further after armed bandits orchestrated a mass abduction. Victims were seized under the guise of attending peace negotiations, underscoring the extreme instability and the challenges of managing non-state actors in the region.
* **Loss of an Artistic Icon:** The art world is in mourning following the passing of British artist David Hockney at the age of 88. King Charles III led the tributes, acknowledging Hockney as a "true original" whose influence profoundly shaped contemporary aesthetics.

## POLAND

* **Economic Milestone:** Elon Musk has reportedly become the world’s first trillionaire, a record-breaking development attracting significant attention in Polish economic media and highlighting the unprecedented growth of the global tech and private space sectors.
* **Enhanced Aviation Rights:** A significant legislative breakthrough has been reached within the European Union, promising expanded protections for air passengers. The new consensus, expected to be ratified by the European Parliament, preserves essential consumer safeguards, including the three-hour delay threshold for compensation claims, marking a victory for travelers across the continent.
* **Diplomatic Logistics:** A notable incident involving the Vatican and the Spanish monarchy saw Pope Francis requiring the assistance of King Felipe VI to return to Rome from Tenerife. The monarch provided his royal aircraft following a technical failure in the original Airbus A320 flight, highlighting the close cooperation between the Holy See and the Spanish state.
