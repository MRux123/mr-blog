## WORLD

*   **Major Escalation in Russia-Ukraine Conflict:** Russia has reported at least seven casualties following what is described as the largest Ukrainian attack of 2026. The situation remains volatile as Moscow launched retaliatory strikes against Kyiv, resulting in widespread fires across the Ukrainian capital.
*   **Devastating Impact of Hurricane Lala:** The natural disaster has caused severe flash flooding in Hawaii. Emergency services report that the intensity of the storm is so high that it may have swept away residential structures, raising significant concerns for the safety of those in the affected areas.
