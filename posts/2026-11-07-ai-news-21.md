## WORLD

* **Political Turmoil in Israel:** Gadi Eisenkot, former military chief and opposition leader, has been tasked with forming a new government. This move signals a significant attempt to oust Benjamin Netanyahu following an inconclusive national election, though analysts suggest the path to a viable coalition remains fraught with obstacles.
* **Escalation in Yemen:** The conflict in Yemen has reached a devastating peak, with reports of over 50 fatalities in the last 24 hours alone. The casualties stem from intense fighting and airstrikes, marking one of the deadliest periods in the ongoing struggle between Houthi rebels and the Saudi-backed coalition.
* **Nuclear Standoff:** Tensions between the United States and Iran have intensified as Washington announces a $20 million reward for information concerning Iran's nuclear program. This development follows reiterated threats from Donald Trump regarding potential military strikes against Iranian nuclear infrastructure.

## POLAND

* **Tragedy in Krakow:** A mass shooting in Krakow's Main Square has resulted in at least six deaths. The situation escalated further as police discovered three additional bodies at the gunman's residence, prompting an investigation into a wider scope of violence. Prime Minister Donald Tusk has officially addressed the nation, praising the heroism of responding officers while highlighting the unprecedented nature of this act of terror in Poland.
* **National Security Response:** In the wake of the Krakow attack, authorities have moved to increase security measures nationwide. Warsaw has implemented emergency protocols, including the installation of road barriers and heightened police vigilance, to prevent copycat incidents and ensure public safety as the investigation into the motives behind the attack continues.
