## WORLD

* **Geopolitical Skepticism on Ukraine’s NATO Integration:** Former Commander-in-Chief Valerii Zaluzhnyi has delivered a stark assessment, suggesting that Ukraine’s path to NATO membership may remain permanently closed. This statement marks a significant shift in the discourse surrounding the security architecture of Eastern Europe.
* **Potential De-escalation in the Strait of Hormuz:** Global energy markets have responded positively to reports that the U.S. government is engaged in advanced talks to reopen the Strait of Hormuz. Following negotiations led by Secretary of State Marco Rubio and Treasury Secretary Scott Bessent, there is cautious optimism that vital oil shipments may soon resume, easing supply-side pressures.
* **Escalation of Drone Warfare:** A disturbing video evidence has emerged of a Russian drone targeting a civilian street vendor in Ukraine. The incident has been widely condemned by international observers as a potential war crime, highlighting the increasingly dehumanized nature of modern remote-controlled aerial warfare.

## POLAND

* **Cyber-Infrastructure Vulnerabilities:** United States authorities are reporting widespread cyber-attacks targeting municipal water systems across several states. While the technical source is being investigated, the breach underscores the critical need for hardened cyber defenses in essential public infrastructure.
* **The Future of Conscription in Germany:** The German government is actively debating the reintroduction of mandatory military service. The central issue remains the legal and ethical framework for conscientious objectors, reflecting a broader European trend of re-evaluating national defense postures in response to evolving geopolitical threats.
