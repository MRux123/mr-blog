## WORLD

* **Escalation in the Strait of Hormuz:** The geopolitical standoff between the United States and Iran has reached a critical juncture, with the waterway re-emerging as a primary fault line for potential military conflict. While President Trump has retracted his threat to impose a 20% tariff on cargo ships traversing the strait, the broader US strategy remains focused on challenging Iran’s dominance in the region as diplomatic ceasefire efforts falter.
* **Breakthrough in Ebola Treatment Trials:** Medical researchers have achieved a significant milestone in the Democratic Republic of the Congo, successfully enrolling the first patients in a record-breaking Ebola treatment trial. Launched just six weeks after the WHO declared a public health emergency, the trial of two experimental drugs offers new hope for reducing mortality rates in a region long deprived of effective medical intervention.

## POLAND

* **Political Polarization Over Ukraine Strategy:** Tensions within the Polish political landscape have intensified following statements by Przemysław Czarnek, who suggested halting support for Ukraine. The remarks drew sharp condemnation from Minister Tomasz Siemoniak, who accused Czarnek of echoing Russian rhetoric, while Jarosław Kaczyński reiterated his complex stance: supporting Russia’s defeat while simultaneously maintaining his opposition to Ukrainian integration into the European Union.
* **Domestic Social Unrest:** A disturbing incident in Łódź highlights the rising dangers of xenophobic sentiment. A Polish man was severely injured in an unprovoked attack by an assailant who misidentified the victim as Ukrainian. The incident has sparked a wave of public concern regarding the security of foreigners and the growing atmosphere of intolerance in the country.
* **Preparation for Future Elections:** Prime Minister Donald Tusk has scheduled a high-stakes meeting with parliamentarians from the Civic Coalition (KO). The gathering is intended to serve as both a strategic consolidation effort and a mobilization campaign as the party begins laying the groundwork for the upcoming national elections.
