## WORLD

*   **Potential Iran-U.S. Peace Deal:** Diplomatic efforts are nearing a critical juncture, with mediators indicating that a comprehensive peace agreement between the U.S. and Iran could be finalized within the next 24 hours. The Prime Minister of Pakistan has expressed optimism regarding the deal, though officials caution that negotiations remain delicate and subject to last-minute derailment.
*   **Decapitation Strike Against Tren de Aragua:** U.S. and Venezuelan authorities have confirmed the death of "Niño Guerrero," a high-ranking leader of the notorious Tren de Aragua criminal organization. The joint operation marks a significant development in international efforts to dismantle the transnational gang's leadership structure.
*   **Legal Action at Kennedy Center:** A judicial ruling has forced the removal of President Donald Trump’s name from the facade of the Kennedy Center. The development follows a court order rejecting a stay on the removal process, reversing a naming decision made in 2025.

## POLAND

*   **Public Trust and Political Landscape:** A new Social Changes survey reveals a shifting landscape in public sentiment. President Karol Nawrocki currently leads in public trust, followed by Foreign Minister Radosław Sikorski and Prime Minister Donald Tusk. Conversely, Szymon Hołownia and other prominent political figures have seen their support ratings decline.
*   **Revival of Regional Rail Infrastructure:** In a significant move to address regional connectivity, Prime Minister Donald Tusk attended the inauguration of the newly restored Ostrołęka–Łomża rail line. The service, which returns after 33 years of inactivity, is part of a broader government pledge to improve rail access across the country, with further extensions planned for next year.
*   **Major Investigation in Lutoryż:** Prosecutors have officially charged a pathologist, identified as Magdalena H., with the desecration of human remains and the illegal disposal of hazardous waste. The case, involving the discovery of human fetal remains on private property, has drawn widespread public attention and prompted a formal request for the suspect's pretrial detention.
