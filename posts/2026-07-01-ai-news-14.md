## WORLD

* **Amnesty International Report on Sudan:** The Rapid Support Forces (RSF) have been accused of committing crimes against humanity and ethnic cleansing during their campaign to capture El Fasher. The report details a systemic campaign of violence against civilians, including murder, torture, and sexual slavery.
* **Diplomatic Standoff in the Middle East:** Despite escalating maritime tensions in the Strait of Hormuz, the United States and Iran are preparing for indirect negotiations in Qatar. The parties remain deeply divided on key strategic issues, though the resumption of talks signals a desperate attempt to de-escalate recent military frictions.
* **Religious Schism Risk:** The traditionalist Society of St. Pius X has moved to consecrate four new bishops, directly defying Vatican warnings. This move threatens to trigger formal excommunications and marks a significant moment of internal instability for the Catholic Church.

## POLAND

* **Political Debate on Vigilante Groups:** Left-wing MP Piotr Kowal has raised alarms in the Sejm regarding the rise of paramilitary groups operating on Polish streets. These groups have been reported harassing and challenging individuals based on their appearance, prompting calls for urgent legislative intervention.
* **Diplomatic Friction with Kyiv:** Polish MEPs from EKR and EPP groups are exerting pressure to include criticism of President Zelensky’s decision to name a military unit after the UPA in an upcoming resolution regarding Ukraine's EU accession process. This development highlights ongoing historical sensitivities impacting current bilateral relations.
* **Legal and Political Fallout of the "Two Towers" Case:** The Warsaw Regional Prosecutor's Office has issued a formal statement defending the dismissal of the investigation into the "two towers" development project. The prosecutor's office emphasized that challenging the independence of their decisions is unacceptable, following the closure of the case due to a lack of evidence regarding criminal conduct.
