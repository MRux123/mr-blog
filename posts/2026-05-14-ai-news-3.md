## WORLD

*   **Hungary’s Diplomatic Pivot:** In a historic shift, the Hungarian government has summoned the Russian ambassador to Budapest. This move follows a Russian military strike on the Zakarpattia region of Ukraine, home to a significant ethnic Hungarian minority. This marks the first such diplomatic action taken by Hungary against Russia since the onset of the conflict, signaling a potential cooling of bilateral relations.
*   **Expansion of Russian Military Powers:** The Russian State Duma has passed legislation granting the President broad authority to deploy military forces beyond national borders under the pretext of protecting Russian citizens. International observers and intelligence agencies warn that this policy creates an extraterritorial framework that significantly increases the risk of provocations against neighboring states.

## POLAND

*   **Clarification on US Troop Deployment:** Polish Deputy Prime Minister and Minister of National Defence, Władysław Kosiniak-Kamysz, has officially refuted reports suggesting the sudden cancellation of the deployment of 4,000 US troops to Poland. Clarifying the situation, the Minister emphasized that the rumors—which originated from international military news sources—do not pertain to Poland’s current security arrangements.
