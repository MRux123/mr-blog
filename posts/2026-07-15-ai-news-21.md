## WORLD

* **Escalating US-Iran Conflict:** The United States has launched a series of fresh strikes against Iranian targets. President Donald Trump has issued a stern warning to Tehran, stating that he has yet to determine whether he will take further action to "finish off" the regime, significantly heightening tensions in the Middle East.
* **Intensifying Russia-Ukraine War:** The conflict shows no signs of de-escalation as Russian attacks on Ukrainian soil have resulted in 14 deaths. In a retaliatory surge, Ukrainian forces have successfully targeted 20 Russian oil tankers in the Black Sea, marking a significant tactical shift in the ongoing naval struggle.
* **Geopolitical Shifts in Belarus:** Reports indicate a surge in military preparedness within Belarus. According to the opposition group BELPOL, there is an ongoing, systematic inspection and modernization of civil defense shelters across the country, raising concerns among neighboring states regarding potential mobilization.

## POLAND

* **Growing Role of Poland on the Global Stage:** Poland’s influence continues to expand, with significant international support for its potential permanent membership in the G20. Experts from the Hudson Institute have highlighted the strategic necessity of this inclusion, a sentiment echoed by the current US administration under Donald Trump.
* **Internal Political Mobilization:** Prime Minister Donald Tusk has held high-level meetings with members of the Koalicja Obywatelska parliamentary group. The sessions focus on intensifying domestic political mobilization, as the government looks to solidify its position amidst a dynamic internal political landscape.
