## WORLD

* **Trump Extends Cease-Fire with Iran:** President Trump has announced an extension of the hostilities pause with Iran, following a request from Pakistan. The diplomatic intervention by Islamabad aims to prevent further escalation in a conflict that has already strained international security frameworks.
* **Fatal Drone Strike in Syzran:** A civilian building in the Russian city of Syzran was partially destroyed after being struck by a Ukrainian drone. Reports indicate the drone deviated from its intended flight path while targeting a nearby oil refinery, resulting in two fatalities and twelve injuries.
* **Leadership Crisis in Iran:** Reports suggest that Mojtaba Khamenei has fled Iran, amid mounting speculation regarding his inability to provide effective leadership during the current state of conflict.

## POLAND

* **Political Maneuvering: Karol Nawrocki Joins Government Training:** Unofficial reports from RMF FM suggest that Karol Nawrocki is participating in a joint training session with members of the current government, fueling speculation about potential shifts in political alignment.
* **The Future of NATO:** In the wake of President Trump's critical comments regarding the alliance, public discourse in Poland has shifted toward the potential survival of the security bloc. A scheduled debate on RMF24 will address whether Europe is prepared for a diminished U.S. role in NATO and the implications for regional defense against Russian aggression.
