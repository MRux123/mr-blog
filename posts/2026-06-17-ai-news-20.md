## WORLD

* **U.S. and Iran Reach Preliminary Reconstruction Agreement:** Following the G7 summit in Évian-les-Bains, President Donald Trump has announced a tentative deal with Iran. The agreement, intended to avert an economic crisis in the Strait of Hormuz, includes a $300 billion reconstruction plan for Iran and a 60-day window for negotiations regarding the nation’s nuclear program. While diplomatic efforts are underway, the U.S. maintains a strict stance, warning that military options remain on the table should Tehran fail to adhere to the deal's stipulations.
* **Finland Strengthens NATO Deterrence:** In a significant shift in regional security policy, the Finnish parliament has passed legislation lifting the long-standing ban on the transit and storage of nuclear weapons within its borders. This move is designed to integrate Finland more deeply into NATO’s nuclear umbrella, enhancing the alliance's collective deterrence posture in Northern Europe.

## POLAND

* **Finland's Strategic Pivot Affects Regional Security:** The Polish public and government are closely monitoring Finland’s decision to remove nuclear prohibitions. As a key NATO member in Central Europe, Poland views this development as a reinforcement of the alliance’s northern flank and a crucial step toward stabilizing regional security amidst shifting geopolitical tensions.
* **Emergency Operations Underway at Eko-Plus Mine:** A major rescue operation is currently in progress in Silesia following a significant seismic event at the Eko-Plus mine. Emergency services are working to secure the area and ensure the safety of trapped personnel, highlighting the ongoing challenges and risks associated with heavy industry in the region.
