## WORLD

* **Iran-Israel Conflict Intensifies:** Tensions in the Middle East have reached a critical point following an Iranian strike near the Dimona nuclear research center in Israel. This action follows allegations from Tehran that the U.S. and Israel were responsible for an earlier attack on the Natanz nuclear complex.
* **New Iranian Missile Threat:** Israeli military officials have raised alarms over Iran’s deployment of new long-range ballistic missiles. With a reported range of 4,000 kilometers, these weapons are theoretically capable of reaching major European capitals, including London, Paris, and Berlin, marking a significant shift in regional security dynamics.
* **U.S. Security Concerns:** President Trump has threatened to deploy ICE agents to airports to maintain security, citing ongoing staffing shortages caused by a protracted government shutdown.

## POLAND

* **Diplomatic Friction Over Presidential Visit:** A heated political dispute has emerged regarding President Nawrocki’s planned visit to Budapest to meet with Viktor Orbán. Prime Minister Donald Tusk has labeled the participation in a summit of eurosceptic and pro-Russian politicians a "fatal mistake," a charge vehemently rejected by the presidential administration.
* **Healthcare Transparency Controversy:** A public debate has ignited over the status of a maternity ward in Lesko. While Prime Minister Tusk claims the facility remains operational, regional officials and members of the ruling coalition suggest the ward is effectively non-functional, leading to accusations of misinformation and political finger-pointing between the government and local opposition leadership.
