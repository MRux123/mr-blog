## WORLD

*   **U.S.-Iran Conflict Deepens:** Despite reports of a potential de-escalation, Iran has publicly denied any recent "productive talks" with the United States regarding its energy infrastructure. The contradictory narratives surrounding the status of negotiations suggest a strategic stalemate that keeps international markets on edge regarding the security of the Strait of Hormuz.
*   **Technological Warfare in Ukraine:** A significant shift in military logistics is emerging as Ukrainian units begin utilizing online marketplaces to procure drones directly. This move away from centralized, bureaucratic procurement represents an agile adaptation to the realities of modern, high-intensity conflict.

## POLAND

*   **Geopolitical Alignment Tensions:** The political discourse in Poland has intensified following controversial statements by MP Michał Wójcik regarding the Hungarian elections. Wójcik’s open support for Viktor Orban, coupled with reports of an SBU-identified Hungarian spy operating within Ukraine, underscores a growing strain on regional diplomatic relations and security cooperation.
*   **Judicial Ruling on Smolensk:** The Court of Appeal has issued a landmark ruling in the defamation dispute between Jarosław Kaczyński and Radosław Sikorski. By dismissing the lawsuit, the court effectively protected the right to critical public debate regarding the historical assessment of the 2010 Smolensk tragedy, marking a significant moment in the ongoing political polarization within the country.
*   **Security Incident in Bucha:** Authorities in the Kyiv region have apprehended a 21-year-old suspect in connection with a double bombing. Investigations suggest the suspect was recruited by Russian intelligence, highlighting the persistent threat of sabotage operations within Ukrainian territory.
