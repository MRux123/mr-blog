## WORLD

* **US-Iran Maritime Standoff:** US Secretary of Defense Pete Hegseth has doubled down on the blockade of Iranian ports, confirming that US forces have intercepted 34 vessels since the operation began. Hegseth explicitly challenged European allies to take a more active military role in the Strait of Hormuz, labeling the conflict as being of greater strategic importance to Europe than to the United States. Iran continues to cite the lifting of these maritime restrictions as a non-negotiable condition for resuming peace talks.
* **Justice Department Clears Jerome Powell:** The US Department of Justice has officially concluded its probe into Federal Reserve Chairman Jerome Powell. The investigation, initiated following allegations by President Donald Trump regarding improper cost overruns during the renovation of the Federal Reserve’s headquarters, has been dropped, effectively ending the political pressure surrounding the Fed’s financial oversight.

## POLAND

* **Strategic Shift in Energy Policy:** Prime Minister Donald Tusk has identified the Bełchatów region as a potential site for Poland’s second nuclear power plant. While the announcement represents a significant step in Poland’s energy transition strategy, the Prime Minister emphasized that final site selection remains subject to expert analysis and long-term planning, following the ongoing development of the first facility in Choczewo.
* **Military Alert Over the Baltic Sea:** The Polish Armed Forces have initiated an immediate defensive response following the detection of Russian military aircraft operating over the Baltic Sea. The incident highlights the ongoing regional security concerns along NATO's eastern flank.
