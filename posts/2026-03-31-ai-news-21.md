## WORLD

*   **Heightened Tensions in Iran-US Relations:** The conflict between Washington and Tehran continues to escalate. Iran’s Foreign Minister, Abbas Araghchi, has officially denied rumors of progress in peace talks with the US. Adding to the volatility, the Iranian Revolutionary Guard Corps has issued direct threats against major American technology and aerospace corporations, including Google, Apple, and Boeing, suggesting potential retaliation for perceived acts of aggression.
*   **Abduction of US Journalist:** The security situation in Baghdad has deteriorated, highlighted by the kidnapping of American journalist Shelly Kittleson. US officials have linked the abduction to the Kataib Hezbollah militia, marking a significant escalation in the risks faced by foreign nationals in the region.
*   **Papal Intervention:** In a rare diplomatic move, Pope Leo XIV has issued a direct appeal to President Donald Trump, urging a peaceful resolution to the escalating conflict with Iran and calling for an immediate exit strategy from the brewing regional war.

## POLAND

*   **World Cup Dream Ends in Stockholm:** The Polish national football team failed to qualify for the World Cup after a dramatic 3-2 defeat against Sweden in the playoff final. Despite a dominant performance for much of the match, a late goal in the 88th minute by Viktor Gyökeres sealed the fate of the Polish squad.
*   **Jan Urban Retains Coaching Position:** Despite the heart-breaking loss in Sweden, the Polish Football Association (PZPN) has confirmed that Jan Urban will remain the head coach of the national team, signaling a decision to maintain continuity in leadership despite the qualifying failure.
*   **Infrastructure Costs Rise:** Motorists face increased financial pressure as tolls for the A4 motorway (Katowice-Kraków section) are set to rise starting April 1st. The operator, Stalexport, cited rising infrastructure maintenance costs and upcoming investment requirements as the primary drivers for the price hike.
