## WORLD

* **Israel Enacts Death Penalty for Palestinian Terrorists:** The Israeli parliament has passed a controversial law introducing the death penalty for Palestinians from the West Bank convicted of terrorism. The legislation, heavily promoted by the far-right coalition, has drawn significant international criticism. Legal experts highlight that the law's specific phrasing effectively excludes Jewish extremists from its scope, raising concerns about equality before the law and the potential for heightened regional instability.

* **Deadly Gang Violence Escalates in Haiti:** A violent surge in Haiti has resulted in at least 70 deaths following an attack by the "Gran Grif" gang in the city of Petite-Riviere. Human rights organizations report that the situation remains critical as clashes with police continue. This tragedy underscores the deepening security crisis and the breakdown of order in the region.

* **European Flag Removals Spark Debate in France:** A growing trend has emerged in France as newly elected far-right mayors begin removing European Union flags from municipal buildings. Replacing them exclusively with the French tricolor, these actions reflect a broader political shift and growing tensions regarding national versus supranational identity within the European political landscape.

## POLAND

* **Procurator General Initiates Legal Action Against Ziobro:** A high-profile legal battle has begun as National Prosecutor Dariusz Korneluk filed a private indictment against former Justice Minister Zbigniew Ziobro. The case centers on allegations of defamation, with Korneluk seeking a suspended one-year prison sentence following Ziobro's public labeling of him as a "party activist."

* **Latest Polling Data Shows Stable Political Landscape:** A new IBRiS survey indicates that the Civic Coalition (KO) maintains its lead with 32.4% of voter support. Law and Justice (PiS) follows at 24.5%, with the Confederation party securing 13.4%. Recent shifts in the lower-polling spectrum show the Left (7.9%) slightly overtaking the Confederation of the Polish Crown (7.5%), suggesting minor fluctuations in the electorate's preferences.
