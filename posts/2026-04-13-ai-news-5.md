## WORLD

*   **End of an Era in Hungary:** The political landscape in Budapest has undergone a seismic shift as Viktor Orbán’s long-standing administration faces defeat. Péter Magyar’s TISZA party has secured a decisive victory, capturing 138 out of 199 parliamentary seats. This historic transition signals a profound rejection of the nationalist policies that defined Orbán's tenure, potentially steering the nation toward a new diplomatic and internal direction.
*   **Escalation in the Middle East:** The U.S. government has announced a strategic blockade on vessels entering or departing Iranian ports, citing concerns over regional security. While the administration has clarified that ships transiting the Strait of Hormuz will not be impeded, the move marks a significant hardening of U.S. policy toward Tehran.
*   **U.S. Political Turmoil:** The California gubernatorial race faces upheaval following the withdrawal of a front-running candidate amidst serious sexual misconduct allegations, further complicating the internal political climate in the United States.

## POLAND

*   **Diplomatic Echoes of Budapest:** The change in power on the Danube is reverberating across Poland, with officials framing the Hungarian election outcome as a manifestation of deep-seated public frustration. Deputy Foreign Minister Ignacy Niemczycki highlighted the significance of the TISZA victory, suggesting it reflects a broader trend of shifting political sentiments in Central Europe.
*   **Trump’s Critique of the Papacy:** The Polish media is closely monitoring the escalating rhetoric between Washington and the Vatican. President Donald Trump has leveled sharp criticisms at Pope Leo XIV, attacking his stance on international security and domestic social issues, a move that is drawing significant attention from global and Polish diplomatic observers.
