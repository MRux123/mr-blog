## WORLD

*   **Nor'easter triggers emergency in the Northeast:** A powerful Nor'easter is currently impacting the U.S. East Coast, spanning from Maine to Virginia. Authorities in New York and New Jersey have declared a state of emergency as severe flooding threatens densely populated areas, forcing emergency services to brace for significant infrastructure impact.
*   **Diplomatic tensions over potential G20 encounter:** The upcoming G20 summit remains a focal point of international scrutiny. Discussions have intensified regarding the potential attendance of Vladimir Putin, with former Polish President Aleksander Kwaśniewski suggesting that while leaders should attend the summit to maintain diplomatic presence, any interaction with the Russian leader must remain strictly non-confrontational and avoid any gestures of legitimacy, such as a handshake.

## POLAND

*   **Poland secures European Volleyball Championship title:** Polish men's national volleyball team has successfully defended their European Championship title after a dominant 3:1 victory over France in the final match held in Milan. This hard-fought win not only solidifies Poland's position as a dominant force in international volleyball but also guarantees the squad an automatic qualification for the 2028 Olympic Games in Los Angeles.
