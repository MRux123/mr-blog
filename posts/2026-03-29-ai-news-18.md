## WORLD

*   **Heightened Conflict Between Iran and Israel:** The regional situation has reached a critical boiling point as Israel launches extensive strikes against infrastructure in Tehran and other Iranian regions. The military campaign follows an Iranian retaliatory strike that hit an agrochemical factory in southern Israel, forcing local evacuations due to the release of hazardous substances. Tensions are further exacerbated by allegations from the Iranian parliament speaker, who claims the current U.S. administration is covertly planning a ground invasion under the guise of diplomatic efforts.
*   **Failed Security Attempt in Paris:** Authorities in France have apprehended three individuals following an attempted bomb attack outside a Bank of America branch in Paris. The incident is being handled by the national anti-terrorism prosecutor’s office, signaling the severity of the threat in the French capital.

## POLAND

*   **Polish Peacekeeper Wounded in Lebanon:** A Polish soldier serving with the UNIFIL mission in Lebanon has sustained serious head injuries. According to Deputy Foreign Minister Władysław Teofil Bartoszewski, the soldier’s condition is stable, and his life is not in danger. The incident highlights the growing risks faced by international peacekeeping forces amidst the deteriorating security situation in the Middle East.
*   **Controversial Incident at the Holy Sepulchre:** A diplomatic and religious stir has emerged following reports that Israeli police prevented the Latin Patriarch of Jerusalem, Cardinal Pierbattista Pizzaballa, and the Custos of the Holy Land, Father Francesco Ielpo, from entering the Basilica of the Holy Sepulchre on Palm Sunday. While Israeli officials cited security concerns for the detention, the incident has drawn significant international attention.
*   **Russia-Ukraine Frontline Atrocities:** Reports from the Ukrainian Ministry of Defense indicate a harrowing trend on the frontlines, with daily footage allegedly showing Russian soldiers either committing suicide or being executed by their own comrades. Officials suggest that March is on track to set a new record for Russian military losses.
