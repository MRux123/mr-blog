## WORLD

* **China Expands Zero-Tariff Access for African Nations**: In a significant move to bolster its geopolitical soft power, China has eliminated tariffs for the vast majority of African countries. Analysts suggest that while this policy strengthens economic ties, it may result in uneven development benefits across the continent, further cementing Beijing's influence in the Global South.
* **Venice Biennale Crisis**: The prestige of the upcoming Venice Biennale has been thrown into doubt following the mass resignation of its jury. The move is a direct protest against the decision to allow Russian representation at the exhibition for the first time since the 2022 invasion of Ukraine, highlighting ongoing cultural and political friction within the international arts community.
* **Civil Unrest in Australia**: Tensions have escalated in an Australian town following the arrest of a suspect in the murder of a five-year-old girl. Public anger manifested in protests outside a hospital where the suspect was receiving treatment, reflecting deep societal trauma and community outcry regarding the violent crime.
