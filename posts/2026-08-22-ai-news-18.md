## WORLD

*   **Canada-US Trade War Escalates:** Canadian Prime Minister Mark Carney has officially suspended trade negotiations with the United States following the imposition of aggressive 50-percent tariffs by President Trump. Carney, who labeled the U.S. move a "miscalculation," has vowed immediate retaliatory measures, signaling a significant breakdown in North American economic cooperation. Analysts suggest this represents a high-stakes gamble for the Canadian leader as he navigates the severe economic consequences of a full-blown trade conflict.
*   **Geopolitical Instability:** Russian President Vladimir Putin has issued a stern warning following a series of attacks on Saturday, pledging a swift and severe response. The international community remains on high alert as tensions continue to rise in the region.

## POLAND

*   **Tragic Accident in Kraków:** A devastating incident occurred in Kraków when a vehicle mounted the sidewalk, resulting in two fatalities and leaving an infant critically injured. Local authorities are currently investigating the circumstances of the crash.
*   **Infrastructure Milestone:** Poland has officially expanded its public transport network with the completion of the country’s longest tram line, spanning 23.4 kilometers. The project marks a significant development in urban infrastructure, aimed at improving connectivity within the capital region.
