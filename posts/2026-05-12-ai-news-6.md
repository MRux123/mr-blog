## WORLD

*   **Israel Enacts Capital Punishment Law:** The Israeli government has passed legislation permitting the death penalty and public trials for individuals involved in the October 7 Hamas-led attacks. This move marks a significant shift in the nation's legal approach to national security and counter-terrorism, drawing immediate international attention.
*   **Netanyahu Challenges European Union:** Prime Minister Benjamin Netanyahu has openly criticized the European Union, labeling recent sanctions imposed on extremist Israeli settlers in the West Bank as a sign of "moral bankruptcy." This diplomatic friction highlights the deepening divide between Israel and its European partners regarding settlement policy.

## POLAND

*   **Zbigniew Ziobro’s US Travels Under Investigation:** Questions are mounting regarding how the former Minister of Justice, Zbigniew Ziobro, entered the United States. With the U.S. State Department citing visa confidentiality, the legal and procedural circumstances surrounding his arrival continue to fuel political debate within Poland.
*   **Public Scrutiny of the Zondacrypto Affair:** Recent polling data reveals a skeptical public sentiment regarding accountability in the Zondacrypto case. As the investigation progresses, the disparity between official findings and public opinion continues to generate significant domestic discourse.
