## WORLD

* **Escalation in the Russia-Ukraine War:** The conflict has entered a worrying phase of increased intensity, marked by the deployment of new, high-speed jet-powered drones by Russia. These weapons, which are significantly harder to intercept, are exacerbating the humanitarian crisis as both nations remain entrenched in a cycle of missile and drone strikes with no clear path to de-escalation.
* **Supercharged El Niño Warning:** The World Meteorological Organization has issued a formal warning regarding a "supersized" El Niño event. Global experts anticipate that this climate phenomenon will cause significant disruptions to international supply chains and national economies, forcing countries to expedite emergency preparedness measures.
* **Passing of a Feminist Icon:** Gloria Steinem, the legendary journalist and pioneering feminist activist, has died at the age of 92. Her passing marks the end of an era for the global civil rights movement, leaving behind a profound legacy of advocacy and social reform.

## POLAND

* **Imminent Parliamentary Vote on Presidential Veto:** The Polish political scene is braced for a high-stakes legislative showdown. Parliament is set to vote on whether to override the President’s veto, a move that could significantly alter the current executive-legislative balance and impact the government's ongoing reform agenda.
* **Russia's Election Maneuvers in Occupied Territories:** Warsaw and Kyiv have expressed strong condemnation following reports that Moscow is preparing to stage illegitimate elections within Ukrainian territories under its occupation. The move is viewed by the international community as a breach of sovereignty and a performative attempt to legitimize military control.
