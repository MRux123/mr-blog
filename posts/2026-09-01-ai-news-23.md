## WORLD

* **US-Iran Conflict Intensifies:** Tensions have reached a critical threshold as the United States launched a new wave of strikes against Iranian targets. The escalation follows retaliatory rhetoric from Tehran, which has vowed a devastating response. Recent reports from Iranian state media suggest civilian casualties, including deaths at a wedding party, further complicating the diplomatic landscape and heightening the risk of an all-out regional confrontation.
* **Geopolitical Realignment:** The Kremlin has signaled the possibility of a high-level summit involving Donald Trump, Vladimir Putin, and Xi Jinping. This proposed meeting highlights shifting global alliances and the ongoing efforts by Moscow to navigate its strategic interests amidst mounting pressure from Western sanctions and international isolation.

## POLAND

* **EU Compensation for Military Aid:** Poland is set to receive a significant financial reimbursement from the European Union for military equipment transferred to Ukraine. The agreement, confirmed by Irish officials and Polish Deputy Minister of National Defence Paweł Zalewski, represents a long-awaited resolution on funding, with the transfer of funds expected to occur next year.
* **Legia Warsaw Eliminated from Polish Cup:** In a major sporting upset, Legia Warsaw has been knocked out of the Polish Cup. The team's exit, alongside the failures of Wisła Kraków and Zagłębie Lubin to progress, marks a significant disappointment for fans and a notable shift in the landscape of the domestic competition.
