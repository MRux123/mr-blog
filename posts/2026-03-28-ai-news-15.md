## WORLD

* **Terror plot thwarted in Paris:** French authorities have successfully intercepted a suspected terrorist attempt in the heart of Paris. Police apprehended a man attempting to plant an explosive device at the Bank of America headquarters in the city center. The swift intervention by security forces reportedly occurred moments before the perpetrator could initiate the detonation.
* **US government shutdown continues:** The United States faces a deepening crisis as House Republicans rejected a bipartisan Senate deal. The ongoing legislative impasse has extended the partial government shutdown, with reports of significant travel disruptions as airport security personnel continue to work without pay for over a month.

## POLAND

* **Fuel price relief finalized:** The Polish government has published new legislation in the Journal of Laws, effective Sunday, aimed at mitigating rising fuel costs. The measures include a reduction in excise duty and the implementation of price caps, which are expected to lower fuel prices by up to 1.2 PLN per liter.
* **Security concern near Mińsk Mazowiecki:** Local authorities have launched an investigation into a mysterious object discovered in a forest near Rudnik, close to Mińsk Mazowiecki. Specialized fire brigade units from Warsaw were deployed to assess the site, with preliminary reports from Polsat News identifying the object as a smuggler's balloon.
* **Diplomatic travel warning for Cuba:** The Polish Ministry of Foreign Affairs has issued its highest-level travel warning (Level 4) for Cuba. Citizens are strongly advised against all travel to the island nation, regardless of the purpose, due to rapidly deteriorating conditions.
