## WORLD

*   **Ukraine’s Critical Defense Gap:** As the conflict continues across multiple fronts, Ukraine is urgently pleading for additional air defense systems. Western allies are increasingly reluctant to supply interceptor missiles, citing the necessity to maintain their own defensive capabilities, which raises questions about the long-term sustainability of international military aid.
*   **Geopolitical Realignments and Ceuta Disaster:** The aftermath of the mass influx of migrants into Ceuta has cast a spotlight on the evolving relationship between Washington, Rabat, and Madrid. Tensions are mounting as Morocco’s bold diplomatic maneuvers—including naming a major highway after Donald Trump—coincide with increased scrutiny over border management and regional stability.
*   **Venezuelan Infrastructure Collapse:** The humanitarian response to recent twin earthquakes in Venezuela has laid bare the catastrophic impact of years of political repression and budgetary neglect, leaving the government unable to effectively conduct rescue operations for those trapped in the rubble.

## POLAND

*   **Economic Performance Indicators:** According to the latest Eurostat data, the Polish economy continues to demonstrate resilience, with growth figures officially surpassing those of Belgium and Sweden. This performance marks a significant milestone in Poland's ongoing economic integration and competitive standing within the European Union.
*   **Regional Security Concerns:** Tensions in the region have escalated as North Korea conducted a new ballistic missile test. This display of military capability is being closely monitored by Polish defense officials and international allies, given the broader implications for global security architecture.
