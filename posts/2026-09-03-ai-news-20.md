## WORLD

*   **Norway Seizes Russian Research Vessel:** In a move described by Moscow as "piracy," Norwegian authorities have seized a Russian research ship operating in the High Arctic. The action, backed by an international court order, marks a significant escalation in the global effort to seize Russian state-owned assets, further straining diplomatic relations between the two nations.
*   **Diplomatic Channels on Ukraine Conflict:** Representatives linked to Donald Trump have reportedly initiated high-level talks in both Moscow and Kyiv. These exploratory discussions signal a potential shift in international diplomatic efforts to seek a resolution to the ongoing war, as global actors closely watch for signs of a concrete negotiation framework.

## POLAND

*   **Heightened Airspace Security:** Defense Minister Władysław Kosiniak-Kamysz has announced an immediate expansion of protection for Poland's airspace in response to recent Russian provocations. The strategy includes deploying a larger contingent of F-16 fighter jets and implementing new restricted flight zones in the eastern part of the country, prohibiting low-altitude flights to enhance monitoring capabilities.
*   **Safety Concerns Amid Infrastructure Alerts:** The country remains on high alert following a series of security incidents, including a hazardous material alarm that triggered an evacuation at Berlin's airport, affecting regional travel routes, and a tragic school stabbing in Sweden that has prompted renewed national discourse on safety and security policies.
