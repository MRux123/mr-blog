## WORLD

* **Escalating Tensions Over Russian-Iranian Military Cooperation:** A leaked Russian military intelligence document has revealed that Moscow offered Tehran thousands of drones and satellite intelligence to counter U.S. naval operations in the Persian Gulf. This disclosure significantly raises the stakes in Middle Eastern security dynamics and signals a deepening strategic alignment between Russia and Iran aimed at challenging U.S. influence.
* **President Trump Declassifies UFO Files:** In a move emphasizing administrative transparency, the U.S. government has released a series of classified documents concerning Unidentified Flying Objects. President Trump stated that the goal is to allow the public to draw their own conclusions regarding potential extraterrestrial phenomena, marking a historic moment in the government's approach to long-held classified data.
* **Political Transition in Hungary:** The landscape of Central European politics is undergoing a seismic shift as Péter Magyar prepares to be sworn in as Prime Minister. His inauguration follows a landslide victory for the Tisza party, effectively ending the 16-year governance of Viktor Orbán and signaling a new chapter for the nation’s political trajectory.

## POLAND

* **Strategic Military Realignment:** Polish authorities have formally expressed openness to hosting American troops currently stationed in Germany. Deputy Foreign Minister Ignacy Niemczycki confirmed that Poland is prepared to accommodate such a transfer if requested by the U.S., emphasizing the country's commitment to strengthening NATO’s eastern flank.
* **Upcoming Diplomatic Engagement:** Alongside the ongoing discussions regarding regional security, the Polish government has announced an upcoming visit from the newly elected Hungarian Prime Minister, Péter Magyar, expected later this month. This meeting is anticipated to be a critical first step in recalibrating Polish-Hungarian relations following the recent regime change in Budapest.
