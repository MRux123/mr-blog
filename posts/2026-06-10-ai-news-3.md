## WORLD

* **Military Confrontation in the Strait of Hormuz:** Tensions in the Middle East have reached a critical point following the downing of a U.S. Apache helicopter by Iranian forces. The incident has triggered a series of retaliatory strikes by the United States against Iranian military installations.
* **Geopolitical Instability and Economic Impacts:** As the conflict intensifies, global markets are bracing for potential disruptions. High-level industry leaders, including executives from the world's largest semiconductor manufacturer, TSMC, have expressed concerns that geopolitical instability and rising operational costs may soon lead to increased prices for global electronics.

## POLAND

* **Direct Response to Gulf Escalation:** Polish media outlets are closely monitoring the developments, highlighting the significant expansion of the conflict. Reports confirm that Tehran has launched coordinated drone and missile strikes targeting U.S. military bases in Bahrain, Kuwait, and Jordan. U.S. officials characterize the recent retaliatory strikes as a stern "warning" to the Iranian regime, while simultaneously expressing hope that these actions will not derail long-term diplomatic efforts to stabilize the region.
