## WORLD

* **Middle East Conflict Intensifies:** Global oil prices have surged following direct threats from President Trump to escalate military actions in the Iran conflict. The situation is further complicated by diplomatic friction, with President Macron publicly labeling recent remarks by the US President regarding international alliances and personal diplomatic etiquette as "inelegant" and "risky."
* **Artemis II Mission Launches:** A historic milestone in space exploration has been achieved as the NASA Artemis II mission successfully launched, marking the first crewed lunar expedition in over half a century.
* **Extreme Weather in Greece:** A severe storm, identified as "Erminio," has caused significant destruction across Greece. The natural disaster, compounded by a rare Saharan dust storm over Crete, has resulted in at least one confirmed fatality.

## POLAND

* **Vaccine Procurement Audit Scandal:** The Ministry of Health has unveiled findings from a damning audit regarding COVID-19 vaccine procurement. Minister Jolanta Sobierańska-Grenda confirmed that large quantities of vaccines are destined for disposal, leading the National Revenue Administration to file ten official notifications to the prosecutor's office regarding potential legal violations.
* **Labor Inspection Reform:** President Karol Nawrocki has signed into law a major reform of the State Labor Inspectorate (PIP), while simultaneously referring the legislation to the Constitutional Tribunal for follow-up review. The move has been cautiously welcomed by the Ministry of Family, Labor and Social Policy as a step forward for employee rights.
* **Fuel Price Stabilization:** In a bid to mitigate economic pressure, the Ministry of Energy has announced a government-backed intervention setting new maximum price caps for motor fuels, with reductions reaching up to 1.40 PLN per liter compared to previous rates.
