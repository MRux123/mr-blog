## WORLD

* **Shifting U.S. Stance on Russia:** The Trump administration is reportedly exploring the lifting of sanctions on Russia’s flagship investment fund. This potential policy pivot appears aimed at providing economic incentives to Moscow to facilitate an end to the conflict in Ukraine, signaling a significant shift in U.S. diplomatic leverage.
* **Political Gridlock in Israel:** Following the latest parliamentary elections, exit polls indicate no clear winner, leaving the political landscape in turmoil. Efforts are currently underway by an opposition bloc, led by a former Army chief of staff, to challenge Prime Minister Benjamin Netanyahu’s coalition, potentially altering the country’s trajectory.
* **Diplomatic Divisions in Europe:** European leaders remain divided over recent high-level diplomatic outreach to Vladimir Putin. While some argue that direct communication is necessary to push for peace talks, others express skepticism regarding the efficacy and intent of such dialogue in the context of the ongoing war in Ukraine.

## POLAND

* **Escalating Institutional Conflict:** Tensions between the executive branch and the Foreign Ministry have intensified, with Radosław Sikorski accusing President Karol Nawrocki of holding the diplomatic corps hostage. The core of the dispute involves the nomination of ambassadors, particularly regarding the key mission in Washington, which Sikorski suggests is being used as leverage in domestic political power struggles.
* **National Security Council Convened:** President Karol Nawrocki has summoned the National Security Council for a high-level, classified meeting on November 3. The primary agenda includes discussions on the potential establishment of a permanent U.S. military base in Poland, a move that would represent a fundamental shift in the country's national defense architecture.
* **Internal Strife in the Ruling Coalition:** The political stability of the current government is under scrutiny following revelations regarding internal dissent within the "Polska 2050" party. Allegations of leadership instability and rumors of potential exits from the ruling coalition suggest growing pressure within the governing bloc following recent personnel changes.
