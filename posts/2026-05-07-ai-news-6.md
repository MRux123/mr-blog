## WORLD

*   **Escalation in Lebanon:** Israel has conducted a targeted strike in the southern suburbs of Beirut, resulting in the death of Malek Balut, a high-ranking commander of Hezbollah’s elite Radwan Forces. This marks the first strike in the area in three weeks, significantly heightening regional tensions.
*   **Security Concerns on NATO’s Flank:** Latvian military officials confirmed that multiple Russian drones entered their airspace, with at least two crashing near the border. Authorities have issued urgent safety directives for residents to remain indoors, raising questions about potential provocations against NATO territory.
*   **Diplomatic Ultimatum:** The Russian Foreign Ministry has issued a formal warning to international diplomatic missions in Kyiv, advising immediate evacuation. Moscow claims this is a precautionary measure regarding potential security threats surrounding upcoming victory celebrations, though it signals a worrying shift in the regional diplomatic climate.

## POLAND

*   **Operation "Hellfire":** Polish authorities have carried out a major law enforcement operation targeting organized crime, resulting in the arrest of over 120 individuals. The scale of the sweep underscores ongoing intensified efforts by security services to dismantle criminal networks.
*   **Environmental Emergency:** A large-scale forest fire is currently ravaging the Solska Forest in southeastern Poland. Emergency services are working under critical conditions, with officials warning that the next 24 hours will be decisive in containing the blaze and preventing further ecological destruction.
*   **Severe Weather Alert:** The Institute of Meteorology and Water Management (IMGW) has issued high-level warnings for parts of southeastern Poland, forecasting violent thunderstorms, heavy rainfall, and potential hail. Citizens are being urged to exercise extreme caution due to expected wind gusts and sudden weather deterioration.
