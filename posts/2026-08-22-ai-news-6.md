## WORLD

*   **US-Canada Trade War Escalates:** Trade negotiations between Ottawa and Washington have collapsed, leading to the immediate implementation of a 50% US levy on $20 billion worth of Canadian imports. Canadian officials have pledged a "dollar-for-dollar" retaliation, signaling a significant deterioration in North American trade relations.
*   **Geopolitical Standoff in the Strait of Hormuz:** Former President Donald Trump has made inflammatory remarks regarding the Strait of Hormuz, declaring the vital waterway to be "US territory." This rhetoric poses a significant challenge to international maritime law and risks heightening military tensions in the Middle East.
*   **Belarus-Finland Diplomatic Rift:** Tensions between Belarus and the European Union have deepened following the announcement that Minsk will close its embassy in Helsinki, further isolating Belarus on the diplomatic stage.

## POLAND
