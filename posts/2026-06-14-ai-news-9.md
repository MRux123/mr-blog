## WORLD

* **G7 Summit Overshadowed by Geopolitical Divisions:** The Group of 7 summit, traditionally a beacon of diplomatic unity, has commenced under the shadow of intense fragmentation. The meeting is reportedly characterized by chaos, with tensions exacerbated by the divergent stances of the United States under President Trump, threatening the coherence of Western policy.
* **Stalled Negotiations on Iran:** Progress toward a peace agreement between the United States and Iran remains precarious. Despite declarations from the White House regarding an imminent deal, Tehran has urged caution, highlighting a significant gap in expectations and timelines between the two powers.
* **Escalating Conflict Impacts Global Security:** Analysts point to a systemic misreading of regional dynamics by major powers, particularly regarding Russia and the U.S. in their respective engagements in Ukraine and Iran. This strategic failure has led to protracted, high-cost deadlocks that continue to destabilize global security.

## POLAND

* **Massive Ukrainian Drone Strikes on Russian Industrial Targets:** In a significant escalation of the ongoing war, Ukrainian drones successfully targeted critical infrastructure deep within Russian territory. Reports confirm major fires at chemical plants in the Tula region and fuel depots in Rybinsk, marking a tactical shift in the reach and intensity of Ukrainian operations.
* **Diplomatic Fallout Over Presidential Honors:** The Polish government has issued a formal ultimatum to Ukrainian authorities concerning the potential revocation of the Order of the White Eagle, previously awarded to Volodymyr Zelensky. Officials have indicated that the move is a direct response to unresolved bilateral grievances, with a definitive decision pending the outcome of the requested diplomatic response.
* **British Intervention in the 'Shadow Fleet':** Prime Minister Keir Starmer has confirmed the interception of a Russian "shadow fleet" tanker in the English Channel by British military forces. This operation serves as a strategic warning to entities facilitating the circumventing of sanctions, further tightening the economic pressure on Russia's war effort.
