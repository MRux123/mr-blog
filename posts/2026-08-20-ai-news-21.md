## WORLD

* **Escalation of Sanctions Against Iran:** The United States Treasury has announced an aggressive strategy aimed at collapsing the Iranian economy and regime. Secretary Scott Bessent has called on international allies to take a firm stance, signaling a potential shift in global geopolitical alignments.
* **Intensified Conflict in Ukraine:** Kyiv continues to face severe Russian strikes targeting critical infrastructure. Recent attacks have resulted in significant casualties and widespread disruptions to basic utilities, including hot water supply, as the humanitarian crisis deepens.
* **International Condemnation of West Bank Settlements:** A coalition of Western powers—including the UK, France, Germany, Italy, and Canada—has issued a collective rebuke of Israel's new settlement projects in the occupied West Bank, highlighting increasing diplomatic friction regarding the status of the territory.

## POLAND

* **Controversy Over Investigation into Damiana Sobol’s Death:** The Polish Ministry of Foreign Affairs has responded to the Israeli decision to close the investigation into the death of Damian Sobol in the Gaza Strip. The move has triggered significant public and political debate regarding accountability and bilateral relations.
* **Violent Incident at Wadowice Wedding:** Authorities have brought charges, including physical assault, against six individuals involved in a fatal altercation following a wedding in Wadowice. The case has gained national attention due to the involvement of a police officer, prompting internal disciplinary procedures.
* **Severe Weather Impacts Southern Poland:** Extreme weather conditions, including powerful winds, have caused substantial damage across southern regions of the country. Emergency services in Małopolska and the Podkarpacie region remain engaged in widespread recovery operations following extensive property destruction and power grid failures.
