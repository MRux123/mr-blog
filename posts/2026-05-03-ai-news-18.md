## WORLD

* **Iran-US Diplomatic Friction:** Tensions have intensified following Tehran's latest peace proposal. While Iran claims the United States has responded, Washington has yet to provide formal confirmation. Reports indicate that Donald Trump has already characterized the proposal as unacceptable, signaling a potential stalemate in diplomatic efforts to de-escalate regional instability.
* **Public Health Alert at Sea:** A major health concern has emerged aboard an Atlantic cruise ship, where a virus outbreak has resulted in three fatalities. The World Health Organization has confirmed the presence of hantavirus, with several additional cases currently under investigation, highlighting the logistical and sanitary challenges of managing infectious diseases in international waters.

## POLAND

* **Secret Service Shooting Case Resolved:** Law enforcement officials have officially closed the investigation into the shooting incident at the White House Correspondents' Association (WHCA) gala on April 26. Prosecutors confirmed that Cole Allen fired the shot that injured a Secret Service agent. The conclusion of this investigation provides clarity on a high-profile security breach that occurred in the presence of President Donald Trump.
* **Diplomatic Engagements in Yerevan:** Prime Minister Donald Tusk has initiated a critical two-day visit to Armenia. The Prime Minister is engaging in high-level multilateral talks with his Canadian and British counterparts, Mark Carney and Keir Starmer. These discussions serve as a strategic prelude to the upcoming European Political Community (EPC) summit, focusing on strengthening international alliances.
* **Infrastructure and Public Safety:** Local authorities are managing two significant incidents: a large-scale fire in a industrial facility in Gliwice, which prompted a major firefighting response, and a train collision in the Greater Poland Voivodeship. While the fire caused no casualties, the rail accident involving an Intercity train required an emergency transit operation for approximately 1,300 passengers, underscoring ongoing concerns regarding industrial and transport safety.
