## WORLD

* **Assassination attempt targets U.S. leadership:** Authorities have apprehended a suspect following a shooting at the White House Correspondents' Dinner. The incident forced the emergency evacuation of President Donald Trump, First Lady Melania Trump, and Vice President J.D. Vance. Officials believe the high-ranking officials were the intended targets of the attack, and the perpetrator now faces federal charges, including the assault of a federal officer.
* **Escalating instability in Mali:** The African nation faces a deepening security crisis as a wave of coordinated attacks by jihadist militants and separatists continues to destabilize the country. The gravity of the situation was underscored by the confirmed death of Mali’s defence minister amidst the ongoing violence.

## POLAND

* **Political shift in Israel draws Polish attention:** The Polish political landscape remains focused on international developments, specifically the consolidation of the Israeli opposition. Naftali Benet and Jair Lapid have announced a strategic alliance, forming a new political entity named "Razem" to challenge Benjamin Netanyahu in the upcoming October 2026 parliamentary elections.
* **Official reaction to the Washington attack:** Polish President Karol Nawrocki has issued a formal statement condemning the attack at the White House Correspondents' Dinner. President Nawrocki expressed strong solidarity with the American administration, emphasizing the resilience of Polish-American diplomatic ties in the face of security threats targeting democratic leaders.
