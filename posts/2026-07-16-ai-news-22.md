## WORLD

* **Escalation in the Middle East:** Military tensions between the United States and Iran have intensified following a new wave of U.S. strikes aimed at degrading Iranian military capabilities near the Strait of Hormuz. Despite the ongoing violence, which has persisted for nearly a week, diplomatic channels remain open, with both nations signaling a potential willingness to return to the negotiating table to prevent further regional instability.
* **Ukrainian Strategy on Crimea:** Analysis from *The Economist* suggests that Russia’s grip on the Crimean Peninsula is weakening. Persistent Ukrainian drone operations have effectively severed logistics, causing severe fuel shortages and marking a significant tactical shift that analysts believe will provide Kyiv with critical leverage in future ceasefire or peace negotiations.

## POLAND

* **Political Stability Concerns:** Jarosław Kaczyński has publicly addressed rumors regarding potential fractures within the Law and Justice (PiS) party. Amidst internal speculation about leadership and party discipline, the Chairman’s comments aim to silence reports of an impending split within the movement.
* **Safety Concerns in Silesia:** A workplace incident in a Radlin mine has resulted in a serious injury to a miner. The event has prompted a local review of safety protocols, drawing attention to the hazardous conditions currently facing industrial workers in the region.
