## WORLD

* **Trump's Strategic Pivot on China:** Following a recent summit, U.S. policy toward China has undergone a significant transformation. President Trump has moved away from the adversarial stance characterizing recent years toward a more conciliatory approach. Notably, the president remained ambiguous regarding potential U.S. defense commitments to Taiwan, stating he "made no commitment either way" when pressed by President Xi Jinping.
* **Escalating Conflict in Mali:** The ruling junta in Mali, supported by Russian mercenaries, has launched a series of airstrikes targeting a rebel coalition composed of Islamist extremists and Tuareg separatists. The military operation aims to secure the regime's control over the volatile northern regions, specifically the key town of Kidal, which was recently lost to rebel forces.

## POLAND

* **Diplomatic Fallout Over U.S. Military Presence:** The Polish government is facing a diplomatic setback following the announcement that a planned U.S. armored brigade will not be deployed to Poland. General Christopher LaNeve, commander of U.S. Army forces, confirmed the decision in the House of Representatives, citing a broader instruction to reduce military forces as the rationale, despite the move being perceived in Poland as a significant slight.
* **Heightened Security Concerns Regarding Belarus:** President Volodymyr Zelenskyy has issued a warning regarding intelligence reports suggesting that Russia is actively working to involve Belarus in the war against Ukraine. According to Kyiv, Moscow is weighing the possibility of launching attacks against NATO territory from Belarusian soil, raising alarms about the potential expansion of the conflict in Eastern Europe.
