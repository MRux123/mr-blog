## WORLD

*   **Heightened U.S.-Iran Standoff:** President Donald Trump has issued a severe warning to Iran, citing the threat of a potential assassination attempt. In a public address, Trump suggested the U.S. military is fully prepared for a strike, rhetoric that significantly elevates geopolitical instability in the Middle East.
*   **Deadly Wildfires in Spain:** Spain is currently facing a catastrophic wildfire crisis, resulting in at least 12 fatalities. The death toll includes four British nationals, with authorities reporting at least 23 people still missing as emergency services struggle to contain the blaze.
*   **The Human Cost of Automation in China:** Reports from Kunshan highlight the deepening socio-economic divide as the shift toward robotic manufacturing leaves thousands of factory workers unemployed, signaling a broader trend of technological displacement in China's industrial hubs.

## POLAND

*   **Escalating Hostilities in Ukraine:** Following a intense night of bombardment, Kyiv remains under pressure after a Russian attack involving ballistic missiles. Local officials, including Mayor Vitali Klitschko, have confirmed at least 10 injuries, including an 11-year-old child.
*   **Meteorological Alerts:** The Institute of Meteorology and Water Management (IMGW) has issued urgent weather warnings for several provinces across Poland. Citizens are advised to exercise caution due to the risk of severe storms.
