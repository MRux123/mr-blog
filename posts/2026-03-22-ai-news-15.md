## WORLD

* **US-Iran Tensions Escalate:** The geopolitical situation in the Middle East has reached a critical juncture. Following threats from President Trump to "obliterate" Iran’s electricity infrastructure if the Strait of Hormuz is not reopened within 48 hours, Tehran has responded with defiance. Iranian missiles have reportedly struck near strategic locations in southern Israel, including areas adjacent to a major nuclear research facility, with the Islamic Revolutionary Guard Corps threatening a total blockade of the strategic strait.
* **Sudan Hospital Tragedy:** A catastrophic drone strike on a key hospital in Sudan during the Eid celebrations has resulted in 64 confirmed deaths, including 13 children, two nurses, and a physician, according to the World Health Organization. This incident marks a significant escalation in the ongoing violence within the region.

## POLAND

* **Asylum Restrictions Extended:** The Polish Ministry of Internal Affairs and Administration has announced the extension of the temporary suspension of the right to apply for international protection at the border with Belarus. This measure, effective for another 60 days starting March 22, is presented as a necessary response to ongoing illegal migration attempts that officials characterize as a persistent threat to national security.
* **Diplomatic Tensions with Hungary:** Prime Minister Donald Tusk has publicly addressed concerns regarding the Hungarian Foreign Minister, labeling him an "instrument of Lavrov." This statement highlights growing friction within European diplomatic circles regarding Hungary's stance on Russian influence.
