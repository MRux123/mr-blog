## WORLD

*   **Benin Heads to the Polls:** The West African nation of Benin is conducting a presidential election today, marking a significant political transition. With President Patrice Talon stepping down after a decade in power, the election is widely viewed as a managed succession rather than a competitive race, with the current finance minister emerging as the clear favorite.
*   **Tragedy at Haiti’s La Ferriere Citadel:** A stampede at the historic Citadelle La Ferriere in northern Haiti has resulted in at least 30 fatalities. The 19th-century fortress, a major national landmark, became the site of a deadly crowd surge on Saturday, prompting an investigation by local authorities.

## POLAND

*   **Breakthrough in Railway Infrastructure:** A critical milestone for the Podłęże-Piekiełko rail project is set for Tuesday, April 14. Workers are expected to breach the main tunnel in Mordarka. Spanning over 3.7 kilometers, this structure will establish a new record as the longest railway tunnel currently under construction in Poland.
*   **Expansion of Border Security:** Authorities have initiated the construction of an additional security barrier along the Polish-Belarusian border. This secondary, four-meter-high mesh fence, reinforced with concertina wire, aims to supplement existing fortifications while incorporating safety measures designed to prevent injury to local wildlife.
