## WORLD

* **U.S. Military Blockades Strait of Hormuz:** In a significant escalation of international tensions, the United States has initiated a naval blockade of the Strait of Hormuz, effectively halting ships entering or leaving Iran. While President Trump claims Iran has expressed interest in renewed negotiations, the military action marks a major shift in the geopolitical landscape, aimed at severing Iran’s oil-derived income.
* **Diplomatic Fallout Between Rome and Washington:** Tensions have emerged between key allies, Italy and the United States, following harsh remarks from President Trump directed at Pope Leo XIV. Prime Minister Giorgia Meloni has publicly condemned Trump’s characterization of the Pontiff as "unacceptable," creating a diplomatic rift between the White House and the Vatican.
* **Political Shifts in Hungary:** Peter Magyar’s victory in the Hungarian parliamentary elections is being framed as a transformative moment for the region. Analysts suggest a complex future for Hungary’s foreign policy, particularly concerning the European Union, with Magyar signaling a skeptical stance toward Ukraine’s potential accession to the bloc.

## POLAND

* **KNF Imposes Major Penalty on XTB:** The Polish Financial Supervision Authority (KNF) has fined brokerage firm XTB 20 million PLN. The regulator cited the dissemination of misleading information to clients, highlighting concerns over transparency and fair practices in the domestic financial sector.
* **Medical Negligence Investigation in Kielce:** A tragic case at the Mental Health Center in Kielce has deepened, with a nurse officially charged in connection with the suicide of a 20-year-old patient. The investigation alleges the nurse failed to perform mandatory checks while falsifying medical records to suggest the observations had taken place, adding to the six medical professionals already facing charges.
