## POLAND

* **Foreign Policy and Defense Strategy:** Polish Deputy Prime Minister Radosław Sikorski has voiced measured support for President Trump’s long-standing criticisms regarding European defense spending. Speaking to Italian media, Sikorski acknowledged that several European nations have historically underinvested in their military capabilities, expressing hope that Warsaw’s significant contributions will be recognized by the new US administration. Additionally, Sikorski condemned alleged financial misconduct by individuals associated with Viktor Orban’s circle, comparing the situation to domestic political corruption.
* **Severe Weather Warnings:** Following a weekend of intense gale-force winds that saw nearly 2,400 emergency interventions, Poland is bracing for a new meteorological threat. The Institute of Meteorology and Water Management (IMGW) has issued first-level warnings for frost, with temperatures expected to drop as low as -6°C at ground level across the country.
