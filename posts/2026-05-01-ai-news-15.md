## WORLD

* **Iran Proposes New Peace Plan:** Tehran has submitted a fresh proposal regarding potential peace talks with the United States. While specific details remain scarce, the initiative follows previous failed attempts to de-escalate regional tensions and reopen the Strait of Hormuz. The announcement has already triggered noticeable fluctuations in global oil prices.
* **Escalation in Ukraine:** A major aerial assault targeted the city of Ternopil in western Ukraine, involving over 50 Shahed-type drones. The strikes targeted critical infrastructure and industrial facilities, resulting in at least ten casualties. In response to the ongoing conflict, President Zelensky has announced a comprehensive military reform, promising increased wages for front-line soldiers and more transparent discharge procedures.

## POLAND

* **Political Debate Over Constitutional Reform:** A significant political disagreement has emerged following President Karol Nawrocki’s announcement of plans to form a council dedicated to drafting a new constitution. Prime Minister Donald Tusk has publicly criticized the move, urging the President to prioritize the strict observance of the existing constitutional framework instead.
* **Security Measures in Warsaw:** The Polish police have issued a formal notification regarding a strict no-fly zone for drones over Warsaw, specifically in the vicinity of the National Stadium, effective May 2nd. The authorities cite security concerns for planned events in the area and have warned of severe legal consequences for those violating the ban.
