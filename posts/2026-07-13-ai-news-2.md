## WORLD

* **Tragic Fire in Bangkok:** A devastating fire broke out at a bar in the Chatuchak district of Bangkok, resulting in at least 27 fatalities and leaving eight others in critical condition. Emergency services reported a chaotic scene as patrons attempted to flee the premises through the flames. Local authorities have launched an investigation into the cause of the disaster.

* **McConnell Addresses Health Concerns:** US Senator Mitch McConnell has officially broken his silence regarding his prolonged absence from the Senate. In a statement addressing weeks of public speculation, the Republican leader confirmed that he is recovering from a fall and a subsequent battle with pneumonia, noting that he does not intend to return to active duties immediately.
