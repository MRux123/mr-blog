## WORLD

* **Escalation in the Iran War:** The geopolitical landscape remains volatile as Iran maintains a near-total blockade of the Strait of Hormuz. Despite intense diplomatic pressure from the White House, President Trump has struggled to secure support from NATO allies and China for a coalition to escort oil shipments. Energy Secretary Chris Wright has officially warned that global markets should prepare for sustained high energy prices due to the ongoing regional conflict.
* **Economic Strain in Ukraine:** Kyiv is currently navigating a critical period on its "financial frontline." As the war continues to deplete state resources, the government is engaged in urgent negotiations with the IMF and the European Union to secure bailout funding while simultaneously implementing domestic tax hikes to sustain the national defense effort.

## POLAND

* **Polish Success at the 98th Academy Awards:** Polish artistry made a significant mark at the 98th Oscar ceremony, with the short animated film "The Girl Who Cried Pearls," co-created by Maciek Szczerbowski, winning the Oscar for Best Animated Short.
* **Mixed Results for Polish Nominees:** While the animation category brought home a win, Polish costume designer Małgorzata Turzańska missed out on an Academy Award. Nominated for her work on Chloe Zhao’s "Hamnet," she was ultimately surpassed by Kate Howley, who was recognized for her work on Guillermo del Toro’s "Frankenstein."
