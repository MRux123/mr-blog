## WORLD

*   **U.S.-Iran Diplomatic Breakthrough:** Washington and Tehran have reached a preliminary agreement to reopen the Strait of Hormuz and address Iran’s uranium enrichment program. While the U.S. frames the development as a significant step toward regional stability, Iranian officials maintain that the deal involves no major concessions, signaling potential friction in the upcoming implementation phases.
*   **Lebanon Conflict Outlook:** The emerging U.S.-Iran rapprochement has sparked cautious optimism regarding the conflict in Lebanon. Analysts suggest that a formal deal could provide the necessary geopolitical leverage to de-escalate hostilities between Israel and Hezbollah, provided that the major stakeholders remain committed to the diplomatic framework.
*   **Political Unrest in Turkey:** Tensions in Ankara have escalated as police stormed the offices of the Republican People’s Party (CHP), using rubber bullets and tear gas to remove the party's leadership following a controversial court ruling. The move has drawn international concern over the state of democratic processes in the country.
*   **Mass Protests in Serbia:** A wave of civil unrest continues to sweep through Belgrade, where over 180,000 demonstrators gathered to demand political change. It marks the largest protest in the nation since the fall of Slobodan Milošević, highlighting deep-seated public dissatisfaction with the current administration.

## POLAND

*   **Macron-Lukashenko Direct Dialogue:** In a significant diplomatic maneuver, French President Emmanuel Macron held a rare telephone conversation with Belarusian leader Alexander Lukashenko. The primary focus of the discussion was to warn Minsk against further involvement in the ongoing conflict between Russia and Ukraine, marking the first high-level communication between the two since February 2022.
*   **Kraków Referendum:** Residents of Kraków have headed to the polls for a pivotal referendum concerning the future of the city's municipal leadership and city council. The result is seen as a bellwether for local governance, requiring a turnout of nearly 27% to be considered legally binding.
*   **Tragic Accident in Pomerania:** Local authorities are investigating a fatal accident in the Pomeranian region, where a 15-year-old riding a cross-country motorcycle collided with a tree. The incident has prompted renewed discussions regarding safety standards for youth in motor sports.
