## WORLD

* **Medical flight disaster over the Atlantic:** A medical transport aircraft has gone missing while en route from Bermuda to Boston with six individuals on board. Authorities confirmed that the pilot reported severe electrical failures before losing contact. Debris fields have been identified off the coast of Nantucket, signaling a major search and recovery operation.
* **University scandal at Cornell:** Cornell University is facing intense scrutiny following grave allegations of drugging and gang rape at a fraternity house. University President Michael Kotlikoff has publicly condemned the incidents as "deeply disturbing," prompting an urgent administrative review into the campus safety protocols and institutional conduct.

## POLAND

* **Maritime security incident near Helsinki:** Security protocols have been triggered in the Gulf of Finland after an unidentified, funnel-shaped metal object was discovered drifting near the coast of Helsinki. Authorities have suspended maritime traffic in the immediate vicinity, and the Finnish Navy has been deployed to conduct an identification and neutralization mission, citing potential national security implications.
