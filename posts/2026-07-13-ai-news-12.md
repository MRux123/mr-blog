## WORLD

* **Middle East Escalation:** The region faces deep uncertainty as Iranian officials call for retaliatory strikes following the death of the late Supreme Leader, Ayatollah Ali Khamenei. The situation is further complicated by ongoing volatility in southern Lebanon, where populations remain under threat of long-term occupation amid the conflict between Israel and Hezbollah.
* **Technological Shifts in Ukraine:** Military tactics are undergoing a paradigm shift in Ukraine, as a "robot army" is increasingly utilized for frontline operations, including evacuations and combat. Simultaneously, Kyiv is intensifying psychological operations aimed at undermining Russian public support for the war effort.
* **Japan’s Strategic Realignment:** Facing heightened pressure from Russia and China, the Japanese government is moving to establish a new intelligence agency, signaling a major shift away from post-WWII security restrictions and closer alignment with Western intelligence frameworks.

## POLAND

* **Diplomatic Fallout over Volhynia:** Tensions have emerged following controversial remarks made by a Polish diplomat regarding the Volhynia region. Foreign Minister Radosław Sikorski has intervened, highlighting the sensitivity of historical memory in current Polish-Ukrainian relations.
* **Geopolitical Friction with the Kremlin:** Russia has labeled the recent meeting of the "coalition of the willing" in Paris—which included Polish Prime Minister Donald Tusk—as a gathering of "war instigators." The Kremlin has threatened to monitor the activities of participating nations, framing their support for Ukraine as a hostile act.
* **Regulatory Shifts in AI and Labor:** Poland is preparing for new EU-wide mandates effective August 2, 2026, which will enforce transparency regarding AI-generated content in customer service. Simultaneously, the country continues to navigate fiscal crises in the public health sector, illustrated by recent cost-cutting measures in the Bartoszyce hospital despite high executive pay concerns.
