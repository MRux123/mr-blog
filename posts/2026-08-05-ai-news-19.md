## WORLD

*   **Ukraine's Critical Air Defense Shortage:** Following a deadly strike in Kyiv, Ukrainian officials have issued urgent warnings regarding the depletion of Patriot interceptors. The lack of air defense assets is leaving civilian infrastructure and urban populations increasingly vulnerable to sustained Russian missile barrages, shifting the focus of the conflict toward a critical need for military replenishment.
*   **Escalation in Aerial Warfare:** As ground operations remain largely stagnant, both Russia and Ukraine are intensifying the development and deployment of innovative aerial weapons. This technological arms race has become the primary theater of the conflict, with both sides seeking a decisive breakthrough through autonomous and advanced strike systems.

## POLAND

*   **Judicial Setback for PiS Subsidies:** The Supreme Administrative Court (NSA) has dismissed a cassation appeal filed by the Law and Justice (PiS) party regarding the withholding of government subsidies. Prime Minister Donald Tusk confirmed the government's stance, signaling a firm approach to the financial oversight of the opposition party's funding.
*   **Fuel Price Records and Economic Pressure:** Poland is facing record-high fuel prices, driven by geopolitical instability in the Middle East and subsequent shocks to global crude oil markets. The situation has prompted government officials, including Finance Minister Domański, to address potential fiscal interventions, while Prime Minister Tusk described the current price levels as "dramatic" for the national economy.
