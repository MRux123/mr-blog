## WORLD

* **Escalating Rhetoric Between Tehran and Washington:** Iran has declared its full readiness to mitigate the impact of new US economic sanctions, which Washington has labeled an "economic D-Day." The situation underscores the deepening instability in geopolitical relations as Tehran seeks to circumvent external financial pressures.
* **Security Concerns Over UK Military Aid:** The Kremlin has issued a stern warning regarding potential attacks on UK drone manufacturing facilities. This follows the British government’s commitment to share technical blueprints for missile components with Ukraine, marking a significant escalation in the discourse surrounding Western military support for Kyiv.
* **Lockerbie Legal Delay:** The long-standing trial concerning the Lockerbie bombing has been postponed for a third time. The delay, occurring just days before the scheduled start, follows the emergence of new evidence regarding the 37-year-old case, prolonging the quest for legal resolution.

## POLAND

* **Political Polarization and Sejm Vacancies:** As September approaches, the Polish political scene is bracing for a contested race to fill a vacant Deputy Marshal position in the Sejm. Simultaneously, inflammatory rhetoric has dominated recent parliamentary discourse, with opposition members like Ewa Zajączkowska-Hernik leveling sharp accusations against government officials and policies regarding migration and international alignment.
* **Legislative and Market Updates:** The government is navigating several domestic issues, including the clarification of deposit bond validity and preparations for the start of the school year, which involves public interest in retail availability. Furthermore, political observers are closely tracking public sentiment shifts, as new polling data suggests that certain political figures, such as Grzegorz Braun, may hold significant influence over future coalition dynamics.
