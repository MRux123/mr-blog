## WORLD

* **Deadly Mosque Attack in Pakistan:** At least 16 people have been killed after an explosives-laden vehicle rammed into a mosque during Friday prayers. Authorities are currently investigating the incident, which underscores the persistent security threats in the region.
* **School Shooting in the Philippines:** A tragic shooting at a school in the Philippines has left three dead, including the perpetrator, and eight others injured. This marks the third such incident in the country since June, sparking renewed debate over school safety and gun control measures.
* **International Pressure on Eritrea:** Over 180 prominent writers, including Nobel laureates Olga Tokarczuk and JM Coetzee, have signed an open letter to the Eritrean government. The group is demanding proof of life for 12 authors who have been held incommunicado since a government crackdown on free speech 25 years ago.

## POLAND

* **Political Backlash Over Legislative Proposals:** The ruling coalition is facing significant criticism following controversial legislative moves regarding alcohol regulations. Critics have labeled the initiatives as being heavily influenced by lobbyists, placing government leaders under intense public and parliamentary scrutiny.
* **Tensions Over Security Preparedness:** During a heated parliamentary session, opposition figures led by Marcin Horała and Sławomir Mentzen sharply criticized the government's preparedness for potential security threats. The debate touched upon the efficacy of current warning systems and the geopolitical situation, resulting in a tense exchange with government ministers.
* **High-Profile Corruption Investigation:** The investigation into parliamentary corruption continues to escalate as MP Wojciech Król was brought before the European Public Prosecutor's Office in Katowice. The session follows the recent revocation of his parliamentary immunity, marking a significant development in the ongoing anti-corruption efforts within the country.
