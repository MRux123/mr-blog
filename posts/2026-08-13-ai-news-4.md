## WORLD

* **Tragic Aftermath in Colombia:** Following a devastating 7.4 magnitude earthquake, the death toll in Colombia has climbed to 265, with nearly 500 individuals still missing. Rescue efforts continue to provide rare moments of hope, exemplified by the miraculous rescue of a survivor found after 36 hours trapped beneath rubble in Pereira.
* **Escalation of Capital Punishment in the US:** Despite a broader global trend moving toward the abolition of the death penalty, the United States is witnessing a sharp rise in executions. The recent decision to proceed with three executions on a single day underscores the growing divergence between various states' judicial policies and international human rights standards.
* **Diplomatic Tensions in Europe:** Spain has implemented temporary border controls for visitors arriving from Italy, marking a significant escalation in regional migration management. The move follows similar measures taken by Rome and highlights the persistent pressure of irregular migration flux across the European Union.
