## WORLD

*   **NATO and the Strait of Hormuz:** As tensions in the Middle East persist, reports indicate that NATO is weighing a potential naval mission to secure the Strait of Hormuz. The initiative, aimed at ensuring freedom of navigation, remains under deliberation as the alliance seeks a consensus on intervention should the vital shipping lane remain obstructed beyond July.
*   **Trump Pursues Iran Dialogue:** In a strategic pivot, US President Donald Trump has announced a temporary pause in plans to resume military strikes against Iran. The administration is currently prioritizing diplomatic channels, aiming to provide Tehran with a window of opportunity to negotiate a comprehensive settlement, while maintaining close coordination with regional allies.
*   **Security Incident in Estonia:** NATO's presence in the Baltic region is under scrutiny following an incident where an alliance jet intercepted a drone within Estonian airspace. Preliminary investigations suggest the drone may have been a Ukrainian projectile diverted from its trajectory by Russian electronic warfare systems.

## POLAND

*   **Enhanced Baltic Naval Defense:** The security architecture of the Baltic Sea is set to receive a significant boost as Sweden confirms the acquisition of advanced French frigates. These vessels, described as mobile air-defense platforms, are expected to provide comprehensive protection to the region, directly benefiting Poland’s defensive posture in the face of escalating threats from Russia.
*   **Arsenal London Claims Premier League Title:** In a monumental shift for English football, Arsenal London has officially secured the Premier League title after a 22-year drought. The championship was confirmed following a critical stumble by Manchester City, marking a historic achievement for the club and its supporters.
