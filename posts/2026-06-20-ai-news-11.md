## WORLD

* **Middle East Tensions Escalate Despite Ceasefire:** Fresh outbreaks of violence have occurred in Lebanon, where Israeli strikes hit Hezbollah targets following a barrage of over 50 projectiles. These ongoing clashes are directly impacting international diplomacy, specifically stalling critical U.S.-Iran peace talks that were intended to stabilize the region.
* **Geopolitical Uncertainty Surrounding Iran Deal:** While President Trump’s interim agreement with Iran has been framed as a step toward economic stability, significant global skepticism remains. The lack of detailed terms in the agreement leaves international leaders wary and creates a climate of continued global tension.
* **Seismic Threat Assessment for Southern California:** Scientific reports indicate that the San Andreas and San Jacinto fault lines have reached historically high levels of tectonic stress. Experts warn that a potential catastrophic earthquake at the Cajon Pass could pose an unprecedented threat to millions of residents and critical infrastructure in the region.

## POLAND

* **Manhunt Underway for Former Police Officer:** Law enforcement agencies in the Świętokrzyskie region are conducting a massive manhunt for a 49-year-old former police officer. The suspect is wanted for the murder of his father-in-law and the attempted murder of his mother-in-law.
* **Antonov Cargo Giant Lands in Poland:** The Ukrainian-operated Antonov transport aircraft, recognized as one of the largest in the world, successfully touched down at the 31st Tactical Air Base in Krzesiny, near Poznań, drawing significant public attention.
