## WORLD

* **Escalation in Ukraine:** A lethal Russian strike, resulting in 21 fatalities, has intensified calls from Kyiv for advanced missile interceptor systems. The Russian defense ministry maintains that the operation targeted logistical hubs and supply infrastructure.
* **US Strategic Pivot:** In response to shifting geopolitical dynamics, the United States is formalizing a new nuclear strategy explicitly designed to address potential conflicts with Russia and China, signaling a major shift in global defense policy.
* **Regional Instability in the Red Sea:** Houthi militants have escalated their rhetoric, threatening to obstruct maritime access routes to Saudi Arabia. The group claims to have targeted a Saudi oil tanker, further complicating the precarious security situation in the region.

## POLAND

* **Political Accountability:** Prime Minister Donald Tusk has adopted a firm stance regarding allegations against Roman Giertych. Tusk emphasized that no individual is above the law, responding to reports regarding the unauthorized release of sensitive legal documents and recordings.
* **Environmental Crisis:** Poland is facing an unprecedented ecological challenge as the Vistula River hits its lowest water levels in 70 years. The extreme drought conditions are not only impacting ecosystems but also threatening industrial infrastructure, specifically the cooling systems of major power plants.
* **Public Perception of the Presidency:** Recent polling data from the Pollster Institute indicates a polarized public sentiment toward President Karol Nawrocki, with approximately 47% of respondents expressing approval of his performance one year into his term.
