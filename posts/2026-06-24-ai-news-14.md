## WORLD

* **First Ebola Case Confirmed in France:** French health authorities have identified the country's first case of Ebola in a doctor recently returned from a humanitarian mission in the Democratic Republic of Congo. The patient is currently isolated in a specialized facility in stable condition, with officials actively tracing all potential contacts to contain any risk of transmission.
* **France Faces Dual Crisis: Heatwave and Power Failures:** Record-breaking temperatures have triggered widespread power outages across France, affecting tens of thousands of homes. The extreme weather has ignited a national debate regarding the country's limited air-conditioning infrastructure in public spaces, including schools, leaving authorities struggling to balance safety protocols with energy grid stability.
* **Geopolitical Tensions in the Middle East:** U.S. Secretary of State Marco Rubio has launched a diplomatic mission to the Persian Gulf to reassure regional allies regarding ongoing Iran negotiations. Simultaneously, IAEA inspectors are preparing for site visits in Iran, though officials remain cautious as access remains contingent on the finalization of a broader agreement.

## POLAND

* **Constitutional Tribunal Internal Standoff:** A significant institutional crisis has unfolded within the Constitutional Tribunal. A scheduled General Assembly of judges failed to convene following an internal boycott, highlighting deep-seated divisions between incumbent judges and those appointed by the Sejm, whose legitimacy remains contested by the Tribunal's leadership.
* **Prosecution of Police in Andrychów Tragedy:** The regional prosecutor has filed an indictment against three police officers in connection with the death of 14-year-old Natalia in Andrychów. The officers face charges of professional negligence and reckless endangerment of life, a case that has sparked national discourse regarding the adequacy of emergency response procedures.
