## WORLD

* **Escalating US-Iran Conflict:** Tensions in the Middle East have reached a critical juncture. Following the reported downing of a US helicopter, President Trump has signaled a new wave of aggressive strikes against Iranian targets. The situation remains volatile, with the US administration expressing growing frustration over stalled diplomatic negotiations, heightening fears of a full-scale regional conflict.
* **Casualties in Lebanon:** Military operations in southern Lebanon have resulted in 17 fatalities, according to reports from state media. The strikes, centered in the town of Tayr Debba, mark a significant intensification of regional hostilities, further complicating the precarious security landscape in the Levant.

## POLAND

* **Kaczyński’s Extended Testimony:** Former Prime Minister and PiS leader Jarosław Kaczyński underwent a nine-hour interrogation at the prosecutor’s office today. The hearing, which focused on the controversial "two towers" development case, reportedly proceeded in a largely calm atmosphere despite minor procedural friction.
* **National Cyber-Defense Readiness:** Amid rising concerns over regional security, General Karol Molenda, commander of the Cyberspace Defence Forces, has confirmed that Poland is proactively prepared to repel digital threats. Poland has been the target of frequent cyber-attacks in recent years, prompting the establishment of dedicated units specialized in active defense and reconnaissance.
