## WORLD

* **US-China Diplomatic Stagnation:** Following a high-profile two-day visit, US and Chinese leadership concluded talks described as "very successful" in tone, yet the meetings failed to yield any concrete trade breakthroughs or significant policy agreements.
* **Nuclear Standoff with Iran:** President Trump has outlined specific conditions for potential de-escalation with Tehran, stating that a 20-year suspension of Iran's nuclear program—coupled with verified commitments to stop uranium enrichment—would be the minimum requirement for a resolution.

## POLAND

* **US Armor Deployment Controversy:** The Pentagon’s decision to halt the deployment of a rotational armored brigade to Poland has sparked a bipartisan backlash in Washington. US lawmakers have criticized the move as potentially unlawful, with senior officials suggesting the decision may be a retaliatory measure by the Trump administration against European interests.
* **Internal Pushback on Military Strategy:** While the decision to cancel the rotation has created significant diplomatic friction between Warsaw and Washington, internal sources indicate that members of the US administration are actively attempting to reverse the directive, citing the strategic "folly" of withdrawing the deployment.
