## WORLD

* **Catastrophic Coal Mine Explosion in China:** A severe disaster in Shanxi province has resulted in at least 90 deaths, marking the country's deadliest mining incident in 16 years. President Xi Jinping has ordered an intensive rescue operation and a formal investigation, emphasizing accountability for those responsible for the safety failures.
* **Escalating Humanitarian Concerns in DRC:** The Democratic Republic of Congo is facing a dual crisis as a widening Ebola outbreak coincides with strained international refugee policies. While the Trump administration has paused deportations to the region due to the virus, experts remain critical of the efficacy of these measures amidst a collapsing local healthcare infrastructure.
* **Conflict Continues in Lebanon:** Despite a fragile truce, Israeli strikes have hit medical facilities in Tyre. The incident has drawn international alarm as casualties continue to rise among healthcare workers and paramedics.

## POLAND

* **Leaked Confidential US Embassy Cable:** A significant diplomatic incident has emerged following the leak of a confidential US embassy dispatch, leading to internal tension and a perceived sense of "betrayal" within Warsaw’s political circles.
* **Public Outrage Over Legislative Travel Expenses:** A new report detailing parliamentary travel costs has sparked controversy, revealing that Polish taxpayers covered over 12 million PLN for travel in 2025. Data indicates that some members of parliament traveled distances exceeding the circumference of the Earth within a single year.
* **Sanctions Against Foreign Influencer:** The Ministry of the Interior and Administration has announced a five-year entry ban for a Ukrainian influencer who drove a sports car into the protected area of Morskie Oko. The move follows criticism regarding the leniency of the initial fine relative to the scale of the environmental violation.
