## WORLD

*   **Escalating Conflict in Ukraine:** A devastating Russian missile strike on a residential block in northern Ukraine has claimed the lives of 19 people, including five children. Meanwhile, on the frontlines, Ukrainian forces have successfully pushed back against Russian attempts to encircle key "fortress" cities in the Donbas region.
*   **Political Shifts in the United States:** President Donald Trump has publicly commented on his strategy regarding the Ukraine-Russia conflict, claiming that he has already ended eight wars and plans to bring the current conflict to a swift conclusion, despite initial assessments that the situation would be "easy" to resolve.
*   **Public Accountability in Israel:** Three years after the October 7 attacks, citizens are intensifying demands for a state inquiry. Prime Minister Benjamin Netanyahu continues to face significant pressure as he refuses to accept personal responsibility for the systemic failures that preceded the crisis.

## POLAND

*   **Security Threat in Ostrołęka:** Authorities have confirmed that a 19-year-old perpetrator behind a knife attack at a school in Ostrołęka—which injured nine people—was also carrying a home-made explosive device in his backpack. The National Prosecutor has moved to reclassify the charges toward attempted murder.
*   **Escalation on the Eastern Flank:** In response to heightened regional instability, Lithuania has deployed military units to its border with Russia, signaling increasing concerns regarding security along the Baltic frontier.
*   **Highway Tragedy Near Stargard:** A fatal accident on the expressway near Stargard has sparked national outrage. Preliminary reports suggest the driver of a BMW was traveling at 270 km/h before colliding with a Toyota, resulting in one death and leaving another person in critical condition. The suspect, who fled the scene, is currently being sought by law enforcement.
