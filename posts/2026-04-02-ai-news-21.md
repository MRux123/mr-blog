## WORLD

* **Iran-Israel Conflict Intensifies:** The economic impact of the ongoing conflict has reached a critical stage as Iran's two largest steel plants were forced to shut down following recent air strikes. The strikes, reportedly coordinated between Israel and the U.S., are severely affecting Iran's domestic economy, fueling public desperation and protests.
* **NATO and U.S. Diplomatic Friction:** Tensions within the Western alliance are rising. French President Emmanuel Macron has publicly criticized Donald Trump for questioning the reliability of NATO, warning that such rhetoric undermines the alliance's collective security. Simultaneously, U.S. Senator Lindsey Graham has suggested a potential withdrawal of American troops from Europe, citing a lack of support from European allies regarding the current conflict with Iran.
* **Leadership Changes in Washington:** In a significant shift in U.S. law enforcement, President Trump has removed Attorney General Pam Bondi from her post, following controversy surrounding the Department of Justice’s handling of the Jeffrey Epstein investigation.

## POLAND

* **Major Accident in Wadowice:** A six-year-old girl is in serious condition after falling from a third-story window in Wadowice. Emergency services, including an LPR (Air Ambulance) helicopter, were deployed to transport the child to a hospital in Kraków.
* **Political Controversy in Kłodzko:** Sejm Deputy Speaker Monika Wielichowska has issued a formal statement addressing public scrutiny regarding her connections to a former PO member convicted in a pedophilia scandal. Wielichowska has categorically denied any private or professional ties to the individual, labeling the accusations as part of a targeted hate campaign.
* **Military Personnel Loss:** The Ukrainian Center for Countering Disinformation reported that Russian General Aleksandr Otroszczenko and several high-ranking officers were killed in a transport plane crash over the occupied territory of Crimea.
