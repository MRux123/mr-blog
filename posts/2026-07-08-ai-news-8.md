## WORLD

*   **Russian Fuel Crisis and Geopolitical Strain:** Reports indicate that Moscow is struggling to maintain domestic fuel supplies. Analysts are closely watching whether this economic instability will force a shift in the Kremlin's strategic approach to the war in Ukraine or if it will trigger further escalation as a diversionary tactic.
*   **FIFA’s Potential Pivot on Russia:** The global football governing body is reconsidering its ban on Russian national teams. This development follows a signal from the International Olympic Committee (IOC) regarding the temporary suspension of the Russian Olympic Committee, raising concerns about the potential normalization of Russian participation in international sports.

## POLAND

*   **Labor Market Reform Takes Effect:** A significant shift in labor law has been implemented, granting the National Labour Inspectorate (PIP) expanded powers. Inspectors can now unilaterally initiate the transformation of "sham" B2B contracts and civil law agreements into formal employment contracts, a move aimed at enhancing job security despite strong opposition from business sectors.
*   **Diplomatic Tensions with Moscow:** The Russian Foreign Ministry, via spokesperson Maria Zakharova, has issued fresh warnings to Warsaw regarding its ongoing support for Ukraine. The rhetoric marks a further hardening of diplomatic relations, with Moscow claiming that Poland is now facing direct consequences for its geopolitical stance.
*   **Political Landscape Shifts:** A new poll indicates that while the Civic Coalition maintains its lead, support for both major parties (KO and PiS) has experienced a slight decline. Conversely, there is a measurable rise in popularity for right-wing opposition parties, specifically Konfederacja and Konfederacja Korony Polskiej, suggesting a possible fragmentation in the current political discourse.
