## WORLD

* **Deepening Conflict in Mali:** The security situation in Mali has deteriorated sharply as an Islamist group has imposed a strict blockade on the capital. This escalation follows the assassination of the country's defense minister, signaling a significant surge in nationwide instability and potential for further humanitarian crisis.
* **Ukrainian Skepticism Over Trump-Putin Dialogue:** Kyiv has responded with indifference to ongoing telephone communications between Donald Trump and Vladimir Putin. Ukrainian leadership and the public remain largely unswayed, as previous diplomatic efforts have failed to yield tangible peace initiatives, reinforcing a sense of exhaustion regarding international mediation attempts.
* **Long-Range Strikes in Russia:** Ukrainian forces have successfully targeted critical infrastructure deep within Russian territory. Explosions were reported at an explosives factory in Dzerzhinsk and incidents in Perm—over 1,500 kilometers from the border—marking a strategic shift in the conflict’s reach and testing the efficacy of Russian defensive capabilities.

## POLAND

* **Sejm Strips Macierewicz of Immunity:** In a landmark parliamentary vote, the Sejm has revoked the parliamentary immunity of Antoni Macierewicz, a prominent figure of the Law and Justice (PiS) party. The decision paves the way for prosecution regarding his past statements concerning the Military Counterintelligence Service (SKW).
* **Government Stability Amid Presidential Vetoes:** Prime Minister Donald Tusk’s government successfully navigated a vote of no confidence concerning two cabinet ministers, with the governing coalition demonstrating internal unity. However, the political climate remains strained as the President issued new vetoes, specifically rejecting legislative proposals involving family law reforms and electoral procedures.
* **Political Realignment within PiS:** The departure of Łukasz Mejza from the Law and Justice (PiS) parliamentary club marks a notable shift in the opposition’s ranks. Party leader Jarosław Kaczyński has signaled a move to close the chapter on the controversy surrounding the MP, highlighting ongoing internal efforts to manage the party’s public image.
