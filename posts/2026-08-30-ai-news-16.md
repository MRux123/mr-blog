## WORLD

* **Iceland Rejects EU Accession Talks:** In a decisive national vote, 52.8% of Icelandic citizens have opted against resuming negotiations for membership in the European Union. The result brings an end to a period of intense domestic debate, signaling a preference for maintaining the country's independent sovereign status over integration into the bloc.
* **Maritime Disaster Off Cyprus Coast:** Rescue operations are underway following the capsizing of a passenger ferry carrying 267 individuals. The vessel, a catamaran-type ferry, began taking on water shortly after departing from Kyrenia. Local authorities are currently managing the crisis as the international community monitors the search and rescue efforts.
* **Violence at Swiss Event:** Authorities have launched a manhunt following a deadly shooting at a rave held at a horseracing track in northern Switzerland. The attack resulted in one fatality and left five others injured, prompting heightened security concerns across the region.

## POLAND

* **Major Industrial Fire in Lublin:** A massive fire has engulfed an 1,800-square-meter warehouse facility in Lublin, necessitating an emergency response from 23 fire brigades. The building, which reportedly stored composite materials for aircraft manufacturing, remains the focus of an intense firefighting operation aimed at preventing the spread of flames.
* **Vandalism Incident in Gdynia:** Police in Gdynia have released the image of a suspect involved in a targeted attack on a local children's play area. The perpetrator reportedly shattered a window and poured butyric acid inside the facility, an act authorities have classified as a malicious act of vandalism. 
* **Aviation Safety Incident in Podkarpacie:** A small aircraft was forced to make an emergency landing in the Podkarpacie region. While the incident caused alarm, local reports confirm it is being investigated as an aviation safety concern, with no immediate reports of significant casualties.
