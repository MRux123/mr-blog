## WORLD

* **Investigation into FlyDubai mid-air attack:** Authorities in the United Arab Emirates are leading a formal investigation into the security breach aboard a FlyDubai aircraft. Israeli Prime Minister Benjamin Netanyahu has described the incident, involving a violent altercation between pilots, as an act of "Islamist radical indoctrination," prompting immediate international aviation repercussions, including Emirates airline's decision to suspend flights to Tel Aviv for affected passengers.
* **Legal challenge following the death of Renee Good:** The family of Renee Good, who was killed by an ICE agent in Minneapolis, has filed a lawsuit against U.S. officials. The legal team alleges that government policies have fostered an environment conducive to state-sponsored violence, marking a significant escalation in the ongoing debate over immigration enforcement practices in the United States.

## POLAND

* **Political fallout from fuel tax legislation:** President Karol Nawrocki has signed a controversial bill regarding the taxation of "excess profits" from fuel corporations. While the government, led by Prime Minister Donald Tusk, frames the move as a victory for the public—promising lower fuel prices starting this weekend—the decision has sparked political friction, with opposition figures questioning whether the move violates prior campaign commitments known as the "Toruń Declaration."
* **Medical milestone in pediatric care:** Polish healthcare professionals have reported a successful cornea transplant in a child, highlighting the long-term medical commitments required for such patients. The case underscores advancements in domestic surgical capabilities, though specialists emphasize the lifelong nature of care necessary following such procedures.
