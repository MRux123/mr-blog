## WORLD

* **Nobel Peace Prize Awarded to Navi Pillay**: Human rights lawyer and former UN official Navi Pillay has been awarded the 2026 Nobel Peace Prize. Recognized for her decades of work in international law and war crimes investigation, the Nobel committee lauded her moral compass. The selection remains highly significant given her recent leadership of a UN panel that determined Israel had committed genocide in Gaza—a finding firmly rejected by the Israeli government.
* **Escalation of Shadow War and European Security**: Tensions between Moscow and the West continue to intensify as European leaders struggle to formulate a coherent response to Russia’s "shadow war." Utilizing a mix of ambiguity and fear-based tactics, Russia’s strategy aims to fracture NATO unity and erode support for Ukraine, forcing Europe to debate the threshold for potential retaliation.
* **Controversial Execution Livestream**: The Pentagon’s decision to livestream an upcoming execution by firing squad has sparked a fierce ethical and legal debate. While some victims' advocates believe the punishment fits the crime, legal experts caution that the move sets an unprecedented and legally volatile precedent for the U.S. justice system.

## POLAND

* **Adam Glapiński Faces Constitutional Tribunal**: A significant political shift is underway as the Sejm's Constitutional Accountability Committee has adopted a report recommending that NBP President Adam Glapiński be brought before the State Tribunal. This move marks a major escalation in the ongoing political scrutiny surrounding the head of the Polish central bank.
* **Tragedy in Wyszków**: A 15-year-old perpetrator has been detained following a fatal attack in a school in Leszczydół-Nowiny. The incident, which resulted in one death and injuries to two others, has shocked the nation, particularly after the school director shared a harrowing account of the confrontation and the suspect's subsequent psychological state.
* **Government Fuel Price Regulation**: Minister of Energy Miłosz Motyka has announced temporary price ceilings for fuel to stabilize the market during the upcoming weekend. The government intervention sets maximum retail prices for petrol and diesel, reflecting ongoing efforts to curb inflationary pressures on energy costs for Polish households.
