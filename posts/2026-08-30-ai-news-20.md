## WORLD

* **Tragic Ferry Capsizing off Cyprus:** A major search and rescue operation is underway following the sinking of a passenger ferry carrying approximately 270 people off the coast of Kyrenia, Cyprus. Authorities confirm at least eight fatalities, with many others currently missing. The incident, involving a catamaran-type vessel, occurred shortly after the ship departed from port.
* **NASA Launches Cutting-Edge Space Telescope:** In a significant advancement for astrophysics, NASA has successfully deployed a new high-powered space telescope. The mission aims to map the universe with unprecedented detail, specifically targeting further exploration into the nature of dark matter and dark energy over the coming years.
* **International Stance on Potential Three-Way Summit:** The Kremlin has responded to reports regarding a prospective meeting between Vladimir Putin, Donald Trump, and Volodymyr Zelenskyy. Spokesperson Dmitry Peskov stated that such a summit could only occur if it were intended to formalize previously negotiated agreements, maintaining a cautious stance on diplomatic breakthroughs.

## POLAND

* **Pressure Mounts on PKOl President:** The International Olympic Committee (IOC) has issued a clear recommendation following the legal troubles of Polish Olympic Committee (PKOl) President Radosław Piesiewicz. The IOC has suggested that a voluntary resignation would be the most stable solution for the organization in light of the ongoing arrest proceedings.
* **Tragedy on Crete:** A heartbreaking incident has occurred in Greece, where an 11-year-old Polish girl has died. According to local reports, the child was recovered unconscious from a hotel pool and subsequently passed away in a hospital.
* **RCB Alert Confusion:** The Government Security Centre (RCB) faced public scrutiny after an air-raid alert regarding events in Ukraine was inconsistently delivered to residents in the Lublin region. The Ministry of the Interior and Administration (MSWiA) clarified that the timing between the alert issuance and the cancellation was extremely narrow, resulting in the confusion.
