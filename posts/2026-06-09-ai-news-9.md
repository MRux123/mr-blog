## WORLD

* **Middle East Conflict Intensifies:** Tensions remain high as Israel warns of new strikes in Southern Lebanon, complicating ongoing peace negotiations between the United States and Iran. Both Washington and Tehran face significant political hurdles as they attempt to define a potential deal that secures domestic support while navigating the precarious security landscape.
* **Geopolitical Realignment:** Xi Jinping has concluded a high-profile visit to Pyongyang, marking his first official trip to North Korea since 2019. The two leaders have publicly committed to strengthening bilateral ties, signaling a shift in regional power dynamics.
* **ICC Leadership Crisis:** The International Criminal Court has officially suspended its top prosecutor, Karim Khan, following the launch of an investigation into allegations of sexual misconduct. Khan has firmly denied all accusations, as legal proceedings move forward.

## POLAND

* **High-Level Legal Scrutiny:** The European Public Prosecutor’s Office has filed a formal request to lift the parliamentary immunity of Wojciech Król, a member of the Civic Coalition (KO). The request has been directed to the Sejm’s regulatory committee by Speaker Włodzimierz Czarzasty, marking a significant moment in national political accountability.
* **Military Exercises in the North:** Residents of the Warmian-Masurian Voivodeship are being advised of increased military activity as the "Ognista Burza-26" (Firestorm-26) exercise commences. Authorities have issued public guidelines to ensure safety and transparency regarding the presence of troops and heavy equipment in the region.
