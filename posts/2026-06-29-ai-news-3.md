## WORLD

*   **Venezuela Earthquake Crisis:** Rescue efforts in Venezuela have entered a critical phase as the window for locating survivors following the twin earthquakes continues to close. Despite the chaos and logistical delays, emergency teams managed to pull 33 people from the rubble over the weekend, including two young boys, offering a brief moment of hope amidst a deepening humanitarian disaster.
*   **Strait of Hormuz Tensions:** The United States has signaled a potential breakthrough in the Strait of Hormuz, reporting an agreement with Iran to suspend hostilities and ensure the free movement of vessels. While the U.S. remains optimistic about stabilizing the region, Iran has yet to formally confirm the deal, leaving the status of the fragile two-week-old cease-fire uncertain.

## POLAND

*   **Russian Offensive Escalation:** The Kremlin has effectively closed the door on any immediate prospects for a truce, with reports confirming that Vladimir Putin is prioritizing a continued military offensive. This strategic shift further diminishes the hope for a diplomatic resolution to the ongoing conflict.
*   **Severe Weather Disruptions:** Intense storms and heavy rainfall have caused significant damage across Poland, particularly in the Lubuskie and Greater Poland (Wielkopolska) provinces. Emergency services have responded to dozens of distress calls, primarily focused on clearing fallen trees and securing infrastructure damaged by the violent weather systems.
*   **Controversial Police Intervention:** Investigative journalists have brought to light new footage concerning the death of 31-year-old Paweł Pułaczewski during a police intervention in Inowrocław. The case, which was previously dismissed by prosecutors, is now under judicial review following a formal complaint filed by the family’s legal representative.
