## WORLD

*   **Massive Cryptocurrency Heist:** In what is being described as the largest cyberattack in history, hackers have allegedly stolen $6.3 billion from the Binance cryptocurrency exchange. Preliminary reports suggest a potential link to North Korean state-sponsored actors, raising significant concerns regarding global digital financial security.
*   **Political Turbulence in the US:** Tensions between the Trump administration and the media have escalated after the President clashed with a Reuters reporter, labeling them "disgusting" following inquiries into his demanding schedule and recent campaign trail fatigue.
*   **China’s Internal Consolidation:** President Xi Jinping has intensified his "party discipline" campaign, resulting in a record number of political purges. Observers characterize this move as a strategic effort to consolidate authority amidst growing internal and external pressure.

## POLAND

*   **New Leadership at CPK:** The government has officially appointed Piotr Borys as the new CEO of the Centralny Port Komunikacyjny (CPK). Borys, an experienced civil engineer with a strong background in PKP PLK investment projects, is tasked with steering the ambitious infrastructure project.
*   **Political Stance on EU Membership:** Adrian Zandberg of the Left party has publicly criticized Jarosław Kaczyński following the latter’s controversial remarks regarding potential negotiations for Poland's exit from the European Union. Zandberg framed Kaczyński’s rhetoric as a major political advantage for Prime Minister Donald Tusk.
*   **Security Incident in Krakow:** A violent attack occurred at a local pharmacy in Krakow, where a 24-year-old individual stabbed three pharmacists. The perpetrator, who entered the premises with an existing hand injury, has been apprehended by law enforcement.
