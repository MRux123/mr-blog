## WORLD

*   **Potential Diplomatic Mediation:** Reports from *Der Spiegel* suggest that former German Chancellor Angela Merkel is being considered as a potential mediator for peace talks between Ukraine and Russia. Her unique background, including her fluency in Russian and previous direct engagement with both Vladimir Putin and Volodymyr Zelensky, positions her as a candidate for this sensitive role, though observers remain divided on whether a German diplomat can remain neutral enough to bridge the divide between Kyiv and Moscow.
*   **Persistent Ambitions of the Kremlin:** Diplomatic efforts to resolve the war in Ukraine remain largely stalled as intelligence suggests President Vladimir Putin’s core strategic ambitions remain unchanged. The situation on the frontlines continues to mirror the political deadlock in international cabinets, with no indications of a compromise in sight.

## POLAND

*   **Artillery Modernization Milestones:** The Polish military has achieved a significant tactical success, setting a national record by striking a target nearly 80 kilometers away using the Homar-K artillery system. This feat, executed during the *Baltic Shield* exercise with Denmark, underscores Poland’s commitment to bolstering regional security and modernizing its military capabilities alongside NATO allies.
*   **Shifting Political Landscape:** A new poll conducted by United Surveys for *Wirtualna Polski* highlights a potential volatility in the Polish political scene. While the Civic Coalition (KO) retains the lead, the research suggests that the ruling coalition may face challenges in maintaining its majority, with support potentially shifting toward a combination of Law and Justice (PiS) and the Confederation, signaling an increasingly competitive parliamentary environment.
*   **Security Concerns:** Local authorities are currently investigating an unidentified object discovered in a forest in the Podkarpacie region, with the Military Gendarmerie leading the official inquiry into the nature and origin of the find.
