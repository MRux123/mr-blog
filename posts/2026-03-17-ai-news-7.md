## WORLD

* **Middle East Conflict Intensifies:** The regional security situation has deteriorated sharply as Iran launched missile and drone attacks on Gulf nations, including Qatar and the UAE. In a significant military escalation, Israel has commenced retaliatory strikes against Iranian targets and Hezbollah. International concerns mount as airspace closures and infrastructure threats, such as potential disruptions to gas networks, signal a broader regional instability.
* **U.S. Foreign Policy Shifts:** President Trump has officially declined diplomatic overtures from Iran, citing concerns over the leadership legitimacy of Supreme Leader Mojtaba Khamenei. Furthermore, the administration has signaled a potential delay in high-level summits with China, prioritizing the management of the Middle East crisis and securing strategic maritime routes like the Strait of Hormuz.

## POLAND

* **Geopolitical Risk in the Baltics:** Tensions are rising in the Estonian city of Narva, where fears of a "Donbas-style" scenario have prompted official denials from the Estonian government. Analysts view the city as a potential "Achilles' heel" for Western security due to its high concentration of ethnic Russians and concerns regarding Russian-backed provocations.
* **Domestic Policy and Public Sentiment:** The Polish government is facing scrutiny over the implementation of the new deposit-return system, with public dissatisfaction growing. Concurrently, political polling indicates that the majority of voters view Przemysław Czarnek as an unsuitable candidate for Prime Minister, highlighting internal friction within the political landscape.
* **Law Enforcement Success:** The Central Bureau of Investigation (CBŚP) has dismantled a significant narcotics smuggling operation in the Wrocław area, resulting in the seizure of over 42 kilograms of cocaine hidden within construction materials.
