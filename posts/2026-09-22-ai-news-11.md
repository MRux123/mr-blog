## WORLD

* **Rising Geopolitical Tensions in the Baltics:** The Lithuanian Prime Minister has signaled that the nation is prepared to "fight back" against increasing threats from Moscow. While evacuation protocols are in place, the situation underscores the mounting security anxiety across Eastern Europe regarding Russian aggression.
* **Escalating Pressure on Iran:** The G7 nations have issued a stern demand for Iran to cease the arming of Houthi rebels in Yemen. The group warned that the ongoing conflict not only destabilizes the region but poses a critical threat to global energy security.
* **Democratic Safeguards in Germany:** In response to the rising influence of the far-right Alternative for Germany (AfD) party, federal officials are moving to restrict the party's access to classified intelligence, specifically regarding secret national war plans, citing significant security concerns.

## POLAND

* **Military Leadership Changes:** The Ministry of National Defense has confirmed the departure of General Mirosław Bodnar from his role at the head of the Inspectorate for Unmanned Systems. The decision reportedly stems from high-level dissatisfaction with the sluggish pace of drone implementation within the Polish Armed Forces.
* **Healthcare Reform Acceleration:** Prime Minister Donald Tusk has announced an expedited timeline for healthcare reforms, aiming to address public and medical sector demands for greater transparency and improved efficiency in the management of medical funds.
* **Support for Lithuania:** In alignment with broader security commitments, Polish officials have confirmed that Poland is ready to assist Lithuania with evacuation planning, solidifying a collaborative approach to the regional defense strategy.
