## WORLD

*   **Rising Death Toll in Germany:** Authorities have confirmed that the death toll following the shooting at a youth welfare centre in Germany has risen to six. The incident, which initially claimed five lives at the scene, has left the nation in shock as police continue their investigation into this violent tragedy.
*   **Supreme Court Ruling Against Trump:** In a significant legal development, the US Supreme Court has rejected an appeal from President Trump regarding the E. Jean Carroll sexual abuse case. The ruling upholds the previous judgment, requiring the president to pay $5 million in damages.
*   **Seismic Crisis in Venezuela:** Relief efforts in Venezuela are facing major challenges as the full scale of the recent twin earthquakes remains unclear. Experts warn that the casualty count may take weeks to ascertain, given the severity of building damage and the high number of missing persons.

## POLAND

*   **Record-Breaking Heat on Śnieżka:** The Karkonosze mountains have witnessed an unprecedented meteorological event, as temperatures on Śnieżka peak have surpassed records held for 44 years. This anomaly highlights the intensifying climatic shifts currently affecting the region.
*   **Security Concerns and NATO Readiness:** Amidst ongoing geopolitical instability, experts are increasingly questioning if Russia will test the cohesion of the NATO alliance. Polish defense analysts emphasize that heightened vigilance and military preparedness are essential in the current climate.
*   **Russian Military Dissent:** Tensions within the Russian military apparatus have surfaced following the arrest of a soldier who had publicly threatened a mutiny against President Vladimir Putin. The incident underscores growing internal pressures within the Russian armed forces.
