## WORLD

*   **U.S.-Iran De-escalation:** The United States has lifted its naval blockade following a controversial agreement with Tehran. While the move marks a significant shift in Gulf tensions, Iran’s supreme leader has characterized the deal as an act of "desperation" by the Trump administration. The agreement has sparked intense scrutiny from analysts regarding the long-term effectiveness of the policy and its impact on regional stability.
*   **Escalation in Russia:** A massive Ukrainian drone strike has targeted oil infrastructure near Moscow, causing widespread fires and reports of "black rain" over the capital. In response, the U.S. Embassy in Russia has issued an unprecedented advisory, urging American citizens to seek immediate shelter, reflecting a sharp increase in the severity of the conflict.
*   **Security Breach in Niger:** A major militant assault on Niger's primary airport has resulted in 35 fatalities. This attack highlights the deteriorating security situation in West Africa as militant groups continue to challenge state authority.

## POLAND

*   **Institutional Failure in Ząbki:** A tragic death of a toddler in a nursery near Warsaw has triggered a government investigation, revealing that staff members involved in the child's care were operating without formal employment contracts. The incident has prompted a broader review of child-care regulations and oversight procedures.
*   **Human Rights Abuses in Suwałki:** Authorities have detained two women following the discovery of a horrific case involving the long-term confinement of 47-year-old twins. The local prosecutor’s office has leveled serious charges, shedding light on a case of systemic abuse that remained hidden for over a decade.
