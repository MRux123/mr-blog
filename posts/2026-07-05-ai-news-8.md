## WORLD

*   **Trump Engages in High-Stakes Diplomacy:** US President Donald Trump has held pivotal conversations with both Vladimir Putin and Volodymyr Zelenskyy. While the discussions served to mark the 250th anniversary of American independence, they also addressed the ongoing conflict in Ukraine, signaling a renewed, proactive stance from Washington regarding regional security.
*   **China Releases Detained Pastor:** In a diplomatic success linked to direct intervention by President Trump, Chinese authorities have released Ezra Jin Mingri, the founder of the Zion Church. His liberation comes as a notable development in the tense landscape of US-China relations and religious freedom.
*   **Humanitarian Crisis in Côte d’Ivoire:** Severe weather and torrential rains have triggered catastrophic flooding across West Africa, with Côte d’Ivoire reporting 59 fatalities since May. The situation remains critical as regional authorities warn of further casualties during the ongoing rainy season.

## POLAND

*   **Political Dialogue in the Spotlight:** The domestic political scene continues to grapple with security and international policy questions, as exemplified by a high-profile debate featuring representatives from across the political spectrum on "Śniadanie Rymanowskiego."
*   **Public Safety Concerns:** The country faces a series of local security challenges, including a fatal road accident near Staszów and a completed manhunt in the Kielce region, where a 65-year-old suspect was apprehended following the discovery of his wife's body.
*   **Meteorological Alerts:** Authorities have issued warnings regarding severe weather patterns, including the potential formation of supercell storms, prompting concern for public safety and infrastructure in the coming days.
