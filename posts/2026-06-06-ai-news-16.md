## WORLD

*   **Middle East Instability:** The regional security situation remains volatile following an Israeli strike in Lebanon that claimed the lives of three Lebanese soldiers, occurring despite a recently signed truce. Concurrently, international scrutiny intensifies as Palestinian officials report the death of an infant in the West Bank due to Israeli military fire, an incident currently under disputed circumstances.
*   **Diplomatic Rifts and Espionage:** The Pentagon has reportedly signaled heightened concerns regarding potential espionage by Israel. This shift in security posture follows a period of increasing friction between U.S. leadership and the current Israeli administration, underscoring a significant cooling in relations between the two longtime allies.
*   **Climate Outlook:** Recent scientific findings have provided a marginal, yet significant, shift in climate projections, with researchers officially removing the "worst-case scenario" for global warming from their models. Experts emphasize, however, that this does not mark the end of the climate crisis, but rather a slight recalibration of the challenges ahead.

## POLAND

*   **French Open Milestone:** Polish tennis player Maja Chwalińska has achieved a career-defining moment, reaching the French Open final. Despite a defeat to Mirra Andreeva, her performance secures a massive 93-place jump in the world rankings, placing her at number 21 and cementing her status as a rising force in global tennis.
*   **Ukraine's Stance on Peace Talks:** Ukrainian Foreign Minister Andrii Sybiha has issued a stark warning to the Kremlin, stating that by rejecting President Zelenskyy’s latest peace proposal, Vladimir Putin has effectively closed the door to a favorable resolution to the conflict. Kyiv suggests that the Russian leadership now faces a future of escalating strategic and diplomatic degradation.
