## WORLD

* **Middle East Conflict Intensifies:** The regional security situation has deteriorated sharply as the conflict between Iran, Israel, and Gulf nations escalates. Reports indicate ongoing exchanges of drone and missile attacks involving Saudi Arabia, the UAE, and Kuwait, while the IDF has confirmed renewed strikes in Tehran. Amidst the chaos, the U.S. has hinted at a potential "winding down" of hostilities despite earlier claims of total military dominance, while Iranian officials are attempting to secure specific maritime access for Japanese vessels through the Strait of Hormuz to alleviate economic pressure.
* **Industrial Tragedy in South Korea:** A devastating fire at an automotive parts factory—a key supplier for Hyundai and Kia—has resulted in at least 10 fatalities and roughly 60 injuries. Emergency services report that survivors were forced to jump from windows to escape the blaze, marking the deadliest factory incident in the country in two years.

## POLAND

* **Pentagon Planning Ground Operations in Iran:** Polish media, citing reports from CBS News, highlight that the U.S. Department of Defense is actively preparing for a potential ground operation in Iran. Current logistical discussions reportedly involve the management and detention protocols for Iranian combatants, signaling a significant shift in U.S. military strategy.
* **New Iranian Leadership Addresses Nation:** In his first major public address, the new Iranian Supreme Leader, Mojtaba Khamenei, emphasized national unity and an "economy of resistance" during the Persian New Year. Khamenei formally denied Iranian involvement in recent attacks targeting Turkey and Oman, attempting to distance Tehran from regional instability as diplomatic and military tensions peak.
