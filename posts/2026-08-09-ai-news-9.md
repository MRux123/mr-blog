## WORLD

* **Political Turmoil in Kosovo:** The parliament in Kosovo was plunged into chaos as opposition MP Time Kadrijaj pelted Prime Minister Albin Kurti with eggs during a plenary session. The aggressive protest, which forced the suspension of the session, underscores deep-seated political divisions and signals a growing likelihood of snap elections in the region.
* **Escalation in Belgorod:** A significant Ukrainian drone strike targeted the Russian city of Belgorod, resulting in multiple fires across the urban area. This incident marks a continuation of intensified cross-border military activities, further straining the regional security architecture.

## POLAND

* **Police Operation in Oborniki:** Authorities in Oborniki, western Poland, launched a major manhunt involving additional reinforcements following an incident initially reported as a robbery. The situation, which turned out to be a complex confrontation between foreign nationals, highlights growing concerns regarding public safety and the scale of international criminal activities currently monitored by local law enforcement.
* **Investigation into Polish Nationals in US Prisons:** New data from the Federal Bureau of Prisons (BOP) reveals that at least 24 Polish citizens are currently incarcerated in US federal facilities. Experts suggest the actual number may be significantly higher due to the fragmented nature of the American judicial and prison reporting systems, sparking a discussion on consular protection and the nature of these legal cases.
