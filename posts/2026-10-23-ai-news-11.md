## WORLD

* **Black Sea Maritime Incident:** A commercial vessel, the *Khoryv*, has sunk in the Black Sea following a drone strike. Authorities confirm one fatality, with search and rescue operations ongoing for three missing crew members. This escalation highlights the growing threat of autonomous technology in maritime conflict zones.
* **Escalating Tensions in the West Bank:** A violent shooting incident has left five Israelis wounded, with two alleged Palestinian attackers killed by security forces. The attack occurs in a climate of heightened instability, just days before Israel's national elections.
* **Human Rights Concerns in Sudan:** Reports indicate that RSF fighters are deliberately targeting hospitals in Sudan. The UN human rights chief, Volker Türk, has warned that these systematic strikes may constitute war crimes, further exacerbating the humanitarian crisis in the region.

## POLAND

* **Aggressive Incident in Opole Province:** A high-stakes police pursuit involving a stolen ambulance ended in gunfire and the injury of a female officer. Authorities have confirmed that the officer is receiving medical care and is in stable condition, marking a dramatic breach of public safety in the region.
* **UK-Style Crisis Preparedness:** Amidst growing geopolitical instability, there is an increasing discourse in Poland regarding civil defense. Following similar trends in the UK, where the government has urged citizens to stockpile 72 hours of supplies, the focus is shifting toward national resilience against potential cyberattacks and infrastructure threats.
* **Judicial and Regulatory Shifts:** The Polish political landscape remains in flux with new legislative projects concerning the judicial system being introduced. Additionally, local debates over health regulations, specifically regarding the certification of deaths by family doctors, indicate a push to address long-standing administrative gaps in the medical sector.
