## WORLD

* **US-Iran Standoff Intensifies:** Tensions have reached a critical juncture on the first day of the U.S.-led naval blockade of Iran. President Trump has issued a stark warning that any Iranian vessels attempting to breach the blockade—intended to cripple Iran's oil revenue—will be neutralized. Iran has vowed immediate retaliation, raising fears of a broader regional conflict.
* **Shift in Chagos Sovereignty:** The diplomatic path toward transferring sovereignty of the Chagos Islands to Mauritius has stalled. UK Foreign Office minister Stephen Doughty confirmed that the deal is now politically "impossible" to ratify, citing a sudden reversal in support from the United States under the current administration.
* **Technological Warfare in Ukraine:** President Volodymyr Zelenskyy reported a tactical milestone in the conflict, announcing that Ukrainian unmanned systems have successfully seized enemy positions without human infantry intervention. This development underscores the accelerating role of autonomous technology in modern warfare.

## POLAND

* **Constitutional Crisis Over Judiciary:** A severe rift has emerged between the Executive and Judicial branches. Justice Minister Waldemar Żurek has ordered a criminal investigation into Kancelaria Prezydenta officials. The probe focuses on allegations that presidential staff advised President Karol Nawrocki to bypass legal requirements by selectively rejecting the swearing-in of four Constitutional Tribunal judges.
* **Corruption and Attrition on the Frontline:** Reports from RMF24 highlight the grim reality for Russian forces, where soldiers are increasingly paying bribes to their own commanders to avoid deployment in "meat grinder" assaults or to secure leave. This systemic corruption underscores the desperate and demoralizing conditions currently facing the Russian military.
