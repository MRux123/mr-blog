## WORLD

*   **Landfill Collapse in Guinea:** A catastrophic landslide at a major waste disposal site in the capital of Guinea has resulted in at least 30 fatalities. The collapse, which buried several residential homes, has prompted an emergency government response as search and rescue operations continue in the disaster zone.
*   **Heatwave-Related Fatalities in France:** The French government has issued a stern warning as the nation reports a sharp increase in drownings, totaling 301 deaths since June 19. Officials have directly linked this trend to recent extreme heatwaves, which have encouraged citizens to seek relief in water bodies, often leading to risky behaviors in hazardous conditions.
*   **Ukraine Election Stance:** President Volodymyr Zelenskyy has officially ruled out holding elections in Ukraine while the country remains in a state of active conflict. Addressing internal pressures, the President emphasized that proceeding with a democratic vote during the ongoing Russian invasion would pose unacceptable security risks to the nation.

## POLAND

*   **Conflict Escalation near Kharkiv:** A Russian drone strike targeted a civilian train in the vicinity of Kharkiv, resulting in two confirmed deaths and five injuries. This incident marks another escalation in the targeting of critical infrastructure and transit routes in the region.
*   **Legal Investigation into Zondacrypto:** The political landscape in Poland is abuzz following reports that Przemysław Kral, the founder of the collapsed cryptocurrency exchange Zondacrypto, has allegedly secured the status of a "small crown witness." The development, addressed by government officials, suggests a potentially significant breakthrough in the investigation regarding the exchange's downfall.
