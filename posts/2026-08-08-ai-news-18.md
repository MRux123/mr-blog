## WORLD

* **Drone Explosion in Bulgaria:** Tensions in the Balkan region have escalated following the explosion of an unmanned aerial vehicle near the Trans-Balkan Pipeline. Bulgarian authorities have identified the drone as being of Ukrainian origin, noting it entered Bulgarian airspace from Romania. While there were no reported casualties, the incident poses significant diplomatic and security questions regarding the integrity of regional energy infrastructure.
* **Biden Health Crisis:** Hunter Biden has confirmed a significant deterioration in the health of his father, former U.S. President Joe Biden. Addressing the public, Hunter Biden disclosed that the former president’s prostate cancer has metastasized, causing the patient severe pain and signaling a critical stage in his ongoing medical battle.

## POLAND

* **Progress on Volhynia Exhumations:** In a significant move toward historical reconciliation, the Ukrainian government has officially authorized further exhumations of Polish victims of the Volhynia massacres. The decision, which covers sites in Huta Pieniacka and Ugły, follows recent assurances provided by President Volodymyr Zelensky, marking a tangible step in addressing historical grievances between the two nations.
* **Public Health Alert in Warsaw:** Warsaw health authorities have launched an epidemiological investigation following the detection of a tuberculosis cluster. The infection was identified in a local educational facility, affecting both a staff member and a child, with additional latent cases currently under medical observation to prevent further spread within the community.
