## WORLD

* **U.S.-Iran Conflict Escalates:** The Middle East remains on edge as U.S. forces carried out their third consecutive night of airstrikes against Iran, targeting key cities including Bushehr and Chabahar. The conflict is being further complicated by President Trump’s announcement of a 20 percent fee on cargo transiting the Strait of Hormuz, a move that prompted retaliatory Iranian attacks on two oil tankers.
* **Legal Blow to Donald Trump:** A U.S. judge has voided a $1.8 billion tax settlement involving Donald Trump and the IRS. The court ruled that the agreement, which previously shielded the former president from tax audits, was obtained for "improper purposes," potentially exposing his financial records to renewed scrutiny.
* **Tragedy in Bangkok:** The death toll following a devastating bar fire in Bangkok has risen to 30. Initial reports and survivor accounts indicate severe safety lapses, specifically citing locked emergency doors and a lack of proper signage, leading to an ongoing investigation into criminal negligence.

## POLAND

* **Reflecting on the Nice Attack Anniversary:** Poland, along with the international community, marks the tragic decade since the terrorist attack in Nice, which claimed 86 lives. The event serves as a somber reminder of the persistent global threat of terrorism.
* **National Security Operations:** Polish authorities, specifically the Central Investigation Bureau of Police (CBŚP), have successfully conducted a major operation recovering luxury vehicles that had been stolen in Germany, demonstrating continued cross-border law enforcement cooperation.
* **Sports Diplomacy and Competition:** As the football semifinals approach, Polish representation remains active on the international stage with referee Tomasz Kwiatkowski appointed to a semifinal match. Meanwhile, local sports media are closely monitoring the health status of Kylian Mbappé ahead of the critical high-stakes matches.
