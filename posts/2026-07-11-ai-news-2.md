## WORLD

*   **US-Iran Negotiations in Oman:** The United States is initiating high-level diplomatic talks in Oman, with Vice-President JD Vance expected to lead negotiations. The primary objective is to secure a binding commitment from Iran to cease hostile actions against commercial and military vessels in the Strait of Hormuz, a critical maritime chokepoint for global oil supplies.
*   **The Rise of Sovereignty-Driven Crypto Billionaires:** A growing group of wealthy cryptocurrency entrepreneurs is openly questioning the efficacy of traditional democratic institutions. These figures are reportedly investing their capital into the development of autonomous, private jurisdictions, effectively attempting to replace conventional state structures with systems where voting power is tied to financial influence.
*   **Political Reversal for the French Far-Right:** Jordan Bardella, long touted as the natural successor to Marine Le Pen, has been forced to step back from the political limelight. Following recent internal shifts and broader electoral constraints, the 30-year-old leader is entering a period of political hibernation, signaling a potential cooling of momentum for the hard-right movement in France.
