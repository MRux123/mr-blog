## WORLD

* **Israel Strikes Air Base in Syria:** Tensions in the Middle East have intensified following an Israeli airstrike on a Syrian air base. The operation, which has drawn criticism from U.S. officials, marks a significant escalation in Israel's long-standing campaign of military interventions within Syrian territory.
* **Zimbabwe Ferry Disaster:** Recovery operations are ongoing at Lake Kariba, where a ferry accident has resulted in at least 93 confirmed deaths. The vessel, which capsized during severe weather, is now officially recorded as the country's deadliest transport catastrophe, with reports confirming that many of the victims were children.
* **Drone Incident at Zaporizhzhia Nuclear Power Plant:** A deadly drone strike near the Russian-controlled Zaporizhzhia nuclear facility has left at least one person dead and 17 injured. The incident, involving a shuttle bus stop for plant personnel, has prompted urgent calls from the IAEA to cease all hostilities in the vicinity of the site to avoid a potential nuclear disaster.

## POLAND

* **Political Fallout over Romanowski Case:** A heated debate has emerged within the Polish political landscape regarding the whereabouts of Marcin Romanowski. Following Prime Minister Donald Tusk’s suggestion that Romanowski may be residing in Transnistria, opposition figures have dismissed the claims, labeling them as unsubstantiated political rhetoric, while others warn that such an escape would constitute a serious breach of national security.
* **Environmental Threat in the Alps:** A major geological hazard is threatening the Swiss village of Kandersteg, where a massive, unstable rock formation known as Spitze Stei risks collapsing into the valley. Experts link the increasing volatility of the mountain range to the accelerated melting of glaciers and permafrost, highlighting a growing climate-related challenge for the region.
