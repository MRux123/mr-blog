## WORLD

* **Political Earthquake in Wales:** The Labour Party’s long-standing dominance in the Welsh Parliament has come to an end. Rhun ap Iorwerth, a former journalist turned politician, successfully led his party to secure the most seats, signaling a significant shift in the regional political landscape and a notable defeat for Labour and Reform U.K.
* **Escalating Paranoia in the Kremlin:** Reports from *Forbes* indicate that Vladimir Putin’s behavior has become increasingly erratic due to the surge in Ukrainian drone and missile strikes on Russian soil. Intelligence and political analysts suggest the Russian president is increasingly retreating into secure, underground bunkers, purportedly driven by both a fear of external military threats and a growing distrust of his own citizenry.
* **Press Freedom Crisis in Somalia:** The detention and physical assault of a *Guardian* journalist, Mohamed Bulbul, along with two colleagues in Mogadishu, has sparked international concern. The incident, which occurred after the journalists reported on human rights abuses, highlights the ongoing dangers faced by the press in Somalia, specifically involving US-trained security units.

## POLAND

* **Leadership Change in Hungary:** In a major development with regional implications for Central Europe, the Hungarian Parliament has officially elected Peter Magyar as the new Prime Minister.
* **Andrzej Poczobut’s Return Prospects:** Following his recent release from a Belarusian prison, Polish activist Andrzej Poczobut has expressed his intention to return to Grodno. Poczobut emphasized that his homecoming is contingent upon medical clearance, underlining his commitment to the Polish community still residing in Belarus.
* **Resolution of Missing Teen Case:** A multi-national search concluded successfully as the 14-year-old Dutch boy who embarked on a solo cycling trip toward Poland was located by authorities in Białystok. The teenager was discovered in good health near the city, bringing a relief-filled end to the cross-border operation.
