## WORLD

* **Escalation of Long-Range Strikes in Russia:** Ukraine has launched an unprecedented, large-scale drone offensive against Russian infrastructure, targeting positions in Crimea and the Moscow region. Ukrainian authorities have framed these operations as "long-range sanctions," reflecting a tactical shift in the conflict. While Moscow attempts to downplay the impact of the strikes, reports of significant damage continue to surface.
* **WHO Declares Ebola Emergency in DR Congo:** The World Health Organization has officially declared the current Ebola outbreak in the Democratic Republic of Congo an international public health emergency. With nearly 250 suspected cases and 80 fatalities, the organization is ramping up containment efforts, though it maintains that the situation does not yet meet the criteria for a full-scale pandemic.

## POLAND

* **Investigation into False Alarm Hoax:** Polish authorities have dropped charges against a 53-year-old man initially detained for bomb threats, identifying him as a victim of identity theft and illegal access to his email account. The case has gained national attention due to the suspect's connection to a police intervention at the residence of Tomasz Sakiewicz, editor-in-chief of Telewizja Republika, raising questions about potential political motivations or cybersecurity breaches in the ongoing investigation.
* **Handball Success:** The Polish national handball team has secured a place in next year’s World Championships in Germany after a tense 30-30 draw against Austria in Olsztyn. Building on their narrow 26-25 victory in the first leg in Graz, the Polish squad successfully defended their aggregate lead, marking a significant achievement for the national sports program.
