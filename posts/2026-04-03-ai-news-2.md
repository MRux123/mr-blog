## WORLD

* **Escalation of Iran Conflict:** Tensions in the Middle East have reached a critical juncture as military strikes hit essential infrastructure in Iran. Following aggressive rhetoric from President Trump, who vowed to dismantle the country’s infrastructure, the destruction of a major highway bridge near Tehran and key public health facilities marks a severe expansion of the conflict.
* **Global Economic Fallout:** The ongoing military campaign in Iran is already triggering supply chain disruptions. Markets are reporting shortages of raw materials for plastic and glass manufacturing, with analysts warning that the cost of consumer staples—including bottled water and beverages—is expected to rise significantly for global consumers.
* **Leadership Purge in the Pentagon:** US Secretary of Defense Pete Hegseth has formally demanded the immediate resignation and retirement of the Army Chief of Staff, Gen. Randy George. The move, confirmed by the Pentagon, signals a potential significant restructuring of the US military leadership under the current administration.

## POLAND

* **Kłodzko Scandal Response:** Deputy Speaker of the Sejm, Monika Wielichowska, has issued a formal statement distancing herself from a corruption scandal involving former PO activist Kamila L. Wielichowska categorically denied any professional or personal ties to the individual currently convicted in the Kłodzko case, seeking to mitigate political fallout within the party.
