## WORLD

* **Direct US-Iran Communication Established:** In a significant shift amid the ongoing regional conflict sparked by the February 28th strikes, the United States and Iran have re-established a direct line of communication. Envoy Steve Witkoff and Iranian Foreign Minister Abbas Araghchi have initiated contact, marking the first known diplomatic exchange since the death of Supreme Leader Ali Khamenei.
* **Trump Postpones Beijing Visit:** Amidst the escalating volatility in the Middle East, Donald Trump has indicated that his scheduled state visit to Beijing, originally planned for early April, may be delayed by up to a month. The adjustment underscores the impact of the current geopolitical instability on broader international diplomatic calendars.

## POLAND

* **Diplomatic Spat Over Druzhba Pipeline:** Tensions between Warsaw and Budapest have flared following public remarks by Polish Foreign Minister Radosław Sikorski. Hungarian Foreign Minister Peter Szijjártó has strongly denied claims that Hungary admitted the Druzhba oil pipeline passing through Ukraine requires urgent repairs, characterizing the Polish minister's statement as a misrepresentation of facts.
