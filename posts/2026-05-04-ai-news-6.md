## WORLD

* **Escalation in Ukraine-Russia Conflict:** Ukrainian forces have successfully executed a drone strike on Moscow, with the impact occurring only six kilometers from the Kremlin. This bold operation signals a significant tactical development in the ongoing conflict, challenging Russian air defenses near the seat of power.
* **Mexican Political Crisis:** The indictment of a Mexican governor has placed President Claudia Sheinbaum in a precarious position, intensifying scrutiny over systemic corruption and straining diplomatic relations with the United States. The case serves as a critical test for the current administration’s commitment to border security and cross-border cooperation.
* **AI Inequality in Kenya:** An investigation reveals that Kenya’s new AI-driven healthcare system, intended to expand coverage, is inadvertently creating a financial barrier for the poor. By prioritizing cost-efficiency models that favor wealthier demographics, the system is failing its primary electoral promise of universal access.

## POLAND

* **Strategic Defense Financing:** The Polish government is set to finalize the SAFE loan agreement this Friday, securing nearly €44 billion for military modernization. The deal, which includes an immediate €6.5 billion advance, marks a pivotal step in Poland's efforts to bolster its national security infrastructure.
* **Constitutional Reform Proposals:** The Presidential office has signaled a move toward a "Next Generation Constitution 2030," sparking a national debate on the necessity of rewriting Poland’s foundational legal framework. Presidential aide Marcin Mastalerek has begun public discussions on the potential scope and implications of this project.
* **Passing of Archbishop Józef Michalik:** The death of Archbishop Józef Michalik, a central figure in the Polish Catholic Church who led the Episcopal Conference for a decade, marks the end of an era for one of the most influential institutions in Polish public life.
