## WORLD

* **Naval Blockade of Iran:** The geopolitical situation in the Strait of Hormuz has reached a critical juncture. President Trump has announced the reinstatement of a naval blockade on Iranian ports, supplemented by a 20% toll on all cargo transiting through the strategic strait. This move marks a significant escalation in Washington's pressure campaign against Tehran.
* **Resignation of Ukrainian Prime Minister:** In a major administrative shake-up, Prime Minister Yulia Svyrydenko has resigned from her post. President Zelensky has indicated that she will be reassigned to a new, vital role focused on strengthening collaboration with one of Ukraine's key international partners, signaling a strategic shift in the country's diplomatic and economic priorities.

## POLAND

* **Judicial Controversy over Wrongful Conviction:** Public attention has turned to the case of Wojciech Pyłka, who has spent nearly two decades behind bars for a murder he maintains he did not commit. New evidence, including a recording brought to light by investigative journalists, has cast significant doubt on the original court verdict, raising questions about the integrity of the judicial process.
* **Accountability for the Tragedies:** The legal system is currently grappling with high-profile accountability cases. Łukasz Żak has issued a formal apology in court regarding the fatal accident on the Trasa Łazienkowska, while defense counsel argues for a distinction between legal culpability and social outrage. Simultaneously, political discourse remains focused on the requirements for Ukraine's future EU membership, with Polish officials emphasizing the necessity of robust anti-corruption reforms.
