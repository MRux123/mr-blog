## WORLD

* **Geopolitical Rivalry at the BRICS Summit:** Leaders from major emerging powers have gathered in New Delhi, where China and India are maneuvering for regional influence. While the summit aims to solidify the bloc's role in challenging Western economic dominance, internal divisions regarding the wars and rising energy costs continue to threaten the group's long-term unity.
* **Escalation in Ukraine:** The security situation in Ukraine remains critical as Russian forces intensify strikes on civilian infrastructure, including a recent attack on a shopping center in Pavlohrad. Meanwhile, cities like Izium are bracing for renewed offensives, highlighting the persistent threat of further territorial loss and humanitarian catastrophe.
* **AI Security Breakthrough:** Anthropic has reported the successful blocking of an attempt to leverage artificial intelligence for the development of biological weapons. This incident underscores the urgent need for stringent global safety protocols in AI development as the technology continues to evolve faster than current regulatory frameworks.

## POLAND

* **Political Confrontation at the Academy of Justice:** Tensions have flared at the Academy of Justice, where opposition leader Jarosław Kaczyński conducted a parliamentary inspection amid protests over changes to the university's governing body. The incident marks a significant escalation in the ongoing friction between the PiS party and the current government.
* **Defense Modernization:** The Polish Ministry of National Defence has signed a landmark contract worth approximately 3 billion PLN for the procurement of 'Piorun' man-portable air-defense systems. The deal, part of a larger 8 billion PLN framework, aims to bolster Poland's military capabilities between 2026 and 2030 in response to the deteriorating security situation in the region.
* **Probes into PKOl Financial Irregularities:** The Polish Olympic Committee (PKOl) is facing intense scrutiny following allegations of financial malpractice. The Gdańsk prosecutor's office has launched an investigation into suspicious loans involving private entities, including those linked to the spouse of PKOl president Radosław Piesiewicz, casting a shadow over the organization’s management.
