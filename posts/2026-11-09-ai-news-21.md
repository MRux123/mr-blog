## WORLD

*   **Cyber Warfare Escalation:** France has officially accused Iran of orchestrating a large-scale cyberattack on its supermarket supply chain. The incident, which resulted in the spoilage of tons of food due to the remote manipulation of commercial refrigeration systems, marks a significant escalation in state-sponsored digital aggression.
*   **Nvidia Shares Plummet:** The technology sector faces volatility as Nvidia’s stock price has crashed following reports of an investigation into CEO Jensen Huang regarding an alleged affair with a Chinese intelligence asset. The scandal has wiped hundreds of billions from the company's market valuation, raising concerns over corporate governance and national security implications.
*   **Political Instability in the U.S.:** The Biden administration and congressional Republicans remain locked in a heated domestic struggle. Tensions have intensified as Representative Thomas Massie initiates a push for an impeachment vote against Defense Secretary Hegseth, while former President Joe Biden has launched sharp public critiques against Donald Trump regarding the integrity of past and future electoral processes.

## POLAND

*   **Kraków Shooting Fallout:** The aftermath of the mass shooting in Kraków has paralyzed national discourse, evolving into a bitter political battle. While the government attributes the tragedy to radicalized rhetoric from the far-right, the opposition claims that the state’s security apparatus is fundamentally flawed and failing to protect its citizens.
*   **Heightened Regional Security:** Poland has raised its alert level in the Podlaskie and Lubelskie regions following renewed Russian aerial attacks on Ukraine. While an initial Alert RCB warning was briefly issued and subsequently rescinded, the government remains on high alert regarding potential border spillover from the conflict in the East.
*   **Combating Information Warfare:** Government officials, including Deputy Prime Minister Krzysztof Gawkowski, have formally acknowledged a surge in disinformation campaigns following the Kraków attack. Authorities are currently engaged in a massive effort to neutralize hostile state-backed narratives that seek to destabilize domestic social cohesion.
