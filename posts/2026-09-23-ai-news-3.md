## WORLD

*   **Trump’s UN Ultimatum to Iran:** In a high-stakes address at the United Nations, President Trump signaled a shift toward potential confrontation, warning that he is weighing the possibility of a peace deal against a directive to "annihilate" Iran. The rhetoric has sent shockwaves through the General Assembly, with global leaders expressing deep concern over the escalating division and the abandonment of diplomatic norms.
*   **Human Rights Crisis in Equatorial Guinea:** Legal experts and human rights organizations have raised alarms following the mistreatment of deportees expelled from the United States. Witnesses report that individuals were subjected to physical violence and detention in sub-human conditions in Malabo, allegedly as retaliation for speaking out about their treatment to the press.
*   **Tragedy in Nigerian Prison:** A humanitarian crisis has unfolded in Minna, Nigeria, where 37 individuals, many of them children, died while in the custody of a paramilitary group. The victims were suspected illegal goldminers, and their deaths have sparked widespread public unrest, leading to police intervention and the imposition of a curfew in the region.

## POLAND

*   **The Future of 'Fort Trump':** Political discourse in Poland remains focused on the potential establishment of a permanent US military presence, colloquially known as "Fort Trump." Official sources indicate that project discussions are ongoing and that realization is viewed as a "matter of time," highlighting the strategic importance of Polish-American defense cooperation.
