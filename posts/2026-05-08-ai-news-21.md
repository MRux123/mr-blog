## WORLD

* **Trump-Mediated Ukraine Ceasefire:** Donald Trump has announced a three-day ceasefire between Russia and Ukraine, effective from May 9th to May 11th. The agreement includes a significant prisoner exchange of one thousand personnel on each side. The move has been acknowledged by both President Volodymyr Zelensky and Kremlin aide Yury Ushakov, marking a rare moment of diplomatic movement in the prolonged conflict.
* **Escalating Tensions in the Strait of Hormuz:** The geopolitical situation in the Middle East has intensified following reports of naval skirmishes between U.S. forces and Iranian tankers. Iranian officials have condemned the U.S. actions as "reckless military adventures," while Washington maintains pressure on Tehran, citing concerns over regional stability and diplomatic obstruction.
* **Political Turmoil in the UK:** The Labour Party, led by Prime Minister Keir Starmer, has suffered severe losses in local elections, losing over a thousand municipal seats. Despite calls for a change in direction, Starmer has confirmed he will not resign, taking full responsibility for the electoral defeat.

## POLAND

* **Prime Minister Fico's Controversial Moscow Visit:** Slovak Prime Minister Robert Fico has sparked diplomatic friction within the European Union by attending Moscow’s Victory Day commemorations. Fico, who openly identified himself as the "black sheep" of the EU, engaged in symbolic gestures at the Tomb of the Unknown Soldier, further distancing his administration from the mainstream Western stance on Russia.
* **Environmental Relief in Puszcza Solska:** Polish authorities have successfully contained the massive wildfire in Puszcza Solska. While firefighting efforts are transitioning into a monitoring and final suppression phase, officials expressed relief that the threat to the forest ecosystem has been neutralized.
* **Presidential Advisory Shakeup:** The President's Council on Energy and Natural Resources has faced a resignation from expert Andrzej Guła. The departure stems from disagreements over President Karol Nawrocki’s proposal to hold a national referendum regarding the European Union’s "Green Deal" policy, which Guła labeled as a potentially detrimental shift in economic strategy.
