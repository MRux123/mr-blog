## WORLD

* **Targeted Killing in the UK:** Counterterrorism authorities have confirmed that the death of Ann Widdecombe was the result of a targeted attack. A 28-year-old suspect is currently in custody, and the investigation remains a priority for British security forces.
* **Escalating Tensions in Gaza:** An Israeli military strike on a police post in northern Gaza has resulted in seven fatalities, including a senior official within the Hamas-run police force. The IDF maintains that the operation was specifically directed at militants.
* **Economic Downturn in the Mining Sector:** De Beers has announced a two-year production halt at its flagship South African diamond mine. The decision, affecting over 4,000 employees, underscores a sharp decline in global demand for diamonds.

## POLAND

* **Political Rhetoric Escalates:** Prime Minister Donald Tusk has publicly questioned remarks regarding Przemysław Czarnek, specifically addressing claims that the former minister could be viewed as a "dream prime minister for Russia," highlighting the current volatility in domestic political discourse.
* **Legal Action Against Hate Crime:** Authorities are pursuing a three-year prison sentence for an individual accused of assaulting young Ukrainian women on a public bus. The case has sparked a wider conversation regarding public safety and the legal consequences for hate-motivated violence.
