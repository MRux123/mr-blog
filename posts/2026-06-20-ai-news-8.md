## WORLD

* **Escalating Diplomatic Friction in Paris:** U.S. Ambassador to France, Charles Kushner, is facing intense backlash from the French political establishment. His assertive brand of "Trumpian" diplomacy, combined with controversial rhetoric regarding antisemitism, has severely strained U.S.-French relations and prompted significant criticism within Paris.
* **Train Collision in the UK:** A serious rail incident near Bedford, England, has resulted in at least one fatality and multiple injuries. Authorities are currently investigating the circumstances surrounding the collision as emergency services manage the aftermath of the tragedy.
* **Diplomatic Fallout Between Italy and the US:** Tensions are mounting between Washington and Rome after President Trump's disparaging remarks regarding Italian Prime Minister Giorgia Meloni. Italian media outlets have strongly condemned the comments, labeling the U.S. President’s behavior as aggressive and disrespectful toward a key European ally.

## POLAND

* **Crisis in Polish-Ukrainian Relations:** A significant diplomatic rift has emerged following the decision by Polish President Karol Nawrocki to revoke the Order of the White Eagle previously awarded to President Volodymyr Zelensky. The move has triggered a wave of protest from Ukrainian officials, including the Ambassador to Poland and the head of the Ukrainian presidential cabinet, who are systematically returning their Polish state honors in protest of what they characterize as an "unfriendly act."
* **Russian Propaganda Exploits Internal Dispute:** Moscow has moved to capitalize on the internal friction between Warsaw and Kyiv. Dmitry Medvedev, Deputy Chairman of the Security Council of the Russian Federation, issued a statement praising the decision to revoke Zelensky’s order, utilizing the event to propagate narratives concerning the conflict in Ukraine.
* **Military Activity in the Northeast:** The 16th Mechanized Division has announced heightened military activity in the Warmian-Masurian Voivodeship. Citizens are advised to expect increased movement of armored columns, drones, and helicopters over the coming days, with authorities urging the public to maintain security protocols and avoid documenting the military deployments.
