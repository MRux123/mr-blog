## WORLD

* **U.S. Military Realignment in Germany:** The United States has confirmed the withdrawal of 5,000 troops from Germany. While the Pentagon maintains a significant presence, the decision—described by critics as a "gift to Putin"—has ignited a geopolitical firestorm. European analysts warn that the move risks undermining NATO’s operational capacity and sends a precarious signal regarding trans-Atlantic security commitments.
* **Environmental Emergency in Czechia:** Authorities have declared a state of emergency in the Czech Switzerland National Park as wildfires consume approximately 900 hectares of forest. The operation to contain the blaze is extensive, involving dozens of fire brigades and critical support from military helicopters.
* **Maritime Security Concerns:** An oil tanker has been hijacked off the coast of Yemen and is currently being diverted toward Somalia. This incident marks the second hijacking in the region in just over a week, raising alarms regarding the security of vital maritime trade routes.

## POLAND

* **Andrzej Poczobut to Receive Order of the White Eagle:** Andrzej Poczobut, the journalist and activist recently released in a prisoner exchange, is scheduled to receive the Order of the White Eagle from President Karol Nawrocki this Sunday. The award recognizes his long-standing resistance against the Lukashenko regime, following his imprisonment since 2021.
* **Górnik Zabrze Claims Polish Cup:** Ending a 54-year drought, Górnik Zabrze has won the Polish Cup after a decisive 2-0 victory against Raków Częstochowa at PGE Narodowy. The win marks the club’s seventh title in the tournament's history.
* **Major Railway Disruptions:** Travelers in Western Poland are facing significant delays following a major infrastructure failure. A damaged overhead line has brought train traffic to a standstill on the route between Poznań and Germany, causing severe logistical issues for international rail connections.
