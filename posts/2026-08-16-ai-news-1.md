## WORLD

* **Tragic Ferry Disaster in Zimbabwe:** The death toll following the capsizing of a ferry on Lake Kariba has risen to 68, with the recovery of 22 additional bodies. The disaster, which included 18 children among the victims, is being attributed to overcrowding combined with severe weather conditions. Authorities are continuing their investigation into the regulatory failures that led to this tragedy.

* **Indonesia Earthquake Devastation:** Rescue operations are intensifying in Indonesia following a powerful earthquake that has leveled hundreds of buildings. Current official reports confirm at least 47 fatalities, though emergency teams are still conducting rapid impact assessments to determine the full extent of the destruction and the number of survivors in need of immediate assistance.

## POLAND

* **Vulnerabilities in Polish Air Defense:** General Michał Marciniak has raised significant concerns regarding national security, stating that major Polish cities currently lack comprehensive anti-missile protection. The comments follow the recent incident involving a Russian missile landing in Tarnawa-Kolonia, which experts suggest may have been a tactical test of Poland's defensive response systems. The military has confirmed that the nation is still in the process of developing a robust, multi-layered air defense infrastructure.
