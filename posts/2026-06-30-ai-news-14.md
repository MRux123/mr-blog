## WORLD

* **Escalating Security Concerns in Monaco:** A violent incident involving a parcel bomb has left three individuals, including a child, injured in Monaco. Authorities have launched an intensive manhunt for the perpetrator, marking a rare and significant security breach in the principality.
* **Geopolitical Tensions in the Middle East:** Following a recent conflict and heightened regional instability, U.S. and Iranian officials are converging in Qatar for high-level mediation talks. Simultaneously, reports indicate that Oman is proposing a new financial framework for transit fees through the Strait of Hormuz as part of efforts to restore regional stability and trade predictability.
* **Migration Tensions in South Africa:** Authorities have mobilized security forces across the country in anticipation of large-scale anti-migrant protests. The government is attempting to prevent a recurrence of the violent outbreaks witnessed in 2008, as pressure mounts regarding the presence of undocumented foreign nationals.

## POLAND

* **Digital Transformation in Education:** The Ministry of Digital Affairs has introduced a new framework allowing universities to issue official digital diplomas, accessible via the mObywatel application. These digital credentials hold the same legal standing as traditional paper documents, marking a significant step in the modernization of state administrative processes.
* **Border Security and Human Trafficking:** Law enforcement agencies in the Warmian-Masurian region have apprehended two Ukrainian citizens involved in the smuggling of illegal migrants. The incident, involving the concealment of individuals in vehicle trunks, highlights ongoing efforts by Polish authorities to disrupt human trafficking networks operating along its borders.
* **Commemoration of Warsaw Uprising Heroine:** Poland mourns the death of Irena Paśnik, known by her nom de guerre "Irka." As a dedicated nurse and liaison for the "Baszta" regiment during the Warsaw Uprising, her passing marks the loss of a significant historical witness, with state officials offering official tributes to her service.
