## WORLD

*   **Deadly Wildfire Devastates Southern Spain:** A catastrophic blaze in Andalusia has claimed the lives of at least 12 people, with over 20 still reported missing. Spanish authorities have confirmed that many of the casualties were international tourists, making this one of the deadliest fire events in the region's recent history.
*   **East Asia Prepares for Typhoon Bavi:** A massive 1,000 km-wide storm is currently barreling toward Taiwan and south-eastern China. Forecasted to be one of the strongest storms in decades, the typhoon has already triggered deadly landslides in the Philippines, resulting in at least 15 fatalities.
*   **Kremlin’s Campaign Against Moldova:** Security services in Moldova have raised alarms regarding an intensified Russian propaganda campaign. Moscow is reportedly targeting the nation's youth on social media to influence upcoming elections and destabilize the country in favor of pro-Russian political factions.

## POLAND

*   **Violent Attack on Police Officers in Warsaw:** A serious assault occurred in Warsaw after an individual armed with a machete and brass knuckles attacked a police officer heading to work. The perpetrator was apprehended at the Kabaty metro station. The incident left four officers injured, one of whom required hospitalization.
*   **Legal Challenges for Jarosław Kaczyński:** A formal motion has been filed to penalize Jarosław Kaczyński following his actions during the recent Smoleńsk monthly anniversary commemoration, which have been described as a "disgraceful attack." 
*   **Tax Authority Inspections Spark Confusion:** Polish citizens have reported surprise at new, unannounced door-to-door activities conducted by tax authorities requesting receipts, signaling a shift in fiscal control measures currently being observed across the country.
