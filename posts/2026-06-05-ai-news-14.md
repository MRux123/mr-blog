## WORLD

*   **Geopolitical Standoff in Eastern Europe and the Middle East:** Tensions remain high as Ukraine reports tactical strikes against Russian shipping in the Sea of Azov, while Russia prepares for a significant address from President Putin regarding a letter sent by President Zelensky. Simultaneously, ceasefire hopes in Lebanon have stalled, as Hezbollah rejects Western-brokered conditions, framing them as a surrender.
*   **Shadow Operations in Azerbaijan:** Recent intelligence reports suggest that Israeli special forces conducted clandestine operations from Azerbaijani territory during the conflict with Iran. Reports indicate these units successfully targeted high-ranking Iranian IRGC intelligence officials, marking a significant escalation in covert military activities between the two nations.
*   **ISS Safety Concerns:** NASA has reported a technical emergency on the International Space Station due to an air leak in the Russian "Zvezda" service module. The crew has been instructed to prepare for potential evacuation as Russian cosmonauts attempt to mitigate the failure.

## POLAND

*   **Diplomatic Friction with Ukraine:** A high-level visit by the head of the Ukrainian Presidential Office to Poland has been overshadowed by ongoing historical disputes. Key discussions are expected to touch upon controversial Ukrainian initiatives, including the naming of military units after figures associated with the UPA, which remains a sensitive topic in Polish-Ukrainian relations.
*   **Escalation in Online Harassment Investigations:** The Polish Prosecutor's Office has launched a major investigation into systematic online hate speech targeting the seven-year-old daughter of Karol Nawrocki. Prosecutors are analyzing approximately 30,000 entries and have requested international legal assistance from the United States and China to identify the perpetrators.
*   **Tragic Incident in Włocławek:** A five-year-old girl is in critical condition after falling from an 11th-floor window in Włocławek. Local authorities have confirmed that the 21-year-old mother was under the influence of alcohol at the time of the incident, sparking a broader national discussion regarding child safety and parental responsibility.
