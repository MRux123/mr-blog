## WORLD

* **US-Iran Diplomatic Engagement Amid Strait of Hormuz Crisis:** High-stakes negotiations have commenced in Switzerland between US officials—led by Vice President JD Vance—and Iranian representatives. The talks follow Tehran's contested claim that the Strait of Hormuz has been closed in retaliation for Israeli military actions in Lebanon. Public sentiment in Israel appears heavily pessimistic, with recent polls indicating that 92% of Israelis perceive Iran as the victor of the recent conflict.
* **Fuel Shortages Hit Occupied Crimea:** As Ukraine intensifies its strategic strikes against Russian supply lines, authorities in occupied Crimea have been forced to halt fuel sales. The move highlights the growing impact of Kyiv’s targeting of logistics infrastructure in annexed territories.

## POLAND

* **Strained Alliance Between Warsaw and Kyiv:** Relations between Poland and Ukraine are facing a significant diplomatic hurdle following President Volodymyr Zelensky's decree honoring World War II-era partisan fighters—a move perceived as provocative by Warsaw. Prime Minister Donald Tusk has publicly urged restraint, warning that the political infighting serves as a strategic error that threatens the bilateral relationship on both business and geopolitical fronts.
* **Industrial Emergency in Pomerania:** Emergency services are currently battling a massive fire at an aerosol factory in Charnowo. Local authorities have implemented an evacuation zone within a 200-meter radius and issued urgent air quality warnings, advising residents to seal windows and doors due to the toxic smoke plume.
