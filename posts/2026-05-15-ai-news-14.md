## WORLD

* **Trump-Xi Summit Concludes Without Tangible Trade Breakthroughs:** Following a high-profile two-day visit to Beijing, President Donald Trump and Chinese leader Xi Jinping emphasized a desire for regional stability. However, the talks yielded no concrete agreements on trade, nor did they resolve underlying tensions regarding Taiwan or the ongoing conflict in Iran.
* **Ebola Outbreak Claims 65 Lives in DRC:** An alarming Ebola surge in the eastern Democratic Republic of the Congo has resulted in at least 65 deaths. Health officials are investigating the potential emergence of a new viral strain, as they struggle to contain the outbreak in Ituri province, which borders Uganda and South Sudan.

## POLAND

* **Strategic Shift in Migration Pressure:** Polish authorities report a tactical shift in irregular migration patterns, with the primary pressure moving from the Polish-Belarusian border to the border with Lithuania. Prime Minister Donald Tusk has reaffirmed the government's commitment to securing the frontier, emphasizing the need for effective protection to avoid humanitarian escalation.
* **National Defense and Security Upgrades:** Deputy Prime Minister and Minister of National Defense Władysław Kosiniak-Kamysz has announced an expansion of the non-military defense preparation program. The initiative is designed to provide increased financial support for the Border Guard, Police, and infrastructure sectors, reflecting heightened concern over regional security.
* **Political Volatility in the Sejm:** In a surprise parliamentary development, non-attached MP Janusz Kowalski publicly criticized former political allies in PiS. The spectacle drew unexpected attention, punctuated by applause from Prime Minister Donald Tusk, signaling ongoing fractures within the opposition's legislative front.
