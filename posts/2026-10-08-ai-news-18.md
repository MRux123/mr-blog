## WORLD

* **Escalation in Ukraine:** Russian forces have intensified their military campaign, with a significant strike on civilian transportation in Kramatorsk resulting in at least 30 deaths. The attack underscores a grim shift in the conflict’s trajectory, further straining international diplomatic efforts.
* **Regional Conflict Risk in the Horn of Africa:** Ethiopia has initiated drone strikes against Eritrean forces following an unauthorized incursion into the Tigray region. This development poses a severe risk of sparking a broader regional war, exacerbating the already fragile security environment in East Africa.
* **Diplomatic Controversy in the Middle East:** Serious allegations have emerged regarding the suppression of reports critical of the Netanyahu government by a top U.S. diplomat. Reports suggest that key information was deliberately skewed to present a more favorable narrative, raising questions about the integrity of diplomatic communications in the region.

## POLAND

* ** heightened Security Alert:** The Polish military has taken immediate defensive measures, scrambling fighter jets following heavy Russian strikes on targets in western Ukraine. The Government Centre for Security (RCB) issued emergency alerts to residents in the Podkarpackie and Lubelskie regions, highlighting the immediate proximity of the conflict to Polish borders.
* **Internal Security and School Safety:** Prime Minister Donald Tusk has called for urgent systemic reforms following a series of violent incidents involving knife attacks in schools. The Prime Minister emphasized that such violence, often rooted in online extremism, poses an existential threat to the safety of Polish youth. The issue has since become a focal point of intense political debate between the government and the Presidential Palace.
