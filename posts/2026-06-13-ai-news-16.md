## WORLD

*   **Death of Iranian Supreme Leader:** Following the death of Ayatollah Ali Khamenei at age 86 amid recent military escalations, his son, Mojtaba Khamenei, has assumed the position of Supreme Leader. The funeral proceedings are scheduled to commence on July 4 in Tehran, coinciding with American Independence Day, marking a sensitive moment in ongoing international tensions.
*   **Targeted Strike in Venezuela:** US President Donald Trump announced that American forces have killed Niño Guerrero, the leader of the Venezuelan criminal organization Tren de Aragua. The administration described the operation as a "swift and lethal kinetic strike," signaling a hardening stance against transnational criminal syndicates.

## POLAND

*   **Zelensky’s Expected Visit to Gdańsk:** Diplomatic circles confirm that Ukrainian President Volodymyr Zelensky is expected to attend the Ukraine Recovery Conference scheduled for June 25–26 in Gdańsk. The visit comes at a time when Ukrainian officials have signaled a desire to mend strained historical and political relations with Warsaw.
*   **Controversy Over Biological Laboratory Allegations:** The Ukrainian Ministry of Foreign Affairs has formally denied claims by US National Intelligence Director Tulsi Gabbard regarding the existence of US-funded biological weapons programs in Ukraine. Kyiv maintains that it has never engaged in the development or production of biological weaponry, refuting the accusations circulating in international discourse.
