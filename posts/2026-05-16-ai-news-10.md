## WORLD

*   **US-Led Operation Targets IS Leadership:** American and Nigerian forces have conducted a joint operation resulting in the death of Abu-Bilal al-Minuki, a senior Islamic State leader described by President Trump as the world's most active terrorist. The mission highlights ongoing international efforts to dismantle terrorist networks in Africa.
*   **Geopolitical Tension Over Taiwan:** President Trump has explicitly characterized potential arms sales to Taiwan as a "negotiating chip" in broader U.S.-China relations. This transactional approach to military support is raising concerns among foreign policy analysts regarding the long-term reliability of U.S. commitments in the Indo-Pacific.
*   **Military Maneuvers in the Baltic:** Finland is set to host the "Narrow Waters 26-1" naval exercise near the Russian border. The inclusion of German and U.S. forces in these drills underscores the intensified defense posture of NATO members in the Baltic region following recent regional security shifts.

## POLAND

*   **Severe Weather Warnings:** The Institute of Meteorology and Water Management (IMGW) has issued Level 1 alerts for four voivodeships in southern and eastern Poland. The country faces a weekend of volatile weather, including heavy rainfall, strong wind gusts, and potential hail, prompting additional warnings from the Government Security Centre (RCB).
*   **Ukrainian Strike on Russian Industrial Targets:** In a significant cross-border action, Ukrainian forces launched drone strikes against a chemical facility in Ninnomyssk, Russia. The plant, which produces materials critical to both the agricultural and defense sectors, was reported to be on fire following the attack.
*   **Political Kuluars at PiS Meeting:** Law and Justice (PiS) politician Janusz Cieszyński has addressed internal rumors regarding a recent party meeting, denying reports of chaos and internal strife. During his appearance, he emphasized the critical importance of U.S. military presence in Poland for national security.
