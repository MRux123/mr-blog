## WORLD

*   **Tensions Escalate in Middle East:** The Iran-Israel conflict continues to dominate the geopolitical landscape. Despite a fragile cease-fire, reports of Iran seizing two ships near the Strait of Hormuz have injected new uncertainty into the region. While the U.S. administration, led by President Trump, has downplayed these actions as non-violative, international observers note that the standoff is creating significant economic volatility.
*   **Economic Impact of Middle Eastern Instability:** China’s export-driven economy is showing signs of strain as the ongoing Middle East conflict disrupts factory orders and increases operational costs. This development suggests that the global supply chain, already pressured by previous trade tariffs, is becoming increasingly vulnerable to localized geopolitical flare-ups.

## POLAND

*   **Political Maneuvering at the Constitutional Tribunal:** A high-stakes internal confrontation is unfolding at the Constitutional Tribunal, as President Bogdan Święczkowski demands information from newly appointed judges regarding potential legal complaints filed against him. The move hints at a deepening institutional crisis and possible disciplinary actions that could further polarize the judicial landscape.
*   **Strategic Defense Funding:** Prime Minister Donald Tusk is reportedly engaged in critical talks with European Commission President Ursula von der Leyen regarding the SAFE (Supporting Ammunition Production) initiative. With the deadline for individual orders in the domestic defense industry approaching on May 30, the government is seeking an urgent loan agreement to ensure the continuity of local armaments production.
*   **Leadership Change in Sports Governance:** Adam Małysz, the iconic Polish ski jumper, has announced his decision not to seek re-election as president of the Polish Ski Federation. Citing health concerns and irreconcilable differences with certain federation officials, his departure marks a significant transition in the management of Polish winter sports.
