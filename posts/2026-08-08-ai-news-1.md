## WORLD

*   **EU Border Crisis Escalates:** Tensions between Italy and Spain have reached a diplomatic breaking point following a massive surge of 78,000 migrants entering the Spanish exclave of Ceuta via Morocco. Italy implemented temporary border controls, prompting a retaliatory move from Madrid. Despite Spain’s ultimatum demanding the removal of these checks, Rome has extended the suspension of Schengen-related movement, signaling a deepening crisis in European migration management.
*   **US Senate Targets Russia with New Sanctions:** In a decisive move, the US Senate has passed a sweeping package of sanctions against Russia with an overwhelming 86-11 vote. Named after the late Senator Lindsay Graham, the legislation introduces aggressive measures, including new tariffs on nations that continue to purchase Russian natural resources, marking a significant escalation in geopolitical pressure on the Kremlin.

## POLAND

*   **Morawiecki Targets Jagodno as Political Symbol:** Former Prime Minister Mateusz Morawiecki visited the Wrocław district of Jagodno, labeling the area a "symbol of the victory of Tuskism." During his address, Morawiecki blamed the current administration for ongoing stagnation and scandals, positioning the local electorate’s high engagement in the 2023 elections as a catalyst for the loss of power for the Law and Justice (PiS) party.
