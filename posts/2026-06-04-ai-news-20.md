## WORLD

* **Zelenskyy’s Open Letter to Putin:** In a significant diplomatic maneuver, Ukrainian President Volodymyr Zelenskyy has addressed an open letter to Vladimir Putin, formally proposing a framework to end the ongoing war. The Kremlin has acknowledged receipt of the statement, marking a rare moment of direct public engagement between the two leaders amidst the long-standing conflict.
* **Mideast Tensions Persist:** The situation in the Middle East remains volatile as Israel and Hezbollah continue to trade strikes. A U.S.-brokered cease-fire remains in doubt, with Hezbollah leadership rejecting the deal brokered between Israel and the Lebanese government, signaling a potential escalation in hostilities.

## POLAND

* **Tragedy in the High Tatras:** A fatal accident occurred on Rysy, the highest peak in Poland, involving two individuals. One person has died following a high-altitude fall, while another remains in critical condition. The incident, managed by 30 TOPR rescue personnel, highlights the extreme dangers posed by current adverse weather conditions in the Tatra Mountains.
* **Breakthrough in Polish-Ukrainian Exhumations:** In a notable gesture toward improved bilateral relations, the Ukrainian Ministry of Culture has granted permission for the Polish Institute of National Remembrance (IPN) to conduct exhumation works in Ostrówki and Wola Ostrowiecka. This move is seen as a positive step in addressing sensitive historical matters between the two nations.
* **Maja Chwalińska’s French Open Success:** Polish tennis has a new sensation, as Maja Chwalińska secured a spot in the French Open final. Her performance, described by international media as a "fairy tale" run, has drawn global attention and marks a significant achievement in her professional career.
