## WORLD

*   **Escalating conflict in Ukraine:** The situation in Kyiv remains dire as rescue operations continue following a series of massive Russian drone and missile strikes. The attacks have resulted in multiple civilian casualties, underscoring the intensified nature of the ongoing hostilities.
*   **Geopolitical instability in the Middle East:** Tensions in the Gulf of Oman have spiked following reports that Iranian military forces have seized a vessel identified as a "floating armoury." This development poses a new challenge to regional maritime security.
*   **Political instability in the UK:** The British government faces its first major internal crisis, as Health Secretary Wes Streeting has resigned from his post. Streeting cited a loss of confidence in Prime Minister Keir Starmer’s leadership, marking a significant blow to the current administration's stability.

## POLAND

*   **Culture sector controversy in Wilanów:** The Ministry of Culture has officially dismissed Paweł Jaskanis, director of the Muzeum Pałacu Króla Jana III in Wilanów. The decision follows a public outcry regarding a techno music concert hosted on the museum's historic grounds.
*   **Political friction regarding the Confederation party:** Tensions in the Sejm have reached a boiling point after members of the Konfederacja Korony Polskiej hosted a press conference featuring politicians from the German AfD party. Defense Minister Władysław Kosiniak-Kamysz issued a sharp condemnation, stating the actions of Grzegorz Braun and his colleagues do not align with Poland's national interests.
*   **Tragic aviation accident in Mazowsze:** Authorities are investigating a fatal aviation incident in Baranów, where a gyrocopter crashed and subsequently caught fire. Emergency services were unable to save the two individuals on board, prompting an inquiry into the cause of the disaster.
