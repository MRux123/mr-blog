## WORLD

*   **Conflict Intensifies in St. Petersburg:** Russian infrastructure, including oil facilities and a major data center, was targeted in a deadly overnight attack. The strike marks a significant escalation, highlighting the vulnerability of critical assets deep within Russian territory.
*   **Zelensky Warns Against U.S. Policy Shift:** Ukrainian President Volodymyr Zelensky has cautioned that any support from Donald Trump for Russia would be a "historic mistake." The tension is further compounded by concerns over potential restrictions on the Starshield satellite network, which is vital for Ukrainian military operations.
*   **Geopolitical Realignment in the Middle East:** Egypt’s defense leadership has pledged military support to Saudi Arabia following Houthi attacks. President el-Sisi has sought legislative authorization, characterizing the potential intervention as a strictly defensive measure to stabilize the region.

## POLAND

*   **Heightened Military Activity at NATO Borders:** Intelligence reports indicate that Russia is actively rebuilding and expanding its military storage facilities near NATO borders, signaling a long-term strategic buildup in the Baltic region.
*   **Diplomatic Fences Mended:** The Ukrainian government has issued a formal apology to Lithuania following the unauthorized incursion of drones into Lithuanian airspace, a move aimed at preserving regional unity amid ongoing security challenges.
