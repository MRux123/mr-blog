## WORLD

*   **Heightened Tensions in Lebanon:** Israel is significantly escalating its military operations against Hezbollah, with reports of infrastructure destruction and targeted strikes on bridges. Lebanese officials view these actions as a clear precursor to a potential large-scale ground invasion, as Israel signals its readiness for a prolonged conflict in the region.
*   **Amnesty for Foreign Fighters:** In an unusual development, the Kenyan government has announced plans to offer amnesty to citizens who have been fighting illegally for Russia in the ongoing conflict in Ukraine, aiming to resolve the status of these individuals who otherwise face severe domestic legal penalties.
*   **Netherlands Leads NATO Drone Initiative:** The Dutch military has announced an ambitious, comprehensive plan to integrate drone technology across all combat units. The initiative, which seeks to recruit up to 1,200 personnel, represents the most extensive deployment of unmanned aerial systems within the NATO alliance to date.

## POLAND

*   **Legislative Push for Retirement Reform:** Two new projects regarding "seniority-based pensions" (emerytury stażowe) have reached the Sejm. With government backing, the proposed changes could allow employees with long professional tenures to retire significantly earlier than the current age threshold, signaling a potential overhaul of the national social security system.
*   **Political Fragmentation:** The political landscape remains fluid as Poland 2050 deals with internal divisions. Party officials have addressed the recent split, while emphasizing the necessity of coalition cooperation, warning that the governing camp risks losing its majority in 2027 if internal unity is not restored.
*   **Sports Achievement:** Polish sprinter Pia Skrzyszowska has secured a prestigious podium finish at the World Indoor Championships in Toruń, marking a significant milestone for Polish athletics on the global stage.
