## WORLD

*   **Long-Range Drone Strike in Russia:** Ukraine has successfully executed a significant drone operation against industrial facilities in Nizhnekamsk, Tatarstan. The strike, which targeted a major petrochemical hub located 1,200 kilometers from the Ukrainian border, marks a notable escalation in the conflict's reach and the capabilities of long-range strike assets.
*   **Growing Anxiety Over Iranian Influence in Europe:** Intelligence and security agencies across Europe are grappling with an increasingly assertive Iran. A series of security breaches and threats attributed to Tehran and its proxies have prompted concerns among European officials, further destabilizing the regional security landscape and fueling domestic anxiety regarding external interference.

## POLAND

*   **Political Controversy Over Digital Services Legislation:** A heated public debate has erupted following a presidential decision regarding the Digital Services Act. Critics accuse the president of yielding to lobbying efforts, while supporters maintain that the veto was a necessary measure to protect freedom of speech and prevent the government from utilizing legislation as a political tool.
*   **Vandalism of Historical Monuments:** Police authorities have launched an investigation into incidents of vandalism involving nationalist slogans—specifically "Slava UPA"—daubed on Polish monuments. One individual has already been apprehended as authorities work to address the provocative act, which has sparked significant public and political outcry.
