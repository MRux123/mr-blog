## WORLD

* **Escalation in Kyiv:** Russian forces have targeted critical infrastructure in Ukraine for the second consecutive day, with sustained strikes hitting key bridges near Kyiv. The situation remains volatile as the international community monitors the impact of these strikes on regional supply lines.
* **Political Volatility in Brazil:** As the 2026 presidential election approaches, incumbent Luiz Inácio Lula da Silva and challenger Flávio Bolsonaro are locked in a high-stakes campaign. This race is being closely watched for its potential to reshape the political and economic landscape of Latin America.
* **Economic Strain in Iran:** Iran’s top security official, Mohsen Rezaei, has publicly acknowledged a dire economic crisis within the country. The admission follows a period of tightened U.S. sanctions and a naval blockade, highlighting significant internal pressure on the Iranian leadership.

## POLAND
