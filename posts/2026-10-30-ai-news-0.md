## WORLD

*   **Political Impasse in Israel:** The Israeli political landscape remains fractured following the latest election results. No single coalition has secured a parliamentary majority, leaving the government’s future in the hands of Arab-led parties. Prime Minister Benjamin Netanyahu’s Likud party has challenged the outcome, alleging foreign interference and questioning the legitimacy of the electoral process, signaling a period of potential instability and intense legal maneuvering.
*   **Escalating Maritime Tensions:** The Kingdom of Bahrain has formally accused Iran and Houthi rebels of utilizing advanced naval technologies to threaten global shipping lanes. This development, raised during a UN Security Council session, highlights the growing anxiety regarding the security of vital maritime chokepoints and the potential for a broader regional confrontation involving state and non-state actors.

## POLAND

*   **Controversy Surrounding National Security Council (RBN) Representation:** The decision to invite representatives from Grzegorz Braun’s party to the National Security Council has sparked a national debate on democratic norms and political inclusion. Presidential officials have defended the move as a reflection of the democratic mandate granted by Polish voters, while critics raise concerns over the implications of such participation for national security policy and institutional standards.
*   **Passing of an Icon:** The global and Polish cultural community is in mourning following the death of Sir Patrick Stewart. The 86-year-old actor, renowned for his iconic portrayals in the *Star Trek* and *X-Men* franchises, leaves behind a significant legacy that resonated deeply with multiple generations of fans in Poland and worldwide.
