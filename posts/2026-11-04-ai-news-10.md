## WORLD

*   **Democratic Surge in U.S. Midterms:** The United States political landscape has undergone a significant transformation as the Democratic Party secured historic victories in Congressional elections. Key wins in South Carolina and Texas, ending decades of Republican dominance in those regions, suggest a major shift in voter sentiment. International observers and allies are now closely monitoring how this "blue wave" will impact the remainder of the current administration’s term, particularly regarding foreign policy, trade, and global stability.
*   **Iran Reassesses Nuclear Strategy:** Following the Republican party's electoral defeat and the cooling of relations with the U.S., Iranian officials and clerics are increasingly calling for a re-evaluation of the country’s nuclear policy. Suggestions that Tehran might abandon its pledge to forgo a nuclear bomb highlight growing regional instability and the potential for a renewed arms race in the Middle East.

## POLAND

*   **Political Escalation over Constitutional Tribunal:** Domestic tensions in Poland have reached a critical point as the Law and Justice (PiS) party announced legal action regarding the ongoing dispute over the Constitutional Tribunal. The situation has drawn international attention, with Pope Leo XIV issuing a statement emphasizing that lasting peace must be built on the respect for law and dialogue rather than violence or confrontation.
*   **Governmental Rhetoric Intensifies:** The political discourse in Warsaw has become increasingly polarized. Recent exchanges between high-ranking officials, including Sejm Marshal Włodzimierz Czarzasty’s sharp critique of presidential associate Karol Nawrocki, signal a deepening rift between the government and the opposition. Meanwhile, the presidential office remains focused on maintaining diplomatic continuity with the United States despite the changing political tides in Washington.
