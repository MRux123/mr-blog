## WORLD

*   **Conflict Escalation in Jordan:** The regional security situation has deteriorated significantly following an Iranian attack on Jordan, which resulted in the deaths of two U.S. service members, with one additional soldier reported missing. The incident, characterized by the launch of multiple missiles into Jordanian airspace, marks a dangerous escalation in hostilities and has prompted aggressive rhetoric from Iranian officials, including warnings from Mojtaba Khamenei.

The move, which involves a constitutional amendment, signals a period of instability within the Hungarian leadership, despite Sulyok’s history as an ally of Prime Minister Viktor Orbán.
