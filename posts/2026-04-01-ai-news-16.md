## WORLD

* **Trump addresses Iran conflict amid NATO friction:** US President Donald Trump is set to deliver a prime-time address regarding the ongoing war with Iran. The administration faces mounting pressure to resolve the conflict, with Trump publicly berating allies for their lack of support, even hinting at a potential U.S. withdrawal from NATO. Mixed signals continue to emerge from the White House regarding the longevity and strategy of the military campaign.
* **Political shift in Hungary:** Peter Magyar has emerged as a significant political threat to Viktor Orban’s long-standing rule in Hungary. As a former ally turned critic, Magyar represents the most formidable challenge to Orban's administration since his ascent to power in 2010.

## POLAND

* **Major legal and financial setback with Pfizer:** A Belgian court has ruled against Poland in a case brought by Pfizer Export, ordering the state to pay 5.6 billion PLN and accept delivery of approximately 64 million COVID-19 vaccine doses. Prime Minister Donald Tusk has publicly criticized the previous administration for the policies that led to this liability, describing the financial penalty as a consequence of past government mismanagement.
* **Security incident in Łódź:** A shooting occurred at a factory in Łódź, where a 42-year-old former employee opened fire on his ex-supervisor. After a lengthy police operation, the perpetrator was taken into custody; no further threats remain.
* **Healthcare system under scrutiny:** The tragic case of an eight-year-old boy, who suffered irreversible brain damage after waiting 24 minutes for an ambulance, has sparked a national debate regarding the efficiency and reliability of Poland’s emergency response services.
