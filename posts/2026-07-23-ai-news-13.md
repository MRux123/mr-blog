## WORLD

* **Trump Ties Saudi Nuclear Deal to Abraham Accords:** Former US President Donald Trump has linked a landmark civil nuclear energy agreement with Saudi Arabia to the kingdom's official recognition of Israel. The condition signifies a broader push to expand the Abraham Accords, recalibrating Middle Eastern diplomatic architecture in exchange for advanced energy cooperation.
* **Political Earthquake in Berlin as AfD Leads Polls:** In a historic shift ahead of local elections, the Alternative for Germany (AfD) party has surged to the top of voter intention polls in Berlin. This development marks a significant transformation in the German political landscape, signaling rising public discontent with traditional mainstream parties.
* **British Military Training Halted in Kenya:** The British Army has been forced to cancel scheduled training exercises in Kenya following a breakdown in negotiations over a bilateral defense agreement. The impasse stems from Kenyan lawmakers’ demands for increased jurisdiction to prosecute British personnel for criminal offenses, reflecting a shift in the bilateral power dynamic.

## POLAND

* **Sikorski Reaffirms Poland’s Stance on West Bank Status:** Polish Foreign Minister Radosław Sikorski has delivered a firm diplomatic statement, explicitly declaring that the West Bank is not part of Israeli territory. The statement aligns Poland with long-standing international consensus, challenging recent regional integration trends and maintaining a clear stance on the Israeli-Palestinian conflict.
* **Internal Friction Within Law and Justice (PiS):** Tensions within the Law and Justice party (PiS) have reached a critical point following a high-stakes meeting between party leader Jarosław Kaczyński and former Prime Minister Mateusz Morawiecki. Observers are closely watching the fallout, as the party struggles to define its strategy and leadership direction amidst internal power dynamics.
