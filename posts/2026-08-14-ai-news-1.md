## WORLD

*   **Aviation Safety Investigation:** U.S. investigators have released a preliminary report concerning a harrowing mid-air incident on a Ryanair flight from Greece to Germany. The report identifies the failure of an engine fan blade as the primary cause of damage, which resulted in the shattering of a cabin window.
*   **Conviction in Australia:** Former Catholic Bishop Christopher Saunders has been found guilty of multiple sexual offenses against two young Indigenous Australians. A jury convicted the 76-year-old on 13 charges, marking a significant legal development in a case involving decades of service in the Broome diocese.

## POLAND

*   **Policy Shift on Ukrainian Refugees:** Germany has announced new restrictions on protection for certain Ukrainian nationals, specifically targeting men of military age, signaling a tightening of migration policy for that demographic.
*   **Government Agreement with SpaceX:** The Polish government, represented by Krzysztof Gawkowski, has reached an agreement with SpaceX regarding Starlink services. Poland is set to be integrated into the pan-European Starlink Roam travel zone, ensuring equal service conditions for Polish users compared to their European counterparts.
*   **Tragic Industrial Accident:** A fatal fire occurred at a smokehouse in Świerczyn, near Płock. Emergency services confirmed the death of a 45-year-old man during the inspection of the site, marking a somber incident for the local community.
