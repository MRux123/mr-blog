## WORLD

*   **Political Mourning in Tehran:** International attention remains fixed on Tehran, where funeral proceedings for the former supreme leader, Ayatollah Ali Khamenei, are underway. Correspondents note a highly charged atmosphere, reflecting both the grief of supporters and the underlying political uncertainty surrounding the transition of power in Iran.
*   **Controversial FIFA Ruling:** In a move that has sparked widespread debate, FIFA has overturned a red card suspension for a prominent U.S. national team player ahead of their match against Belgium. The decision, which received public praise from Donald Trump, has drawn criticism from observers questioning the consistency and impartiality of international football governance.

## POLAND

*   **Diplomatic Incident at Rakowicki Cemetery:** Tensions between Warsaw and Kyiv have risen following the vandalism of the tomb of Ukrainian poet Bohdan Lepky at the Rakowicki Cemetery in Kraków. Ukrainian officials have labeled the theft of the poet's sculpture a deliberate provocation aimed at straining bilateral relations, with local police currently investigating the incident.
*   **Public Safety Concerns at the Baltic Coast:** Polish rescue services have issued urgent warnings as thousands of beachgoers ignore red flag warnings across approximately 140 Baltic swimming areas. Authorities highlight the extreme danger posed by high waves and rip currents, emphasizing that the disregard for safety protocols is creating life-threatening situations for both adults and children.
*   **Animal Welfare Scandal:** An investigative report has exposed the grim realities of illegal horse trading in Poland. The findings reveal a clandestine network operating outside of regulatory oversight, where animals are trafficked under sub-standard conditions, ultimately bypassing legal protections intended to monitor the transport and slaughter of livestock.
