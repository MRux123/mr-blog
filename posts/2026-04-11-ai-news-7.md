## WORLD

* **High-Stakes Peace Negotiations in Pakistan**: A high-ranking U.S. delegation, including Vice President JD Vance, has arrived in Islamabad for critical peace talks with Iran. The international community is closely monitoring whether the two nations can transition from a volatile conflict to a durable long-term diplomatic agreement, despite the uncertainty surrounding the current cease-fire.
* **Hezbollah’s Resurgence in Conflict with Israel**: Contrary to widespread reports suggesting the militant group had been severely incapacitated, Hezbollah has launched a series of high-intensity attacks against Israel. This tactical defiance has forced a reevaluation of the group's military capabilities in the ongoing regional conflict.
* **Cracks in Orbán’s Political Base**: Ahead of a pivotal election this Sunday, Hungarian Prime Minister Viktor Orbán is facing an unprecedented internal crisis. A growing number of loyalists have begun defecting, signaling potential vulnerability within the institutions that have long underpinned his administration.

## POLAND

* **Diplomatic Support in Budapest**: Polish opposition figures from the PiS party have arrived in Budapest to publicly endorse Viktor Orbán’s re-election campaign. The visit underscores the deepening ideological alignment between Polish conservatives and the current Hungarian leadership, with reports also confirming meetings between these officials and Polish politician Zbigniew Ziobro.
* **Tragic Incident in Bystrzyca Kłodzka**: Local authorities are investigating a fatal shooting in Bystrzyca Kłodzka. While one suspect is currently in custody, police are actively searching for additional individuals involved in the incident, which has left one young man dead.
