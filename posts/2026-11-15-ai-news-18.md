## WORLD

*   **Intelligence Agencies Warn of Threat to Donald Trump:** Following alerts from the Mossad, multiple Western intelligence agencies have confirmed credible reports of Iranian-backed plots targeting U.S. President Donald Trump and senior administration officials. This development marks a significant escalation in the ongoing geopolitical friction between Washington and Tehran.
*   **Estonia Closes Russian Border:** In a radical shift in regional security policy, Estonia has announced the complete closure of all border crossings with Russia, effective December 1. The decision follows the sabotage of a drone manufacturing facility, an incident which Estonian intelligence has explicitly attributed to the Russian GRU.
*   **Renewed Hostilities in the Red Sea:** The balance of power in Yemen has shifted as Saudi-backed forces retreat from the Red Sea region. The advancement of Houthi militias, combined with the stalling of U.S.-Iran peace talks, poses a direct threat to maritime security and regional stability.

## POLAND

*   **Security Breach in Kraków:** Polish law enforcement has released a strongly worded statement following a violent attack in Kraków. The incident has prompted a broader debate regarding police presence and the effectiveness of public safety measures in major urban centers.
*   **Constitutional Tribunal Dispute:** The political impasse surrounding the Constitutional Tribunal remains a focal point of domestic discourse. High-level discussions, including those involving the President and opposition figures like Grzegorz Schetyna, continue to seek a resolution to the ongoing legal and institutional deadlock.
