## WORLD

* **Humanitarian Crisis on Cuba:** The nation is facing its most severe economic collapse in recent history, characterized by a staggering 91.5% shortage of essential medicines. With the government rejecting U.S. humanitarian aid, citizens are increasingly forced to rely on illicit black markets for survival.
* **Hantavirus Outbreak at Sea:** The MV Hondius has arrived in Tenerife under quarantine conditions following a deadly hantavirus outbreak on board. Medical authorities are currently stationed at the port, awaiting protocols for the processing and screening of passengers.
* **Maritime Disaster in Florida:** A significant explosion occurred on a tourist vessel near Miami Beach. Emergency services have transported over a dozen individuals to local hospitals, with authorities currently investigating the cause of the blast.

## POLAND

* **Ecological Dilemma at Mewia Łacha:** Environmental services are grappling with the discovery of a decaying whale carcass washed ashore at the Mewia Łacha nature reserve. The situation is complicated by the sensitive location, which is situated in close proximity to active bird nesting grounds, complicating removal and investigative efforts.
* **Security Breach at Prague Derby:** Football matches in the region faced a major disruption after the Prague derby was abandoned in stoppage time. The incident involved fans invading the pitch and attacking players, sparking a wider debate regarding security protocols at high-stakes sporting events.
