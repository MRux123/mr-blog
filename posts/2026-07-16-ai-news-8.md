## WORLD

* **Escalation in the Middle East:** Military tensions have reached a critical peak as Iran reports explosions at its military bases following a series of precision strikes attributed to the United States. The situation remains volatile as regional powers assess the strategic implications of this direct military confrontation.
* **Political Shifts in Ukraine's Defense Sector:** President Volodymyr Zelensky has signaled a significant government reshuffle, confirming that Minister of Defense Mykhailo Fedorov will not be reappointed. This decision, linked to internal friction between ministry leadership and military commanders, coincides with a proposal to appoint Serhiy Koretsky as Prime Minister.

## POLAND

* **Warnings Over Potential False-Flag Operations:** Former Minister of National Defence, Janusz Onyszkiewicz, has issued a stark warning regarding national security. He cautioned that Russia may orchestrate a provocation targeting Polish critical infrastructure in the coming weeks, likely aiming to shift the blame onto Ukraine.
* **Legal Action at Auschwitz-Birkenau:** Polish authorities have detained a Colombian national on charges of promoting Nazism while visiting the former concentration camp site, highlighting the ongoing strict enforcement of laws prohibiting hate speech and extremist symbolism at historical memorial sites.
