## WORLD

* **Israel-Iran Conflict Intensifies:** Tensions have reached a critical juncture as Israel launched a series of strikes targeting critical infrastructure in Tehran, prompting retaliatory actions from Iran. Amidst the escalating volatility, regional diplomats from Saudi Arabia, Egypt, and Turkey have convened in Pakistan, seeking an urgent diplomatic resolution to prevent further regional instability.
* **Iranian Military Rhetoric:** The Speaker of the Iranian Parliament, Mohammad Bagher Ghalibaf, has issued aggressive warnings directed at the United States, threatening to confront any potential ground invasion by American forces. This posturing is accompanied by heightened threats against U.S. naval presence in the region, signaling a significant risk of direct military confrontation.
* **Security Alert in Paris:** French anti-terror authorities have made additional arrests in connection with a foiled plot to attack a Bank of America branch in Paris. The incident, involving a makeshift explosive device, is being investigated for possible links to the broader geopolitical instability currently unfolding in the Middle East.

## POLAND

* **Polish Soldier Injured in Lebanon:** A Polish soldier serving with the UNIFIL peacekeeping mission has sustained non-life-threatening injuries following the detonation of an improvised explosive device (IED) near a military vehicle. The Polish Ministry of National Defense has confirmed the soldier is receiving medical care and that the incident is being monitored at the highest level.
* **Tragedy in Ukraine:** The human cost of the ongoing conflict remains devastating, as a 13-year-old boy was among the civilians killed in a recent Russian shelling attack in Kramatorsk. Local officials have condemned the strike as an act of deliberate terror against the civilian population.
* **Political Realignments:** Deputy Prime Minister Władysław Kosiniak-Kamysz has confirmed that internal discussions regarding the formation of a new political entity are underway ahead of the 2027 elections. The move suggests a potential shift in the Polish political landscape as parties begin to position themselves for the upcoming cycle.
