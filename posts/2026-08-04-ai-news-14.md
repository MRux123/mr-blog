## WORLD

*   **Conflict Intensifies in Russia:** The ongoing shadow war between Russia and Ukraine has reached new levels of intensity. Ukrainian strikes have targeted multiple Wildberries distribution centers, while Russian drone retaliation is increasingly focusing on civilian infrastructure, specifically gas stations, effectively opening a new, lethal front in the logistical conflict.
*   **Economic Intervention in Moscow:** Facing internal instability, Vladimir Putin has mandated state monitoring of the entire food supply chain. This move aims to curb the rapid, uncontrolled rise in food prices that has been threatening the stability of the Russian domestic market.

## POLAND

*   **Baltic Airspace Provocation:** The Polish Armed Forces were forced to scramble fighter jets after a Russian Ił-20M reconnaissance aircraft was detected just 56 km from Koszalin. Minister of National Defense Władysław Kosiniak-Kamysz characterized the incursion as a deliberate attempt by Moscow to test NATO’s defensive readiness.
*   **Internal Crisis in PiS:** The political landscape is witnessing a deepening divide within the Law and Justice (PiS) party. Party leader Jarosław Kaczyński has publicly attacked former Prime Minister Mateusz Morawiecki, suggesting that Morawiecki is in a "strange state" and has positioned himself as an adversary to the party’s leadership.
*   **Infrastructure Stress:** A combination of extreme weather and regional power shortages has forced the Połaniec and Kozienice power plants to reduce capacity due to critically low water levels in the Vistula River. Polish Power Grids (PSE) have initiated emergency power calls to maintain grid stability during peak consumption hours.
