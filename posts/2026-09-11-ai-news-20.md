## WORLD

* **Escalating Crisis in the Red Sea:** The Houthi militia has launched a significant ground offensive, reportedly seizing the strategic island of Perim. This advancement directly threatens the Bab al-Mandab strait, a vital maritime "choke point" for global oil shipments and international trade, signaling a severe escalation in regional instability.
* **Ukraine Proposes Energetic Truce:** President Volodymyr Zelensky has signaled a potential shift in diplomatic strategy, calling for an "energy armistice" with Russia. The proposal aims to halt mutual strikes on critical energy infrastructure and secure merchant shipping routes in the Black Sea, even as Kyiv continues to advocate for further international sanctions against Moscow.

## POLAND

* **Political Tensions Over Judiciary and Appointments:** A sharp confrontation has erupted between the government and the opposition regarding the Constitutional Tribunal. Interior Minister Marcin Kierwiński publicly criticized opposition figure Zbigniew Bogucki, dismissing his critiques of judicial appointments and escalating the ongoing institutional conflict surrounding the rule of law.
* **Electoral Deadlock in Kraków:** The municipal political scene in Kraków remains in a state of high volatility, with the latest polling data indicating a razor-thin 0.5 percentage point gap between leading candidates. This suggests a deeply divided electorate and a high-stakes campaign as the city heads toward a critical electoral decision.
