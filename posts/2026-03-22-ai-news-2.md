## WORLD

*   **Iranian Missile Strike Breaches Israeli Defenses:** In a significant escalation of the ongoing conflict, Iranian missiles have successfully bypassed Israel’s defensive systems, striking the cities of Dimona and Arad. Reports indicate that at least a dozen people were seriously injured, marking this as one of the most volatile days in the current war. The IAEA has issued an urgent plea for maximum military restraint to prevent a regional catastrophe.
*   **Humanitarian Tragedy in Sudan:** A devastating drone attack on a hospital in East Darfur has left at least 64 people dead and nearly 90 wounded. The victims include children and medical personnel, drawing widespread condemnation from the World Health Organization and international observers regarding the targeting of critical healthcare infrastructure.
*   **Grid Collapse in Cuba:** Cuba is grappling with its second total national blackout in the span of a week. The collapse of the power grid highlights the severe socio-economic strain the nation faces, exacerbated by the ongoing fuel blockade.

## POLAND

*   **Political Response to Middle East Escalation:** The Polish media is closely monitoring the impact of the Iranian attack on Israel. Prime Minister Benjamin Netanyahu has described the current situation as a "very difficult evening," with over 100 people reported injured across Arad and Dimona. Polish news outlets are focused on the strategic implications of the failure of Israel's defensive layers against incoming ballistic missiles.
