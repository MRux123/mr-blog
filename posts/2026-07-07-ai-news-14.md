## WORLD

*   **NATO Summit in Turkey Amidst Geopolitical Volatility:** As leaders convene in Turkey, the alliance faces a critical juncture. The agenda is dominated by the need for a new strategic model, influenced by President Trump’s demands for increased burden-sharing among European members. Concurrently, President Zelensky has intensified his plea for advanced air defense systems following a wave of devastating Russian strikes, positioning these deliveries as the benchmark for the summit’s success.
*   **Regional Instability in the Middle East:** A high-level diplomatic visit by French President Emmanuel Macron to Damascus was overshadowed by violent explosions, causing 18 injuries. This incident underscores the fragility of the regional security landscape during ongoing power shifts.
*   **Political Upheaval in Iran:** The nation remains in a state of mourning following the death of Supreme Leader Ayatollah Ali Khamenei. The ongoing state-led funeral rites reflect a period of profound uncertainty regarding the future trajectory of the Islamic Republic's leadership and policy.

## POLAND

*   **Industrial Strain in the Russian Defense Sector:** Reports citing a Russian minister indicate that the country's military-industrial complex is reaching a critical threshold of its operational capacity, suggesting potential long-term implications for the regional security balance near the Polish border.
*   **Ecological Crisis on the Roztocze:** Local authorities and environmentalists are sounding the alarm over a growing ecological threat in the Roztocze region, where the drying up of popular, ecologically vital ponds threatens biodiversity and local tourism.
*   **Military Recruitment Initiatives:** The government has launched the "Vacation with the Army" program, offering 6,000 PLN gross for 27 days of training, as Poland continues to prioritize the strengthening of its defense readiness in response to the deteriorating geopolitical environment.
