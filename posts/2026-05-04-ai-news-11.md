## WORLD

* **Escalating Drone Strike in Moscow:** Tensions have reached a new peak as a Ukrainian drone struck a high-rise building in Moscow. The incident coincides with the Kremlin’s final preparations for scaled-back Victory Day celebrations, underscoring the vulnerability of the capital despite heightened security measures.
* **Rising Conflict in the Strait of Hormuz:** Iran has issued a stern warning to international vessels, threatening to treat any ship crossing the Strait of Hormuz without permission as a military risk. This move follows the U.S. administration's announced plans to disrupt Iranian blockades, signaling a potential for direct naval confrontation in the region.
* **Geopolitical Realignment in Armenia:** As Russia’s regional influence appears to face challenges, European leaders are converging on Armenia. The summit highlights a significant diplomatic pivot for a nation traditionally considered a cornerstone of Russian regional policy.

## POLAND

* **Strategic Defense Treaty with the UK:** Prime Minister Donald Tusk has confirmed that Poland is set to sign a landmark defense and cooperation treaty with the United Kingdom on May 27. The agreement marks a significant step in strengthening bilateral military ties and deepening security integration between Warsaw and London.
* **Procedural Hurdles in Ziobro Case:** The legal proceedings concerning the European Arrest Warrant (ENA) for Zbigniew Ziobro face further delays. The situation remains a point of high political tension, as the judicial process struggles to move forward due to ongoing procedural complications.
* **Security Breach During Matura Exams:** The integrity of the Polish school-leaving examinations (matura) has been compromised following reports of exam sheets leaking online. Authorities are currently investigating the security breach, which has sparked widespread debate regarding the fairness and administration of the national assessment process.
