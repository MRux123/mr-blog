## WORLD

* **Ebola Outbreak Expands in DRC:** The Ebola epidemic in the Democratic Republic of the Congo has reached a sixth province. Health officials warn that the current trajectory suggests this could become the deadliest outbreak in history, far surpassing the death toll of the 2014-2016 West African crisis.
* **Geopolitical Tensions Rise:** Russian Foreign Minister Sergey Lavrov has issued stern warnings to the United Kingdom, characterizing London's ongoing support for Ukraine as direct participation in the conflict. This diplomatic escalation coincides with reports of a technical failure involving a U.S. destroyer in the South China Sea, further straining international maritime stability.

## POLAND

* **Political Debate on Migration:** Former Prime Minister Mateusz Morawiecki has proposed the creation of a specialized deportation agency. The proposal includes implementing automated alert systems for tracking and removing migrants, framing the initiative as a move toward strict assimilation policies.
* **National Tragedy and Accountability:** Authorities have dismissed a police officer following his involvement in a fatal altercation during a wedding in Wadowice, which resulted in the death of a 38-year-old man. Meanwhile, the nation is in mourning following a tragic bus accident involving Polish pilgrims in Hungary, which has prompted a message of condolence from the Vatican.
