## WORLD

*   **Trump’s NATO Ultimatum:** Global security remains in flux as President Trump continues to threaten US withdrawal from NATO. Recent reports indicate that the President is using support for Ukraine as leverage, conditioning future military aid on NATO members joining a US-led coalition to force open the Strait of Hormuz amidst escalating tensions with Iran.
*   **Historical Artemis II Mission:** Marking a milestone in space exploration, the Artemis II mission is scheduled for launch tonight. This represents the first crewed flight to the lunar orbit in over 53 years, signaling a new era in human space travel and NASA's long-term goal of returning to the Moon.

## POLAND

*   **Constitutional Crisis Over Judicial Appointments:** Minister of Justice Waldemar Żurek has issued an urgent appeal to President Karol Nawrocki regarding the appointment of Constitutional Tribunal judges. The dispute centers on the President's selective swearing-in process, with critics labeling the exclusion of four elected judges as a potential violation of the Polish Constitution.
*   **Public Health Alert:** Authorities have issued an urgent warning in the Lower Silesia region, specifically the Wrocław district. A contamination of the water supply in the Żórawina municipality has forced an immediate ban on tap water consumption, triggering a response from the Government Security Center.
