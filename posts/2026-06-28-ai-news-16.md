## WORLD

* **Fatal Skydiving Incident in France:** A tragic aviation accident occurred near Nancy on Sunday morning, resulting in the loss of 11 lives. The aircraft, carrying five instructors, five students, and a pilot, crashed in a field under circumstances currently under investigation by local authorities.
* **Helicopter Crash Near Saudi Oil Infrastructure:** A helicopter crash in the Eastern Province of Saudi Arabia has claimed the lives of 14 people. The incident took place near the Ras Tanura refinery, a critical hub for global oil exports. While the cause remains under investigation, the proximity of the crash to such sensitive energy infrastructure has prompted global attention.

## POLAND

* **Historic Temperature Records Broken:** Poland is currently grappling with a severe heatwave that has shattered historical temperature records. The extreme weather conditions have triggered widespread concerns regarding public safety, infrastructure resilience, and potential environmental impacts.
* **Workplace Rights During Extreme Heat:** In response to the ongoing heatwave, authorities and labor organizations are emphasizing the importance of employee rights. Current regulations mandate that employers provide access to cool beverages and ensure appropriate working conditions, with an increasing emphasis on remote work possibilities where applicable to mitigate the health risks associated with the record-breaking temperatures.
