## WORLD

* **Tensions Rise Over U.S.-Israeli Relations:** Vice President Vance has issued a stern warning to Israeli critics regarding the current deal backed by the Trump administration. The rhetoric underscores a growing rift between the U.S. executive branch and certain segments of Israeli leadership, as Washington emphasizes the necessity of maintaining a strategic alliance despite domestic and international opposition to the agreement.

## POLAND

* **Kyiv’s Diplomatic Push for Conflict Resolution:** Ukrainian President Volodymyr Zelenskyy has officially signaled a desire to conclude the war with Russia before the onset of winter. The strategy relies on a combination of diplomatic efforts and intensified pressure on Moscow. However, Kyiv remains pragmatic, acknowledging that should the conflict persist beyond the autumn, a comprehensive "winter support package" will be critical to sustain the nation’s defense and infrastructure.
