## WORLD

* **Record-Breaking Heatwave:** Europe is grappling with a severe heatwave, with Germany reporting a record-breaking 41.7°C. The WHO has confirmed that these extreme temperatures are linked to over 1,300 deaths, prompting warnings that the continent remains inadequately prepared for the accelerating climate crisis.
* **Venezuela Earthquake Aftermath:** Rescue operations in Venezuela remain critical as the window of opportunity to find survivors following twin earthquakes continues to shrink. Despite ongoing efforts, rescue teams face significant delays and chaotic conditions due to persistent aftershocks.

## POLAND

* **Tragedy in Międzywodzie:** A 42-year-old man has died while successfully saving two children from drowning at the beach in Międzywodzie. The incident has deeply affected the local community, highlighting the dangers posed by current coastal conditions.
* **Infrastructure Strain from Extreme Weather:** The ongoing heatwave has caused significant disruptions to Poland’s railway network, with several regions experiencing total stoppages and severe delays due to damage to the power grid infrastructure.
* **Emergency Services Accident:** Four rescue workers are in critical condition following a severe ambulance accident on the A4 motorway. A public appeal for blood donations has been issued as medical teams continue to fight for the lives of those involved.
