## WORLD

*   **Escalation in the Ukraine War:** The conflict has increasingly shifted toward aerial warfare. With ground combat largely stalled, both Russia and Ukraine are prioritizing the development and deployment of innovative aerial weaponry, transforming the skies into the primary theater of operations.
*   **Security Incident in Germany:** A DHL cargo plane collided with an unidentified object near Leipzig Airport. This follows recent discoveries of explosive-laden drones at the site, prompting heightened security concerns regarding aviation safety and potential sabotage in the region.

## POLAND

*   **Constitutional Dispute:** Prime Minister Donald Tusk has publicly accused President Karol Nawrocki of violating the Constitution. The dispute centers on the nomination of judicial assessors, with Tusk claiming the President bypassed the mandatory requirement for prime ministerial countersignature, labeling the actions a "direct assault on the Constitution."
*   **Smolensk Investigation Concluded:** The long-standing investigation into allegations of "diplomatic treason" following the 2010 Smolensk air disaster has officially been closed. Prosecutors announced the termination of the case after ten years of inquiry, during which nearly 500 witnesses were questioned and extensive international legal assistance was processed.
*   **Heatwave Emergency:** Amidst forecasts of extreme temperatures reaching 40°C in Poland, Prime Minister Tusk issued an urgent appeal to employers to prioritize the safety and health of their staff, emphasizing that climate control measures are a matter of public health rather than just comfort.
