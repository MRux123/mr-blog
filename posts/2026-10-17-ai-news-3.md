## WORLD

*   **Trump Adjusts Russia Sanctions:** In a controversial move, the U.S. administration has waived several sanctions against Russia. President Trump justified the decision by claiming that parts of the new legislation, intended to punish Moscow for the ongoing war in Ukraine, directly conflict with U.S. national interests.
*   **Violent Escalation in the West Bank:** Israeli settlers have launched fresh attacks against Palestinians and members of the international press, including a Washington Post journalist, during the critical olive harvest season. The incident marks a significant deterioration in regional stability.
*   **Amazon Deforestation Milestone:** Amidst the buildup to a major election, Brazil has reported a record decline in Amazon rainforest deforestation. This positive trend follows years of environmental policy shifts aimed at curbing the extensive clearing seen under the previous administration.

## POLAND

*   **EU Summit Tensions:** The Hungarian Prime Minister has issued a firm statement following the recent EU summit, characterizing current geopolitical developments as a direct "threat to the security of Europe," highlighting growing fissures within the bloc regarding regional stability.
*   **G20 Diplomatic Controversy:** Speculation is mounting over the guest list for the upcoming G20 summit. Reports suggest that President Trump is actively advocating for the inclusion of Vladimir Putin, a move that has sparked intense debate regarding international protocol and the diplomatic isolation of the Russian leadership.
