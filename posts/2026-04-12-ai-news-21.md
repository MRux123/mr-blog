## WORLD

*   **Orban Era Ends in Hungary:** In a historic shift, Viktor Orban has conceded defeat in the Hungarian parliamentary elections to opposition leader Peter Magyar. After sixteen years in power, the Fidesz party has lost its mandate, driven by widespread public dissatisfaction with corruption and economic stagnation. International leaders, including Emmanuel Macron and Ursula von der Leyen, have welcomed the outcome, signaling Hungary's potential return to a more integrated European path.
*   **Strait of Hormuz Crisis Deepens:** International security concerns are mounting as President Trump threatens to impose a blockade on the Strait of Hormuz. The move follows the failure of diplomatic negotiations aimed at stabilizing the region. The situation remains a high-stakes standoff, testing both Iran’s economic resilience and the administration's willingness to sustain the long-term costs of a potential conflict.

## POLAND

*   **Government Reaction to Hungarian Elections:** Polish political leaders have expressed strong support for the political transition in Hungary. Prime Minister Donald Tusk lauded the election results, describing the defeat of Fidesz as a "wonderful victory" for democratic processes. The shift in Budapest is being closely watched in Warsaw, as it marks a significant realignment of regional political alliances.
*   **Tragic Aviation Accident in Slovakia:** Polish authorities have confirmed the death of a Polish pilot following a mid-air collision of two gliders over the Greater Fatra mountain range in Slovakia. An investigation is underway to determine the circumstances of the crash, which also involved Czech and Austrian nationals.
