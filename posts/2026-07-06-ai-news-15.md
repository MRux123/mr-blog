## WORLD

* **Diplomatic Pressure on the Kremlin:** U.S. President Donald Trump has publicly stated that Vladimir Putin is feeling significant pressure to bring an end to the war in Ukraine. The White House is actively engaged in peace discussions, with the conflict expected to be a central topic at the upcoming NATO summit.
* **Hamas Shifts Governance in Gaza:** In a major geopolitical development, Hamas has announced the dissolution of its government in the Gaza Strip. This move, part of a peace framework proposed by the Trump administration, paves the way for a technocratic committee to assume administrative control over the territory post-conflict.
* **Ukrainian Infrastructure Targeted:** The situation in Ukraine remains critical following a massive Russian assault involving 68 missiles and 351 strike drones, resulting in 22 casualties in the Kyiv region. Ukraine is reporting a severe shortage of interceptor missiles, complicating defense efforts against ongoing aerial bombardment.

## POLAND

* **Transparency on Military Aid:** The Polish Ministry of National Defense (MON) has officially begun the process of declassifying historical data regarding military aid provided to Ukraine. Minister Władysław Kosiniak-Kamysz confirmed that between 2022 and 2023, Poland delivered support estimated at approximately 15 billion PLN.
* **Safety Crisis at the Baltic Sea:** Authorities have closed 140 beaches along the Polish coast due to severe environmental or safety hazards, marking a significant challenge for the peak summer tourist season and raising concerns about regional maritime security.
* **Mountain Rescue Incident:** A high-profile legal case is emerging following a tragedy on the Orla Perć trail in the Tatra Mountains, where a group of children became stranded during severe weather. The incident has led to potential criminal charges against the supervising trainer, sparking a national debate on mountain safety protocols.
