## WORLD

*   **US-Iran Diplomacy:** Negotiations regarding a potential ceasefire extension and the reopening of the Strait of Hormuz are entering a critical phase. While U.S. and Iranian officials suggest a framework is nearing completion, the final approval from President Trump remains pending. This development is viewed as a pivotal moment for regional stability in the Middle East.
*   **Technological Evolution in Asymmetric Warfare:** Security analysts are closely monitoring Hezbollah’s recent adoption of fibre-optic guided drones. Drawing lessons from the conflict in Ukraine, the group has successfully integrated this technology, which is proving difficult to counter and has become a primary tactical threat to Israeli military and civilian assets.

## POLAND

*   **Regional Security Incident:** A Russian-launched drone deviated from its target in Ukraine and impacted an apartment building in the Romanian city of Galați. The incident, which caused injuries and a fire, marks a concerning escalation in the spillover risks for NATO member states bordering the conflict zone.
*   **Major Wildfire Outbreak in Mazovia:** A massive forest fire near Warsaw has prompted a large-scale emergency response, with nearly 400 firefighters and 75 vehicles currently engaged in suppression efforts. Minister of National Defence Władysław Kosiniak-Kamysz has mobilized the Territorial Defence Forces (WOT) to assist, as initial investigations by the local forestry service suggest the fire may have been the result of deliberate arson.
