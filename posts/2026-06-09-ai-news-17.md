## WORLD

* **US-Iran Tensions Reach Breaking Point:** Global markets and security analysts are on high alert following the downing of a U.S. Apache helicopter over the Strait of Hormuz. President Trump has officially confirmed the incident and vowed a swift, necessary response. Simultaneously, the Middle East remains volatile as Israeli airstrikes continue in Tyre, Lebanon, despite Iranian warnings to cease operations against Hezbollah.
* **Ebola Health Crisis Looms:** International health officials, led by the U.S. CDC, have warned that the current Ebola outbreak in Central Africa is on a "dangerous trajectory." Modeling suggests that the epidemic could reach a scale comparable to the 2014-2016 crisis, which resulted in over 11,000 fatalities. This health emergency is already sparking civil unrest, evidenced by deadly protests in Kenya against the establishment of US-led quarantine facilities.

## POLAND

* **Opposition Launches Oversight of Defense Sector:** Members of the PiS party have initiated parliamentary inspections at key state-run defense facilities, including the "Łucznik" arms factory in Radom and the Stalowa Wola Steelworks. The lawmakers allege that the "SAFE" loan program has failed to enhance the operational capabilities of the Polish military, characterizing the initiative as a significant misuse of public resources.
* **Diplomatic Maneuvering by Zelenskyy:** President Volodymyr Zelenskyy has commented on a recent diplomatic letter addressed to Moscow, implying that the document achieved its intended strategic objective, despite confusion within the Russian government regarding its true purpose and target.
