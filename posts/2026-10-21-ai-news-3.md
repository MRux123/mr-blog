## WORLD

*   **Geopolitical Realignments and US-Ukraine Ties:** The relationship between Washington and Kyiv remains complex despite recent optimistic signals. While President Zelensky confirmed that US intelligence sharing has resumed following talks with a Trump envoy in Oslo, underlying tensions persist regarding the long-term trajectory of US support, as experts remain skeptical about the stability of the current diplomatic thaw.
*   **Tesla-SpaceX Merger Under Review:** In a move that could reshape the global tech and aerospace landscape, Elon Musk has announced that both Tesla and SpaceX have formed special committees to evaluate a potential "mega-merger." The consolidation of these two industry giants would mark one of the most significant corporate restructuring events in modern history.
*   **Ebola Outbreak Fears:** Health authorities have confirmed an Ebola case in Abuja, Nigeria, involving a traveler from Uganda. This development has sparked concerns regarding the potential for further cross-border transmission of the virus across Western Africa.
