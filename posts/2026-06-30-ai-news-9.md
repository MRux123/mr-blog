## WORLD

* **Rising Geopolitical Tensions in Monaco:** Security agencies have launched a massive manhunt following a targeted explosive attack on a Ukrainian oligarch and his family. The device, described as containing shrapnel, suggests a sophisticated and coordinated assassination attempt, raising concerns about the security environment for high-profile figures in Europe.
* **Escalating Pressure Between China and Japan:** Beijing has intensified both military and economic pressure on Tokyo. Recent provocations include bomber incursions into Japanese airspace, the detention of business personnel, and restrictive export policies on rare earth elements, signaling a significant deterioration in regional stability.
* **Humanitarian Crisis in Venezuela:** Five days after twin earthquakes devastated the region, the official death toll of 1,719 is being heavily scrutinized. International experts and local residents argue that government negligence and a lack of support have obscured the true scale of the catastrophe, leaving thousands of survivors in desperate need of aid.

## POLAND

* **"Coup D'état" Investigation Dismissed:** The Polish Prosecutor's Office has officially closed two threads of an investigation into an alleged coup d'état. The proceedings concerned the nullification of parliamentary mandates and allegations of political pressure regarding the presidential inauguration process. This decision marks a significant closure to one of the most contentious political narratives of recent months.
* **Explosives Discovered at Romanian Border:** Polish authorities and sappers have responded to the discovery of explosive materials within the debris of a drone found near the Romanian border. Preliminary assessments suggest the remains are likely of Russian origin, further heightening border security concerns in the region.
* **Russian Rhetoric Towards Warsaw:** Vyacheslav Volodin, a senior official close to Vladimir Putin, has made provocative statements targeting Poland, suggesting the country should "award" Volodymyr Zelenskyy the "Order of Judas." The comment highlights the ongoing antagonistic diplomatic posture adopted by the Kremlin toward Poland.
