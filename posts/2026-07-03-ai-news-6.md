## WORLD

* **Massive Russian Offensive on Kyiv:** The Ukrainian capital has been struck by what is reported as the most significant Russian aerial attack to date. The barrage, involving a record number of munitions, has hit widespread locations across the city, resulting in at least 30 confirmed fatalities. This escalation marks a dangerous turning point in the ongoing conflict.
* **Tensions in the Strait of Hormuz:** Against the backdrop of the broader US-Israeli conflict with Iran, the port city of Bandar Abbas is experiencing an uneasy calm. Reports indicate an increase in seized maritime assets, reflecting the severe impact of geopolitical regional tensions on civilian life and commercial shipping in one of the world's most critical maritime chokepoints.

## POLAND

* **Industrial Fire in Gdańsk Port:** Emergency services are currently engaged in a large-scale operation to contain a blaze on a scrap-metal vessel docked at the Szczecin Quay in Gdańsk. With 17 fire units deployed, including specialized firefighting vessels, authorities report that the situation remains critical as the exact nature of the burning materials remains undetermined.
* **Political Jockeying and Polling Shifts:** Recent polling data from the Pollster Institute for *Super Express* indicates the Civic Coalition (KO) leading with 32.45% support. The survey is particularly notable for its inclusion of a potential new political entity, reflecting ongoing volatility in the Polish political landscape. Simultaneously, high-profile rhetoric continues between political figures like Adam Bodnar and Zbigniew Ziobro, signaling increased polarization in the lead-up to future electoral cycles.
