## WORLD

*   **US Administration Sets Timeline for Iran Conflict:** President Donald Trump has announced that the ongoing military operations against Iran are expected to conclude within two to three weeks. Claiming a regime change has already occurred, the administration expressed confidence in the long-term stability of the region, asserting that Iran will be unable to develop nuclear capabilities. Trump further predicted a decline in global fuel prices once the conflict officially ends.
*   **Starlink Satellite Disintegrates in Orbit:** SpaceX has reported a significant incident on low Earth orbit, where a Starlink satellite (number 34343) broke into dozens of fragments. While communication with the unit has been completely lost, SpaceX engineers maintain that the debris cloud poses no threat to the International Space Station or upcoming NASA missions.

## POLAND

*   **Fatal Military Air Crash in Occupied Crimea:** A Russian An-26 military transport aircraft has crashed on the occupied Crimean peninsula. Official reports confirm the deaths of all 29 individuals on board, including six crew members and 23 passengers. The Russian Ministry of Defense has preliminarily attributed the catastrophe to technical failure.
*   **Uncertainty Surrounding Polish National Football Team:** Following recent matches, questions regarding the future of the Polish national team are intensifying. The performance of the squad, the leadership of coach Jan Urban, and the long-term participation of captain Robert Lewandowski have become the primary focus of national sports discourse. Expert analysis is expected to provide further clarity on the necessary structural changes.
