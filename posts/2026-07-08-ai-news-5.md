## WORLD

*   **Escalation in the Strait of Hormuz:** The geopolitical situation in the Middle East has reached a critical juncture as the US and Iran engage in reciprocal military strikes. The conflict follows the targeting of oil tankers, prompting direct engagement between US missile sites and Iranian-backed command centers.
*   **NATO Defense Expansion:** Amidst shifting security landscapes, NATO allies have committed £37 billion to a new, major missile development project. Prime Minister Sir Keir Starmer is set to coordinate the initiative with a dozen leaders in Ankara, signaling a unified strategic response to regional threats.
*   **Political Resurgence in France:** Marine Le Pen has moved quickly to initiate her presidential campaign following a court ruling that confirmed a guilty verdict, underscoring the resilience of nationalist movements within the current European political climate.

## POLAND

*   **National Defense Readiness Concerns:** A critical assessment of Polish military preparedness has emerged, suggesting that in the event of a potential conflict, the nation may currently be limited to a defensive capacity of only two operational divisions, sparking a national debate on defense spending and structural readiness.
*   **Extreme Weather Disruptions:** Meteorological hazards continue to impact the country, with severe wind warnings reaching up to 120 km/h. Authorities have been forced to initiate the evacuation of scout camps and close coastal infrastructure, such as the pier in Brzeźno, as a precautionary measure against the storm.
