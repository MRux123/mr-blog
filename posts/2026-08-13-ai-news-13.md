## WORLD

*   **Tensions Escalate Over Territorial Disputes:** Russian President Vladimir Putin’s visit to islands historically seized from Japan during World War II has drawn sharp condemnation from Tokyo. Prime Minister Sanae Takaichi, a vocal critic of the Kremlin, faces heightened diplomatic friction as the move underscores the deepening rift between Moscow and regional powers over wartime legacies and ongoing international sanctions.
*   **Geopolitical Maneuvering in the Energy Sector:** The United Kingdom has intensified its crackdown on the "shadow fleet" of tankers used to bypass international sanctions. British authorities assert that seizing these vessels is a necessary step to disrupt illicit trade routes, despite threats of retaliation from Moscow, marking a significant escalation in the economic battle over energy exports.
*   **China’s Changing Political Memory:** Public tributes to former Premier Zhu Rongji have highlighted a growing nostalgia for his pragmatic, globalist approach to China’s economy. Analysts view these commemorations as a subtle, yet significant, contrast to the ideological rigidity characterizing the current era under Xi Jinping.

## POLAND

*   **Massive Data Breach Threatens Millions:** Poland is grappling with a severe cyberattack targeting the medical platform MyDr, which has resulted in the exposure of sensitive data—including PESEL identification numbers—belonging to approximately 19 million citizens. Digital Affairs Minister Krzysztof Gawkowski has urged the public to immediately secure their digital identities, as the government scrambles to contain the fallout of one of the largest data breaches in the country's history.
*   **Strategic Preparedness for Eastern Borders:** Following recent security concerns, authorities have confirmed that residents in Eastern Poland will receive improved, direct notifications regarding potential threats. This move follows internal RMF FM findings regarding the need for more efficient communication channels during security incidents involving airspace violations.
*   **Political Controversy Surrounding Public Officials:** The Ministry of National Defense is pushing for reforms regarding general appointments for intelligence heads, with a direct appeal issued to Karol Nawrocki. This administrative tension unfolds against a backdrop of ongoing political scrutiny, including a notable legal case involving the prosecution's withdrawal of charges against a local official who had disparaged Nawrocki in a social media controversy.
