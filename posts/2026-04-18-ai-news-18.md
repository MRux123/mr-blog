## WORLD

* **Escalating Conflict in the Strait of Hormuz:** Regional tensions have spiked as Iran’s Revolutionary Guards announced the closure of the Strait of Hormuz, citing a U.S. blockade. The situation has turned violent, with multiple commercial vessels reported hit by fire. President Donald Trump has dismissed Iranian rhetoric as "blackmail," while international markets react to the threat of a sustained maritime confrontation.
* **Political Earthquake in Hungary:** In a historic shift, the TISZA party, led by Peter Magyar, has secured a constitutional majority in the Hungarian parliamentary elections. This result ends Viktor Orban’s 16-year tenure, signaling a potential radical change in Hungary’s domestic policy and its relationship with the European Union, specifically regarding the unfreezing of structural funds.
* **Tragedy in Kyiv:** A violent hostage situation at a supermarket in the Ukrainian capital has resulted in at least six fatalities. The assailant was neutralized by security forces after a protracted shoot-out; officials are currently investigating the motives behind the attack.

## POLAND

* **Polish Convoy Under Fire in Lebanon:** A Polish peacekeeping convoy operating under the UNIFIL mission in southern Lebanon was struck by a mortar shell. While no Polish personnel were injured in the incident, the event underscores the precarious security environment in the region, which has seen ongoing military activity despite a nominal truce.
* **Impending Extreme Weather:** The Government Security Centre (RCB) has called for an emergency meeting as meteorological models predict heavy, potentially dangerous rainfall across Poland, prompting concerns regarding localized flooding and infrastructure damage.
