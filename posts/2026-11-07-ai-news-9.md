## WORLD

* **Political Collapse in Paraguay:** President Santiago Peña has been impeached, removed from office, and placed under house arrest. The ousting follows a severe national banking crisis that left millions of citizens unable to access their funds, triggering widespread public unrest and political instability.
* **Escalation in Kyiv:** Russian forces launched a major nighttime strike on the Ukrainian capital, causing significant damage to the city’s energy infrastructure and the Northern Bridge over the Dnieper River. Mayor Vitali Klitschko reported casualties, including a child, and warned of an extremely difficult situation regarding water and heating supplies as winter approaches.

## POLAND

* **Major Traffic Incident in Oświęcim:** A massive multi-vehicle collision involving 12 cars has been reported in Oświęcim. Emergency services are currently working at the scene, and investigations into the cause of the pile-up are underway.
* **Legislative Controversy:** Former KO MP Marcin Józefaciuk has publicly apologized for his vote on a restrictive measures bill. Józefaciuk has declared his intention to urge Karol Nawrocki to veto the legislation, which was narrowly passed by the Sejm on Friday, citing his own error in the decision-making process.
* **Tragic Crime in Kadłub:** An 18-year-old suspect has been charged with the murder of his relatives in Kadłub. Investigators have revealed that the perpetrator live-streamed parts of the event on social media, reportedly seeking to gain notoriety and online attention through the horrific act.
