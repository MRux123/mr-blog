## WORLD

* **Trump Signals Potential Shift in Iran Conflict:** Reports from the Wall Street Journal indicate that President Donald Trump has signaled a willingness to conclude hostilities with Iran, even if the strategic Strait of Hormuz remains closed. The administration's strategy reportedly focuses on neutralizing Iranian naval and missile assets before shifting to diplomatic pressure. Simultaneously, Israeli Prime Minister Benjamin Netanyahu claims that Israel has successfully achieved more than half of its military objectives in the ongoing campaign.
* **Hope Amidst Conflict in Gaza:** In a poignant moment amidst the ongoing humanitarian crisis, a Gazan mother has been reunited with her daughter after a two-year separation. The child was among a group of premature newborns evacuated during the early stages of the war.

## POLAND

* **Military Leadership Refutes Offensive Claims:** The Operational Command of the Polish Armed Forces has issued a formal denial regarding rumors that Poland had opened its airspace for offensive operations against the Russian Federation. General Maciej Klisz emphasized that such allegations are entirely unfounded and lack any basis in fact, countering concerns about Poland's direct involvement in regional escalations.
* **New Fuel Price Regulations:** Effective today, the Polish government has implemented a new system of maximum price caps for motor fuels. The regulation, which came into force at midnight, is part of a broader effort to maintain price stability across the country.
* **Major Arms Trafficking Bust:** Police forces in Białystok have dismantled an organized criminal group involved in the illegal trade of weapons. The successful operation resulted in the seizure of a significant arsenal, including handguns, shotguns, and a submachine gun, leading to formal charges against three individuals.
