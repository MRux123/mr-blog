## WORLD

*   **Retaliatory Strikes in Iran:** Tensions have reached a critical inflection point as Iran has initiated missile and drone attacks against U.S. targets. This military escalation follows a deadly U.S. strike that resulted in the deaths of four civilians during a wedding party, marking a significant deterioration in regional stability.
*   **High-Value Heist in Vienna:** Authorities in Austria are conducting an urgent manhunt following the theft of an irreplaceable 200-carat platinum necklace. The artifact, which belonged to Egyptian royalty and features 673 diamonds, was stolen during operating hours from the Museum of Applied Arts (MAK) in a brazen smash-and-grab operation.

## POLAND

*   **Fatal Incident in Crete:** A 76-year-old Polish tourist has died following an emergency incident at a beach in Rethymno, Crete. Despite immediate intervention by rescue services and transportation to a local hospital, the victim could not be resuscitated. Greek authorities have launched a formal investigation into the circumstances surrounding the tragedy.
