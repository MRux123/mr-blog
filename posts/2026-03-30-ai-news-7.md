## WORLD

*   **Escalation in the Middle East and Global Energy Impact:** The ongoing conflict between Israel and Iran continues to destabilize global markets. While President Trump claims Iran has agreed to allow more oil shipments through the Strait of Hormuz as a "sign of respect," reports suggest the U.S. is considering aggressive military posturing, including the potential seizure of the strategic Iranian island of Chark. The economic fallout is already being felt globally, with soaring fuel prices forcing transit authorities in Australia to waive fares and experts warning of significant price hikes in global consumer goods, particularly clothing.
*   **U.S. Government Shutdown Crisis:** The United States faces an unprecedented domestic challenge as the partial government shutdown has become the longest in the nation's history. The 44-day funding lapse at the Department of Homeland Security is now severely disrupting travel infrastructure and internal administrative stability.

## POLAND

*   **Political Polarization as Top National Threat:** A recent CBOS survey highlights deep-seated anxiety among the Polish public, with 46% of respondents identifying political polarization and internal disputes as the primary threats to the nation's stability over the next five years.
*   **Death of Key Smoleńsk Sub-Committee Figure:** The passing of Dr. Kazimierz Nowaczyk, a prominent physicist and former head of the Smoleńsk sub-committee, has been confirmed. His work with politician Antoni Macierewicz remains a significant, albeit controversial, chapter in recent Polish political history.
*   **Legislative and Economic Friction:** Political tensions remain high as opposition figures, including Tobiasz Bocheński of PiS, continue to attack the government’s handling of fuel prices, labeling the administration’s response as insufficient and delayed. Simultaneously, internal cracks within the PiS party have emerged, as some members diverge from the party line regarding judicial and legal policy voting, signaling potential shifts in parliamentary strategy.
