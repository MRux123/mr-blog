## WORLD

*   **Myanmar Genocide Verdict:** The international community is bracing for a historic ruling at the UN court regarding the Rohingya massacres. This marks a significant moment in international law, as no state has previously been held guilty of genocide by such a tribunal.
*   **Israel-Iran Escalation:** Tensions have reached a critical juncture, with Israel reportedly increasing pressure on the United States for potential military action against Iran. This development represents a pivotal shift in Middle Eastern security dynamics.
*   **Political Reforms in Israel:** The Israeli Knesset has initiated the repeal of several controversial policies enacted by Benjamin Netanjahu’s previous administration. The legislative shift, led by Gadi Eizenkot’s camp, seeks to dismantle reforms related to the judiciary and media oversight that previously sparked nationwide unrest.

## POLAND

*   **Diplomatic Friction with Ukraine:** A diplomatic dispute has emerged between Rome and Kyiv after several Italian politicians were placed on an official "blacklist" by Ukraine, prompting the Italian government to demand formal explanations.
*   **Kraków’s Stance on Ukraine Partnership:** The Kraków City Council, led by the Civic Coalition (KO), has rejected President Łukasz Gibała’s proposal to suspend the city's partnership with Lviv. The proposal was prompted by the Lviv Regional Council’s decision to designate 2027 as the "Year of the UPA," highlighting ongoing sensitivities regarding historical memory between the two nations.
