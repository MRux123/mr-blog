## WORLD

*   **Trump's Energy Ceasefire Proposal:** Ukrainian President Volodymyr Zelensky has expressed openness to a potential "energy ceasefire" proposed by Donald Trump. While Kyiv signals willingness to negotiate, Moscow has yet to provide an official response regarding the initiative to halt attacks on energy infrastructure.
*   **Escalation in Riyadh:** The Saudi Arabian capital continues to face security threats, with another Houthi-led strike on the Riyadh airport resulting in one fatality and 11 injuries. This incident marks the second such attack in as many days, highlighting the ongoing instability in the region.
*   **Violence at Sudan-Egypt Border:** Reports from human rights organizations indicate that dozens of gold prospectors were killed in military strikes on the Egypt-Sudan border. Neither government has officially commented on the incident, which occurred at an artisanal mining camp.

## POLAND

*   **Political Earthquake in Krakow:** Łukasz Gibała has been officially elected as the new President of Krakow, securing a decisive victory with 62.12% of the vote. His opponent, Monika Piątkowska, garnered 37.88%. The result is being analyzed as a significant setback for the ruling Koalicja Obywatelska (KO) coalition.
*   **National Political Fallout:** The outcome in Krakow has triggered intense debate regarding the strength of the current government. Political analysts and opposition figures suggest that the results serve as a warning to Prime Minister Donald Tusk, challenging the effectiveness of his personal endorsement in local elections.
*   **Tragedy in Bydgoszcz:** A 44-year-old man has died following a police intervention in Bydgoszcz. The circumstances surrounding the incident are currently under investigation, marking a somber end to a local police operation.
