## WORLD

*   **Security Incident in Romania:** A maritime drone explosion in the port of Constanta has triggered a "Red Intervention Plan." Authorities have recovered four additional uncrewed vessels in the area, prompting international concern regarding the expansion of regional hostilities into NATO-member waters.
*   **Political Instability in Armenia:** Prime Minister Nikol Pashinyan faces a critical re-election challenge amid reports of extensive Russian disinformation campaigns. Moscow is allegedly attempting to undermine his administration to maintain influence, as the country navigates a complex period involving issues of national sovereignty, democracy, and security.

## POLAND

*   **Diplomatic Friction over Historical Memory:** Polish Minister of National Defense Władysław Kosiniak-Kamysz has issued a strong appeal to Ukrainian leadership, condemning the state-level glorification of the UPA. The Minister stated that such policies are unacceptable to the Polish public, emphasizing that "true friendship requires truth," thereby highlighting a persistent tension between the two nations regarding historical narratives.
*   **Regional Geopolitical Shifts:** Recent reporting suggests a potential recalibration of U.S. defense policy in Europe, with Washington allegedly considering halting the sale of Tomahawk cruise missiles to Germany. This move is being interpreted by observers as a sign of renewed caution regarding "red lines" and a potential strategic shift aimed at managing escalation risks with Russia.
