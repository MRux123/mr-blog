## WORLD

*   **Escalating Regional Security Risks:** A Russian strike on a railway station near the Ukraine-Poland border has been characterized by international observers as a deliberate provocation aimed at Kyiv's Western allies. This tactical escalation occurs amidst heightened tensions, further underscored by a Saudi request for increased U.S. military support following an aggressive offensive by Iranian-backed Houthi forces targeting critical oil infrastructure.
*   **Economic Milestone in Africa:** Nigerian business magnate Aliko Dangote is preparing for what is projected to be the continent’s largest-ever initial public offering. The potential flotation of the Dangote Petroleum Refinery is expected to significantly reshape the regional energy market and substantially increase the industrialist's net worth.

## POLAND

*   **Security Incident in Rusinów:** The discovery of a Russian "Gerbera" type drone on a beach in Rusinów has prompted an official response from the Ministry of National Defence. Minister Władysław Kosiniak-Kamysz has called for public restraint as officials analyze the potential implications of the breach of airspace and its connection to the ongoing conflict in Ukraine.
*   **Political Realignment in Gdynia:** Mayor Aleksandra Kosiorek has formally announced the dissolution of the city's governing coalition. The move follows a period of mounting instability, with the mayor declaring that the coalition no longer serves the city's interests or her core policy objectives.
*   **Leadership Controversy in State Security:** President Karol Nawrocki has approved the dismissal of the head of the State Protection Service (SOP). The decision comes amidst high-profile internal scrutiny and reports of sensitive security failures involving the family of Prime Minister Donald Tusk.
