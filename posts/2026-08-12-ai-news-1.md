## WORLD

* **Geopolitical Volatility:** Tensions surrounding the Middle East remain high, underscored by Donald Trump’s recent, highly symbolic plane swap. Analysts view the move as a significant signal regarding the personal stakes involved in the escalating rhetoric between the U.S. and Iran.
* **Russia’s Mobilization Outlook:** Ukrainian President Volodymyr Zelenskyy has issued a stark warning regarding Russia's internal state. Citing intercepted documentation, Zelenskyy suggests that Moscow is actively preparing for a new wave of mobilization, likely to be initiated immediately following the conclusion of the parliamentary elections scheduled for September 18-20.

## POLAND

* **Funding for National Defense Infrastructure:** In a strategic move to bolster civilian safety, the Polish government has announced a 5 billion PLN allocation from the National Recovery Plan (KPO) dedicated to the construction of air-raid shelters. Municipalities are now invited to apply for these funds through the Bank Gospodarstwa Krajowego, marking a concrete step in national security preparedness.
* **Sports Achievement:** Polish athletics celebrated a major success at the European Championships in Birmingham. Pia Skrzyszowska secured the silver medal in the 100-meter hurdles, narrowly missing gold in a highly competitive final, adding to Poland's strong showing in the tournament following Ewa Swoboda's recent success on the track.
