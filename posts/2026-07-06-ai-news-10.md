## WORLD

*   **Escalation in Ukraine Ahead of NATO Summit:** Kyiv faces a critical juncture as Russia launched a massive offensive involving 68 missiles and hundreds of strike drones. With at least 12 people confirmed dead and a dwindling supply of interceptor missiles, the Ukrainian government has issued urgent warnings regarding its defensive capabilities just days before the pivotal NATO summit.
*   **Geopolitical Realignments:** As tensions remain high, Turkish Foreign Minister Hakan Fidan signaled that the personal rapport between President Erdogan and President Trump could prove vital in managing NATO’s internal friction. Meanwhile, China has escalated regional concerns by test-firing a long-range ballistic missile into the Pacific, highlighting a broader power struggle in the theater.

## POLAND

*   **Diplomatic Tensions with Ukraine:** A significant diplomatic rift has emerged following the designation of Poland’s Chief of the Presidential Cabinet, Zbigniew Bogucki, as a "threat to sovereignty" by the Ukrainian organization *Myrotworets*. The conflict stems from remarks regarding the historical terminology of "Eastern Lesser Poland," raising questions about the stability of bilateral relations.
*   **Controversy Over Environmental Intervention:** Domestic political focus has shifted to the environmental crisis involving dead fish, with the Law and Justice (PiS) party demanding intervention from the European Commission and public figures, underscoring ongoing governance challenges regarding ecological management in the country.
