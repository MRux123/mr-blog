## WORLD

* **Escalation of Russian air strikes on Kyiv:** Russia has launched a massive, coordinated air assault on the Ukrainian capital. The intensity of the attacks prompted President Volodymyr Zelensky to urgently cut short his official visit to Ireland and return to Kyiv to oversee the national defense response. Air defense systems are currently engaged in intercepting incoming threats, though debris continues to pose a significant risk to the city's infrastructure and civilians.
* **Geopolitical shifts in North American trade:** The United States has blocked the long-term renewal of the North American trade agreement. Instead of a 16-year commitment, the deal will now be subject to annual rolling reviews, signaling a shift toward more protectionist trade policies and creating uncertainty for regional markets.
* **Humanitarian rescue in Venezuela:** In a rare moment of relief following the recent deadly earthquakes, rescuers in Venezuela have pulled a two-year-old boy from the rubble six days after the catastrophe. The child is currently in the care of his aunt, who has vowed to provide the necessary support for his recovery.

## POLAND

* **Polish diplomatic push for increased US military presence:** The Polish government has intensified its diplomatic efforts in Washington, pushing for an accelerated deployment of American troops to Polish soil. Warsaw views this military fortification as a strategic necessity amidst the ongoing regional instability.
* **Tensions and dialogue in Polish-Ukrainian relations:** President Zelensky has signaled a shift in tone regarding the ongoing diplomatic friction with Poland. During his visit to Dublin, he stated that Ukraine is prepared to engage in open dialogue to resolve outstanding conflicts, emphasizing that both nations must remain focused on their shared goal of regional security in the face of a common aggressor.
* **Financial transparency in the Ministry of Health:** The Polish Ministry of Health has faced public scrutiny regarding the financial disclosures of its leadership. Following inquiries, the minister has publicly released details concerning 725,000 PLN identified as "other earned income," an move intended to address growing transparency concerns.
