## WORLD

* **Strait of Hormuz Closure:** The geopolitical situation in the Middle East has reached a critical juncture. Iran has reversed its decision to reopen the Strait of Hormuz, warning that the strategic waterway will remain blocked as long as the United States maintains its blockade on Iranian ports. This move has paralyzed a vital global shipping route, fueling fears of a broader economic crisis.
* **Qatar Faces Strategic Reckoning:** The war between the U.S. and Iran has pushed the gas-rich Gulf nation of Qatar into a state of "strategic shock." The conflict has severely impacted the nation’s economy, creating ripple effects that are being felt in global energy markets.
* **Gen Z as a Catalyst for Change:** Amid global uncertainty, Nepal has emerged as a focal point for youth-led political movements. Unlike many protests elsewhere that have failed to yield results, Nepal’s new government is actively attempting to address the demands of its younger generation, making the country a key case study for global democratic reform.

## POLAND

* **Archbishop Jędraszewski’s Controversial Remarks:** A sharp divide has emerged following comments made by Archbishop Marek Jędraszewski at Jasna Góra. The former Metropolitan of Krakow drew widespread criticism after comparing the current government's educational policies to those of Adolf Hitler, alleging a plan to turn young Poles into a "pliant mass for labor."
* **Escalating Political Infighting:** Internal tensions within the Law and Justice (PiS) party have spilled into the public domain, with prominent figures trading heated insults regarding their political strategies.
* **Emergency Response to Severe Weather:** The Polish government has initiated a series of crisis meetings following severe weather warnings. The Ministry of the Interior has placed services on high alert across Western and Northwestern provinces, including the Lower Silesian and West Pomeranian regions, in anticipation of heavy rainfall and potential flooding.
