## WORLD

*   **US Sanctions and Global Tensions:** Following President Trump's signing of a sweeping sanctions bill against Russia, named after the late Senator Lindsey Graham, global markets and diplomatic channels are bracing for impact. Moscow, via Deputy Chairman of the Security Council Dmitry Medvedev, has explicitly threatened retaliation, signaling an intensified phase of confrontation between the two powers.
*   **Greenland Security Agreement:** NATO has officially welcomed a new security deal between Denmark and the United States concerning Greenland. While the U.S. maintains the pact secures "permanent security control" over the territory, the local population remains deeply skeptical and anxious regarding the transparency and implications of the agreement, which followed months of geopolitical maneuvering.
*   **U.K.-Israel Diplomatic Friction:** Tensions between the United Kingdom and Israel have escalated significantly over the issue of West Bank settlements. The diplomatic breakdown stems from conflicting interpretations of promises regarding construction in the E1 area, a region considered critical to the viability of a future Palestinian state.

## POLAND

*   **Diplomatic Offensive Against Russian Influence:** High-ranking Polish officials have launched a global appeal for the closure of "Russian Houses," framing the initiative as part of an urgent "global battle for the human mind" to counteract Kremlin propaganda and foreign influence operations.
*   **Controversies in Political Circles:** The presence of Janusz Korwin-Mikke in Moscow has sparked domestic debate following his public comments praising the city, highlighting the ongoing political polarization within Poland regarding the country's stance toward the Russian Federation.
*   **Defense Logistics and International Constraints:** Reports indicating significant delays in U.S. weapon deliveries—reaching up to five years—have created concern in Warsaw. The shortfall, attributed to the depletion of Pentagon stockpiles, underscores the increasing difficulty of maintaining defense supply chains for regional allies amidst global instability.
