## WORLD

* **Middle East De-escalation:** Following a volatile exchange of fire, both Iran and Israel have signaled a temporary pause in direct hostilities. Prime Minister Benjamin Netanyahu confirmed that Israeli strikes are "on hold" after high-level communication with President Trump, though both nations maintain that they remain prepared to resume operations if threatened.
* **Catastrophic Earthquake in the Philippines:** A powerful 7.8 magnitude earthquake has struck the southern Philippines, resulting in at least 35 confirmed fatalities. The seismic event triggered tsunami warnings across the region, impacting coastal areas in Indonesia and Japan.
* **Xenophobic Unrest in South Africa:** Malawi has begun the repatriation of its citizens from South Africa due to escalating violence and xenophobic tensions, highlighting a growing humanitarian concern for migrant communities in the region.

## POLAND

* **Collapse of the FCAS Fighter Jet Program:** A major blow to European defense integration has been confirmed as the Future Combat Air System (FCAS) project—valued at over 100 billion euros—has officially ceased. The failure to align Franco-German industrial and political interests is viewed as a significant strategic and personal setback for President Emmanuel Macron.
* **Review of Presidential Honors:** The Chapter of the Order of the White Eagle has convened to discuss the status of the distinction previously awarded to Ukrainian President Volodymyr Zelensky. Presidential spokesperson Rafał Leśkiewicz indicated that a final decision on the matter is currently pending.
* **Aviation Tragedy in Bielsko-Biała:** Authorities are investigating a fatal accident involving a glider that crashed onto the runway at an airport in Bielsko-Biała. The incident has prompted an immediate inquiry into safety protocols.
