## WORLD

* **US Military Strikes Iran in Escalating Regional Conflict:** Following an attempted assault on American forces, U.S. Central Command has launched a new wave of military strikes against Iran. President Trump has vowed a "very hard" response, signaling a significant intensification of hostilities in the Middle East.
* **Tragedy in Southern Europe as Wildfires Claim Lives:** Three Greek firefighters have perished while battling catastrophic wildfires. Gale-force winds on the island of Crete have fueled the inferno, forcing the emergency evacuation of multiple villages as the region struggles to contain the blazes.
* **Conviction in Salman Rushdie Case:** Hadi Matar, 28, has been found guilty of terror-related offenses. The court determined that Matar’s attack on British-Indian author Salman Rushdie was an attempt to provide material support to Hezbollah.

## POLAND

* **Poland Monitors Escalating U.S.-Iran Tensions:** Polish media outlets are closely tracking the unfolding military confrontation between the United States and Iran. Government officials and public broadcasters are emphasizing President Trump’s warning of a "very hard" retaliatory strike, highlighting the potential implications for global stability and national security.
