## WORLD

*   **Escalation in Ukraine:** Russian missile attacks near Kyiv have resulted in the tragic death of three civilians, including a child. This strike highlights the increasingly precarious security situation in Ukraine, particularly as President Zelensky has publicly warned of critical shortages in air defense interceptors, leaving urban centers vulnerable to continued aerial bombardment.
*   **Security Incidents in Germany:** German military authorities have reported multiple sightings of unidentified drones hovering over the Bundeswehr base in Mechernich. The facility, which plays a strategic role in maintaining Patriot missile systems, has been subject to increased scrutiny following these incursions, raising questions about potential espionage or tactical reconnaissance by foreign actors.

## POLAND

*   **Political Debate on Immigration:** The Law and Justice (PiS) party has intensified its calls for the deportation of foreign nationals, prompting a public release of government data regarding the employment status of Ukrainian citizens in Poland. This move marks a significant escalation in the ongoing national discourse concerning labor migration and social policy.
*   **Proposed Legislative Changes for Traffic Enforcement:** The government is currently drafting new regulations aimed at closing loopholes that have historically allowed drivers to avoid fines captured by speed cameras. These reforms signal a stricter approach to road safety and administrative accountability for traffic violations.
