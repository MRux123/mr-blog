## WORLD

*   **Geopolitical Crisis in the Middle East:** Military analysts warn of a severe depletion in US defense stockpiles following weeks of active engagement with Iran. Reports indicate that a significant portion of Patriot and THAAD interceptor missiles has been exhausted, with Pentagon sources suggesting that replenishing these critical assets will require years of industrial effort. Meanwhile, President Donald Trump has linked the potential for a diplomatic resolution to the continued naval blockade of Iranian ports and the Strait of Hormuz.
*   **Climate Change and Democratic Stability:** A landmark report from the Conflict Insights Group and other researchers highlights an emerging, non-traditional threat to global democracy. Data suggests that climate-related disasters—including floods, wildfires, and extreme heatwaves—have disrupted at least 94 elections across 52 nations over the last two decades. The report posits that the climate crisis is increasingly acting as a destabilizing force that undermines the integrity and accessibility of electoral processes worldwide.
*   **Mercenary Involvement in Sudan:** New research into the Sudanese conflict has exposed the deep entanglement of international actors, with phone tracking data linking Colombian mercenaries to the Rapid Support Forces (RSF). The findings further implicate the United Arab Emirates in providing logistical and material support, illustrating the complexity and transnational nature of the ongoing humanitarian catastrophe in Sudan.

## POLAND

*   **Public Trust in the Constitutional Tribunal:** Recent polling data from CBOS indicates a significant crisis of confidence regarding the Constitutional Tribunal (TK). Over 55% of Polish citizens surveyed expressed a lack of trust in the institution, reflecting deep-seated societal divisions regarding the rule of law and the judicial system in Poland.
*   **Political Frictions within the Ruling Coalition:** Internal instability continues to plague the governing alliance, with internal accusations of unprofessional conduct and disloyalty. Specifically, Poland 2050 leadership has publicly criticized members of the Centrum political camp, highlighting a growing rift that threatens to hamper the government's legislative effectiveness and internal cohesion.
