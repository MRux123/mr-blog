## WORLD

*   **Escalation in the Iran Conflict:** Tensions in the Middle East have reached a critical point as Iranian forces continue strikes on energy infrastructure, including the Mina Al-Ahmadi refinery in Kuwait, despite direct warnings from the U.S. administration. The conflict is already causing significant shifts in global economic policy, with the U.S. Treasury Department contemplating the release of sanctioned Iranian oil reserves to stabilize global fuel prices.
*   **Industrial Tragedy in South Korea:** A massive fire at an automotive parts factory in Daejeon has left at least 53 people injured and 14 missing. The incident, which triggered a nationwide emergency response, highlights ongoing concerns regarding industrial safety in the region's manufacturing sector.
*   **Diplomatic Faux Pas:** U.S.-Japan relations faced an awkward moment following a reported "joke" by President Donald Trump regarding the attack on Pearl Harbor, made during a meeting with Japanese Prime Minister Sanae Takaichi. The incident has drawn attention to the unpredictable nature of current high-level diplomatic exchanges.

## POLAND

*   **Key Judicial Ruling on Marriage Equality:** The Supreme Administrative Court (NSA) has issued a landmark decision concerning same-sex marriages, a move expected to have significant legal and social implications for the future of civil rights legislation in Poland.
*   **Political Shifts and Government Integrity:** The political landscape is being shaken by the resignation of Łukasz Mejza from his parliamentary immunity amidst serious allegations of legal misconduct. Simultaneously, Prime Minister Donald Tusk has touted a "revolution" in EU-Poland relations, claiming that European institutions are now closely aligned with Polish policy priorities following recent summits.
*   **Security Concerns:** Local authorities have uncovered organized, paramilitary-style training activities in the Mietków Reservoir area, where a group of over 80 individuals disguised as hikers were found to be preparing for violent confrontations, marking a concerning trend in radicalized activities.
