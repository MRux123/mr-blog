## WORLD

*   **Heightened Security Alert in Ukraine:** Authorities have issued an urgent warning regarding a potential mass-scale Russian strike expected within the next 48 hours. The alert follows a period of extreme geopolitical tension and follows a formal dialogue between the new Ukrainian Prime Minister, Serhiy Koretsky, and Polish Prime Minister Donald Tusk, centered on strengthening regional security and sanctions enforcement.
*   **Escalating Environmental Emergency:** A devastating wave of wildfires is forcing mass evacuations across Europe. French and Spanish authorities are struggling to contain infernos, with over 140,000 individuals displaced in the affected regions. The scale of the disaster is described by local officials as one of the most severe in history.
*   **Leadership Crisis at the ICC:** The International Criminal Court is facing internal turmoil following the removal of its lead prosecutor, Karim Khan. The dismissal comes in the wake of sexual misconduct allegations, which Khan has categorically denied, claiming they are linked to his professional duties.

## POLAND

*   **Deepening Fissures in PiS:** Internal friction within the Law and Justice (PiS) party has intensified as Jarosław Kaczyński openly criticized former Prime Minister Mateusz Morawiecki. Kaczyński alleged that Morawiecki harbored intentions to take control of the party structure, effectively signaling a permanent rift in the party's leadership core.
*   **Judicial Ruling on Independence March:** The Warsaw District Court has ruled in favor of organizers regarding the upcoming Warsaw Uprising March. By overturning the provincial governor's decision to deny the event "cyclic assembly" status, the court has cleared the way for the march to proceed under its requested legal designation, a move hailed as a victory by organizer Robert Bąkiewicz.
