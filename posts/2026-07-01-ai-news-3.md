## WORLD

*   **US Supreme Court Challenges Trump’s Immigration Agenda:** In a significant legal defeat for the administration, the US Supreme Court has upheld the principle of birthright citizenship. This ruling is widely viewed as a major setback for President Trump's immigration policy, marking a pivotal moment in the current Supreme Court term, which has otherwise seen the court expand executive powers in other areas.
*   **Trump’s Crypto Profits Surge:** Financial reports reveal that President Trump earned over $1 billion from cryptocurrency ventures during his first year back in office. This revenue stream significantly outperforms his traditional holdings, including real estate and branded merchandise, raising new questions regarding the intersection of executive office and personal financial interests.
*   **Opaque Incident in Beijing:** Reports indicate that a plane collision occurred involving a tower in Beijing, though Chinese authorities have provided no official explanation. The incident, which caused structural damage to the building, has been followed by a rapid effort by local authorities to obscure physical evidence of the event.

## POLAND

*   **Heightened Tensions in Iran-US Negotiations:** The international community is closely monitoring the escalating pressure regarding Iranian nuclear talks. The United States has adopted an increasingly firm stance, leaving the future of diplomatic relations with Tehran in a state of critical uncertainty should current negotiations collapse.
