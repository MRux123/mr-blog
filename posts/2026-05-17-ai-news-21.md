## WORLD

*   **Trump’s Ultimatum to Iran:** President Donald Trump has significantly intensified rhetoric toward Tehran, warning that "time is running out" for a peace agreement. Reports suggest the U.S. administration may be preparing for further strategic action if a deal is not reached, with high-level meetings regarding potential military options rumored for the coming days.
*   **Aviation Tragedy in the U.S.:** A fatal mid-air collision involving two fighter jets occurred during an air show at Mountain Home Air Force Base in Idaho. The incident, captured on video, has prompted an immediate investigation into the safety protocols of military aerial demonstrations.
*   **Espionage Crackdown in Norway:** Norwegian authorities have arrested a Chinese national on charges of espionage. This development in the Bodoe region—an area of high strategic and military significance—marks the second such arrest in the country recently, signaling growing concerns over foreign intelligence activities in Scandinavia.

## POLAND

*   **Unidentified Drone Crash in Lithuania:** A military-grade drone, suspected to be of Ukrainian origin, has crashed in the Uytanian region of Lithuania. Local authorities, including the National Crisis Management Center, are investigating the circumstances, as this marks the second incident of this nature in recent months, raising questions about regional airspace security and military border protocols.
