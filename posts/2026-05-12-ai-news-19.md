## WORLD

*   **Heightened Tensions in the Middle East:** President Trump has issued a stern ultimatum to Iran, demanding a new diplomatic deal to prevent renewed military action. The situation remains volatile as regional instability deepens following the death of ten individuals, including paramedics, in recent Israeli strikes in Lebanon.
*   **UK Bolsters Persian Gulf Security:** In a coordinated effort to secure global shipping lanes, the United Kingdom is deploying a significant military package—including the HMS Dragon destroyer, Typhoon fighters, and advanced mine-clearing drones—to the Strait of Hormuz, supported by international allies.
*   **Political Instability in London:** Prime Minister Keir Starmer is facing mounting pressure to resign following substantial losses for the Labour Party in recent local elections. Despite the internal dissent, Starmer has vowed to remain in office and continue his agenda.

## POLAND

*   **NATO Tactical Gaps Exposed:** Recent military exercises on the island of Gotland, which included Ukrainian forces for the first time, have highlighted critical areas for improvement in NATO's defensive capabilities. The simulated scenario of defending Sweden against a Russian invasion underscored that current alliance readiness requires modernization to meet contemporary warfare standards.
*   **Judicial Appointments Controversy:** A parliamentary justice committee has finalized the list of 15 judicial candidates for the National Council of the Judiciary (KRS). The list includes controversial figures such as Łukasz Piebiak, sparking a political debate ahead of the upcoming Sejm vote this Friday.
*   **Strategic Defense Discussions:** President Karol Nawrocki has signaled Poland’s openness to an increased U.S. military presence, following high-level discussions with President Trump. The move aligns with broader efforts to bolster the country’s security architecture in the region.
