## WORLD

* **Escalating Ebola Crisis in DR Congo:** The Democratic Republic of the Congo is grappling with a severe and deadly outbreak of the Ebola virus, now involving a rare strain in conflict-affected regions. With at least 100 deaths reported and international exposure confirmed, health experts are raising alarms regarding the world’s shrinking resilience to infectious diseases, urging immediate investment in global pandemic preparedness.
* **Middle East Tensions and Strategic Shifts:** Geopolitical instability is deepening as the U.S. intensifies pressure on Iran regarding its nuclear program, with President Trump warning that the "clock is ticking." Simultaneously, the blockade of the Strait of Hormuz is triggering economic realignments, creating unexpected opportunities for regional players like Syria to leverage their geographic position.
* **North Korean Militarization:** In a move signaling further regional isolation, Kim Jong Un has ordered the North Korean military to fortify the border with South Korea, explicitly directing that the frontier be transformed into an "impenetrable fortress."

## POLAND

* **Polish Citizens Detained in Gaza Flotilla:** A diplomatic incident has unfolded following the Israeli military's interception of the "Sumud" humanitarian flotilla. Polish nationals were reported among the crew members, with at least one individual confirmed to be in detention. The situation remains fluid as the Polish Ministry of Foreign Affairs seeks to provide consular support.
* **Pentagon Clarification on U.S. Troops:** Following public confusion regarding the deployment of American military personnel in Poland, the Pentagon has issued a formal statement. Officials clarified that the status of the U.S. brigade in the region was not a result of a last-minute decision, aiming to stabilize the narrative surrounding Poland's national security commitments.
* **Legal Accountability in High-Profile Case:** The judicial system in Poland has moved against lawyer Paweł K., who was previously embroiled in a controversial legal case following a fatal accident. Following his arrest after a period as a fugitive, the court has denied lenient sentencing terms and he has been formally removed from the registry of practicing attorneys.
