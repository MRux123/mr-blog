## WORLD

*   **Major Escalation in the Persian Gulf:** The regional conflict has intensified significantly as Iran launched a barrage of missiles and drones targeting Bahrain and Kuwait. This development comes just as the U.S. Secretary of State, Marco Rubio, suggested the Iran war was reaching a conclusion, marking a major intelligence and diplomatic failure.
*   **Lebanese Ceasefire Under Strain:** Israeli airstrikes have resulted in nine casualties in Lebanon following rocket fire from Hezbollah, directly challenging the fragile partial ceasefire established earlier this week.
*   **Ebola Crisis in DRC:** The World Health Organization has issued a warning regarding an Ebola outbreak in the Democratic Republic of the Congo, which may have originated as early as January. Director-General Tedros Adhanom Ghebreyesus cited high levels of community mistrust and restrictive travel policies as primary obstacles to containment.

## POLAND

*   **Legal Proceedings Against Zbigniew Ziobro:** The National Prosecutor's Office has filed a motion to exclude Judge Dariusz Drajewicz from the appellate proceedings regarding the European Arrest Warrant (EAW) for Zbigniew Ziobro. The prosecution argues that there are justified concerns regarding the judge's impartiality.
*   **Arson Incident in Płock:** A 66-year-old suspect has confessed to charges related to a major hall fire in Płock, marking a significant development in the local investigation.
