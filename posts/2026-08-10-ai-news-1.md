## WORLD

* **Unidentified Drones Over German Military Facilities:** German authorities have launched an investigation following reports of drone sightings over a military base housing components of the Patriot missile defense system. The incident occurs in a heightened security climate, shortly after a suspicious bomb-related event in Leipzig, raising concerns regarding potential sabotage and the vulnerability of NATO-aligned infrastructure.

* **Prosecution Case Links South African Special Forces to Detective’s Murder:** Leaked details from a prosecution case, obtained by the BBC, point to the alleged involvement of South African special forces in the assassination of high-ranking detective Frans Mathipa. The revelations suggest a deep-seated conspiracy within security apparatuses, as prosecutors prepare to bring eight accused men to trial for the fatal shooting of Mathipa while he was driving.
