## WORLD

* **Massive Drone Strike on Moscow:** Ukrainian forces launched a significant aerial offensive against the Russian capital, with reports confirming 108 drones intercepted. The strike caused widespread disruption, forcing a temporary halt to operations at all major Moscow airports, marking the largest-scale attack on the city this year.
* **Energy Crisis Deepens in Iran:** Tensions remain high as the Strait of Hormuz remains largely closed to U.S. and allied vessels. U.S. Energy Secretary Chris Wright warned that global oil prices are unlikely to drop in the near term, prompting President Trump to urge international partners to deploy warships to secure the critical maritime passage.
* **Middle East Security Deterioration:** IDF operations continue along the Lebanese and West Bank borders, resulting in significant civilian casualties. Simultaneously, reports indicate that Israel is in advanced talks with Somaliland to establish a strategic military base near the Gulf of Aden to counter Houthi insurgent activity in the region.

## POLAND

* **Political Ties to Budapest:** Polish politicians Romanowski and Bąkiewicz attended a rally in Hungary alongside Viktor Orbán, emphasizing a joint ideological struggle for the sovereignty and values of both nations.
* **Tragic Road Accident:** Local authorities in the Gorzów region confirmed a fatal collision involving two heavy goods vehicles and a passenger car, resulting in two deaths.
* **Deepening Concerns Over Social Media Pathology:** Polsat News has launched a high-profile investigative report into the "patostreaming" industry, exposing how creators exploit vulnerable individuals for profit while navigating the persistent inadequacies of the current legal and regulatory framework in Poland.
