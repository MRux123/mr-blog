## WORLD

* **Escalation in the Iran-US Conflict:** Tensions in the Middle East have reached a critical point as Iran largely closes the Strait of Hormuz. U.S. officials warn that energy prices are likely to remain elevated, while the recent loss of six US crew members in a plane crash during combat operations against Iran highlights the deepening military engagement.
* **Massive Drone Strike on Moscow:** In a significant shift in the Russo-Ukrainian conflict, Russian authorities reported a major aerial assault involving 108 drones targeting the capital. The attack forced the suspension of flight operations at all airports surrounding Moscow, marking the largest such breach of Russian airspace this year.
* **Instability in the Pakistani-Afghan Border Region:** The border conflict has intensified, with Pakistani airstrikes reported to have killed at least 75 civilians and displaced over 115,000 people. Both sides have abandoned diplomatic efforts, signaling a protracted and dangerous escalation in the region.

## POLAND

* **Succession Controversy in Iran Reported by Intelligence:** New intelligence findings have surfaced regarding the leadership in Iran, revealing that the late Ayatollah Ali Khamenei had strong reservations about his son, Mojtaba, succeeding him, allegedly describing him as lacking the necessary intellect. Despite these objections, Mojtaba has ascended to the role of Supreme Leader.
* **Fatal Collision Near Gorzów:** A tragic road accident occurred involving two trucks and a passenger vehicle, resulting in two fatalities. Local authorities are currently investigating the circumstances of the crash.
* **Political Polarization and Activism:** Polish public discourse remains focused on international alliances and internal political struggles, evidenced by figures such as Romanowski and Bąkiewicz participating in recent political demonstrations in Hungary, framing their activities as a battle for national sovereignty.
