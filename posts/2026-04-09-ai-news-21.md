## WORLD

*   **Heightened Middle East Volatility:** Israel has initiated direct talks with Lebanon; however, the lack of a formal ceasefire agreement keeps the region on a knife-edge. Simultaneously, Iran’s supreme leadership has signaled a strategic shift regarding the Strait of Hormuz, vowing to "seek revenge" following recent escalations, casting a shadow over international shipping and security.
*   **Ukrainian Peace Prospects:** President Volodymyr Zelensky has acknowledged a narrow window of opportunity for peace talks regarding the conflict in Ukraine. While diplomatic channels may reopen, the atmosphere remains skeptical as both sides grapple with the complexities of ending the prolonged hostilities.

## POLAND

*   **Russian Easter Ceasefire:** Russian authorities have announced a short-term, temporary ceasefire in Ukraine scheduled for the Orthodox Easter period, spanning from the afternoon of April 11 through the following day. 
*   **Healthcare Ethics Controversy:** A scandal involving preferential medical treatment has erupted, as the director of a district hospital in Aleksandrów Kujawski confirmed that the son of a prominent Coalition Civic (KO) senator underwent a procedure out of turn. Official records regarding the incident are currently missing, fueling public outcry over potential nepotism in the healthcare system.
*   **Baltic Security Strains:** Polish Air Force F-16s have been mobilized again as regional tensions over the Baltic Sea continue to fluctuate, highlighting the ongoing defensive readiness of the Polish military in response to current geopolitical volatility.
