## WORLD

* **Escalating Civil Unrest in France:** Student-led protests have intensified, spreading across more than 40 French cities. What began as a localized demand for better educational resources has evolved into a broader movement involving teachers, parents, and union members, signaling deepening societal dissatisfaction.
* **Geopolitical Tensions in the Black Sea:** A merchant vessel has sunk following a drone attack in the Black Sea, further escalating security concerns as Russia intensifies its naval strikes. The incident underscores the volatile maritime environment in the region amid the ongoing war in Ukraine.
* **Espionage Scandal in Germany:** The arrest of a former German intelligence chief on charges of espionage and treason marks a significant security breach. The official is accused of funneling classified information to a foreign power, an event expected to have far-reaching implications for European intelligence cooperation.

## POLAND

* **Economic and Political Challenges for Ukraine's EU Accession:** Polish and European authorities are grappling with Ukraine's rejection of potential agricultural sales limits. The Ukrainian government maintains that such restrictions are fundamentally incompatible with the principles of EU membership, creating a complex diplomatic hurdle for both Warsaw and Brussels.
* **Internal Political Disputes:** The ongoing legal and political confrontation regarding the status of politician Marcin Romanowski continues to dominate domestic discourse, with members of the opposition characterizing the prosecution's actions as a performative "spectacle" rather than a pursuit of justice.
* **Metropolitan Infrastructure Ambitions:** Plans for the development of the Kraków metro system have hit financial obstacles. Officials are openly considering government loans to bridge the funding gap, highlighting the difficulty of balancing ambitious urban development with the city's current fiscal constraints.
