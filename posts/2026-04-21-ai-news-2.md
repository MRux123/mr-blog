## WORLD

* **Geopolitical Standoff in the Strait of Hormuz:** President Trump has declared that the U.S. will maintain the blockade of the Strait of Hormuz until a formal agreement is reached with Iran. The situation remains volatile, with mixed signals emanating from Tehran regarding potential peace negotiations in Pakistan. U.S. Vice President JD Vance is expected to lead a diplomatic delegation to facilitate discussions, a move that could prove pivotal in de-escalating the regional crisis.

* **Leadership Transition at Apple:** Technology giant Apple has announced a significant change in its executive structure. Tim Cook is stepping up to the role of Executive Chairman, while John Ternus has been named as the company's new Chief Executive Officer. This transition marks a new era for one of the world's most valuable companies as it navigates evolving market demands.

* **Tragedy at Teotihuacán:** A Canadian national was killed during a shooting incident at the historic Teotihuacán pyramid complex in Mexico. Local authorities are currently investigating the attack, which also resulted in several injuries, casting a shadow over the popular tourist site.
