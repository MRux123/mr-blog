## WORLD

* **Tragic Escalation in Ukraine:** Russian forces launched a series of drone strikes targeting critical civilian infrastructure, including a maternity hospital in Odesa. President Volodymyr Zelenskyy condemned the assault as an act of "pure terror," noting that the strikes lacked any military objective. Casualties, including injuries to children, have been reported across Odesa, Kryvyi Rih, and the Poltava region.
* **Casualties Among Journalists in Lebanon:** In a separate conflict zone, three Lebanese journalists have been confirmed killed in an Israeli military strike. The Israeli Defense Forces have acknowledged the targeted killing of Ali Shoeib, a journalist associated with the Hezbollah-affiliated Al Manar TV, marking a significant escalation in the regional media landscape of the conflict.

## POLAND

* **Political Conflict Over Fuel Pricing:** A heated dispute has erupted between the opposition, led by Jarosław Kaczyński, and the current government regarding fuel prices. Kaczyński has accused Orlen of artificial price inflation and obstructing legislative efforts to lower costs. In response, government officials, including Minister Miłosz Motyka, have defended current policies, promising that price adjustments at the pump—driven by new excise regulations and price caps—will become visible to consumers starting Tuesday.
* **Concerns Over Polish Geographical Indications:** Trade tensions have surfaced following the recent EU-Australia trade agreement. Diplomatic circles are expressing frustration as, once again, only two Polish products—"Polska Wódka" and "Żubrówka"—have been included in the list of protected geographical indications. This development mirrors similar concerns raised during previous negotiations with the Mercosur bloc, fueling debate over Poland’s effectiveness in protecting its national branding in international trade deals.
