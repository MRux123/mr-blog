## WORLD

* **Strait of Hormuz Blockade:** Iran has announced the closure of the Strait of Hormuz, citing Israeli military strikes in Lebanon as the primary catalyst. While Tehran asserts control over the waterway, the US military has publicly disputed the claim. The situation remains highly volatile ahead of scheduled high-level talks between US and Iranian delegations in Switzerland.
* **Middle East Conflict Intensifies:** Recent Israeli airstrikes in Gaza have resulted in six fatalities, including a journalist identified by the Israeli military as a combatant—a claim currently unsupported by external evidence. These developments have escalated regional instability, drawing global focus to the renewed fighting and the potential for a wider confrontation in the region.

## POLAND

* **Diplomatic Fallout Over State Honors:** A diplomatic row has erupted between Warsaw and Kyiv following President Karol Nawrocki’s decision to revoke the Order of the White Eagle awarded to Volodymyr Zelenskyy. In a show of solidarity, former Ukrainian presidents Leonid Kuchma, Viktor Yushchenko, and Petro Poroszenko have officially renounced their Polish state honors.
* **Internal and International Reaction:** The decision has drawn mixed reactions; while figures like former Ukrainian PM Arseniy Yatsenyuk urge restraint, Polish political figures like Leszek Miller have responded sharply, suggesting that such symbolic gestures should be accompanied by the return of military aid. Meanwhile, German Ambassador Miguel Berger has publicly criticized the move, dismissing claims that Germany intends to leverage this diplomatic friction to secure Ukrainian reconstruction contracts.
