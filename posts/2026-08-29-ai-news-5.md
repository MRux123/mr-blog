## WORLD

* **Trump Secures Major Venezuelan Oil Agreement:** Donald Trump has announced a "historic" deal granting the United States control over 65 billion barrels of Venezuelan oil. The agreement, supported by the Venezuelan interim government, is framed as a critical step toward economic revitalization for the South American nation, though it marks a significant shift in regional energy geopolitics.
* **Escalating Humanitarian Crisis in Asia:** The scale of the flood disaster in Nepal continues to grow, with authorities confirming that 2,400 people are currently missing. Simultaneously, rescue operations in China remain underway as over 550 individuals are unaccounted for, with officials expressing deep concerns regarding potential secondary flooding in Himalayan regions.
* **Nordic Military Integration:** In response to heightened regional instability, Sweden has committed to bolster Finland’s defense capabilities. Under a new military pact, Stockholm will deploy Gripen fighter jets and Visby-class corvettes to assist Helsinki in surveillance and border security through the end of the year.

## POLAND

* **Arrest of PKOl President:** The Polish judicial system has acted decisively in the high-profile case involving Radosław Piesiewicz, the president of the Polish Olympic Committee (PKOl). Following a night-long hearing, the court ordered a three-month arrest, citing a high probability of criminal activity, the risk of witness tampering, and the severity of the potential sentence.
* **Extreme Weather Alerts:** Polish authorities have placed the Territorial Defence Forces (WOT) on high alert as the country faces widespread, severe weather warnings. Emergency services are preparing for potential disruptions as extreme conditions move across the nation.
