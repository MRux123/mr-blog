## WORLD

* **Israel-Lebanon Cease-Fire:** Israel has reached an agreement on a 10-day cease-fire in Lebanon. While the pause halts attacks on Hezbollah, Prime Minister Benjamin Netanyahu confirmed that Israeli troops will maintain their positions without withdrawing. The move is viewed as a significant development that could potentially de-escalate the broader regional conflict involving the U.S. and Iran.
* **Bulgarian Electoral Integrity:** Bulgarian authorities have launched a massive crackdown on election irregularities ahead of the upcoming parliamentary vote. Over 500 individuals have been detained, as the Interior Ministry warns against widespread attempts to "trade democracy" through the illegal buying of votes.

## POLAND

* **Internal Divisions in PiS:** Former Prime Minister Mateusz Morawiecki’s move to establish a new, independent association named "Rozwój Plus" has triggered a sharp reaction from the Law and Justice (PiS) leadership. Party officials have labeled the initiative as an extra-party move that stands in direct contradiction to the party's statute, signaling deepening internal tensions within the conservative camp.
* **Legal Recognition of Same-Sex Marriages:** Włodzimierz Czarzasty, Marshal of the Sejm and leader of the Left, has announced that municipal offices governed by the Left will move to implement European and national court rulings regarding the recognition of same-sex marriages concluded abroad. This initiative aims to facilitate the transcription of marriage certificates, reflecting a push for state protection of diverse family structures.
* **Controversy over Presidential Advisory Body:** The appointment of a social media influencer to President Nawrocki’s Council for New Media has sparked intense public and political backlash. Critics, including Sławomir Cenckiewicz, have pointed to the nominee's alleged history of promoting Russian disinformation, raising concerns about the security and credibility of the newly formed presidential advisory council.
