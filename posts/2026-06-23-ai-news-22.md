## WORLD

* **Nationwide Rail Paralysis in Germany:** Deutsche Bahn, Germany's primary rail operator, has suspended all train services across the country due to a critical IT malfunction. Reports indicate that a failure in the radio communication system has forced operators to halt trains at the nearest stations, leading to significant logistical disruption. The incident is currently being treated as a major infrastructure emergency, with authorities working to restore full connectivity.

## POLAND

* **Szymon Hołownia Steps Down as Deputy Chairman of Poland 2050:** In a significant shift within the Polish political landscape, Szymon Hołownia has resigned from his position as deputy chairman of the Poland 2050 party. Sources suggest this move follows the recent establishment of an online social media group titled "Umiarkowani" (The Moderates) by the politician, sparking speculation regarding a potential shift in his political strategy and the future internal direction of the party.
