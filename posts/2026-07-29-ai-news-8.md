## WORLD

* **Earthquake Devastates Kyushu, Japan:** A powerful earthquake has struck the island of Kyushu, leaving at least 13 people dead. Rescue operations are currently in a critical "race against time" as emergency teams struggle to locate and reach survivors trapped beneath the debris of collapsed structures.
* **Escalation in the Middle East:** Saudi Arabia has joined U.S.-led military operations against Iran-backed militias in Iraq. The strikes come in direct response to a series of coordinated attacks by proxy groups targeting U.S. military bases and critical Saudi energy infrastructure.

## POLAND

* **Major Anti-Corruption Operation in Mazovian Hospitals:** The Central Anti-Corruption Bureau (CBA) has launched a significant crackdown across hospitals in the Mazovian region. Several individuals have been detained as authorities investigate irregularities and alleged criminal activity within the healthcare sector.
* **Government Announces New Ministerial Role:** The Polish government has officially established a new position of a "plenipotentiary," aimed at streamlining administrative functions. Officials have emphasized that the role is intended to be non-political, focusing strictly on its designated mandate.
