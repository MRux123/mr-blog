## WORLD

* **US-China Standoff Over Taiwan:** President Trump has explicitly framed potential arms sales to Taiwan as a "negotiating chip" in ongoing diplomatic discussions with Beijing. This rhetoric marks a significant shift in U.S. foreign policy, casting doubt on the long-term reliability of American security commitments in the Indo-Pacific region.
* **Geopolitical Stakes in the Strait of Hormuz:** As the critical waterway remains blocked due to regional instability, European powers have entered direct negotiations with Iran. The initiative aims to restore international shipping lanes, highlighting Europe's renewed attempt to act as a stabilizer in the Middle East energy corridor.
* **Humanitarian Crisis in Nigeria:** More than 50 schoolchildren, including toddlers, have been abducted in Borno state. The scale of the kidnapping has devastated the town of Mussa, with no militant group yet claiming responsibility for the atrocity, underscoring the ongoing security vacuum in the region.

## POLAND

* **Political Realignment in the Opposition:** Former Prime Minister Mateusz Morawiecki has signaled a push for unity within the Law and Justice (PiS) party, announcing an upcoming collaborative event with Przemysław Czarnek. The move is aimed at consolidating the party’s base and preparing for future electoral challenges.
* **Power Transition in Hungary Impacting Local Discourse:** The political shift in Budapest, marked by the new administration's decision to deny Viktor Orbán his severance pay, is being closely monitored in Poland. The incident, framed by the new Hungarian leadership as a measure against corruption, provides a sharp contrast to local political debates regarding state funding and accountability.
* **Heightened Rhetoric on Border Security:** Presidential candidate Karol Nawrocki has taken a firm stance regarding the situation at the Polish border. Defending the actions of the Border Guard against recent criticisms, Nawrocki emphasized that there will be no apologies for state security efforts during the hybrid attacks, further fueling the polarization surrounding national security policy.
