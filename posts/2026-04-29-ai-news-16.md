## WORLD

* **Oil Prices Spike Amid Middle East Instability:** Global markets have reacted sharply to regional volatility, with crude oil prices soaring to $119 per barrel. This follows reports of a potentially extended blockade involving Iran, exacerbating fears of supply chain disruptions and sustained geopolitical uncertainty in the Middle East.
* **Russia Scales Back Victory Day Commemorations:** In a move signaling internal security concerns or military constraints, the Kremlin has announced a significant reduction in the annual World War II Victory Day parade. The event will proceed without the traditional display of military hardware or cadet participation, citing threats stemming from the ongoing conflict in Ukraine.

## POLAND

* **Political Realignment in the Sejm:** The Law and Justice (PiS) parliamentary club has confirmed the departure of MP Łukasz Mejza. While party spokespeople describe the move as an official decision, Mejza has characterized the exit as a voluntary choice made in the name of his political convictions, marking another shift in the current legislative landscape.
* **EU Legal Action Regarding the Oder River:** The European Commission has officially initiated legal proceedings against Poland concerning environmental restoration efforts for the Oder River. The Commission asserts that Warsaw has failed to adequately address the ecological damage caused by the 2022 environmental catastrophe, potentially setting the stage for future litigation at the European Court of Justice (TSUE).
* **Violent Attack on Local Government Official:** Law enforcement authorities are investigating a severe assault in Stara Kornica, where a local government official (wójt) was attacked with a sharp object. The victim required urgent medical evacuation via an LPR helicopter, and the suspect is currently in police custody.
