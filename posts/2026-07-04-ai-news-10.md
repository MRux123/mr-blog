## WORLD

*   **Political Transition in Iran:** The funeral proceedings for Ayatollah Khamenei have commenced in Tehran, drawing massive crowds. While the state displays public mourning, behind-the-scenes political analysts note deep divisions among the leadership, further exacerbated by the notable absence of Mojtaba Khamenei from the state proceedings, raising critical questions about the future of the nation’s power structure.
*   **Election Results in Peru:** Keiko Fujimori has been officially declared the winner of the presidential election in Peru. The result, which follows weeks of uncertainty and a tightly contested race, concludes a volatile period in the country's political landscape following the tenure of her father, Alberto Fujimori.
*   **UK Foreign Aid Shift:** In a significant policy reversal, the British government has terminated the "Strengthening higher education for female empowerment" (SHEFE) program. The move, coming just two years after the project's inception, results in the cancellation of a £45m initiative designed to support the education of 1 million girls across Asia, Africa, and the Middle East.

## POLAND

*   **Countering Disinformation:** Security agencies have flagged a new strategic threat involving planned Russian state-sponsored information operations aimed at distorting historical narratives regarding Volhynia. The move is viewed as a calculated attempt to incite social friction and manipulate public opinion.
*   **Diplomatic Engagement:** Prime Minister Donald Tusk has received official correspondence from former Ukrainian President Viktor Yushchenko. The communication initiates a high-level dialogue regarding the next steps in bilateral cooperation and potential strategic shifts in Ukraine’s regional policy.
*   **Investigation into Domestic Tragedies:** Local authorities are currently handling two high-profile criminal investigations. In Skawina, investigators have identified a male suspect in connection with a recent family tragedy, while police continue an active search for a husband following the death of a 64-year-old woman in the vicinity of Kielce.
