## WORLD

* **US-Iran Diplomatic Tensions Over Nuclear Inspections:** The United States has eased sanctions on Iranian oil, yet the diplomatic situation remains fragile. Conflicting reports have emerged following talks in Switzerland; while U.S. Vice President JD Vance stated that Iran agreed to allow U.N. nuclear inspectors back into the country, the Iranian foreign ministry explicitly denied making any new commitments. This discrepancy highlights the ongoing volatility in post-2018 nuclear relations.
* **Tragic Discovery in France:** Authorities in France are investigating the deaths of two children found inside a sweltering vehicle in a residential parking area. The incident occurred amidst an extreme heatwave sweeping the nation, with temperatures reaching 40 degrees Celsius. The local prosecutor confirmed that the victims suffered fatal cardiac arrest.

## POLAND

* **Political Transition in Hungary:** Prime Minister Peter Magyar has officially announced that Hungary will undergo a presidential transition no later than August 20. The announcement, delivered in Budapest, signals a significant shift in the Hungarian political landscape, with observers closely watching for the specific implications of this timeline on the nation’s governance.
