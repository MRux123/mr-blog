## WORLD

*   **AI Development Risks:** Dario Amodei, CEO of Anthropic, has issued a stark warning regarding the rapid advancement of artificial intelligence. He advocates for a deceleration in development, citing significant concerns that current models could potentially inflict global-scale damage if left unchecked, with some projections suggesting a loss of control over digital infrastructure within the next year.
*   **Escalating Tensions in the Middle East:** The geopolitical landscape remains volatile as Iran leverages recent escalations to gain strategic influence over critical oil shipping routes. This shift follows a significant drone attack on a key Saudi Arabian oil pipeline, which has disrupted energy supply chains and forced the Kingdom to seek alternative workarounds to bypass potential blockades.

## POLAND

*   **Leadership Change in ECR:** Adam Bielan has been elected as the new head of the European Conservatives and Reformists (ECR) party, succeeding former Prime Minister Mateusz Morawiecki. The change marks a tactical pivot for the party as it navigates European political challenges.
*   **Institutional Tensions:** Former Prime Minister Leszek Miller has raised alarms regarding the current administration's approach to the Constitutional Tribunal. Miller warned that any perceived state intervention in the judiciary, specifically involving police access to judges' offices, could inadvertently provide political ammunition to the opposition, led by Jarosław Kaczyński.
