## WORLD

*   **U.S. Launches Retaliatory Strikes in Middle East:** Following a lethal attack by Iranian-backed forces that resulted in the deaths of two American soldiers in Jordan, the United States has initiated a series of retaliatory strikes. This escalation marks the most significant direct military engagement since the April truce, heightening concerns of a broader regional conflict.
*   **Massive Ballistic Missile Attack on Kyiv:** Russia has conducted one of its largest ballistic missile barrages targeting the Ukrainian capital. President Zelensky confirmed the intensity of the strike, which resulted in at least one confirmed fatality. The event signals a renewed and aggressive shift in aerial tactics against Ukrainian infrastructure.

## POLAND

*   **Public Dissatisfaction with Government Accountability:** A recent SW Research poll for *rp.pl* indicates that the majority of Polish citizens remain unsatisfied with the government's progress in fulfilling its promise to account for the previous Law and Justice (PiS) administration. The survey highlights a growing gap between voter expectations and the results delivered by the current coalition after more than two years in power.
*   **Escalating Tensions in Polish-Ukrainian Relations:** Political discourse in Poland has become increasingly polarized regarding cooperation with Kyiv. Prominent opposition voices have signaled a tougher stance on diplomatic relations, citing the need to prioritize domestic interests, while government and coalition representatives warn that weakening support for Ukraine poses a direct long-term security threat to Poland’s borders.
*   **National Alert Over Impending Severe Weather:** The Government Security Centre (RCB) has issued nationwide warnings as extreme weather patterns move across the country. Meteorologists predict severe storms with wind gusts reaching up to 100 km/h, primarily affecting the southern and central regions of Poland.
