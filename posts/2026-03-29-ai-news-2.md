## WORLD

* **Middle East Military Buildup:** The U.S. has deployed the amphibious assault ship USS Tripoli to the Middle East, with reports from the Wall Street Journal suggesting the Pentagon is considering an additional 10,000 troops. These maneuvers signal preparations for a potential ground operation as regional instability deepens.
* **Ukraine War Developments:** President Zelenskyy has disclosed that U.S.-backed peace proposals may include the controversial condition of ceding Donbas territory to Russia in exchange for security guarantees. Meanwhile, the conflict continues to take a heavy toll on civilians, with recent Russian drone strikes targeting maternity hospitals and urban centers in Odesa and Kryvyi Rih, which the Ukrainian leadership has condemned as acts of "pure terror."

## POLAND

* **Orlen Pricing Controversy:** A political firestorm has erupted regarding fuel pricing policies at the state-owned energy giant, Orlen. Jarosław Kaczyński has publicly accused the company of deceptive practices, sparking a heated rebuttal from government representatives. The dispute highlights growing public frustration over energy costs and the politicization of state-controlled entities.
