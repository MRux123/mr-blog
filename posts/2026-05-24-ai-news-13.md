## WORLD

*   **Russian Missile Strike on Kyiv:** Ukrainian President Volodymyr Zelenskyy has confirmed that Russia utilized the Oreshnik intermediate-range ballistic missile system during a massive, sustained attack on the capital, Kyiv. The incident, which caused significant structural damage throughout the city, marks a dangerous escalation in the ongoing conflict.
*   **Fragile Hopes for U.S.-Iran De-escalation:** The United States and Iran appear to be signaling a potential move toward an initial peace agreement. While President Trump has publicly stated that terms have been "largely negotiated," official narratives from both sides remain contradictory and ambiguous, leaving the exact status of the deal uncertain.
*   **Tragic Mining Disaster in China:** At least 82 people have been confirmed dead following a devastating gas explosion at the Liushenyu mine in China. This incident marks the deadliest mining catastrophe in the country in over a decade, drawing international attention to regional safety standards.

## POLAND

*   **Damage to Polish-Managed Monastery in Kyiv:** Amidst the heavy Russian bombardment of the Ukrainian capital, a monastery managed by Polish Dominican friars has sustained severe damage. While the facility's windows, doors, and chapel were destroyed, the community remains in the city, holding onto hopes for a resolution to the conflict.
*   **Political Scrutiny in Gdańsk:** Local authorities in Gdańsk have initiated an intervention regarding the city's president. The inquiry centers on alleged irregularities or questions surrounding the disclosure of a property in the president’s official asset declaration, a move that has sparked significant public interest.
