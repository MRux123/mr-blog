## WORLD

* **Middle East Escalation:** The fragile regional stability has shattered as Iran launched a direct missile strike against Israel, marking the first such incident since the April cease-fire. The attack comes in retaliation for Israeli strikes on the outskirts of Beirut, significantly heightening the risk of a broader regional conflict.
* **Diplomatic Pressure on Ukraine:** European allies of President Zelensky have finalized five core conditions for future peace negotiations. This development occurs against a shifting geopolitical backdrop, as the U.S. administration pivots its strategic focus toward escalating tensions with Iran.

## POLAND

* **Strategic Air Power Showcase:** The Polish Ministry of National Defence has announced a "Welcome to Poland" tour for the newly acquired F-35 fighter jets. Known as the "Hussars," the aircraft will perform low-altitude flyovers across major Polish cities, including Kraków, Gdańsk, and Warsaw, on June 12, serving as a landmark moment in the modernization of the Polish Air Force.
* **Armenian Election Shift:** Initial results from the presidential election in Armenia indicate a lead for Prime Minister Nikol Pashinyan's "Civil Contract" party. The election is being closely monitored by regional powers, as the leading opposition blocs maintain platforms centered on restoring closer ties with Russia, potentially impacting the current balance of power in the Caucasus.
