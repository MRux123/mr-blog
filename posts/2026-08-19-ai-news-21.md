## WORLD

*   **Ebola Outbreak Reaches Critical Levels in DRC:** The Democratic Republic of the Congo is currently facing the deadliest Ebola outbreak in its history. Official data indicates that at least 2,325 people have succumbed to the virus, with confirmed cases climbing to 4,945. The alarming infection rate suggests the crisis is outpacing previous medical interventions, marking a dark chapter for regional public health.
*   **Tragic Helicopter Crash in Kenya:** A safari helicopter crash in a remote region of northern Kenya has resulted in the deaths of seven individuals, including five American citizens. Among the victims were a high-ranking Telemundo executive and the head of Ecuador’s national intelligence service, prompting an investigation into the cause of the disaster.
*   **Tensions Rise at the Spanish-Moroccan Border:** Moroccan authorities have arrested over 100 individuals attempting to breach the border into Ceuta. The incident follows a period of heightened security and recent mass migration attempts that have reignited anti-immigration rhetoric and caused significant diplomatic strain within the European Union.

## POLAND

*   **End of an Era: Death of WWII Veteran Józef Kowalczyk:** The nation mourns the loss of ppor. Józef Kowalczyk, who passed away at the age of 102. As a veteran of the Battle of Monte Cassino and a soldier of the 12th Podolian Uhlan Regiment, his death marks the passing of another witness to Poland's fight for sovereignty during World War II.
*   **Political Fallout Regarding Flood Relief Efforts:** Internal stability within the ruling coalition (KO) has been tested, as a prominent party activist was expelled following a controversy surrounding the management of aid for flood victims. The event highlights growing public scrutiny over the government's crisis management strategies.
*   **Controversy Over Constitutional Tribunal Nominations:** Former President Bronisław Komorowski has publicly criticized the current government’s legislative tactics, specifically regarding the nomination of Maciej Berek to the Constitutional Tribunal. Komorowski’s remarks underscore a growing divide between traditional political figures and the current executive's approach to state institutions.
