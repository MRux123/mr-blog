## WORLD

*   **Escalation in the Middle East:** Military tensions have reached a critical point as the United States and Iran report fresh strikes near the Strait of Hormuz. Simultaneously, Israel has issued evacuation orders for the southern outskirts of Beirut, a Hezbollah stronghold, heightening fears of a broader regional conflict as both Iranian and Israeli officials threaten further military operations.
*   **Human Rights Concerns in Ghana:** International rights groups have expressed alarm following Ghana's parliamentary approval of a stringent law criminalizing the promotion of LGBTQ+ activities. If ratified by the President, the legislation—which carries prison sentences of three to 10 years—threatens to systematically marginalize the local community, sparking widespread panic regarding housing, employment, and healthcare access.

## POLAND

*   **Diplomatic Fallout with Ukraine:** Former Polish ambassador to Ukraine, Bartosz Cichocki, has officially returned his state order to President Volodymyr Zelensky. The protest follows a controversial decision by the Ukrainian government to name a military unit after UPA commanders, a move seen as deeply divisive in Polish-Ukrainian historical relations.
*   **Public Safety and Administrative Crisis at Zakrzówek:** The popular Zakrzówek reservoir in Kraków has been closed to the public following the discovery of an unknown chemical substance in the water. Preliminary analysis suggests the contaminant shares properties with heavy-duty descaling and rust-removal agents, forcing an immediate cessation of all recreational activities in the area.
*   **Internal Dispute at POLSA:** A significant public and professional conflict has emerged regarding the Polish Space Agency (POLSA). Following the historic space mission of Sławosz Uznański-Wiśniewski, critics are now questioning the agency’s strategic management of the mission’s aftermath, with accusations that the potential for long-term domestic aerospace development has been squandered.
