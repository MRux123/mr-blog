## WORLD

*   **Escalating Mpox Outbreak:** Public health officials are expressing growing concern as the mpox virus spreads into new territories, with children being particularly vulnerable. Following major outbreaks in 2022 and 2024, the virus has now been reported in 145 countries, complicating global containment strategies and prompting calls for enhanced international cooperation.
*   **Medical Tragedy in Pakistan:** A catastrophic fire at a Pakistani hospital has claimed the lives of 14 infants. While initial reports suggested that a locked ward door may have hampered escape, hospital officials maintain that the ward was actively managed by security personnel for protection, leaving investigators to determine the exact circumstances of the fatal incident.
*   **Rare Malaria Incident in Germany:** A fatal case of malaria at Germany's busiest airport has highlighted the unintended consequences of global travel. An airport worker succumbed to the disease after being bitten by a mosquito believed to have arrived on an aircraft, sparking an investigation into biosafety protocols.

## POLAND

*   **Tensions Rise with Meta:** Deputy Prime Minister Krzysztof Gawkowski has issued a stern warning to Meta following an unsatisfactory response regarding fraudulent advertisements using the images of public figures. The government has signaled a "no-nonsense" approach, suggesting stricter regulatory pressure on social media giants to ensure user protection against deepfakes and impersonation.
*   **Military Modernization:** The Polish Ministry of National Defense continues to prioritize national security through the appointment of six new high-level military commanders and the expansion of the Air Force's capabilities. Minister Władysław Kosiniak-Kamysz announced collaborative procurement plans with Spain for advanced aircraft, marking a significant step in the modernization of the Polish armed forces.
*   **AI Integration in Education:** Polish schools are testing an innovative approach to health education, utilizing an AI-driven assistant to help teachers navigate extensive, complex textbooks. By providing reliable, source-based information, the tool aims to improve the quality of health instruction while alleviating the administrative burden on educators.
