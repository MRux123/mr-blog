## WORLD

* **Trump’s Ultimatum to Iran:** President Donald Trump has issued a stark warning to Tehran, threatening to destroy Iranian infrastructure, including power plants and bridges, should the country fail to reach a diplomatic agreement by Tuesday. This follows intense military operations, including a large-scale bombing campaign by U.S. forces intended to facilitate the rescue of a downed American airman.
* **Energy Crisis Looming:** As Middle Eastern instability continues, the global energy supply chain is feeling the impact. Italy has reported critical fuel shortages at multiple airports, including Brindisi, raising concerns from airline executives that sustained conflict could lead to widespread aviation fuel deficits across Europe.

## POLAND

* **Growing Xenophobic Incidents:** Tensions have spiked in Poznań, where a 35-year-old woman was detained following a documented hate crime against a Ukrainian national. The incident has drawn significant attention from local authorities, highlighting ongoing social strains.
* **Political Polarization:** The domestic political landscape remains volatile as leading figures exchange heated rhetoric. The discourse, ranging from personal attacks involving family members of the Prime Minister to debates over Poland's security posture within NATO without U.S. support, underscores the deep divisions within the Polish political establishment regarding national defense and internal governance.
