## WORLD

*   **Heightened Tensions in the Strait of Hormuz:** U.S. intelligence reports confirm that Iran has deployed two distinct types of naval mines in the Strait of Hormuz. As one of the world's most critical maritime chokepoints for oil and gas transport, this strategic escalation poses a direct threat to global energy stability and maritime security.
*   **OpenAI Shifts Strategy with Sora:** OpenAI has officially terminated its partnership with Disney and shuttered the Sora video-making application. The decision marks a significant pivot for the company, coming less than two years after the tool’s debut sent shockwaves through the global media and creative industries.

## POLAND

*   **Landmark Verdict Against Meta:** A New Mexico jury has ordered Meta to pay $375 million in damages, ruling that the tech giant failed to adequately protect minors from sexual exploitation on its platforms. This high-profile verdict sets a critical legal precedent that is expected to influence future litigation against major digital corporations regarding child safety standards.
