## WORLD

*   **Escalating Flood Disaster in Nepal:** The humanitarian crisis in Nepal has deepened, with the official count of missing persons surging to 1,900. Rescue operations are severely hampered by the scale of the destruction at local hydropower projects and fears of secondary flooding events.
*   **Intensifying Drone Warfare:** President Volodymyr Zelensky has mandated a massive escalation in Ukraine's drone operations, aiming for a capacity of 1,000 strikes per day. Despite this tactical shift, the Ukrainian government has acknowledged that human resources remain a critical challenge on the front lines, as analysts warn that Russia's territorial ambitions extend well beyond the current conflict in Donbas.
*   **Security Concerns regarding NATO:** Western officials report that while Moscow is ramping up aggressive rhetoric and hybrid warfare tactics against NATO members, there appears to be a strategic intent to avoid direct, large-scale military confrontation at this stage.

## POLAND

*   **Tusk’s Warning on Security:** Prime Minister Donald Tusk has issued a stern warning regarding the coming months, describing them as "critical" in the context of the ongoing security threat posed by Russia. This follows discussions at the "Campus Polska Przyszłości" conference, where the Prime Minister also addressed the frustrations of his three-year term, noting the lack of progress on specific social rights issues.
*   **Shifts in Labor Migration:** Economic data indicates a historic reversal in labor migration patterns, with Polish citizens now leaving Germany in significant numbers, marking a notable change in the European labor market landscape.
*   **Archaeological Significance at Książ Castle:** Researchers have reported a breakthrough in Lower Silesia following the discovery of the remains of the former owners of the historic Książ Castle, providing new historical insights into the region's aristocratic legacy.
