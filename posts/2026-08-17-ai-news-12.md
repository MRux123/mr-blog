## WORLD

* **Kushner's Middle East Diplomacy:** Jared Kushner has arrived in Israel for high-stakes meetings with Prime Minister Benjamin Netanyahu. The mission aims to break the prolonged deadlock regarding the Gaza peace plan. While the Trump administration faces mounting pressure to demonstrate tangible progress, Israel maintains a firm stance, insisting on the complete disarmament of Hamas before any military withdrawal.
* **Security Breach in Downing Street:** In an embarrassing lapse in protocol, UK Prime Minister Andy Burnham engaged in an exchange of text messages with an individual falsely posing as White House Chief of Staff, Susie Wiles. British officials have confirmed the incident, raising questions about vetting procedures and cyber-security awareness at the highest levels of government.

## POLAND

* **Vatican Diplomacy in Moscow:** The Vatican has dispatched its top diplomat, Archbishop Paul Richard Gallagher, to Moscow. The visit carries two primary objectives, signaling a significant attempt at back-channel communication amid complex geopolitical tensions involving the Holy See and the Kremlin.
* **Environmental Alert in Silesia:** Local authorities are investigating a potential ecological disaster in the Gliwice Canal, where an alarming number of dead fish have been discovered. Environmental experts are currently assessing water samples to determine the cause of the mass mortality and the extent of the impact on the local ecosystem.
* **Educational Reforms:** Starting September 1st, Polish schools will implement a comprehensive set of new dietary regulations for students. The legislation aims to improve nutritional standards in school canteens as part of a broader push to combat childhood obesity and promote healthier lifestyles among the youth.
