## WORLD

* **Contradictory Signals in US-Iran Negotiations:** A significant diplomatic rift has emerged regarding potential talks between Washington and Tehran in Islamabad. While U.S. envoys Witkoff and Kushner are heading to Pakistan, Iranian officials, including Foreign Minister Abbas Araghchi, have denied that any direct negotiations concerning the war will take place, asserting that meetings are limited to Pakistani leadership.
* **Taiwan’s Diplomatic Isolation Intensifies:** President Lai Ching-te has been forced to abandon a planned state visit to Eswatini—Taiwan’s final African ally—after multiple nations revoked overflight permits. Taipei attributes this collapse of diplomatic travel to intense political pressure exerted by Beijing.

## POLAND

* **Air Defense Alert Amid Russian Missile Strikes:** The Polish Armed Forces scrambled fighter jets and early-warning aircraft early Saturday morning in response to a massive Russian missile attack targeting Ukraine. While the operation caused significant public alarm, the Operational Command confirmed that no Russian munitions breached Polish sovereign airspace, and the mission concluded without further incident.
