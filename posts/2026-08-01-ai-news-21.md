## WORLD

* **EU Crisis Over Ceuta Migrant Influx:** European Union officials have scheduled an emergency meeting of interior ministers for Tuesday to address the escalating situation in Ceuta. Following the arrival of over 50,000 migrants in the Spanish territory, the bloc remains deeply divided on how to manage the border crisis and coordinate a unified response.
* **Deadly Explosion in Moscow:** An improvised explosive device detonated at an upscale restaurant in Moscow, resulting in three fatalities and leaving 21 others injured. Russian authorities are currently investigating the circumstances, noting that one of the deceased was a woman reportedly carrying the suspicious package.
* **Tragedy in Peru:** A sightseeing plane carrying tourists crashed while flying over the historic Nazca Lines archaeological site. Local reports confirm that all 13 individuals on board perished in the incident.

## POLAND

* **Armed Confrontation in Wielkopolska:** A police intervention in Trzcianka turned violent when officers were attacked by a suspect. During the exchange of fire, the perpetrator was shot and one police officer was injured; both remain hospitalized. Additionally, the body of a man was discovered outside the apartment building where the incident occurred.
* **Severe Weather Disruptions:** A powerful storm front has moved across Poland, causing significant infrastructure damage. Reports indicate widespread power outages, numerous uprooted trees, and multiple residential buildings left with destroyed roofs.
* **Political Accountability and Media Shifts:** Former Prime Minister Mateusz Morawiecki publicly addressed past policy decisions—including the "Safe Credit 2%" program and pandemic-era measures—acknowledging mistakes during a broadcast appearance. Meanwhile, the long-running investigative program "Interwencja" has officially concluded its 23-year run on television.
