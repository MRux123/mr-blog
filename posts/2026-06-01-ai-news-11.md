## WORLD

* **Middle East Conflict Intensifies:** Tensions have reached a critical juncture as the U.S. and Iran engage in reciprocal military strikes near the Strait of Hormuz. Satellite imagery confirms that Iranian attacks have caused significant damage to at least 20 U.S. military sites, a development initially underreported. Concurrently, Kuwait has formally accused Iran of violating its territory through drone and missile attacks, further destabilizing the region.
* **Israel-Hezbollah Escalation:** Prime Minister Benjamin Netanyahu has ordered strikes on the Dahieh suburbs of Beirut, signaling a major intensification of the conflict with Hezbollah. The move follows persistent attacks on Israeli civilian targets, pushing the regional security situation toward an all-out confrontation.

## POLAND

* **Expansion of In Vitro Funding:** Prime Minister Donald Tusk has announced an increase in state funding for the in vitro fertilization (IVF) program, raising annual spending from 500 million to 600 million PLN. The government reports that the program has already facilitated the birth of 15,000 children, with approximately 40,000 couples currently participating.
* **Espionage Investigation:** Authorities have launched an investigation into an employee of the Polish Armaments Group (PGZ) on suspicion of espionage. The case is particularly sensitive as the suspect was reportedly identified shortly before his planned retirement, raising concerns regarding potential long-term security breaches within the defense sector.
