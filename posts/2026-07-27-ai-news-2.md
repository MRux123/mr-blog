## WORLD

*   **Geopolitical Standoff in New York:** Tensions between Prime Minister Benjamin Netanyahu and NYC Mayor Mamdani have reached a flashpoint. Following earlier threats of arrest related to an International Criminal Court warrant, Mamdani has walked back his stance, clarifying that he lacks the legal authority to execute such an order. The incident underscores the intensifying international legal and diplomatic pressures surrounding the conflict.
*   **Ebola Precautionary Measures in London:** A UK health worker, recently evacuated from the Democratic Republic of the Congo, is currently under observation at a specialist hospital in London. The UK Health Security Agency (UKHSA) has initiated monitoring protocols after the individual was potentially exposed to the Ebola virus while providing medical care in an outbreak zone.

## POLAND

*   **Travel Safety Advisories:** As the peak holiday season continues, Polish authorities and media outlets are emphasizing the necessity of thorough travel preparations. Essential advice focuses on document validity, health insurance, and financial security to mitigate risks for citizens traveling abroad.
