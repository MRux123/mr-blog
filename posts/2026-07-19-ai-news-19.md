## WORLD

*   **Major Escalation in Ukraine:** Kyiv has been struck by one of the largest missile assaults of the ongoing conflict. This intense bombardment comes as a significant shift in military engagement, following Ukrainian strikes on Russian logistics warehouses that resulted in multiple casualties. The situation marks a notable intensification of hostilities, signaling a broader and more aggressive phase of the war.

*   **Climate Alert:** Meteorological organizations warn that a record-breaking El Niño event, the strongest in 75 years, is imminent. With an 81% probability of reaching peak intensity by autumn, experts anticipate significant global climate disruptions, including potential severe weather patterns across Europe.

## POLAND

*   **Severe Weather Crisis:** Poland is currently battling the aftermath of violent storms that have swept across the country. Emergency services have conducted over 1,500 interventions as the severe weather caused structural damage and power outages. Tragically, six people were injured in the Małopolska region when a tree fell onto a carriage, highlighting the severity of the natural disaster.

*   **Political Instability within PiS:** The Law and Justice (PiS) party faces internal fragmentation following a provocative social media appeal by Jarosław Kaczyński, who warned of internal divisions. Critics and political analysts, including Marek Sawicki, suggest that the party’s cohesion is deteriorating, noting a shift in the conservative leadership dynamic toward President Karol Nawrocki.
