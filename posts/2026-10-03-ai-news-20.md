## WORLD

* **Failed Execution Sparks Leadership Shake-up in Tennessee**: Tennessee's prison chief is set to resign following the botched lethal injection of death row inmate Christa Pike. Pike remains in critical condition after surviving two separate attempts, prompting intense scrutiny of the state's judicial and penal procedures. Legal experts argue that the systemic failure points to broader issues within the correctional infrastructure.
* **Massive Housing Protests Erupt Across Spain**: Tens of thousands of demonstrators have taken to the streets in Spain to voice their frustration over the deepening housing crisis. The wave of protests, spanning over 50 cities, follows the government's failure to push essential emergency legislation through parliament, highlighting a growing disconnect between political administration and the socioeconomic realities of citizens.
