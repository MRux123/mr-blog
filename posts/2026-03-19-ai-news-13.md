## WORLD

* **Global Energy Markets Under Pressure:** Tensions in the Middle East have severely disrupted energy infrastructure, with a significant strike on a Qatari hub driving gas prices up by 25%. The U.S. administration is currently evaluating emergency measures, including the potential release of strategic oil reserves, to mitigate the impact of the ongoing U.S.-Israeli war against Iran.
* **NATO Security Posture in the Middle East:** Member states have reached a consensus to temporarily suspend training missions in Iraq. The withdrawal of approximately 600 troops reflects the worsening security landscape in the region, marking a major shift in Western military presence.
* **Diplomatic Friction with Russia:** Following a breach of Estonian airspace by a Russian Su-30 fighter jet, the Estonian government has summoned a Russian diplomatic representative to issue a formal protest, signaling continued volatility along NATO's eastern flank.

## POLAND

* **Regional Security and Energy Infrastructure:** Russian military actions have intensified near the Polish border, with strikes targeting energy infrastructure in the Volyn region. The incident has caused power and water supply disruptions, while a separate drone strike near Lviv impacted a security services building, underscoring the immediate spillover risks for Poland.
* **Advancements in National Defense:** The Polish Ministry of National Defense (MON) has officially accelerated the development of domestic autonomous drone systems. Described by officials as the "Polish Shahed" program, this initiative is now being centralized under a newly opened Autonomous Systems Center, aiming to bolster Poland's drone-based defensive capabilities.
