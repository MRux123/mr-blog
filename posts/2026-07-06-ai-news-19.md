## WORLD

* **Escalation in Ukraine:** Kyiv faces an intensified wave of Russian missile and drone attacks on the eve of the NATO summit. With 68 missiles and over 350 strike drones reported, Ukraine’s government warns of a critical shortage of interceptor missiles, highlighting the urgent need for bolstered air defense capabilities.
* **Geopolitical Tensions in Turkey:** As Turkey hosts the NATO summit, concerns regarding internal governance are mounting. Human rights groups and observers point to a systematic crackdown on political critics, including the jailing of protesters and the arrest of a prominent comedian in the lead-up to the high-stakes diplomatic gathering.
* **Accountability for Syrian War Crimes:** In a landmark legal development, an Austrian court has convicted a former high-ranking Syrian intelligence chief of torture and sexual abuse. The ruling serves as a significant step in the ongoing international efforts to hold former Assad regime officials accountable for human rights violations.

## POLAND

* **Military Aid Debate:** Tensions in the Polish political landscape have sharpened regarding the country’s defense strategy. Former Defense Minister Mariusz Błaszczak has publicly criticized the current government’s decision to transfer missile components to Ukraine, questioning the strategic wisdom of such support while simultaneously warning of potential Russian aggression against Poland.
* **Public Safety Alert in Wrocław:** Authorities in Wrocław are managing a significant security operation following the discovery of an unexploded ordnance. The situation necessitates the evacuation of 3,000 residents, underscoring the lingering risks posed by historical munitions in urban areas.
* **Sanctions Against MEP:** The European Parliament has issued a formal penalty against Grzegorz Braun, who will face a suspension of his daily allowance and a 20-day ban from security committee meetings. The disciplinary action follows reports of unprofessional conduct directed at EU diplomacy chief Kaja Kallas, which the Parliament deemed a violation of institutional dignity.
