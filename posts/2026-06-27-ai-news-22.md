## WORLD

*   **Heightened Tensions in the Middle East:** Hostilities between the United States and Iran have intensified, putting the fragile truce to the test. Following a series of drone attacks in Bahrain and renewed maritime aggression in the Strait of Hormuz, U.S. forces have conducted retaliatory airstrikes on Iranian targets for two consecutive nights.
*   **Climate Emergency Across Europe:** A severe heatwave has gripped the continent, with temperatures exceeding 35°C affecting approximately 150 million people. Germany, Denmark, and the Czech Republic have officially reported record-breaking temperature levels as authorities scramble to manage the health risks associated with the prolonged extreme heat.
*   **Venezuela Earthquake Aftermath:** Rescue efforts in Venezuela continue under harrowing conditions as families remain at the sites of collapsed structures. While the task of clearing heavy debris remains difficult, search teams are operating in a race against time, with every survivor pulled from the rubble being treated as a miraculous event.

## POLAND

*   **Unprecedented June Heatwave:** Poland is currently experiencing a historic climate event, with record-breaking temperatures recorded in the western part of the country. This anomaly aligns with the broader European trend, signaling an exceptionally harsh start to the summer season.
*   **Ongoing Humanitarian Crisis Response:** Polish media remain focused on the tragic earthquake in Venezuela, highlighting the emotional toll on the affected population as rescue teams successfully recovered an infant alive from the debris, providing a rare glimmer of hope amidst the devastation.
