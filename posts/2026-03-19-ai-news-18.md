## WORLD

* **Escalating Crisis in Iran:** President Donald Trump has signaled a strategy of economic pressure to stabilize oil markets amidst the ongoing conflict with Iran. While Trump has explicitly ruled out the deployment of ground troops, the situation remains volatile following reports of a US F-35 being hit over Iranian territory. Meanwhile, the UAE and Kuwait are bolstering their air defense capabilities with a $16.4 billion US arms deal to protect critical infrastructure.
* **Global Defense Shortages:** Armin Papperger, CEO of Rheinmetall, has issued a stark warning regarding the state of global security, stating that stockpiles of air defense missiles in the US, Europe, and the Middle East are critically low or entirely depleted, raising concerns about the West's readiness for sustained conflicts.
* **Terrorist Attack in Nigeria:** A series of suspected suicide bombings in Maiduguri has resulted in at least 23 deaths and over 100 injuries. The attacks targeted key public areas, including a hospital and a marketplace, during the Ramadan iftar, marking a violent disruption to the relative stability previously seen in the region.

## POLAND

* **Political Prisoner Release in Belarus:** Following negotiations with a US delegation, Belarusian authorities have released 250 political prisoners. While the list of those freed is still being verified, there is significant speculation regarding the possible release of prominent activist Andrzej Poczobut, which would mark a major development in bilateral relations.
* **Election Campaign Rhetoric:** Przemysław Czarnek, the PiS candidate for Prime Minister, has placed the exit from the European Union’s Emissions Trading System (ETS) at the center of his political platform, setting the stage for a potential major clash with European climate policy.
