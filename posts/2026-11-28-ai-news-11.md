## WORLD

*   **Escalating Russian Sabotage Threats:** A report by the International Institute for Strategic Studies (IISS) warns of an intensified campaign of Russian sabotage operations across Europe. The study identifies Germany and Poland as the most vulnerable targets, citing a rise in coordinated hostile incidents that pose a growing threat to regional security.
*   **Ukraine’s Strategic Deep Strikes:** Despite criticism from the Trump administration, Ukraine has expanded the scale of its military operations deep inside Russian territory. The campaign, aimed at dismantling Moscow’s logistical capabilities, specifically targets fuel, weapon caches, and financial assets essential to the Russian war effort.
*   **Tragedy in Afghanistan:** An explosion at an explosives depot in Afghanistan has claimed the lives of at least ten students, leaving over 100 people injured. Preliminary investigations by local authorities suggest the blast was the result of negligence in storage safety protocols.

## POLAND

*   **Political Gridlock Over Tax Legislation:** The government is preparing to challenge a presidential veto concerning new tax regulations, including changes to tax brackets and the corporate income tax (CIT). This move sets the stage for a significant parliamentary confrontation between the cabinet and President Karol Nawrocki’s office.
*   **Diplomatic Tension and Parliamentary Etiquette:** A heated exchange occurred in the national media spotlight between members of the ruling coalition and the opposition. The disagreement, which arose from the use of formal titles during a televised debate, highlights the deep partisan polarization currently paralyzing substantive discourse in the Polish parliament.
*   **Demographic Challenges:** New data confirms a deepening demographic crisis across the Lower Silesia region, with only one subregion showing resilience. The trend underlines the long-term socio-economic hurdles facing regional development policies in Western Poland.
