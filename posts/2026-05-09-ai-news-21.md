## WORLD

* **Escalating Tensions in the Middle East:** Despite the ceasefire agreement announced last month, violence continues to surge in the region. Recent Israeli airstrikes in Lebanon have resulted in 39 fatalities, signaling the fragility of ongoing de-escalation efforts.
* **Trump’s Global Military Realignments:** President Donald Trump continues to reshape U.S. defense commitments. Following the announcement of a troop reduction in Germany, reports indicate that the U.S. is now considering similar withdrawals from Italy, with the President citing insufficient support from Rome regarding conflicts in Iran.

## POLAND

* **Debate Over Potential U.S. Troop Relocation:** The prospect of moving American forces from Germany to Poland has sparked a national debate among political figures. While government officials, including the head of the BBN, have hailed the possibility as a "groundbreaking declaration" reflecting strong bilateral ties, political veterans remain divided on the strategic and logistical implications for Poland.
* **Diplomatic Maneuvering with Moscow:** In a controversial move, Slovak Prime Minister Robert Fico visited Russia, acting as an intermediary between Kyiv and Moscow. Fico reported that President Putin is currently awaiting a direct communication from President Volodymyr Zelensky regarding a potential meeting, placing Poland's neighbor once again at the center of complex European diplomacy.
