## WORLD

* **Escalating Tensions in the Strait of Hormuz:** The United States and Iran are locked in a high-stakes standoff, both claiming operational control over the Strait of Hormuz. While U.S. Defense Secretary Pete Hegseth maintains that Washington’s presence is part of a defensive "Freedom Project" to secure maritime traffic, Iranian state media asserts that Tehran’s influence over the vital waterway has significantly intensified, raising concerns about potential regional destabilization.
* **Russia-Ukraine Conflict:** President Zelensky has publicly condemned Russia’s strategic strikes occurring in the lead-up to a proposed ceasefire. The diplomatic situation remains volatile as both nations continue to trade accusations regarding the breach of trust surrounding the May 8-9 truce timeline.
* **Public Health Alert:** A rare outbreak of Hantavirus has been identified aboard a cruise ship. With two confirmed cases and three fatalities, the incident has prompted urgent international monitoring, given the virus's atypical transmission pattern in this environment.

## POLAND

* **Political Disinformation Dispute:** A sharp war of words has broken out between government spokesperson Szłapka and President Andrzej Duda, with the government explicitly labeling the President’s recent statements as misinformation. This confrontation highlights the deepening polarization within the Polish political landscape.
* **EU Regulatory Shifts for Senior Drivers:** The European Union is moving forward with its "Vision Zero" strategy, proposing new regulations that may affect the validity and renewal of driver's licenses for senior citizens. While the EU sets the framework, individual member states will retain the autonomy to determine the specifics of license duration adjustments for older populations.
