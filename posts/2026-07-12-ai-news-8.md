## WORLD

* **Death of Senator Lindsey Graham:** The political landscape in Washington is reeling following the sudden death of influential US Senator Lindsey Graham. The Republican stalwart, known for his hawkish foreign policy and his recent diplomatic mission to Kyiv to meet with President Volodymyr Zelensky, passed away after a brief illness. His death marks a significant loss for the Republican Party, as he was a pivotal, albeit complex, figure in American foreign policy.
* **Mass Evacuations as Typhoon Bavi Strikes China:** A powerful typhoon has triggered a massive emergency response in China, leading to the evacuation of nearly two million people in Zhejiang province. The storm, which caused significant casualties in the Philippines and affected Japan and Taiwan, has brought widespread flooding and infrastructure disruptions, paralyzing transport and daily life in the region.
* **Ebola Treatment Trials in DRC:** In a race against time, the Democratic Republic of the Congo has launched a record-breaking clinical trial for two experimental drugs to combat the latest Ebola outbreak. Scientific teams have managed to organize the trials just six weeks after the outbreak was declared a public health emergency, offering a glimmer of hope in curbing mortality rates.

## POLAND

* **Strategic Energy Infrastructure Targeted:** Tensions in the conflict have escalated as reports confirm a major fire at one of Russia's key oil refineries. Ukrainian forces are reportedly intensifying their efforts, simultaneously targeting Russia’s "shadow fleet," a move that could significantly impact energy logistics and revenue streams.
* **Legal Reckoning for Military Commanders:** Authorities have intensified efforts to bring justice to those responsible for war crimes in Ukraine. A Ukrainian commander, Stanisław Łuczanow, and nine soldiers from the 155th Motorized Rifle Brigade are now officially sought on charges of kidnapping and murdering civilians in the Kyiv region, signaling a rigorous pursuit of accountability for atrocities committed during the conflict.
