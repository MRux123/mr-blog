## WORLD

* **Escalating Civil Unrest in Europe:** Protests regarding the rising cost of living have turned violent across major German cities, including Frankfurt, Berlin, and Hamburg, with reports of widespread looting. Similarly, France is experiencing significant social upheaval, as demonstrations involving over 300,000 people have descended into violence despite a festive atmosphere earlier in the day.
* **Mass Mobilization in India:** The opposition is organizing a massive rally in New Delhi to demand the resignation of the chief election officer. The protest stems from concerns over a contentious voter roll revision process that threatens to disenfranchise an estimated 130 million citizens.

## POLAND

* **Constitutional Tribunal Standoff:** The political crisis surrounding the Constitutional Tribunal (TK) has reached a critical juncture. While government officials signal a firm stance, opposition figures, including Mateusz Morawiecki, have described the situation as being on the "brink of civil war," calling for a total reset of the institution.
* **Deepening Political Polarization:** The conflict has prompted intense rhetorical escalation. Advisor to the president, Błażej Poboży, has harshly criticized Prime Minister Donald Tusk’s recent address, going so far as to label the Prime Minister a "putschist" regarding his administration's actions concerning the Tribunal. Meanwhile, representatives of the TK maintain that its judges remain steadfast against government pressure.
