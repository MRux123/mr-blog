## WORLD

* **Escalation of Iran-U.S. Tensions:** Reports indicate that Iran is evaluating potential retaliatory strikes against U.S. military assets stationed in Europe, specifically naming Bulgaria and Cyprus as potential targets. This comes amidst the UAE's announcement of a halt to trade with Tehran, aligning with the current U.S. strategy to further isolate the Iranian regime.
* **Trump’s North Korean Outreach:** U.S. President Donald Trump has signaled a intent to engage in direct talks with North Korean leader Kim Jong Un. The administration continues to calibrate its defense posture, evidenced by the recent scaling back of joint military drills with South Korea, despite ongoing concerns over Pyongyang’s nuclear capabilities.
* **Accountability in Gaza:** Following international outcry, Israel has officially launched a criminal investigation into the killing of five-year-old Hind Rajab, acknowledging that Israeli soldiers fired on the vehicle in which she and her relatives were traveling.

## POLAND

* **NATO Air Defense Exercises:** Polish skies are seeing a significant surge in military aviation activity as part of the "Eastern Sentry" operation. NATO officials confirm these exercises are a proactive response to the shifting security landscape in the region, emphasizing the alliance's readiness to defend its eastern flank.
* **Political Clash Over Tax Policy:** A public dispute has erupted between the government and the Presidential Palace regarding tax reform. Government officials criticized the Palace for labeling their proposal to raise tax thresholds as a "complication of the system," framing the disagreement as a deliberate attempt to obstruct necessary fiscal changes aimed at increasing household disposable income.
