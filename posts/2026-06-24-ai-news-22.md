## WORLD

* **Extreme Heat Grips Western Europe:** A historic heatwave is causing widespread disruption across France, the UK, and Spain. Meteorological authorities have issued red-level alerts as tens of millions of citizens face record-breaking temperatures, highlighting the growing intensity of summer climate volatility in the region.
* **Geopolitical Shift in the Strait of Hormuz:** The U.S. Department of Energy has confirmed that the Strait of Hormuz has been restored to pre-war operational status. Recent reports indicate a significant resumption of maritime traffic, with approximately 20 million barrels of oil flowing through the strategic chokepoint within the last 24 hours, signaling a major de-escalation in regional logistics.

## POLAND

* **Tensions Surrounding Ukraine Reconstruction Conference:** Gdańsk is bracing for a high-profile, two-day conference focused on the reconstruction of Ukraine. However, the event is set to be overshadowed by planned protests at Targ Rybny and Plac Solidarności, where demonstrators intend to voice opposition regarding ongoing debates over Ukrainian historical policy and recent administrative decisions by President Zelensky.
* **Whistleblower Revelations on Healthcare Corruption:** The systemic integrity of the Polish healthcare sector has come under scrutiny following statements from Jakub Kosikowski, spokesperson for the Supreme Medical Council. He revealed that medical professionals face professional retaliation and suppression when attempting to expose institutional malpractice, sparking a broader public debate on transparency and safety in national hospitals.
