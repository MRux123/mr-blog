## WORLD

* **Elon Musk Becomes First Trillionaire:** Marking a historic milestone in financial history, Elon Musk has become the world’s first trillionaire. This surge in net worth follows the successful Nasdaq debut of SpaceX, which hit a market valuation of $2.2 trillion, solidifying Musk's dominant position in the global economy.
* **Potential Breakthrough in Hormuz Conflict:** The United States, Iran, and Pakistani mediators have announced that they are nearing a final agreement to end hostilities in the region. If successful, the deal is expected to pave the way for the reopening of the Strait of Hormuz, a critical artery for global energy supplies.

## POLAND

* **Ukraine Strengthens Ties with the European Union:** In a significant geopolitical development, President Volodymyr Zelensky hailed the opening of the first negotiation cluster for Ukraine's EU membership. Zelensky emphasized that Ukraine’s integration efforts are inextricably linked to the security and defense of the entire European continent.
* **Tragic Accident in West Pomerania:** A serious incident occurred in Jarogniew near Kołobrzeg, where an eight-year-old child suffered severe burns covering 70 percent of their body. The child was injured after gasoline from canisters, which three children were playing with, ignited. The victim has been transported to a specialized hospital in Szczecin for urgent treatment.
