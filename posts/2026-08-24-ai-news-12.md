## WORLD

*   **Escalation in US-Iran Economic Standoff:** US Treasury Secretary Scott Bessent has declared an unprecedented financial offensive against Iran, announcing that the United States will sever all economic ties with the nation. Furthermore, the US has warned of international isolation for any state that continues to partner with Tehran, signaling a significant tightening of global sanctions.
*   **Ukrainian Strikes Target Russian Logistics:** Ukrainian military operations have successfully targeted Ozon, Russia’s second-largest online retailer, striking warehouses in the southern regions of Dagestan and Krasnodar. The attack marks a strategic shift in targeting critical domestic distribution infrastructure within Russia.
*   **Mpox Epidemic in Guinea-Bissau:** Health authorities in Guinea-Bissau have raised an alarm as a national mpox outbreak continues to spread, with children under the age of four accounting for a disproportionate number of cases.

## POLAND

*   **Energy Security Concerns:** The Polish government, alongside five other EU member states, has issued an urgent warning regarding rising energy prices, signaling a renewed debate over market stability and energy policy within the European Union.
*   **Rail Infrastructure Modernization:** Poland is launching a massive, multi-billion zloty project aimed at revolutionizing the regional rail network in Pomerania. This initiative is expected to significantly improve transport connectivity in Northern Poland.
*   **Aviation Accident in Hungary:** A Hungarian Air Force Gripen fighter jet crashed in central Hungary, approximately 100 kilometers from Budapest. While the pilot successfully ejected, the incident has prompted a high-level investigation into the causes of the failure.
