## WORLD

*   **Migration Crisis in Ceuta Sparks EU Border Debate:** Following the mass crossing of tens of thousands of migrants into the Spanish enclave of Ceuta, European Commission President Ursula von der Leyen has called for a significant tightening of EU borders. The incident has exposed deep divisions within the bloc and prompted accusations of facilitation against Morocco, turning the enclave into a critical migration flashpoint.
*   **European Rivers Reach Record Lows:** A severe drought is crippling major European waterways, including the Rhine, Danube, and Po. The record-low water levels are causing significant disruptions to the transport of goods and putting a strain on electricity generation across the continent, highlighting the growing impact of climate change on European infrastructure.
*   **Rising Middle East Tensions:** Analysts warn that the threat to oil tankers in the Middle East has reached its highest level since the start of the Iran war. The situation has been exacerbated by fresh attacks on shipping routes, further complicating the geopolitical landscape in the region.

## POLAND

*   **Drought Conditions Impact Vistula River:** Poland is facing a severe hydrological crisis as the water level in the Vistula River drops to near-record lows. This environmental stress reflects broader regional drought patterns, raising concerns about local water security and ecological stability.
*   **Heightened Security and Military Procurement:** In a move to bolster defense capabilities, the Pentagon has signed multi-billion dollar contracts for missile systems, which will supply both the United States and its allies. This development comes amidst ongoing regional instability and aggressive rhetoric from Moscow regarding NATO and EU involvement in the conflict with Ukraine.
*   **Weather Alerts and Public Safety:** The Institute of Meteorology and Water Management (IMGW) has issued nationwide warnings for heatwaves and severe storms. Residents are advised to take precautions as extreme weather patterns continue to pose risks of power outages and infrastructure damage across the country.
