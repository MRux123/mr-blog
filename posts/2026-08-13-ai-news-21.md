## WORLD

* **Rising Tensions in the West Bank:** The situation in the occupied territory remains precarious as Israeli settlers have resumed a siege on Palestinian homes following a brief eviction. The military's inability to maintain a buffer between extremists and local families underscores the deepening instability and lack of control in the region, raising urgent concerns about humanitarian safety.
* **Black Sea Truce Proposal:** In a significant diplomatic maneuver, Ukraine has formally proposed a truce with Russia specifically concerning operations in the Black Sea. According to Reuters, this move signals a potential attempt to de-escalate maritime hostilities, though the feasibility and Russian response remain uncertain as the conflict enters a volatile new phase.
* **Rail Accident in England:** Authorities in Southern England are responding to a serious train derailment. Current reports indicate that at least two individuals have sustained critical injuries, with emergency services conducting an ongoing rescue operation.

## POLAND

* **Jagiellonia’s European Success:** Polish football saw a major milestone as Jagiellonia secured a decisive victory against Rangers. This result guarantees the team a place in the UEFA Conference League, marking a significant achievement for the club on the international stage.
* **SpaceX Dialogue with Warsaw:** Minister Krzysztof Gawkowski confirmed that SpaceX has officially responded to government demands regarding operational transparency and requirements. While the specific details of the declaration were not fully disclosed, the government characterizes the communication as clear, marking a positive development in the collaboration between Poland and the private space entity.
* **Raków Advances in European Qualifiers:** In a dramatic turn of events, Raków Częstochowa has successfully advanced to the next stage of competition, following a high-stakes match against Górnik Zabrze. The game, decided in the closing minutes, highlights the intense competition and shifting momentum currently defining the Polish football scene.
