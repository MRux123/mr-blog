## WORLD

*   **Escalation of Legal Action Against James Comey:** Former FBI Director James Comey faces formal charges regarding alleged threats against U.S. President Donald Trump. The indictment, stemming from social media posts interpreted by the Department of Justice as calls to violence, has led to an arrest warrant, marking a significant escalation in the ongoing political friction between the administration and its critics.
*   **Geopolitical Instability in the Middle East:** The U.S. administration is reportedly struggling with a "frozen conflict" in Iran. Sources indicate that a prolonged military presence will be required in the region, dashing hopes for a near-term energy price drop and creating significant policy challenges for the White House.
*   **International Condemnation of Political Violence:** King Charles III addressed the U.S. Congress, denouncing the recent assassination attempt on President Trump and emphasizing the necessity of unity against political violence regardless of ideological differences.

## POLAND

*   **State Funeral for MP Łukasz Litewka:** The Presidential Office has confirmed that Karol Nawrocki, accompanied by First Lady Marta Nawrocka, will attend the state funeral of MP Łukasz Litewka in Sosnowiec.
*   **Coalition Internal Consultations:** Minister of Climate Paulina Hennig-Kloska is set to meet with the Poland 2050 parliamentary group. The discussion is expected to focus on the urgent need to address systemic failures in the current deposit-refund system and the "Clean Air" (*Czyste Powietrze*) government program.
