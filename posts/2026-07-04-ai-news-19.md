## WORLD

*   **Escalation in Ukraine:** Russian leadership has taken a confrontational stance, with Vladimir Putin visiting front-line positions to vow further territorial expansion. Dismissing Ukrainian military successes as mere "play-acting," the Kremlin remains entrenched in its aggressive posture, while reports from Crimea indicate a significant tactical blow to the Russian air force, with a MiG-29 destroyed by a Ukrainian drone strike.
*   **Instability in the Strait of Hormuz:** The critical maritime chokepoint is seeing increased activity as vessels are reported to be turning back, signaling growing international concern over security and potential supply chain disruptions in the region.

## POLAND

*   **Internal Governance Reform Proposals:** Former Prime Minister Leszek Miller and former President Bronisław Komorowski have publicly challenged the current administration, calling for a radical reduction in the number of government secretaries of state. This critique of the "overstaffing" of ministerial positions highlights ongoing domestic concerns regarding the efficiency and cost of the Polish public sector.
*   **Criminal Investigation in Borków:** Polish authorities have launched a nationwide manhunt following the discovery of a 64-year-old woman's body in Borków. The police have released the image of the prime suspect, the victim's husband, as the search intensifies across the province.
