## WORLD

*   **Fatal Crash in Oakland, California:** A catastrophic road incident in Oakland has resulted in multiple casualties after a juvenile driver plowed into a group of pedestrians. The accident left three people dead and five others injured. Local authorities have launched an investigation into the circumstances surrounding the driver's actions and the factors leading to the collision.

## POLAND

*   **Polish Family Caught in Modena Attack:** A Polish family residing in Castelfranco Emilia is among the victims of a deliberate vehicle-ramming attack that occurred this Saturday in Modena, Italy. Eight people in total were injured, with three reported to be in serious condition. Local witnesses successfully apprehended the perpetrator, who has been handed over to the police for further processing.
*   **Fatal Accident in Turek:** A tragic incident occurred in Turek, Greater Poland, where a 20-year-old woman lost her life after being struck by a BMW. According to initial police reports, the driver of the vehicle struck and subsequently ran over the pedestrian while performing a reversing maneuver during the night. Prosecutors and local police are currently investigating the incident to determine the exact cause and legal liability.
