## WORLD

*   **Heightened Security Concerns Following RAF Air Base Incident:** British authorities have arrested a 25-year-old British-Iranian dual national in connection to an incident at the R.A.F. Fairford air base. Officials are investigating potential Iranian state involvement, marking a significant escalation in geopolitical tensions.
*   **Flydubai Pilot Attack Investigation:** Following the attempted takeover of a Flydubai flight, Prime Minister Benjamin Netanyahu has identified the perpetrator as having undergone "Islamist radical indoctrination." The UAE is spearheading an international investigation into the motive, which is increasingly being treated as a terrorist act aimed at downing the aircraft.
*   **Russian Rhetoric on Kaliningrad:** Vladimir Putin has issued a stern warning to the West, stating that Russia is prepared to utilize all available weapons to protect the Kaliningrad enclave. The Kremlin maintains this is a defensive posture in response to recent Western discourse.

## POLAND

*   **Governmental Conflict Escalates:** Minister Radosław Sikorski has publicly condemned the President’s latest address, describing it as a mix of nonsense and insolence. The sharp criticism highlights a deepening divide within the Polish executive branch.
*   **Security Breach Investigation:** Polish authorities are investigating an attempted attack on a military facility, resulting in the arrest of a 27-year-old with dual citizenship. The incident has raised questions regarding national security protocols and foreign infiltration.
*   **Economic Relief Planned:** The Ministry of Finance has announced that fuel prices are expected to drop starting next week, a move aimed at alleviating the cost-of-living pressure on Polish citizens amidst broader economic challenges.
