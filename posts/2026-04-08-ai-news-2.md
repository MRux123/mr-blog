## WORLD

* **US-Iran Ceasefire Agreement:** A 14-day ceasefire has been established between the United States and Iran, following intense diplomatic pressure and a looming deadline set by President Trump. The agreement, brokered with the help of Pakistan, seeks to de-escalate the conflict and includes provisions to reopen the strategically vital Strait of Hormuz for international shipping. While global markets have reacted with an immediate drop in oil prices, analysts note that the high cost of this deal may reshape international perceptions of U.S. foreign policy.
* **Regional Escalation:** Prior to the ceasefire announcement, the Gulf region faced a volatile night, with Qatar and the UAE reporting successful interceptions of Iranian missile and drone attacks. Concurrently, reports of significant explosions emerged from Baghdad, underscoring the precarious nature of the regional security landscape even as diplomatic efforts moved toward a temporary truce.

## POLAND

* **Analysis of the Middle Eastern Crisis:** Polish media outlets, including Polsat News and RMF24, have focused heavily on the security implications of the conflict for the Gulf states and the geopolitical shift represented by the U.S.-Iran negotiations. The Polish reporting emphasizes the gravity of the potential humanitarian and economic crisis, highlighting the direct impact of the two-week truce on global energy security and the immediate cessation of hostile military operations in the region.
