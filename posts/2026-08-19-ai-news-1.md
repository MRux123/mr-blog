## WORLD

* **Meta Faces Landmark Trial Over Child Safety:** A high-stakes legal battle has commenced in the United States, with Meta standing accused of deliberately engineering social media platforms to induce addiction in children. The company has officially challenged the premise of the lawsuit, arguing that the medical and psychological concept of "social media addiction" lacks scientific validity. This trial is expected to set a critical precedent for how global tech giants are held accountable for their impact on adolescent mental health.

## POLAND

* **New Migration Strategy Proposed:** Former Prime Minister Mateusz Morawiecki has unveiled a comprehensive five-pillar immigration policy, placing "unconditional assimilation" at the core of the proposed framework. The initiative highlights a significant shift in the discourse surrounding national identity and the integration of foreign nationals, signaling a hardening stance from the political opposition regarding Poland's long-term migration strategy.
