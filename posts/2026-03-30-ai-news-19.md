## WORLD

*   **Escalating Tensions in the Iran War:** Global markets remain volatile as President Trump threatens intensified strikes against Iranian oil and energy infrastructure if a diplomatic resolution to the ongoing conflict is not reached soon. The potential for further disruption has fueled international energy security concerns.
*   **Strategic Pressure on Energy Infrastructure:** Amidst soaring energy prices linked to the conflict, reports indicate that Western allies have urged President Zelensky to scale back drone and missile attacks on Russian energy facilities, fearing further global price instability.
*   **Humanitarian and Migration Challenges:** German government officials, led by Friedrich Merz, have signaled a shift in migration policy, expressing expectations for the return of most Syrian refugees within three years as the political landscape in Germany shifts due to the influence of the AfD.

## POLAND

*   **Government Intervention on Fuel Costs:** The Polish government has announced a significant intervention to mitigate rising energy costs. Finance Minister Andrzej Domański confirmed that, effective midnight, consumers will see lower VAT rates, reduced excise duties, and the implementation of price caps on fuel at stations nationwide.
*   **Diplomatic Rifts over Hungary:** Foreign Minister Radosław Sikorski has openly criticized Jarosław Kaczyński’s public support for Viktor Orbán, labeling the Hungarian Prime Minister as the "most corrupt and pro-Russian" politician in Europe. Sikorski argued that the PiS leader's alignment with Budapest is detrimental to Poland's national interests.
*   **Referendum Momentum in Krakow:** Following a successful verification of signatures, Krakow is set to hold a public referendum. This democratic exercise signals a significant shift in local governance as citizens prepare to weigh in on key municipal issues.
