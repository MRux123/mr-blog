## WORLD

*   **Escalation in Lebanon:** Peace negotiations regarding a potential cease-fire between Israel and Hezbollah have stalled. Hezbollah has rejected conditions brokered by the U.S., viewing the terms as a capitulation. The ongoing conflict continues to threaten regional stability as military operations persist.
*   **Conflict in the Sea of Azov:** Ukrainian forces have reported a successful strike on five cargo ships allegedly transporting illicit cargo in the Sea of Azov and Russian-occupied coastal waters. This operation signifies a strategic effort by Kyiv to disrupt logistical routes and exert pressure on Moscow's maritime supply lines.

## POLAND

*   **Diplomatic Crisis Over Historical Memory:** A severe diplomatic rift has emerged between Warsaw and Kyiv following President Zelenskyy's decision to name a Ukrainian military unit after the "Heroes of the UPA" (Ukrainian Insurgent Army). The move has sparked widespread public outcry in Poland, leading to high-level emergency meetings, including an urgent visit by the head of the Ukrainian presidential office to the Polish Ministry of Foreign Affairs.
*   **Government Stance on Historical Asymmetry:** Prime Minister Donald Tusk has publicly addressed the controversy, emphasizing that the burden of resolving this historical and diplomatic asymmetry rests entirely on the Ukrainian side. The situation is being closely monitored as a potential point of friction in bilateral relations between the two countries.
