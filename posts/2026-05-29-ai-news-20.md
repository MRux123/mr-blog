## WORLD

* **Stalled Iran Peace Negotiations:** US President Donald Trump has refrained from making a final determination regarding a potential cease-fire extension with Iran. Following a two-hour session in the Situation Room, the administration remains in a period of deliberation, delaying a decision on the proposed agreement intended to secure navigation through the Strait of Hormuz.
* **Escalating Security Concerns in Romania:** NATO and the EU have issued a formal condemnation of Russia following a drone strike on a residential block in Romania. Preliminary reports indicate the drone originated from the conflict in Ukraine, with its trajectory altered after being intercepted by regional air defenses.
* **The "Poison Seller" Case:** Kenneth Law has officially admitted to criminal charges in Canada concerning the sale of toxic chemicals online, which have been linked to international suicide cases. Families of victims in the UK are currently advocating for expanded legal proceedings to address the 79 deaths attributed to his activities within Britain.

## POLAND

* **Diplomatic Standoff with Ukraine:** Tensions between Warsaw and Kyiv have intensified following President Volodymyr Zelenskyy's decision to name an elite Ukrainian military unit after "Heroes of the UPA." The Ukrainian Foreign Ministry has issued a statement expressing regret over the diplomatic friction, emphasizing that the move was not intended to offend Poland and suggesting that the resulting discord primarily serves the interests of Moscow.
* **Major Incident in Krakow:** A high-speed police pursuit in the Nowa Huta district of Krakow resulted in severe injuries to two officers. The incident began when authorities attempted to intercept a motorcyclist performing dangerous stunts; the suspect remains a focal point of an ongoing investigation.
* **Political Developments:** In preparation for the upcoming election, the Confederation party (Konfederacja) has officially named Bartosz Bocheńczak as its candidate for the presidency of Krakow. The announcement was made by party leader Sławomir Mentzen during a campaign event held in the city's Main Market Square.
