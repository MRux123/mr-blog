## WORLD

* **Artemis Mission Success:** The Artemis crew has safely returned to Earth, splashing down in the Pacific Ocean after a historic nine-day mission. This achievement marks a significant milestone in deep-space exploration, as the crew traveled further from Earth than any humans in history, successfully concluding a complex lunar voyage.
* **Geopolitical Instability in the Middle East:** Diplomatic efforts are intensifying as U.S. and Iranian officials engage in separate discussions with Pakistani mediators. Amidst ongoing regional military actions, including reports of U.S.-Lebanese pressure on Israel to cease hostilities with Hezbollah, the prospects for a long-term cease-fire remain uncertain.
* **U.K. Chagos Islands Deal Stalled:** The British government has suspended the agreement to transfer control of the Chagos Islands to Mauritius. This decision follows sharp criticism from U.S. President Donald Trump, who publicly denounced the plan as a strategic error, citing the importance of the joint U.S.-British military base located on the territory.

## POLAND

* **Political Restructuring in the Civic Coalition:** During the National Council meeting, the Civic Coalition (KO) announced a significant internal reorganization, electing 15 new vice-chairs, as well as a new treasurer and secretary. Prime Minister Donald Tusk has also designated a core team of ten individuals tasked with spearheading the party's upcoming election campaign strategy.
* **Heightened Security Concerns:** Security forces are on alert following the discovery of an unidentified balloon near the outskirts of Warsaw. Simultaneously, the regional security landscape remains tense as Estonia announces a cessation of naval intercepts of Russian oil tankers, citing an unacceptably high risk of direct military escalation in the Baltic region.
* **Holiday Truce in Ukraine:** President Volodymyr Zelenskyy has outlined the specific protocols for a holiday cease-fire in Ukraine. These guidelines are intended to provide a period of respite amidst the ongoing conflict, reflecting the government's approach to observing upcoming seasonal truces.
