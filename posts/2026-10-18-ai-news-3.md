## WORLD

* **Civil Unrest Escalates in France:** Major cities across France have been gripped by widespread violence, looting, and arson. The unrest follows mass demonstrations against the rising cost of living, with over 500,000 protesters taking to the streets, resulting in more than 1,000 arrests. The situation remains volatile as authorities struggle to contain the civil disorder.
* **Fatal Stampede During Hindu Festival in India:** A devastating crush during the Durga Puja festival in Kolkata has left at least 17 people dead, with many more suffering injuries. Local authorities have initiated an investigation and detained the event's organizers as questions arise regarding safety protocols at mass religious gatherings.
* **Diplomatic Tensions in the West Bank:** European diplomats have reportedly been attacked by Israeli settlers in the West Bank. While the Israeli military has officially condemned the incident, the Prime Minister's office has characterized the report as part of a broader "smear" campaign, highlighting the increasingly strained diplomatic climate in the region.

## POLAND

* **Political Discourse and Confrontation in Media:** A televised debate on Polsat News between journalist Grzegorz Jankowski and Deputy Speaker of the Sejm Marcin Horała drew significant attention today. The exchange, which centered on the development of the CPK (Centralny Port Komunikacyjny) project, underscored the deep polarization in Polish political discourse and the intensity of public scrutiny regarding national infrastructure investments.
