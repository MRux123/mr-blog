## WORLD

* **Escalating Climate Crisis in Europe:** France and Spain are currently grappling with catastrophic wildfires, exacerbated by extreme heatwaves. The destruction, particularly in the Bordeaux region and central Spain, has necessitated the mass evacuation of hundreds of thousands of residents, marking one of the most severe fire seasons on record for the region.
* **Geopolitical Tensions in the Middle East:** Prime Minister Benjamin Netanyahu is heading to Washington for high-stakes talks with President Trump. The agenda is dominated by the ongoing regional conflict involving Iran and the strategic pressure of upcoming elections in Israel, highlighting a complex diplomatic balancing act.
* **Security Concerns in Germany:** A report from the Federal Office for the Protection of the Constitution has identified approximately 9,100 individuals classified as potential extremist threats, underscoring growing domestic security anxieties amidst recent public safety incidents in Berlin.

## POLAND

* **Cultural Recognition for Gdynia:** UNESCO has officially inscribed the modernist city center of Gdynia onto its World Heritage List. This recognition marks the 18th Polish site to be included, cementing the city's architectural significance on a global scale.
* **Public Health Initiative:** A nationwide medical program targeting the early detection of lung cancer is launching for current and former smokers. The initiative is expected to significantly improve early intervention rates and patient outcomes across the country.
* **Imminent Extreme Weather:** Meteorological services have issued warnings for an incoming "hellish" heatwave, with temperatures across Poland expected to reach as high as 40°C, posing significant health risks to the population.
