## WORLD

*   **Massive Russian Offensive on Kyiv:** A brutal and unprecedented scale of Russian missile strikes has targeted the Ukrainian capital, resulting in at least 27 casualties. The attack, described as one of the most intense since the start of the conflict, hit a wide array of locations, signaling an escalation in the ongoing war.
*   **Climate Crisis in France:** Southern France is facing a severe ecological emergency as wildfires consume over 900 hectares of land. Prime Minister Sebastien Lecornu has convened an emergency government session, while political tensions rise, with the Green Party filing a motion of no confidence against the administration in response to the handling of the disaster.

## POLAND

*   **Geopolitical Economic Pivot:** Recent reports highlight a growing divergence in the European Union’s trade strategies. While Southern European nations are increasingly turning toward China as a primary economic partner, Poland is doubling down on strengthening trade ties with the United States.
*   **Healthcare Wage Disparities:** The Polish medical sector is facing internal strife regarding working conditions and pay. The President of the Supreme Medical Council (NIL), Dr. Łukasz Jankowski, reported that 84% of doctors support limitations on working hours. The debate underscores deep-seated frustrations over wage inequality, where extreme salary gaps between medical staff within the same facilities are fueling public outcry.
