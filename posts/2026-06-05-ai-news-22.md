## WORLD

* **Desert Tragedy in the Sahara:** A humanitarian catastrophe has occurred in the heart of the Sahara Desert, where at least 49 individuals, traveling to a religious festival, perished after their truck broke down. Investigations reveal that the victims died from extreme dehydration, having been unable to repair their vehicle or signal for emergency assistance in the remote location.

## POLAND

* **Controversy Surrounding the Order of the White Eagle:** The political landscape in Poland is heating up following comments made by PiS MP Michał Kowalski, who publicly questioned the legitimacy of the Order of the White Eagle recipients. The debate centers on reports that the Committee of the Order of the White Eagle may consider motions to revoke honors previously bestowed upon the President of Ukraine, a move that has sparked intense scrutiny and debate regarding the politicization of state distinctions.

* **Emergency Response in Starogard Gdański:** Firefighting crews are currently managing a major industrial blaze at a warehouse facility in Starogard Gdański. Sixteen units are on-site, with emergency personnel operating under heavy smoke conditions to prevent the fire from spreading to neighboring structures. Authorities have confirmed that there are no casualties resulting from the incident.
