## WORLD

* **Yemen Enters New Phase of Conflict:** The Yemeni government has officially declared an all-out military offensive to reclaim territories currently held by Houthi rebels. This strategic escalation follows a period of intense cross-border strikes involving the Houthi forces and the Saudi-led coalition, marking a significant intensification of a war that has already caused a profound humanitarian crisis in the region.

## POLAND

* **Vandalism in Sosnowiec:** Authorities are investigating a major act of vandalism after the memorial dedicated to the victims of the Sosnowiec Ghetto was destroyed. The incident has drawn widespread condemnation, with local officials and community leaders calling for a swift investigation into the desecration of the historic site.
* **Massive Industrial Fire in Słubice:** A major fire broke out at a large warehouse facility in Słubice. Emergency services were deployed to control the blaze, which caused significant material damage. Investigations are currently underway to determine the cause of the fire and assess the environmental impact.
