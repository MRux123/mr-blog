## WORLD

* **Escalating Conflict in Gaza:** Hospitals in Gaza City report multiple fatalities, including children, following an Israeli airstrike reportedly aimed at a high-ranking Hamas commander. The incident marks another grim chapter in the ongoing regional instability.
* **Hormuz Strait Diplomatic Shift:** U.S. officials indicate significant progress toward a framework to reopen the Strait of Hormuz. While President Trump has not yet formalized the deal, the potential agreement could pave the way for an extended ceasefire and more comprehensive regional negotiations.
* **Vienna Terror Plot Sentencing:** A 21-year-old Austrian man has been sentenced to 15 years in prison for his role in a foiled terrorist plot targeting a Taylor Swift concert in Vienna, highlighting the persistent threat of extremism in Europe.

## POLAND

* **Major Wildfire Crisis on Mazovia:** A massive forest fire in the Wołomin district has prompted the evacuation of multiple villages. The Polish government, led by Prime Minister Donald Tusk, has deployed extensive aerial firefighting assets, including Dromadery aircraft and a police Black Hawk helicopter, to contain the blaze.
* **Diplomatic Fallout over Historical Memory:** Tensions have emerged between Lech Wałęsa and Ukrainian President Volodymyr Zelenskyy. The former Polish president publicly renounced his support for the Ukrainian leader following Kyiv's decision to name a military unit after UPA figures, a move Wałęsa described as a personal and national affront.
* **Domestic Political Friction:** The Polish political scene remains polarized as opposition figures, including Jacek Sasin (PiS), criticize the current government's involvement in the EU's "SAFE" armaments program, claiming it infringes upon national sovereignty, while government officials continue to manage ongoing domestic legislative debates.
