## WORLD

* **Escalation between Washington and Tehran:** U.S. President Donald Trump has issued a stark ultimatum to Iran, threatening to destroy the country in a single night. The Iranian military responded by labeling the President's rhetoric as "crude and arrogant." Furthermore, President Trump has demanded that journalists disclose sources regarding a missing American pilot in Iran, threatening members of the press with imprisonment if they fail to comply, citing risks to the rescue operation.
* **Baltic Security Concerns:** Estonian military intelligence has officially advised Ukraine to adjust its drone flight paths following recent incursions of unmanned aerial vehicles into Estonian, Lithuanian, and Latvian airspace. Intelligence officials identify Russia as the primary actor responsible for diverting these drones, prompting a collective security review among NATO nations in the region.

## POLAND

* **Ecclesiastical Preparedness for Security Crisis:** The Catholic Church in Poland is actively preparing for potential security threats. Archbishop Tadeusz Wojda, President of the Polish Bishops' Conference, confirmed that the Church is coordinating with the Ministry of Defense and the Ministry of Interior to prepare for various crisis scenarios, acknowledging growing public anxiety regarding the spillover of the conflict in the East.
* **Parliamentary Accountability:** Recent financial reports from the Sejm (Polish Parliament) reveal significant spending within its restaurants. Although annual revenues reach hundreds of thousands of zlotys, there has been a notable decline in spending following a recent prohibition on alcohol sales introduced by the Deputy Speaker, Włodzimierz Czarzasty.
