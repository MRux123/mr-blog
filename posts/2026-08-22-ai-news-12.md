## WORLD

*   **Tragedy in Ukraine:** Rescuers are searching through the wreckage of a shopping mall following a strike that President Zelensky condemned as "despicable." The attack has resulted in 130 injuries and multiple casualties, with several people still reported missing.
*   **Aviation Disaster in Kenya:** A tragic safari helicopter crash in a remote region of northern Kenya has claimed the lives of all seven individuals on board. Among the victims are five Americans, including a high-ranking Telemundo executive and the director of Ecuador’s national intelligence service.
*   **Ebola Crisis in DRC:** The Democratic Republic of the Congo is currently facing the most lethal Ebola outbreak in its history, with the death toll surpassing 2,325 people. Official data indicates that the outbreak is escalating, with over 4,900 confirmed cases.

## POLAND

*   **Severe Weather Impacts:** Heavy storms described as a "weather armageddon" have swept across Poland, forcing the fire service to conduct thousands of emergency interventions to secure property and ensure public safety.
*   **Industrial Accident in Zakościele:** A gas explosion at an OSP (Volunteer Fire Department) facility has left six women with first and second-degree burns. The victims were preparing for upcoming local harvest festivals when the blast occurred.
*   **Security Concerns Near Berlin:** German authorities are seeking the extradition of a suspect from Romania following the discovery of an underground weapons cache in a forest near the border. There is growing international speculation regarding the potential involvement of foreign intelligence services.
