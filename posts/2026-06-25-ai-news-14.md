## WORLD

* **Devastating Earthquakes Strike Venezuela:** A series of severe earthquakes, measuring 7.2 and 7.5 on the Richter scale, has crippled Venezuela. With a death toll currently at 164 and hundreds still missing, the humanitarian crisis is compounding an already volatile political situation in the country. Rescue operations remain ongoing amidst significant infrastructural damage.
* **Europe Faces Record-Breaking Heatwave:** A deadly heatwave continues to sweep across Europe, shifting eastward. Authorities have escalated health alerts, with fatalities linked to extreme temperatures reported in France, Italy, and Spain. Record-breaking temperatures in Britain and forecasts of 40C in Germany underscore the growing climate emergency facing the continent.
* **Rising Tensions in the Strait of Hormuz:** The geopolitical situation in the Middle East has intensified as Iran issues threats regarding shipping lanes in the Strait of Hormuz. This development follows high-level diplomatic meetings involving the U.S. and Gulf leaders, further complicating international efforts to secure a regional peace framework.

## POLAND

* **Eastern Flank NATO Unity:** In a significant display of regional solidarity, leaders of NATO's eastern flank gathered in Gdańsk for the Reconstruction of Ukraine Conference. Prime Minister Donald Tusk emphasized that the unified stance serves as a strategic "warning signal" to potential aggressors, reinforcing security guarantees and pledging continued, long-term support for Ukraine.
* **Aviation Tragedy in Warsaw:** A fatal plane crash occurred at the Bemowo airport in Warsaw. Authorities confirmed that two individuals aboard the aircraft lost their lives, while one bystander on the ground sustained injuries during the landing attempt. An investigation into the cause of the accident is currently underway.
