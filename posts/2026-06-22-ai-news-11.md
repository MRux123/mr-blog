## WORLD

* **Political Earthquake in the United Kingdom:** Prime Minister Keir Starmer has announced his resignation as leader of the Labour Party. The move has triggered an immediate succession race, with Andy Burnham emerging as the frontrunner to take the helm, signaling potential significant shifts in British domestic and foreign policy.
* **Diplomatic Breakthrough in U.S.-Iran Negotiations:** Following the first round of high-stakes talks in Switzerland, mediators report encouraging progress toward a comprehensive agreement. Vice President JD Vance confirmed that, as part of the initial framework, Iran has signaled a willingness to permit the return of U.N. nuclear inspectors, marking a critical turning point in regional stability.
* **Escalation in Eastern Ukraine:** Russian military forces are intensifying their offensive in the Donbas region. The build-up of troops near key strategic cities, such as Kostyantynivka, has forced thousands of civilians to flee, as Moscow seeks to breach Ukraine’s remaining defensive "fortress belt."

## POLAND

* **Diplomatic Friction Over State Honors:** A brewing controversy involving President Volodymyr Zelensky and the Order of the White Eagle continues to dominate headlines. Karol Nawrocki’s public stance regarding the honor has drawn reactions from Kyiv and, notably, an unusual intervention from the Russian Embassy, further complicating the complex historical and diplomatic discourse between the nations.
* **Economic Pressures on Essential Goods:** The Polish government has issued new maximum fuel price mandates, effective June 23, reflecting a shifting energy market where diesel prices are expected to decrease while gasoline costs will rise. Simultaneously, a recent inspection by the IJHARS revealed that nearly 80% of retail outlets failed to meet quality standards for ground meat, raising concerns regarding consumer protection and food safety.
* **High-Profile Investigation into Medical Sector Compensation:** The Warsaw Medical Chamber has launched an inquiry into the financial earnings of physician and former local official Dawid Kacprzyk. The investigation follows reports that the doctor earned 1.6 million PLN in the previous year, prompting questions regarding professional ethics and the organization of the healthcare labor market.
