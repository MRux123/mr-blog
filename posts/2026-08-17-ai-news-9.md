## WORLD

*   **Humanitarian Crisis in DRC:** The Ebola outbreak in the Democratic Republic of the Congo has escalated to become the deadliest in the nation's history, with official death tolls reaching at least 2,325. Public health authorities report a critical surge in confirmed cases, placing immense pressure on the healthcare infrastructure.
*   **Geopolitical Strains in the Middle East:** Hopes for a diplomatic resolution to the conflict between the United States and Iran are fading as both nations appear to have missed a crucial deadline for a ceasefire agreement. Simultaneously, Iran’s leadership has signaled a move toward further hardening its stance, appointing loyalists to key positions to maintain a "war footing."
*   **Technological Competition:** China is aggressively positioning itself as a primary source for the data driving global Artificial Intelligence models. This expansion has sparked international concern regarding the potential for Beijing to export its ideological narratives through the ubiquitous medium of AI chatbots.

## POLAND

*   **Tragic Bus Accident in Hungary:** A major tragedy occurred involving a Polish bus on Hungarian soil, resulting in 12 fatalities and 10 serious injuries. The Polish government has initiated emergency response protocols, with the first group of survivors scheduled to be transported back to the country by Monday evening.
*   **Security Concerns and Regional Stability:** In response to the evolving security environment, the Ministry of National Defense has emphasized active preparations for potential threats. Deputy Minister Cezary Tomczyk highlighted the necessity of readiness, acknowledging the possibility of provocations from the East in the coming months.
*   **Social Tensions:** Local authorities in Gdańsk are investigating a disturbing incident involving a hate-motivated attack by adults against Ukrainian children. The Ukrainian Consulate has formally requested a thorough legal investigation, emphasizing that such acts of xenophobia will not be tolerated.
