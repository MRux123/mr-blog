## WORLD

* **Shooting Incident Near the White House:** A violent confrontation occurred in the immediate vicinity of the White House in Washington, D.C. According to reports from the Secret Service, a civilian opened fire, leading to a direct exchange with a law enforcement officer. Two individuals, including a teenager, sustained injuries during the incident.
* **Escalating Tensions Between Turkey and Israel:** Regional stability in the Middle East faces a new challenge as reports emerge of a 20-vessel flotilla organized in Turkey, aimed at breaking the Israeli naval blockade of the Gaza Strip. Israeli media outlets are expressing unprecedented concern, warning that this maneuver significantly increases the risk of a direct military confrontation.
* **Industrial Disaster in China:** A catastrophic explosion at a fireworks manufacturing facility in Liuyang has resulted in at least 21 fatalities and 61 injuries. The force of the blast was severe enough to cause structural damage to surrounding homes and dislodge heavy boulders, highlighting ongoing safety concerns in the region's industrial sector.

## POLAND

* **Conflict of Interest Allegations Involving Zondacrypto:** A controversy has emerged regarding ties between cryptocurrency exchange Zondacrypto and the Polish justice system. Reports suggest that exchange representatives and industry figures provided training to prosecutors and police officers. The discovery that a prosecutor involved in an ongoing investigation into the exchange had participated in a Zondacrypto-sponsored conference has led to his resignation from the case, raising questions about procedural integrity.
* **Legal Standoff Over Pfizer Vaccine Contract:** The Polish Ministry of Health remains in a stalemate regarding a Belgian court ruling issued on April 1st. The court ordered Poland to pay approximately 6 billion PLN and accept delivery of 64 million doses of Pfizer vaccines. Despite the severity of the financial and logistical implications, the Ministry reports that it has yet to receive the official enforcement copy of the verdict.
