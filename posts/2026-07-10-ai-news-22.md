## WORLD

* **Escalation in the Azov Sea:** Tensions in the region have reached a critical point as Russia has suspended navigation on the channel connecting the Don River to the Sea of Azov. This move follows a series of successful Ukrainian strikes on commercial vessels and shadow fleet tankers. Analysts warn that the disruption could severely impact global grain exports and marks a tactical success for the Ukrainian military in challenging Russian maritime logistics.

* **Royal Rapprochement:** In a move signaling a potential thawing of historically strained relations, Prince Harry and Meghan Markle held a rare meeting with King Charles and Queen Camilla. The encounter, which took place in the UK, marks a significant diplomatic development within the royal family following the couple's relocation to the United States in 2020.

## POLAND

* **Constitutional Council Inaugurated:** President Karol Nawrocki officially convened the newly formed "Council of the New Constitution" at the Belweder Palace. The body, which includes prominent political figures such as Przemysław Czarnek, Paweł Kukiz, and Małgorzata Manowska, is tasked with addressing constitutional reforms, sparking significant debate regarding the future of the Polish legal and political landscape.

* **Deteriorating Diplomatic Ties with Ukraine:** Tensions between Warsaw and Kyiv have surfaced publicly, highlighted by comments from Deputy Foreign Minister Władysław Teofil Bartoszewski regarding Ukrainian Foreign Minister Andrij Sybiha’s recent visit to Poland. Bartoszewski described the visit as unproductive, citing a lack of concrete proposals on historical disputes. Simultaneously, MEP Michał Dworczyk noted that Poland’s military support has reached a threshold where domestic defense readiness is now the primary concern, further straining bilateral cooperation.
