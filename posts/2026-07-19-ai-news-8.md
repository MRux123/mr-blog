## WORLD

* **Escalating US-Iran Military Confrontation:** The geopolitical situation in the Middle East has reached a critical juncture as the United States and Iran continue a series of reciprocal strikes. Following the deaths of two US soldiers in Jordan, American forces have targeted Iranian-affiliated positions for the eighth consecutive night. Iran has responded by deploying drones against US military installations in Kuwait, signaling a dangerous expansion of the conflict.
* **Political Exile for Cuban Dissident:** Luis Manuel Otero Alcántara, a prominent figure in the Cuban opposition, has transitioned into exile in the United States. His departure marks the end of a long period of incarceration that began in 2021 following historic anti-government demonstrations in Havana.

## POLAND

* **Widespread Social Media Outage:** Facebook and related Meta services have experienced a significant technical failure, leaving Polish users unable to access their accounts. The platform reported widespread "temporarily unavailable" statuses, disrupting digital communication for millions of users.
* **Aggressive Rhetoric from Kadyrov:** Ramzan Kadyrov, a staunch ally of the Kremlin, has issued provocative statements regarding NATO. His latest rhetoric, which includes references to "the smell of TNT," has drawn international attention as a potential indicator of deepening tensions between Russian hardliners and Western security structures.
