## WORLD

*   **Renewed U.S.-Iran Diplomatic Efforts Amid Regional Instability:** High-level talks between the U.S. and Iran are set to commence in Switzerland. Despite the involvement of Vice President JD Vance, the ongoing conflict in Lebanon and the strategic volatility of the Strait of Hormuz present significant hurdles to establishing a broader regional peace framework. Analysts remain skeptical, noting that previous agreements have failed to neutralize core security threats posed by Tehran.
*   **Contested Infrastructure Projects in Albania:** A high-end luxury tourism development backed by the Trump family has become a flashpoint for local dissent. While the protests in Albania are ostensibly driven by local socio-political concerns, the project has evolved into a symbolic battlefield for broader political opposition against the Trump administration's international interests.

## POLAND

*   **Deepening Diplomatic Rift with Ukraine:** Former Ukrainian Prime Minister Volodymyr Hrojsman has returned his Polish Order of Merit, citing a need to address historical grievances and the "asymmetry of memory" between the two nations. While Hrojsman emphasized his continued gratitude for Poland’s support since the 2022 invasion, the gesture signals an escalating strain in bilateral relations concerning historical interpretations.
*   **Fuel Crisis in Russian-Occupied Crimea:** Following targeted Ukrainian drone strikes against logistics infrastructure, Russian authorities have suspended civilian fuel sales on the Crimean Peninsula. This disruption highlights the growing impact of asymmetric warfare on the occupation's internal stability and supply chain resilience.
