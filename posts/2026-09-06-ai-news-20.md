## WORLD

* **AfD Secures Historic Victory in German Regional Elections:** The far-right Alternative for Germany (AfD) party has achieved a decisive result in eastern Germany, capturing over 44% of the vote. This significant shift in the political landscape is being closely monitored by international observers as a sign of rising populist influence in Europe.
* **Ukraine Prepares for Prolonged Conflict:** President Volodymyr Zelenskyy has indicated that the war in Ukraine is likely to extend into the winter months. Recent diplomatic discussions with U.S. envoys, including Steve Witkoff and Jared Kushner, concluded without signs of a major breakthrough, underscoring the ongoing difficulty in reaching a peaceful settlement.
* **Humanitarian Crisis Following U.S. Deportations to Haiti:** Hundreds of Haitians deported from the United States have been left in precarious conditions. Reports indicate many are stranded without financial resources or adequate protection from local gangs, highlighting the instability facing returnees in the region.

## POLAND

* **Political Polarization Over German Election Results:** The success of the AfD in Germany has sparked heated rhetoric within Poland. Prime Minister Donald Tusk issued a sharp critique, labeling those who celebrate the AfD’s victory as "idiots or traitors." The divide reflects deep concerns regarding how political shifts in Germany might impact Polish national security and European unity.
* **Expert Concerns on Russian Geopolitical Strategy:** Publicist Zbigniew Parafianowicz has analyzed the potential impact of the German election results, suggesting that the rise of right-wing parties in Western Europe serves the strategic interests of Vladimir Putin. Experts warn that Moscow is actively leveraging European political volatility to undermine support for Ukraine and destabilize the continent.
