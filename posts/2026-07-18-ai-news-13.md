## WORLD

* **Escalating Logistics War in Russia:** Ukrainian drone strikes have successfully targeted Wildberries warehouses near Moscow and Tambov. These facilities, identified by Kyiv as key logistics hubs for sanctioned military components, signify a strategic expansion of Ukraine's campaign to cripple the Russian supply chain deep within its territory.
* **Russian Naval Defense Shifts:** In a notable tactical pivot, Moscow has redeployed up to 200 drone operators from the front lines to the Black and Azov Seas. This shift aims to fortify the "shadow fleet" against ongoing threats, highlighting the growing importance of maritime security in the broader conflict.
* **Trump’s Environmental Diplomacy:** Donald Trump has publicly criticized Canada over cross-border air pollution caused by wildfires. The former U.S. leader has threatened to impose tariffs on Canada, signaling a confrontational approach to environmental management and international trade relations.

## POLAND

* **Referendum Bid on EU Climate Policy:** Karol Nawrocki, through the Presidential Palace, is set to renew his push for a national referendum concerning the European Union's climate strategy. This move indicates a sustained effort to challenge Brussels' environmental mandates within the domestic political arena.
* **Logistical Challenges in Poprad:** Authorities remain in a critical phase of recovery operations following a freight train derailment in the Małopolska region. The ongoing complexity of the cleanup, specifically the extraction of submerged train cars from the Poprad River, continues to pose significant engineering and environmental challenges.
