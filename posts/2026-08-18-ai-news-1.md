## WORLD

*   **Violation of Moldovan Airspace:** Tensions in Eastern Europe have spiked after a Russian-origin "Banderol" missile breached Moldovan airspace. The projectile caused an explosion near the town of Tamalza, prompting strong condemnation from Moldovan authorities, who labeled the incident a blatant violation of national sovereignty.
*   **Tragic Incident on Mount Etna:** A 29-year-old American tourist has died after being struck by lightning while trekking on the active Etna volcano in Sicily. Local authorities emphasized the high risks associated with weather unpredictability in the volcanic region.

## POLAND

*   **Public Health Alert:** The Chief Sanitary Inspectorate (GIS) has issued an urgent recall for a batch of "NAN OPTIpro 1" infant formula. The decision follows the detection of a toxin capable of inducing vomiting, with the manufacturer proactively recalling additional batches to ensure consumer safety.
*   **Boćki Tragedy:** A disturbing incident occurred on a private property in Boćki, where a 47-year-old man struck his 4.5-year-old son with a vehicle. The boy is currently receiving medical care, and his condition is stable. The case has gained national attention due to the father's history, as he was involved in a similar, fatal accident involving another son three years ago.
