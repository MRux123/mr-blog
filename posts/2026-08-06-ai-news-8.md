## WORLD

* **Escalation in the Black Sea:** The security situation in Odesa has deteriorated significantly as Russia intensifies its strikes against Ukraine's coastline. The targeted destruction of dock infrastructure and merchant vessels highlights a hardening of military tactics in the region.
* **European Strategic Vulnerability:** A combination of ongoing conflicts in Iran and Ukraine, coupled with extreme environmental challenges like wildfires and mounting migration pressures, has tested the resilience of European nations. Recent reports suggest that major powers, including Britain and France, are struggling to maintain control over these converging crises.

## POLAND

* **Political Milestone and Friction:** Poland marks the first anniversary of President Karol Nawrocki’s term. The milestone has been defined by clear friction between the executive and the cabinet, with Foreign Minister Radosław Sikorski publicly criticizing the President’s use of the veto power.
* **Severe Weather Warnings:** Rządowe Centrum Bezpieczeństwa (RCB) has issued urgent alerts for 13 provinces across Poland. The country faces a volatile weather pattern, oscillating between violent storms with high winds and persistent, hazardous heatwaves, complicating local emergency response efforts.
* **Defense Supply Shortages:** President Volodymyr Zelensky has voiced concerns regarding the dwindling support for Ukraine, noting that deliveries of critical air defense systems have dropped significantly in comparison to the previous year. This development has direct implications for Poland’s regional security posture.
