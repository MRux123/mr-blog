## WORLD

* **Stalled Iran Peace Prospects:** Despite President Trump’s claims that a "great settlement" to end the conflict is near, Iranian officials have officially dismissed these reports as speculative. The situation remains in a fragile, dangerous limbo, characterized by ongoing low-intensity violence between Iran, Israel, and the United States.
* **Shift in NATO Strategy:** Reports indicate that the U.S. administration is planning to withdraw approximately one-third of the fighter jets it currently provides to NATO for European defense. This move signals a significant shift in U.S. foreign policy and raises critical questions regarding the future of the alliance's operational capabilities.

## POLAND

* **Poland Welcomes F-35s:** The Polish Armed Forces have officially received their first F-35 fighter jets, marking a historic milestone in the country's defense modernization. The induction ceremony in Łask was accompanied by celebratory flyovers across major Polish cities, including Warsaw, Gdańsk, and Kraków, highlighting the strategic upgrade to the national air force.
* **Lutoryż Investigation:** Authorities are conducting a major investigation in Lutoryż, near Rzeszów, following the discovery of human fetal remains on a private property. A 57-year-old pathologist has been detained in connection with the case, which has shocked the public as prosecutors continue to verify the scope of the discovery.
