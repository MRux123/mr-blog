## WORLD

* **France’s Political Shift Amid Civil Unrest:** The far-right National Rally is consolidating significant political influence as the country grapples with widespread rioting. The party is successfully positioning itself as the primary force capable of restoring law and order, a narrative that is resonating with a public shaken by ongoing civil disturbances.
* **Escalating Technology in Ukraine:** The tactical landscape of the war in Ukraine has evolved, with the military increasingly deploying F-16 fighter jets to intercept Russia’s jet-powered drones. While effective, analysts warn that this reliance on high-cost aviation to counter asymmetric, lower-cost drone threats represents an unsustainable strategic trade-off.
* **Global Humanitarian Funding Crisis:** The World Food Programme has been forced to enact drastic cuts to aid in Gaza, reducing its reach from 1.6 million to 1.1 million people. This development highlights the intensifying strain on international humanitarian efforts as funding shortages reach critical levels.

## POLAND

* **Espionage Arrest by Military Gendarmerie:** Polish authorities have confirmed a major security operation, with the Military Gendarmerie (Żandarmeria Wojskowa) apprehending an individual suspected of espionage. The case underscores heightened vigilance regarding national security threats in the region.
* **Safety Concerns Following Medical Demonstration Incident:** A serious accident has occurred during a medical rescue demonstration, resulting in a three-year-old child being struck by an ambulance. The incident has triggered an immediate investigation into safety protocols during public emergency service displays.
