## WORLD

* **Far-Right AfD Gains Significant Ground in Germany:** The Alternative for Germany (AfD) party has secured a historic electoral result in the eastern state of Saxony-Anhalt, projected to win approximately 44% of the vote. While the party leads the polls significantly ahead of conservatives, it remains short of a parliamentary majority. This outcome underscores a growing shift in German politics and highlights the challenges faced by traditional democratic center parties in forming future governing coalitions.
* **Stalemate in Ukraine-Russia Peace Efforts:** Despite recent diplomatic initiatives involving US envoys and direct contacts with leadership in both Kyiv and Moscow, the prospects for a near-term resolution to the conflict remain dim. Fundamental differences between the warring parties persist, with expectations in Kyiv suggesting that active military engagements will continue unabated.

## POLAND

* **Diplomatic Concerns over AfD Surge:** Former Polish Ambassador to Germany, Janusz Reiter, has issued a stark warning regarding the rise of the AfD. Reiter cautioned that the party’s historical ties to nationalist movements should be a cause for concern in Warsaw. He framed the election results as a symptoms of a deeper weakness within the German political mainstream, rather than solely the strength of the radical right.
