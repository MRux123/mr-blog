## WORLD

* **Elon Musk Awarded Order of Freedom by Zelensky:** In a significant move acknowledging the strategic importance of technology in modern warfare, Ukrainian President Volodymyr Zelensky has awarded the Order of Freedom to Elon Musk. The recognition follows the critical role of Starlink satellite communication systems in both military operations and maintaining essential civilian infrastructure throughout the ongoing conflict with Russia.
* **Russia Tightens Grip on Critical Infrastructure:** Vladimir Putin has issued a decree granting the Russian state authority to seize control of critical infrastructure facilities. The measure is framed as a security mandate, targeting private owners who fail to adequately protect their assets against the increasing threat of Ukrainian drone strikes, effectively compelling corporate investment in defensive repairs.
* **Hungarian Policy Shift Regarding Russia:** Budapest has signaled a notable change in its diplomatic stance, with official rhetoric hardening against the Kremlin. Acknowledging the shifting geopolitical landscape, the Hungarian government has explicitly identified the current conduct of Russia as a direct threat to the nation’s security.

## POLAND

* **Switzerland Cites Poland as Defense Model:** In a major diplomatic nod, Switzerland has publicly identified Poland as a "model to follow" regarding emergency preparedness and civil defense. The assessment highlights Poland’s robust approach to national security planning in the face of potential regional conflict, marking a significant endorsement of the current government's defense strategy.
* **Police Action in Żory:** Tensions flared in the Silesian region during a law enforcement operation in Żory. Officers were forced to use their service weapons to stop a suspect who attempted to evade arrest by striking an officer with a vehicle. While the officer sustained injuries, officials confirm that his life is not in danger.
* **Salmonella Alert for Imported Eggs:** Health authorities have issued a warning regarding potential contamination in poultry products. Following a salmonella outbreak linked to a Belgian farm that has affected over 300 individuals, European sanitary services are currently tracing distribution chains to ensure that potentially contaminated eggs are removed from the Polish market.
