## WORLD

*   **Peru at a Political Crossroads:** As the nation heads to its presidential runoff, voters face a decisive choice between a moderated left-wing platform and a right-wing contender tied to the country’s autocratic history. The election occurs against a backdrop of escalating domestic violence, leaving the future of Peru’s governance and international relations in flux.
*   **Mass Tourism Challenges in Cuba:** The island nation is currently grappling with a severe economic blow as nearly 100 hotels have ceased operations within a few days. The closures are a direct consequence of new regulatory pressures originating from U.S. sanctions, specifically targeting foreign entities linked to the GAESA military conglomerate, signaling a deepening crisis for the Cuban tourism sector.

## POLAND

*   **Terrorist Attack in Israel:** Reports confirm a tragic security incident in Israel, resulting in at least one fatality and multiple injuries. The situation remains a focal point for Polish foreign affairs coverage given the ongoing geopolitical instability in the region.
*   **Political Tension Regarding Ukraine:** Domestic political discourse has intensified following a heated debate on the state of Polish-Ukrainian relations. Controversial remarks regarding President Zelensky and alleged corruption scandals within his administration have sparked significant friction, leading to polarized discussions among Polish political figures during national morning programs.
*   **Severe Weather Alerts:** The Institute of Meteorology and Water Management (IMGW) has issued urgent weather warnings for 11 provinces, forecasting hazardous conditions, including hail and strong winds, prompting public safety concerns across the country.
