## WORLD

* **Escalating Tensions in Iran:** The security situation in the Middle East has deteriorated as Tehran launched a fresh wave of strikes following a significant round of US military operations in southern Iran. US Central Command reported hitting 90 targets, while Iranian health officials confirm 14 fatalities since Tuesday, signaling a dangerous escalation in regional hostilities.
* **Musk's USAID Controversy:** Global health experts have raised alarms over the impact of Elon Musk’s influence on the dismantling of US Agency for International Development (USAID) programs. Critics link these administrative cuts directly to the hindered response to the Ebola outbreak in the Democratic Republic of the Congo, which has resulted in a significant number of preventable deaths.

## POLAND

* **Diplomatic Dialogue on Historical Disputes:** President Volodymyr Zelensky has publicly addressed his recent meeting with Karol Nawrocki, emphasizing the need for a "delicate" and constructive approach to historical grievances. The Ukrainian leader signaled an urgent desire to preserve strong neighborly relations with Poland, avoiding further friction regarding sensitive historical issues.
* **Civil Unrest and Mobilization Challenges:** Reports from Lviv highlight growing social tension, including riots sparked by resistance to military conscription efforts. This domestic instability in Ukraine, coupled with reports of anti-Ukrainian incidents in Poland, underscores the intensifying strain on regional social cohesion as the war continues.
* **Modernizing the Polish Armed Forces:** In a move to streamline logistics, the Ministry of National Defense (MON) has announced a new pilot program for the Polish Armed Forces. Starting in 2027, soldiers will be able to order uniforms and military equipment through a centralized system and receive their gear via automated package lockers, marking a significant step in the digital modernization of the military supply chain.
