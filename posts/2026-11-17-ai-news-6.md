## WORLD

* **Strategic Escalation in Ukraine:** Russian forces have launched a significant strike on key infrastructure in Kyiv, targeting major bridges and causing disruptions to transport and utility networks. This assault follows a massive Ukrainian drone offensive against Russian territory, marking a sharp deterioration in the conflict's intensity.
* **NATO-Russia Tensions:** The Estonian government has summoned a senior Russian diplomat following allegations of a GRU-backed arson attempt on a local defense firm. The incident occurs alongside Estonia’s preparations to seal its border with Russia, further isolating the Kremlin within the Baltic region.
* **Corporate and Legal Turmoil in Tech:** OpenAI is facing a major lawsuit from the California Attorney General over security failures related to an AI-driven cyberattack. Simultaneously, Nvidia is navigating a high-profile, politically backed campaign to reinstate former CEO Jensen Huang, involving endorsements from high-profile figures such as Donald Trump and Elon Musk.

## POLAND

* **Executive Deadlock:** President Duda is reportedly delaying key government nominations, sparking concerns over the functional stability of state institutions and the potential paralysis of necessary administrative changes within the executive branch.
* **Legal Disputes Over High-Profile Immunities:** The status of immunity for officials such as Święczkowski and Sochański remains under intense scrutiny. The legal battle has triggered public debate, with critics characterizing the ongoing proceedings as being devoid of substantive merit.
* **Infrastructure Upgrades:** Residents of Kraków face upcoming travel disruptions as major maintenance works commence on the city’s ring road, a move aimed at long-term improvements to regional connectivity.
