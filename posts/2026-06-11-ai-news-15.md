## WORLD

* **UK Defense Secretary Resignation:** John Healey has stepped down from his post, citing a critical breakdown in communication with Prime Minister Keir Starmer regarding defense investment. This resignation creates a significant political vacuum during a period of heightened geopolitical instability and signals potential friction within the current British administration over military funding priorities.
* **Pentagon Security Incident:** The U.S. Department of Defense headquarters in the Pentagon has been placed under lockdown following a security incident involving hazardous materials. Emergency services are currently responding to the situation, with staff evacuations underway as officials assess the nature of the threat.
* **El Niño Climate Warning:** Scientists have officially confirmed the onset of an El Niño weather event. The global meteorological community is bracing for significant weather extremes and a projected rise in average global temperatures, raising concerns about the potential impact on food security and infrastructure.

## POLAND

* **Implementation of the AI Act:** Poland has officially begun the implementation of the European Union’s AI Act. This move marks a pivotal shift in the national approach to the regulation and oversight of artificial intelligence, balancing the need for technological innovation with strict ethical and safety compliance standards.
* **Presidential Vetoes:** President Nawrocki has issued new vetoes regarding recent legislation, specifically impacting regulations related to the cryptocurrency market and the professional licensing of foreign medical personnel. The move highlights ongoing legislative friction between the Presidency and the ruling coalition regarding economic and healthcare policy.
