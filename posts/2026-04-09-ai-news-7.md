## WORLD

* **Fragile Cease-Fire and Middle East Standoff:** The situation in the Strait of Hormuz remains volatile despite a proclaimed cease-fire. While both the U.S. and Iran have expressed an interest in maintaining the current truce, conflicting reports regarding the status of shipping lanes and mutual threats of renewed hostilities highlight the precarious nature of the peace. Donald Trump has ordered American military assets to maintain a heightened presence in the region, warning that any failure to adhere to the agreement will result in a military response.
* **Artemis Mission Success:** The Artemis crew is returning to Earth, carrying significant scientific data and lunar findings. This marks a pivotal moment for international space exploration, as the mission concludes with the astronauts bringing back extensive photographic evidence and geological discoveries from the Moon.

## POLAND

* **Constitutional Tribunal Appointment Dispute:** A severe legal standoff has emerged regarding the status of new Constitutional Tribunal judges. The Presidential Chancellery has declared that any attempt to swear in these judges outside of the President's presence is legally invalid. Officials argue that such actions would be tantamount to a resignation, further deepening the political rift over the judicial selection process.
* **Reform of Health Education:** Education Minister Barbara Nowacka has officially confirmed that health education will become a mandatory subject in Polish schools. While the curriculum will cover physical health, hygiene, nutrition, and mental health, the modules pertaining to sexual education will remain optional, representing a compromise in the government's educational reform agenda.
