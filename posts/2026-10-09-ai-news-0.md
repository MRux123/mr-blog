## WORLD

*   **Security Alert at RAF Molesworth:** Counterterrorism authorities have arrested two Latvian nationals who breached the perimeter of RAF Molesworth, a UK air base utilized by the United States. The incident has triggered a high-level investigation into base security protocols and potential foreign interference.
*   **Escalating Unrest in France:** Massive student-led protests continue across France, now entering their third week. The demonstrations, driven by grievances over failing school infrastructure and chronic teacher shortages, have evolved into a significant political crisis, drawing widespread support from adult populations and posing a serious challenge to the government's stability.

## POLAND

*   **National Nighttime Alcohol Prohibition:** In a landmark legislative move, the Sejm has passed a law introducing a nationwide nighttime ban on alcohol sales. The act also implements a prohibition on alcohol advertising and increases the fiscal burden associated with the sale of spirits, marking a major shift in public health and trade policy.
*   **Fiscal Planning for 2027:** The Polish parliament has finalized key budgetary decisions for 2027. Government officials, led by Finance Minister Andrzej Domański, have outlined the primary economic pillars and spending targets intended to stabilize the national economy in the coming year.
*   **Digital Security and Youth Radicalization:** Law enforcement agencies have detained a 17-year-old following the circulation of alarming online content claiming to warn of coordinated school attacks. This incident has prompted an urgent response from the Inspector General of Police, who highlighted the growing threat of youth radicalization facilitated by social media platforms and the urgent need for a societal response to this evolving digital challenge.
