## WORLD

* **Russia-Ukraine War Spills Over Borders:** Security tensions in Eastern Europe have escalated as a military drone crashed into a residential building in Romania. The incident serves as a stark reminder of the regional risks posed by the ongoing conflict, forcing local populations to confront the reality of the war at their doorsteps.
* **AUKUS Expands Undersea Defense:** The United States, United Kingdom, and Australia have announced a collaborative effort to develop advanced underwater drone technology. Part of the AUKUS pact, this initiative is primarily focused on securing vital undersea cable infrastructure and strengthening naval deterrence capabilities against emerging maritime threats.
* **Ghana Enacts Controversial Legislation:** The Parliament of Ghana has officially passed a strict anti-LGBTQ+ bill. The new law mandates jail terms for individuals identifying as gay, lesbian, or transgender, marking a significant and contentious shift in the country's social and legislative landscape.

## POLAND

* **Modernization of Polish Armed Forces:** Minister of National Defence Władysław Kosiniak-Kamysz has signed a new contract for the procurement of 48 specialized support vehicles from Rosomak S.A. The fleet will consist of 30 medical evacuation vehicles and 18 command vehicles, funded under the EU’s SAFE program. The Minister characterized the acquisition as a critical step in addressing deficiencies inherited from previous administrations.
* **Diplomatic Tensions in the U.S.:** The Polish consulate in the United States has been targeted by vandals for the second time in three days, with the building defaced by red paint. Authorities are investigating the repeated attacks, which raise concerns regarding the security of Polish diplomatic missions abroad.
