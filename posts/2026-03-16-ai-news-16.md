## WORLD

* **Strait of Hormuz Crisis:** Tensions continue to rise as international allies rebuff former President Trump’s call for a coordinated naval operation to secure the Strait of Hormuz. While NATO has officially ruled out an intervention, the situation remains volatile as the Israeli military intensifies ground operations against Hezbollah in Lebanon. Iran has further complicated the geopolitical landscape by issuing direct threats to Romania, citing concerns over the potential use of Romanian bases by U.S. forces.
* **Algorithmic Accountability:** Whistleblowers have leveled serious accusations against Meta and TikTok, alleging that both platforms intentionally allowed harmful content to proliferate. According to internal reports, the companies recognized that outrage-driven engagement metrics significantly boosted their platform algorithms, prioritizing profitability over user safety.

## POLAND

* **Security Interception:** The Polish Armed Forces have conducted an emergency interception of a Russian aircraft over the Baltic Sea. The incident highlights the ongoing security challenges and the heightened state of readiness required of the Polish military in the current regional climate.
* **Strait of Hormuz Diplomacy:** Polish Foreign Minister Radosław Sikorski has commented on the possibility of an EU-led peacekeeping mission in the Strait of Hormuz. While Sikorski indicated that such an initiative could be constructive for regional stability, he clarified that Poland does not intend to participate in military operations in the region.
* **PZPN Regulatory Action:** The Polish Football Association (PZPN) has finalized its decision regarding the recent match between Wisła and Śląsk, imposing a fine of one million PLN. This administrative penalty follows disciplinary reviews conducted by the association.
