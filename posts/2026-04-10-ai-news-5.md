## WORLD

*   **Trump Threatens Troop Withdrawal from Europe:** President Donald Trump is reportedly considering a partial withdrawal of U.S. forces from Europe, fueled by frustrations over international policy disputes involving the Strait of Hormuz and Greenland. While no final decision has been reached, the move signals a potential shift in U.S. security commitments to the continent.
*   **Escalation in the Strait of Hormuz:** President Trump has issued a stern warning to Iran, accusing Tehran of violating truce terms by imposing transit fees on oil tankers passing through the Strait of Hormuz. The administration has explicitly demanded an end to these practices, raising concerns over potential maritime instability.
*   **Easter Truce in Ukraine:** Following a proposal by President Volodymyr Zelenskyy, the Kremlin has confirmed a ceasefire in the ongoing conflict between Russia and Ukraine to mark the Orthodox Easter celebrations, providing a temporary reprieve for the region.

## POLAND

*   **Poland Seeks Increased U.S. Military Presence:** Minister of National Defense Władysław Kosiniak-Kamysz has publicly affirmed Poland's desire to bolster the American military presence on its soil, regardless of shifting global priorities. The Minister also addressed recent criticisms from Washington regarding NATO, characterizing them as excessive pressure on the alliance.
*   **Political Mobilization in Hungary:** With upcoming parliamentary elections, Prime Minister Viktor Orban has intensified his campaign, focusing on sovereignty and economic concerns. His rhetoric suggests that the election outcome will determine the future of Hungarian financial support for Ukraine, while simultaneously attacking the opposition over alleged foreign intelligence ties.
