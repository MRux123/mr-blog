## WORLD

* **Russia Suspends Cross-Border Rail Traffic with EU/NATO:** In a significant escalation of regional tensions, the Russian Federation has officially suspended all railway traffic at seven crossings with Finland, Estonia, and Latvia. The Kremlin has characterized the move as "temporary," though the measure effectively isolates transit routes to several NATO member states.
* **Lukashenko Seeks Diplomatic Refuge in Beijing:** Following intense behind-the-scenes negotiations with Vladimir Putin, Belarusian leader Alexander Lukashenko has made a sudden, unscheduled trip to Beijing. Analysts suggest this maneuver represents a desperate attempt to secure Chinese backing as pressure mounts regarding Belarus's strategic role in the ongoing war with Russia.
* **Miracle Rescue in Venezuela:** Rescuers have successfully extracted a three-year-old child from the rubble of a collapsed building in La Guaira state, six days after a devastating earthquake. The event provides a rare glimmer of hope amidst the ongoing disaster relief efforts.

## POLAND

* **Severe Weather Disruptions:** Large parts of Poland are currently battling an "atmospheric armageddon," characterized by violent storms, heavy hail, and gale-force winds. Emergency services are working at full capacity to mitigate the damage caused by the extreme weather patterns.
* **High-Profile Financial Restitution:** Dawid Kacprzyk has initiated a series of financial repayments, with a recent transfer of 80,000 PLN directed to the Bródnowski Hospital in Warsaw. The repayment follows an investigation into discrepancies concerning reported working hours within the medical facility.
