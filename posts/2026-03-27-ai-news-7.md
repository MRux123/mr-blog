## WORLD

* **Escalating Iran-US Standoff:** Global markets remain on edge as President Trump extended the deadline for Iran to open the Strait of Hormuz until April 6, 2026. The threat of potential strikes on Iranian power grids has triggered a sharp rise in oil prices and market volatility.
* **Geopolitical Realignment:** Belarus and North Korea have solidified their military and political ties through the signing of a new friendship treaty. During his visit to Pyongyang, Alexander Lukashenko gifted a rifle to Kim Jong Un, signaling a strengthened alliance between key supporters of the Russian war effort in Ukraine.
* **IDF Personnel Crisis:** The Israel Defense Forces (IDF) are facing a severe recruitment crisis. Military leadership has issued urgent warnings regarding troop shortages, reigniting intense domestic political debate over the mandatory mobilization of the ultra-Orthodox Charedi community.

## POLAND

* **Foreign Business Influx:** Poland continues to attract international investment, with over 10,500 new companies founded by foreigners in 2025. Data indicates a significant shift in the market, with Ukrainian, Belarusian, Chinese, and German entrepreneurs leading the growth in the private sector.
* **Stranded Passengers at Sea:** A critical infrastructure issue continues to affect travel, as approximately 200 passengers remain stranded on a Stena Line ferry in Gdynia. The vessel, bound for Poland from Karlskrona, has been unable to depart for over 33 hours due to a persistent technical malfunction.
