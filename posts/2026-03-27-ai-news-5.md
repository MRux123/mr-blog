## WORLD

* **China Provides Critical Food Aid to Cuba:** In a significant move to alleviate the ongoing humanitarian crisis on the island, China has dispatched over 15,000 tons of rice to Cuba. This shipment marks the second major delivery this year, aimed at addressing the nation's most severe food shortage in decades. The Cuban authorities have pledged to distribute these resources free of charge to the most vulnerable segments of the population.

## POLAND

* **Poland Advances to World Cup Playoff Final:** The Polish national football team secured a critical 2-1 victory against Albania in the semi-final of the World Cup qualification playoffs. Goals from Robert Lewandowski and Piotr Zieliński have propelled the squad to the final stage, where they are set to face Sweden in Stockholm. Following the victory at the National Stadium, President Karol Nawrocki met with the players in the locker room to express national support as the team prepares for the decisive match.
