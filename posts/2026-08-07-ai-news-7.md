## WORLD

*   **Geopolitical Instability in Russia:** Reports continue to emerge regarding a potential security breach at a high-profile Moscow restaurant, which may have resulted in the death of General Valeriy Plakhotniuk. The Kremlin has remained silent regarding the identities of victims, though conflicting evidence, including social media footage and administrative records, suggests an high-level casualty.
*   **Expansion of Economic Warfare:** Tensions between regional powers have escalated, with reports of a significant strike reaching deep into Russian territory, specifically targeting major logistics hubs referred to as the "Russian Amazon" located beyond the Ural Mountains.
*   **Space Observation:** Scientific agencies are monitoring a 300-meter-wide asteroid (2019 NY2) passing by Earth. While classified as "potentially hazardous," experts confirm it poses no immediate threat to our planet.

## POLAND

*   **Public Safety and Tragedy:** A tragic incident occurred at the Blue Lagoon in Siechnice, where a 19-year-old lost his life while attempting to save a drowning colleague. Meanwhile, on the Mazury lake district, a large-scale rescue operation was successfully conducted after four boats capsized, putting over 30 people, mostly children, in danger.
*   **Energy and Economic Policy:** The Ministry of Energy is preparing a new framework for the national fuel program (CPN) aimed at curbing rising costs. Minister Miłosz Motyka indicated that formal decisions regarding fuel pricing strategies and the taxation of windfall profits in the energy sector are expected early next week.
*   **Infrastructure Developments:** The Ministry of Defense has announced plans for the construction of 20 specialized military bunkers, intended to store over 1,300 tons of explosives, signaling a strategic increase in domestic defense capabilities.
