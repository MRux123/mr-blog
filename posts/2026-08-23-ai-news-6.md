## WORLD

* **Escalating Trade Tensions:** Tensions between the United States and Canada have reached a critical juncture, with Prime Minister Justin Trudeau announcing retaliatory tariffs following Donald Trump’s provocative suggestion that Canada should consider the benefits of US statehood. The escalating rhetoric signals a deepening trade war between the two North American neighbors.
* **Seismic Activity in Japan:** A powerful 5.9 magnitude earthquake struck eastern Japan, heavily impacting the Tokyo metropolitan area. Local authorities report 37 injuries, with emergency response teams continuing to assess potential structural damage and the possibility of a rising casualty count.
* **Crypto Industry Instability:** The disappearance of a high-profile cryptocurrency exchange successor has reignited global concerns regarding the industry's regulatory framework and its persistent association with criminal elements, as the mysterious collapse continues to baffle investigators.

## POLAND

* **Legislative Crackdown on Pathostreaming:** A significant shift in Polish digital culture occurred this Sunday as a new amendment to the Criminal Code came into effect, officially banning "patostreaming." The legislation follows a successful, year-long advocacy campaign driven by media reports highlighting the detrimental impact of these broadcasts on public safety and youth.
* **Infrastructure and Travel Disruptions:** Major travel disruptions were reported as Norway’s largest airport closed its operations, resulting in the cancellation of numerous flights connecting Norway to Poland. Travelers are advised to monitor carrier updates closely.
* **Energy Taxation Proposal:** Poland has officially joined a coalition of European nations calling for the implementation of a new, extraordinary tax targeted at oil corporations. The move is part of a broader European effort to manage energy market volatility and redistribute excess industry profits.
