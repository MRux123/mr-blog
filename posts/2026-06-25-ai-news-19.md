## WORLD

*   **Europe Faces Unprecedented Heatwave:** A severe and record-breaking heatwave continues to grip Europe, pushing temperatures toward 40C in Germany and exacerbating public health risks across the continent. French officials have noted a worrying trend of heat-related fatalities even among younger populations, highlighting the increasing intensity of climate-driven weather events.
*   **Maritime Security Concerns in the Strait of Hormuz:** The United Nations has been forced to pause critical evacuation plans in the Strait of Hormuz following an attack on a cargo ship by an "unknown projectile." While no casualties were reported, the incident underscores the escalating volatility in the region, threatening international shipping lanes and humanitarian efforts.

## POLAND

*   **Strategic Escalation by Ukraine:** President Volodymyr Zelensky has authorized a new 40-day, covert operation to be carried out by the Security Service of Ukraine (SBU) against Russia. The initiative aims to exert sustained pressure on the aggressor state to force a de-escalation of the ongoing conflict, though specific tactical details remain classified.
*   **Internal Turmoil in PiS:** In a significant shift within Poland's opposition landscape, long-time party stalwart and close associate of Jarosław Kaczyński, Janina Goss, has been removed from the Law and Justice (PiS) party membership list in Łódź. Official statements attribute the dismissal to long-standing failures to pay membership dues, signaling potential underlying fractures within the party structure.
