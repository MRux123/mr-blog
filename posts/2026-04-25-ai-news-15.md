## WORLD

* **Escalating Violence in Mali:** The security situation in Mali has reached a critical point as armed groups launched a series of coordinated, large-scale attacks across the center and north of the country. International observers describe this as the most significant jihadist offensive witnessed in years, raising concerns about regional stability.
* **Diplomatic Tensions over Afghan Resettlement:** The Trump administration is reportedly considering relocating 1,100 Afghan allies, who previously supported U.S. forces, to the Democratic Republic of Congo. This development follows a policy shift that terminated the specific initiative allowing these individuals to seek resettlement within the United States.
* **Slovenian Political Deadlock:** Slovenia’s political landscape remains paralyzed as President Natasza Pirc Musar announced there is no viable path to forming a majority coalition. Following the resignation of Prime Minister Robert Golob, the President has ruled out appointing a minority government, leading to further uncertainty.

## POLAND

* **Passing of Andrzej Olechowski:** Poland is mourning the death of Andrzej Olechowski, a prominent statesman who served as Minister of Finance and Minister of Foreign Affairs. As a co-founder of the Civic Platform (PO), his passing has triggered reflections from across the political spectrum regarding his contributions to the transformation of the Polish state.
* **Severe Weather Warnings:** The Institute of Meteorology and Water Management (IMGW) has issued urgent weather alerts for 13 provinces. Forecasters warn of powerful wind gusts, reaching up to 95 km/h in coastal areas, which threaten to disrupt local infrastructure and transport, including potential flight delays and property damage.
* **Ongoing Investigation into Deputy's Death:** Authorities continue to process the investigation into the fatal accident involving Member of Parliament Łukasz Litewka. Prosecutors have confirmed that the driver responsible for the collision has been placed in custody, with ongoing forensic examinations of the suspect's mobile device to determine if distracted driving contributed to the incident.
