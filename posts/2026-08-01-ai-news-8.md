## WORLD

* **Renewed Russian Offensive in Kyiv**: Moscow has launched a deadly barrage on the Ukrainian capital for the second consecutive day. This surge in violence follows a controversial retreat by Donald Trump regarding his previous air defense support pledges, signaling a potential vulnerability in Ukraine’s defensive posture as interceptor supplies dwindle.
* **Mass Migration Crisis in Ceuta**: A significant geopolitical challenge unfolded in Spain’s African exclave, Ceuta, where tens of thousands of Moroccans surged across the border. While most have since departed, the incident highlights ongoing tensions regarding migration flows and border security in Southern Europe.
* **Seismic and Thermal Disasters**: Japan is currently grappling with the aftermath of a major earthquake, exacerbated by extreme heat conditions that complicate rescue efforts. Simultaneously, Italy experienced a magnitude 4.7 earthquake near Naples, resulting in over 20 injuries and infrastructure damage, further underscoring the regional vulnerability to natural disasters.

## POLAND

* **82nd Anniversary of the Warsaw Uprising**: Prime Minister Donald Tusk commemorated the 82nd anniversary of the Warsaw Uprising, emphasizing the necessity of national unity. In a poignant address, he stressed that the historical lessons of the struggle against oppression remain vital in the current geopolitical climate.
* **Extreme Weather Alarms**: The Institute of Meteorology and Water Management (IMGW) has issued nationwide weather alerts. Poland is bracing for a hazardous combination of heatwaves, with temperatures peaking at 35°C, and severe storms accompanied by hail and winds reaching up to 95 km/h, disrupting daily life across the country.
