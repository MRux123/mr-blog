## WORLD

* **Iran-Israel Conflict Escalation:** The situation in the Middle East has reached a critical juncture, with reports of widespread power outages in Tehran following targeted Israeli airstrikes on critical infrastructure. The conflict is sending shockwaves through global markets, with stocks sliding and oil prices rising as the International Energy Agency warns of a potential energy crisis.
* **Environmental Emergency:** The United Nations has issued a stark warning that Earth’s climate is being pushed beyond its limits. As the El Niño phenomenon approaches, scientists report that the climate system is in its most imbalanced state since record-keeping began.
* **Tragedy at LaGuardia:** A fatal ground collision occurred at New York’s LaGuardia Airport, where an incoming aircraft struck a firefighting vehicle. The incident resulted in the loss of both pilots, prompting an immediate investigation into the airport’s emergency response protocols.

## POLAND

* **Geopolitical Stance:** Prime Minister Donald Tusk, during the inauguration of an Apache helicopter service center in Łódź, reaffirmed Poland's strategic alignment. He explicitly emphasized that there is no conflict of interest between NATO nations, framing Poland, Europe, and the U.S. as a unified family.
* **Security Incident in the Baltic:** A significant incident was reported in the Russian port of Primorsk, where an attack on an oil export terminal resulted in a major fire, raising concerns about the stability and security of infrastructure in the Baltic region.
* **13th Pension Scheme:** The Polish government confirmed the rollout of the "13th pension" starting in April. Authorities have released updated calculations indicating that seniors can expect payments exceeding 1600 PLN net, depending on their individual tax and social security contributions.
