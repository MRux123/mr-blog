## WORLD

*   **Massive Russian Assault on Kyiv:** The Ukrainian capital has been struck by one of the most intense Russian aerial barrages to date. Preliminary reports confirm at least 25 fatalities, as the offensive targeted a wide range of civilian and infrastructure locations across the city.
*   **Violent Explosion in Damascus:** A deadly blast occurred near the Palace of Justice in the Syrian capital. While investigations are underway to identify the perpetrators, the incident has further destabilized the central district, leaving several victims in its wake.
*   **Vatican Sanctions Sect:** In a significant ecclesiastical move, the Vatican has announced the excommunication of followers of the Society of Saint Pius X, impacting approximately 600,000 members worldwide.

## POLAND

*   **Regional Security and Drone Incidents:** Concerns regarding airspace integrity have intensified as reports surface of unidentified drones operating over European and NATO territory. A recent incident in Turkey, involving a crashed drone suspected to have originated from the Ukrainian conflict zone, has prompted an urgent review of defense protocols.
*   **Legal and Political Standoff:** Former Minister of Justice Zbigniew Ziobro has publicly addressed ongoing extradition proceedings and challenges to his legal status. The discourse centers on the validity of his refugee status and the broader implications for the Polish judicial system.
*   **Public Safety and Coastal Warnings:** Authorities have issued widespread bans across the Baltic coast, with over 40 swimming areas closed to the public. The measures are driven by hazardous meteorological conditions in Western Pomerania and severe cyanobacteria blooms in the Pomeranian Voivodeship.
