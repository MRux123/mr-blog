## WORLD

* **Middle East Energy Infrastructure Under Fire:** Global markets are reeling as oil prices approach $110 per barrel following a significant escalation in regional conflict. Iranian forces have launched strikes against key energy infrastructure in Qatar, specifically targeting the Ras Laffan industrial city—home to one of the world's largest LNG production facilities. This follows previous retaliatory strikes against Iran's South Pars gas field, marking a dangerous shift in the ongoing geopolitical standoff.
* **Escalation in Lebanon:** Military operations have intensified in southern Lebanon, where Israeli forces have systematically targeted and destroyed river bridges. The humanitarian toll continues to climb, with the health ministry reporting nearly 1,000 fatalities, including over 100 children, since early March.
* **Geopolitical Realignment of Energy Supplies:** In response to shifting US sanctions policy, Russia has diverted seven tankers carrying approximately 10 million barrels of crude oil from their original route to China, redirecting them toward India. This logistical shift highlights the volatility of global energy corridors amidst ongoing international sanctions.

## POLAND

* **Political Turmoil within PiS:** The Law and Justice (PiS) party has formally moved to expel Senator Jacek Włosowicz from its parliamentary club. Party leadership cited Włosowicz’s recent public criticisms as evidence of his misalignment with the party’s values, accusing him of being "mentally on the side of Donald Tusk."
* **Diplomatic Incident in Brussels:** Tensions flared in the European Parliament after Polish politician Grzegorz Braun faced sharp rebuke from committee chair David McAllister. Braun was ordered to "shut up" following controversial statements regarding the Middle East conflict and disparaging remarks directed at EU foreign policy chief Kaja Kallas.
* **Strengthening Ties with Lithuania:** In a notable administrative development, Ana Burkovskiene, a representative of the Polish minority in Lithuania, has been appointed as the Deputy Minister of the Interior. She will oversee critical infrastructure protection and civil defense, as well as preparations for Lithuania’s upcoming presidency of the Council of the EU.
