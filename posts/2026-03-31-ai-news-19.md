## WORLD

* **Escalating Brinkmanship in Eastern Europe:** Ukrainian President Volodymyr Zelensky has issued a grave warning regarding Russian ultimatums, stating that Moscow has given Ukraine a two-month deadline to withdraw from the Donbas region. Furthermore, Zelensky warned the Baltic states—Lithuania, Latvia, and Estonia—to remain vigilant, suggesting that current Russian mobilization efforts may indicate plans for wider regional aggression.
* **Middle East Security Crisis:** The geopolitical landscape in the Middle East continues to deteriorate as Iran’s Islamic Revolutionary Guard Corps (IRGC) has issued direct threats against 18 major American technology and industrial giants, including Google, Microsoft, and Tesla, setting a specific timeline for potential operations. Simultaneously, Israel has announced plans to maintain a continued security presence in southern Lebanon even after the current conflict concludes, including the demolition of border-adjacent residential structures.
* **Diplomatic Realignment:** In a significant shift in U.S.-Russia relations, the U.S. Treasury has lifted sanctions on several Russian cargo ships, despite evidence that at least one vessel was involved in the illicit transport of grain from occupied Ukrainian territories.

## POLAND

* **Tusk Condemns Hungarian Pro-Russian Stance:** Polish Prime Minister Donald Tusk has issued a scathing critique of the Hungarian government, specifically targeting Foreign Minister Péter Szijjártó following leaked audio recordings. Tusk labeled the relationship between Budapest and Moscow as "disqualifying" and "disgusting," emphasizing the deep political dependency of the Orban administration on the Kremlin.
* **Constitutional Crisis and Executive Impasse:** The internal political dispute regarding the composition of the Constitutional Tribunal remains unresolved. Deputy Marshal of the Sejm, Piotr Zgorzelski, characterized the President’s current approach as passive and largely ceremonial, calling for immediate action to swear in the remaining judges to mitigate the ongoing legislative stagnation.
* **Security Concerns in the Middle East Mission:** Amidst heightening tensions in the region, the Polish Ministry of National Defense confirmed that a Polish patrol in Lebanon was struck by a landmine, leaving one soldier injured. This incident coincides with the broader regional instability involving UN missions and local military operations.
