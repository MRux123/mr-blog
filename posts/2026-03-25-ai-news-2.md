## WORLD

* **Military Buildup in the Middle East:** Tensions surrounding the Iran conflict have reached a critical juncture. The Pentagon has ordered 2,000 airborne troops to the region, with reports suggesting the deployment of an elite 82nd Airborne Division contingent is imminent. This military maneuvering coincides with diplomatic efforts, as Washington reportedly presented a 15-point peace plan to Tehran via intermediaries in Pakistan. 
* **Global Economic Warning:** BlackRock CEO Larry Fink has issued a stark warning regarding the global economy, stating that sustained oil prices at $150 per barrel would likely trigger a worldwide recession. The ongoing geopolitical instability in the Middle East remains the primary driver of this market anxiety.

## POLAND

* **Slovak Leader Warns of Diplomatic Shifts:** Slovak Prime Minister Robert Fico has commented on the potential political transformation in neighboring Hungary. Fico cautioned that a victory for opposition leader Peter Magyar could sour diplomatic relations between the two nations, while reaffirming his commitment to supporting Viktor Orban against political pressure.
