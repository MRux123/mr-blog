## WORLD

*   **Escalation in US-Iran Conflict:** A violent cycle of retaliatory strikes has intensified following the funeral of Ayatollah Ali Khamenei. US Central Command reports hitting 90 targets, while Iranian officials confirm 14 fatalities since Tuesday. Diplomatic efforts for a long-term peace agreement appear increasingly fragile as both nations remain locked in a direct military confrontation.
*   **SK Hynix Milestone:** Semiconductor giant SK Hynix has successfully raised $26.5 billion in a landmark US share offering. The move, set to debut on the Nasdaq this Friday, marks the largest initial public offering by a foreign firm to date, signaling robust investor confidence in the chip sector despite geopolitical instability.
*   **Environmental Crisis in Spain:** A severe heatwave across southern Europe has culminated in a deadly wildfire in the province of Almería, Spain. The disaster has resulted in 12 confirmed deaths and multiple injuries, highlighting the growing impact of extreme climate conditions on public safety in the region.
