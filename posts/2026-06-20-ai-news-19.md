## WORLD

*   **Strait of Hormuz Tensions:** The Strait of Hormuz has become a focal point of global concern as Iran announced the closure of the strategic waterway. Tehran claims this move is a direct response to Israel’s continued military operations in Lebanon, which Iran labels a breach of prior agreements with the United States. While Iranian military command maintains the closure is in effect, U.S. officials report that traffic continues to flow, with further diplomatic negotiations between Washington and Tehran expected to resume Sunday.
*   **Tragic Loss in Lebanon:** The humanitarian toll of the conflict has been underscored by the death of Mona Khalil, a prominent Lebanese turtle conservationist. Khalil, who dedicated years to protecting the local coastline, was killed in an Israeli strike after refusing to evacuate the beach she served to safeguard.

## POLAND

*   **Diplomatic Friction with Ukraine:** President Volodymyr Zelensky has returned the highest Polish honour previously awarded to him. This move follows the revocation of the award, signaling a period of complex diplomatic relations between Warsaw and Kyiv, as both nations navigate "difficult and painful chapters" of their shared history.
*   **Severe Weather Warnings:** Rządowe Centrum Bezpieczeństwa (RCB) has issued nationwide alerts regarding severe weather conditions. Residents are warned of intense rainfall and strong winds, which pose a significant risk of localized flooding throughout the country. In a separate incident highlighting local hazards, a 38-year-old woman was struck by lightning in Jakuszyce; she is currently receiving medical treatment after being rescued by mountain responders.
