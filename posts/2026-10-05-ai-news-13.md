## WORLD

* **Spain Heads to Early Polls Amid Housing Crisis:** Prime Minister Pedro Sánchez has officially called for an early general election on November 29. The decision follows a series of legislative defeats and mounting pressure from widespread public protests over the nation's severe housing crisis and ongoing corruption allegations within his administration.
* **Escalating Tensions in France:** A 15-year-old student has suffered a traumatic injury—losing a hand—during violent clashes between protestors and police. The incident has intensified the atmosphere of unrest, as students across France continue to demonstrate against government policies regarding the national education system.
* **Fatal Drone Attack on Black Sea Merchant Vessel:** A Turkish-owned cargo ship transporting grain from Ukraine was struck by Russian drones in the Black Sea. The incident resulted in the loss of two crew members and left 11 others injured. The vessel reportedly sank, prompting a significant regional rescue operation and raising concerns over maritime security in the grain corridor.

## POLAND

* **Poland Recognized as European Economic Success Story:** During a formal address in Warsaw, European Council President Antonio Costa praised Poland's rapid development, noting that 22 years after its EU accession, the country has ascended to become the sixth-largest economy in the European Union. Costa cited Poland as a model for successful European integration.
* **New Regulatory Caps on Fuel Prices:** The Polish Ministry of Energy has announced updated maximum retail prices for fuels effective Tuesday. While gasoline prices are set to rise slightly (to 6.79 PLN/l for 95-octane and 7.66 PLN/l for 98-octane), the price for diesel is expected to decrease to 7.82 PLN/l, reflecting shifting market dynamics.
