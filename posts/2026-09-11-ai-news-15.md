## WORLD

*   **Geopolitical Instability in the Middle East:** Escalating conflicts in the region have reached critical thresholds. Israel has announced the successful destruction of a major underground Hezbollah base, an operation so significant that the blast registered as a 4.1 magnitude earthquake. Simultaneously, Houthi militias have seized a strategic island in the Red Sea, directly threatening one of the world's most vital oil transit chokepoints near the Bab al-Mandab strait.

## POLAND

*   **Political Turbulence at the Academy of Justice:** Tensions have flared at the Academy of Justice (AWS) following a change in leadership and the appointment of new vice-rectors. The situation drew significant attention, resulting in protests and the physical presence of opposition leader Jarosław Kaczyński at the scene.
*   **National Security Threats:** Authorities have taken swift action against a 52-year-old man from Oława, who has been arrested and charged for making credible death threats against Prime Minister Donald Tusk. The investigation was initiated following the detection of extremist content online by the State Protection Service (SOP).
