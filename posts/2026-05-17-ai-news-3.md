## WORLD

* **Escalation in Hezbollah's Aerial Tactics:** Analysis of recent footage by BBC Verify reveals a dangerous evolution in Hezbollah's operational capabilities. The Lebanese armed group has increasingly utilized fibre-optic-guided drones in strikes against Israel, marking a significant shift in precision and counter-measure evasion strategies.
* **Geopolitical Purge in Venezuela:** The extradition of billionaire tycoon Alex Saab to the United States signals a profound shift in the stability of Nicolás Maduro’s regime. Saab, deeply entrenched in high-level graft schemes, was a crucial figure in maintaining the deposed president’s grip on power, and his transfer to U.S. custody is expected to expose further systemic corruption within the Venezuelan government.

## POLAND

* **Eurovision 2026 Results:** Bulgaria’s representative, Dara, has been crowned the winner of the 70th Eurovision Song Contest held in Vienna. Poland’s representative, Alicja Szemplińska, secured 12th place. Notably, her performance achieved the highest jury recognition for a Polish entry in over twenty years, signaling a strengthening of Poland’s standing in the international music competition.
