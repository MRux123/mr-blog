## WORLD

* **Escalation in Ukraine:** Russia launched a major coordinated aerial offensive involving drones and missiles, targeting key Ukrainian population centers, including Kyiv and Kryvyi Rih. The strike marks a significant intensification of the ongoing conflict, leading to widespread infrastructure damage and heightened regional tensions.
* **Logistics Hub Struck in Russia:** A massive fire has engulfed the primary Wildberries logistics center in Podolsk, Moscow Oblast, following what appears to be a large-scale drone attack. Local authorities confirmed casualties, marking a strategic blow to Russian domestic supply chains.

## POLAND

* **Bus Accident in Hungary:** A Polish coach carrying passengers has been involved in a tragic accident in Hungary, resulting in 12 fatalities. Local authorities and emergency services, coordinated by the Hungarian government, are currently managing the aftermath; preliminary investigations point to driver fatigue as a potential cause of the crash.
* **Cultural Heritage Loss:** A devastating fire has destroyed a 17th-century wooden church at the Museum of Folk Architecture in Sanok. The blaze necessitated a large-scale intervention by 140 firefighters, resulting in one injury and forcing the indefinite closure of the historically significant site.
