## WORLD

* **Escalating Wildfires in Western Europe**: Authorities in France and Spain are struggling to contain massive wildfires, which have already forced over 300,000 people to evacuate their homes. Officials warn of a critical situation near Bordeaux and other high-risk regions as weather conditions continue to hamper suppression efforts.
* **Sentencing of Russian Nationals in Angola**: An Angolan court has delivered guilty verdicts against two Russian citizens, Lev Lakshtanov and Igor Ratchin, for their involvement in espionage and terrorism. The duo, sentenced to prison terms of eight and eleven years, were accused of attempting to orchestrate a coup and inciting deadly protests related to national fuel price hikes.

## POLAND

* **Tragedy in Berlin**: Reports have emerged regarding a vehicle colliding with a crowd in Berlin. While details remain fluid, the incident has drawn immediate international attention due to the scale of the potential impact on public safety and the ongoing investigation into the driver’s motives.
