## WORLD

* **Diplomatic Deadlock in Ukraine Conflict:** Russian President Vladimir Putin has formally rejected the possibility of a face-to-face meeting with Ukrainian President Volodymyr Zelensky. Responding to an open letter from the Ukrainian leader, Putin dismissed the request during the St. Petersburg International Economic Forum, labeling the communication as "impolite" and signaling that a diplomatic solution remains out of reach for now.
* **Security Incident at Zaporizhzhia Nuclear Plant:** Reports have emerged of a "serious incident" at the Zaporizhzhia Nuclear Power Plant involving Russian personnel. While details remain limited, the situation at the facility continues to be a focal point of international concern regarding regional stability and nuclear safety.

## POLAND

* **Public Alarm Over Release of Convicted Attacker:** Warsaw residents are on high alert following the release of a notorious criminal known as the "Łomiarz" (The Crowbar Attacker). The individual, responsible for a series of brutal assaults on women in the past that resulted in several fatalities, has finished his sentence, prompting widespread concern regarding public safety and the judicial system.
* **Political Fallout Over Diplomatic Rhetoric:** Tensions between Warsaw and Kyiv have surfaced again following remarks by European Parliament member Tobiasz Bocheński. The politician has called for "serious repercussions" against the Ukrainian administration, citing dissatisfaction with how the current government in Kyiv addresses historical sensitivities involving Poland.
* **Internal Politics in Krakow:** Prime Minister Donald Tusk has announced that the Coalition Platform (KO) will reveal its candidate for the upcoming mayoral election in Krakow this coming Monday. The nomination is expected to be a joint effort, potentially representing a unified front between the KO and the Polish People's Party (PSL).
