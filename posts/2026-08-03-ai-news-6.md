## WORLD

* **Humanitarian Crisis at the Spanish Border:** A massive migration surge in the Spanish enclave of Ceuta has resulted in at least 57 confirmed fatalities. Approximately 50,000 individuals crossed from Morocco within a 24-hour window, precipitating a severe political crisis for the Spanish government and sparking intense debates regarding the integrity of the Schengen zone. Prime Minister Pedro Sánchez has condemned the event as a direct violation of territorial sovereignty.
* **Diplomatic Tensions and Conflict:** In the Middle East, geopolitical instability remains high as President Trump initiates a push for new negotiations with Iran, despite a history of failed de-escalation attempts. Meanwhile, regional unrest in Pakistan-administered Kashmir continues to escalate, marked by violent clashes between protesters and security forces in Rawalakot.

## POLAND

* **Criminal Investigation in Warsaw:** Police are currently investigating a gruesome murder case in the Ochota district of Warsaw, where a body was discovered concealed within a shopping bag. The incident has drawn significant public attention and sparked safety concerns in the capital.
* **Economic Forecasts:** New data from Statistics Poland (GUS) indicates a grim outlook for domestic fruit production, with projections showing a decline of nearly 20 percent. Experts warn that this supply-side shock is likely to cause a significant spike in prices at local markets, impacting consumer inflation.
* **Cybersecurity Vulnerabilities:** The vulnerability of state infrastructure has been underscored by a major hack targeting the Portuguese government, highlighting the ongoing threat of cyber-espionage and data theft facing European nations. In the context of broader conflicts, cross-border infrastructure remains a target, as evidenced by drone strikes on logistics hubs in the ongoing regional proxy conflict.
