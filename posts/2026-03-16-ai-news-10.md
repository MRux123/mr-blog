## WORLD

* **Middle East Conflict Intensifies:** The regional conflict has escalated significantly as Israeli forces have launched a ground operation into Southern Lebanon, targeting Hezbollah infrastructure. Simultaneously, President Trump has issued a stern ultimatum to NATO allies, pressuring them to assist in securing the Strait of Hormuz, threatening that a lack of cooperation could undermine the future of the alliance.
* **Political Shifts in Iran:** Amidst the ongoing military tensions, internal instability in Tehran persists following a power struggle for succession. The Revolutionary Guards have reportedly solidified their grip on power, maneuvering against moderate factions to install new leadership.
* **Drone Warfare in Russia:** The conflict between Ukraine and Russia has reached a new intensity, with a massive, unprecedented surge in Ukrainian drone strikes targeting Moscow. Russian air defenses claim to have intercepted hundreds of devices, though the frequency of attacks remains persistent.

## POLAND

* **Tensions Over NATO and Sovereignty:** Polish Foreign Minister Radosław Sikorski has expressed concern regarding the rhetoric of U.S. President Donald Trump concerning NATO. Sikorski warned against the harmful discourse surrounding "Polexit" circulating in domestic political debates, emphasizing the need for unity within the alliance.
* **Presidential Veto and Public Sentiment:** Political fallout continues following President Karol Nawrocki’s decision to veto the SAFE act. Recent polling indicates a majority of the public (56.9%) opposes the President's move, signaling a growing divide in the domestic political landscape.
* **Public Rejection of the President:** In a notable incident at a football match in Zabrze, President Nawrocki faced public disapproval, being audibly booed by sections of the crowd, highlighting the current social polarization regarding his administration's policies.
