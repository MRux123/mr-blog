## WORLD

*   **Escalation in Ukraine:** Russia has intensified its missile barrages against Ukraine, exploiting the country's depleted inventory of air defense interceptors. This strategic shift has led to a significant increase in civilian casualties, raising concerns about the immediate sustainability of Ukraine's defense capabilities.
*   **War Crimes Accountability:** In an unprecedented move, Lebanon has extradited former Syrian General Adel Issa to Damascus. The development marks a significant turn in the pursuit of justice for war crimes committed during the Syrian conflict, as the current regime initiates legal proceedings against members of the former administration.

## POLAND

*   **Security Crackdown:** The Internal Security Agency (ABW) has successfully apprehended a Ukrainian national in Warsaw, suspected of acting on behalf of Russian intelligence services. The individual was allegedly tasked with orchestrating an assassination attempt against a high-ranking official within the Ukrainian defense industry.
*   **Legislative Reforms:** Presidential representative Rafał Leśkiewicz has outlined plans for a new legislative bill aimed at "eliminating charlatans." The proposal, which is expected to be detailed further in upcoming public appearances, signals a move to tighten regulatory standards in professional and public sectors.
*   **Public Accountability Scandal:** The government is facing intense scrutiny following allegations that a state-funded report—costing 200,000 PLN—was produced using generative artificial intelligence. Critics, including analysts from the Sobieski Institute, point to structural errors and faulty bibliographical references as evidence of a lack of professional oversight.
