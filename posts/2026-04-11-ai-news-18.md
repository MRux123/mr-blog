## WORLD

*   **Easter Truce Strained by Fresh Conflict:** Despite a ceasefire agreement between Moscow and Kyiv timed for the Orthodox Easter, both sides have already traded accusations of violations. Ukrainian authorities reported Russian strikes immediately preceding the truce, while Moscow has alleged retaliatory drone attacks in the Kursk region.
*   **Diplomatic Strains Over Iran-US Conflict:** International oil supplies and transport infrastructure remain under pressure due to ongoing protests against high fuel prices. These demonstrations are linked to the broader, escalating conflict between the US, Israel, and Iran, which continues to impact geopolitical stability and trade routes.
*   **Nigeria Imposes Mass Sentences:** In a significant legal development, Nigerian courts have sentenced nearly 400 individuals to prison terms ranging from five years to life, following mass trials for their documented links to militant Islamist groups.

## POLAND

*   **Frost Alerts Issued Nationwide:** The Institute of Meteorology and Water Management has issued first-degree frost warnings for almost the entire country. Despite the onset of spring, temperatures are expected to drop below freezing, posing a significant risk to agriculture and infrastructure throughout the weekend.
*   **IPN Clarifies Status of Historic Virtuti Militari:** The Institute of National Remembrance (IPN) has issued a critical correction regarding a Virtuti Militari order previously listed on an auction site. Initially dismissed as a forgery, the medal has been verified as an original, creating a complex situation for the family of the late Captain Juliusz Heinzl, who believed they were in possession of the authentic artifact.
