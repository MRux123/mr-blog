## WORLD

* **Middle East Cease-Fire Prospects:** Israel and Lebanon have moved toward a potential cessation of hostilities, contingent on Hezbollah halting its attacks. This U.S.-brokered effort seeks to stabilize the region, though skepticism remains following the failure of previous agreements.
* **U.S. Congressional Move on War Powers:** In a significant legislative rebuke, the U.S. House of Representatives has voted to restrict President Trump’s war powers regarding Iran. The bipartisan vote signals a shifting dynamic within U.S. foreign policy oversight.
* **Escalation in the South China Sea:** Regional powers are increasingly mirroring China's tactics by engaging in land reclamation projects, raising concerns over territorial sovereignty and the militarization of contested waters.

## POLAND

* **Dramatic Draw for Poland against Nigeria:** The Polish national football team secured a hard-fought 2-2 draw against Nigeria in a friendly match at PGE Narodowy. The match was defined by a last-second long-range strike from defender Przemysław Wiśniewski, which salvaged the result for Jan Urban’s squad.
