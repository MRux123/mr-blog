## WORLD

* **Escalating U.S.-Iran Tensions:** Tensions in the Middle East have reached a critical point following reports of Iranian-backed strikes against U.S. military personnel across at least four countries. Donald Trump has issued a stern warning, stating that Iran will pay a "multiple" price for every American soldier killed, as details emerge regarding undisclosed attacks in Jordan that resulted in injuries to dozens of U.S. troops and damage to military assets.
* **Houthi Maritime Embargo:** The Houthi movement has officially declared a "maritime embargo" on Saudi Arabia. This move is presented as a direct retaliatory measure against the ongoing blockade of Yemeni ports and airports, further destabilizing shipping routes in the region.
* **Justice for Sinaloa Cartel Leadership:** In a significant development for international law enforcement, Ismael "El Mayo" Zambada, co-founder of the Sinaloa Cartel, has been sentenced to life in a U.S. prison. Alongside the custodial sentence, Zambada has been ordered to forfeit $15 billion for his role in orchestrating vast drug trafficking operations into the United States.

## POLAND

* **Probes into PiS-era Governance:** The political landscape remains focused on the accountability of the previous administration. Senator Adam Bodnar has signaled that the current coalition is reviewing the efficiency of ongoing prosecutorial efforts, setting clear conditions for future cooperation with figures associated with former Prime Minister Mateusz Morawiecki’s political circle.
* **Health Service Scandals:** The Polish healthcare system is facing scrutiny following reports of alleged financial irregularities at the Southern Hospital in Warsaw. The National Health Fund (NFZ) has notified the prosecutor's office regarding a senior doctor suspected of falsifying medical records to secure illicit financial gains. Meanwhile, the university hospital in Łódź continues to address public concerns regarding the continuity of oncology care amidst funding disputes.
