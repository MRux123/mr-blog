## WORLD

* **Escalation in Middle East Tensions:** The United States is weighing a significant intensification of strikes against Iran. President Trump has convened his cabinet to discuss military strategies in the region, a critical development occurring just days before a high-stakes meeting with Israeli Prime Minister Benjamin Netanyahu. The ongoing cycle of mutual attacks between U.S. forces and Iranian proxies remains a focal point of international security concerns.
* **Environmental Crisis in Western Europe:** France and Spain are grappling with a catastrophic wildfire season, resulting in the mass evacuation of over 220,000 citizens. Officials in Madrid have characterized the current blazes as the most severe in the region's history, as French firefighters struggle to contain fires advancing toward Bordeaux.
* **Political Volatility in Ukraine:** President Zelensky is facing mounting domestic pressure following the controversial removal of his defense minister. The subsequent protests and political fallout have raised questions regarding the stability of the Ukrainian administration and the President's personal political standing during the ongoing conflict.

## POLAND

* **82nd Rally Poland Kicks Off:** The prestigious 82nd Rally Poland has officially commenced, marking a major event in the national sporting calendar. Italian driver Andrea Mabellini has taken an early lead, securing victory in the opening stage of the competition.
