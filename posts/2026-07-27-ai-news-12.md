## WORLD

* **Tragedy at Berlin Pride:** A violent assault during the Berlin Pride parade has claimed the life of a 65-year-old Polish woman, with her daughter among the 29 injured. Investigations reveal the perpetrator had a history of extremist ties, having previously served time for plotting violence against the state. This event has intensified public anxiety regarding safety within the LGBTQ+ community and rising Islamist extremism in Germany.
* **Geopolitical Standoffs:** Tensions persist as the US and Iran pause direct conflict to allow diplomatic space, causing oil prices to fluctuate. Concurrently, the Iran-backed Houthis continue to challenge Saudi Arabia in Yemen, adding a layer of instability to the Middle East. Meanwhile, Ukraine's ongoing military campaign in Crimea, which has resulted in infrastructure failures, continues to test Moscow's resilience amidst economic and diplomatic pressures.

## POLAND

* **Political Fallout Post-PiS Split:** The political landscape in Poland is shifting following a formal split within the Law and Justice (PiS) party. Prime Minister Donald Tusk has downplayed the situation while simultaneously signaling a potential, temporary return to regulated fuel prices to curb inflation, a move that highlights the government's sensitivity to domestic economic pressures.
* **Escalating Security Concerns:** The regional security situation has become increasingly precarious, with Romania forced to scramble F-16 fighter jets for four consecutive days due to unauthorized drone incursions linked to the ongoing war in Ukraine. These incidents underline the risks of regional spillover as tensions at the EU's eastern border remain elevated.
