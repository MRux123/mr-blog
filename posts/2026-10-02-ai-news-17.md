## WORLD

* **Ukraine Deploys Domestic Ballistic Missile:** In a significant escalation of its defense capabilities, Ukraine has reported the first combat use of a domestically produced ballistic missile. While the weapon’s current range is limited, officials in Kyiv are actively developing larger variants capable of reaching targets deep within Russian territory, marking a shift in the strategic landscape of the ongoing conflict.
* **Intensified Strikes on Kyiv:** The situation in Ukraine’s capital remains dire. Mayor Vitaliy Klitschko has warned of a "very dramatic" humanitarian and infrastructure crisis as Russian forces continue to bombard critical facilities, leading to widespread destruction across the city.
* **Escalating Unrest in France:** Massive student-led protests have swept across France, with over 1,000 schools facing disruption. Demonstrations against educational standards have devolved into violent clashes with riot police, who are increasingly deploying tear gas to suppress widespread blockades.

## POLAND

* **Political Confrontation Over Economic Policy:** A major domestic conflict has erupted as President Karol Nawrocki issued a severe critique of the current government. The President accused the ruling coalition of dismantling the socio-economic progress achieved over the last 36 years, signaling a deep divide within the Polish state administration.
* **EU Budget Stance:** In a significant move on the international stage, Poland has joined a coalition of 17 nations in formally opposing proposed cuts to the European Union budget, highlighting the government’s commitment to maintaining existing levels of regional funding despite fiscal pressure.
* **Allegations of Foreign Subversion:** Authorities have reported the conviction of a Ukrainian national accused of working as an operative for the Hungarian intelligence services. According to the SBU, the network was tasked with gathering sensitive military intelligence to assess local reactions to potential external intervention in the Zakarpacie region.
