## WORLD

* **Escalation on the Korean Peninsula:** Military tensions have spiked following a breach of the demarcation line by North Korean soldiers, prompting warning shots from South Korean forces. This marks the first such incident this year. Amidst the standoff, South Korean President Lee Jae Myung has issued a formal appeal for dialogue, seeking an urgent discussion on officially ending the state of war on the peninsula.

## POLAND

* **Tragic Bus Crash in Hungary:** A devastating road accident involving a Polish tour bus has left 12 people dead and at least ten others in critical condition. The disaster, which has been described by local officials as a "dark day" for the affected community of Siedliska, has prompted the Polish government to intervene. Prime Minister Donald Tusk has dispatched a special government aircraft to Hungary to coordinate medical assistance and consular support, while the District Prosecutor's Office in Krosno has formally opened an investigation into the cause of the tragedy.
* **Environmental Alert in Gliwice:** Authorities are responding to alarming reports of dead fish discovered in the Gliwice Canal. While the investigation into the ecological incident is ongoing, the discovery has raised immediate concerns regarding water quality and potential industrial contamination in the region.
