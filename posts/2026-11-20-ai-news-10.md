## WORLD

*   **Escalation in Ukraine:** Kyiv faces a major Russian missile offensive, resulting in significant damage to critical infrastructure, including three key bridges. Casualties have been confirmed following the strikes, which reportedly follow Ukraine’s tactical operations against the Crimean Bridge.
*   **Geopolitical Alignment:** In a move observers describe as a symbolic victory, Vladimir Putin has secured high-level diplomatic stature at a pivotal summit, appearing alongside U.S. and Chinese leadership to solidify Russia's standing on the global stage.
*   **Shifting EU-UK Ties:** Prime Minister Andy Burnham has arrived in Brussels for a landmark summit aimed at resetting the relationship between the United Kingdom and the European Union, with expected agreements on youth mobility, food trade, and carbon emissions.

## POLAND

*   **Security Concerns at the Border:** The Polish Ministry of National Defence has confirmed that multiple unidentified objects, including a likely Ch-101 cruise missile and a jet-powered drone, violated Polish airspace in the Lublin and Podkarpacie regions. Authorities have deployed police and security services to secure debris found in several locations.
*   **Government Reconfiguration:** President Karol Nawrocki has formally appointed Andrzej Domański as Deputy Prime Minister. The nomination follows a tense political backdrop, with the President publicly criticizing the state of the national economy and the government's previous fiscal performance.
