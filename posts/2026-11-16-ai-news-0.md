## WORLD

* **Escalation of Drone Warfare in Moscow:** The Russian capital has faced a massive drone offensive, marking a significant escalation in aerial hostilities. This development signals a shift in the operational capability of Ukrainian strikes reaching deep into Russian territory.
* **Geopolitical Realignment in the Middle East:** Saudi-backed forces have retreated in Yemen, handing strategic control of key straits to the Houthi movement. This shift suggests a potential tilting of the civil war's momentum in favor of Iran-backed factions.
* **NATO Internal Friction:** Reports indicate that the planned high-level NATO summit in Albania has been canceled. The move highlights deepening divisions within the alliance regarding strategic approaches to the conflict in Ukraine, the Iranian nuclear program, and defense expenditure commitments.

## POLAND

* **Economic Impacts of Strategic Strikes:** President Volodymyr Zelensky has reported that Ukrainian long-range strikes have successfully neutralized over 55% of the operational capacity of Russian oil refineries, framing the action as a direct response to attacks on critical Ukrainian energy infrastructure.
* **Public Unrest Among Neighbors:** Massive protests have erupted in the Czech Republic, with approximately 450,000 demonstrators gathering in Prague to oppose government-led changes to the funding of public media, marking one of the largest displays of public dissent in the country's recent history.
* **Domestic Political Standoff:** Internal political tensions in Poland continue to intensify, with key figures from the Law and Justice (PiS) party openly criticizing the current administration's approach to the Constitutional Tribunal, signaling a deepening polarization regarding the rule of law and judicial reform.
