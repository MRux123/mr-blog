## WORLD

* **Escalation of Iran Conflict:** The regional conflict involving Iran, the United States, and Israel has reached a critical stage following operations initiated on February 28. The ongoing warfare has significantly disrupted global markets and continues to destabilize neighboring territories, marking a period of sustained international tension.
* **Political Vacuum in Cameroon:** Concerns are mounting over the disappearance of 93-year-old President Paul Biya, the world's oldest head of state. Having been absent from the country for over two months under the guise of a private European trip, his prolonged absence has triggered a domestic crisis and growing criticism regarding the governance of the nation.

## POLAND

* **Extreme Weather Alerts:** The Institute of Meteorology and Water Management (IMGW) has issued nationwide weather alerts as Poland braces for a volatile combination of severe thunderstorms, hail, and high temperatures reaching 33 degrees Celsius.
* **Cultural Loss:** The Polish arts community is in mourning following the death of Jarosław Abramow-Newerly, a renowned writer and composer celebrated for his significant contributions to the national culture, including collaborations with iconic figures such as Agnieszka Osiecka.
