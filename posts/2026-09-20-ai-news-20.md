## WORLD

*   **Russian Election Results and International Condemnation:** Following a three-day voting period, the ruling party "United Russia" has claimed victory with 57.54% of the vote. The international community, including the Polish Ministry of Foreign Affairs, has formally condemned the conduct of these elections, specifically protesting their implementation in occupied Ukrainian territories and other contested regions, declaring them legally null and void.
*   **Germany’s Political Crisis:** Chancellor Friedrich Merz is facing severe pressure following a "disastrous" performance by the CDU in recent state elections. The party suffered significant losses, finishing behind both far-right and far-left factions in eastern states, prompting questions regarding the stability of his leadership.
*   **Trump’s Escalating Rhetoric on Iran:** President Donald Trump has signaled a potential shift in US-Iran policy, openly discussing "three scenarios" during a recent interview, which include aggressive military action, further sanctions, or a diplomatic pivot. The president also hinted at a potential high-level meeting with President Masud Pezeshkian in New York.

## POLAND

*   **Investigation into Allegations of "Political Corruption":** Robert Biedroń has publicly called for a prosecutorial investigation into allegations regarding corporate sponsorship packages linked to events featuring President Karol Nawrocki. The President's office has formally denied the accusations, which Biedroń described as a severe case of "political corruption."
*   **Security Incident in Bukowno:** A major police operation took place in the Małopolska region following a shooting incident that left two men injured. Authorities launched a manhunt, urging residents to remain indoors, which successfully concluded with the arrest of a 52-year-old suspect late Sunday evening.
