## WORLD

*   **Fragile Cease-Fire Between US and Iran:** A two-week truce between the United States and Iran is under severe strain due to ongoing Israeli military operations in Lebanon. While the US maintains that the truce agreement did not explicitly cover the Lebanese front, Iranian officials view the escalation as a breach of the deal. High-stakes peace negotiations are scheduled for this Saturday in Islamabad, Pakistan, with US Vice President J.D. Vance leading the American delegation.
*   **Hungarian Policy Shift Following US Engagement:** Following a visit by Vice President J.D. Vance, the Hungarian government has announced significant trade and defense acquisitions from the United States. This includes a $700 million deal for HIMARS missile systems and a $500 million oil purchase, signaling a deepening strategic alignment between Budapest and the current US administration.
*   **Canadian Political Realignment:** The Liberal Party in Canada, led by Prime Minister Mark Carney, is on the verge of securing a parliamentary majority. The shift follows the defection of key opposition members, which is expected to grant the government significantly expanded legislative authority.

## POLAND

*   **Escalating Political Rhetoric:** The domestic political landscape remains polarized as Zbigniew Bogucki, head of the Presidential Chancellery, launched a sharp critique against Prime Minister Donald Tusk. Bogucki accused the government of manufacturing distractions—such as the debate over crypto-assets—to mask administrative incompetence and policies that he claims are undermining the state.
*   **Sieradz Criminal Case:** Local judicial authorities have ordered the three-month detention of a 37-year-old teacher in Sieradz. The individual is accused of supplying illegal drugs to a minor in exchange for sexual favors, a case that has sparked significant public outrage and highlights ongoing concerns regarding child safety in the educational system.
