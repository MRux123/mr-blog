## WORLD

* **Rising Tensions in the Strait of Hormuz:** The geopolitical situation in the Middle East has reached a critical juncture following reports of attacks on three oil tankers. The incident occurs amid a broader diplomatic deadlock, as Iran continues to challenge maritime transit, significantly increasing the risk of a wider regional conflict.
* **EU Secures Critical Funding for Ukraine:** After months of intense negotiations and an impasse caused by Hungarian objections regarding Russian oil supplies, the European Union has moved to finalize a €90 billion financial support package for Ukraine, signaling a unified commitment to the country's economic and defense efforts.

## POLAND

* **Pentagon's Massive Spending Plan Faces Scrutiny:** The Polish media is closely monitoring the Biden administration's proposed $1.5 trillion defense budget. The sheer scale of the investment has sparked significant debate within the U.S. Congress, with repercussions for global security architecture and Polish-American military cooperation.
* **Political Transition in Hungary:** Following the recent parliamentary elections in Hungary, Peter Magyar, leader of the TISZA party, has announced his official inauguration date. The event, scheduled for May 9, is being closely observed by regional partners as a potential turning point for domestic and foreign policy in Central Europe.
* **National Fuel Price Adjustments:** The Ministry of Energy has announced new maximum fuel prices for April 23. This regulatory intervention comes as the economy navigates inflationary pressures, reflecting the government's ongoing effort to stabilize energy costs for consumers and local businesses.
