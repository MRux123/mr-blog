## WORLD

*   **Escalation in the Middle East:** Military tensions between the United States and Iran have reached a critical juncture. Following strikes on oil tankers in the Strait of Hormuz, the U.S. launched retaliatory military operations against Iranian naval assets. President Trump has signaled the end of the preliminary ceasefire, casting doubt on the future of diplomatic negotiations as Iran reports targeting U.S. military installations in Bahrain and Kuwait.
*   **NATO Summit Turmoil:** The ongoing NATO summit in Turkey has become a theatre of diplomatic friction. President Trump has openly criticized European allies for their lack of military participation in the Iran conflict and engaged in pointed rhetoric against specific member states, further complicating the alliance's unified response to the deepening regional crisis.

## POLAND

*   **Diplomatic Fallout in the European Parliament:** In a significant shift in legislative stance, the European Parliament has formally adopted a resolution expressing regret regarding recent escalatory actions by Ukrainian President Volodymyr Zelenskyy, specifically concerning the naming of military units after UPA figures.
*   **High-Level Consultations at NATO:** On the sidelines of the Ankara summit, intense diplomatic efforts are underway to facilitate a meeting between Polish President Karol Nawrocki and President Zelenskyy. The talks, aimed at aligning schedules during a period of complex regional geopolitical challenges, underscore Poland's active role in navigating the evolving situation in Ukraine.
*   **Continued Security Threats:** The ongoing conflict continues to take a heavy toll, with overnight Russian missile strikes targeting major Ukrainian cities, including Kyiv and Kharkiv. The attacks have resulted in civilian casualties and significant infrastructure damage, further emphasizing the volatility of the security landscape bordering Poland.
