## WORLD

* **G7 Coordinated Oil Release:** In a strategic move to stabilize global energy markets and preempt potential US diesel export bans, the G7 nations have announced a coordinated release of 100 million barrels of oil and diesel. This intervention aims to mitigate volatile price spikes and secure supply chain continuity amidst rising geopolitical tensions.
* **Escalation in Yemen:** The security situation in Yemen has deteriorated rapidly following intense overnight bombardment of the capital, Sanaa. Reports of significant clashes near Taiz, the country’s third-largest city, have sparked international concern regarding the possibility of a return to full-scale warfare.
* **Terrorism Charges in UK:** British authorities have formally charged 28-year-old Joshua Kerry with the murder of former lawmaker Ann Widdecombe. The investigation has taken a grave turn as prosecutors disclosed charges related to the preparation of acts of terrorism, including specific threats aimed at political figures like Nigel Farage.

## POLAND

* **US Military Presence:** Marcin Przydacz, head of the Presidential International Policy Bureau, has confirmed that the White House has given the "green light" regarding the expansion of US military infrastructure within Poland. This development signals a deepening of the strategic defense partnership between Warsaw and Washington.
* **Counter-Sabotage Efforts:** Deputy Minister of National Defense Cezary Tomczyk addressed the growing domestic security concerns, revealing that a number of saboteurs are currently in custody. Tomczyk emphasized the reality of the threat, highlighting ongoing efforts to neutralize hostile activities within the country.
* **Nuclear Energy Progress:** Minister Miłosz Motyka has provided a long-awaited update on the status of Poland's first nuclear power plant. The project represents a cornerstone of the country's transition toward energy independence and long-term decarbonization goals.
