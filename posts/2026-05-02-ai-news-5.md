## WORLD

* **US Military Realignment in Germany:** The Pentagon has confirmed the withdrawal of several thousand US troops from Germany. This strategic pivot follows President Trump’s criticism of Berlin’s stance on the ongoing US-Iran conflict. The move reflects growing tensions within NATO as Washington reassesses its military footprint in Europe amidst a challenging geopolitical landscape.
* **Humanitarian Crisis in Gaza:** Reports from the Gaza Strip indicate a worsening humanitarian situation, with displaced persons' camps facing severe sanitary threats. The infestation of pests, including rats and weasels, has become a critical public health concern, complicating the already dire conditions for those seeking shelter in the conflict zone.

## POLAND

* **Defense Logistics and Supply Delays:** Poland, along with other European allies such as the UK, Estonia, and Lithuania, faces potential delays in receiving US military hardware. According to the Financial Times, the US defense industry is experiencing strain due to the high consumption of resources in the conflict with Iran, forcing a recalibration of international supply chains.
* **National Commemoration:** Poland observes Flag Day on May 2nd, a symbolic moment of national unity and historical reflection. The white-and-red banner remains the central focus of patriotic celebrations, highlighting the country's collective identity during a period of significant international uncertainty.
