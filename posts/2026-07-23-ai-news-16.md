## WORLD

* **Oil Prices Surge to $100:** Brent crude has climbed past the $100-per-barrel mark for the first time since May, triggered by escalating tensions in the Middle East. The surge follows claims from Iran-backed Houthi rebels in Yemen regarding attacks on Saudi oil tankers, which have heightened fears of a broader regional conflict.
* **Geopolitical Instability:** The conflict has further intensified as the United States launched additional strikes against Iran-aligned targets. Simultaneously, President Trump faces domestic legislative pressure as Congress moves to curb his war powers, reflecting a deepening internal debate over U.S. intervention in the region.
* **Nuclear Diplomacy:** While Saudi Arabia explores nuclear energy, the U.S. has explicitly linked any potential nuclear cooperation agreement to Riyadh’s willingness to join the Abraham Accords and normalize relations with Israel.

## POLAND

* **Political Tensions over Referendum:** President Karol Nawrocki has formally submitted a second request to the Senate for an nationwide referendum regarding the European Union's "Green Deal" climate policies. The proposed vote is currently scheduled for September 27, 2026, marking a significant point of contention in domestic policy.
* **Diplomatic Fallout:** The decision to strip Ukrainian President Volodymyr Zelenskyy of the Order of the White Eagle continues to dominate public discourse. The move has prompted widespread debate regarding the current state of Polish-Ukrainian relations and the motivations behind the administration's recent shift in diplomatic posture.
