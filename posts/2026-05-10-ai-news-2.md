## WORLD

*   **Geopolitical Shifts in Ukraine:** Russian President Vladimir Putin has signaled a potential shift in the trajectory of the conflict in Ukraine, suggesting that the war may be nearing a resolution. While expressing an openness to negotiations, Putin simultaneously intensified his criticism of Western military and political support for President Volodymyr Zelensky, framing the international involvement as a primary obstacle to peace.
*   **Hantavirus Outbreak at Sea:** Authorities in Tenerife are preparing for the emergency docking of the MV Hondius, a cruise ship currently grappling with a severe hantavirus outbreak. Three individuals, including a British expedition guide, have already been medically evacuated. The situation has highlighted the complexities of managing infectious diseases on international vessels and prompted urgent cooperation between maritime authorities and Spanish health officials.
*   **Human Rights Concerns in Somalia:** International attention has turned to Somalia following reports of the detention and alleged torture of 27-year-old activist Sadia Moalim Ali. The rickshaw driver, imprisoned for participating in peaceful anti-government protests, has provided harrowing testimony regarding her treatment in custody, shedding light on the ongoing crackdown against political dissent in the region.
