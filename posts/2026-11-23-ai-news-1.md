## WORLD

* **Russian Naval Assets Targeted in Libya:** A Russian navy corvette, *Merkury*, was reportedly sunk in the port of Benghazi following a suspected Ukrainian sea drone attack. This incident, part of a broader series of maritime challenges to Russian logistics, has caused significant damage and highlights the widening geographic scope of the conflict.
* **ICJ Genocide Ruling Sparks Diplomatic Anxiety:** The International Court of Justice is set to deliver a verdict on whether Myanmar committed genocide against the Rohingya. The case has triggered fears of a potential diplomatic crisis, with reports suggesting the United States may consider sanctions against ICJ judges if the ruling is perceived as setting a problematic precedent for other ongoing international disputes.
* **Heightened Rhetoric from Moscow:** Russian Foreign Minister Sergey Lavrov has escalated tensions by questioning Ukraine’s sovereignty, publicly characterizing large swaths of Ukrainian territory, including Kyiv, as "inherently Russian." The rhetoric signals a hardened stance on the Kremlin's long-term war objectives.

## POLAND

* **Critical Infrastructure Incident in Gdańsk:** Authorities have reported a major fire at the Port of Gdańsk, prompting local officials to issue urgent appeals to residents. The situation remains under close observation due to the strategic importance of the maritime hub.
* **International Recognition of Polish Defense Efforts:** The Polish government has received notable acknowledgement from high-ranking Pentagon officials, who publicly highlighted the contributions of two Polish cabinet members, underscoring Poland's growing influence and integration within NATO's strategic framework.
