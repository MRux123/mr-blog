## WORLD

*   **Middle East Brinkmanship:** Diplomatic efforts to de-escalate the conflict between Iran and the United States have reached a critical juncture. Despite ongoing Israeli airstrikes in Lebanon, officials from both Washington and Tehran suggest that a formal agreement could be finalized within days. Analysts remain cautious, noting that while the Iranian leadership appears more resilient to economic pressure than previously expected, the potential for last-minute disruptions remains high.
*   **Escalating Defense Deficits in Ukraine:** As the war persists, the disparity between Russia’s ballistic missile inventory and Ukraine’s defensive capabilities is widening. Kyiv has issued an urgent plea for additional Patriot interceptors, warning that their current air-defense supplies are reaching a critical point of depletion.
*   **Civil Unrest in Kenya:** A protest against a proposed US-led Ebola quarantine facility in Nanyuki turned fatal after police intervened to disperse demonstrators. The incident, which resulted in the death of a protest organizer, underscores growing local tensions regarding foreign-led health infrastructure projects.

## POLAND

*   **Lutoryż Investigation Deepens:** Authorities are continuing an extensive search at a site in Lutoryż, near Rzeszów, following the discovery of numerous human fetal remains. A pathologist has been detained and hospitalized as part of the ongoing investigation, with the prosecution currently processing 32 sets of remains recovered from the scene.
*   **Future of the 'CPN' Fuel Package:** Prime Minister Donald Tusk has announced that the government intends to conclude the "Ceny Paliwa Niżej" (Lower Fuel Prices) program this summer. The initiative, launched in March as a response to economic turbulence, is being phased out as the administration evaluates the stability of current energy markets.
