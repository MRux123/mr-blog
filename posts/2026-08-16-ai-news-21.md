## WORLD

*   **Diplomatic Push in Gaza:** Jared Kushner has initiated high-level meetings with Hamas leadership in Egypt in a renewed attempt to revive peace negotiations for Gaza. The intervention follows a recent rejection of the Trump administration's peace framework by Israel, marking a critical and volatile juncture in Middle Eastern diplomacy.
*   **Major Ukrainian Offensive inside Russia:** A massive coordinated drone attack by Ukraine has hit several Russian regions, resulting in at least 11 fatalities. The strikes, which targeted critical infrastructure including a warehouse facility in Podolsk, represent one of the most significant cross-border escalations in recent months.
*   **Natural Disasters:** Significant international concern follows a deadly hurricane in Hawaii, causing widespread destruction, and devastating wildfires on the Greek island of Salamina, which have resulted in two deaths and necessitated emergency evacuations.

## POLAND

*   **Fatal Coach Accident on Hungarian Soil:** A tragic accident involving a Polish tourist coach in Hungary remains a focal point for the Polish Ministry of Foreign Affairs. While one driver has been released, the second remains in custody as Hungarian authorities investigate the specific circumstances surrounding the incident.
*   **Search for Missing Child:** Law enforcement agencies have launched an urgent search for 11-year-old Sara, a resident of Krzanowice, who has been reported missing. Authorities are currently appealing for any information that could assist in locating the child.
*   **Security Incident near Poznań:** Police have successfully apprehended a 51-year-old suspect in connection with a shooting that occurred in the vicinity of Poznań, specifically in Swarzędz, where a woman was wounded. Investigation into the motive and circumstances of the attack is ongoing.
