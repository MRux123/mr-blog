## WORLD

* **Escalating Wildfires in Spain:** A dire emergency is unfolding in central Spain, where massive forest and brush fires have forced the evacuation or confinement of at least 175,000 residents. The regions of Madrid and Castile-La Mancha are currently experiencing the most severe conditions, straining emergency resources.
* **Fatal Shooting at Seattle Festival:** Security at a public event in the United States turned tragic after a mass shooting at the "Bite of Seattle" culinary festival. Local authorities report two fatalities and at least five injuries, with two suspects currently in custody. 
* **Diplomatic Tensions in South America:** Relations between Argentina and Brazil have further deteriorated following inflammatory remarks by Argentine President Javier Milei. During a rally in Brazil supporting right-wing candidate Flávio Bolsonaro, Milei publicly labeled the Brazilian president a "thief" and a "convict," marking a significant shift in regional political rhetoric.

## POLAND

* **Medical Malpractice Allegations:** A disturbing report has surfaced regarding the death of a 61-year-old patient at the Mazowiecki Szpital Bródnowski. Allegations suggest that the patient, suffering from a severe heart attack, was treated with paracetamol upon arrival at the ER, with an accurate diagnosis delayed by over five hours.
* **Internal Political Friction:** The Polish political landscape remains volatile as public disputes intensify. Lawmaker Paweł Jabłoński has openly challenged former TVP head Jacek Kurski, inviting legal action, while cabinet members have publicly clashed over contentious policy statements, highlighting growing divisions within the current government and major opposition circles.
* **Conflict of Interest Controversy:** A potential ethical concern has emerged involving politician Ireneusz Raś, who is currently drafting legislation concerning "micro-apartments" despite previously profiting from the rental market of such properties, raising questions about legislative impartiality and transparency.
