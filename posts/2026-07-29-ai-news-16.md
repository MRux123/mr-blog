## WORLD

* **Middle East Conflict Escalation:** Tensions have reached a critical juncture as the U.S. and Saudi Arabia conducted joint airstrikes against Iranian-backed proxies in Iraq. In a retaliatory escalation, Iran has launched direct strikes on sites in Jordan, raising grave concerns about the conflict spreading further across the region.
* **Public Health Alert in London:** A humanitarian worker evacuated from the Democratic Republic of the Congo is currently under medical observation at a specialist hospital in London. The individual is being monitored for potential exposure to the Ebola virus following their work at the heart of the current outbreak.

## POLAND

* **Debate on EU Defense Capabilities:** A significant political and strategic discussion has emerged regarding the efficacy of European Union defense clauses, with experts suggesting they could potentially serve as a more robust mechanism for security than the traditional Article 5 of the NATO treaty.
* **Surge in Antisemitic Incidents:** Recent data indicates a disturbing trend, with 2025 recorded as the worst year for antisemitic attacks in decades. The rise in hate-fueled incidents has sparked urgent calls for increased security measures and social awareness campaigns.
* **Extreme Weather Warnings:** A severe heatwave is set to grip Poland, with the Institute of Meteorology and Water Management (IMGW) issuing warnings for 15 provinces. Temperatures are expected to climb to 36 degrees Celsius, prompting authorities to advise the public on health precautions as the heat wave begins this Thursday.
