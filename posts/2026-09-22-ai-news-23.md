## WORLD

* **Rising Tensions Between the U.S. and Iran**: The UN General Assembly has been overshadowed by President Trump’s aggressive rhetoric, in which he stated he must choose between a peace deal or the total "annihilation" of Iran. The statement has sparked widespread concern among world leaders regarding the potential for deep geopolitical fractures. In response, Iranian officials have rejected U.S. pressure, firmly asserting that their nation will not be treated as a political pawn.
* **Rescue in Malawi**: International authorities confirmed a successful police operation in Blantyre, resulting in the rescue of a kidnapped British woman, Nusrat Osman. The operation, which involved a high-stakes shootout, led to the deaths of four alleged abductors, ending a week-long ordeal that had mobilized local law enforcement.

## POLAND

* **Melania Trump Highlights Polish-U.S. Cooperation at the UN**: During the UN summit, First Lady Melania Trump publicly endorsed an initiative led by Poland’s First Lady, Marta Nawrocka. The project, titled "Shaping the Future Together," aims to bridge the digital and educational divide for children, marking a significant moment of diplomatic collaboration between the two nations.
* **Challenges for Urban’s National Squad**: The Polish national football team is facing personnel setbacks following news of yet another injury within the squad. Head coach Urban has been forced to issue an emergency call-up, highlighting the team's ongoing struggle to maintain depth as they prepare for upcoming international fixtures.
