## WORLD

*   **Escalating Conflict Impacts:** Russian President Vladimir Putin has made a rare public admission regarding the impact of Ukrainian strikes, acknowledging that the attacks are causing noticeable fuel supply problems across Russia. While downplaying the severity as non-critical, the admission signals a shift in the operational reality of the conflict.
*   **Humanitarian Crisis in DRC:** Public health officials have raised alarms over an escalating Ebola outbreak in the Democratic Republic of the Congo. Authorities have lost track of nearly 300 individuals who tested positive for the virus, fueling concerns over massive community transmission in conflict-affected regions where humanitarian access remains severely restricted.
*   **Tragedy in Germany:** A fatal shooting at a centre for mothers and children in Germany has left six people dead. Authorities have apprehended a male suspect, who, according to preliminary police reports, was involved in an ongoing custody dispute regarding his young daughter.

## POLAND

*   **Environmental Threat in Chernobyl:** A major fire has broken out within the Chernobyl Exclusion Zone. The incident has resulted in a marked decline in air quality in Kyiv, raising concerns about the potential spread of smoke and the safety of the surrounding regions.
*   **Public Safety Incident in Dębica:** A major evacuation effort was carried out on a train in Dębica after smoke was detected in the carriages. Approximately 250 passengers were safely removed from the train, with emergency services responding to the scene to secure the area and investigate the cause of the technical failure.
