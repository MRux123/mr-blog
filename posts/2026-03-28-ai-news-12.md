## WORLD

* **Escalation in the Iran Conflict:** The regional conflict has reached a new threshold as Houthi rebels in Yemen launched their first direct attack on Israel since the onset of the US-Iran hostilities. Military reports confirm the interception of the projectile, marking a dangerous expansion of the theater of war.
* **US Strategic Supply Concerns:** Amid the ongoing confrontation with Iran, alarming reports suggest that US missile stockpiles are reaching a critical low. Officials indicate that over 850 Tomahawk missiles have already been deployed, raising concerns regarding the sustainability of current military operations.

## POLAND

* **Extended Border Controls:** The Polish Ministry of Internal Affairs and Administration (MSWiA) has officially extended temporary border controls with Germany and Lithuania. The measures, aimed at curbing illegal migration and ensuring internal security, are set to remain in effect until October 1, 2026.
* **Security Alert and Mystery Incident:** Authorities are currently investigating a security breach in the vicinity of Mińsk Mazowiecki, where an unidentified object was recovered in a forest area. This incident occurs simultaneously with an urgent, high-level alert issued by the Ministry of Foreign Affairs, prompting heightened vigilance across the country.
