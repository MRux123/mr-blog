## WORLD

*   **Geopolitical Realignment in the Middle East and East Asia:** US President Donald Trump’s administration is recalibrating its strategic approach. Jared Kushner has arrived in Israel for high-level talks concerning the Gaza peace plan following rare communications with Hamas. Concurrently, President Trump announced a downsizing of military exercises with South Korea, citing his personal rapport with North Korean leader Kim Jong Un and South Korea's decision to remain neutral regarding Iran.
*   **Escalating Tensions in Russia:** The Belgorod region faces a surge in cross-border violence, with Ukrainian strikes resulting in six deaths, including a 14-year-old child. This marks a significant escalation in the ongoing conflict, raising concerns about civilian safety in border areas.
*   **Humanitarian Crisis in DRC:** The Ebola outbreak in the Democratic Republic of the Congo has reached a grim milestone, becoming the deadliest in the country's history. The rapid spread of the rare Bundibugyo strain presents a critical challenge for international health authorities.

## POLAND

*   **Tragedy in Hungary:** Authorities have identified the seventh victim of the fatal Polish bus accident in Hungary. The incident remains a focal point of national mourning and ongoing investigation into the safety of international transport.
*   **Political Discourse on Defense:** Tensions within the Polish political landscape have flared following the recent military parade. Opposition figures, including Radosław Fogiel, have criticized the government for integrating partisan messaging into national celebrations, labeling the move as "intrusive propaganda" regarding defense programming.
