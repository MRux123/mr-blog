## WORLD

* **Escalating U.S.-Iran Confrontation:** Hostilities between the United States and Iran have entered their fourth day, with both nations exchanging direct threats. Reports indicate that U.S. allies in the Gulf have intercepted Iranian drones, signaling a precarious situation with few signs of immediate de-escalation in the region.
* **Controversy Surrounding Trump Mining Deal:** Investigative reports reveal that a billion-dollar tungsten mining agreement between the U.S. and Kazakhstan has created a conflict of interest, as American investors with close ties to the U.S. President and the commerce secretary stand to profit from the deal.
* **Political Exploitation in Venezuela:** Following a recent earthquake, the Venezuelan government led by interim president Delcy Rodríguez faces accusations of politicizing relief efforts. Critics argue the tragedy is being leveraged for political gain, further deepening the divide between the administration and the opposition.

## POLAND

* **Strategic Defense Talks with the U.S.:** Polish Defense Minister Władysław Kosiniak-Kamysz has announced the commencement of a new phase of negotiations regarding the establishment of a permanent U.S. military base in Poland, marking a significant development in national security policy.
* **Questionable Police Conduct Under Scrutiny:** Polsat News has unveiled investigative footage involving a police intervention in Inowrocław, which resulted in the death of Paweł Pułaczewski. The release of body-camera footage has prompted renewed public interest and calls for clarification regarding the circumstances of the fatality.
* **Historical Memory and Political Manipulation:** New revelations have surfaced regarding the death of the youngest victim of the June 1956 Poznań protests. Historical analysis confirms that communist authorities deliberately falsified the victim's age on his obituary, highlighting long-standing efforts to suppress the truth surrounding the uprising.
