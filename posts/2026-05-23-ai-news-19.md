## WORLD

*   **Tragic Mining Disaster in China:** At least 82 people have died following a catastrophic explosion in a coal mine in Shanxi province. This marks China's deadliest mining accident in 16 years. President Xi Jinping has ordered an immediate, thorough investigation and an all-out rescue operation, emphasizing that those responsible for safety failures must be held accountable.
*   **Diplomatic Progress Between the US and Iran:** Former President Donald Trump has indicated that the United States and Iran are moving significantly closer to a new agreement. While Tehran has signaled a willingness to engage in progress, the Iranian government has clarified that its nuclear weapons program remains outside the scope of the initial framework currently under discussion.
*   **France Imposes Sanctions on Israeli Minister:** The French government has officially banned Israeli National Security Minister Itamar Ben-Gvir from entering the country. The move follows widespread international condemnation regarding his handling of detained flotilla activists and his ongoing controversial policies toward Palestinian prisoners.

## POLAND

*   **Security Alert Regarding the Trump Family:** Intelligence services have uncovered a credible threat against the family of Donald Trump. Authorities successfully apprehended an individual who was found in possession of detailed blueprints of the residence belonging to Trump's daughter, signaling a high-level security risk.
*   **Conclusion of the Ekstraklasa Season:** The Polish football league has concluded its current campaign, with the final round of matches determining the final standing. The results have officially confirmed the identity of the third team to be relegated from the top division, marking a major shift in the league's roster for the upcoming year.
