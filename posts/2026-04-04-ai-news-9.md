## WORLD

* **Escalating Conflict in Iran:** The geopolitical landscape has shifted significantly as the United States and Iran enter a direct, volatile confrontation. Iran has successfully downed a U.S. F-15E warplane, marking a critical escalation. While U.S. special forces are engaged in a race against time to recover missing aircrew, the situation is further complicated by Iran issuing a bounty for the captured personnel, raising the stakes for potential U.S. retaliation.
* **Middle East Destabilization:** The broader regional conflict is intensifying, with reports confirming that debris from ongoing missile strikes has reached Dubai. This expansion of the theater of war suggests an increasing risk to regional security and global trade routes.

## POLAND

* **Diplomatic Pivot to Asia:** Prime Minister Donald Tusk has announced a strategic diplomatic mission to East Asia. The upcoming visit to South Korea and Japan signals a shift in Polish foreign policy, focusing on strengthening security and technological cooperation with key Indo-Pacific partners amid shifting global alliances.
* **Social and Domestic Oversight:** As the Polish public observes the Easter period, the government is highlighting neglected administrative issues, specifically regarding the recovery of assets from ZUS subaccounts. Furthermore, the tragic incident in Ujsoła, where a drunk driver struck pedestrians returning from church, has sparked a renewed national debate regarding road safety and the enforcement of traffic laws.
