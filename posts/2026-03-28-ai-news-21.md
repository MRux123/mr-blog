## WORLD

*   **Direct Conflict in Iran and Saudi Arabia:** The Middle East conflict has escalated significantly following Iranian strikes on a military base in Saudi Arabia, which resulted in injuries to U.S. troops. In retaliation and response, aerial bombardments are now battering residential zones and educational facilities in Tehran. The U.S. has bolstered its presence, deploying Marines to the region as Houthi militants actively engage in the war.
*   **Wider Regional Instability:** The conflict's fallout is spreading globally; French authorities have linked a thwarted terrorist attack on an American bank in Paris directly to the ongoing war. Meanwhile, Egypt has imposed energy-related restrictions on business hours due to the regional economic crisis, and Ukraine is leveraging the situation to transition from a military aid recipient to a defense supplier for Gulf nations.
*   **Journalistic Casualties:** The volatility of the front lines was highlighted by the death of three journalists in Southern Lebanon. While Israeli forces claim one reporter was a Hezbollah operative, the incident has drawn international condemnation regarding the safety of media personnel in conflict zones.

## POLAND

*   **Political Shifts at CPAC:** Polish political figures, including Karol Nawrocki, have taken the stage at the CPAC conference in Texas. Discussions centered on a firm condemnation of Russian aggression and critical stances toward EU policies, positioning Poland as a key ideological player in the transatlantic conservative alliance.
*   **The Iranian Opposition:** Highlighting the global interest in the Middle East crisis, Cyrus Reza Pahlawi, son of the last Shah of Iran, used his appearance at CPAC to garner support for a transition to democracy in Iran, seeking American alignment for his efforts to "make Iran great again."
