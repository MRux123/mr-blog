## WORLD

* **UAE Exits OPEC:** In a move that has sent shockwaves through global energy markets, the United Arab Emirates has officially withdrawn from OPEC. This decision signals a major realignment in regional geopolitics, reflecting growing friction with Saudi Arabia and the UAE’s intent to pursue a more independent national agenda amidst the escalating tensions between the U.S. and Iran.
* **Ukrainian Strike on Russian Infrastructure:** Tensions remain high as a recent Ukrainian strike hit a Russian oil refinery in Tuapse, triggering mass evacuations. The Kremlin has characterized the attack as an attempt by Kyiv to destabilize global energy markets, further deepening the conflict’s economic impact.
* **Resurgence of Somali Piracy:** The maritime security landscape is deteriorating, with three vessels hijacked off the coast of Somalia in the last week. Analysts suggest that pirates are actively exploiting a security vacuum created by the diversion of international naval assets to the Middle East.

## POLAND

* **Poland Positions as Energy Hub:** President Karol Nawrocki, speaking at the Dubrovnik summit, has formally declared Poland’s readiness to serve as the "Gateway to the North." This strategic pivot aims to establish Poland as the central distribution hub for American natural gas within the Three Seas region, prioritizing trans-Atlantic energy security.
* **Political Transition in Hungary:** Following a 16-year tenure, Viktor Orbán is set to step down as Prime Minister of Hungary. The transition includes a significant exit package, featuring a pension-equivalent payout and continued logistical support. Despite his intent to also resign as Fidesz chairman, the party leadership has moved to maintain continuity by rejecting his resignation from the party post.
