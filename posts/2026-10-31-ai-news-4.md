## WORLD

* **US Approves Major Patriot Missile Sale to Ukraine:** The United States has authorized the sale of 500 Patriot interceptor missiles to Ukraine. This move is viewed as a critical step in bolstering Kyiv's air defense capabilities, providing the only effective means currently available to counter sophisticated Russian ballistic missile attacks.
* **El Salvador Intervenes in Haiti:** In an extraordinary development, the Salvadoran government has deployed over 2,000 troops to Haiti. The intervention raises questions about the regional role of President Bukele’s administration and the potential for a new paradigm in international security cooperation within the Caribbean.

## POLAND

* **Institutional Crisis at the Constitutional Tribunal:** Poland is facing a profound political and constitutional crisis following an attempt to suspend senior justices and the subsequent intervention by state authorities. President Karol Nawrocki, accompanied by the First President of the Supreme Court, entered the Tribunal’s premises, leading to physical confrontations and significant unrest.
* **Escalating Political Fallout:** The crisis has prompted a series of high-level resignations among Polish ambassadors, including the envoys to Romania and China, who cited the legal instability surrounding the Tribunal as a primary reason for stepping down. 
* **Governmental Emergency Consultations:** In response to the escalating situation and concerns over national security, President Nawrocki has called for urgent cross-party consultations, characterizing the current instability as a direct threat to the constitutional order of the Republic.
