## WORLD

* **Escalation in the Strait of Hormuz:** U.S. President Donald Trump has issued a severe ultimatum to Iran, threatening to target critical infrastructure, including power plants and bridges, should the blockage of the Strait of Hormuz persist. The rhetoric marks a significant spike in regional instability as the strategic waterway remains a central point of geopolitical friction.
* **Strategic Implications of Recent Rescue Operations:** Following a successful rescue operation, the White House is reportedly recalibrating its military posture regarding Iran. Analysts suggest that the tactical success of the mission may influence future decisions on potential ground operations, specifically concerning the occupation of Kharg Island or the neutralization of enriched uranium facilities.
