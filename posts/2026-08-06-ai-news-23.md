## WORLD

*   **Escalating Instability in Yemen:** Houthi militia attacks on Yemeni military positions in resource-rich provinces have put a fragile ceasefire at risk. The surge in violence signals a potential reignition of the civil war, further destabilizing the region.
*   **Child Safety Ruling Against Meta:** A New Mexico judge has ordered Meta to pay $567 million in fines. This landmark ruling marks the largest financial penalty to date regarding the company's handling of child safety across its social media platforms, including Instagram and Facebook.
*   **Public Health Crisis in DRC:** Health officials have expressed growing concern that the Ebola virus currently causing a massive outbreak in the Democratic Republic of the Congo, which has now exceeded 4,000 confirmed cases, may be mutating. Authorities are escalating their response to "door-to-door" containment efforts.

## POLAND

*   **Energy Crisis Sparks Political Conflict:** Tensions are rising in domestic politics following power shortages caused by low water levels in the Danube, which forced reduced operations at the Paks nuclear plant. Political opposition figure Péter Magyar has accused the Orbán cabinet of being aware of the energy infrastructure's vulnerability since 2002 while failing to take necessary preventative actions.
*   **Cultural Governance Controversy:** Renowned actor Daniel Olbrychski has publicly protested a decision by the Ministry of Culture to block his participation in the Gdynia Film Festival jury. The move, which the actor equated to tactics used under totalitarian systems, was eventually reversed following his open letter to Prime Minister Donald Tusk.
*   **Military Incident Investigation:** The Military Gendarmerie has launched an investigation into an incident in the village of Zacisze, where a power line was severed. Preliminary reports from local residents suggest that a military helicopter conducting a training flight may be responsible for the damage.
