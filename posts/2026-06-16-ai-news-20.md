## WORLD

* **US-Iran Peace Framework:** A historic shift appears to be underway as Donald Trump signals a desire to move past the Iran conflict. A massive $150 billion private investment fund has been established, earmarked for Iran's economy pending the formalization of a definitive peace agreement. Concurrently, Tehran has announced that new nuclear and sanctions-related negotiations are scheduled to commence this Friday.
* **Western Diplomatic Realignment:** European leaders have adopted a more conciliatory tone toward Donald Trump during the G7 summit in France. This diplomatic pivot, influenced by the burgeoning peace framework with Iran and renewed hopes for stability in Ukraine, marks a departure from the previously strained transatlantic relations.

## POLAND

* **Assassination of Siemion Skriepiecki:** The European Commission has issued a formal statement following the murder of Russian dissident and artist Robert Kuzowkow (known as Siemion Skriepiecki). The 44-year-old critic of the Moscow regime was fatally shot in Biała Podlaska. The Commission has expressed confidence in the Polish authorities' ability to conduct a thorough and transparent investigation.
* **Diplomatic Incident in Berlin:** A controversy involving the "Border Defense Movement" occurred in Berlin, where Polish activist Robert Bąkiewicz was briefly detained by German police. The incident, which took place during an attempt to place a cross at a memorial for WWII victims, highlighted ongoing sensitivities in Polish-German diplomatic relations.
* **Public Health Crisis in Świętokrzyskie:** Residents in parts of Starachowice, Wąchock, and the Brody municipality are currently facing a critical water supply interruption due to the detection of dangerous bacteria. Emergency response teams and water tankers have been deployed as the government issued urgent security alerts.
