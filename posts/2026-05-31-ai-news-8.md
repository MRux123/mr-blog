## WORLD

* **Japan Shifts Defense Posture amid Rising Tensions:** The Japanese Defense Minister has issued a robust defense of the nation’s recent military build-up, explicitly rejecting accusations of reviving militarism. Instead, Tokyo is highlighting China’s significant military expansion, signaling a pivotal shift in regional security rhetoric that emphasizes deterrence over appeasement.
* **Taiwan's Democracy Faces Infiltration Challenges:** As regional instability grows, Taiwan is grappling with the complex issue of "Mainland Spouses"—migrants from China. Recent accusations of infiltration have sparked a delicate domestic debate on how the island can protect its democratic institutions and national security without resorting to the profiling of its own residents.

## POLAND

* **Security Alert in Warsaw:** Authorities have detained a prominent Warsaw-based attorney following credible reports linking the individual to threats against the President of Poland and the Minister of Foreign Affairs. The investigation into these grave security allegations remains active.
* **Severe Weather Hits Opole Region:** A powerful storm system swept through Balcarzowice in the Opolszczyzna province overnight, causing significant structural damage. Local reports confirm at least 16 buildings were damaged and critical energy infrastructure was severely compromised, prompting a large-scale emergency response from regional fire services.
