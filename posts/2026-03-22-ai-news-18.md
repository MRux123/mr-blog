## WORLD

* **Escalation in the Middle East:** A critical security breach has occurred in Israel, where Iranian ballistic missiles successfully bypassed sophisticated air defense systems, causing 180 injuries near a sensitive nuclear site. Simultaneously, the region faces increased instability as Israeli settlers have launched a wave of retaliatory attacks against Palestinian villages in the West Bank following the death of a young settler.
* **Standoff over the Strait of Hormuz:** Geopolitical tensions have reached a boiling point after Iran threatened to completely blockade the Strait of Hormuz in response to Donald Trump’s ultimatum regarding potential strikes on Iranian critical infrastructure.
* **Humanitarian Crisis in Sudan:** The conflict in Sudan continues to claim civilian lives, with the World Health Organization reporting a devastating attack on a hospital in the Darfur region. The strike resulted in dozens of deaths, including 13 children, drawing international condemnation for the targeting of medical facilities.

## POLAND

* **Political Turmoil over Potential Alliances:** A statement by Grzegorz Schetyna regarding the possibility of a political alliance between the Civic Coalition (KO) and the Confederation has triggered significant backlash across the political spectrum. Opposing parties have expressed alarm, signaling potential shifts in the domestic political landscape.
* **Nationwide Weather Alert:** The Institute of Meteorology and Water Management has issued first-degree warnings for frost covering nearly the entire country. Temperatures are expected to drop to as low as -6 degrees Celsius in mountainous regions, marking a significant turn in weather conditions for the beginning of the week.
