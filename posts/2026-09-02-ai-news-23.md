## WORLD

* **Internal Conflict Within Ukrainian Security Services:** President Volodymyr Zelensky has condemned a violent altercation in Kyiv between agents of the Security Service of Ukraine (SBU) and the military intelligence (HUR). Labeling the incident as "absolutely shameful," the President has demanded immediate personnel changes and a thorough investigation, signaling a major crackdown on institutional misconduct during wartime.
* **Escalating Pressure on Russia:** President Zelensky announced that international airlines are increasingly withdrawing from the Russian market. The move is framed as a logical and necessary global response to ongoing geopolitical tensions, further isolating the Russian aviation sector.
* **Migratory Crisis and Political Unrest in Spain:** The Spanish government is facing intense backlash over its management of the migrant crisis in Ceuta. Opposition parties have publicly accused Prime Minister Pedro Sánchez of neglecting the residents of the enclave, threatening to escalate domestic political tensions.

## POLAND

* **Cup Upset in Domestic Football:** The Polish Cup witnessed a significant sensation as Cracovia was eliminated from the tournament by Znicz Pruszków. The match was defined by a standout performance from a 17-year-old talent whose defensive efforts effectively neutralized the opposition.
* **Hubert Hurkacz Exits US Open:** Polish tennis star Hubert Hurkacz has been eliminated from the US Open in the second round. Despite his high profile, Hurkacz struggled to find his rhythm, marking a disappointing early exit from the prestigious Grand Slam tournament.
