## WORLD

* **Escalation in Lebanon:** Tensions in the Middle East have intensified following a targeted Israeli strike on Beirut, a location that had largely been spared throughout recent conflicts. This development comes despite ongoing diplomatic efforts to uphold the ceasefire agreement, with both Israel and Hezbollah trading accusations of breaches.
* **US-Iran Diplomatic Uncertainty:** While reports from Axios suggest that negotiators have drafted a framework for a potential deal to end the conflict between the US and Iran—including a 60-day extension of the current truce—the path to peace remains fragile. The agreement awaits a final decision from President Donald Trump, leaving the international community in a state of high alert.
* **Tragedy in Kenya:** At least 16 students have died following a devastating fire at a dormitory at the Utumishi Girls Academy in Nakuru County. Local authorities are currently investigating the circumstances surrounding the midnight blaze.

## POLAND

* **Economic Milestone for SAFE:** Prime Minister Donald Tusk has announced the formal signing of the first contracts under the "SAFE" program. With funds totaling 100 billion PLN aimed at supporting over 10,000 companies, the government views this as a landmark initiative for national economic security.
* **Security Crackdown:** The Polish government has intensified its response to a series of coordinated fake alarms targeting the residences of government officials. Minister Tomasz Siemoniak confirmed the arrest of a fourth individual involved in the operation, as investigations continue to identify the perpetrators.
* **Geopolitical Tensions:** The ongoing conflict in Ukraine remains a priority for Polish national security, as reports emerge of Ukrainian forces utilizing Storm Shadow missiles to strike critical Russian military infrastructure. Simultaneously, Russian rhetoric regarding potential renewed strikes on Kyiv continues to keep the regional security situation highly volatile.
