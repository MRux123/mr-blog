## WORLD

* **Strait of Hormuz Blockade:** Following the collapse of marathon negotiations, U.S. President Donald Trump has announced a formal blockade of the Strait of Hormuz. The administration plans to initiate an extensive mine-clearing operation in the region. The U.S. is currently building a coalition to secure the waterway, with the President confirming that the United Kingdom and other allies are expected to deploy minesweepers to the Gulf. The situation remains volatile as Washington warns Iran against any interference with these military actions.
* **Hungarian General Elections:** Citizens in Hungary are heading to the polls today in a pivotal election that could redefine the nation’s political landscape. Prime Minister Viktor Orbán, a central figure in global populism, faces an uphill battle to retain power. Voter turnout remains high, with figures reaching 66.01% by 3:00 PM, suggesting a potential record-breaking participation as the country decides the composition of its 199-seat unicameral National Assembly.

## POLAND

* **Port Fire in Gdańsk:** Emergency services are currently responding to a fire on board the cargo ship *AAL Gladstone*, docked in the Nowy Port district of Gdańsk. Local fire brigades, including specialized port units, are on-site managing the situation. Preliminary reports indicate the presence of waste materials in the hold, prompting an intensive effort to contain the blaze and prevent environmental or structural damage to the port infrastructure.
