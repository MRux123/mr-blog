## WORLD

*   **Humanitarian and Military Toll in Iran Conflict:** As the search for a missing US crew member continues in increasingly dangerous conditions, the Pentagon has released updated figures confirming that 365 American service members have been wounded in operations against Iran. Rescue teams describe the ongoing search efforts as "harrowing," prioritizing the recovery of potential survivors amidst regional instability.
*   **Economic Strain on the US:** The conflict is having significant repercussions for the American domestic economy. A new study by LendingTree indicates that nearly one-third of US households have been forced to curtail spending due to inflation and price hikes in energy and transportation sectors directly linked to the war effort. Rising mortgage interest rates further underscore the financial burden placed on the average consumer.

## POLAND

*   **Russian Strike on Sumy:** Tensions remain high as reports emerge of a Russian drone strike hitting a high-rise residential building in Sumy. Local authorities confirm multiple fatalities, and emergency response teams are currently engaged in evacuation efforts at the site. 
*   **Easter Preparations Amid Tradition:** Despite the volatile geopolitical climate, the country prepares for the upcoming Easter weekend. As a significant period of reflection and prayer, the traditional blessing of food baskets remains a central element of the observance, highlighting the enduring cultural significance of Holy Saturday in Poland.
