## WORLD

*   **Escalation in the Middle East:** The geopolitical landscape remains volatile as the conflict involving Iran and its proxies continues to widen. Recent reports indicate that the U.S. and Saudi Arabia have conducted airstrikes against Iranian-backed militias in Iraq. Simultaneously, American military sites in Jordan were targeted, and a U.S.-owned vessel was attacked in an Egyptian port, raising fears of a broader regional conflagration.
*   **Humanitarian Crisis in Uganda:** A severe drought in the Karamoja region has reached a critical stage, resulting in at least 19 deaths due to starvation. With crop failures affecting 1.5 million people, the government has initiated emergency food distribution efforts to mitigate the rising rates of acute malnutrition.

## POLAND

*   **Heightened Security Alerts:** President Zelensky has issued a warning regarding the potential for a significant Russian aerial offensive against Ukraine tonight, a development that keeps Polish and regional defense apparatuses on high alert. 
*   **Military Setback:** The Ukrainian armed forces have reportedly lost an F-16 fighter jet during a combat mission, marking a tactical challenge for the country's ongoing defensive operations.
*   **Public Health Warning:** Health authorities have issued a stark warning regarding the Baltic Sea following the deaths of two elderly individuals. The fatalities were attributed to infections from *Vibrio* bacteria, which thrive in warmer waters, prompting concerns about swimming safety during the current summer season.
