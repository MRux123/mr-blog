## WORLD

* **Middle East Energy Crisis:** Global energy markets have reacted sharply following a joint strike by the U.S. and Israel on the South Pars gas field in Iran. The facility, which is among the world's largest, has sustained significant damage, triggering concerns over potential supply chain disruptions and a spike in oil and gas prices.
* **Assassination of Intelligence Chief:** Tensions in the region reached a new peak as it was confirmed that Iran’s intelligence minister, Esmail Khatib, was killed in the recent offensive. The loss of a high-ranking official closely tied to Supreme Leader Ali Khamenei marks a significant escalation in the ongoing conflict between Iran and the coalition of the U.S. and Israel.

## POLAND

* **Justice for Paulina Antczak:** After twelve years of procedural hurdles, the case surrounding the death of Paulina Antczak has taken a dramatic turn. Following an exhumation and new expert analysis suggesting potential poisoning, the court has officially reclassified the proceedings as a homicide investigation, offering a long-awaited breakthrough for the victim's family.
* **Industrial Safety Incident:** Emergency services have successfully contained a major fire at a facility in Warsaw's Białołęka district. The incident, which caused widespread alarm due to thick plumes of black smoke, resulted in the hospitalization of a crane operator who was evacuated from the scene.
* **Infrastructure Tragedy:** A fatal accident occurred involving a gondola lift, which detached from its cable and tumbled down a slope. Authorities are currently investigating the circumstances of the malfunction that led to the loss of life.
