## WORLD

*   **Heightened Tensions in Iran:** The geopolitical landscape remains volatile as U.S. President Donald Trump has rejected Tehran's latest peace proposal. Iranian military officials have responded by warning that an escalation of conflict with the United States is increasingly probable, signaling a potential move toward prolonged military confrontation.
*   **Ukraine's Diplomatic and Military Maneuvers:** President Zelensky has intensified his diplomatic outreach in the Gulf region, seeking to bolster Ukraine’s military standing. Observers suggest that these shifts in global power dynamics, partially influenced by the ongoing situation in Iran, may be indirectly impacting the possibilities for a future ceasefire with Russia.
*   **Crackdown and Humanitarian Concerns:** The human rights situation in Iran continues to deteriorate, highlighted by the critical condition of a jailed Nobel laureate. Simultaneously, clandestine efforts to bypass internet censorship through the smuggling of Starlink terminals persist, underscoring the ongoing struggle for information transparency in the region.

## POLAND

*   **Security Concerns on the Eastern Border:** President Zelensky has issued a formal warning regarding "unusual activity" observed along the Polish-Belarusian border. While the specific nature of the movements remains under scrutiny, the warning highlights the ongoing risk of peripheral conflict spillover and the need for heightened vigilance regarding the sovereignty of the region.
*   **Critical Infrastructure Failure:** A major railway disruption has paralyzed the connection between Poznań and Berlin. The failure of the traction network has necessitated the implementation of emergency bus services, with officials estimating that repairs will extend well into Sunday morning, impacting cross-border transit efficiency.
