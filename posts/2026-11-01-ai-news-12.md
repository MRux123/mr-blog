## WORLD

*   **Putin’s Diplomatic Resurgence:** Russian President Vladimir Putin is actively breaking out of international isolation, bolstered by a shifting geopolitical landscape and potential support from the incoming U.S. administration under Donald Trump. Observers note that the prospect of future summit meetings could return the Russian leader to the center of global diplomacy.
*   **Netanyahu’s Coalition Struggle:** Israeli Prime Minister Benjamin Netanyahu faces a defining moment in his political career. Following an election outcome that saw a decline in his party's support, he must now navigate complex coalition building while contending with a fragmented parliament and the rising influence of Arab-led opposition parties, who have secured a record number of seats.

## POLAND

*   **Institutional Crisis at the Constitutional Tribunal:** Tensions surrounding the Constitutional Tribunal have escalated following a series of confrontations. The Chief of Police, Marek Boroń, has issued a formal statement defending the actions of law enforcement, while union leaders and opposition politicians remain locked in a heated debate regarding the limits of police conduct and the broader political stability of the country.
*   **Deepening Political Polarization:** The divide between the ruling coalition and the opposition has reached new heights of vitriol. Recent televised debates have seen personal attacks overshadow substantive policy discussions, signaling a breakdown in cross-party communication and an increasingly volatile parliamentary environment.
