## WORLD

* **Political Turmoil in Ukraine:** Tensions are mounting in Kyiv as protesters demand the removal of army chief Oleksandr Syrskyi. The movement is calling for the reinstatement of former defence minister Mykhailo Fedorov, setting a firm deadline for the transition by Friday.
* **Hungarian Opposition Under Pressure:** In a significant escalation of political conflict, Hungarian authorities have conducted a raid on the offices of Fidesz’s political opposition. The operation, aimed at seizing server systems and communication databases, highlights the intensifying struggle for political control in the country.
* **UK Aid Funding Crisis:** New Foreign Office figures reveal that the United Kingdom is set to slash bilateral aid to several African nations by up to 90%. Critics argue this move signals a major shift in Britain's international policy and diminishes its role on the global stage.

## POLAND

* **PiS Ultimatum:** The internal crisis within the Law and Justice (PiS) party has reached a critical juncture. Party spokesperson Rafał Bochenek has issued an ultimatum to former Prime Minister Mateusz Morawiecki, demanding he choose between the party structure and his independent "Rozwój Plus" association by the end of Thursday.
* **Political Realignment:** Amidst the instability in the conservative camp, Confederation leader Krzysztof Bosak has signaled potential for a leadership change on the right. Bosak noted that the ongoing discord within PiS could provide his party with an opening to consolidate the conservative electorate.
* **Taxation and Big Tech:** The Polish government is currently navigating the complexities of implementing a new digital tax. Legislators are debating how to effectively capture revenue from major tech corporations, raising questions about whether these entities will manage to bypass the new financial obligations.
