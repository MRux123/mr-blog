## WORLD

*   **Humanitarian Crisis in Venezuela:** International rescue operations are intensifying in northern Venezuela following devastating twin earthquakes that have claimed over 900 lives. The window for finding survivors is rapidly closing, as search teams navigate the aftermath of the disaster, which has affected both locals and foreign nationals in the region.
*   **Australia’s Regulatory Crackdown on Social Media:** The Australian government has announced a significant escalation in its regulatory stance against social media giants. Citing a failure to protect users under 16, authorities are introducing stricter fines and enhanced regulatory powers to enforce age bans, signaling a major shift in how the state manages youth digital safety.
*   **Geopolitical Instability in the Middle East:** Tensions have reached a new high after Hezbollah officially declared the recent peace agreement between Israel and Lebanon void, raising fears of a renewed escalation in the region.
