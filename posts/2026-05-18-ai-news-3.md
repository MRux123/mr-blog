## WORLD

*   **Surge in Political Executions in Iran:** Following the military escalations initiated on February 28, human rights observers report a harrowing rise in political executions within Iran. The United Nations has verified the deaths of at least 32 political prisoners, with reports surfacing of inmates signaling that they are facing their final moments amidst the worsening geopolitical conflict.
*   **Trump Administration Faces Legal Challenges over Deportations:** Federal scrutiny has intensified regarding the current administration’s deportation policies. A U.S. judge has ordered the return of a Colombian national who was erroneously deported to the Democratic Republic of Congo. Simultaneously, the UN has issued a rare appeal to Equatorial Guinea to halt the forced repatriation of U.S. asylum seekers, citing the high risk of political violence and extrajudicial retribution upon their return to their countries of origin.
*   **Stalled Peace Negotiations:** Diplomatic channels between Washington and Tehran remain strained. President Trump has issued a formal warning, stating that the "clock is ticking" for a peaceful resolution, while Iranian officials maintain that the U.S. has failed to offer substantive concessions to revive stalled progress.
