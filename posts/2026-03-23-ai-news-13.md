## WORLD

* **Middle East Standoff:** Tensions between the United States and Iran have reached a critical juncture. While President Trump announced that "productive" talks regarding the cessation of hostilities were held, leading to a temporary postponement of strikes on Iranian energy infrastructure, Tehran has officially denied these claims. The situation remains volatile, with market stability hinging on the outcome of the standoff over the Strait of Hormuz.
* **EU-Mercosur Trade Deal:** The European Commission has set a definitive timeline for the implementation of the long-negotiated free trade agreement with the Mercosur bloc. The deal is set to take effect on a provisional basis starting May 1, 2026, marking a significant shift in transcontinental trade relations.
* **Espionage Allegations:** The European Commission has demanded formal explanations from the Hungarian government following reports that Foreign Minister Peter Szijjarto allegedly shared confidential EU-related information with his Russian counterpart, Sergey Lavrov. The accusations have sparked significant concern regarding internal security and diplomatic integrity within the Union.

## POLAND

* **Legal Controversy Surrounding Grzegorz Braun:** A significant political and legal incident occurred as Member of the European Parliament Grzegorz Braun left a prosecutor's office during a hearing. Braun, who was summoned to face charges related to an incident at a hospital in Oleśnica involving doctor Gizela Jagielska, failed to return after a scheduled break. The Prosecutor’s Office is now considering requesting the European Parliament to lift his immunity to allow for his detention.
* **Shifting Public Sentiment:** Recent IBRiS polling conducted for Polsat News reveals a complex landscape regarding Poland’s relationship with the European Union. While 72.8% of respondents favor remaining in the EU, a notable 57% of those familiar with the Emissions Trading System (ETS) support Poland's withdrawal from the mechanism. Furthermore, nearly one in five Poles expressed support for a total exit from the European Union.
