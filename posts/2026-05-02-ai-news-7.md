## WORLD

*   **Spirit Airlines Ceases Operations:** The airline has officially announced it is shutting down following the collapse of crucial rescue negotiations with the Trump administration regarding a requested $500 million bailout. This marks a significant contraction in the North American aviation market.
*   **Medical Breakthrough for Infants:** In a major public health milestone, the World Health Organization has approved the first malaria treatment specifically formulated for babies. This development addresses a critical gap in global health, as children under six months have historically lacked safe, effective treatment options.
*   **Mexican Political Scandal:** The Governor of a Mexican state, Rubén Rocha Moya, has stepped down following serious accusations from U.S. prosecutors, who allege he protected the Sinaloa Cartel in exchange for bribes and political support.

## POLAND

*   **Strategic Border Fortifications:** Construction of physical barriers along the border with the Russian Kaliningrad Oblast continues as part of the "Eastern Shield" (Tarcza Wschód) initiative. Military officials emphasize that the fortifications serve as a strategic deterrent designed to slow potential aggression and manage the movement of adversaries during a crisis.
*   **Geopolitical Frustrations:** Reports from the *Kyiv Independent* suggest rising tensions between Warsaw and Washington regarding diplomatic priorities. Speculation grows as special envoys Jared Kushner and Steve Witkoff have yet to visit Kyiv, signaling a potential shift in the U.S. approach to the conflict in Ukraine.
*   **Tragic Incident in Salino:** A fatal boating accident occurred in the Wejherowo district, resulting in the deaths of two individuals. Local authorities have confirmed that the operator of the vessel was intoxicated, with a blood-alcohol level exceeding 1.5 promille, leading to an active police investigation.
