## WORLD

*   **US Administration Faces Internal Turmoil Over Middle East Policy:** The resignation of Joe Kent, the Director of the National Counterterrorism Center, highlights deep divisions within the U.S. government regarding the current conflict with Iran. Kent’s assertion that the war was driven by external lobbying rather than national security interests underscores the mounting pressure on the Trump administration to shift its diplomatic course.
*   **Airstrike Escalation in Afghanistan:** A deadly airstrike in Kabul, which struck a drug rehabilitation facility, has resulted in significant casualties and ignited tensions between Pakistan and Afghanistan. The Afghan government’s vow to retaliate threatens to destabilize the region further, marking a dangerous turn in cross-border security dynamics.
*   **Global Spy Hubs Under Scrutiny:** Intelligence concerns are mounting in Europe as reports indicate that Moscow is utilizing Vienna as a central node for large-scale espionage operations. The situation in the Austrian capital is being described by international observers as reaching Cold War levels of intensity.

## POLAND

*   **Norway’s Security Shift Impacts Regional Cooperation:** In a clear signal of the changing security environment in Europe, the Norwegian government has ordered all 357 municipalities to update their crisis preparedness plans. This directive, which includes securing critical infrastructure and coordinating with military forces, highlights the growing anxiety regarding potential regional conflicts.
*   **Investigation into Iranian Leadership Speculation:** Polish media continues to track the health and status of Iran's new Supreme Leader, Mojtaba Khamenei. Despite official denials from the Iranian embassy regarding rumored medical treatment in Moscow, the Kremlin’s refusal to comment has fueled widespread geopolitical speculation about the stability of the Iranian regime.
*   **Cracking Down on Organized Crime:** Authorities have successfully dismantled a major criminal group with ties to the post-Soviet "thief-in-law" hierarchy. The operation, which led to the arrest of a high-ranking associate near Warsaw, signifies a significant success for Polish law enforcement in targeting international organized crime syndicates.
