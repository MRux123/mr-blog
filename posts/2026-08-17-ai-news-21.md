## WORLD

*   **Security Architecture in Eastern Europe:** Escalating tensions have been reported as a Russian "Banderol" drone-missile breached airspace near Moldova. President Maia Sandu has publicly referenced Poland in the context of regional security, signaling potential shifts in defense coordination and alarm across the neighboring states regarding Russian military maneuvers.
*   **Defense Policy Pivot:** Turkey has signaled a significant departure from its Russian military partnership by confirming plans to scrap its S-400 missile defense systems. The decision follows a failure to secure international buyers, marking a potential turning point in Ankara’s procurement strategy and its complex relationship with NATO partners.
*   **Diplomatic Echoes:** Former U.S. President Donald Trump has reaffirmed his unique approach to foreign diplomacy, highlighting a personal rapport with North Korean leader Kim Jong Un. The comments underscore a continued divergence in Western political discourse regarding the management of nuclear-armed adversaries.

## POLAND

*   **Mountain Rescue Crisis:** A major search and rescue operation is underway on Mount Elbrus following the disappearance of eight climbers. Concern is mounting as reports indicate that a 14-year-old may be among the group, heightening the urgency of the rescue mission in the challenging high-altitude terrain.
