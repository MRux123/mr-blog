## WORLD

* **High-Stakes Beijing Summit:** US President Donald Trump’s visit to China has been marked by both diplomatic pageantry and underlying geopolitical friction. While Beijing has attempted to thaw economic relations by resuming American beef imports, President Xi Jinping issued a stern warning regarding the status of Taiwan, explicitly framing the issue as a potential flashpoint for direct conflict between the two superpowers.
* **Escalation in Lebanon:** Tensions in the Middle East have intensified following a series of Israeli air strikes in southern Beirut. The strikes have resulted in 22 confirmed deaths, including eight children, raising significant international concern over the expanding scale of the conflict.
* **Renewed Attacks in Kyiv:** Despite recent signals from the Kremlin suggesting a potential end to the conflict, Russia has launched a new wave of drone and missile strikes against the Ukrainian capital. The attack, which left at least one person dead, follows the expiration of a short-lived three-day truce.

## POLAND

* **US Military Presence Reaffirmed:** Following media speculation regarding the withdrawal of American forces, Minister of National Defence Władysław Kosiniak-Kamysz has confirmed that the number of US troops in Poland remains unchanged. The statement comes after intense high-level consultations involving the Prime Minister and national security officials to address concerns over potential shifts in NATO’s Eastern Flank strategy.
* **Political Crisis in Latvia:** A significant government collapse has occurred in the Baltic region, as Latvian Prime Minister Evika Silina announced her resignation. The move effectively dissolves the governing coalition in Riga, creating political instability just months ahead of the scheduled parliamentary elections.
