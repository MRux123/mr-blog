## POLAND

* **Constitutional Crisis Deepens:** The standoff surrounding the Constitutional Tribunal (TK) has reached a critical stage. Prime Minister Donald Tusk has publicly criticized President Karol Nawrocki for choosing "confrontational" tactics, suggesting that the crisis could be resolved by the President recognizing legally elected judges. Simultaneously, the first formal legal notifications have been filed with the prosecutor's office regarding the recent unrest at the Tribunal's headquarters.
* **Institutional Paralysis:** The deadlock remains absolute as the First President of the Supreme Court, Zbigniew Kapiński, expressed doubt that the scheduled General Assembly of TK judges will reach a quorum, highlighting the systemic inability of the current administration to conduct official business amidst political polarization.
