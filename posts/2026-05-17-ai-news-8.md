## WORLD

* **Global Health Emergency:** The World Health Organization has officially declared the Ebola outbreak in the Democratic Republic of Congo a global health emergency. With cases now confirmed in the capitals of both the DRC and Uganda, authorities are raising concerns over the rapid transmission of the virus, noting that there is currently no approved vaccine for the specific strain involved.
* **Massive Drone Offensive:** A large-scale drone operation, involving over 500 units, targeted critical infrastructure in Russia, including a refinery in the Moscow region. Russian officials reported at least three fatalities and twelve injuries, while maintaining that their defense systems intercepted the bulk of the aerial strike.

## POLAND

* **Tragic Incident in Italy:** A violent incident in Modena, Italy, has left eight people injured after a man drove a vehicle into a crowd before initiating a knife attack. Among those reported wounded is a Polish national, according to local Italian sources.
* **Kraków Referendum:** Citizens of Kraków are preparing for a pivotal referendum scheduled for May 24th. The vote will determine the political future of Mayor Aleksander Miszalski and the local city council, an event that analysts suggest could fundamentally realign the power dynamics within the Małopolska region.
* **Road Safety Reform:** The Polish Ministry of Infrastructure has announced a significant overhaul of the driving examination system. Proposed reforms, aimed at simplifying the process, include the elimination of the practical maneuverability test on closed circuits for B and B1 categories, as well as a reduction in the volume of questions for the theory exam.
