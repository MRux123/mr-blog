## WORLD

* **Escalation in the Middle East:** US forces have launched fresh strikes against Iranian targets, citing the need to degrade Iran's capability to threaten maritime traffic in the Strait of Hormuz. The military action follows direct warnings from President Trump regarding retaliations for the deaths of American soldiers, marking a significant intensification of hostilities in the region.
* **US-Canada Trade War:** In a move poised to disrupt North American supply chains, President Trump has announced a 50% tariff on all Canadian imports. The duties, set to take effect within 30 days, represent a drastic escalation in diplomatic and economic tensions between the two neighbors.
* **UK Leadership Transition:** Andy Burnham has officially assumed the role of Britain's seventh Prime Minister in a decade. His first day in office was marked by symbolic efforts to contrast his leadership style with his predecessors, focusing on social issues including homelessness and public service reform.

## POLAND

* **Middle East Crisis Impacts:** The ongoing escalation between the United States and Iran remains a primary focus of Polish security observers, as the conflict in the Strait of Hormuz creates global energy and geopolitical instability. 
* **Urban Wildlife Concerns:** Local authorities in Warsaw are addressing a growing public safety issue following a rare and aggressive incident in which a resident was attacked by a wild boar on private property, highlighting the increasing challenge of urban-wildlife cohabitation.
