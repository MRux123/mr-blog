## WORLD

* **Civil Unrest in France:** President Emmanuel Macron has issued an urgent national appeal for calm following a surge of violent rioting across the country. The unrest, which saw the city hall of Limoges set ablaze, has raised significant concerns regarding domestic stability and the government's ability to maintain public order.
* **Tragedy at Canadian Military Base:** A catastrophic fire at the CFB Wainwright base in Alberta has claimed at least four lives and left dozens injured. Prime Minister Mark Carney has categorized the incident as one of the most severe peacetime tragedies for the Canadian Armed Forces on domestic soil.
* **Escalating Geopolitical Tensions:** U.S. relations with the European Union are under strain following threats of retaliatory measures concerning technological independence. Meanwhile, tensions remain high in the Middle East as U.S. officials condemn extremist violence in the West Bank despite denials from Prime Minister Benjamin Netanyahu.

## POLAND

* **Diplomatic Clash Over NATO Contributions:** Polish Foreign Minister Radosław Sikorski has sharply rebuked U.S. Secretary of Defense Pete Hegseth, who recently questioned the reliability of NATO allies in combat operations. Sikorski emphasized the sacrifice of Polish soldiers to defend the alliance's reputation.
* **Ukrainian Sovereignty Concerns:** President Volodymyr Zelensky has expressed critical views regarding the current U.S. administration's policy, alleging that pressure is being exerted on Ukraine to consider capitulation. These concerns are compounded by reports of U.S. sanctions relief provided to Russia, which critics fear will embolden the Kremlin.
* **Security and Regional Stability:** In response to the broader European security crisis, domestic authorities are increasing their focus on internal safety measures, with high-level security consultations being convened to address spillover effects from international instability.
