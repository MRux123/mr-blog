## WORLD

*   **Escalating US-Iran Conflict:** Tensions in the Middle East have reached a critical point following a U.S. military strike that reportedly killed four civilians at a wedding. Iran has responded with a barrage of missiles and drones targeting U.S. assets, leading to an intense exchange of fire and mutual accusations of war crimes.
*   **Rising Geopolitical Instability in Europe:** NATO and the EU have signaled a hardening stance against Russia following a failed drone attack on Leipzig airport in Germany. The incident has been characterized as a "new escalation," prompting Western allies to vow increased pressure on Moscow as fears of further regional instability grow.
*   **Black Sea Supply Chain Risks:** Ongoing military strikes on ports and vessels by both Russian and Ukrainian forces are threatening to cripple global grain supplies, raising concerns about a potential worldwide food security shock.

## POLAND

*   **Political Shifts in Presidential Approval:** A new CBOS poll indicates a notable decline in public support for President Karol Nawrocki, whose approval rating dropped in August following record-high numbers in July. Parliament and the Senate also faced low approval ratings in the same study.
*   **Internal Security Incident in Kyiv:** Media reports confirm a rare and concerning skirmish between two Ukrainian security services, the HUR and SBU, involving gunfire in Kyiv. The incident reportedly occurred during an operation aimed at disrupting a Russian conspiracy.
*   **Legislative and Legal Controversies:** Domestic political discourse remains focused on potential parliamentary efforts to override presidential vetoes regarding the cryptocurrency market, alongside legal issues surrounding public figures, including the suspension of lawmaker Łukasz Mejza's driving license due to accumulated penalty points.
