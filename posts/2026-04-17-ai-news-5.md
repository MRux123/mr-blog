## WORLD

*   **Middle East Ceasefire Strains Relations:** A surprise ceasefire between Israel and Lebanon, announced by Donald Trump via Truth Social, has triggered significant internal friction within the Israeli government. Cabinet ministers have expressed deep skepticism, claiming the deal was forced upon them without prior consultation, highlighting a growing disconnect between international diplomatic efforts and domestic political stability.
*   **Hungarian Political Landscape Realignment:** Viktor Orbán has spoken out for the first time following his electoral defeat. The Hungarian leader described the aftermath as a period of profound shock and emptiness, signaling a potential period of uncertainty and transition for the country's governance as the political establishment adjusts to life outside of power.
*   **Security Concerns in the Baltics:** Reports indicate that U.S. weapon deliveries to the Baltic and Scandinavian regions may face delays. Officials cite the ongoing conflict with Iran as the primary driver, as the U.S. prioritizes its existing inventory to support military operations in that theater.

## POLAND

*   **Legal Challenges Facing National Security Bureau:** The Polish Prosecutor’s Office has launched an investigation into the head of the National Security Bureau (BBN), Sławomir Cenckiewicz. The probe concerns his participation in a National Security Council meeting held in February, raising questions about procedural compliance and the scope of his executive authority.
*   **International Fugitive Apprehended:** A former high-ranking Filipino parliamentarian, Elizaldy Co, has been detained at the Czech-German border. Wanted in the Philippines for the alleged embezzlement of significant public funds intended for flood defense projects, his extradition is currently being complicated by his dual citizenship, making him a complex case for European authorities.
