## WORLD

* **Escalation in Ukraine:** Russia has launched its most significant assault on Ukrainian energy infrastructure since the spring. The overnight barrage targeted 11 regions, impacting residential areas and critical railway infrastructure, further destabilizing the nation's energy grid.
* **U.S. Military Withdrawal from Iraq:** Marking the end of a two-decade presence, American forces have officially withdrawn from Iraq. This departure leaves the nation at a precarious geopolitical crossroads, caught between the influence of Washington and Tehran, sparking mixed reactions ranging from celebration to fear regarding the country's future stability.
* **Aviation Security Incident:** A dangerous mid-air incident occurred on a Flydubai flight to Israel, where a pilot was involved in a stabbing incident within the cockpit. Passengers intervened to subdue the attacker, narrowly averting a major disaster. 

## POLAND

* **NATO Readiness and Nuclear Posture:** NATO Secretary General Mark Rutte has reaffirmed the Alliance's defensive superiority over Russia, dismissing immediate concerns of a nuclear conflict despite heightened rhetoric. Meanwhile, the Kremlin has issued a formal warning to NATO regarding the defense of the Kaliningrad region, stating it is prepared to utilize its full arsenal, including nuclear weapons, to protect its territory.
* **Institutional Changes in Security:** In response to the evolving geopolitical climate, Poland has officially launched a new Center for Combating Disinformation, to be headed by Paweł Olszewski, signaling a strategic focus on strengthening national information security.
