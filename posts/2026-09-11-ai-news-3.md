## WORLD

* **International Diplomatic Initiative:** Plans are underway for a high-level summit of First Ladies to be held in Poland this autumn. The initiative, announced by Marta Nawrocka, aims to foster international cooperation on critical contemporary issues. The agenda will focus on the future of global society and the protection of children in the digital landscape, with an invitation extended to Melania Trump to join European counterparts in these discussions.

## POLAND

* **Lottery Results Update:** Totalizator Sportowy has officially released the winning numbers for the September 10, 2026, draw. The results cover the full spectrum of Polish national lottery games, including Lotto, Lotto Plus, Mini Lotto, and Multi Multi, marking another routine cycle in the country's state-run gaming operations.
