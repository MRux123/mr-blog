## WORLD

*   **Russian Maritime Aggression:** The intensification of Russian strikes against major Ukrainian ports is escalating fears regarding global food security. Analysts warn that this maritime blockade is effectively weaponizing grain supplies, echoing historical conflicts over critical shipping lanes.
*   **Shifting Strategic Realities in Ukraine:** Dmitry Muratov, a Nobel Peace Prize laureate, has asserted that the Kremlin's objectives in Ukraine have shifted from a campaign of conquest to one of systemic destruction, marking a grim turning point as the conflict enters its fifth year.
*   **Diplomatic Friction in the Far East:** A rare visit by Vladimir Putin to the Kuril Islands has triggered a sharp protest from Japan, further straining already fragile relations in the region and highlighting Russia’s assertive stance on territorial disputes.

## POLAND

*   **Political Controversy Surrounding Romanowski:** The whereabouts of Marcin Romanowski continue to dominate national headlines. Opposition figures have labeled the uncertainty regarding his location as a "discrediting" situation for the Polish state, raising significant questions about the efficacy of domestic security services.
*   **Strategic Re-alignment with Kyiv:** Reports suggest that the Ukrainian government, led by President Volodymyr Zelensky, is initiating a new PR and diplomatic strategy aimed at improving its image in Poland. The initiative seeks to bolster awareness regarding the common Russian threat and repair recent bilateral tensions.
*   **Military Aviation Incident:** A Polish MiG-29 fighter jet experienced a dangerous landing at the Malbork airbase on Wednesday evening. Due to a brake malfunction and subsequent tire damage, the aircraft was forced into an emergency procedure; however, authorities confirmed the pilot remained unharmed.
