## WORLD

*   **Artemis II Mission Concludes:** Humanity has successfully completed the first crewed lunar-vicinity mission since 1972. The Orion spacecraft splashed down safely in the Pacific Ocean off the coast of California after a 10-day journey. The crew, having traveled over 1.1 million kilometers, set a new record for the furthest distance a human has ever been from Earth (406,771 km), marking a pivotal moment in the new era of space exploration.
*   **Historic US-Iran Diplomatic Shift:** Observers are closely watching the potential for direct face-to-face negotiations between the United States and Iran. Such a meeting would represent the most significant diplomatic engagement between the two nations since the 1979 Islamic Revolution, with experts noting that bridging deep-seated institutional distrust remains the primary hurdle to any potential breakthrough.

## POLAND

*   **Maritime Innovation in the EU:** Poland is observing the broader European defense landscape with the successful launching of the NRP D. Joao II in Romania. This specialized drone-carrying vessel, built for the Portuguese Navy at a cost of 132 million euros, marks the first of its kind in the European Union, signaling a shift toward autonomous naval warfare capabilities in the region.
*   **Discovery on the Antarctic Frontier:** An international research team aboard the icebreaker Polarstern has identified a previously uncharted island in the northwestern Weddell Sea. The discovery, which was absent from all existing nautical charts, is currently undergoing official verification and naming, highlighting the continued importance of polar exploration in identifying shifting geographical features.
