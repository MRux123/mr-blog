## WORLD

* **Human Rights Crisis in Niger:** A severe crackdown on the LGBTQ+ population is underway, with at least 40 individuals arrested following the introduction of a new, restrictive penal code. The situation is exacerbated by fears of a public health crisis, as targeted individuals have lost access to essential HIV prevention treatments.
* **Security Incident in Monaco:** Authorities are investigating a suspicious explosion involving an injured Ukrainian-born oligarch. Reports suggest that investigators have not ruled out the involvement of Ukrainian security services in the incident.
* **Miracle Survival in Venezuela:** In a rare glimmer of hope amidst catastrophe, a three-year-old child was rescued alive after spending six days under the rubble following a major earthquake.

## POLAND

* **Massive Police Operation:** Law enforcement conducted a coordinated, single-day search operation across several regions, involving over 600 officers. The effort resulted in the detention of 80 wanted individuals, including high-priority suspects and foreign nationals.
* **New Healthcare Regulations:** As of July 1st, a new policy comes into effect aimed at streamlining the prescription fulfillment process in select pharmacies, marking a shift in national health service accessibility.
* **Regional Water Restrictions:** Due to recent heatwaves and subsequent infrastructure strain, local authorities in several municipalities have introduced strict bans on using municipal water for non-essential purposes, such as watering gardens or filling pools, with non-compliance subject to fines.
