## WORLD

* **Middle East Crisis Intensifies:** The regional conflict has reached a critical juncture as Iran-backed Houthi forces have launched missile attacks against Israel. Tensions were further exacerbated following an Iranian strike on a U.S. military base in Saudi Arabia, which left 12 American troops injured, marking one of the most severe escalations since the outbreak of hostilities.
* **Diplomatic Friction at G7:** Internal Western cohesion is being tested, as evidenced by a heated exchange between U.S. Secretary of State Marco Rubio and EU foreign policy chief Kaja Kallas during a G7 meeting. The disagreement centers on the strategic approach toward Russia, highlighting growing impatience within the EU regarding the current American stance on the conflict in Ukraine.
* **Economic Strain in Russia:** The Kremlin is reportedly pressuring oligarchs to financially support the national budget as war-related costs mount. This move follows reports of small businesses, such as bakeries, facing astronomical tax increases, signaling that the Russian economy is beginning to buckle under the strain of a prolonged war effort.

## POLAND

* **Political Maneuvers on Fuel Prices:** The Polish government has announced a temporary reduction in VAT and excise duties on fuel. PSL politician Piotr Zgorzelski confirmed the measure, aiming to provide financial relief to households during the upcoming holiday travel period, following the signing of new legislation by President Nawrocki.
* **Zelenskyy’s Frustration with Allies:** President Volodymyr Zelenskyy has expressed significant disappointment regarding the current negotiation processes with the U.S. He noted that Ukraine increasingly feels sidelined in its own conflict, perceiving a shift in roles where they are treated more as mediators than as the primary party defending their sovereignty.
