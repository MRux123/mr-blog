## WORLD

*   **Humanitarian Crisis in Ceuta:** A massive influx of up to 60,000 migrants into the Spanish enclave of Ceuta has triggered a severe geopolitical crisis. With at least 57 deaths reported, the incident has strained relations between Morocco and Spain, while sparking intense debate within the European Union regarding the stability of the Schengen Area. 
*   **Escalation in Gaza:** Despite ongoing discussions regarding the disarmament of Hamas, the path toward a sustainable ceasefire remains precarious. Deep-seated mistrust and the overwhelming toll of the conflict continue to complicate diplomatic efforts, leaving the future of Gaza in a state of volatile uncertainty.
*   **Wildfires Across Europe:** Mediterranean nations, including Greece, are grappling with catastrophic wildfires. The evacuation of hundreds of tourists by sea highlights the increasing vulnerability of European coastal regions to extreme climate-driven events, forcing a re-evaluation of emergency response protocols.

## POLAND

*   **Tusk Calls for Border Integrity:** In response to the crisis in Ceuta, Polish Prime Minister Donald Tusk has asserted that the survival of the Schengen zone depends on stricter border management. Citing Poland’s own experiences with migration, Tusk urged Spanish authorities to adopt more robust measures to secure territorial integrity.
*   **De-Sovietization Efforts:** Municipal authorities have begun the removal of historical monuments deemed as propaganda glorifying the former Soviet occupation. The dismantling of these structures represents a continuing effort to reconcile with the country's past and solidify national historical memory.
*   **Increased Border Vigilance:** Amidst the growing instability in Europe, the German government is considering the extension of temporary border controls. This development is being closely monitored by Polish officials, reflecting broader European concerns regarding migration flows and regional security.
