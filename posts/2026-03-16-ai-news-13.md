## WORLD

*   **Escalation in the Strait of Hormuz:** The geopolitical landscape has shifted dramatically following Iran's strikes on key UAE oil infrastructure and Dubai airport. The international community is struggling to respond, as President Trump’s call for a coalition to reopen the vital shipping route has been met with significant hesitation from European allies, including the UK, Germany, and Greece. The blockade is already threatening global supply chains, with experts warning of dire economic consequences, particularly for African nations dependent on Gulf fertilizers.
*   **Broadening Middle East Conflict:** Israel has intensified its ground offensive against Iran-backed Hezbollah in Lebanon. As the conflict widens, there is a marked increase in "prepper" behavior among global elites, with a recorded surge in demand for private luxury bunkers by politicians and billionaires, signaling a lack of confidence in immediate de-escalation.

## POLAND

*   **Security Alert on the Eastern Border:** Authorities have confirmed the recovery of remnants of an unidentified military object in a forest in the Włodawa district, Lubelszczyna. While the investigation by the Military Gendarmerie is ongoing, the incident has heightened regional security concerns regarding the integrity of Polish airspace.
*   **Diplomatic Stance on Middle East Crisis:** Responding to international pressure, Polish Foreign Minister Radosław Sikorski publicly commented on the US request regarding the Strait of Hormuz. Highlighting the need for proper NATO procedural compliance, Sikorski signaled a cautious approach, reflecting the broader European reluctance to be drawn into an expanded military confrontation in the Middle East.
