## WORLD

*   **U.S. Claims Control Over Strait of Hormuz:** In a controversial move, Donald Trump has declared the Strait of Hormuz as new United States territory. The announcement, justified by the administration as a strategic necessity, is expected to escalate tensions significantly in the Middle East and challenge existing international maritime laws.
*   **Controversy Surrounding Amazon Drilling:** Brazilian President Lula has confirmed a major oil discovery near the mouth of the Amazon River. While the government highlights the economic potential, the decision to pursue exploratory drilling in this ecologically sensitive region has drawn sharp condemnation from environmental groups worldwide.
*   **Tensions in Ukraine:** Amidst ongoing hostilities, Russia has reported the destruction of a Ukrainian MiG-29 fighter jet. Concurrently, Kyiv continues to focus on national identity and history, marking the reburial of Jewhen Konowalec, a key historical figure, at the newly established National Military Memorial Cemetery.

## POLAND

*   **Security Concerns and Military Analysis:** Renowned military analyst Jarosław Wolski has provided a stark assessment of Poland’s geopolitical situation. Wolski emphasized that Ukraine's continued resistance remains the primary buffer for Polish national security against potential Russian aggression.
*   **Public Safety Incidents:** Authorities in Warsaw conducted an emergency operation involving the disposal of unexploded ordnance in the Wola district. The operation necessitated the evacuation of residential and office buildings, highlighting the ongoing risks associated with historical remnants of conflict in urban centers.
*   **Law Enforcement Conduct:** Local authorities in Wadowice have dismissed a police officer following their involvement in a violent brawl at a wedding. The incident has prompted a broader discussion regarding the conduct and standards expected of public service officials.
