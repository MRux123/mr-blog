## WORLD

* **Escalation in the Red Sea:** Amidst the widening conflict between the United States and Iran, Saudi Arabia has spearheaded a new military coalition. Including nations like Egypt and Turkey, the alliance aims to secure critical shipping routes in the Red Sea against ongoing threats.
* **UEFA-FIFA Stand-off:** European football faces a historic schism as UEFA threatens to boycott all future World Cups. The conflict stems from FIFA’s proposal to sell 20% of commercial rights to private investors through the "FIFA Forward Enterprise," a move UEFA leaders have labeled as selling the integrity of the tournament.

## POLAND

* **Security Incident on the Lubelszczyzna:** Polish authorities are investigating the remains of a missile found near the Ukrainian border. While the impact site has been secured, security experts warn that such provocations are likely to increase before winter, describing the situation as a deliberate attempt by Russia to destabilize public sentiment in the region.
* **Polish Football Success:** Despite the off-pitch turmoil in global football governance, Polish clubs had a successful evening in the UEFA Conference League. Both GKS Katowice and Raków Częstochowa secured progression to the next qualifying rounds, with Raków delivering a dominant performance on the road.
