## WORLD

* **Breakthrough in US-Iran Negotiations:** The first round of diplomatic talks between the United States and Iran in Switzerland has concluded with mediators reporting "encouraging progress." Qatari and Pakistani facilitators confirmed that both parties have agreed on a roadmap aimed at securing a final peace deal within a 60-day window. While specialized deconfliction units—specifically focusing on the volatile situation in Lebanon—have been established, underlying tensions remain, as evidenced by President Trump’s rhetoric during the process.
* **Political Upheaval in Colombia:** Legal professional Abelardo De La Espriella appears poised for a significant electoral victory in Colombia. Backed by Donald Trump, the newcomer’s surge represents a potential pivot point for South American politics, signaling a strong conservative rebuke to existing left-wing governance in the region.

## POLAND

* **Stability of the UK Leadership in Focus:** Polish media outlets are closely monitoring reports of political instability in London following statements made by President Donald Trump on Truth Social. The American president suggested that British Prime Minister Keir Starmer is on the verge of resignation, with ongoing speculation that a formal timeline for his departure could be announced as early as Monday. 

* **Close Scrutiny of Mideast Developments:** Polish observers remain cautious regarding the US-Iran negotiations. Reporting on the technical teams' continued involvement, media outlets emphasize that the outcome of these talks—specifically the deconfliction efforts in Lebanon—could have significant implications for European geopolitical stability and energy security.
