## WORLD

* **Catastrophic Earthquake in Venezuela:** The death toll following the twin earthquakes in Venezuela has reached 589, with nearly 3,000 injured and a staggering 50,000 individuals reported missing. International rescue efforts are currently underway as the scale of structural collapse in La Guaira continues to overwhelm local emergency services.
* **Escalating Tensions in the Strait of Hormuz:** Iran has formally challenged international navigation rights in the Strait of Hormuz. This aggressive posturing follows an incident involving Iranian military fire directed at a commercial container ship near the coast of Oman, raising significant concerns regarding global energy supply chain security.
* **Ebola Crisis in the DRC:** A major public health emergency is unfolding in the Democratic Republic of the Congo, where health officials have lost track of nearly 300 confirmed Ebola patients. Due to ongoing regional conflicts, access to displaced populations is severely limited, increasing the risk of widespread community transmission.

## POLAND

* **Judicial Compensation Milestone:** In a significant ruling regarding civil liberties and the rule of law, a Polish court has awarded over 3 million PLN in damages to a citizen who was wrongfully detained for over three years. This decision highlights ongoing debates regarding the efficiency and accountability of the national justice system.
* **Passing of Sergei Ivanov:** The death of Sergei Ivanov has been noted, sparking reflection on his role in contemporary Russian politics. His passing marks the end of a controversial chapter involving figures closely associated with the Kremlin’s inner circle.
