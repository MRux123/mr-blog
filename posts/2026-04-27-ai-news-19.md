## WORLD

* **Mali Defense Minister Assassinated by JNIM**: The security situation in the Sahel has deteriorated sharply following the killing of Mali's Defense Minister, Gen. Sadio Camara, by the Al-Qaeda-linked militant group JNIM. This high-profile attack undermines the military junta's claims of stability and highlights the limitations of Russian military support in the region.
* **Iran Proposes Regional Thaw**: Iran has signaled a potential shift in geopolitical strategy, offering to prioritize shipping security in the Strait of Hormuz in exchange for delaying contentious nuclear negotiations. The move, reportedly supported by pledges of Russian cooperation, suggests an attempt to alleviate economic pressure while maintaining long-term nuclear ambitions.
* **Middle East Tensions Persist**: Despite a formal cease-fire, daily skirmishes continue between Israel and Hezbollah, resulting in 14 reported fatalities in Lebanon. The persistence of violence remains a major obstacle to regional stability.

## POLAND

* **EU Legal Pressure on Polish MEPs**: The European Parliament has formally received a request to strip Mariusz Kamiński of his parliamentary immunity. Furthermore, a broader legal assessment regarding the immunities of several other prominent Polish politicians, including Daniel Obajtek and Grzegorz Braun, is scheduled for a pivotal vote this Tuesday.
* **Tragedy in the Tatra Mountains**: A fatal accident has occurred on Świnica, where a tourist fell to his death on the Slovakian side of the mountain range. The incident triggered a large-scale joint rescue operation between Polish and Slovakian emergency services, underscoring the ongoing hazards in the region during current weather conditions.
* **Justice Served in Lithuania**: A court in Panevėžys has sentenced Ernestas M. to seven years in prison for the murder of a Polish football fan. The ruling includes a significant compensatory payment to the victim's family, closing a case that has been closely watched by public opinion in both nations.
