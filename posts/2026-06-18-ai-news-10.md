## WORLD

* **Escalation in the Russia-Ukraine War:** Moscow has faced its most significant drone offensive since the onset of the full-scale conflict. Nearly 200 Ukrainian drones targeted infrastructure south-east of the Russian capital, causing fires at a major refinery and a shopping center. The assault forced a temporary closure of Moscow's airports, marking a substantial shift in the reach and intensity of Ukrainian aerial operations.
* **Diplomatic Tensions in the Middle East:** Israeli Foreign Minister Gideon Saar has announced a cessation of contacts with EU foreign policy chief Kaja Kallas. The diplomatic fallout follows reports that Kallas allegedly drew parallels between the Israeli state and the apartheid-era regime of South Africa, signaling a deepening rift between Israel and European leadership.
* **Geopolitical Realignment with Iran:** A new, albeit initial, economic agreement has been reached with Iran. The deal provides Tehran with a crucial financial lifeline in exchange for what observers describe as minimal concessions, signaling a potential shift in the regional power balance as the most difficult negotiations are deferred to a later date.

## POLAND

* **High-Profile Arrest in Biała Podlaska:** Polish authorities, including the Internal Security Agency (ABW), have successfully apprehended a suspect in connection with the murder of a Russian national in Biała Podlaska. Prime Minister Donald Tusk confirmed the arrest, noting that the suspect utilized a Georgian passport, and stated that investigations are now focused on identifying the masterminds behind the operation.
* **Internal Audit at Szpital Południowy:** Political tension has escalated in Warsaw following a control operation led by PiS parliamentarians at Szpital Południowy. The facility's management office has been sealed amidst a scandal regarding the compensation of a 28-year-old physician, sparking a heated public debate between opposition and governing coalition politicians regarding the boundaries of parliamentary oversight in healthcare institutions.
* **Anti-Corruption Crackdown in the Railway Sector:** The Central Anti-Corruption Bureau (CBA) has detained seven individuals, including employees of the PESA Mińsk Mazowiecki company and private entrepreneurs, as part of an investigation into systemic corruption regarding train maintenance contracts.
