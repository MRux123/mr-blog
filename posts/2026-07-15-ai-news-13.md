## WORLD

* **Middle East Standoff:** The geopolitical situation in the Middle East has entered a dangerous new phase as Iran threatens to disrupt vital trade routes following a series of retaliatory strikes by the United States. President Donald Trump has issued a stark ultimatum, warning of strikes against Iranian infrastructure, including power plants and bridges, should Tehran refuse to resume diplomatic negotiations.
* **Intensification of the Russia-Ukraine Conflict:** The conflict has seen a violent escalation, with reports of eight civilian fatalities following Russian attacks. Simultaneously, Kyiv has launched a significant maritime operation, reportedly striking 20 Russian vessels in the Black Sea, further complicating the logistical stability of the region.

## POLAND

* **Increased Military Activity:** Polish air defense forces have intercepted two Russian Su-30SM2 fighter jets near the border, characterizing the incursion as the latest in a series of deliberate provocations by Moscow aimed at testing NATO’s eastern flank.
* **Direct Threats from Moscow:** The Kremlin has issued a stern warning that any international forces deployed to Ukraine will be considered legitimate military targets, marking a sharp increase in rhetorical aggression toward neighboring states and international partners.
