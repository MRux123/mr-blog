## WORLD

* **Israel-Iran Conflict:** Israel has intensified its military campaign against Hezbollah, signaling a commitment to ongoing strikes despite the collapse of cease-fire negotiations. Iran has publicly linked its participation in future peace talks with the United States to a mandatory expansion of any potential truce into Lebanon, further complicating regional diplomacy.
* **Geopolitical Posturing:** As the Strait of Hormuz remains a critical flashpoint, Iran is effectively exerting control over maritime traffic, forcing nations into individual negotiations that risk distancing them from U.S.-led policy. Simultaneously, China’s leadership is attempting to project an image of regional stability ahead of high-stakes summits with the U.S., utilizing outreach to Taiwan as a strategic lever.

## POLAND

* **Pipeline Diplomacy:** Tensions surrounding the "Druzhba" pipeline remain high as President Zelensky announced plans for repairs this spring. This follows recent friction with Hungary, where Prime Minister Viktor Orbán halted gas transit to Ukraine in response to the disruption of oil supplies earlier this year.
* **Fiscal Constraints:** Finance Minister Domański has ruled out potential tax relief for citizens, citing a projected 11 billion PLN deficit in the state budget. The administration is currently prioritizing the management of rising state expenditures over fiscal easing.
* **Smolensk Anniversary:** The nation observed the 16th anniversary of the Smolensk air disaster, with official commemorations held at the Military Powązki Cemetery in Warsaw and the Rakowicki Cemetery in Krakow, attended by key state officials and government representatives.
