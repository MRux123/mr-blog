## WORLD

* **US Imposes Broad Tariffs Over Forced Labour Concerns:** The United States has initiated a significant trade action, imposing tariffs on 60 international trading partners. The administration justifies the move by citing failures to adequately address forced labour within their supply chains, marking a major escalation in global trade enforcement.
* **Escalating Tensions in the Middle East:** Iran has reacted sharply to recent US airstrikes and subsequent comments from Donald Trump. Following American strikes on military bases and maritime targets in the Strait of Hormuz, Tehran has issued warnings of "chaos," signaling a potential for further regional instability.

## POLAND

* **Internal Crisis Within PiS Party:** The Law and Justice (PiS) party is facing a critical moment as a self-imposed ultimatum regarding political activities within the organization has expired. Loyalists of Mateusz Morawiecki have been vocal in their resistance against the leadership's pressure, leaving the party on the brink of a significant internal fracture ahead of a planned statement by Jarosław Kaczyński.
* **Government Intervention for "Clean Air" Victims:** The Polish government has announced a forthcoming support package for citizens defrauded under the "Czyste Powietrze" (Clean Air) subsidy program. Officials confirmed that specific legislative solutions are expected to be adopted during the summer period to rectify the issues faced by beneficiaries.
