## WORLD

* **Trump and Xi Jinping Open High-Stakes Talks in Beijing:** U.S. President Donald Trump has begun a pivotal two-day visit to China. Early reports highlight a tense atmosphere, with President Xi Jinping delivering a stern warning regarding the status of Taiwan. The delegation includes prominent business leaders, including Elon Musk, signaling a complex focus on trade, technology, and geopolitical stability.
* **Political Fallout in Latvia:** The Latvian Prime Minister has resigned following a significant political crisis triggered by the discovery of stray Ukrainian drones crashing within Latvian territory. The incident has raised critical questions regarding regional security and the spillover effects of the ongoing conflict.
* **Audacious Taiwan Presidential Mission:** New details have emerged regarding a clandestine diplomatic mission that saw the President of Taiwan travel to southern Africa. The operation, involving satellite communication and a borrowed aircraft, is being viewed as a significant strategic achievement in navigating Chinese efforts to isolate the island internationally.

## POLAND

* **Lubin Shooting Investigation:** Authorities are continuing their search for a 21-year-old suspect identified in connection with a deadly shooting in a residential building in Lubin. Forensic examinations of the two victims are scheduled for Friday as the investigation into the motives behind the violence intensifies.
* **Morawiecki’s New Political Initiative:** Former Prime Minister Mateusz Morawiecki has officially registered "Stowarzyszenie Rozwój Plus" (Development Plus Association). The organization aims to build an expert-led foundation for future policy proposals, signaling a long-term strategic effort to shape the programmatic direction of the Law and Justice (PiS) party.
* **Fuel Price Adjustments:** The Minister of Energy has issued an updated notice regarding maximum fuel prices. While price caps for gasoline remain stable, the maximum price for diesel fuel has seen a decrease of 13 groszy, providing minor relief for consumers.
