## WORLD

*   **Middle East Ceasefire:** Israel and Lebanon have entered a 10-day ceasefire agreement. While President Trump has invited leadership from both nations to Washington, the situation remains fragile, with Prime Minister Netanyahu insisting on a continued military presence in southern Lebanon and uncertainty regarding Hezbollah's adherence to the truce.
*   **Political Earthquake in Hungary:** Prime Minister Viktor Orbán has officially acknowledged the defeat of his party in the recent parliamentary elections. Orbán signaled that a political era has ended, stating that the right-wing movement in Hungary requires a comprehensive renewal following a strong campaign by Peter Magyar’s TISZA party.
*   **Diplomatic Friction at the Vatican:** Pope Leo XIV is facing mounting pressure from the Trump administration due to his refusal to back the war in Iran. The Pope has reiterated his commitment to peace, explicitly warning against the manipulation of religion for political and military objectives.

## POLAND

*   **Internal Crisis in PiS:** Former Prime Minister Mateusz Morawiecki is defying party leadership following the establishment of his "Rozwój Plus" association. Despite warnings from party officials that the initiative violates the Law and Justice (PiS) statute, Morawiecki has confirmed he will not withdraw the registration, escalating internal disciplinary tensions.
*   **Poland’s Strategic Elevation:** During recent high-level talks, President Donald Trump reaffirmed the strength of US-Polish relations, highlighting Poland's official inclusion in the G20. This move is seen by the Polish government as a major success, securing a seat for Warsaw at the most influential table of global economic and political decision-making.
*   **Military Success in Donbas:** Ukrainian forces, utilizing a coordinated operation between the SBU and unmanned systems, have successfully crippled Russian logistics in the Luhansk region. The destruction of fuel trains and ongoing tactical gains by the "Azow" brigade signify a shift in the operational capability of the Ukrainian front line through advanced drone warfare.
