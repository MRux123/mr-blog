## WORLD

* **US Deploys Space-Based Defensive Assets**: For the first time in history, the United States has officially confirmed the deployment of space weapons. According to the US Air Force Secretary, these measures are a strategic necessity designed to bolster national defense capabilities against emerging threats, signaling a significant escalation in the militarization of space.
* **Escalation Over Lithuanian Airspace**: NATO jets have intercepted and downed an unidentified drone that breached Lithuanian airspace. While the aircraft's origin remains unconfirmed, officials suspect it originated from Belarus, a close military ally of Russia, prompting increased security monitoring in the Baltic region.

## POLAND

* **Military Air Operations and Public Alerts**: Following a renewed Russian attack on Ukraine, the Polish military has initiated emergency air operations to secure its borders. Authorities have issued an RCB alert, urging the civilian population to remain vigilant as the intensity of regional conflict continues to pose a direct security challenge to Poland’s airspace.
