## WORLD

*   **White House Personnel Shakeup:** President Donald Trump has officially appointed Stephen Miller as the new White House Chief of Staff. Miller, a long-term advisor, replaces Susie Wiles, who will transition to managing the administration's political operations as the White House undergoes a strategic restructuring.
*   **Escalating Tensions in Ukraine:** President Volodymyr Zelensky has warned of imminent large-scale Russian attacks targeting critical energy infrastructure, bridges over the Dnieper, and civilian sites. Citing recent intelligence, the Ukrainian leadership has mobilized defense forces while issuing an urgent plea to international partners for the expedited delivery of military and logistical support.
*   **New Zealand Elections and Global Economic Discontent:** As New Zealand heads to the polls, the election serves as a barometer for the global trend of voter dissatisfaction driven by surging costs of living. With no clear front-runner, the outcome remains highly unpredictable, suggesting that minor parties will likely play a decisive role in the formation of the next government.

## POLAND

*   **S&P Downgrades Poland’s Rating Outlook:** While S&P Global Ratings has maintained Poland's credit rating at A-, it has revised the outlook to negative. Finance Minister Andrzej Domański attributed this downward pressure to ongoing legislative gridlock with President Nawrocki, signaling a challenging period for the country's fiscal policy and state finances.
*   **Deepening Strategic Ties with the U.S.:** Deputy Prime Minister and Minister of National Defence, Władysław Kosiniak-Kamysz, is scheduled to visit the Pentagon on November 10. He will meet with U.S. Secretary of War Pete Hegseth to discuss crucial security matters, most notably the permanent stationing of American troops in Poland amidst the deteriorating regional security landscape.
