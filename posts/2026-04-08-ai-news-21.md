## WORLD

*   **NATO Unity Under Threat:** The geopolitical landscape is shifting as the Trump administration reportedly considers punitive measures against NATO allies that failed to support US and Israeli objectives in the recent conflict with Iran. Reports from the *Wall Street Journal* suggest that the White House is weighing the withdrawal of US troops from these nations and has even discussed a potential exit from the alliance entirely.
*   **Continued Conflict in Lebanon:** Despite a previously announced ceasefire, the situation in the Middle East remains volatile. A significant wave of Israeli airstrikes has hit southern Lebanon, Beirut's suburbs, and the Bekaa Valley, resulting in at least 182 fatalities and further destabilizing the region.
*   **UNIFIL Incident Strains Italy-Israel Relations:** Diplomatic friction has intensified between Rome and Tel Aviv following an Israeli strike on a vehicle belonging to the UNIFIL peacekeeping mission. Italy has summoned the Israeli ambassador, with Prime Minister Giorgia Meloni labeling the violation of UN resolutions as "inadmissible."

## POLAND

*   **Constitutional Court Seating Dispute:** A significant political impasse is unfolding in Warsaw regarding the swearing-in of four new Constitutional Tribunal judges. The head of the Presidential Office, Zbigniew Bogucki, has confirmed that the oath will not be taken in the presence of President Karol Nawrocki, with both sides trading accusations of constitutional violations as the standoff over the court's legitimacy continues.
*   **Legal Consequences in French Rail Tragedy:** A Polish truck driver involved in a fatal collision with a TGV high-speed train in northern France has been formally charged with vehicular homicide. The accident, which occurred between Bethune and Lens, resulted in the death of the train's conductor and left sixteen others injured. The suspect is currently under judicial supervision.
