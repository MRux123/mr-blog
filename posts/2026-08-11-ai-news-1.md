## WORLD

* **Devastating 7.4 Magnitude Earthquake in Colombia:** A powerful seismic event has struck Colombia, resulting in at least 111 confirmed fatalities. The quake impacted major urban centers, including Bogotá, Cali, and Medellín. Rescue operations are currently underway as authorities warn that the death toll is expected to rise while teams sift through the rubble of collapsed structures.
