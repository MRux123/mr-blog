## WORLD

* **Geopolitical Pressure on Energy Strikes:** President Zelensky has confirmed that some Western allies have advised Ukraine to scale back strikes on Russian oil infrastructure. This push for restraint comes amid a global fuel market crisis, exacerbated by ongoing volatility in the Middle East, highlighting the difficult balance between Ukraine's tactical military needs and global energy stability.
* **Myanmar’s Transition to Military-Civilian Rule:** Senior Gen. Min Aung Hlaing has stepped down as commander in chief, positioning himself to be formally installed as the civilian president of Myanmar by the country’s parliament. This move represents a calculated effort by the military junta to consolidate power under a veneer of administrative legitimacy.
* **Diplomatic Tension in the Middle East:** In an unusual development, the Latin Patriarch was temporarily blocked from entering a holy site in Jerusalem, with Israeli officials citing security concerns. Meanwhile, the region continues to grapple with extreme weather events, as abnormal jet stream patterns triggered severe flooding across the UAE and Saudi Arabia.

## POLAND

* **Fuel Market Regulation:** The government has released its first official proclamation regarding maximum fuel prices. This move signifies an active attempt to stabilize the domestic energy market in response to the broader international pressures impacting fuel availability and pricing.
* **Loss of a Literary Icon:** Poland mourns the death of Wiesław Myśliwski, the celebrated author who passed away at the age of 94. Myśliwski was a central figure in Polish contemporary literature, known for his profound exploration of the human condition and rural identity.
* **Soldier Safety in Lebanon:** Concerns regarding a Polish soldier injured in a booby-trap explosion in Lebanon have been assuaged, as official reports confirm he has been discharged from the hospital and has returned to his unit without having suffered serious, long-term harm.
