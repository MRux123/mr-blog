## WORLD

*   **Tension Escalates in Gaza Following Trump’s Peace Proposal:** The situation in the Middle East has reached a critical juncture. While a new peace plan aimed at the disarmament of Hamas and Israeli withdrawal was proposed, recent Israeli military strikes have cast significant doubt on the viability of the initiative, suggesting a widening gap between diplomatic efforts and ground realities.
*   **Alarming Surge in Iranian Executions:** UN human rights chief Volker Türk has expressed profound concern over a sharp increase in state executions in Iran since March. Reports indicate that 56 individuals have been executed on national security charges, with 27 of these cases directly linked to the widespread anti-government protests that began in January.

## POLAND

*   **Extreme Heatwave Triggers Nationwide Emergency Alerts:** Poland is currently battling a severe meteorological crisis, with temperatures in the south-east forecasted to reach a staggering 40°C. The Institute of Meteorology and Water Management has issued the highest level of warnings, complemented by emergency RCB alerts sent to millions of residents across 11 provinces to mitigate risks associated with extreme heat and impending violent storms.
*   **Tragic Drowning Incidents Highlight Safety Risks:** The onset of summer heat has been marred by a tragic incident in Wągrowiec, where two 13-year-olds lost their lives while swimming in an unguarded, wild beach at Lake Durowskie. The tragedy has prompted renewed calls from authorities for increased public vigilance and adherence to swimming safety regulations at local water bodies.
