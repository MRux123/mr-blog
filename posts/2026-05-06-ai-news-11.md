* **European Court Ruling on Judicial Appointments:** The European tribunal has issued a significant decision regarding the legitimacy of judges appointed to the Constitutional Tribunal. This ruling carries profound implications for the rule of law and the independence of the judiciary, marking a pivotal moment in the ongoing legal discourse concerning institutional integrity within the European framework.

## POLAND

* **Verdict Reached in Case of Kamilek:** The court has delivered its final verdict in the tragic case of the 8-year-old boy from Częstochowa, whose death sparked national outrage and a profound debate on child protection mechanisms in Poland.
* **NFZ Penalizes Hospital Over Senator's Son Case:** The National Health Fund (NFZ) has imposed a fine of 134,000 PLN on the hospital in Aleksandrów Kujawski. An investigation confirmed irregularities regarding a medical procedure performed on the son of Senator Tomasz Lenz, specifically concerning the lack of formal authorization and bypass of standard waiting lists.
* **Tragedy Involving a 13-Year-Old:** Authorities are investigating harrowing details surrounding the death of a 13-year-old girl. Evidence suggests that a peer attempted to stage the scene as a suicide, a case that has shocked the public and raised urgent questions regarding youth safety and criminal responsibility.
