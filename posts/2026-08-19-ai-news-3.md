## WORLD

* **Trump pauses new tariffs on Canada:** The US administration has announced a three-day delay in imposing new tariffs on Canadian goods. President Trump indicated that negotiations are in the final stages, suggesting a breakthrough in trade relations between the two nations is imminent.
* **Legal scrutiny of Meta intensifies:** A landmark trial has commenced in the United States, addressing allegations that Meta deliberately designed its platforms, Facebook and Instagram, to be addictive to children. The company denies these claims, arguing that "social media addiction" is not a clinically recognized condition.
* **Ukrainian leadership debate:** Amid ongoing martial law, the former Ukrainian defense minister has publicly advocated for the resumption of presidential elections, highlighting internal pressure regarding the country’s political roadmap during the protracted conflict.

## POLAND

* **Poland mourns the passing of Wanda Traczyk-Stawska:** The nation is in mourning following the death of Wanda Traczyk-Stawska, a legendary figure of the Warsaw Uprising and an iconic activist. Tributes are pouring in for a woman widely regarded as a hero for multiple generations, whose life and advocacy left an indelible mark on Polish history and civil society.
