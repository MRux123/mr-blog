## WORLD

*   **Devastating Earthquakes in Venezuela:** A catastrophic double earthquake with a magnitude exceeding 7.0 has struck Venezuela, resulting in at least 235 deaths and over 4,300 injuries. Rescue operations are currently intensifying as authorities warn that the casualty count is expected to rise significantly, with hundreds still feared trapped under the debris in cities like La Guaira.
*   **Tensions Escalate in the Strait of Hormuz:** Security in one of the world's most critical shipping lanes has been compromised following an alleged attack by the Iranian Islamic Revolutionary Guard Corps on a merchant vessel. This incident poses a direct challenge to the recently signed 60-day maritime safety agreement between Iran and the United States, leaving the international community awaiting a response from the White House.

## POLAND

*   **Violent Attack at Jastrzębie-Zdrój Hospital:** A 19-year-old assailant has been apprehended after a knife attack at the Provincial Specialized Hospital in Jastrzębie-Zdrój. The perpetrator initially targeted a patient, subsequently wounding a head doctor and a nurse who intervened to protect the victim. Police successfully neutralized and detained the suspect at the scene.
*   **Continued Controversies over Deposit Systems:** The Polish Ministry of Climate has addressed mounting public dissatisfaction regarding the malfunctioning of automated "bottle machines." While the government signaled that future regulations regarding small-format alcohol containers (so-called "małpki") are expected this autumn, officials have maintained that the current operational failures fall under the responsibility of private market operators.
