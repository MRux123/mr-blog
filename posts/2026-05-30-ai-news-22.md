## WORLD

*   **Drone Strike at Zaporizhzhia Nuclear Power Plant:** The International Atomic Energy Agency (IAEA) has confirmed that a drone struck the Zaporizhzhia Nuclear Power Plant, Europe's largest nuclear facility. Preliminary reports indicate the unmanned aerial vehicle impacted the turbine building, causing structural damage. This incident raises significant concerns regarding nuclear safety in the ongoing conflict region.

## POLAND

*   **Extreme Weather Disrupts Southern Poland:** A wave of violent storms and heavy rainfall has caused widespread destruction across the Opole and Silesia regions. Emergency services report numerous fallen trees, damaged rooftops, and impassable roads.
*   **Tornado Confirmed in Strzelce County:** Meteorological authorities and local reports confirm that the severe weather included the first tornado of the year. Firefighters are currently engaged in extensive cleanup and rescue operations, particularly in the towns of Balcarzowice and Rudziniec, to address the aftermath of the storm system.
*   **Political Tensions Over Military Procurement:** Foreign Minister Radosław Sikorski has publicly criticized Karol Nawrocki and his political faction. The dispute centers on the transparency and execution of the "SAFE" program, with Sikorski accusing the previous administration of providing misleading information regarding international defense equipment contracts.
