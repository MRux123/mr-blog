## WORLD

*   **Himalayan Flood Disaster:** A catastrophic wall of water has swept through a river valley between Nepal and Tibet, resulting in at least 160 confirmed fatalities. With hundreds still missing—including international tourists—rescue operations are currently facing significant logistical challenges in the remote region.
*   **Growing Concerns Over Russian Escalation:** Security analysts and reports from Bloomberg indicate that Russia is preparing for a significant intensification of strikes against Ukraine. Emerging intelligence suggests a rise in factions within the Kremlin advocating for the use of tactical nuclear weapons, as traditional peace negotiations remain effectively stalled.

## POLAND

*   **Political Fragmentation in the European Parliament:** The Law and Justice (PiS) delegation faces internal instability as three prominent MEPs have announced their departure. The move, linked to Mateusz Morawiecki’s "Rozwój Plus" initiative, signals potential shifts in the alignment of Polish conservative forces on the European stage.
*   **Security Incidents Targeting Local Officials:** Tensions have risen following a reported assassination attempt on the Mayor of Mysłowice, Dariusz Wójtowicz, who claimed that bolts on his vehicle's wheels were deliberately loosened. This follows a similar security concern reported recently by the Mayor of Gdynia, Aleksandra Kosiorek, prompting calls for increased protection for municipal leaders.
*   **Government Stance on Education and Church Relations:** Minister of Education Barbara Nowacka has publicly criticized the Catholic Church's stance on proposed health education reforms. Comparing the church's appeals to the rhetoric of fringe politicians, the Minister reaffirmed the government's commitment to secular educational standards despite mounting pressure from conservative factions.
