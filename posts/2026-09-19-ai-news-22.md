## WORLD

* **Escalation in the Middle East:** The security situation in the Middle East has reached a critical juncture following a coordinated Houthi assault on the Saudi capital, Riyadh. Using ballistic missiles and drones, the Iranian-backed militia targeted critical infrastructure, including oil facilities and the international airport. This significant escalation marks a renewed challenge to regional stability.
* **Artificial Intelligence Policy Shift:** U.S. President Donald Trump has announced plans to establish an "AI Force" and appoint a dedicated artificial intelligence tsar. The administration emphasized its intention to foster technological growth, diverging from more restrictive regulatory frameworks currently being debated globally.
* **Controversial Pardon in Israel:** The Israeli President has officially pardoned Elor Azaria, a former soldier convicted for the 2016 killing of an incapacitated Palestinian attacker. The decision has reignited intense domestic and international debate regarding the proportionality of force and military ethics in the ongoing conflict.

## POLAND

* **NATO Leadership Transition:** In a pivotal move for European security, German General Carsten Breuer has been appointed as the new head of the NATO Military Committee. He succeeds Italian Admiral Giuseppe Cavo Dragone, taking charge of the Alliance's highest military body at a time of heightened geopolitical tension.
* **Diplomatic Tensions and Presidential Visit:** Polish President Karol Nawrocki is currently in New York to attend the United Nations General Assembly. During his visit, the President paid tribute to the victims of the September 11, 2001, terrorist attacks at the "Survivor Tree" memorial, reinforcing Polish-American ties amidst ongoing security dialogues.
* **Political Rhetoric Concerning NATO:** Slovak Prime Minister Robert Fico has issued harsh criticisms of NATO, labeling its current stance as "fueling the fire" of conflict. These comments reflect growing internal divisions within the Central European political landscape regarding the bloc's strategic direction.
