## WORLD

* **Conflict Escalation in the Gulf:** Tensions between the United States and Iran have reached a critical juncture following a series of military engagements. U.S. Central Command reported the destruction of six Iranian speedboats after reports of drone attacks in the United Arab Emirates. President Donald Trump has issued a stern warning, threatening severe consequences for any further aggression against U.S. naval assets, while simultaneously acknowledging ongoing diplomatic efforts.
* **Tragedy in Leipzig:** A vehicle-ramming incident in Leipzig, Germany, has resulted in multiple casualties, with reports confirming at least two deaths and approximately 20 injuries. Local authorities have apprehended the 33-year-old German suspect, and an investigation into the motive behind the attack is currently underway.
* **Health Emergency at Sea:** International authorities are coordinating an emergency evacuation of the cruise ship *MV Hondius*, currently stranded off the coast of Cape Verde. A suspected outbreak of hantavirus has reportedly claimed three lives, leaving 150 passengers in mandatory isolation as health officials scramble to contain the respiratory disease.

## POLAND

* **Diplomatic Breakthrough in Ukraine:** In a significant development for regional security, Russia has announced a ceasefire for the upcoming Victory Day celebrations (May 8-9), a move matched by Ukrainian President Volodymyr Zelenskyy. The truce is expected to take effect starting midnight on Tuesday.
* **Security and Deployment Debates:** Amidst shifting European defense postures, the Polish Ministry of Interior and Administration has confirmed a readiness to accept U.S. troops redeployed from Germany. The government emphasizes the need for national unity regarding defense policy, dismissing criticism from opposition factions as detrimental to collective security.
* **Fatal Incident in Warsaw:** A police intervention on Warsaw’s Żoliborz district resulted in a fatality after a man fell from a third-story window. Local law enforcement is conducting a formal inquiry into the circumstances surrounding the incident.
