## WORLD

* **Political Earthquake in Hungary:** Péter Magyar has officially assumed the office of Prime Minister, marking a historic end to Viktor Orbán’s 16-year rule. Magyar has pledged a systemic transformation of the Hungarian government, signaling a potential shift in the nation's political trajectory and its relationship with the European Union.
* **NATO and European Security Future:** Former NATO Secretary General Anders Fogh Rasmussen has sounded an alarm regarding the future of the alliance, calling for the creation of a new, high-standard European military coalition. Rasmussen explicitly highlighted the need for Ukraine's inclusion, framing the country as a vital bastion against Russian influence.
* **Victory Day Parade Reflects Russian Fragility:** Moscow’s scaled-back Victory Day parade has served as a visual indicator of Russia’s growing vulnerability. The reduced scope of the event underscores the strain placed on the Russian state by the ongoing war in Ukraine, challenging the internal perception of security within the country.

## POLAND

* **Leadership Change in Hungary (Polish perspective):** Polish media is closely tracking Péter Magyar’s ascent, emphasizing his promise to restructure the Hungarian political system. His transition from opposition figure to Prime Minister is being watched as a barometer for Central European democratic trends.
* **Tragedy in Płock:** A fatal accident occurred during the Polish Jet Ski Championships in Płock, where an 18-year-old competitor died following a collision between two vessels. Local authorities are investigating the circumstances of the incident.
* **Extreme Weather Warnings:** The Institute of Meteorology and Water Management (IMGW) has issued first-degree weather alerts for nine Polish provinces. Citizens are warned of a dangerous combination of storms, heavy rain, and unseasonal ground frosts expected throughout the coming night.
