## WORLD

* **US Military Engages Iran-Bound Tanker:** The US military has confirmed firing a missile at an oil tanker bound for Iran. According to officials, the vessel's engine room was struck after the crew repeatedly ignored warnings, marking a significant escalation in maritime tensions in the region.
* **Breakthrough in Quantum Computing:** Microsoft has unveiled a new quantum chip, claiming it is 1,000 times more reliable than its predecessor. The company projects that this advancement puts them on track to deliver a commercially viable quantum computer by the end of the decade.
* **Armenia's Strategic Move Against Russian Interference:** In a bold response to alleged Kremlin-backed efforts to influence upcoming parliamentary elections, the Armenian government has declared that citizens arriving from Russia may be subject to mandatory 25-day military drills upon entry. This initiative aims to disrupt organized efforts to manipulate the electoral process.

## POLAND

* **Prime Minister Starmer Addresses the Death of Henry Nowak:** UK Prime Minister Sir Keir Starmer has called for a formal investigation into police conduct following the death of 18-year-old student Henry Nowak. The case has sparked outrage in the UK after footage emerged showing the student in distress while being handcuffed, with officers reportedly ignoring his pleas that he could not breathe. The situation is further complicated by controversial allegations of racism directed at the victim, which are currently being scrutinized by authorities.
