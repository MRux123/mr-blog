## WORLD

*   **Drone Debris Found in Romania:** Tensions on NATO’s eastern flank have intensified following the discovery of drone debris on Romanian territory. The incident has triggered increased operational activity of F-16 fighter jets patrolling the border region, highlighting the ongoing security risks posed by conflicts in neighboring areas.
*   **Tragic Collision of Firefighting Aircraft in Greece:** A fatal accident occurred in Greece involving two firefighting helicopters. The crash resulted in the death of one Danish and one Greek crew member, while a British pilot and another Greek national survived the incident. The tragedy underscores the extreme conditions faced by emergency teams battling wildfires in the Mediterranean.

## POLAND

*   **Naval Security Operation:** Polish support has been instrumental in a high-stakes maritime operation, where Italian military personnel successfully boarded a tanker belonging to the so-called "shadow fleet." This action represents a significant step in monitoring and countering vessels operating outside of standard international regulations.
*   **Political Mobilization:** Former Prime Minister Mateusz Morawiecki has signaled a shift in political strategy, announcing a nationwide campaign. Moving beyond initial smaller-scale engagement efforts, the opposition is preparing for a "great march" across Poland, signaling an escalation in domestic political activity as parties position themselves for future challenges.
