## WORLD

* **Tragedy in Berlin:** A vehicle has plowed into a crowd in the center of Berlin, near an ongoing Pride Parade event. Authorities have confirmed one fatality and 17 injuries. The local police have identified a suspect with links to Islamist extremist circles, and an active manhunt is currently underway to secure the area and ensure public safety.
* **Geopolitical Standoff in Kenya:** The British Ministry of Defence has been forced to cancel planned military training exercises in Kenya. The breakdown in cooperation stems from the Kenyan government's refusal to ratify a bilateral defence agreement, as local lawmakers demand increased jurisdiction to prosecute British soldiers for criminal offences committed on Kenyan soil.

## POLAND

* **Fatal Accident in the High Tatras:** A 21-year-old Polish climber has died following a tragic accident in the Slovakian High Tatras. The victim fell from a height of 150 meters. Despite a rapid deployment of helicopter rescue services, the man could not be saved. His climbing partner remained unharmed.
* **Escalation in Kyiv:** The Ukrainian capital faces renewed destruction following a brutal morning of Russian airstrikes. The attacks have caused significant damage, leaving parts of the city in flames and once again highlighting the volatile security situation in the region.
