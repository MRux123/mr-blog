## WORLD

* **Escalating Wildfires in France:** South-west France is facing a critical situation as authorities prepare for a new 40°C heatwave. Firefighters are struggling to contain the blazes in the Gironde region, which are being exacerbated by extreme weather conditions.
* **Ecological Mystery in Kenya:** An urgent investigation has been launched following the discovery of 14 dead elephants in southern Kenya. This incident marks the highest recorded mortality rate for the species in the region in decades, raising concerns among conservationists.

## POLAND

* **Internal Conflict Within Law and Justice (PiS):** The party is currently grappling with significant internal divisions. Following a meeting of the Political Committee, leadership has referred 44 members—who refused to sign declarations of non-association with external groups—to the party's disciplinary court.
* **Jacek Kurski Sanctioned:** In a move highlighting the party’s deepening fractures, former TVP head Jacek Kurski has been banned from public media appearances. This decision follows his public criticism of former Prime Minister Mateusz Morawiecki, who has simultaneously been consolidating his own political project, "Rozwój Plus," amid the ongoing party turmoil.
* **Emergency at the Danube:** A maritime operation resulted in the evacuation of nearly 200 passengers from a cruise ship stranded on the Danube, which has suffered from severe water level drops, highlighting the broader impact of European drought conditions.
