## WORLD

*   **US Soldier Arrested in UK for Espionage:** A 41-year-old senior US Army soldier has been taken into custody in London on charges of spying for Russia. The joint operation, involving the FBI and US military intelligence, marks a significant breach in national security, raising alarms among NATO allies regarding potential infiltration.
*   **Iran-Linked Terror Plot Thwarted:** The US Department of Justice has revealed a foiled series of attacks linked to Iran-backed suspects, including a targeted plot against RAF Fairford, a critical UK airbase utilized by US forces. This incident underscores the intensifying geopolitical friction between Western forces and Iranian-aligned actors.
*   **Closure of Novaya Gazeta:** In a blow to global press freedom, Russia’s independent newspaper *Novaya Gazeta* has officially ceased operations. The closure signals a final stage in the long-term suppression of critical media within Russia, as the state consolidates control over the domestic information landscape.

## POLAND

*   **Orlen Terminates LNG Deal with Qatar:** The Polish energy giant Orlen has officially withdrawn from a long-term supply contract with QatarEnergy. The decision, justified by the company as a result of force majeure and persistent delivery failures, marks a strategic shift in Poland's efforts to secure its energy infrastructure.
*   **Cyberattack on ePUAP System:** Polish authorities are investigating a significant cyberattack targeting the national ePUAP electronic communication system. While the government claims the threat has been neutralized, the incident highlights ongoing vulnerabilities in Poland’s digital administrative infrastructure.
*   **Tensions Ahead of Independence Day:** Warsaw Mayor Rafał Trzaskowski has issued a formal appeal to the public ahead of the upcoming Independence Day march. Officials remain on high alert following recent internal debates regarding public safety and the political climate surrounding national commemorative events.
