## WORLD

*   **Widespread Civil Unrest in Europe:** Major cities across France and Italy are experiencing severe social turmoil. In France, massive protests have escalated into violent clashes between demonstrators and police, resulting in nearly 1,000 arrests and serious injuries. Similarly, Italy is facing widespread rioting in Rome, Milan, and Turin, where protesters have confronted security forces with fireworks and projectiles, prompting a heavy-handed response from local authorities.
*   **Geopolitical Escalation in the Middle East:** Tensions have intensified following a series of Houthi missile strikes targeting Saudi Arabian airports and Aramco oil installations. The U.S. government has responded by raising its travel advisory to the highest level for Lebanon and Iraq, urging citizens to avoid travel due to the heightened risk of regional conflict. Meanwhile, in Hebron, Israeli forces have initiated home demolitions, exacerbating local tensions ahead of the upcoming national elections.
*   **Canada-U.S. Diplomatic Friction:** The U.S. administration has formally accused Canadian officials of interfering in the upcoming midterm elections to favor the Democratic Party. Canadian Prime Minister Mark Carney has categorically denied these allegations, marking a significant diplomatic rift between the two neighbors.

## POLAND

*   **Severe Weather Warning:** The Government Security Centre (RCB) has issued a critical alert for the Lower Silesian region. Strong winds, forecasted to reach speeds of up to 120 km/h in the Sudetes, pose a significant threat to infrastructure and public safety, prompting officials to urge residents to secure their property.
*   **Police Misconduct Investigation:** A police officer, identified as Katarzyna S., has been suspended and charged with complicity in a criminal investigation involving the ZOO Łączna case. Prosecutors allege that the officer compromised ongoing investigative efforts by leaking confidential information to a suspect.
