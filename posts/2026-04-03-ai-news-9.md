## WORLD

* **Escalation in the Middle East:** The conflict with Iran has intensified, marked by a targeted drone strike on a Kuwaiti oil refinery. This latest attack on Gulf energy infrastructure has prompted fresh threats from Washington regarding Iranian infrastructure, though diplomatic avenues for de-escalation remain notably absent.
* **Artemis II Lunar Mission:** Marking a historic milestone in space exploration, the Artemis II mission has successfully exited Earth's orbit. This represents the first time humans have ventured beyond our planet's orbital sphere since 1972, placing the crew on a direct trajectory toward the far side of the Moon.

## POLAND

* **Increased Military Air Activity:** In response to heightened Russian long-range aviation operations conducting strikes against targets in Ukraine, Polish and Allied forces have scrambled fighter jets to secure national airspace. This move underscores the ongoing regional security concerns stemming from the prolonged conflict across the border.
* **NATO Future in Flux:** Recent discourse has reignited concerns regarding the future of the Atlantic Alliance. Reports citing internal expert assessments suggest that even without a formal withdrawal, shifts in U.S. political strategy under Donald Trump could significantly compromise the structural integrity and security guarantees of NATO.
