## WORLD

* **Military Escalation in the Gulf:** The conflict between the United States and Iran has reached a critical phase. U.S. officials confirmed the loss of an F-15E jet, shot down by Iranian forces—the first such incident in the ongoing five-week war. Additionally, a U.S. A-10 attack aircraft crashed in the Strait of Hormuz, further straining military resources in the region.
* **Diplomatic Impasse:** Prospects for a short-term ceasefire remain bleak. According to reports from the Wall Street Journal, Tehran has rejected a U.S.-proposed 48-hour truce. Intelligence suggests that Iranian military capabilities remain largely intact, with half of their ballistic missile launchers still operational, contrary to earlier optimistic assessments by the White House.

## POLAND

* **Tragedy in Kępice:** A fatal accident occurred in the Pomeranian region, where a car plunged into a river. While two passengers managed to escape the vehicle, a third man could not be saved despite the arrival of rescue services and resuscitation efforts.
* **Mass Casualty Accident in Ujsoły:** In the Silesian Voivodeship, a severe traffic incident resulted in five people being injured, including a 13-year-old child. The incident occurred in the immediate aftermath of a collision between two vehicles, leading to the group being struck by a car on the scene.
