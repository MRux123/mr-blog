## WORLD

* **Trump's Russian Fuel Deal Sparks International Backlash:** President Trump has announced a deal to acquire Russian diesel, a move intended to curb rising fuel costs ahead of the midterm elections. The decision has drawn sharp condemnation from President Zelensky, who labeled the agreement an "investment in war," arguing that it undermines international efforts to isolate Russia's economy.
* **Hurricane Isaias Threatens US Gulf Coast:** A life-threatening storm is currently making landfall in Florida. Emergency services have been placed on high alert as the hurricane is expected to traverse a path toward Alabama, Georgia, and Louisiana, prompting large-scale evacuation protocols.

## POLAND

* **RCB Alerts Issued for Southeastern Regions:** The Government Security Centre (RCB) has issued further weather-related alerts for the Lublin and Podkarpacie regions. Authorities are maintaining constant monitoring of the situation as weather volatility continues to pose risks to the area.
* **EU Budget Negotiations Loom:** Ireland is preparing to propose significant spending cuts in the upcoming European Union budget. This development is expected to trigger intensive diplomatic negotiations, with Poland’s reaction being a critical factor in the potential approval of the new fiscal framework.
