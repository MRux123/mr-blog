## WORLD

*   **The Geopolitical Ascendancy of Beijing:** Analysis of recent diplomatic maneuvers suggests a significant shift in global power dynamics. Following Vladimir Putin’s visit to Beijing, observers note that Xi Jinping is effectively maneuvering both the United States and Russia, asserting Chinese global dominance as the two traditional superpowers face internal and external strain.
*   **Iran’s Shadow Government:** Investigative reports from the New York Times reveal that the decision-making apparatus in Tehran remains firmly under the control of a clandestine military fraternity associated with the Islamic Revolutionary Guards Corps, complicating international diplomatic efforts.
*   **Public Health Crisis in DRC:** The Democratic Republic of Congo has been forced to cancel its World Cup training camp due to a severe outbreak of a rare species of Ebola, which has already claimed 139 lives, underscoring the ongoing challenges in regional health security.
