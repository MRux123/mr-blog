## WORLD

*   **Houthi Escalation in the Red Sea:** The geopolitical landscape in the Middle East has grown increasingly volatile as Houthi forces seized the strategic port of Mokha. This move strengthens their position to disrupt global trade routes, fueling concerns over a wider regional escalation involving Iran and Saudi Arabia.
*   **Trump’s Midterm Pledges:** Former President Donald Trump has made a controversial election promise to distribute $5,000 to every adult American if Republicans win the upcoming midterms. The proposal, which lacks fiscal details, has sparked an immediate debate regarding its legality and economic feasibility.
*   **Far-Right Gains in Germany:** The Alternative for Germany (AfD) party is gaining significant momentum by adopting political messaging that mirrors the Kremlin’s rhetoric. The party now stands on the precipice of potentially governing a German state for the first time, signaling a notable shift in European domestic politics.

## POLAND

*   **Death of Prof. Andrzej Elżanowski:** The scientific and animal welfare communities are mourning the loss of Professor Andrzej Elżanowski, a prominent expert and a steadfast advocate for animal rights. His passing marks a significant loss for the environmental ethics movement in Poland.
*   **Intelligence Insights into the Kim Dynasty:** New reports from intelligence agencies have shed light on the secretive family life of North Korean leader Kim Jong Un, revealing further details about his children and the potential future of the regime's hereditary power structure.
*   **Administrative Support for Seniors:** The Polish government has highlighted updates regarding the "nursing supplement" (dodatek pielęgnacyjny), clarifying the eligibility criteria for the additional 366 PLN pension benefit aimed at supporting senior citizens.
