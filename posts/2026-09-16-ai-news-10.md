## WORLD

* **EU Proposes Associate Membership for Canada**: Amidst fraying diplomatic relations with the United States under the Trump administration, the European Union has extended an offer to Canada to become an "associate member." This move signals a significant realignment in Western trade and security cooperation, as both Ottawa and Brussels seek to mitigate the impact of isolationist policies originating from Washington.
* **Escalating Violence in Gaza**: A war-damaged residential block in Gaza City has collapsed, resulting in at least 20 deaths. The tragedy highlights the ongoing humanitarian crisis in the region, where displaced families continue to inhabit structurally compromised buildings despite repeated warnings, further straining international efforts to manage the conflict's aftermath.
* **Sahel Region Faces Record Islamist Insurgency**: Marking the 25th anniversary of the 9/11 attacks, data from conflict monitors indicates that violence linked to Al-Qaida and the Islamic State in Africa’s Sahel belt is projected to reach record levels this year, underscoring the shift of extremist volatility from the Middle East to the African continent.

## POLAND

* **Political Confrontation at the Academy of Justice**: A serious security breach occurred at the Academy of Justice, where Member of Parliament Dariusz Matecki reportedly forced his way through the facility's fence. The incident, linked to ongoing administrative disputes at the university, has resulted in a formal notification to the police filed by the acting rector, Sławomir Cudak.
* **Security Consultations Amid Heightened Risks**: President Karol Nawrocki is set to convene with Defense Minister Władysław Kosiniak-Kamysz and Chief of the General Staff General Wiesław Kukuła. The urgent meeting aims to address the deteriorating regional security situation and evaluate the state's defensive readiness in light of recent intelligence regarding localized threats.
