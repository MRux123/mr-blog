## WORLD

*   **Narendra Modi’s Diplomatic Strategy:** Indian Prime Minister Narendra Modi continues his pattern of receiving numerous international honors during his overseas visits. His recent stop in the Seychelles, where he was awarded the "Guardian of the Blue Horizon," highlights a broader trend of the Indian leader actively collecting state accolades, which analysts suggest serves to bolster his international standing and diplomatic leverage.

## POLAND

*   **Promoting Active Lifestyles:** New initiatives in Poland are focusing on public health, specifically targeting sedentary habits associated with screen time. Health experts are urging the population to integrate regular physical exercise into their daily routines to improve long-term health outcomes and combat the risks associated with prolonged inactivity.
