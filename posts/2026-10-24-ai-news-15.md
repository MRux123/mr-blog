## WORLD

*   **Starlink Stability Restored in Ukraine:** Following a week of operational disruptions that hampered military and drone coordination, the Starlink network in Ukraine has been stabilized. Kyiv has publicly acknowledged the resolution, marking a critical return to reliable communications for frontline operations.
*   **Arson Attack at Eagle’s Nest:** German authorities have apprehended a suspect following an arson attack on the "Eagle’s Nest," the historic Alpine residence associated with the Nazi era. The structure sustained severe damage, with officials deeming the site likely beyond repair.
*   **Geopolitical Unrest in the West Bank:** The Israeli military has launched a significant security operation in Hebron following the deaths of two soldiers. The raid has triggered widespread displacement of Palestinian families, underscoring the deteriorating security climate in the region.

## POLAND

*   **Episcopal Conference Responds to Abortion Law:** The Polish Episcopate has issued a formal statement regarding recent legislative changes passed by the Sejm concerning abortion. Archbishop Tadeusz Wojda, speaking for the Church, has reiterated the moral imperative to protect the unborn, setting the stage for a renewed confrontation between the Church and the current legislative trajectory.
*   **Political Tensions within the Ruling Coalition:** Włodzimierz Czarzasty, head of the Left, has issued a sharp critique of his coalition partners, accusing them of pandering to right-wing interests and the Church. His remarks reflect growing internal friction regarding the government’s ideological direction and its posture toward international figures.
*   **Tragedy and Solidarity in Jedlicze:** A somber act of communal solidarity has emerged following the death of a three-year-old child in Jedlicze. The parents have announced the donation of their son's organs for transplant, a decision that has garnered significant public empathy and highlighted the profound personal sacrifices accompanying local tragedies.
