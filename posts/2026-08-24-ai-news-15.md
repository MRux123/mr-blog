## WORLD

* **Mass Abduction in Nigeria:** An armed group has abducted approximately 600 people, including women and children, following a violent raid on a mosque in Niger State. A video circulating on social media confirms the scale of the crisis, highlighting an escalating security threat in the region as victims remain held captive in a forested area.
* **Middle East Tensions:** Israel has issued a stern warning regarding a "forceful" response to security threats emanating from Gaza, specifically citing the use of incendiary kites found in border communities. The situation remains volatile as officials weigh the risks of further escalation.

## POLAND

* **Diplomatic Friction with Russia:** The Kremlin, through its ambassador to Poland, has leveled accusations of "provocations" ahead of the Russian Duma elections. Moscow has explicitly identified Poland as a country where, according to them, elites are prone to anti-Russian sentiments, further straining bilateral relations.
* **Deepfake Disinformation Campaign:** A sophisticated deepfake video featuring Karol Nawrocki has been circulating online, used by fraudsters to lure citizens into illicit investment schemes. The incident has prompted a sharp response from the Ministry of Digital Affairs, signaling a growing battle against AI-driven financial scams.
* **Loss of a Literary Icon:** Poland mourns the death of renowned author Ignacy Karpowicz, who passed away at the age of 50. Karpowicz, a critically acclaimed writer known for works such as "Ości," is remembered for his significant contribution to contemporary Polish literature.
