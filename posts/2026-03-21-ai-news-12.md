## WORLD

* **Escalation in Iran:** Military tensions in the Middle East have reached a critical juncture following a strike on the Natanz nuclear complex. Reports indicate that U.S. and Israeli forces targeted the facility, prompting international concern. Simultaneously, the U.S. government has lifted sanctions on Iranian oil in a move aimed at stabilizing global markets, marking a significant reversal of the previous "maximum pressure" policy.
* **Russian Communications Collapse:** The strategic landscape in the ongoing conflict has shifted as Russian forces face severe operational degradation. The loss of access to SpaceX’s Starlink satellite network has crippled Russian battlefield communication, allowing Ukrainian forces to regain lost territory and effectively blunt the threat posed by enemy drone activity.

## POLAND

* **Political Rebranding:** The political party previously known as Polska 2050 has undergone a formal structural shift. During the "Congress of New Opening," the party officially adopted the name "Polska 2050 Rzeczpospolitej Polskiej." The leadership emphasized that this move marks a new chapter for the movement, with founder Szymon Hołownia expressing strong optimism for the party's future.
* **Presidential Mission to the U.S.:** President Karol Nawrocki is set to depart for the United States next week. His itinerary includes participation in the CPAC conference and a high-level visit to an F-35 fighter jet production facility, signaling a strengthening of Polish-American defense and political cooperation.
* **Security Incidents:** Authorities have reported a surge in illicit activities, including the discovery of smuggling operations involving balloons carrying contraband cigarettes across the Podlaskie region, as well as a violent attack in Warsaw that has left a 20-year-old victim in critical condition.
