## WORLD

* **Iran-US Standoff:** With the deadline for reopening the Strait of Hormuz hours away, the situation in the Middle East has reached a critical juncture. President Trump has threatened direct military action against Iranian infrastructure if the transit corridor remains closed. As of now, international observers report no signs of a diplomatic breakthrough, heightening fears of a major regional conflict.
* **Geopolitical Realignment:** U.S. Vice President JD Vance is set to visit Hungary to bolster Prime Minister Viktor Orban’s re-election campaign. The move underscores the strengthening ties between the current U.S. administration and the Hungarian leadership, signaling a broader strategic shift in transatlantic relations.
* **Space Exploration Milestone:** NASA has confirmed that the crew of Artemis II has established a new record for human space travel, venturing further from Earth than any previous mission. The achievement follows a brief period of signal loss as the capsule navigated behind the Moon.

## POLAND

* **Crisis Preparedness:** In response to growing concerns over regional stability, the Polish Episcopate, in collaboration with the Ministry of Interior and the Ministry of National Defense, has established a specialized task force. The group is drafting contingency plans for potential crises, focusing on humanitarian aid, shelter security, and the preservation of national heritage sites.
* **Political Polemics:** Prime Minister Donald Tusk’s recent statements regarding the potential risks of Poland being drawn into Middle Eastern conflicts have sparked a sharp reaction from the opposition. Law and Justice (PiS) representatives have dismissed the remarks as provocative, accusing the government of attempting to damage Poland’s strategic alliance with the United States.
* **Security Operations:** Authorities have launched a high-profile security operation in Warsaw, involving counter-terrorism units and the deployment of Black Hawk helicopters. The intervention, concentrated near Rondo Zgrupowania AK "Radosław", highlights the heightened state of alert currently maintained within the capital.
