## WORLD

This mobilization has prompted widespread concern, marking a significant escalation in the ongoing geopolitical strain surrounding the island nation.

The incumbent "Civil Contract" party, led by Nikol Pashinyan, advocates for deeper integration with the U.S. and the EU, while the opposition maintains a pro-Russian stance, highlighting deep societal divisions.

This incident underscores the increasing reach of the conflict into Russia's major urban centers, disrupting the narrative of isolation from the war's realities.
