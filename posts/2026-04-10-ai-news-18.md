## WORLD

* **Escalation in US-Iran Negotiations:** As Vice President J.D. Vance travels to Pakistan for crucial talks, the situation remains volatile. Tehran has introduced a new demand for the release of frozen assets as a precondition for negotiations, prompting a stern warning from President Trump against "overplaying their hand."
* **Middle East Security Crisis:** Lebanon has formally conditioned its participation in diplomatic talks with Israel on the prior implementation of a ceasefire. Simultaneously, violence in the West Bank has reached a critical point following the killing of a Palestinian during a settler attack, with former security officials openly warning that state-sanctioned extremism is spiraling out of control.
* **Political Volatility in Hungary:** As the nation heads to the polls, international observers and analysts are expressing deep concern over the stability of the electoral process. Prof. Bogdan Góralczyk has warned that Viktor Orbán’s reluctance to accept potential defeat could lead to extreme measures, including the possible cancellation of elections via decree.

## POLAND

* **Political Integrity and Accountability:** The "Partia Razem" movement is calling for legislative reforms following a corruption-related scandal involving a local Coalition of the Kind (KO) councilor. The party is using this case to advocate for stronger anti-graft measures within Polish local government.
* **Orlen Leadership Shake-up:** Significant attention is focused on the personnel changes within the state-owned energy giant Orlen, where former Warsaw police chief Michał Domaradzki has been appointed to oversee security, control, and anti-corruption operations, replacing the former regional police head Rafał Batkowski.
* **Diplomatic Provocations in Russia:** Tensions between Warsaw and Moscow have flared again, as the Polish Embassy reported anti-Polish provocations and hostile slogans directed at groups commemorating the Katyn massacre and the Smolensk air disaster victims on Russian soil.
