## WORLD

* **Escalation in Ukraine:** A series of Russian strikes across Ukraine has resulted in at least 10 fatalities, marking another violent chapter in the ongoing conflict. Simultaneously, Kyiv claims to have successfully targeted Russian "shadow fleet" oil tankers and a terminal using drones, signaling an intensification of asymmetric maritime and energy-related warfare.
* **Missing US Personnel in Morocco:** A major search and rescue operation is underway in south-western Morocco after two US service members went missing during the "African Lion" multinational military exercises. US Africa Command (Africom) is coordinating with local authorities to locate the personnel near the city of Tan Tan.

## POLAND

* **Political Reform Debates:** During the 3rd of May Constitution Day celebrations, President Karol Nawrocki delivered a sharp critique of the current state of Polish governance. Nawrocki argued that internal political conflicts have damaged state institutions and eroded the rule of law, openly calling for a significant modernization or a full revision of the 1997 Constitution to address the systemic chaos.
* **Andrzej Poczobut Speaks Out:** After five years of isolation, journalist and activist Andrzej Poczobut has made a poignant statement, asserting that he does not view himself as a hero, shedding light on the human cost of his long-term detention.
* **Ekstraklasa Relegation:** In sports news, the PKO BP Ekstraklasa landscape has shifted as Bruk-Bet Termalica Nieciecza suffered a crushing 1:5 defeat against GKS Katowice. This result officially confirms the team’s relegation to the 1. liga for the upcoming season.
