## WORLD

*   **Escalating Ebola Outbreak:** The World Health Organization has expressed grave concern regarding the rapid spread of Ebola in the Democratic Republic of the Congo. With over 500 suspected cases and 130 fatalities reported, the outbreak is proving to be more volatile than initial projections suggested. Health authorities are racing to contain the virus as confirmed cases emerge across provincial borders and neighboring countries.
*   **Environmental Toll of the Ukraine-Russia Conflict:** Recent Ukrainian strikes on Russian oil infrastructure have triggered significant ecological damage. While these tactical maneuvers aim to diminish the Kremlin’s war financing capabilities, they have resulted in localized environmental disasters, mirroring the ecological destruction previously attributed solely to Russian military operations in the region.
*   **ICC Prosecution in Libya:** In a landmark development for international justice, a former Libyan militia commander is set to appear before the International Criminal Court. The trial, centered on allegations of war crimes, torture, and human trafficking, represents a major step toward accountability for the systemic abuses endured by refugees attempting to reach Europe.

## POLAND

*   **Tragic Incident on Lake Juksty:** A major search and rescue operation is underway on Lake Juksty in the Warmian-Masurian Voivodeship following a pedal boat accident involving four young men. While three individuals were accounted for, one 18-year-old remains missing, highlighting safety concerns as graduation trips to the region increase in frequency.
*   **Legal Ambiguities in Ustka Case:** Psychiatric experts have failed to provide a definitive assessment regarding the mental state of a former State Protection Service (SOP) officer accused of a high-profile violent crime. The suspect is alleged to have killed his four-year-old daughter and attempted to murder his spouse and family members in January, with the legal proceedings currently stalled by the lack of a conclusive medical opinion.
*   **High-Stakes Investigation into Billionaire's Death:** Catalan authorities have detained the son of the founder of the Mango fashion empire in connection with the death of the billionaire. Initially treated as a hiking accident, the case has shifted toward a homicide investigation following reports of contradictory witness testimony and an ongoing, multi-billion euro inheritance dispute.
