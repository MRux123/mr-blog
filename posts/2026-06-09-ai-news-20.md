## WORLD

*   **Political Instability in Russia:** Reports have emerged regarding a targeted attack near Moscow, allegedly claiming the life of a high-ranking official within the country’s missile department. While official state sources remain silent, the incident highlights ongoing internal security concerns within the Russian power structure.
*   **Civil Unrest in France:** The murder of an 11-year-old girl, Lyhanna, has sparked widespread public outrage across France. The incident has intensified political pressure on the government, particularly as public frustration mounts over revelations that the suspect had previously been reported to police, raising critical questions regarding law enforcement efficacy.

## POLAND

*   **Government Fiscal Projections:** Prime Minister Donald Tusk has announced the government's economic proposals for 2027, confirming a 3% pay increase for public sector employees. The cabinet also debated future projections for minimum wage and pension indexation, signaling a cautious approach to state budget management.
*   **Political Realignment within PiS:** The Law and Justice (PiS) party is undergoing internal restructuring, marked by the formation of a new association. This development follows recent political maneuvers involving former Prime Minister Mateusz Morawiecki, suggesting evolving dynamics within the opposition.
*   **Controversial Debate on Minimum Wage:** Member of Parliament Ryszard Petru has publicly advocated for a freeze on further rapid hikes in the minimum wage. Petru argues that current growth rates in real earnings are sufficient and that policy should now prioritize wage stability over aggressive legislative intervention.
