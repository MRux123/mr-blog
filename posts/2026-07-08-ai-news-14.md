## WORLD

*   **US-Ukraine Defense Pact:** President Donald Trump has officially authorized Ukraine to produce Patriot missile system components domestically. This strategic move, discussed during his meeting with President Volodymyr Zelenskyy, marks a significant shift in Western military support, with Trump signaling that allowing deeper strikes within Russian territory could be a decisive factor in ending the conflict.
*   **NATO Reinforces Eastern Flank:** Following a high-stakes summit in Ankara, NATO leaders reaffirmed the criticality of Article 5, labeling Russia a long-term threat to global security. The Alliance is actively strengthening its eastern flank in response to ongoing regional instability, stressing that the defense of one member is an attack on all.
*   **German Healthcare Scandal:** A German palliative care physician has been sentenced to prison for the murder of 15 patients. The case has sent shockwaves through the medical community, highlighting systemic failures in oversight within palliative care facilities between 2021 and 2024.

## POLAND

*   **RPP Interest Rate Decision:** The Monetary Policy Council (RPP) has officially announced its latest decision regarding interest rates. As the economic landscape remains volatile, the decision on whether to maintain or adjust current rates serves as a critical indicator for Poland’s inflation outlook and monetary policy direction.
*   **Environmental Concerns Regarding White Storks:** Polish researchers have raised concerns regarding the dietary habits of the white stork population. Observations indicate that birds are increasingly feeding at waste landfills, which, while aiding temporary development and weight gain, poses long-term health risks to the species that was once considered highly threatened.
