## WORLD

* **Catastrophic Flooding in Nepal and Tibet:** A humanitarian crisis is unfolding in the Himalayas as extreme weather conditions have resulted in hundreds of fatalities and over 1,400 individuals reported missing. Emergency services are currently struggling to reach stranded populations amidst massive destruction of local infrastructure.
* **Death of Ratko Mladić:** The former Bosnian Serb military leader, convicted of genocide and war crimes committed during the 1992-95 conflict in the former Yugoslavia, has died at the age of 84 while serving a life sentence.
* **Shift in Academic Landscape:** Canadian universities have initiated a strategic recruitment drive targeting top-tier scholars from Harvard and other major U.S. institutions, capitalizing on the shifting political climate within the American academic sector under the current administration.

## POLAND

* **Legal Charges Against PKOl President:** The President of the Polish Olympic Committee (PKOl), Radosław P., has been officially charged by the prosecutor's office. The allegations, linked to the "Zondacrypto" investigation, involve corruption, influence peddling, and financial malpractice regarding creditor prioritization.
* **Industrial Disaster in Greater Poland:** A major fire has devastated a 3,000-square-meter production facility in Bielsko, Wielkopolska. Emergency crews are currently engaged in a massive operation to contain the blaze, with the Government Security Centre (RCB) issuing urgent safety alerts to local residents.
* **Lech Poznań Advances in Europe:** In a significant sporting achievement, Lech Poznań successfully secured a spot in the UEFA Europa League league phase following a decisive draw, providing a morale boost for Polish football fans despite recent stadium safety incidents involving other clubs.
