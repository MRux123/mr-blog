## WORLD

*   **Slovak PM Assassination Verdict:** The Slovak judiciary has reached a definitive conclusion regarding the 2024 assassination attempt on Prime Minister Robert Fico. Juraj Cintula, 73, has been sentenced to 21 years in prison for terrorism. This sentencing concludes a high-profile legal battle that has significantly impacted the regional political landscape.
*   **UK Demographic Crisis:** In a historic shift, the United Kingdom is facing a demographic turning point in 2026. Official data indicates that the number of deaths will exceed the number of births for the first time. This trend, which analysts describe as unprecedented, poses long-term structural risks to the nation’s socio-economic stability.

## POLAND

*   **EU Legal Action Over Oder River:** The European Commission has initiated infringement proceedings against Poland concerning the state of the Oder River. Brussels alleges that Warsaw failed to take sufficient restorative action following the 2022 ecological disaster. Poland faces a two-month deadline to respond before the case is referred to the European Court of Justice.
*   **Security Crackdown in Poznań:** Local law enforcement in Poznań has successfully dismantled a violent criminal group responsible for a series of brutal robberies and assaults along the city’s Wartostrada. Five suspects are currently in custody, facing potential sentences of up to 15 years for their role in the recent surge of public violence.
