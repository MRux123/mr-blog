## WORLD

* **Diplomatic Breakthrough in Ukraine-US Relations:** President Volodymyr Zelenskyy has signaled a potential shift in diplomatic momentum following a productive conversation with Donald Trump. The two leaders are scheduled to meet in New York to discuss specific frameworks for a ceasefire, with a particular emphasis on stabilizing maritime security and energy infrastructure, marking a critical juncture in the ongoing conflict.
* **Progress in Jovenel Moïse Assassination Case:** Legal proceedings surrounding the 2021 assassination of Haitian President Jovenel Moïse have advanced significantly. Authorities have confirmed the arrest of thirty additional suspects, including 18 individuals who were successfully extradited from Haiti to Miami, signaling a major milestone in the international effort to bring the conspirators to justice.
* **Strengthening EU-Canada Ties:** In a historic geopolitical move, French President Emmanuel Macron and Canadian leadership met to reaffirm shared values. This follows the European Union’s landmark invitation for Canada to become its first-ever "associate member," a development that underscores a tightening of trans-Atlantic economic and political cooperation.
