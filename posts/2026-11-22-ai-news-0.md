## WORLD

*   **Geopolitical Flare-up in the Middle East:** Iran has launched a direct military strike against Bahrain, explicitly targeting U.S. military installations. According to the Islamic Revolutionary Guard Corps, the action was a retaliatory measure for alleged violations of Iranian airspace, specifically targeting Patriot missile defense systems.
*   **Security Concerns in the Baltic Region:** Reports of explosions near the Russian naval base in Baltiysk (Kaliningrad Oblast) have surfaced. This incident marks an unprecedented development in the region, as it is the first reported attack on the Russian exclave bordering NATO members Poland and Lithuania, raising concerns about regional stability.

## POLAND

*   **Investigation into Russian Missile Debris:** Polish prosecutors have disclosed significant findings regarding the missile found in Nowy Lipowiec. Forensic analysis confirms the projectile was manufactured in the final quarter of this year. Investigators have identified the specific production facility, providing crucial evidence regarding recent Russian aerial activity near Polish territory.
*   **Defense Strategy and NATO Cooperation:** Defense Minister Władysław Kosiniak-Kamysz has signaled a deepening of military cooperation with NATO. Following ongoing regional security challenges, the Polish government is set to unveil new, concrete proposals for strengthening collective defense measures during the upcoming week.
