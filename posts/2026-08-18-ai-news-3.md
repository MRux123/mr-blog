## WORLD

* **Escalation in Russia-UK Relations:** The Kremlin has issued a formal warning to the United Kingdom regarding the supply of military drones to Ukraine. The British Ministry of Defence has remained firm in its stance, asserting that the UK continues to stand in unwavering solidarity with Kyiv despite Moscow's diplomatic threats.
* **Shifting Geopolitics in East Asia:** Significant changes in U.S. military strategy have emerged, as Washington prepares to "substantially reduce" joint military exercises with South Korea. This shift follows President Trump’s characterization of his relationship with Kim Jong Un as highly positive, signaling a notable recalibration of regional security dynamics.

## POLAND

* **Drone Strikes on Moscow Impact Aviation:** A major overnight drone attack on the Russian capital has led to widespread operational disruptions, forcing authorities to impose strict flight restrictions across four major airports. The incident highlights the growing volatility and the reach of aerial operations within the ongoing conflict.
* **Meteorological Alerts Issued:** The Institute of Meteorology and Water Management (IMGW) has officially upgraded the alert status for four Polish provinces. Authorities are urging residents to exercise caution as potential severe weather conditions approach the region.
