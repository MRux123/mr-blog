Local reports indicate that Palestinian families are currently deprived of electricity and water, prompting a deployment of additional troops to contain the volatile confrontation.

The election, triggered by controversy surrounding Farage’s financial disclosures, is being closely watched as a bellwether for current populist sentiment in the United Kingdom.

## POLAND

* **Massive Data Breach at MyDr:** A cyberattack on the MyDr platform has exposed the personal data of nearly 19 million Poles. While the company claims the leaked information is largely historical, the scale of the incident represents one of the most significant cybersecurity threats in the country to date, sparking widespread concern over data privacy.
* **ABW Recruitment Surge:** Poland’s Internal Security Agency (ABW) has seen an unprecedented interest in recruitment, with over 20,000 applications submitted in just two months. This surge highlights a growing public awareness and willingness to engage in state security roles amidst rising regional instability.
* **Legal and International Hurdles:** Polish authorities have officially requested confirmation from Moldova regarding the whereabouts of Marcin Romanowski, marking a significant step in the ongoing legal proceedings surrounding his case.
