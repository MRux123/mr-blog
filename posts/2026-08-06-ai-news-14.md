## WORLD

* **Escalation in Ukraine-Russia Conflict:** Ukraine has successfully executed long-range strikes against two oil refineries deep within Russian territory. Kyiv maintains these operations are critical to diminishing the financial resources fueling the Russian war effort. Meanwhile, concerns grow regarding Western support, as reports indicate a potential decline in the provision of air defense systems, with allies increasingly reluctant to deplete their own stockpiles amid the ongoing conflict.
* **Mass Hostage Rescue in Nigeria:** In a significant security development, Nigerian forces have successfully liberated over 300 hostages held by militants in the Kainji Lake national park. President Bola Ahmed Tinubu characterized the joint military and police operation as the largest single-day rescue mission in the nation’s history, highlighting ongoing efforts to combat endemic kidnapping and regional instability.

## POLAND

* **Political Standoff Over Referendum:** The Polish Senate has rejected the President’s proposal for a national referendum on European Union climate policy for the second time. This move reflects a deepening divide between the presidential office and the legislative branch regarding the nation's strategic alignment with EU environmental directives.
* **Parliamentary Rejection of IPN Leadership:** The Senate has formally blocked the candidacy of Dr. Mateusz Szpytma for the position of President of the Institute of National Remembrance (IPN). The decision marks another point of friction in the current political landscape, impacting the leadership and future trajectory of one of Poland's most influential state historical institutions.
