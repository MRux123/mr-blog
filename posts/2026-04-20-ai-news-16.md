## WORLD

* **Diplomatic Efforts to Contain Iran Conflict:** As tensions remain high, Tehran and Washington are reportedly planning new talks in Islamabad. Vice President JD Vance is expected to lead the U.S. negotiating team in a bid to address the escalating situation in the Middle East.
* **Technological Shifts in the Ukraine Conflict:** Facing troop shortages, Ukraine has begun integrating advanced unmanned ground vehicles—equipped with munitions and weaponry—into its defense strategy, signaling a new phase in the tactical landscape of the war.
* **Seismic Warnings in Japan:** Following a significant tremor, the Japanese meteorological agency has issued a high-alert warning for a potentially larger earthquake in the coming week, placing disaster response teams on standby across the region.

## POLAND

* **High-Level Security Dialogue:** Prime Minister Donald Tusk and French President Emmanuel Macron met in Gdańsk to solidify bilateral cooperation. Key discussions centered on nuclear energy, military security, and a unified commitment to supporting Ukraine amidst shifting geopolitical realities.
* **Prosecutorial Action Against Grzegorz Braun:** The Wrocław prosecutor’s office has officially requested the European Parliament to strip MEP Grzegorz Braun of his immunity. The move seeks to bring charges regarding the 2025 incident involving the unlawful detainment of a physician at a hospital in Oleśnica.
* **Internal Friction in the Ruling Coalition:** The political landscape remains fluid as tensions mount within the government. Reports indicate uncertainty regarding the position of Climate Minister Paulina Hennig-Kloska, while the government faces increasing scrutiny over its handling of the cryptocurrency sector following the Zondacrypto controversy.
