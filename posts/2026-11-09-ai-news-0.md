## WORLD

* **Escalation in Saudi Arabia:** Houthi militants have launched a significant strike against the Aramco Tower in the Saudi capital, Riyadh. Verified footage confirms structural damage to the facility, marking a critical intensification of regional hostilities and raising concerns over the security of global energy infrastructure.
* **Political Tensions in Argentina:** Pope Francis has publicly urged President Javier Milei to ensure that national economic policies prioritize the needs of the vulnerable. The visit underscores the deepening ideological rift between the libertarian administration and the Catholic Church regarding social justice mandates.
* **Armed Confrontations in South America:** The ELN has retaliated against a U.S.-backed security operation in Colombia, launching a drone strike against a military base. The attack highlights the challenges facing joint counter-insurgency efforts between Colombian and Venezuelan authorities.

## POLAND

* **Krakow Shooting Investigation:** Polish authorities are reeling from a mass shooting in Krakow, where a 24-year-old suspect killed his family before murdering six others. The investigation has moved into the political sphere, as government officials and opposition leaders, including Jarosław Kaczyński, clash over the accuracy of initial reports regarding the perpetrator's potential political motivations.
* **Legislative Response to Gun Violence:** In the wake of the Krakow tragedy, the Ministry of Internal Affairs has announced plans to revise regulations regarding black powder firearms. The government is pushing for a mandatory registry system, citing the recent attack as an urgent catalyst for tightening firearm accessibility.
* **Public Mourning in Krakow:** The Krakow Main Market Square has reopened to the public following the massacre. The atmosphere in the city remains somber, with citizens gathering to lay flowers and light candles in a display of collective grief.
