## WORLD

*   **Political Instability in the UK:** Pressure on Prime Minister Keir Starmer has intensified significantly, with dozens of Labour lawmakers calling for his resignation. Following the departure of several government aides, speculation regarding a formal leadership challenge has reached a critical threshold.
*   **Russia's War Stance:** President Vladimir Putin has publicly signaled that the war in Ukraine may be nearing a conclusion. However, his rhetoric remains complex, as he balances growing domestic discontent with a refusal to abandon his original strategic objectives.
*   **Geopolitical Tensions in the Strait of Hormuz:** Iran has confirmed the deployment of Ghadir-class submarines to the Strait of Hormuz. This move is characterized as an asymmetric response to the increased military presence of the United States in the region.

## POLAND

*   **Legal Controversies Surrounding Zbigniew Ziobro:** Political discourse in Poland remains polarized regarding the status of Zbigniew Ziobro. While his party associates defend his right to travel to the United States, opposition figures, including Bartosz Arłukowicz, have alleged that the former minister is attempting to evade accountability for the "Justice Fund" investigation, suggesting he may ultimately seek refuge abroad.
*   **Internal Politics within the Left:** The political landscape in the Silesian region faces shifts as Rafał Adamczyk, a prominent local government official, formally announced he will not assume the parliamentary seat vacated following the passing of Łukasz Litewka. Adamczyk cited his commitment to local government initiatives as the primary reason for his decision.
