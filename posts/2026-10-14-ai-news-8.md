## WORLD

* **Escalating Conflict in Ukraine:** Russian strikes on critical infrastructure have left approximately 600,000 people without power. President Zelensky confirmed the damage to vital systems, while emergency services report at least three casualties resulting from the overnight attacks.
* **Geopolitical Flashpoint in Saudi Arabia:** Tensions between the Houthi militia and the Saudi-led coalition have reached a critical juncture, with reports of an attack on a major Saudi energy facility. The incident follows explicit threats from the Yemeni militia against Aramco infrastructure.
* **Security Breach at OpenAI:** In a bizarre and concerning development, a Saudi employee has been charged with planting a recording device inside an OpenAI executive conference room. The device was discovered hidden in a USB port behind a television, marking a significant breach in the security of a leading AI firm.

## POLAND

* **Constitutional Crisis Surrounding Maciej Berek’s Oath:** The political landscape in Poland is severely polarized following the swearing-in of Maciej Berek as a Constitutional Tribunal judge. The ceremony took place in the Sejm without the presence of President Karol Nawrocki, sparking fierce criticism from the opposition, who described the event as a "grotesque" mockery of democratic procedures.
* **Protests and Clashes at the Tribunal:** The legislative controversy spilled into the streets, with protesters gathering in front of the Constitutional Tribunal headquarters. The situation escalated into physical confrontations between demonstrators—including members of parliament—and police, underscoring the deep social and political divisions currently paralyzing the nation’s judiciary institutions.
