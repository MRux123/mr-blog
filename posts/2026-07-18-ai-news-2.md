## WORLD

* **Persistent Military Strikes in Iran:** For the seventh consecutive night, United States forces have conducted precision strikes against targets within Iran. The campaign, aimed at degrading Iranian military capabilities, has expanded to include critical infrastructure, including bridges and water treatment facilities. The U.S. military justifies these actions as necessary defensive measures, while international observers raise concerns over the humanitarian impact of targeting civilian-dual-use infrastructure.
* **U.S.-Canada Trade Tensions:** Former President Trump has signaled a potential shift in North American trade relations, threatening to impose new tariffs on Canada. The justification cited involves wildfire-related air pollution, which Trump describes as "filthy air" entering U.S. cities. Canadian officials have dismissed the characterization, emphasizing the shared responsibility for addressing climate-driven environmental challenges.

## POLAND

* **Anti-Corruption Protests in Kyiv:** While regional instability remains the primary focus for Central European security, domestic tensions in Ukraine have surfaced following the dismissal of the Minister of Defense. Protesters in Kyiv gathered under the slogan "Army without fraud," signaling growing public demand for transparency and systemic reform within the Ukrainian defense sector as the conflict continues to test the nation's institutional resilience.
