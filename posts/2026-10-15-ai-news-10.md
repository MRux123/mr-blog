## WORLD

* **Ukraine Resumes Refinery Strikes:** President Volodymyr Zelensky has officially announced the resumption of Ukrainian drone strikes against Russian oil refineries. This strategic pivot marks a departure from a previous tactical pause requested by the United States, signaling a hardened stance in the ongoing conflict as Ukraine continues to innovate with AI-guided, jet-powered interceptors to counter Russian aerial threats.
* **Human Rights Crisis in Senegal:** An escalating crackdown on the LGBTQ+ community in Senegal has resulted in over 320 arrests under laws criminalizing "acts against nature." Reports from human rights organizations highlight severe abuses, including forced HIV testing and systemic barriers to legal defense, reflecting a deteriorating human rights environment in the region.

## POLAND

* **Prosecutor General Moves Against Daniel Obajtek:** In a significant political and legal development, Prosecutor General Waldemar Żurek has formally requested that the European Parliament lift the immunity of MEP Daniel Obajtek. The motion also seeks authorization for his detention and potential temporary arrest, marking a major escalation in the investigation surrounding the former oil executive.
* **Collegium Humanum Trial Delayed:** The highly anticipated legal proceedings involving 23 defendants, including Wrocław Mayor Jacek Sutryk, have been postponed until December 3. The court cited administrative procedural errors—specifically the failure to notify one of the accused regarding the hearing date—as the reason for the delay, prolonging a case that has gripped national public opinion.
* **Heightened Security for Constitutional Tribunal Judges:** Minister of Interior Marcin Kierwiński confirmed that two judges of the Constitutional Tribunal have been granted protection by the State Protection Service (SOP) following credible threats. The government has signaled a zero-tolerance policy toward the obstruction of the judiciary, promising ongoing police intervention to ensure the safety and functionality of the institution.
