## WORLD

*   **UN Adopts New Global Map Standard:** In a significant move toward geographical accuracy, the United Nations has formally retired the 16th-century Mercator projection in favor of the "Equal Earth" design. The resolution, sponsored by Togo, aims to address long-standing cartographic distortions that misrepresented the true scale of the African continent.
*   **Escalation in Ukraine:** Russian forces have launched a precise drone strike targeting a Ukrainian security headquarters in the heart of Kyiv. President Zelensky confirmed the attack, which occurred without warning, signaling a persistent threat to the country's central command infrastructure.

## POLAND

*   **Minister Domański Escalates Rhetoric on 'Zondacrypto' Scandal:** Finance Minister Andrzej Domański has delivered sharp criticism of right-wing politicians, accusing them of being deeply compromised by the ongoing "Zondacrypto" scandal. He alleged that high-ranking officials closely associated with Jarosław Kaczyński are implicated, marking a significant intensification of political hostilities.
*   **Foreign Minister Questions Historical Narrative:** Radosław Sikorski has publicly challenged the current political stance toward Germany. Referencing recent comments by President Nawrocki regarding "anti-Polish hysteria," Sikorski urged a departure from historical resentment, emphasizing that Poland's tangible national interests should take precedence over anti-German rhetoric.
*   **Public Approval of the Tusk Cabinet:** New data from the CBOS poll indicates a growth in public support for Prime Minister Donald Tusk’s government in August. The survey reflects an upward trend in both approval ratings and general perception of the cabinet's recent performance.
