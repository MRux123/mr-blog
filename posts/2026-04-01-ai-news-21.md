## WORLD

* **Escalating Rhetoric Between Iran and the U.S.**
The geopolitical climate in the Persian Gulf remains volatile as President Trump publicly dismisses concerns regarding Iran's uranium enrichment, citing the depth of the stockpiles. In a counter-move, the Iranian government has issued an open letter to the American public, framing the U.S. military presence as contrary to the interests of both nations and warning of the dire consequences of further conflict.

* **Humanity's Return to the Moon**
Space exploration marks a historic milestone tonight with the scheduled launch of the Artemis II mission. After a 52-year hiatus, a crewed mission will depart from the Kennedy Space Center, heading toward the Moon. This mission aims to set a new distance record for human space travel, signifying a major leap forward for international lunar exploration efforts.

* **Russian Territorial Gains in Ukraine**
Data from the DeepState project indicates a concerning trend in the ongoing conflict, with Russian forces occupying 27% more Ukrainian territory in March compared to the previous month. Analysts warn that if this trajectory persists, the control of the entire Donetsk region could theoretically be at risk by late 2029.

## POLAND

* **Political Turmoil within PiS**
Krzysztof Szczucki has been suspended from the Law and Justice (PiS) party by order of Jarosław Kaczyński. The disciplinary action follows internal disagreements and reports that Szczucki voted against the party line regarding a presidential veto. The situation highlights ongoing leadership challenges as the party navigates internal governance and candidate selection strategies.

* **Diplomatic Sparring Between Orban and Tusk**
Viktor Orban has publicly addressed Prime Minister Donald Tusk following a Koalicja Obywatelska (Civic Coalition) campaign video that criticized the Hungarian Foreign Minister's ties to the Kremlin. Orban’s retort, asserting that he acts in the best interest of Hungary just as Tusk once did for Poland, underscores the deepening diplomatic friction between the two leaders regarding foreign policy alignment.
