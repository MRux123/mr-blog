## WORLD

*   **Escalating Conflict in Ukraine:** Rescue operations continue at a shopping mall in Ukraine following a "despicable" Russian missile strike that resulted in at least 16 deaths and over 130 injuries. International condemnation is mounting as emergency services search for the four individuals still reported missing.
*   **Tragedy in Myanmar:** A meditation retreat site in Myanmar was struck twice by aerial attacks, resulting in the deaths of at least fourteen civilians. The incident highlights the deteriorating security situation for non-combatants in the region.
*   **Goldmine Collapse in CAR:** A catastrophic landslide at an artisanal goldmine in the Central African Republic has claimed the lives of over 100 people. Recovery efforts are ongoing, with local officials warning that the death toll is expected to rise as rescuers reach the buried site near the border with Cameroon.

## POLAND

*   **Severe Weather Disrupts Power Grid:** A significant portion of northeastern Poland is currently facing a power blackout, with tens of thousands of residents left without electricity following extreme weather conditions. Local emergency services are working to restore infrastructure.
*   **Public Safety Warnings at the Baltic Sea:** The Polish sanitary inspectorate has issued a critical warning regarding water safety at Baltic resorts. While many beaches have closed due to dangerous sea conditions and strong currents, specific concerns have been raised in Darłówko, where the detection of *Escherichia coli* bacteria has forced the closure of local swimming areas.
