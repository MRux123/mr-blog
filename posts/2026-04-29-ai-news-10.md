## WORLD

*   **Escalation in Hormuz Strait:** Aid organizations are urgently calling for a "humanitarian corridor" through the Strait of Hormuz. The ongoing conflict involving the US, Israel, and Iran has caused severe disruptions to global shipping, leading to soaring oil prices and the blockage of essential food and medical supplies, placing millions of lives at risk.
*   **North Korean Battlefield Tactics:** Reports indicate that North Korean troops are being instructed to commit suicide by detonating grenades rather than risking capture by Ukrainian forces. This development highlights the extreme ideological indoctrination and the brutal nature of the current deployment.
*   **Instability in Mali:** The French government has issued an urgent directive for its citizens to depart Mali following a surge in rebel attacks. The United Kingdom has mirrored this travel advisory, warning that those who choose to remain do so at significant personal risk.

## POLAND

*   **European Commission Targets Meta:** The European Commission has officially accused Meta of violating the Digital Services Act (DSA). The regulatory body asserts that the tech giant has failed to implement sufficient safeguards to prevent minors under the age of 13 from accessing Facebook and Instagram, sparking a major legal confrontation over child safety online.
*   **Environmental Crime near Poznań:** Authorities have discovered an illegal dump site containing hundreds of liters of hazardous solvents in the Mechowo forest district. The incident, currently under investigation, has prompted forestry officials to issue a nationwide warning regarding the rising trend of industrial waste abandonment in protected natural areas.
*   **Violent Crime in Kraków:** Police in Kraków have launched a manhunt for a suspect involved in a stabbing incident on Starowiślna Street. The force has released images of the individual to the public as the investigation into the violent attack continues.
