## WORLD

*   **Global Fuel Crisis Escalates:** The International Energy Agency (IEA) has issued a dire warning, labeling the current situation the "largest fuel crisis in history." With global oil shortages reaching a staggering one billion barrels, analysts warn that the continuation of Middle East supply chain blockades will exhaust global reserves within a month, threatening widespread economic instability.
*   **Escalation in Ukraine-Russia Conflict:** Tensions have intensified following a drone strike by Ukrainian forces on a critical pumping station in the Samara region of Russia. The facility is a key node in the "Druzhba" pipeline, a vital artery for energy supplies to several European nations, including Hungary and Slovakia.

## POLAND

*   **CBA Raid at Climate Ministry:** Central Anti-Corruption Bureau (CBA) officers have raided the Ministry of Climate and Environment to secure documentation related to the "Clean Air" (Czyste Powietrze) program. The investigation is being conducted under the supervision of the European Public Prosecutor's Office, marking a significant legal development in the administration of environmental subsidies.
*   **Political Internal Strife in PiS:** Following a seven-hour negotiation session, Law and Justice (PiS) leaders Jarosław Kaczyński and Mateusz Morawiecki announced a compromise regarding the future of the "Rozwój Plus" association. However, the move has triggered public discord within the party, with senior members openly clashing over the interpretation and implications of the agreement, highlighting growing divisions within the conservative camp.
