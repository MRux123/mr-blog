## WORLD

* **Shooting at White House Correspondents' Dinner:** A major security incident occurred at the White House Correspondents' Association dinner in Washington, D.C., where President Donald Trump was in attendance. A gunman opened fire in the hotel lobby with the stated intent of targeting administration officials. One Secret Service agent was reported injured, and the President and other top officials were successfully evacuated. 
* **Political Transition in Hungary:** Following a significant defeat at the polls, Hungary’s ruling party, Fidesz, is facing a major internal shift. Long-standing Prime Minister Viktor Orbán has announced his intention to step down from his parliamentary seat, though he aims to maintain his leadership role within the party as it navigates this period of political realignment.
* **Escalation in the Middle East:** Prime Minister Benjamin Netanyahu has ordered the Israeli military to "vigorously attack" Hezbollah in Lebanon. This directive follows a surge in violence that resulted in six deaths, occurring despite an ongoing three-week ceasefire agreement.

## POLAND

* **Polish Officials Safe After Washington Attack:** The Polish Ministry of Foreign Affairs has confirmed that Charge d’Affaires Bogdan Klich, along with accompanying diplomats and Polish journalists, are safe following the shooting at the White House Correspondents' dinner in Washington. Foreign Minister Radosław Sikorski issued a formal statement confirming their well-being.
* **Tragic Accident in Masuria:** A fatal road accident took place Sunday morning on the route between Pisz and Ruciane-Nida. Two passenger vehicles collided and caught fire, resulting in the deaths of both drivers. Emergency services successfully rescued a passenger from one of the vehicles, and an investigation into the circumstances of the crash is currently underway.
