## WORLD

* **Escalating Middle East Conflict:** The geopolitical landscape remains volatile as the conflict involving Iran continues to expand. The Pentagon confirmed the death of another U.S. service member in northern Iraq during an operation to neutralize an Iranian drone. Furthermore, authorities are investigating unidentified remains found in Jordan, linked to a previous attack that left two soldiers dead and one missing.
* **Political Transition in the UK:** Andy Burnham is set to become Britain's first openly Roman Catholic Prime Minister. The transition marks a significant cultural milestone in British politics, with Burnham framing his faith as a component of his cultural identity rather than a dogmatic political force.

## POLAND

* **Heightened Security Concerns Regarding Russia:** The Lithuanian Minister of National Defence has warned that the Baltic region and Poland remain the most immediate targets for potential Russian escalation. In response to these warnings, Polish Deputy Prime Minister Władysław Kosiniak-Kamysz announced an emergency meeting of the Council of Ministers’ Security Committee to address potential Russian provocations.
* **Economic Pressures at the Fuel Pump:** Polish motorists are facing a sharp increase in fuel costs. Analysts from the Reflex bureau warn that gasoline prices are trending toward the 8 PLN per liter threshold, a milestone already crossed by some stations in Wrocław, signaling potential long-term inflationary pressure on household budgets.
