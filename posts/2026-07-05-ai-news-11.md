## WORLD

* **Political Instability in Iran Following Leadership Transition:** Public mourning for the late Ayatollah Ali Khamenei continues, marked by intense displays of grief and cries for revenge. The persistent absence of Mojtaba Khamenei, the presumed successor, has deepened the power vacuum and geopolitical uncertainty surrounding the Iranian regime.
* **Infrastructure Vulnerability Exposed in Venezuela:** Recent earthquake activity has highlighted systemic failures in Venezuelan public housing. Experts and residents alike are pointing to years of neglected safety warnings, suggesting that the scale of the damage was an avoidable consequence of political and economic mismanagement.
* **Natural Disaster Threat in the Pacific:** Guam is currently bracing for the landfall of super typhoon Bavi. With wind speeds forecasted to exceed 160mph and potential storm surges reaching 11 meters, mass evacuations are underway as authorities prepare for a catastrophic weather event.

## POLAND

* **Controversy Over Patriot Missile Transfers:** A political dispute has erupted regarding the potential transfer of Polish Patriot missile systems to Ukraine. Marcin Przydacz, head of the BPM in the President's Chancellery, claims the government likely provided both the hardware and relinquished their priority position in American supply chains, sparking a fierce debate over national security and transparency.
* **Internal Friction in the Governing Coalition:** Tensions within the ruling coalition have surfaced following Mayor Rafał Trzaskowski’s recent personnel decisions in Warsaw. Left-wing coalition partner Anna Maria Żukowska has publicly criticized the move as politically motivated, suggesting that the dismissal of deputy mayors was designed to protect the professional interests of the mayor’s political allies.
* **Meteorological Alerts Shift:** Following a period of extreme heat, Poland is facing a rapid shift in weather patterns. The Institute of Meteorology and Water Management (IMGW) has issued first-degree alerts for eight provinces as the country transitions to cooler temperatures accompanied by strong winds and localized heavy precipitation.
