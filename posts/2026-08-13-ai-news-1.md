## WORLD

*   **Ebola Outbreak Escalation:** The World Health Organization has issued a grave warning regarding the current Ebola outbreak, which began on May 15th. With the death toll surpassing 2,000 and thousands of infections confirmed, health officials are concerned that the current crisis is on track to become the deadliest in history, prompting urgent calls for an international response.
*   **Legal Challenges for Trump Administration:** US President Donald Trump faces a new lawsuit filed by media organizations challenging the sale of preferential access to his social media posts on the Truth Social platform. Additionally, the White House has confirmed that Press Secretary Karoline Leavitt will step down later this month, citing personal reasons, amid a broader period of administrative transition.

## POLAND

*   **Legal Battle over Marcin Romanowski:** The defense team representing politician Marcin Romanowski has announced immediate steps to challenge the European Arrest Warrant issued against him. Legal representatives have also confirmed that a formal request for a letter of safe conduct has been filed, following reports that correspondence from the politician originated in Tiraspol, Transnistria, raising questions regarding his current legal standing and location.
*   **Escalation in Public Discourse:** Tensions in the Polish media landscape reached a boiling point during a recent televised debate, where a heated exchange between political representatives required intervention from the host, Agnieszka Gozdyra. The incident underscores a growing volatility in domestic political discourse. Additionally, local authorities in Zabrze are investigating an aggressive incursion by a group into a restaurant, signaling concerns over public order and safety.
