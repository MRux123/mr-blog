## WORLD

*   **Escalation in the Middle East:** A fragile two-month truce between Iran and Israel has collapsed, leading to a new cycle of direct military strikes. Iran has launched barrages of missiles, while Israeli forces have retaliated by targeting Iranian military installations and petrochemical infrastructure. President Trump has publicly urged both nations to immediately cease hostilities to prevent further regional destabilization.
*   **Devastating Earthquake in the Philippines:** A powerful 7.8-magnitude earthquake has struck the southern island of Mindanao. The disaster has resulted in at least 15 to 19 confirmed deaths, left dozens injured, and displaced tens of thousands of people. The seismic event also triggered tsunami warnings across parts of the Philippines, Indonesia, and Japan.
*   **Political Shifts in Armenia:** Prime Minister Nikol Pashinyan’s Civil Contract Party has secured a decisive victory in the national elections, capturing nearly 50% of the vote. Despite intense political pressure from Moscow, the mandate suggests a public desire to continue the current government's pro-Western path and peace negotiations.

## POLAND

*   **Tusk Calls for High-Level Dialogue:** Prime Minister Donald Tusk has issued a public appeal to President Karol Nawrocki and Ukrainian President Volodymyr Zelenskyy. Citing the failure of traditional diplomatic channels, Tusk urged for an immediate, direct, and sincere conversation between the leaders to resolve outstanding issues and prevent the erosion of bilateral solidarity.
*   **Record-Breaking Drug Seizure:** Polish law enforcement, including the CBŚP and the National Revenue Administration, have intercepted a massive shipment of heroin in the port of Gdynia. With a street value estimated at approximately 220 million PLN, officials have described the operation as the largest drug bust in the country in over a decade.
*   **Legal Developments Regarding Zbigniew Ziobro:** The legal process concerning the European Arrest Warrant (ENA) for Zbigniew Ziobro has entered a new phase. Following the disqualification of the previous presiding judge, the Warsaw Regional Court has assigned Judge Tomasz Grochowicz to oversee the case.
