## WORLD

* **Ebola Outbreak Escalates:** The World Health Organization (WHO) has officially declared a public health emergency of international concern as the Ebola outbreak in the Democratic Republic of Congo spreads at an alarming rate. With over 500 suspected cases and 130 deaths reported, health officials are urgently considering the deployment of experimental vaccines to contain the Bundibugyo strain. The crisis has gained global attention, with reports of international patients being transported for specialized care.
* **Geopolitical Tensions:** The Middle East remains on edge following U.S. President Trump's admission that he postponed a "very major attack" on Iran. Meanwhile, international efforts to stabilize regional conflicts continue as Pakistan leads mediation talks. In a separate development, the International Criminal Court (ICC) has reached a significant milestone by holding a hearing for a Libyan militia commander accused of severe human rights abuses, marking a landmark step in the pursuit of justice for refugees.

## POLAND

* **NATO and Defense Readiness:** Amid rising regional security concerns, Poland remains at the center of strategic discussions. While the Ministry of National Defense confirmed that there are no immediate plans to reduce U.S. troop capabilities in the country, the Pentagon is actively refining its deployment plans for Europe. The urgency is underscored by warnings from high-ranking German officials, including General Carsten Breuer, who identified 2029 as a potential window for a significant escalation in Russian military aggression toward NATO.
* **Legal and Political Developments:** The Polish judicial and political landscape is currently dominated by the ongoing efforts to address the legal status of former Minister of Justice Zbigniew Ziobro. Authorities have finalized an extradition request and are seeking to revoke his travel documentation, while simultaneously managing the fallout from a diplomatic incident involving the alleged mistreatment of a Polish judge by foreign club staff in Bielsko-Biała.
