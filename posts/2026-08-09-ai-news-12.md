## WORLD

* **Israel-Gaza Peace Plan Stalls:** Diplomatic efforts face a major setback as the Israeli government formally rejects a 15-point disarmament and withdrawal plan for Gaza proposed by the Trump administration. Prime Minister Netanyahu maintains that military operations will continue until Hamas is effectively dismantled.
* **Escalating Ebola Crisis in DRC:** Health authorities in the Democratic Republic of the Congo are reporting a massive surge in Ebola cases, now exceeding 4,000. Experts have expressed concerns regarding potential viral mutations, prompting an urgent, high-intensity response including house-to-house patient tracing.
* **Heightened Financial Scrutiny on Hezbollah:** Both the United States and Israel are intensifying their strategic campaign against Hezbollah by targeting the organization's financial infrastructure, aiming to cripple its operational capabilities beyond traditional military engagements.

## POLAND

* **Mass Casualty Incident at Cycling Race:** A severe accident during a youth cycling event in Kijewo Królewskie resulted in 19 injuries. Emergency services, including Poland’s Medical Air Rescue (LPR), were deployed to the scene to provide critical care.
* **Agricultural Sabotage in Gliwice:** Local authorities have initiated legal proceedings against a farmer who destroyed municipal property by plowing up an asphalt road in Ostropa. The damages are estimated at 400,000 PLN, and prosecutors are moving to request his arrest.
* **Diplomatic Tensions over Aerial Incidents:** Bulgaria has summoned the Ukrainian ambassador following the crash of an unmanned aerial vehicle on its territory. The incident, involving a drone identified as a decoy, has sparked an international investigation into the navigation and operational control of the device.
