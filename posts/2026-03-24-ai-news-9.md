## WORLD

* **Escalation in the Middle East:** Israel has reported significant missile damage in Tel Aviv as the conflict with Iran continues to intensify. Despite reports of diplomatic overtures from President Trump, the situation remains volatile, with Israel maintaining its military campaign against Hezbollah in Lebanon.
* **Geopolitical Posturing:** North Korean leader Kim Jong-un has utilized the ongoing Iran-Israel conflict to bolster his domestic narrative, arguing that an expanded nuclear arsenal is essential for shielding his nation from perceived American hostility.
* **Diplomatic Friction:** Tensions have surfaced between the U.S. administration and the United Kingdom, as President Trump expressed open dissatisfaction with Prime Minister Keir Starmer’s refusal to participate in direct military actions against Iran.

## POLAND

* **Judicial Ruling on 'Neojudges':** The European Court of Justice (ECJ) has issued a significant ruling concerning the status of Polish judges appointed after the 2017 judicial reforms. The court clarified that mere procedural irregularities in a judge's appointment are insufficient to automatically invalidate their independence, mandating a case-by-case analysis of appointment circumstances.
* **Infrastructure and Public Safety Concerns:** Emergency services are responding to a tragic fire in the Greater Poland (Wielkopolska) region, which resulted in the deaths of two children. Concurrently, rail safety is under scrutiny following a dangerous incident near Gniezno, where two passenger trains were directed onto the same track, narrowly avoiding a collision.
* **Energy Market Adjustments:** State-run energy giant Orlen has announced a reduction in wholesale fuel prices, with both petrol and diesel seeing price decreases. This adjustment follows ongoing market fluctuations and is expected to have an immediate impact on consumer fuel costs across the country.
