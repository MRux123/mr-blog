## WORLD

* **Israel Rejects Trump’s Gaza Peace Initiative:** The Israeli government has formally declined a 15-point peace plan proposed by Donald Trump. Prime Minister Benjamin Netanyahu cited concerns over the demilitarization process, stating that Israel will not withdraw until Hamas is fully dismantled, fearing the group intends to use the lull to rebuild its military capabilities.
* **Russia Secures New Military Status in Syria:** Following 18 months of intensive diplomatic negotiations, Syria and Russia have reached an agreement on the restructuring of Russian military presence within the country. Syrian officials have described this as the beginning of a "new phase" in bilateral relations, solidifying Moscow's long-term strategic footprint in the region.
* **Escalating Tensions in the Strait of Hormuz:** Iran is intensifying its pressure on Washington, utilizing its control over the Strait of Hormuz as leverage to demand the release of frozen assets and the lifting of economic sanctions. The standoff remains a critical focal point for global energy security and naval stability.

## POLAND

* **Record-Breaking Military Parade:** Poland is set to host the largest military parade in its history. The display of advanced hardware, including Abrams tanks, Rosomak vehicles, and the newly acquired F-35 fighter jets, signals a significant strengthening of the nation's defense posture amidst regional instability.
* **Public Sentiment Toward the Current Government:** A new survey conducted for Wirtualna Polska highlights growing domestic dissatisfaction, with 58% of respondents expressing a negative view of the coalition government led by Prime Minister Donald Tusk. Only 35.9% of those polled provided a positive assessment of the administration's performance to date.
