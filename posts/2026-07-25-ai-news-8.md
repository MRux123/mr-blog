## WORLD

*   **Escalating Wildfires in Southern Europe:** A series of devastating wildfires continues to ravage Southern Europe, particularly in Spain and France. With tens of thousands evacuated and vast swathes of land destroyed, the international response is intensifying. Notably, Polish firefighters have been deployed to the Iberian Peninsula to assist local crews in containing the spreading inferno.
*   **Regional Security Concerns in Romania:** Tensions in Eastern Europe remain high following reports of another drone, suspected to be a Russian-made Shahed, being intercepted over Romanian territory. This incident raises further questions regarding the spillover risks of the ongoing conflict in Ukraine into NATO member states.

## POLAND

*   **Political Clash Over Economic Policy:** Prime Minister Donald Tusk has publicly criticized Karol Nawrocki’s decision to refer the legislation regarding windfall taxes on fuel companies to the Constitutional Tribunal. The Prime Minister labeled the move "shocking," framing the issue as a matter of consumer interest during his public addresses.
*   **Healthcare System Reforms:** The Ministry of Health has unveiled a draft amendment aimed at restructuring compensation for medical professionals. The proposal sets significant new benchmarks, including a maximum hourly rate of 240 PLN gross and a monthly cap of 76,800 PLN gross, while also seeking to expand the supervisory and regulatory powers of the National Health Fund (NFZ).
*   **Controversial Changes in Hungarian Judiciary:** Concerns have been raised by Amnesty International regarding the rapid appointment process for a new judge in the Hungarian Constitutional Tribunal. The organization characterizes the pace of the selection, scheduled for this coming Monday, as an "astonishing rush" that threatens to cement a one-sided influence on the court.
