## WORLD

*   **President Trump Highlights Internal Ideological Threats:** On the eve of the 250th anniversary of American independence, President Donald Trump delivered a significant address at Mount Rushmore. He framed communism as the most profound threat to U.S. freedom, asserting that its internal danger surpasses that of historic global conflicts, including World War II and the 9/11 terrorist attacks.
*   **Escalating Tensions in Eastern Ukraine:** The frontline in the Russia-Ukraine conflict has seen a critical development as Russian forces reportedly seized control of the strategic town of Kostyantynivka. While Moscow claims a significant tactical gain, the Ukrainian military has yet to issue an official comment on the status of the settlement.
*   **U.S.-China Diplomatic Friction:** Despite a period of relative stabilization, relations between Beijing and Canberra have hit a new hurdle. The Chinese ambassador to Australia has issued a stark warning regarding a "Cold War mentality," signaling renewed diplomatic pressure despite recent efforts to thaw bilateral tensions.

## POLAND

*   **Political Stance on EU Integration:** Jarosław Kaczyński, leader of the Law and Justice (PiS) party, has issued a formal declaration concerning the future of Ukraine's European Union aspirations. Kaczyński stated that his party would block Ukraine’s accession to the EU as long as the country maintains what he describes as a "cult of Bandera" and the glorification of UPA/OUN forces, further criticizing the current government's policy toward Kyiv.
*   **Tragic Accident in Pomerania:** A devastating road accident occurred near Sztum in the Pomeranian Voivodeship. A vehicle carrying four individuals, including a 12-year-old girl, veered off the road and rolled over, resulting in the death of all occupants. Local authorities are currently investigating the circumstances surrounding the 22-year-old driver's loss of control.
