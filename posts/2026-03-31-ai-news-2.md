## WORLD

* **Escalation in the Persian Gulf:** Tensions between the United States and Iran have reached a critical point following an attack on a Kuwaiti oil tanker near Dubai. The incident, occurring just a day after President Trump threatened to target Iranian infrastructure, has raised fears of a regional conflict and potential disruptions to global oil supplies.
* **Australia’s Social Media Crackdown:** Regulators in Australia have issued a stern warning to major technology firms, including Meta, Snap, and TikTok, regarding their compliance with the nation's under-16 social media ban. The eSafety Commissioner cited significant concerns over the efficacy of current enforcement mechanisms, signaling a tightening of digital oversight.

## POLAND

* **Diplomatic Controversy in Hungary:** Polish media is closely tracking the fallout from a "Freudian slip" by Hungarian Foreign Minister Peter Szijjarto, who suggested Hungary should exist outside the European Union. The remark has drawn sharp criticism from Peter Magyar, the leader of the opposition Tisza party, who reaffirmed that Hungary’s strategic future remains firmly anchored in the EU and NATO.
