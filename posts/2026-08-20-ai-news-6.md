## WORLD

* **Escalation in Ukraine and Regional Threats:** Russia has launched a massive missile offensive targeting Kyiv and surrounding regions, resulting in at least eight fatalities. The intensity of these strikes has triggered security concerns beyond Ukraine's borders, as an unauthorized drone reportedly entered NATO airspace before crashing in Romania.
* **Donald Trump Threatens Iran:** Former U.S. President Donald Trump has issued a stern warning to Iran, announcing plans for economic isolation "on an unprecedented scale." Trump emphasized that Iran has failed to pursue peace, threatening severe economic consequences not only for Tehran but also for nations that continue to provide support to the Iranian regime.
* **Evergrande Founder Sentenced:** In a significant development for the Chinese property market, the founder of the collapsed real estate giant Evergrande has been sentenced to life in prison. The ruling marks a pivotal moment in the ongoing fallout of the company’s insolvency, which continues to impact China's economic stability.

## POLAND

* **Military Response to Nighttime Strikes:** The Polish Armed Forces scrambled fighter jets in response to the Russian aerial assault on Ukraine. The operation was coordinated with national security protocols, including the issuance of emergency RCB (Government Security Centre) alerts to residents in the Lublin and Podkarpackie regions as a precautionary measure against potential border violations.
* **Ongoing Investigation into Tragedy in Hungary:** Authorities are examining the causes of a fatal bus accident involving Polish citizens in Hungary. The investigation is currently focused on witness accounts from secondary drivers to determine the precise sequence of events leading to the tragedy.
