## WORLD

* **Widening Middle East Conflict:** Diplomatic efforts face severe strain as Iran prepares for talks with Gulf Arab states amidst renewed aggression. The situation in the Strait of Hormuz remains highly volatile following targeted ship attacks, which have effectively derailed previous peace negotiations involving the Trump administration.
* **AI Ethics and Human Safety:** Leading experts, including researchers formerly with Anthropic, have voiced grave concerns regarding the trajectory of artificial intelligence. The internal alarms come as the firm’s leadership formally requests a deceleration in development, citing potential existential risks to humanity.
* **Economic Fallout of Trade Policies:** New U.S. tariffs on Canadian steel—reaching 50 percent—are causing significant industrial distress in Ontario. The move is threatening long-standing cross-border economic ties and triggering mass layoffs in twin town economies.

## POLAND

* **Systemic Attacks Near the Border:** Following a wave of Russian drone strikes near the Polish-Ukrainian border, which included an attack on a train running the Kyiv-Warsaw route, the Polish government has convened an emergency security meeting. Prime Minister Donald Tusk has warned that the coming months will likely see an intensification of hostile actions.
* **Diplomatic Response to Escalation:** Foreign Minister Radosław Sikorski, speaking from Kyiv, emphasized the inseparable nature of Polish and Ukrainian security. The recent targeting of transport infrastructure near the Dorohusk-Jagodzin border crossing is being framed by Polish authorities as a definitive moment for regional security policy.
