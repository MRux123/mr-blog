## WORLD

* **U.S. Escalates Military Pressure on Iran:** Following statements by former President Trump regarding the slow pace of diplomatic negotiations, the United States has launched renewed strikes against Iran. U.S. Secretary of Defense Pete Hegseth signaled a decisive shift in strategy, stating that the administration is prepared to use military force as a primary tool for "negotiation" to target critical infrastructure.
* **Paleontological Discovery in the Indian Ocean:** Researchers have uncovered a massive "whale graveyard" estimated to be five million years old. Experts describe the site as unprecedented in scale, offering a significant window into marine life history that exceeds all previous scientific expectations.

## POLAND

* **Papal Visit to Barcelona Concludes with Historic Blessing:** Pope Leo XIV has officially blessed the Tower of Christ at the Sagrada Família. The ceremony, which marked the fulfillment of architect Antoni Gaudí’s vision exactly one century after his death, served as a platform for the Pontiff to address both the regional unity of Catalonia and the broader global impact of ongoing conflicts. Polish media have closely tracked the visit, highlighting the Pope's calls for concord during this significant moment in ecclesiastical history.
