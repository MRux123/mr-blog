## WORLD

*   **Laos cave rescue operation:** Search and rescue teams in Laos are intensifying efforts to locate two individuals who remain trapped in a flooded cave system. While five survivors have been successfully extracted, the operation remains critical as authorities work under difficult, high-risk conditions to reach the final two missing men.

## POLAND

*   **Emergency medical evacuation to China:** A specialized medical transport flight is being mobilized to China as part of an urgent, high-stakes mission to save the life of a 24-year-old Polish citizen. Authorities are treating the situation as a race against time, coordinating international logistics to provide necessary medical intervention.
*   **Severe weather impact in Opole:** Meteorological experts are currently assessing the structural damage and atmospheric intensity following a powerful tornado that struck the Opole region. Local authorities are focusing on recovery efforts and analyzing the strength of the phenomenon.
*   **Legal proceedings against former cleric:** Prosecutors have announced an additional, severe charge against a former parish priest from the Tarczyn municipality. The suspect, accused of a violent assault involving an axe and arson, now faces potential life imprisonment as the investigation into his actions reaches its final stages.
