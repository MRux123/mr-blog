## WORLD

*   **Macron initiates debate on colonial reparations:** French President Emmanuel Macron has officially acknowledged the necessity of addressing the historical legacies of slavery and the potential for reparations. While stopping short of providing specific policy proposals, Macron emphasized that the issue is one that France can no longer ignore, marking a significant shift in the discourse surrounding the country's colonial past.
*   **Rising tensions between Havana and Washington:** The diplomatic friction between the U.S. and Cuba has intensified following remarks by Marco Rubio, who labeled the island nation a threat to American interests. The Cuban government has retaliated by accusing Rubio of inflammatory rhetoric aimed at justifying potential military aggression.

## POLAND

*   **Strengthening of US-Poland defense ties:** U.S. President Donald Trump has announced the deployment of an additional 5,000 troops to Poland. The decision follows the recent electoral victory of Polish President Karol Nawrocki, signaling a deepening of the strategic military partnership between Warsaw and Washington. Specific logistics regarding the troop movement have yet to be disclosed.
