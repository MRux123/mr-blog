## WORLD

*   **Escalation and Diplomacy in the Iran Conflict:** The ongoing military confrontation between the U.S. and Iran has entered its sixth day. While the intensity of attacks continues to escalate, both parties have signaled a potential willingness to engage in diplomatic negotiations to de-escalate the conflict.
*   **Russian Elite Capital Flight:** Faced with a deteriorating domestic economy and fears of state-led asset seizures, prominent Russian oligarchs—including several close to Vladimir Putin—have moved billions of dollars in capital abroad over the past year, increasingly targeting investments in the Persian Gulf region.
*   **UK Aid Policy Shift:** The British Foreign Office has confirmed significant cuts to bilateral support for various African nations, with some regions facing reductions of up to 90%. The move has sparked criticism regarding the UK's long-term strategic presence and humanitarian commitments on the international stage.

## POLAND

*   **Fresh Developments in the Wieczorek Case:** The public prosecutor's office has confirmed the discovery of a new, significant piece of evidence regarding the long-standing disappearance of Iwona Wieczorek. Officials state the finding is tied directly to the location and timeline of the event, with investigators currently testing multiple new theories.
*   **Irregularities in MFA Funding:** The Ministry of Foreign Affairs has suspended future tranches of grants for the "Polonia Connect" association following reports of potential irregularities. The controversy centers on the allocation of approximately 2 million PLN to an organization that had undergone a recent name change and possessed limited experience in the projects it was funded to execute.
*   **Health Disinformation Crisis:** A concerning trend of medical disinformation is spreading rapidly across Polish digital platforms, accumulating over 6 billion views. The scale of the phenomenon highlights an urgent challenge for public health authorities in addressing the proliferation of false narratives online.
