## WORLD

*   **Escalating Middle East Instability:** The geopolitical situation in the Middle East has reached a critical juncture. The United States has conducted its second series of strikes against Iranian targets in three days, focusing on drone infrastructure in an effort to contain regional aggression. Simultaneously, the Israeli military has issued broad evacuation orders for southern Lebanon, designating areas south of the Zahrani River as active combat zones, signaling a potential intensification of the conflict with Hezbollah.
*   **Health and Conflict Crisis in DRC:** The World Health Organization has sounded the alarm on a "catastrophic collision" of the ongoing Ebola outbreak and armed conflict in the Democratic Republic of the Congo. WHO Director-General Tedros Adhanom Ghebreyesus warned that the epidemic is currently outpacing response efforts, prompting urgent calls for a ceasefire to allow humanitarian aid to reach the affected regions.

## POLAND

*   **Diplomatic Friction Over Security Policy:** Tensions between the government and the President’s Office have flared, with Deputy Prime Minister and Minister of National Defence, Władysław Kosiniak-Kamysz, publicly criticizing the Kancelaria’s interference. The dispute centers on the lack of communication regarding a significant defense treaty with the United Kingdom, highlighting a deepening rift regarding national security decision-making processes.
*   **Russian Battlefield Casualties:** In a significant intelligence update, British officials have estimated that Russia has suffered nearly 500,000 casualties since the beginning of the full-scale invasion of Ukraine. This assessment represents one of the most stark confirmations of the scale of attrition facing Russian forces to date.
