## WORLD

* **Escalating Conflict in the Strait of Hormuz:** Tensions in the Middle East have intensified following a deadly attack on a Saudi tanker in the Strait of Hormuz. Saudi authorities confirmed the incident, which resulted in the deaths of two Filipino sailors, and have condemned the aggression as a direct threat to regional stability.
* **Geopolitical Posturing on the Ukraine-Russia Conflict:** U.S. President Donald Trump has addressed the possibility of high-level diplomatic talks with Vladimir Putin, stating that he remains open to a meeting only when both Ukraine and Russia are prepared to negotiate a formal peace treaty. Trump noted a "new energy" in ongoing diplomatic discussions regarding the conflict.
* **Justice System Controversy in Malta:** A Maltese tycoon has been acquitted of orchestrating the 2017 car bomb murder of investigative journalist Daphne Caruana Galizia. The verdict has sparked significant international scrutiny regarding the effectiveness of judicial proceedings in cases involving high-profile corruption.

## POLAND

* **Environmental Crisis on the Vistula:** The Institute of Meteorology and Water Management (IMGW) has issued a critical warning as water levels in the Vistula River in Warsaw have reached record-breaking lows. The situation highlights growing concerns over regional water management and the impact of prolonged drought conditions.
* **Legislative Shift in Housing Regulations:** The Polish Parliament is currently reviewing a legislative project that would grant residential communities the authority to prohibit short-term apartment rentals. If passed, the law could fundamentally reshape the local tourism and housing markets in major cities.
