## WORLD

*   **NATO Unveils Defensive Strategy Against Russian Aggression:** Amid a significant rise in sabotage, cyberattacks, and arson targeting European nations aligned with Ukraine, NATO has released a comprehensive blueprint aimed at deterring further Kremlin-backed hostilities. The plan underscores the alliance's commitment to shielding member states from the expanding scope of hybrid warfare.
*   **Continued Strikes on Ukrainian Energy Grid:** Despite reports of a potential "energy ceasefire" following discussions involving the US, Ukrainian officials report that Russia has continued to strike energy infrastructure. This discrepancy highlights the fragility of diplomatic efforts and the ongoing intensity of the conflict.
*   **Saudi Arabia Under Escalating Pressure:** The Saudi government has vowed a "serious response" following a series of Houthi drone and missile attacks on critical infrastructure, including the Riyadh international airport. The incident marks a significant escalation in regional instability, with Iran offering to mediate in the aftermath.

## POLAND

*   **US Pressure on Ukraine Regarding Energy Targets:** Reports indicate that the current US administration has issued firm warnings to Ukrainian officials, demanding an immediate cessation of strikes on Russian oil refineries. This development adds a new layer of complexity to the geopolitical landscape, suggesting a strategic shift in Washington's approach to the ongoing war.
*   **Retailers Pull Halloween Items Amid Violence Concerns:** Major retail chains in Poland, including Biedronka and Smyk, have removed Halloween-themed accessories from their shelves. The decision, prompted by public concern, follows a recent surge in knife-related attacks within schools, leading to a broader debate on youth safety and cultural influence.
*   **Expansion of Toll Road Network:** The Polish government has announced plans to significantly increase the number of toll-paying roads by 2027. The project aims to expand the e-TOLL system to approximately 6,750 km, with the generated revenue directed toward the National Road Fund to support infrastructure development.
