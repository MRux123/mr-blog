## WORLD

*   **US-Iran Diplomatic Thaw:** Negotiations regarding a potential peace deal between the United States and Iran have entered a critical phase. While both sides have reportedly reached an agreement in principle concerning the reopening of the Strait of Hormuz and the disposal of Iran's highly enriched uranium, officials warn that a formal signing may still take days to finalize. The prospect of easing tensions has already triggered a downward trend in global oil prices.
*   **A.I. Governance in Focus:** Pope Leo XIV has issued a historic encyclical addressing the ethical implications of artificial intelligence in the modern world. This intervention marks a significant moment in the intersection of traditional religious doctrine and rapidly evolving technological advancements, highlighting the church's concern over the societal impact of AI.
*   **Security Incident Near Russian Border:** Tensions in Northern Europe have spiked following reports that a British Royal Air Force plane, carrying Defense Secretary John Healey, experienced deliberate GPS signal interference while flying near the Russian border. Intelligence sources suggest Russian involvement in what is being described as an electronic warfare incident.

## POLAND

*   **Political Destabilization and Security Threats:** Polish authorities are investigating a surge of 18 coordinated, false alarms targeting the residences of individuals associated with the parliamentary opposition. The Central Bureau for Combatting Cybercrime is handling the investigation, with initial findings suggesting a centralized orchestration of these provocations.
*   **Referendum Fallout in Local Governance:** Following the successful recall of Krakow’s mayor, Aleksandra Miszalski, the political landscape in Poland's major cities faces further instability. In Rzeszów, City Councilor Jacek Strojny has formally proposed a referendum to oust Mayor Konrad Fijołek, signaling a potentially growing trend of local government challenges across the country.
*   **Judicial and Financial Scandals:** The Polish legal system faces ongoing scrutiny regarding the European Arrest Warrant (ENA) for former minister Zbigniew Ziobro, with prosecutors moving to replace the presiding judge. Simultaneously, the financial sector is reeling from a massive fraud case involving a high-ranking bank official, whose alleged pyramid scheme caused nearly 9 million PLN in losses to clients.
