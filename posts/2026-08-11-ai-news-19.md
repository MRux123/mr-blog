## WORLD

* **US-Russia Diplomatic Development:** Former President Donald Trump has announced the release of American citizen Robert Gilman from Russian custody. Trump attributed the breakthrough to a direct phone conversation with Vladimir Putin, citing humanitarian grounds for the release of the former soldier, who is reportedly in need of medical treatment.
* **Escalating Climate Crisis in the UK:** An extreme weather alert has been issued for England, where temperatures are expected to reach 38 degrees Celsius. The nation is currently grappling with a historic drought, exacerbating concerns over infrastructure stability and public health.
* **Maritime Security Concerns:** Military forces in Romania have responded to the presence of Russian drones near a key natural gas platform. The incident highlights the growing tension and potential for escalation in the Black Sea region.

## POLAND

* **Labor Market Dependence:** Deputy Interior Minister Maciej Duszczyk has emphasized the critical role of Ukrainian workers in the Polish economy. Addressing political discourse regarding the potential deportation of military-age Ukrainian men, Duszczyk warned that their mass departure would lead to immediate economic stagnation.
* **Information Warfare and Public Perception:** Ahead of upcoming military parades in Warsaw, authorities are combatting a surge in disinformation. Manipulated footage showing military movements in the capital is being used by hostile actors to spread false narratives suggesting that Poland is preparing for an imminent war.
* **Church Appointments:** The Vatican has announced the appointment of Krakow Metropolitan Archbishop Cardinal Grzegorz Ryś to the Council for the Economy, a specialized body established by Pope Francis to oversee the administrative and financial governance of the Holy See.
