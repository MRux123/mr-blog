## WORLD

*   **Escalating Conflict in the Middle East:** A high-ranking Lebanese general was killed during an Israeli military strike on a vehicle in southern Lebanon. The Israeli military has initiated an investigation into the incident, which underscores the deepening regional instability as tensions between Israel and Hezbollah continue to intensify.
*   **Volcanic Activity in Russia:** Authorities in Russia have issued a red alert for aviation due to the eruption of the Shiveluch volcano on the Kamchatka Peninsula. Concurrently, government officials have warned of the imminent risk of eruption from the Klyuchevskaya Sopka, considered the most powerful volcano in Eurasia.
*   **Diplomatic Stance of Belarus:** Amid ongoing geopolitical shifts, President Alexander Lukashenko issued a public address attempting to clarify his country's position regarding the conflict in Ukraine. While maintaining a strong alliance with Russia, Lukashenko explicitly stated that Belarus has no desire to engage in direct military conflict with Poland, Lithuania, or Ukraine.

## POLAND

*   **Political Controversy Over Presidential Honors:** MEP Anna Bryłka has formally appealed to the President of the European Parliament to revoke the European Order of Merit awarded to Volodymyr Zelensky. The motion follows the Ukrainian government's decision to name a military unit after UPA figures, a move that has sparked significant diplomatic friction and domestic debate regarding Polish-Ukrainian relations.
*   **Corruption Investigation in Gdańsk:** Central Anticorruption Bureau (CBA) agents have detained a municipal official from the Gdańsk Road Authority. The arrest, confirmed by the spokesperson for the Mayor of Gdańsk, is currently under investigation for suspected corrupt practices, highlighting ongoing scrutiny of local administrative processes.
*   **Aviation Incident in Krosno:** A glider crash occurred on Saturday at the airport in Krosno, Podkarpackie Voivodeship, after the aircraft collided with the facility's perimeter fence. Authorities have confirmed that the pilot survived without serious injury, and the incident is currently being reviewed by the State Commission for Aircraft Accident Investigation.
