## WORLD

*   **Escalating North American Trade Tensions:** The United States has intensified its economic pressure on Canada through the implementation of new tariffs. The move, intended to force trade concessions, is being met with firm resistance from Canadian officials, including Mark Carney, signaling a potential period of prolonged economic friction between the two major partners.
*   **France Moves to Restrict Social Media for Minors:** In a landmark legislative shift, France is preparing to implement strict regulations regarding social media access for children. The proposed law, which seeks to ban specific account types for individuals under 15, marks the first effort of its kind in Europe to address the mental health and digital safety concerns linked to social platform usage.
*   **Tragic Loss of British Politician:** Authorities have confirmed that Ann Widdecombe, a prominent British politician, has been murdered in her home. The investigation, supported by security footage, is currently focused on an intruder who targeted the victim during a home invasion.

## POLAND

*   **Rising Approval for President Nawrocki:** A recent CBOS survey highlights a significant shift in the domestic political landscape, with President Karol Nawrocki reaching a new peak in public approval, now sitting at 56%. This contrasts sharply with the lower approval ratings currently reported for the Sejm and the Senate.
*   **Tightening Tobacco Regulations:** The Polish government has officially adopted a project aimed at increasing excise taxes on e-cigarettes and vaping products. The policy is a strategic attempt to curb consumption, specifically targeting the younger demographic, with significant price hikes planned for both devices and refill liquids.
*   **Severe Weather Devastates Infrastructure:** The southern region of Poland is grappling with the aftermath of intense storms and a tornado that swept through the Osiek municipality. Preliminary damage assessments estimate losses exceeding one million PLN, with several residential buildings rendered uninhabitable.
