## WORLD

* **Netanyahu Defies Diplomatic Boycott at UN:** Israeli Prime Minister Benjamin Netanyahu delivered a combative address to the UN General Assembly, staunchly defending ongoing military operations in the Middle East. The speech was marked by high tensions, as dozens of delegates walked out in protest. Netanyahu responded by labeling those who exited as "moral cowards," underscoring a deepening divide between Israel and significant portions of the international diplomatic community.
* **Historic US-China Breakthrough:** In a development likely to reshape global trade and political dynamics, Chinese President Xi Jinping has announced a new agreement with Donald Trump. Terming the pact a "historic moment," the announcement signals a potential shift in the volatile US-China relationship, though the specifics of the deal remain under close scrutiny by international observers.

## POLAND

* **Political Friction Over Foreign Aid:** The German Bundestag witnessed a heated debate as the AfD party officially demanded an immediate cessation of all military and financial aid to Ukraine. The proposal has sparked intense political controversy, highlighting the growing domestic pressure within Germany regarding its role in the ongoing conflict.
* **Security Alerts Amid Regional Escalation:** Poland has maintained a state of high alert following a recent surge in Russian aerial attacks on Ukraine. The Government Security Centre (RCB) issued emergency alerts to the population, while the Polish military took precautionary measures, including the scramble and subsequent return of fighter jets to bases, reflecting the persistent security threats posed by the war on its eastern border.
