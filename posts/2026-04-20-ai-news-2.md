## WORLD

* **US Seizes Iranian Cargo Ship:** In a significant escalation of maritime tensions, the United States has intercepted and seized an Iranian-flagged cargo vessel. Tehran has vehemently condemned the move, labeling it "armed piracy," and has issued warnings of imminent retaliation. The incident occurs at a delicate time, as both nations were preparing for a new round of diplomatic negotiations.

* **High-Stakes Arrest in Los Angeles:** Federal authorities have arrested a 44-year-old Iranian-American woman at Los Angeles International Airport on charges of arms trafficking. Prosecutors allege that the suspect was operating on behalf of the Iranian government, facilitating the illegal transfer of weaponry to contacts in Africa, specifically Sudan. The arrest underscores growing concerns regarding Iranian clandestine activities and global security networks.
