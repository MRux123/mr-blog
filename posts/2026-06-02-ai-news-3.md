## WORLD

* **Large-Scale Russian Assault on Ukrainian Cities:** A coordinated, massive missile and drone attack targeted multiple Ukrainian urban centers, including Kyiv, Dnipro, and Kharkiv. Reports confirm at least nine fatalities, with significant destruction to residential infrastructure. Kyiv’s Mayor, Vitali Klitschko, indicated that emergency services are responding to fires in high-rise buildings, with grave concerns that civilians remain trapped under the debris.

## POLAND

* **Military Response to Regional Instability:** In direct response to the intensive aerial bombardment of Ukraine, the Polish Armed Forces (Operational Command) scrambled fighter jets during the night to secure national airspace. The move reflects heightened regional security protocols aimed at preventing unauthorized border incursions during escalated conflict.
* **Emergency Operations in Płock:** Local authorities are currently managing a major industrial fire involving a large warehouse facility in Płock. The incident has resulted in a massive plume of smoke visible across the city, prompting an immediate emergency response to contain the blaze and mitigate potential environmental or public safety hazards.
