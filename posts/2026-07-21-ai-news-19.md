## WORLD

* **Escalating Trade Conflict:** The North American trade landscape faces significant instability as the U.S. administration moves to impose 50% tariffs on Canada. Canadian officials, led by Mark Carney, are evaluating a broad range of countermeasures in response to what is being described as a major escalation in bilateral trade tensions.
* **Geopolitical Posturing:** Tensions in the Middle East have intensified following reports of increased rhetoric from the U.S. regarding Iran. Concerns are mounting as the administration considers potential strikes against Iranian uranium stockpiles, signaling a significant shift in regional containment strategies.
* **Social Unrest in South Africa:** The nation is currently grappling with a surge in xenophobic sentiment, marked by organized campaigns to expel foreign nationals. While activists engage in extra-legal actions, analysts warn that the movement is largely driven by persistent economic myths that fail to align with empirical data regarding the impact of immigration.

## POLAND

* **Political Internal Strife:** Former Prime Minister Mateusz Morawiecki has publicly addressed internal tensions within the Law and Justice (PiS) party. His comments, directed at "intriguers" within the party ranks, highlight growing fragmentation and a struggle for influence following the party's recent electoral challenges.
* **Academic Loss:** The Polish intellectual and academic community is mourning the death of Rev. Prof. Andrzej Szostek, a distinguished philosopher and former Rector of the Catholic University of Lublin (KUL). His legacy remains a cornerstone of Polish academic and ethical discourse.
