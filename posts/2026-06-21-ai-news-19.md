## WORLD

*   **US-Iran Nuclear Talks Strained by Conflicting Messages:** Negotiations in Switzerland have hit an immediate impasse as the U.S. administration displays internal division. While Vice President JD Vance attempts to pursue diplomatic channels, President Trump has renewed threats of military action against Iran, demanding they restrain Hezbollah. Iran has responded by hardening its position, conditioning further progress on an immediate cessation of the war in Lebanon.
*   **Ethiopian Election Sparks Landslide Victory Amid Instability:** Prime Minister Abiy Ahmed’s party has secured a massive majority in the recent elections. Despite the electoral success, the result arrives against a backdrop of growing internal unrest and persistent regional tensions, raising concerns regarding the country’s future stability.

## POLAND

*   **Diplomatic Fallout Over Presidential Decision:** Tensions between Poland and Ukraine have intensified following President Karol Nawrocki’s decision to revoke the Order of the White Eagle awarded to Volodymyr Zelensky. The move has drawn criticism from domestic political circles, with the PSL party labeling the diplomatic friction a potential long-term strategic loss for Poland, while Russian officials have begun exploiting the rift in their state media.
*   **Severe Weather Disrupts Infrastructure:** Large swathes of Poland continue to face extreme weather conditions. Recent storms have resulted in over a thousand emergency interventions, with significant damage reported to educational facilities in the West Pomeranian Voivodeship. The national weather service (IMGW) maintains high-alert warnings for heatwaves and further storms.
*   **Controversy Over Hospital Management:** A public inquiry has been sparked by reports from a Tomaszów Lubelski hospital, where the director—also a local councilor—allegedly earned 864,000 PLN annually while maintaining an intensive clinical workload of 300 hours per month. The case has fueled public debate regarding the ethics of dual professional and political roles in the public health sector.
