## WORLD

* **Escalation in the Strait of Hormuz:** President Trump has issued stern warnings to Iranian leadership, threatening military strikes on power plants if the Strait of Hormuz is not fully reopened. This geopolitical standoff has intensified, with calls from some quarters for NATO involvement in securing the critical waterway, sparking heated political debates.
* **Deadly Russian Strike on Odesa:** A massive drone attack targeted residential districts in Odesa, resulting in the deaths of three individuals, including a child. At least 10 others were reported injured, with two in critical condition, marking a brutal intensification of hostilities during the holiday period.

## POLAND

* **Holiday Traffic Carnage:** Police data released following the Easter weekend reveals a grim statistic: 16 people died and 189 were injured in road accidents since Friday. Authorities have clamped down on traffic violations, detaining over 800 intoxicated drivers in a concentrated effort to restore road safety.
* **Severe Weather Warnings:** The Institute of Meteorology and Water Management (IMGW) has issued high-alert warnings for twelve provinces across Poland. Strong winds, reaching up to 95 km/h, are expected to persist, posing significant hazards particularly along the coastline.
* **Bizarre Discovery in Germany:** A routine Easter egg hunt in Vaihingen an der Enz turned into a major security operation after residents discovered a container labeled "Polonium-210." Specialized units were dispatched to handle the hazardous material, highlighting a rare and potentially dangerous incident.
