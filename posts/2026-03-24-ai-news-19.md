## WORLD

* **Middle East Conflict Spiral:** The conflict involving Iran has intensified, with Tehran launching missile barrages targeting Tel Aviv and Iraqi Kurdish regions. In a significant escalation, an Iranian strike hit Emirati military forces in Bahrain. The volatility has destabilized global energy markets, with Asia facing severe fuel shortages and rising prices, while the U.S. continues to engage in complex diplomatic efforts to prevent total regional war.
* **Largest Drone Attack on Ukraine:** Russia has executed its most massive aerial assault on Ukraine to date, deploying 948 drones over a 24-hour period. The widespread strikes have severely damaged civilian infrastructure and residential areas across the country, further straining Ukraine's defense capabilities and highlighting the brutal trajectory of the ongoing war.
* **Political Setback for Meloni:** Italian Prime Minister Giorgia Meloni’s administration faces a period of instability following a significant referendum defeat. The result has challenged the perception of her government’s invulnerability and placed her proposed judicial overhaul in jeopardy.

## POLAND

* **Massive Russian Attacks Impact Ukraine:** The recent escalation in drone warfare by Russia has had direct humanitarian consequences, with reports of maternity hospitals being damaged and civilians forced to seek shelter during childbirth. These events have drawn immediate attention from the Polish government and international observers regarding the security of Ukraine's urban centers.
* **Legal and Political Controversy:** Domestic tensions have risen following the sentencing of Adam Borowski, a veteran anti-communist opposition figure, to six months in prison for the defamation of MP Roman Giertych. Jarosław Kaczyński has labeled the court’s decision as a politically motivated attack on democratic discourse, signaling a deepening divide within the Polish political landscape.
* **Diplomatic Presence in Washington:** Polish First Lady Marta Nawrocka has joined an international summit in Washington D.C., hosted by Melania Trump. The delegation is focused on child safety in the digital age, with the summit gathering several first ladies to coordinate policy responses to online threats.
