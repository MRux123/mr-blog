## WORLD

*   **Escalating Crisis in the Strait of Hormuz:** Regional security is deteriorating rapidly as Iran’s Revolutionary Guards have declared the Strait of Hormuz closed until the U.S. lifts its blockade on Iranian ports. The situation has intensified following reports of direct attacks on two vessels attempting to navigate the critical maritime chokepoint.
*   **Bulgaria’s Eighth Election in Five Years:** Bulgaria heads to the polls once again in a desperate attempt to break years of political deadlock. Voters are expressing profound frustration, seeking a path toward the economic prosperity and stability enjoyed by their European peers, as the nation struggles to find a sustainable governing coalition.

## POLAND

*   **Tragic Loss of Renowned Physician:** The medical community and the public are in mourning following the death of Dr. Jerzy Bentkowski, a respected orthopedic surgeon and medical director at the Lesko County Hospital. Dr. Bentkowski was fatally struck by a vehicle while cycling in Paszowa on Easter; the driver is now facing formal charges.
*   **A Living Monument to the Warsaw Ghetto:** In a poignant reflection on history, Krystyna Budnicka, one of the last remaining survivors of the Warsaw Ghetto Uprising, has shared harrowing accounts of her survival. As an ambassador for the "Żonkile" (Daffodils) campaign, her testimony serves as a vital reminder of the horrors of the Holocaust and the enduring duty to preserve the memory of the victims.
