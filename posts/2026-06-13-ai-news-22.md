## WORLD

*   **US-Iran Peace Deal Uncertainties:** Discrepancies have emerged between Washington and Tehran regarding the timeline for a potential peace agreement. While President Trump publicly announced that a deal is set to be signed this Sunday, Iranian officials have cast doubt on this schedule, stating that no final date has been established and suggesting that an agreement is likely to be finalized in the coming days.
*   **Global Oil Market Alert:** CNN analysts have issued a warning regarding the stability of the global oil market. Data indicates that crude oil reserves at the critical Cushing hub in Oklahoma have fallen to dangerously low levels. This supply contraction poses a significant risk of sudden, sharp increases in fuel prices worldwide, potentially destabilizing energy markets.

## POLAND

*   **Legal Status of Detained Journalist:** Deputy Minister of Justice Maria Ejchart has issued a clarification regarding the detention of journalist Leszek K. Responding to recent media reports, Ejchart confirmed that the individual has not been classified as a "dangerous prisoner," nor is he considered a high-risk threat to the internal security of the correctional facility.
