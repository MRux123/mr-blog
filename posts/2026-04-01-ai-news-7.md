## WORLD

* **Political Uncertainty in Ukraine:** Reports from media outlets, including Meduza, suggest that President Volodymyr Zelensky may be reconsidering his candidacy for the next presidential election. Speculation is mounting regarding potential successors, with Kyrylo Budanov, the former head of military intelligence, frequently mentioned as a prominent figure in these discussions.
* **Trump Signals Military Withdrawal:** U.S. President Donald Trump has indicated that the current American military campaign is approaching an end. The White House has announced that an "important update on Iran" is scheduled for Wednesday, fueling global anticipation regarding U.S. foreign policy shifts.

## POLAND

* **Institutional Conflict Over Judicial Appointments:** Political friction in Poland has intensified following President Karol Nawrocki’s decision to accept the oath of only two out of six judges selected by the Sejm for the Constitutional Tribunal. This move has drawn sharp criticism from coalition partners, with Minister of Infrastructure Dariusz Klimczak accusing the President of hypocrisy.
* **Domestic Political Landscape:** A new poll indicates that while the Civic Coalition (KO) would win the upcoming elections with 31.1% of the vote, the current governing coalition remains short of a majority. The parliamentary arithmetic suggests a deadlocked scenario between the government and the opposition bloc, highlighting the ongoing fragility of the current political balance.
