## WORLD

*   **US-Iran Peace Talks Collapse:** Diplomatic efforts to stabilize relations between the US and Iran have hit a standstill. President Trump has officially called off a planned trip to Pakistan by his envoys, following Tehran's refusal to engage in direct negotiations. While the administration downplayed the prospect of renewed military action, the breakdown marks a significant setback for regional de-escalation efforts.
*   **Climate Crisis Threatens Democratic Integrity:** A new analysis from The Guardian reveals a disturbing trend: environmental disasters are increasingly destabilizing democratic processes. Research indicates that climate-related events, including floods and wildfires, have disrupted at least 94 elections across 52 countries in the last two decades, suggesting that ecological instability is becoming a critical variable in global governance.
*   **Diplomatic Standoff in Mexico:** Tensions have emerged between the US and Mexico following a fatal car crash involving two American CIA operatives. The Mexican government has publicly clarified that the agents lacked the necessary authorization to conduct operations within its borders, highlighting ongoing friction regarding sovereignty and cross-border security cooperation.

## POLAND

*   **Severe Weather Disrupts National Infrastructure:** Polish authorities have issued urgent warnings as fierce gales sweep across the country. The Ministry of Interior and Administration has placed emergency services on high alert, following over 120 rescue interventions by the State Fire Service in a single day due to the damage caused by the high winds.
*   **Sporting Setback:** The national sporting community has been impacted by the withdrawal of Iga Świątek from her match in Madrid. The athlete has cited health-related reasons for her decision to step down, causing widespread concern among fans and sports analysts alike.
