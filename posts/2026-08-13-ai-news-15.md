## WORLD

*   **Ebola Outbreak Escalation:** The Ebola virus in the Democratic Republic of the Congo has officially spread to a sixth province, Bas-Uele. Health officials, including the leadership of the Africa CDC, have expressed grave concerns as the outbreak is on track to become the deadliest in the nation's history, threatening to surpass previous records of over 11,000 casualties.
*   **Controversy in the Central African Republic:** The French government has launched disciplinary proceedings against its ambassador to the CAR. Allegations suggest the diplomat hosted dozens of women at the official residence, raising significant security concerns at a time when Paris is attempting to diplomatically recalibrate its influence in the region against the backdrop of growing Russian presence.

## POLAND

*   **Tragic Assault in Jaworzno:** A brutal incident has shaken the local community after a 17-year-old was set on fire by three suspects during his own birthday celebration. The victim remains in a medically induced coma, and the local prosecutor's office has launched an investigation into attempted murder and incitement.
*   **Political Maneuvering Surrounding Marcin Romanowski:** Speculation regarding the whereabouts of former Deputy Minister of Justice Marcin Romanowski continues to mount. While rumors suggest he may be hiding in the breakaway, pro-Russian region of Transnistria, the Moldovan government has stated it has received no official request from Polish authorities regarding his presence in the territory.
*   **Legislative Developments:** President Karol Nawrocki has signed five new acts into law. The legislation spans critical areas, including the regulation of real estate acquisition by foreign nationals, accessibility for individuals with disabilities, and significant reforms aimed at advancing Poland's nuclear energy sector.
