## WORLD

*   **Political Instability in Paraguay:** President Santiago Peña has resigned following a major bank collapse and the initiation of impeachment proceedings by the lower house of Congress. The situation marks a significant period of volatility for the nation.
*   **Escalating Regional Violence in Turkey:** A massive manhunt is underway in western Turkey following a series of shootings that resulted in eight deaths. Authorities are currently engaged in a large-scale security operation.
*   **Diplomatic Shifts in Iran:** Iran has reportedly begun reviewing "concrete proposals" regarding its nuclear program. This development, facilitated by Qatari-led diplomatic efforts, suggests a potential de-escalation in tensions between Tehran and Washington.

## POLAND
