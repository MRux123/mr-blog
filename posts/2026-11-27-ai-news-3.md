## WORLD

*   **Escalation in Ukraine:** President Volodymyr Zelensky has issued an urgent warning, citing intelligence reports that Russia is preparing another massive missile strike targeting Ukraine's energy infrastructure and civilian facilities. Amidst this, Zelensky has expressed public frustration with the United States' focus on the Middle East, arguing that Washington is not applying sufficient pressure on Moscow and questioning the political rhetoric regarding Vladimir Putin.
*   **NATO-Russia Tensions:** Political analyst Vytautas Bruveris has characterized the current situation between Russia and NATO member states as an active "terrorist war." According to experts, the conflict is no longer confined to hybrid warfare but is expanding, signaling a dangerous shift in the security architecture of the region.

## POLAND

*   **New Diplomatic Initiative in Latin America:** Karol Nawrocki, currently visiting Chile, has signaled a strategic shift in Poland's foreign policy. Engaging in high-level talks with President Jose Antonio Kast, Nawrocki emphasized that Poland intends to deepen its presence in Latin America, focusing on sectors such as mining, geology, and astronomy to boost economic and investment cooperation.
*   **Domestic Infrastructure Incident:** Authorities in Opole are responding to a fire in a multi-family residential building. The incident, which has resulted in five injuries, highlights ongoing domestic safety challenges during the winter season.
