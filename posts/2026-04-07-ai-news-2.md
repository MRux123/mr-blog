## WORLD

* **Trump's Ultimatum to Iran:** U.S. President Donald Trump has issued a severe warning to Tehran, demanding the reopening of the Strait of Hormuz—a vital global energy artery—before a Tuesday deadline. Trump threatened that failure to reach an agreement would result in devastating military consequences, claiming the country could be "destroyed in one night."
* **Geopolitical Tensions:** The situation remains highly volatile as international observers see little room for a diplomatic breakthrough. The administration’s rhetoric suggests a shift toward a "total destruction" strategy if the Iranian government does not comply, effectively threatening to cripple the nation's infrastructure.
* **Relations with Russia:** During his briefing, President Trump also touched upon his stance on international powers, asserting that Russian President Vladimir Putin is not intimidated by NATO but holds a profound fear of the United States.

## POLAND

* **Domestic Reaction to Iran Crisis:** Polish media, including RMF24, are closely monitoring the escalation, emphasizing the gravity of President Trump's threat. Reports focus on the potential for a catastrophic conflict should the ultimatum expire without a resolution, highlighting the U.S. leader's rhetoric regarding Iran's infrastructure and the "return to the Stone Age" warning.
