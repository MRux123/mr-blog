## WORLD

* **Middle East Fragility:** Israel and Lebanon have moved to renew their cease-fire agreement. The U.S.-brokered pact aims to enforce a complete cessation of hostilities from Hezbollah. This development follows a period of renewed volatility in the region, where previous diplomatic efforts have struggled to hold.
* **Escalation in Crimea:** Tensions in the occupied territory of Crimea have intensified following accusations that Ukraine is responsible for a strike that resulted in at least four deaths and ten injuries. The situation remains volatile as Russian-backed officials report the impact of the attacks.
* **Humanitarian Crisis in DRC:** Eastern Democratic Republic of the Congo faces a dual crisis as rebel groups linked to the Islamic State continue deadly raids. The violence, which has claimed over 30 lives in recent days, is severely hampering critical efforts to contain an ongoing Ebola outbreak in the North Kivu region.

## POLAND

* **Tragedy in the Małopolska Region:** Search operations have concluded with the tragic discovery of a missing light helicopter in the Limanowa district. Authorities had lost contact with the aircraft during the early hours of the morning, leading to an extensive search and rescue mission.
* **Severe Weather Impacts:** The Silesian Voivodeship is reeling from intense storms and heavy rainfall that occurred overnight. Emergency services reported nearly 380 interventions, primarily in the Rybnik district, Ruda Śląska, and Jastrzębie-Zdrój, as citizens deal with widespread flooding of roads and residential properties.
* **Fatal Accident in Wielkopolska:** Local authorities are investigating the circumstances surrounding a fatal road accident in Osiek nad Notecią, where a vehicle plunged into a river, resulting in the death of a 23-year-old man.
