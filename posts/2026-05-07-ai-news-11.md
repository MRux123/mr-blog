## WORLD

* **UK Political Landscape at a Crossroads:** Prime Minister Keir Starmer’s Labour Party faces a critical test in today’s local and parliamentary elections. Analysts anticipate significant losses for the governing party, signaling a potential shift toward a multiparty era driven by gains from the anti-immigrant Reform U.K. party.
* **Geopolitical Realignment:** U.S. President Donald Trump is set to host Brazil’s President Luiz Inácio Lula da Silva at the White House. Despite a historically volatile relationship, the two leaders are expected to focus their talks on critical security, trade, and mineral resource cooperation.
* **Security Concerns in Germany:** Tensions have surfaced between German Chancellor Friedrich Merz and intelligence chiefs regarding the domestic threat posed by Iran. While security officials have privately warned of potential hybrid attacks, the government has officially maintained a more cautious stance, downplaying the immediate risks.

## POLAND

* **Crisis in the Strait of Hormuz:** Security concerns have spiked following an attack on the container ship *CMA CGM San Antonio* in the Strait of Hormuz. The incident is being closely monitored by Polish authorities, as the vessel is under the command of a Polish captain.
* **Constitutional Standoff Continues:** A judicial impasse has intensified as four newly elected judges attempted to assume their duties at the Constitutional Tribunal. The judges formally presented the President of the Tribunal with an ECHR ruling demanding an end to administrative obstructions, though the Tribunal’s leadership continues to refuse their integration into the bench.
* **Executive Push for Climate Referendum:** President Nawrocki has formally requested that the Senate initiate a national referendum regarding the European Union's climate policy. The move aims to allow citizens a direct voice in the country's energy and environmental strategy, marking a potential point of conflict between the executive branch and Brussels.
