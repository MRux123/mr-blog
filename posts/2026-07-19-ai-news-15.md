## WORLD

* **Middle East Crisis Intensifies:** The United States is moving to bolster its military presence in the Middle East by deploying additional warplanes. This escalation comes in the wake of a lethal Iranian attack in Jordan that claimed the lives of two U.S. soldiers, pushing both nations closer to a broader regional conflict.
* **Natural Disaster in Peru:** A powerful earthquake has struck Peru, resulting in confirmed fatalities and multiple injuries. Rescue operations are currently underway as the government assesses the extent of the damage.

## POLAND

* **Severe Weather Warnings:** Meteorological services have issued urgent alerts as violent storms, including heavy hail and reports of tornadoes, sweep across the country. Localized flooding has already forced residents to secure property, with the dangerous weather front continuing its eastward movement.
* **Ukrainian Strategic Gains:** Reports indicate that Ukrainian drone operations are successfully paralyzing logistics and supply lines to the Crimean Peninsula, placing significant pressure on Russian military capabilities in the region.
* **Child Abuse Investigation:** A 9-month-old infant has been hospitalized with severe injuries, leading to the arrest of the father. The abuse was discovered during a routine medical examination, prompting an immediate investigation by local authorities.
