## WORLD

* **Russia-China Joint Declaration**: Moscow and Beijing have issued a formal joint statement warning against a global shift toward a "law of the jungle" mentality. This rhetorical pivot signals a hardening of the strategic partnership between the two powers amidst rising geopolitical tensions.
* **Pakistan Death Sentence**: A court in Pakistan has sentenced a man to death for the murder of a teenage TikTok star. The verdict has reignited intense national debate regarding systemic violence against women and the efficacy of judicial protection for vulnerable individuals in the country.

## POLAND

* **Diplomatic Tensions with Israel**: The Polish Ministry of Foreign Affairs has issued an urgent demand for clarification following the detention of Polish citizens by Israeli forces. The individuals were intercepted during an operation involving a vessel associated with the Global Flotilla Sumud in international waters, prompting a significant diplomatic protest.
* **National Food Safety Alert**: European food safety authorities have issued a formal warning after the detection of *Salmonella* in frozen poultry kebab products branded as "Doner King Kebab." The contamination was identified in all samples tested, affecting both the Polish and Slovakian markets and triggering widespread recall procedures.
* **Tragic Incident in Mazury**: Prosecutors in Mrągowo have launched an official investigation into the death of an 18-year-old student who drowned in Lake Juksty. The investigation seeks to clarify the circumstances surrounding the capsizing of a pedal boat, with authorities currently evaluating the role of potential negligence and substance influence during the incident.
