## WORLD

*   **Conflict Escalation in the Middle East:** Tensions have reached a critical juncture as U.S. forces engaged in renewed strikes against Iran following Tehran's rejection of a ceasefire proposal. The situation is further complicated by rising violence in the West Bank, where a shooting incident resulted in six fatalities, including both Palestinians and Israelis.
*   **Russia-Ukraine Air War Intensifies:** The ongoing conflict has seen a significant escalation in air campaigns. Ukrainian forces launched successful drone and missile strikes targeting critical logistics infrastructure in Russia, while Russian forces responded with heavy bombardment of the Ukrainian capital, signaling a shift toward more direct, high-impact targeting.
*   **Security Crisis in Pakistan:** A major militant attack on a military outpost in Pakistan has claimed at least 15 lives, highlighting the volatile security landscape in the region.

## POLAND

*   **Judicial Controversy Deepens:** The Ministry of Justice has initiated formal disciplinary proceedings against several judges in connection with the ongoing "hate affair," marking a significant development in the scrutiny of the Polish judiciary.
*   **Political Accountability:** Public attention is fixed on the release of a comprehensive list identifying key political figures associated with former Prime Minister Mateusz Morawiecki, as investigations and political maneuvering continue to reshape the domestic landscape.
