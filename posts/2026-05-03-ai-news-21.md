## WORLD

* **Suspected Hantavirus Outbreak on Atlantic Cruise Liner:** Health authorities are responding to a critical situation aboard a cruise ship in the Atlantic, where three passengers have died following a suspected hantavirus outbreak. The World Health Organization (WHO) has confirmed one laboratory-positive case, with five additional individuals currently under medical investigation. Among those affected is a 69-year-old British national currently receiving intensive care in South Africa. The incident has triggered international protocols to contain the potential spread of the virus.

## POLAND

* **New National Security Strategy Finalized:** The National Security Bureau (BBN) has officially concluded its work on the latest iteration of Poland's National Security Strategy. According to acting BBN head General Andrzej Kowalski, the comprehensive document has been forwarded to the government for review. Following the Prime Minister's signature, the strategy is slated for final approval by the President, marking a significant step in the nation's strategic defense and security policy planning.
