## WORLD

*   **Escalation in the Black Sea:** Ukrainian forces have successfully targeted two tankers belonging to Russia’s "shadow fleet" near the port of Novorossiysk. This strike, acknowledged by President Zelensky, represents a significant escalation in the economic war, as these vessels are critical to financing Russia's military operations. 
*   **Geopolitical Friction over US Troop Cuts:** The decision to withdraw 5,000 U.S. service personnel from Germany has drawn sharp criticism from leading U.S. Republicans. Lawmakers warn that the move undermines NATO’s deterrence strategy and sends a dangerous signal to Moscow during a period of sustained regional tension.
*   **Corruption Crisis in Mexico:** A new U.S. indictment against a prominent Mexican governor has exposed deep-rooted corruption involving drug cartels, further complicating the diplomatic landscape for President Claudia Sheinbaum and testing the stability of cross-border relations.

## POLAND

*   **Constitutional Reform Controversy:** President Karol Nawrocki has formally established a "New Constitution Council," appointing figures such as former Constitutional Tribunal head Julia Przyłębska. The initiative has been met with skepticism from Prime Minister Donald Tusk, who dismissed the move as a political maneuver and questioned the credibility of those involved in the proposal.
*   **State Honors for Andrzej Poczobut:** In a symbolic ceremony at the Royal Castle, President Nawrocki awarded the Order of the White Eagle to journalist and activist Andrzej Poczobut. The honor recognizes Poczobut's long-standing dedication to human rights and his advocacy for the Polish minority in Belarus.
*   **Deepening Political Polarization:** The national discourse remains dominated by conflicting views on the strength of the NATO alliance, following remarks from Prime Minister Tusk regarding the bloc's vulnerability. The rhetoric has sparked a heated debate between the governing coalition and the Law and Justice (PiS) party, with both sides accusing the other of playing into Russian interests.
