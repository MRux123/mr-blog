## WORLD

* **Ukrainian Military Command Shake-up:** President Volodymyr Zelenskyy has officially dismissed Ukraine's top army commander, Oleksandr Syrskyi. The decision follows days of public unrest and civil protests, which were initially sparked by the removal of the widely popular Defence Minister, Mykhailo Fedorov. Analysts view this as a significant internal political crisis that may impact the strategic direction of the war effort.
* **Civil Unrest in Bologna:** Reports from Italy indicate that violent riots have broken out in the city of Bologna. The unrest was triggered by the death of an individual known as Fakir, leading to clashes that have drawn international attention to the social volatility in the region.

## POLAND

* **Lech Poznań Nears European Qualification:** In a dominant performance on the European stage, Lech Poznań secured a commanding victory against Danish side Aarhus. The result puts the "Kolejorz" in a strong position to advance to the next round of the competition, marking a significant boost for Polish club football.
* **Mixed Results for Górnik Zabrze:** While Lech Poznań celebrated, Górnik Zabrze faces a steeper challenge after suffering a defeat in their away match in Istanbul. The team must now look to overturn the deficit in the upcoming decisive second leg, which will be hosted at their home stadium in Zabrze.
