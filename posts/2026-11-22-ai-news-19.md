## WORLD

* **Escalation in Libya:** A Russian naval vessel, the *Merkury*, was severely damaged following a suspected Ukrainian drone strike at a naval base in Benghazi, Libya. Libyan officials report that several Ukrainian sea drones targeted the ship, which was docked for refueling. This incident marks a significant expansion of the conflict theater, highlighting the reach of asymmetric naval warfare.
* **Climate Diplomacy Stalls:** The COP31 climate summit in Turkey concluded without a breakthrough agreement. Deep divisions regarding the inclusion of specific language concerning the phase-out of fossil fuels led to a formal stalemate, leaving international climate policy efforts in a state of uncertainty.

## POLAND

* **Investigation into Missile Debris:** The District Prosecutor's Office in Lublin has confirmed that the objects discovered in Nowy Lipowiec, Rudnik, and Mołodycz were remnants of three Ch-101 cruise missiles. This follows initial reports that suggested a mix of missiles and drones, raising concerns about the origin and intent of the projectiles.
* **Shift in Bundeswehr Logistics:** German forces have decided to utilize rail transport to move tanks to Lithuania through Poland, abandoning previous plans for road transport. The decision stems from German government concerns regarding the potential for perceived provocation toward Russia, illustrating the heightened sensitivity of logistics near the eastern flank of NATO.
