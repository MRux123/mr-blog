## WORLD

*   **Geopolitical Tensions Following Khamenei’s Procession:** Massive crowds have gathered to mourn the late Iranian leader, Ayatollah Khamenei, as his procession moves toward Qom. The atmosphere is marked by intense rhetoric and widespread calls for retaliation, signaling potential instability in the region.
*   **The AI Existential Debate:** Prominent voices in the technology sector have issued a stern warning, labeling current developments in artificial intelligence as a potential "Hiroshima moment." This rhetoric underscores the growing urgency among experts to establish global safety frameworks to mitigate existential risks posed by rapidly advancing autonomous systems.

## POLAND

*   **Strategic Defense Exchange with NATO and the US:** Poland is currently negotiating a significant security deal involving the transfer of Patriot missile system components to Ukraine. In return, the Polish government is seeking guarantees and potential hardware replacements from NATO allies and the United States to maintain the integrity of its own national air defense capabilities.
*   **Mass Evacuation in Wrocław:** Authorities have ordered a large-scale evacuation of approximately 3,000 residents in the Szczepin district of Wrocław. The measure follows the discovery of an unexploded World War II ordnance, which is scheduled for removal by military sappers on Tuesday morning. Local officials are coordinating emergency response efforts to ensure public safety during the operation.
