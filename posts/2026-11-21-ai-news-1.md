## WORLD

* **Market Turmoil and Tech Sell-off:** Global equity markets are facing significant pressure, with the S&P 500 and Dow Jones entering correction territory. The downturn is largely driven by a sharp sell-off in technology stocks, exacerbated by concerns surrounding the future of major AI firms like Anthropic.
* **Brexit Reversal:** In a historic shift, the United Kingdom and the European Union have begun a systematic "Brexit rollback," removing various trade and regulatory barriers. Driven by global economic volatility and shifting policies in the U.S. under President Trump, both sides appear motivated to deepen cooperation to stabilize their respective economies.
* **AI Scientific Breakthrough:** In a development that could reshape computational mathematics, researchers at Anthropic claim to have utilized AI to provide a proof for the Riemann hypothesis. While the verification process is ongoing, the implications for cryptography and advanced mathematics are profound.

## POLAND

* **Security Concerns Following Russian Missile Incursion:** A significant security incident occurred when a Russian missile violated Polish airspace, flying over the municipality of Aleksandrów before landing in the neighboring district of Księżpol. Local officials have raised critical alarms regarding the effectiveness of early warning systems and the safety of rural populations.
* **High-Stakes Legal Ruling:** A Polish surgeon, Tomasz Kosowski, has been sentenced to death in the United States following a high-profile murder case on the Florida courts. The verdict marks a severe conclusion to a case involving the killing of an American attorney.
* **Economic Headwinds:** Echoing global trends, the Polish stock market is feeling the repercussions of the international financial downturn, recording its most significant weekly decline since the onset of the pandemic.
