## WORLD

* **Political Uncertainty in Iran Following Supreme Leader’s Funeral:** The interment of Iran’s late supreme leader at the Imam Reza shrine has drawn massive crowds, yet the absence of Ayatollah Mojtaba Khamenei at the ceremony has ignited intense speculation. Analysts warn of a potential power vacuum within the regime’s hierarchy, raising concerns about future stability in a deeply divided nation.
* **China Advances Reusable Rocket Technology:** In a significant milestone for the global space race, China has successfully landed a reusable rocket. This technological breakthrough, achieved with the Long March 10B, signals China’s intent to challenge the dominance of U.S.-based firms like SpaceX, potentially lowering costs and increasing the frequency of satellite deployments.
* **Economic Strain in Developing Nations:** A report from UNESCO reveals a concerning global trend where 113 developing countries are prioritizing foreign debt repayments over investment in education. With educational aid predicted to plummet by up to 30%, experts warn of long-term developmental setbacks for millions of children in the Global South.

## POLAND

* **Tensions Rise at Jedwabne Anniversary:** The 85th anniversary of the Jedwabne pogrom has been marred by attempts from far-right groups to disrupt official commemorations. The events serve as a poignant reminder of historical divisions and the ongoing struggle to confront the darkest chapters of Poland's past.
* **Political Confrontation at Smolensk Commemorations:** Recent monthly observances of the Smolensk catastrophe turned into a scene of conflict, with Jarosław Kaczyński announcing plans to form a "civic guard." The rhetoric highlights the deepening polarization within Polish politics and the increasingly hostile interaction between opposition figures and state security services.
