## WORLD

* **Catastrophic Earthquake in Colombia:** A major earthquake has struck Colombia, resulting in at least 169 confirmed fatalities and triggering a national state of emergency. Rescue operations are currently underway in regions like Cali, where teams are engaged in a critical race against time to locate survivors amidst the widespread destruction.
* **Political Transition in Hungary:** The Hungarian Parliament has elected András Baka, a former President of the Supreme Court, as the new President of Hungary. Nominated by the ruling TISZA party of Prime Minister Péter Magyar, Baka’s appointment marks a significant shift in the country's political landscape, underscored by the opposition Fidesz party’s decision to boycott the parliamentary vote.

## POLAND

* **Political Accountability in Regional Development:** Following a controversy regarding the distribution of relief funds for flood victims, Anna Konieczyńska has been dismissed from her position as Vice President of the Karkonoska Agencja Rozwoju Regionalnego (KARR S.A.). The decision was confirmed by the spokesperson for the Marshal of the Lower Silesian Voivodeship.
* **Record-Breaking Heatwave Data:** Newly released data from the Institute of Meteorology and Water Management (IMGW) confirms that Poland has experienced the highest temperatures in its recorded history. While the town of Słubice previously held the record at 40.5°C, new verified data indicates that temperatures in Kołuda Wielka surpassed this figure during the same extreme heat event.
* **Infant Found in "Window of Life":** Authorities in Białystok report that a healthy two-month-old girl was left in a "window of life" facility. The infant was accompanied by a note providing essential medical information and personal details, and has since been transferred to a hospital for routine health assessments.
