## WORLD

*   **Far-Right Gains in Germany:** Following a significant state election win, Germany's AfD party is actively seeking coalition partners to form a government. The development marks a major shift in the country's political landscape, with established parties facing mounting pressure to address the populist group's demand for governance participation.
*   **Escalation in Lebanon:** Tensions in the Middle East have spiked as recent Israeli strikes in southern Lebanon resulted in nine fatalities. The intensification of the conflict threatens to undermine the fragile, US-brokered ceasefire, raising global concerns about the potential for a prolonged regional occupation and widened hostilities.
*   **Global Trade Friction:** Canada is preparing to implement retaliatory tariffs of up to 50 percent this Tuesday. The move follows warnings from Washington regarding a new cycle of American protectionist measures, highlighting a growing instability in North American trade relations under the current political climate.

## POLAND

*   **Political Fallout in Germany's Neighborhood:** Polish political observers are closely watching the "tectonic shock" following the German state elections. Chancellor Merz has initiated a critical period of self-reflection within his ranks, an event that carries significant implications for the stability of Poland's western partner and future European Union policy.
*   **Constitutional Court Controversy:** Tensions are rising regarding the appointment of Maciej Berek as a judge to the Constitutional Tribunal. Legal experts and opposition figures have voiced "significant doubts" surrounding the process, with the political scene bracing for developments scheduled for the middle of September.
*   **Oncology Crisis:** The Polish healthcare sector is facing a severe human resources crisis, specifically in oncology. The government is under scrutiny as experts warn that current restrictions—preventing specialists from practicing in the private sector—could jeopardize the continuity of cancer care for patients nationwide.
