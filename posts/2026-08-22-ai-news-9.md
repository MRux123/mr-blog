## WORLD

*   **Escalating Conflict in Ukraine:** A devastating Russian "double-tap" strike on a shopping mall has claimed at least 16 lives and left 130 injured, including a significant number of children. President Volodymyr Zelensky has vowed a firm response, condemning the perpetrators as "animals" as search and rescue operations continue for the nine missing individuals.
*   **Canada-U.S. Trade War:** Economic relations between Ottawa and Washington have reached a critical breaking point. Following an impasse in high-stakes negotiations, Mark Carney has suspended talks, triggering the implementation of retaliatory tariffs on both sides of the border. This escalation threatens to destabilize North American supply chains.
*   **Regional Stability in Lebanon:** Efforts to curb Hezbollah's influence are facing significant hurdles amid the ongoing war with Israel. President Joseph Aoun’s administration is struggling to reassert state authority as the militant group continues to entangle the nation in regional hostilities.

## POLAND

*   **Extreme Weather Disrupts Warsaw:** A severe windstorm has crippled parts of the capital, forcing the closure of the Warsaw Zoo and major historical landmarks, including the Łazienki and Wilanów parks. Emergency services have logged over 250 interventions as high-speed winds continue to pose risks to public safety.
*   **Political Accountability Poll:** A new survey reveals a deep divide in public opinion regarding the failure to deliver on President Karol Nawrocki’s campaign promises. The data indicates that 32.9% of voters hold the President personally responsible, while 31.3% attribute the failures to the current ruling coalition, highlighting a polarized political climate.
