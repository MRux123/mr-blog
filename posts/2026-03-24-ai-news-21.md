## WORLD

*   **US Seeks De-escalation with Iran:** Amidst growing regional instability, the U.S. administration has reportedly sent a 15-point peace proposal to Iran via Pakistan. The initiative aims to provide an "offramp" from the current conflict as both nations navigate the economic consequences of ongoing hostilities.
*   **Military Buildup in the Middle East:** In a move that underscores the fragility of the situation, the Pentagon is preparing to deploy approximately 3,000 soldiers from the elite 82nd Airborne Division to the Middle East, according to reports from Reuters and The Wall Street Journal. This occurs simultaneously with the withdrawal of the aircraft carrier USS Gerald R. Ford due to technical failures and fire damage.
*   **Political Shifts in Europe:** Denmark is facing a period of significant political uncertainty as the ruling Social Democratic Party suffers its worst electoral result in over a century, leaving the government without a clear majority and complicating the nation's legislative future.

## POLAND

*   **Diplomatic Outcry Over Lviv Attack:** The Polish government has strongly condemned the Russian attack on Lviv, specifically highlighting the strike on a building adjacent to a church. Minister of Culture Marta Cienkowska described the assault as a direct attack on shared identity and heritage, calling for an immediate and robust international response.
*   **Legislative Shifts in Housing Policy:** A new legislative proposal has been submitted to the Sejm by the PSL party, aiming to reform the previous "SAFE 0%" housing program. The project proposes shifting 90% of the National Bank of Poland's (NBP) profits toward security, infrastructure, and military healthcare by 2035, signaling a pivot in national fiscal priorities.
