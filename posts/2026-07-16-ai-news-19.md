## WORLD

* **Zelensky Shakes Up Defense Leadership:** Ukrainian President Volodymyr Zelensky has dismissed his defense minister, a move that has sparked public protests. The decision reportedly stems from a deep-seated rift between the minister and Commander-in-Chief Oleksandr Syrskyi, highlighting ongoing challenges in managing the country's wartime military hierarchy.
* **Breakthrough in Exoplanet Research:** In a significant scientific milestone, astronomers have for the first time detected an atmosphere on a rocky, potentially habitable planet. This discovery marks a new era in the search for life beyond our solar system, providing researchers with a tangible target for further atmospheric analysis.

## POLAND

* **Internal Conflict Escalates within PiS:** The Law and Justice (PiS) party is facing significant internal friction centered on former Prime Minister Mateusz Morawiecki’s new association, "Rozwój Plus." High-ranking party officials have openly criticized the move, warning that the emergence of an alternative political agenda could fracture the party at a critical time, while loyalists urge unity to challenge the current government.
* **Public Outcry Over Medical Income Disparities:** Former Health Minister Łukasz Szumowski has ignited a public debate after labeling the multi-million zloty annual earnings of some private medical practitioners as "frightening." The comments have sparked a broader national discussion regarding the ethics of high-income medical corporations within the Polish healthcare system.
* **Safety Crisis in the Tatra Mountains:** Mountain rescue teams (TOPR) are currently facing an overwhelming influx of emergencies following a surge of accidents in the Tatra Mountains. The situation underscores the risks associated with current trail conditions and the seasonal strain on search and rescue operations.
