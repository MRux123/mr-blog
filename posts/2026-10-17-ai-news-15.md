## WORLD

*   **Escalating Tensions in Ukraine:** President Volodymyr Zelenskyy has issued a stark warning to European Union leaders in Brussels, stating that a potential defeat against Russia could trigger a mass migration of over 20 million Ukrainians into the EU. Simultaneously, domestic instability is mounting, with protests erupting in Kyiv demanding the resignation of Mayor Vitali Klitschko amid corruption allegations and infrastructure concerns.
*   **Widespread Social Unrest in France:** The nation is currently facing significant domestic turmoil as student-led demonstrations merge with broader protests against the rising cost of living. The situation has intensified into violent clashes with police and incidents involving public buildings, signaling a profound national reckoning.
*   **Pope Leo XIV Health Update:** There is positive news regarding the Vatican, as Pope Leo XIV has been discharged from the Gemelli Hospital in Rome following his surgery for lung cancer. The Pontiff has returned to the Vatican, and medical reports indicate a successful recovery.

## POLAND

*   **Political Polarization Over Constitutional Tribunal:** Tensions have reached a new high following a demonstration in front of the Constitutional Tribunal. The event, which featured remarks from Jarosław Kaczyński, saw police intervene in a controversial manner. While political figures claimed excessive force was used by officers, police have released footage to dispute those allegations, highlighting the deepening divide within the country's political landscape.
*   **Major Security Operation in Wrocław:** Authorities are preparing for a massive evacuation involving approximately 3,000 residents in Wrocław due to the discovery of an unexploded German bomb at a local construction site. Emergency services are coordinating the operation to ensure public safety.
