## WORLD

*   **Israel-Iran Conflict Escalates:** Israel’s recent targeted strikes against Iranian steel infrastructure have ignited a debate over the legality and strategic scope of military operations. While Israel claims these facilities support weapons production and regime financing, the resulting disruption to the civilian economy raises concerns about the proportionality of the attacks.
*   **Health Alert in DRC:** A humanitarian worker from the United States has tested positive for Ebola in the Democratic Republic of Congo. The Centers for Disease Control and Prevention (CDC) is monitoring the situation, highlighting the persistent health risks faced by international staff in high-stakes conflict zones.

## POLAND

*   **Seismic Incident in Copper Basin:** A powerful tremor struck the Zagłębie Miedziowe region, forcing an emergency evacuation of miners. The event underscores the ongoing safety challenges and inherent risks associated with deep-level industrial mining operations in Poland.
*   **Traffic Tragedy in Kashubia:** A reckless incident occurred where a group of individuals was found transporting passengers on the roof of a vehicle, driven by an intoxicated teenager. The event has prompted public outcry regarding road safety and the necessity for stricter enforcement of traffic laws among young drivers.
