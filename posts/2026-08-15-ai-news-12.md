## WORLD

* **Tragic Earthquake Hits Indonesia:** A powerful 7.7 magnitude earthquake has struck the island of Flores, Indonesia. Search and rescue operations are currently underway, with local authorities confirming at least 38 fatalities.
* **Escalating Tensions in Lebanon:** The regional conflict has intensified following Israeli strikes in southern Lebanon. Health officials report nine casualties, including several children, while the Israeli military states the operation was directed at Hezbollah infrastructure.
* **Diplomatic Overtures on the Korean Peninsula:** In a notable attempt to thaw decades of frozen conflict, South Korean official Lee Jae Myung has proposed formal talks with North Korea, aiming to officially end the war that has persisted since the 1953 armistice.

## POLAND

* **Armed Forces Day Celebrations:** Poland marks its military holiday with the largest military parade in its history. During the event, key political figures, including Karol Nawrocki and Prime Minister Donald Tusk, delivered speeches emphasizing the importance of sovereignty, national security, and the integrity of the country’s borders.
* **Aviation Accident in the Łódź Region:** Emergency services were mobilized in the Łowicz district after an ultralight aircraft crashed in a forested area. The pilot survived the impact and was airlifted to a hospital in Łódź by an LPR (Air Ambulance) helicopter.
