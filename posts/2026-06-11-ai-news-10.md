## WORLD

* **U.S.-Iran Conflict Enters Critical Phase:** The confrontation between the United States and Iran has escalated significantly, with both nations exchanging direct strikes for the second consecutive day. Tehran has declared that recent U.S. military actions have effectively nullified existing cease-fire agreements, raising international concerns that the region is sliding toward an all-out war.
* **Ukrainian Conflict Surpasses World War I Duration:** The ongoing war in Ukraine has now officially persisted longer than the entirety of World War I. Analysts highlight sobering parallels between the two conflicts, noting how the grueling nature of trench-like attrition and the rapid integration of new military technologies have redefined modern warfare.

## POLAND

* **Political Polarization Intensifies in Sejm:** Tensions have reached a boiling point in the Polish parliament, as Deputy Speaker Piotr Zgorzelski (PSL) sharply rebuked MP Janusz Kowalski, labeling his rhetoric as "brown-shirt" extremism. The exchange highlights deep-seated ideological divides regarding recent parliamentary discourse and social policy.
* **EU Advocate General Criticizes European Commission:** In a significant legal development, the Advocate General of the Court of Justice of the European Union (CJEU) has criticized the European Commission for failing to provide adequate public access to COVID-19 vaccine procurement contracts. A final ruling from the CJEU is expected to follow, potentially setting a major precedent for institutional transparency within the bloc.
* **Legislative Dispute Over Anti-Mobbing Laws:** The Sejm has recommitted a government bill on anti-mobbing regulations to a committee. The legislation, which seeks to enhance protections for employees and establish a minimum threshold for compensation, remains a point of contention as lawmakers attempt to refine its impact on the labor market.
