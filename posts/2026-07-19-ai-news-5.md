## WORLD

*   **Heightened Tensions Between the US and Iran:** The geopolitical situation in the Middle East has reached a critical juncture as the United States enters its eighth consecutive night of retaliatory strikes against Iranian targets. These military operations follow the deaths of two American service members in Jordan. In a reciprocal escalation, Iran reports having deployed drones against US military assets stationed in Kuwait, marking a dangerous expansion of the conflict.
*   **Democratic Transition in São Tomé and Príncipe:** The island nation of São Tomé and Príncipe is holding a pivotal presidential election. Incumbent Carlos Vila Nova is seeking a second term while running as an independent, aiming to maintain democratic stability in one of Africa's smallest and least populous states.

## POLAND

*   **Massive Ballistic Attack on Kyiv:** The most significant escalation in the ongoing conflict between Russia and Ukraine has occurred with a massive ballistic missile strike on the Ukrainian capital. Reports indicate that this is the largest assault of its kind since the start of the full-scale invasion, resulting in casualties and significant infrastructure damage. 
*   **High-Profile Detention in the US:** International media attention has shifted toward the arrest of Andrew Tate and his brother by authorities in the United States, an operation that occurred shortly before a scheduled public event. The detention has sparked widespread discussion regarding the legal implications for the brothers.
