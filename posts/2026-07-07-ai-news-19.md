## WORLD

* **Marine Le Pen Cleared to Run in 2027**: Following a successful appeal, French far-right leader Marine Le Pen has had her ban on holding elected office shortened, paving the way for her presidential bid. While her embezzlement conviction was upheld, the judiciary’s decision significantly alters the trajectory of French domestic politics ahead of the next election cycle.
* **Hungarian State Media Overhaul**: In a drastic move to dismantle the remnants of the Orban-era media influence, Hungarian public news broadcasts have been suspended. The main state channel issued a public apology for previous partisan output, signaling a potential shift toward greater institutional transparency in the country's media landscape.
* **IOC Lifts Suspension of Russian Olympic Committee**: In a controversial reversal of a policy dating back to October 2023, the International Olympic Committee has provisionally lifted the suspension of the Russian Olympic Committee. The organization maintains, however, that its stance against the ongoing invasion of Ukraine remains unchanged.

## POLAND

* **Diplomatic Scrutiny of Ziobro’s US Presence**: Polish authorities are intensifying efforts regarding the presence of former Justice Minister Zbigniew Ziobro in the United States. Reports indicate that a formal request is being prepared for the U.S. Immigration and Customs Enforcement (ICE) agency, suggesting a new phase in the legal and political scrutiny surrounding the former official.
* **Political Volatility: Mentzen vs. Czarnek**: Domestic political tensions have escalated following a public clash between Konfederacja leader Sławomir Mentzen and PiS politician Przemysław Czarnek. The exchange, centered on accusations of hypocrisy regarding potential coalitions and policy stances toward Ukraine, highlights the sharpening divisions within the Polish opposition as parties realign.
