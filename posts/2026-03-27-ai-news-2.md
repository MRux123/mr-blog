## WORLD

* **Road to the World Cup:** Sweden has secured its place in the final qualification round after a decisive 3-1 victory over Ukraine. The Swedish side will now face Poland in Stockholm this Tuesday, with a coveted spot in the upcoming World Cup at stake.

## POLAND

* **Crucial Play-off Victory:** The Polish national team successfully advanced to the final stage of the World Cup qualification play-offs after defeating Albania 2-1 at the PGE Narodowy stadium. Goals from Robert Lewandowski and Piotr Zieliński kept the nation's footballing aspirations alive ahead of the decisive match against Sweden.
* **Legislative Action on Energy Costs:** The Polish Sejm has moved to address rising fuel prices, with two government-proposed bills currently undergoing urgent committee review. Lawmakers have agreed to fast-track the legislative process, reflecting the political pressure to mitigate inflationary pressures on the energy market.
