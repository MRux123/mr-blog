## WORLD

*   **US-Saudi Nuclear Cooperation:** The Trump administration is set to announce a landmark civilian nuclear deal with Saudi Arabia. This "123 Agreement" represents a significant shift in Middle Eastern geopolitical relations, aimed at fostering energy cooperation while incorporating strict safeguards intended to prevent the proliferation of nuclear weapons.
*   **Youth-Led Unrest in India:** A growing movement of young protesters across India is challenging the government’s long-standing authority. Driven by frustrations over the current education system and a pervasive lack of employment opportunities, these demonstrations mark a rare and intense domestic pressure point for the ruling administration.
*   **Death of Epstein Associate in France:** The discovery of Daniel Siad’s body in France has drawn renewed attention to the Jeffrey Epstein investigation. Siad, who served as a model scout, was linked to the facilitation of meetings between Epstein and young women, and his death occurs at a critical juncture in the ongoing scrutiny of the late financier's network.

## POLAND

*   **New Human Rights Ombudsman Appointed:** The Polish Senate has officially approved Sylwia Gregorczyk-Abram as the new Ombudsman (Rzecznik Praw Obywatelskich). She is set to succeed Professor Marcin Wiącek, whose five-year term concludes on July 23, marking a significant transition in the country's institutional leadership.
*   **Internal Turmoil Within PiS:** The Law and Justice (PiS) party is facing visible internal friction. Party leader Jarosław Kaczyński has scheduled a high-stakes meeting with Mateusz Morawiecki amidst reports of an ultimatum issued to party members involved in external political associations, highlighting broader instability within the opposition’s leadership structure.
*   **Judicial Appointment Proceeding:** The President of Poland is set to swear in Sławomir Patyra as a judge of the Constitutional Tribunal on July 28. This move, following his selection by the Sejm in June, remains a focal point of the ongoing debate regarding the composition and legitimacy of the judicial body.
