## WORLD

* **Ukraine Strikes Critical Russian Oil Infrastructure:** In a significant escalation of the conflict, Ukraine successfully targeted a major oil terminal in St. Petersburg. President Volodymyr Zelensky characterized the facility as a vital piece of infrastructure that fuels Russia's war effort, signaling a strategic focus on degrading the enemy's economic and logistical capabilities.
* **Escalating Violence in Sudan:** The city of El Obeid has become a primary flashpoint in the ongoing war between the Sudanese armed forces and the Rapid Support Forces (RSF). Reports from aid workers confirm that the city is under relentless drone bombardment, resulting in civilian casualties, including students, as critical infrastructure like schools and fuel stations are being targeted.
* **Political Unrest in Germany:** Thousands of protesters gathered in Erfurt to oppose a conference held by the far-right party, Alternative for Germany (AfD). The demonstrations resulted in significant clashes with law enforcement, the blocking of critical transport infrastructure, and injuries among the press corps covering the event.

## POLAND

* **Severe Coastal Conditions:** Polish authorities have issued strict warnings for Baltic Sea beachgoers, closing 130 swimming areas. High waves and dangerous rip currents have rendered the water hazardous, leading to a widespread ban on swimming in popular resorts such as Świnoujście, Mielno, and Ustronie Morskie.
* **Public Safety Concerns:** Police in the Świętokrzyskie region have launched a manhunt for a 65-year-old suspect following the discovery of a deceased woman in an apartment in Borków. This follows a string of violent incidents reported recently, including a shooting in Zawiercie and a knife-related assault in Sosnowiec, highlighting a turbulent period for local public safety.
* **Weather Shifts:** After an intense, multi-day heatwave, parts of Poland are experiencing a brief period of cooling. While temperatures are moderating to around 23 degrees Celsius, the Institute of Meteorology and Water Management (IMGW) has issued first-degree warnings for potentially dangerous storms in the north-eastern regions of the country.
