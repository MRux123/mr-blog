## WORLD

*   **Security Concerns in Northern Europe:** Swedish authorities have issued a stark warning regarding Russian military ambitions, suggesting that the Kremlin may attempt an attack against a NATO member state in the near future, well before Russian forces fully regain their pre-conflict combat capabilities. This assessment highlights growing anxiety in the Baltic region regarding potential escalation.
*   **Armenian Political Instability:** The "Strong Armenia" party, known for its pro-Russian stance, has formally challenged the results of recent parliamentary elections. Alleging significant voting irregularities, the group has petitioned the Central Electoral Commission for annulment. Despite these claims, international observers have reported no substantial evidence of fraud, creating a tense political standoff in Yerevan.
*   **Kremlin’s Alleged Sabotage Plots:** Newly surfaced reports implicate Russian intelligence services in planned efforts to destabilize Belarus by targeting oil infrastructure. Analysts suggest these operations were designed to frame Ukrainian intelligence through coerced confessions, underscoring a complex web of regional hybrid warfare and disinformation campaigns.

## POLAND

*   **New Economic-Liberal Political Initiative:** Reports suggest that Ryszard Petru is positioning himself to spearhead a new political project following the summer break. Described in media as a potential "light" version of the Confederation party, this group aims to capture the interest of Polish entrepreneurs and voters favoring a strictly liberal economic agenda, signaling a potential shift in the Polish political landscape.
