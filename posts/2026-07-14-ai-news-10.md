## WORLD

*   **Bangkok Fire Tragedy:** An investigation into the fatal fire in Bangkok has revealed critical safety failures. As the death toll climbed to 30, reports from survivors and emergency responders highlighted locked fire exits and flammable interior decorations as primary factors that hindered escape and contributed to the high mortality rate.
*   **Escalating Regional Tensions:** Following recent airstrikes on the Sanaa airport, Houthi rebels in Yemen have launched retaliatory missile attacks targeting Saudi Arabia’s Abha airport. This move marks a significant intensification of hostilities between the Iran-backed group and the Saudi-led coalition, raising fears of further instability in the region.

## POLAND

*   **Military Discipline Reforms:** The Polish Minister of National Defence, Władysław Kosiniak-Kamysz, has addressed reports regarding potential legislative changes to military discipline. Proposals under discussion suggest significantly stricter penalties for disobedience in combat situations, including the possibility of life imprisonment, though the Ministry emphasizes that no final decisions have been reached.
*   **Controversial Hospital Spending:** Public scrutiny has intensified regarding the financial management of indebted healthcare facilities. Reports indicate that some hospitals, despite carrying massive multi-million PLN debts, have been paying individual specialists salaries exceeding 100,000 PLN per month, fueling a national debate on the sustainability of current labor market practices in the public health sector.
*   **Fatal Accident Near Szczytno:** A tragic traffic accident on national road DK58 near Szczytno resulted in the death of a 17-year-old driver. Preliminary investigations suggest the teenager did not hold a driver's license and was driving under the influence of alcohol, with reports indicating that all passengers in the vehicle were also intoxicated.
