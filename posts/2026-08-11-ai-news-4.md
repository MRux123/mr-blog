## WORLD

* **Devastating Earthquake in Colombia:** The country is facing a national emergency following its most powerful earthquake in recent history. Reports confirm at least 132 fatalities, with the death toll expected to rise as search and rescue teams navigate collapsed structures. Over 480 individuals have sustained injuries, and authorities are currently prioritizing the extraction of survivors feared trapped under the rubble.
* **Tupac Shakur Murder Trial Commences:** The legal proceedings against Duane "Keffe D" Davis, the 63-year-old former gang leader accused in the 1996 murder of Tupac Shakur, have officially begun. Davis has entered a formal plea of not guilty as the trial moves into the evidentiary stage, marking a significant milestone in one of music history's most high-profile cold cases.

## POLAND

* **Escalating Russian Strikes on Ukraine:** The conflict continues to intensify, with Russian forces launching a series of deadly nighttime attacks targeting key Ukrainian cities, most notably Zaporizhzhia and Kyiv. Regional officials report the use of ballistic missiles and guided aerial bombs, resulting in significant civilian casualties, including six confirmed deaths and over 50 injuries in the Zaporizhzhia region alone.
* **Diplomatic Shifts in Ceuta:** Morocco has initiated a new diplomatic and operational strategy in response to the ongoing migration crisis at the border of the Spanish enclave of Ceuta. The move signifies a heightened effort to manage regional tensions and address the complex humanitarian challenges stemming from the influx of migrants attempting to cross the border.
