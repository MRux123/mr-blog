## WORLD

* **Deadly Humanitarian Strike in Ukraine:** A devastating Russian aerial attack on a humanitarian aid center in the Zaporizhzhia region has resulted in at least 26 fatalities. The assault, characterized by the use of heavy 3,000kg bombs, follows a short-lived humanitarian ceasefire in the neighboring Kherson region, signaling a return to intense, high-stakes military engagement.
* **Diplomatic Realignment and Iran Deal:** The United States and Iran appear to be nearing a diplomatic breakthrough regarding the Strait of Hormuz. Following a Qatari-mediated mission to Tehran, reports suggest that Iran may be willing to reopen the strategic waterway within days as part of a broader negotiation framework aimed at de-escalating regional tensions.

## POLAND

* **Constitutional Crisis Deepens:** The standoff within the Polish Constitutional Tribunal continues to intensify. With a backlog of over 1,170 cases, the institution remains paralyzed by internal friction, prompting high-level criticism from political figures, including former Prime Minister Leszek Miller, who labeled the current state of affairs as a failure of institutional oversight and protection services.
* **Geopolitical Vulnerability:** International observers have noted that Moscow is actively exploiting Poland’s current domestic political instability. Following the ouster of nationalist governments in Serbia and Hungary, the Kremlin is reportedly focusing its efforts on Poland to weaken the broader European coalition supporting Ukraine, leveraging local constitutional disputes for strategic gain.
