## WORLD

*   **US Military Retaliates Against Iran:** Following attempted ballistic missile strikes on American forces in Jordan and Iraq, the United States has launched "heavy" military strikes against Iranian targets. The escalation marks a significant deepening of regional instability, exacerbated by simultaneous incidents involving maritime threats, including fires on ships in Egypt.
*   **Shifting Perspectives on African Aid:** A new survey highlights a complex shift in geopolitical sentiment, with 25% of young Africans suggesting that cuts to USAID funding could yield positive results. Respondents in nations like Nigeria and Ghana argue that reduced dependency may force local leaders to address systemic challenges independently, signaling a growing desire for sovereignty over foreign influence.

## POLAND

*   **Unidentified Object Enters Polish Airspace:** Polish military authorities are investigating an unidentified object that entered the country's airspace amid a massive Russian aerial assault on Ukraine. An impact site, characterized by a 10-meter crater, has been identified near the village of Tarnawa-Kolonia. 
*   **Heightened Defense Readiness:** In response to the Russian strikes on neighboring Ukraine, the Polish Armed Forces scrambled fighter jets to secure the border. The security situation caused audible air-raid sirens in cities like Lublin, reflecting the high-alert status of NATO's eastern flank as the conflict in Ukraine intensifies.
