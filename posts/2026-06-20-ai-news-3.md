## WORLD

*   **Middle East Conflict:** The diplomatic landscape in the Middle East remains tense as Israel confirms a commitment to a new cease-fire agreement in Lebanon. Despite this potential breakthrough, Israeli officials have stated that military troops will maintain their presence in the region. This development comes amidst an ongoing delay in broader peace negotiations regarding the U.S.-Iranian conflict, with Hezbollah yet to provide a formal response.
*   **Public Health Crisis in South Africa:** A significant shift in international aid is underway as the United States moves to cease funding for HIV programmes in South Africa. This decision poses a severe challenge for the country, which currently manages the highest HIV burden globally, with over eight million people affected. Experts warn that the withdrawal of support could jeopardize long-term stability and health outcomes in the region.
