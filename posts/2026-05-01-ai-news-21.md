## WORLD

* **Escalating Trade War with the EU**: U.S. President Donald Trump has announced a significant tariff hike, raising duties on European Union-manufactured automobiles to 25 percent. The administration claims the move is a retaliatory measure for alleged trade agreement violations, sparking immediate concern across European automotive sectors and political circles regarding the future of transatlantic economic stability.
* **Long-Range Strike in Russia**: In a major escalation of the conflict, Ukrainian drone forces successfully targeted four Russian military aircraft at an airfield in Chelyabinsk. The operation is highly notable due to its range, as the location sits 1,700 kilometers from the Ukrainian border, signaling a shift in the reach and capability of Ukrainian long-range strike operations.
* **New York Archdiocese Settlement**: The Catholic Archdiocese of New York has agreed to a landmark $800 million settlement to resolve claims brought by 1,300 survivors of sexual abuse. This settlement represents one of the largest financial resolutions in the history of clergy abuse litigation in the United States, highlighting ongoing efforts to address institutional accountability.

## POLAND

* **Agricultural Crisis Due to Frost**: Polish Minister of Agriculture Stefan Krajewski has reported catastrophic conditions in domestic orchards following a week of severe late-spring frosts. In some regions, farmers are reporting a total loss of their crops, prompting the government to pledge emergency financial support for the affected agricultural sector.
* **Diplomatic Breakthrough for Andrzej Poczobut**: Journalist Andrzej Poczobut has been released by the Belarusian regime as part of a prisoner exchange. According to government official Michał Dworczyk, Poczobut had previously rejected earlier offers of release from Minsk because they were conditioned on a forced, one-way exile, underscoring the political nature of his long-term detention.
* **Industrial Incident in Dąbrowa Górnicza**: A serious industrial accident occurred at the ArcelorMittal steelworks in Dąbrowa Górnicza, where equipment from a blast furnace fell onto parked vehicles. While the material damage is significant, authorities confirmed that no personnel were injured in the incident, which occurred just days after the furnace had been returned to service.
