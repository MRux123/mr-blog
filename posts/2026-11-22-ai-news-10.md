## WORLD

* **Escalating Tech Espionage:** Reports confirm that Chinese intelligence agencies have been conducting a long-term, coordinated campaign to secure advanced AI chips and gather critical sector intelligence, predating the recent Nvidia controversy. The findings highlight deepening strategic tensions regarding technological sovereignty and global supply chain security.
* **South Korean Education Crisis:** The Minister of Education has resigned following a chaotic college entrance exam season. The sudden implementation of aggressive, AI-focused anti-cheating measures—including intrusive equipment inspections—triggered a massive public backlash, forcing political accountability at the highest level of the ministry.
* **Successful Counter-Cartel Operation:** U.S. and Mexican authorities have successfully captured Fausto Isidro Meza Flores, one of Mexico's most wanted drug cartel leaders. The operation, which utilized advanced intelligence sharing and drone surveillance, marks a significant tactical victory in the ongoing effort to dismantle high-level narcotics operations.

## POLAND

* **Political Polarization Over Airspace Security:** Tensions flared during a live television debate regarding the recent breach of Polish airspace by Russian projectiles. The disagreement between political figures over whether the military should have engaged the targets underscores a wider national debate on defense strategy and tactical readiness in the face of regional instability.
* **Infrastructure and Disaster Response:** Rescue teams are currently operating in the Kyiv region following the collapse of a five-story residential building, an incident that has drawn immediate international attention. Meanwhile, at home, emergency services are containing a significant industrial fire at a scrap heap in the Port of Gdańsk, signaling ongoing concerns regarding industrial safety in critical logistics hubs.
