## WORLD

* **Escalating Tensions in the Middle East:** The United States and Iran have engaged in direct strikes for the fifth consecutive day. While both nations appear to be hardening their positions, high-level diplomatic signaling suggests an openness to negotiation, with Iranian officials emphasizing the need to navigate the fine line between conflict and diplomacy.
* **Political Instability in Ukraine:** Widespread protests have erupted across Ukrainian cities following President Zelensky's decision to dismiss Defense Minister Mykhailo Fedorov. The lack of an official explanation for the sudden move has sparked significant concern among both the military leadership and civil society, raising questions about internal stability during wartime.
* **Russia's Warning to the West:** The Kremlin has issued a sharp response to the prospect of a "coalition of the willing" deploying troops in Ukraine. Foreign Ministry spokesperson Maria Zakharova labeled any such intervention as "unacceptable," warning that Russia would view foreign personnel as legitimate military targets.

## POLAND

* **Judicial Independence and EU Relations:** The European Court of Justice (ECJ) has issued a landmark ruling regarding judicial independence. Concurrently, the ECJ's advocate general has provided a critical opinion on the Polish Supreme Court, suggesting that the current "mixed" composition of the court fails to meet the standards required to be considered an independent tribunal.
* **Government Audit of Previous Administration:** The Polish government has initiated a comprehensive review of state expenditures during the PiS administration. The Minister of Finance recently disclosed significant financial irregularities identified during the audit, signaling a major push for public accountability regarding past fiscal policies.
* **Military Readiness:** The Ministry of National Defense has officially announced the timeline for the upcoming military qualification process. Approximately 245,000 Polish citizens are expected to receive summons, reflecting the state's continued focus on defensive readiness in the current security climate.
