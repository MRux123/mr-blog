## WORLD

*   **Explosion in Moscow:** A significant blast occurred at a restaurant in the center of Moscow, resulting in multiple fatalities and injuries. Reports indicate that the attack may have targeted high-ranking Russian military officials, marking a high-profile incident in the heart of the capital.
*   **Escalation of Ceuta Border Crisis:** The European Union has convened an emergency meeting of interior ministers following a sharp diplomatic fallout between Spain and several EU member states. Spanish Prime Minister Pedro Sánchez criticized the response to the arrival of 50,000 migrants in the Spanish enclave of Ceuta, labeling the reaction of some partners as "selfish" and obstructive to regional unity.
*   **Middle East Security Developments:** Intelligence reports reveal that Iran had been covertly devising a strategy to increase the costs of conflict for the U.S. administration, utilizing its network of proxies during the recent period of fragile truce.

## POLAND

*   **Investigation into Russian Missile Breach:** Prosecutors from Lublin have returned to the site in Tarnawa-Kolonia, where a Russian Ch-101 missile recently violated Polish airspace. Additional fragments of the projectile were recovered during the follow-up investigation, further confirming the breach of sovereignty.
*   **Commemoration of the Warsaw Uprising:** Political activity surrounding the anniversary of the Warsaw Uprising intensified as Sławomir Mentzen and members of the "Nowa Nadzieja" party gathered at the Brandenburg Gate in Berlin. The group aimed to highlight the scale of the atrocities committed by German forces during the 1944 uprising.
*   **Tragedy in the Tatra Mountains:** A grim start to the month in the Tatra Mountains has left two people dead following separate hiking accidents. Search and rescue operations have issued urgent warnings as severe weather conditions continue to threaten safety in the region.
