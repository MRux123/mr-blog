## WORLD

*   **Historic Gaza Peace Initiative:** U.S. President Donald Trump has officially announced a breakthrough agreement regarding the disarmament of Hamas. The multi-stage plan, overseen by the Peace Council, includes the gradual withdrawal of Israeli forces from the Gaza Strip. International observers view this as a potentially decisive step toward ending the long-standing conflict, contingent on the successful execution of disarmament protocols.
*   **Aerial Alert in Romania:** Tensions along the Ukrainian border remain high after the Romanian Ministry of Defense scrambled F-16 fighter jets to intercept unidentified aerial targets. While the objects did not breach Romanian airspace, the event underscores the persistent risk of spillover from the ongoing conflict in Ukraine, as explosions were subsequently reported on the Ukrainian side of the border.

## POLAND

*   **Mysterious Disappearance of Sports Team:** Authorities have launched an international search operation following the sudden and unexplained disappearance of an entire sports team from an ongoing tournament. The nature of the vanishing has raised significant concerns, prompting a coordinated effort to locate the missing athletes.
*   **Critical Incident Involving Hot Air Balloon:** A serious accident involving a hot air balloon has resulted in three injuries. Local services are currently investigating the circumstances of the crash, which has highlighted ongoing safety concerns regarding recreational aviation in the region.
