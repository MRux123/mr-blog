## WORLD

* **Escalating Tensions in the Middle East:** Israel has moved to re-establish a closed settlement in the West Bank. This move, driven by a surge in nationalist policy, defiance of international protests, and subsequent tension with Palestinian residents, signals a significant escalation in regional instability.
* **Resurgence of Somali Piracy:** Regional security experts have raised alarms over a potential comeback of Somali piracy, marked by two hijackings in just four days. Analysts suggest that the broader conflict in Iran and evolving regional dynamics are creating an enabling environment for maritime criminal networks to reorganize.
* **U.S.-Japan Friction over ICC Sanctions:** Japan’s diplomatic efforts to protect its judiciary have failed, as the White House moved forward with sanctions against Tomoko Akane, the president of the International Criminal Court. This development underscores the deepening divide between the U.S. and its allies regarding the jurisdiction and political independence of international legal bodies.
