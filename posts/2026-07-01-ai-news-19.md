## WORLD

* **Humanitarian Crisis in Venezuela:** The aftermath of a devastating earthquake has overwhelmed Venezuela’s infrastructure, with the death toll exceeding 2,000. Reports indicate that local forensic capabilities have been pushed to their limit, forcing the conversion of ports into temporary morgues as international scrutiny of the government’s response intensifies.
* **Escalating Geopolitical Risks:** Tensions have spiked in Europe following reports that a Ukrainian national has been charged in Germany regarding the Nord Stream pipeline blasts. This development threatens to complicate the strategic partnership between Berlin and Kyiv, while separate reports of targeted bombings against political figures in Greece highlight a growing climate of regional volatility.

## POLAND

* **Strained Relations with Ukraine:** The political atmosphere between Poland and Ukraine remains tense. High-profile remarks by Polish politician Grzegorz Schetyna, who attributed the current friction to the rhetoric of President Volodymyr Zelenskyy, suggest that internal political pressures are mounting as both nations seek to navigate a challenging diplomatic environment.
* **Legal Developments regarding Ziobro:** The Polish judiciary has moved to uphold the decision to issue a temporary arrest warrant for former Justice Minister Zbigniew Ziobro. The dismissal of the defense's appeal marks a significant moment in the ongoing legal proceedings surrounding the former official, signaling continued scrutiny of the previous administration.
