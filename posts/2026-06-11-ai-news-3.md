## WORLD

* **Military Tensions Mount Between U.S. and Iran:** The conflict in the Middle East has entered a critical phase as the United States and Iran exchanged strikes for a second consecutive day. U.S. forces targeted Iranian military assets in response to what the Pentagon described as "continued aggression," while Tehran has responded with threats to target American bases in the Gulf. President Donald Trump has signaled that further military action remains an option unless a diplomatic agreement is reached, raising global concerns regarding a potential return to full-scale regional war.
* **Economic Impact of Geopolitical Instability:** As the conflict intensifies, domestic implications in the U.S. are becoming increasingly apparent. Inflation rates have reached a three-year high, with analysts attributing the rising cost of living to the economic strain caused by the escalating military confrontation.

## POLAND

* **Legislative Clampdown on "Patostreaming":** The Polish Sejm’s parliamentary committee has officially approved amendments to a bill aimed at banning "patostreaming." The proposed legislation targets the broadcasting of illegal acts, simulated criminal behavior, and the exploitation of minors or violence against animals online. The initiative marks a significant effort by regulators to curb the distribution of harmful content across domestic digital platforms, reflecting growing public pressure to sanitize online spaces.
