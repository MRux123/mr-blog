## WORLD

*   **Massive Drone Assault on Russia:** In one of the most significant escalations of the ongoing conflict, Russia reported a massive wave of Ukrainian drone strikes, with over 550 drones intercepted across more than a dozen regions, including Moscow. While casualties remain relatively low at four confirmed deaths, the scale of the attack marks a strategic shift in aerial warfare.
*   **Discovery of Covert Israeli Bases in Iraq:** Regional officials have confirmed that Israel maintained two secret outposts in the Iraqi desert for over a year. These facilities were reportedly established to support covert operations against Iran, highlighting the deepening regional security implications of the current geopolitical instability in the Middle East.

## POLAND

*   **Escalating Political Conflict over Security Policy:** Tensions between the government and the opposition have peaked following Prime Minister Donald Tusk’s sharp criticism of opposition figures, including Karol Nawrocki. Tusk accused them of undermining Poland's image abroad during the Bucharest summit, specifically regarding the government's stance on U.S. military presence. This exchange has triggered a fierce domestic debate, with opposition members labeling the Prime Minister's rhetoric as "sabotage."
*   **Leadership Dynamics within the Coalition:** As the political climate heats up, discussions regarding the future leadership of the Koalicja Obywatelska (Civic Coalition) have entered the public discourse. Aleksandra Gajewska, Deputy Minister of Family, recently emphasized that leadership is not a gift but something that must be earned through boldness and proactive effort, signaling potential shifts in the party's internal hierarchy as it prepares for future electoral challenges.
