## WORLD

* **US-China Standoff Over Taiwan:** Amid escalating geopolitical friction, President Xi Jinping is set to pressure President Trump to restrict arms sales to Taiwan. Beijing continues to designate the island as a core national interest, signaling a potential new phase in US-China diplomatic confrontation.
* **Record Displacement Crisis:** The Internal Displacement Monitoring Centre has reported a historic surge in migration caused by conflict, with 32.3 million people displaced in 2025. For the first time, human-driven violence has surpassed natural disasters as the primary cause of internal displacement globally.
* **EU Social Media Regulation:** European Commission President Ursula von der Leyen has called for significant delays in social media access for minors. An expert panel is currently developing a framework to bolster online protections for children by July.

## POLAND

* **Tusk Addresses Marriage Equality:** Prime Minister Donald Tusk issued a public apology regarding the state's historical failure to support same-sex couples. He announced that the government is moving forward with the transcription of same-sex marriage acts, though he clarified that this does not currently extend to adoption rights.
* **US Military Presence:** Deputy Defense Minister Paweł Zalewski has confirmed that the Polish government is in discussions regarding the potential relocation of US troops currently stationed in Germany. Several strategic locations across Poland are being considered to bolster national and regional security.
* **ZondaCrypto Investigation:** The Polish National Prosecutor's Office has confirmed it is handling nearly 2,000 notifications regarding the ZondaCrypto platform. The massive scale of the investigation, now involving an European Investigation Order sent to the Czech Republic, highlights growing scrutiny over crypto-asset operations in the region.
