## POLAND

*   **Security and Digital Safety Warnings:** The Polish government is actively addressing public safety concerns, with Deputy Minister of Internal Affairs and Administration Czesław Mroczek expected to provide clarity regarding the effectiveness of the RCB Alert system. Concurrently, CERT Polska has issued a nationwide warning regarding a sophisticated WhatsApp phishing campaign targeting high-profile individuals and the general public.
*   **Infrastructure and Weather Incidents:** Severe weather conditions caused significant damage in the Wielkopolska region, where a powerful storm destroyed property in Bógwidze. Meanwhile, transport safety remains a concern following a serious collision between a tram and a bus in Wrocław, which resulted in multiple injuries and prompted renewed calls for improved transit safety measures.
