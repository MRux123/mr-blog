## WORLD

* **Unprecedented Heatwaves Fuel European Wildfires:** A record-breaking heatwave continues to ravage France and Spain, leading to "catastrophic" wildfire conditions. Near Bordeaux, authorities are preparing for large-scale evacuations as the fire perimeter encroaches on urban areas. The scale of the emergency has forced hundreds of thousands to flee their homes across the region, marking an unprecedented fire season in Western Europe.
* **Security Incidents in Paris:** French authorities have apprehended an assailant following a knife attack in Paris that left three women injured, including a pregnant woman. While motives remain unclear and officials are calling for caution, the incident has heightened security concerns during an already volatile period in the country.
* **Geopolitical Posturing:** Former U.S. President Donald Trump has made headlines by suggesting he would confront Vladimir Putin regarding reports of potential Russian military support for Iran, highlighting the ongoing tensions in global security alliances.

## POLAND

* **Wrocław Hate Crime Sparks National Outrage:** A brutal assault on a Ukrainian couple in Wrocław has triggered a strong response from both law enforcement and the political class. Two suspects have been detained by police, with Minister of Interior Marcin Kierwiński confirming that the perpetrators had prior criminal records. The incident has intensified the domestic debate regarding migration, manifesting in heated exchanges within the Polish parliament.
* **Political Shifts Within the Opposition:** Internal dynamics in the PiS (Law and Justice) party are shifting as reports suggest a strategic attempt to cultivate a more moderate faction under the influence of figures like Mateusz Morawiecki. Simultaneously, government figures are looking toward the 2030 presidential horizon, with officials suggesting Radosław Sikorski as a potential candidate for the Civic Coalition (KO).
* **Preparations for Armed Forces Day:** Despite political turbulence, state preparations for the upcoming Armed Forces Day celebrations are underway, with the government finalizing the program for commemorative events in Warsaw and Gdynia.
