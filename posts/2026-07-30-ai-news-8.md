## WORLD

* **Escalating Tensions in Iraq:** A U.S.-Saudi joint operation targeting Iran-aligned militias in Iraq has heightened fears of a broader regional conflict. Analysts warn that the strikes, intended to force disarmament, may instead embolden these groups to engage more deeply in the ongoing war.
* **Climate Crisis Hits Europe:** Scotland is currently mobilizing emergency resources to contain severe wildfires sweeping through the Cairngorms. The event underscores a growing climate crisis, as unprecedented heat causes massive ecological damage to moorlands and forests across the European continent.

## POLAND

* **Airspace Violation Incident:** Tensions in Poland are high following reports of a Russian cruise missile entering Polish airspace during a massive aerial assault on Ukraine. Authorities have confirmed the intrusion, with Ukrainian Foreign Minister Andrij Sybiha explicitly identifying the projectile as Russian. Military operations in the region have been intensified, supported by NATO’s advanced aerial refueling capabilities.
* **Political Realignment:** The Polish parliamentary landscape has shifted with the official registration of "Rozwój Plus," a new political club formed by 40 deputies, primarily consisting of former Law and Justice (PiS) members. The formation of this group marks a significant evolution in the country's opposition structure.
