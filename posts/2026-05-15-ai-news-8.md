## WORLD

*   **US-China Tech Standoff:** Following the recent summit between President Trump and Xi Jinping, the future of Nvidia’s operations in China remains highly uncertain. The geopolitical tension is accelerating China’s domestic "chip sovereignty" drive, with firms increasingly pivoting to local alternatives like Huawei to mitigate reliance on Western technology.
*   **Technological Warfare in Ukraine:** Ukraine’s Defense Minister, Mykhailo Fedorov, has intensified the focus on autonomous military technologies. Positioning the country as a laboratory for modern warfare, Fedorov views "killer robots" and AI-driven combat systems as the cornerstone of Ukraine's future defense strategy against conventional Russian military superiority.
*   **Supreme Court Abortion Ruling:** The US Supreme Court has intervened to temporarily restore access to abortion medication, providing a brief reprieve in a volatile legal battle that continues to shape the American reproductive rights landscape.

## POLAND

*   **Government Friction Over IPN Leadership:** The Sejm has abruptly removed the vote for a new president of the Institute of National Remembrance (IPN) from its agenda. The move highlights a widening rift within the ruling coalition, where the Civic Coalition (KO) opposes the nomination of Mateusz Szpytma, while the Polish People's Party (PSL) and segments of Poland 2050 remain in support.
*   **Crypto-Asset Legislation Advances:** The Polish Sejm has passed a significant government-led bill regulating the crypto-asset market. The vote, which saw 241 members in favor, represents a long-awaited regulatory milestone aimed at bringing transparency and oversight to the rapidly growing digital assets sector in Poland.
*   **Rising Inflation Pressures:** Data from the Central Statistical Office (GUS) confirms a slight uptick in inflation, which reached 3.2% year-on-year in April. The rise is driven largely by increasing costs in the services sector, which outpaced commodity price growth, signaling a challenging economic environment for the national treasury.
