## WORLD

* **Iran Escalates Tensions with Gulf States:** Iran's national security adviser, Mohsen Rezaei, has issued a stern warning to neighboring Gulf states, demanding they refrain from participating in the Trump administration's economic sanctions against Tehran. The rhetoric threatens to destabilize regional energy flows, with Iran vowing to halt oil exports from the region.
* **Ukrainian Political Discord:** A significant rift has emerged in Kyiv as the former defense minister publicly criticized President Zelensky regarding government corruption and called for wartime elections. President Zelensky has formally rejected these demands, maintaining that holding elections during the ongoing conflict would pose an existential risk to the country’s stability.
* **US-Liberia Deportation Accord:** The Trump administration has initiated a new large-scale deportation program, beginning with the transfer of 20 individuals to Liberia. This move marks the first phase of an agreement to deport up to 1,200 migrants to the West African nation as part of a tightened immigration policy.

## POLAND

* **Tensions Mount in Belarus:** The regional judicial system in Grodno has officially labeled the Polish Catholic weekly *Gość Niedzielny* as an "extremist" organization. This move, which restricts the publication's online presence, is viewed as a continued crackdown on independent media and Polish influence within the country.
* **Diplomatic Fallout with Moscow:** Russian President Vladimir Putin has commented on recent statements made by Polish Prime Minister Donald Tusk. The exchange, centered on the investigation into the Nord Stream pipeline sabotage, highlights the hardening stance between Warsaw and Moscow regarding judicial sovereignty and international security.
* **Social Policy Challenges:** Legislative shifts concerning same-sex partnerships are meeting resistance at the local government level, with the mayor of Zakopane publicly signaling opposition to the enforcement of the new regulations, highlighting the persistent cultural and legal divide within the country.
