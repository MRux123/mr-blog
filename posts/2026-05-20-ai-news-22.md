## WORLD

* **US Legal Action Against Raúl Castro:** Federal prosecutors have brought formal charges against former Cuban leader Raúl Castro and five other individuals. The indictment relates to the 1996 downing of two planes, alleging conspiracy to kill US nationals, murder, and the destruction of aircraft. This unprecedented legal move marks a significant escalation in tensions between the United States and the Cuban leadership regarding historical human rights violations.

## POLAND

* **Senate Rejects Presidential Referendum Request:** The Polish Senate has formally denied President Karol Nawrocki’s motion to hold a nationwide referendum concerning European Union climate policy. The proposal was defeated by a vote of 62 to 32, with one abstention, signaling a major legislative setback for the President’s political agenda.
* **Tragic Death at Daycare Facility:** Authorities in Ząbkach, near Warsaw, have launched an investigation following the death of a toddler under two years of age at a local daycare center. Police and the public prosecutor are currently examining the circumstances of the incident, which has prompted calls for a review of safety protocols in early childhood education facilities across the region.
