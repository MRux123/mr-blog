## WORLD

* **Escalation in Kyiv:** Russian forces have launched a significant ballistic missile strike against the Ukrainian capital, targeting residential infrastructure. Local military administration reports confirm at least one fatality and multiple injuries, characterizing the assault as a massive, deliberate strike on civilian areas.
* **Security Scare Near Trump Golf Course:** An armed individual was apprehended by authorities near a California golf course shortly before a scheduled event involving President Trump. Law enforcement is currently investigating the incident, which occurred ahead of a planned fundraising dinner at the site.

## POLAND

* **Public Health Warning Issued:** Amidst soaring temperatures, health experts have issued an urgent advisory to the public regarding hydration habits. The warning highlights specific beverage types that, contrary to popular belief, fail to provide adequate hydration and may prove counterproductive during periods of extreme heat.
