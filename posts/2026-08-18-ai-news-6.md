## WORLD

* **Escalating Rhetoric Between London and Moscow:** Tensions have reached a new high as the Russian embassy in London issued a stern warning, stating that the UK "will pay" for supplying drones to Ukraine. Moscow contends that British involvement in supplying military hardware for strikes on Russian territory makes London a direct accomplice in the conflict. In response, the UK Ministry of Defence maintains its steadfast commitment to supporting Ukraine, affirming that Britain stands "shoulder to shoulder" with its ally.
* **Trump’s Shifting Strategy on the Korean Peninsula:** Former President Donald Trump has signaled a significant shift in his approach to North Korea, citing a "very good relationship" with Kim Jong Un. Trump announced that the U.S. intends to "substantially reduce" joint military exercises with South Korea, a move that critics suggest could alter the security architecture of the region.

## POLAND

* **Flash Floods Return to Głuchołazy:** The town of Głuchołazy is once again grappling with severe flooding, prompting a defensive response from the government. Government spokesperson Adam Szłapka, while addressing the crisis, drew a controversial comparison to Japan’s approach to natural disasters, arguing that certain weather catastrophes remain beyond the reach of human engineering and full prevention.
* **Economic Pressures on Polish Students:** The cost-of-living crisis is hitting the education sector hard, with students reporting "absurd" rental prices for accommodations. Reports indicate that a basic 7-square-meter room now commands prices as high as 1,800 PLN, raising concerns about the accessibility of higher education and the stability of the housing market ahead of the new academic term.
* **Hydrological Alerts Issued by IMGW:** The Institute of Meteorology and Water Management (IMGW) has issued urgent flood warnings for five provinces across eastern and central Poland. Authorities expect a rapid rise in water levels and are advising residents to monitor local updates as the alerts remain in effect through midday.
