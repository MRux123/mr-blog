## WORLD

* **Strained UK-Israel Relations:** Diplomatic ties between the United Kingdom and Israel have reached a historic low. This escalation follows London's implementation of a trade ban targeting products from Israeli settlements in the occupied West Bank, a move that has drawn a sharp response from Israeli authorities and underscores deepening geopolitical friction.

## POLAND

* **Arrest of Piotr Niemczyk:** In a development drawing significant public attention, former opposition figure Piotr Niemczyk has been taken into custody. Reports link his detention to the recent activities and protests staged by the "Ostatnie Pokolenie" (Last Generation) activist group.
* **Recognition for Infrastructure Development:** Minister of Infrastructure Dariusz Klimczak has been honored with the "Man of the Year 2026" award at the Economic Forum in Karpacz. The accolade recognizes his role in implementing national development plans, marking a significant political milestone for the current administration's economic agenda.
