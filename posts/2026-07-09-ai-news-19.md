## WORLD

*   **Heightened Tensions in Iran:** The regional conflict has intensified following a series of significant strikes by Tehran. U.S. Central Command reports that 90 Iranian targets were hit in the latest military engagement. The casualty toll has reached 14 since Tuesday, according to the Iranian health ministry, creating a climate of extreme volatility in the Middle East.
*   **Maritime Disruptions in Hormuz:** The ongoing instability has led to a noticeable decline in maritime traffic. Data indicates that a growing number of oil, gas, and cargo vessels are avoiding the vital Hormuz waterway in response to this week’s heightened military activity, raising concerns about potential impacts on global energy supply chains.
*   **Ukrainian Offensive in Crimea:** Ukraine has launched a coordinated strike against Russian naval assets in the vicinity of Crimea. This operation is viewed as a strategic pivot in the conflict, aimed at choking off vital logistics and fuel supply lines that support the Russian occupation of the peninsula.

## POLAND

*   **Legal Scrutiny for Former NIK President:** The Polish political landscape remains focused on accountability, as a formal indictment has been issued against Marian B., the former head of the Supreme Audit Office (NIK). This move marks a significant development in the ongoing investigation into his tenure and professional conduct.
*   **Diplomatic Tensions with Kyiv:** Addressing historical friction, KO politician Paweł Kowal emphasized the difficulty of reconciling Polish historical findings regarding the Volhynia massacre with current Ukrainian interpretations. Kowal stressed that acknowledging these differences is essential for a mature, future-oriented diplomatic relationship between Warsaw and Kyiv.
*   **Right-Wing Alignment:** Speculation is mounting regarding a potential meeting between PiS leader Jarosław Kaczyński and Hungarian Prime Minister Viktor Orbán. Sources suggest that the two prominent figures are coordinating schedules for a possible meeting in Poland this coming weekend, potentially signaling an attempt to consolidate conservative political strategies in Central Europe.
