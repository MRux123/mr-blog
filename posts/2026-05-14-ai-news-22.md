## WORLD

*   **US-Iran Military Standoff:** Admiral Brad Cooper, commander of US forces in the Middle East, claims that 90 percent of Iran's defense industry has been neutralized during the current conflict. However, international observers express significant skepticism regarding these figures, suggesting that Tehran’s military capabilities remain far more substantial than official US reports indicate.
*   **Diplomatic Moves in Cuba:** In an effort to address the deepening energy crisis on the island, a high-ranking CIA official has reportedly visited Havana. The move follows a renewed offer of humanitarian aid from the United States, aimed at mitigating the impact of the ongoing oil blockade.
*   **Russian Disinformation Campaign:** A state-sponsored bot network, known as "Matryoshka," has launched a massive disinformation campaign targeting Armenia. The propaganda focuses on stoking fears of an impending war with Russia to destabilize Prime Minister Nikol Pashinyan’s administration ahead of the upcoming elections.

## POLAND

*   **Extradition Proceedings Against Ziobro:** The Ministry of Justice has finalized a formal extradition request for former Justice Minister Zbigniew Ziobro, currently residing in the United States. While the prosecution is prepared to act, the initiation of the legal procedure remains contingent upon a ruling by the District Court in Warsaw regarding an appeal against Ziobro’s temporary arrest warrant.
*   **Internal Political Friction:** The political landscape within the opposition remains volatile, as evidenced by a public confrontation between former PiS senator Jacek Włosowicz and the party’s spokesperson, Rafał Bochenek. This exchange highlights the deepening internal divisions and the aggressive rhetoric currently characterizing the political discourse within the party ranks.
