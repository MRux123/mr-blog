## WORLD

*   **Escalation in Russia:** A massive, unprecedented drone assault targeted the Moscow region on the final day of Russian elections. Authorities reported hundreds of drones downed, while Ukraine claims the use of newly developed ballistic missiles. The strikes, which also hit oil refineries, resulted in at least two fatalities and underscored the widening scope of the conflict.
*   **German Political Crossroads:** Germany is holding pivotal elections today as Chancellor Merz faces intense scrutiny. The outcomes are expected to significantly influence the country's political trajectory, with particular focus on the electoral performance of the far-right Alternative for Germany (AfD) party in eastern regions, which could signal a broader shift in European political sentiment.

## POLAND

*   **Security and NATO Posture:** Amid growing geopolitical instability, the Chairman of the NATO Military Committee has reaffirmed the alliance's readiness to respond to any Russian escalation, signaling a heightened state of preparedness for the region.
*   **Kraków Mayoral Debate:** The race for the presidency of Kraków has reached a critical stage, with the Strefa Czystego Transportu (Clean Transport Zone) emerging as a primary point of contention among candidates. Recent polling indicates a tight contest between frontrunner Łukasz Gibała and Monika Piątkowska as the city prepares for a potential runoff.
*   **Legal Developments:** Authorities have moved forward with an indictment in the high-profile case involving the theft of a vehicle belonging to the family of Prime Minister Donald Tusk, marking a significant step in a case that garnered substantial public attention.
