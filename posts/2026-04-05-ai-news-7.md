## WORLD

* **U.S.-Iran Military Confrontation:** Tensions have reached a boiling point after U.S. forces conducted a high-risk rescue operation to extract an injured crew member from a downed fighter jet deep within Iranian territory. While President Trump confirmed the successful recovery of the officer and additional crew, Iranian Revolutionary Guard officials claim to have downed several U.S. "flying objects," including a C-130 transport plane, and have threatened further unspecified retaliation.
* **Tragedy at Louisiana Parade:** A severe incident occurred in New Iberia, Louisiana, where a vehicle plowed into a crowd gathered for a Laotian New Year celebration. Authorities report at least 18 individuals injured, many critically. Emergency services remain on the scene as the investigation into the motives and circumstances of the crash continues.

## POLAND

* **Political Discourse and Security:** As the nation observes the Easter holiday, political focus remains sharp regarding the regional security crisis. High-ranking officials, including presidential advisors, are scheduled to address the implications of the U.S.-Iran conflict and the potential strategic shifts for Poland’s defense policy in upcoming Sunday morning political broadcasts.
* **Easter Observance:** The country marks the central event of the Christian calendar, with the presidential couple extending holiday wishes to the public. Meanwhile, the Church highlights the spiritual significance of the Resurrection, serving as the foundational moment for the faithful during this festive period.
