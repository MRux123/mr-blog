## WORLD

* **Energy Crisis Deepens in Romania:** The Cernavodă nuclear power plant, responsible for 20% of Romania's electricity, has been forced to shut down due to a critical drop in water levels in the Danube River caused by extreme heat. Projections suggest a prolonged outage, raising concerns about energy security in the region.
* **Tensions Aboard the USS Abraham Lincoln:** The US aircraft carrier has been at sea for over 250 days, leading to a breakdown in crew welfare. Reports of food shortages, failing infrastructure, and a deteriorating mental health climate among sailors have sparked serious concern regarding the sustainability of such extended naval deployments.

## POLAND

* **Major Data Breach Affects Millions:** A massive cyberattack has compromised the personal data of 19 million Poles, linked to the medical platform MyDr. Prime Minister Donald Tusk has characterized the event as an extortion attempt, and a formal investigation by the prosecutor’s office is currently underway.
* **Controversial Legislative Shift on Juvenile Justice:** The government is pushing for a hardening of the penal code ahead of the upcoming elections. The proposed changes would allow 14-year-olds to be tried as adults and potentially serve prison sentences, a move that has sparked intense debate regarding the threshold of criminal responsibility.
* **Geopolitical Tensions over Judicial Appointments:** The nomination of Maciej Berek—a former government minister—for a seat on the Constitutional Tribunal has drawn sharp criticism. Opposition figures and political commentators have questioned the procedural propriety of moving directly from a government executive role to a judicial position, labeling it a potential threat to institutional independence.
