## WORLD

* **Middle East Crisis Deepens:** President Trump has cast significant doubt on the viability of the recent cease-fire with Iran, openly threatening further military strikes. Following an exchange of hostilities, the U.S. remains poised for potential escalation, as Tehran warns of a "fearless" response.
* **Strategic Shift for Ukraine:** In a pivotal development at the NATO summit, President Trump announced that Ukraine will be granted a license to domestically produce Patriot missile systems. This move is intended to bolster Ukrainian defensive capabilities against Russian ballistic missile threats, despite concerns over production timelines.
* **ICC Leadership Scandal:** The International Criminal Court is facing internal turmoil following a confidential report revealing that the chief prosecutor engaged in sexual harassment. Member states are currently deliberating the potential removal of the official, casting a shadow over the institution's integrity.

## POLAND

* **NATO Summit Outcomes:** Following the high-stakes summit in Ankara, the Polish government is evaluating the strategic gains achieved for national security. The dialogue between Polish officials and the alliance remains a focal point for regional stability.
* **Domestic Healthcare Reform:** A heated political debate has emerged between government ministers and opposition figures regarding the future of the healthcare system. Proposed reforms—including the potential replacement of health contributions with a dedicated health tax—have sparked criticism, with opposition leaders labeling the suggestions as outdated initiatives.
* **Environmental Alert in the Baltic:** Scientists have issued a stern warning regarding a massive green bloom detected in the Baltic Sea via satellite imagery. The phenomenon, caused by rapid phytoplankton and cyanobacteria growth, poses a serious risk due to the presence of harmful toxins, impacting coastal safety.
