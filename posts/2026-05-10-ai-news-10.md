## WORLD

* **Escalation in Ukraine despite ceasefire:** Tensions have spiked as Russia reportedly violated a US-brokered three-day ceasefire, launching drone strikes against Ukraine. The attacks have resulted in civilian casualties, further complicating diplomatic efforts to restore stability in the region, while reports from the battlefield indicate that Russian forces continue to struggle with limited advancement due to pervasive drone warfare.
* **Public health emergencies and regional conflicts:** An outbreak of hantavirus on the cruise ship MV Hondius has led to multiple fatalities, prompting emergency evacuations in the Canary Islands. Simultaneously, global economic instability and wartime pressures are triggering mass layoffs in Iran, as the country faces severe internal challenges and communication shutdowns.
* **Geopolitical shifts in the Americas:** Following the ousting of the Venezuelan leadership, questions remain regarding the future of the nation’s displaced population and whether current political changes will facilitate the return of refugees. Additionally, the U.S. administration is signaling a shift in military alliances, with reports suggesting a potential withdrawal of forces from Italy.

## POLAND

* **Zbigniew Ziobro’s migration to the U.S.:** Emerging reports, including findings from Polsat News and RMF24, suggest that former Minister of Justice Zbigniew Ziobro has left Hungary and is currently residing in the United States. The situation remains a subject of intense political debate, with opposition figures calling for him to face the Polish judicial system.
* **Jarosław Kaczyński’s polarizing remarks:** During recent commemorations of the Smolensk catastrophe anniversary, PiS leader Jarosław Kaczyński delivered a controversial speech. His rhetoric, which focused on the future of the judicial system and the status of his political opponents, has sparked significant public and political reaction.
* **Public health warning:** A surge in reports regarding the presence of the "flesh-eating" *Vibrio* bacteria along coastal waters has raised alarms. Health experts emphasize the lethal danger of the pathogen, which has become more prevalent in the North Atlantic, urging caution regarding water exposure and the consumption of raw seafood.
