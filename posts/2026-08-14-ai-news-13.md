## WORLD

*   **Russian Interference in Moldova:** Recent investigations reveal a systematic campaign by Moscow to destabilize Moldova’s pro-Western administration. Efforts included the co-opting of Orthodox clergy, organized vote-buying schemes, and the establishment of paramilitary training camps to undermine the nation's democratic trajectory.
*   **Climate-Driven Transportation Failures:** As extreme heatwaves plague the European continent, transport infrastructure is reaching a breaking point. Experts warn that regional railways and roads, designed for a temperate climate that no longer exists, are struggling to maintain functionality under persistent, record-breaking temperatures.
*   **Release of Kidnapped Missionary in Niger:** Kevin Rideout, an American pilot and missionary, has been safely released after being held captive for nine months in Niamey. The mission organization, SIM International, confirmed he is currently under the care of U.S. officials and is in good physical condition.

## POLAND

*   **Crisis in Regional Development Agency:** The regional government of Lower Silesia has dismissed the CEO of the Karkonosze Regional Development Agency (KARR), Hubert Papaj, alongside the entire supervisory board. The decision follows a corruption scandal involving the mismanagement of state funds intended for flood relief efforts.
*   **Shifting Energy Policy:** In response to fluctuating global markets and the ongoing economic impact of geopolitical tensions, the Polish government has reinstated the "CPN" program. This initiative introduces new, lower maximum fuel price caps to alleviate the cost-of-living pressure on consumers and businesses.
