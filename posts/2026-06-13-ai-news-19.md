## WORLD

*   **US-Iran Peace Negotiations:** Global attention is fixed on a potential peace deal between the United States and Iran. While President Donald Trump has announced that a deal is scheduled to be signed this Sunday, Tehran has expressed caution, disputing the timeline and stating that negotiations remain ongoing for the coming days.
*   **Ethical Supply Chain Exposure:** A major investigation by Global Witness has revealed that multinational giants, including Amazon, Sony, and Ericsson, are likely linked to the use of conflict minerals. Coltan sourced from mines occupied by the M23 militia in the Democratic Republic of the Congo—a group notorious for human rights abuses—has reportedly entered the supply chains of these global brands.

## POLAND

*   **Trump’s Diplomatic Marathon:** President Trump is set to engage in a high-stakes diplomatic schedule, transitioning from a domestic event to the G7 summit in France. His agenda includes a critical meeting with Ukrainian President Volodymyr Zelensky and consultations with Middle Eastern leaders, underscoring the intensity of current international geopolitical realignments.
*   **War Impacts Reach Moscow:** The ripple effects of the ongoing conflict have reached the Russian capital. Major fuel networks in Moscow are reporting shortages and limiting sales of gasoline and diesel, a direct consequence of the consistent and systematic Ukrainian strikes on Russian oil infrastructure.
*   **Tragedy in the Alps:** The mountaineering community is mourning a devastating loss after seven climbers perished in the Alps within a 24-hour period. Accidents occurred across several major peaks, including the Mont Blanc massif, prompting authorities to reiterate safety warnings for high-altitude expeditions.
