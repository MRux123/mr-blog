## WORLD

* **Republican Midterm Collapse:** The United States political landscape has shifted significantly following a Democratic sweep of both the Senate and the House of Representatives. Vice President J.D. Vance acknowledged the magnitude of the defeat, attributing the loss to the current climate of war. The results have triggered internal turmoil within the Republican Party, with critics pointing to Donald Trump's legacy and voter alienation as primary causes for the electoral disaster.
* **Geopolitical Ripple Effects:** International observers are closely monitoring the impact of the U.S. election outcome. While Canada eyes potential relief in trade negotiations, Israel faces a complex political reality as bipartisan support in Washington appears to be fraying due to Democratic concerns regarding the war in Gaza. Simultaneously, global markets are bracing for volatility concerning oil prices and shifting foreign policies.
* **Escalating Conflict in Ukraine:** Tensions remain high as Kyiv reports a devastating "double-tap" Russian bombing in a Ukrainian village, resulting in at least 40 fatalities. The incident, purportedly captured by bodycam footage, highlights the ongoing brutality of the conflict.

## POLAND

* **Constitutional Crisis Deepens:** The domestic political situation has reached a boiling point regarding the status of the Constitutional Tribunal. Minister of Climate and Environment Paulina Hennig-Kloska has leveled severe accusations against President Andrzej Duda, labeling his recent actions as a "coup d'état." Tensions remain high as the Tribunal prepares for a plenary assembly to discuss internal regulatory changes amid claims of bias.
* **Institutional Imbalance:** Political discourse continues to focus on the interplay between the presidency and the government, with recent disputes at the National Security Council (RBN) serving as a flashpoint for the growing institutional deadlock in the country.
