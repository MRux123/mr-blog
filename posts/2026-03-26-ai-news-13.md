## WORLD

* **Middle East Conflict Escalation:** Regional instability has reached a critical juncture following the reported death of Alireza Tangsiri, a key Iranian naval commander, in an Israeli airstrike. The incident coincides with heightened military activity on Kharg Island, a vital oil hub now reportedly being fortified by Iran, and intensifying pressure from the Trump administration on Tehran to accept a peace proposal.
* **Global Tech Regulation:** The social media landscape faces a potential paradigm shift following a landmark legal verdict regarding platform addiction. Industry analysts suggest this ruling could trigger unprecedented regulatory changes for major tech corporations, fundamentally altering how these platforms operate globally.
* **Sudan Crisis:** The humanitarian situation in Sudan continues to deteriorate as the civil war approaches its fourth year. Recent drone strikes on civilian targets, including a market in North Darfur, have resulted in at least 28 deaths, highlighting the persistent violence between the army and the Rapid Support Forces.

## POLAND

* **Government Response to Fuel Prices:** Prime Minister Donald Tusk has officially announced the implementation of the "CPN" package in response to the rising global fuel prices spurred by the conflict in the Middle East. The government aims to stabilize the domestic market through these emergency measures.
* **Political Standoff on Judiciary Reform:** Legislative efforts regarding the amendment of the Code of Criminal Procedure have hit a stalemate. Reports indicate that the government coalition lacks the necessary support to override the presidential veto issued by President Karol Nawrocki, effectively stalling the controversial legal reform.
* **Public Readiness and Weather Alerts:** As the nation prepares for the high-stakes 2026 World Cup qualifying match between Poland and Albania, authorities have confirmed the event will take place under a closed roof at PGE Narodowy. Concurrently, the Institute of Meteorology and Water Management (IMGW) has issued nationwide weather warnings, urging citizens to exercise caution.
