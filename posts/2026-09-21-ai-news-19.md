## WORLD

*   **Escalation in the Middle East:** A convergence of conflicts is destabilizing the region. Following US and Israeli military actions against Iran, Houthi forces in Yemen have re-engaged in active warfare, creating a volatile secondary front that threatens global energy markets. Concurrently, Iraq’s new leadership faces the daunting challenge of dismantling Iran-backed militias, a task that remains critical for regional stability.
*   **Controversy Over U.S. Military Strikes:** The United Nations has issued a grave warning regarding U.S. operations against alleged drug-trafficking vessels. International legal experts and U.N. human rights investigators suggest that the Trump administration's "deadly attacks" may violate international law and potentially qualify as crimes against humanity, sparking intense global scrutiny.
*   **Political Consolidation in Russia:** Recent parliamentary election results underscore President Vladimir Putin's firm grip on power. Analysts suggest the process was engineered to reaffirm the dominance of the autocratic system, ensuring that despite external and internal pressures, the Kremlin’s control over the state apparatus remains unchallenged.

## POLAND

*   **Military Alert in Eastern Poland:** The Operational Command of the Polish Armed Forces has scrambled fighter jets following significant Russian air attacks against Ukraine. An Rządowe Centrum Bezpieczeństwa (RCB) alert has been issued for the Lublin province, reflecting the heightened state of readiness due to the spillover risk from the ongoing conflict across the border.
*   **Shifts in EU Sanctions Policy:** A notable diplomatic turn is occurring within the European Union regarding sanctions on Russia. Sources indicate that France and Slovakia are advocating for the removal of two prominent oligarchs—Alisher Usmanov and Mikhail Fridman—from the EU’s sanctions list. This proposal, reportedly tied to a broader agreement to extend existing sanctions against other entities for another three years, marks a potential fracture in the unified approach to Russian financial restrictions.
*   **Internal Political Friction:** The ruling coalition is facing internal turbulence, underscored by public disagreements between PSL (Polish People's Party) and other coalition members. Tensions regarding ministerial expectations and policy direction, specifically concerning liquor regulations, highlight the growing strain on the current administration's cohesion.
