## WORLD

*   **Escalation in the Strait of Hormuz:** Regional stability is under severe pressure after Iranian forces opened fire on a tanker in the Strait of Hormuz. While the crew remains safe, the incident marks a significant escalation following Tehran's claims of re-establishing control over the vital maritime chokepoint, drawing immediate international concern.
*   **Estonia Bolsters Border Security:** In response to recurring Russian provocations, Estonia has launched an advanced border surveillance project. The deployment of a 14-tower radar system along the Narva river and Lake Peipus is designed to enhance detection capabilities against drones and other incursions, representing a unique technological approach to regional security.
*   **Global Concerns Over AI Autonomy:** The White House has held critical discussions with Anthropic regarding the "Mythos" AI model. The engagement underscores the growing dependency of government infrastructure on sophisticated AI, alongside mounting fears regarding the potential risks posed by highly advanced, autonomous models.

## POLAND

*   **Kremlin’s Historical Revisionism:** The Russian Foreign Ministry, through spokesperson Maria Zakharova, has targeted Poland with accusations of complicity during World War II. Warsaw is being placed on a Kremlin-curated "list of complicit nations," a move seen by observers as a calculated diplomatic provocation ahead of the upcoming Remembrance Day for Victims of the Soviet People.
*   **Tragic Loss of Young Life:** A national search operation in Częstochowa has concluded in tragedy following the discovery of the body of 17-year-old Miriam W. Local authorities and prosecutors have launched a formal investigation to determine the circumstances surrounding the teenager's death after she went missing while returning from school.
*   **Leadership Turbulence in Sports:** The Polish Ski Association (PZN) faces potential leadership changes as Apoloniusz Tajner, a former head of the organization, announced his intention to run for the presidency. His challenge to incumbent Adam Małysz signals internal friction within the association regarding its future strategic direction.
