## WORLD

* **Seeking Accountability and Finding Solidarity:** A powerful narrative of collective courage has emerged from Indonesia, where former students successfully campaigned for justice against an abusive head teacher. Their long-term efforts in gathering evidence not only led to legal accountability but also forged a deep personal bond, culminating in the marriage of two of the key survivors. This story highlights the long-term impact of institutional abuse and the resilience of those who unite to seek systemic change.
* **Music and Geopolitics:** Ed Sheeran has publicly addressed the controversy surrounding his recent tour, specifically the removal of rapper Macklemore. During his performance in Philadelphia, Sheeran spoke openly about the situation, including the sensitive topic of the Israel-Gaza conflict, marking a significant moment where an artist navigated the intersection of live entertainment and global political discourse.

## POLAND

* **Autumn Health Alert:** As September progresses, medical experts in Poland are cautioning the public against misinterpreting emerging health symptoms. While many associate the current seasonal changes with the common cold, doctors warn that autumn remains a high-risk period for allergy sufferers. Distinguishing between allergic reactions and respiratory infections is essential for proper treatment, as late-summer and early-autumn allergens continue to affect a significant portion of the population.
