## WORLD

* **Renewed hostilities despite peace dialogue:** A significant missile barrage has struck Kyiv, occurring immediately after Moscow signaled a willingness to resume peace talks. 
* **Trump administration optimism:** US President Donald Trump claims the world is closer than ever to ending the ongoing conflict, highlighting a stark contrast between high-level diplomatic optimism and the deteriorating security situation on the ground.

## POLAND

* **Heightened regional security alert:** The Polish Government Security Centre (RCB) has issued an urgent alert for residents of the Lublin region.
* **Military response to airspace breach:** Polish and Allied air forces were scrambled once again as a defensive measure following intense Russian aerial activity directed at targets within Ukraine, underscoring the ongoing security risks to Poland's eastern border.
