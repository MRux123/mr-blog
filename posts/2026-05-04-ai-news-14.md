## WORLD

* **Escalation in Moscow:** Russian air defenses were breached as a drone struck a high-rise in Moscow, occurring just days before a major military parade. This incident marks a significant development in the ongoing conflict, as Ukraine continues to extend the reach of its long-range strike capabilities.
* **Hantavirus Crisis at Sea:** A cruise ship has been barred from docking in Cape Verde following a suspected hantavirus outbreak that has already claimed three lives. Global health authorities are investigating the situation to prevent further transmission of this rare, rodent-borne respiratory virus.
* **Geopolitical Realignment in India:** The BJP’s success in West Bengal marks a pivotal moment in Indian politics. By securing a victory in one of the country's most challenging political frontiers, the party has significantly altered the power dynamics of the state and bolstered the long-term political trajectory of Prime Minister Narendra Modi.

## POLAND

* **Executive Clash over Constitutional Reform:** Prime Minister Donald Tusk has publicly dismissed the newly formed "Council of the New Constitution," initiated by President Karol Nawrocki. Tusk characterized the initiative as a political maneuver aimed at centralizing executive power rather than a serious effort to reform the constitutional order.
* **Legal Scandal:** A high-profile manhunt is underway for lawyer Paweł Kozanecki, a figure widely recognized from a controversial legal case dubbed the "coffin on wheels." The issuance of an arrest warrant follows his disappearance, sparking intense public interest.
* **National Examinations:** Thousands of Polish students have commenced their *Matura* exams, the critical final assessment for secondary education. The event has gained media attention not only for its academic weight but also for the participation of public figures, such as professional athlete Oskar Pietuszewski, who balanced championship duties with his examination requirements.
