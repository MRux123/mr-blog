## WORLD

* **Escalating Conflict in the Middle East:** Hostilities between the U.S. and Iran have intensified following the collapse of a recent cease-fire. Reports indicate a significant expansion in the scope of military operations, with critical infrastructure, including water facilities, now becoming direct targets of strikes, raising concerns about humanitarian stability in the region.
* **Lamine Yamal and Spain's Cultural Evolution:** As 19-year-old soccer star Lamine Yamal prepares for the World Cup final, his presence has sparked a profound national conversation. His identity as a son of African migrants has made him a focal point in the debate over the definition of Spanish identity within an increasingly multicultural society.
* **Commercial Space Milestone:** Skyroot Aerospace has successfully launched India’s first commercial orbital rocket, the Vikram-1. This achievement marks a pivotal moment for India's space-tech sector, positioning the unicorn start-up as a serious competitor in the growing global private space industry.

## POLAND

* **Political Polarization Deepens:** Recent polling data from SW Research suggests a public preference for Prime Minister Donald Tusk over former Prime Minister Mateusz Morawiecki. The political tension is further evidenced by a public exchange between the President's Chief of Staff, Zbigniew Bogucki, and the Prime Minister regarding the veto of legislation on the status of "next of kin," signaling a continued stalemate between the current government and presidential administration.
* **Severe Weather Warnings:** The Institute of Meteorology and Water Management (IMGW) has issued urgent weather alerts for 14 provinces across Poland. With second-degree warnings in the south and an active RCB alert, authorities are warning citizens of severe thunderstorms, hail, and potential power outages caused by high winds.
