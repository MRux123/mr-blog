## WORLD

*   **Tragedy in Venezuela:** A catastrophic earthquake has struck Venezuela, resulting in 920 confirmed fatalities. International rescue teams have been deployed to the region, where hundreds remain trapped under rubble. The United States has launched a significant humanitarian relief operation, dispatching naval support, aircraft, and specialized search-and-rescue personnel to assist the overwhelmed local government.
*   **Escalation in the Middle East:** Tensions between the United States and Iran have reached a critical peak following a retaliatory U.S. military strike. The action was taken in response to an earlier attack on a commercial cargo ship. Iran has formally accused the U.S. of violating bilateral agreements and has initiated further strikes against American-linked assets, signaling a volatile new phase in regional instability.
*   **Strategic Conflict in Ukraine:** Ukrainian forces have successfully targeted a key facility within Russia's defense industry located in Volgograd. The strike, acknowledged by President Volodymyr Zelensky, hit a plant responsible for manufacturing advanced missile launchers, marking a significant tactical blow to Russian military production capabilities.

## POLAND

*   **National Heatwave Alert:** Poland is currently under a state of high alert due to an extreme heatwave, with temperatures reaching up to 41°C. The Institute of Meteorology and Water Management (IMGW) has issued the highest, third-degree warnings across the country. Government agencies have urged citizens, particularly the elderly and vulnerable, to exercise extreme caution to avoid heat-related health emergencies.
*   **Puźniki Search Operations:** Official search efforts for the victims of the UPA in Puźniki have concluded without the discovery of the burial site. Despite the lack of immediate results, the Ministry of Culture has emphasized that the search mission is not terminated and that further research will be conducted to resolve this historical inquiry.
*   **Debate on Military Service:** The Ministry of National Defense has signaled potential shifts in mobilization strategy. Deputy Minister Magdalena Sobkowiak-Czarnecka stated that the possibility of introducing universal military service remains a topic for discussion, reflecting the changing security environment in the region.
