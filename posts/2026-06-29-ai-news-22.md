## WORLD

*   **Humanitarian Catastrophe in Venezuela:** The death toll following the catastrophic twin earthquakes in Venezuela has surged to over 1,700, with officials warning that this number is likely an undercount. Over 5,000 people are injured, and nearly 16,000 have been displaced. Search and rescue operations are increasingly desperate as survivors use basic tools to sift through rubble, with authorities describing the event as the worst disaster in the nation's history.
*   **Violent Incident in Germany:** German police have reported that a shooting near Hamburg, involving a perpetrator of Turkish origin born in Germany, was likely motivated by a domestic dispute concerning child custody.

## POLAND

*   **Lewandowski Joins Chicago Fire:** In a major shift for Polish football, Robert Lewandowski has officially confirmed his move to the Major League Soccer side Chicago Fire. The striker has signed a contract spanning until the end of the 2027–2028 season, joining the club as a Designated Player.
*   **Infrastructure Crisis and Rail Disruptions:** The Polish railway network is facing significant operational challenges due to record-breaking heatwaves. PKP PLK reported the cancellation of 258 trains on Monday alone due to thermal damage. Additionally, a separate incident saw a fire break out on a train in Dębica, necessitating the evacuation of 250 passengers and resulting in one injury.
