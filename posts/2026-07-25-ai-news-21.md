## WORLD

*   **Escalating Wildfires in Europe:** A severe environmental crisis is unfolding across Spain and France, as massive wildfires threaten major population centers, including areas near Madrid and Bordeaux. Hundreds of thousands have been forced to evacuate as emergency services struggle to contain the rapidly spreading blazes.
*   **Security Incident at Berlin Pride:** German authorities have launched an intensive manhunt following a violent incident in central Berlin, where a vehicle was reported to have driven into a crowd during Pride celebrations, resulting in multiple injuries. 
*   **Geopolitical Instability in Georgia:** Authorities in Tbilisi are investigating a major power grid failure as a potential act of sabotage. This is the second such incident in two days, raising concerns about regional stability near the Russian border.

## POLAND

*   **Tragic Loss of a Cultural Icon:** The Polish art world is mourning the passing of Józef Hulka, a renowned folk artist who has died at the age of 100. Hulka was widely celebrated for his contributions to traditional Polish craftsmanship.
*   **Mountain Fatality in the Tatras:** A Polish citizen has died in the Slovakian Tatra Mountains after a fatal fall from a height of 150 meters. Local mountain rescue teams have confirmed the tragic incident, which serves as a stark reminder of the dangers facing hikers in the region.
