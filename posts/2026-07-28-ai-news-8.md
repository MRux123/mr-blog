## WORLD

*   **Trump-Zelensky Summit in Washington:** President Trump is set to host Ukrainian President Volodymyr Zelensky at the White House this week. The high-stakes meeting, which coincides with Zelensky's attendance at the funeral of Senator Lindsey Graham, will be closely watched to determine the current trajectory of US-Ukraine diplomatic relations and potential shifts in policy.
*   **Seismic Crisis in Japan:** A powerful 7.1 magnitude earthquake has struck Japan’s Kumamoto prefecture, triggering widespread power outages and prompting the government to issue urgent emergency alerts. Seismological data suggests the tremors reached intensities that pushed the limits of standard measurement scales.
*   **Climate Reality Check in Spain:** Severe wildfires are currently encroaching upon the outskirts of Madrid, serving as a stark reminder of the environmental challenges facing the economically booming Spanish capital.

## POLAND

*   **MSZ Refutes Russian Disinformation:** The Polish Ministry of Foreign Affairs (MSZ) has issued a sharp response to recent claims made by Vladimir Putin regarding territorial ambitions in Western Ukraine. The Ministry officially declared that Poland has no territorial claims toward Ukraine and characterized Putin’s statements as a "primitive attempt at disinformation."
*   **PiS Political Realignment:** Following a period of internal conflict, senior politician Ryszard Terlecki has publicly forecasted a permanent split within the Law and Justice (PiS) party. While acknowledging the division, he signaled that a coalition between the factions remains a strategic possibility for the 2027 parliamentary elections.
*   **Ziobro Faces Extradition Request:** Former Justice Minister Zbigniew Ziobro has publicly rejected the government's extradition request, framing the move as a politically motivated act of lawlessness against him.
