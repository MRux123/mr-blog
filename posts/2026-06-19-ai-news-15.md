## WORLD

*   **Mideast De-escalation Efforts:** Following intense recent exchanges of fire, reports indicate that Israel and Hezbollah have reached a ceasefire agreement. The breakthrough comes despite earlier claims that Iran had delayed diplomatic talks in response to Israeli military actions in Lebanon.
*   **Diplomatic Crisis Between Rome and Washington:** Tensions between Italy and the U.S. have spiked after President Trump claimed Prime Minister Giorgia Meloni "begged" him for a photo at the G7 summit—a narrative Meloni has firmly dismissed as fabricated. The friction has reached a critical point, leading Italian Foreign Minister Antonio Tajani to cancel his scheduled visit to the United States.
*   **Escalation in the Russia-Ukraine War:** The conflict has entered a new phase of intensity as Ukraine executed a successful drone strike on Moscow. While military analysts view the attack as a significant morale boost for Kyiv, experts emphasize that only the introduction of ballistic missiles would be sufficient to force a shift in Russia's long-term war objectives.

## POLAND

*   **Civil Defense Readiness in Russia:** Regional authorities in southern Russia and occupied Ukrainian territories have initiated exercises focused on mass mobilization. These are the first such organized activities since the national mobilization order of 2022, signaling potential preparations for an escalation or rotation of forces.
*   **Political Controversy in Warsaw:** New information has surfaced regarding the management of the Southern Hospital (Szpital Południowy) in Warsaw. Allegations concerning irregularities have raised questions about Mayor Rafał Trzaskowski's oversight and prior knowledge of the situation, potentially impacting local administrative stability.
*   **Extreme Weather Alerts:** The Government Security Centre (RCB) has issued urgent weather warnings for northwestern Poland, cautioning residents against incoming violent thunderstorms accompanied by high-velocity winds.
