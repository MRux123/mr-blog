## WORLD

* **Brazilian Election Fallout and Trump's Involvement:** The political crisis in Brazil has deepened following the election loss of the incumbent administration. Flávio Bolsonaro has declared himself the "leader of Brazil" from the United States, a move facilitated by Donald Trump and his aides. Trump has publicly denounced the Brazilian election results as a "disgrace," signaling a period of significant diplomatic friction and potential instability in the region.
* **Escalation in Ukraine:** A deadly Russian drone strike has hit a railway station in the central city of Dnipro. The attack has resulted in at least 14 fatalities and numerous injuries, including children, marking a severe escalation in the ongoing conflict and drawing immediate international condemnation.
* **Geopolitical Sanctions on Rwanda:** The U.S. Treasury Department has imposed new sanctions on Rwanda, accusing the nation of undermining a U.S.-brokered peace agreement by providing support to rebel groups in the eastern Democratic Republic of Congo. This action highlights a major point of contention in U.S.-African relations, particularly as the administration continues to manage conflicts previously declared as "ended."

## POLAND

* **Tusk Frames 2027 Election as Referendum on EU Membership:** Prime Minister Donald Tusk has declared that the upcoming parliamentary elections in 2027 will serve as a crucial referendum on Poland’s future within the European Union. Tusk emphasized the fundamental nature of this choice, linking the nation's security, civil rights, and democratic freedoms to Poland's continued integration with the bloc.
* **Internal Coalition Friction over Church Funding:** The ruling coalition is facing significant internal disagreement regarding the proposal by the Left to abolish the Church Fund. Władysław Kosiniak-Kamysz, leader of the PSL, has openly opposed the initiative, suggesting that any changes to religious financing should be handled through tax deductions at the individual level, creating a notable legislative hurdle for the current government.
