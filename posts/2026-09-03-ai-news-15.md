## WORLD

*   **Internal Strife in Kyiv:** President Volodymyr Zelensky has ordered an investigation into a violent confrontation between rival Ukrainian intelligence units in the capital. The incident, which occurred on the streets of Kyiv, has been labeled by the president as a "shameful event," raising concerns regarding military cohesion and internal security amidst the ongoing conflict.
*   **Escalating Regional Conflict:** A U.S. strike in a small Iranian town has resulted in significant civilian casualties, with local authorities reporting five deaths and dozens wounded at a wedding ceremony. Analysis of recovered weapons fragments suggests the use of American-manufactured munitions, further complicating the precarious geopolitical landscape in the Middle East.
*   **Passing of Gloria Steinem:** Renowned feminist activist, journalist, and cultural icon Gloria Steinem has died at the age of 92. Her passing marks the end of a transformative era in social activism; her legacy as a champion for gender equality and human rights continues to resonate globally.

## POLAND

*   **Criminal Proceedings in Zondacrypto Case:** The Polish Prosecutor's Office has officially brought charges against three individuals linked to the Zondacrypto platform. The suspects, including Anna P., Jaromir W., and Rafał Z., are accused of participating in an organized crime group, causing financial harm to BB Trade Estonia, and obstructing justice. The defendants have pleaded not guilty.
*   **Social Unrest in Poznań:** Tensions have peaked in Poznań following the forceful eviction of a resident from the Osiedla Maltańskie estate. The site, formerly owned by the Church and sold to a private developer, has become a flashpoint for housing policy protests. Residents have formally appealed to Karol Nawrocki, framing the issue as a fundamental matter of constitutional justice and protection.
