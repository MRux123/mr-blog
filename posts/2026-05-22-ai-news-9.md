## WORLD

* **US Strategic Shift:** The US Navy has confirmed a pause on a $14 billion arms sale to Taiwan. Officials cited the need to prioritize munitions stockpiles for ongoing operations related to the Iran war, signaling a significant shift in US military logistics and a prioritization of current conflict needs over long-term strategic commitments.
* **Russia's New Elite:** President Vladimir Putin is actively reshaping the Russian political landscape by installing military veterans into key regional leadership roles. This strategy aims to solidify domestic support by elevating those who have fought in the war in Ukraine to the status of a "new elite."
* **Europe’s Diplomatic Dilemma:** EU nations are currently debating the merits and objectives of appointing a special envoy for potential peace talks with Russia. The discourse highlights deep divisions within the continent regarding what terms should be negotiated and whether the Kremlin is currently engaging in good faith.

## POLAND

* **Warnings on Russian Aggression:** Foreign Minister Radosław Sikorski has issued a stark warning regarding Moscow's recent propaganda campaign against the Baltic states. Sikorski described these narratives as potential "propaganda preparation for something insane," specifically pointing to new Russian legislative mandates that authorize military interventions abroad.
* **Cinkciarz.pl Scandal:** The CEO of the prominent financial service Cinkciarz.pl, Marcin P., has been arrested in the United States. Polish authorities allege that the platform's collapse resulted in over 5,000 customers losing approximately 185 million PLN, marking a significant development in the ongoing investigation.
* **Political Tensions in Warsaw:** The Head of the Chancellery of the President, Zbigniew Bogucki, was summoned to the National Prosecutor's Office as a witness. The investigation concerns the President’s refusal to swear in judges appointed by the Sejm to the Constitutional Tribunal, a move that the administration continues to characterize as politically motivated.
