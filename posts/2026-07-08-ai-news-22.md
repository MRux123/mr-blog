## WORLD

* **U.S. Military Escalates Strikes Against Iran:** Following the bombing of merchant vessels in the Strait of Hormuz, U.S. forces have launched a new series of targeted strikes in southern Iran. CENTCOM reports that the operations are intended to neutralize threats to international shipping routes. President Trump has officially declared the previously established three-week-old cease-fire over, while Tehran has vowed a stronger retaliation, raising concerns about the potential for a full-scale regional conflict.

## POLAND

* **Judicial Setback for Trump:** A significant legal development has occurred as a court ruled against Donald Trump, ordering a multi-million dollar compensation payment in a sexual assault case. The verdict marks a major headline in Polish media, reflecting the global interest in the former president's legal challenges.
* **Public Debate on Aid to Ukraine:** A recent SW Research survey for Onet reveals a deepening divide in Polish public opinion regarding foreign policy. Approximately 44.8% of respondents expressed the view that the military support provided by Poland to Ukraine has been excessive, sparking a national conversation on the country's involvement in the ongoing conflict.
* **Aggressive Confrontation at Office:** Authorities have intervened following an incident where two individuals attempted to forcibly enter an office run by a Ukrainian national under the pretext of ideological vetting. The Minister of Internal Affairs, Marcin Kierwiński, has condemned the act, emphasizing a zero-tolerance policy toward hate-motivated aggression.
