## WORLD

* **Trump Signals Aggressive Stance on Strait of Hormuz:** President Donald Trump has announced plans to declare the Strait of Hormuz, a critical global maritime chokepoint, as United States territory. The move, accompanied by threats of severe economic sanctions against Iran, marks a significant escalation in regional tensions, raising concerns regarding international maritime law and global energy security.
* **Ferry Tragedy in Zimbabwe:** At least 44 people have lost their lives following the capsizing of an overcrowded ferry on Lake Kariba. With initial reports suggesting the vessel was carrying 114 passengers—well beyond its 90-person capacity—rescue operations are currently struggling to locate dozens still missing in the aftermath of the disaster.
* **Human Rights Crisis in Afghanistan:** Five years into the Taliban’s administration, women in Afghanistan report a systemic dismantling of their rights. Testimonies provided to the BBC reveal a grim reality of public floggings, exclusion from the workforce, and severe barriers to essential healthcare services.

## POLAND

* **Escalating Migration Pressure at Ceuta:** Polish media outlets are tracking a surge in organized attempts to breach the Spanish-Moroccan border at Ceuta. Security forces have been reinforced following alarming social media campaigns—often orchestrated by influencers—which encourage mass illegal crossings into European territory.
* **Lech Poznań Advances in European Competition:** In a significant sporting achievement, Polish club Lech Poznań secured a decisive victory against KI Klaksvik. The win guarantees their advancement to the next qualifying round, where they are set to face Switzerland's FC Thun.
