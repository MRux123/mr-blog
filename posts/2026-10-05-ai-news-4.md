## WORLD

* **Brazilian Presidential Race Enters Run-off:** The first round of the Brazilian presidential election has concluded without an outright winner, setting the stage for a decisive second round on October 25. The race between incumbent Luiz Inácio Lula da Silva and challenger Flávio Bolsonaro remains highly contested, signaling a potential shift in the country's political direction.
* **Political Transition in Bosnia and Herzegovina:** Preliminary results from the general elections in Bosnia and Herzegovina indicate that Denis Bećirovic, Darijana Filipović, and Zeljka Cvijanović have secured seats in the Presidency, while the Party of Democratic Action has emerged as a frontrunner for the Federation's parliament.

## POLAND

* **Eastern Border Security Alert:** Polish authorities briefly activated air defense measures, including the scrambling of fighter jets and the issuance of a Government Security Centre (RCB) alert for the Lublin region. The action was taken in response to intensive Russian aerial bombardment of targets in neighboring Ukraine, highlighting the persistent security spillover risks on NATO's eastern flank.
* **Cultural and Political Discourse:** In the domestic sphere, the 30th Nike Literary Award was presented to Szczepan Twardoch for his novel "Null," a work reflecting on the ongoing conflict in Ukraine. Meanwhile, public debate continues to focus on the fiscal sustainability of Poland's extensive social and military spending programs, as highlighted by recent remarks from former Prime Minister Mateusz Morawiecki.
