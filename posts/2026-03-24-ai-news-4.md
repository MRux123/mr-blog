## WORLD

*   **Surge in Jihadist Violence:** Recent reports indicate a sharp rise in extremist activity across Nigeria and the Democratic Republic of Congo. Despite a decade-low decline in global terrorism-related deaths, Nigeria recorded a 46% increase in fatalities, solidifying its position as a high-risk zone within the Global Terrorism Index.
*   **Diplomatic Turbulence:** Tensions in the Middle East remain at a fever pitch as the U.S. administration navigates a complex strategy involving potential negotiations with Iran, juxtaposed with unconventional diplomatic gestures, signaling a volatile approach to international relations.
*   **Tragic Aviation Incident:** Colombian authorities are investigating a catastrophic military plane crash that claimed the lives of at least 66 individuals. Defense officials have labeled the event a "tragic accident," though the technical or external causes remain under active investigation.

## POLAND

*   **Heightened Aerial Readiness:** Polish and allied air forces have been scrambled in response to a massive Russian aerial assault on neighboring Ukraine. The Operational Command of the Polish Armed Forces confirmed that fighter jets and early-warning aircraft were deployed to ensure the integrity of Polish airspace following intense Russian long-range aviation activity.
