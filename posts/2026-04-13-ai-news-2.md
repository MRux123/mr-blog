## WORLD

* **Political Earthquake in Hungary:** Viktor Orbán’s 16-year tenure has come to an abrupt end following a landslide victory by Péter Magyar and his TISZA party. The result marks a seismic shift in Central European politics, signaling a potential departure from the populist trajectory that has defined the region for over a decade.
* **Escalation in the Middle East:** Global energy markets have reacted sharply as oil prices surged above $100 per barrel. The U.S. Central Command has announced a formal blockade on vessels entering or departing Iranian ports following the collapse of peace negotiations. While the Strait of Hormuz remains open to transit, the blockade marks a significant escalation in the ongoing regional conflict.
* **U.S. Political Turmoil:** Congressman Eric Swalwell has officially withdrawn from the California gubernatorial race amid mounting pressure from Democratic leadership following allegations of sexual misconduct.

## POLAND

* **Tusk Welcomes "New Hungary":** Prime Minister Donald Tusk held a congratulatory call with Péter Magyar, signaling a major shift in diplomatic alignment between Warsaw and Budapest. Tusk publicly welcomed Hungary "back to Europe," framing the election result as a vital democratic pivot for the region.
* **International Acclaim:** Former U.S. President Barack Obama has endorsed the results of the Hungarian election, drawing a direct parallel between the victory of the Hungarian opposition and Poland's 2023 electoral outcome, characterizing both as decisive triumphs for democratic institutions.
