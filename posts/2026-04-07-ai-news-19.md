## WORLD

*   **Heightened Iran-US Standoff:** Tensions have reached a critical juncture as the US and Israel intensify strikes against Iranian infrastructure, including steel plants and critical supply lines. With an 8 p.m. Eastern deadline set by President Trump for a deal to reopen the Strait of Hormuz, the situation remains volatile, amid reports that Iranian authorities are taking precautionary measures, such as distributing iodine tablets to the population.
*   **Journalist Release in Iraq:** American journalist Shelly Kittleson has been released from captivity in Iraq after being held for a week by an Iran-aligned militia. The release was reportedly conditioned on her immediate departure from the country, following negotiations that may have involved the exchange of detained militia members.

## POLAND

*   **Escalating Diplomatic Row over Hungary:** Prime Minister Donald Tusk has publicly challenged opposition leaders Karol Nawrocki and Jarosław Kaczyński, questioning their stance on Viktor Orbán. This follows reports that the Hungarian Prime Minister allegedly offered his services to Vladimir Putin, sparking a sharp political debate regarding the alignment of Polish conservative figures with Budapest.
*   **Aviation Industry Crisis Warnings:** Experts and industry leaders are warning of a potential collapse in European air traffic, with projections suggesting a decrease of up to 90%. Concerns center on a critical shortage of jet fuel linked to the ongoing regional conflicts, which could severely impact European markets, including Poland, by the start of the summer season.
*   **Anti-Ukrainian Hate Crime in Poznań:** A 35-year-old resident of Poznań has been placed under police supervision following a targeted attack on two Ukrainian women. The incident, which was documented by one of the victims, has triggered a broader conversation regarding the safety of Ukrainian nationals in Poland amidst rising nationalist tensions.
