## WORLD

* **Strategic Houthi Advance in Yemen:** Iranian-backed Houthi forces have seized control of the strategic port of Mokha. This development significantly strengthens their position to disrupt shipping through the Bab al-Mandab Strait, a vital artery for global trade, escalating concerns regarding maritime security in the Red Sea.
* **Geopolitical Disinformation Claims:** Moscow has formally accused Ukraine of orchestrating a plot designed to provoke a conflict between Russia and the United Kingdom. British officials have dismissed these allegations, categorizing them as part of a broader Russian disinformation campaign aimed at fracturing the alliance between Ukraine and its Western partners.
* **Archaeological Breakthrough in Peru:** Researchers have uncovered a series of ancient, undisturbed tombs belonging to the Chimu kingdom. Described by experts as a "discovery of the decade," these burial sites provide unprecedented insights into the region’s pre-Columbian history, having escaped centuries of looting.

## POLAND

* **Melania Trump’s Anticipated Visit:** In a diplomatic move aimed at discussing digital security and the well-being of children, Poland is preparing to host a summit of First Ladies this autumn. Marta Nawrocka has confirmed that an invitation has been extended to Melania Trump, marking a notable highlight in the country's upcoming international agenda.
* **Increased Border Security Protocols:** Amidst ongoing regional instability, the Ukrainian government has officially implemented a specialized security regime along its borders with Russia and Belarus. The move is intended to tighten control and enhance defensive posture in response to heightened border threats.
* **Tragic Bus Accident in Switzerland:** A fatal bus crash in the Swiss canton of Grisons has resulted in multiple casualties. The vehicle, which was reportedly carrying 48 passengers, has triggered a significant emergency response as authorities work to coordinate aid for the survivors and identify the victims.
