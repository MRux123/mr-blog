## WORLD

* **Escalating Tensions in the Strait of Hormuz:** Iran has conducted a military strike against a commercial vessel in the Strait of Hormuz. This aggressive action directly undermines ongoing international efforts to restore stability to one of the world's most critical maritime trade routes. The incident, confirmed by both U.S. and Iranian officials, complicates Washington’s regional strategy to secure support for a broader peace agreement and signals a heightened risk of maritime conflict.

## POLAND

* **Internal Shake-up within PiS:** The Law and Justice (PiS) party has undergone a significant personnel restructuring, marked by the removal of a high-ranking associate close to party leader Jarosław Kaczyński. This move reflects ongoing internal power dynamics and potential shifts in the party's strategic direction.
* **National Security Breach:** A colonel within the Security Service of Ukraine (SBU) has been sentenced to life imprisonment for treasonous activities. The conviction underscores the persistent challenge of internal sabotage and espionage threats currently facing security apparatuses in the region.
* **Violent Incident at Jastrzębie-Zdrój Hospital:** Local authorities are investigating a disturbing security failure at a hospital in Jastrzębie-Zdrój, where an assailant armed with a sharp object attacked both patients and medical staff, raising urgent questions regarding safety protocols in public healthcare facilities.
