## WORLD

*   **Attempted Assassination of Donald Trump:** A 31-year-old California man has been charged following an attempted assassination of Donald Trump at a dinner in Washington. Investigators indicate the suspect was driven by an extremist motive to target high-level government officials, underscoring the growing volatility of the American political climate.
*   **Prevented Attack on Taylor Swift Concert:** Authorities in Vienna have successfully averted a major tragedy after a man admitted to plotting an attack on a Taylor Swift concert. Law enforcement officials recovered an almost-completed explosive device during a raid on the suspect's residence, highlighting the persistent threat of terrorism against large-scale public events.
*   **Rising Discontent in Russia:** Internet restrictions implemented by the Kremlin have inadvertently triggered a wave of public questioning regarding President Vladimir Putin’s policies. Despite the tightening of information control, diverse segments of Russian society, including influencers and remaining opposition voices, are increasingly vocal about the nation's political trajectory.

## POLAND

*   **Wave of Immunity Waivers in European Parliament:** The European Parliament has reached a series of consequential decisions regarding the legal immunity of several Polish MEPs. High-profile figures, including Grzegorz Braun, Daniel Obajtek, and Patryk Jaki, have seen their immunity statuses addressed by the assembly, signaling a shift in the legal accountability of Polish representatives at the EU level.
*   **Government Security Drills:** The Polish administration, in coordination with the President’s office, has conducted a set of classified exercises. While the details remain sensitive, the resulting report highlights the state’s focus on enhancing national security protocols and crisis management strategies in an increasingly unpredictable geopolitical environment.
