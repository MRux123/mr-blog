## WORLD

* **U.S. Abruptly Pauses Strait of Hormuz Mission:** Tensions in the Gulf have reached a critical juncture as President Trump has unexpectedly halted the U.S.-led escort operation in the Strait of Hormuz just one day after its announcement. The move, which contradicts previous declarations by Secretary of State Marco Rubio, has injected further uncertainty into the region, heightening fears of a slide into an all-out conflict between the U.S. and Iran.
* **Escalating Humanitarian Crisis in Lebanon:** The impact of recent Israeli airstrikes has left a devastating imprint on the civilian population in Lebanon. Reports indicate that short but intense periods of bombing have caused widespread destruction, shattering local lives and leaving families to mourn victims of the sudden military escalations.
* **Civilian Entrapment in Oleshky:** The humanitarian situation in the Ukrainian frontline city of Oleshky has become increasingly dire. Residents remain trapped and isolated from essential supplies, including medicine and food, as the "Road of Death" remains the only, albeit treacherous, route out of the conflict zone.

## POLAND

* **Major Forest Fire in Lubelskie Region:** Emergency services are battling a massive forest fire in the Lublin district, which has already resulted in the tragic death of an experienced pilot during firefighting efforts. The Rządowe Centrum Bezpieczeństwa (RCB) has issued urgent safety alerts for several municipalities, including Łukowa, Józefów, and Aleksandrów, urging residents to remain indoors due to heavy smoke.
* **National Security and U.S. Military Presence:** High-level discussions are underway regarding the potential increase of U.S. troop deployments in Poland. Marcin Przydacz, head of the International Policy Bureau of the President of Poland, is expected to address whether such declarations were made during recent diplomatic exchanges with the Trump administration, alongside broader questions concerning internal state reform via the newly established Council for a New Constitution.
