## WORLD

* **Houthi Missile Strike on Aden Government Headquarters:** A lethal attack by Houthi militia has struck the Maashiq Palace in Aden, Yemen. Government officials report that at least five people were killed in the incident, marking a significant escalation in the ongoing regional conflict and posing a new threat to the stability of the Yemeni government.
* **Bus-Train Collision in Channel Tunnel:** A bus collided with a stationary train on the French side of the Channel Tunnel. While 19 people sustained injuries, authorities have confirmed that there are no fatalities and no patients are currently in life-threatening condition. The incident caused significant operational disruptions, though rescue efforts have been effectively managed by local services.

## POLAND

* **Standoff Over Constitutional Tribunal Appointments:** Political tensions surrounding the Constitutional Tribunal remain high, with Maciej Berek signaling a potential breakthrough despite the current stalemate. The conflict centers on the refusal of high-ranking officials to accept oaths from Sejm-appointed judges, with Berek emphasizing that the next steps effectively rest upon the actions of the tribunal's current leadership.
* **Lech Poznań Suffers Heavy European Defeat:** In the realm of sports, Polish football club Lech Poznań faced a demoralizing loss against Bayer Leverkusen in the Europa League. The lopsided scoreline highlights the growing gap between the Polish side and top-tier European competition, leaving Lech at the bottom of their group table.
