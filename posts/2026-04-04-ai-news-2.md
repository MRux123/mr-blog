## WORLD

* **Escalation in Iran Conflict:** The regional conflict has reached a critical juncture following the loss of a U.S. F-15E warplane, the first of its kind to be downed by Iran during the ongoing five-week confrontation. With a U.S. crew member reported missing and a separate combat plane crash in the Gulf region, military stakes are rising significantly, threatening to broaden the scope of the engagement.
* **Viktor Orbán’s Political Future:** Hungary stands at a democratic crossroads as the nation prepares for elections in nine days. After a 16-year tenure, Viktor Orbán faces what is widely considered his most challenging path to re-election, with the international community closely monitoring whether this cycle will finally result in a change of leadership.
* **Severe Weather in the Middle East:** Unprecedented meteorological conditions have battered the Arabian Peninsula, as a strong jet stream triggered flash floods across the UAE and Saudi Arabia. The event, which saw desert regions receive up to 150mm of rain, serves as a stark reminder of shifting climate patterns impacting historically arid zones.

## POLAND

* **Tragic Traffic Incident in Ujsoły:** A serious road accident occurred in Ujsoły, in the Żywiec district, leaving five people injured. Local authorities confirmed that the incident was a secondary event triggered by a collision between two vehicles, resulting in a pedestrian casualty involving a 13-year-old child. Emergency services remain on-site, and the investigation into the cause of the collision is ongoing.
