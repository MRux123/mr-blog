## WORLD

*   **Humanitarian Crisis in Venezuela:** International rescue operations are racing against time in northern Venezuela following devastating twin earthquakes. The death toll has tragically surpassed 1,400, with at least 3,200 people injured and thousands left homeless. Search efforts remain the primary international priority as the window to find survivors continues to narrow.
*   **Political Resignation in Serbia:** President Aleksandar Vučić has announced his intention to step down from his position in the coming weeks. This decision, disclosed during a political rally in Belgrade, marks a significant moment of instability in the Balkan region, signaling a major shift in the Serbian political landscape.
*   **Hezbollah Rejects Lebanon-Israel Accord:** The leadership of Hezbollah, led by Naim Kassem, has formally rejected the framework agreement signed in Washington between Israel and Lebanon. Describing the deal as "humiliating" and a violation of national sovereignty, the group’s stance threatens to undermine the diplomatic efforts toward de-escalation in the Middle East.

## POLAND

*   **Political Friction Over Healthcare Management:** Former Prime Ministers Leszek Miller and Waldemar Pawlak have publicly criticized current Prime Minister Donald Tusk for his involvement in the controversies surrounding the Southern Hospital (Szpital Południowy). The debate focuses on the government's handling of medical staff issues, specifically regarding the treatment of Dr. Emil Jędrzejewski.
*   **Systemic Failures in Healthcare Under Scrutiny:** A tragic case involving the death of a 45-year-old patient who initially presented with a dislocated shoulder at a Radom emergency department (SOR) has triggered a prosecutor's investigation. The inquiry, prompted by an anonymous report from medical rescuers, highlights potential negligence and systemic failures within the facility.
*   **Sports Success and Heat-Related Disruptions:** On a positive note, the Polish national volleyball team continues its dominant streak in the Nations League. Meanwhile, the domestic sporting scene has been affected by extreme weather; for the first time in the history of the Służewiec racetrack, horse racing events were canceled due to a severe heatwave.
