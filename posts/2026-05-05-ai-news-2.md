## WORLD

* **Escalating Tensions in the Strait of Hormuz:** US military forces have engaged Iranian fast boats following an attack on a UAE oil facility. The incident occurred as Maersk successfully navigated a US-flagged commercial vessel out of the volatile strait under American military protection. This marks a significant escalation in maritime security concerns within the region.
* **Hantavirus Outbreak on MV Hondius:** A medical emergency has been declared aboard the luxury cruise ship MV Hondius after a suspected hantavirus outbreak. The World Health Organization is investigating reports of seven confirmed or suspected cases. Tragically, three individuals—a German national and a Dutch couple—have lost their lives. Authorities are currently conducting urgent medical evacuations for a British crew member and a UK passenger who remains in critical condition.
