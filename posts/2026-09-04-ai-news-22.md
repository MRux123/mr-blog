## WORLD

*   **Trump Initiative on Ukraine:** President Donald Trump has deployed a diplomatic delegation led by Steve Witkoff and Jared Kushner to Kyiv. The mission aims to negotiate a framework for ending the ongoing conflict. Trump expressed optimism regarding the potential for a breakthrough, signaling a shift in U.S. strategy toward a more active role in de-escalation efforts.
*   **Security Concerns in Europe:** A recent simulation conducted by U.S. experts regarding a potential Russian attack on Europe in 2028 has yielded concerning results. As reported by *The Washington Post*, the findings suggest that the current U.S. response capacity might be insufficient. Most critically, the study raises alarms about the perceived efficacy of NATO’s Article 5, with some experts questioning its practical deterrent value in a high-intensity conflict scenario.

## POLAND

*   **Papal Visit Preparations:** Paris is currently under a state of high alert as authorities finalize security arrangements for the upcoming visit of Pope Leo XIV. The mobilization reflects the significant diplomatic and logistical weight of the Pontiff's late-September arrival in the French capital.
