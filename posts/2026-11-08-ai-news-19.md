## WORLD

* **Train Collision in Egypt:** A tragic rail accident has occurred in the Giza province, south of Cairo. Reports from the BBC, NYT, and The Guardian confirm that two passenger trains collided, resulting in at least seven fatalities and over 100 injuries. Authorities have ordered an immediate investigation into the disaster.
* **Houthi Attack on Riyadh:** Saudi authorities reported that the Saudi Aramco tower in the capital was damaged following a projectile attack by Yemen’s Houthi rebels. While the strike hit a high-profile financial district, officials stated that there were no casualties reported from the falling shrapnel.
* **Political Executions in Iran:** The Iranian government has executed three men—Hassan Dioband, Hamid Kazemi, and Reza Zanganeh—convicted of "armed unrest" in connection with the anti-government protests that took place earlier this year.

## POLAND

* **Mass Shooting in Krakow:** A violent shooting spree in Krakow and an adjacent town has left at least nine people dead and 16 injured. The motive behind the attack remains unknown, and the incident has triggered a significant police investigation and a nationwide discussion on public safety.
* **Post-Crisis Response:** Following the tragedy, local authorities and officials, including Krakow's leadership, are addressing public concerns regarding security protocols. Meanwhile, the city center, including the Main Market Square, has been reopened to the public after being secured by law enforcement.
* **Medical Heroism:** Amidst the tragedy, stories of bravery have emerged. Surgeons and medical staff from the University Hospital in Krakow are being praised for their swift response in treating the victims of the attack, openly sharing their accounts of the harrowing situation they faced.
