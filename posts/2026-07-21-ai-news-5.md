## WORLD

* **Trump Imposes New Tariffs on Canadian Goods:** The geopolitical landscape has shifted following Donald Trump’s decision to impose a 50% tariff on selected Canadian imports. In response, Canadian Prime Minister Mark Carney has signaled a readiness to engage in negotiations while simultaneously pledging robust protective measures for the Canadian economy, marking a potential escalation in North American trade tensions.
* **IMF Releases Fresh Funding for Ukraine:** The International Monetary Fund (IMF) has officially approved the fourth tranche of financial aid for Ukraine, amounting to $690 million. This payment is part of an ongoing $15.6 billion, four-year assistance package designed to support Ukraine's economic stability amidst the ongoing conflict.

## POLAND

* **Educational Crisis Deepens:** Poland is facing a severe administrative and human resources crisis within its education sector. Data indicates an alarming surge in job vacancies for teachers, with new postings appearing every two minutes, highlighting a systemic struggle to maintain staffing levels across the country.
* **Fiscal Reform Controversies:** The Polish Senate has launched an inquiry into the government's latest fiscal proposal. The plan, which reportedly suggests imposing taxes despite a lack of taxable income, has drawn sharp criticism and raised significant questions regarding the legislative logic and economic impact of the new measures.
* **Political Instability in PiS:** The Law and Justice (PiS) party is currently navigating a period of internal turmoil as a critical deadline for party negotiations approaches. High-ranking members have publicly stated their refusal to sign required declarations, underscoring deep divisions within the opposition party's leadership.
