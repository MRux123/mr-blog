## WORLD

*   **US-Iran Stand-down:** Following an exchange of strikes, the United States and Iran have reached an agreement to de-escalate tensions. The move is aimed at ensuring the free movement of vessels through the Strait of Hormuz, though the fragile nature of the ceasefire remains a point of concern as Iran reportedly continues to use its strategic position as leverage in ongoing peace talks.
*   **Escalation on the Afghan Border:** Deadly hostilities have erupted between Pakistan and Afghanistan. Pakistani airstrikes resulted in over 30 fatalities; while Pakistan maintains that the operations were targeted at militants, the Taliban government has reported significant civilian casualties, further destabilizing the volatile region.
*   **Venezuela Earthquake Rescue:** Amidst the devastation of recent earthquakes in Venezuela, rescue operations continue to provide rare moments of hope, most notably with the survival of an 18-day-old infant, Juan David, who was saved from the rubble.

## POLAND

*   **Financial Market Disruption:** The Polish Financial Supervision Authority (KNF) has revoked the license of mPay, sending shockwaves through the local fintech and mobile payment sectors.
*   **Security Crackdown:** The Internal Security Agency (ABW) has detained citizens of Ukraine and Belarus on allegations of funding participants in public demonstrations, raising questions about foreign interference in domestic civil activity.
*   **Fuel Prices and Infrastructure:** As the government prepares to end fuel price subsidies tomorrow, the country has simultaneously cleared the path for the long-awaited construction of the S19 expressway, marking a significant step in national infrastructure development.
