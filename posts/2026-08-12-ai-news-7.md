## WORLD

* **Escalating Tensions in the Korean Peninsula:** North Korea has conducted another ballistic missile test, firing a projectile toward the Sea of Japan. This marks the second such provocation in a single week, occurring against the backdrop of heightened military alertness ahead of joint exercises between South Korea and the United States, raising concerns about regional stability.
* **Security Shift in Ukraine:** There has been a significant tactical development following reported diplomatic pressure from J.D. Vance, with reports indicating that Ukraine has agreed to a temporary cessation of certain offensive operations. The move highlights the evolving complexities of the ongoing conflict and the increasing influence of international diplomatic maneuvers on battlefield strategy.

## POLAND

* **Legislative Hurdles Impact Waste Management:** Local municipalities are facing potential hikes in waste collection fees as the government continues to delay the implementation of critical legislation. This regulatory stagnation places additional financial pressure on local governments and, ultimately, on taxpayers.
* **Political Polarization Over Deposit Systems:** Minister of Climate and Environment, Paulina Hennig-Kloska, has publicly criticized the Confederation party’s stance on the national deposit-return system. The debate underscores broader friction regarding the implementation of green policies and the government's efforts to streamline environmental management systems against political opposition.
