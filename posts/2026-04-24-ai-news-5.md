## WORLD

* **European Strategic Autonomy Beyond NATO:** As the geopolitical landscape shifts, European Union nations are increasingly debating the framework of mutual defense obligations outside the NATO structure. While the EU maintains little-known protective clauses, experts emphasize that these mechanisms are intended to supplement, not replace, the traditional NATO security architecture, highlighting a growing push for European self-reliance in defense.
* **Tensions Rise in India’s Bengali Heartland:** The political rivalry between Prime Minister Narendra Modi’s party and West Bengal’s leadership has intensified. The region is currently a critical battleground for the Hindu Right, with the ongoing audit of voter rolls—which resulted in the removal of nine million voters, many of them Muslim—sparking significant concerns regarding electoral integrity and communal inclusivity.

## POLAND

* **Public Consensus on School Digitization:** A landmark study by CBOS reveals a significant national consensus regarding children's interaction with technology. Approximately 85% of Poles support a complete ban on smartphones in primary schools, while 78% favor restricting social media access for minors under 15. These figures suggest a strong public mandate for legislative intervention in the digital habits of Polish youth.
* **Diplomatic Speculation Following G20 Developments:** President Donald Trump has commented on the potential attendance of Vladimir Putin at the upcoming G20 summit in Florida. In response to media inquiries, Trump signaled an openness to dialogue, a stance that has prompted international scrutiny regarding the diplomatic strategy toward Moscow in the current global climate.
