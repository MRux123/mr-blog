## WORLD

*   **Regional Cease-Fire Negotiations:** Amidst the ongoing conflict between Israel and Iran, international pressure is mounting for a broader cease-fire. Diplomats are actively pushing for Lebanon to be integrated into the peace negotiations, while Prime Minister Benjamin Netanyahu has officially directed his government to initiate direct talks with Lebanon, aimed at disarming Hezbollah and establishing long-term stability.
*   **Iran's Geopolitical Strategy:** Analysts are closely monitoring Tehran's position, as its theocratic leadership frames survival against the U.S.-Israeli offensive as a strategic victory. While appearing confident, Iran faces significant internal challenges, including the long-term psychological impact of the conflict on the civilian population, particularly children.
*   **NATO Security Tensions:** Concerns over regional maritime security have intensified, with reports of Russian submarines operating within British waters, potentially threatening critical subsea infrastructure such as power cables and pipelines.

## POLAND

*   **Constitutional Crisis Deepens:** The dispute surrounding the Constitutional Tribunal (TK) has reached a critical impasse. Following the controversial swearing-in of new judges at the Sejm, the President of the Constitutional Tribunal, Bogdan Święczkowski, has officially refused to recognize the legitimacy of the ceremony, dismissing it as a "media spectacle."
*   **Calls for Legal Reset:** Government officials, including Minister of Agriculture Stefan Krajewski, have publicly labeled the ongoing situation a "farce," advocating for a "constitutional reset" to resolve the deepening gridlock between the executive branch, the President’s office, and the judiciary.
*   **Criminal Investigations:** Law enforcement is currently handling two major criminal cases: a tragic investigation into the death of a five-month-old infant in Poznań involving severe physical abuse, and a murder investigation in Ciechanów following the discovery of the body of 23-year-old Adrian P., a case being prosecuted as a kidnapping involving "special torture."
