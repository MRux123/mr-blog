## WORLD

*   **Escalation in Lebanon:** Tensions in the Middle East have reached a critical point following a rapid, ten-minute intensive bombing campaign in Lebanon, resulting in widespread infrastructure damage and chaos. Analysts are closely monitoring the region for potential retaliatory actions.
*   **Fragile Ceasefire in Ukraine:** The conflict continues to test international diplomacy as Russia reportedly violated the latest ceasefire agreement. Ukraine’s leadership has condemned the move, describing the Kremlin's actions as a prioritisation of military posturing over civilian lives, casting doubt on the effectiveness of ongoing peace efforts.

## POLAND

*   **Intelligence Oversight:** The Internal Security Agency (ABW) has taken a significant step toward transparency by publishing the public portion of its annual report for the first time in years. The document highlights the intensifying threat landscape within Poland, specifically focusing on clandestine operations and sabotage efforts orchestrated by Russian intelligence services.
*   **National Security Urgency:** Defence Minister Władysław Kosiniak-Kamysz has issued a stark warning regarding the current security climate. Describing the present moment as a "pre-war era," the Minister has called for an urgent transition to a 24/7 production cycle within the Polish defense industry to bolster national readiness.
*   **Political Turbulence:** A recent poll indicates a tightening race for legislative control, suggesting that despite Koalicja Obywatelska's (KO) lead, a potential coalition between PiS and Konfederacja could challenge the current administration's hold on power. Simultaneously, the political discourse remains heated, as evidenced by former Justice Minister Zbigniew Ziobro’s sharp public criticism regarding the government's handling of financial fraud cases.
