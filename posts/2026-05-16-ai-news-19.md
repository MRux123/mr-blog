## WORLD

* **Escalating Concerns Over Iranian Proxy Activities:** Intelligence reports and recent legal charges against Mohammad al-Saadi in the United States have sparked significant alarm regarding Iran’s potential to orchestrate military-style operations beyond the Middle East. Security agencies are closely monitoring potential threats linked to Iranian-backed groups operating globally.
* **Tensions Rise Over Taiwan's Sovereignty:** Following high-level discussions in Beijing, U.S. leadership has issued stern warnings against a formal declaration of independence by Taiwan. The stance underscores the fragile geopolitical balance in the Pacific as the island maintains its insistence on sovereign status.
* **Security Challenges in Europe:** London has faced massive, opposing protests necessitating a major police mobilization to prevent violence between far-right and pro-Palestinian factions, highlighting increasing social polarization within major Western urban centers.

## POLAND

* **NATO Border Security Breach:** Authorities in Romania have reported the discovery of an unexploded missile on private property near the Ukrainian border. This incident marks another alarming security lapse for a NATO member state, directly attributed to the ongoing conflict and Russian air strikes near the Ukrainian frontier.
* **Public Information Warfare:** Analysts are observing a concerted effort by the Kremlin to utilize contradictory rhetoric—ranging from dismissive references to "Mr. Zelensky" to feigned diplomatic politeness—as a tool of information warfare aimed at destabilizing international perception and creating confusion within the European media landscape.
