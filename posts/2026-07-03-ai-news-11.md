## WORLD

*   **Europe Faces Escalating Heatwave Crisis:** Authorities have reported over 2,000 excess deaths linked to the recent, record-breaking heatwave. As extreme temperatures persist, governments across the continent are bracing for further climate-related pressures and health emergencies.
*   **Emergency Measures in Munich:** The city of Munich has declared a state of emergency following critical water shortages caused by the prolonged heat. A specialized task force has been assembled to manage resource distribution and implement strict water usage restrictions.
*   **NASA’s Orbital Rescue Mission:** In a high-stakes technical endeavor, NASA has launched a robotic mission to stabilize a falling space telescope. The operation aims to prevent the instrument from de-orbiting and burning up in the atmosphere.

## POLAND

*   **Resignations in Warsaw Administration:** Mayor Rafał Trzaskowski has accepted the resignations of deputy mayors Renata Kaznowska and Aldona Machnowska-Góra. The move follows a directive requiring city officials to choose between public service roles and political party memberships, with the decision partially linked to ongoing controversies surrounding the Southern Hospital.
*   **Prime Minister Tusk’s Ultimatum on Healthcare:** Tusk has issued a stern warning regarding the management of public health facilities. He has demanded actionable recommendations for systemic reform by next Tuesday, threatening personal consequences for officials if the current standards of service and transparency—including the abolition of "VIP" treatment—are not addressed.
*   **Tensions in Polish-Ukrainian Relations:** Prime Minister Donald Tusk has signaled a shift in tone regarding diplomatic relations with Ukraine. Citing the need for a more pragmatic approach, Tusk emphasized that current bilateral dynamics require significant adjustment to better serve Polish interests.
