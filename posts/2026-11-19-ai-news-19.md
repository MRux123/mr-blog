## WORLD

* **Historic ICJ Ruling on Myanmar:** The International Court of Justice has issued a landmark verdict, officially finding Myanmar guilty of committing genocide against the Rohingya minority. This ruling marks the first time a state has been held accountable under the 1948 Genocide Convention, setting a major precedent for international human rights law.
* **Trilateral Summit in China:** Presidents Trump, Putin, and Xi held an unprecedented trilateral meeting. While the summit was characterized by symbolic posturing, the aftermath suggests little progress on ending the conflict in Ukraine, with Vladimir Putin explicitly rejecting cease-fire negotiations following recent Ukrainian strikes on the Crimean Bridge.
* **Shifting Stance on European Security:** Amidst the ongoing tensions, European leaders have signaled a newfound readiness for talks with Moscow regarding the war in Ukraine, aiming to establish a unified negotiating position at the Berlin summit, despite continued Russian brinkmanship.

## POLAND

* **Constitutional Tribunal Standoff:** Efforts to resolve the long-standing crisis surrounding the Constitutional Tribunal have stalled. Zbigniew Bogucki, representing the President, confirmed that current talks are suspended as parties retreat to formulate new proposals to break the deadlock.
* **Strategic Energy Cooperation:** The United States continues to prioritize the development of civil nuclear energy in Poland. Former U.S. Ambassador Georgette Mosbacher reiterated during the P-TEC forum that this sector remains a top priority for the U.S. Department of Energy, underscoring the deep strategic alignment between Warsaw and Washington.
* **Diplomatic Rhetoric on Russia:** High-ranking Polish officials, including Minister Marcin Przydacz, have adopted a sharp tone regarding Russia’s geopolitical standing, labeling the Kremlin a "diminished power" and highlighting its increasingly clientelistic reliance on China as observed during recent global summits.
