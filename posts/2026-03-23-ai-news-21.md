## WORLD

*   **US-Iran Conflict and Diplomatic Ambiguity:** Tensions in the Middle East remain high as the U.S. and Iran offer conflicting reports regarding potential peace negotiations. While President Trump has suggested that talks are underway, Iranian officials have expressed skepticism or denied direct communication, despite reports of back-channel messaging. The human toll continues to mount, with recent data indicating that children account for over 15% of civilian casualties in the ongoing hostilities.
*   **Energy Crisis in Europe:** Slovenia has become the first European Union nation to implement mandatory fuel rationing. Citing unprecedented supply constraints, the government has capped fuel purchases at 50 liters per motorist per day, highlighting the growing strain on energy security across the continent.
*   **Political Instability in Italy:** Prime Minister Giorgia Meloni faces a period of heightened political vulnerability following a significant defeat in a constitutional reform referendum. The result is widely viewed as a vote of no-confidence in her government's current direction, complicating her legislative agenda moving forward.

## POLAND

*   **Security Alert in Ukraine:** Polish media report that President Volodymyr Zelenskyy has issued an urgent warning to Ukrainian citizens, advising them to remain vigilant against potential mass missile or drone attacks expected in the overnight hours between March 23 and 24.
*   **Military Procurement and Diplomacy:** The Polish government has finalized a major defense agreement for the procurement of 96 AH-64 Apache attack helicopters. During the signing ceremony, the U.S. Ambassador to Poland compared Prime Minister Donald Tusk’s negotiation style to that of Donald Trump, labeling him a "tough guy" in the context of international statecraft.
*   **Corporate Leadership Changes:** Grupa Azoty Police, a critical state-owned chemical enterprise, has announced a leadership reshuffle. Małgorzata Królak will take over as CEO on April 1, with Dominik Nowak assuming the role of Vice President, marking a significant change in the firm's strategic management.
