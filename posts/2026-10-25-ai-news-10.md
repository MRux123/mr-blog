## WORLD

* **Serbia’s Parliamentary Elections:** Voters in Serbia are heading to the polls in a high-stakes legislative election. This vote represents the most significant challenge to the governing party of Aleksandar Vučić in over a decade, following nearly two years of sustained, student-led anti-government protests. The outcome could signal a major shift in the political landscape of a country long seen as a pro-Russian outlier in the region.
* **Mass Protests in France:** France remains in a state of civil unrest as more than 1,300 people were arrested during another day of widespread mass demonstrations. Hundreds of thousands took to the streets across the country, with the movement echoing broader instability currently felt across several European nations.
* **Escalating Tensions in Israel:** As the country prepares for a critical election, the Israel Defense Forces have announced the closure of West Bank crossings. This move, aimed at restricting the movement of Palestinian workers, highlights the heightened security state as voters prepare to choose a new prime minister in a race that is expected to result in a complex and prolonged government formation process.

## POLAND

* **Internal Conflict within the Governing Coalition:** Public political discourse in Poland has been dominated by the fallout from Katarzyna Pełczyńska-Nałęcz’s departure from the government. The situation has triggered sharp exchanges between coalition members, with senior politicians trading accusations of "hysterical egocentrism" and allegations of workplace toxicity involving Prime Minister Donald Tusk’s administration.
* **Long-Range Security Concerns:** Security analysts are focusing on the increasing reach of drone strikes within Russia. Recent reports confirm attacks on industrial sites in the Perm region, located approximately 1,500 kilometers from the Ukrainian border, following similar incidents in Omsk. These strikes demonstrate an expanding capacity for long-range disruption, forcing Russia to acknowledge the growing vulnerability of its deep interior.
