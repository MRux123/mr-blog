## WORLD

* **Escalating US-Iran Standoff:** President Trump has issued a stark ultimatum to Iran, demanding a new deal or facing renewed military action. While Trump claims the conflict is "under control" ahead of his summit in China, the geopolitical stability of the Middle East remains precarious as Tehran refuses to yield to current demands.
* **Trump’s 'Golden Dome' Defense Strategy:** New findings from an independent budget office reveal that President Trump's proposed missile defense system, the "Golden Dome," is projected to cost $1.2 trillion—nearly seven times the initial estimate—casting doubt on both its economic viability and its technical ability to provide comprehensive protection.
* **UK Leadership Instability:** Prime Minister Keir Starmer remains in a fragile position despite surviving recent calls for his resignation. While he has managed a temporary political reprieve, the underlying systemic issues within his leadership continue to threaten his administration's longevity.

## POLAND

* **Zbigniew Ziobro's Controversial Washington Presence:** Former Justice Minister Zbigniew Ziobro has emerged in Washington, D.C., where he is reportedly seeking to influence the American Polish diaspora while acting as a commentator for Telewizja Republika. His presence in the U.S. has triggered diplomatic tension, as Polish authorities continue to seek his return in connection with ongoing investigations into the Justice Fund.
* **Alicja Szemplińska Reaches Eurovision Final:** In a moment of national excitement, Polish representative Alicja Szemplińska has successfully qualified for the grand final of the 70th Eurovision Song Contest in Vienna. Her performance of "Pray" secured her a place among the finalists, despite the dramatic, last-minute announcement of her qualification by organizers.
* **Mountaineering Achievement:** Bartek Ziemski has etched his name into history by successfully descending the 8516-meter peak of Lhotse on skis. This feat marks his eighth 8000-meter peak conquered without supplemental oxygen, underscoring a remarkable level of physical endurance and technical skill in extreme high-altitude conditions.
