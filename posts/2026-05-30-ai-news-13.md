## WORLD

* **Escalating Tensions in the Middle East:** U.S. President Donald Trump is approaching a critical "final determination" regarding the future of the Iran nuclear deal. The administration's wavering stance has kept global markets and regional stakeholders on edge as the potential for a renewed agreement or a complete collapse of diplomacy hangs in the balance.
* **Geopolitical Realignment in Eurasia:** Russia has recalled its ambassador to Armenia following Yerevan's warming ties with the European Union. President Vladimir Putin issued a thinly veiled warning to the former Soviet ally, suggesting that deepening cooperation with the West could lead to consequences reminiscent of the instability faced by Ukraine.
* **Pentagon Criticizes Defense Spending:** In a sharp rebuke of international security commitments, U.S. Secretary of Defense Pete Hegseth accused allies failing to meet a 3.5% GDP defense spending threshold of "parasitism" on the American military apparatus, specifically highlighting New Zealand's lack of contribution.

## POLAND

* **Tusk Calls for NATO Unity Following Russian Provocations:** Prime Minister Donald Tusk has urged the NATO alliance to take Russian threats seriously after recent aggressive rhetoric from former Russian President Dmitry Medvedev. In response to the heightened security climate, Tusk announced the signing of 29 new contracts worth 79 billion PLN, aimed at bolstering Poland's domestic defense manufacturing capabilities under the EU’s SAFE initiative.
* **Sporting Breakthrough at Roland Garros:** Polish tennis continues to see a rise on the international stage as Maja Chwalińska secured a place in the 1/8 finals of the French Open. Her victory over Greece's Maria Sakkari underscores a significant milestone in her professional career and adds to the growing list of Polish successes in Grand Slam tournaments.
