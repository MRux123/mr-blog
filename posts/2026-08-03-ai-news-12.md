## WORLD

* **Iran-US Tensions Over the Strait of Hormuz:** Despite recent claims by President Trump regarding progress in negotiations, Iran has officially denied that any formal talks are currently underway. The situation remains fluid as Tehran emphasizes its own diplomatic channels with Oman, framing the recent de-escalation as a display of regional strategic leverage.
* **Ceuta Migration Crisis:** The migration crisis in the Spanish territory of Ceuta has intensified, highlighting deeper socioeconomic despair among Moroccan youth. Investigations suggest that government intelligence warnings regarding the border breach may have been ignored due to shifting priorities in Madrid, leading to a complex political fallout for Prime Minister Pedro Sánchez.
* **Japan’s Currency Intervention:** In a rare and significant move, the US and Japan have collaborated to stabilize the yen. This joint intervention signals a firm commitment from both nations to prevent further currency volatility, with officials indicating that further market actions remain an option if instability persists.

## POLAND

* **Political Landscape Shift:** A new poll conducted by IBRIS for Polsat News reveals a cooling of support for the ruling PiS party. Notably, the political landscape is diversifying, with Mateusz Morawiecki’s new movement, "Rozwój Plus," polling at 7.8%, while the Koalicja Obywatelska maintains a lead at 27.5%.
* **Government Appointments:** President Zelensky has confirmed the appointment of Umerov to lead the foreign intelligence agency, a strategic move aimed at restructuring Ukraine's security apparatus amid the ongoing conflict.
* **Public Health Alert:** The Chief Sanitary Inspectorate (GIS) has issued a warning regarding a significant surge in gastrointestinal illnesses across Poland, colloquially referred to as the "dirty hands disease," prompting heightened surveillance by regional health authorities.
