## WORLD

* **Middle East Escalation:** The volatile situation in the Strait of Hormuz has intensified, with reports of direct military engagements between U.S. forces and Iran. Following Iranian fire on warships, the U.S. has conducted targeted strikes on Iranian military facilities, specifically at the ports of Qeshm and Bandar Abbas. Simultaneously, the regional instability has been further complicated by a strike near Beirut, attributed to Israel, targeting a Hezbollah leader.
* **U.S.-EU Trade Ultimatum:** President Donald Trump has issued a high-stakes ultimatum to the European Union regarding a pending trade deal. Demanding that the EU enact previously agreed-upon terms and eliminate tariffs on American goods, Trump has set a deadline of July 4. While he has agreed to delay further tariff increases on European automobiles until this date, the threat underscores the fragility of trans-Atlantic economic relations amidst global economic concerns.

## POLAND

* **Domestic Tragedy in Raszyn:** A double tragedy has occurred in the Warsaw metropolitan area. A 67-year-old man shot and killed his partner in their home in Raszyn, where children were present at the scene. The perpetrator was later discovered deceased at a cemetery in Prażmów.
* **Constitutional Crisis Rhetoric:** The Polish government has signaled a potential confrontation regarding the status of the Constitutional Tribunal (TK). Deputy Prime Minister Krzysztof Gawkowski stated in a recent interview that the government is prepared to "break down doors" if necessary to overhaul and restore the functionality of the Tribunal, highlighting a deepening institutional standoff.
* **Diplomatic Shift on Ukraine:** Poland continues to monitor European diplomatic efforts regarding the war in Ukraine. Recent reports indicate that the European Union is preparing for potential peace talks with Russia, a plan which reportedly has the approval of Ukrainian President Volodymyr Zelenskyy.
