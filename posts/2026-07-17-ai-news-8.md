## WORLD

*   **Security Threat Against Donald Trump:** Tensions in the Middle East have escalated following reports from Iraq, where pro-Iranian militias have allegedly placed a $10 million bounty on the life of former US President Donald Trump. This development represents a significant security challenge and a sharp increase in geopolitical hostility.
*   **Diplomatic Standoff over AI Content:** The Philippines has officially condemned a viral AI-generated video circulated on Chinese media, which depicted a monkey dressed in Filipino garments. Manila characterizes the imagery as racist and a provocative attempt to mock the Philippines' claims in the South China Sea.
*   **Imperial Succession in Japan:** The Japanese government has implemented legal changes to succession rules, allowing the adoption of male relatives from distant branches of the imperial family. However, the move has drawn international attention for maintaining the prohibition on female emperors, keeping the debate on traditional succession intact.

## POLAND

*   **Presidential Veto on Civil Partnership Law:** President Karol Nawrocki has formally vetoed the bill concerning "close persons" (osoby najbliższe). Nawrocki stated that the proposed legislation functions as an alternative to marriage, which he argues is inconsistent with the constitutional protection of traditional marriage.
*   **Systemic Failures in Emergency Care:** A new report by the Supreme Audit Office (NIK) has exposed severe irregularities within Poland’s Hospital Emergency Departments (SOR). The investigation highlights extreme labor violations, with some doctors reported to have worked shifts lasting up to 144 consecutive hours, raising concerns regarding patient safety and staff burnout.
*   **Rising Hate Crimes Against Ukrainian Refugees:** Police data indicates a concerning trend, with reports of hate-motivated crimes against Ukrainian citizens increasing by over 30% compared to the previous year. This surge in hostility has prompted calls for a stronger official response to ensure the security of refugees residing in the country.
