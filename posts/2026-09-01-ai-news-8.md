## WORLD

*   **Humanitarian Crisis at Nepal-China Border:** A catastrophic flash flood in the remote Himalayan region has resulted in a death toll exceeding 1,000. Rescue operations are currently underway to locate survivors trapped in mud-filled tunnels, while international concerns grow regarding the fate of over 100 Indian nationals missing at a key trade crossing.
*   **Mass Deportations in Afghanistan:** The humanitarian situation in Afghanistan continues to deteriorate as six million deportees face an uncertain future. Following aggressive repatriation policies from Pakistan and Iran, thousands are being forced to restart their lives in a country many have never actually visited.
*   **Legal Challenges for Amazon:** The US Federal Trade Commission has launched a significant legal offensive against Amazon, alleging a $20 billion scheme to rig advertising prices. The company has formally denied the claims, stating that the regulatory body fundamentally misinterprets the dynamics of the digital ad market.

## POLAND

*   **Fuel Price Surge:** The conclusion of the government's "Ceny Paliwa Niżej" (CPN) relief program has triggered immediate and significant fuel price hikes at stations across the country. Prices for 95 and 98 octane petrol have surged by approximately one zloty per liter, surpassing initial market forecasts and impacting household budgets.
*   **Tragic Accident on A1 Motorway:** A fatal traffic incident occurred on the A1 motorway near Mykanów, resulting in five deaths. Preliminary reports indicate that a truck collided with a road maintenance crew, prompting a large-scale emergency response including an air ambulance. 
*   **PKOl Sponsorship Controversy:** The Polish Olympic Committee (PKOl) is facing intense scrutiny over a controversial contract with the Zondacrypto exchange. Reports indicate that the agreement granted the exchange rights to utilize the personal images and data of Polish Olympians—both active and retired—sparking concerns among officials regarding the ethical and legal risks of the deal.
