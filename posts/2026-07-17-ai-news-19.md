## WORLD

*   **Escalating Conflict in the Middle East:** Military tensions between the United States and Iran have reached a critical point following fresh U.S. strikes. While the Pentagon maintains that the operations were strictly aimed at degrading Iranian military capabilities, Iranian officials have accused the U.S. of targeting civilian infrastructure, including bridges and water treatment facilities. The situation remains fluid, with international observers monitoring the potential for further regional destabilization.
*   **EU Climate Policy Adjustment:** In a significant policy pivot, the European Union has proposed a relaxation of its carbon emissions trading system. The initiative aims to provide businesses with more flexibility by slowing down the schedule for mandated emission cuts, reflecting growing economic concerns regarding industrial competitiveness and the transition to green energy.

## POLAND

*   **Breakthrough in Polish-Ukrainian Relations:** A major development has occurred regarding historical reconciliation, as Ukrainian President Volodymyr Zelenskyy announced the opening of state archives related to the Volhynia massacre. The move includes facilitating Polish-led search and exhumation efforts. Prime Minister Donald Tusk welcomed the decision, signaling readiness for a comprehensive and sincere dialogue to address long-standing historical grievances.
*   **Internal Crisis within PiS:** The political landscape of the Law and Justice (PiS) party is currently marked by internal friction. High-profile meetings between Jarosław Kaczyński and Mateusz Morawiecki have reportedly concluded without a resolution to fundamental disputes. Public disagreements between party figures, such as Tobiasz Bocheński and Piotr Müller, have further fueled speculation regarding the stability of the party’s leadership hierarchy and the future of the Morawiecki faction.
