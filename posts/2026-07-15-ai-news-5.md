## WORLD

* **US-Iran Conflict Intensifies:** Tensions have reached a critical juncture as the US has resumed blockades on Iranian ports, prompting direct military engagement for the fourth consecutive day. President Trump has issued a stark ultimatum, threatening to target vital infrastructure, including bridges and power plants, should Iran refuse to return to the negotiating table.
* **China's Economic Slowdown:** China is facing significant headwinds as economic growth figures have fallen short of official targets. Analysts attribute the cooling trend to a combination of weak domestic demand and global market volatility, exacerbated by the rising price of oil resulting from the escalating conflict in the Middle East.

## POLAND

* **Security Alert Regarding International Shipments:** Polish authorities have launched an investigation into mysterious explosions occurring in postal sorting facilities. Preliminary reports suggest that parcels originating from the east are being treated as a high-security risk, with services remaining on high alert.
* **Severe Weather Warnings:** The Government Security Centre (RCB) has issued urgent weather alerts for six provinces. Residents are being warned of violent storms, heavy rainfall, and hail, which pose a significant threat to infrastructure and public safety.
* **Public Health Reform Dialogue:** The medical community is actively engaging with the government regarding the proposed healthcare system overhaul. Following internal assessments, doctors are preparing to submit formal feedback to ensure that the planned changes address systemic inefficiencies.
