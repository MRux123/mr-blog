## WORLD

*   **Military Escalation in Iran:** The conflict between the U.S./Israel coalition and Iran has intensified following direct strikes on key petrochemical facilities in Marvdasht and Asalujeh. The situation remains volatile as major powers reject a proposed 45-day ceasefire. President Donald Trump has refused the truce, signaling a rigid stance as the deadline for a diplomatic resolution looms.
*   **Artemis Mission Milestone:** The Artemis crew is entering a critical phase of their mission, preparing for a 40-minute period of complete communication blackout as they pass behind the Moon. This "silence" represents both a technical challenge and a significant milestone in deep-space exploration.

## POLAND

*   **Political Clash over Middle East Policy:** Prime Minister Donald Tusk has publicly rebuked members of the presidential administration regarding Poland's potential involvement in the Middle East conflict. The dispute centers on whether Poland should support U.S.-led operations in the Strait of Hormuz, with Tusk accusing the opposition of reckless posturing.
*   **Diplomatic Alignments:** Tensions are high ahead of the upcoming parliamentary elections, as J.D. Vance, U.S. Vice President, prepares to visit Budapest to signal stronger ties with Viktor Orbán, a move being closely monitored by European political observers.
*   **International Extradition Case:** A Warsaw court has approved the extradition of Russian archaeologist Aleksandr Butiagin to Ukraine, where he is wanted for unauthorized excavations in Crimea. The decision has prompted a legal challenge from his defense, highlighting the ongoing geopolitical complexities involving Russian citizens in Poland.
