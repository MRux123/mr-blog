## WORLD

* **Escalation in the Middle East:** Israeli Prime Minister Benjamin Netanyahu confirmed that Israel conducted an independent strike on an Iranian gas field, despite explicit requests from the U.S. administration to refrain from further military actions. This move marks a significant departure from current U.S. diplomatic strategy in the region.
* **Potential U.S. Policy Pivot:** In a surprising potential shift, the U.S. Treasury Department is considering a waiver for Iranian oil currently in transit. This reversal of longstanding sanctions policy reflects the high stakes surrounding global energy stability, particularly as traffic in the Strait of Hormuz remains heavily scrutinized.
* **Human Rights Crisis in Iran:** International concern is mounting as Iranian state media reports the execution of the first individuals linked to the recent anti-government protests, including a member of the national wrestling team. The sentencing has drawn condemnation from global observers monitoring the internal stability of the Iranian regime.

## POLAND

* **Political Polarization over EU Climate Policy:** Tensions between Law and Justice (PiS) and the Confederation party have intensified during recent public debates. The focal point of the dispute is the European Union's ETS (Emission Trading System), with PiS advocating for its dissolution. The rhetoric surrounding the debate reached a peak when representatives of the Confederation dismissed PiS's sudden anti-ETS stance as disingenuous, comparing it to "trusting a fox in a henhouse."
