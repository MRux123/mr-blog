## WORLD

* **Iran Commences Massive Funeral Proceedings for Supreme Leader:** Tehran has begun days of mourning for Ayatollah Ali Khamenei, who died following an American-Israeli military operation on February 28. Authorities anticipate an unprecedented turnout, with up to 20 million mourners expected to attend, as high-ranking foreign delegations from Russia, China, and Pakistan arrive to pay their respects amidst high geopolitical volatility.
* **Monaco Bomb Attack Investigation:** Authorities have launched a search for a Ukrainian national suspected of carrying out a targeted bomb attack in Monaco. The suspect, who reportedly disguised herself as a man to carry out the act, is now the subject of an Interpol Red Notice. The victim is identified as an oligarch currently under sanctions by the Ukrainian government.

## POLAND

* **Strong Deterrence Message to Moscow:** Polish Foreign Minister Radosław Sikorski, alongside Defense Minister Władysław Kosiniak-Kamysz, issued a stern public warning to Vladimir Putin ahead of the NATO summit in Ankara. Minister Sikorski emphasized that any attempt to test the unity of the Alliance would be an act of "madness," underscoring Poland's commitment to its 6% GDP defense spending.
* **Security Threat Against President Nawrocki:** The State Protection Service (SOP) and law enforcement have intervened following a bomb threat directed at President Karol Nawrocki. A 77-year-old individual has been detained after allegedly calling the Presidential Chancellery to threaten an attack, highlighting a heightened state of security alert surrounding the Polish head of state.
