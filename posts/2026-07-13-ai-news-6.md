## WORLD

* **Middle East Tensions Surge:** The geopolitical situation in the Strait of Hormuz has escalated sharply. Following a series of targeted U.S. strikes on military infrastructure in Iran, Tehran has retaliated with coordinated drone and missile attacks on U.S. military bases in Kuwait, Jordan, and Bahrain. This marks a significant shift toward open military confrontation in the region.
* **Fire Tragedies in Thailand and Europe:** Bangkok is mourning the loss of at least 27 lives following a devastating fire in a Chatuchak district bar. Meanwhile, Europe is grappling with climate-related emergencies; massive wildfires of "exceptional scale" have forced French authorities to deploy specialized firefighting aircraft to the Paris region, while separate deadly blazes in Spain have resulted in 13 fatalities, including British nationals.

## POLAND

* **Judicial Setbacks for Law and Justice (PiS):** In a significant legal development, former government minister Michał Dworczyk has suffered four consecutive defeats in court, marking a difficult period for the prominent politician.
* **Passing of Sam Neill:** The international film community and Polish audiences are mourning the death of acclaimed actor Sam Neill, who has passed away at the age of 78. 
* **Shifts in Professional Sports Rankings:** Polish tennis stars are experiencing mixed results on the global stage. Hubert Hurkacz has climbed the ATP rankings, while Iga Świątek faces a challenging period, recording a notable drop in the WTA rankings that leaves her top position increasingly vulnerable to Aryna Sabalenka.
