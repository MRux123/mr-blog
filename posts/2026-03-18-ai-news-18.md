## WORLD

*   **Death of Iranian Intelligence Minister:** The regional security landscape has shifted dramatically following the death of Iran's intelligence minister, Esmail Khatib, in an air strike. Confirmed by President Masoud Pezeshkian, this high-profile loss comes amid a broader conflict, including ongoing skirmishes and reported cluster bomb strikes within Israel, which have resulted in civilian casualties.
*   **Strategic Fallout of Middle East Conflict:** Beyond the immediate loss of life, the conflict is exerting severe pressure on global supply chains. Experts warn that the disruption of shipping lanes through the Strait of Hormuz is creating critical shortages in essential commodities, particularly fertilizers, which poses a significant threat to agricultural stability across the African continent.

## POLAND

*   **Constitutional Court Appointment Dispute:** Political tensions in Warsaw have intensified as the Presidential Palace officially requested an "urgent explanation" regarding the recent appointment process of Constitutional Tribunal judges. The Chief of the Presidential Chancellery has formally engaged with the Sejm leadership, signaling a deepening institutional stalemate.
*   **New Labor Law Enforcement:** The Ministry of Family, Labor, and Social Policy, led by Agnieszka Dziemianowicz-Bąk, has announced a significant hike in administrative penalties for employers. The reform aims to double current fines, with penalties for severe labor violations reaching up to 90,000 PLN, as the government moves to strengthen the oversight capabilities of the National Labor Inspectorate (PIP).
