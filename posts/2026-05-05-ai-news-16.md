## WORLD

*   **Heightened Tensions Amidst Victory Day Preparations:** Russia is intensifying security measures ahead of the May 9th Victory Day celebrations. In a move to mitigate the risk of targeted strikes, Moscow and St. Petersburg have restricted mobile internet access. Reports indicate widespread infrastructure disruptions, including localized outages in banking and transport services, as the Kremlin pushes for a scaled-down but tightly controlled military display.
*   **Competing Ceasefire Proposals:** The ongoing conflict between Russia and Ukraine has entered a complex phase with the announcement of rival ceasefire plans. While Moscow has publicly demanded a pause in fighting on May 8–9, Kyiv has proposed an earlier timeline, effectively framing any subsequent Russian military activity as a breach of the agreed-upon ceasefire.
*   **Political Instability in Romania:** The Romanian government has faced a significant setback following a successful no-confidence vote against Prime Minister Ilie Bolojan. The ousting occurred after a fracture within the ruling coalition, with the leading party aligning with the far-right opposition to dissolve the current administration.

## POLAND

*   **EU Financial Milestone:** Poland is set to become the first European nation to finalize a major loan agreement under the EU’s "SAFE" program. The signing, scheduled for this Friday in Warsaw, will provide the country with an initial liquidity injection of 6.5 billion euros, marking a strategic advancement in Poland’s economic integration with European funding mechanisms.
*   **Integrity Concerns in State Examinations:** The Central Examination Board (CKE) is currently addressing reports of significant irregularities regarding the security of high school final exam (matura) materials. Investigations are underway to determine if the unauthorized online publication of exam papers warrants criminal proceedings, a move that could have serious legal repercussions for those involved.
*   **Industrial Retrenchment:** The labor market in Legnica faces a sharp contraction following the announcement that Eisberg Polska, a major producer of prepared salads and lunch products, is entering liquidation. The closure will result in the loss of nearly 200 jobs, signaling a significant economic blow to the local region.
