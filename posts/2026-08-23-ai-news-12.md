## WORLD

*   **Global Food Security Under Pressure:** A convergence of geopolitical instability, climate change, and trade disputes is placing unprecedented strain on the world's "breadbaskets." Analysts warn that deteriorating harvests and disruptions to critical trade routes are likely to drive food prices upward, threatening global food security in the coming months.
*   **Geopolitical Alignment in Latin America:** A new wave of right-wing leaders across Latin America is increasingly coordinating across borders. By openly endorsing allies and sharing political rhetoric against ideological opponents, these leaders are actively influencing each other's national elections and reshaping the region's diplomatic landscape.
*   **Geophysical Anomalies:** Scientists have reported an unprecedented shift within the Earth's core, where a massive current of liquid iron beneath the Pacific Ocean has unexpectedly reversed its direction. While the precise drivers of this phenomenon remain under investigation, researchers are closely monitoring satellite data to understand its long-term implications.

## POLAND

*   **Legal Recognition of Same-Sex Marriages:** In a significant shift for civil administration, Polish Registry Offices (USC) have begun accepting the transcription of foreign same-sex marriage certificates. This development follows recent rulings by the Court of Justice of the European Union (TSUE) and the Supreme Administrative Court (NSA), marking a major change in the implementation of family law within the country.
*   **Controversial Ukrainian Legislation:** President Volodymyr Zelensky has signed a law establishing a "National Pantheon," a move that has sparked diplomatic friction with Poland. Warsaw has expressed deep concerns that the initiative could honor figures historically linked to atrocities committed against Poles, complicating the narrative of shared history and national memory.
*   **Aviation Sector Strains:** A large-scale strike by air traffic controllers in Norway has caused widespread disruption to European travel, leading to the cancellation of at least 150 flights. The situation, which prompted the temporary closure of Oslo Gardermoen Airport, has directly impacted connections to Poland, with experts warning that service interruptions may persist beyond the weekend.
