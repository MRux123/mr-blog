## WORLD

* **Escalation in Ukraine:** Russian strikes have targeted critical infrastructure in Kyiv, resulting in significant casualties, including children. The attack represents a continuation of the aggressive campaign against Ukrainian rail and civilian workers.
* **Geopolitical Realignment:** A sharp contrast in global leadership was on display as Xi Jinping hosted the leaders of Russia and Iran, while European powers confronted the Trump administration at the G20. These meetings highlight a growing "split-screen" reality in international diplomacy.
* **Security Alert at German Infrastructure:** Security services are responding to the discovery of explosive devices near a coal power plant in Jänschwalde, Brandenburg. The incident is being investigated as a potential act of sabotage against critical energy infrastructure.

## POLAND

* **NATO Defense Obligations:** A significant political controversy has erupted following comments by former Defense Minister Mariusz Błaszczak regarding the collective defense of NATO’s Baltic members. His questioning of Poland's commitment to assist Estonia, Latvia, and Lithuania in the event of a Russian attack has sparked widespread criticism, with opponents arguing he is undermining the foundational principles of the Alliance.
* **Defense Funding Breakthrough:** Progress has been reported regarding the reimbursement of funds for military equipment provided to Ukraine. Negotiations are underway to determine the final amount due to Warsaw, marking a key milestone in Poland's ongoing support for its neighbor's defense efforts.
