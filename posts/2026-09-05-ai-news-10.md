## WORLD

*   **Political Shift in Germany:** The state of Saxony-Anhalt faces a historic political turning point, as the far-right Alternative for Germany (AfD) party surges in the polls. This development threatens the long-standing postwar taboo against extremist parties holding power, signaling a potential shift in the regional political landscape.
*   **Escalation in Sudan:** A secret dossier has provided compelling evidence regarding the use of chemical weapons by the Sudanese military. These findings implicate the military in the systematic deployment of toxic munitions, further complicating the already devastating civil war and raising concerns about international humanitarian law violations.
*   **Russian Mobilization Concerns:** Heightened anxiety persists across Russia as rumors of a new mass mobilization circulate among the public. Despite official denials from the Kremlin, many military-age men are actively seeking ways to avoid potential conscription, reflecting deep-seated distrust and the precarious nature of the ongoing conflict.

## POLAND

*   **Security Concerns and Diplomatic Tensions:** Security alerts remain elevated following reports of Russian strikes on airfields just as Trump administration envoys are scheduled to visit the region. This provocative timing adds a significant layer of tension to ongoing international diplomatic efforts.
*   **Political Confrontation:** Domestic political friction has intensified after Zbigniew Ziobro publicly challenged Prime Minister Donald Tusk. The confrontation centers on accusations regarding the management of state affairs and the investigation into the collapse of the Zondacrypto exchange, highlighting the deepening rift between the ruling coalition and the conservative opposition.
*   **Demographic Shifts:** Data from Eurostat indicates a consistent downward trend in the number of Ukrainian war refugees residing in Poland. The statistics show a steady decrease on both a monthly and annual basis, suggesting evolving patterns in the displacement and settlement of those fleeing the conflict.
