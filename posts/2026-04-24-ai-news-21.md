## WORLD

* **Diplomatic Channels Open in Pakistan:** In a high-stakes diplomatic move, U.S. figures Witkoff and Kushner have arrived in Pakistan to engage in direct discussions with Iranian officials, including Foreign Minister Abbas Araghchi. This development marks a significant effort to de-escalate ongoing regional tensions through unconventional mediation.
* **Macron’s Vision for European Autonomy:** French President Emmanuel Macron issued a stern warning regarding the geopolitical landscape, categorizing the leaders of the United States, Russia, and China—Donald Trump, Vladimir Putin, and Xi Jinping—as fundamentally opposed to European interests. Macron emphasized the urgent necessity for the European continent to prioritize self-reliance in security and strategic policy.

## POLAND

* **Special Prosecutor Team to Investigate Zondacrypto:** The Polish National Prosecutor's Office has established a specialized task force to investigate alleged criminal activities linked to the cryptocurrency platform Zondacrypto. The unit, which includes seasoned experts from the Cybercrime Division, highlights a hardening stance on financial sector oversight in the digital assets market.
* **Internal Strife Within PiS:** Former Prime Minister Mateusz Morawiecki has publicly addressed internal fractures within the Law and Justice (PiS) party. By suggesting that party candidate Przemysław Czarnek was "misled" by external influences, Morawiecki has signaled significant friction among the party's leadership and strategic vision.
* **Fatal Incident Investigation:** The legal proceedings following the death of Łukasz Litewka have intensified. Authorities have expressed skepticism regarding the testimony provided by the 57-year-old driver who admitted to the fatal collision, leading the prosecution to seek a temporary detention order pending further investigation.
