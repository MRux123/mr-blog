## WORLD

* **Iran-US Ceasefire Takes Hold:** A fragile two-week truce between the United States and Iran has officially commenced, leading to a significant rally in global stock markets and a sharp 15% plunge in crude oil prices. While world leaders, including Pope Leo XIV, have cautiously welcomed the pause, uncertainty remains regarding the long-term sustainability of the agreement and the ongoing conflict in Lebanon involving Hezbollah.
* **Economic Ripple Effects:** Energy experts warn that despite the immediate market relief, restoring normal flow to the Persian Gulf's energy systems will be a complex process taking months, rather than days. Meanwhile, world leaders continue to grapple with the unpredictable nature of President Trump’s foreign policy, which has caused significant economic and political instability across Europe.

## POLAND

* **Legal Actions Against Former Official:** The Warsaw District Prosecutor's Office has formally indicted Krzysztof Szczucki, the former head of the Government Legislation Center (RCL). Szczucki faces charges of abuse of power, an offense carrying a potential prison sentence of one to ten years.
* **Constitutional Crisis Deepens:** Four newly selected Constitutional Tribunal judges have taken the unprecedented step of inviting President Karol Nawrocki to the Sejm to administer their oaths of office. This move represents a significant escalation in the ongoing standoff regarding judicial appointments and the constitutional impasse between the legislative and executive branches.
* **Security Investigation:** Polish authorities, in cooperation with the Internal Security Agency (ABW), have detained a 23-year-old Polish national in Zielona Góra. The suspect is accused of aiding in the intentional arson of a factory in Pardubice, Czech Republic, highlighting continued cross-border security concerns.
