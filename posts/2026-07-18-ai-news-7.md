## WORLD

* **Political Turmoil in Ukraine:** Significant civil unrest has erupted across major Ukrainian cities, including Kyiv and Lviv, following President Volodymyr Zelenskyy's decision to dismiss Defense Minister Mykhailo Fedorov. Tens of thousands of citizens have taken to the streets, with protesters also demanding the resignation of the Commander-in-Chief of the Armed Forces, Oleksandr Syrskyi, signaling a deepening internal crisis amidst the ongoing conflict.
* **Escalation of Ukrainian Strikes on Russian Infrastructure:** Ukrainian drone operations targeting Russian logistical and fuel infrastructure have intensified significantly. A notable strike in Kotovsk, Tambov Oblast, resulted in the destruction of a major logistics center and claimed the lives of seven workers, marking a shift toward more lethal and disruptive long-range operations within Russian territory.
* **Espionage Scandal in Morocco:** A former intelligence insider has exposed the widespread deployment of Pegasus spyware by Moroccan security services. The revelations detail the systematic surveillance of foreign diplomats, journalists, and government officials from France and Spain, further straining international relations and reigniting debates over the use of NSO Group’s controversial surveillance technology.

## POLAND

* **Progress on the Volhynia Issue:** In a pivotal move, the Ukrainian government has signaled a new approach to historical reconciliation with Poland. By opening archives and granting new permits for exhumations regarding the events in Volhynia, President Zelenskyy is seeking to address long-standing historical grievances. The head of Poland's National Security Bureau, Bartosz Grodecki, welcomed the move as a necessary step for the future of bilateral relations.
* **Security and Law Enforcement Challenges:** Polish authorities are addressing a series of internal security incidents, including the sentencing of football hooligans who conducted a violent attack on motorists along the A9 highway, as well as an investigation into fuel theft from a military facility in Olsztyn, highlighting ongoing challenges in maintaining public safety and internal military discipline.
