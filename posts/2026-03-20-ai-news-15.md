## WORLD

* **Escalation in the Strait of Hormuz:** Military tensions have reached a critical threshold as U.S. forces initiate targeted strikes against Iranian assets. The campaign, involving warplanes and attack helicopters, aims to secure the Strait of Hormuz, causing significant volatility in global energy markets as strikes hit key Gulf infrastructure.
* **Geopolitical Realignment:** In an effort to decouple from Chinese supply chains, the U.S. is aggressively pushing for a critical minerals partnership with Brazil. The move reflects a broader strategic shift in Washington to secure rare earth elements despite initial resistance from Brazilian authorities.

## POLAND

* **Military Withdrawal from Iraq:** The Polish Ministry of National Defence has confirmed the successful evacuation of the Polish Military Contingent from Iraq. Minister Władysław Kosiniak-Kamysz stated that the primary objective of the operation was the safety of personnel amidst deteriorating regional stability.
* **Legal Controversy Over Same-Sex Marriage:** The Supreme Administrative Court (NSA) has issued a ruling mandating the transcription of a same-sex marriage certificate issued abroad. The decision has sparked an immediate political reaction, with opposition party PiS announcing a formal challenge to the Constitutional Tribunal, signaling a new escalation in the domestic ideological debate.
