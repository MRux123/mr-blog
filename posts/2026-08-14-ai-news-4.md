## WORLD

* **US Uncovers Massive Tariff Evasion Scheme:** A new report indicates that dozens of nations have been instrumental in helping China bypass US tariffs. Beijing allegedly routed goods through third-party countries with more favorable trade terms to circumvent high import levies, highlighting significant vulnerabilities in global supply chain transparency.
* **Capital Punishment Milestone:** In a rare and somber development, authorities have executed multiple inmates on the same day, marking the first time such an event has occurred in over a decade. The move has reignited international debates regarding state-sanctioned capital punishment.

## POLAND

* **E-commerce Market Contraction:** The introduction of new European Union tariffs has triggered a significant decline in the popularity of Chinese e-commerce platforms among Polish consumers. The added costs associated with small-package imports from outside the EU appear to be effectively reshaping local shopping habits.
* **Regional Security Measures:** Estonian authorities have extended restrictions on night-time border crossings, underscoring ongoing geopolitical tensions and the commitment to hardening regional security protocols in the Baltic states.
* **Infrastructure Crisis on the Danube:** Hungarian political figures are evaluating a drastic, high-cost engineering operation involving the deliberate sinking of barges to ensure the continued operation of vital power plants amidst fluctuating water levels, illustrating the growing impact of environmental stability on national energy security.
