## WORLD

*   **US Election Momentum:** As the nation heads to the polls, Donald Trump has signaled early Republican optimism following preliminary results from Guam, where candidate Tony Ada is projected to win the gubernatorial race. While the former president views this as a bellwether for the broader US elections, recent polling data suggests significant voter skepticism regarding his overall candidacy.
*   **Aviation Investigation:** The NTSB has launched a critical investigation into a Florida plane crash after discovering key aircraft components nearly three miles away from the primary wreckage site. Investigators believe these parts detached mid-air prior to the impact, significantly altering the scope of the safety inquiry.
*   **Spanish Government Resignation:** Following the devastating railway disaster that claimed lives last week, the Spanish Minister of Transport has officially stepped down. The move comes amid growing pressure for political accountability regarding infrastructure safety and emergency response oversight.

## POLAND

*   **Judicial Independence Protests:** Under the banner "Light for the Tribunal," citizens gathered in major cities, including Warsaw, Gdańsk, and Poznań. Organized by the Committee for the Defence of Democracy (KOD), the demonstrations were a direct response to perceived political interference in the judiciary and intimidation tactics directed at judges.
*   **Internal Police Conflict:** Tensions within the law enforcement ranks have intensified as formal disciplinary proceedings were initiated against Jacek Łukasik, head of the "Policyjna Solidarność" union. The action follows Łukasik’s vocal public criticism regarding police operations conducted in front of the Constitutional Tribunal and his formal complaints filed with the Police Chief.
