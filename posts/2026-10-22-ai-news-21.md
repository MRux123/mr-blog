## WORLD

*   **Escalating Conflict in Yemen:** The regional security situation has deteriorated significantly following a deadly Houthi strike on a Saudi airport near the Dammam oil infrastructure. The U.S. State Department has responded by ordering the departure of non-essential diplomatic staff, highlighting growing concerns over the targeting of energy and aviation sectors.
*   **International Criminal Court Recognized:** In a move that signals support for international rule of law amid geopolitical friction, the European Union has awarded the International Criminal Court its top human rights prize. This recognition arrives as the Trump administration continues efforts to challenge the Court’s jurisdiction and investigations.

## POLAND

*   **Government Crisis in Warsaw:** Minister Katarzyna Pełczyńska-Nałęcz has officially resigned from the government, citing a fundamental disagreement over values and legislative priorities within the ruling coalition—specifically regarding term limits in local government. The move has sent shockwaves through the political landscape, with reactions ranging from confusion among coalition partners to accusations of political opportunism from opposition figures.
*   **Coalition Instability:** The sudden departure of a key minister has sparked intense speculation regarding the structural integrity of the ruling coalition. Senior political figures, including Szymon Hołownia, have acknowledged the friction, raising concerns about the potential for further instability within the cabinet in the coming weeks.
