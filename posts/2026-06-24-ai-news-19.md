## WORLD

* **Extreme Heat Wave Sweeps Europe:** A record-breaking heat wave is currently affecting several European countries, with Italy facing severe impacts as temperatures reach lethal levels. Authorities report multiple casualties, underscoring the growing frequency and intensity of extreme weather events across the continent.
* **Geopolitical Shifts in the Middle East:** Secretary of State Marco Rubio is conducting a high-stakes diplomatic mission across Gulf Arab states to bolster security assurances following recent regional conflicts involving Iran. Concurrently, the Israeli government has declared that there are no current American demands for a withdrawal of its forces from Lebanon, signaling a potential hardening of military positioning in the region.

## POLAND

* **Russia Faces Fuel Crisis:** Following targeted Ukrainian drone strikes on critical infrastructure, including the key Moscow refinery, Russia is grappling with a severe fuel shortage. Domestic gasoline prices have seen their sharpest increase in two decades, forcing Moscow to seek emergency fuel imports from Kazakhstan as aviation sectors also sound the alarm.
* **Belarusian De-escalation:** Under pressure from a Ukrainian ultimatum, Belarus has reportedly deactivated relay equipment previously utilized by Russian forces to command drones. President Zelensky confirmed the move, marking a significant tactical shift on the northern front.
* **Diplomatic Strategy for Ukraine's Reconstruction:** Prime Minister Donald Tusk has reaffirmed that Poland will proceed with the planned Reconstruction of Ukraine conference in Gdańsk. Following a meeting of the E5 group in Berlin, Tusk emphasized the importance of maintaining trans-Atlantic unity and structural support for Kyiv ahead of the upcoming NATO summit in Ankara.
