## WORLD

*   **Escalation in Southern Lebanon:** Satellite imagery confirms the systematic demolition of over 1,400 buildings in Southern Lebanon since March 2, indicating an intensified military strategy in the region.
*   **Shifting Defense Paradigms in Japan:** The Japanese government has initiated a historic policy change by relaxing restrictions on the export of defense equipment. This move, aimed at bolstering regional stability, reflects Tokyo's growing concern over the unpredictable trajectory of U.S. foreign policy.
*   **Diplomatic Efforts in the Middle East:** Pakistani mediators have arrived in Iran to sustain ongoing peace dialogues. Despite recent White House skepticism regarding potential ceasefire extensions, diplomatic channels remain active in an effort to reach a lasting agreement.

## POLAND

*   **Political Fractures within the Right:** Tensions within the Law and Justice (PiS) party have reached a critical point. Reports of a leaked list of MPs aligned with former Prime Minister Mateusz Morawiecki, coupled with his move to form a new association, are being viewed as a direct challenge to Jarosław Kaczyński’s long-standing authority over the party.
*   **Fiscal Policy Debate:** A rare convergence of opinion has emerged between presidential candidate Karol Nawrocki and the Third Way (Trzecia Droga) party, as both sides express support for raising the second tax threshold from 120,000 to 140,000 PLN, signaling a potential shift in economic policy priorities.
*   **Institutional Leadership:** The Polish People's Party (PSL) has confirmed its support for Dr. Mateusz Szpytma as the next head of the Institute of National Remembrance (IPN), marking a significant step in the ongoing reshuffling of key state institutions.
