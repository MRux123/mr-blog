## WORLD

* **Middle East Tensions Escalate:** The geopolitical situation in the Middle East has reached a critical juncture following joint U.S. and Saudi airstrikes against Iranian-backed proxies in Iraq. The offensive has triggered an immediate spike in global oil prices and raises significant concerns regarding a broader regional confrontation.
* **Japan’s Ongoing Earthquake Crisis:** Rescue efforts in Kumamoto, Japan, have entered a desperate phase as emergency teams work to extract survivors from buildings collapsed by a 6.8-magnitude earthquake. The situation remains volatile, with authorities warning that over 100 aftershocks have already struck, with the potential for further significant tremors.
* **Europe’s Climate Reckoning:** The continent is currently grappling with an unprecedented summer characterized by extreme temperatures and catastrophic wildfires. Experts warn that the current climate crisis is no longer a future threat but a present reality, with governments struggling to coordinate effective emergency responses to the widespread devastation.

Internal instability could prove costly for the party's future electoral prospects as the power struggle among its leadership intensifies.
