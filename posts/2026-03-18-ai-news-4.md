## WORLD

* **Full-Scale Conflict Escalation:** Tensions in the Middle East have reached a critical breaking point. Following the deaths of high-ranking Iranian officials, including Ali Larijani, Iran has launched a direct missile strike against Tel Aviv. Simultaneously, Israeli forces conducted extensive overnight bombardment across Beirut, resulting in at least six fatalities and dozens of injuries.
* **US Military Intervention:** In a significant escalation of military engagement, the United States Central Command (CENTCOM) confirmed a major strike against Iranian subterranean missile facilities near the Strait of Hormuz. The mission utilized 2-ton bunker-busting bombs, signaling a direct attempt to neutralize Iran's anti-ship capabilities in this vital maritime corridor.

## POLAND

* **Diplomatic Crisis over Energy:** Hungarian Prime Minister Viktor Orbán has escalated his standoff with the European Union, threatening to block a €90 billion aid package for Ukraine. Orbán demands the restoration of Russian oil shipments via the Druzhba pipeline, which have been curtailed by Ukrainian transit policies. The move has led to fuel shortages across Hungary, further straining internal EU cohesion during a period of intense geopolitical instability.
