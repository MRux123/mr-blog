## WORLD

* **U.S. Military Strikes Iranian Targets:** Following an attack on a container ship in the Strait of Hormuz, the United States has launched retaliatory strikes against Iranian infrastructure. The operation targeted weapons storage sites, drone facilities, and radar installations. Tensions remain at a critical high, with the Islamic Revolutionary Guard Corps issuing threats of further retaliation, sparking fears of a broader regional conflict.
* **Diplomatic Breakthrough in Middle East Negotiations:** A framework agreement has been signed by Israel, Lebanon, and the United States, representing a significant step toward a long-term peace process. Announced by U.S. Secretary of State Marco Rubio, the deal follows four days of intense negotiations in Washington and includes provisions for the partial withdrawal of Israeli forces from parts of Lebanon.
* **Diplomatic Rift Between Trump and Meloni:** The once-solid political alliance between Italian Prime Minister Giorgia Meloni and U.S. President Donald Trump has significantly deteriorated. Observers note a transition from their previous alignment—where Meloni was dubbed the "Trump whisperer"—to a public exchange of personal insults and political friction that threatens to undermine bilateral cooperation.

## POLAND

* **Allegations of Malpractice at Warsaw Hospital:** Corruption allegations have emerged regarding the Grochów Hospital in Warsaw. City Councilor Paweł Lech, formerly of the Civic Coalition, has publicly detailed claims of systemic irregularities, including "ghost employment" and the widespread use of dual employment contracts. The situation has prompted calls for a deeper investigation into the facility's management practices.
* **Record-Breaking EuroJackpot Win:** A massive €360 million (PLN) windfall has been claimed by a lucky participant in the EuroJackpot lottery. The win marks one of the largest payouts in the history of the lottery in Poland, fueling public interest in state-run gambling results and domestic economic sentiment.
