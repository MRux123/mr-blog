## WORLD

*   **Andriy Yermak faces corruption charges:** The former head of the Ukrainian presidential office has been officially charged by the National Anti-Corruption Bureau of Ukraine (NABU) and the Specialized Anti-Corruption Prosecutor's Office (SAP). The investigation focuses on the alleged laundering of 460 million hryvnias derived from criminal activities, specifically tied to luxury real estate developments near Kyiv.
*   **Trump criticizes Iran ceasefire:** US President Donald Trump has dismissed the current month-long ceasefire with Iran, describing it as "unbelievably weak" and on "massive life support," suggesting a potential escalation in diplomatic tensions.
*   **High-level US business delegation to China:** Elon Musk and Tim Cook are among 17 prominent US executives expected to accompany President Trump on his upcoming visit to China, where the group is set to hold high-stakes meetings with President Xi Jinping.

## POLAND

*   **Tomasz Lenz expelled from Civic Coalition (KO):** Senator Tomasz Lenz has been formally excluded from the Koalicja Obywatelska parliamentary club. The decision, reportedly made at the request of Donald Tusk, follows a public scandal regarding allegations that Lenz’s son received preferential treatment during a medical procedure in a state hospital, bypassing standard queues and protocols.
*   **Zbigniew Ziobro’s US trip scrutinized:** The ongoing stay of former Justice Minister Zbigniew Ziobro in the United States continues to draw political commentary. Marcin Przydacz from the Presidential Palace confirmed that the administration was not involved in the trip, while further criticizing Ziobro's past impact on the Polish judicial system and expressing doubt regarding the fairness of potential legal proceedings against him.
