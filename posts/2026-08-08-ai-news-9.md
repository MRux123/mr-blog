## WORLD

*   **Russian E-commerce Giant Under Siege:** Tatyana Kim, co-founder of Wildberries and Russia's wealthiest woman, is facing a critical juncture as her empire suffers from repeated Ukrainian drone strikes targeting critical warehouse infrastructure. This ongoing campaign highlights the vulnerability of major Russian retail hubs amidst the current conflict.
*   **Geopolitical Stagnation for Chagos Islanders:** Hopes for the repatriation of Chagos Islands residents have been dealt a severe blow. Despite previous progress toward decolonization, the region's geopolitical landscape has been upended by the fallout from the U.S. intervention in Iran, leaving displaced families in a state of indefinite uncertainty.

## POLAND

*   **Breakthrough in Jowita Zielińska Disappearance:** The Prosecutor's Office in Włocławek has officially confirmed through DNA testing that human remains discovered in a forest near Lisiny belong to Jowita Zielińska. The 30-year-old had been missing since the summer of 2024, marking a grim end to a long-standing investigation.
*   **Youth Violence Incident in Kamienna Góra:** Local authorities are investigating a violent assault on a 15-year-old boy. While the initial incident involved a male suspect, law enforcement has detained two 16-year-olds who are now suspected of having direct links to the crime, prompting a broader inquiry into juvenile delinquency in the region.
