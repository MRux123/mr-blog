## WORLD

*   **Nuclear Facility Targeted:** Ukrainian officials have reported a Russian drone strike targeting a spent nuclear fuel storage facility near the Chornobyl plant. President Zelensky has condemned the act as "vile," raising significant international concerns regarding nuclear safety and the potential for ecological catastrophe amidst the ongoing conflict.
*   **Escalating Violence in Israel:** A deadly terrorist attack has occurred in central Israel, resulting in at least one fatality. While Hamas has publicly praised the assault, they have stopped short of claiming direct responsibility, further destabilizing the fragile security situation in the region.
*   **Political Shifts in Armenia:** As Armenia heads to the polls, Prime Minister Nikol Pashinyan is seeking a third term. The election takes place under significant diplomatic pressure from Russia, which continues to challenge the current administration's pro-Western foreign policy trajectory.

## POLAND

*   **Healthcare Crisis Investigation:** A tragic case involving the death of 20-year-old Julia Liegmann has sparked national outrage regarding the efficiency of the Polish medical system. Reports indicate that despite an urgent need for an Intensive Care Unit (ICU) admission, the patient waited eight hours without receiving necessary care, despite available capacity nearby.
*   **Tragic Death of Teenager:** Authorities have confirmed that the disappearance of a 16-year-old girl in the Lublin region ended in a homicide. Police have apprehended a suspect in his 20s, and the investigation is currently being managed by the prosecutor's office.
*   **Workforce Productivity Debate:** A new report from the Polish Economic Institute highlights that Polish citizens work some of the longest hours in the European Union. However, the data suggests that these extended hours do not correlate with higher productivity levels, igniting a debate regarding current labor market structures and management practices.
