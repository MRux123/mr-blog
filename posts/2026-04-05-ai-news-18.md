## WORLD

*   **Tensions Peak Following Iran Jet Downing and US Rescue:** The geopolitical situation in the Middle East has reached a critical juncture after an American F-15 fighter jet was shot down over southern Iran. Following a daring US ground operation to extract the surviving airman, both nations have claimed a strategic victory. Analysts warn that this aggressive posturing and the subsequent rhetoric from Washington could fuel a dangerous cycle of escalation.
*   **Pope Leo XIV Calls for Global Peace:** In his inaugural Easter Mass, Pope Leo XIV issued a poignant plea for global reconciliation. The pontiff explicitly condemned those who "wage war," marking a clear shift in the Vatican's diplomatic tone as international conflicts continue to destabilize global security.

## POLAND

*   **Political Crisis Brews Over Balkan Stream Sabotage Attempt:** Hungary has reported a foiled plot to sabotage the Balkan Stream pipeline, a critical artery for Russian gas imports. While the Hungarian government frames the incident as a direct attack on its sovereignty, the opposition has cast doubt, alleging the narrative may be a staged government operation intended to sway voters ahead of next week's elections.
*   **Diplomatic Ultimatum Regarding Iran:** Reports indicate that the US administration is pushing for a decisive diplomatic breakthrough with Iran, with President Donald Trump suggesting that a resolution regarding the Strait of Hormuz must be reached by Monday. The Polish geopolitical landscape remains on high alert as these developments threaten to destabilize energy markets and regional security.
