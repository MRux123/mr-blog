## WORLD

* **Escalation in Liberia:** Former Vice President Jewel Howard-Taylor has been formally charged in a major drug-trafficking investigation. Allegations include the facilitation, importation, and distribution of illegal substances, as well as complex money laundering schemes, marking a significant development in the region's political and legal landscape.
* **Arctic Ecosystem Transformation:** Climate change continues to reshape the Greenlandic environment as retreating sea ice creates new corridors for marine life. Scientists are documenting a shift in whale migration patterns, with giant whales increasingly utilizing these ice-free waters for intensified feeding activities.

## POLAND

* **Military Response to Regional Conflict:** The Polish Armed Forces have deployed fighter jets following a massive, coordinated Russian air assault against Kyiv. This defensive maneuver underscores the heightening regional tension and Poland's strategic commitment to maintaining airspace security in the wake of intensifying cross-border aggression.
