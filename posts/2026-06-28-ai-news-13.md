## WORLD

*   **Humanitarian Crisis in Venezuela:** Following the devastating twin earthquakes that struck the Venezuelan coast over three days ago, the situation remains dire. While rescue efforts have yielded success—including the miraculous recovery of two young boys from the rubble—authorities are struggling with logistical delays. The absence of heavy machinery and the occurrence of persistent aftershocks continue to hinder search operations, significantly diminishing the window for finding further survivors.
*   **Aviation Tragedies in France and Saudi Arabia:** The international aviation sector is reeling from two fatal incidents. In eastern France, an aircraft carrying skydivers crashed shortly after takeoff, resulting in the loss of all 11 people on board. Simultaneously, a separate helicopter disaster in Saudi Arabia has claimed the lives of 14 individuals, prompting immediate investigations into both tragedies.

## POLAND

*   **Controversy Over National Pantheon:** President Volodymyr Zelenskyy has announced plans for the construction of a National Pantheon, asserting the state's autonomy in determining its historical narrative. The initiative emphasizes that the nation will decide which figures to honor, signaling a firm stance against external pressure regarding historical remembrance.
*   **Infrastructure Failures Cause Travel Chaos:** Public transport in the Mazovia region faced significant disruption today as a technical failure forced the emergency evacuation of approximately 400 passengers from a train. The situation was compounded by extreme heat, turning the commute for passengers on the "Chełmoński" line into a precarious ordeal and highlighting ongoing concerns regarding rail infrastructure reliability during the summer season.
