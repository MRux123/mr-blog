## WORLD

*   **Trump Mediates Lebanon-Israel Cease-Fire:** Donald Trump has announced a brokered cease-fire between Israel and Hezbollah, following a period of intense volatility. While the move is being viewed as a significant diplomatic breakthrough, international observers remain cautious, awaiting formal confirmation from the involved parties.
*   **Vatican-US Diplomatic Spat:** Pope Leo XIV has issued a sharp critique of world leaders prioritizing military expenditure over human development. The remarks, delivered during his visit to Cameroon, come in the wake of a highly public feud with Donald Trump, who recently characterized the Pope as "weak on crime." This friction has triggered a broader conversation about the influence of religious leadership in modern geopolitical discourse.
*   **Escalation in Ukraine:** Russia has launched its most lethal aerial offensive in several months, resulting in 18 reported casualties in Ukraine. This surge in violence follows a series of drone attacks within Russian borders, signaling a fragile state of affairs following the Orthodox Easter period.

## POLAND

*   **Macron’s Upcoming Visit to Poland:** Diplomatic ties between Warsaw and Paris are set to strengthen as President Emmanuel Macron prepares for an official visit on April 20. The trip, initiated by Prime Minister Donald Tusk, will focus on the Polish-French Friendship Day and will include a ceremony in Gdańsk where the Bronisław Geremek Award will be presented.
*   **Security Clearance Controversy:** The national security debate continues as Gen. Jarosław Stróżyk, head of the Military Counterintelligence Service (SKW), reaffirmed that Sławomir Cenckiewicz, chief of the National Security Bureau (BBN), currently lacks the necessary security clearance to access classified information, pending further judicial review.
*   **Domestic Political Tensions:** The political landscape remains fluid as prominent figures, including Ryszard Kalisz, express growing concerns over the strategic moves of Mateusz Morawiecki, warning that recent actions could lead to further fragmentation within the political sphere.
