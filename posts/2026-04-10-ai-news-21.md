## WORLD

* **Iran-US Peace Talks:** Diplomatic efforts to de-escalate tensions between the United States and Iran have moved to Pakistan. As the Iranian delegation arrives for critical peace negotiations, US Vice President JD Vance is currently en route to the summit. The international community remains cautious, closely monitoring the durability of the current ceasefire and the potential for a long-term resolution.
* **Middle East Diplomacy:** High-level officials from Israel and Lebanon are scheduled to meet in the United States this coming Tuesday. The talks, aimed at addressing ongoing regional instability, are contingent upon the maintenance of an active ceasefire, which remains a primary condition for Lebanese participation.

## POLAND

* **Political Polling Trends:** A new Opinia24 poll indicates a significant shift in the Polish political landscape. The Civic Coalition (KO) has solidified its lead with 35.2% support, widening the gap between them and the Law and Justice (PiS) party, which currently trails at 22%. The Confederation party rounds out the top three with 12.8%.
* **Environmental Emergency in Pomerania:** Emergency services are currently battling a large-scale fire at a tire recycling facility in Rożental. Local authorities have issued an urgent warning to residents in the Pelplin area, advising them to remain indoors and keep windows closed due to the presence of hazardous substances detected in the air as a result of the combustion.
