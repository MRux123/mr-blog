## WORLD

* **Papal Appeal for Migrant Integration:** During a poignant visit to the Italian island of Lampedusa, Pope Leon XIV has called upon European nations to adopt a more comprehensive strategy regarding the ongoing migration crisis. The pontiff emphasized that Europe possesses the capacity to welcome, protect, and integrate migrants, while simultaneously addressing the root causes that force individuals to flee their home countries.
* **Wimbledon Setbacks:** The tennis world faces significant disruption as Serena Williams has officially withdrawn from her planned doubles appearance with her sister Venus due to a recurring knee injury. This follows a major upset in the singles bracket, where defending champion Iga Świątek was eliminated in the third round by Alexandra Eala.

## POLAND

* **Political Tensions Rise at PiS Convention:** Przemysław Czarnek, a leading figure within the Law and Justice (PiS) party, utilized the party's recent convention to deliver a confrontational address. Czarnek criticized political opponents, specifically targeting Rafał Trzaskowski and Prime Minister Donald Tusk, while positioning conservative values as the benchmark for a "normal society."
* **Tragic Loss in Polish Sports:** The sports community is in mourning following the death of Grzegorz Miętus at the age of 33. A former ski jumper and dedicated coach for the national junior team, his passing was confirmed by the Polish Ski Association, marking a premature end to a career that significantly influenced the development of young Polish athletes.
