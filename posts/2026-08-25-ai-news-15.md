## WORLD

*   **High-Stakes Diplomacy in Moscow:** Intelligence channels remain active as the CIA chief has arrived in Moscow for unannounced talks. The visit, confirmed by flight-tracking data, occurs against a backdrop of escalating global tensions, with the Vatican simultaneously urging for a diplomatic resolution to the Russia-Ukraine conflict.
*   **Escalating Global Trade Conflict:** The international economic landscape is fracturing further as Canada initiates a robust retaliatory response to recent U.S. tariffs. By imposing levies of up to 50% on American goods, Ottawa aims to shield domestic manufacturers, signaling a broadening trade war.
*   **Sanctions and Geopolitical Friction:** The United States has ramped up pressure on international partners, threatening to isolate nations that continue to facilitate trade with Iran. The policy places significant strain on Beijing, a primary buyer of Iranian oil, creating a new focal point for U.S.-China economic confrontation.

## POLAND

*   **Combating Hybrid Warfare:** Authorities and intelligence experts have warned of a sophisticated disinformation campaign targeting the stability of Polish-Czech relations. Recent reports suggest that state-sponsored actors, suspected to be linked to Russia, are attempting to incite social unrest through the fabrication of public protests on the border.
*   **Honoring a Legacy:** Warsaw is preparing for the funeral of Wanda Traczyk-Stawska, a profound symbol of resistance and a national heroine. Her passing marks a significant moment of reflection for the country as it commemorates its modern history.
*   **Infrastructure Reforms Following Tragedy:** In direct response to a fatal road accident in Kraków, local authorities have announced urgent safety measures, including the installation of traffic calming devices and separators on streets identified as high-risk. The decision comes after years of public appeals for improved pedestrian safety in the area.
