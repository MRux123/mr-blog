## WORLD

* **Middle East Conflict Intensifies:** Tensions between the United States and Iran remain critical as a potential truce hangs in the balance. The Pentagon has reported that the direct cost of the conflict has surged to $29 billion, an increase of $4 billion in just two weeks, driven largely by urgent equipment repairs and military readiness requirements. U.S. Defense Secretary Pete Hegseth has signaled that further escalation remains an option if diplomatic efforts fail.
* **Legal and Accountability Measures:** In the aftermath of the Baltimore bridge collapse, the operator of the *Dali* cargo ship faces formal charges. Meanwhile, in Israel, the government has moved to establish a military tribunal to try individuals involved in the October 7, 2023, Hamas attacks, with the body authorized to issue death sentences in a series of proceedings to be broadcast live to the public.

## POLAND

* **Diplomatic Inquiry into Ziobro’s U.S. Travel:** The Polish Ministry of Foreign Affairs has officially requested clarification from the United States Embassy in Warsaw regarding the entry of former Justice Minister Zbigniew Ziobro into the U.S. The government is seeking information concerning the legal and factual basis for his presence in the country, marking a significant step in the ongoing scrutiny of the former official.
* **New Legislative Efforts Against "Pseudo-medicine":** The Polish government has introduced a new legislative project, colloquially dubbed "lex szarlatan" (lex charlatan). The proposed regulation aims to combat medical fraud by targeting individuals and entities operating as unlicensed medical specialists. Violators of these new standards will face severe financial penalties, with fines reaching up to one million Polish Zloty, as the state seeks to regulate the growing sector of alternative and unverified health services.
* **Passing of Stanisława Celińska:** The Polish cultural community is in mourning following the death of renowned actress and singer Stanisława Celińska at the age of 79. Celebrated for her versatile performances in films by directors such as Andrzej Wajda and Stanisław Bareja, Celińska leaves behind a legacy of profound artistic reflection and emotional depth.
