## WORLD

* **Total Solar Eclipse Sweeps Across Europe:** Large swathes of Europe, from Iceland to Spain, experienced a rare total solar eclipse today. The astronomical phenomenon drew massive crowds to viewing points, as the moon completely obscured the sun, marking a significant scientific and cultural event for the continent.
* **Escalation in Black Sea Port Attacks:** Shipping infrastructure in both Russia and Ukraine has come under renewed fire, with major grain export terminals suffering direct hits. This intensified military targeting is expected to further disrupt international supply chains and suppress grain exports from the region.
* **Trump Administration Advances Immigration Policy:** A federal judge has ruled in favor of the Trump administration’s efforts to terminate Temporary Protected Status (TPS) for South Sudanese nationals in the United States, following recent precedents regarding protections for other immigrant groups.

## POLAND

* **Massive Data Breach Affects Millions:** A critical cybersecurity incident has compromised the personal data of approximately 19 million Polish citizens. The official government portal, *bezpiecznedane.gov.pl*, which was intended to allow citizens to verify if they were affected by the breach, remains inaccessible, sparking widespread public concern.
* **Political Controversy Over Romanowski Case:** Former minister Marcin Romanowski has surfaced at the center of a new diplomatic and legal row, with a letter appearing to originate from the pro-Russian territory of Transnistria. The development has triggered intense debate regarding his location and the legality of his actions.
* **Marcin Budkowski Appointed Cadillac F1 Team Principal:** In a significant move for Polish representation in motorsports, Marcin Budkowski has been named the team principal for the Formula 1 newcomer, Cadillac. His appointment brings high-level executive experience from his previous roles at Ferrari, McLaren, and the FIA to the new racing outfit.
