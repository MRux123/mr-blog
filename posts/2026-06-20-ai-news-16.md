## WORLD

* **Diplomatic Rift Between Washington and Rome:** Tensions have flared between US President Donald Trump and Italian Prime Minister Giorgia Meloni. The public dispute centers on remarks made by Trump questioning Meloni’s popularity and implying desperation for recognition at the G7 summit. Meloni has hit back, firmly rebuking the US President and urging him to focus on his own domestic political challenges.
* **Bolivian State of Emergency:** The Bolivian government has formally declared a state of emergency following weeks of intense civil unrest. The ongoing protests have severely disrupted supply chains, leading to widespread shortages of essential goods and escalating concerns over the country’s stability.
* **Reparatory Justice Framework:** A significant milestone in international human rights was reached in Accra, Ghana, where a global framework for reparatory justice was adopted. African and Caribbean leaders have established a 19-point roadmap, calling for formal apologies and addressing the historical legacies of the transatlantic slave trade.

## POLAND

* **Diplomatic Crisis Over Order of the White Eagle:** A major escalation has occurred in Polish-Ukrainian relations after Polish President Karol Nawrocki officially rescinded the Order of the White Eagle, previously awarded to Ukrainian President Volodymyr Zelenskyy.
* **The "Threshold of Pain":** President Nawrocki justified the revocation by citing a "threshold of pain" regarding historical memory, specifically referencing the controversial decision by a Ukrainian military unit to name itself after figures associated with the UPA. In a swift response, President Zelenskyy confirmed that the insignia has already been returned to the Polish state, marking a definitive rupture in this chapter of bilateral relations.
