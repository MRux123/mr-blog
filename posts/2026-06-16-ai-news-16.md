## WORLD

*   **US-Iran Preliminary Deal:** A significant diplomatic breakthrough has been reached between Washington and Tehran. While the US administration, led by President Trump, expresses hope that the conflict will soon be relegated to the "rearview mirror," the domestic reaction in Iran remains complex, with the government framing the deal as a victory while citizens view it primarily as a necessity for economic relief. 
*   **G7 Summit and Geopolitical Fractures:** Despite the easing of tensions in the Middle East, discord persists among Western allies. President Trump has notably downplayed the impact of the ongoing war in Ukraine on US interests, highlighting deepening divisions within the G7 regarding security priorities and international alliances.
*   **Maritime Escalation:** The UK government has launched an investigation into reports that a Russian frigate, the *Admiral Grigorovich*, fired warning shots at a yacht in the English Channel. This incident underscores the increasing frequency of provocative military maneuvers in strategic European waters.

## POLAND

*   **PKOl Leadership Crisis:** The Polish Olympic Committee (PKOl) is facing an existential governance crisis. Following the rejection of board reports during the General Assembly, a majority of delegates are calling for an extraordinary session to oust president Radosław Piesiewicz. The dispute has moved into legal territory, with both the management and the opposition preparing for potential litigation.
*   **Rising Political Extremism in Europe:** Domestic concern in Poland is mirrored by broader regional trends, specifically the record-breaking polling numbers for the *Alternative for Germany* (AfD). The surge of the far-right in Germany signals a period of profound social and political volatility that is likely to impact bilateral relations and EU policy.
*   **Political Violence:** Concerns regarding the safety of political dissidents in Poland have been heightened following the targeted killing of a Russian artist and vocal Vladimir Putin critic, known for his satirical caricatures. Authorities are currently investigating the circumstances surrounding his death.
