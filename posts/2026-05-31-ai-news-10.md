## WORLD

* **Colombia's High-Stakes Presidential Election:** Colombia heads to the polls in a pivotal election that marks a potential turning point in international relations. The race, which pits current leftist leadership against rising conservative forces, is being closely monitored for its implications on the nation's fragile diplomatic and economic ties with the United States following a period of public strain between President Gustavo Petro and former U.S. President Donald Trump.
* **Escalating Conflict in the Ukraine-Russia War:** The conflict has entered a new phase of intensification, with significant aerial engagements reported. Ukraine launched drone strikes against critical energy infrastructure, including a refinery in Saratov, while Russian forces responded with a massive barrage of nearly 230 drones across Ukraine, resulting in civilian casualties and widespread infrastructure damage.
* **Civil Unrest in France:** Following the Champions League, France experienced severe civil disturbances, resulting in hundreds of arrests and over 200 injuries, including a significant number of police officers. The scale of the violence highlights ongoing security challenges in managing large-scale sporting events.

## POLAND

* **Security Crackdown on Bomb Threats:** Polish authorities have expanded their investigation into a series of false bomb threats that have plagued the country. The latest development includes the detention of a prominent Warsaw attorney, marking a significant escalation in the government's effort to curb these disruptive activities.
* **Political Polarization Intensifies:** Domestic political discourse remains heated, as evidenced by public exchanges between key figures. The debate surrounding the rescinding of state honors, involving high-level government officials and opposition members, underscores the deep ideological divisions currently shaping the Polish political landscape.
* **Spectacular Feat in Warsaw:** On a lighter note, Estonian slackliner Jaan Roose successfully completed a high-altitude walk between the Palace of Culture and Science and the Varso Tower. The feat, performed 180 meters above the ground, served as a notable moment of public interest in the capital.
