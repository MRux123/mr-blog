## WORLD

* **High-Stakes Diplomatic Signals Between US and Iran:** As the ceasefire between the United States and Iran nears its expiration, both nations are engaging in high-stakes signaling. President Trump has indicated that an extension is unlikely, while Vice President JD Vance is set to lead a delegation to Pakistan in a bid to secure a regional peace deal. Private overtures from Tehran suggest a potential willingness to engage in talks if the U.S. demonstrates serious intent, though the situation in the Strait of Hormuz remains volatile.
* **Escalating Tensions in Lebanon:** A diplomatic incident has erupted following the vandalism of a statue of Jesus by an Israeli soldier in Lebanon. The Israeli military has condemned the act as a grave violation, while the U.S. ambassador has demanded immediate accountability to prevent further regional instability.
* **Violence at Teotihuacan:** A tragic shooting incident has occurred at the Pyramid of the Moon in the historic site of Teotihuacan, Mexico. Reports confirm two fatalities and multiple injuries, prompting an urgent investigation into the security breach at the internationally recognized landmark.

## POLAND

* **Tragic Fall in Bydgoszcz:** A four-year-old child has died after falling from a fifth-story balcony in Bydgoszcz. Local authorities, under the supervision of the prosecutor's office, have launched an investigation to determine the circumstances surrounding the fatal incident.
* **Potential Energy Supply Breakthrough:** Reports indicate a possible resolution to the interruption of the "Druzhba" pipeline. Following appeals from Hungarian political leadership, Ukraine is reportedly preparing to restore oil transit operations, which could ease energy concerns across the region by Tuesday.
* **Political Shifts and Polling Trends:** A new United Survey poll reveals that a political faction led by Mateusz Morawiecki would secure 5.1% of the vote if it were to run independently. This result would translate into approximately 14 seats in the Sejm, signaling a potential shift in the Polish parliamentary landscape.
