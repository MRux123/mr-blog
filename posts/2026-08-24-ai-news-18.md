## WORLD

* **Escalation in Ukraine-Russia Tensions:** British Prime Minister Burnham has reaffirmed his commitment to supporting Ukraine despite explicit threats from Moscow. The Kremlin's warnings follow reports of Kyiv utilizing British-supplied drone technology, marking a significant point of contention in the ongoing conflict.
* **Human Rights Controversy at African Parks:** Prince Harry has stepped down from the board of the wildlife conservation charity African Parks. The move follows a formal admission by the organization that its rangers were involved in human rights abuses within the Republic of the Congo.
* **Violence Escalates in Haiti:** A violent surge in gang activity in Port-au-Prince has resulted in numerous civilian casualties. Reports confirm that armed groups have been targeting residential areas, including the burning of homes, exacerbating the humanitarian crisis in the region.

## POLAND

* **Political Shift in Voter Sentiment:** A new CBOS poll indicates that the Law and Justice (PiS) party has recorded its lowest support levels since the 2023 parliamentary elections. Meanwhile, the Civic Coalition remains the frontrunner, as several other parties struggle to clear the electoral threshold.
* **Escalating Political Rhetoric:** Former Prime Minister Mateusz Morawiecki has issued a stern warning regarding the "Zondacrypto" scandal, alleging that testimonies from the exchange’s leadership could have severe repercussions for the current government. He also advised his parliamentary club to uphold the President’s veto concerning the "status of the closest person" bill.
* **Criminal Investigation in Kraków:** Polish authorities have detained two individuals in connection with the fatal beating of a 36-year-old man in Kraków. Police are currently working to determine the specific roles of the suspects in the incident.
