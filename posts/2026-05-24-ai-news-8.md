## WORLD

* **Escalation in Ukraine:** A major Russian offensive has resulted in at least four fatalities and dozens of injuries. Reports indicate the use of the Oreshnik missile, a hypersonic weapon capable of traveling at ten times the speed of sound, marking a significant intensification in the ongoing conflict.
* **Global Surge in "Food-Related Violence":** A concerning analysis reveals that hunger is increasingly being weaponized in global conflicts. Since 2018, over 20,000 incidents have been recorded involving attacks on markets, farmland, and food distribution systems, signaling a deterioration in the protection of civilian essentials during wartime.

## POLAND

* **Security Alert Following Harassment of Presidential Family:** Government officials, including Prime Minister Donald Tusk, have launched a high-level investigation into a series of false emergency alarms directed at the home of President Karol Nawrocki. The government has characterized these provocations as a direct threat to state stability and has ordered security services to expedite the identification and apprehension of the perpetrators.
* **Heightened Military Readiness:** In response to the intensified Russian aerial strikes against neighboring Ukraine, the Polish Armed Forces have mobilized, including the scrambling of fighter jets, to monitor and secure the integrity of the national airspace.
